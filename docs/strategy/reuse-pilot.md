# Reuse pilot kit (GM-039; plan day-30 and day-90 gates)

Ready to run, not started. This kit puts the reuse-and-comparison stage of
[validation.md](../specs/senior-first/validation.md) into practice and answers two
questions from [the plan](../plan/README.md):

- **Reuse gate (day 30):** do people come back to Golden Moments on another day without being
  reminded? Continue if at least 8 of 20 use units return voluntarily and at
  least 12 would choose it for their next comparable session.
- **Segment decision (day 90):** do activity staff get more repeat value than families? If staff show at
  least twice the return or print rate, they become the first segment.

These thresholds are chosen product decisions, not benchmarks or clinical
endpoints. A small convenience sample supports iteration, not a market claim.

Everything that contacts a person (sending messages, any thank-you gift,
consent wording) is the maintainer's action. Nothing here has been sent.

## Who

| Group | Use units | Where to find them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family caregivers using it with someone at home | 12 | Memory cafés, caregiver support groups, Area Agencies on Aging, organizations already contacted in [link-building.md](link-building.md) |
| Activity staff (memory care, adult day, library memory programs) | 8 | Activity directors at local programs; the same organizations |

A use unit is one caregiver and the person they spend time with, or one staff
member and their group. Count a pair once, and record the helper's and the
participant's responses separately. Do not ask for a diagnosis, stage or age.

## Safeguards (from validation.md)

- Explain what the pilot is, what is collected and that anyone can stop at any
  time. Get consent from the caregiver or staff member and ongoing assent from
  the person living with dementia; their wish to stop overrides everything.
- Collect no diagnoses, no photos, no recordings and no names in the
  repository. Use codes (F01–F12, S01–S08). Keep the contact sheet off GitHub
  and delete it after the analysis.
- No reminders from the team during the 7 days. Voluntary reuse is the
  measure; a reminder would invalidate it. The app's own optional reminder is
  fine if the caregiver chooses to turn it on, and must be recorded.
- Any medical misunderstanding, data surprise or distress is reported and
  stops that unit's participation.

## What participants do

1. **Day 0:** a short message or call. Share the website
   (`https://dementiaactivities.games/games/`) and the app store links. Suggest
   one first activity: Picture pairs with four tiles and pictures showing, or
   Photo pairs / Your family photos if they want to use their own pictures.
   Say plainly that using it again is entirely up to them.
2. **Days 1–7:** use it, or not, as they wish. On days they do something else
   together, that alternative is part of the data.
3. **Day 7:** answer six questions (below) by reply, form or a 10-minute call.

## Day 7 questions

1. On which days did you use Golden Moments (web, app or printout)? Did anyone
   remind you, including the app's reminder?
2. Which activities did you use, and on which device or on paper?
3. How did it go: well, partly, or not well? What made the difference?
4. On the other days, what did you do together instead?
5. Next time you want something to do together, would you choose Golden
   Moments, the alternative from question 4, or something else? Why?
6. What got in the way, confused you, or felt wrong?

For staff, add: how many people took part in a session, and whether you printed
anything.

## Record sheet (kept off GitHub)

| Code | Group | Days used (1–7) | Reminded? | Activities | Device / paper | Went well / partly / not well | Alternative used | Next choice | Barriers | Printed? | Stopped early? Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Returned voluntarily** = used on at least one day after day 0 without a
reminder from the team. **Prefers GM** = chose Golden Moments in question 5.
Report every denominator, including people who did not reply; a missing
answer is not a success.

## Message drafts (the maintainer sends)

### To an organization

> Subject: A free activity resource, and a small 7-day test
>
> Hello [name],
>
> I make Golden Moments, free picture games and printable activities for people
> living with dementia and the people who spend time with them
> (dementiaactivities.games). There are no accounts, ads or fees.
>
> I'm looking for about 20 families or activity staff to try it for a week and
> tell me honestly whether it was useful, including if it wasn't. It takes one
> short message at the start and six questions at the end. I don't collect
> diagnoses, photos or names.
>
> Would you be willing to share this with families or staff who might want to
> take part? I'm happy to send a one-page handout.
>
> Thank you,
> [name]

### To a caregiver or staff member

> Thank you for offering to try Golden Moments. For the next 7 days, use it only
> if and when you want to. That is exactly what I'm trying to learn. A good
> first activity is Picture pairs with four tiles: [link]. If you'd like to use
> your own family photos, the app keeps them on your phone: [store link].
>
> On [date] I'll ask six short questions. You can stop at any time, and the
> person you care for should never feel they have to keep going.

### Day 7

> Thank you for this week. Six quick questions, and short answers are perfect:
> [questions 1–6]. If it didn't help, please say so; that is just as useful.

## After the pilot

1. Record the reuse and segment results in the [plan](../plan/README.md) scorecard (section 3) and day-30 gate (section 4)
   with the counts and denominators, and summarize barriers in WORKBOARD.
2. If the reuse gate fails, diagnose setup and activity fit before adding anything.
3. Do not quote participants publicly without separate written permission, and
   never as an endorsement of health benefit.
