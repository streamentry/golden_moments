Started 2026-10-25. Goal: earn editorial links from pages that caregivers and
activity staff already use, so the site gets found and trusted. Rules:

- Only pitch where the site genuinely fills a gap on the page. Name the page and
  the specific gap; no mass mail, no templates sent unchanged.
- No paid links, link exchanges, private blog networks, comment spam or
  self-promotion in caregiver communities. They break search guidelines and
  would undermine trust with the people we want to reach.
- One polite follow-up at most, after two weeks. Stop if anyone asks.
- Describe the site the way the site does: for enjoyment and time together, no
  medical or memory-improvement claims (see `site/CONTENT_GUIDE.md`).
- Every email is approved by the maintainer before it is sent.

## Linkable pages on the site

- `/for-organizations/`: how care homes, libraries, aging agencies and support
  groups use the site, with copy-ready descriptions for resource lists.
- `/handout/`: a one-page printable for support groups and caregiver packets.
- `/activities/trivia/`, `/activities/printable/`, `/games/picture-bingo/`: the
  pages activity staff most often look for.
