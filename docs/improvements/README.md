Goal: research trustworthy guidance about Alzheimer's, vascular, Lewy body, frontotemporal and mixed dementia; Huntington's disease, Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease, normal-pressure hydrocephalus and Parkinson's disease; and caregiver activities. Choose one evidence-led app or site improvement at a time, and record its source, gap, change, checks and remaining verification. Target: 500 improvements, with changed fixed copy covered in all 185 registered language codes.

- [sources.md](sources.md): what was read, what was only seen in search results, and what could not be reached.
- [audit-2026-11-07.md](audit-2026-11-07.md): the app and website measured against those sources, with file references.

Maintainer decision (2026-11-12): the target increased from 100 to 500 improvements; existing completed rounds carry forward.

Maintainer decision (2026-11-12): an improvement counts toward the 500 only when every user-facing text change is available in the 185 unique language codes in docs/languages.csv (English source plus 184 translated locales). Machine translations must pass the guide/locale validators and remain marked unreviewed. Earlier rounds need a coverage audit and backfill before the goal is complete.
