# Loop run 2026-11-08: research pass, no code change

Purpose: the 4-hour loop asked for a search of dementia and Alzheimer's sites, one best improvement, and a log here so later runs do not repeat work. This run read the existing log first ([TRACKING.md](TRACKING.md), [README.md](README.md) rounds 1 to 24, backlog G1 to G7) and then searched.

## Searched (standard search; snippets only, pages not opened)

1. Dementia-friendly app design guidance. Hits: a 2023 University of Twente thesis on touch-screen apps for older adults with dementia, Nesta's Dementia Citizens work, the Dementia-Friendly Home app, and the DementiaInduct best-practice guidance. Recurring points: co-design with people with dementia and carers, simple navigation, plain language with clear images, information in chunks, and self-paced interaction.
2. Alzheimer's Association caregiver resources. The Association offers some printed materials at a fourth-grade reading level on request. No public web-design or reading-level standard for websites was found.
3. Memory games and dementia activity sites, 2026. No independent accessibility audit was found. Results were SEO scores, vendor claims and affiliate reviews, so none was used as evidence.

Most of the points above are already covered by the existing rounds (touch targets, untimed play, plain sentences, chunked guides, self-paced gentle mode). They were not reopened.

## Candidates checked and not changed

- **Fourth-grade reading level for participant-facing app copy.** A rough Flesch-Kincaid pass over `lib/l10n/arb/app_en.arb` flagged 139 of 271 strings. Not acted on: the formula is unreliable for short labels, most flagged text is legal or privacy copy that needs legal review, the benchmark is for printed caregiver materials rather than app screens, and sentence length was already cut in round 2. Needs a maintainer decision on scope before any change.
- **"Next" and "Previous" on the picture pager** (backlog G5 remainder; `togetherNext` and `togetherPrevious` in `lib/features/gentle_activities/presentation/photo_together_page.dart`). The ARB notes say to keep these under about 12 characters. "Next picture" fits; "Previous picture" (16 characters) does not. Needs a layout decision.
- **`commonContinue` fallback** in `photo_collection_page.dart`. All three callers pass their own label, so the fallback is effectively unused. No change.
- **Pop-ups, interstitials and cookie banners on the website.** A search of `site/src` for popup, modal, dialog, cookie, newsletter, interstitial and toast found only the language menu and the 404 recovery code. None is a pop-up. A guard test would be possible but adds no fix.
- **Helpline on a Today screen.** The helpline already appears in the app's Today tips (`todayWornOutTry2`).

## Result

No application or website code changed in this run. Nothing was tested because nothing changed. The only change is this log and the pointers in [README.md](README.md) and [TRACKING.md](TRACKING.md).

## Decisions needed before another code round

1. Is there an approved reading-level target for participant-facing app copy, and does it exclude legal text?
2. May the picture pager use "Next picture" and "Previous picture" (16 characters) despite the 12-character note?

## Guidance for later runs

A new code round should start only from a new source family, or from one of the decisions above. Another round of high-contrast or forced-colors CSS tweaks, like rounds 6 to 24, is not supported by new evidence and should not be repeated.
