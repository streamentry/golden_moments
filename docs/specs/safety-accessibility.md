# Safety, accessibility and claims

A product-risk checklist, not legal advice or regulatory clearance.

## Intended use

Recreation, familiar pictures and optional conversation. Mentioning a disease describes the intended audience but does not establish efficacy or remove legal obligations. Review the build, metadata, screenshots, privacy and distribution together.

No improved-memory, disease-slowing, treatment, prevention, symptom-reduction, clinical-proof or universal-suitability claims. No medical badges, brain-age scores or fabricated testimonials. Unverified quotes in older English marketing are not reused.

## Respectful design

Start small with visible pictures. Do not force naming relatives, correct recollections, require completion or automatically increase difficulty. Prompts are optional. Looking or stopping is valid. Do not score a shared moment or infer disease progression.

## Device checklist

- Screen-reader focus, actions and states work; hidden content is not announced. Personal-photo labels are currently positional.
- Essential actions remain reachable at 200% text scale, portrait and landscape, iPhone and iPad.
- Verify contrast and at least 48 logical-pixel targets; matching is indicated by shape as well as color.
- Test cancellation, invalid/oversized images, low storage, missing files, restart and upgraded installations.
- Verify fresh offline start, distinguishing bundled/imported content from remote downloads.
- Audit native SDKs, permissions, privacy policy, App Privacy answers and advertising/age-rating declarations.

Record device, OS, build/commit, steps, result, tester and date in release evidence. Metadata validation, a mockup and a disclaimer are not device testing, clinical proof or legal approval.
