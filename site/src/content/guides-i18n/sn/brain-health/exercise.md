Kufamba-famba ndeimwe yetsika dzakanyanya kuongororwa mutsvakurudzo dzeutano hwehuropi. Tsvakurudzo dzinoratidza kuti kunogona kuve kwakabatana nenjodzi yakaderera yedementia, asi hadzina kuratidza kuti kurovedza muviri kunodzivirira dementia. Chiri pachena ndechekuti kufamba-famba kunobatsira kusimba kwemuviri, kuenzanisa, manzwiro nekurara pazera ripi zvaro. Peji rino rinotsanangura zvinotaurwa netsvakurudzo, kuti vakuru vanokurudzirwa kurovedza muviri zvakadii, uye dzimwe nzira dzakapfava dzekufamba-famba nemunhu anorarama nedementia.

## Kurovedza muviri kunoderedza njodzi yedementia here?

Tsvakurudzo dzinoratidza kuti vanhu vanofamba-famba kazhinji havawanzoonekwa vaine dementia, asi humbowo uhu hune painogumira. [Mushumo weLancet Commission wa2024 wedementia](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) unoti kusafamba-famba zvakakwana ndechimwe chezvinhu gumi nezvina zvinogona kuchinjwa. Pamwe chete, zvinhu izvi gumi nezvina zvinobata chikamu chinosvika 45% chedementia pasi rose mumuenzaniso wavo.

Hezvino zvinoita humbowo:

- **Tsvakurudzo dzekutarisa dzinoratidza divi rimwe chete.** Ongororo yetsvakurudzo 58, yakakurukurwa neCommission, yakaona kuti vanhu vanofamba-famba kazhinji vaive nenjodzi yakaderera inosvika 20% yedementia. Musiyano mukuru waive pakati pevanhu vaisambofamba-famba zvachose neavo vaiita zvishoma.
- **Ukama uhwu hunogona kushanda kumativi ose.** Commission inotaura kuti kusafamba-famba kunogona kukwidza njodzi. Inotaurawo kuti kuchinja kwehuropi kunotanga pakutanga kwedementia, kunotanga makore akawanda kusati kwaonekwa, kunogona kuita kuti vanhu vasanyanye kufamba-famba.
- **Miedzo haina kunyatsojeka.** Mune mumwe muedzo, vakuru 945 vaive neavhareji yemakore 78 vakakamurwa kuita mapoka: rimwe rakaita kurovedza muviri kaviri pavhiki richitariswa kwemakore mashanu, rimwe raive boka rekuenzanisa. Pakupera, hunyanzvi hwekufunga hahuna kusiyana zvakajeka pakati pemapoka. Vanhu vazhinji vaive muboka rekuenzanisa vaifambawo-famba, izvo zvinoita kuti mhedzisiro ive yakaoma kunzwisisa.

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know) inotaura zviri pachena. Hapana ukama hwakajeka hwakaratidzwa pakati pekurovedza muviri nekudzivirira Alzheimer's, uye hapana humbowo hwakakwana kubva mumiedzo hwekuti kurovedza muviri kunodzivirira kana kunonotsa chirwere ichi. Kunyange zvakadaro, kurovedza muviri kunoderedza njodzi yekudonha, kuora mwoyo, chirwere cheshuga, BP yakakwira nesitiroko. Mamwe emamiriro ezvinhu aya anobatanidzwawo nekudzikira kwekufunga.

Chidzidzo chakatendeseka ndechekuti fambai-fambai nekuti zvinobatsira kuti munzwe zviri nani uye muite zviri nani nhasi. Kubatsirika kupi zvako kuuropi mukana unogona kuvapo, kwete vimbiso.

## Vakuru vanofanira kurovedza muviri kwakadii?

[Nhungamiro dzeCDC dzevanhu vane makore 65 zvichikwira](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/older-adults.html) dzinokurudzira marudzi matatu ekurovedza muviri vhiki rega rega:

| Rudzi | Huwandu pavhiki | Mienzaniso |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kunosimbisa mwoyo | Maminitsi anosvika 150 ekurovedza muviri kuri pakati nepakati, sekuita maminitsi 30 pamazuva 5 | Kufamba netsoka nekukurumidza, kutamba, kurovedza muviri mumvura |
| Kunosimbisa tsandanyama | Mazuva anosvika 2, kuchishandiswa mapoka makuru etsandanyama | Kusimudza masimbi akareruka kana magaba esupu, kutambanudza mabhande |
| Kuenzanisa | Nguva dzose | Kufamba chitsitsinho pamberi pechigunwe, kusimuka kubva pachigaro |

Izvi zvinangwa, kwete zvinodiwa kuti munhu atange. CDC inoti kana musingakwanisi kuzvisvitsa, itai zvakanyanya sezvinobvumirwa nekugona kwenyu neutano hwenyu. Kuita zvishoma kuri nani pane kusaita.

## Nzira dzakapfava dzekufamba-famba dzevakuru

- **Fambai mune vamwe.** Kufamba zuva nezuva neshamwari, muvakidzani kana boka rekufamba kunowedzera hurukuro uye kuchengeteka.
- **Kurovedza muviri wakagara.** Kusimudza maoko, kutenderedza zviziso zvetsoka, kufamba-famba wakagara uye yoga yepachigaro kunosimbisa muviri pasina njodzi huru yekurasikirwa nekuenzanisa.
- **Tambai mukicheni.** Ridzai mimhanzi yenguva yeudiki hwemunhu iyeye mozunguza muviri, kuridza tsoka kana kutsika-tsika zvinyoro. Gwaro redu re[mabasa emimhanzi](/activities/music/) rine dzimwe pfungwa.
- **Simbisai muviri nezvinhu zvepamba.** Simudzai mabhodhoro emvura akazara kana magaba esupu muchiona chirongwa chamunofarira.
- **Rimai kana itai mabasa epamba.** Kudiridza zvirimwa, kurakisa, kutsvaira nekupeta hembe zvese zvinobatsira. Onai [mabasa ekunze nekurima gadheni](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) uye [mabasa epamba](/activities/household-tasks/).
- **Kurovedza muviri mumvura.** NIA inoti makirasi ekurovedza muviri mumvura isarudzo yakanaka kune vanhu vanoona kurovedza muviri pasi kwakaomera mapfundo avo.

## Maitiro ekurovedza muviri nemunhu anorarama nedementia

Kune munhu anorarama nedementia, chinangwa ndechekuti agadzikane, afare uye arambe achikwanisa kuita zvinhu zvezuva nezuva, kwete kusvika pahuwandu hwakati. NIA inoti kurovedza muviri kunobatsira kuchengetedza tsandanyama, mapfundo nemwoyo zvemunhu ane Alzheimer's zviri mumamiriro akanaka, uye kunogonawo kuvandudza manzwiro nekurara. Bvunza chiremba wavo musati matanga nzira itsva. Mazano anobva mu[gwaro reNIA revanochengeta](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) anosanganisira:

1. **Fambai mese zuva rega rega.** Sarudzai nzira yakachengeteka, yakajairika uye isina zvipingamupinyi. Kana munhu achifamba ari oga, bhandi reID rine nhamba yenyu yerunhare ipfungwa yakanaka.
2. **Edzai mavhidhiyo ekurovedza muviri evakuru.** Tevedzerai mese, makagara kana makamira.
3. **Itai zvidimbu zvipfupi.** Kurovedza muviri kupfupi kakawanda kunogona kushanda zviri nani pane kamwe chete kwakareba.
4. **Wedzerai mimhanzi.** Nziyo dzinonyatsozivikanwa dzinoita kuti kufamba-famba kunzwe sekunakidzwa, kwete basa. Tambai kana munhu achifarira.
5. **Ipai mvura neshangu dzakagadzikana.** Ipai mvura kana jusi panguva yekurovedza nepashure, uye sarudzai hembe dzakagadzikana neshangu dzakakodzera dzakagadzirirwa kurovedza muviri.

Kana kufamba netsoka kwakaoma, [gwaro reNIA rekuchengeta munhu ane Alzheimer's](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) rinokurudzira sarudzo dzekuita wakagara. Izvi zvinosanganisira kurovedza muviri kana yoga yepachigaro, kukanda masaga madiki, kukanda bhora rakapfava kana bharumu, kutambanudza mabhande, kusimudza zvinhu zvepamba zvakareruka, uye michina yekutsika netsoka wakagara. Mabasa ari nyore akaita sekutsvaira nekupukuta guruva anobatsirawo.

Ivai maonero echokwadi pamusoro pezvinogona kuitwa nekufamba-famba. Lancet Commission haina kuwana humbowo hwekuti kurovedza muviri kunoderedza kuchinja kwemaitiro kwakaita sekushushikana. Fambai-fambai mese nekuti zvinogona kunakidza, kwete senzira yekugadzirisa masikati akaoma. Kana munhu akati kwete, hazvina kuipa. Gwaro redu re[kana munhu akati kwete](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) rine nzira dzakapfava dzekuedza zvakare pane imwe nguva. Kupedza nguva isati yakwana, kana kungogara panze mese, mhedzisiro yakanaka zvakare.

Mushure mekufamba-famba, kumbomira zvinyoro-nyoro kwakaita se[Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) kunogona kubatsira kuti mese mudzikame. Kune chiitiko chekuita makagara chine kufamba-famba kwemaoko zvinyoro, [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) ne[Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) zvinoshanda zvakanaka patafura.

## Kuenzanisa nekuchengeteka pakudonha

Kudonha kunoitika kazhinji uye kunogona kuve kwakakomba. [CDC inoshuma](https://www.cdc.gov/falls/data-research/facts-stats/index.html) kuti vakuru vanopfuura mumwe chete pavana vanodonha gore rega rega, asi vashoma pane hafu vanoudza chiremba wavo. Kudonha kamwe chete kunoita kuti mukana wekudonhazve uwedzere kaviri. Zvinhu zvinowedzera njodzi izvo CDC inotaura zvinosanganisira:

- Kusasimba kwemakumbo nezvidya
- Kunetseka kufamba nekuenzanisa
- Mimwe mishonga, kusanganisira mishonga yekurara uye mimwe mishonga yekuora mwoyo
- Matambudziko ekuona
- Kurwadziwa kwetsoka kana shangu dzisina kunaka
- Njodzi dziri mumba dzakaita semhirizhonga, machira epasi anotsvedza nematanho akaputsika

Kuti mufambe-fambe zvakachengeteka:

- **Shandisai chigaro chakasimba** chisina mavhiri, uye chengetai kaunta kana njanji padyo kuti mubate kana muchisimuka.
- **Bvisai zvinhu munzvimbo yacho** zvakaita semachira epasi, tambo nezvinhu zvakapararira kutanga.
- **Nonokai pakusimuka**, uye mirai kana munhu achinzwa dzungu kana kusaenzana.
- **Pfekai shangu dzinotsigira tsoka**, kwete masokisi kana masiripa akasununguka.
- **Dzidzirai kuenzanisa muri pedyo nepekubata.** Kusimuka kubva pachigaro nekufamba chitsitsinho pamberi pechigunwe mienzaniso yeCDC yemabasa ekuenzanisa.

CDC inoti munhu mukuru anenge adonha uye arova musoro wake anofanira kuenda kuna chiremba nekukurumidza, kunyanya kana achinwa mishonga inoderedza kugwamba kweropa.

## Taurai nachiremba

Bvunza chiremba musati matanga nzira itsva yekurovedza muviri, kunyanya kumunhu ane matambudziko emwoyo kana mapapu, kurwadziwa kwemapfundo, matambudziko ekuenzanisa, kana dementia. Chiremba kana murapi wemuviri anogona kukurudzira mabasa akachengeteka uye kutarisa kana mishonga ichikanganisa kuenzanisa. Udza chiremba nezvekudonha kupi zvako, kunyange kusina kukuvadza. Kana chimwe chinhu chichirwadza kana kunzwa chisina kunaka panguva yekurovedza muviri, mirai. Kana paine kurwadziwa kwechipfuva kamwe kamwe, kunetseka kufema, kana kuvhiringika kamwe kamwe, fonerai 911.

Peji rino ruzivo rwakajairika, harisi zano rekurapwa. Onai [chiziviso chedu chezvekurapwa](/medical-disclaimer/).
