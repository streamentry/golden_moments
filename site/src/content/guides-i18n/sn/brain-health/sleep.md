Kurara neutano hweuropi zvakabatana zviri pachena, asi kuti zvakabatana sei kuchiri kuongororwa. Dzimwe ongororo dzinobatanidza kurara kwenguva pfupi nenjodzi yakakura yedementia. Dementia, zvakare, inowanzovhiringidza kurara. Peji rino rinotsanangura zvinoratidzwa netsvakurudzo nezvisingaratidzwi, rinopa tsika dzinobatsira pakurara, uye rinotaura nezvematambudziko ekurara anowanzoitika mudementia. Haripi zano nezvemishonga yekurara; hurukuro iyoyo inofanira kuitwa nachiremba.

## Kurara kusina kunaka kwakabatana nedementia here?

Dzimwe ongororo dzenguva refu dzinobatanidza kurara kwenguva pfupi nenjodzi yakakura yedementia, asi humbowo hahuna kugadzikana.

- **Ongororo yekuUK yemakore 25.** Vatsvakurudzi vakatevera vakuru vangangosvika 8,000 mu[ongororo yeWhitehall II](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22354-2). Vanhu vairara kwemaawa matanhatu kana mashoma pazera remakore 50 kana 60 vaiva nenjodzi yakakura yedementia gare gare kupfuura avo vairara kwemaawa manomwe. Vanhu vairara kwemaawa matanhatu kana mashoma pazera re50, 60 ne70 vaiva nenjodzi yakakura inenge 30% kupfuura avo vaigara vachirara kwemaawa manomwe. Iyi yaiva ongororo yekutarisa, saka inoratidza kubatana, kwete chikonzero.
- **Maonero eDare reLancet.** [Dare reLancet ra2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) harina kuwedzera kurara parunyorwa rwaro rwezvinhu 14 zvinogona kuchinjwa zvenjodzi. Rakaona kuti humbowo hahuna kunyatsoenderana. Chimwe chikonzero ndechekuti shanduko muuropi dzinokonzerwa nedementia dzinotanga makore akawanda isati yaonekwa uye dzinogona kuvhiringidza kurara. Muongororo imwe huru, kubatana kuri pakati pekurara kwenguva refu nedementia kwakanyangarika pakabviswa makore mashanu ekutanga ekutevera.

Saka husiku humwe kana huviri husina kurara zvakanaka hahusi chikonzero chekunetseka. Kurara zvakanaka nguva dzose kwakakodzera kuedza kuita nekuti kunobatsira manzwiro, kumuka zvakanaka uye kuchengeteka nhasi.

## Tsvakurudzo ye“kucheneswa kweuropi” pakurara chii?

Pamwe makambonzwa kuti kurara “kunoshambidza” uropi. Pfungwa iyi inobva mutsvakurudzo yechokwadi, asi ichiri kutanga.

- **Mumakonzo.** Ongororo ya2013 [yakatsanangurwa neNIH](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/brain-may-flush-out-toxins-during-sleep) yakawana kuti nzvimbo iri pakati pemasero europi yakakura neinenge 60% makonzo paairara. Mvura yaifamba nyore nyore muuropi, kuburikidza nezvinonzi nevatsvakurudzi glymphatic system. Beta-amyloid, puroteni yakabatana neAlzheimer’s, yaibviswa nekukurumidza muuropi hwemakonzo airara.
- **Muvanhu.** [Ongororo diki ya2018](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1721694115) yakaongorora vakuru 20 vane hutano vane makore 22 kusvika 72 mushure mehusiku hwakajairika uye mushure mehusiku vasina kurara. Mushure mehusiku husina kurara, chiratidzo chekuongorora uropi chebeta-amyloid chakakwira neinenge 5% munzvimbo inosanganisira hippocampus, chikamu chakakosha pakurangarira. Vanyori vakati uhwu humbowo hwekutanga. Havana kuziva kana shanduko iyi inopera mushure mekutandara, kana kuti inoratidza kubviswa kunononoka kana kugadzirwa kwakawanda kwebeta-amyloid.
- **Zvinoshanda kumativi ose.** Dare reLancet rinotaura kuti kuungana kwebeta-amyloid kunogonawo kuvhiringidza kurara muvakuru.

Zvakawanikwa izvi zvinobatsira kutsanangura nei vatsvakurudzi vachifarira kurara. Hazviratidzi kuti kurara zviri nani kunodzivirira Alzheimer’s.

## Ndedzipi tsika dzekurara dzingabatsira?

Vakuru vanoda maawa angangoita 7 kusvika ku9 ekurara husiku, zvakafanana nevamwe vakuru, sekureva kwe[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/sleep/sleep-and-older-adults). Mazano ayo anosanganisira:

- **Chengetai nguva yakafanana.** Endai kunorara uye mukai panguva imwe chete zuva rega rega, kunyange pakupera kwevhiki.
- **Ivai nemuitiro wekudzikama musati marara.** Verengai, teererai mimhanzi inonyaradza kana kugeza nemvura inodziya musati marara.
- **Itai kuti imba yekurara ive yakasununguka,** isinganyanyi kupisa kana kutonhora.
- **Deredzai caffeine masikati kana manheru.** Kofi, tii, soda nechokoreti zvinogona kukutadzisai kurara.
- **Dzivisai doro,** kunyange hushoma, sezvinorairwa neNIA.
- **Dzivisai kudya kwakawanda mumaawa maviri kusvika matatu musati marara,** uye kunwa zvakawanda manheru.
- **Itai maekisesaizi nguva dzose, asi kwete mumaawa matatu musati marara.** Onai [gwara redu remaekisesaizi](/brain-health/exercise/).
- **Siyai zviratidziro kunze kweimba yekurara.** NIA inokurudzira kusatarisa TV kana kushandisa kombiyuta, foni kana piritsi imomo.
- **Dzivisai kurara masikati kwenguva refu kana manheru** kana zvichibvira.

## Matambudziko ekurara mudementia

Shanduko pakurara dzinowanzoitika mudementia. [NIA inonyora](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/sleep/managing-sleep-problems-alzheimers-disease) kurara zvakanyanya kana kushoma, kumuka kakawanda husiku, uye kurara masikati zvakanyanya. Inotsanangurawo sundowning: kusagadzikana, kushushikana kana kuvhiringika kunotanga kana kuwedzera chiedza chezuva pachinoderera.

Mazano evanochengera anobva kuNIA anosanganisira:

1. **Wanai chiedza chemasikati nekufamba-famba mangwanani.** Endai panze kana kugara pedyo nehwindo masikati, uye batsirai munhu kuita zvinhu zuva rega rega. Chiedza chakajeka chinogona kubatsira kugadzirisa wachi yemuviri.
2. **Rongai zviitiko zvine simba mangwanani.** Edzai kugeza mangwanani kana kudya kudya kukuru kwemasikati.
3. **Chengetai kurara kwemasikati kupfupi uye mangwanani.** Deredzai kutsumwaira masikati kwave kunopera.
4. **Itai kuti manheru ave akadzikama.** Deredzai mwenje, deredzai ruzha uye ridzai mimhanzi inonyaradza kana munhu achiifarira. Gwara redu re[kusagadzikana masikati kwave kunopera](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) rine mamwe mazano.
5. **Chengetai muitiro wakafanana wekurara.** Kuverengera zvinonzwika panguva imwe chete husiku hwega hwega muenzaniso unopiwa neNIA. [Muitiro wakafanana wezuva nezuva](/activities/daily-routine/) unobatsirawo.
6. **Deredzai caffeine nedoro.**
7. **Shandisai mwenje midiki yehusiku** muimba yekurara, mukoridho nemuimba yekugezera.

Pakumuka husiku, [mazano ekuchengeteka pamba eNIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips) anosanganisira mwenje midiki yehusiku kana masensa emwenje anozvibatidza, mazambara ekubata muimba yekugezera, tepi ine ruvara rwakajeka pamipendero yematanho, uye mishonga yakachengetwa munzvimbo yakakiyiwa.

Chiitiko chipfupi, chakanyarara manheru chinogona kubatsira imi mose kudzikama. Edzai kumbomira zvishoma ne[Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) kana makadhi mashoma akasununguka e[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/). Siyai chero chinhu chinomutsa pfungwa nguva yekurara isati yasvika, uye mirai chero nguva kana munhu anzwa kuti zvakwana.

Kurara kunovhiringidzwa kunonetesa vanochengera zvakare. [Gwara redu rekuzvichengetera kwevanochengera](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinotaura nezvedzimwe nzira dzekuwana zororo nerubatsiro.

## Ko kumbomira kufema pakurara?

Kumbomira kufema pakurara kunoita kuti kufema kumbomira kwenguva pfupi pakurara, kazhinji kakawanda husiku humwe. NIA inoti kana kukasarapwa, kunogona kukonzera matambudziko akadai seropa rakakwira, sitiroko kana kurasikirwa nendangariro. Zviratidzo zvinosanganisira kuhope zvakanyanya uye kunzwa kurara masikati, uye vanhu vazhinji havazivi kuti vanazvo. Dare reLancet rinotsanangura ongororo yezvidzidzo 11 umo vanhu vane kumbomira kufema pakurara vaiva nenjodzi yakakura inenge 43% yedementia. Zvisinei, zvidzidzo zvishoma zveizvi zvakatarisa huremu hwemuviri, saka kubatana uku hakuna kunyatsojeka.

Kumbomira kufema pakurara kunogona kuonekwa uye kurapwa. NIA inotaura kuti muchina weCPAP unowanzobatsira. Kana imi kana munhu wamunochengera muchihope zvakanyanya, mukambomira kufema pakurara, kana mukanzwa kurara zvakanyanya masikati, udzai chiremba.

## Chinyorwa nezvemishonga yekurara

Hatipi zano nezvemishonga yekurara kana zvinowedzera. NIA inotaura kuti mishonga yekurara inogona kubatsira kwenguva pfupi asi haisi mushonga wekushaya hope. Inotaurawo kuti mimwe mishonga inopiwa kubatsira vanhu vane Alzheimer’s kurara inogona kuwedzera njodzi yekushaya simba, kudonha uye kuvhiringika. Musatange, kumisa kana kuchinja chero mushonga wekurara, kusanganisira zvinotengwa pasina gwaro rechiremba, musina kutaura nachiremba kana wemishonga.

## Taurai nachiremba

Endai kuna chiremba kana imi kana munhu wamunochengera muchigara makaneta, muine dambudziko rekurara husiku huzhinji, muchihope zvakanyanya, kana muine kumbomira kufema husiku. Kana matambudziko ekurara mudementia achiramba aripo, [NIA inorayira](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) kutsvaga zano rekurapwa. Kuongororwa kunogona kuwana zvikonzero zvakadai sekurwadziwa, rimwe dambudziko rekurara, chirwere kana mhedzisiro yemushonga. Kuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwatanga, kana shanduko yekurara inongoerekana yaitika pamwe nekuvhiringika, zvinoda rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi. Verengai nezve[nguva yekuona chiremba](/brain-health/warning-signs/).

Peji rino ruzivo rwakajairika, harisi zano rekurapwa. Onai [chiziviso chedu chekurapwa](/medical-disclaimer/).
