Mitambo yepfungwa iri kwese kwese, uye mizhinji inovimbisa kuchengetedza pfungwa dzako dziri nyowani. Kana uchitarisira munhu anorarama nedementia, kana kuti uchinetseka nezvekurangarira kwako, zvakajairika kushamisika kana kuitamba kungabatsira. Hezvino zvinoratidzwa netsvagiridzo chaizvo, kusanganisira zvikamu zvinopa tariro nezvikamu zvisingadaro.

Iyi iruzivo rwemazhinji, kwete zano rekurapa. Ona [chiziviso chedu chezvekurapa](/medical-disclaimer/).

## Mitambo yepfungwa inodzivirira dementia here?

Hapana mutambo wepfungwa wakaratidzwa kuti unodzivirira dementia. National Institute on Aging (NIA) inoyambira vanhu kuti vangwarire zvirevo zvekuti mitambo yepakombuta nepaindaneti inogona kuvandudza kurangarira, uye inoti hapana humbowo hwakakwana hwekuti chero app yekudzidziswa kwepfungwa yekutengesa ine mhedzisiro yakafanana nekudzidziswa kwakaedzwa muzvidzidzo zvekiriniki ([NIA nezvekudzivirira Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Izvi hazvirevi kuti mitambo haina basa. Zvinoreva kuti kukosha kwayo kuri mukunakidzwa, kuva pamwe chete uye tsika yemazuva ese, kwete mukudzivirira chero munhu kubva kudementia.

## Chii chinonzi “near transfer”, uye nei zvichikosha?

Paunodzidzira basa, kazhinji unova nani pabasa iroro. Vatsvagurudzi vanoti ichi chiitiko chekudzidziswa. Mubvunzo wakaoma ndewekuti kuvandudzika uku **kunoenda** kune zvimwe zvinhu here: kumabasa akafanana (near transfer) kana kuupenyu hwezuva nezuva, sekurangarira nguva dzekusangana nachiremba kana kutarisira mari (far transfer).

Ongororo huru ya2016 yakaongorora zvidzidzo izvo makambani ekudzidziswa kwepfungwa aiti humbowo hwavo hwakanakisa. Yakaona humbowo hwakawanda hwekuti vanhu vanovandudza pamabasa avakadzidzira, humbowo hushoma hwekuti vanovandudza pamabasa akada kufanana, uye humbowo hushoma kwazvo hwekuti kudzidziswa kunovandudza kufunga kwezuva nezuva. Zvidzidzo zvakawanda zvakare zvaiva nematambudziko ekurongwa kwazvo zvaiita kuti mhedziso dzakasimba dzisakwanise kuitwa ([Simons nevamwe, Psychological Science in the Public Interest](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100616661983)).

## Ongororo dzinotii nezvekudzidziswa kwepfungwa pakombuta?

Ongororo dzeCochrane, dzinounganidza zvidzidzo zvakanakisisa zviripo, dzakaongorora kudzidziswa kwepakombuta kunotora mavhiki gumi nemaviri kana kupfuura:

- **Vakuru vane hutano vane makore makumi matanhatu nemashanu kana kupfuura:** zvidzidzo zvisere zvine vanhu 1,183. Humbowo hwacho hwaiva hwemhando yakaderera kana yakaderera kwazvo. Panogona kuva nebhenefiti diki pakarepo mushure mekudzidziswa, pasina humbowo hwakajeka hwekuti inogara kwenguva refu ([Cochrane review, vakuru vane hutano](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32104914/)).
- **Vanhu vane mild cognitive impairment:** zvidzidzo zvisere zvine vanhu 660. Hapana chidzidzo chakataura kana vanhu vakazoita dementia, uye humbowo hwakanga husina kusimba zvekuti paitwe mhedziso ([Cochrane review, mild cognitive impairment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/)).

## ACTIVE trial yakawanei?

ACTIVE ichidzidzo chikuru chekuUS chakatanga muna 1998 chine vakuru 2,802 vane makore makumi matanhatu nemashanu kana kupfuura vaigara vakazvimiririra uye vasina matambudziko makuru ekurangarira kana ekufunga. Vanhu vakagoverwa nenzira isina kurerekera kune rimwe remhando nhatu dzekudzidziswa mumapoka madiki (speed of processing, memory, kana reasoning) kana kuboka risina kubatwa. Kudzidziswa kwaiva kwezvikamu zvinosvika gumi mumavhiki mashanu kusvika matanhatu. Vamwe vanhu vakapedza zvikamu zvakawanda vakapiwawo nenzira isina kurerekera mukana we **zvikamu zvebooster** makore angaita rimwe kusvika matatu gare gare.

Kudzidziswa kwekumhanya kwaisanganisira kuona nekugadzirisa ruzivo rwekuona munguva pfupi iri kuramba ichidzikira, padanho raienderana nekuvandudzika kwevanhu.

Muna 2026, vatsvagurudzi vakabatanidza vanhu 2,021 vaipinda nemarekodhi avo eMedicare kwemakore makumi maviri ([ongororo yeACTIVE yemakore makumi maviri](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12884427/)). Hezvino zvavakawana chaizvo:

- **Kudzidziswa kwekumhanya pamwe nechikamu chimwe chebooster kana kupfuura:** chiyero chakaderera chekuonekwa kwedementia pane boka risina kubatwa (hazard ratio ye0.75, zvinoreva kuti chiyero chakaderera nezvikamu zvinoda kusvika makumi maviri nezvishanu kubva muzana).
- **Kudzidziswa kwekumhanya pasina booster:** hapana chiyero chakaderera.
- **Kudzidziswa kwekurangarira kana kufunga:** hapana mhedzisiro yakakosha pakuonekwa kwedementia.
- **Boka rega rega rekudzidziswa rose richienzaniswa neboka risina kubatwa:** paiva nehunhu hwekudzikira kwezviyero, asi zvaiva zvisina kukosha pazviverengero.

### Miganhu yechawanikwa ichi

- Inoshanda ku **rudzi rumwe chete rwekudzidziswa kwekumhanya rwakatarisirwa pamwe nezvikamu zvebooster**, kwete kumitambo yepfungwa yose.
- Mhedzisiro yakakosha yakabva mu **boka diki** (vanhu vakapedza zvikamu zvakawanda zvekutanga uye vakapiwa mukana webooster), kwete kubva kuboka rose rekumhanya.
- Dementia yakayerwa pachishandiswa **makodhi ekuonekwa mumarekodhi eMedicare**, kwete nekuongorora munhu mumwe nemumwe. Vanhu vangaita chikamu chimwe muzvina chevakatanga vakasiyiwa kunze nekuti vaiva muMedicare Advantage, iyo isina marekodhi akazara.
- Vaipinda vakanga vasina matambudziko makuru ekufunga pavaikatanga. Mhedzisiro iyi haitaure nezvemitambo yevanhu **vatova kurarama nedementia**.

Ichi chawanikwa chinonakidza uye chinoda kumwe kutsvagisa. Hachisi humbowo hwekuti chero app inodzivirira dementia.

## FTC yakatii nezveLumosity?

Muna Ndira 2016, Lumos Labs, kambani inoita Lumosity, yakabvuma kubhadhara mamiriyoni maviri emadhora kugadzirisa mhosva dzeFederal Trade Commission dzekushambadzira kunotsausa. FTC yakati kambani iyi yakaita zvirevo, isina humbowo hwakakwana hwesainzi, zvekuti mitambo yayo yaigona kuvandudza kushanda kubasa nekuchikoro uye yaigona kunonotsa kudzikira kunouya nezera, mild cognitive impairment, dementia neAlzheimer's. Murairo uyu unoda kuti kambani ive neumbowo hwesainzi hwakavimbika uye hwakakodzera isati yaita zvirevo zvakadaro mune ramangwana ([chiziviso cheFTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program)).

Chidzidzo kumhuri: ngwarirai chero mutambo, app kana chirongwa chinovimbisa kuchengetedza kurangarira kana kudzivirira dementia.

## Mitambo inogona kubatsira munhu atova nedementia here?

Inogona kuwedzera kunaka kwezuva, uye izvo zvakakosha zvikuru. Nzira yakadzidzwa zvakanyanya inonzi **cognitive stimulation**: zviitiko zvinonakidza zvemuboka zvakaita sehurukuro, kuyeuka zvakapfuura, mitambo yemashoko, mapuzzle nemimhanzi, kazhinji muboka diki rinotungamirirwa nemufambisi.

Ongororo yeCochrane ya2023 yezvidzidzo makumi matatu nezvinomwe zvine vanhu 2,766 vane dementia yakapfava kusvika yepakati yakawana ([Cochrane review yecognitive stimulation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39804128/)):

- **bhenefiti diki yenguva pfupi** pabvunzo dzekufunga (umbowo hwemhando yepakati);
- **kuvandudzika kunokosha pakutaura nekudyidzana nevamwe** (umbowo hwemhando yepamusoro, kubva kuzvidzidzo zvishoma);
- **mabhenefiti madiki** pahupenyu hwakanaka, manzwiro nemaitiro.

Mabhenefiti aiwanzo kuyerwa pakarepo mushure mezvirongwa zvemavhiki angaita gumi, uye zvidzidzo zvishoma zvakatarisa kana zvakagara kwenguva refu. Cognitive stimulation chirongwa chakarongeka chemuboka, saka mhedzisiro iyi haishandi yega kumunhu ari kutamba mutambo ari ega patablet.

Lancet Commission ya2024 inotiwo zvirongwa zvezviitiko zvakakosha kuvanhu vanorarama nedementia, kuchengetedza kunakidzwa nechinangwa ([Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)).

## Mitambo yakanaka pachii chaizvo?

Kana yakasarudzwa zvakanaka, mitambo inogona kupa:

- **Kunakidzwa.** Hafu yeawa inofadza inokosha pachayo.
- **Kubatana.** Kutamba makagara padivi kunokupai chimwe chinhu chiri nyore chekugovana kana hurukuro yakaoma. Ona [matambiro amunogona kuita pamwe chete](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).
- **Tsika yemazuva ese.** Chiitiko chinjairika panguva imwe chete zuva rega rega chinogona kuunza nyaradzo.
- **Kuzvivimba.** Zviitiko zvinoenderana nezvinogona kuitwa nemunhu zvakanaka zvinomubatsira kunzwa achikwanisa, kwete achiongororwa.
- **Hurukuro.** Mufananidzo kana chirevo chinjairika zvinogona kuvhura nyaya. Edzai [makadhi ehurukuro](/games/conversation-cards/) kana [pedzisa chirevo](/games/finish-the-saying/).

Zvamunofanira kudzivisa: nguva inoverengwa, zvibodzwa, streaks uye chero chinhu chinonzwa sebvunzo yekurangarira. Kana munhu akatsamwiswa, mirai. Kupedza nguva isati yakwana, kana kungogara pamwe chete, mhedzisiro yakanaka. Gwaro redu re [kusarudza maapp nemitambo](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) rine rondedzero yekutevera.

## Golden Moments inopinda papi

Mitambo yeGolden Moments, yakaita se [mifananidzo miviri yakafanana](/games/picture-pairs/), [puzzle yemufananidzo](/games/picture-puzzle/) uye [kutsvaga mazwi ane mavara makuru](/games/word-search/), yakagadzirirwa kunakidzwa nenguva yekuva pamwe chete. Haina wachi uye haina zvibodzwa. **Haisi** kurapwa, uye **haidziviriri** kana kunonotsa dementia. Tinoda kukuudzai izvozvo zviri pachena pane kuvimbisa chinhu chisingagone kuitwa nemutambo.
