Kupenda nderimwe remabasa ekugadzira ari nyore kupa munhu anorarama nedambudziko rekuyeuka rinonzi Alzheimer’s kana rimwe rudzi rwedambudziko rekuyeuka. Haridi kuverenga, haridi kuyeuka uye hapana mhinduro chaiyo inotarisirwa. Maoko anofamba, mavara anobuda, uye panova nemufananidzo wekutaura nezvawo. Gwaro iri rinotsanangura nzira yekusarudza mapeji, zvekugadzirira, uye nzira yekupenda pamwe chete pasina kuita kuti zvioneke sezvevana kana sebvunzo.

## Nei kupenda kuchikodzera vanhu vazhinji vanorarama nedambudziko rekuyeuka

Kupenda kunopa maoko chinhu chekuita uye maziso chinhu chekutevera. Kunogona kuitwa wakagara patafura kana pachigaro pedyo nehwindo, uye zviri nyore kutanga nekumisa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) inotsanangura unyanzvi sebasa rinobatsira kuratidza manzwiro uye kunakidzwa nenguva iripo, uye inoti hazvidi hunyanzvi hwekudhirowa.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inopa zano rekusarudza mabasa anoenderana nezvinogona kuitwa nemunhu iye zvino, uye kuachinja sezvo kugona kuchichinja. Kupenda kuri nyore kuchinja: mufananidzo mukuru, makrayoni akakora, mavara mashoma, kana chidzitiro chekupenda nekubaya.

## Sarudzai mifananidzo yakagadzirirwa vakuru

Vakuru kazhinji havafariri mifananidzo inoita seyevana. Tsvakai:

- **Mamiriro ezvisikwa:** bindu rine maruva makuru, dhamu rine chikepe, shiri dziri pabazi.
- **Zvinhu zvinozivikanwa:** tii poto, bhasikoro, muchero, ngowani.
- **Mapatani makuru, ari nyore:** mandala nemaumbirwo makuru ane zvikamu zvishoma zvakakura.
- **Mifananidzo yehunyanzvi kana nzvimbo** iyo munhu wacho angaziva kubva panzvimbo yaanofarira kana pamufananidzo waanoziva.

Dzivisai mapeji ane nzvimbo diki, dzakatsvikinyidzana, dzinonetsa kuzadza. Mapeji evakuru ekudhinda isarudzo yakanaka, uye [mabasa emahara ekudhinda evanhu vanorarama nedambudziko rekuyeuka](/activities/printable/) edu anosanganisira mapeji ane mavara makuru.

## Zvekugadzirira

- **Zvishandiso zvakakora, zviri nyore kubata.** Makrayoni mahombe, zvinyoreso zvakakora, kana mapenzura ane mubato wakakura zviri nyore kushandisa kupfuura zvinyoreso zvitete.
- **Mavara mashoma.** Mavara matatu kana mana anokwana. Sarudzo dzakawandisa dzinogona kuvhiringa.
- **Bepa rakasimba kana kadhibhodhi.** Bepa rakatetepa rinobvaruka uye rinotsvedza. Namatirai makona pasi kana kushandisa clipboard.
- **Tafura yakachena.** Bvisai zvinhu zvisina basa uye isai peji rimwe panguva.
- **Chinhu chekupukuta kana kusanganisa,** sechidimbu chejira kana chipanje, kuti pave nemhedzisiro yakapfava.

## Kupenda patablet

[Mutambo wekupenda](/games/coloring/) uri paGolden Moments unoshanda nekubaya ruvara wobva wabaya chikamu chemufananidzo. Hauidi kudzora ruoko kwakanyanya, saka unogona kukodzera vanhu vane kubata kwas weakened. Garai padivi pemunhu wacho kuti mugovane chidzitiro, uye muregei asarudze mavara. Dzimaizve zviziviso kuitira kuti mufananidzo urambe uri iwo unonyanya kutariswa.

## Maitiro ekupenda pamwe chete

1. **Tangai mutsara wekutanga pamwe chete.** Batai krayoni naye, kana isai ruoko rwenyu pamusoro perwake kwemitsara mishoma, mozoregedza.
2. **Taurai nezvemavara nenzvimbo.** „Iro ibhuruu rakanaka. Makamboona mvura yakaita seruvara irworwo kupi?“ zvinokoka hurukuro pasina kumubvunza semuedzo.
3. **Rumbidzai zvaasarudza, kwete kurongeka.** „Masarudza mavara anodziya zvikuru“ zvinonzwa zviri nani pane „Magara mukati memitsetse.“
4. **Regai atungamire.** Kana achida kunyora-nyora pamusoro pepeji rese, muregei.
5. **Mirai paanomira.** Isai makrayoni pasi, uye kurudzirai kunwa tii kana kufamba zvishoma.

## Kuchinja sezvo dambudziko rekuyeuka richichinja

Padanho rekutanga, vanhu vanowanzonakidzwa nemapeji ane zvinhu zvakawanda uye vanogona kusarudza mavara nemifananidzo yavo. Padanho repakati, mifananidzo mikuru nesarudzo shoma zvinobatsira. Pamatanho ekupedzisira, edzai chimiro chimwe chete chikuru, bepa rine ruvara rwakapfava, kana kungobata krayoni imi muchiimba rwiyo zvinyoro. Vamwe vanhu vanonakidzwa nekutarisa mufananidzo wapera kupfuura kuuita.

Kuti muwane zvimwe nezvebasa rekugadzira padanho rimwe nerimwe, onai [mabasa emaoko evakuru vanorarama nedambudziko rekuyeuka](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) uye [mapurojekiti ehunyanzvi evanhu vanorarama nedambudziko rekuyeuka](/activities/art-projects/).

## Nguva yekumira

Mirai kana munhu wacho ava kushushikana, achisundira bepa kure, kana achiratidza kuneta. Kupedza mushure memaminitsi maviri kubudirira kana anenge anakidzwa nazvo. Munogona kudzoka gare gare, kana kuedza mufananidzo wakasiyana kana rimwe basa rakaita se[mitambo inobata pfungwa nezvinonzwiwa](/activities/sensory/).
