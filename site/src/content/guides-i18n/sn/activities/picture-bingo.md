Bingo mutambo wakajairika uye wekugarisana kune vakuru vazhinji, asi nhamba dzinogona kunetsa kutevera kumunhu anorarama nedementia. Bingo remifananidzo rinochengeta mafambiro manyoro mamwe chete asi richitsiva nhamba nemifananidzo mikuru, yakajeka. Rinogona kuitwa kumba, muchirongwa chemasikati, kana munzvimbo inochengeterwa vakuru. Gwaro iri rinotsanangura matambiro, magadzirirwo, uye kuti mungachengeta sei pfungwa dziri pamufaro.

## Mashandiro anoita bingo remifananidzo

Mutambi wega wega ane kadhi rine mifananidzo yakarongwa, semuenzaniso tii poto, katsi, ruva, chikepe kana bhasikoro. Anodana anoratidza kana kutaura mufananidzo mumwe panguva. Vatambi vanomaka mufananidzo unoenderana uri pakadhi ravo. Mutambi anomaka mutsara wakazara, kana seti inobvumiranwa neboka, anogona kudanidzira kuti "Bingo," asi mapoka mazhinji anosiya nyaya yemunhu anohwina.

Munogona kushandisa makadhi anodhindwa kubva ku [mabasa emahara anodhindwa](/activities/printable/) edu, kana [mutambo webingo remifananidzo](/games/picture-bingo/), unosanganisira mudziyo unodana mifananidzo pachidzitiro wemapoka. Zvose zvinoshanda zvakanaka patablet yakasimudzwa kuti boka rose rione.

## Kugadzirira nguva yekutamba

- **Sarudzai boka diki.** Vanhu vana kusvika vasere inhamba yakanaka. Mapoka makuru anonetsa kutevera uye anogona kunzwa sekumhanyiswa.
- **Itai kuti mifananidzo ive mikuru.** Shandisai mitsetse yakasimba uye chinhu chimwe chete chinozivikanwa pachikwere chimwe nechimwe. Dzivisai makadhi akazara zvakanyanya.
- **Garisai vanhu zvakanaka.** Isai mutambi wega wega paanogona kuona anodana nekadhi rake, uye pedyo nemubatsiri kana achida.
- **Shandisai zvimakisi zviri nyore.** Zvimedu zvikuru, mari, kana kubaya nemunwe zvinoshanda zviri nani pane zvinyoreso. Chengetai zvimwe zvekuwedzera.
- **Rongai nguva pfupi.** Maminitsi makumi matatu anowanzokwana. Mirai nguva isati yakwana kana simba rikadzikira.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) rinopa zano rekugadzirisa mabasa zvinoenderana nezvinogona kuitwa nemunhu, saka rerutsai makadhi nemitemo pose pazvinobatsira.

## Madaniro emifananidzo

1. **Simudzai mufananidzo** kana munongedzere pauri pachidzitiro musati mataura.
2. **Taurai zita rawo zvakajeka,** nemashoko ari nyore: "Tii poto." Dzokororai kamwe chete, zvishoma nezvishoma.
3. **Mirirai.** Ipai vanhu nguva yekutarisa nekumaka. Musadana mufananidzo unotevera kusvikira vanhu vazhinji vawana mukana.
4. **Tsanangurai, kana zvichibatsira.** "Ruva dzvuku" kana "ratakanga tinaro pikiniki" zvinogona kumutsa hurukuro.
5. **Chengetai mafambiro akadzikama.** Munhu anodana zvinyoro anokosha kupfuura anodana nekukurumidza.

## Kuita kuti kuhwina kusave kwekumanikidzwa

Makwikwi anogona kuva dambudziko. Munhu ari kurasikirwa anogona kunzwa sekunge ari kuedzwa, uye munhu ari kunetseka nezvekuhwina anogona kusagadzikana. Hedzino dzimwe nzira:

- **Munhu wose anohwina mufananidzo.** Mutambi wega wega anodana mufananidzo mumwe waanofarira, uye boka rinoutarisa pamwe chete.
- **Govanai kadhi rakazara.** Kumbirai vatambi kuti vataure chimwe chinhu nezvemufananidzo mumwe uri pakadhi ravo.
- **Tambai kusvikira mifananidzo yapera.** Danai mufananidzo wega wega uri pakadhi, mozotanga rimwe, pasina anofanira kuva wekutanga kuhwina.
- **Chengetai mubairo uri mudiki kana musiupe.** Keke diki kana rwiyo zvinoshanda zviri nani pane mukombe mukuru.

## Kugadzirira vanhu vari mumatanho anotevera

Kune vanhu vanoona zvakaoma kuwana mufananidzo, shandisai kadhi rine zvikwere zvina kana zvitanhatu chete, kana ipai mutambi wega wega kadhi rimwe panguva. Regai mubatsiri abate kadhi munhu achinongedzera, kana fambisai chimedu chekumakisa zvinyoro nemvumo yake. Vamwe vanhu vanoona zviri nyore kungoteerera anodana uye kunakidzwa nekurira kwemashoko.

Tarirai zviratidzo zvekusagadzikana, sekudzokera shure kana kufinyama kumeso, uye mirai kana zvikaonekwa. Kuti muwane dzimwe pfungwa dzemabasa akanyarara ekugara pasi, onai [mabasa ematanho anotevera](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo munzvimbo dzinochengeterwa vakuru nezvirongwa zvemasikati

Mabasa emapoka anoshanda zviri nani kana akarongwa maererano nevanhu vari mumba. [Gwaro redu remabasa emapoka munzvimbo dzinochengeterwa vakuru nezvirongwa zvemasikati](/activities/care-home-groups/) rinotsanangura kuronga nguva, kugara kwevanhu, uye kubata avo vanoda kubva. Bingo remifananidzo rinobatanawo zvakanaka nekuimba pamwe chete kana nenyaya, uye ne[finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) semutambo wakanyarara gare gare panguva yacho.

## Zvinogona kuitwa neBingo nezvasingagoni

Bingo remifananidzo mutambo wekugarisana uye unofadza. Rinogona kuunza vanhu pamwe chete, kupa chikonzero chekutarisa mifananidzo nekuteerera, uye kuita kuti boka rinzwe kudziya. Harirapi dementia kana kunonotsa mafambiro ayo. Tongai kubudirira kwaro nemaonerwo uye manzwiro anoita vanhu panguva iyoyo, kwete neanohwina.
