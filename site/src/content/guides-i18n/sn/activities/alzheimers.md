Kana munhu wamunoda ane Alzheimer's, pamwe makambonyatsoona kuti zvimwe zvinhu zvinokanganika nokukurumidza asi zvimwe zvinoramba zviripo. Anogona kukanganwa runhare rwa mangwanani asi anenge achiri kuimba rwiyo rwose rwechitendero, anopeta tauro sezvaaimboshanda, kana kugovera makadhi pasina kufunga. Zviitwa zvakanaka zvinovakwa pazvinhu zvinoramba zviripo. Peji ino rinotsanangura nei, richipa mazano echikamu chimwe nechimwe nemitambo yemahara iri pawebhusaiti ino inokodzera.

## Ko Alzheimer's yakasiyana nedementia pazviitwa here?

Kwete chaizvo. Dementia ishoko rinobatanidza, uye chirwere cheAlzheimer's ndicho chinonyanya kukonzera: [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) inoti inosvika 60 kusvika 80 muzana yemhosva dzedementia. Zvinhu zvinenge zvose zvakanyorwa nezvezviitwa zvedementia zvinoshandawo kuAlzheimer's, kusanganisira [mazano edu ezviitwa 101](/activities/ideas/).

Chinobatsira kuziva kutevedzana kwekuchinja. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-alzheimers-disease) inotsanangura kuti Alzheimer's inotanga kukuvadza nzvimbo dzehuropi dzine chekuita nendangariro, uye gare gare inokanganisa kutaura, kufunga uye maitiro ekugarisana. Alzheimer's Association inoti chiratidzo chinowanzoonekwa pakutanga ndiko kunetseka kurangarira ruzivo rwava kudzidza. Munhu mumwe nomumwe akasiyana, uye vanhu vazhinji vane zvikonzero zvakasiyana, asi patani iyi inobatsira kuona zvinowanzoshanda.

## Ndezvipi zviitwa zvinokodzera Alzheimer's zvikuru?

Zviitwa zvinovimba neruzivo rwekare rwakadzidziswa kazhinji zvinoenda zviri nani pane izvo zvinoda kudzidza chimwe chinhu chitsva uye kuchichengeta mupfungwa.

- **Unyanzvi hwakadzidziswa kare.** Alzheimer's Association inoti munhu anogona kuramba achiridza nziyo dziri nyore adzidza papiyano makore akapfuura. Kuruka, kugaya huni, kutenderedza makadhi, kukanya hupfu uye kupeta hembe zvakafanana.
- **Nziyo dzekare nemimhanzi inojairika.** Vanhu vazhinji vachiri kuziva mazwi ose enziyo dzehudiki hwavo, nziyo dzechitendero nenziyo dzemuchato wavo. Ona [zviitwa zvemimhanzi](/activities/music/).
- **Mabasa ezuva nezuva ane chinangwa.** [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inotaura kushambidza ndiro, kugadzira tafura, kuronga masokisi nekupeta hembe pakati pezviitwa zvinogona kufadza vanhu. Kunzwa kuva nechokwadi kunokosha.
- **Kuziva pane kurangarira.** Kutaridza mufananidzo unoenderana zviri nyore pane kuutaura kubva mundangariro. Ndokusaka mitambo yemifananidzo ine zvose zvichionekwa ichishanda zviri nani pane mibvunzo.
- **Kunzwa.** Kubata, kunhuhwirira, kudziya, kuravira uye ruzha zvinoramba zvichireva chinhu kunyange mashoko akunetsa. Ona [zviitwa zvekubata](/activities/sensory/).

Zvinowanzoenda zvakaoma: chero chinhu chine nguva yakatarwa, mitemo mitsva yekudzidza, matanho mazhinji ekurangarira, kana mibvunzo inoedza ndangariro, yakaita sekuti "Unorangarira here uyu?" Izvi zvinoisa chikamu chakaoma cheAlzheimer's pamberi.

## Zviitwa zvechikamu chekutanga cheAlzheimer's

Muchikamu chekutanga, [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/stages) inoti munhu anogona kuramba achityaira, kushanda uye kutora chikamu muzviitwa zvevanhu, asi achiona kuti ndangariro dzinokanganika dzakadai sekukanganwa mazwi anojairika kana kwaanoisa zvinhu. Nzira yakanaka kushanda pamwe chete: bvunza zvaanoda kuramba achiita uye rubatsiro rwaangada.

1. **Ramba uchiita zvinhu zvaaimbofarira.** Golf, bhiriji, kuruka maketeni, makwaya nemapoka echechi, nemagadzirirwo madiki akadai semapoka madiki uye nguva dzakanyarara.
2. **Tamba makadhi nemitambo yebhodhi yavajaira.** [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) inotaura trivia, kutsvaga mazwi, mitambo yemakadhi nemitambo yebhodhi yechikamu chekutanga. Tambai semubatanidzwa kana kuchengeta zvibodzwa kuchitanga kumunetsa, kana kurega zvibodzwa zvachose.
3. **Bikayi pamwe chete.** Regai atungamirire resipi yaaziva nemusoro apo imi muchibata kuverenga matanho.
4. **Tanga chirongwa chenyaya yehupenyu hwake.** Mifananidzo, mapeji ezvikafu nenyaya mubhuku raachitungamira. Ona [zviitwa zvekudzokorora](/activities/reminiscence/).
5. **Endai kunzvimbo dzakasiyana.** Resitorendi yaanofarira panguva yakanyarara, miziyamu yekushanya kupfupi, kufamba mumupaka.
6. **Edza mapuzzle aanosarudza.** Mazwi akadhindwa makuru, kutsvaga mazwi nemajigsaw. Ona [mapuzzle edementia](/activities/puzzles/).

Mazano akawanda: [zviitwa zvechikamu chekutanga](/activities/early-stage/).

## Zviitwa zvechikamu chepakati cheAlzheimer's

Alzheimer's Association inotsanangura chikamu chepakati sechinowanzo gara kwenguva refu, uye inoti chinogona kugara makore mazhinji. Mashoko anovhiringika, kutsamwa kunouya nyore nyore, uye munhu anoda kutarisirwa zvakawanda. Alzheimer's Foundation of America inowedzera kuti vanhu vazhinji vanoda rubatsiro kutanga kana kuenderera mberi nezviitwa. Basa renyu rinobva rakachinja kuva kugadzira zvinhu, kuratidza panzvimbo pekutsanangura, uye kuita zvikamu zvakaoma.

1. **Peta, ronga uye enzanisa.** Matauro, masokisi, midziyo yesirivha, mabhatani nemavara. Mabasa chaiwo, muchiita padivi nepadivi.
2. **Imbai uye fambai nemimhanzi.** Rova maoko, ngusvi kana kutamba mukicheni. Tanga kuimba iwe womubvumira kupinda.
3. **Tarirai mualbum emifananidzo.** Tsanangura zvaunoona ukokorodza nyaya: "Motokari yakanaka sei. Ndiudze nezvayo." Tenda mazita ose aanoshandisa.
4. **Tambai mitambo iri nyore yemifananidzo.** [Mapeya emifananidzo](/games/picture-pairs/) ane mataira mana kusvika matanhatu uye mifananidzo ichiramba ichionekwa, kana [Tsvaga zvakafanana](/games/find-the-same/) nesarudzo mbiri kana nhatu.
5. **Pedzisa mashoko akajairika.** "Apple rimwe pazuva rinochengeta..." Mashoko akadzidziswa kare kazhinji anouya nyore. Edza [Pedzisa mashoko](/games/finish-the-saying/), paunogona kuratidza chero magumo nekubata kamwe.
6. **Dira mvura uye chengeta zvirimwa.** Mapoto emumba kana mubhedha wakasimudzwa, nejagi diki remvura.
7. **Gadzira mutambo wekuenzanisa kubva pamifananidzo yemhuri.** [Mapeya emifananidzo](/games/photo-pairs/) inoshandisa mifananidzo yaunosarudza mumudziyo wako, uye haibvi mauri.

Mazano akawanda: [zviitwa zvechikamu chepakati](/activities/middle-stage/).

## Zviitwa zvechikamu chekupedzisira cheAlzheimer's

Muchikamu chekupedzisira, Alzheimer's Association inoti vanhu vanorasikirwa nekukwanisa kupindura kune zvavanenge vakapoteredza nekutaura, kunyange zvazvo vangangoramba vachiti mashoko mashoma. Chiitwa zvino zvinoreva kunyaradza nekuva pamwe chete, zvinoitirwa kwaari kwaazorora.

1. **Ridza mimhanzi yaanofarira** zvinyoro nyoro, pasina terevhizheni ichikanganisa.
2. **Kwiza ruoko rwake** nezvizoro zvine kunhuhwirira kwaanoda.
3. **Mupire zvinhu zvakapfava zvekubata:** nguwo inodziya, fleece, dombo rakatsetseka.
4. **Verengera** mapisarema, nhetembo kana nyaya yaanoziva. Rhythm inokosha kupfuura zvarinoreva.
5. **Gari panze** mumumvuri, kana pedyo nepahwindo paanogona kuona shiri.
6. **Tarirai mufananidzo mumwe mukuru pamwe chete.** Sunflower kana imbwa ichizadza skrini yetablet, imi muchiti zvamunoona.

Mazano akawanda: [zviitwa zvechikamu chekupedzisira](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ndeapi mitambo inoshanda kumunhu ane Alzheimer's?

Sarudza mitambo isingaiti kuti munhu akundwe. Mitambo yose iri pawebhusaiti ino yemahara, inomhanya mubhurawuza uye haina nguva yakatarwa, zvibodzwa kana kunyoreswa.

| Mutambo | Zvaunoita | Kazhinji inokodzera |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mapeya emifananidzo](/games/picture-pairs/) | Enzanisa mifananidzo mikuru; inogona kuramba yakatarwa | Chikamu chekutanga nechepakati |
| [Tsvaga zvakafanana](/games/find-the-same/) | Bata mufananidzo unoenderana neumuri pamusoro | Chikamu chepakati nechekupedzisira, nerubatsiro |
| [Pedzisa mashoko](/games/finish-the-saying/) | Sarudza shoko rekupedzisira remashoko akajairika, kana kuriratidza | Chikamu chekutanga nechepakati |
| [Puzzle yemifananidzo](/games/picture-puzzle/) | Chinja zvidimbu 4 kana 9 kuti zvive panzvimbo, mufananidzo uchiratidzwa | Chikamu chekutanga nechepakati |
| [Makadhi ekutaura](/games/conversation-cards/) | Mufananidzo nemubvunzo wakavhurika wekutaura nawo | Chero chikamu, nemunhu wekutaura naye |
| [Mapeya emifananidzo](/games/photo-pairs/) | Mutambo wekuenzanisa wakagadzirwa nemifananidzo yako | Chikamu chekutanga nechepakati |
| [Kupenda](/games/coloring/) | Bata ruvara, wobva wabatisa chikamu chemufananidzo; kana kudhinda | Chero chikamu, nerubatsiro |

Kuti mugare sei pamwe chete, rubatsiro rwakawanda sei uye nguva yekumira, verengai [matanho ekutamba nemunhu ane dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/). Kana muchishamisika nezve "mitambo yendangariro", onai [mitambo yendangariro kune vakwegura vane dementia](/activities/memory-games/).

## Maitiro ekuita kuti chiitwa chero chipi zvacho chive nyore

Mazano eNIA anouya nemaitiro mashoma: enzanisa chiitwa nezvinokwanisa munhu, mubatsire kutanga, tarisa kana akatsamwa, ita kuti anzwe abudirira, uye rega atarise chete kana ndiko kwaanoda.

- **Sarudza nguva yezuva yaanonyatsova nani,** uye chengetedza nguva dzipfupi.
- **Gadzira zvose pakutanga** kuti mutange nokukurumidza.
- **Koka, usarayira.** "Ungandibatsira here?" kunoshanda zviri nani pane "Nguva yechiitwa."
- **Ita nhanho yekutanga iwe** womubvumira kupinda.
- **Mupire nhanho imwe nesarudzo imwe panguva.**
- **Rega zviitwe nenzira yake.** Matauro asina kururama akatenderwa.
- **Mirei chiitwa chichiri kunakidza.** Kumira nguva isati yasvika mugumisiro wakanaka, kwete kukundikana. Kana akaramba, onai [kana munhu ane dementia ati kwete](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) inoti zviri nyore: tarisa pakunakidzwa, kwete pakubudirira.
