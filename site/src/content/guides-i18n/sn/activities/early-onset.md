Mazhinji mashoko pamusoro pekukanganwa anofunga kuti munhu ane makore makumi manomwe kana makumi masere. Kune munhu anoonekwa aine izvi ari makore ake makumi mana kana makumi mashanu, hupenyu hwemazuva ose hwakasiyana. Angave achiri kushanda, ane chikwereti chemba, vana vari kuyaruka kumba kana kuti ari kuchengeta mumwe munhu. Mabasa anofanira kuratidza hupenyu ihwohwo hwomukuru. Gwara iri rinotsanangura zvinochinja, zvaungayedza, uye kwavanogona kuwana rutsigiro vakaroorana nevana vakuru.

## Chii chinonzi Kukanganwa Kwakangoti Kuvhiringidzika kwokutanga?

Kukanganwa Kwakangoti Kuvhiringidzika kwokutanga inzira yokukanganwa inotanga vanhu vasati vasvitsa makore 65, dzimwe nguva inonzi inotanga chechetere. [Blog yeSangano reAlzheimer's](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) inotsanangura chirwere cheAlzheimer's sechinotanga chechetere kana chikabata munhu ari pasi pemakore 65, uye inoti kuchengeta munhu mudiki "kunouya nematambudziko akasiyana." Zviratidzo zvacho zvingave zvakafanana nezvinowanikwa paupenyu hwakare, asi zvinotarisirwa nomunhu, mabasa ake uye ushamwari hwake hazvisi izvo.

Nemhaka yokuti hazviwanzoitiki, vanhu vanogona kumirira nguva refu kuti vaoneke chirwere chacho, uye mhuri dzinogona kunzwa sedzisina munhu wavangataura naye. Kuziva kuti vamwewo vanosangana nazvo kunowanzoita kuti mufunge zororo.

## Chengetai munhu mukuru mubasa

Vanhu vakuru vane Kukanganwa Kwakangoti Kuvhiringidzika kwokutanga kazhinji havadi kurapwa sevana. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inokurudzira kuchinja mabasa zvichienderana nezvinogona kuita munhu achiri kufara nazvo. Kune mumwe ari makore ake makumi mashanu, izvozvo zvinogona kureva:

- **Chengetai chinhu chaaiwanzoita,** zvakadai sokushanda nematanda, mutambo, makadhi, boka rekuverenga mabhuku kana kuti boka rokuimba, nerubatsiro ruduku kana kuti nguva ipfupi.
- **Fambai kana kutasva bhasikoro nemumwe anofamba nemi,** munzira yaanonyatsoziva.
- **Bikirei mhuri chikafu,** munhu achiita zvikamu zvaanogona kubata.
- **Ita mabasa okuzvipira kana kubatsira** mune basa raanosarudza, zvakadai sokuronga mipiro kana kubatsira pamutambo wechikoro.
- **Rongai rwendo** kunzvimbo inomubata mwoyo, nemumwe anofamba nemi uye hurongwa huri nyore.
- **Pedzai nguva nevazukuru, vana vehama kana hanzvadzi,** muchitamba mutambo kana kuita chirongwa pamwe chete.

Mubvumire kusarudza. Kubvunza kuti "Ungada kuita sei nhasi masikati?" kunoremekedza kupfuura kumusarudzira.

## Shandisai mimhanzi nendangariro dzemakore ake

Mimhanzi nemifananidzo yemakore ake makumi maviri, makumi matatu nemakumi mana zvinogona kumutsa manzwiro akanaka uye kutaura kuri nyore. Gwara redu re[mabasa emimhanzi](/activities/music/) rinotsanangura kuti mungagadzira sei playlist, uye [mabasa okurangarira](/activities/reminiscence/) anoratidza kuti mungagovera sei mifananidzo nenyaya musina kuvabvunza mibvunzo. Kuti muwane mutambo wakakodzera mukuru, edzai [makadhi ekutaura](/games/conversation-cards/), ane mibvunzo yakavhurika pamusoro pebasa, kufamba, shamwari nemhuri.

Mifananidzo yakasanganiswa inoitwa kubva pamifananidzo yemhuri inogonawo kushanda zvakanaka, nokuti mifananidzo yacho ndeyavanhu nenzvimbo dzinomubata mwoyo. [Mifananidzo yakasanganiswa](/games/photo-pairs/) inochengeta mifananidzo mubrowser pachigadziro ichocho.

## Basa, kutyaira uye hupenyu hwemazuva ose

Vanhu vazhinji vane Kukanganwa Kwakangoti Kuvhiringidzika kwokutanga vachiri kushanda, kutyaira kana kubata mari. Izvi hazvisi mabasa nenzira yakajairika, asi zvinoratidza zvingaitwa nomunhu. Taura nachiremba wavo pamusoro pokutyaira nekuchengeteka kwebasa, uye fungai kana vachida rubatsiro nemari kana nguva dzekuonana nachiremba. Chinangwa ndechokuchengetedza rukudzo apo munhu achiri kubatanidzwa sezvaanoda.

Kuti muwane nhungamiro pamusoro petsika dzemazuva ose uye kuramba muchiita zvinhu, onai [tsika yemazuva ose yakapfava](/activities/daily-routine/) uye [mabasa epamba semabasa](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Rutsigiro rwevakaroorana nevana vakuru

Vakaroorana nevana vakuru kazhinji vanonzwa vakasurukirwa. Kuchengeta munhu kunogona kunzwa sebasa rechipiri pamusoro pebasa nemhuri. Blog yeSangano reAlzheimer's inotaura kuti vanochengeta vari mumapoka evanotanga chechetere vakawana achibatsira panyaya dzinoshanda dzakadai soronga remitemo, uye nokungoita senge vanonzwisiswa. Inotiwo Sangano rine mapoka ekutsigira vanochengeta mahara, kusanganisira mapoka evanhu vanotarisira mumwe ane Kukanganwa Kwakangoti Kuvhiringidzika kwokutanga, uye matatu acho anosangana painternet neMuvhuro neChitatu.

Kuti mubvunze nezvemapoka, fonai Runhare Rwavo rweRubatsiro rweMazuva Ose pa 800.272.3900. Gwara redu re[kuzvichengeta kwoanochengeta](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinotsanangura zororo, nguva yokuzorora uye zviratidzo zvokuneta kwazvo.

## Nhanho dzichiri kukosha

Danho romunhu rekukanganwa richiri kuratidza zvinobatsira. Pakutanga, vanhu vazhinji vanogona kuita mabasa avo emazuva ose nemamwe magadzirirwo, uye gwara redu re[mabasa ekutanga](/activities/early-stage/) rine mamwe mazano. Gare gare, mabasa anova akareruka uye anobata manzwiro, sezvinotsanangurwa mu[mabasa ekupedzisira](/activities/late-stage/). Chinjai basa sezvinotsiva munhu, uye mubvunze zvaanoda chero paanogona kutaura.
