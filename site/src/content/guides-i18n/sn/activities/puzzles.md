Jigsaw yakawaridzwa patafura yekudyira ndechimwe chezviitwa zviri nyore kugoverana. Munogona kugara pamwe chete musingade kutaura zvakawanda, mufananidzo unokupai chinhu chekukurukura nezvacho, uye kuisa chidimbu chekupedzisira kunonzwa zvakanaka. Zvisinei, mapuzzle anofanira kuchinja sezvo dementia ichichinja. Puzzle yakaoma zvakanyanya inogona kushandura masikati akadzikama kuita anonetesa. Peji rino rinotsanangura kusarudza nekugadzirisa majigsaw, mapuzzle emashoko nemapuzzle ari nyore paindaneti, uye zvekuita kana aoma.

## Mapuzzle akanakira vanhu vanogara nedementia here?

Vanhu vazhinji vanoanakidzwa, kunyanya avo vaimboita mapuzzle kare. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) inonyora kutsvaga mazwi, mitambo yemakadhi nemitambo yebhodhi pakati pezviitwa zvedanho rekutanga, uye [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) inotaura nezvemitambo yepaindaneti nemapuzzle pamapiritsi senzira imwe yekuramba uchiita zvinhu.

Mapuzzle haasi kurapwa. Hapana puzzle yakaratidzwa kunonotsa kana kumisa dementia; gwara redu re[mitambo yehuropi nedementia](/brain-health/brain-games/) rinotsanangura tsvakiridzo. Chikonzero chekuita puzzle ndechekuti inogona kuva nguva inofadza uye inobata pfungwa yamunopedza pamwe chete. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) inozvipfupisa ichiti: tarisai kunakidzwa, kwete kubudirira.

## Mungasarudza sei jigsaw puzzle yemunhu anogara nedementia?

Tevedzerai izvo munhu waanofarira uye kuti puzzle yekupedzisira yakafamba sei, kwete zita redanho.

### Huwandu hwezvidimbu

Hapana nhamba yakarurama. Munhu agara achiita mapuzzle anogona achiri kunakidzwa nemazana mashoma ezvidimbu pakutanga, asi mumwe anonyanya kufara negumi nezviviri kana zvakadaro. Mutemo unoshanda: kana puzzle yekupedzisira yainzwa sebasa, sarudzai imwe ine zvidimbu zvinenge hafu. Kana yakapedzwa nekukurumidza uye munhu wacho achida imwezve, wedzerai zvishoma.

### Kukura uye kunzwa kwezvidimbu

- **Zvidimbu zvakakura** zviri nyore kuona, kubata nekutenderedza.
- **Zvidimbu zvakakora, zvakasimba** zvinogara panzvimbo uye hazvikuvadziki nyore kana zvikadonhedzwa.
- **Kupedzisa kusina kupenya** kunoderedza kupenya kwemwenje nemahwindo.
- **Dzivisai zvidimbu zvidiki** kana munhu wacho achigona kuisa zvinhu mumuromo, izvo zvinogona kuitika gare gare mudementia.

### Mufananidzo

- **Misoro yevakuru.** Mapindu, makicheni, mota dzekare, mhuka dzepapurazi, taundi riri pedyo negungwa, mutambo waanofarira. Kwete makatuni kana chinhu chinoita sechitoyi chevana.
- **Nzvimbo dzemavara dzakasiyana.** Danga dzvuku, chikepe chebhuruu nemunda wakasvibira zviri nyore kupfuura denga rinotora hafu yepuzzle.
- **Zvakajairika uye zvemunhu.** Mufananidzo wenzvimbo yaaimbogara kana chinhu chaaifarira chinoita kuti muve nezvakawanda zvekutaura nezvazvo. Dzimwe nzvimbo dzekudhinda dzinogona kushandura mufananidzo wemhuri kuita jigsaw.

## Mungaita sei puzzle pamwe chete?

Igadzirirei kuitira kuti munhu wacho akwanise kutanga pakarepo pachikamu chinonakidza.

1. **Sarudzai nzvimbo yakanaka.** Chiedza chakanaka, chigaro chine maoko, uye tafura kana tireyi panogona kugara puzzle.
2. **Misimudzai chivharo chebhokisi** pamunogona kuchiona mese.
3. **Tenderedzai zvidimbu zvitarise kumusoro** musati matanga.
4. **Rongai zvemumucheto** imi, kana gadzirai muganhu kutanga mosiyira munhu wacho zviri pakati.
5. **Rongai zvidimbu nemavara** mumatireyi mashoma asina kudzika kana mumahwendefa.
6. **Ipai chidimbu chimwe panguva** kana murwi wacho wakawandisa. "Ndinofunga ichi ndechechikamu chedanga."
7. **Taurai nezvemufananidzo,** kwete nezvekufambira mberi. "Izvozvo zvinoita sechikepe chaiva nababa vangu."
8. **Siirai yakadaro.** Dzokerai kwairi kwemazuva akati wandei. Hapana zvekumhanyira.
9. **Mirai panguva yakanaka,** mushure mekunge chidimbu chapinda, zvichiri kunzwa zvakanaka.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inopa zano rekubatsira munhu wacho kutanga, kutarisa kushushikana uye kumurega achingotarisa kana izvo zvichimufadza. Kukutarisa uchiiisa zvidimbu, uye kunongedzera painganogona kuenda chidimbu, ichiri nzira yekutora chikamu.

## Mapuzzle ari nyore paindaneti

Puzzle yepapiritsi ine zvakanakira chaizvo: hapana zvidimbu zvinorasika, mifananidzo mikuru inozadza sikirini, uye hapana tafura yekuchenesa.

[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) yedu yepachena inocheka mufananidzo kuita zvidimbu 4 kana 9. Tinya zvidimbu zviviri kuti zvichinjane nzvimbo kusvikira mufananidzo wava wakazara. Mufananidzo wakapedzwa unoramba uri pasikirini segwara, hapana wachi, uye munogona kumira chero nguva. Zvidimbu zvina zvinokodzera vanhu vazhinji vari padanho repakati kana paine mumwe munhu ari parutivi pavo.

Kuti muwane kutamba nemifananidzo kuri nyorezve, edzai [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/), apo munobaya mufananidzo unoenderana neumwe uri kumusoro, kana [Sort together](/games/sort-together/), apo mifananidzo inoiswa mumabhasikiti akaita sekicheni kana bindu.

## Mapuzzle emashoko: kutsvaga mazwi nemacrossword

Mapuzzle emashoko anokodzera vanhu vaigara vachianakidzwa nawo, kazhinji pakutanga kwedementia.

- **Kutsvaga mazwi mumavara makuru.** [Word search](/games/word-search/) yedu yepachena inoshandisa mavara makuru nemashoko mashoma chete, anofamba nepakati nepakudzika, kwete nepakayambuka kana kudzokera kumashure. Itambe pasikirini kana uiprinte.
- **Verengai runyorwa zvinonzwika.** Mumwe anoverenga izwi, mumwe anoritsvaga. Kutenderedza bhii rekutanga chete zvakakwana.
- **Macrossword emavara makuru** ari mumabhuku emapuzzle anogona kushanda pakutanga. Verengai mazano zvinonzwika, goveranai chinyoreso uye zadzai mhinduro pamwe chete. Macrossword ari nyore, ane musoro mumwe ari nani pane akaoma.
- **Taurai nezvemashoko.** "Bindu" rinogona kutungamirira kumaminitsi gumi ekutaura nezvemadomasi.

Kuti muwane zvimwe zviitwa zvepamapepa uye maitiro ekuzviprinta kuti zvive nyore kuona nekubata, onai [zviitwa zvepachena zvedementia zvinodhindwa](/activities/printable/).

## Munofanira kuita riini kuti puzzle ive nyore, kana kumira?

Tarisisai kugomera, kusundira zvidimbu kure, kudzokorora kuti "Handikwanisi" kana kutaridzika kwekunetseka. Izvi zviratidzo zvekuti chinhu chinofanira kuchinjwa, kwete zvekumanikidza kuenderera mberi.

- **Iitei diki.** Zvidimbu zvishoma, kana musiye zvishoma zvekupedzisira chete kwaari.
- **Itai zvimwe zvayo imi** uye mukokei kubatsira nezvikamu zviri nyore.
- **Chinjaenda kuchimwe chiri pedyo:** kuronga zvidimbu nemavara, puzzle yepaindaneti ine zvidimbu 4, kana [coloring](/games/coloring/).
- **Mirai.** Isiyei parutivi uye munwe tii. Munogona kudzoka rimwe zuva, kana kusadzokazve.

Kana mapuzzle akatanga kunzwa sechiyeuchidzo chezvinhu zvakaoma, zvakanaka kuarega. Pane dzimwe nzira dzakawanda dzekupedza nguva yakanaka pamwe chete; [pfungwa 101 dzezviitwa](/activities/ideas/) inzvimbo yakanaka yekutarisa.
