Mabasa eunyanzvi inzira yakanaka yekupedza nguva nemunhu ane dementia. Anoshandisa maoko, maziso uye pfungwa dzekunzwa nekubata, uye anokupai chimwe chinhu chekutaura nezvacho. Peji rino rinopa mabasa chaiwo ane matanho ari nyore, pamwe neruzivo rwekuti mungasarudza sei zvekushandisa zvakachengeteka. Kuti munzwisise misimboti iri shure kweunyanzvi hwevanhu vakuru, sekudzivisa zvinhu zvinoita sezvevana, onai [unyanzvi nemabasa emaoko evanhu vakuru vanorarama nedementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Maitiro ekutanga chero basa reunyanzvi

1. **Gadzirirai musati matanga.** Isai chinhu chimwe kana zviviri, jira rekudzivirira, uye jira rakanyorova.
2. **Ratidzai danho rekutanga.** Nyikai bhurasho, bvarurai chidimbu chekutanga, kana svinai kamukanyiwa keivhu, mokoka munhu wamunotarisira kuti aite zvimwe chetezvo.
3. **Shandai makagara padivi nepadivi.** Chengetai basa renyu padyo kuitira kuti chiitiko chinzwe sekugovana, kwete sekutarisiwa.
4. **Taurai nezvezvamunoona.** Rondedzerai mavara, manzwire ezvokubata uye maumbirwo pane kutonga basa racho.
5. **Misai simba risati radonha.** Kazhinji maminitsi gumi kusvika makumi maviri inguva yakanaka.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) inoti unyanzvi inzira yekuratidza manzwiro uye kuva nekugadzira pasina kudiwa kweunyanzvi hwekudhirowa. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inopa zano rekutsemura mabasa kuita matanho madiki, ari nyore kuita.

## Kugeza bepa nemvura yemavara

Shandisai bepa gobvu remvura yemavara, bhurasho rakakura rakapfava uye mavara mashoma mumakapu asina kudzika. Nyorovesai bepa nemvura yakachena, mobva marega munhu wamunotarisira adonhedze ruvara pairi. Rerekesai bepa zvinyoro nyoro kuti mavara ayerere. Mhedzisiro inova mufananidzo wakapfava, usina hurongwa hunodiwa.

- **Danho rekutanga:** Muregei asarudze mavara uye apende nzvimbo.
- **Danho repakati:** Mupei mavara maviri kana matatu uye munyorovese bepa imi.
- **Danho rekupedzisira:** Batai bepa iye achirova nesiponji.

## Collage yemifananidzo yemumagazini

Unganidzai mapeji emagazini ane mifananidzo yakajeka yemaruva, zvekudya, mhuka, mota kana nzendo. Bvarurai mifananidzo kuita zvidimbu zvakakura, izvo zviri nyore pane kucheka, moinamira pabepa rakasimba. Collage inoshanda zvakanaka kutanga hurukuro, nekuti mufananidzo wega wega unogona kumutsa ndangariro. Inokodzera munhu anofarira kuronga nekusarudza.

## Ivhu rakapfava

Ivhu rinooma rega kana ivhu rakapfava rekuumba rinogona kutsikirirwa, kukungurutswa nekusvinwa. Gadzirai mbiya iri nyore kana kuti tira yakati sandara. Ivhu rinonakidza kubata, saka rinogona kunyaradza vanhu vanofarira kubata zvinhu. Chengetai zvidimbu zvakakura, uye dzivisai maturusi ane miromo inopinza.

## Choko chepastel pabepa dema

Bepa dema rinoita kuti mavara epastel apenye. Shandisai mapastel makuru akakora uye regai munhu wamunotarisira aite mitsetse mikuru kana kusanganisa nemunwe kana netishu. Mapastel ane guruva, saka gadzirirai patireyi uye pukutai maoko mushure. Sarudzai mapastel akapfava akanyorwa kuti haana muchetura.

## Kudhinda mashizha

Torerai mashizha makuru mubindu, zorai pendi inoshambika patsinga dzawo, mobva matsikirira shizha pabepa. Ribvisei zvinyoro nyoro moona mufananidzo wabuda. Basa iri rinoenderana zvakanaka ne[mabasa ekunze neekurima gadheni](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), uye rinogona kutanga nekufamba kunotora mashizha.

## Kuronga machira nemaruva

Rongai zvidimbu zvemachira zvine manzwire akasiyana zvichienderana nekubata kwazvo, kana ronganisai maruva mashoma matsva muvhaasi. Aya mabasa anoshandisa pfungwa dzekunzwa nekubata ane kumanikidzwa kushoma. Anokodzerawo vanhu vari pamatanho ekupedzisira. Kuti muwane mamwe mazano, onai [mabasa anoshandisa pfungwa dzekunzwa nekubata evanhu vanorarama nedementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Kusarudza zvekushandisa zveunyanzvi

Zvekushandisa zvakakosha pakuchengeteka nekunyaradzwa kupfuura pamhando yazvo. Pamunosarudza, fungai nezvezvinotevera:

- **Zvisina muchetura uye zvinoshambika:** Tsvagai zvinyorwa zvinoti hazvina muchetura uye tarisisai kuti mapendi anoshambika.
- **Zvibatiso zvakakura:** Makrayoni mahombe, mabhurasho akakora uye mapastel makuru zviri nyore kubata.
- **Hapana zvidimbu zvidiki:** Dzivisai mabeads, mabhatani, sequins uye glitter inopararira inogona kumedzwa.
- **Hapana maturusi anopinza:** Shandisai chigero chisina njodzi kana bvarurai bepa nemaoko.
- **Maallergy:** Tarisisai latex, uye chero kusagadzikana kweganda kunozivikanwa.
- **Musiyano unooneka:** Mavara akasimba pabepa jena kana pabepa dema ari nyore kuona.
- **Runyorwa rupfupi:** Zvinhu zviviri kana zvitatu zvakakwana. Sarudzo dzakawandisa dzinogona kuvhiringa.
- **Kuchenesa kuri nyore:** Shandisai tireyi, jira repurasitiki uye jira rakanyorova, kuitira kuti tsvina isava chikonzero chekurega.

## Kuchinja zvinoenderana nedanho

Padanho rekutanga, vanhu vazhinji vanogona kuronga nekupedza basa nerubatsiro rudiki. Padanho repakati, vapei sarudzo shoma uye nhungamiro yakawanda. Padanho rekupedzisira, tarisai pamaitiro anoshandisa pfungwa dzekunzwa nekubata sekusvina ivhu kana kurova pendi nesiponji, uye farirai mhedzisiro pane kupedzisa.

Kuti muwane mamwe mazano anoenderana nedanho, onai [mabasa edanho rekutanga](/activities/early-stage/), [edanho repakati](/activities/middle-stage/) uye [edanho rekupedzisira](/activities/late-stage/). Kana munhu wamunotarisira akaramba kana kushungurudzika, gwara redu pamusoro pe[kana mumwe munhu achiti kwete](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) rinotsanangura kuti mungadzokera kumashure sei.

## Sanganisai nemutambo

Mushure mekupenda, edzai [kupenda mavara](/games/coloring/) patablet semutambo wakanyarara unotevera, kana [puzzle yemifananidzo](/games/picture-puzzle/) semutambo wekupedzisa. Zvose zvinoita kuti maoko arambe achishanda pasina kukumbira unyanzvi hwakanyanya.
