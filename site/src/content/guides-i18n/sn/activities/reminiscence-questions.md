Kurangarira zvakapfuura itsika yekukurukura mese nezvehupenyu hwekare: dzimba dzehudiki, mabasa ekutanga, zvokudya zvinodiwa, nziyo, michato nemazororo. Kune vanhu vazhinji vanorarama nedementia, hurukuro inodziya nezvehupenyu hwavo inogona kuva chimwe chezvinhu zvinofadza zvikuru muzuva. Chinokosha ndechekubvunza nenzira inokoka nyaya, isingamboedzi ndangariro. Mibvunzo iyi yakaunganidzwa zvichienderana nedingindira, pamwe nemazano ekuti moishandisa sei.

## Mitemo yemibvunzo yekurangarira zvakapfuura

- **Bvunzai mibvunzo yakasununguka.** „Ndiudzei nezve…“ uye „Zvaiva zvakaita sei…“ zvinokoka nyaya. „Munoyeuka here…?“ kunokumbira mhinduro yakarurama.
- **Musamboita muedzo.** Dzivisai mibvunzo yakaita sekuti „Uyu ndiani?“, „Aiva gore ripi?“ uye „Munoyeuka zita re…?“
- **Gamuchirai zvose zvinodzoka.** Kana nyaya ikachinja kana nzvimbo dzikasangana, farirai nyaya yacho mobvunza nezvemanzwiro.
- **Shandisai mubvunzo mumwe panguva.** Mirirai, teererai, mobva mabvunza chimwe chinhu chiri nyore.
- **Regai munhu wacho atungamire.** Kana achida kutaura nezvechimwe chinhu, endai naye.

[Nhungamiro yeNational Institute on Aging yekukurukurirana](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) inokurudzira mitsara mipfupi, inzwi rakadzikama, uye mwoyo murefu apo munhu wacho ari kutsvaga mashoko. Mitemo iyi yakakosha pano sekune dzimwe nzvimbo.

## Imba nehudiki

- Ndiudzei nezveimba yamakarerwa. Imba yekubikira yaiva yakaita sei?
- Maifarira kuitei panze pamaiva mudiki?
- Ndivanaani vamaimbotamba navo mumugwagwa menyu?
- Imba yenyu yekurara yaiva yakaita sei? Maigovana nemumwe munhu here?

## Basa nekuita zvinhu

- Nderipi basa ramaifarira zvikuru?
- Ndiudzei nezvezuva rebasa rakakuitai kuti muzvirumbidze.
- Chii chamaifarira kugadzira kana kugadzirisa nemaoko enyu?
- Ndiani akakudzidzisai zvakawanda nezvebasa renyu?

## Chikafu nekubika

- Mhuri yenyu yaidyei neSvondo?
- Ndiudzei nezvechikafu chaibikwa zvakanaka nemumwe munhu mumhuri yenyu.
- Pamaiva mudiki, maiva nenzvimbo yamaienda kunotenga chimwe chinhu chinotapira here?
- Ndeapi manhuhwirira anokuyeuchidzai nezvekumba?

Kutaura nezvezvekudya kunoenderana zvakanaka negwaro re[zviitwa zvinoshandisa pfungwa dzekunzwa nekubata](/activities/sensory/), uye pamwe nekubvumira munhu kuravira chimwe chinhu chidiki kana zvakachengeteka.

## Mimhanzi nekutamba

- Ndedzipi nziyo dzamaimbotamba kana kuimba pamaiva mudiki?
- Ndiani muimbi kana boka renziyo ramaifarira?
- Maimbonzwa mimhanzi kupi panguva iyoyo: kumba, pamatambiro, kana kuchechi?

Kuti muwane mazano enziyo nemazita ekuteerera, onai [zviitwa zvemimhanzi zvevanhu vanorarama nedementia](/activities/music/).

## Mazororo nemhemberero

- Nderipi zororo ramaiyemura zvikuru pamaikura?
- Ndiudzei nezvemhemberero yezuva rekuzvarwa yamunoyeuka.
- Mhuri yenyu yaiitei manheru ezhizha?
- Mhuri yenyu yaipemberera sei mwaka yegore?

Kuti muwane mazano anoenderana nezororo rakati, onai [zviitwa zvemazororo zvemunhu anorarama nedementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Mhuri neshamwari

- Ndiudzei nezvemunhu ainyanya kukusekesei.
- Zuva remuchato wenyu raiva rakaita sei?
- Ndiudzei nezvevana venyu pavaiva vadiki.
- Shamwari dzenyu dzaikudanai nezita ripi?

Bvunzai mibvunzo yemhuri zvinyoro nyoro. Kana munhu wacho achichema mumwe munhu, teverai zvaanoda kutaura uye dzivisai kumukumbira kuyeuka zvinhu zvingamurwadza.

## Nzvimbo nezvisikwa

- Ndiudzei nezvenzvimbo yamunofarira kufunga nezvayo.
- Mamiriro ekunze aiva akaita sei kwamakarerwa?
- Maiva nebindu, kana muti wamaiyemura?
- Ndiudzei nezverwendo rwamakafarira.

## Kushandisa mifananidzo nezvinhu

Mifananidzo inzira yakanaka yekutangisa. Bata mufananidzo pamaziso enyu muti, „Ndiudzei nezveizvi.“ [Nhungamiro yeAlzheimer's Association yezviitwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) inokurudzira kushandisa zvinhu zvinonyatsozivikanwa, mifananidzo nemimhanzi kutanga hurukuro. Bhokisi rendangariro rine zvinhu zvishoma kubva muhupenyu hwemunhu wacho rinogonawo kushanda zvakanaka; onai [zviitwa zvekurangarira zvakapfuura](/activities/reminiscence/) kuti muone kuti mungarigadzira sei.

## Kana ndangariro ichirwadza

Dzimwe ndangariro dzinounza kusuwa, kutya kana kutsamwa. Kana munhu wacho akatsamwa kana kusuwa, musamanikidza. Taurai zvinyoro nyoro zvakaita sekuti, „Zvinoita sekuti yaiva nguva yakaoma. Mungada here kutaura nezvechimwe chinhu chinofadza?“ Mobva machinja nyaya, momupa kapu yetii, kana kuridza rwiyo rwaanofarira.

## Sanganisai nemutambo kana kufamba

Mushure mehurukuro yekurangarira zvakapfuura, mutambo wakapfava unogona kuchengetedza manzwiro akanaka. Edzai [makadhi ehurukuro](/games/conversation-cards/) kuti muwane mimwe mibvunzo yakasununguka, kana [pedzisai chirevo](/games/finish-the-saying/) nezvirevo zvinonyatsozivikanwa kubva paudiki hwemunhu wacho. Kuti muwane mamwe mazano ekukurukurirana, onai [mazano ekukurukurirana nemunhu anorarama nedementia](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
