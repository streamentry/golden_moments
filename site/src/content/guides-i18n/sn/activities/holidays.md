Mazororo anogona kumutsa ndangariro dzinodziya, uye anogonawo kuuya neruzha, vanhu vazhinji neshanduko pazuva renguva dzose. Kune munhu anogara nedementia, mhemberero dzakanakisa dzinowanzova diki, dzinononoka uye dzakavakirwa pazvinhu zvaanogona kuramba achinakidzwa nazvo. Mazano aya anoshanda paZuva Rekutenda, Kisimusi, Hanukkah, Gore Idzva nemazuva ekuzvarwa emhuri.

## Ndingaronga sei zororo rakadzikama?

Tangai netsika dzemazuva ese dzemunhu wacho uye rongai zuva muchidzitevedzera. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/6-steps-to-make-thanksgiving-dementia-friendly/) inopa zano rekuita mhemberero mangwanani, nekuti kuvhiringidzika nekusagadzikana zvinowanzowedzera masikati nemanheru (dzimwe nguva zvinonzi sundowning). Kudya kwemasikati kana kwemangwanani kwezororo kunogona kuva nyore kupfuura kudya kwemanheru.

- **Chengetai kukura kwakakodzera.** Musangano mudiki, wakasununguka, kana vaenzi vanouya vari vashoma panguva, unowanzova nyore kupfuura tafura imwe yakazara.
- **Goverai mabasa.** Kumbirai hama kuuya nechikafu, kunotenga kana kupedza nguva nemunhu wacho imi muchibika. Taurai zvakajeka kuti chii chingabatsira.
- **Chengetai zvishongo zviri nyore.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) inocherekedza kuti mwenje inopenya-penya inogona kuvhiringidza, uye kuti zvihwitsi kana michero yekushongedza inogona kufungidzirwa sechikafu.
- **Sarudzai zvekusiya.** Kana tsika yava kunetsa kana ine njodzi, zvakanaka kuisiya kana kuedza imwe yakanyarara.

## Ndezvipi zvinhu zvatinogona kuita pamwe chete pazororo?

Sarudzai mabasa nezvinhu zvinokodzera zvaanokwanisa kuita nhasi. Kuva chikamu chezuva kwakakosha kupfuura kuti zvabuda sei.

1. **Gadzirai tafura pamwe chete.** Isai maplate kutanga, mozotevera mapepa etafura, mozoisa makapu. Kupeta mapepa etafura ibasa rakanaka rekuita makagara pasi.
2. **Gezai uye sarudzai muriwo.** Kusuka mbatatisi kana kutyora bhinzi patafura yekubikira kwakachengeteka uye kwakajairika kuvanhu vazhinji vaimbobika.
3. **Kurungai, sanganisai kana rongai.** Kukurunga muto wecranberry, kusanganisa mukanyiwa uri nyore kana kuisa makeke mundiro kunoita kuti maoko ashande kure nechitofu nemapanga.
4. **Petai chipo.** Gurai bepa netepi pachine nguva, mosiya munhu wacho achipeta nekutsvedzerera. Kupeta kwakachena handicho chinangwa.
5. **Turikai zvishongo zvishoma.** Sarudzai zvishongo zvisingaputsike nenzvimbo imwe diki, sewreath kana muti mudiki.
6. **Itai basa remaoko riri nyore.** Maketani emapepa, mufananidzo wemashizha kana kadhi rezororo rine mastamp zvinoshanda zvakanaka. Gwaro redu re[arts and crafts](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) rine mamwe mazano.
7. **Imbai uye teererai.** Nziyo dzemazororo dzakajairika dziri nyore kupinda nadzo, kunyange kumunhu ave kutaura zvishoma. Onai gwaro redu re[music](/activities/music/) kuti muumbe rondedzero yenziyo.
8. **Tarirai mifananidzo yekare yemazororo.** Budisai maalbum emhemberero dzakapfuura uye kumbirai nyaya ("Ndiudzei nezvetafura iyi") pane kubvunza kuti ndiani ndiani. Gwaro redu re[reminiscence](/activities/reminiscence/) rinotsanangura nzira yacho.
9. **Tambai mutambo wakapfava weboka.** [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) rinodhindwa mumaminitsi mashoma uye rinoshanda kumazera ese, uye [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) zvinopa munhu wese zvekutaura nezvazvo.
10. **Pendai pamwe chete.** Mapeji ari nyore e[coloring](/games/coloring/) anogona kudhindwa kuti vana nasekuru nambuya vagovane patafura.
11. **Onai bhaisikopo remazororo ravanofarira.** Firimu rakajairika rinopa zororo, uye zvakanaka kuona chikamu charo chete.
12. **Endai parwendo rupfupi nemota kuona mwenje.** Kutenderera nemota zvinyoro nyoro padzimba dzakashongedzwa kunogona kuva nyore kupfuura chiitiko chakazara vanhu.

Kana kubika kwaiva chikamu chikuru chehupenyu hwemunhu wacho, gwaro redu re[household tasks as activities](/activities/household-tasks/) rine dzimwe nzira dzekuita kuti mabasa ekubikira ave akachengeteka uye anogutsa.

## Ndingagadzirira sei vaenzi?

Udza vaenzi zvekutarisira vasati vasvika, kunyanya hama dzisati dzashanya kwenguva yakareba. Alzheimer's Association inopa zano rekutsanangura kuti shanduko mundangariro nemaitiro dzinobva kuchirwere, kwete kumunhu wacho.

- **Goverai mazano mashoma ari nyore:** taurai mumwe panguva, ipai nguva yekupedzisa pfungwa, musamururamisa kana kumubvunza, uye pindurai kumanzwiro ari muzwi rake kana mashoko akaoma kunzwisisa.
- **Taurai zvinobatsira nezvinogumbura.** Kana manzwi makuru, kukurumidziswa kana dzimwe nyaya zvichikonzera kushushikana, taurirai.
- **Zivisai vaenzi pachine nguva.** Ratidzai munhu wacho mifananidzo yevari kuuya uye goverai nyaya imwe kana mbiri, kana ruramisai kufona kupfupi parunhare kana pavhidhiyo zuva risati rasvika.

Gwaro redu re[communication tips](/caregiving/communication-tips/) rinotsanangura zvakawanda.

## Ko kana zvanyanya kurema?

Gadzirai kamuri yakanyarara vaenzi vasati vasvika, kure neruzha, iine zvinhu zvinonyaradza zvakajairika zvakadai segumbeze raanofarira, bhachi kana chiitiko chiri nyore. Ipei mukana uyu nguva nenguva: "Tingagara here pane imwe nzvimbo yakanyarara kwechinguva?"

Kubva patafura, kuenda kunorara nguva isati yakwana kana kusaita chimwe chikamu chezuva mhedzisiro yakanaka, kwete kukundikana. Kana munhu wacho akatsamwa, izwi rakadzikama, rwiyo rwakajairika kana kufamba kuenda kune imwe kamuri zvinowanzobatsira kupfuura kukakavadzana. Gwaro redu re[late-day restlessness](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) rine mamwe mazano emanheru.

## Mhuri iri kure ingatora sei chikamu?

Kufona kwevhidhiyo kunoita kuti hama dzibatane pasina kuwedzera vanhu vazhinji. Alzheimer's Association inopa zano rekupa mafoni chimiro, semuenzaniso kuimba nziyo dzemwaka, kugovana mifananidzo yemhemberero dzakapfuura, kubika resipi imwe chete pamwe chete kana kuona bhaisikopo remazororo panguva imwe chete. Chengetai mafoni ari mapfupi, uye kumbirai vanhu kuti vataure mumwe panguva, nekuti kutaura panguva imwe chete kwakaoma kutevera. Gwaro redu re[phone and video call guide](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) rine mazano anotevedzana.

Kana munhu wacho achigara munzvimbo yekuchengeta vanhu vane memory care, tarisai mitemo yekushanya uye kumbirai vashandi kubatsira kuronga kufona. Gwaro redu re[visiting someone in memory care](/activities/visiting/) rinotsanangura zvekutora uye nzira yekuonekana zvakanaka.

## Zvipo zvipi zvakakodzera munhu ane dementia?

Zvipo zviri nyore, zvakachengeteka zvaanogona kunakidzwa nazvo izvozvi zvinowanzoshanda zvakanaka: chikafu chaanofarira, gumbeze rakapfava kana kadhiigan, mimhanzi yakajairika, karenda ine mavara makuru, kana album yemifananidzo yemhuri. Alzheimer's Association inopa zano rekudzivisa maturusi ane njodzi, zvemagetsi zvakaoma nemitambo yebhodhi inonetsa.

Kupa kunogona kukosha sekugamuchira. Kurongedza makeke munhu wacho aakabatsira kubika, kana kumuita kuti apete chipo chamakatenga, zvinomuita kuti ave chikamu chemwaka. Mutambo wemifananidzo yemhuri imwewo pfungwa: [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) inoshandura mifananidzo mishoma yakasarudzwa kuita mutambo wakapfava wekufananidza mubrowser.

## Zvichengetei nemiwo

Mazororo anowedzera basa pakuchengeta. Regai mumwe munhu atambire vaenzi kana kubika, ramba zviitiko zvichanetsa, uye tora zororo vamwe vachipedza nguva nemunhu wacho. Kana imi kana munhu wamunotarisira musiri kunzwa zvakanaka, kukwazisana kubva pamota kana kufona kwevhidhiyo inzira yakanaka yekushanya. Gwaro redu re[caregiver self-care](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rine rumwe rutsigiro.
