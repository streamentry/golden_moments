Mutambo wokupedzisa tsumo unoshandisa chirevo chinozivikanwa chinomira pashoko rekupedzisira risati rataurwa. Kune vanhu vazhinji, inogona kuva nzira inofadza uye isina kumanikidza yokugoverana nguva mese. Mutambo uyu unoshanda nokuti mashoko acho ekare uye anozivikanwa, uye munhu haafaniri kurangarira chero chinhu nezvanhasi. Gwaro iri rinotsanangura matambiro, kusarudza tsumo, uye kuti mungaramba makasununguka sei.

## Chii chinonzi mutambo wokupedzisa tsumo?

Umutambo mupfupi umo mumwe munhu anoverenga chikamu chekutanga chetsumo uye mumwe achipedzisa magumo acho. Mienzaniso inosanganisira “Actions speak louder than...” uye “The early bird catches the...”. Munhu anogona kutaura magumo acho, kuanongedzera, kana kusarudza kubva pamagumo maviri kana matatu amunopa. Vamwe vanhu vanofarira kuva ndivo vanoverenga chikamu chekutanga.

Golden Moments inopa izvi se[mutambo wemahara wokupedzisa tsumo](/games/finish-the-saying/) une mavara makuru. Munogonawo kutamba musina chiratidziro, nokungotaura chikamu chekutanga uye kumirira.

## Matambiro, nhanho nenhanho

1. **Sarudzai nguva yakanyarara** mogara makatarisana, makatarisana nemunhu wacho.
2. **Verengai chikamu chekutanga zvishoma nezvishoma,** nezwi rine ushamwari, mobva mambomira. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) inokurudzira kutaura zvishoma nezvishoma uye kushandisa mitsara mipfupi, iri nyore.
3. **Mirirai.** Ipai masekondi mashoma. Kana pasina chinouya, ipai magumo maviri kana matatu, rimwe racho riri iro chairo.
4. **Farirai mhinduro ipi neipi.** “Hongu, ndiro iro” kana “Handina kumbofunga izvozvo” zvinoita kuti zvive zvine hushamwari.
5. **Taurai nezvetsumo yacho.** Bvunzai zvinoreva kwaari, kana kwaakainzwa. Apa ndipo pane kukosha chaiko.
6. **Mirai vasati vaneta.** Tsumo shoma nekuseka zvishoma zvinogona kuva nguva yakakwana.

## Kupa sarudzo pasina kubvunza semuedzo

Sarudzo dzemagumo dzakawanda dzinogona kuita kuti mutambo uve nyore, asi dzinogonawo kunzwika semuedzo kana dzikapiwa sedambudziko. Chengetai matauriro akasununguka. Itii “Munoda kunzwa magumo acho here kana kusarudza rimwe?” pane kuti “Nderipi chairo?” Ipai sarudzo mushure mekumbomira, kwete munhu asati awana mukana wekupindura.

Kana munhu akatsamwa kana kunyara, siyai mutambo wacho muende kune chimwe chinhu. [Mazano edu ekukurukurirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) anotsanangura maitiro ekuchinja pasina kuita ruzha.

## Kusarudza tsumo dzinokodzera munhu

- **Shandisai tsumo dzemunhu wacho.** Bvunzai mhuri kuti mubereki wavo kana wavakaroorana naye aigara achiti chii. Tsumo dzinobva paudiki dzinowanzova nyore.
- **Sarudzai tsumo pfupi, dzemazuva ose.** Mitsara pamusoro pemamiriro ekunze, kukicheni, bindu, mhuri nemwaka yakajairika uye iri nyore kutevera.
- **Chengetai zviri nyore.** Tsumo dzinovimba nemashoko ekare kana chidzidzo chakadzama dzinogona kuvhiringa. Dzichengeterei vanhu vanodzinakidzwa.
- **Tarirai mutauro.** Tsumo dzakawanda hadzishandurike zvakanaka. Shandisai tsumo dzinozivikanwa nemunhu mumutauro wake, kunyange kana muchifanira kutsvaga itsva.

## Tsumo nemisoro

Muenzaniso wega wega pazasi unoratidza magumo ari mumaparenthesis kwamuri. Verengai chikamu chekutanga chete, mobva mamirira.

- **Mamiriro ekunze:** “Rain before seven...” (magumo: “fine by eleven”); “Every cloud has...” (a silver lining).
- **Kukicheni nechikafu:** “Too many cooks...” (spoil the broth); “Don't count your chickens...” (before they hatch).
- **Pamba nemhuri:** “Home is where the heart...” (is); “Blood is thicker than...” (water).
- **Mwaka nebindu:** “April showers bring...” (May flowers); “You reap what you...” (sow).

## Ko kumunhu asingakwanisi kupindura?

Vamwe vanhu vanotadza kuwana mashoko zvachose pamatanho okupedzisira. Musarega tsumo yacho. Iverengei, nyemwererai, uye kokai mhinduro nechiratidzo, kugutsurira musoro, kana kusarudza mufananidzo. Kugoverana mutinhimira unozivikanwa kuchiri nguva yakagovanwa. Sanganisai mutambo uyu nemimhanzi, chinwiwa chinodziya, kana kukwiza ruoko zvinyoro nyoro, sezviri mu[mitambo yematanho okupedzisira](/activities/late-stage/).

## Dzimwe nzira dzokushandisa tsumo

Tsumo dzinogona kutungamirira zvakanaka kuhurukuro nezvenhoroondo yemunhu. Munogona kukoka nyaya nemibvunzo inobva mu[mibvunzo yekurangarira](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), kana kutamba mutambo wakafanana se[makadhi ehurukuro](/games/conversation-cards/). Kuti muwane mamwe mazano ezuva rose, onai [mitambo yevanhu vanorarama nedementia](/activities/ideas/).
