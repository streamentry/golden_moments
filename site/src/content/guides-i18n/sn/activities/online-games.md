Mhuri dzakawanda dzinotsvaga mitambo yepamhepo yemahara yedementia nekuti dzinoda chinhu chekuita pamwe chete chisingade chitoro, kupinda muaccount kana kadhi rechikwereti. Sarudzo dzakanakisisa dziri nyore, dzinoshandisa mifananidzo, uye dziri nyore kumisa. Dzinofanira kunzwa senguva yekugoverana, kwete bvunzo. Gwaro iri rinotsanangura zvekutarisa, nzira yekugadzirira mutambo, uye zvinogona nezvisingagoni kuitwa nemitambo iyi.

## Chii chinoita kuti mutambo wepamhepo ukodzere vanhu vanorarama nedementia?

Mutambo unowanzo kukodzera vanhu vazhinji vanorarama nedementia kana uchikumbira kuziva kwete kuyeuka. Kuenzanisa mifananidzo miviri yakafanana, kubaya mufananidzo unoenderana nemumvuri, kana kuisa chinhu mubhasikiti rakakodzera zvese zvinoshandisa izvo maziso ari kuona panguva iyoyo. Mitambo inovimba nekuyeuka mutemo, izwi, nhamba kana danho rapfuura inowanzokonzera kushungurudzika.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) inokurudzira zviitwa zvinovakira pane zvinonakidzwa nazvo munhu uye zvaanogona kuita, uye zvinomurega abudirire pasina rubatsiro. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inokurudzira kurerutsa chiitiko pane kuchisiya kana kugona kukachinja.

## Mitambo yeGolden Moments yaunogona kuvhura mubrowser

Mutambo wega wega uri pasi apa unovhurika pawebhusaiti pasina kudhawunirodha. Mizhinji inotambwa patablet kana komputa; mimwe inogona kudhindwawo.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Mapeya makuru ekufananidza ane mifananidzo inoratidzwa, pasina nguva inoverengwa kana zvibodzwa. Wakanaka semutambo wekutanga.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Mufananidzo mumwe uri pamusoro, uye mumwe chete wakafanana uri pakati pesarudzo mbiri kusvika ina pasi.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Mumvuri wakapfava unoonekwa pamusoro; bayai mufananidzo unoenderana nawo.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Bayai mufananidzo, mozo baya bhasikiti raunopinda mariri, sekicheni kana bindu.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Mufananidzo wakachekwa kuita zvidimbu uye mufananidzo wese unoramba uchionekwa segwara.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Tsumo dzinozivikanwa dzisina izwi rekupedzisira; bayai sarudzo kana ratidzai magumo.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Makadhi emifananidzo ane mibvunzo yakasununguka yekukurukura, kwete kubvunza. Anogonawo kudhindwa.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Mapeya ekufananidza akagadzirwa kubva pamapikicha amunosarudza pamudziyo wenyu. Mapikicha anoramba ari mubrowser pamudziyo iwoyo.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Kumbomira kwakadzikama, kwakanyarara kwekufema pamwe chete. Ndimi munotanga uye ndimi munomisa.

Kutsvaga mazwi, kupenda nemavara, uye picture bingo zviripowo sezviitwa zvinogona kudhindwa. Onai [zviitwa zvedementia zvemahara zvinodhindwa](/activities/printable/) kuti muwane zvimwe.

## Maitiro ekugadzirira nguva yemutambo

1. **Sarudzai nguva yakanyarara** apo munhu wamunotarisira akazorora. Masikati anononoka anowanzo kuomera vamwe vanhu, saka tarisai maitiro enyu kutanga.
2. **Shandisai sikirini hombe.** Tablet iri pachigadziko, kana komputa ine browser yakakuriswa, zviri nyore kuona nekubata.
3. **Vharai mamwe matabhu uye dzimai zviziviso.** Zvinhu zvinobuda kamwe kamwe zvinovhundutsa uye zvinogona kupedza nguva yakanaka.
4. **Garai padivi pemunhu, kwete pamberi pake.** Kuona pamwe chete kunonzwa sekuperekedzana kwete bvunzo.
5. **Tangai nemutambo mumwe kwemaminitsi mashanu kusvika gumi.** Regai munhu abate sikirini kana kunongedzera muchichinjana.
6. **Pedzisai panguva yakanaka.** Pedzisai kutenderera kumwe, mobva mataura nezvemufananidzo mumwe kana mokurudzira tii.

## Kuvanzika nekuchengeteka pamidziyo inoshandiswa nevakawanda

- **Mapikicha anoramba ari pamudziyo.** Photo pairs inoshandisa mapikicha amunosarudza, uye anoramba ari mubrowser pamudziyo iwoyo. Tarisai kuti ndiani mumwe anogona kuvhura komputa kana tablet.
- **Dzivisai malink ekunze nekushambadzira.** Shandisai mitambo zvakananga kubva pawebhusaiti, uye musabaye mabhana kana zvikumbiro zvinokumbira ruzivo rwemunhu.
- **Chengetai kubhadhara nemaaccount zvakasiyana.** Musasiye munhu ari ega pachikirini chinokumbira password kana nhamba yekadhi.
- **Chenjerai kufamba-famba.** Chengetai tablet kure kana munhu wamunotarisira angabuda nayo mumba.

Kuti muwane runyorwa rwakakura, onai [kusarudza maapp nemitambo yedementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) uye [kugadzirira tablet kana foni](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Zvinogona nezvisingagoni kuitwa nemitambo iyi

Mitambo yepamhepo inopa vanhu vanorarama nedementia mukana wekunakidzwa nenguva, kuona mifananidzo pamwe chete, uye kutora chikamu mune chimwe chinhu pasina kumanikidzwa. Hairapi dementia, hainonotse, uye haidziviriri. Muenzaniso unozivikanwa zvikuru ndewe [danho rakatorwa neFederal Trade Commission muna 2016 richipokana neLumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) pamusoro pezvirevo zvemitambo yeuropi zvaisakwanisa kutsigirwa. Chenjerai mutambo, app kana webhusaiti ipi neipi inovimbisa ndangariro iri nani, inopa zvibodzwa kuropi renyu kana inoti inogona kudzosera kudzikira.

Shandisai mitambo iyi sechikamu chezuva chinosanganisirawo mimhanzi, kufamba, kukurukura uye kuzorora. Kuti muverenge nezvekuita kuti mitambo ive yakasununguka pane kuva yakaoma, onai [kutamba mitambo nemunhu anorarama nedementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) uye [mitambo yendangariro isingabvunze](/activities/memory-games/).

## Nguva yekumira

Mirai kana munhu wamunotarisira achiratidza kuneta, kusagadzikana, kushungurudzika kana kusanzwa zvakanaka. Mirai kana arasikirwa nechido. Kumira nguva isati yakwana mhedzisiro yakanaka. Munogona kudzoka rimwe zuva, kana kuchinjira kukukurukura kana kufamba.
