Kushanyira munhu wamunoda kwaanogara achichengetwa nedementia, mumusha wevanochengetwa kana muchipatara kunogona kumutsa manzwiro akawanda. Munogona kuzvibvunza kuti motaura sei, motakura chii, kana kuti kushanya kwacho kune basa here kana asingazokurangariri. Kunokosha. National Institute on Aging inotaura kuti vanhu vane chirwere cheAlzheimer [kazhinji vanonakidzwa nekuva nemumwe munhu](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kunyange vasingarangariri kuti mushanyi ndiani. Peji rino rinopa matanho anoshanda ekuita musati mashanya, panguva yekushanya uye mushure mekushanya, pamwe nemazano chaiwo ekugara muchipatara.

## Ndingagadzirira sei kushanya?

Kuronga zvishoma kunoita kuti kushanya kuve nyore kwamuri mose.

- **Sarudzai nguva yakanaka.** Dementia Australia inokurudzira kushanyira panguva iyo munhu wacho anowanzova akasvinura, iyo kune vanhu vazhinji inguva yemangwanani kana masikati. Family Caregiver Alliance inokurudzira kudzidza hurongwa hwenzvimbo yacho uye kushanyira panguva [isina zvimwe zviitwa](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/residential-care-options-visiting-someone-dementia-care/).
- **Bvunzai vashandi.** Bvunzai kuti munhu wacho anga akaita sei nhasi, uye tarisai mitemo ipi zvayo pamusoro pezvekudya, mhuka dzinovaraidzwa kana kubuda kunze.
- **Rongedzai bhegi diki rekushanya.** Chinhu chimwe kana zviviri zvekuita mose zvakakwana (onai runyoro pazasi).
- **Rongedzai nyaya shoma dzekutaura.** Munhu wacho angasa va nenhau dzekutaurira, saka uyai nedzenyu: nhau dzemhuri, mamiriro ekunze, mutambo wenhabvu, kana nyaya inosekesa.
- **Gadzirirai zvaunotarisira iwe pachako.** Dzimwe nguva kushanya kuchava nekudziya uye kutaura kwakawanda. Dzimwe nguva kuchava kwakanyarara kana kwakaoma. Zvose izvi zvakajairika.

## Ndinofanira kutakura chii?

Takurai zvinhu zvekuita mose, kwete zvinhu zvinomuedza. Zvinhu zvakanaka zvinosanganisira:

- **Bhuku diki remifananidzo** rine mifananidzo mikuru, yakajeka. Nyorei mazita kumashure kwayo kuitira vashandi.
- **Runyoro rwenziyo** dzaanofarira pafoni, nemahedhifoni ari nyore kana sipika diki.
- **Chinhu chakapfava chekubata,** chakadai segumbeze, sikavha kana juzi razivikanwa.
- **Mafuta emaoko** ekukwiza maoko zvinyoro, kana munhu wacho achifarira kubatwa.
- **Magazini kana bhuku remifananidzo** rezvaaimbofarira, zvakadai sekurima, mota, shiri kana kubika.
- **Chikafu chaanofarira**, kana vashandi vakati zvakachengeteka.
- **Zvinhu zvazivikanwa zvekushongedza kamuri.** Family Caregiver Alliance inokurudzira maruva, zvirimwa, mifananidzo yemhuri nemifananidzo yekushongedza, kana posita hombe yemifananidzo yemhuri yekare.
- **Tabhureti**, yekutarisa mifananidzo yemhuri kana kutamba mutambo wakapfava pamwe chete.

## Tingaita sei pamwe chete panguva yekushanya?

Kuita chimwe chinhu pamwe chete kunobvisa kumanikidzwa kwekutaura. Dementia Australia inokurudzira kutarisa mifananidzo, basa remaoko riri nyore, kudyara tumiti, mimhanzi, kudyira pamwe chete, kana kufamba zvishoma munzvimbo yacho. Mamwe mazano:

1. **Tarisai mifananidzo.** Taurai zvamuri kuona: “Onai ngowani iyo.” “Mahombekombe aya anotaridzika zvakanaka.” Regai munhu wacho atungamirire. Dzivisai kubvunza kuti “Ndiani uyu?”
2. **Ridzai mimhanzi muimbe.** Nziyo dzazivikanwa dzinogona kusimudza manzwiro uye kuunza nguva yekugovana, kunyange kutaura kuchinetsa.
3. **Fambai zvishoma.** Family Caregiver Alliance inokurudzira kufamba munzvimbo yacho, kunyanya mubindu kana riripo. Kufamba newiricheya kunokwanisikawo.
4. **Kwizai maoko.** Mafuta mashoma nekukwiza maoko zvinyoro kunogona kudzikamisa.
5. **Batsirai pakuzvigadzirira.** Dementia Australia inotaura nezvekukama bvudzi, kupenda nzara kana kubatsira munhu wacho kupfeka zvipfeko zvaanofarira, kana achizvifarira.
6. **Tambai mutambo uri nyore.** Family Caregiver Alliance inokurudzira makadhi, domino, kana chero chaaimbotamba. Patabhureti, [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) inogona kutambwa nemifananidzo yakasiiwa ichioneka uye pasina nguva inoverengwa. [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) inoshandura mifananidzo yamunosarudza kuita mutambo wekufananidza, uye mifananidzo yacho inoramba iri mubrowser yenyu.
7. **Onai chimwe chinhu pamwe chete.** Family Caregiver Alliance inokurudzira mafirimu anosekesa nemafirimu ekare. Kunyange munhu wacho asingakwanise kutevera nyaya, munogona kugara pedyo mogovana nguva yacho.
8. **Verengai zvinonzwika.** Nhetembo pfupi, chidimbu chaanofarira, kana tsamba inobva kunhengo yemhuri.
9. **Ingorai makagara pamwe chete.** Kubatana maoko kana kugara makanyarara kushanya chaiko. Alzheimer’s Association inotaura kuti hazvina kuipa kusaziva zvekutaura, uye kuti kuvapo kwenyu ndiko kunonyanya kukosha.

[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) inogona kubatsira kana mapererwa nezvekutaura. Kadhi rimwe nerimwe rine mufananidzo nemubvunzo wakasununguka.

## Ndinofanira kutaura sei panguva yekushanya?

- **Zviudzei nguva dzose.** “Kwaziwai Baba, ndini Mark, mwanakomana wenyu.” NIA inokurudzira kuti vashanyi vazviudze muchidimbu pakutanga kwekutaura.
- **Swederai kubva kumberi** uye garai pamwero wemaziso ake.
- **Musagadzirisa kana kupikisana.** NIA inopa zano rekuti vashanyi vasagadzirisa munhu wacho kana akanganisa kana akakanganwa chinhu.
- **Ivai nemoyo murefu** kana mashoko achinetsa kuwana. Ipai nguva yakakwana.
- **Pindurai manzwiro.** Kana munhu wacho achiratidzika kusuwa kana kunetseka, bvumai manzwiro acho musati machinja nyaya.
- **Dzivisai mibvunzo inobvunza kuyeuka** yakadai sekuti “Munondirangarira here?” kana “Madyei masikati?”

Onai [mazano ekutaura](/caregiving/communication-tips/) edu kuti muwane zvimwe.

## Ndoita sei kana akati “Ndinoda kuenda kumba”?

Pindurai manzwiro ari shure kwemashoko (gwaro redu pamusoro pe[kana munhu akaramba achikumbira kuenda kumba](/caregiving/asking-to-go-home/) rinotsanangura zvakawanda). Family Caregiver Alliance inotsanangura kuti “Ndinoda kuenda kumba” kazhinji inzira yekuti “Handisi kunzwa kugadzikana,” uye inokurudzira mhinduro dzakadai sekuti “Ndiudzei nezvekumba kwenyu” kana “Chii chamunonyanya kufarira kumba kwenyu?” Dementia Australia inowedzera kuti “kumusha” kunogona kureva nguva kana nzvimbo iyo munhu wacho ainzwa akachengeteka, zvakadai sepaudiki hwake.

Edzai:

- kubvuma manzwiro acho: “Zvinoita sekuti munosuwa kumba.”
- kupa nyaradzo kuburikidza nekubata, kana achizvigamuchira
- kutarisa mifananidzo kana kutaura nezvekumba kwemhuri
- kukumbira kufamba, kunwa kana kudya chimwe chinhu

Dzivisai kupikisana kana kutsanangura kuti haakwanise kuenda. Kazhinji hazvibatsiri uye zvinowanzowedzera kushungurudzika.

## Kushanya kunofanira kutora nguva yakareba sei?

Kushanya kupfupi, kunowanzoitwa kazhinji kunoshanda zviri nani. Family Caregiver Alliance inotaura kuti munhu wacho kazhinji haayeuki kuti makagara kwemaminitsi mashanu kana maawa mashanu, uye inokurudzira kuti kushanya kupfupi katatu pavhiki kunogona kuva nani pane kushanya kumwe chete kurefu. Dementia Australia inoti hapana nhamba yakarurama yekushanya kana nguva yakarurama.

Mumavhiki ekutanga mushure mekunge munhu atamira kunzvimbo yekuchengetwa kwedementia, Family Caregiver Alliance inokurudzira kushanyira kakawanda, mozodzikama zvishoma nezvishoma muhurongwa hwenguva dzose. Bvunzai vashandi zvavari kuona.

## Ndingabva sei ndisina kumushungurudza?

Kuenda kunogona kuva chinhu chakaomesesa. Dementia Australia inokurudzira:

- **Taurai kuti mungagara kwenguva yakareba sei pamunosvika.** “Ndinogona kugara kweawa imwe chete, ndobva ndaenda kunotenga.”
- **Shandisai chiitiko senzira yakajairika yekupedzisa.** “Patinopedza rwiyo urwu, ndinozoenda.”
- **Rongedzai kuenda nenguva yekudya kana yechimwe chiitiko,** kana kukumbira vashandi kubatsira munhu wacho kutanga chimwe chinhu.
- **Chengetai nzira yekusiirana iri pfupi uye yakafanana nguva dzose.** Kunonoka, kukumbira ruregerero kana kugara “kwechinguva chidiki” kunogona kuita kuti kusiirana kweramangwana kuome.

Mushure mekunge maenda, zvakajairika kunzwa kusuwa. Zviperei zororo. Kana munhu wacho akashungurudzika, vashandi vanowanzo kukwanisa kumubatsira kudzikama nguva pfupi yapfuura.

## Kushanyira munhu anorarama nedementia muchipatara

Chipatara inzvimbo ine ruzha, isina kujairika uye izere nevanhu vaasingazivi, izvo zvinogona kuva zvakaoma kumunhu anorarama nedementia. Gwaro reNIA pamusoro pe[kuenda nemunhu ane chirwere cheAlzheimer kuchipatara](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/taking-person-alzheimers-disease-hospital) rinokurudzira:

- **Ivai nemumwe munhu anogara nemunhu wacho** zvakanyanya sezvinobvira: nhengo yemhuri, shamwari yaanovimba nayo kana munhu anobhadharwa kumuchengeta.
- **Ipai vashandi bepa reruzivo rwemunhu wacho** rine maitiro ake ezuva nezuva, zita raanofarira kudanwa naro, zvaanoda nezvaasingadi, zvinhu zvinogona kumunetsa, nezviratidzo zvemarwadzo zvaasingatauri nemashoko. Isai makopi mukamuri nepanzvimbo yevanamukoti.
- **Batsirai pakudya nekunwa.** Vanhu vakuru vanogona kushaya kudya kwakakwana vari muchipatara. Vhurai midziyo, batsirai pakudya, idyai pamwe chete, uye mupai zvinwiwa.
- **Uyai nezvinhu zvinonyaradza** zvakadai semifananidzo kana zvinhu zvekunamata.
- **Deredzai ruzha.** Dzimai TV uye deredzai ruzha rwefoni nerwezviziviso.
- **Edzai tsika dzinodzikamisa,** dzakadai semimhanzi inonyaradza, kuverenga, kunamata, kuimba kana kutarisa mifananidzo yekare.
- **Kurudzirai kufamba-famba** zvakanyanya sezvinogoneka zvakachengeteka, nemvumo yevashandi.
- **Udzai chiremba pakarepo kana munhu wacho achiratidzika kunge anyanyisa kuipa kana kuti asiyana.** NIA inotsanangura kuti kuvhiringika kukuru kunouya kamwe kamwe, kunonzi delirium, kunogona kukonzerwa nematambudziko akadai seutachiona, fivha, kushaya mvura mumuviri kana mhedzisiro yemishonga, uye kunoda kurapwa.

Kushanya kupfupi, kwakadzikama kune chiso chazivikanwa nerwiyo raanofarira kunogona kuva chinhu chinonyanya kunyaradza pazuva riri muchipatara.

## Kuenda nevana pakushanya

Vana vanogona kuuya nekudziya kukuru. Vagadzirirei pachine nguva nemashoko ari nyore, echokwadi, chengetai kushanya kuri kupfupi, uye vapei basa, rakadai sekuratidza mufananidzo wavakadhirowa kana kupa mufananidzo. NIA inotaura kuti vana nevanhu vane chirwere cheAlzheimer vanogona kunakidzwa nezviitwa zviri nyore pamwe chete, zvakadai sekutarisa bhuku remifananidzo kana kuteerera mimhanzi. Dementia Australia inokurudzira kurongedza bhegi diki rezvekudya zvishoma kana zvinhu zvakanyarara zvekuchengeta vana vadiki vakabatikana.

## Zvichengetei nemiwo

Kushanya kunogona kunetesa uye kunobata pamanzwiro, kunyanya kana munhu wacho achinja zvakanyanya. Hazvina kuipa kushanyira kashoma kana muchida, kuuya neshamwari kuti ikutsigirei, kana kuenda nekukurumidza pazuva rakaoma. Kuti muwane mazano ekuzvichengeta, onai [kuzvichengeta kwemunhu anotarisira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/). Kana muchigara kure, onai gwaro redu pamusoro pe[kufona nefoni uye vhidhiyo](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).
