Mimhanzi ndechimwe chezvinhu zviri nyore zvamunogona kugovera nemunhu anogara nedementia. Haidi chinhu: hapana mhinduro dzakarurama, hapana matanho ekutevera. Rwiyo rwaanoziva runogona kuuya nenyemwerero, kutsika kwetsoka kana mashoko mashoma ekuimba pamwe chete, kunyange pazuva rinonetsa kutaura. Zvese zvamunoda mimhanzi yakakodzera munhu uyu uye nguva yakanyarara yekuiridza.

## Ndezvipi zviitwa zvemimhanzi zvinoshanda kuvanhu vanogara nedementia?

Kuteerera, kuimba, kuombera maoko, kuzununguka uye kutaura nezvemimhanzi zvose zvinoshanda zvakanaka. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inokurudzira kuridza mimhanzi, kutaura nezvemimhanzi nemuimbi, kuimba kana kutamba nziyo dzinozivikanwa, uye kuenda kumakonzati kana zvirongwa zvemimhanzi munharaunda. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) inowedzera kuti kufamba kwakaita sekuombera maoko kana kutamba kunogona kuwedzera mufaro, uye kuti mimhanzi inogona kuva nzira yekubatana kana mashoko ava kunetsa.

## Munogadzira sei runyorwa rwenziyo rwemunhu?

Chinhu chinonyanya kubatsira chamunogona kugadzira runyorwa rwenziyo dzine chirevo kumunhu uyu. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) inosimbisawo mimhanzi ine chirevo chemunhu. Heino nzira iri nyore yekuigadzira.

### Tangirai nehupenyu hwake

- **Udiki hwake.** Fungai nezvezvairidzwa paredhiyo, pamitambo yekutamba uye mumafirimu achiri mudiki.
- **Nguva dzakakosha.** Mimhanzi yepamuchato wavo, rwiyo rwekutamba kwavo kwekutanga, kana dambarefu ravaifarira parwendo rwemhuri.
- **Kutenda netsika.** Nziyo dzekereke, mimhanzi inoyera, kana nziyo dzemutauro wake wekutanga.
- **Basa nebasa rekushandira.** Mabhendi ekufora, nziyo dzebasa, kana mimhanzi ine chekuita nenguva yaaiva muchiuto.
- **Zvinorangarirwa nemhuri.** Bvunza hama, vana neshamwari dzekare kuti ndeipi mimhanzi yavaigaroridza kana kuimba.

### Fungai nezvemarudzi emimhanzi, kwete nziyo chete

Kana musina chokwadi pekutangira, edzai maminitsi mashoma kubva pane chimwe nechimwe chezvinotevera uye cherechedzai mhinduro: big band neswing, doo-wop, Motown nesoul, rock and roll yekutanga, country newestern, bluegrass, gospel nenziyo dzekereke, nziyo dzeBroadway, jazz standards, mimhanzi yechiLatin yakaita seboleros kana mariachi, polka, folk neclassical. Chengetai zvinounza nyemwerero kana kutsika kwetsoka; siyai zvinounza kusafara.

### Itai kuti zvive nyore kuridza

- **Pasina kushambadzira.** Alzheimer's Association inoyambira kuti kushambadzira kunogona kukonzera kuvhiringidzika. Shandisai mimhanzi yakatorwa, maCD, marekodhi kana sevhisi isina kushambadzira.
- **Mudziyo mumwe uri nyore.** Spika ine bhatani rimwe guru, kana runyorwa rwenziyo rwakachengetwa rwagadzirirwa, kuti chero ani zvake akwanise kuitanga.
- **Mazita mapfupi maviri.** Rimwe rakadzikama rekudzikamisa uye rimwe rine simba rekusimudzira manzwiro.
- **Nyorei zita.** Nyora zita remunhu wacho uye "mimhanzi yaanofarira" pamudziyo kuitira kuti mhuri dzinouya kuzoshanya nevashandi vekuchengeta vakwanise kuishandisa zvakare.

## Zviitwa 15 zvemimhanzi zvekuedza

1. **Teererai mese.** Garai padivi nepadivi pasina chimwe chinhu chiri kuitika. Kuteerera chiitiko chakakwana.
2. **Imbai pamwe chete.** Tangai kuimba imi. Kungoimba muridzo kana kufambisa miromo nemashoko zvakaringana.
3. **Omberai maoko kana kurova mutinhimira.** Rovaibvai mabvi, rovaibvai tafura, kana kushandisa ngoma diki yeruoko kana chinoridzwa chinodedera.
4. **Tambai zvishoma nezvishoma.** Batanai maoko muzununguke mukicheni, kana tambai makagara pasi, muchifambisa maoko pamwe chete.
5. **Kutamba nemachira.** Zunungusai machira akareruka muchitevedza mutinhimira, makamira kana makagara pasi.
6. **Taurai nezvemimhanzi.** Kokai, musaedze: “Izvi zvinonzwika senzvimbo yekutamba. Ndiudzei nezvemitambo yekutamba.” Musambobvunza kuti “Munorangarira rwiyo urwu here?”
7. **Ridzai dambarefu rose.** Isai rekodhi ravaida, kubva pakutanga kusvika kumagumo, muine kofi.
8. **Tarirai mavhavha emadambarefu.** Mavhavha ekare emarekodhii akanakira kutaura nezvemafashoni, mota nezviso.
9. **Onai mumhanzi wemitambo.** Firimu remumhanzi kana konzati yenguva yavo inoita manheru akanaka. Imbomirai kuti muzorore.
10. **Ridzai chiridzwa chaanoziva.** Aimbova mutambi wepiyano kana gitare anogona kufara kuridza zviri nyore. Gare gare, kungoridza manotsi mashoma pamwe chete zvakakwana.
11. **Mimhanzi muchishanda.** Ridzai nziyo dzavanofarira muchipeta mbatya kana kugadzira tafura.
12. **Rwiyo rwemangwanani.** Tangai zuva rega rega nerwiyo rumwe chete runofadza sechikamu chetsika yezuva nezuva.
13. **Imbai nziyo dzekereke kana dzinoyera.** Ridzai mimhanzi yekutenda kwavo uye muimbe pamwe chete.
14. **Joinai boka rekuimba.** NHS inotsanangura mapoka ekuimba seakanaka pamanzwiro nekugara zvakanaka. Bvunza munharaunda menyu nezvemakwaya ane hushamwari kuvanhu vanogara nedementia.
15. **Endai kunonzwa mimhanzi mhenyu.** Konzati pfupi yenharaunda kana bhendi rekunze. Garai pedyo nemukova wekubuda uye budai chero nguva yamunoda.

Kuti muwane chiitiko chakareruka chekutamba nemashoko nemitsara inozivikanwa, edzai [pedzisa chirevo](/games/finish-the-saying/), uko munogona kugara muchiratidza magumo acho.

## Kuenzanisa mimhanzi nenguva

Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kushandisa mimhanzi kugadzira mamiriro aunoda: mimhanzi yakadzikama kuitira mamiriro erunyararo, uye rwiyo rune simba rweudiki hwemunhu kusimudzira mweya.

| Nguva | Edzai | Zano |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kumuka | Rwiyo rwaanoziva, runofadza | Rwiyo rumwe chete mangwanani ega ega runova chiratidzo chinyoro |
| Mabasa epamba | Nziyo dzine simba dzavanofarira | Mimhanzi inogona kuita kuti basa rinzwe sekunge mune shamwari |
| Kudya | Mimhanzi yakapfava isina mazwi | Ichengetei yakaderera kuti isakwikwidzane nekudya |
| Masikati anononoka | Mimhanzi yakadzikama, yaanoziva muchiedza chakanaka | Onai [kusagadzikana kwemasikati anononoka](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |
| Nguva yekurara | Mimhanzi inononoka, yakapfava | Dzikisai ruzha nezviedza pamwe chete |
| Danho repamusoro | Nziyo dzake dzaanoda zvakanyanya, zvinyoro nyoro | Garai pedyo uye mubate ruoko |

## Mazano ekuita kuti chiitiko chive chakanaka

- **Dzimai rumwe ruzha.** Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kuvhara mahwindo nemagonhi uye kudzima TV kuti mimhanzi ive ndiro ruzha chete.
- **Chengetai ruzha rwakaderera.** Mimhanzi inofanira kunge yakagadzikana, kwete ine ruzha. Kana munhu wacho achishandisa zvinobatsira kunzwa, ivai nechokwadi kuti zvakaiswa uye zviri kushanda; onai [kunzwa](/brain-health/hearing/).
- **Cherechedzai nziyo dzinounza kusuwa.** Dzimwe nziyo dzinounza ndangariro dzekusuwa, hondo kana kurasikirwa. Kana rwiyo rukamushungurudza, munyaradzei uye muchinje.
- **Muregei atungamirire.** Kana zvichibvira, regai munhu wacho asarudze mimhanzi, uye gamuchirai kana akati kwete.
- **Mirei chero nguva.** Maminitsi mashoma emimhanzi chiitiko chakanaka. Kudzima uye kugara makanyarara zvakanaka zvakare.

## Tsvakurudzo inotii nezvemimhanzi nedementia?

Umbowo hushoma. [Ongororo yeCochrane ya2025](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) yakaongorora zvidzidzo 30 zvezvirongwa zvemimhanzi zvakarongwa, kazhinji nemapoka mudzimba dzevanochengetwa. Yakaona kuti zvirongwa izvi zvinogona kuderedza zvishoma zviratidzo zvekuora mwoyo zvichienzaniswa nekuchengeta kwakajairika, asi haina kuwana mhedzisiro yakajeka pakusagadzikana, kugara zvakanaka kana kufunga nekurangarira, uye hapana mhedzisiro yakaramba iripo mushure mekunge zvirongwa zvapera.

Tinogovera mimhanzi nekuti inzira inofadza, isina kumanikidza yekupedza nguva pamwe chete, kwete sekurapwa. Kune nzvimbo dzemapoka, onai [zviitwa zvemapoka mumusha wevanochengetwa](/activities/care-home-groups/); pakushandisa mimhanzi pamwe chete nemifananidzo nenyaya, onai [zviitwa zvekurangarira](/activities/reminiscence/).
