Mhuri dzakawanda dzinoda mutambo wekutamba pamwe chete unonzwa sekukurukurirana, kwete kuongororwa. Mibvunzo makumi maviri nemimwe mitambo yekufungidzira inogona kushanda zvakanaka, nekuti mitemo yayo iri nyore uye hurukuro inogona kuenda kwese kwese. Chinokosha kushandura mabasa akajairika: munhu wacho anofunga mhinduro, uye imi ndimi munobvunza. Peji rino rinotsanangura matambiro emitambo iyi zvinyoro nyoro, uye kuti ndeipi mitambo yekukurukurirana inokodzera vanhu vari kurarama nedementia.

## Nei tichifanira kushandura mitemo yakajairika?

Mumubvunzo wakajairika, mumwe munhu anobvunza uye mumwe anofanira kuziva. Izvi zvinoisa munhu ari kurarama nedementia panzvimbo paanogona kutadza, uye kutadza kunogona kumurwadza. Kana iye akasarudza chinhu uye imi mofanira kufungidzira, iye ndiye nyanzvi. Iye ndiye akabata mhinduro, ndiye anosarudza kuti otaura hungu kana kwete riinhi, uye anogona kunakidzwa nekuona imi muchiedza.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) inopa zano kuvanhu vanotarisira kuti vashandise mitsara mipfupi uye iri nyore, mubvunzo mumwe panguva, uye inzwi rakadzikama uye rine mwoyo murefu. Maitiro aya anokodzera mitambo yekufungidzira.

## Matambiro eMibvunzo makumi maviri, zvinyoro nyoro

1. **Sarudzai musoro** unobva muupenyu hwemunhu wacho, zvakaita sekicheni, gadheni, mhuka kana gungwa.
2. **Regai afunge chinhu** ochitaura chinyararire, kana kuratidza kadhi rine mufananidzo kuitira kuti musafanire kuchinzwa.
3. **Bvunzai mubvunzo mumwe unopindurwa nehungu kana kwete panguva.** “Chinhu chinodyiwa here?” “Chakakura kupfuura shangu here?”
4. **Gamuchirai mhinduro ipi neipi.** Kugutsurira, kuzunza musoro, kana kuseka zvese zvinogamuchirwa.
5. **Fungidzirai zvinonzwika** uye munakidzwe nazvo. Kana mafungidzira zvakanaka, farai mese.
6. **Ipai mazano madiki** kana munhu achiita seasina chokwadi: “Chinhu chiri mukicheni.”
7. **Musamboverenga mibvunzo** uye musamboverenga zvibodzwa.

Kana munhu asingakwanisi kutevera mitemo, chinjirai kune imwe nzira iri nyore: imi munodoma zvinhu zvishoma uye iye anonongedzera chakanaka.

## Zvinhu zvakanaka zvekusarudza

- Muchero, muriwo kana ruva raanofarira
- Mhuka yepapurazi, yemuzoo kana chipfuwo chaakambova nacho
- Chishandiso, chakaita sesando, reki kana tsono yekusona
- Fenicha kana chinhu chemumba
- Nzvimbo yaanoziva zvikuru, yakaita sepaki kana taundi
- Chipfeko chaaifarira kupfeka

Regai mazita evanhu, chero chinhu chinogona kukonzera kusuwa, uye zvinhu zvinogona kuva nengozi kana kuvhiringidza zviri nyore.

## Mimwe mitambo yekukurukurirana

- **Ichi kana icho.** Ipai sarudzo mbiri mobvunza kuti ndechipi chaanofarira: tii kana kofi, makomo kana gungwa, rokwe dzvuku kana rebhuruu. Hapana mhinduro isiri iyo.
- **Ungasarudza chii.** Bvunzai mubvunzo wakapfava uye wakareruka: “Ungasarudza kupikiniki here kana kudya kunze?”
- **Pedzisa chirevo.** Chirevo chinonyatsozivikanwa chasiiwa chisina kupera. Edzai [mitambo yekupedzisa zvirevo](/activities/finish-the-saying/) kuti muwane gwara.
- **Doma nzvimbo.** Mukumbirei kutsanangura kamuri, taundi kana nzvimbo yaanofarira, uye mumukoke kuti muifungidzire pamwe chete.
- **Makadhi emibvunzo yakasununguka.** [Makadhi edu ekukurukurirana](/games/conversation-cards/) anopa mibvunzo yakasununguka nemisoro, anogona kudhindwa kuti ashandiswe patafura.

Kuti muwane mamwe mazano anobva pandangariro nenyaya dzehupenyu, onai [mibvunzo yekurangarira](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Kuti muwane mafaro emibvunzo anoitwa zvinyoro nyoro, onai [mibvunzo iri nyore yevakuru vari kurarama nedementia](/activities/trivia/), inoshandisa sarudzo uye isina zvibodzwa.

## Zvinoita sei kana munhu akatsamwa kana kushungurudzika?

Mirai, nyemwererai uye taurai chimwe chinhu chakaita se, “Ngatitambei chimwe chinhu.” Kushungurudzika chiratidzo chekuti chinjai mutambo, kwete kuti musundire mberi. Gwaro redu rekuti [matambiro emitambo nemunhu ari kurarama nedementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) rinotsanangura mashanduriro amungaita mitemo panguva yemutambo.

## Zvinogona kuitwa nemitambo yekukurukurirana nezvaisingakwanisi

Mitambo yekukurukurirana inofadza uye inosimbisa ukama. Inogona kubatsira vanhu kubatana uye kugoverana nyaya. Haina kurapa dementia, haina kuidzikamisa kana kuidzivirira. Chengetai chinangwa chiri nyore: nguva yakanaka pamwe chete.
