Kuongororwa dementia panguva yamuchiri kushanda kunogona kumutsa mibvunzo pamusoro pemari, zvakavanzika, kuzivikanwa kwenyu uye kuchengeteka. Vamwe vanhu vanoda kuramba vari pabasa ravo razvino, vamwe vanofunga nezvekuchinja, uye vamwe vanosarudza kusiya basa. Hapana nzira imwe chete inokodzera munhu wese. Zvamunoda, mabasa ebasa, hutano hwenyu uye rutsigiro zvese zvinokosha.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association nezveyounger-onset Alzheimer's](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset) rinotaura nezvebasa, mari yemhuri uye rubatsiro rwemushandirwi. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead/work-and-dementia) ne[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) vanotsanangura sarudzo dzekuramba uri pabasa, kushandura basa kana kusiya basa. Mitemo yebasa inosiyana nenzvimbo, saka gwaro iri rinopa mibvunzo yekufunga nezvayo, kwete mazano emutemo kana emari.

## Kuongororwa kunoreva here kuti ndinofanira kurega kushanda?

Kwete. Kuongororwa kwega hakusarudzi kuti munogona here kuramba muchishanda. [Alzheimer's Society inoti](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) sarudzo inoenderana nemanzwiro enyu, basa renyu uye mamiriro enyu. Vamwe vanhu vanoenderera mberi vari pabasa rimwe chete, vamwe vanochinja mabasa kana maawa, uye vamwe vanosiya basa.

Fungai nezvezviri kufamba zvakanaka uye nezvezvava kuoma. Munogona kunyora pasi mabasa anoita seakajairika, nguva yamunonzwa kuneta kana kukanganiswa, uye kana paine zvikanganiso kana zvinonetsa zvekuchengeteka zvakaitika. Mienzaniso yakajeka inogona kukubatsirai imi nemurapi wenyu kutaura nezvebasa pasina kutora zuva rimwe rakaoma sechisarudzo pamusoro pekugona kwenyu.

Kuchengeteka kwakakoshawo. Kana basa renyu richisanganisira kutyaira, kushandisa michina, kutarisira vamwe vanhu kana rimwe basa rinoda kuchengeteka kwakanyanya, bvunzai murapi wenyu kuti mungatarisa sei njodzi. Mitemo yemunzvimbo, mitemo yebasa uye zvibvumirano zvebasa zvinogonawo kushanda. Peji rino harigoni kukuudzai kana rimwe basa rakachengeteka kana kuti mushandirwi wenyu anofanira kuita sei.

## Ndingataura sei nemurapi wangu nezvebasa?

Tsanangurai mabasa anosanganisirwa mubasa renyu uye shanduko dzamaona. Bvunzai kana dambudziko rehutano ringakanganisa rimwe basa, ndezvipi zviratidzo zvinoreva kuti munofanira kutsvaga rubatsiro, uye kana mumwe nyanzvi achigona kuongorora rimwe basa. Mungada kuuya nemunhu wamunovimba naye, asi sarudzai mese kuti angakutsigirai sei pasina kutaura pachinzvimbo chenyu.

[Gwaro rekushanyira chiremba](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) rine nzira dzekugadzirira mibvunzo nezvinyorwa. Munogona kukumbira murapi wenyu kuti atsanangure zvaanogona kuongorora uye zvinoda kuongororwa kwakasiyana kwebasa kana kwemabasa. Gwaro rakajairika redementia harigoni kutsiva kuongororwa kwehutano nekuchengeteka kwemunhu mumwe nemumwe.

## Ndedzipi shanduko pabasa dzingabatsira?

Kana muchida kuramba muchishanda, bvunzai kuti ndezvipi shanduko dzingaitika mubasa renyu. Dementia Australia inokurudzira sarudzo dzakaita sekushandisa maitiro akajairika, kuita basa rimwe panguva, kunyora pasi zvinhu uye kugadzira gwara renhanho nenhanho remabasa anowanzoitwa. Zvichienderana nebasa renyu, mungabvunzawo nezvemirayiridzo yakanyorwa, karenda, kukanganiswa kushoma kana zororo rakarongwa.

Torai izvi sepfungwa dzekukurukura, kwete runyorwa runofanira kushandiswa nemushandirwi wese. Sarudzai shanduko imwe inogadzirisa basa ramunoona rakaoma. Kana imi nemushandirwi wenyu mukaedza, bvumiranai kuti muchaiongorora sei uye kana ichibatsira. Shanduko inoshanda kune rimwe basa inogona kusakodzera rimwe.

## Ndinofanira kuudza mukuru wangu webasa here?

Imi ndimi munosarudza ruzivo rwepauzima rwekugovera, kunze kwekunge mutemo wemunzvimbo, chibvumirano kana chinodiwa chekuchengeteka chichishanda pabasa renyu. Zvinodiwa zvinosiyana nenyika, dunhu uye basa. Musati masarudza, tarisai chibvumirano chenyu, mutemo webasa uye nhungamiro yemunzvimbo. Kana musina chokwadi, tauriranai nechibangamazano webasa ane ruzivo, mumiriri wemubatanidzwa wevashandi kana gweta anoziva mitemo yemunogara.

Kana mukasarudza kuudza mumwe munhu, fungai nezvezvamunoda kuti azive uye nerutsigiro rwamuri kukumbira. Mungada kutanga kutaura nemukuru wamunovimba naye kana munhu weHR. Bvunzai kuti ruzivo rwenyu ruchachengetwa sei uye ndiani acharuona; musafungidzira kuti mabasa ese ane nzira imwe chete yezvakavanzika.

Mamwe mabasa ane mabasa anoda kuchengeteka kwakanyanya. Kana musina chokwadi kuti mombomira kana kuchinja rimwe remabasa iwayo, tauriranai nemurapi wenyu nechibangamazano webasa ane ruzivo wemunzvimbo menyu nekukurumidza. Musavimbe negwaro rakajairika kuti musarudze kana rimwe basa rakachengeteka.

## Nderupi rubatsiro nemari zvandinofanira kutarisa?

Musati mashandura maawa enyu kana kusiya basa, ongororai rubatsiro rwuripo kuburikidza nemushandirwi wenyu. Bvunzai HR kana anoona nezverubatsiro nezvezororo, rubatsiro rweurema kana rwemari, inishuwarenzi yehutano uye mazuva ekupedzisira. Kumbirai mitemo yazvino yakanyorwa uye chengetai kopi yeruzivo rwamunowana.

Kukodzera, mutero uye nguva zvinoenderana nemushandirwi wenyu, chirongwa cheinishuwarenzi uye nzvimbo yamunogara. [Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kuongorora rubatsiro rwemushandirwi uye kuchengeta chisimbiso chakanyorwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset). Pesarudzo yenyu, tauriranai nechibangamazano werubatsiro kana wemari ane ruzivo anogona kuongorora magwaro anoshanda kwamuri. Musasaina kana kumisa inishuwarenzi muchivimba chete nechinyorwa chakajairika.

Mhuri yenyu inogona kukanganiswawo nekushanduka kwemari kana kwehurongwa. Kana muchida, tauriranai nevanhu vamunovimba navo nezvezvinonyanya kukosha uye sarudzo dzamunoda kuongorora. [Gwaro rematanho ekutanga mushure mekuongororwa](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) rinotaura nezverutsigiro rwakawanda uye mibvunzo yekuronga ramangwana.

## Ko kana ndikasarudza kuderedza maawa kana kusiya basa?

Kusiya basa kunogona kuva sarudzo yakakodzera kune vamwe vanhu. Hazvirevi kuti makundikana. Mungada kufunga nezvemari, inishuwarenzi yehutano, maitiro ezuva nezuva uye vanhu vamuchasuwa kuona. Bvunzai nezverutsigiro rwemunzvimbo musati maita shanduko, uye zvipei nguva yekuenzanisa sarudzo dzinokosha kwamuri.

Basa ingori chikamu chimwe chehupenyu. Kana mukamisa kana kuderedza maawa, sarudzai zvimwe zviitiko kana hukama hunonzwa hune chinangwa pamunenge magadzirira. Vemhuri vanogona kupa rubatsiro, asi sarudzo inofanira kukusanganisirai imi. [Gwaro rekuzvitarisira kwemutarisiri](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rine pfungwa dzekukumbira vamwe kuti vagovane rutsigiro pasina kuisa mutoro wese pamunhu mumwe chete.
