Kuona, kunzwa, kunhuhwidza, kuravira kana kunzwa chinhu chisingaonekwi nevamwe kunogona kunzwika sechokwadi kumunhu wacho. Zvinogona kumutyisa, kana kuti zvingasavatyisi. Tangai nezvekuti munhu ari kunzwa sei uye kana paine ari munjodzi, pane kuedza kuratidza kuti chii chakaitika kana chisina kuitika.

Chiitiko ichi chinogona kuva kuona kana kunzwa zvisipo, asi chinogonawo kuva kusanzwisisa kana kuona chinhu sechimwe. Mumvuri unogona kuita seunoratidza munhu; munhu anozivikanwa anogonawo kuvhiringidzwa nemumwe. Hamufaniri kusarudza kuti chii chiri kuitika. Tsanangurai zvinotaurwa nemunhu uye zvamunoona, uye bvunzai chiremba kana mune hanya nazvo.

## Ndinofanira kuita sei panguva yacho?

Rambai makadzikama, bvunzai kuti munhu ari kunzwa sei, uye mupe rutsigiro musingapokane naye. [Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kuongorora kana chiitiko chacho chiri kunetsa kana chine njodzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/hallucinations). Kana munhu akasununguka uye akachengeteka, panogona kunge pasina chikonzero chekumukanganisa.

- **Bvumai manzwiro ake.** Munogona kuti, „Ini handisi kuona munhu, asi ndiri kunzwa kuti izvi zviri kukutyisai. Ndiri pano nemi.“ Izvi kutaura chokwadi uye zvinoratidza munhu kuti muri kumuteerera zvakakomba.
- **Chengetai mashoko mapfupi.** Tsananguro refu kana kuramba muchimugadzirisa kunogona kuwedzera kushushikana. Regaai munhu atsanangure zvaari kuona kana achida.
- **Kumbirai, musamanikidze, kuchinja.** Bvunzai kana vangada kugara kune imwe nzvimbo, kubatidza rimwe chiedza kana kuteerera rwiyo rwavanoziva. Kuenda mukamuri rakajeka, ravanogara vachiziva kunogona kubatsira; mirai kana munhu asingadi kuenda.
- **Garaai pedyo kana vachida kuti muvepo.** Musabata munhu kamwe kamwe. Bvunzai musati mamupa ruoko kana kumumbundira.

Kana chiitiko chacho chisina njodzi uye chisiri kumunetsa, teverai zvinodiwa nemunhu. Musashandure nguva iyi kuita bvunzo kana kumukumbira kuti aratidze zvaakaona.

Vamwe vanhu vanogona kunzwa chiitiko ichi chichinyaradza kana kuti chichizivikanwa. Kana munhu akadzikama uye akachengeteka, panogona kunge pasina chikonzero chekuchinja nyaya kana kumuendesa kune imwe nzvimbo. Bvunzai zvaangada; musashandure nguva dzese dzakadaro kuita hurukuro yezvekurapwa. Kana achitya, pindurai manzwiro iwayo kunyange imi musingaoni chinhu chimwe chete.

## Pane chiri mukamuri chingavhiringidzwa semunhu kana ruzha here?

Ongororai nzvimbo yacho nemunhu kana achigamuchira rubatsiro irworwo. [Alzheimer's Society inosiyanisa kuona kana kunzwa zvisipo nekusanzwisisa zvakanaka](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations), apo chinhu chaicho kana maumbirwo anoonekwa zvisiri izvo.

- **Wedzerai chiedza.** Batidzai mwenje uye tarisai mimvuri kana zvinoratidzwa negirazi nemahwindo.
- **Tarisai ruzha rwakajairika.** Terevhizheni, feni kana mudziyo wemumba unogona kunzwika senzwi kana tsoka. Dzikisai kana kudzimai chete kana munhu achibvumirana nazvo.
- **Tarisai maumbirwo.** Kapeti, keteni kana bepa remadziro rine maumbirwo akawanda rinogona kuvhiringidzwa sekufamba kana chinhu. Bvunzai kana munhu angada kuona ari pane imwe nzvimbo kana kuchinja zvaari kuona.
- **Tarisai midziyo yekubatsira.** Kana munhu achishandisa magirazi emaziso kana mudziyo wekunzwa, bvunzai kana achida rubatsiro rwekutarisa kuti zviripo uye zviri kushanda sezvakajairwa.

Kana chiitiko chacho chikaitika munzvimbo imwe chete, tarisai chinhu chinodzokororwa: chinoonekwa pagirazi, maumbirwo akasimba pasi, terevhizheni inonzwika kubva mune imwe kamuri kana chiedza chakaderera manheru. Tarisai kana paine ruzha kana chinhu chaicho musati mafunga kuti maonero emunhu achinja. Kana mukaedza kuchinja nharaunda, tsanangurai uye bvunzai kana zvave nani.

Edzai kuchinja kudiki kumwe chete panguva. Musarongazve zvinhu zvemunhu kana kubvisa chinhu chaanokoshesa musina kubvunza. Maitiro aanoziva uye nzvimbo yakadzikama, ine chiedza chakanaka zvinogona kunzwa zviri nyore.

## Ndinofanira kubata chiremba riini?

Batai chiremba wemunhu nezvezviitiko zvitsva, zvinodzokororwa, zvinotora nguva kana zvinonetsa. Kuchinja kwekuona kana kunzwa, kurwara, mishonga nezvimwe zvinhu zvinogona kubatsira. Chiremba anogona kuongorora zviri kuitika uye kana paine chimwe chinofanira kuteverwa. [Gwaro reDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) rinokurudzirawo kutarisa zvinhu zvemuviri, zvekunzwa kana kuona, zvemishonga uye zvenzvimbo.

Kuchinja kunongoerekana kwaitika kwakasiyana. [NHS inoti kuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika kunoda rubatsiro rwechiremba nekukurumidza](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), uye [Alzheimer's Society inokurudzira rubatsiro rwechiremba nekukurumidza kana zviitiko izvi zvichibata pfungwa dzinopfuura imwe](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations). Batai rubatsiro rwekurapa rwechimbichimbi rwemunharaunda yenyu kana munhu akangoerekana avhiringika kana kuti chiitiko chacho chinobata pfungwa dzinopfuura imwe. Kana paine ari munjodzi nekukurumidza, shandisai rubatsiro rwenjodzi rwemunharaunda yenyu.

Musatanga, musamisa kana kuchinja mushonga mega. Kumbirai munhu akanyora mushonga kana wemishonga kuti aongorore kuchinja kuchangobva kuitika nemishonga yese inoshandiswa nemunhu, kusanganisira mishonga inotengwa pasina gwaro rechiremba. [Gwaro redu rekushanya kuna chiremba](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) rinogona kubatsira kugadzirira mibvunzo.

## Ndeapi mashoko anogona kubatsira pakushanya kuna chiremba?

Kana munhu achibvumirana, nyorai pasi zvamakaona muchidimbu kuti mugovane nechikwata chinomuchengeta:

- zvavakati vakaona, vakanzwa kana vakanzwa pamuviri, muchishandisa mashoko avo pazvinogoneka;
- kuti zvakaitika riini uye kupi, uye kuti zvakatora nguva yakadii;
- kuti munhu airatidza kutya, kudzikama kana kuva munjodzi here;
- kuti kamuri raive rakaita sei, kusanganisira chiedza, mimvuri, ruzha uye kuti ndiani aive pedyo;
- kurwara kuchangobva kuitika, kuchinja kwekuona kana kunzwa kana kuchinja kwemishonga kwamunoziva nezvako.

Shandisai mashoko emunhu uye tsanangurai zvamakaona. Semuenzaniso, nyorai kuti „akati pane munhu ari pahwindo“ pane kupa zita kuchiitiko chacho. Goveranai zvamakanyora nemvumo yemunhu pazvinogoneka.

Mashoko aya haatsananguri chikonzero. Kana chiitiko chikatanga kamwe kamwe pamwe nekuvhiringika kana kuchinja kukuru kwekusvinura, tsvagai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi pane kumirira kuunganidza zvinyorwa.

[Gwaro rekusagadzikana kwemanheru](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) rinotaura nezvechiedza nemaitiro emanheru. Nezvekupomerwa pamusoro pezvinhu zvakarasika, onai [kana munhu anorarama nedementia achifungira vamwe](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/).
