Kana dementia ichichinja munhu anodiwa nemwana, mwana anogona kuzviona mukuru asati atanga hurukuro. Mwana anogona kunzwa kunetseka, kutsamwa, kunyara, kuva nemhosva kana kusaziva maitiro ekuita. Mwana mumwe nemumwe nemhuri imwe neimwe zvakasiyana. Ruzivo rwakajeka nerutsigiro rwakasimba zvinogona kubatsira mwana kunzwisisa zviri kuchinja pasina kumukumbira kutakura mabasa evakuru.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association rekutaura nevana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) rinokurudzira kupindura mibvunzo zvakatendeseka nemashoko anonzwisiswa nevana. [Gwaro remhuri reDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) rinosimbisawo hurukuro dzakatendeseka, kuvimbisa uye kuchengetedza mukana wemudiki wekuti arambe ari mwana. Shandisai mazano aya senzvimbo yekutangira, kwete sechinyorwa chinofanira kuteverwa nemhuri dzose.

## Ndingatanga sei hurukuro nezvedementia?

Tangai neshanduko yakaonekwa nemwana kana mubvunzo waakabvunza. Munogona kuti, “Wakaona kuti Ambuya vanodzokorora mibvunzo. Vane chirwere chinonzi dementia chinokanganisa kurangarira kwavo nekufunga kwavo.” Shandisai zita chairo rekuongororwa kwemunhu wacho chete kana richizivikanwa. Kana mhuri ichiri kumirira kuongororwa, tsanangurai shanduko yacho pasina kuipa zita.

Sarudzai nguva yakadzikama yamunogona kugara muhurukuro yacho. Shandisai mitsara mipfupi uye mirai kuti mwana apindure. Hamufaniri kutsanangura zvose kamwe chete. Bvunzai zvavanonzwisisa uye zvavanoda kuziva zvinotevera. Mwana mudiki anogona kubatsirwa nebhuku remifananidzo kana muenzaniso uri nyore; mwana mukuru anogona kuda ruzivo rwakawanda kana nguva ari oga yekufunga.

Siyai munhu anorarama nedementia achibatanidzwa kana achida uye achikwanisa kutora chikamu. Taurai nemwana nezvezvinodiwa nemunhu wacho musati magovera ruzivo rwekurapwa rwakavanzika nevamwe vanhu. Mwana anogona kuda kuti mubereki kana mukuru waanovimba naye ave pedyo paanoshanya, kana angasada kutaura nezvekuongororwa kwacho neshamwari kana kuchikoro panguva ino.

## Ndinofanira kuudza mwana chii nezvedementia?

Tsanangurai zvikamu zvinokosha muupenyu hwezuva nezuva hwemwana. Dementia chirwere chinogona kuchinja marangariro, mafungiro kana matauriro anoita munhu. Haitapukiri, uye mwana haana kuikonzeresa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) inotaura kuti vana vanogona kunetseka pamusoro pekubatirwa dementia kana kuzvipa mhosva kana vakuru vachinetseka. Pindurai kutya ikoko zvakananga.

Ivai vakatendeseka pamusoro pezvamunoziva nezvamusingazivi. Munogona kutsanangura zvakacherechedzwa nemhuri, rubatsiro runowanikwa nemunhu wacho uye zvirongwa zvinogona kukanganisa maitiro emwana. Dzivisai kuvimbisa kuti munhu wacho achapora, kuti hapana chichachinja kana kuti munoziva kuti shanduko ichaitika rinhi. Kana musingazivi mhinduro, taurai kudaro uye mupe mukana wekutsvaga ruzivo rwakavimbika pamwe chete.

Vana vanogona kuda kunzwa tsananguro imwe chete kakawanda. Izvi zvakajairika. Siyai mukana wekuti mwana abvunze zvakare gare gare, uye tarisai zvavanzwisisa pasina kushandura hurukuro kuita bvunzo. Kana mwana achishandisa mamwe mashoko kurondedzera chirwere chacho, teererai kutanga uye zvinyoro nyoro mupe mashoko akajeka avanogona kushandisa.

## Ndingapindura sei mibvunzo yakaoma?

Pindurai kunetseka kuri seri kwemubvunzo, mozoshandisa mhinduro yakajeka uye yakatendeseka yamunogona kupa. Chengetai mhinduro iri pfupi uye musawedzere ruzivo rwamusingazivi.

- **“Ndingazvibatawo here?”** Itii kwete: dementia haitapukiri. Mwana anogona kuramba achipedza nguva nemunhu wacho.
- **“Ndini ndakaita kuti izvi zviitike here?”** Muvimbisei kuti chirwere hachina kukonzerwa nechimwe chinhu chaakataura kana chaakaita.
- **“Sei Sekuru vachindidana nerimwe zita?”** Tsanangurai kuti dementia inogona kuchinja marangariro aanoita mazita. Haisi mhosva yemwana, uye vanogona kunge vasingazviiti kuti vamugumbure.
- **“Neniwo ndichava nedementia here?”** Musafungidzire ramangwana remunhu mumwe chete. Njodzi yemumhuri inoenderana nerudzi rwechirwere nenhoroondo yayo. Kana paine kunetseka chaiko pamusoro perudzi runogarwa nhaka, bvunzai chiremba wemunhu wacho kana nyanzvi yezvemajini inogona kubatsira.
- **“Achapora here?” kana “Achafa here?”** Musavimbise mhedzisiro kana nguva. Udza mwana zvakataurwa neboka rinotarisira munhu wacho, kana mhuri ichida kugovera izvozvo, uye tsanangurai kuti muchataura zvakare rinhi.
- **“Achiri kundida here?”** Musaisi mwana mutoro wekutsvaga uchapupu hwemhinduro. Munogona kuti ukama hwacho hunokosha uye motsvaga nzira yakasununguka yekupedza nguva pamwe chete iye zvino.

Zvakakwana kumbomira kana mubvunzo uchinzwa wakakura zvikuru. Udza mwana kuti muchadzoka kwauri rinhi, uye zadzisai zvamataura. Mhinduro yakadzikama uye yechokwadi yekuti “Handisati ndaziva” inobatsira kupfuura fungidziro ine chivimbo.

## Ndingabatsira sei ndisingaiti kuti mwana ave munhu anotarisira?

Ipai mukana wekubatana nekusarudza kwemwana. Kana mwana nemunhu wacho vose vachida, vanogona kuteerera mimhanzi yavanotoziva, kuona mafoto, kudhirowa, kuverenga kana kuita chiitiko chiri nyore chepamba pamwe chete. [Gwaro redu rekurangarira](/activities/reminiscence/) ne[gwaro redu remimhanzi](/activities/music/) zvine mazano. Teererai maitiro emunhu wacho neemwana; zvakakwana kumbomira, kuchinja chiitiko kana kushanya imwe nguva.

Siyai basa rekutarisira nekuchengeteka kuvakuru. Mwana anogona kusarudza kugadzirira zvekudya zvishoma kana kuuya nebhuku, asi haafaniri kuva nebasa rekutarisa, remishonga, rekutarisira munhu, renguva dzekuonana nachiremba kana renjodzi. Musareva kuti kushanya, kubatsira kana kugara pedyo ibasa remwana. [Gwaro reDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) rinoyambira mhuri kuti dziremekedze miganhu yevadiki uye kuda kwavo kuramba vari vana.

Chengetedzai nguva yakagadzirirwa mwana chete. Chengetai chikoro, ushamwari, zvavanofarira uye maitiro emhuri zvakadzikama sezvinobvira. Kana kutarisira kuchichinja zvirongwa izvozvo, tsanangurai chikonzero uye tsvagai mumwe mukuru anogona kubatsira kuchengetedza chiitiko kana rwendo rwemwana. [Gwaro rekuzvichengeta remunhu anotarisira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinotsanangura nzira dzekukumbira vamwe vakuru kuti vagovere rutsigiro.

Musati maudza mudzidzisi, murairidzi kana mumwe mukuru, sanganisirai mwana musarudzo yacho sezvinokodzera. Anogona kuda kuti munhu mumwe chete waanovimba naye azive, kana angada kumirira. Kana mukagovera ruzivo, bvumiranai zvekutaura uye zvinofanira kuramba zvakavanzika.

## Ndirini pandinofanira kutsvaga rumwe rutsigiro?

Bvunzai kuti mwana ari kuita sei nekufamba kwenguva, kwete chete mushure mekuongororwa. Vamwe vana vanotaura pachena; vamwe vanoratidza kushushikana kuburikidza nekuchinja mukurara, kuchikoro, muushamwari kana mumaitiro. Kuchinja uku kunogona kuva nezvikonzero zvakawanda uye hazvirevi kuti mwana anoda kuongororwa. Teererai uye bvunzai kuti nderupi rutsigiro rungabatsira.

Kana mwana achida mumwe munhu ari kunze kwemhuri wekutaura naye, funga nezvemudzidzisi waanovimba naye, chipangamazano wechikoro, chiremba wevana kana nyanzvi yehutano hwepfungwa dzevana. Bvunzai musati mabata vashandi vechikoro nezvekuongororwa kwacho kana zvichibvira; mwana anogona kuda kuva chikamu chehurukuro iyoyo. Mhuri dzakabatwa nedementia inotanga munhu achiri mudiki dzinogonawo kuda rutsigiro nebasa, chikoro uye kuchinja kwemabasa emumhuri.

Kana musina chokwadi chekuti mungatsanangura sei imwe shanduko, bvunzai boka rinotarisira munhu wacho kana sangano redementia kuti rikutungamirirei. Mwana haadi mhinduro dzakakwana kubva kwamuri. Anoda ruzivo rwakatendeseka, mukana wekubvunza zvakare uye vakuru vanoramba varipo kwaari.
