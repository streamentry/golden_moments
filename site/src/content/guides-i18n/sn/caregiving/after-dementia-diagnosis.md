Kuongororwa chirwere chekukanganwa kunogona kuuya nemibvunzo pamusoro pehutano, hupenyu hwezuva nezuva, rutsigiro neramangwana. Vanhu vanogamuchira nhau iyi nenzira dzakasiyana: vamwe vanoda nguva yekunzwisisa nhau yacho, vamwewo vanoda kutanga kuronga. Hapana maitiro kana nguva imwe chete yakarurama. Tangirai nezvinonyanya kukosha kumunhu parizvino, mozodzoka kune mimwe mibvunzo kana agadzirira.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association pamusoro pehupenyu mushure mekuongororwa](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) rinobatsira vanhu kuwana rubatsiro rwekuonana nachiremba, rutsigiro rwemunzvimbo yavanogara uye kuronga ramangwana. [Mazano eDementia Australia ekugamuchira kuongororwa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) anosimbisa kuti vanhu vanoita zvakasiyana uye vanogona kutsvaga rutsigiro pavanoda. Peji ino pokutangira, haisi hurongwa hwekurapwa, hwemutemo kana hwemari hwemunhu mumwe nemumwe.

## Nderipi danho rimwe chete rinobatsira kutanga?

Sarudzai mubvunzo mumwe chete unogona kuita kuti vhiki ino ive nyore. Zvingava kuziva kuti ndiani acharonga kuonana kunotevera kwemunhu wamunotarisira nachiremba, kubvunza kwekuwana ruzivo rwakavimbika pamusoro pekuongororwa kwake, kana kuziva munhu waanovimba naye wekutaura naye. Munhu wacho angada kutungamira, kukumbira mumwe munhu kuti abatsire kana kumirira asati asarudza.

Pamunoonana nachiremba kunotevera, nyorai mibvunzo mishoma pamwe chete kana munhu wacho achida. Munogona kubvunza kuti kuongororwa uku kunorevei, chii chisati chanyatsojeka, kuonana kunotevera kuchaitwa riini uye ndiani wamunofanira kubata kana paine chimwe chinhu chitsva chiri kunetsa. Kumbirai chiremba kuti atsanangure izwi kana zano ripi neripi risina kujeka. [Gwaro redu rekugadzirira kuonana nachiremba](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) rine runyoro rupfupi rwekubatsira kugadzirira.

Munhu haafaniri kudzidza zvese nezvechirwere chekukanganwa kamwe chete. Sarudzai ruzivo rwakavimbika pamusoro pekuongororwa kwaakaitwa, uye nyorai pasi mibvunzo inouya gare gare. Chenjerai nezvezvipikirwa, zvirevo zvekuti pane mushonga unopora kana mazano anoudza munhu wese kuti atevere kurapwa kana hurongwa humwe chete.

## Tingawanepi rutsigiro?

Bvunzai munhu kuti ndeupi rutsigiro rwaangada uye ndiani waanoda kuti abatanidzwe. Shamwari kana hama yaanovimba nayo inogona kuva munhu akanaka wekutanga kutaura naye. Vamwe vanhu vanosarudza boka rerutsigiro, chipangamazano, sangano renharaunda rinobatsira vanhu vari kurarama nechirwere chekukanganwa kana kutaura nechikwata chavo chezvehutano. Sarudzo yakanaka inoenderana nemunhu uye nezviripo pedyo naye.

Rutsigiro nderwemunhu akaongororwa pamwe nemhuri neshamwari. Munhu anotarisira angada zororo kana rubatsiro nemabasa ezuva nezuva; munhu akaongororwa angada ruzivo kana mukana wekusangana nevamwe vari kurarama nechirwere chekukanganwa. Zvinodiwa izvi zvinogona kusiyana. Bvunzai musati maronga boka, musati maita nguva yekusangana kana musati magovera ruzivo rwemunhu.

[Gwaro redu rekuzvichengetedza semunhu anotarisira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rine mazano ekukumbira vamwe kuti vagovane rutsigiro. Kana munhu achida rubatsiro rwakawanda masikati, magwaro pamusoro pe[zvirongwa zvemasikati zvevakuru](/caregiving/choosing-adult-day-program-dementia/) uye [rubatsiro rwepamba](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) anotsanangura mibvunzo yekubvunza pamusoro pemabasa emunzvimbo yenyu.

## Munhu angaramba sei ari chikamu chezvisarudzo?

Kuongororwa chete hakukuudzei kuti munhu anogona kusarudza chii kana kuti anoda kubatanidzwa sei. Bvunzai munhu wacho zvakananga. Mupei nguva, tsanangurai sarudzo nemashoko ari nyore uye bvunzai kuti nderupi rubatsiro rwaizoita kuti hurukuro ive nyore. Zvimwe zvisarudzo zvingava nyore kukurukura munzvimbo yakanyarara kana muhurukuro dzinopfuura imwe.

Kana munhu achida kuronga nezveramangwana rehutano, mari kana pekugara, tangirai nezvinonyanya kukosha kwaari uye nevanhu vaanovimba navo. [Gwaro reDementia Australia rekuronga ramangwana](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) rinokurudzira kukurukura zvido nevanhu vanovimbwa navo nenyanzvi dzakakodzera. Magwaro emutemo nemitemo yekuita zvisarudzo zvinosiyana nenzvimbo, saka bvunzai nyanzvi yemutemo yemunharaunda yenyu musati mavimba negwaro rakajairika. Kuti muwane rubatsiro rwezvekubhadhara mabhiri ezuva nezuva uye kunetseka nezvehutsotsi, onai [gwaro redu rekubata mari kana munhu ari kurarama nechirwere chekukanganwa](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/).

### Tingabvunza chii muhurukuro yekutanga pamusoro perubatsiro rweramangwana?

Bvunzai kana munhu achida kutaura izvozvi, mobva mataura nezvenyaya imwe panguva. [Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira hurukuro diki dzakawanda, mashoko ari nyore uye kuteerera zvido, zvitendero uye zvinokosheswa nemunhu](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [Alzheimer's Society inoti vanhu vanosiyana pakugadzirira kwavo kufunga nezveramangwana](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Zvakanaka kumbomira mozodzoka rimwe zuva.

Kana munhu achida mazano ekubatsira kutanga, edzai mumwe wemibvunzo iyi:

- „Ndezvipi zvikamu zvezuva renyu zvamungada kuchengetedza?“
- „Ndiani wamungada kuti abatanidzwe patinotaura nezverubatsiro rweramangwana?“
- „Chii chinokubatsirai kunzwa makasununguka uye kuremekedzwa pamunoda rubatsiro?“
- „Pane chinhu chamungada kuti chikwata chenyu chezvehutano chizive nezvezvinokosha kwamuri here?“
- „Mungada here kuti ndinyore pasi zvamataura, uye ndiani anofanira kuzviona?“

Teererai musingakumbiri kuti asarudze ipapo ipapo. [National Institute on Aging inotsanangura kuti hurukuro dzine zvadzinoreva pamusoro pezvinokosheswa nezvinodiwa nemunhu dzakakosha pakuronga rubatsiro rweramangwana](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Kana munhu achibvuma, nyorai mashoko ake, zuva uye vaivapo. Kumbirai mvumo musati magovera zvamanyora. Zvinyorwa izvi zvinogona kubatsira muhurukuro dzemunguva inotevera, asi hazvisi magwaro emutemo kana mirairo yekurapwa.

Musafungidzira zvinhu muchingoona kuongororwa chete kana hurukuro imwe chete. Zvido zvemunhu zvinogona kuchinja, uye anogona kukumbira kumbomira kana kudzoka kunyaya iyi gare gare. Ramba uchimusanganisira muzvisarudzo zvinomubata zvakanyanya sezvaanoda.

## Ndiani anofanira kuziva nezvekuongororwa uku?

Munhu ndiye anosarudza kuti oudza ani, riini uye sei, kunze kwekunge paine mamiriro ezvinhu chaiwo ekuchengetedzeka kana emutemo anoda mazano enyanzvi. Bvunzai kuti ndiani waanoda kuudza uye chaanoda kuti vanhu ivavo vazive. Anogona kusarudza kuudza munhu mumwe chete waanovimba naye kutanga, kana kusada kugovera nevakawanda.

Kana munhu achida rubatsiro pakutaura nevamwe, bvunzai kuti anoda kuti mutaure chii uye kuti anoda kuvapo here. Goverai chete ruzivo rwaabvuma kuti rugoverwe. [Gwaro redu rekukurukurirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) rinotsanangura nzira dzekukurukurirana neruremekedzo nemhuri nevamwe.

## Hupenyu hwezuva nezuva hungaramba sei tichiri kujaira?

Rambai muchiita maitiro nemabasa anokosha kumunhu kana achiri kunzwa akasununguka nawo. Kuongororwa hakurevi kuti chitandadzo chose, kushanya kana basa repamba rinofanira kumira. Kana chimwe chinhu chava kuoma, bvunzai munhu kuti anoda kuchinja chii. Kushanya kupfupi, mimhanzi yaanoziva, rubatsiro padanho rimwe chete kana nguva yakasiyana yezuva zvinogona kumunakira zviri nani.

Munhu angadawo nzvimbo yekugamuchira nhau yacho pasina kukurukura hurongwa. Rambai muchibvunza kuti ari sei musingashanduri hurukuro dzese kuita musangano wezverubatsiro. Zvakanaka kumbomira kuronga mombopedza nguva muri pamwe chete nenzira yakajairika.

## Tinofanira kubvunza nyanzvi riini?

Bvunzai chiremba wemunhu mibvunzo ine chekuita nekuongororwa kwake, hutano hwake, mishonga yake kana zviratidzo zvake. Pazvisarudzo zvemutemo nezvemari, taurai nenyanzvi dzakakodzera dzinonzwisisa mitemo yeko kwaanogara. Webhusaiti yeruzivo rwakajairika haigoni kukuudzai kuti ndeupi kurapwa, gwaro kana rubatsiro rwakakodzera munhu mumwe nemumwe.

Kuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika kana chimwe shanduko yehutano inongoerekana yaitika kunoda rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwekurapwa. [Mazano eNHS pamusoro pekuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) anoti tsvakai rubatsiro rwekurapwa nekukurumidza. Shandisai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi kana rwekurapwa nekukurumidza rwuri munharaunda yenyu; musafungidzira kuti shanduko inongoerekana yaitika chikamu chemaitiro ake enguva dzose.

Kana kuronga kuchinzwa kurema, sarudzai munhu mumwe chete wamunovimba naye kana rubatsiro rumwe chete runogona kubatsira kuronga mibvunzo muzvinhu zvinoda kuitwa izvozvi nezvinogona kumirira. Munhu anogona kusarudza kumhanya kwaanoita uye kuti anoda rubatsiro pachii. Kuongororwa ruzivo rwakakosha, asi hazvitsananguri zvaanofarira, hukama hwake kana hupenyu hwake hwezuva nezuva.
