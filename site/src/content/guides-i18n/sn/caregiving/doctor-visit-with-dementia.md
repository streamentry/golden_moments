Kushanya kuna chiremba kunogona kusanganisira zvakawanda munguva pfupi. Hurongwa hupfupi hunogona kubatsira munhu wacho nemunhu anomutsigira kubvunza mibvunzo inokosha zvikuru, kunzwisisa mhinduro uye kubuda vaine matanho anotevera akajeka. Munhu anorarama nedementia anofanira kuramba ari muchikamu chehurukuro nezvisarudzo zvakanyanya sezvaanoda.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association rekushanda nachiremba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/working-with-the-doctor) rinokurudzira kunyora mibvunzo pasi, kucherechedza shanduko dzakananga uye kukumbira chiremba kuti adzokorore hurongwa. [Zano reDementia Australia rekushanda navanachiremba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/working-doctors) rinokoshesa mvumo, kuteerera munhu wacho uye kugovera ruzivo zvakangwarira pazvinenge zvichidiwa. Peji rino rinopa mazano ekugadzirira, kwete mazano ekurapwa.

## Ndingagadzirira sei ndisati ndaenda?

Bvunza munhu wacho kuti chii chaanonyanya kuda kuti chiremba azive. Nyora pamwe chete runyoro rupfupi rwemibvunzo, uye isa mubvunzo unokosha zvikuru pekutanga. Kana runyoro rwacho rwakareba, bvunza kukiriniki kana nguva yakareba yekusangana kana kushanya kwekutevera kungabatsira. Munhu wacho angada kuuya netsamba yakanyorwa pane kuedza kurangarira zvinhu zvose panguva yekushanya.

Tsanangura shanduko nenzira dzakananga. Nyora zvakaitika, pazvakatanga, kakawanda kazvinoitika uye kuti chii chaiitika panguva iyoyo. Semuenzaniso, “Ava nemwedzi warega kuenda kuboka remimhanzi revhiki nevhiki” zvinobatsira kupfuura “Ari kuramba achiwedzera kuipa.” Sanganisirawo maonero emunhu wacho nezveshanduko yacho, uye musataure fungidziro sekunge kuongororwa kwechiremba.

Uyai nerunyoro rwemishonga rwazvino, kusanganisira mishonga yakanyorerwa nachiremba, mishonga isina kunyorerwa, mavhitamini nezvekuzora muviri. Munhu wacho angadawo magirazi, rubatsiro rwekunzwa kana mazita evamwe vanachiremba. Bvunza kukiriniki kuti ndeapi mafomu kana zvinyorwa zvinodiwa pane kuunganidza ruzivo rwakavanzika rwakawanda kupfuura runodiwa pakushanya.

## Ndingasanganisira sei munhu wacho panguva yekusangana?

Bvunza kana munhu wacho achida kuti mupinde naye pakusangana nachiremba. Angada rubatsiro rwekufambisa asi achida kutaura nachiremba ari oga, kana kuti angafara kuti muvepo semunhu anonyora manotsi. Bvunza kuti angada kuti mumubatsire sei musati matanga.

Rega munhu wacho apindure mibvunzo yachiremba kutanga. Wedzera rumwe ruzivo chete kana achida kuti mudaro, kana kana chiremba akakumbira ruzivo rwungasayeukwa nemunhu wacho. Taurai nemunhu wacho, kwete nachiremba chete. Kana achida nguva yekupindura, mirirai pane kuzadza kumbomira kwose.

Munhu wacho angada nguva yega nachiremba kuti ataure nezvenyaya yake. Remekedzai chikumbiro ichocho. Kana muine chinetso chakaoma kutaura munhu wacho aripo, bvunzai kukiriniki kuti mungatumira sei chinyorwa chakanyorwa zvakangwarira. Tsanangurai zvakajeka zvamakacherechedza uye musakumbire kukiriniki kuviga ruzivo kana kuita zvisarudzo pasina kubatanidza munhu wacho.

## Ndedzipi mibvunzo inogona kubatsira kuita kuti hurongwa hujeke?

Kumbirai tsananguro iri nyore kunzwisisa kana paine chisina kujeka. Munhu wacho nemutsigiri vangabvunza:

- Tinofanira kutarisa chii, uye tinofanira kukubatai riini?
- Ndedzipi sarudzo dziripo, uye zvakanaka kana matambudziko angavapo ndeapi?
- Nderipi danho rinotevera, uye ndiani acharironga?
- Tinofanira kutevera riini?

Iyi mienzaniso, kwete runyoro rwunofanira kupedzwa pakushanya kwose. Munhu wacho angava nemimwe mibvunzo kana kuda kutarisa pane chinetso chimwe chete. Kumbirai chiremba kuti adzokorore zvinhu zvikuru musati mabuda.

## Ndinofanira kunyora chii mushure mekushanya?

Nyora pfupiso pfupi yetsananguro yachiremba, mabasa akabvumiranwa uye nguva yekutevera. Nyora kuti ndiani achabata basa rimwe nerimwe, sekufonera kukiriniki kana kuronga kumwe kushanya. Kana munhu wacho achibvumira, goverai ruzivo irworwo nenhengo yemhuri kana mubatsiri anofanira kuzviziva.

Chengetai manotsi ezvehutano zvakavanzika uye munzvimbo inowanika nyore nemunhu wacho. Musaatumire kuburikidza neakaundi yakagovaniswa kana meseji yeboka kana munhu wacho asina kubvumira. Kana murayiridzo usina kujeka pamunosvika kumba, fonerai kukiriniki mukumbire tsananguro. Musatanga, musamisa kana kushandura mushonga zvichibva pamanotsi enyu moga musina kutanga mazvitaura nemunhu akanyora mushonga.

Musati mabuda, tarisai kana kushanya kwekutevera kana bvunzo zvichifanira kurongwa uye kuti ndiani wekubata kana muine mibvunzo. Munhu wacho angada kuchengeta kopi yehurongwa hwakanyorwa kana kusarudza munhu waanovimba naye kuti ahuchengete. Bvunzai pane kusarudza pachinzvimbo chake.

## Ko kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringika?

Musamirira nguva yekusangana yakajairika kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringika. [NHS inopa zano rekutsvaga rubatsiro rwechiremba nekukurumidza](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), nekuti kuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika kunogona kuva nezvikonzero zvinoda kutarisirwa nekukurumidza. Shandisai rubatsiro rwekukurumidzira rwemunharaunda yenyu kana kutevera nzira yekurapwa nekukurumidza kwenzvimbo yamunogara.

Shanduko inouya zvishoma nezvishoma inogona kukurukurwa nachiremba, asi yakasiyana nekuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika. Kana musina chokwadi chekuti shanduko inoda kukurumidzira here, batai rubatsiro rwezvehutano rwakakodzera kuti muwane nhungamiro pane kuedza kuzviongorora moga.

## Ndingatsigira sei munhu wacho kana kushanya kuchimunetsa?

Bvunza kuti chii chingaita kuti kushanya kuve nyore. Munhu wacho angada nguva yekusangana yaajaira, kumirira kwenguva pfupi kana munhu waanovimba naye. Fonera kukiriniki kare kuti ubvunze nezvezvido zvekupinda, rubatsiro rwekunzwa kana rwemutauro, uye kana mibvunzo ingatumirwa kusati kwasvika nguva yekushanya.

Kana munhu wacho achinetseka, tsanangura hurongwa nenzira yakadzikama uye yakajeka inokodzera iye. Uya nechinhu chaajaira kana chinhu chekuita panguva yekumirira, kana munhu wacho achichida. Bvunza kuti anoda kuziviswa nguva yakareba sei zvisati zvaitika: vamwe vanhu vanoda nguva yekugadzirira, vamwe vangada chiyeuchidzo chipfupi nguva yekushanya isati yasvika. Shandisai tsananguro yakatendeseka inoshanda kwaari.

Kushanya kwakanaka kunoita kuti munhu wacho anzwe kunzwikwa, uye matanho anotevera anyatsonzwisiswa. Gadzirirai pamwe chete, chengetedzai zvakavanzika uye kumbirai kukiriniki kuti ijekerese chero chinhu chisati chanyatsojeka.
