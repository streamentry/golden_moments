Chirongwa chemasikati chinogona kupa shanduko yenzvimbo nerubatsiro mumaitiro emazuva ese. Chinogonawo kupa munhu anotarisira mhuri nguva yebasa, zvekuita kana yekuzorora. Zvirongwa zvinosiyana, uye nzvimbo inokodzera mumwe munhu inogona kusakodzera mumwe. Kunyaradzwa nezvido zvemunhu wacho zvinofanira kutungamira sarudzo yacho.

[Pfupiso yeAlzheimer's Association pamusoro penzvimbo dzemasikati](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/adult-day-centers) uye [rondedzero yayo yekusarudza nzvimbo](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-an-adult-day-center-ts.pdf) zvinokurudzira kutarisa mabasa, vashandi, zviitiko, mutengo nezvekufambisa musati manyoresa. Peji rino rinokubatsirai kugadzirira mibvunzo; harienzanisi kana kukurudzira imwe nzvimbo chaiyo.

## Ndingasarudza sei kana ndichifanira kufunga nezvechirongwa chemasikati?

Tangai nekubvunza munhu wamunotarisira zvaangafarira uye rudzi rwerubatsiro rwaangagamuchira. Anogona kuda chiitiko chekudyidzana nevamwe, kudya nevamwe, kuva nemumwe munhu paanenge achishanda, kana chimwe chinhu. Chirongwa isarudzo, kwete chinhu chinomanikidzwa.

Fungai nezvenzendo dzinoda kuitwa nemunhu wacho, maitiro ake emazuva ese nerubatsiro rwaanoda masikati. Fungai kana nguva dzenzvimbo yacho, zvekufambisa nezviitiko zvichienderana nezvinodiwa izvozvo. Kana munhu wacho asingafariri mapoka kana akaneta munzvimbo ine vanhu vakawanda, bvunzai kana paine nzvimbo yakanyarara kana kushanya kupfupi.

Chirongwa chinogonawo kubatsira munhu anotarisira kuzorora kana kuita mamwe mabasa. Izvozvo zvakakosha, asi ruzivo rwemunhu wacho rwakakoshawo. Tauriranai nezvezvinodiwa zvese zviri zviviri motsvaga urongwa hunogamuchirika kune vese vanobatanidzwa.

## Ndeapi mabasa andinofanira kubvunza nezvawo?

Mabasa anosiyana. Dzimwe nzvimbo dzinonyanya kutarisa kushamwaridzana nezviitiko; dzimwe dzinogonawo kupa zvekudya, zvekufambisa, rubatsiro rwemunhu kana mabasa ane chekuita neutano. Bvunzai kuti ndeapi mabasa aripo pamazuva angapindwa nemunhu wacho uye kuti vashandi vanogona here kutsigira zvinodiwa nemunhu wacho pari zvino.

Bvunzai nezvezviitiko, kwete zita repurogiramu chete. Bvunzai kuti vatori vechikamu vanoitei chaizvo, kana vachigona kusarudza pakati pezvakasiyana uye kana chirongwa chacho chichisanganisira zvinhu zvavanotoziva zvakaita semimhanzi, unyanzvi, kurima bindu kana kukurukura. Munhu wacho haafaniri kupinda muchiitiko chese. Bvunzai kana paine nzvimbo yakanyarara kana nguva yekuzorora.

Kana munhu wacho achida rubatsiro nemishonga, kufamba, kudya kana rubatsiro rwemunhu, bvunzai chaizvo kuti ndiani anopa rubatsiro irworwo uye kuti ane rudzidziso rwupi. Musafungire kuti chirongwa chemasikati chinopa rubatsiro rwekurapwa kana kuti chinogona kugadzirisa zvinodiwa zvese. Simbisai mirairo ipi neipi ine chekuita neutano nechikwata chinotarisira munhu wacho.

## Ndezvipi zvandinogona kutarisa pandinoshanyira?

Rongei kushanya uye, kana munhu wacho achida, muuyewo naye. Cherechedzai kana vashandi vachitaura neruremekedzo, vachipa vatori vechikamu nguva yekupindura uye vachikoka vanhu kuti vatore chikamu pasina kumanikidzwa. Tarirai zviitiko zvinoenderana nezvinofarirwa uye zvinokwaniswa nemunhu wacho, kwete karenda yakazara chete.

Bvunzai kuti nzvimbo yacho inodzidza sei nezvezvinodiwa nemunhu mumwe nemumwe uye kuti inopindura sei kana mumwe munhu aneta, akanetseka kana achida kuenda. Bvunzai kuti nzvimbo yacho inoitei kana paine dambudziko rekukurumidza, kuti nhengo dzemhuri dzinowana sei ruzivo uye kuti ndiani wekutaura naye kana paine zvinonetsa.

Kana zvichikwanisika, shanyirai panguva yechiitiko kana yekudya. Cherechedzai kana nzvimbo yacho ichinzwa yakasununguka kumunhu wacho, kana zviri nyore kufamba-famba uye kana vashandi vachiratidzika kuwanikwa. Bhurocha kana rwendo rwekutungamirwa zvinogona kubatsira kugadzirira mibvunzo, asi kuona zuva rakajairika kunogona kukuudzai zvakawanda.

## Ndezvipi zvinhu zvinoshanda zvandinofanira kutarisa?

Bvunzai nezvenguva, mazuva, mari, huwandu hudiki hwekupinda, mitemo yekukanzura uye mimwe mibhadharo yekuwedzera. Bvunzai kuti ndezvipi zvekudya kana zvekudya zvidiki zvinosanganisirwa uye kana kudya kwakakosha kuchigona kutsigirwa. Mari nemabasa zvinosiyana nenzvimbo, saka kumbirai nzvimbo yacho kuti itsanangure mitemo yayo yakanyorwa.

Tsvagai kana zvekufambisa zviripo, kuti zvinodhura marii uye kuti zvakakodzera munhu wacho here. Bvunzai nezvenguva yekunhonga, rubatsiro rwekukwira nekuburuka, uye zvinoitika kana mutyairi akanonoka. Kana nhengo yemhuri ichizopa zvekufambisa, sanganisirai nguva nemutengo iwoyo muhurongwa hwenyu.

Bvunzai kana nzvimbo yacho ichigona kupa rubatsiro runodiwa nemunhu wacho kana utano hwake kana kugona kwake kuita zvinhu zvezuva nezuva kukachinja. Kana nzvimbo yacho isingakwanisi kuzadzisa zvinodiwa izvozvo, bvunzai kuti ndeipi imwe nzira kana rubatsiro rwayingakurudzira. [Gwaro rekutarisirwa pamba](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) rinotsanangura rumwe rudzi rwerubatsiro rungakodzera dzimwe mhuri.

## Munhu wacho angaedza sei chirongwa pasina kumanikidzwa?

Bvunzai kana nzvimbo yacho ichipa kushanya kupfupi kwekutanga, kudya kana chiitiko. Regai munhu wacho asarudze zvaanoda kuedza uye kuti anoda kugara kwenguva yakareba sei kana zvichikwanisika. Kushanya kwekutanga kunogona kunzwa kusina kujairika. Bvunzai munhu wacho mushure mezvo kuti chii chaafarira, chaasina kufarira kana chaangade kuchinja.

Kana munhu wacho achizeza, tsvagai chikonzero. Rwendo, ruzha, vanhu vaasingazive kana chimwe chiitiko zvinogona kunge zviri izvo zvinomunetsa. Munogona kubvunza nzvimbo yacho kana kuchinjwa kudiki kuchigoneka, kana kushanyira rimwe zuva. Musashandise kunzwa mhosva kana kumanikidza kuti munhu wacho apinde.

Mushure mekushanya kushoma, tauriranai nemunhu wacho nevashandi venzvimbo yacho kuti urongwa hwacho huri kufamba sei. Chirongwa chakanaka chinogona kuda kugadziriswa kwenguva, zviitiko kana rubatsiro. Kana munhu wacho achiri kunetseka kana basa racho risingakodzere, zvine musoro kufunga zvakare.

## Ndingabatana sei nenzvimbo yacho?

Muchibvumirana nemunhu wacho, govai ruzivo runobatsira vashandi kumutsigira: zita raanoda kushandiswa, matauriro aanoda, maitiro ake emazuva ese, zvaanofarira uye rudzi rwerubatsiro rwaanogamuchira. Bvunzai kuti nzvimbo yacho inochengetedza sei ruzivo urwu uye kuti ruzivo rutsva runogoverwa sei nemhuri.

Bvunzai kuti ndiani wekutaura naye kana munhu wacho achirwara, asipo kana achida shanduko murubatsiro. Ivai nehurongwa hwekutsiva pamazuva apo chirongwa chakavharwa kana zvekufambisa zvisipo. Kana vanhu vakawanda vachibatsira, bvumiranai kuti ndiani achabata rwendo rumwe norumwe uye achapa ruzivo rutsva.

Chirongwa chemasikati chinogona kuva chimwe chikamu chehurongwa hwakakura hwerubatsiro. [Gwaro rekuzvitarisira remutarisiri](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinosanganisira nzira dzekuronga kuzorora, uye [zviitiko zvemapoka enzvimbo dzekuchengetera](/activities/care-home-groups/) zvinopa mazano ezviitiko zviri nyore zvemapoka.

Chirongwa chakanaka ndechinosarudzwa nemunhu wacho kuti aedze uye chinogona kutsigira zvaanoda. Shanyirai, bvunzai mibvunzo yakajeka uye itai shanduko zvichienderana nemanzwiro emunhu wacho.
