Kutyaira kunogona kuva chikamu cheunhu hwemunhu, kuzvimiririra kwake nemaitiro ake ezuva nezuva. Kutaura nezveshanduko dzingavapo mune ramangwana kunogona kuomera munhu wese. Kutanga nekukasika kunogona kupa mukana wakawanda wekuteerera, kuita sarudzo uye kuedza dzimwe nzira dzekufamba. Peji rino harisi ongororo yekutyaira kana sarudzo yekuti mumwe munhu akachengeteka here kutyaira.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association nezvedementia nekutyaira](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving), [mazano eDementia Australia nezvekutyaira](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/driving-and-dementia) uye [gwara reNHTSA revanotarisira](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/older-drivers) zvinokurudzira kuronga pachine nguva uye kusanganisira munhu wacho. Chiremba, nyanzvi yezvekudzoreredza kugona kutyaira uye chiremera chemunharaunda chinopa marezinesi vanogona kubatsira nemibvunzo yemamiriro emunhu.

## Ndirini pandinofanira kutanga hurukuro?

Tangai pasati paitika chiitiko chekutyaira chinomanikidza kuita sarudzo nekukurumidza, kana zvichikwanisika. Sarudzai nguva yakadzikama apo munhu wacho asiri kugadzirira kuenda kumwe kunhu kana achangobva parwendo runonetsa. Iyi inogona kuva yekutanga yehurukuro dzinoverengeka, kwete chiziviso chimwe chete.

Bvunzai kuti kutyaira kunorevei kumunhu wacho. Kunogona kuva nzira yaanoshandisa kushanyira shamwari, kutenga zvekudya, kubatsira mhuri, kuita basa kana kunzwa akazvimiririra. Kubvuma kukosha kwazvo hakugadzirise mubvunzo wekuchengeteka. Zvinoratidza kuti munonzwisisa zvingarasika uye nei kuronga kuchikosha.

Sanganisirai munhu wacho musarudzo dzemafambiro emunguva inotevera panguva yaanoda kutora chikamu. Angafarira mumwe munhu chaiye kuti amutyairise, nzira yaanoziva kana imwe nguva yezuva. Nyora pasi sarudzo dzaanofarira uye edzai kuita dzadzo pasati pasvika zuva rinonetsa, kana achibvuma.

## Ndingamutsa sei zvinondinetsa ndisingashanduri hurukuro kuita nharo?

Taurai nezvechiitiko chimwe chaicho pane kupa mutongo wakakura. Semuenzaniso, tsanangurai kurasika munzira yaanoziva, kupotsa chiratidzo chekumira kana kukwenya kwemotokari kuchangobva kuitika. Taurai zvamakaona uye bvunzai kuti munhu wacho anoyeuka chii kana kuti akanzwa sei. Musamuti mutyairi akaipa kana kumuedza ndangariro nemibvunzo.

Teererai maonero emunhu wacho. Iye anogonawo kunge akaona kuvhiringidzika, kupenya kwechiedza, kuneta kana rimwe dambudziko. Kana kuti anogona kusabvumirana nezvinokunetsai. Musaone hurukuro imwe chete sehumbowo hwekuti anonzwisisa kana haanzwisisi njodzi. Chengetai matauriro ane ruremekedzo uye dzokerai kunyaya iyi zvakare kana mumwe wenyu akatsamwa.

Dementia Australia inokurudzira hurukuro pfupi, dzinodzokororwa uye kutarisa pakugona kwemunhu wacho uye pasarudzo dzakanaka dzekufamba. Inoyambirawo kuti kuviga makiyi kana kukanganisa mota muchivande kunogona kunzwika sehasha panguva yekuronga kwekutanga. Kana paine kunetseka kukuru kwekuchengeteka panguva iyoyo, torai matanho ekudzivirira rwendo runotevera rungava nenjodzi uye tsvakai gwara renyanzvi nekukurumidza.

## Nderuzivo rupi rungabatsira pakukurukura nenyanzvi?

Nyora pasi zviitiko chaizvo uye nguva yazvakaitika. Mienzaniso inogona kusanganisira kuvhiringidzika pamharadzano, kurasikirwa nenzira kuenda kunzvimbo inozivikanwa, kusaona zviratidzo kana kukuvara kutsva pamotokari. Sanganisirai zvakataurwa nemunhu wacho nezverwendo uye chero shanduko dzeutano kana dzemishonga dzaanoda kukurukura.

Chengetai zvinyorwa zviri zvechokwadi uye zvakavanzika. Musarekodhe, musatevere kana kuedza munhu wacho muchivande. Kana munhu wacho achibvuma, munhu wemumhuri anga ari mumotokari anogona kugovera zvaakaona. Tsananguro yakajeka inopa chiremba kana nyanzvi chinhu chaicho chekukurukura; haiongorore chikonzero kana kusarudza kugona kutyaira.

Zviratidzo zvekuchenjerera zviri mu[gwara reAlzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving) zvikonzero zvekutsvaga zano renyanzvi. Hazvitsivi ongororo yemamiriro emunhu. Bvunzai chiremba wemunhu wacho nezvekutumirwa kuna occupational therapist kana imwe nyanzvi yezvekudzoreredza kugona kutyaira yakakodzera. Tarisa mitemo yemarezinesi emunharaunda kuchiremera chedunhu kana chemunharaunda chinopa marezinesi.

## Tingaita sei hurongwa hwekufamba pamwe chete?

Tangai nemafambiro ayo munhu wacho anoda kuchengeta: kushanyira shamwari, kutenga, kuenda kunoongororwa nachiremba, kunonamata kana kuita zviitiko zvaanoziva. Pane rumwe norumwe, kurukurai kuti ndiani angatyaira, ndeapi marongerwo ekufamba anonzwa achigamuchirika uye nerubatsiro rupi rungaita kuti rwendo rubudirire.

Sarudzo dzingasanganisira kukwidzwa nemhuri kana shamwari, matekisi kana masevhisi ekukumbira mota, zvifambiso zveveruzhinji, zvifambiso zvemunharaunda kana kuti zvinhu zvitumirwe kumba pane kumwe kuenda. Masevhisi, mutengo uye nyore kwazvo zvinosiyana nenzvimbo. Tarisa zvinowanikwa munharaunda menyu pane kufungidzira kuti sarudzo imwe ichashandira munhu wese.

Edzai nzira kana sevhisi pamwe chete isati yava iyo chete sarudzo. Bvunzai kana munhu wacho achida shamwari yekufamba nayo kana kuti anoda kufamba ari ega. [Gwaro redu rekuronga mafambiro](/caregiving/traveling-with-dementia/) rine mazano ekuchengetedza mafambiro achizivikanwa uye kusiya mukana wekuchinja hurongwa.

## Ko kana ndichifunga kuti rwendo runotevera rungava nenjodzi?

Tora kunetseka kwacho zvakakomba uye ronga imwe nzira yekufamba uchitsvaga zano nekukurumidza. Kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringidzika, [NHS inokurudzira rubatsiro rwekurapwa nekukurumidza](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/); shandisa mabasa ekukurumidzira emunharaunda. Shanduko inongoerekana yaitika mumaitiro emunhu haifaniri kuonekwa sekusawirirana kwakajairika pamusoro pekutyaira.

Pane kumwe kunetseka kukuru, taura nachiremba wemunhu wacho uye nechiremera chemunharaunda chinopa marezinesi nezvematanho anoshanda kwaanogara. Usavimbe nevimbiso yemhuri kana peji rino pakusarudza zvinodiwa nemutemo. Kana zvichikwanisika, tsanangurira munhu wacho hurongwa hwepanguva iyoyo uye ramba uchimusanganisira pane zvichaitika zvinotevera.

## Ndingatsigira sei munhu wacho kana arega kutyaira?

Kurega kutyaira kunogona kunzwika sekurasikirwa chaiko. Bvuma izvozvo zviri pachena. Ramba uchikoka munhu wacho kunzvimbo nezviitiko zvinokosha kwaari. Ipai sarudzo pamusoro pekukwidzwa, nguva neshamwari dzekufamba nadzo pane kuona mafambiro sebasa rekuitirwa iye.

Goveranai mabasa ekutyaira nehama kana shamwari kana munhu wacho achibvuma. Bvunzai masangano emunharaunda nezvezvifambiso zvevakuru kana zvevanhu vane hurema. Tarisa mutengo, hurongwa, mitemo yekusimudza vanhu uye kuti shamwari inobvumirwa here. Zvinhu zvidiki zvinogona kusarudza kuti sarudzo inonzwa ichibatsira here.

[Gwaro rekuzvichengetedza kwevanotarisira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinotaura nezvekukumbira vamwe kuti vagovere rutsigiro. Munhu wacho anofanira kuva nesimba rekutaura zvakanyanya sezvinobvira pamusoro pekuti anoramba akabatana sei nehupenyu hwezuva nezuva.

Chinangwa ndechekuchengetedza kuchengeteka tichiremekedza zvinoreva kutyaira kumunhu wacho. Tangai hurukuro nekukasika, teererai, tsvakai zano kubva kunyanzvi uye gadzirai hurongwa hwekufamba pamwe chete.
