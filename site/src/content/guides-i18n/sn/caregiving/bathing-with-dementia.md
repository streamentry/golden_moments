Kugeza inyaya yechivande. Kune munhu anorarama nechirwere chekanganwa, shawa kana kugeza mubhavhu kunogona kunzwa kuchitonhora, kuchifumura muviri, kuvhiringidza kana kusachengeteka. Tangai nekubvunza kuti chii chingaita kuti basa iri rive nyore, mogadzirira imba musati mopa rubatsiro. Chinangwa ndechekutsigira tsika dzemunhu wacho pasina kushandura kuramba kwake kuita gakava.

[Gwaro rekugeza reAlzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/bathing) rinoti kugamuchira rubatsiro pabasa rechivande kunogona kunzwa kusagadzikana. [Mazano eutsanana eDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/hygiene) anokurudzira kutevera zvido zvemunhu, kushandisa matanho ari nyore uye kubvunza kuti anoda rubatsiro rwakaita sei. Vanhu vakasiyana; edzai chete shanduko dzinokodzera munhu uyu.

## Ndingagadzirira sei ndisati ndapa rubatsiro?

Bvunzai nezvetsika dzemunhu wacho dzekugeza. Anoda shawa kana bhavhu, mangwanani kana manheru, rubatsiro kubva kune mumwe munhu chaiye, kana kuzvigezera dzimwe nzvimbo ega? Teverai zvaagara achida pazvinogoneka. Kana achida sarudzo, mupei sarudzo mbiri dzakajeka, dzakadai sekuti: “Munoda kugeza izvozvi kana mushure mekudya kwemangwanani?”

Musati mamukoka kupinda muimba yekugezera:

- **Unganidzai zvinhu zvose kutanga.** Isai matauro, hembe dzakachena, sipo, shampoo nejira rekuzvigeza zvichitevedzana sezvazvichashandiswa. Izvi zvinodzivisa kusiya munhu wacho ari ega muchitsvaga zvinhu.
- **Itai kuti imba igadzikane.** Tarisai kuti inodziya zvakakwana, vhenekerai imba, vharai gonhi kana maketeni, uye chengetai pasi pakaoma sezvinobvira.
- **Tarai mvura.** Edzai kupisa kwayo munhu asati apinda mubhavhu kana mushawa. Musavimba nekugona kwake kuona kuti mvura inopisa zvakanyanya here.
- **Ivai nezvinhu zvechiremera zvakagadzirira.** Tauro guru, nguo yekupfeka kana gumbeze rekugezera zvinogona kubatsira munhu kuti arambe akafukidzwa paanenge achifamba kubva padanho rimwe kuenda kune rimwe.

[Gwaro redu rekuchengeteka pamba](/caregiving/home-safety/) rine zvimwe pamusoro pemwenje muimba yekugezera, nzvimbo dzinotsvedza uye kubvunza nyanzvi yakakodzera nezvembariro dzekubata kana chigaro cheshawa.

## Ndingabatsira sei ndisingamutoreri basa rake?

Kumbirai mvumo musati mabata munhu wacho kana kumubatsira panzvimbo yechivande. Tsanangurai zvamava kuda kuita nemutsara mupfupi, mobva mambomira kuti apindure. Mashoko akadzikama akadai sekuti “Heri jira rekuzvigeza” anonzwisisika zviri nani pane mirairo mirefu yakawanda.

Regai munhu ageze nzvimbo dzaanokwanisa uye dzaanoda kugeza. Munogona kuramba makaisa tauro pamusoro pake muchifumura chete chikamu chiri kugezwa. Kana achida, mupei jira rekuzvigeza kana sipo kuti abate. Munhu wacho angada nguva yakawanda kuti atange kana apedze; mirirai pane kumhanyisa matanho.

Kana munhu wacho akatsamwa, akazvibvisa kana kuti akati kwete, mirai. Mupei mukana uye edzai kunzwisisa zviri kumunetsa. Imba inogona kunge ichitonhora, mvura inogona kunzwa isina kujairika, kana kuti munhu wacho angada mumwe anomubatsira. Munhu waanoziva anogona kunzwa zviri nani kana ari iye waanosarudza. Musashandise kushamisira, kumanikidza kana kumutungamirira nemuviri kuti apedze kugeza.

## Ndingachengeta sei tsika iyi ichijairika?

Chengetai nguva yezuva nehurongwa hwematanho sezvazvagara zvichishanda zvakanaka. Isai sipo nematauro zvaanoziva paanotarisira kuzviwana, uye gadzirai hembe dzakachena musati matanga. Tsanangurai danho rimwe panguva; kana murairo wekutaura usinganzwisisike, ratidzai jira rekuzvigeza kana kunongedzera chinhu chinotevera pane kudzokorora tsananguro refu. Kana kugeza bvudzi kuchimunetsa, zviitei zvakasiyana kana kuzvisiya kune rimwe zuva. Shanduko diki idzi dzinogona kuita kuti basa iri rive nyore kutevera pasina kubvisa basa remunhu wacho.

## Ko kana kugeza kwese kana shawa zvakaoma zvakanyanya?

Kugeza kwese hakusi iko chete nzira yekubatsira munhu kuti anzwe akachena. Kana munhu wacho achinzwa zvakanaka nazvo, edzai kugeza pasingi, kushandisa jira rinodziya rakanyorova kana kugeza nzvimbo imwe panguva. Kugeza bvudzi kunogona kuitwa rimwe zuva kana chiri icho chikamu chaasingade. Chengetai munhu wacho akafukidzwa uye achibatanidzwa, uye mirai kana imwe nzira iyi ikamunetsawo.

Kana munhu wacho akaramba nhasi, gamuchirai mozodzokazve gare gare. Kuzvichenesa murunyararo mangwana kuri nani pane kuita kuti kuedza kwanhasi kuve kunotyisa. [Gwaro rekuti munhu paanoti kwete](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) rine dzimwe nzira dzekupa rubatsiro pasina kumanikidza.

## Ndingaita sei kuti imba yekugezera ive yakachengeteka?

Chengetai nzira inoenda kubhavhu kana kushawa isina zvipingamupinyi uye shandisai zvinotsigira zvinoenderana nezvinodiwa nemunhu wacho. Nzvimbo dzisingatsvedzi, chigaro chakatsiga kana mbariro dzekubata zvinogona kubatsira, asi midziyo iyi inoda kuiswa munzvimbo yakakodzera. Bvunzai murapi wezvemabasa emumba kana imwe nyanzvi yakakodzera musati maisa mbariro kana kuita shanduko pazvivakwa. Musapinza hita inotakurika muimba yekugezera, uye teverai mirairo yemidziyo chero ipi yemagetsi.

Kana munhu wacho asina kugadzikana kana kuti akambodonha, musaedza kumusimudza kana kumufambisa nenzira inogona kukuvadza imi kana iye. Tsvagai mazano enyanzvi pamusoro pemidziyo nerubatsiro runodiwa. Chengetai hurongwa hwekuchengeteka huripo huchienderana nekodzero yemunhu yekuva nechiremera uye yekuita sarudzo.

## Ndirini pandinofanira kukumbira mazano enyanzvi?

Taurai nenyanzvi yezvehutano kana kugeza kwava kunetsa nekuda kwemarwadzo matsva, kudonha, dambudziko reganda kana shanduko mukugona kwemunhu kuzvigeza zvakachengeteka. Chiremba wemazino, chiremba, mukoti kana murapi wezvemabasa emumba anogona kubatsira padambudziko rakati. Musashandise gwaro rakajairika repadandemutande kusarudza kuti munhu anoda here kutarisirwa nemaoko.

Kana munhu wacho ave nenguva asingakwanisi kuzvigeza, musamupomere. Bvunzai kuti chikamu chipi chiri kumuomera uye kuti shanduko diki ingabatsira here. Tsika yaanoziva, jira rekuzvigeza kana rubatsiro kubva kumunhu waanovimba naye zvingakwana; kana zvisina kukwana, kumbirai rubatsiro kuchikwata chinomutarisira.
