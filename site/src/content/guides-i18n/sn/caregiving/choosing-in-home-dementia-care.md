Kutsvaga rubatsiro rwepamba kunotangira pamunhu, kwete parondedzero yemabasa. Bvunzai zvavanoda kuramba vachizviita vega uye rudzi rwerubatsiro rwavangagamuchira. Mupi werubatsiro anogona kubatsira pakushamwaridzana, mabasa ezuva nezuva, mabasa epamba kana rubatsiro rwehutano runoda unyanzvi. Musanganiswa wakakodzera unoenderana nemunhu, mabasa uye rutsigiro rwuripo kare.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association pamusoro perubatsiro rwepamba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/in-home-care) ne[pfupiso yeDementia Australia pamusoro perubatsiro rwepamba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/home-care) zvinotsanangura marudzi akasiyana emabasa. [Rondedzero yekuongorora mupi werubatsiro yeAssociation](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-in-home-care-professional-ts.pdf) inokurudzira kubvunza nezvekudzidziswa, ruzivo, mareferensi, rutsigiro kana mushandi asipo uye mutengo. Mabasa, zvitupa nemirau yemari zvinosiyana nenzvimbo; peji rino harikurudziri mupi werubatsiro kana kutsanangura kuti ndiani anokodzera.

## Munhu anoda rubatsiro rwakaita sei?

Tangai nemabasa anokosha muzuva remunhu. Vanogona kuda mumwe munhu anouya kuzoshanya nekutaura, kubatsira kutenga zvinhu kana kugadzirira chikafu, kana kubatsira pakugeza kana kupfeka. Rubatsiro rwehutano runoda unyanzvi, sekurapwa neanesi kana kurapwa kwemuviri, ibasa rakasiyana uye ringada nyanzvi ine rezinesi kana kutumirwa nemupi wehutano.

Nyora pasi rubatsiro runodiwa, nguva yarunobatsira uye zvinodiwa nemunhu. Sanganisirai zvavanokwanisa kuita vasina rubatsiro uye nguva dzavanoda kuva vega. Izvi zvinogona kubatsira kuti basa rirambe richitsigira rusununguko rwavo, pane kutora zuva rose.

Kana munhu asina chokwadi, tangai nechimwe chinodiwa chinoshanda kana kushanya kupfupi kana zvichibvira. Bvunzai kuti vakanzwa sei mushure mazvo. Vanogona kuda kuchinja basa, nguva kana munhu anovabatsira. Hurongwa hwerubatsiro hunogona kuchinjwa sezvo zvinodiwa nezvinodiwa nemunhu zvichichinja.

## Ndezvipi zvandinofanira kubvunza mupi werubatsiro ndisati ndamusarudza?

Fonai vapi verubatsiro vashoma uye tsanangurai mabasa anoda kubatsirwa nemunhu. Bvunzai kana vachikwanisa kuzadzisa zvinodiwa izvozvo, nguva dzavanowanikwa uye kana vakambotarisira munhu ari kurarama nedementia. Bvunzai kuti vashandi vavo vanowana kudzidziswa kwakaita sei kwedementia uye vanotsigira sei munhu ari kuvhiringidzika, ane kunetseka kana aneta.

Jekesai zvinhu zvinokanganisa zuva nezuva: ndiani achauya, kangani, kushanya kwega kwega kunotora nguva yakareba sei, chii chinoitika kana mushandi arwara, uye ndiani wamunofanira kufonera kana muine dambudziko. Kana munhu achida mubatsiri mumwe chete pazvinogoneka, bvunzai kana basa racho richikwanisa kupa kuenderera kwakadaro.

Kumbirai mareferensi uye kuti sangano rinoongorora sei vashandi varo. Zivai kana kuongororwa kwemamiriro evashandi, kudzidziswa uye zvitupa zvichiongororwa, uye kuti mushandi anotenderwa kuita mabasa api. Mirau nemazita emabasa zvinosiyana nenzvimbo, saka simbisa munharaunda menyu chero zvinodiwa zvehunyanzvi kana zverezinesi.

## Ndingatarisa sei kana munhu nemupi werubatsiro vachikwanirana zvakanaka?

Kana munhu achibvuma, ronge dzai musangano pamba kushanya kwekutanga kusati kwaitwa. Cherechedzai kana mupi werubatsiro achitaura zvakananga nemunhu, achiteerera zvaanoda uye achimupa nguva yekupindura. Bvunzai munhu kuti musangano wakanzwika sei, nenzira yaanokwanisa kupindura akasununguka.

Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kuti mumwe munhu wamunovimba avepowo panguva yekubvunzurudza kuitira kuti mugokurukura zvenyu mushure mazvo. Shandisai musangano kutaura nezvekushanya kwakajairika: kusvika, kukwazisana, basa rakarongwa uye kuti munhu angakumbira sei kumira kana kuzorora.

Kana zvichibvira, bvumiranai pakushanya kwekutanga kupfupi uye muzviongorore nemunhu. Mupi werubatsiro anogona kuita seanokodzera pabepa asi asiri munhu akakodzera kwavari. Remekedzai maonero emunhu uye kurukurai dzimwe sarudzo kana mumwe wenyu aine zvinomunetsa.

## Nderuzivo rupi rwandinofanira kugovera nemubatsiri?

Goverai chete ruzivo runobatsira mupi werubatsiro kutsigira munhu zvakachengeteka uye neruremekedzo. Nemvumo yemunhu, tsanangurai kuti vanoda kudaidzwa sei, tsika dzezuva nezuva dzakakosha kwavari, zvinovabatsira kutaura uye zvavanoda kuzviita vega.

Kana mirairo yehutano ichikanganisa basa racho, bvunzai chiremba wemurwere kuti mupi werubatsiro anofanira kuzivei. Musakumbire mushandi asina rezinesi kuita mabasa ekurapa ari kunze kwekudzidziswa kwake kana basa rake. Chengetai ruzivo rwemunhu rwakavanzika, uye tarisai kuti ndiani anokwanisa kuona zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa kana zvinyorwa zverubatsiro.

[Gwaro rekugadzirira kushanya kuna chiremba](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) rinogona kubatsira kuronga mibvunzo yenyanzvi yehutano. [Gwaro rekuchengeteka pamba](/caregiving/home-safety/) rinotaura nezvekuchinja kwemumba uye nguva yekukumbira nyanzvi yakakodzera.

## Ndingatarisa sei hurongwa uhu nekufamba kwenguva?

Taurai nemunhu mushure mekushanya kwega kwega. Bvunzai zvakanzwika zvakanaka, zvavakanakidzwa nazvo uye zvavangada kuchinja. Kana vasingatauri mashoko akawanda, cherechedzai kana vachigamuchira mubatsiri, vachiratidzika vakasununguka uye vachida kuti kushanya kuenderere mberi. Izvi hazvisi zviyero; zvishandisei kutungamirira hurukuro.

Bvumiranai nemupi werubatsiro kuti muchagovera sei ruzivo rwenguva dzose uye kuti muchabata sei zvinonetsa. Kana kushanya kukanzurwa, zivai kuti ndiani anogona kutsiva kana kuti munhu anoda kuita sei panzvimbo pezvo. Ongororai mabasa kana tsika dzezuva nezuva dzemunhu, hutano kana zvinodiwa pakutsigirwa zvachinja.

Mutengo, hurongwa hwenguva nemabasa anowanikwa zvinosiyana. Kumbirai mupi werubatsiro mitemo yakanyorwa, kusanganisira mitengo, nguva shoma yekushanya, mitemo yekukanzura uye muripo wekuwedzera. Chiremba wemunhu, mabasa emunharaunda evakuru kana masangano edementia anogona kukwanisa kukurudzira nzvimbo dzekutarisa, asi enzanisai vapi verubatsiro imi nemunhu.

Kana munhu akangoerekana avhiringidzika kana kurwara zvakanyanya, musafungidzira kuti shanduko iyi inokonzerwa nedementia. [NHS inorayira kuti kutsvaga rubatsiro rwekurapa nekukurumidza kana munhu akangoerekana avhiringidzika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Panyaya dzerubatsiro dzinoenderera mberi, taurai nachiremba kana imwe nyanzvi yakakodzera.

Chinangwa ndechekutsigira kunoenderana nehupenyu hwemunhu. Tsanangurai basa, tarisai ruzivo rwemupi werubatsiro nehurongwa hwekutsiva, batanidzai munhu muchisarudzo chega chega uye ongororai kuti hurongwa uhu hunonzwika sei nekufamba kwenguva.
