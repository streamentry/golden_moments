Kushanya kuchipatara kunogona kukanganisa maitiro ezuva nezuva anozivikanwa uye kunogona kunetsa kunzwisisa. Munhu ari kurarama nedementia angada nguva yakawanda, tsananguro uye rubatsiro pamabasa ezuva nezuva paanenge aripo. Kuronga zvamuchagovera nevashandi kunogona kutsigira munhu wacho nevanhu vari kumubatsira.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association pamusoro pekugara muchipatara nekubuda](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/d5afc74a-9d22-4a15-9c30-6315f6eb5f35/alzheimers-dementia-hospitalization-discharge-planning-ts.pdf) ne[gwaro reDementia Australia pamusoro pekuenda kuchipatara](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/going-hospital) zvinokurudzira kugadzirira ruzivo runobatsira, zvinhu zvinozivikanwa uye mibvunzo yekutevera. Mirairo yevashandi vechipatara inokosha kupfuura gwaro iri rakajairika.

## Ndingagadzirira sei kushanya kuchipatara kwakarongwa?

Kana kushanya kwakarongwa, bvunzai munhu wacho kuti anonzwisisa chii nezvazvo uye kuti angada kuziva chii. Mubatanidzei muzvisarudzo zvakanyanya sezvaanoda. Vamwe vanhu vanoda kunzwa nezvekushanya kwacho nguva refu isati yasvika; vamwe vanogona kunzwa vasina kunyanya kunetseka kana vakapihwa tsananguro pfupi uye yakadzikama nguva yacho yava pedyo. Teverai zvaanoda kana zvichibvira.

Bvunzai chipatara kuti ndeupi ruzivo nezvinhu zvamunofanira kuuya nazvo. Rondedzero yemishonga yaari kunwa parizvino, zvinhu zvaasingawirirani nazvo kana zvichizivikanwa uye mazita nenhamba dzevashandi vehutano vaanowanzoona zvinogona kubatsira vashandi. Uyai magirazi, rubatsiro rwekunzwa, hembe dzinozivikanwa, zvinhu zvekuzvichenesa uye chinhu chinomunyaradza kana chipatara chichizvitendera. Chengetai magwaro ezvemutemo kana ezverubatsiro rwemberi nemunhu wacho chete kana achikodzera uye munhu wacho kana ane mvumo yekusarudza akasarudza kuuya nawo.

Rongai kuti ndiani achaperekedza munhu wacho uye kuti mhuri kana vabatsiri vaanovimba navo vachagovana sei mashoko. Kana mumwe munhu asingakwanisi kugara, kumbirai mumwe munhu waanovimba naye kuti achinjane naye. Munhu wacho angada kuva nemumwe panguva dzimwe dzekushanya uye kuva ari oga pane dzimwe nguva.

## Nderupi ruzivo rwandinofanira kugovera nevashandi vechipatara?

Goverai ruzivo runobatsira vashandi kutaurirana nemunhu uyu uye kumuchengeta. Nemvumo yemunhu wacho, nyorai chinyorwa chipfupi chinosanganisira zita raanoda kudaidzwa naro, matauriro aanoita, chii chinomubatsira kunzwisisa, uye maitiro ezuva nezuva kana zvinhu zvinomunyaradza zvinokosha kwaari. Munogonawo kutaura kuti anoratidza sei kurwadziwa kana kushungurudzika kana zvichigona kuti haashandise mazwi kuzvitaura.

Chengetai chinyorwa chipfupi uye chakavanzika. Goverai chete nenhengo dzechikwata chinomuchengeta dzinoda kuchiziva. Bvunzai vashandi kuti vanoda kugamuchira ruzivo urwu sei uye kuti vanoda kuruchengeta kupi. Musaise ruzivo rwakavanzika pabhodhi rinoonekwa nevakawanda kana kugovana nevaenzi vasingarudi.

Munhu wacho anofanira kuva muhurukuro pese pazvinogoneka. Kumbirai vashandi kuti vataure naye zvakananga, vashandise mazwi ari nyore uye vamupe nguva yekupindura. Kana muchida kuwedzera chimwe chinhu chaanogona kunge asingayeuki, tsanangurai zvamaona pasina kufungidzira chikonzero chacho.

## Ndingabatsira sei panguva yekushanya kwechimbichimbi kuchipatara?

Kana rwendo rwacho rusina kurongwa, tsanangurai zvakaitika, zvakatanga riini uye zvachinja kubva pamaitiro aanowanzoita. Vashandi vanogona kubvunza mubvunzo mumwe chete kanopfuura kamwe. Chinyorwa chipfupi chakanyorwa chinogona kukubatsirai kupa nhoroondo imwe chete yakajeka kuvanhu vakasiyana.

Udzai vashandi kuti munhu wacho ari kurarama nedementia uye kuti ndeupi rubatsiro runowanzo mubatsira. Bvunzai kuti ndiani achava munhu mukuru wekufonerwa kuti muzive zvinenge zvichiitika. Kana munhu wacho akamirira, inzwi rinozivikanwa, chinhu chakanyarara chekutarisa kana magirazi ake kana rubatsiro rwekunzwa zvinogona kubatsira, kana vashandi vakati zvakachengeteka kuzvishandisa.

Teverai mirairo yechikwata chekurapa. Musati mabatsira nezvekudya, zvinwiwa, mishonga, kufamba kana kuzvichengeta, bvunzai vashandi kuti chii chakachengeteka panguva iyoyo. Kuongororwa kana kurapwa muchipatara kunogona kuchinja zvaanokwanisa kuita kwechinguva.

## Ndinofanira kuita sei kana munhu wacho achiratidzika akangoerekana asiyana?

Udzai mukoti kana chiremba nekukurumidza kana munhu wacho akangoerekana anyanya kuvhiringika kana kuita zvakasiyana zvikuru nezvaanowanzoita. Musafunge kuti ingori dementia chete. [Nhungamiro yeNHS pamusoro pekuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) inoti kuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika kunoda kutariswa nachiremba nekukurumidza.

Goverai kuti kuchinja kwakatanga riini uye zvamaona. Munhu anogona kuratidza kusagadzikana nekuchinja kwechiso, mafambiro, kurara kana kudya kunyange kana asingakwanisi kuzvitsanangura. Kumbirai chikwata chinomuchengeta kuti chiongorore kuchinja kwacho pane kuedza kuzviongorora pachenyu.

## Ndingatsigira sei munhu wacho paanenge ari muchipatara?

Chengetai maitiro ezuva nezuva anozivikanwa kana vashandi vakati zvakachengeteka. Uyai mufananidzo unozivikanwa kana chimwe chinhu chinomunyaradza, deredzai ruzha rwakawandisa kana zvichibvira, uye tsanangurai nhanho imwe panguva. Regai munhu wacho aite zvaanokwanisa uye zvaanoda kuita. Bvunzai vashandi musati mafambisa midziyo, kuchinja kamuri kana kubatsira nezvekudya nekuzvichengeta.

Munhu wacho angada rubatsiro kushandisa bhatani rekudana, kuwana imba yekugezera kana kunzwisisa hurongwa hutsva. Udzai vashandi kuti ndeupi rubatsiro rwaanowanzo da, uye bvunzai kuti mungakumbira sei rubatsiro pakati pekushanya. Kana munhu wacho achida kuti mumwe munhu agare naye, bvunzai kuti chipatara chinobvumira kushanya kana kugara usiku kusvika papi.

Kugara muchipatara kunogona kunetesa vachengetiwo. Rongerai mumwe munhu waanovimba naye kuti achinjane nemi kana munhu wacho achibvuma. Bvunzai waanofonera muchipatara kana mushandi wezvemagariro pamusoro perutsigiro rwemunhu wacho nemhuri paanenge achiri muchipatara.

## Ndezvipi zvandinofanira kutarisa munhu asati abuda?

Kumbirai hurongwa hwakanyorwa hwekubuda. Tarisisai kuti hunotsanangura here nguva dzekutevera, mabasa ekuchengeta, kuchinja kupi zvako kwemishonga uye wekufonera kana pakaita dambudziko. Kana mirairo isina kujeka, kumbirai chikwata chekubudisa kuti chitsanangure musati mabuda.

Kurukurai kuti ndeupi rubatsiro ruchadikanwa kumba. Fungai kuti ndiani achabatsira nezvekudya, kufamba, kugeza, nguva dzekuona chiremba uye midziyo mitsva kana rubatsiro rwepamba. Taurai chokwadi pamusoro pezvinogona kupihwa nemhuri neshamwari. Bvunzai murongi wekubudisa muchipatara pamusoro pemamwe masevhisi emunharaunda kana hurongwa hacho huchiita sehunonetsa.

Mushure mekudzokera kumba, ipai nguva yekuzorora nekudzokerazve kumaitiro ezuva nezuva anozivikanwa. Goverai hurongwa hwakanyorwa chete nevanhu vakabvumidzwa nemunhu wacho kuti vabatanidzwe. Kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringika kana kurwara zvakanyanya, tsvakai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwekurapwa pane kumirira nguva yakajairika yekutevera.

Kushanya kwakadzikama hakugari kuchibvira, kunyanya muchiitiko chechimbichimbi. Kugadzirira, kutaurirana kwakajeka uye hurongwa hwakanyorwa hwekubuda zvinogona kubatsira kuti munhu wese azive zvinoitika zvinotevera.
