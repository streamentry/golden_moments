Kugara kure kunogona kuita kuti zvinyanye kuoma kuziva kuti rubatsiro rwupi runodiwa, asi kure hakubvisi nzira dzose dzekubatsira. Unogona kukwanisa kuronga masevhisi, kuita rimwe basa raunenge wabvumirana nemunhu, kana kutsigira munhu anopa rubatsiro zuva nezuva. Tanga nekubvunza munhu anorarama nedementia zvaanoda uye kuti ndiani waanoda abatanidzwe.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association rekubatsira uri kure](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) rinotsanangura mabasa akadai sekuronga nguva dzekuonana nachiremba, kutsvaga masevhisi emunharaunda, kushanda nevabatsiri vari pedyo uye kupa muchengeti wemazuva ose zororo. [Family Caregiver Alliance inotaurawo](https://www.caregiver.org/faq/how-can-i-help-with-caregiving-from-far-away/) kuti kutsvaga masevhisi uye kuronga kushanya kunogona kuva kwakaoma uri kure. Gwaro rino rinopa pfungwa dzekuronga dzakajairika; masevhisi emunharaunda nemitemo zvinosiyana.

## Ndingaita sei ndiri kure?

Sarudza mabasa anoshanda anoenderana nezvinodiwa nemunhu uye nezvaunogona kuita nguva dzose. Rutsigiro runogona kusanganisira:

- **Ronga nguva dzekuonana nachiremba.** Bvunza kana munhu achida rubatsiro rwekufonera kiriniki, kuchengeta karenda kana kuronga zvekufambisa.
- **Tsvaga masevhisi emunharaunda.** Tsvaga kuendeserwa chikafu, zvekufambisa, rubatsiro rwepamba, zvirongwa zvemasikati zvevakuru kana mapoka evachengeti kwaanogara. Tarisa ruzivo nemupi werubatsiro; kuwanikwa kunosiyana nenzvimbo.
- **Batsira nemapepa kana maodha amunenge mabvumirana.** Unogona kubatsira kuronga mafomu kana kuronga zvinhu zvinodiwa kana munhu akakumbira. Marekodhi emari, inishuwarenzi uye ezveutano anoda kungwarirwa zvakanyanya; shandisa gwaro redu re[rubatsiro rwemari nekudzivirira kubiridzirwa](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/) uye tarisa kana paine mvumo inodiwa.
- **Ipa muchengeti ari pedyo nguva yekuzorora.** Panguva yekushanya, bvunza kuti ndeupi basa chairo kana zororo ringabatsira. Unogona kuronga kudya, kubatsira panguva yakarongwa yekuonana nachiremba kana kuva nemunhu wacho apo mubatsiri ari pedyo achizorora.
- **Rambai muchitaurirana nenzira inonakidza munhu wacho.** Bvunza kana achida kufona, vhidhiyo kufona, tsamba kana kushanya. Kufona kunogona kuva hurukuro, kwete bvunzo pamusoro pezvekudya, mishonga kana kurangarira.

[Alzheimer's Association inotaura kuti vanhu vanopa rutsigiro vari kure vanogona kusakwanisa kuona zvido zvemunhu zvakananga](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage). Kana zvichibvira, taura nevanhu vanoona munhu wacho zuva nezuva uye ronga kushanya kunzvimbo yaanogara. Maonero iwayo anogona kupa ruzivo runobatsira, asi haatsivi kubvunza munhu wacho kana kuwana ongororo yenyanzvi.

## Ndingashanda sei nevabatsiri vari pedyo?

Tanga nekubvunza munhu wacho kuti ndiani waanoda abatanidzwe uye ndedzipi nhau dzaanonzwa akasununguka kugovera. Anogona kuda kuti hama imwe ironge nguva dzekuonana nachiremba, muvakidzani atarise chinhu chimwe chete, kana kuti pasave nekutarisa kwenguva dzose. Usawedzera munhu muhurukuro yerubatsiro nekuti anogara pedyo chete.

Kana munhu wacho achibvumirana, gadzirai hurongwa hwakapfava hwekutaura nevanhu vaanosarudza:

- **Sarudzai munhu mumwe chete wekupa mashoko enguva dzose.** Bvumiranai kuti ndiani achatuma mashoko enguva dzose uye kakawanda sei.
- **Sarudzai mumwe anotsiva.** Sarudzai kuti ndiani anogona kutarisa kana mubatsiri wemazuva ose ari pedyo asipo.
- **Paradzai mashoko enguva dzose nezvinonetsa zvechimbichimbi.** Bvumiranai kuti munhu ari pedyo achakusvikirai sei kana kuchengeteka kana utano hwemunhu hukachinja kamwe kamwe.
- **Chengetai mabasa akajeka.** Nyora pasi kuti ndiani achaita nezvenguva dzekuonana nachiremba, zvekufambisa, kufona kumasevhisi kana rubatsiro rwepamba. Bvunzai musati machinja hurongwa.

[Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kutaurirana nguva dzose nemhuri, vavakidzani nevapa rubatsiro](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Unogonawo kubvunza mubatsiri ari pedyo kuti ndeupi rutsigiro rwaanoda. Chikumbiro chipfupi uye chakajeka chinogona kuva nyore kushandisa pane kungoti “ndizivise kana uchida rubatsiro.”

## Ndedzipi nhau dzingagoverwa nachiremba kana mupi werubatsiro neni?

Bvunza chiremba kana mupi werubatsiro wemurwere kuti vanoda mvumo ipi kana mapepa api vasati vagovera mashoko. [Alzheimer's Association inotaura kuti mupi werubatsiro anogona kuda mvumo yakanyorwa asati ataura nezverubatsiro nehama iri kure](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Mafomu nemitemo zvinosiyana, saka bvunza mupi werubatsiro pane kufungidzira kuti kuva hama kunokupa mvumo yekuwana mashoko.

Remekedza zvido zvemunhu pamusoro pekuti ndiani anofanira kugamuchira mashoko. Govera chete zvinodiwa pabasa ramunenge mabvumirana, uye chengeta ruzivo rwekufonerana kana manotsi zviri zvakavanzika. Kana paine gwaro repamutemo kana mvumo yakarongeka, bvunza nyanzvi yakakodzera munzvimbo inogara munhu wacho kuti rinorevei. Gwaro rino haritauri kuti ani zvake ane mvumo yepamutemo.

## Ndingaita sei kuti kushanya kubatsire tese?

Bvunza munhu wacho kuti angada kupedza sei nguva yacho. Siyai nzvimbo yekudya pamwe chete, kufamba, mimhanzi kana hurukuro yakadzikama, kwete nguva dzekuonana nachiremba nemabasa chete.

Kana munhu wacho achida rubatsiro rwekuronga, shandisai chimwe chikamu chekushanya kusangana nevabatsiri vari pedyo, kutaura nachiremba kana kuona kana hurongwa huri kushanda. Bvunza usati waongorora nzvimbo dzepachivande kana marekodhi. Taurai zvinonetsa makadzikama uye tarisai pachinhu chiri kuchinja, kwete kutonga kuti mumwe munhu ari kubata sei.

[Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kushandisa kushanya kuti musanganazve uye mubatsirane nevachengeti vari pedyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Kana ukaona chinhu chinonetsa, taurirana nemunhu wacho nevanhu vaanovimba navo. Kushanya kumwe chete hakugoni kuratidza mufananidzo wakazara weutano kana kugona kwemunhu.

## Ko kana zvido zvemunhu kana rutsigiro rwake zvachinja?

Ongororai hurongwa zvakare kana munhu wacho akakumbira rumwe rubatsiro, kana muitiro wava kuoma kana mubatsiri ari pedyo asingachakwanisi kuenderera mberi. Bvunza kuti munhu wacho anoda kuramba achiitei uye ndeupi basa rinoda kutariswa iye zvino. Chiremba, mushandi wezvemagariro kana sevhisi yemunharaunda yevakuru inogona kubatsira kuona zvingasarudzwa. Kuti muwane ruzivo nezverubatsiro rwemunharaunda, onai magwaro edu e[rubatsiro rwepamba](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) uye [kugara uri wega une dementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/).

Iva nechokwadi chekuti pane munhu ari pedyo anogona kupindura kana paine mamiriro ezvinhu echimbichimbi. Gwaro redu re[kuronga zvechimbichimbi](/caregiving/emergency-planning-dementia/) rinogona kubatsira munhu wacho nevabatsiri vaanosarudza kunyora mazita ekufonerana nehurongwa hwekutsiva. Kana paine kusawirirana pamusoro pekuti ndiani anofanira kuita chii, onai [hurukuro dzemhuri pamusoro pemabasa erubatsiro](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/).

Kure kunogona kuderedza rubatsiro rwemaoko, asi hakusarudzi kuti munhu ane hanya zvakadii kana zvaanogona kupa. Chengetai hurongwa hwakavakirwa pazvido zvemunhu, pamabasa chaiwo uye parutsigiro rwuripo pedyo.
