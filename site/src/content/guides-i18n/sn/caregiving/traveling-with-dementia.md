Kufamba kunogona kuramba kuri sarudzo kana munhu wacho achida kuenda uye hurongwa huchienderana nezvaanoda. Kushanyira mhuri kwenguva pfupi kunogona kuva nyore kupfuura rwendo rwakazara nezviitiko zvakawanda; rwendo rwezuva rimwe kunogona kuva nani pane kurara kunze. Bvunzai kuti munhu wacho anodei parwendo, mobva magadzira hurongwa hunopa mukana wekumbomira kana kuchinja.

[Gwaro rekufamba reAlzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) uye [mazano ekufamba eDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) ose anosimbisa kuronga zvichienderana nokunyaradzwa netsika dzenguva dzose dzemunhu wacho. Gwaro iri rinopa mazano ekugadzirira akajairika, kwete sarudzo yekuti rwendo rwakachengeteka here kune mumwe munhu chaiye.

## Tingasarudza sei kana rwendo rwakatikwanira?

Taurai nezverwendo nemunhu wacho, muchishandisa mashoko aanoziva netsananguro iri nyore yekwamuri kuenda uye kuti ndiani achavapo. Kana asingadi kuenda, gamuchirai izvozvo. Munogona kuronga kudya pamwe chete, kufona kana chiitiko chaanoziva pamba.

Fungai nezvezvinhu zvinokwaniswa nemunhu wacho iye zvino zvakanaka. Tarisaizve maitiro aanoita pakuchinja kwetsika dzenguva dzose, ruzha, vanhu vazhinji, madaro ekufamba, kumirira nemakamuri aasingazivi. Kana zvikamu zvakawanda zverwendo zvichigona kumunetsa, zvirerutsai kana kusarudza imwe nzira yekupedza nguva pamwe chete. Dementia Australia inokurudzira kubvunza chiremba kana musina chokwadi chekuti rwendo runoenderana neutano kana nerutsigiro rwunodiwa nemunhu wacho.

Kana munhu wacho aine dambudziko rehutano idzva, achida rubatsiro nemishonga, kana aine zvaanoda pakufamba zvingachinja panguva yerwendo, bvunzai nyanzvi yezvehutano yakakodzera musati maronga rwendo. Musamire, kuchinja kana kurongazve mishonga yakanyorwa kuti muende parwendo pasina mazano kubva kuna chiremba akainyora kana mupharamasiti.

## Nderudzii rwerwendo rwatinofanira kuronga?

- **Sarudzai nzvimbo yaanoziva pazvinogoneka.** Nzvimbo inozivikanwa nemunhu wacho, kana kushanyira vanhu vaanoziva, kungava nekuchinja kushoma pane nzira itsva uye hurongwa hwakazara hwekuona nzvimbo.
- **Chengetai nzira iri nyore.** Sarudzai nzira dzine kuchinjana kushoma uye dzivisai kubatana kwakasimba kwenguva. Tarisaizve kwadziri zvimbuzi, nzvimbo dzekugara nenzvimbo dzakanyarara musati maenda.
- **Fambai panguva yakanaka yezuva.** Rongai muchitarisa simba nenguva dzezuva nezuva dzemunhu wacho. Siyai nguva yekudya, yekunwa mishonga, yekuzorora uye yekugadzirira.
- **Chengetai hurongwa husina kuzara.** Chiitiko chimwe chikuru chinogona kukwana. Dzivisai kuronga awa yoga yoga; siyai nzvimbo yekunonoka kana yekudzoka nekukurumidza.
- **Bvunzai nezverubatsiro musati maenda.** Makambani endege, echitima, mahotera nenzvimbo dzezviitiko ane masevhisi nemitemo yakasiyana. Vabatanidzei nenguva kuti mubvunze nezverubatsiro rwekufamba, pekugara, zvimbuzi zvinokwanisika kushandisa uye rubatsiro rwekuwana nzvimbo chaiyo.

[Gwaro remazororo akanyarara](/activities/holidays/) rine mazano ekushanya nekuungana kwemhuri. Kana muchizosangana nehama, [gwaro redu rekushanya](/activities/visiting/) rinotaura nezvekukurukurirana, kushanya kupfupi uye kuti mungaoneka sei pasina kumanikidza.

## Tinofanira kurongedza chii?

Chengetai bhegi diki rekufambisa rine zvinhu zvakakosha zvinogara nemunhu wamunovimba naye kwete mumikwende inoendeswa yoga.

- **Mishonga neruzivo rwehutano.** Uyai nemishonga yaanowanzo shandisa yemunhu wacho iri mumapakeji ayo ekutanga, yakakwana rwendo rwakarongwa pamwe nekunonoka kunogoneka, pamwe nerondedzero yemishonga yazvino. Tarisaizve mitemo yazvino yekufambisa neyenzvimbo yamunoenda musati mafamba nemishonga yakanyorwa nachiremba. Chengetai mwero nenguva sezvazvakanyorwa.
- **Magwaro ekuzvizivisa neruzivo rwekufonera.** Takurai magwaro ekuzvizivisa emunhu wacho, kero yenzvimbo yamunogara, ruzivo rwevanhu vekufonera pakukurumidzira uye hurongwa hwerwendo hwakanyorwa. Pazvinobatsira uye muchibvumirana, sanganisirai kadhi rine ruzivo rwekufonera rwemunhu anochengeta.
- **Zvinhu zvinozorodza.** Rongedzai magirazi, rubatsiro rwekunzwa nemabhatiri aro kana chaja, zvekudya zvakareruka zvaanoziva, mvura, mapepa ekupukuta mhino, hembe yakareruka uye mbatya dzekuchinja. Chinhu chaanoziva kana mufananidzo zvinogona kuita kuti kamuri yaasingazivi rive nyore kugara mariri.
- **Makopi eruzivo rwakakosha.** Chengetai kopi yepabepa kana yedhijitari yematikiti, marongero, inishuwarenzi neruzivo rwehutano rwakakodzera. Goverai hurongwa hwerwendo nemunhu wamunovimba naye asiri kufamba nemi.

Huwandu hweruzivo rwekufambisa hunoenderana nerwendo. Chengetai ruzivo rwemunhu rwakachengeteka uye mugovane chete nevanhu vanoruda kuti vape rubatsiro.

## Tingaite sei kuti zuva rekufamba rive nyore?

Taurirai munhu wacho zvichaitika zvinotevera, nhanho imwe panguva. Ipai nguva yakawedzerwa yekugadzirira, kupinda, kushandisa chimbuzi uye kufamba kubva panzvimbo imwe kuenda kune imwe. Kana hurongwa hwachinja, tsanangurai nhanho inotevera zviri nyore pane kudzokorora hurongwa hwese hurefu.

Garaizve pamwe chete munzvimbo dzine vanhu vazhinji kana dzisingazivikanwi. Kana munhu wacho aneta kana kusagadzikana, imbomirai panzvimbo yakanyarara, mupai mvura kana chiitiko chaanoziva, mobva masarudza pamwe chete kana muchizoenderera mberi. Musasiye munhu wacho ari oga munzvimbo yaasingazivi maanogona kuvhiringika kana kushungurudzika.

Kana muchifamba nendege kana kushandisa chiteshi chikuru, bvunzai anopa rubatsiro kuti ndeupi rubatsiro rwuripo uye kuti mungarukumbira sei. Maitiro anogona kuchinja, saka simbisaizve ruzivo zvakananga nekambani yendege, chiteshi kana nzvimbo yezviitiko musati mafamba. Musavimba nerondedzero yekare yemitemo yazvino yekuchengeteka kana yekuwanikwa kwerubatsiro.

## Ndeupi urongwa hwekutsigira hunobatsira?

Sarudzai pachine nguva mberi zvamuchaita kana munhu wacho achida kuenda, rwendo rwakanonoka kana mumwe munhu achida kuzorora. Sarudzai munhu mumwe wekufonera anogona kubatsira parunhare. Chengetai kero yenzvimbo yamunogara nenzira yekudzoka zviri nyore kuwana, uye zivai kwamunogona kugara makanyarara kana kushandisa chimbuzi.

Kana muchifanira kupfupisa rwendo, zviitei. Kusvika panzvimbo yamaida kana kudzoka nekukurumidza hazvirevi kutadza. Kana kufamba kukanetsa, sarudzai chiitiko chakanyarara pamunosvika kumba. [Gwaro rekukurukurirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) rine mazano ekutaura makanyarara uye kupa ruzivo rumwe panguva.

## Tinofanira kuudza hotera kana anopa rubatsiro rwekufamba here?

Kana munhu wacho achibvumirana, udzai vashandi chete zvavanofanira kuziva kuti vape rubatsiro runobatsira. Semuenzaniso, mungakumbira hotera kuti icherechedze zvaanoda pakufamba kana kukumbira kamuri rinokwanisika kushandisa. Kana munhu wacho asingadi kuti mamiriro ake agoverwe, tauriranai imwe nzira yekukumbira rubatsiro inodzivirira zvakavanzika zvake.

Parwendo rwakareba, chengetai kudya, kurara nenguva dzekunwa mishonga zvemunhu wacho zviri pedyo ne zvakajairika sezvinobvumirwa nehurongwa. Rongai zuva rekutanga risina kukurumidza uye siyai nguva yekuzorora mushure mekudzoka. Sarudzo diki, dzakajairika dzinogona kuita kuti rwendo ruve nyore kujairira.
