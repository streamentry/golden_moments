Kudonha kunogona kuvhundutsa munhu pamwe nevamwe vari pedyo. Rambai makadzikama uye tarisai pakuchengetedzeka pakarepo. Musafungidzire kuti munhu haana kukuvara nokuti haakwanisi kutsanangura zvakaitika kana kutaura panorwadza.

## Chii chandinofanira kuita pakarepo mushure mekudonha?

Tarisai kana munhu akamuka, achiita seakakuvara kana kuti haasi kunzwa zvakanaka, uye kuti anokwanisa kupindura sezvaanowanzoita. Musamumhanyire kumumutsa amire kana kuedza kumusimudza mega. [NHS inoti rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi runodiwa kana munhu angave akakuvara musoro, musana, mutsipa kana hudyu, ane kumwe kukuvara kwakakomba, kana kuti akatadza kusimuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/).

- **Fonera rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwemunharaunda yenyu** kana chimwe chezviratidzo izvi zvakakomba chingave chiripo. Teverai mirairo yeanenge achipindura runhare.
- **Chengetai munhu akasununguka uye achidziya** muchimirira rubatsiro. Musamufambisa kana muchifungidzira kuti akakuvara zvakakomba kana kuti musina chokwadi kuti zvakachengeteka here.
- **Garai naye kana zvichikwanisika.** Shandisai mashoko mapfupi uye akadzikama. Musamubvunze mibvunzo yakawanda pamusoro pezvakaitika kana kumumanikidza kuti amire.
- **Kana munhu achirwadziwa, akakuvara kana kuti haasi kunzwa zvakanaka asi pasina njodzi iripo ipapo,** tauriranai nerubatsiro rwezvehutano rwechimbichimbi rwemunharaunda yenyu kana nyanzvi yezvehutano yemunhu iyeye kuti muwane gwara.

Musaedze kutakura kana kusimudza munhu mega. Kana paine hurongwa hwekuchengetwa hunotaura nezvekudonha, teverai chete kana huchikodzera zviri kuitika izvozvi; tsvakai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi kana munhu angave akakuvara zvakakomba.

## Ko kana munhu achiita seanokwanisa kusimuka?

Musamumanikidze. Mubvunzei kuti ari kunzwa sei uye kuti anoda rubatsiro here. Kana achiita seasina kukuvara uye achikwanisa kusimuka achishandisa rutsigiro rwaanowanzo shandisa pasina kusimudzwa, mupei nguva uye garai pedyo. Mirai kana achinzwa kurwadziwa, achidzengerera kana kuti asingakwanisi kuzviita. Kana musina chokwadi, bvunzai nyanzvi yezvehutano pane kuedza kumubatsira kufamba nenzira yamusina kumbodzidziswa.

[NHS inorayira kuti munhu atore nguva uye azorore mushure mekusimuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Mirairo yayo yekuzvibatsira ndeyemunhu anonzwa achikwanisa kusimuka; haisi mvumo yekudhonza kana kutakura munhu adonha. Kugona kufamba, kuenzanisa muviri uye zvinodiwa zvehutano zvemunhu zvinosiyana.

## Ndinofanira kubata chiremba kunyange kana ndisingaoni kukuvara here?

Hongu. [Alzheimer's Society inokurudzira kutaura nachiremba wemhuri (GP) wemunhu mushure mekudonha, kunyange kana muchifunga kuti haana kukuvara](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/blog/why-person-dementia-more-likely-have-fall-and-what-can-you-do-help). Kana makaona musoro warova, kurwadziwa, kuvhiringidzika kutsva, kushaya simba, dzungu kana shanduko kubva pamaitiro ake enguva dzose, taurai izvozvo pamunofona. Musashandise runyorwa rwepawebhusaiti kusarudza kuti kuongororwa nachiremba hakudiwi.

Kana pakaita kukuvara kwemusoro, teverai zano renyanzvi yezvehutano kana rebasa rezvehutano rwechimbichimbi remunzvimbo yenyu. [Gwaro reNHS rekukuvara kwemusoro](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) rinotsanangura zviratidzo zvinoda kutariswa nekukurumidza. Kana munhu akangoerekana avhiringidzika, akarara zvakanyanya, akarutsa kana achiita seasinganzwi zvakanaka zvikuru, tsvakai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi pane kumirira nguva yekuongororwa kwakajairika.

## Ndeupi ruzivo rungabatsira nyanzvi yezvehutano?

Kana zvinodiwa zvekukurumidza zvagadziriswa, nyorai zvishoma zvamaona kuti mugovane, munhu wacho achibvumirana nazvo:

- kuti kudonha kwakaitika riini uye kupi, kana muchiziva;
- zvanga zvichiitwa nemunhu kudonha kusati kwaitika;
- kuti kudonha kwakaonekwa nemumwe munhu here uye kuti munhu angave akarova musoro here;
- kurwadziwa, kukuvara kana shanduko yamakaona;
- chero shanduko ichangobva kuitika pautano, pakufamba, mutsika dzemazuva ese kana pamishonga yamunoziva.

Tsanangurai zvamaona pane kufungidzira kuti sei munhu adonha. Chinyorwa chinogona kubatsira pahurukuro; hachiratidzi chikonzero. Kana paine mukana wekukuvara kwakakomba kana shanduko inokurumidza, tsvakai rubatsiro kutanga pane kupedza kunyora.

## Chii chandinofanira kubvunza pakuteverwa?

Nyanzvi yezvehutano inogona kubatsira kusarudza kuti munhu anoda kuongororwa here uye zvekutarisisa mushure mezvo. [NHS inoti kuongororwa kwekudonha kunogona kutarisa simba, kuona, kunzwa, mishonga uye kugadziridzwa kweimba](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Munogona kubvunza:

- Munhu anoda kuongororwa mushure mekudonha uku here, kunyange kana pasina kukuvara kuri pachena?
- Ndedzipi shanduko kana zviratidzo zvinofanira kuita kuti titsvage rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi?
- Kuenzanisa muviri, kuona, kunzwa, kurwadziwa kana mishonga zvinofanira kuongororwa here?
- Basa rekubatsira vanodonha kana murapi webasa (occupational therapist) ringabatsira here kuongorora kufamba kana midziyo yemumba?
- Munhu kana mhuri inofanira kubata ani kana kudonhazve kukaitika?

Udzai nyanzvi yezvehutano zvamunoziva uye zvamusina kuziva. Kana kudonha kusina kuonekwa nemumwe munhu, taurai izvozvo pane kufungidzira kuti kwakaitika sei. Musamisa kana kuchinja mishonga, kutenga mudziyo wekufambisa kana kuisa midziyo muchingovimba nepeji rewebhusaiti chete. Rutsigiro rwakakodzera runoenderana neutano hwemunhu, kugona kwake kufamba, imba yake uye zvaanoshuva.

## Tingafunge sei nezvekudonhazve?

Kana zvinonetsa zvehutano zvekukurumidza zvagadziriswa, fungai nezvezvanga zvichiitika munzvimbo uye mutsika dzemazuva ese. Nzira yakazara nezvinhu, kuchinja kwemwenje kana nzira isina kujairika zvinogona kunge zvine chekuita nazvo, asi musafungidzire kuti njodzi imwe chete ndiyo yakonzera kudonha. Bvunzai munhu wacho zvaakaona uye zvingaita kuti nzvimbo ive nyore kushandisa. Kana munhu asingakwanisi kuyeuka kana kutsanangura kudonha, goverai zvamaona musingamumanikidzi kutaura zvakawanda. Nyora nguva, nzvimbo, shangu, mwenje, zvaanga achiita uye chero shanduko kubva pakufamba kana maitiro ake enguva dzose. Chinyorwa chipfupi chinogona kubatsira nyanzvi yezvehutano kutsvaga maitiro anodzokororwa; hachiratidzi chikonzero.

[Gwaro redu rekuchengetedzeka pamba](/caregiving/home-safety/) rinotsanangura zvekutarisa mukamuri yega yega. Kana kudonha kwakaitika panguva yekugeza, onai [gwaro redu rekugeza](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). Chiremba, murapi webasa (occupational therapist) kana basa rekubatsira vanodonha remunharaunda yenyu vanogona kuongorora zvinonetsa zvemunhu uye kurayira shanduko dzakakodzera. Musaise midziyo kana kuchinja mushonga wakanyorerwa nachiremba muchingovimba nepeji ino chete.

Kudonha kwega hakuratidzi zvese zvinogona kana zvisingagoni kuitwa nemunhu. Ramba uchimubatanidza muzvisarudzo pamusoro perutsigiro, uye tevera zano rechikwata chekutarisira chemunharaunda yake.
