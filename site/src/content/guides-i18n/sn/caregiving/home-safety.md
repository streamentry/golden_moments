Shanduko yekuchengetedzeka kwepamba inofanira kugadzirisa dambudziko rakasangana nemunhu waunotarisira, kwete kushandura kamuri yega yega kuita rondedzero yezvirambidzo. Tangai nekamuri imwe uye chinonetsa chimwe: nzira ine rima inoenda kuimba yekugezera, tambo iri munzira yekufamba, kana kusava nechokwadi pamusoro pemudziyo uri mukicheni. Chinangwa ndechekuita kuti maitiro ezuva nezuva arambe achiitika zviri nyore.

[Alzheimer's Association home safety checklist](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/home-safety) inotarisa njodzi dziri mukicheni, muimba yekugezera, mudzimba dzekurara, munzvimbo dzekuchengetera zvinhu uye kunze. Inotaurawo kuti shanduko dzinofanira kutsigira zvido zvemunhu waunotarisira nekuzvimiririra kwake. [Alzheimer's Society's home equipment guide](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/what-equipment-improve-adapt-home-person-dementia) inotsanangura kuti shanduko diki dzinogona kuita kuti maitiro ezuva nezuva ave nyore uye akachengeteka. Hapana rondedzero inokodzera munhu wese kana imba yega yega.

## Ndingatanga sei kuongorora kuchengetedzeka kwepamba?

Sarudzai kamuri imwe pane pakambova nedambudziko kana panonzwa kuoma. Kana munhu waunotarisira achida kubatsira, fambai mese muongorore uye mubvunze zvinomunetsa. Munhu anotarisira anogona kuona tambo yakasununguka; munhu waunotarisira anogona kutaura kuti chibatiso chemwenje chakaoma kusvika kana kuti chigaro chajairika chaendeswa kune imwe nzvimbo. Maonero ese ari maviri anobatsira kusarudza shanduko inonzwisisika.

Nyora pasi dambudziko racho nemashoko ari nyore: “Mukoto mune rima manheru,” “Gonhi reimba yekugezera rakaoma kuona usiku,” kana “Chinhu chekuchenesa chinochengetwa padivi pesingi.” Wobva wasarudza danho rimwe rinoshanda woona kana richibatsira. Haufaniri kutenga midziyo padambudziko rega rega. Mwenje uri nani, nzira yakacheneswa kana kuisa chinhu chinoshandiswa kazhinji panzvimbo iri nyore kusvika inogona kukwana.

[Alzheimer's Society's home safety tips](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/tips-staying-safe-secure-home-dementia) dzinosanganisira mienzaniso yakagoverwa nevanhu vanorarama nedementia nemhuri dzavo, sekuchengeta ruzivo runobatsira panzvimbo iri nyore kuwana uye kushandisa mwenje unovheneka wega usiku. Tora izvi semazano ekufunga nezvawo, kwete semirairo inofanira kuteverwa nemunhu wese.

## Ndezvipi zvandinofanira kutarisa mukamuri yega yega?

### Mikova yekupinda, mikoto nemasitepisi

- **Ita kuti nzira ionekwe zviri nyore.** Tarisa kuti panopindwa napo, masitepisi uye nzira iri pakati peimba yekurara neimba yekugezera zvine mwenje wakadzikama. Mwenje weusiku kana mwenje unovheneka kana paine munhu anofamba unogona kubatsira kana munhu waunotarisira achiwanzomuka kwasviba.
- **Chengetedza nzira dzekufamba dzakajeka.** Bvisa tambo dzakasununguka, zvinhu zvakapararira uye zvinhu zvinogona kugumburwa nazvo zviri nyore. Tarisa kuti macarpet akarara zvakanaka; bvisa kana kusunga rimwe chete kana richikonzera dambudziko.
- **Tarisa panoshanduka kukwirira kwepasi.** Kana danho kana muganhu wepasi zvakaoma kuona, bvunza nyanzvi yezvekubatsira pamabasa epamba kana nyanzvi yekuchengetedzeka kwepamba kuti ndeipi shanduko inogona kuvandudza kuonekwa pasina kugadzira rimwe divi rinogona kugumburwa naro.
- **Chengeta zvinhu zvajairika panzvimbo dzinoita musoro.** Chigaro, rambi kana chimwe chinhu chinoshandiswa kazhinji chinogona kubatsira munhu kutevera maitiro ake. Kuendesa zvinhu zvese kuti paita kunge pakachena kunogona kuita kuti nzira isanyanya kujairika.

### Kicheni

- **Ongorora mashandisirwo emidziyo.** Kana pakambova nedambudziko nesitofu kana mumwe mudziyo, tsvaga nzira dzekuchengetedza dzakagadzirirwa mudziyo iwoyo. Alzheimer's Association inotaura nezvemidziyo inozvidzima yega uye midziyo yekuchengetedza sitofu semikana. Wana zano kubva kunyanzvi yakakodzera usati wachinja midziyo yegasi kana yemagetsi.
- **Chengeta mishonga nezvinhu zvisiri zvekudya kure nezvekudya.** Association inokurudzira kuchengetedza mishonga zvakachengeteka pane kuisiya patafura. Kana munhu waunotarisira achizvichengetera mishonga yake, bvunza mufamasiti kana nyanzvi yezvehutano nezveurongwa hunochengetedza maitiro iwayo.
- **Chengeta zvinhu zvekuchenesa nezvigadzirwa zvinogona kukuvadza zvakachengeteka.** Zvichengete mumidziyo yazvo yepakutanga, kure nezvekudya uye pasingasvikirwi pazvinenge zvichidiwa. Usaise makemikari mubhodhoro rechinwiwa kana muchinu chekudya.
- **Chengeta zvinhu zvinobatsira mukicheni zviri nyore kusvika.** Urongwa hwakachengeteka hunofanira kubvumira munhu waunotarisira kuramba achiita mabasa aanofarira pazvinogoneka. Kana ukachinja kabhodi kana mudziyo, tsanangura shanduko yacho uye tarisa kana ichikonzera kuvhiringika.

Kana chinonetsa chikaonekwa panguva yebasa ramunoita mese, gwara redu re[household tasks guide](/activities/household-tasks/) rine mazano ekugadzirisa basa racho muchiri kuita kuti munhu waunotarisira abatsire.

### Imba yekugezera

- **Vhenekera nzira nekamuri.** Tarisa chibatiso chemwenje, mwenje weusiku uye nzira inoenda kuchimbuzi. Alzheimer's Society inotaura nezvemwenje weusiku kana mwenje unovheneka wega kana zvichibatsira munhu kuwana kamuri.
- **Tarisa nzvimbo dzinotsvedza nezvinotsigira.** Kana pasi kana bhavhu richitsvedza, bvunza nyanzvi yakakodzera nezvenzvimbo isingatsvedzi, njanji yekubata kana chimwe chinhu chinotsigira chakakodzera. Nzvimbo yakakodzera inoenderana nemunhu waunotarisira neimba yekugezera.
- **Ita kuti kamuri ive nyore kuziva nekushandisa.** Isa zvinhu zvemazuva ese panzvimbo dzinotarisirwa nemunhu waunotarisira. Kana achinetseka kuwana chimbuzi, chikwangwani chiri nyore kana kusiya gonhi rakavhurika zvinogona kubatsira, kana zvichikodzera imba yacho.
- **Usaunze hita inotakurika muimba yekugezera.** Chengeta midziyo yemagetsi kure nenzvimbo dzine mvura uye tevera mirairo yemugadziri.

### Imba yekurara nenzira yeusiku

- **Chengetedza nzira iri pakati pemubhedha neimba yekugezera yakajeka.** Bvisa zvinhu pasi uye iva nechokwadi chekuti munhu waunotarisira anogona kusvika pamwenje asina kuyambuka kamuri ine rima.
- **Tarisa mahita nemidziyo yekudziisa.** Kana gumbeze remagetsi, padi inodziya kana hita inotakurika ichishandiswa, ongorora nzvimbo yayo nemirairo yayo. Kumbira zano kana paine njodzi yekutsva kana kuti munhu waunotarisira asingakwanisi kuishandisa zvakachengeteka ari oga.
- **Ita kuti zvinhu zvemazuva ese zvive nyore kusvika.** Chengeta hembe, magirazi, foni kana zvimwe zvinhu zvinodiwa munzvimbo dzajairika uye pahurefu hunogona kusvika munhu waunotarisira asina kukwira.

### Imba yekuwachira, nzvimbo dzekuchengetera nekunze

- **Chengeta zvinhu zvekuchenesa, mishonga nemidziyo zvakachengeteka.** Tarisa makabhodhi, nzvimbo dzekuwachira, magaraji, mashedhi nemapasi epasi. Chengeta makemikari mumidziyo yawo yepakutanga uye chengetedza midziyo inocheka kana michina pazvinenge paine njodzi chaiyo.
- **Tarisa midziyo nemaaramu.** Iva nechokwadi chekuti maaramu eutsi neemacarbon-monoxide aripo uye ari kushanda. Tevera mirairo yawo yekuyedza nekuchinja mabhatiri.
- **Chengeta rondedzero yenhamba dzekukurumidzira panzvimbo iri nyore kuwana.** Sanganisira nhamba yekukurumidzira yenzvimbo yacho, vanhu vakakosha kumunhu waunotarisira uye ruzivo rwupi zvarwo rungadiwa nemubatsiri waunovimba naye. Usaisa ruzivo rwakavanzika panzvimbo inogona kuonekwa nemuenzi.
- **Tarisa nzira iri kunze.** Tarisa mwenje usina kukwana, nzvimbo dzisina kuenzana, hosipombi kana midziyo iri munzira yekufamba. Itai shanduko imwe panguva kuti nzira dzajairika dzirambe dzichizivikanwa.

Nezvemaitiro eusiku, [sleep guide](/brain-health/sleep/) ine rumwe ruzivo nezvekuchinja kwekurara nemaitiro ajairika emanheru.

## Ndingachengeta sei shanduko dziine ruremekedzo?

Kuchengetedzeka nekuzvimiririra zvinofanira kushanda pamwe chete. Kana shanduko ichitadzisa mumwe munhu kushandisa kamuri kana kuita basa rajairika, mirai mumbofunga imwe nzira isinganyanyi kumumanikidza. Mamwe masangano anotaura nezvezvikiyi panjodzi chaidzo; ichi hachisi chirevo chinofanira kushandiswa kwese. Usati wachinja gonhi kana kudzivisa nzira, wanai zano renzvimbo rinoenderana nekuchengetedzeka kwepamba nemoto uye fungai kuti munhu waunotarisira achabuda sei panguva yenjodzi. Siyanai nzira yakachengeteka yekubuda nayo.

Pazvinogoneka, tsanangurai shanduko musati maiita uye ipai munhu waunotarisira nguva yekujaira. Alzheimer's Society inokurudzira kufunga kana mudziyo uchizove wakachengeteka kushandiswa ari oga, kana uchida kuiswa nenyanzvi, uye kana ungaita kuti rimwe basa repamba riome. Bvunzai munhu waunotarisira zvaanoona mushure meshanduko. Kana akatanga kudzivisa kamuri kana kurega kushandisa chinhu chaanokoshesa, gadzirisai urongwa.

Ongororai pamba zvakare kana maitiro, mafambiro kana kuona zvachinja, kana pakaitika chinhu chainge choda kukonzera tsaona. Kamuri yaishanda zvakanaka mwedzi wapfuura inogona kuda mwenje wakasiyana kana nzira yakajeka zvino. Munogona kuchengeta rondedzero pfupi yezvachinja nezvichiri kushanda, pane kuedza kugadzirisa imba yese kamwe chete.

## Ndiani anogona kubatsira nekugadzirisa pamba?

Nyanzvi yezvekubatsira pamabasa epamba inogona kuongorora mafambiro anoita munhu waunotarisira pamba uye kupa mazano emidziyo kana shanduko dzemabasa ezuva nezuva. Zvichienderana nedambudziko racho, nyanzvi yezvekurovedza muviri, mukoti wemunharaunda, mufamasiti kana nyanzvi yezvedzimba vanogonawo kubatsira. Alzheimer's Society inotaura kuti nyanzvi yezvekubatsira pamabasa epamba inogona kuwanikwa kuburikidza nachiremba, chipatara kana sevhisi yekuchengetwa kwevanhu yenzvimbo muUK; nzira dzinosiyana kune dzimwe nzvimbo. Pakuiswa kana kuchinjwa kwemidziyo yegasi, yemagetsi, yekuvaka kana yekuchengetedza moto, shandisai nyanzvi yemunzvimbo yakakodzera.

Kana muchirenda, bvunzai muridzi weimba kana anopa pekugara musati maita shanduko dzisingadzokeri kumashure. Sevhisi yemunzvimbo inobatsira vakuru kana vanhu vane hurema inogona kutsanangura ongororo kana rubatsiro ruripo. Kana dambudziko rakurumidza, gadzirisai njodzi iyoyo kutanga uye kumbirai rubatsiro rwenyanzvi pane chero chinhu chamusina chokwadi chekuchinja zvakachengeteka.

## Ndinofanira kuongorora pamba kakawanda sei?

Ongororai kana basa rajairika rava kuoma, mafambiro kana kuona kwachinja, kana pakaitika chinhu chainge choda kukonzera tsaona. Tangirai nekamuri kana maitiro ane chekuita neshanduko iyoyo. Haufaniri kupedzisa chinhu chese chiri parondedzero; chengetai zviri kushanda uye dzokerai kushanduko imwe inoshanda panguva. Kana munhu waunotarisira akambodonha, [guide to what to do after a fall](/caregiving/responding-after-a-fall-with-dementia/) yedu inotsanangura matanho ekutanga nekutevera.
