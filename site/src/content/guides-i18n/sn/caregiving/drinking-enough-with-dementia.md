Munhu anorarama nechirwere chekukanganwa anogona kukanganwa kunwa kana kusanzwa nyota. Chinwiwa chisiri pameso kana chakaoma kubata chinogonawo kupotsa kucherechedzwa. Shanduko diki pamaitiro ezuva nezuva dzinogona kuita kuti zvinwiwa zvive nyore kugamuchira, uku munhu iyeye achiramba aine simba rekusarudza.

[Zano reAlzheimer's Association nezvekudya nekunwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) rinokurudzira kupa mikombe midiki yezvinwiwa muzuva rose uye zvekudya zvine mvura mazviri. [Zano reDementia Australia nezvekudya kunovaka muviri](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/nutrition-and-dementia) rinokurudzirawo zvinwiwa zvajairika uye kuti zvive nyore kusvika. Peji rino rinopa mazano akajairika, kwete huwandu hwemvura hunofanira kunwiwa nezuva nemunhu mumwe nemumwe.

## Ndingaita sei kuti chinwiwa chionekwe uye chisvike zviri nyore?

Isai chinwiwa chinodiwa nemunhu iyeye paanogona kuchiona uye kuchisvika. Shandisai mukombe kana bhodhoro raajaira rinonzwa rakanaka kubata. Kana munhu iyeye achifarira kupisa kana kutonhora kwakati, kuravira kwakati kana mukombe wakati, chengetai izvozvo mupfungwa. Sarudzo imwe diki inogona kuva nyore kupfuura kumukumbira kuti asarudze kubva pazvinwiwa zvakawanda.

Ipai zvinwiwa sechikamu chemaitiro aatojaira, akadai senguva yekudya, yekuzorora kana yekushanya kunze. Mukombe mudiki unogona kunzwa uri nyore kupfuura girazi guru. Mupei chimwe chinwiwa gare gare pasina kuzviita bvunzo kana kumuyeuchidza kuti akanganwa.

Kana munhu iyeye achikwanisa kudurura kana kutakura chinwiwa ari ega, muregei azviite. Kana achida rubatsiro, bvunzai musati madurura kana kuisa mukombe muruoko rwake. Mukombe mutsva, chubhu yekunwira kana nzvimbo itsva zvinogona kunzwa zvisina kujairika, saka shandurai chinhu chimwe panguva uye chengetai zvinomushandira.

Pakubuda panze, torai chinwiwa chinodiwa nemunhu iyeye uye mupe panguva yekuzorora kana yekudya. Kana vanhu vakawanda vachibatsira, goveranai chinwiwa nemukombe zvaanofarira nemvumo yake. Chengetai chinwiwa paanosvika, asi bvunzai musati machifambisa kana kumupa.

## Ndezvipi zvinwiwa kana zvekudya zvandingapa?

Tangai nezvinwiwa zvinofarirwa nemunhu iyeye uye zvaanogona kunwa zvakachengeteka. Mvura isarudzo imwe; mukaka, muto, zvinwiwa zvakakanyiwa kana zvimwe zvinwiwa zvajairika zvinogonawo kuenderana nezvaanoda uye nehurongwa hwake hwekutarisirwa. Mimwe michero nezvimwe zvekudya zvinewo mvura mazviri. [Alzheimer's Association inonyora michero, muto, milkshake nezvinwiwa zvakakanyiwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) pakati pezvisarudzo zvine mvura yakawanda.

Hazvina basa kumanikidza chinwiwa chimwe kana munhu iyeye achiramba. Mupei chimwe chisarudzo chaajaira kana edzai zvakare gare gare. Bvunzai chiremba kana nyanzvi yezvekudya kunovaka muviri yemunhu iyeye musati mashandura hurongwa hwake hwekudya kana hwekunwa, kunyanya kana atova nemirayiridzo yezvehutano pamusoro pezvinwiwa.

## Ndinofanira here kuderedza zvinwiwa kuti ndideredze kuenda kuchimbuzi?

Musaderedze zvinwiwa kuti mudzivise tsaona kunze kwekunge nyanzvi yezvehutano yakupa hurongwa hwakakodzera munhu iyeye. [Alzheimer's Association inoyambira](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence) kuti kuderedza zvinwiwa kunogona kukonzera kushaikwa kwemvura yakakwana mumuviri nemamwe matambudziko. Munhu anoda rubatsiro rwekutsvaga kana kushandisa chimbuzi anogona kubatsirwa nerumwe rudzi rwerutsigiro. [Nhungamiro yedu yekutadza kuzvibata](/caregiving/toileting-and-incontinence-with-dementia/) inotaura nezvemaitiro echimbuzi uye kuvanzika.

Goverai kupiwa kwezvinwiwa muzuva rose pane kukumbira munhu iyeye kunwa zvakawanda kamwe chete. Tevedzerai mirayiridzo yezvehutano iripo uye bvunzai chiremba kuti huwandu hwakakodzera munhu iyeye hwakadii. Musavimbe nenhamba yakajairika inowanikwa paindaneti; zvinodiwa zvemvura zvinosiyana, uye vamwe vanhu vane hurongwa hwezvinwiwa hwakapihwa nachiremba.

## Ndezvipi shanduko dzandinofanira kucherechedza?

Cherechedzai zviri kusiyana nemaitiro ajairika emunhu iyeye. Anogona kunwa zvishoma zvikuru kupfuura zvamazuva ese, kusiya chinwiwa chaanofarira chisina kubatwa kana kuda rubatsiro rwakawanda kupfuura kare. Muromo wakaoma, dzungu, kuneta kusina kujairika kana shanduko pakuita weti zvinogonawo kuva zvikonzero zvekubvunza nyanzvi yezvehutano. Zviratidzo izvi zvinogona kuva nezvimwe zvikonzero, saka musazviongorora mega kuti munhu asina mvura yakakwana mumuviri.

Nyorei zvamaona uye nguva yazvakaitika. Sanganisirai kuti munhu iyeye anga achirwara here, ari munzvimbo inopisa kana aine dambudziko rekubata kana kumedza chinwiwa. [Alzheimer's Association inotaura kuti zviratidzo zvekushaikwa kwemvura yakakwana mumuviri zvinogona kuva zvakaoma kuona mumunhu anorarama nechirwere chekukanganwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster). Kana paine shanduko inongoerekana yaitika kana muine hanya, taurai nachiremba kana imwe nyanzvi yezvehutano.

## Ko kana kunwa kwaoma?

Kana munhu iyeye achikosora, achidzipwa, achichengeta zvekudya kana chinwiwa mumuromo, kana ava nedambudziko idzva rekumedza, mirai uye bvunzai nyanzvi yezvehutano yakakodzera kuti muwane zano. Musakobvise zvinwiwa, musashandure magadzirirwo ezvekudya kana kuedza nzira dzekumedza kunze kwekunge nyanzvi yakurudzira hurongwa ihwohwo kumunhu iyeye. [Nhungamiro yedu yenguva yekudya](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) inotaura nezvekuita kana paine matambudziko matsva ekutsenga kana kumedza.

Kana munhu iyeye aine muromo wakaoma, mazino anorwadza kana mazino ekubvisa anonzwa asina kugadzikana, mubvunzei zvaari kunzwa uye taurai nachiremba wemazino kana chiremba. Kurwadziwa mumuromo kunogona kuita kuti kunwa kuome. Onai [nhungamiro yedu yekuchengeta muromo](/caregiving/oral-care-with-dementia/) kuti muwane nzira dzakapfava dzekubatsira pakuchengeta muromo.

Kuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika kunoda rubatsiro rwekukurumidza rwezvehutano. [Zano reNHS nezvekuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) rinokurudzira kuti vanhu vasazviongorore vega. Shandisai rubatsiro rwekukurumidzira kana maitiro erubatsiro rwekuchimbidza anowanikwa kwamunogara.

## Ndingapa sei zvinwiwa pasina kumanikidza?

Pai chinwiwa, mobva mamu pa nguva yekuti asarudze. Anogona kuda kunwa gare gare kana kusarudza mukombe wakasiyana. Dzivisai kumutsiura, kutaurirana zvekumanikidza kana kumanikidza kunwa. Kana achiramba kazhinji, tarisai kana chinwiwa chiri nyore kusvika, kana achifarira kuravira nekupisa kana kutonhora kwacho, uye kana munhu iyeye akasununguka munzvimbo yaari.

Bvunzai munhu iyeye zvinomubatsira uye goveranai zvaanofarira nevamwe vanobatsira chete kana iye achibvumirana nazvo. Mukombe waajaira, nzvimbo yaakasarudza kana nguva yakadzikama zvinogona kuita kuti kunwa kuve nyore. Kana kunetseka kuchienderera mberi, bvunzai chiremba kana nyanzvi yezvekudya kunovaka muviri kuti muwane zano rakanangana nemunhu iyeye.

Chinangwa ndechekuita kuti zvinwiwa zviwanikwe nenzira inogamuchirwa nemunhu iyeye. Ipai zvinyoro nyoro, remekedzai sarudzo yake uye tsvakai zano renyanzvi kana zvaanoda zvachinja.
