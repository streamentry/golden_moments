Kana munhu ane dementia asingachakuzivei, nguva iyi inogona kurwadza uye kuvhiringidza. Munogona kushamisika kuti moti chii kana kuti mutsanangurezve ukama hwenyu here. Mamiriro ezvinhu ega ega akasiyana. Tangai nezvinoita sokubatsira munhu wacho kuti anzwe akachengeteka uye akasununguka, kwete nokumuedza kuti aone zvaanoyeuka.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association pamusoro pekurasikirwa nendangariro nekuvhiringika](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/memory-loss-confusion) rinosanganisira kusaziva vemhuri pakati peshanduko dzinogona kuitika. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) ne[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/how-dementia-changes-perception) vanotsanangura kuti munhu anogona kukuvhiringidzai nomumwe munhu waanoziva. Urwu rutungamiro rwakajairika; harugoni kukuudzai kuti nei shanduko yakati iri kuitika.

## Chii chingave chiri kuitika kana munhu wacho asingandizivi?

Dementia inogona kukanganisa ndangariro uye nzira iyo munhu anonzwisisa nayo zvaanoona. Mumwe munhu anogona kukufungirai sehama imwe kana kusava nechokwadi chokuti mune ukama hupi naye. Izvi hazvikuudzei chaizvo zvaanonzwa pamusoro penyu. Tarisai pakusununguka kwake nemaitiro aanoita panguva iyoyo.

Kuzivikanwa kunogonawo kukanganiswa nechiedza chisina kukwana, matambudziko ekuona kana ekunzwa, kurwara, mhedzisiro yemishonga, nzvimbo isingazivikanwi kana shanduko mumaitiro ezuva nezuva. Izvi zvinhu zvinogona kukonzera, asi hazvirevi kuti ndicho chikonzero chaicho. Kana shanduko yacho iri itsva kana ichiramba ichimushungurudza, udzai nyanzvi yake yezvehutano zvakaitika uye bvunzai kana achifanira kuongororwa.

Kana kamuri kacho kune rima kana kuine ruzha, edzai kuchinja chinhu chimwe chete: batidzai chiedza, deredzai ruzha rwuri kumashure kana endai kune imwe nzvimbo inozivikanwa. Bvunzai kana munhu wacho achida magirazi ake kana rubatsiro rwekunzwa kana achizvishandisa. Matanho aya anogona kubatsira, asi haangatsananguri nguva dzose dzaasingakuzivei.

## Ndingati chii panguva iyoyo?

Swederai pamunogona kuonekwa nomunhu wacho uye taurayi nezwi rakadzikama, rinozivikanwa. Taurai zita renyu neukama hwenyu zviri nyore, sezvamungaita kana muchimukwazisa rimwe zuva: “Mhoro, ndini Alex, mwanasikana wenyu.” Mozorora zvishoma moona maitiro aanoita.

- **Kana achiratidza kugadzikana,** endererai mberi nehurukuro yakajairika. Taurai zviri kuitika pari zvino, kana bvunzai kana angada kugara nemi.
- **Kana achiratidza kusava nechokwadi,** mumupe ruvimbo rupfupi: “Makachengeteka. Ndiri pano nemi.” Mupei nguva yokupindura.
- **Kana achiratidza kutya kana kukukumbirai kuti muende,** dzokerai shure mumupe nzvimbo. Musamumanikidza kumumbundira, musamumanikidza kuti akuzivei kana kuramba muchidzokorora mashoko anoita sokumushungurudza.
- **Kana achikudaidzai nerimwe zita,** munogona kuzvisuma kamwe chete musingazviiti sekumururamisa. Kana akaramba akagadzikana, endai kune imwe nyaya kana chiitiko chaanofarira.

[Gwaro rekukurukurirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) rinokurudzira kutaura muchidimbu, makadzikama uye kudzivisa mibvunzo yokuedza kuyeuka kana kupokana. Edzai kusabvunza kuti, “Munoyeuka kuti ndini ani here?” Mubvunzo uyu unogona kunzwika somuedzo uye ungasabatsira munhu wacho kukuzivai.

## Chiyeuchidzo chinozivikanwa chingabatsira here?

Dzimwe nguva chiyeuchidzo chinogona kubatsira munhu wacho kukuzivai, asi hazvishandi kumunhu wose. Edzai chiyeuchidzo chimwe chete panguva, zvinyoro, uye regai kana chichiwedzera kumanikidzwa.

- **Taurai zita renyu sezvamunowanzoita.** Inzwi rinozivikanwa kana kuzvisuma muchidimbu kungakwana.
- **Shandisai mufananidzo kana chinhu chamunogovana.** Kana munhu wacho achifarira mifananidzo, munogona kutarisa mumwe chete musingamukumbiri kuti adome mazita. [Gwaro redu rekuyeuka zvakapfuura](/activities/reminiscence/) rine mazano ekushandisa mifananidzo pasina kumubvunza mibvunzo.
- **Edzai chiitiko chamunowanzoita mese.** Rwiyo rwunozivikanwa, chinwiwa kana basa ringaita kuti nguva iyi ive nyore. Teverai maitiro omunhu wacho; zvakanaka kuchinja nyaya kana kupedza kushanya.
- **Deredzai kuvhiringika kwakakupoteredzai.** Tarisai kuti mukamuri mune chiedza chakakwana uye kuti munhu wacho ane magirazi ake kana rubatsiro rwekunzwa kana achizvishandisa. Nzvimbo ine runyararo inogona kubatsira kana vanhu vazhinji vari kutaura.

Musaramba muchiwedzera zviyeuchidzo kana munhu wacho achiri kusagadzikana. Tsananguro refu kana mifananidzo yakawanda inogona kumuremedza. Kuvapo makadzikama kungakwana; zvakanaka zvakare kuzorora moedza zvakare pane imwe nguva.

## Ndingaita sei kuti kushanya kuve nyore?

Kana munhu wacho achigara panzvimbo inochengeterwa vanhu kana achiona vabatsiri vakawanda, bvunzai vanhu vanoziva maitiro ake kuti anowanzo kunzwa akasununguka riini. Munogona kushanya panguva ine runyararo, kuenda nomumwe munhu waanoziva kana kuenda nechinhu chimwe chinozivikanwa. Idzi isarudzo dzokufunga nezvadzo, kwete hurongwa hunofanira kuteverwa nemhuri dzose.

Musati mashanya, sarudzai zvinokuitirai nyore imiwo. Munogona kuronga chiitiko chipfupi chimwe chete, kuenda nebhuku remifananidzo kana kungogara mese. Kana munhu wacho achiratidza sokusaziva kuti ndimi ani, munogona kuramba muchimubatsira nezvaanoda panguva iyoyo. Musaita kuti kushanya kuenderane nokuti ataura zita renyu here kana kuti kwete.

Zvinonzwisisika kunzwa kusuruvara, kurambwa kana kushungurudzika. Taurai nomumwe munhu wamunovimba naye, chipangamazano kana boka rinotsigira vanotarisira vamwe kana ruzivo urwu ruchikuremerai. [Gwaro rekuzvitarisira kwevanotarisira vamwe](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rine nzira dzokukumbira vamwe rubatsiro.

## Ndinofanira kukumbira rubatsiro rwezvehutano rinhi?

Kana kusaziva mumwe munhu kuri patani itsva, kuchiitika kakawanda kana kuchikonzera kushushikana, udzai chiremba womunhu wacho. Nyorei kuti zvinoitika riini uye kuti zvimwe zvinhu zvipi zvanga zvichiitika, zvakadai seshanduko pautano, pakurara, pachiedza, pakunzwa, pamishonga kana munzvimbo. Musamisa kana kuchinja mishonga muchishandisa rugwaro rwakajairika chete.

Kuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika kana shanduko inokurumidza pamaitiro kana pautano kunoda rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwezvehutano. [Rutungamiro rweNHS pamusoro pekuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) runoti tsvagai rubatsiro rwezvehutano pakarepo. Shandisai hurongwa hwekukurumidzira kana hwerubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwezvehutano munzvimbo inogara munhu wacho.

Kusazivikanwa kunogona kuomera vanhu vese vari vaviri. Chengetai mashoko enyu ari nyore, ipai chiyeuchidzo chete kana chichibatsira uye regai kusununguka kwemunhu wacho kutungamirire zvinoitika zvinotevera.
