Kuchengeta muromo inyaya yako wega. Munhu ari kurarama nechirwere chekuyeuka anogona kukanganwa tsika yakajairika, kuona bhurasho remazino richinetsa kushandisa kana kusanzwa zvakanaka kana mumwe munhu achipa rubatsiro. Chengetai tsika iyi yakadzikama uye neruremekedzo. Munhu anofanira kuita zvakawanda sezvaanokwanisa uye sezvaanoda kuita.

[Gwaro rekuchengeta mazino reAlzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dental-care) uye [mazano ekuchengeta mazino eDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/dental-care) anokurudzira kushandisa tsika yakajairika, mirairo mipfupi nerubatsiro runobva kuna chiremba wemazino kana matambudziko amuka. Peji rino ruzivo rwakajairika, harusi zano rachiremba wemazino kana hurongwa hwekuchengeta hwemunhu mumwe.

## Ndingachengeta sei tsika yezuva nezuva yekuchenesa muromo yakajairika?

Shandisai nguva nenzvimbo zvakajairika zvemunhu kana zvichiri kushanda. Isai bhurasho remazino, mushonga wemazino netauro paanotarisira kuzviwana. Tsanangurai zvamuri kuita musati matanga, mobva mapiwa chiratidzo chimwe chiri nyore panguva. “Tora bhurasho remazino” zviri nyore kutevera pane mirairo yakawanda yakareba.

Ipai munhu nguva yekuita danho rimwe nerimwe. Kana achikwanisa kuzvikwesha ega, muregei adaro. Kana chiyeuchidzo chakakwana, mirirai musati mapiwa rumwe rubatsiro. Vamwe vanhu vanogona kuona zviri nyore kugara pasi pesingi kana kushandisa bhurasho rine mubato wavanokwanisa kubata zvakanaka. Bvunza chiremba wemazino nezvesarudzo dziripo kana bhurasho rakajairika riri kunetsa kushandisa.

Musaite kuti kukwesha mazino kuve bvunzo kana makwikwi. Kana munhu akati kwete, akabvunda kana akatsamwa, imbomirai. Edzai zvakare gare gare, shandurai nzvimbo kana bvunza chiremba wemazino kuti akupei zano. Musamanikidza bhurasho mumuromo memumwe munhu kana kumubata kuti apedze basa.

Kana tsika yaimboshanda ikatanga kunetsa, cherechedzai kuti ndeipi nhanho iri kunetsa. Munhu anogona kunge asina chokwadi chekuti oitei nebhurasho, asingade mushonga mutsva wemazino, akaneta kana kuti ari kunzwa kusagadzikana mumuromo. Izvi zvinogona kuva zvikonzero, hazvirevi kuti mune dambudziko rakati. Chinyorwa chipfupi nezvenguva inoitika dambudziko rinogona kubatsira chiremba wemazino kukurukura matanho anotevera. Shandurai chinhu chimwe panguva mutsika iyi kuitira kuti zvinyatsooneka izvo munhu anoda.

## Ndingabatsira sei ndichichengetedza zvakavanzika?

Kumbirai mvumo musati mabatsira, kunyange kana munhu amboda rubatsiro kare. Muudzei zvamunoronga kuita uye mirirai mhinduro yake. Kana abvuma, chengetai gonhi rakavharwa, shandisai tauro kuchengetedza zvipfeko uye fukurai chete nzvimbo inodiwa. Bvumanai kuti ndiani anobatsira waanganzwa akasununguka naye.

Kana munhu achida rubatsiro rwemaoko, tsanangurai danho rimwe nerimwe musati maruita uye mirai kana aratidza kushushikana. Chiremba wemazino anogona kuratidza tsika yakachengeteka inoenderana nezvinodiwa nemunhu. Musaite zvekufungidzira pakubata muviri kana kushandisa nzira yamusina kuratidzwa nenyanzvi yakakodzera.

## Chii chandinofanira kuziva nezvemazino ekubatsira?

Chengetai mazino ekubatsira munzvimbo yakajairika yemunhu uye inozivikanwa zviri nyore. Bvunza chiremba wemazino kuti anofanira kucheneswa nekuchengetwa sei, uye kuti kukwana kwawo kunofanira kuongororwa here kana munhu achiratidza kusagadzikana. Kana mazino ekubatsira achipfekwa munzvimbo inogara vanhu vachichengetwa, nyorai chiratidzo pamudziyo wekuaachengeta kuitira kuti vashandi vakwanise kuaziva.

Musamanikidza mazino ekubatsira mumuromo memunhu kana aramba kana achiratidza kurwadziwa. Zino rekubatsira rakasununguka kana risingagadzike rinogona kuita kuti kudya kuome; bvunza chiremba wemazino zvekuita. [Gwaro redu rekudya](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) rinotaura nezvekugadzirira kudya kwakadzikama uye kuti kunetseka pakudya kunoda rini zano renyanzvi.

## Tingagadzirira sei kushanya kuna chiremba wemazino?

Bhukai panguva iyo munhu anowanzenge akazorora. Kana munhu abvuma, udzai hofisi yachiremba wemazino pachine nguva kuti angangoda nguva yakawanda, tsananguro yakadzikama kana rubatsiro kubva kumunhu waanovimba naye. Uyai nerondedzero yemishonga yaari kushandisa parizvino uye chinyorwa chipfupi chezvava kunetsa, zvakaita sekukwesha mazino, kutsenga kana kupfeka mazino ekubatsira.

Kumbirai boka rachiremba wemazino kuti ritsanangure zvichaitika danho rimwe panguva. Munhu waanoziva anogona kubatsira kuti anzwe akagadzikana, asi munhu wacho anofanira kuramba ari chikamu chehurukuro nesarudzo. Kana kuenda kuhofisi yakajairika kuchinetsa, bvunza chiremba wemazino nezvesarudzo dzekuongororwa dzinosvikika munharaunda yenyu.

## Vamwe vabatsiri vangachengeta sei tsika iyi ichienderana?

Kana nhengo yemhuri, mushandi anobatsira kumba kana vashandi vekumba inochengeterwa vanhu vachibatsira, bvumanai nemunhu nezvetsika yaanonzwa akasununguka nayo. Shandisai nzvimbo imwe chete yakajairika nechiratidzo chipfupi pazvinogoneka. Bvunza kana achida kuti munhu mumwe chete amubatsire nguva dzose.

Kana munhu abvuma, goverai chete ruzivo ruchabatsira mubatsiri anotevera: nguva yaanoda, zvaanokwanisa kuita ega, danho riri kumunetsa uye nguva yaanoda kuti rubatsiro rumire. Chengetai chero chinyorwa chakanyorwa chakavanzika uye tevedzerai nzira yakajairika yenzvimbo yekuchengeta. Tsika yakanyorwa igwara, haisi mvumo yekuenderera mberi kana munhu ati kwete. Zvaanoda zvinogona kuchinja, saka bvunzai zvakare uye chinjai zvinoenderana. Kana nzira dzakati wandei dzava kunetsa, tsanangurirai boka rachiremba wemazino zvamaona uye kumbirai zano rakakodzera munhu iyeye.

## Ndezvipi zviratidzo zvandinofanira kutaura kuna chiremba wemazino?

Kurwadziwa kwemuromo hakusi nyore nguva dzose kutsanangura. Kubata kumeso, kuramba kukwesha mazino, shanduko pakudya kana kurara zvakaoma zvinogona kuva zviratidzo, asi hazvirevi kuti pane dambudziko remazino. Udzai chiremba wemazino zvamaona uye kuti zvakatanga riini. Chiremba wemazino anogona kuongorora kana paine kurwadziwa, matambudziko emazihwa, mazino akaora kana mazino ekubatsira asisakwane zvakanaka.

Kana munhu aine dambudziko idzva pakutsenga kana kumedza, bata nyanzvi yehutano yavo kana chiremba wemazino. Musachinje magadzirirwo echikafu, musashandise mishonga yemuromo kana kuedza nzira dzekumedza muchishandisa chinyorwa chakajairika. Tsvagai rubatsiro rwekukurumidza pakurwadziwa kukuru, kuzvimba, kubuda ropa kana chimwe chinhu chinoda kurapwa nekukurumidza.

Chinangwa ndechekuva netsika inogamuchirwa nemunhu, nerubatsiro rushoma runodiwa. Chiyeuchidzo chakajairika, nzvimbo yakagadzikana kana kuedza zvakare gare gare zvinogona kukwana. Kana dambudziko rikaramba riripo, bvunza chiremba wemazino kuti ndeupi rubatsiro rwakakodzera munhu iyeye.
