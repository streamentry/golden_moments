Kupfeka chinhu chepauzima. Munhu anorarama nedementia anogona kuona kusarudza hembe, mabhatani kana matevedzero ematanho zvakaoma kupfuura kare. Anogonawo kuda zvakanyanya hembe dzaanoziva kana chimiro chaakati. Chinangwa ndechekubatsira kuti asununguke uye akwanise kusarudza, kwete kutora basa rese kana kumanikidza kuti apfeke sezvamunoda imi.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association nezvekupfeka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) uye [mazano eDementia Australia ekupfeka](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/getting-dressed) anokurudzira kupa nguva yakakwana, kuchengeta sarudzo dziri nyore uye kutsigira munhu kuita zvaanokwanisa. Peji rino rinopa mazano akajairika. Bvunzai chiremba nezvematambudziko matsva ekupfeka kana zvimwe zvine chekuita neutano.

## Ndingapa sei sarudzo ndisingatori basa rese?

Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti anoda kupfeka chii. Kana kusarudza muwadhirobe yakazara zvakaoma, ipai sarudzo mbiri dzaanoziva. Anogona kusarudza pakati pehembe yebhuruu neyegirini, kana pakati pejuzi nekadhigani. Mbiri ingori nzvimbo yekutangira, haisi mutemo. Kana munhu achida kusarudza kubva pahembe dzakawanda, teverai zvaanoda.

Rambai muchifunga zvaanowanzo farira. Mavara, machira uye hembe dzaanoziva zvinogona kuita kuti kusarudza kuve nyore. Musatsiva maitiro ake neamunofunga kuti ndiwo akanaka. Hembe dzisingaenderane hadzisi chikonzero chekukakavadzana. Kana munhu akasununguka uye hembe dzakakodzera mamiriro ekunze, remekedzai sarudzo yake.

Pamunotenga hembe, funga nezvekusununguka uye nezvezvinogona kuitwa nemunhu wacho. Machira akapfava, maumbirwo aanoziva uye zvekuzarura zvaanokwanisa kuvhura zvinogona kubatsira. Mabandi kana zvekuzarura zviri nyore zvinogona kuita nyore kune vamwe, asi dhizaini itsva inogona kunzwawo isingazivikanwe. Bvunza musati matsiva hembe dzaanofarira kana kuchinja maitiro aanoziva.

## Ndeapi matanho madiki anoita kuti kupfeka kuve nyore?

Sarudzai nguva panenge paine mukana wekuita zvishoma nezvishoma. Kumhanyirisa kunogona kuita kuti basa racho rinyanye kunetsa. Nzvimbo yakadzikama, ine chiedza chakanaka uye hembe dziri nyore kuona inogona kubatsira. Bvisai zvimwe zvinhu munzvimbo iyo munhu anopfekera, uye isai hembe dzasarudzwa padyo naye.

Rongai hembe maererano nekutevedzana kwaanoda kudzishandisa. Ipai chinhu chimwe panguva uye shandisai zano pfupi, sekuti “Heyi hembe yenyu.” Murairo wakakura sekuti “Pfekai” unogona kuva wakaoma kutevera. Mbozororai pakati pematanho kuti munhu ave nenguva yekunzwisisa nekupindura.

Regai munhu aite chikamu chega chega chaanokwanisa uye chaanoda kuita. Kana asina chokwadi nezvinotevera, muyeuchidzei kana kubvunza kana rubatsiro rungabatsira. Musamukwevera hembe kana kufambisa muviri wake pasina kubvumirana. Kana rimwe danho richimushungurudza, zororai mobvunza kuti chii chingaita kuti zvive nyore. Dzimwe nguva kuedza zvakare gare gare kwakakwana.

## Ndingachengetedza sei kuvanzika nekuremekedzwa kwake?

Bvunzai musati magara mumba imomo kana kubatsira nekuisa hembe. Munhu wacho anogona kunzwa akasununguka kupfeka ega, kana angada mumwe munhu ari pedyo asingabatsiri nemaoko. Teverai zvaanokumbira. Kana rubatsiro ruchidiwa, tsanangurai zvamunoronga kuita uye tarisai kuti achiri kunzwa akasununguka sezvamunoenderera.

Chengetedzai muviri wemunhu wakafukidzwa sezvaanoda paanenge achichinja. Vharai gonhi kana maketeni, uye musakurukure zvinhu zvepauzima panogona kunzwa vamwe vanhu. Kana nhengo yemhuri kana mubatsiri achibatsira, bvunzai kana munhu iyeye achigamuchirwa. Zvido zvinogona kuchinja kubva pazuva rimwe kuenda kune rimwe, saka bvunzai nguva dzose.

Kuyeuchidza munhu kuchinja hembe kunogona kumunyadzisa. Ipai hembe yakachena, yaanoziva musingatsiure kana kumugadzirisa pamberi pevamwe. Kana munhu achida kupfeka hembe yaanofarira kakawanda, hembe dzakafanana kana imwe yakafanana nayo zvinogona kubatsira kuti sarudzo irambe ichizivikanwa. Musashandure kuchinja hembe kuita bvunzo yekurangarira kana yekuzvimiririra.

[Gwaro redu rekugeza](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) rine mamwe mazano ekuchengetedza kuvanzika pane rimwe basa rekuzvichengeta.

## Ndedzipi hembe neshangu dzinganzwa dzakasununguka?

Kusununguka kunoenderana nemunhu wacho. Hembe dzakapfava, dziri nyore kupfeka uye dzaanoziva dzinogona kudiwa kupfuura hembe itsva ine zvakawanda zvekuzarura. Sarudzai shangu dzinoita kuti munhu anzwe akasununguka uye dzinobata pasi zvakanaka. [Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association rekupfeka](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/3e31dbc7-baf0-4f18-9c1b-7a8779af3d17/alzheimers-dementia-dressing-ts.pdf) rinopa zano rekutarisa kuti kureba kwehembe kwakakodzera kuitira kuderedza njodzi yekugumburwa.

Fungai nezvetembiricha yezuva nezvirongwa zvezuva iroro. Munhu anogona kufarira kupfeka akaturikidzana, asi tarisai kana achinzwa kupisa kana kutonhora zvakanyanya. Kana muchibuda kunze, torai hembe yekuwedzera inogona kupfekwa kana kubviswa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) inopa zano rekusarudza shangu dzakasununguka uye kufunga nezvemamiriro ekunze. Izvi zvinongova zvekubatsira pakukurukura nemunhu wacho, kwete mutemo wezvekupfeka.

## Ko kana kupfeka kwava kuoma zvitsva?

Kana kupfeka kwachinja zvinooneka, bvunzai munhu wacho kuti chii chiri kuoma uye taurai nachiremba wake. Dementia Australia inotaura kuti matambudziko ekuona, mhedzisiro yemishonga kana chimwe chirwere zvinogonawo kuita kuti kupfeka kuome. Chiremba anogona kubatsira kuongorora zvikonzero zvinogona kunge zviripo. Musamisa kana kuchinja mushonga zvichibva pachinyorwa chakajairika.

Kana munhu akangoerekana avhiringidzika, tsvakai rubatsiro rwechiremba nekukasika kuburikidza nevabatsiri vezvechimbichimbi vemunharaunda yenyu. [Nhungamiro yeNHS nezvekuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) inoti izvi zvinoda kutarisirwa nekukasika. Yambiro iyi ndeyekuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika, kwete kuchinja kunoitika zvishoma nezvishoma mumaitiro ekupfeka.

Cherechedzai kuti ndechipi chikamu chemaitiro chachinja: kusarudza hembe, kudziona, kubata zvekuzarura kana kuramba akatsiga. Goveranai zvamaona nemunhu wacho nachiremba wake, kana vachibvuma. Dambudziko idzva haritsanangure roga chikonzero charo. Musafungidzira kuti munhu ari kuita nemaune kana kuti haana hanya.

Kana muchibatsira kumba kana munzvimbo yekuchengeta, bvunzai munhu wacho kana achida kuti zvaanofarira zviziviswe kune mumwe mubatsiri. Chinyorwa chipfupi nezvehembe dzaanofarira, matevedzero aanoda uye zano rinoshanda zvinogona kubatsira vamwe kutevera maitiro mamwe chete. Chengetai chinyorwa ichocho pachivande uye chigadzirisei kana zvido zvemunhu zvachinja.

Munhu wacho anoramba aine masimba pamusoro pehembe dzake sezvaanoda. Ipai chete rubatsiro rwaanogamuchira, ipai nguva uye remekedzai sarudzo dzinoita kuti anzwe akasununguka.
