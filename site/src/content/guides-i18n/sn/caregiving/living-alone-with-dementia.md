Kugara ari ega hakuzvirevi, pachako, kuti munhu anoda rubatsiro rwakakura sei. Alzheimer's Association inotaura kuti vanhu vazhinji vanorarama neAlzheimer's vanoramba vachigara vari voga, kunyanya padanho rekutanga, nekuchinja kudiki, kungwarira nezvekuchengeteka nerutsigiro kubva kune vamwe. Mubvunzo unobatsira zvikuru ndewekuti: munhu uyu anoda kuramba achiita chii, uye chii chingaita kuti mabasa iwayo ave nyore?

Tangai hurukuro iyoyo kana mune nguva uye pasina dambudziko rinoda kugadziriswa nekukasika. Bvunzai kuti chii chinonzwa chakanaka, ndeapi tsika dzakakosha uye ndeupi rudzi rwerubatsiro rwaangafara kugamuchira. Urongwa hunoitwa naye hunogona kunyatsokodzera hupenyu hwake kupfuura runyorwa rwemabasa rwakarongwa nezvinonetsa zvemumwe munhu.

## Chii chingaita kuti hupenyu hwezuva nezuva huve nyore?

Shandisai rutsigiro rushoma rwakajairika runoenderana netsika dzemunhu wacho. [Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira rubatsiro runoshanda](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) rwakadai semakarenda, zvinyorwa nezviyeuchidzo zvezvinodiwa zvezuva nezuva.

- **Sarudzai nzvimbo imwe chete yezvinhu zvinokosha.** Chengetai makiyi, magirazi, chikwama nefoni munzvimbo imwe chete yakabvumiranwa. Tireyi pedyo nesuo kana sherufu yakanyorwa zita rinogona kuita kuti tsika iyi ive nyore kutevera.
- **Shandisai karenda rimwe chete kana nzira imwe chete yechiyeuchidzo.** Sarudzai karenda repamadziro, bhodhi jena, dhayari rebepa kana karenda yefoni iyo munhu wacho atojaira kushandisa. Chengetai nguva dzekusangana nezviitwa zvenguva dzose munzvimbo imwe chete panzvimbo pekuzviparadzira mumapurogiramu nezvinyorwa zvakawanda.
- **Itai kuti tsika dzezuva nezuva dzionekwe.** Kana basa richiwanzokanganwika, nyorai chinyorwa chipfupi kana kuisa chinhu chinodiwa pabasa iroro munzvimbo yakajairika. Chengetai matanho ari mapfupi uye muongorore pamwe chete kana akarega kubatsira.
- **Itai kuti mabasa anodzokororwa ave akajairika.** Kutengeserwa zvinhu zvenguva dzose, kushanyirwa nemunhu anobatsira pabasa repamba kana kurongwa kwekudya kungaita kuti zvisarudzo zvinofanira kuitwa nemunhu wacho ari ega zviite zvishoma.

Gwaro redu re[tsika dzezuva nezuva](/activities/daily-routine/) rine nzira dziri nyore dzekuita kuti zuva rive rakajairika pasina kurimisa zvachose. Chinangwa ndechekutsigira zvisarudzo zvemunhu wacho, kwete kuzadza awa imwe neimwe.

## Mhuri neshamwari dzingapa sei rubatsiro runobatsira?

Bvumiranai pahuwandu hudiki hwevanhu vanobata munhu wacho nguva dzose. Kushanya kupfupi kana runhare panguva inozivikanwa kunogona kubatsira kupfuura vanhu vazhinji vachionana naye nenzira isina kurongeka. Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti ndiani waanoda kuti abatanidzwe uye kuti mumwe nemumwe angaita chii.

Itai kuti rubatsiro rwamunopa ruve rwakananga. “Zvingakubatsira here kana ndikakuunzira zvekutenga China?” zvinonyanya nyore kupindura pane kuti “Ndizivise kana uchida chimwe chinhu.” Munhu waanovimba naye anogona kubatsira nekutenga zvinhu, zvekufambisa, zvekudya, kubhadhara mabhiri kana karenda yenguva dzekusangana. [Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kuronga rubatsiro](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) pamabasa epamba, zvekudya, zvekufambisa nezviyeuchidzo zvemishonga kana zvinodiwa izvozvo zvamuka.

Kana vanhu vakawanda vari kubatsira, bvumiranai kuti ndiani achabata basa rimwe nerimwe uye kuti vachazivisana sei nhau dzinokosha. Chengetai ruzivo ruchizivikanwa nevanhu vakasarudzwa nemunhu wacho chete, kunze kwekunge pane dambudziko rekuchengeteka rinoda rubatsiro nekukasika. Kana muchigara kure, onai [gwaro redu rekutarisira muri kure](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) kuti muwane nzira dzekurongeka nevanhu vanosarudzwa nemunhu wacho. [Gwaro rekukurukurirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) rine rumwe ruzivo rwekukurukura nezverutsigiro pasina kuita kuti zvive semuedzo.

## Munhu angaramba sei aine ukama nevamwe achigara ari ega?

Sarudzai zviitwa uye kuonana kunofadza munhu wacho. Zvingava runhare rwevhiki nevhiki, kushanya kwemuvakidzani, kudya pamwe chete munharaunda, kirabhu yakajairika kana kufamba nemunhu waanovimba naye. Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti ndeapi marongero aanonzwa achigamuchirika; musafunga kuti vashanyi vazhinji kana mapoka akawanda zvinogara zviri nani.

Nguva yekusangana nevamwe nguva dzose inogona kuita kuti vhiki ive nyore kuziva uye ichipa munhu wacho chimwe chinhu chaanotarisira. Kana kuenda kuchiitwa kwava kuoma, fungai nezverunhare, kufambiswa nemunhu waanovimba naye kana chiitiko chidiki chiri pedyo nepamba. Gwaro redu re[ukama nevamwe](/brain-health/social-connection/) rine nzira dzisina kumanikidza dzekuramba muchibatana.

## Tinoona sei kuti urongwa hunoda kuchinjwa?

Hapana runyorwa rumwe chete runosarudza kuti munhu anogona kugara ari ega here. Pane kudaro, cherechedzai shanduko chaidzo muhupenyu hwezuva nezuva: basa raaimboita zvakanaka rava kumunetsa kakawanda, kudya kuri kusiiwa, nguva dzekusangana dziri kukanganwika, kana kuti anoti rimwe basa rava kuoma.

Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti chii chaakaona. Ipai zano rekuchinja chinhu chimwe chete chinoshanda uye muone kana richibatsira. Kana paine zvinonetsa zvinodzokororwa nezvekudya, mishonga, mari, kufamba kana kuchengeteka pamba, bvunzai munhu wacho kana angada kutaura nachiremba wake kana nyanzvi yezvekuchengetwa kwevanhu. Kuongororwa kwakarongeka kunogona kubatsira kuona sarudzo dzerutsigiro; hazvirevi kuti munhu wacho anofanira kugamuchira zano rimwe nerimwe.

Kana munhu wacho achiratidza kusava nechokwadi nezverubatsiro, tangai nezvidiki. Anogona kusarudza kutengerwa zvekutenga vhiki nevhiki pane kuva nemunhu anouya kumba kuzomubatsira, kana rubatsiro rwemapepa asi kwete rwekuzvichengeta. Mubatanidzei pakusarudza munhu achabatsira uye kuti kushanya kuchaitika riini. [Gwaro rekuchengeteka pamba](/caregiving/home-safety/) rinotsanangura shanduko dzimba nemba kana chinonetsa chiri nezveimba pachayo.

## Ko kana munhu wacho asingadi rubatsiro?

Teererai chikonzero chake musati mapa rimwe zano. Anogona kunetseka nezvekuvanzika, mutengo, kurasikirwa nekutonga zvinhu zvake kana kuva nemunhu waasingazivi pamba. Bvunzai kuti ndeupi rudzi rwerubatsiro rwaanganzwa ruchigamuchirika, mobva maona kana kuedza kudiki kuchishanda. Vamwe vanhu vanosarudza chiyeuchidzo kana basa rekuita pamwe chete; vamwe vanoda rubatsiro kubva kumunhu mumwe waanovimba naye pane basa idzva.

Dzivisai kuita kuti kuonana kuve semuedzo. Runhare runogona kuva hurukuro, kwete runyorwa rwemibvunzo yekuti munhu wacho akadya here, akanwa mishonga here kana kuti akayeuka nguva yekusangana. Kana paine njodzi iri pedyo, fonerai mabasa ekukurumidzira emunharaunda menyu. Kana paine zvinonetsa zvinoramba zviripo, rambai muchibatanidza munhu wacho uye tsvakai zano kubva kuchikwata chinomuchengeta kana kubasa rerutsigiro remunharaunda.

Kugara ari ega kunogona kuramba kuchishanda nemusanganiswa unochinja wetsika dzenguva dzose, ukama nerubatsiro runoshanda. Ongororai urongwa pamwe chete nemunhu wacho, chengetai zvinonzwa zvichibatsira uye gadzirisai zvikamu zvisisakodzere.
