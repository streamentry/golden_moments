Chipfuwo chingava chikamu chemutoo wezuva nezuva weupenyu hwemumwe munhu nemhuri yake. Kupa chikafu, kufamba-famba kana kungogara pedyo nemhuka kunogonawo kuva chiitiko munhu waanoda kuramba achiita. Dementia ingaita kuti mamwe mabasa ave akaoma, asi izvo hazvirevi kuti mumwe munhu anofanira kutora zvoga.

[Mazano ekuchengeta chipfuwo aAlzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/how-plan-your-pet-care-after-diagnosed-dementia) anokurudzira kuronga mberi nezvekuchengeta chipfuwo uye kushandisa maitiro kana kuyeuchidza. [Mazano echiitiko aDementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/activities-people-dementia) anosanganisira zvipfuwo pakati pezviitiko zvinogona kuva nemusoro. Chinangwa apa rubatsiro runoshanda kumunhu nemhuka, kwete kutaura kuti zvipfuwo zvinorapa dementia.

## Tingaita sei kuti mutoo wekuchengeta chipfuwo uve nyore?

Tanga nekubvunza munhu mabasa aanofarira uye waanoda kuramba achiita. Mumwe munhu angada kupa chikafu kukati asi asingadi kuchenesa mubhedha wevakati; mumwe angada kufamba-famba nembwa nemunhu wemumhuri. Chengetai chirongwa chichinyatsoenderana nemunhu, chipfuwo uye rubatsiro ruripo.

- **Batanidzai kuchengeta nemutoo wamunotogara muchiita.** Isai chikafu mushure mekusarudza, ongororai mvura paunenge uchigadzira tii, kana kuti fambai nembwa mushure kwechirongwa cheterevhizheni chamunoziva. Chiratidzo chatova chikamu chezuva chingave chiri nyore kutevera pane zano idzva.
- **Chengetai zvigadzirwa panzvimbo imwechete yamunoziva.** Chengetai chikafu chechipfuwo, tambo kana bhurasho pamwechete pamunotarisira munhu kuti zvive, asi muchengetedze zvinhu zvinorema kana makemikari zvakachengetedzeka. Chinyorwa chipfupi kana tsamba chinogona kubatsira kana munhu achichida.
- **Ita kuti kuyeuchidza kumwe kuoneke.** Tsamba pfupi padyo nezvinhu zvechipfuwo kana chinyorwa pakarenda chingakwana. Musazadza imba nekuyeuchidza kunoita munhu kuti anzwe seari kutariswa kana kugadziriswa.
- **Siyai nguva yebasa iri.** Kufamba-famba kana kupa chikafu kungatora nguva yakareba kupfuura kare. Musakurumidza munhu kana achinakidzwa nenguva pamwe chete.

Kana vanhu vakawanda vachigoverana kuchengeta chipfuwo, shandisai rondedzero pfupi kuratidza ndiani anoita basa rimwe nerimwe zuva iroro. Itai pedyo nezvinhu kana kushandisa kuyeuchidza munhu waanotogara achifarira. Musagadzira maaramu kana tsamba dzakawanda apo chiratidzo chimwechete chakajeka chikakwana.

[Gwaro remutoo wezuva nezuva](/activities/daily-routine/) rine mamwe mazano ekubatanidza chiitiko nechikamu chamunoziva chezuva.

## Ndeipi rubatsiro shamwari kana hama dzingapa?

Ipa basa rakatarwa pane kungovimbisa. Shamwari ingatora chikafu chechipfuwo kamwe chete pamwedzi, muvakidzani angabatana nekufamba-famba, kana munhu wemumhuri angapinda kubvunzurudzwa nachiremba wemhuka. Kana munhu achida kuramba achipa chikafu kana kubhurasha chipfuwo, rongai rubatsiro rwakakomberedza basa iroro pane kuribvisa.

Bvumiranai ndiani achabatsira kana muchengeti wenguva dzose asipo kana munhu akarwara. Udzei mubatsiri iyeye kuti zvinhu zviri kupi, mutoo munhu waanofarira uye waanozotaura naye kana mhuka ikaonekwa ichirwara. Onai kuti mubatsiri iyeye akasununguka nemhuka uye mhuka yakasununguka naye.

Kana munhu aine rubatsiro kubva kumunopa basa rekuchengeta, bvunzai kuti kuchengeta chipfuwo kungapinda sei mushanya. Mvumo nemitemo yemumba zvakakosha: musafungira kuti munhu anobhadharwa anogona kufamba-famba, kupa chikafu kana kubata mhuka sechikamu chebasa rake. Bvumiranai nezvebasa iri nemunhu, anopa basa rekuchengeta uye munhu wemumhuri anotarisa chipfuwo.

## Tingaziva sei apo panodiwa rumwe rubatsiro?

Cherechedzai zviitiko zvakatarwa pane kutonga munhu nechikamu kana chibodzwa. Ndiro inogara isina chinhu, kufamba-famba kwakakanganikwa kunosiya mhuka ichinetseka, kana kuti munhu anoratidzika kuneta zvikuru mushure mekuchengeta mhuka zvinogona kuratidza kuti basa rimwe rinoda rumwe rubatsiro. Kuchinja mumaitiro emhuka, chokudya kana chitarisiko chayo ndizvo zvinogonawo kuva chikonzero chekuenda nachiremba wemhuka.

Taurai nemunhu zvaakaona. Angafarira kuyeuchidza, kufamba-famba pamwe chete kana mubatsiri webasa rimwe chete apo achichengeta mamwe maitiro. Kana nyaya yehutano, kufamba kana kuchengetedzeka ichikanganisa kukwanisa kwemunhu kuchengeta mhuka, bvunzai timu yake yehutano kana basa rekutsigira renzvimbo yenyu kuti vabatsire kutsvaga mhinduro.

Musamirire kusvika panjodzi kuti mufunge nezvekuchengetedza mudzoka. [Gwaro rekugara wega aine dementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/) rine nzira dzekubvumirana nezvekutarisa nekubatsira kunoshanda muchichengeta zvasarudzwa nemunhu zviri pamberi.

## Tinofanira kuronga sei nezvekuchinja kweramangwana?

Bvunza munhu waanovimba naye kuti achachengeta chipfuwo kana ari muchipatara, asipo pamba kana asisakwanisi kuita basa rimwe chete. Nyorai pasi mutoo wenguva dzose wemhuka, chikafu, nhamba dzechiremba wemhuka uye vanhu vakabvuma kubatsira. Chengetai ruzivo urwu pane pavanogona kuwana munhu nevabatsiri vaanovimba navo, nemvumo yemunhu.

Chirongwa ichi chingachinja nekufamba kwenguva. Chiongororei kana mutoo wemunhu, kufamba kana rubatsiro rwakachinja, kana kuti zvinodiwa nemhuka zvakachinja. Kana kufamba kuchidikanwa, batanidzai munhu kusarudza kuti mhuka ichagara kupi uye kuti angaramba achiita nayo basa kana zvichiita. Kuronga kare kunopa munhu wese nguva yakawanda yekufunga nezvemhinduro dzinodzivirira zvaanoda munhu uye kuchengetedzwa kwemhuka.

## Ko kana kushanya nemhuka kana chiitiko chisiri nyore?

Regai munhu nemhuka zvizviitire nemutoo wazvo. Ipai nguva pamwe chete chete kana zvese zvichiratidzika kunge zviri nyore. Kushanya kupfupi, kugara pedyo kana kutarisa mufananidzo wemhuka zvinogona kukwana. Mumise kana munhu akatanga kunzwa asina kugadzikana, mhuka ikaedza kuenda kure kana mhuka ikaonekwa ichinetseka. [Mazano echiitiko](/activities/ideas/) esaiti anoratidza dzimwe nzira dzaunoziva dzekushandisa nguva pamwe chete.
