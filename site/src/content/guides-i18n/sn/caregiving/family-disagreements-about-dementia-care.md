Kana mumwe wemumhuri ari kurarama nedementia, hama dzingasawirirana nezvekuchengeteka, rubatsiro kana ramangwana. Mumwe munhu angaona matambudziko ezuva nezuva; mumwe angashanya kashoma kana kurangarira munhu wacho sezvaaiva kare. Mari, nhambwe, basa nemabasa emumhuri zvinogona kuwedzera kumanikidzwa. Kusawirirana kunorwadza, asi hazvirevi zvega kuti mumwe munhu ane hanya shoma.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association nezvekusawirirana kwemhuri](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts) rinokurudzira kuteerera pasina kupa mhosva, kukurukura mabasa chaiwo erubatsiro uye kudzokera kuhurukuro sezvo zvinodiwa zvichichinja. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/news/how-do-i-navigate-changes-my-relationships) inotaurawo kuti mhuri dzinogona kusawirirana pamusoro pesarudzo dzerubatsiro uye dzingada rubatsiro kubva kune vamwe. Gwaro iri rinopa nzira yekukurukura; harigadzirise masimba epamutemo kana kusarudza kuti ndeupi rubatsiro rwakakodzera munhu mumwe.

## Tingaita sei kuti munhu arambe ari pakati pehurukuro?

Bvunzai munhu ari kurarama nedementia kuti chii chakakosha kwaari uye kuti anoda kubatanidzwa sei. Kuonekwa kuti ane dementia hakubvisi nzvimbo yake muhurukuro pamusoro pehupenyu hwake hwezuva nezuva. Bvunzai kuti ndiani waanoda kuti abatanidzwe uye kuti ndeapi mashoko aanonzwa akasununguka kugovera. Kana achida rubatsiro pakutaura zvaanoda, mupei nguva uye kurukurai sarudzo imwe panguva.

[Gwaro rematanho ekutanga mushure mekuonekwa kwedementia](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) rine mazano ekubatanidza munhu pakuronga ramangwana. Kana munhu wacho akasarudza mumwe munhu anomubatsira pakuita sarudzo, sanganisirai munhu iyeye muhurukuro muchiremekedza kuvanzika nezvido zvake. Kana vemumhuri vasingabvumirani nezvemasimba epamutemo ekuita sarudzo, bvunzai nyanzvi yemunzvimbo yakakodzera pane kufungidzira.

## Tinofanira kutanga sei hurukuro yemhuri?

Kana zvichikwanisika, sarudzai nguva yakadzikama pasati pava nedambudziko rinokurumidza. Sarudzai nyaya imwe chete yekukurukura, sekuti ndiani anogona kumutakura kana kuti mungaronga sei nguva inotevera yekuona chiremba. Rondedzero refu yesarudzo dzakaoma inogona kuita kuti zviomere munhu wese kuteerera.

Musati makurukura, nyora pasi zvamakaona uye zvamuri kufungidzira. Semuenzaniso, “Baba vakapotsa nguva mbiri dzekuona chiremba mwedzi uno” chinhu chakaonekwa chaicho. “Baba havachakwanisi kubata chero chinhu” imhedziso yakakura inogona kusaratidza zvavanokwanisa kana zvavanoda.

Ipa munhu mumwe nemumwe mukana wekutsanangura zviri kumunetsa. Teerera pasina kukanganisa, kupa mhosva kana kuenzanisa kuti mumwe nemumwe anobatsira zvakadii. Munhu anogara kure angaona zvido zvezuva nezuva; munhu ari kupa rubatsiro rukuru anogona kunge akaneta zvakanyanya. Zvakaitika izvi zvose zvakakosha, kunyange zvichitungamirira kumaonero akasiyana.

## Tinofanira kukurukura chii mumusangano?

Chengetai hurukuro iri pamusoro pezvinhu zvinoshanda. Rondedzero pfupi yezvinofanira kukurukurwa inogona kubatsira mhuri kuti ibve iine danho rinotevera rakajeka:

| Musoro | Mubvunzo wekukurukura |
| --- | --- |
| Zvido zvemunhu | Munhu anoda kuramba achiitei? Anoda kuti ndiani abatanidzwe? |
| Zviri kuitika iye zvino | Ndiani akaona chii, uye riinhi? Chii chinoda mazano echiremba? |
| Mabasa nezvinokwaniswa | Nderupi rubatsiro runodiwa? Ndiani ane nguva nezvaanokwanisa kuti aite basa rimwe nerimwe? |
| Matanho anotevera | Ndiani achaita chii, panosvika riinhi, uye mhuri ichatarisa zvakare riinhi? |

Kana nyaya ichida mazano ezvehutano, nyora pasi mubvunzo wacho mobvunza chikwata chinopa rubatsiro. [Gwaro rekuenda kunoona chiremba](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) rinogona kubatsira kugadzirira hurukuro iyoyo. Chengetai kusawirirana pamusoro pemagwaro epamutemo, mabhenefiti kana zvibvumirano kune nyanzvi dzakakodzera dzinoziva mitemo yemunzvimbo.

## Tingagovera sei mabasa erubatsiro zvakanaka?

Kugovera zvakanaka hazvirevi nguva dzose kuti zvakaenzana. [Alzheimer's Association inokurudzira kuita rondedzero yemabasa inosanganisira nguva, mari nesimba rinodiwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts). Zvadaro bvunzai kuti mumwe nemumwe anoda uye anokwanisa kuita chii zvechokwadi.

Vamwe vanhu vanogona kubatsira nekushanya nguva dzose kana rubatsiro rwepauzima. Vamwe vangakwanisa kuronga nguva dzekuona chiremba, kutakura munhu, kuendesa chikafu, mapepa kana kufona kwevhiki nevhiki. Hama inogara kure inogona kuronga mafoni, kutsvaga rubatsiro rwemunharaunda kana kuronga zvekushandisa, kana munhu wacho achibvuma. [Gwaro redu rekupa rubatsiro uri kure](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) rinopa urongwa hwakazara hwemabasa, ruzivo rutsva nekushanya.

Isai mabasa amabvumirana pakarenda imwe chete yakagoveranwa kana rondedzero yakanyorwa. Sanganisirai kuti ndiani ane basa, kangani uye ndiani wekufonera kana zvirongwa zvachinja. Ongororai rondedzero kana hutano hwemunhu, purogiramu yebasa kana kugona kupa rubatsiro zvikachinja. [Gwaro rekuzvichengeta kwemunhu anopa rubatsiro](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinotsanangura nzira dzekukumbira vamwe vanhu kuti vagovane rubatsiro.

## Tingaitei kana tikaramba tisingabvumirani?

Dzokerai kune zviri kuzikanwa, kune zvinodiwa nemunhu uye kumubvunzo uchiri kusara usina kupindurwa. Mungasagadzirisa zvinhu zvose pamusangano mumwe. Bvumiranai pane chiito chimwe chakachengeteka uye chinogamuchirika iye zvino, mozogadza nguva yekudzokorora sarudzo huru.

Kana hama dzisingabvumirani nezvekutamira kune imwe nzvimbo, danho rekutanga ngarive rekuunganidza ruzivo pane kuvhota hongu kana kwete. Bvunzai kuti ndirupi rubatsiro rusipo kumba, kuti munhu angada chii uye kuti ndeapi sarudzo dzinoda kudzidzwa nemhuri. Kushanyira imba yerubatsiro kana kukurukura nemupi werubatsiro wepamba kunogona kupa ruzivo kuhurukuro pasina kusungira munhu kuti atame. [Gwaro rerubatsiro rwekugara](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/) rine mibvunzo yekuenzanisa sarudzo.

Kana kusawirirana kukaramba kuripo, kumbirai munhu wechitatu akavimbika kuti abatsire. Zvichienderana nenyaya yacho, anogona kuva chiremba wemunhu, mushandi wezvemagariro evanhu, chipangamazano, murevereri kana mutungamiri wechitendero. Boka rerutsigiro rinogonawo kupa hama nzvimbo yekutaura nevanhu vakatarisana neshanduko dzakafanana. Mazano enyanzvi anonyanya kukosha pamibvunzo yezvehutano, yepamutemo kana yezvemari.

Kana kusawirirana kwemhuri kuchikanganisa kuchengeteka kana rubatsiro rwemunhu, batai basa remunharaunda rakakodzera kuti mubatsirwe. Musasiye munhu ari pakati pehondo. Kana paine njodzi iri pedyo, shandisai mabasa ekukurumidzira emunzvimbo.

Mhuri hadzingagari dzichibvumirana nguva dzose, uye hurukuro imwe ingangogadzirisa nyaya yacho. Ramba uchiteerera munhu, itai zvitsidzo zvakajeka uye kumbirai rubatsiro mumwe wehama asati atakura zvose ari oga.
