Chimbichimbi chinogona kukanganisa maitiro anozivikanwa uye kuita kuti zvinyanye kuomera munhu anorarama nedementia kunzwisisa zviri kuitika. Chirongwa chinogona kubatsira munhu wacho nevabatsiri vake kuita zvinhu pasina kumanikidzwa kwakanyanya kwekupedzisira. Chinofanira kuenderana nezvinodiwa nemunhu wacho, nzvimbo yaanogara uye njodzi dzinogona kuitika munzvimbo iyoyo.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association rekugadzirira zvechimbichimbi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster) rinokurudzira kugadzirira mashoko, zvinhu zvinodiwa uye rutsigiro rwekutsigira. [Gwaro reFEMA revakuru nevane hurema](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_hm-preparedness-for-older-adults_english.pdf) rinosimbisawo kutaurirana, zvekufambisa, mishonga uye midziyo inobatsira. Aya mashoko ekuronga akajairika; teverai mirayiridzo yechimbichimbi yenzvimbo.

## Ndingaita sei chirongwa chinoenderana nemunhu wacho?

Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti chii chingamubatsira kunzwa akachengeteka uye kuti ndiani waanovimba naye. Kana zvichibvira, muisei pakusarudza nzvimbo yekusangana, vanhu vekufonera panguva yechimbichimbi uye zvinhu zvinozivikanwa zvekutora. Chengetai mirayiridzo iri mipfupi uye yakajeka. Chirongwa chinogona kuchinja sezvo maitiro, utano kana kugona kufamba kwemunhu wacho zvichichinja.

Nyora vanhu vanopfuura mumwe chete vanogona kubatsira. Munhu anotarisira nguva dzose anogona kunge ari kubasa, asipo pamba kana akabatwawo nechimbichimbi chimwe chetecho. Bvumiranai kuti ndiani achafonera ani, ndiani anogona kupa chokufambisa uye ndiani anogona kutarisa munhu wacho kana zvirongwa zvachinja. Ipai mubatsiri wega wega kopi yemashoko aanoda.

Fungai nezvezvinhu zvinogona kukanganisa rutsigiro rwemazuva ese rwemunhu wacho. Kana achigara mufurati, achipinda chirongwa chemasikati kana achiwana rubatsiro pamba, bvunzai kuti basa iroro rinobata sei kutama, kudzima kwemagetsi kana kuvharwa. Zivai kuti ndiani achazivisa mhuri uye kuti zvinodiwa pakutsigira munhu wacho zvichazadziswa sei.

## Ndeapi mashoko nezvinhu zvandinofanira kuchengeta zvakagadzirira?

Gadzirai bhegi diki rinotakurika zviri nyore. Isai vanhu vekufonera panguva yechimbichimbi vazvino, rondedzero yemishonga, mashoko akakosha ezveutano, chaja yefoni, magirazi akawedzera kana zvinobatsira kunzwa kana zvichidiwa, mbatya dzinoenderana nemamiriro ekunze uye chinhu chinozivikanwa chinonyaradza. Wedzerai zvinhu zvekufamba kana zvekutaurirana zvinoshandiswa nemunhu wacho. Tarisa pano neapo kuti zvinhu zvacho zvichiri zvemazuva ano.

Chengetai magwaro anokosha panzvimbo yamunogona kuasvikira nekukurumidza, asi chengetedzai mashoko epachivande. Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti ndezvipi zvinhu zvaanoda kugoverwa uye naanani. Mubatsiri angada zita remunhu wacho, nzira yaanosarudza kutaurirana nayo, mashoko akakosha evanhu vekufonera uye mashoko emishonga, asi haadi magwaro ese epachivande.

Zvinhu zvakanakisa ndezviya zvinoshandiswa nemunhu uyu chaizvo. Bhachi raanofarira, mufananidzo, bhuku kana chimwe chinhu chidiki chinozivikanwa chinogona kunyaradza kupfuura zvinhu zvakawanda zvaasingazive. Chengetai bhegi riri nyore kuti mumwe munhu aritakure kana kurisimudza zvakachengeteka.

## Chirongwa chakanyorwa chinofanira kuti chii?

Chirongwa chipfupi zviri nyore kugovera nemubatsiri wekutsigira kupfuura zvinyorwa zvirefu. Nemvumo yemunhu wacho, isai:

- **Ndiani wekufonera.** Nyora vabatsiri vekutanga nevekutsigira, vanopa rubatsiro kumunhu wacho uye nzira yekusvika kune mumwe nemumwe. Taurai kuti ndiani achazivisa vamwe kana mhuri ikatama.
- **Matauriro ekutaurirana.** Nyora zita rinosarudzwa nemunhu wacho, kuti anonzwisisa sei mashoko zviri nani, uye mazwi kana maitiro anozivikanwa anogona kumubatsira kunzwa akatevedzana nezviri kuitika.
- **Mafambiro ekuita.** Nyora sarudzo yekufambisa uye midziyo yekufamba kana yekutaurirana yaanoshandisa. Bvunzai mupi werubatsiro kana hofisi yechimbichimbi yenzvimbo nezvesarudzo dzinowanikwa zviri nyore chimbichimbi chisati chaitika.
- **Kwekutsvaga zvinhu zvinokosha.** Nyora kuti bhegi nerondedzero yemishonga yazvino zvakachengetwa kupi. Chengetai magwaro epachivande munzvimbo yakachengeteka, uye goverai chete mashoko anodiwa nemubatsiri wega wega.
- **Zvinoitwa nebasa rerutsigiro.** Kana munhu wacho achiwana rubatsiro kunze kwemba, nyora munhu wekufonera akataurwa uye panowanikwa chirongwa chechimbichimbi chebasa iroro chazvino.

Tarisa kuti mubatsiri wekutsigira anoziva kuti chirongwa chiri kupi uye mashandisirwo acho. Chengetai kopi yepepa iripo kana magetsi kana foni zvisingawanikwe, asi musaisa mashoko ezveutano akavanzika panzvimbo panogona kuverengwa nevashanyi.

## Ndezvipi zvandinofanira kuziva nezvekuvanda kana kutama?

Zivai kuti mirayiridzo yechimbichimbi yenzvimbo inoburitswa kupi uye kuti yambiro dzinosvika sei kumba. Sarudzai kwekuenda kana vakuru vane masimba vakakurudzira kutama, uye kuti munhu wacho achasvika sei ikoko. Kana munhu wacho achishandisa wiricheya, mudonzvo wekufamba, okisijeni kana mimwe midziyo, bvunzai mupi werubatsiro akakodzera kuti ichatakurwa sei uye kuti ndezvipi zvimwe zvinhu zvinodiwa zvingave zvakakodzera.

Hapana mutemo mumwe chete unoshanda kune vese wekuti mugare here kana muende. Mhando yechimbichimbi, zvinodiwa nemunhu wacho uye mirayiridzo yevakuru vane masimba enzvimbo zvese zvakakosha. Bvunzai hofisi inotarisira zvechimbichimbi yenzvimbo kana mupi werubatsiro kuti mungaronga sei zvekufambisa nenzvimbo yekuvanda zvinowanikwa zviri nyore. Musavimbe negwaro iri kuita sarudzo panguva yechimbichimbi chakati.

Kana imba inochengeta vanhu kana chirongwa chemasikati chichibatanidzwa, kumbirai chirongwa chayo chechimbichimbi chakanyorwa. Zivai kuti ndiani ane basa remunhu wacho, kuti mhuri ichawana sei mashoko matsva uye kuti chii chinoitika kana zvirongwa zvekumutora kana zvekufamba zvachinja. [Gwaro redu rekuchengeteka pamba](/caregiving/home-safety/) rinotaura nezvenjodzi dzemazuva ese mukati nekunze kwemba.

## Ndingatsigira sei munhu wacho panguva yechimbichimbi?

Garai nemunhu wacho kana zvakachengeteka kuita saizvozvo. Tsanangurai zviri kuitika nemitsara mipfupi, yakadzikama uye dzokororai mashoko acho kana zvichidiwa. Chengetai maitiro anozivikanwa pazvinogoneka, uye udzai vanopindura kuti munhu wacho anosarudza kudaidzwa sei uye chii chinomubatsira kutaurirana.

Kana munhu wacho akatanga kunetseka, deredzai ruzha rwakawedzera uye deredzai vanhu vari kutaura panguva imwe chete. Ipai chinhu chinozivikanwa kana zvakachengeteka uye chiripo. Dzivisai kukakavadzana kana kupa tsananguro refu apo munhu wese ari pasi pekumanikidzwa. Teverai mirayiridzo yevashandi vechimbichimbi nevashandi vanopa rubatsiro.

Udza vashandi vekurapa nezveshanduko ipi neipi inongoerekana yaitika mumaitiro, kutaurirana kana utano hwemunhu wacho. [NHS inoti kuvhiringika kunongoerekana kwaitika kunoda rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwekurapa](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Musaona shanduko itsva kana yakakura sekungova chikamu chedementia.

## Ndingachengeta sei chirongwa ichi chichibatsira?

Ongororai rondedzero yevanhu vekufonera, mashoko emishonga nemidziyo kana gadziriro dzerubatsiro dzachinja. Tarisa mazuva ari pazvinhu uye vandudzai chirongwa mushure mekutama kana kana paine chido chitsva chekufamba. Bvunzai munhu wacho kana chirongwa chichiri kumunyaradza uye kuti ndiani waanoda kuti abatanidzwe.

Vanotarisira vanodawo rutsigiro rwekutsigira. [Gwaro rekuzvichengeta remunhu anotarisira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rine mazano ekukumbira vamwe vanhu kuti vagovane rutsigiro. Chirongwa chiri nyore chine vabatsiri vashoma vamunovimba navo chinogona kuva nyore kushandisa kupfuura rondedzero refu isina kumbotariswa nemunhu.

Chinangwa ndechekugadzirira zvakakwana kuti mukwanise kuita chimwe chinhu, muchichengeta munhu wacho achibatanidzwa uye akasununguka. Teverai mirayiridzo yechimbichimbi yenzvimbo uye bvunzai nyanzvi dzakakodzera nezvezvinodiwa zvakanangana nemunhu wacho.
