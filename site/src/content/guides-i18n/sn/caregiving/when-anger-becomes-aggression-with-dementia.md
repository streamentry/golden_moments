Kutsamwa kunogona kushanduka kuva kushevedzera, kusunda kana kurova, uye izvi zvinogona kuvhundutsa munhu wese ari pedyo. Maitiro aya anogona kunge asiri ekuda nemaune. Hazvirevi kuti munhu wacho ane chinangwa chekukuvadza mumwe. Tangai nekuita kuti mamiriro acho ave akachengeteka, mozoona kuti munhu wacho angave achida chii.

## Ndoitei kana kutsamwa kukava hasha?

Misai zvamanga muchiita, dzokerai kumashure uye shandisai mutsara mupfupi, wakadzikama. [Alzheimer’s Association inorayira vanochengeta kuti vatarise manzwiro, vataure zvishoma nezvishoma uye vaderedze zvinokanganisa pfungwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Kana muri munjodzi iri pedyo kana paine munhu akakuvara, endai kunzvimbo yakachengeteka uye batai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwemunzvimbo menyu.

- **Misai zviri kuitika kana zvichikwanisika.** Kana kugeza, kupfeka, kushanya kana rimwe basa richiita seriri kugumbura munhu wacho, misai. Zvinogona kumirira kunze kwekunge paine njodzi iri pedyo kana chinodiwa chehutano.
- **Siyai munhu aine nzvimbo.** Dzokerai kumashure uye chengetai nzira yakajeka yekuti mumwe nemumwe abude. Musamuvharira, musamudzivisa, musamusundire pedyo kana kumubata asingatarisiri.
- **Chengetai inzwi renyu rakadzikama uye mashoko enyu mapfupi.** Munogona kuti, “Ndinoona kuti makatsamwa. Ndichakupai nzvimbo.” Musapokane naye, musatyisidzira kana kumanikidza tsananguro.
- **Endesai vamwe kure.** Mwana, mushanyi kana mubatsiri anogona kuenda kune rimwe kamuri munhu wacho achidzikama. Munhu mumwe akadzikama ari kutaura anogona kuva nyore pane vanhu vakawanda vari kuedza kubatsira.
- **Budai kana pakaitika mhirizhonga yemuviri.** Endai kunzvimbo yakachengeteka uye kumbirai mumwe rubatsiro. Musaedza kubata munhu wacho nechisimba.

[Alzheimer’s Society inokurudzira kudzokera kumashure, kusabata munhu nechisimba kana kumubata kamwe kamwe, uye kubuda kunotsvaga rubatsiro kana pakaitika mhirizhonga yemuviri](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). [Alzheimer’s Association inorayirawo kukumbira mvumo, kuderedza zvinomutsa pfungwa uye kusavharira kana kubata munhu nechisimba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Shandisai nhamba yechimbichimbi yemunzvimbo menyu kana paine njodzi iri pedyo; musamirira kuti chiitiko chipfuure kana paine munhu angakuvara.

## Chii chingave chiri kukonzera maitiro aya?

Panogona kuva nezvinhu zvinopfuura chimwe chete, uye munhu wemumhuri haakwanisi kuzviongororera chikonzero acho. Alzheimer’s Association inotaura kusagadzikana kwemuviri, kushushikana kwemunzvimbo uye matambudziko ekutaurirana sezvimwe zvezvinogona kukonzera.

Fungai nezvezvakaitika nguva pfupi shanduko isati yaitika:

- Munhu wacho anga akaneta, ane nzara, ane nyota, achipisa kana kutonhora, kana asina kugadzikana here?
- Basa racho rakanga rakaoma, richiitwa nekukurumidza kana risingazivikanwi here? Pane munhu akaramba achienderera mberi kunyange munhu wacho aratidza kushushikana here?
- Paiva neruzha rukuru, vanhu vakawanda vachitaura, zvinhu zvakavhiringika kana nzvimbo isingazivikanwi here?
- Munhu wacho ainetseka kuona kana kunzwa zvaiitika here?
- Pane mushonga, tsika yezuva nezuva, muchengeti kana mamiriro ehutano akachinja nguva pfupi yadarika here?

[Alzheimer’s Society inokurudzira kutsvaga maitiro anodzokororwa uye kufunga kuti mamiriro acho angave ainzwika sei kumunhu wacho](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Mibvunzo iyi ndeyekubatsira kucherechedza, haina humbowo hwekuti chimwe chinhu chakonzera maitiro acho. Musaedza kuongorora, kubata nechisimba kana kupa munhu wacho mushonga mega.

## Ndingaderedza sei mukana wekuti zviitikezve?

Shandisai zvamakaona kugadzirisa matauriro amuchaita nguva inotevera. Izvi zvinogona kubatsira, asi hazvivimbisi kuti maitiro acho haazoitikezve. Edzai shanduko imwe panguva kuti zvive nyore kuona kana munhu wacho achiita seakagadzikana.

- **Sarudzai nguva yakadzikama.** Kana basa richiwanzonetsa kana munhu wacho akaneta, bvunzai kana imwe nguva ingashanda zviri nani.
- **Itai kuti nzvimbo ive nyore.** Dzimai ruzha rwekumashure, deredzai huwandu hwevanhu vari mukamuri uyeunganidzai zvamunoda musati matanga.
- **Tsanangurai danho rimwe uye mirirai.** Swederai kubva kumberi, taurayi zvamuri kuita, kumbirai mvumo musati mabatsira uye ipai munhu wacho nguva yekupindura.
- **Mirai kana paine zviratidzo zvekushushikana.** Kuramba, kuzvibvisa, chiso chakawomarara kana inzwi rakakwira zvinogona kuva chiratidzo chekuti mumire. Musaona kupedza basa sekunyanya kukosha pane kugadzikana kwemunhu.
- **Goverai hurongwa huri nyore.** Kana vanhu vakawanda vachichengeta, bvumiranai pazviratidzo zvinoreva “mira,” mutsara wakadzikama wekushandisa uye wekubata kana maitiro acho akachinja.

Kuti muwane mazano ane chekuita nebasa chairo, onai nhungamiro dzedu pamusoro pe[kugeza](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) uye [kupfeka](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/). Nhungamiro ye[mazano ekutaurirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) inotsanangura nzira dzekutaura nekuteerera pasina kuwedzera kumanikidzwa.

## Ndingaitawo chii kana mamiriro acho adzikama?

Ipai rutsigiro uye dzokerai kubasa rakajairika, risingaremi kana munhu wacho achida izvozvo. Musamutsiura, musapa mhosva kana kumuranga. Alzheimer’s Society inocherechedza kuti munhu wacho anogona kuramba achinzwa kusagadzikana kunyange maitiro acho apfuura, kunyange asingayeuki zvakaitika.

Kana muchida kunzwisisa maitiro anodzokororwa, nyorai zvipfupi, muchivanzika, pamusoro pezvakaitika nguva pfupi zvisati zvaitika, zvamakaona, kuti zvakatora nguva yakareba sei uye zvinoita sezvakabatsira. Chengetai zvinyorwa izvi zviri zvechokwadi chete uye goverai neboka rinotarisira munhu wacho kana zvakakodzera. Musatora vhidhiyo kana kurekodha munhu wacho kana kugovera ruzivo rwakavanzika pasina mvumo.

Kana basa richiwanzokonzera kushushikana, shandurai nguva kana nzira yamunoita nayo, kana kumbirai mumwe munhu wamunovimba naye kuti abatsire. Munogona kugamuchira kuramba uye muedzezve gare gare. Nhungamiro yedu pamusoro pe[zvekuita kana munhu achiti kwete](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) inopa dzimwe nzira dzekumira pasina kumanikidza.

## Ndinofanira kutsvaga zano rachiremba riini?

Batai chiremba chemunhu wacho nezvehasha itsva, dzinoerekana dzatanga, dzinodzokororwa kana dziri kuwedzera kuipa. [Alzheimer’s Association inorayira kuongororwa nachiremba kana maitiro achichinja, kunyanya kana akatanga kamwe kamwe](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Marwadzo, kurwara, kurara, nzvimbo yakapoteredza nemishonga zvinogona kuda kuongororwa. Chiremba anogona kuongorora mamiriro emunhu wacho.

Tsvagai rubatsiro rwekukurumidza rwemunzvimbo menyu kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringika, akakuvara kana achiita seasina kunyatsogadzikana pahutano. Kana imi kana mumwe munhu muri munjodzi iri pedyo, batai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwemunzvimbo menyu. Musamirira kunyorera zvinyorwa kana kuedza kunzwisisa chikonzero kutanga.

Kuchengeteka kwenyu kwakakoshawo. Taurai nemumwe munhu wemumhuri wamunovimba naye, chiremba kana rubatsiro rwedementia rwemunzvimbo menyu kana zviitiko izvi zvichiramba zvichiitika kana muchinzwa kutya. Kumbirai hurongwa hunodzivirira munhu wacho nevanhu vakamupoteredza. Makakodzera rubatsiro; hamufaniri kutarisana nazvo mega.
