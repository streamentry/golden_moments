Sarudzo dzemari dzinokanganisa rusununguko nemararamiro ezuva nezuva emunhu. Kana rimwe basa rava kuoma, tangai nebasa iroro pane kufungidzira kuti mumwe munhu anofanira kutora zvese. Bvunzai munhu kuti anoda rubatsiro rwakaita sei uye muvachengete vachibatanidzwa.

Gwaro iri rinopa ruzivo rwakajairika kune vanoverenga vari muU.S. Sarudzo dzebhanga nemitemo yepamutemo zvinosiyana nenzvimbo. [Gwaro reAlzheimer's Society rekutarisira mari](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) rinotsanangura nzira dzekutsigira munhu muchichengetedza basa ravo. [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) inotsanangura kuti rubatsiro rusina kurongwa, trusted contacts, nemvumo yepamutemo zvinhu zvakasiyana.

## Ndingapa sei rubatsiro ndisingatore zvese?

Bvunzai munhu kuti ndeipi nyaya yemari yaangada kubatsirwa pairi. Vanogona kuda kuongorora bhiri mese, kuronga tsamba, kurangarira zuva rekubhadhara kana kutarisa chikumbiro chisingatarisirwi. Munhu anogona kuda rubatsiro pane mamwe mabasa uye achida kuita mamwe ega.

- **Bvunzai musati maita.** Edzai kuti, “Ungade here kuti tiongorore bhiri iri tese?” Ipai munhu nguva yekusarudza.
- **Tarirai pabasa rimwe chete.** Kana munhu achibvumirana, ongororai mari yacho, zuva rekubhadhara uye sarudzo dzekubhadhara mese. Regai vaite sarudzo dzavanoda kuita.
- **Itai chinyorwa chipfupi mese.** Rondedzero pfupi yemabhiri, mazuva ekubhadhara kana matanho amakabvumirana inogona kubatsira mese kuona zvakatoitwa.
- **Bvunzai bhanga nezvesarudzo dzarinaro.** Rinogona kuva nenzira dzinoita kuti kubhadhara kana kutarisa mafambiro eakaundi kuve nyore. Bvunzai kuti sarudzo imwe neimwe inobvumira chii musati mashandura akaundi.
- **Dzokai mutarise zvakare gare gare.** Zvido zvemunhu zvinogona kuchinja. Chengetai rubatsiro rwuchisvika pane zvavanoda uye zvavanoda izvozvi.

CFPB inotsanangura rubatsiro rusina kurongwa sekutarisa mabhiri nekuronga mashandisirwo emari nemumwe munhu, asi munhu iyeye achiri ndiye anopa mvumo yekubhadhara. Izvozvo zvinogona kusiyana nekurega mumwe munhu achibvisa mari kana kuita mabhadharo. Musagovana mapassword, maPIN kana makodhi ekuchengetedza enguva imwe chete senzira pfupi.

## Trusted contact inobvumirwa kuita chii?

MuUnited States, chinhu chetrusted contact chinogona kuita kuti sangano rezvemari rifonere mumwe munhu mune dzimwe nguva, sekunge paine kunetseka nezvekubiridzirwa kungangoitika. CFPB inoti kudoma trusted contact hakupi munhu iyeye mukana wekuwana akaundi kana mvumo yekufambisa mari. Chinhu ichi chinogona kunge chisipo paakaundi dzese, saka bvunzai bhanga kana sangano rezvemari kuti chinoshanda sei.

| Rudzi rwerubatsiro | Zvarinoreva |
|---|---|
| Kuongorora mabhiri mese | Munhu nemubatsiri vanoongorora mabhiri pamwe chete; munhu ndiye anosarudza uye anopa mvumo yekubhadhara. |
| Trusted contact | Sangano rezvemari rinogona kubata munhu akasarudzwa mune dzimwe nguva. Zita chete haripi mukana wekuwana akaundi. |
| Mvumo yeakaundi kana yepamutemo yakarongeka | Mvumo yebhanga, power of attorney kana gadziriro yakafanana inogona kubvumira kubhadhara. Simba nemabasa ayo zvinoenderana neakaundi nemutemo wemunharaunda. |

Musati mashandura mukana wekuwana akaundi kana muridzi wayo, bvunzai bhanga nenyanzvi yepamutemo yemunharaunda yakakodzera kuti vatsanangure sarudzo nenjodzi dzacho. Kuonekwa kuti mune dementia chete hakukuudzei kuti munhu anogona kubata mabasa api emari kana kuti anoda kuita sarudzo dzipi. Vachengetei vari muchikamu chesarudzo sezvavanoda. Kana vachida kuti mumwe munhu avamiririre, bvunzai kuti izvi zvingaitwa sei zviri pamutemo pane kushandisa login yemumwe munhu.

## Ndezvipi shanduko dzandinofanira kutarisa?

Bhiri rakasabhadharwa kana kutenga kusina kujairika kunogona kuva nezvikonzero zvakasiyana. Izvi hazvirevi kuti munhu haachakwanisi kutarisira mari kana kuti mumwe munhu amushandisa zvisina kunaka. Bvunzai kuti chii chakaitika uye, kana munhu achibvumirana, ongororai chirevo cheakaundi kana bhiri rakakodzera mese.

[CFPB inonyora zviratidzo zvinogona kuratidza kushandiswa kwemari zvisina kunaka](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), zvinosanganisira mari kana zvinhu zviri kunyangarika, kubviswa kwemari kutsva kusingajairike, mabhiri asina kubhadharwa, vanhu vasingazivikanwe vari kuwedzerwa kumaakaundi, kana mumwe munhu ari kudzikamisa mafoni nekushanya kwemunhu. Cherechedzai maitiro neshanduko kubva pane zvinowanzoitika kumunhu uyu; musatora chiratidzo chimwe chete sehumbowo.

Kana munhu achiti mari yashaikwa, torai kunetseka kwacho zvakakomba. [Gwaro redu rekupindura fungidziro](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) rinopa nzira dzekutarisa kunetseka pasina kukuramba. Bvunzai munhu muchivanzika kuti chii chaona uye kuti anoda kuti chii chiitike chinotevera. Musapomera hama, mutarisiri kana mushandi webhanga musati matarisa chokwadi. Kana muchifungidzira kuti mubatsiri wamunovimba naye angave ari iye ane chekuita nazvo, tsvagai mazano kubva kune imwe nyanzvi kana sevhisi yemunharaunda.

## Tingaderedza sei njodzi yekubiridzirwa?

Vanobiridzira vanogona kuzviita sebhanga, hofisi yehurumende, bhizinesi, shamwari kana hama. Vanogona kuti pane dambudziko rekukurumidza uye vokumanikidzai kubhadhara nekukasika. [Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association rekuchengeteka patekinoroji](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) rinokurudzira kumbomira, kutarisa kuti ari kubata ndiani uye kutsvaga rubatsiro musati magovana ruzivo kana mari.

- **Mira kana shoko richinzwa sekumanikidza.** Musatumira mari kana kupa ruzivo rweakaundi panguva iyo munhu arikufona kana shoko riri kukumanikidzai.
- **Tarisa muchishandisa munhu wamunovimba naye.** Dimburai runhare uye fonerai bhanga panhamba iri pachirevo cheakaundi chazvino kana pakadhi, kana kushandisa app yaro yepamutemo. Musashandisa link kana nhamba yatumirwa nemunhu arikufona.
- **Chengetai ruzivo rwekuchengetedza rwakavanzika.** Musatumira mapassword, ruzivo rwakazara rweakaundi kana makodhi ekusimbisa mukupindura shoko risingatarisirwi. [FTC inokurudzira kupedza runhare rusingatarisirwi runokumbira kodhi uye kubata bhanga kuburikidza nenhamba yamunovimba nayo](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Bvumiranai nerubatsiro nemunhu.** Bvunzai kuti angada kutarisa chikumbiro chinoshamisa naani. Chinhu chetrusted contact chebhanga chingabatsira pakutaurirana, asi hachipi mvumo yekubhadhara pachacho.

## Ndinofanira kuita sei kana mari inogona kunge yaenda kune anobiridzira?

Taurai nebhanga, app yekubhadhara, kambani yekadhi kana kambani yakashandiswa kutumira mari nekukurumidza sezvinobvira. Shandisai nhamba iri pakadhi kana pachirevo cheakaundi, kana app kana webhusaiti yepamutemo yesangano. Bvunzai kana vachigona kumisa kana kudzosera muripo. [Matanho ekudzoreredza eFTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) anotsanangura kuti sarudzo dziripo dzinoenderana nekuti mari yatumirwa sei; hakuna kuvimbiswa kuti inogona kudzoserwa.

Chengetai mameseji, marisiti, mazuva nezviziviso zveakaundi. MuUnited States, munogona kuzivisa nezvekubiridzirwa ku[FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Kana muchifungidzira kuti mumwe munhu ari kushandisa munhu mukuru zvisina kunaka, [Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) inoti taurai neAdult Protective Services mudunhu rinogara munhu. Kana mumwe munhu ari munjodzi yekukurumidza, taurai nemasevhisi ekukurumidzira emunharaunda. Kunze kweUnited States, shandisai masevhisi emunharaunda ekudzivirira vatengi nekuchengetedza vanhu.

Taurai musina kupomera. Kubiridzirwa kwakagadzirwa kuti kunyengedze vanhu. [Gwaro reCFPB rerubatsiro panyaya dzekubiridzirwa nekunyengera](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) rinonyora sarudzo dzekuzivisa muU.S. uye rinokurudzira kushandisa mawebhusaiti nenhamba dzenhare dzamunovimba nadzo.

## Ndingawanepi rubatsiro panyaya chaiyo yemari?

Bvunzai bhanga remunhu kuti ndezvipi zvishandiso nemvumo zviripo. Kana paine gwaro repamutemo kana shanduko yemuridzi weakaundi, taurai nenyanzvi yakakodzera inoziva mitemo yenzvimbo inogara munhu. Kana muchinetseka nezvekushandiswa zvisina kunaka kungangoitika, shandisai sevhisi yemunharaunda yekuchengetedza vanhu. Peji rino harigoni kusarudza kuti mumwe munhu anofanira kuva nemvumo ipi kana kuti muripo wakati inyaya yekubiridzirwa here.

[Gwaro rematanho ekutanga mushure mekuonekwa kwedementia](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) rinogona kubatsira munhu nemhuri kusarudza mubvunzo umwe chete wekuronga wekutarisa. [Gwaro rekusawirirana kwemhuri](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) rinopa nzira dzekukurukura kunetseka kumwe chete panguva.
