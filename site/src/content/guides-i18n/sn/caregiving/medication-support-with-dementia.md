Maitiro ekushandisa mishonga anogona kuchinja sezvo zvinodiwa zvichichinja. Munhu angada rubatsiro rwekuyeuka muyero, kuverenga chinyorwa chiri pamushonga, kana kutsvagazve mushonga wakanyorerwa, asi achiri kuda kuzvitarisira mune dzimwe nzvimbo. Chinangwa ndechekubatsira kushandisa mishonga zvakachengeteka pasina kutora basa rakawanda kupfuura rinodiwa.

[Gwaro rekuchengetedzeka kwemishonga reAlzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/medication-safety) rinokurudzira kushanda navanachiremba nemafemesi, kuchengeta rondedzero inoenderana nenguva, uye kubvumirana pamaitiro. Ruzivo rwuri papeji ino nderwemunhu wose. Teverai mirairo inobva kumunhu akanyora mushonga uye kumufemesi yemishonga yemunhu wacho.

## Ndingabatanidza sei munhu wacho mumaitiro emishonga?

Bvunzai kuti ndeupi rubatsiro rwunodiwa nemunhu wacho. Angada chiyeuchidzo, rubatsiro rwekuvhura mudziyo, kana kuti mumwe wemhuri atarise rondedzero. Kana munhu wacho achikwanisa kutarisira mimwe mishonga ari oga, chengetai kugona ikoko mupfungwa. Maitiro matsva ekuyeuchidza anofanira kutaurwa naye uye kuchinjwa kana achinzwa sokupindirwa kana kuvhiringidzwa nawo.

Shandisai mutauro wakajeka, wevanhu vakuru. Kana munhu wacho achinwa mushonga panguva yaanowanzoita, batanidzai chiyeuchidzo nemaitiro ezuva nezuva aanoziva chete kana izvo zvichienderana nemirairo inobva kumufemesi. Ipai munhu wacho nguva yekupindura. Musamubvunza semuedzo, musamunyadzisa nekukanganwa, kana kukakavara pamusoro pekuti akanwa muyero here kana kuti kwete.

Kana chiyeuchidzo chisiri kushanda, bvunzai kuti chikamu chipi chakaoma. Chinyorwa chingava chakaoma kuverenga, mudziyo ungava wakaoma kuvhura, kana mushonga ungakonzera kunzwa kusina kunaka. Chiremba kana mufemesi anogona kubatsira kuona sarudzo dziripo. Musachinje maitiro emishonga nekufungidzira.

## Nderuzivo rwupi runofanira kuva parondedzero yemishonga?

Chengetai rondedzero imwe chete inoenderana nenguva ine zita remushonga wega wega wakanyorerwa, muyero wawo nenguva, uye chikonzero chawakanyorerwa kana muchiziva. Sanganisirai mishonga inotengwa pasina tsamba, mavhitamini, mishonga yemishonga yemakwenzi, uye zvimwe zvinowedzera muviri. Nyora pasi kusawirirana nemimwe mishonga kana mhedzisiro dzakambovapo uye mazita evashandi vehutano vanotarisira munhu wacho.

Uyai nerondedzero iyi kumisangano yekurapwa uye muivandudze kana munhu akanyora mushonga achinja chimwe chinhu. Kana munhu wacho achiona vanachiremba vanopfuura mumwe, goverai rondedzero nenhengo yega yega yechikwata chekutarisira chete kana munhu wacho abvuma. Chengetai rondedzero panzvimbo painogona kuwanikwa nemunhu wacho nevabatsiri vaakasarudza, uye muitarise seruzivo rwehutano rwakavanzika.

Kumbirai chiremba kana mufemesi kuti atarise kana paine kudyidzana kungaitika kana mushonga mutsva wanyorerwa. Kana munhu wacho akava nedambudziko idzva mushure mekuchinja kwemushonga, nyora pasi kuti rakatanga riini wobva wabata munhu akanyora mushonga. Kuchinja kwemaitiro kunogona kuva nezvikonzero zvakawanda, saka musafungidzira kuti mushonga ndiwo une mhosva musina zano renyanzvi.

## Bhokisi rinoronga mapiritsi kana chiyeuchidzo zvinogona kubatsira here?

Karenda, chati yakanyorwa, kana bhokisi rinoronga mapiritsi zvinogona kubatsira vamwe vanhu. Chishandiso chakakodzera chinoenderana nemishonga yemunhu wacho uye zvaanokwanisa. Bvunzai mufemesi kana bhokisi rinoronga mapiritsi rakakodzera musati maendesa mushonga kubva mupakeji yawo yepakutanga. Mimwe mishonga inoda kubatwa kana kuchengetwa nenzira yakakosha.

Bvumiranai kuti ndiani achazadza kana kutarisa bhokisi iroro uye kuti muchadzivirira sei miyero yakanganwika kana yakapetwa. Kana vanhu vanopfuura mumwe vachibatsira, shandisai nzira imwe chete yakagoverwa yekunyora zvakapiwa. Kana munhu wacho achida kuzvitarisira bhokisi racho ari oga, bvunzai mufemesi kuti izvozvo zvingaitwa sei zvakachengeteka uye zvinobvira.

Kana muyero wakanganwika, tarisai mirairo yakanyorwa yemushonga iwoyo kana fonerai mufemesi musati masarudza zvekuita. Musafungidzira kana kupeta muyero muchitevedza gwaro remunhu wose.

## Ko kana munhu wacho akaramba kana kuti aine dambudziko rekumedza?

Mirai zvishoma mobvunza kuti chii chiri kunetsa. Munhu wacho anogona kusada kuravira kwacho, kunzwa kurwara, kuneta, kana kusanzwisisa kuti mushonga wacho ndewei. Ipai tsananguro yakajeka nenguva yekupindura. Musamanikidzira mushonga mumuromo memunhu wacho kana kuuvanza muzvokudya pasina kubvumirana uye zano renyanzvi.

Musambopwanye piritsi, kuvhura kapisi, kana kusanganisa mushonga nezvokudya kunze kwekunge munhu akanyora mushonga kana mufemesi asimbisa kuti zvakachengeteka. Mimwe mishonga inogona kusashanda sezvainofanira kana kukonzera kukuvadza kana ikachinjwa. Kana kumedza kwakaoma, bvunzai kana paine mushonga wemvura kana imwe nzira iripo. Batai chiremba kana mufemesi pane kuchinja muyero mega.

## Mishonga inofanira kuchengetwa nekubatwa sei?

Chengetai mishonga maererano nezviri pachinyorwa uye nemirairo yemufemesi. Kana munhu wacho achigona kunwa muyero wekuwedzera netsaona, bvunzai munhu wacho nechikwata chekutarisira kuti ndedzipi nzira dzekuchengetedza kana dzerutsigiro dzakakodzera. Kabati yakakiyiwa inogona kubatsira mune dzimwe nguva, asi kuwana mishonga uye rusununguko zvine basawo. Ongororai hurongwa sezvo kugona kwemunhu wacho kuchichinja.

Chengetai mishonga yakapera kana yakaregedzwa yakaparadzana uye bvunzai mufemesi kuti mungairasa sei zvakachengeteka. Musachengete mushonga wekare sewekushandisa kana chimwe chinhu chikashaya, uye musagovana mushonga pakati pevanhu. Kana mushonga usipo kana kuti muyero unogona kunge wakadzokororwa, batai mufemesi, rubatsiro rwechepfu, kana rubatsiro rwenjodzi kuti muwane zano nekukurumidza.

## Ndinofanira kutsvaga rubatsiro rwekurapwa riini?

Batai chiremba kana mufemesi kana munhu wacho aine mhedzisiro dzinonetsa, dambudziko rekumedza, miyero yakanganwika kakawanda, kana kuchinja kwechimbichimbi mushure memushonga mutsva. Kumbirai mirairo yakajeka yakanyorwa. Musamisa kana kuderedza mushonga mega.

Kana muchifunga kuti munhu wacho anwa mushonga wakawandisa, fonerai rubatsiro rwechepfu rwemuno kana rubatsiro rwenjodzi nekukurumidza. Kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringidzika, [NHS inokurudzira rubatsiro rwekurapwa nekukurumidza](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Shandisai rubatsiro rwenjodzi rwemuno kana maitiro erubatsiro rwekukurumidza anowanikwa kwamunogara.

Pamigadziri yenguva dzose, [gwaro rekushanyira chiremba](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) rinogona kukubatsirai kugadzirira mibvunzo nerondedzero yemishonga. [Gwaro rekushanyira kuchipatara](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) rinotsanangura kugovera ruzivo rwemishonga nechikwata chekutarisira.

Maitiro akachengeteka ndeaya anogamuchirwa nemunhu wacho uye anonzwisiswa nechikwata chekutarisira. Bvunzai mibvunzo, chengetai rondedzero ichienderana nenguva, uye tsvagai zano renyanzvi musati maita shanduko.
