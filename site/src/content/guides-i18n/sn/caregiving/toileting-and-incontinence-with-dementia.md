Matambudziko echimbuzi anogona kushungurudza munhu wacho pamwe nevanhu vari kumubatsira. Torai netsaona sechinodiwa cherubatsiro, kwete sekukundikana. Bvunzai kuti rubatsiro rupi runonzwa rwakakodzera, chengetai munhu wamunotarisira achibatanidzwa uye chengetedzai zvakavanzika zvake. Munhu wacho anogona kukwanisa kuita mamwe matanho ari ega kana angada mubatsiri waanoziva ari pedyo.

[Nhungamiro yeAlzheimer's Association pamusoro pekushaya kuzvidzora](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence), [mazano eDementia Australia pamusoro pekuzvidzora](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/continence) uye [gwara reNHS rekutarisira munhu ane dementia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/looking-after-someone/) zvinokurudzira kuremekedza, rubatsiro rwuri nyore uye zano renyanzvi kana matambudziko akatanga. Peji rino ruzivo rwakajairika, harisi kuongororwa kwechirwere kana hurongwa hwekutarisira hwemunhu mumwe nemumwe.

## Dambudziko idzva rechimbuzi rinogara richikonzerwa nedementia here?

Kwete. Dambudziko idzva kana riri kuwedzera rinogona kuva nezvikonzero zvakawanda. Dementia inogona kuita kuti zviome kuziva kudiwa kwekuenda kuchimbuzi, kuwana chimbuzi kana kubata zvipfeko. Zvimwe zvinogona kukonzera zvinosanganisira utachiona, kuvimbiswa, mhedzisiro yemishonga kana kuomerwa nekufamba. Munhu anotarisira haakwanise kuziva chikonzero kubva panetsaona imwe chete.

Taurai nachiremba kana imwe nyanzvi yezvehutano kana kuzvidzora dundira kana ura kuchichinja. Kana zvichibatsira, nyorai kuti shanduko yakatanga riini, zvamakacherechedza uye kana munhu wacho akataura nezvemarwadzo kana kusagadzikana. Izvi zvinogona kubatsira nyanzvi kubvunza mibvunzo yakajeka. Musamisa kana kuchinja mushonga zvichibva pagwara rakajairika.

Kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringika, tsvakai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwekurapwa kuburikidza nebasa renzvimbo renjodzi. [Mazano eNHS pamusoro pekuvhiringika kunotanga kamwe kamwe](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) anotsanangura kuti kuvhiringika kunotanga kamwe kamwe kunoda kutarisirwa nekukurumidza. Shanduko inouya zvishoma nezvishoma mumaitiro echimbuzi yakasiyana, asi ichiri kuda kukurukurwa nenyanzvi.

## Ndingaita sei kuti chimbuzi chive nyore kuwana nekushandisa?

Itai kuti nzira ive yakajeka uye iri nyore kuona. Chiedza cheusiku, nzvimbo yakakwana yekufamba uye kuiswa kwakajaira kwezvinhu zvemazuva ese zvinogona kubatsira. Shoko kana mufananidzo uri nyore pamusuwo unogona kubatsira kana munhu wacho achizviziva. Bvunzai musati masiya musuwo wechimbuzi wakavhurika, sezvo munhu wacho angada kuva pakavanzika.

Zvipfeko zviri nyore kuti munhu wacho azvibate zvinogona kuderedza nhanho dzekuwedzera. Chengetai zvipfeko zvenguva dzose zviri pedyo, uye dzivisai chisungo chitsva kana shanduko yekuchengetera kana zvichiita kuti maitiro acho asanyanya kujaira. [Gwara redu rekuchengeteka pamba](/caregiving/home-safety/) rinotaura nezvechiedza nenzira yakajeka inoenda kuchimbuzi. Bvunzai murapi webasa kana imwe nyanzvi yakakodzera musati machinja midziyo yechimbuzi kana kuwedzera njanji.

Imba yacho inofanira kunzwa iri pakavanzika uye yakajaira. Deredzai zvinhu zvakawanda zvingakanganiswa nemunhu wacho sechimbuzi kana chimwe chinhu. Chengetai chibatiso chemwenje chiri nyore kuwana. Chinjai chinhu chimwe panguva, uye chichengetei chete kana munhu wacho akasununguka nacho.

## Ndingati sei kana munhu wacho angada kuenda kuchimbuzi?

Shandisai mazwi akajairika evakuru uye inzwi rakadzikama. Kupawo pfupi kwakaita sekuti, “Mungade kushandisa chimbuzi here?” kunopa munhu wacho mukana wekupindura. Dzivisai kutaura semwana mudiki, kuseka, zviyeuchidzo pamberi pevamwe kana kubvunza pamberi pevamwe vanhu kana izvozvo zvichigona kuvanyadzisa.

Vamwe vanhu vanoratidza kudiwa kwacho nezviito panzvimbo pemashoko. Vanogona kusagadzikana, kufamba-famba, kudhonza zvipfeko kana kuenda kukamuri kajaira. Zviratidzo izvi hazvisi humbowo hwekuti munhu wacho anoda chimbuzi, saka ipai rubatsiro zvinyoro nyoro uye teererai mhinduro yavo.

Kana kushanya kuchimbuzi panguva yakajaira kuchishandira munhu wacho, mupei sechikamu chezuva. Maitiro ajaira kukoka, kwete murairo. Kana vakaramba, musapokane navo kana kuvamanikidza kugara pasi. Mirirai, mupeizve imwe nguva kana vachida, uye teverai maitiro ajaira emunhu wacho pane kuisa hurongwa hwakasimba.

## Ndingapindura sei mushure menetsaona?

Chengetai inzwi renyu rakadzikama uye chengetedzai kuremekedzwa kwemunhu wacho. Dzivisai kupa mhosva, kuratidza kusemeswa kana kuvatsiura pamberi pevamwe. Kutaura kuri nyore kwakaita sekuti, “Ndiudzei zvingabatsira,” kunoita kuti munhu wacho akutungamirirei. Ipai zvipfeko zvakachena kana nzvimbo yakavanzika yekuchinja, asi bvunzai musati mabatsira.

Ipai munhu wacho nguva yekuita nhanho dzaanokwanisa. Anogona kuda kuzvichinjira zvipfeko, kuva nemunhu waanovimba naye ari pedyo kana kusiiwa ari ega kwekanguva. Kana vachida rubatsiro runoshanda, tsanangurai mumatanho mapfupi uye tarisai kuti vakasununguka here. Musavabata kana kuvamhanyisa pabasa rakavanzika.

Munogona kugadzirira zvipfeko zvakachena zvekuchinja kana bhegi risinganyatsoonekwi kana muchienda kunze, kana munhu wacho achida izvozvo. Bvunzai musati machengeta zvinhu zvekuzvidzora munzvimbo inogoverwa nevamwe kana kuudza vamwe vanhu nezvenetsaona. Kana zvinhu zvekudzivirira kana zvemubhedha zvichigona kubatsira, bvunzai nyanzvi yezvehutano kana mupi wemazano ekuzvidzora kuti ndezvipi zvingakodzera zvinodiwa nezvido zvemunhu wacho.

## Ndinofanira kuderedza zvinwiwa kana kusarudza zvinhu zvekuzvidzora here?

Musaderedze zvinwiwa zvemunhu wacho kuti mudzivise netsaona kunze kwekunge nyanzvi yavo yezvehutano yapa zano chairo. Alzheimer's Association inoyambira kuti kuderedza zvinwiwa kunogona kukonzera kupera mvura mumuviri nedzimwe matambudziko. Vanhu vanorarama nedementia vanogonawo kuda zviyeuchidzo kana rubatsiro kuti vanwe zvakakwana. Teverai hurongwa hwekutarisira hwemunhu mumwe nemumwe pane chero miganhu yezvinwiwa.

Mapedhi, zvipfeko zvemukati zvekudzivirira kana machira emubhedha asingapindi mvura isarudzo dzemunhu. Zvinogona kubatsira vamwe vanhu, asi hapana chigadzirwa chimwe chinokodzera munhu wese. Taurai nachiremba kana mupi wemazano ekuzvidzora nezvesarudzo, kunyaradzwa, kukwana, zvinonetsa ganda uye kuti munhu wacho anoda kuzvishandisa sei. Musaise chigadzirwa pamunhu ari kuramba; bvunzai kuti chii chinganzwa chakakodzera.

## Ndinofanira kukumbira rubatsiro rwenyanzvi riini?

Bvunzai chiremba nezvematambudziko matsva, ari kuwedzera kana anorwadza echimbuzi. Utachiona, kuvimbiswa, mhedzisiro yemushonga kana chimwe chinhu cheutano chinogona kuda kutarisirwa. Uyai netsananguro pfupi yezvakachinja uye kuti zvakachinja riini. Mupi wemazano ekuzvidzora anogonawo kubatsira nesarudzo dzinoshanda mune dzimwe nzvimbo; bvunzai chiremba kuti mungawana sei rubatsiro rwemunharaunda.

Tsvakai rubatsiro rwechimbichimbi rwekurapwa kana munhu wacho akangoerekana avhiringika. Pane zvimwe zvinonetsa zvekuzvidzora, taurai nenyanzvi yezvehutano kuti muwane zano pane kuedza kuziva chikonzero moga. Musaderedze zvinwiwa, musachinje mishonga kana kutanga kurapwa zvichibva pachinyorwa chepawebhusaiti.

## Ndingagovera sei basa neruremekedzo?

Kana vanhu vakawanda vachibatsira, bvunzai munhu wacho kuti akasununguka kugovera chii. Chinyorwa chakavanzika chine mazwi aanoda, maitiro ake uye rudzi rwerubatsiro rwaanoda zvinogona kudzivirira kutsanangura kakawanda. Goverai chete izvo zvinofanira kuzivikanwa nemubatsiri anotevera, uye gadziridzai chinyorwa ichocho kana zvido zvemunhu wacho zvachinja.

Rubatsiro rwechimbuzi runogona kunetesa vanotarisira zvakare. Kumbirai munhu wamunovimba naye kana basa rekutarisira kuti atorewo nguva kana munhu wacho achibvumirana nazvo. [Gwara rekuzvichengetedza kwevanotarisira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rine nzira dzekutsvaga rubatsiro nekuzorora. Kutarisira neruremekedzo zvinoreva kuchengetedza zvakavanzika zvemunhu wacho pamwe nekugona kwemunhu anotarisira kuramba achienderera mberi.

Chinangwa ndechekuita kuti rubatsiro ruvepo pasina kumanikidza. Pindurai murunyararo, chengetedzai zvakavanzika, chengetedzai sarudzo dzemunhu wacho uye bvunzai nyanzvi nezvematambudziko matsva.
