Kupomerwa kuti makatora chikwama, makafambisa bhegi kana makachinja ruzivo rwebhangi kunogona kurwadza. Munhu ane dementia anogona kuisa chinhu panzvimbo isiri iyo, kukanganwa kuti akachifambisa kana kusanzwisisa zvakaitika. Asi dementia hairevi kuti mhosva yacho ndeyenhema. Tangai nekuteerera uye nekutarisa chokwadi.

[Gwaro reAlzheimer's Association pamusoro pekufungira nekutenda zvisiri izvo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/suspicions-delusions) rinokurudzira kuramba makadzikama, kupa mhinduro pfupi uye kusapokana. [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) inokurudzirawo kuongorora kunetseka kwacho pane kukuramba nekungoti munhu wacho ane dementia. Peji rino rinopa mazano akajairika ekutaurirana, kwete nzira yekuona kuti mhosva chaiyo ndeyechokwadi here. Kuti muwane rumwe ruzivo pamusoro pekuona kana kunzwa zvinhu zvasingaonekwe nevamwe, onai [gwaro redu pamusoro pekuona zvinhu zvisipo nedementia](/caregiving/hallucinations-with-dementia/).

## Ndingati chii kana mumwe munhu achifungira?

Pindurai kunetseka kwacho musina kubvuma chinhu chamusingazivi kuti ndechechokwadi here. Mhinduro pfupi, yakadzikama inogona kubatsira kuchengetedza hurukuro yakavhurika. Semuenzaniso: “Muri kunetseka kuti chikwama chenyu charasika. Ngatitsvagei tese.” Mobva mambomira moona kuti munhu wacho anopindura sei.

- **Teererai musati matsanangura.** Bvunzai kuti ndechipi chinhu chakarasika kana kuti chii chakaita kuti munhu wacho anetseke. Anogona kunge achirwadziwa nezvinopfuura chinhu chacho pachacho.
- **Chengetai mhinduro yenyu iri pfupi.** Kutsanangura kwenguva refu kunogona kuwedzera ruzivo rwakaoma kutevera uye kuita kuti mose muwedzere kushungurudzika.
- **Dzivisai kupokana kana kubvunza mibvunzo yakaoma.** Musamanikidza munhu wacho kuti ayeuke kwaakaisa chinhu kana kuratidza kuti hamuna kuchitora.
- **Ipai rubatsiro runobatsira.** Kana munhu wacho achida, tsvagai mese munzvimbo dzakajairika kana kubvunza kana achida kuti mumwe munhu abatsire.
- **Mirai kana kurwadziwa mupfungwa kukawedzera.** Ipai munhu wacho nguva uye dzokerai kunyaya yacho gare gare kana zvakachengeteka kuita kudaro.

Kupomerwa uku kunogona kurwadza pamoyo. Edzai kuparadzanisa kutya kwemunhu wacho nezvinozivikanwa nezve zvakaitika. Hamufaniri kugamuchira mhosva yechinhu chamusina kuita, uye hamufaniri kugadzirisa kusawirirana kwega kwega panguva iyoyo.

## Kunetseka uku kungava nezvechokwadi nezvechinhu chakarasika here?

Hongu. Tangai nekutarisa kuti chinhu chacho chakarasika zvechokwadi here. Munhu anogona kunge akachiisa panzvimbo isingawanzo, mumwe munhu anogona kunge akachifambisa kana kuti chinhu chacho chingave chakabiwa. Musaramba chichemo chekuba, kuregeredzwa kana kushungurudzwa nekungoti munhu wacho ane dementia.

Kana chinhu chacho chakaiswa panzvimbo isiri iyo, tsvagai mese kana munhu wacho achida. Tarisai munzvimbo dzaanowanzochichengeta uye teverai zvaiitwa nguva pfupi yadarika. Kana chinhu chacho chichikosha zvikuru, chakakosha pakuchengeteka kana chichiri kurasika, uudzei munhu wacho zvamunoziva uye sanganisirai munhu wenyanzvi wamunovimba kana mumwe wemhuri.

Kana mari kana mabasa epamba achiitwa mese, shandisai zvinyorwa zvakajeka zvinogamuchirwa nemunhu wacho. Chengetai marisiti kana zvinyorwa zvezvamakabvumirana uye muzvitsanangure nemashoko ari nyore. Bvunzai munhu wenyanzvi wemunzvimbo yenyu ane ruzivo nezvemagadzirirwo emari kana mabasa emutemo; gwaro rino harigoni kuongorora nyaya idzodzo.

Kana musina chokwadi chekuti kunetseka kwacho kwakaringana here, kumbirai mumwe munhu asina kwaakarerekera kuti abatsire kutarisa. Sarudzai munhu anovimbwa nemunhu wacho uye goverai chete ruzivo runodiwa kuti munzwisise zvakaitika.

## Maitiro akajairika angabatsira sei pazvinhu zvinorasika?

Ipai zvinhu zvinowanzorasika nzvimbo yakajairika. Ndiro yemakiyi, tireyi yechikwama kana dhirowa rakanyorwa zita zvinogona kuita kuti zvive nyore kuwana chinhu gare gare. [Alzheimer's Society inopa zano rekushandisa nzvimbo imwe chete yezvinhu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) zvakaita semakiyi kana magirazi. Bvunzai munhu wacho kuti ndeipi nzvimbo inonzwisisika kwaari.

Edzai shanduko imwe panguva. Kufambisa zvinhu musina kuzvitsanangura kunogona kuita kuti munhu wacho asanyanya kuva nechokwadi chekuti zvinhu zviri kupi. Kana chinhu chisingadhuri chichiwanzorasika, chimwe chechipiri chinogona kubatsira mune dzimwe dzimba, asi hazvifaniri kutsiva kutarisa kana chekutanga chakabiwa kana chakaiswa panzvimbo isiri iyo.

Iva nechokwadi chekuti munhu wacho anogona kuona nekunzwa zvakanaka sezvinobvira. Dementia Australia inoti matambudziko ekuona kana kunzwa, kurwara, mhedzisiro yemishonga, shanduko mumaitiro ezuva nezuva kana nzvimbo inoremedza zvinogona kubatsira mukushanduka kwemaonero. Bvunzai munhu wacho kana mukamuri mune ruzha rwakanyanya kana kuti munonzwa musina kujaira, uye tarisai kuti ane magirazi ake kana rubatsiro rwekunzwa kana achizvishandisa.

## Ndinofanira kukumbira rubatsiro rwechiremba kana rwekunze riini?

Udzai murapi wemunhu wacho kana kufungira kuri kutsva, kuri kuwedzera kuitika kana kuchikonzera kurwadziwa mupfungwa. Nyorei kuti zvinoitika riini, zvaiitika panguva iyoyo uye kuti pane chakachinja here pautano hwemunhu, mumaitiro ezuva nezuva, munzvimbo yaanogara kana mumishonga yake. Musatanga, musamisa kana kuchinja mishonga zvichibva pachinyorwa chakajairika.

Kuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika kana shanduko inokurumidza mumaitiro kana pautano kunoda kurapwa nekukurumidza. [Nhungamiro yeNHS pamusoro pekuvhiringidzika kunongoerekana kwaitika](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) inoti tsvagai rubatsiro rwekurapwa nekukurumidza. Shandisai hurongwa hwenharaunda hwekukurumidzira kana hwekurapwa nekuchimbidza huripo munogara munhu wacho.

Kana mhosva yacho ichigona kusanganisira kuba, kuregeredzwa kana kushungurudzwa, ivai nechokwadi chekuti munhu wacho akachengeteka uye tsvagai zano kumunhu wenyanzvi wemunzvimbo yenyu wamunovimba kana kubasa rinoona nezvekuchengetedzwa kwevanhu vakuru. Kana paine ari munjodzi nekukurumidza, batai mabasa ekukurumidzira emunzvimbo yenyu. Musaongorora nyaya yakakomba mega kana kuita izvozvo kuchigona kuisa imi kana munhu wacho panjodzi.

## Ndingazvichengeta sei ini pachangu?

Kupomerwa kunoramba kuchiitika kunogona kunetsa hukama. Taurai nemunhu wamunovimba kana chipangamazano, uye kumbirai rubatsiro pamabasa ekuchengeta kana muchinzwa kuremerwa. [Gwaro rekusawirirana kwemhuri](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) rinopa mazano ekugovana mabasa, uye [gwaro rekuzvichengeta kwemuchengeti](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rinotaura nezvekuzorora nerutsigiro.

Rambai makatarisa pane zvinogona kutariswa, manzwiro emunhu wacho uye rutsigiro runodiwa. Hamufaniri kuratidza kutarisira kwenyu muhurukuro yega yega. Kana kunetseka kuchiramba kuchidzoka, kumbirai boka rekuchengeta rubatsiro kuti muwane nzira inoremekedza munhu wacho uye inodzivirira vese vanobatanidzwa.
