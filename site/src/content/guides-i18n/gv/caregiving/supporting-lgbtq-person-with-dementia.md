She ayrn jeh quoi t’ayn eh enney persoon LGBTQ+. Fenaght cre ta scanshoil daue, jean ymmyd jeh ny focklyn t’ad reih, as lhig daue goaill toshiaght er quoi oddys fys persoonagh y ve oc. Ny jean toiggal mychione enney, sheshey, cooney lught-thie ny reihyssyn preevaadjys persoon er bun cummey, shenn recortyssyn ny quoi ta cheet er çheet.

Ta [Lioar-çheet Alzheimer’s Society mychione cur cooney da persoon LGBTQ+ lesh meenaghtys cooinaghtyn](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/supporting-lgbtq-dementia) coyrlaghey dy heirrey mychione reihyssyn y phersoon as dy haghney toiggalyn mychione caarjysyn. Ta [stoo ghoon Alzheimer’s Association da’n cho-phobble LGBTQ+](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) myrgeddin cur sheese imneaghyn mychione oltooan as feddyn kiarail failt. Ta dy chooilley phersoon anchasley; jean ymmyd jeh ny smooinaghtyn shoh myr brooighyn son coloayrt, cha nee myr rolley ta cur cummey er persoon.

## Kys oddym fenaght mychione ennym, pronoonyn as preevaadjys?

Reih traa preevaadjagh, gyn siyr. Foddee oo fenaght, “Cre’n ennym as ny pronoonyn by vie lhiat shin dy ymmydey?” ny “Vel red erbee mychione dty enney ny dty chaarjysyn by vie lhiat dy beagh fys ec ny daoine ta cur cooney dhyt?” Foddee yn persoon freggyrt dy ayrn, gra nagh vel, ny cheet er ash huggey ny sanmey. Cha lhiass dhyt feyshtyn persoonagh y chur nagh vel bentyn rish y chiarail oc.

Fenaght quoi oddys fys y ve oc er cre. Foddee dy by vie lesh fer ennagh dy beagh yn sheshey oc er ny enaghtyn ec lught-obbrey, agh nagh by vie lesh dy beagh yn lught-thie elley oc toiggit mychione nyn slane-chredjue ny enney-ghender oc. Foddee myrgeddin dy jean persoon reih focklyn anchasley ayns boaylyn anchasley. Ny rheynn manylyn enney marish mooinjer, keayrtee ny lught-obbrey ynrican er y fa dy vel fys ayd orroo.

My by vie lesh y persoon dy ve recortyssit reih, fenaght cre dy screeu as quoi lhisagh eh y akin. Jean shickyr kys ta’n churrymagh dellal rish ennym ta anchasley rish recortyssyn elley, goaill stiagh my oddys lught-obbrey yn ennym reih’t y ymmydey ayns coloayrt. Jean freayll arrey er preevaadjys y phersoon; cha lhiass da dy chooilley phersoon ta cur cooney lesh kiarail dy hoiggal dy chooilley nhee.

Foddee reihyssyn caghlaa. Jean shickyr reesht tra oddys y persoon reih y hoilshaghey, er-lheh roish gleashaghey ny cormaghey kiarail noa. Mannagh vel eh baghtal cre by vie lesh y rheynn, ny jean toiggal. Eiyr er reih chruinn t’er ny chur roie, as shir cooney veih persoon leighoil ynnydoil er son feyshtyn mychione pooar kiarail ny reillyn rheynn-fys.

## Quoi lhisagh ve goaill stiagh ayns coloayrtyn mychione kiarail?

Fenaght jeh’n phersoon quoi t’eh ny t’ee treishteil as by vie lesh goaill stiagh. Foddee yn sheshaght choonee oc goaill stiagh ben-phoosee ny fer-poosee, sheshey, carrey mie, mooinjer ny “lught-thie reiht” — deiney ta cur cooney daue myr lught-thie ga nagh vel ad mooinjerys. Ta paart dy leih croghey er mooinjer ruggyragh; ta feallagh elley nagh vel. Lhig da’n phersoon cur sheese nyn gaarjysyn ayns ny focklyn oc hene.

Fenaght feyshtyn ymmydoil: Quoi oddys cheet er çheet? Quoi lhisagh lught-obbrey cur fys da my ta plannyn caghlaa? Quoi oddys naight y gheddyn? My ta feme ec churrymagh er kied ny pabyr roish rheynn fys, fenaght cre ta feme er as cur sheese ny reihyssyn da’n phersoon. Cha vel ve dty heshey ny dty chooneyder reiht cur pooar leighoil dy ghoaill toshiaghtyn gyn smooinaght; ta reillyn anchasley liorish boayl.

Jean ymmyd jeh’n ennym-caarjys by vie lesh y persoon. Foddee obbree kiarail ta enmys sheshey myr “carrey” ny “mooinjer” tra by vie lesh y persoon dy ve enmyssit myr sheshey cur trimshey orroo. Foddee noteyn giare, er bun kied, ginsh da lught-obbrey kys dy chur enney er deiney as cre’n fys oddys ve er ny rheynn.

## Cre oddym y heirrey jeh churrymagh kiarail?

Tra t’ou cosoylaghey kiarail chummal, cooney sy thie ny shirveishyn laa son sleih aasit, fenaght jeh’n phersoon cre ny feyshtyn by vie lesh y hroggal as my by vie lesh oo ve ayns shen maroo. Ta [lioar-çheet SAGE mychione shirveishyn coadey](https://www.sageusa.org/resource/10-tips-for-finding-lgbt-affirming-services/) gra dy vod rheynn fys persoonagh marish churrymagh gennaghtyn annoon. Shirrey er y churrymagh dy chur sheese ny cliaghtaghyn oc ec y traa ayns focklyn aashagh.

- Kys ta shiu recortys ennym as pronoonyn reiht? Cre’n lught-obbrey oddys y fys shen y akin?
- Kys oddys persoon reih quoi ta cheet er çheet as traa preevaadjagh y ve oc marish sheshey ny cooneyder elley?
- Kys ta shiu gynsaghey as cur arrym da reihyssyn y phersoon mychione shamyr, eaddagh as kiarail persoonagh?
- Cre’n trimmid ta lught-obbrey geddyn mychione sleih aasit LGBTQ+ as meenaghtys cooinaghtyn?
- Kys oddys cummaltagh ny cooneyder imnea y hroggal mychione preevaadjys ny mee-arrym, as cre ta taghyrt ny lurg shen?

Ta [lioar-çheet SAGE as Human Rights Campaign Foundation mychione kiarail-foddey goaill stiagh](https://www.sageusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/lei-consumer-guide-finding-lgbtq-inclusive-ltcc.pdf) coyrlaghey dy yeeaghyn er polaseeyn screeut mychione keayrtee, shamyraght as cliaghtey lught-obbrey. V’eh foill’sit ayns 2021; jean ymmyd jeh son feyshtyn dy chur, cha nee myr raaittys mychione leigh ynnydoil ec y traa t’ayn. Ta polaseeyn as shirveishyn anchasley liorish boayl.

Cha vod cowrey, lioar-vraar ny freggyrt gerjoil un ry-heet shickyraghey cre’n aght vees kiarail gagh laa. My by vie lesh y persoon çheet er çheet, cur tastey da kys ta lught-obbrey loayrt roo as freggyrt da’n chaarjys ny ny cooneyderyn reiht oc. Shirrey loayrt dy preevaadjagh marish oltey jeh’n lught-obbrey my oddys shen cur er y persoon gennaghtyn sauçhey.

## Kys oddym cooney ’chur da’n persoon slane?

Fenaght mychione ny redyn cadjin sy vea oc, cliaghtaghyn, çhengey, cultoor, credjue, caarjyn as co-phobble. Foddee enney ve scanshoil dy loayrt my-e-chione, ny foddee dy by vie lesh cur traa er ayrnyn elley jeh bea. Ny jean dagh çheet er çheet ny chooish mychione enney as ny jean toiggal dy vel dagh persoon LGBTQ+ er jeet trooid ny h-eiraghtyn cheddin.

Ta paart dy leih er n’immeeaght trooid obbal ny oltooan as foddee ad gennaghtyn kiarailagh mychione shirveishyn. Foddee feallagh elley nagh vel shoh ny imnea daue. Eaisht rish gyn shirrey er y persoon dy chur sheese ny dy phrowal red erbee. My ta cooinaghtyn, jalloyn ny skeealyn mychione yn traa t’er gholl cur trimshey orroo, fenaght my by vie lhieu scuirr ny caghlaa cooish.

My she sheshey, carrey ny oltey jeh’n lught-thie oo, ta dty chooney hene scanshoil myrgeddin. Fenaght jeh’n phersoon kys by vie lesh oo dy ghoaill ayrn, as jean shirrey son sheshaght meenaghtys cooinaghtyn ny sheshaght da sleih aasit LGBTQ+ ynnydoil my ta feme ayd er coyrle. Ta [duillag stoo Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) kiangley rish stoo cho-phobble as stoo da fir-choonee; ta ny ta ry-gheddyn croghey er ynnyd vaghee oo.

Son rolley ny s’lheead tra t’ou cosoylaghey thieyn kiarail, jeeagh er [reih kiarail chummal son meenaghtys cooinaghtyn](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/). Son cooney lesh plannal lurg feddyn magh, jeeagh er [ny chied chesmadyn lurg feddyn magh meenaghtys cooinaghtyn](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
