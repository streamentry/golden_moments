Foddee sleih ta loayrt ny smoo na un çhengey loayrt er aghtyn dy liooar my ta meechraueys caghlaa yn aght t’ad feddyn focklyn ny toiggal reddyn. Ta paart dy leih toshiaght ymmyd y yannoo jeh çhengey dynsee ad ny s’leaie; cha nel feallagh elley. Yn chied cheum s’cooie dy mennick, shen briaght cre’n çhengey s’fassey daue nish, as eisht geiyrt er y phersoon.

[Ta Alzheimer’s Society gra dy vod roie-çhengey caghlaa as t’ad coyrlaghey ymmyd y yannoo jeh’n çhengey ta’n persoon cliaghtey loayrt ayn](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Ta Alzheimer’s Association myrgeddin cur stiagh chyndaa gys çhengey dooghyssagh myr caghlaa posssanagh ayns loayrtys](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Cha nel ny caghlaaghyn shoh taghyrt da dagh ooilley pheiagh as cha nel ad ginsh diu stait persoon erbee.

## Cre’n çhengey lhisagh dou ymmyd y yannoo jeh?

Briaght da’n phersoon cre’n çhengey by vie lhieu ymmyd y yannoo son y loayrtys shoh. Foddee yn roie oc caghlaa lurg y chooish, y boayl, ny traa yn laa. My t’ad caghlaa çhengey, ny jean eh y ghoaill myr scrutaght ny êignaghey ad dy hannaghtyn ayns çhengey ta toiggal ayd’s.

My ta’n çhengey by vie lhieu ayd’s, jean ymmyd jeh focklyn ennagh as cur traa daue dy reggyrt. My ta agh kuse dy ’ocklyn ayd, bee oney as jean ymmyd jeh cowraghyn, nheeghyn, ny jallooyn myrgeddin. [Ta stiurey çhengey Alzheimer’s Society](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) soilshaghey dy foddee ablid çhengey caghlaa veih laa gy laa as dy fod skeeys, pian, ny doghan cur er.

## Cre oddys cooney ayns loayrtys gaghlaa?

Cum ny coyrleyn cadjin ayns cooinaghtyn, as cur cooney ta cooie son çhengey yn phersoon:

- **Jean ymmyd jeh focklyn as enmyn ennagh.** Briaght da’n phersoon ny da persoon ta treisht orroo cre ny focklyn t’ad ymmyd y yannoo son nheeghyn gaghlaa, olteynyn lught-thie, as cliaghtaghyn.
- **Soilshaghey chammah as gra.** Cowree gys cappan, cum seose daa reih, ny jean ymmyd jeh jalloo ny nhee ennagh. Foddee cowrey cooney tra ta’n fockle kiart doillee dy gheddyn.
- **Fuirriaght.** Cur traa da’n phersoon dy chlashtyn as dy reggyrt. Ny jean jerrey er dagh raa ny aarloo yn eysht ayns çhengaghyn dy liooar ec y traa cheddin.
- **Cum un smooinaghtyn ec y traa.** Foddee coontey liauyr ve doillee ayns çhengey erbee. Jean ymmyd jeh raa giare, scuirr, as eisht jeeagh vel ad geearree ny smoo.
- **Jeeagh er clashtyn as fakin.** Foddee speckleyn, claareyn-clashtyn, soilshey mie, as boayl feagh cur er çhengey ve ny sassey dy eiyrt.

My s’mian lesh y persoon lhaih, briaght vel fysseree screeut scanshoil ry-gheddyn ayns y çhengey oc. Ny jean treishteil er çhyndaays seyr-obbragh son reih leigh, cordail, ny curmyn mychione cowraghey; jean shickyr jeh fysseree scanshoil marish y phroayltee kiarail.

## Cre my ta mee nagh vel loayrt çhengey by vie lesh y persoon?

Briaght vel ad geearree dy jig carrey ny oltey lught-thie enmyssagh daa-hengeyagh mâroo son loayrtys gaghlaa. Ny jean toiggal dy vel ad geearree persoon elley dy hyndaays; foddee dy by vie lhieu cowraghyn, jallooyn, ny çhengey elley ta toiggal oc ny-neesht.

Son coinney leigh ny loayrtys elley ta feme er fysseree cronnal, cur feysht da’n chlinnic ny shirveish kiarail ro-laue. Insh daue cre’n çhengey by vie lesh y persoon as briaght cre’n cooney chyndaays t’ry-gheddyn. [Ta U.S. Department of Health and Human Services soilshaghey](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) nagh vod lught-thie ny caarjyn dy kinjagh fysseree leigh scanshoil y hyndaays dy cronnal; foddee yn shirveish soilshaghey ny reihyn as ny kiarailyn ynnydoil oc. Foddee yn persoon reih vel fer-cooney goll mâroo, choud’s ta’n proayltee cur cooney çhengey erbee ta feme er ayns ordyr.

Rish y cheet, loayr dy jeeragh rish y persoon as cur traa da’n chyndaarey dy loayrt. Jean shickyr dy vel y persoon toiggal yn eysht as dy vel caa oc dy reggyrt ayns ny focklyn oc hene. My t’ou cur cooney daue, cur stiagh coontey myrgeddin ny lomarcan tra by vie lhieu.

## Tra lhisagh dou shirrey coyrle leigh?

Foddee doilleeidyn çhengey caghlaa harrish traa, agh ta caghlaa obbragh ny red elley. [Ta’n NHS coyrlaghey dy gheddyn cooney leigh chelleeragh tra ta persoon cheet dy ve meehroosagh dy obbragh](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Ny jean toiggal dy nee ayrn jeh meechraueys ny lomarcan t’ayns caghlaa obbragh ayns loayrtys. My ta’n persoon cheet dy ve meehroosagh dy obbragh, feer ching, ny doillee dy ghoostey, cur fys da shirveishyn leigh ynnydoil çhelleeragh.

Son caghlaaghyn mall, cur tastey jeh ny t’ou fakin as briaght da fer-lheihys y phersoon son coyrle. Screeu sheese cre’n çhengey v’ad ymmyd y yannoo, cre ren cooney lhieu loayrt, as row ad skee ny doghan. She coontey son loayrtys t’ayn shoh, cha nee feyshtys leigh. Foddee yn [stiurey ain son cheet gys y fer-lheihys](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) cooney lhiat aarloo feyshtyn.

## Cre oddym’s ginsh da cooneyderyn elley?

Lesh cordail y phersoon, cum noteyn giare son sleih by vie lhieu ve goaill ayrn. Foddee eh cur stiagh y çhengey ny yn chengey-fo by vie lhieu son loayrt as lhaih, focklyn ny enmyn ennagh, as cre ta cooney lhieu loayrtys y eiyrt. Briaght vel ad geearree persoon erbee er lheh ve goaill ayrn ayns coinneeyn ny fys kiarail. Ny jean recortyssyn jeh fysseree persoonagh nagh vel feme er son cooney, as insh da’n phersoon quoi oddys fakin yn noteyn. Ta noteyn çhengey cooney lesh sleih toshiaght loayrtys ny s’fassey; cha nel eh goaill ynnyd chyndaarey son loayrtys leigh cronnal.

## Kys oddym’s cur cooney da reihyn y phersoon?

Ta çhengey ny ayrn jeh’n aght ta persoon jannoo kiangley, agh cha nee yn slane persoon t’ayn. Briaght mychione nyn gliaghtaghyn, ny reddyn s’laik lhieu, ny s’mian lhieu lhaih, as ny sleih by vie lhieu ve goaill ayrn. My ta’n persoon cordail rish, screeu sheese focklyn ny raaghyn cooie son cooneyderyn elley as insh daue kys dy ymmyd y yannoo jeu. Cum noteyn preevaadjagh as rheynn ny lomarcan ny ta’n persoon er chordail rish.

Ny jean êignaghey yn persoon dy chleayney, dy hyndaays son feallagh elley, ny dy phrowal cre ta toiggal oc. Foddee paart dy loayrtyssyn ve ny sassey ayns un çhengey as feallagh elley ayns çhengey elley. Ta comfys, preevaadjys, as reih y phersoon ny smoo scanshoil na freayll rish un çhengey ny lomarcan.

Ta ny [coyrleyn loayrtys ain](/caregiving/communication-tips/) cur cooney mychione loayrt dy aashagh, clashtyn, as ymmyd y yannoo jeh jallooyn. Son cooney lesh plannal lurg feyshtys, jeeagh er [ny chied cheimyn lurg feyshtys meechraueys](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
