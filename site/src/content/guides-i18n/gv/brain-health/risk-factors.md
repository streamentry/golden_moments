## Cre ny 14 factanyn gaue son meeinaghtys?

Ren [Co-choyrle Lancet 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0),
scruit ec 27 rannsheyder meeinaghtys, towse dy vel 14 factanyn gaue oddys
caghlaa cochianglt rish mysh 45% jeh caseyn meeinaghtys çheer-oaylleeagh. She
towse y Cho-choyrle t’ayn ayns y cheead son dagh factoor heese, yn ayrn jeh
caseyn çheer-oaylleeagh ta kianglt rish, cruinnit gys earrooyn slane. Ta ny
earrooyn goll harrish y cheilley, er-yn-oyr dy vel ymmodee gaueyn ec ymmodee
sleih ec yn un traa, as shen-y-fa ta’d cur 45% as cha nee ny smoo.

## C’red te dy lhaih ny fo-sheidyn shoh?

Dy kiaralagh. Ta tuarastyl y Cho-choyrle hene cur raaueyn feeu:

- **T’ad cur coontey jeh pobblaghtyn, cha nee persoonyn.** Ta
  [giare-choontey y tuarastyl ec University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors)
  gra dy vel lheeadaghey gaue gobbraghey trooid pobblaght, as cha jean eh shickyr
  nagh bee meeinaghtys er persoon er-lheh.
- **T’ad goaill rish oyr as eiyrtys.** Cha ren ny ughtaryn cur stiagh agh
  factanyn lesh toshiaght niartal, agh t’ad gra foddee nagh vel paart jeh ny
  kianglaghyn agh paartagh oyragh.
- **She earrooyn çheer-oaylleeagh t’ayn.** Ta’n chooid smoo jeh’n fysseree
  çheet veih çheeraghyn berçhagh, as bee yn ayrn firrinagh son çheer ny
  co-phobble erbee anchasley.
- **Foddee fo-sheidyn beggey foast ve scanshoil.** Ta factoor ta kianglt rish
  2% jeh caseyn çheer-oaylleeagh foast bentyn rish earroo feer vooar dy leih.

## Cre ny factanyn gaue va currit rish ayns 2024?

Ren tuarastyl 2024 cur daa factoor rish ny 12 va enmyssit ayns 2020:

- **LDL cholesterol ard ayns mean-vea**, kianglt rish mysh 7% jeh caseyn.
- **Coayl shilley gyn lheeys ayns jerrey vea**, kianglt rish mysh 2% jeh caseyn.

## Cre ny factanyn gaue nagh vod ve caghlaa?

Ta’n National Institute on Aging gra
[dy nee eash yn factoor gaue smoo scanshoil ta er enn son galar Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease),
as cha nod peiagh erbee eh y chaghlaa. Ta ayrn ec genyn myrgeddin; she gene
ennoil ta bentyn rish gaue Alzheimer APOE. Ta’n NIA gra nagh vel toiggal lane ec
sheanseyderyn foast er cre ta cur galar Alzheimer er y chooid smoo dy leih. Ta
Co-choyrle Lancet gra dy vod ny gaueyn oddys caghlaa ve er nyn lheeadaghey,
cre-erbee yn gaue genagh ec persoon.

Er-yn-oyr dy vel ayrn mooar ec eash as genyn, ta meeinaghtys cheet foast er
ymmodee sleih nagh vel veg jeh ny 14 factanyn gaue oc. Cha nee foill persoon
erbee t’ayn.

## Cre ta toshiaght ny prowalyn gra mychione caghlaa ny gaueyn shoh?

Cha nee yn un red eh kiangley factoor gaue rish meeinaghtys as prowal dy vel
caghlaa eh lheeadaghey gaue meeinaghtys. Shoh kuse dy sampleyryn jeh stayd
toshiaght ny prowalyn:

- **Brastyl-folley.** Ayns prowal SPRINT MIND, ren cowrey brastyl-folley ny sloo
  lheeadaghey dy scanshoil moalys smooinaght eddrym. Cha ren eh lheeadaghey dy
  scanshoil meeinaghtys tebyssagh, ga dy row foddee yn prowal ro veg dy hoilshaghey
  shen ([SPRINT MIND ayns JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Saaseyn-clashtyn.** Ta Co-choyrle Lancet cur coontey jeh prowal tree bleeaney
  jeh saaseyn-clashtyn nagh ren feddyn eiyrtys cadjin er smooinaght, agh vondeish
  ayns fo-phossan dy leih lesh gaue smoo. Ta studeyryn ta jeeaghyn er sleih
  harrish traa kiangley ymmyd saaseyn-clashtyn rish gaue ny sloo. Ta ny smoo ayns
  nyn [stiu-gheddyn clashtyn](/brain-health/hearing/).
- **Gleashaght corp.** Ta’n National Institute on Aging gra nagh vel dy liooar
  toshiaght veih prowalyn clinagh dy ghra dy vod gleashaght moalys smooinaght
  eddrym ny Alzheimer y haghney ny y moalley, ga dy vel paart dy studeyryn coyrt
  coyrle dy vod eh cooney lesh moalys bentyn rish eash
  ([NIA er shaghney Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Ta prowalyn liauyr ta farkiaght son meeinaghtys dy heet moal as deyr, myr shen
son ymmodee factanyn hig yn toshiaght share veih studeyryn ta jeeaghyn er sleih
harrish bleeantyn. Ta’n toshiaght shen ymmydoil, agh t’eh ny s’faase na prowal.

## Kys mychione cadley as bee?

Ren y Cho-choyrle scrutaghey cadley as bee agh cha ren eh cur ad rish y rolley,
er-yn-oyr nagh row yn toshiaght dy liooar lajer ny kiart. Ta nyn stiughyn er
[cadley](/brain-health/sleep/) as [bee](/brain-health/diet/) soilshaghey cre
t’er enn.

## Loayr rish docteyr

She traa mie t’ayn ayns scrutaghey bleeanagh dy hroggal ny cooishyn shoh. Oddys
oo y rolley shoh y chur lhiat as feysht:

- Cre my earrooyn brastyl-folley, shugyr-folley as cholesterol, as vel feme er
  kiarail er fer erbee jeu?
- Lhisagh my chlashtyn as my hooillyn ve er nyn scrutaghey?
- Vel y ghleashaght ta mee jannoo sauçhey as dy liooar dou?
- Vod fer erbee jeh my leighysyn bentyn rish cooinaghtyn ny smooinaght?

Roish caghlaa leighysyn, bee, trostey, gleashaght ny co-choyrleyn, loayr rish
docteyr hoshiaght. My ta mee-hushtey doaltattym, annooinidys er un cheu, ny
doilleeid loayrt ort hene ny er y persoon t’ou kiarail er y hon, cur foon da
999.
