Foddee dy vel oo er lhaih dy vod bee “mie son yn inchyn” Alzheimer’s y chummal
ersooyl. Ta’n feysht ny sloo. Ta paart dy aghtyn gee kianglt rish gaue sloo ayns
stooidyssyn ta jeeaghyn er sleih harrish traa, agh cha ren y trial share
feddyn vondeish baghtal. Ta’n duillag shoh cur magh y daa chooish, as eisht
t’eh jeeaghyn er ny ta ny smoo scanshoil son persoon ta hannah bio lesh
meenaghtys: gee as giu dy liooar, as taitnys y ghoaill ass.

She fys cadjin t’ayn shoh, cha nee coyrle leighys. Loayr rish fer-lhee ny
dietitian recortyssit roish caghlaa dty vee hene ny bee persoon ennagh elley.
Jeeagh er y [obbyr-medical](/medical-disclaimer/) ain.

## Cre’n bee t’ayn er y vee Mediterranean as y vee MIND?

Ta’n National Institute on Aging (NIA) cur coylley jeu myr shoh
([NIA mychione bee as Alzheimer’s](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-do-we-know-about-diet-and-prevention-alzheimers-disease)):

- **Bee Mediterranean:** palchey mess, lossreeyn, gronney slane, poanrey,
  eeast as bee marrey, as ooil olive, lesh beggan feill jiarg, oohyn as
  reddyn millish.
- **Bee MIND** (Mediterranean–DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay):
  co-chionnal jeh’n vee Mediterranean as y vee DASH son brouttaghey-folley.
  T’eh cur geill er lossreeyn, goaill stiagh ny glassyn duillagagh; smeyryn;
  gronney slane; poanrey; croan; eeast ec y chooid sloo un cheayrt ’sy
  chiaghtin; as ooil olive. T’eh lhiettal feill jiarg, reddyn millish, caashey,
  eeym ny margarine, as bee friyt ny bee tappee.

## Vel ny beeyn shoh cur gaue meenaghtys er lheh?

Ta’n feanish mestit. Ta’n NIA gra dy vel paart, agh cha nel ooilley, stooidyssyn
jeeaghyn kianglt ny beeyn Mediterranean as MIND rish gaue meenaghtys sloo
cosoylit rish bee gollrish bee y Heear, lesh ny smoo feill jiarg, sailley
jeant as shugyr.

Ren Co-chorp obbree Lancet 2024 jeeaghyn er y ronsaghey ny s’doillee
([Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)):

- Va aarlaghey jeh stooidyssyn cohort kianglt caalidys mie bee rish mysh 20%
  gaue meenaghtys sloo.
- Va bun-ronsee smoo jeh three cohortyn kianglt eiyrtys ny sniessey da’n vee
  MIND rish gaue sloo.
- Agh ren stooish Soolynnagh va jeeaghyn er 28,025 persoonyn mean-eashagh son
  mysh 20 bleeaney nagh row gaue sloo lesh beeyn coyrlit ny bee gollrish
  Mediterranean.

Cha vod stooidyssyn jeeaghyn prowal oyr as eiyrtys. Ta sleih ta gee myr shoh
dy mennick anchasley ayns aghtyn elley myrgeddin, gollrish argid,
ynsagh, gleashaght as entreilys da kiarail slaynt. Ren y Cho-chorp obbree y
vriwnys nagh vel feanish dy liooar ayn dy ghra dy vel beeyn slayntoil cur bac
er meenaghtys dy jeeragh, ga dy vel gee palchey mess as lossreeyn as ny sloo
bee lane-obbrit mie son ymmodee staydyn slaynt as goaill orroo gaueyn gollrish
ro-trome, diabetes as brouttaghey-folley ard. Cha ren eh cur bee rish e rolley
jeh [14 gaue](/brain-health/risk-factors/).

## Cre honnick trial bee MIND 2023?

Y phrowal share derrey nish va trial gyn ourys currit magh ayns y New England
Journal of Medicine ayns 2023
([trial bee MIND](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10513737/)):

- **Quoi:** 604 persoonyn 65 bleeaney d’eash as shinney ayns ardjyn Chicago as
  Boston, gyn doilleeidyn smooinaghtyn, va mooinjer faggys oc lesh meenaghtys,
  va ro-trome, as va bee sloo slayntoil oc ec y toshiaght.
- **Cre:** son three bleeaney, ren yn derrey lieh geiyrt er y vee MIND as ren
  y lieh elley geiyrt er nyn mee hene. Va’n daa phossan er nyn ghra dy ee
  mysh 250 calorie ny sloo ’sy laa as ren ad geddyn coyrle dy kinjagh veih
  dietitianyn recortyssit.
- **Eiyrtys:** ren eiyrtyssyn testyn smooinaghtyn bishaghey beggan ayns y **daa
  phossan**. Va’n anchaslys eddyr ny possanyn beg as **cha row eh scanshoil
  dy staitistagh**. Ren scaneyn yn inchyn soilshaghey caghlaaghyn cosoylagh
  ayns y daa phossan myrgeddin.
- **Cooid:** chaill y daa phossan mysh 5 kilogram (mysh 11 punt).

### Cre’n fa nagh ren y trial feddyn anchaslys?

Ren ny ronshee cur magh ymmodee oyryn. Foddee dy ren y possan reirey cur
feabhas er e vee hene myrgeddin, er y fa dy chaill ad yn un towse dy chooid.
Foddee dy jean testyn reesht cur feabhas er eiyrtyssyn er nyn son hene, myr ta
sleih goll rish ny testyn. Foddee dy vel three bleeaney ro ghiare. Va ynsagh
mie ec y chooid smoo jeh ny persoonyn as va ad jeh sluight Oarpagh, myr shen
foddee nagh jean ny eiyrtyssyn bentyn rish dy chooilley pheiagh.

Myr shen, yn fockle oneyr: cha ren y vee MIND roshtyn ny smoo na bee reasoonagh
lesh ny sloo calorieyn harrish three bleeaney. Cha nel shen dy ghra nagh vel
gee slayntoil feeu. T’eh dy ghra nagh lhisagh peiagh erbee jerkal rish bee er
lheh dy haghney meenaghtys.

## Cre mychione bee er lheh as stoo cur-my-ner?

Ta’n NIA gra nagh vel feanish ayn dy vod gee ny shaghney bee er lheh
Alzheimer’s ny meehrys smooinaghtyn bentyn rish eash y haghney. Ta smeyryn
gormey, duillagyn glass as turmeric ooilley er ve studeyrit, gyn prowal. Ta’n
NIA gra myrgeddin nagh vel vitamin ny stoo cur-my-ner erbee prowit dy haghney
Alzheimer’s, as nagh vel fer erbee coyrlit son yn oyr shen. Ta’n NIA cur
raaue dy vod stoo nagh vel prowit, er nyn greck dy haghney Alzheimer’s, ve
neuhauchey, cur argid ersooyl gyn fa, ny y daa red, as dy vod ad cur mess er
leddanyn recortyssit, myr shen jean briaght rish fer-lhee roish prowal fer
erbee
([NIA mychione haghney Alzheimer’s](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

## Kys oddys gee tannaghtyn slayntoil as taitnyssagh?

Son y chooid smoo dy phersoonyn shinney, ta’n kiar dy ve bee ta beayn, ennoil
as taitnyssagh, cha nee plan creoi:

- **Jean bee mygeayrt mysh reddyn s’mie lhiat** veih’n aght Mediterranean:
  lossreeyn, mess, poanrey, gronney slane, eeast as ooil olive.
- **Cum reddyn millish s’mie lhiat.** Ta taitnys as accrys scanshoil, er lheh
  ny s’anmey ’sy vea.
- **Ee marish sleih elley** tra oddys oo. Ta bee cooie marish sheshey mie.
- **Jean aarlaghey bee ry-cheilley.** Ta niee lossreeyn ny stiurey phot
  obbyr vie ry yannoo ry-cheilley; jeeagh er [obbyryn thie](/activities/household-tasks/).
  Foddee resipey lught-thie ennoil myrgeddin cur er ash
  [cooinaghtyn](/activities/reminiscence/).

Cha lhisagh persoonyn shinney giarey calorieyn ny coayl cooid gyn loayrt rish
fer-lhee, as ta gaueyn er lheh ec trostey. Jeeagh er y
[stiu-ghuide trostey](/brain-health/fasting/) ain.

## Kys oddys oo cooney lesh persoon ta bio lesh meenaghtys dy ee dy mie?

Son sleih ta bio lesh meenaghtys, ta gee as giu **dy liooar** dy mennick yn
red smoo scanshoil. Ta’n Alzheimer’s Association cur tastey dy vod sleih
jarrood dy ee, smooinaghtyn dy vel ad er n’ee hannah, ny gennaghtyn fo
lhiettrimys kyndagh rish reihyssyn, as dy vod drogh veeaghey cur er symptoyn
ymmyrkey bishaghey as coayl cooid y chur er
([Alzheimer’s Association mychione bee as gee](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating)).
Ta ny coyrleyn shoh veih’n Alzheimer’s Association, y NIA as yn Alzheimer’s
Society:

1. **Cur bee dy hroggal lesh ny meïryn.** Ta meer veg dy hroggal, gollrish
   meer dy cheeishyn, meer dy chirkey, maidjyn eeast, meer dy oorag ny
   broccoli brisht, cur caa da persoon bee ad hene gyn greienyn.
2. **Jean ymmyd jeh anchaslys er y voayrd.** Foddee caghlaaghyn ’sy raa cur
   er persoon ve doillee bee y veih plait. Foddee plaittyn ny claareenyn
   baney er soie-plait lesh anchaslys cooney. Jean shaghney claareenyn as
   aanaartyn lesh pattrynyn.
3. **Cur un ny daa horch dy vee ec y traa.** Foddee plait lane ve ro
   lhiettrimagh.
4. **Cum traaghyn bee sheeoil.** Cur y çhellveeish ass, cum y voayrd
   aashagh, as cur traa dy liooar. Ta’n NIA coyrlaghey gyn siyr ny broo.
5. **Cur bee veg as greimeenyn dy mennick.** Ta’n NIA coyrlaghey bee ny sloo,
   agh ny smenkey, as greimeenyn lane calorieyn mannagh vel y persoon gee
   dy liooar
   ([Coyrleyn NIA son gee dy mie](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/tips-caregivers-helping-people-alzheimers-disease-eat-well)).
6. **Cooin lesh giu.** Cur cappanyn beg dy ushtey ny joughyn elley trooid y
   laa, marish bee lesh palchey ushtey gollrish mess, anvroie, milkshakeyn
   as smoothieyn. Mannagh vel y persoon geearree ushtey, ta’n NIA coyrlaghey
   dy phrowal soo ooyl, ushtey blastanagh, broth, bainney, ny kaffeey ny tey
   gyn caffeine.
7. **Jean tastey jeh’n chiass.** Foddee nagh jean persoon toiggal dy vel bee
   ny jough ro cheh.
8. **Caghlaa greienyn as cappanyn.** Foddee claareenyn ayns ynnyd jeh
   plaittyn, spainyn lesh laueyn mooarey, as cappanyn lesh coodaghyn gee y
   yannoo ny sassey. Ny jean imnea mychione neatness.
9. **Jean bee ny sassey dy chorraa as dy hluggey.** Jean bee ayns meer veg
   ny cur bee bog. My ta’n persoon cassaghey ny plooghey tra t’eh gee, insh
   da’n fer-lhee.
10. **Jean shaghney broo dy ee.** Ta’n NIA cur tastey dy vel accrys goll er
    lheh dy dooghyssagh ayns ny jerrey keimyn dy Alzheimer’s.

### Cowraghyn dy insh da’n fer-lhee

Foddee coayl cooid, gobbal bee, skeeys ny annooinidys ve cowraghyn nagh vel
persoon gee dy liooar. Ta’n Alzheimer’s Society cur tastey dy vod feeackleyn
ny fiaclaghyn goaill, trimshid, cughteeaght as caghlaaghyn leighys ooilley
cur er accrys goll er lheh
([Alzheimer’s Society mychione accrys](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/poor-appetite-dementia)).
Ta cowraghyn paaish ushtey er nyn rolley ec y NIA goaill stiagh beeal
çhirrym, my-laue, cree tappee as ashlishyn
([NIA mychione doilleeidyn leighys cadjin](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/common-medical-problems-alzheimers-disease-information-caregivers)).

## Loayr rish fer-lhee

Roish jannoo caghlaaghyn mooarey ayns dty vee hene, ny bee persoon ennagh
elley, loayr rish fer-lhee ny dietitian recortyssit, er lheh my ta diabetes,
doghan kidney, doilleeidyn sluggee ny coayl cooid er y gherrid ayd. Son
persoon ta bio lesh meenaghtys, feysht cre wheesh lhisagh ad jerkal rish dy
ee as dy iu dagh laa, as vel dietitian ny stoo cur-my-ner foddee cooney.

Fow cooney tappee my ta persoon plooghey, ny my ta doilleeid keintyssagh
obbragh, my-laue atchimagh ny mee-chraueeaght oc. (Ayns Mannin, cur eam er 999 ny 112.)
