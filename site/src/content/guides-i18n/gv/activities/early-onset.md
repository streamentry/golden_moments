Ta’n chooid smoo dy hushtey mychione meenaghtys goaill rish dy vel yn persoon ayns ny shiaghtadyn ny hoghtadyn oc. Son persoon ta geddyn feysht ayns ny da-eedyn ny jeihyn as da-eed oc, ta’n laa goll er feer anchasley. Foddee ad ve foast ec obbyr, lesh morgage, aeglaghyn ec y thie ny ayr ny moir dy chur tastey daue. Lhisagh reddyn goaill rish y vea aasit shen. Ta’n stiurey shoh cur-my-ner cre ta caghlaa, cre dy hrial, as c’raad oddys sheshee as cloan aasit feddyn cooney.

## Cre ta meenaghtys ec aash aeg?

She meenaghtys ta goaill toshiaght roish 65 bleeaney d’eash eh meenaghtys ec aash aeg, as t’eh enmyssit meenaghtys ec aash aeg ny keayrtyn. Ta [blog yn Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) cur sheese Alzheimer myr red ec aash aeg tra t’eh bentyn rish persoon fo 65, as t’eh gra dy vel “doilleeidyn er lheh” ec kiarail son persoon cho aeg. Foddee ny cowraghyn ve yn un chooid as ayns bea ny s’anzaie, agh cha nel jerkalys, currymyn ny bea heshoil y pheiagh cheddin.

Er-yn-oyr nagh vel eh cha cadjin, foddee sleih fuirraghtyn ny s’foddey son feysht, as foddee lught-thie gennaghtyn nagh vel peiagh erbee oc dy loayrt rish. Ta fys dy vel eiraght y lou ec sleih elley dy mennick gerjagh.

## Cum yn persoon aasit ayns y red

Cha nel sleih aasit lesh meenaghtys ec aash aeg geearree dy mennick dy ve dellit roo myr paitçhyn. Ta’n [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) coyrlaghey reddyn y chaghlaa gys ny reddyn foddee persoon foast jannoo as goaill soylley jeu. Son persoon ayns ny jeihyn as da-eed oc, foddee shen cowraghey:

- **Cum spoyrt ennagh oc,** myr sampleyr obbyr-fuygh, spoyrt, kaartyn, club lioar ny coir, lesh beggan dy chooney ny shirveish ny s’girrey.
- **Shooyl ny markiagh roayr marish carrey,** er raad ta enney mie oc er.
- **Jean bee lught-thie,** as lhig da’n persoon jannoo ny ayrnyn oddys ad.
- **Jean obbyr sharroo ny cur cooney** ayns cowrey t’ad reih, myr sampleyr sortal giootyn ny cooney ec cuirrey scoill.
- **Jean plannyn son troailt** dys boayl ta scanshoil daue, marish carrey as claar aashagh.
- **Cur traa marish oghyn ny inneenyn-’ayrey,** as jannoo gamman ny projeyt ry-cheilley.

Lhig da’n persoon reih. Ta briaght “Cre by vie lhiat jannoo yn fastyr shoh?” ny s’ooasle na reih er nyn son.

## Jean ymmyd jeh kiaull as cooinaghtyn veih ny bleeantyn oc hene

Foddee kiaull as jallooyn veih ny feedyn, jeihyn as feed as da-eedyn jeh persoon cur lesh geurey mie as taggloo aashagh. Ta’n stiurey ain mychione [reddyn kiaull](/activities/music/) soilshaghey magh kys dy yannoo rolley-kiaull, as ta [reddyn cooinaghtyn](/activities/reminiscence/) soilshaghey magh kys dy rheynn jallooyn as skeealyn gyn feyshtyn. Son gamman ny s’aasit da sleih aasit, jean eab er [kaartyn taggloo](/games/conversation-cards/), lesh feyshtyn foshlit mychione obbyr, troailt, caarjyn as lught-thie.

Foddee caghlaaghyn jalloo jeant ass jallooyn lught-thie gobbraghey mie myrgeddin, er-yn-oyr dy vel ny jallooyn jeh sleih as boaylyn ta scanshoil da’n persoon. Ta [caghlaaghyn jalloo](/games/photo-pairs/) freayll ny jallooyn ayns y jeeagheyder er yn ynnyd shen.

## Obbyr, gimman as bea gaghlaa

Ta ymmodee sleih lesh meenaghtys ec aash aeg foast gobbraghey, gimman ny stiurey argid. Cha nel ad shoh reddyn ayns y chooid chadjin, agh t’ad cur er bun cre oddys y persoon jannoo. Loayr rish y fer-lhee oc mychione gimman as sauçhys ec obbyr, as smooinaght my oddys ad cooney y laccal lesh argid ny cuirraghyn. Yn ayrn smoo scanshoil eh ooashley y reayll choud’s ta’n persoon chammah as t’ad geearree ve goaill ayrn.

Son coyrle mychione cliaghtaghyn gaghlaa as tannaghtyn giennaghtagh, jeeagh er [cliaghtey laa meein](/activities/daily-routine/) as [obbyryn-thie myr reddyn](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Cooney son sheshee as cloan aasit

Ta sheshee as cloan aasit dy mennick gennaghtyn ny lomarcan. Foddee kiarail gennaghtyn myr obbyr elley er mullagh obbyr as lught-thie. Ta post blog yn Alzheimer’s Association ginsh dy row possanyn son sleih lesh meenaghtys ec aash aeg ymmydoil da lught-kiarail son cooishyn y laa, myr sampleyr plannal leighoil, as myrgeddin son gennaghtyn dy row ad toiggit. T’eh gra myrgeddin dy vel possanyn cooney son lught-kiarail ec yn Cheshaght dy nastee, goaill stiagh possanyn son sleih ta kiarail son persoon lesh meenaghtys ec aash aeg, as ta three jeu çheet ry-cheilley er-linney Jemayrt as Jecrean.

Dy vriaght mychione possanyn, cur eam er Helpline 24/7 yn Cheshaght ec 800.272.3900. Ta’n [stiurey cooney-lhee son lught-kiarail](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) cur-my-ner aash, seyraaid as cowraghyn jeh skeeys.

## Ta’n stayd foast scanshoil

Ta stayd meenaghtys y pheiagh foast cur er bun cre ta cooney. Sy toshiaght, foddee yn chooid smoo dy leih jannoo ny reddyn cadjin oc lesh caghlaaghyn beggey, as ta tooilley smooinaghtyn ayns y stiurey ain mychione [reddyn ec y toshiaght](/activities/early-stage/). Ny s’anmey, ta reddyn ny s’aashagh as ny s’ennaghtoil, myr t’eh er ny chur sheese ayns [reddyn ec y jerrey](/activities/late-stage/). Caghlaa yn red myr ta’n persoon caghlaa, as briagh daue cre t’ad geearree tra oddys ad gra.
