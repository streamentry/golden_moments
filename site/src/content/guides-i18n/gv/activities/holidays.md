Foddee laaghyn feailley cooinaghtyn gerjoil y chur lhieu, as foddee ad myrgeddin feiyr, çhaghlymyn mooarey as caghlaaghyn ’sy laa cadjin y chur lhieu. Son persoon ta beaghey marish co-chooinaghtys failleil, ta ny ceiliuridyn share dy mennick ny sloo, ny s’kiune as troggit mygeayrt reddyn oddys ad foast goaill soylley jeu. Ta ny raaueyn shoh cooie son Laa Booise, y Nollick, Hanukkah, y Vlein Noa as laaghyn ruggyree lught-thie.

## Kys neem’s laa feailley ny s’kiune y churrym?

Toshiaght lesh cummey laa cadjin y phersoon as jean y laa y churrym mygeayrt y mysh. Ta’n [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/6-steps-to-make-thanksgiving-dementia-friendly/) cur coyrle dy chummal y cheiliurid ny s’leaie ’sy voghrey, er-yn-oyr dy vel kuse dy vriwnys as mee-heastys dy mennick gaase ’syn astyr anmagh as ’syn oie (enmyssit paart dy cheayrtyn sundowning). Foddee lhong ny brishey trostey laa feailley ve ny s’aashagh na jinnair anmagh.

- **Cum eh er y chooid chair.** Ta çhaglym ny sloo as ny s’aashagh, ny aoiee cheet ayns possanyn beggey, dy mennick ny s’aashagh na un voayrd lane.
- **Rheynn ny hobbraghyn.** Jean briaght er mooinjer dy chur lhieu bee, dy yannoo y chooidys ny dy cheau traa marish y phersoon choud’s t’ou aarlaghey bee. Abbyr dy baghtal cre vees cooney.
- **Cum reddyn soiaghey aashagh.** Ta’n [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) gra dy vod soilshyn ta blinkal ve cramp, as dy vod millishyn ny messyn foalsey currit magh myr soiaghey ve goit son bee.
- **Jean briwnys cre dy lhiggey shaghey.** My ta cliaghtey er jeet dy ve stroshey ny gaueagh, ta shen mie dy liooar dy lhiggey shaghey ny dy eiyrt er fer ny s’kiune.

## Cre’n obbraghyn laa feailley oddys mayd jannoo cooidjagh?

Reih obbraghyn as reddyn dy yannoo ta cooie rish ny oddys y persoon jannoo jiu. Ta ve goaill ayrn ’sy laa ny smoo scanshoil na kys ta’n obbyr goll er.

1. **Soie yn voayrd cooidjagh.** Cur magh plaittyn hoshiaght, eisht aanrittyn, eisht cuppyn. Ta filley aanrittyn obbyr mie dy yannoo ny hoie.
2. **Niee as sortal lossreeyn.** Ta niee praaseyn ny brishey poanrey glassey ec y voayrd choggyl sauçhey as ennagh da ram sleih va cliaghtey coooney ayns y choggyl.
3. **Mestey, co-chum ny cur er plait.** Ta mestey soos smeyr, co-chum taishtey aashagh ny cur briosgadyn er plait freayll laueyn goll er foayr veih yn stoyl as skynnyn.
4. **Fill gioot.** Jean y phabyr as y tape y yiarey ro-laue, as lhig da’n phersoon filley as meeley. Cha nel filley neat yn red scanshoil.
5. **Croghey kuse dy reddyn soiaghey.** Reih reddyn nagh vel brishey dy aashagh as un voayl veg, myr sampleyr attey ny billey beg.
6. **Jean obbyr-lauee aashagh.** Ta geiyghyn pabyr, collaish duillagyn ny cairt laa feailley lesh stampyn gobbraghey dy mie. Ta ny smoo raaueyn ayns yn [lanyn obbyr-lauee as ellyn](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).
7. **Gow arrane as eaisht.** Ta carvallyn as arraneyn laa feailley ennagh aashagh dy ghoaill ayrn ayndaue, eer son persoon ta loayrt ny sloo nish. Jeeagh er yn [lanyn kiaullee](/activities/music/) son cur rolley-kiaullee ry-cheilley.
8. **Jeeagh er shenn jallooyn laa feailley.** Cur lhieu albymyn jeh ceiliuridyn shaghey as cuir skeealyn (“Insh dou mychione y voayrd shoh”) ny s’leaie na briaght quoi t’ayns y jalloo. Ta’n [lanyn cooinaghtyn](/activities/reminiscence/) soilshaghey magh kys.
9. **Cloie gamman keayrt cooidjagh.** Ta [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) ry phrental ayns minnidyn as cooie son dagh eash, as ta [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) cur red ennagh da dy chooilley pheiagh dy loayrt mychione.
10. **Jean daah cooidjagh.** Foddee duillagyn [daah aashagh](/games/coloring/) ve prentit son paitçhyn as mooinjer shinney dy rheynn ec y voayrd.
11. **Jeeagh er scannane laa feailley share.** Ta scannane ennagh aashagh, as ta shen mie dy liooar dy yeeaghyn ynrican ayrn jeh.
12. **Gow turys giare ayns gleashtan dy yeeaghyn soilshyn.** Foddee turys sheeoil shaghey thieyn soiaghit ve ny s’aashagh na çhaglym lane sleih.

My va cooishal cooid vooar jeh bea yn phersoon, ta ny smoo saaseyn ayns yn lanyn ain mychione [obbraghyn thie myr obbraghyn](/activities/household-tasks/) dy yannoo obbraghyn coggyl sauçhey as taitnyssagh.

## Kys neem’s aoiee y yannoo aarloo?

Insh da aoiee ro-laue cre t’eh orroo jerkal rish, er lheh mooinjer nagh vel er ve er çheet ayns tammylt. Ta’n Alzheimer's Association coyrlaghey dy hoilshaghey dy vel caghlaaghyn ayns cooinaghtyn as ymmyrkey cheet voish y ghooghys, cha nee voish y phersoon.

- **Rheynn kuse dy choyrleyn aashagh:** loayrt un pheiagh ec y traa, cur traa dy chur jerrey er smooinaght, gyn cur lesh ny çhestynee ny feyshtyn, as freggyrt da aght loayrt y phersoon my ta focklyn doillee dy hoiggal.
- **Abbyr cre ta cooney as cre ta cur seaghyn.** My ta coraaghyn ard, ve siyragh ny cooishyn ennagh cur trimshey, abbyr shen.
- **Cur enney er aoiee ro-laue.** Jeeagh jallooyn jeh ny aoiee marish y phersoon as rheynn skeeal ny ghaa, ny jean çhellvane ny çhellvane-fysseree giare roish y laa.

Ta ny smoo fysseree ayns nyn [n'goyrleyn loayrt](/caregiving/communication-tips/).

## Cre my t’eh cheet dy ve ro hrome?

Jean seomra sheeoil aarloo roish my jig aoiee, ersooyl veih’n veiyr, lesh reddyn co comfortagh ennagh myr blonkey ny jiuley share, ny obbyr aashagh. Cur eh daue dy moghey as dy mennick: “Jean mayd soie boayl ny s’kiune son tammylt?”

Ta faagail y voayrd, goll dy lhie ny s’leaie ny lhiggey shaghey ayrn jeh’n laa coontit myr jerrey mie, cha nee failleil. My ta’n persoon cheet dy ve seaghnit, ta coraa sheeoil, arrane ennagh ny shooyl gys seomra elley dy mennick ny s’cooney na resooney. Ta ny smoo raaueyn ayns yn lanyn ain mychione [mee-heastys ’syn astyr anmagh](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

## Kys oddys mooinjer foddey magh goaill ayrn?

Ta çhellvaneyn-fysseree lhiggey da mooinjer goaill ayrn gyn cur rish y çhaglym. Ta’n Alzheimer's Association coyrlaghey dy chur cummey ennagh er ny çhellvanyn, myr sampleyr goaill arraneyn laa feailley, rheynn jallooyn jeh çhaglymyn shaghey, cooishal yn un resipey cooidjagh ny jeeaghyn er scannane laa feailley ec y traa cheddin. Cum ny çhellvanyn giare, as jean briaght er sleih loayrt un pheiagh ec y traa, er-yn-oyr dy vel eh doillee geaishtagh tra ta ram sleih loayrt ry-cheilley. Ta ny smoo raaueyn ayns yn [lanyn çhellvane as çhellvane-fysseree](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).

My ta’n persoon beaghey ayns kiarail cooinaghtyn, jean shickyr jeh ny reillyn çheet dy yeeaghyn as jean briaght er y lught-obbrey dy chooney çhellvane y chur er bun. Ta’n lanyn ain mychione [çheet dy yeeaghyn persoon ayns kiarail cooinaghtyn](/activities/visiting/) cur stiagh cre dy chur lhieu as kys dy aagail dy kenjal.

## Cre’n giootyn ta cooie son persoon marish co-chooinaghtys failleil?

Ta giootyn aashagh, sauçhey oddys ad goaill soylley jeu nish dy mennick gobbraghey share: bee share, blonkey ny cardigan bog, kiaull ennagh, daiederyn lesh clou mooar, ny albym jallooyn lught-thie. Ta’n Alzheimer's Association coyrlaghey dy haghney greienyn gaueagh, elleaghtyn cramp as gammanyn buird doillee.

Foddee cur giootyn ve cho scanshoil as feddyn ad. Ta cur briosgadyn v’ad er chooney dy hey ayns poagey, ny lhiggey daue gioot v’ou er chionnaghey y illaghey, cur caa daue goaill ayrn ’syn imbagh. Ta gamman jallooyn lught-thie ny raaue elley: ta [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) chyndaa kuse dy jallooyn reiht dys gamman cochiangley meein ’sy jeeagheyder.

## Cur kiarail ort hene myrgeddin

Ta laaghyn feailley cur obbyr er kiarail. Lhig da peiagh elley cur y çhaglym ny cooishal y turkee, abbyr “cha nel” rish reddyn vees doillee, as gow aash choud’s ta feallagh elley marish y phersoon. My t’ou uss ny yn persoon t’ou kiarail er y hon doghan, ta craaghey veih’n raad ny çhellvane-fysseree ny çheet dy yeeaghyn mie dy liooar. Ta ny smoo cooney ayns yn lanyn ain mychione [kiarail persoonagh son adsyn ta cur kiarail](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
