Ta cur daah ny ghooiney cruthtey sassey da sleih ta cummal lesh Alzheimer ny co-inçhynys elley. Cha lhiass eh lhaih, cooinaghtyn, ny freggyrt kiart. Ta laueyn gleashaghey, ta daahghyn çheet magh, as ta jalloo ayn dy loayrt mysh. Ta’n stiurey shoh cur coontey myr dy reih duillagyn, cre dy chur magh, as myr dy chur daah ry cheilley gyn jannoo eh jeeaghyn myr red paitçhey ny myr prow.

## Cre’n fa ta cur daah cooie da ram sleih lesh co-inçhynys

Ta cur daah cur red ennagh da ny laueyn dy yannoo as red ennagh da ny sooillyn dy eiyrt. Foddee eh ve jeant soie ec boayrd ny ayns caairrey liorish uinnag, as t’eh aashagh dy ghoaill toshiaght as dy chur sheese. Ta’n [Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) cur coontey jeh ellyn myr aght dy hoilshaghey ennaghtyn as dy ghoaill soylley jeh’n traa t’ayn, as t’eh gra nagh lhiass eh schleï ellynagh.

Ta’n [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) coyrlaghey dy reih reddyn ta cooie da ny reddyn foddee persoon foast y yannoo, as dy chaghlaa ad myr ta ablidyn caghlaa. T’eh aashagh cur daah y chaghlaa: jalloo ny smoo, crayonyn ny s’thiuickey, ny sloo daahghyn, ny scaaean tap dy chur daah.

## Jean reih jeh jalloonyn son deiney as mraane

Cha nel ram deiney as mraane goaill soylley jeh jallooyn paitçhey. Jean shirrey son:

- **Cooishyn dooghyssagh:** garey lesh blaaghyn mooarey, loghan lesh baatey, ushagyn er banglane.
- **Reddyn ennagh:** pot tey, roar, mess, edd.
- **Pattyryn mooarey, aashagh:** mandala as cummaghyn cronnal lesh kuse dy ayrnyn mooarey.
- **Prentyn ellyn ny cooishyn-thie** foddee yn persoon toiggal veih boayl ny ellyneyder smooie lesh.

Jean shaghney duillagyn lesh ayrnyn beggey, lane, t’ad cur seose. Ta duillagyn prentit son deiney as mraane ny ndoyrtyn mie, as ta [reddyn prentit nastee son co-inçhynys](/activities/printable/) ain lesh duillagyn lesh clou mooar.

## Cre dy chur magh

- **Greienyn aashagh dy chummal.** Ta crayonyn mooarey, markeyryn çhiu, ny penselynn daahit lesh greim lhean ny sassey na pennyn thanney.
- **Palette beg.** Ta tree ny kiare daahghyn dy liooar. Foddee ram reihghyn ve ro lane.
- **Pabyr ny cairt lajer.** Ta pabyr thanney raip as skirraghey. Jean tape er ny corneilyn ny jean ymmyd jeh clipboayrd.
- **Boayrd glen.** Jean seose jeh stoo elley as cur magh un duillag ec y traa.
- **Red ennagh dy dip ny dy vlenney,** myr eaddagh ny sponk, son bree ny s’meeley.

## Cur daah er tablet

Ta’n [cluich cur daah](/games/coloring/) er Golden Moments gobbraghey liorish tap er daah as eisht tap er ayrn jeh’n jalloo. Cha lhiass eh reill laue-ghleck, myr shen foddee eh cooie da sleih ta’n greim oc er jeet ny sloo. Soie marish y persoon dy vod shiu yn scaaean y rheynn, as lhig da reih ny daahghyn. Jean fograghyn ass dy vod yn jalloo tannaghtyn ayns y vean.

## Myr dy chur daah ry cheilley

1. **Toshiaght y chied streean ry cheilley.** Cum y chrayon maree, ny cur dty laue harrish e laue son kuse dy streeanyn, as eisht lhig shaghey.
2. **Loayr mychione daahghyn as boaylyn.** "Shen daah gorrym feer aalin. C’raad t’ou er nakin ushtey jeh’n daah shen?" T’eh cur cuirrey da coloayrt gyn ceistey.
3. **Cur moylley da ny reihghyn, cha nel da’n neatys.** "T’ou er reih daahghyn feer çheh" ta ny share na "T’ou er tannaghtyn cheusthie jeh ny linyaghyn."
4. **Lhig da leeideil.** My by vie lesh scryssey harrish y duillag ooilley, lhig da.
5. **Scuirr tra t’eh scuirr.** Cur ny crayonyn sheese, as cur roish cappan tey ny shooyl.

## Caghlaa myr ta co-inçhynys caghlaa

Sy chied ayrn, t’adsyn dy mennick goaill soylley jeh duillagyn ny smoo cruinn as foddee ad reih nyn ndaahghyn as jalloonyn hene. Sy lhiass ayrn, ta jallooyn ny smoo as ny sloo reihghyn cooney. Sy jerrey, jean eab er un chummey mooar, duillag lesh daah meeley, ny ynrican crayon y chummal choud’s t’ou goaill arrane. Ta paart dy leih goaill ny smoo soylley jeh jeeaghyn er jalloo jeant na jannoo fer.

Son tooilley mychione obbyr cruthtey ec dagh ayrn, jeeagh er [obbyr laue son deiney as mraane lesh co-inçhynys](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) as [prowaltyssyn ellyn son sleih lesh co-inçhynys](/activities/art-projects/).

## Tra dy scuirr

Scuirr my ta’n persoon goll seose, cur y phabyr ersooyl, ny jeeaghyn skee. Ta cur jerrey erreish daa vinnid ny chooney my ren ad goaill soylley jeh. Foddee oo çheet er ash ny s’anmey, ny prowal jalloo elley ny ghooiney elley myr [cloie ennaghtyn](/activities/sensory/).
