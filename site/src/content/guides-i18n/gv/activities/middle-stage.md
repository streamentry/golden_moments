Dy mennick she yn chied stayd mean jeh co-innaghtyn yn fer s'foddey, as shen tra ta obbraghyn toshiaght dy laccal cooney firrinagh. Ta focklyn ny s'doillee dy gheddyn, ta keimyn goll trooid-a-cheilley, as foddee toshiaght er red erbee gennaghtyn doillee. Agh dy mennick ta'n yeearree dy ve ymmydoil as goaill ayrn tannaghtyn. Lesh beggan soiaghey, foddee persoon ayns y stayd mean foast goaill taitnys ass mooarane reddyn, er-lheh obbraghyn ennoil, kiaull as jallooyn jeant lhiat ry-cheilley.

## Cre ny h-obbraghyn ta gobbraghey dy mie ayns co-innaghtyn mean-yrjid?

Ta obbraghyn giare, ennoil, lesh laueyn, brisht ayns keimyn un-ry-hoi, gobbraghey share. Ta'n [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/middle-stage) gra nagh lhiass dhyt reddyn noa y churnaghtyn: foddee obbraghyn ve ayrn jeh bea gaghlaa, lheid as jannoo jinnair ry-cheilley, obbraghey 'sy gharey, clashtyn kiaull ny goll er shooyl.

Ta'n [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) gra dy vel yeearree lajer ec ymmodee sleih 'sy stayd shoh dy ve ymmydoil ny dy chur kiarail er red ennagh, as dy vel cooney orroo dy toshiaght ny dy hannaghtyn er obbraghyn. Ta'n rolley echey son y stayd mean goaill stiagh fillaghey eaddagh niee, niee claareyn, sorçhal stockyn, cleayşhyn aashagh, daunsin lesh kiaull ennoil, shooyl marish fer elley, goaill arrane ry-cheilley, jannoo jalloo aashagh as jeeaghyn trooid albymyn jallooyn.

## Kys t'ou soiaghey obbyr myr shen dy vel eh gobbraghey?

Ta'n chooid smoo dy obbraghyn speeideil ny failleil 'sy soiaghey. Ta ny cliaghtaghyn shoh, jeh'n [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) as y [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/), jannoo yn caghlaa smoo:

1. **Jean yn boayl glen.** Cur yn çhellveeish er y fa as dooin yn dorrys, as cur magh ynrican ny ta ymmydoil son yn obbyr.
2. **Toshiee oo hene.** Jean toshiaght er fillaghey ny cuirrey stiagh eh: «Vod oo cooney lhiam lesh shoh?»
3. **Jeeagh, eisht fuirree.** Jean y chied cheim. Jean cowrey lesh dty laue, myr sampleyr jeeaghyn raad ta'n claare goll.
4. **Cur un cheim ec y traa.** Ta «fillee eh 'sy vean» ny sassey na «fillee ad shoh as cruinnaghey ad rere daah».
5. **Cur daa reih, cha nee feyshtyn foshlit.** Ta «ny towallyn gorrym ny ny towallyn baney?» ny sassey dy reggyrt na «Cre by vie lhiat jannoo?»
6. **Jean yn ayrn doillee oo hene.** T'ou uss towse, t'eshyn cuirrey. T'ou uss cur yn snaie trooid, t'eshyn cassey yn snaie.
7. **Cur fa da.** Ta «ta ny napkynyn shoh son jinnair Jedoonee» cur er yn obbyr gennaghtyn firrinagh.
8. **Lhig da jeeaghyn my by vie lesh.** Ta'n [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) gra dy foddee jeeaghyn ny lomarcan ve taitnyssagh myrgeddin.

## Smooinaghtyn er obbraghyn son y stayd mean

### Obbraghyn ymmydoil

- **Fillaghey towallyn as eaddaghyn beggey.** She ny reddyn beggey, çheh, sassey. Foddee bascaid dy eaddaghyn beggey çheh ve fillit, jeant rea as fillit reesht.
- **Cur stockyn ry-cheilley.** Toshiaght lesh shiartanse dy phaarantyn feer anchasley. Ta sorçhal rere daah ny lomarcan mie dy liooar myrgeddin.
- **Niee ny syrraghey claareyn nagh brishey.** Ta ushtey çheh, soieagh taitnyssagh er ny laueyn. Ta syrraghey ny hoie ny sassey er ny cassyn.
- **Soiaghey yn voayrd.** Cur stiagh un sorçh nhee ec y traa, as soie un ynnyd oo hene myr sampleyr.
- **Cooney lesh jinnair.** Niee praaseyn, raipey liss ny cuirrey ayns bollan. T'ou uss goaill kiarail jeh skynnyn as çhiass.
- **Skeabey, glenney joan ny rubbal.** Eaddagh son y voayrd, skeabey eddrym son y phorch.

Jeeagh [obbraghyn thie](/activities/household-tasks/) son tooilley.

### Kiaull as gliare

- **Goaill arrane lesh shenn arraneyn ennoil.** Toshiaght er goaill arrane oo hene; ta ymmodee sleih goaill ayrn ayns arraneyn t'ad er n'ennaghtyn rish foddey. Jeeagh [obbraghyn kiaullee](/activities/music/).
- **Daunsin dy seyr.** Croymmey, bwoalley bassyn ny gliare er aght erbee ta gennal. T'eh mie dy ve ny hoie.
- **Tilgey baloon.** T'eh gliare dy moal as aashagh dy ghoaill veih stoyl.
- **Goll er shooyl marish fer elley.** Shooyl coorse giare, ennoil ry-cheilley, roie-laue my ta shen cooney.

### Jallooyn as skeealyn

- **Jeeaghyn trooid albym jallooyn.** Jean taggloo er ny t'ou fakin ayns ynnyd jeh cur feyshtyn: «Jeeagh er yn gleashtan shen. Insh dou my-e-chione.» Jeeagh [obbraghyn cooinaghtyn](/activities/reminiscence/).
- **Jannoo ymmyd jeh kaartyn taggloo.** Ta [kaartyn taggloo](/games/conversation-cards/) cur jalloo marish feysht foshlit, gyn quiz.
- **Jeeaghyn er shenn scannane ry-cheilley.** Reih fer ennoil, fuirree faggys as scuirr son aash.

### Gammanyn aashagh, gyn tranlaase

- **Kiartaghyn jalloo lesh jallooyn ry-akin.** Ta [Kiartaghyn jalloo](/games/picture-pairs/) lhiggey da jallooyn tannaghtyn ry-akin, myr shen t'eh mychione cur tastey, cha nee cooinaghtyn.
- **Fow yn un chooid.** Ta [Fow yn un chooid](/games/find-the-same/) jeeaghyn un jalloo as eer daa ny kiare reihyssyn.
- **Sorçhey ry-cheilley.** Ta [Sorçhey ry-cheilley](/games/sort-together/) gollrish sorçhal yn eaddagh niee er y scáileán: crig er jalloo, eisht er e vascad.
- **Bingo jallooyn.** Ta [Bingo jallooyn](/games/picture-bingo/) gobbraghey dy mie son chaglymyn lught-thie as possanyn.

My ta gamman toshiaght dy chur seaghyn er, scuirr as jean red ennagh elley. Jeeagh [kys dy chloie ry-cheilley](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).

## Hyndaa neu-veg dys obbyr

Dy mennick ta fa ec ymmyrkey neu-veg ny my ta persoon jannoo yn un red harrish. Ta'n Family Caregiver Alliance gra dy vod persoon ta follaghey coodagh magh harrish as harrish ve shirrey dy ve gobbraghey as ymmydoil. Ta'n Alzheimer's Association coyrlaghey dy hyndaa lheid y niart dys obbyr cooie, ayns ynnyd jeh cur jerrey er.

| Ny t'ou fakin | Red ennagh dy chur da |
| --- | --- |
| Rubbal ny tayrn er boayrd | Eaddagh fliugh as shirrey dy rubbal y voayrd |
| Cassyn neu-veg ny tappal | Kiaull lesh bwoailley baghtal dy happal ny bwoalley bassyn marish |
| Follaghey magh taffishyn ny coodaghyn | Bascaid dy eaddagh ny eaddaghyn beggey dy hillaghey as dy horçhal |
| Cur yn un feysht reesht | Gerjagh, eisht arrane ny currey dy yannoo ry-cheilley |
| Shooyl mygeayrt | Shooyl ry-cheilley, ny obbyr ta goaill stiagh cur lesh reddyn |
| Neu-veg moghrey-anmagh | Currey feagh, ennoil ayns soilshey mie; jeeagh [neu-veg moghrey-anmagh](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |

## Tra dy scuirr ny dy chaghlaa coorse

Scuirr tra t'ou fakin cowraghyn jeh skeeys, seaghyn, nearey ny mee-haitnys, lheid as gliare beg, frownagh, cur reddyn ersooyl ny gra «cha noddym». Ta'n Alzheimer's Association coyrlaghey dy eabey reesht ec traa elley jeh'n laa ny yn obbyr y yannoo ny sassey. Ny jean eh eginit. Foddee obbal cheet veih aggle ny veih obbyr ta ro ghoillee; jeeagh [tra t'ad gra nagh vel](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

Ta cur jerrey er ro-veg mie dy liooar. Myrgeddin ta soie ry-cheilley lesh cappan dy hey. Myr ta ablidyn caghlaa ny smoo, foddee ny smooinaghtyn ayns [obbraghyn y stayd s'anmey](/activities/late-stage/) ve ny share, as ta ymmodee elley ry-eabey ayns [101 obbyr](/activities/ideas/).
