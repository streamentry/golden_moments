Ta ymmodee lughtyn-thie geearree gamman y chloie ry cheilley ta gennaghtyn myr cooish, cha nee scrutaght. Foddee Feed Feysht as gamman tuairmys elley gobbraghey dy mie, er-yn-oyr dy vel ny reillyn aashagh as foddee yn chooish goll raad erbee. Yn ch trick t’ayn dy hyndaa ny curmyssyn cadjin: ta’n persoon smooinaghtyn er yn ansoor, as ta uss cur ny feyshtyn. Ta’n duillag shoh soilshaghey magh kys dy chloie ny gamman shoh dy meen, as cre ny gamman cooishagh ta cooie son sleih ta cummal lesh meeinaghtys coayl.

## Cre’n fa dy hyndaa ny reillyn cadjin?

Ayns scrutaght cadjin, ta un persoon cur feysht as shegin da’n fer elley toiggal. Ta shen cur yn persoon ta cummal lesh meeinaghtys coayl ayns stayd raad oddagh ad failleil, as foddee failleil ve seaghnagh. Tra t’ad reih yn nhee as ta uss jannoo yn tuairmys, t’adsyn y fer-tushtey. T’ad cummal yn ansoor, t’ad reih tra dy ghra ta ny cha nel, as foddee ad goaill soylley jeh dty akin geabey.

Ta’n [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) coyrlaghey da churrymaghyn dy ymmydey raaghyn giare, aashagh, un feysht ec y traa, as coraa feagh, surransagh. Ta ny cliaghtaghyn shen cooie dy mie son gamman tuairmys.

## Kys dy chloie Feed Feysht, dy meen

1. **Reih cooish** veih bea y phersoon, lheid as y choggyl, y gharey, beiyn ny yn traie.
2. **Lhig daue smooinaghtyn er red ennagh** as gra eh dy kiune, ny taishbyney cart jalloo myr shen nagh lhisagh oo clashtyn eh.
3. **Cur un feysht ec y traa ta goll er freggyrt lesh ta ny cha nel.** “Vel eh ny red t’ou gee?” “Vel eh ny smoo na braag?”
4. **Gow rish dagh freggyrt.** Ta craa kione, craa kione dy vel, ny gearey ooilley coontit.
5. **Jean tuairmyn ass dty hie** as gow soylley jeu. My ta dty huairmys kiart, jean jee ry cheilley.
6. **Cur cowraghyn** tra ta’n persoon jeeaghyn myr dy vel eh ny ish doilleeit: “She red ayns y choggyl eh.”
7. **Ny jean coontey ny feyshtyn rieau** as ny jean score rieau.

Mannagh vod yn persoon geiyrt er ny reillyn, caghlaa gys cummey sassey: enmys kuse dy reddyn as lhig daue cowrey er y fer kiart.

## Reddyn mie dy reih

- Mess, losserey ny blaa mie oc
- Baagh veih’n valley, y soo ny peht va oc
- Greie, lheid as ord, raam ny snaid whaaley
- Peesh thie ny nhee thie
- Boayl ta enney mie oc er, lheid as pairk ny balley
- Peesh eaddagh v’ock dy ghraihagh er ceau

Jean shaghney enmyn deiney, red erbee oddagh trimshey y chur er, as reddyn oddagh ve dangereagh ny aashagh dy chur bun ry cheilley.

## Gamman cooishagh elley

- **Shoh ny shen.** Cur daa reih as briaght cre’n fer s’ferr lhieu: tey ny caffee, ny sleityn ny yn aarkey, goun coar ny goun gorrym. Cha nel ansoor cairagh ayn.
- **Cre’n fer yinnagh oo reih.** Cur feysht meen, eddrym: “Yinnagh oo ny s’ferr lhiat piknik ny dinee mooie?”
- **Cur jerrey er y raa.** Raa ennoil lesh y jerrey faagit foshlit. Jean eab er nyn [gamman cur jerrey er y raa](/activities/finish-the-saying/) son coyrle.
- **Enmys y boayl.** Briaght daue cur coontey jeh shamyr, balley ny reayrt share oc, as cuirrey uss dy smooinaghtyn er myrgeddin.
- **Cartyn lesh feyshtyn foshlit.** Ta nyn [gartyn cooishagh](/games/conversation-cards/) cur feyshtyn foshlit rere cooish, ry phrental son y voayrd.

Son tooilley smooinaghtyn ta tayrnit veih cooinaghtyn as skeeal bea, jeeagh er [feyshtyn cooinaghtyn](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Son aittys myr scrutaght er ny jannoo dy meen, jeeagh er [scrutaghtyn aashagh son sleih shinney ta cummal lesh meeinaghtys coayl](/activities/trivia/), ta ymmydey reihghyn as gyn score.

## Cre my ta’n persoon frustyragh?

Cur stad, gearey, as abbyr red gollrish, “Lhig dooin gamman elley y chloie.” Ta frustyraght ny cowrey dy chaghlaa yn gamman, cha nee dy phrowal ny s’cruaie. Ta nyn goyrle er [kys dy chloie gamman marish persoon ta cummal lesh meeinaghtys coayl](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) soilshaghey magh kys dy chaghlaa reillyn er y chooyl.

## Cre oddys gamman cooishagh y yannoo as ny nod ad y yannoo

Ta gamman cooishagh taitnyssagh as sheshoil. Foddee ad cooney lesh sleih dy ve cochianglt as dy rheynn skeealyn. Cha nel ad cur lheeys da meeinaghtys coayl, cha nel ad moalley eh ny ny lhiettal eh. Freill yn aigney aashagh: traa çheh ry cheilley.
