Ta gamman cur raaghyn er jerrey jannoo ymmyd jeh raagh ennagh ta scuirr roish y fockle s’jerree. Son ymmodee persoonyn, t’eh aght taitnyssagh, gyn aash, dy rheynn traa ry-cheilley. Ta’n gamman gobbraghey er yn oyr dy vel ny focklyn shenn as ennagh, as cha lhiass da’n persoon cooinaghtyn er nhee erbee mychione jiu. Ta’n stiurey shoh soilshaghey kys dy chloie, kys dy reih raaghyn, as kys dy chumail eh sheeoil.

## Cre ta gamman cur raaghyn er jerrey?

She gamman giare eh ayn ta un persoon lhaih y chied ayrn jeh raa as ta’n fer elley cur jerrey er. Ta sampleyryn goaill stiagh «Ta jannoo loay ny s’niartal na...» as «Ta’n ushag moghey goaill y...» Foddee yn persoon y jerrey y ghra magh, cowraghey huggey, ny reih veih jees ny three jerreyyn ta uss cur da. Ta paart dy leih goaill taitnys ayns ve yn fer ta lhaih y chied lieh.

Ta Golden Moments cur shoh myr [gamman cur raaghyn er jerrey nastee](/games/finish-the-saying/) lesh clou mooar. Foddee oo cloie eh myrgeddin gyn scaalhean, liorish yn chied lieh y ghra as fuirraghtyn.

## Kys dy chloie, cammey er cammey

1. **Reih traa sheeoil** as soie ec keim ny sooillyn, jeeaghyn er y persoon.
2. **Lhaih y chied ayrn dy moal,** lesh coraa çheh, as scuirr. Ta’n [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) coyrlaghey dy loay dy moal as dy ymmyd raaghyn giare, aashagh.
3. **Fuirree.** Cur da paart dy hiyraghtyn. Mannagh jig red erbee, cur da jees ny three jerreyyn, as fer jeu kiart.
4. **Jean soiaghey jeh dagh freggyrt.** Ta «She, shen eh» ny «Cha row mee er smooinaghtyn er shen» freayll eh caarjoil.
5. **Loay mychione yn raa.** Briaght cre t’eh er mayrn da, ny raad ren eh clashtyn eh. Shen raad ta’n feeu firrinagh.
6. **Scuirr roish my t’eh skee.** Ta kuse dy raaghyn as garey ny lhieeney lane.

## Cur reihyn gyn prowal

Foddee jerreyyn reih-ymmyrkagh y gamman y yannoo ny sassey, agh foddee ad myrgeddin gennaghtyn myr prowal my t’ad er nyn goyrt myr eab. Cum yn aght aalin. Abbyr «By vie lhiat y jerrey y chlashtyn ny fer y reih?» ayns ynnyd jeh «Cre’n fer t’ayn?» Cur ny reihyn lurg tammylt, cha nee roish my vel caa ec y persoon dy reggyrt.

My ta’n persoon seaghnit ny nearey, lhig shaghey y gamman as gow er. Ta nyn [oyryn loayrys](/caregiving/communication-tips/) soilshaghey kys dy cheaghley yn aght gyn fuss.

## Reih raaghyn ta cooie da’n persoon

- **Jean ymmyd jeh raaghyn y phersoon hene.** Briaght jeh’n lught-thie cre v’ad dy kinjagh gra. Ta raaghyn veih yn aegid dy mennick ny sassey dy chur enn orroo.
- **Reih raaghyn giare, gagh-laaoil.** Ta raaghyn mychione yn emshir, y choggyl, y gharey, lught-thie as ny imbaghyn cadjin as aashagh dy eiyrt orroo.
- **Cum eh plain.** Foddee raaghyn creeney ta croghey er focklyn shenn ny er coyrle ve doillee. Freill ad son persoonyn ta goaill taitnys ayndoo.
- **Jeeagh er y çhengey.** Cha vel ymmodee raaghyn goll er çhyndaa. Jean ymmyd jeh raaghyn ta enney ec y persoon ayns e çhengey hene, ga dy lhiass dhyt feer elley y gheddyn.

## Raaghyn liorish cooish

Ta dagh sampleyr heese soilshaghey y jerrey ayns (crooaghyn) dhyt’s. Lhaih yn chied ayrn ynrican, eisht fuirree.

- **Emshir:** «Fliaghey roish shiaght...» (jerrey: «aalin roish nane-jeig»); «Ta dagh bodjal lesh...» (oirr argid).
- **Coggyl as bee:** «Ro-ymmodee coagyderyn...» (milley yn an-vroie); «Ny jean coontey ny kirkeyn...» (roish my jig ad magh ass ny huill).
- **Thie as lught-thie:** «Ta’n thie raad ta’n cree...» (ayn); «Ta fuill ny s’chee na...» (ushtey).
- **Imbaghyn as garey:** «Ta fliaghyn Averil cur lhieu...» (blaaghyn Boaldyn); «T’ou buinn y red t’ou...» (cuirr).

## Cre mychione persoon nagh vod freggyrt?

Cha vod paart dy leih focklyn y gheddyn ayns ny keimyn s’anmey. Ny jean treigeil yn raa. Lhaih eh, jean craidey, as cuirrey freggyrt lesh cowrey, craa-king, ny liorish jalloo y reih. Ta rheynn rhythm ennagh foast ny hraa ry-cheilley. Jean y gamman marish kiaull, jough çheh, ny rubbey laue, myr ayns nyn [obbyrghyn son ny keimyn s’anmey](/activities/late-stage/).

## Aghyn elley dy ymmyd raaghyn

Ta raaghyn cur lesh taggloo mie mychione shenn laghyn persoon. Foddee oo cuirrey skeealyn lesh coyrleyn veih [ceisteen cooinaghtyn](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), ny cloie gamman cochianglt myr [cardyn taggloo](/games/conversation-cards/). Son ny smoo coyrleyn trooid y laa, jeeagh er [obbyrghyn son persoonyn lesh meayllys cooinaghtyn](/activities/ideas/).
