Ta ram lughtyn-thie shirrey gammanyn er-linney nastee son mee-hushtey er-yn-oyr dy vel ad gearree red ennagh dy yannoo ry cheilley nagh lhiass da shop, loggal stiagh ny kaart credjal. Ta ny reihyssyn share aashagh, bunneydagh er jallooyn, as aashagh dy scuirr. Lhisagh ad gennaghtyn myr traa cooidjagh, cha nel myr prowal. Ta’n stiurey shoh soilshaghey cre lhisagh oo yeeaghyn er e hon, kys dy chur gamman er bun, as cre oddys ny gammanyn shoh y yannoo as nagh vod ad y yannoo.

## Cre ta cur er gamman er-linney ve cooie son mee-hushtey?

Ta gamman cooie son yn chooid smoo dy leih ta cummal lesh mee-hushtey tra t’eh shirrey ennaghtyn ny red ennaghtyn, cha nel cooinaghtyn. Ta cochiangle daa jalloo kiart goll rish y cheilley, bentyn rish y jalloo ta cochiangley rish scadoo, ny cur nhee stiagh ayns y vaskad chair ooilley croghey er cre oddys ny sooillyn fakin nish. Ta gammanyn ta croghey er cooinaghtyn rule, fockle, earroo ny caghlaa roie dy mennick cur er-shaghryn.

Ta’n [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) coyrlaghey obbraghyn ta troggal er cre ta’n persoon foast goaill soylley jeh as oddys ad y yannoo, as ta lhiggey daue bishaghey gyn cooney. Ta’n [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) coyrlaghey dy yannoo obbyr ny sassey ny cur seose urree tra ta ablidyn caghlaa.

## Gammanyn Golden Moments oddys oo foshley ayns jeeagheyder

Ta dagh gamman heese foshley vo’n ynnyd-eggey gyn doonloadey. Ta’n chooid smoo cloie er tablet ny co-earrooder; foddee kuse jeu ve prentit myrgeddin.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Paaryn cochiangley mooarey lesh ny jallooyn ry-akin, as gyn timer ny score. Mie son y chied ghamman.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Un jalloo ec y vullagh, as yn un jalloo mastey daa gys kiare reihyssyn heese.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Ta scadoo meein ry-akin ec y vullagh; bentyn rish y jalloo ta jannoo eh.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Bentyn rish jalloo, eisht y vaskad ta bentyn rish, myr sampleyr coggyl ny garey.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Ta jalloo giarit ayns peeshyn as ta’n slane jalloo ry-akin myr stiureyder.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Raaghyn ennoil lesh y fockle s’jerree ayns shen; bentyn rish reihys ny taishbyney yn jerrey.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Kaartyn jalloo lesh breearyn foshlit son taggloo, cha nel son quizzyn. Foddee ad ve prentit myrgeddin.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Paaryn cochiangley jeant ass jallooyn t’ou reih er dty ghlare. Ta ny jallooyn tannaghtyn ayns y jeeagheyder er y ghlare shen.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Pauley moal, feagh dy hayrn ennal ry cheilley. T’ou goaill toshiaght er as cur jerrey er.

Ta ronsaghey focklyn, cur daah as bingo jalloo ry-gheddyn myrgeddin myr obbraghyn oddys oo prentey. Jeeagh er [obbyraghyn prentit nastee son mee-hushtey](/activities/printable/) son tooilley.

## Kys dy chur shirveish gamman er bun

1. **Reih traa feagh** tra ta’n persoon er ghoaill aash. Ta anmagh fastyr dy mennick ny s’doillee da kuse dy leih, myr shen jeeagh er dty chliaghtey hene hoshiaght.
2. **Jean ymmyd jeh skrin ny smoo.** Ta tablet er stand, ny co-earrooder lesh y jeeagheyder mooarrit, ny sassey dy akin as dy ventyn.
3. **Dooin tabyn elley as cur magh fograghyn.** Foddee pop-upyn doaltattym cur er-shaghryn as cur jerrey er shirveish vie.
4. **Soie marish y persoon, cha nel noi oc.** Ta co-akin gennaghtyn ny smoo goll rish sheshaght as ny sloo goll rish prowal.
5. **Toshiaght lesh un ghamman son queig gys jeih minnidyn.** Lhig da’n persoon bentyn rish y skrin ny cowraghey choud’s ta shiu goaill kiartyn.
6. **Cur jerrey er lesh red mie.** Cur jerrey er cruinnaght, eisht loayrt mychione un jalloo ny cur coyrle son cuppan tey.

## Preevaadjys as sauçhys er glaraghyn cooidjagh

- **Ta jallooyn tannaghtyn er y ghlare.** Ta Photo pairs gymmydey jallooyn t’ou reih, as t’ad tannaghtyn ayns y jeeagheyder er y ghlare shen. Jeeagh quoi elley oddys y co-earrooder ny tablet y foshley.
- **Shaghney kianglaghyn mooie as fys kiart.** Jean ymmyd jeh ny gammanyn dy jeeragh vo’n ynnyd-eggey, as ny jean crig er bratteeyn ny breearyn ta shirrey fys persoonagh.
- **Freill eeckaghyn as coontaghyn er lheh.** Ny faag y persoon nyn lomarcan er skrin ta shirrey fockle-follit ny earroo kaart.
- **Cur tastey da shooyl ersooyl.** Freill tablet ass roshtyn my oddys y persoon faagail y thie lesh.

Son rolley-jeeagh ny smoo, jeeagh er [kys dy reih appyn as gammanyn son mee-hushtey](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) as [cur tablet ny foon er bun](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Cre oddys ny gammanyn shoh y yannoo as nagh vod ad y yannoo

Ta gammanyn er-linney cur caa da sleih lesh mee-hushtey soylley y ghoaill jeh traa, jeeaghyn er jallooyn ry cheilley, as goaill ayrn ayns red ennagh gyn tranlaase. Cha vel ad shirrey mee-hushtey, moalley eh, ny lhiettal eh. Sampleyr ta er ny ve imraait dy mennick eh [tohiaght yn Federal Trade Commission ayns 2016 noi Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) mychione graaghyn traenal inchyn nagh row ad abyl dy phrowal. Bee er dty hwoaie noi gamman, app ny ynnyd-eggey erbee ta gialdyn cooinaghtyn ny share, cur score da dty inchyn ny gra dy vod eh lheimyraght y hyndaa.

Jean ymmyd jeh ny gammanyn shoh myr ayrn jeh laa ta goaill stiagh myrgeddin kiaull, shooyl, taggloo as aash. Son lhaih mychione kys dy yannoo gammanyn feagh ny sloo chroie, jeeagh er [kys dy chloie gammanyn marish persoon ta lesh mee-hushtey](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) as [gammanyn cooinaghtyn nagh vel prowaltagh](/activities/memory-games/).

## Tra dy scuirr

Scuirr tra ta’n persoon jeeaghyn skee, neu-veeley, er-shaghryn ny neu-chiart. Scurir tra ta anaase er choayl oc. Ta scuirr ro-veg ny eiyrtys mie. Foddee oo dy kinjagh cheet er ash laa elley, ny caghlaa gys taggloo ny shooyl ayns ynnyd jeh.
