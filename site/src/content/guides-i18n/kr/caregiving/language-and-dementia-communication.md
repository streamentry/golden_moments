Kam tǝlam falnzǝ kozǝna manajin, dementia-a kalma matǝ au asutǝ faltǝgǝna, mana cadǝnza faltǝyin. Kam laa tǝlam burwon kǝrǝngata-a faidatǝ badizayin; laa faidatǝnyi. Nzǝrǝwo ngǝla-a adǝ: tǝlam karmadǝ ngǝla-a manano, kuru kamdǝbe-a ga bǝlin.

[Alzheimer's Society-a tǝlam ra’anaa gadegade mbeji kuru kamdǝ faidatǝnanzǝ tǝlamdǝ-a faidatǝro shawari cina](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association-a tǝlam bǝlinzǝbero walta-a mana faltǝna fal futu fǝlezǝna](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Faltǝna adǝ kam ndusoro wakkǝnyi, kuru shawa kamdǝbe-a fǝletinba.

## Tǝlam fi-a faidatǝgǝkke?

Kamdǝbe-a mana adǝro tǝlam fi faidatǝro ra’anaa manano. Ra’anaa nzǝ kǝla mana, na-a au loktu kawu-a faltǝyin. Tǝlam faltǝnaa, adǝ-a jarabu futu tǝma au tǝlam ni asunǝma-a dǝgaaro tǝma.

Tǝlam ra’anaanzǝ-a notǝnama, kalma notǝna faidatǝ kuru loktu jaawurowo yin. Kalma gana suron notǝnama, adǝ-a jire gulzǝne kuru alamaram, awo au foto-a faidatǝne. [Bayan tǝlam Alzheimer’s Society-be](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language)-a, kǝndaram tǝlam-a kawu ndusoro gadegade walzayin kuru bana, kaziyi au dondi-a asutǝyin.

## Mana kǝla kawu-ayen awi bana cadiyin?

Shawari mana cadǝbe adǝ-a hangalro gǝnatǝ, kuru bana tǝlam kamdǝbe-aro dagatǝ:

- **Kalma-a su-a notǝna faidatǝ.** Kamdǝ au kam amintǝnanzǝ-a kǝla awo kawu-abe, yalla-a au adǝ-a kalma fi-a faidatǝnza manano.
- **Fǝletǝ kuru mane.** Kofi fǝletǝ, awo indi kartǝne, au foto au awo notǝna faidatǝne. Kalma ngǝla matǝ tǝssǝna, alamaram bana cadiyin.
- **Kǝnta.** Kamdǝ-a loktu fanoro au jaawurowo yin. Mana nduso nzǝkko-a kǝlasǝnyi au koro adǝ-a tǝlam gadegadelan waltǝnyi.
- **Loktu fallon mana fal.** Bayan kuruwa tǝlam ndusoro tǝssǝyin. Mana gǝrtǝ fal mane, kǝnta, kuru awima kura-a manazǝna rui.
- **Fanotǝ-a kuru rui-a kulasne.** Karau ndǝgǝlam, awo fanotǝbe, nur ngǝla au na cidiyiya tǝlam-a asutǝro ngǝla sadin.

Kamdǝ karantǝ ro ra’anaa, labar ruwo-a mǝradǝbe tǝlam nzǝkkolan mbejiya manano. Kǝla shawari lafiya, amartǝ au bayan kǝndawu kǝndawu-be-a fassara kǝla mashinbero runzǝro tawakkul tǝnyi; labar mǝradǝbe-a cidawu lafiya-beladǝn tabbatǝne.

## Kamdǝ ra’anaanzǝ tǝlamdǝ-a notǝniya, abi diyǝ?

Sawa amintǝna tǝlam indi nozǝna mana kǝla kawu-ayen kǝltǝro ra’anaa manano. Kamdǝ wunduma kǝla tǝlam fasartǝro ra’anaa futu tǝma; alamaram, foto au tǝlam gade sandi indin nozǝna ra’anaa cadiyin.

Kǝla luwoye lafiya au mana gade bayanna jire mǝradǝna, cidawa-a kawu manazǝne. Tǝlam kamdǝ ra’anaa gulzǝne kuru bana tǝlam fasartǝma mbejiya manano. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services-a bayan cina](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) kǝla yalla au sawa laa labar lafiya-a jirelan fasartǝro tǝssǝyin; cidawa-dǝ bannanzǝ-a au sharudǝnza-a bayan cadiyin. Kamdǝ wunduma bana-ma kǝltǝro kartǝyin, kuru cidawa-dǝ bana tǝlam mǝradǝna-a dabbargǝyin.

Luwoyedǝ, kamdǝro manane kuru tǝlam fasartǝma-a loktu manaro yin. Kamdǝ koro adǝ-a asutǝna kuru kalmanzǝlan jaawuro daraja mbejiya tabbatǝne. Bana-ma, kamdǝ ra’anaa run bayan gade yirǝne.

## Sa’ayin shawari lafiya matǝgǝkke?

Tǝssǝna tǝlam-a loktulan faltǝyin, amma faltǝna sadin fal gade. [NHS-a shawari cina kǝla kam fal hangal nzǝ sadin faltǝna cidawa lafiya-be-a sadin matǝro](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Mana cadǝ-a sadin dawu adǝ-a shawa dementia-be futu tǝma. Kamdǝ sadin hangal nzǝ faltǝna, zauro dondi au fando-a tǝssǝna, cidawa lafiya-be mǝradǝbe na nǝmben-a lezǝne.

Faltǝna ngawon zau, awi rumma ruwo kuru likita kamdǝbe-a shawariro manano. Tǝlam fi faidatǝna, awi mana nzǝ-a banazǝna, kuru bana-a au dondi-a mbejiya ruwo. Adǝ bayan manabe, shawari lafiya-ba. [Kǝla luwoye likita-be](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) koro dabbartǝro bana cadiyin.

## Kam bana gade cin-a awi gulgǝkke?

Amartǝ kamdǝbelan, ruwo gǝrtǝ kam bana-a ciza ro ra’ananzǝro gǝnatǝ. Tǝlam au karin tǝlam mana-au karantǝbero ra’anaa, kalma-a su-a notǝna, kuru awi mana nzǝ-a asutǝro banazǝna-a kǝltǝyin. Luwoye au kǝla kǝndobe-ayen wunduma kǝltǝro ra’anaa manano. Labar sirri bana-ro mǝradǝtǝni-a ruwonyi, kuru wunduma ruwo adǝ-a ruyin gulzǝne. Ruwo tǝlam-be mana cadǝ-a ngǝlaro badizǝro bana cadiyin; amma mana lafiya tǝssǝnaro tǝlam fasartǝma-a badǝnza.

## Futu ra’anaa kamdǝbe-a banagǝkke?

Tǝlam shawa kam fal-a kǝltǝ nasha-a, amma kamdǝ sammaso-ba. Kǝla adǝnzǝ, kasuwa nzǝkko, karantǝ ra’anaa au kam sandilan kǝltǝro ra’ananzǝ-a manano. Kamdǝ amartǝnaa, kalma au mana bana ciza kam bana gade cin-aro ruwone kuru futu faidatǝdǝ-a bayanne. Ruwo-a sirrin gǝnatǝ kuru kamdǝ amartǝna run-a yade.

Kamdǝ-a jarabtǝro, kam gadero fasartǝro au awi asutǝna-a fǝletǝro dǝgaaronyi. Mana laa tǝlam fallan ngǝla cadiyin, laa tǝlam gadelan. Ngǝla, sirri au ra’anaa kamdǝbe tǝlam fallan dagaro-a kozǝna.
