Ambe kǝltǝram-a kǝndoram-a ro alaktǝna, adǝye saa ngawo-a lan faltinba. Kulashiye fǝlezǝna kǝla saa ngawo-a, kǝndaram-a cida-a dementia samno-a ngǝwuro sǝkkǝna, kuru hangal kǝrzǝye-a kuru hangal kǝnji bawo-a ro. Kulashiye dǝye sǝdǝn sayin adǝye ngǝwunza ndaye sǝbab gǝnyi au faida. Shafi adǝye bayanna dǝye fǝlezǝna, futu kǝltǝramro kǝndoram-a, kuru awo kǝltǝramro dio-a am dementia-a kǝnzanama ro ndaye faidajin.

## Kǝndaram-a cida-a dementia-a sǝkkǝna wa?

Aa, kulashi ngǝwu sǝkkǝna, amma futu-nzǝye cistǝna gǝnyi. [Lancet Commission 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) cida-a saa ngawo-a kǝla awo 14 dementia samno-a faltǝrambo lan tilo ro gotǝna. [Bayanna UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) adǝye kǝla lardǝ 5% yero dǝniya sammaa lan fǝlezǝna.

Bayanna dǝye fǝlezǝna:

- **Zǝkkǝ gana, samno ngǝwu.** Commission kulashi indi-a bayannazǝna, kǝltǝram gana-a kuru dementia samno ngǝwu-a sǝkkǝna. Tilo samno kǝla 57% yero samzǝna, tilo kǝla 18% yero.
- **Kǝndaram kǝrma.** Kǝndaram, maanaa kǝla kǝnji cidatǝna-a, shi lan dementia samno ngǝwuro sǝkkǝna kulashi ngǝwu-a suro-nzǝn.
- **Ruyi Surgeon General.** Manta May 2023 lan, Surgeon General US [bayan kǝla kǝndaram-a cida-a lan](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) sǝdǝna. Kǝndaram-a cida-a kǝrma-a am ngawo-a ro dementia samno-a kǝla 50% yero samzayin yen gulzǝna. Kǝltǝram ngǝla bawo-a shi lan hangal kǝrzǝye samno kǝla 29% kuru stroke samno kǝla 32% yero sǝkkǝna.
- **Ndaye ngǝwuwa.** [Bayanna National Academies](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557972/) fǝlezǝna kǝla am saa 65-a kozǝna Amerika-a suro-nzǝn, 24% yero cida-a ro kǝnzana.

Faktǝ kǝla faidaa: Lancet Commission bayannazǝna kǝla am laaye dementia fǝrzǝna saa-nzǝn awo kǝltǝrambe-a ganazayin. Adǝye maanaa laaye kǝla-a dǝye cida-a sadin, cida-a runzǝ samno-a sadin gǝnyi. Bayanna dǝye ngǝwuso kǝla ruyi ro.

## Kǝndaram-a cida-a faraknzǝ fin?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/loneliness-and-social-isolation/loneliness-and-social-isolation-tips-staying-connected) adǝgaima bayannazǝna. **Cida-a** maanaa am ngǝwu-a kǝltǝramro zǝkkǝram bawo-a au zǝkkǝram gade-a. **Kǝndaram-a** kǝla kǝnji kǝndoram bawo-a au cidatǝna-a. Kam tilonzǝ kǝnzan kǝnji ngǝla ro kǝnzayin, kuru kam jama ngǝwu-a suro-nzǝn kǝndaram fanjizayin.

NIA awo adǝye-a samno-a samzayin ro fǝlezǝna:

- Fǝno-a au ruyi-a hangal mbeji
- Lele-a hangal au mota bawo
- Tilonzǝ kǝnza, au awo kura bawo-a (misallo kamaye bawo au cidawa koltǝ)
- Taktǝ-a au hangal kǝrzǝye-a faltǝ
- Kam dondi bawo-a kǝnana
- Kungǝna bawo au tǝlam hangal

Fǝno-a faida ngǝwu. NIA bayannazǝna kǝla am fǝno-a ngǝla bawo-a laaye cidazayin, dalilnzǝ mana-a hangalnzǝ bawo au nanguwanzǝ. [Shafi hangal kǝrzǝye-a kuru fǝno-a ye](/brain-health/hearing/) kǝla fǝno ruyi-a kuru bana fǝno-a ro bayannazǝna.

## Futu kǝltǝramro kǝndoram-a

NIA shawari ngǝwu-a kǝnzǝna. Tilo au indi-a nǝmro kǝskǝna-a faltǝ:

- **Zǝkkǝ samma ro dǝ.** Loktu samma ro kǝla yimbi au manta sammaa ro bowotǝ, sǝmo, email au katab lardǝbe kǝla am fado-a, sawa-a au kǝriwabe-a ro.
- **Video zǝkkǝ au naura waye faidatǝ.** Naura dǝye bǝlinro waljiya, lardǝye karatu bǝwobe-a kuru cidawa lardǝbe-a ngǝwuso darasǝ kǝnzayin. [Shafi bowotǝ-a video-a ye](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) rui.
- **Awo tiloro ga.** Darasǝ, jama addini, lele jama au cidawa lardǝye kǝltǝram-a faidajin.
- **Awo ngawo-a kǝrma-a badiyaram** au awo bǝlin am gade-a ro kǝliso.
- **Am gade-a ro tǝnama.** Sawa-a au jama-a kǝltǝramro tǝnama. [Shafi tǝnama ye](/brain-health/exercise/) futu fǝledu-a kǝnzǝna.
- **Dal sǝtǝma** tǝma runǝm-a kǝnanaro yeyiyaa.
- **Bana lardǝbe faidatǝ.** Cidawa ngawo-a lardǝbe, Area Agencies on Aging kuru Eldercare Locator (800-677-1116) futu-a kuru bana-a fǝlezayin.

## Awo kǝltǝramro dio-a dementia-a ro ndaye faidajin?

Kam dementia-a kǝnzanama ro, kǝltǝram-a kǝla kǝnji, kǝskǝ kǝnji-a kuru suro-nzǝn kǝnza-a ro, samno-a ganatǝro gǝnyi. Lancet Commission bayannazǝna awo-a am dementia-a kǝnzanama-a kǝnji-a kuru faida-a kǝltǝramro dio-a ro banazayin. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) bayannazǝna, kǝnji kǝskǝbe-a samzǝyin ba, awo-a laaye hangal bawo-a (misallo lele-a gade au hangal kǝskǝ bawo) ganazayin. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) shi lan bayannazǝna kǝla am Alzheimer-a kǝnzanama-a tǝnama-a kuru awo kǝnjibe-a dio-a mbeji.

Awo ngǝla-a:

1. **Foto-a kǝltǝramro rui.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) kǝriwu kasuwu bawo-a kǝnzǝna, misallo "Wulse..." [Shafi taktǝ ye](/activities/reminiscence/) rui.
2. **Kǝltǝramro bǝri.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) foto-a fǝlejin kuru loktu au lamba bawo, adǝye nanka hangal kǝskǝ bawo. [Shafi futu kǝltǝramro bǝri](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) rui.
3. **Foto fado-a faidatǝ.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) foto nǝm kǝrtǝna-a bǝriro faltǝyin, kuru foto-a naura-nǝmben kǝnzan.
4. **Am-a sammaa ro kǝltǝ.** [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) fado-a kuru [jama cidawa lardǝbe-a](/activities/care-home-groups/) ro ngǝla.
5. **Yim, cidawa fado-a au kulo-a kǝltǝramro dǝ.** [Awo yimbe](/activities/music/), [cidawa fado-a](/activities/household-tasks/) kuru [awo-a sammaa](/activities/ideas/) rui.
6. **Kǝram-a fǝleduro dǝ.** [Shafi kǝram ye](/activities/visiting/) kǝram gajim-a fǝledu-a ro bayannazǝna.

Awo-a gana-a fǝledu-a ro dǝ. Nasha fǝledu-a kam tilo au indi-a ro, jama ngǝwu-a ro ngǝla. Kam dǝye kǝngatǝ au faida bawo fanjiya, dawtǝ ngǝla. Kǝltǝramro napkǝ sha-a ro kǝlanzayin shi ma ngǝla.

Kam dementia-a kǝnzanama tilonzǝ kǝnzanama ro, NIA shawari kǝnzǝna kam amanatǝma kǝramro au video ro zǝkkǝ samaa-a kuru loktu lawalaye ro kǝrambo-a cistǝ. Shi lan memory cafe lardǝbe, na fǝledu-a am taktǝ bawo-a kuru fado-nzabe-a ro, bayannazǝna.

## Am kǝnanabe shi ma kǝndaram fanjizayin

NIA kam dondi bawo-a kǝnana-a cida-a samno-a ro fǝlezǝna. Loktu gana-a sawa-nǝmbe, jama bana-a kuru tusu-nǝmbe ro go. [Shafi kǝnana kǝlanza ye](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) shawari faidaa kǝnzǝna.

## Luwotǝma-a ro manǝ

Nyi au kam nǝm kǝnanama-a kǝndaram au cida-a loktu ngǝwu-a ro fanjiya, luwotǝma-a ro wulse. NIA am-a bayanna faltǝ kura-a (misallo kamaye bawo) luwotǝma-a ro gulzaro shawari kǝnzǝna, luwotǝma-a kǝla sammaa nojinro. Kǝnji bawo-a manta ngǝwu-a ro kǝnza, shi hangal kǝnji bawo (depression) waljizayin, kuru bana mbeji. Kam ngawo sawa-a au awo kǝnjibe-a koltǝna-a cidazǝna, faltǝ adǝye luwotǝma-a ro gulzǝ faidaa. [Alama fǝrzǝna-a kuru saa luwotǝma-a ruyinma-a](/brain-health/warning-signs/) rui.

Shafi adǝye bayanna amma ro, shawari luwotǝye gǝnyi. [Bayanna luwotǝye yede](/medical-disclaimer/) rui.
