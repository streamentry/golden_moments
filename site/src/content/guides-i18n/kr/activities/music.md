Sauti awo kalkallo ro kam dementia-a kǝla nzasobe-a rokko sawandin. Awo tamaro kǝltǝnyi: jaawu kalkal bawo, futu cidibe mbeji bawo. Sauti notǝna kǝnǝgǝri, shi lǝgǝrma, au kǝla gǝname rokko yirta kutǝro banaajin, yalla manabe kǝskǝ ma. Awo mǝradǝnǝm sauti kam adǝbe-a kalkallo kuru loktu nafsu cistǝma lan fanjǝro.

## Cidawa sautiye futube am dementia-a kǝla nzasobero faidajin?

Fanjǝ, yirko, mukko bǝkko, lǝgǝrko, kuru sauti-a kǝla manabe samma ngǝla cidin. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) sauti fanjǝ, sauti-a kuru yirmabe-a kǝla manabe, sauti notǝna lan yirko au biyabe, kuru cidawa sautiye jamabe-ye ro letǝ shawariyin. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ye lǝgǝr, misallo mukko bǝkko au biyabe, kǝji-a sǝkǝro banaajin gulzǝna, kuru sauti kǝla manabe kǝskǝnǝ ngawo-a ye kǝltǝram ro waljin.

## Futu playlist nasha nǝmngalibe tǝtǝnǝmi?

Awo ngǝla faidatǝma tǝtǝnǝm, shi playlist sauti kam adǝbero maana-a tǝdǝnabe. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ye sauti maana nasha-a tǝdǝnabe-a bayanzǝna. Adǝ futu kalkallo lan tǝtǝnǝmi.

### Kǝla nǝmngalibenzǝ lan burone

- **Saa ngǝla nǝmgana-be saa fiyakkǝnbe.** Sauti radiyo lan, biyaram lan, kuru film lan loktu ngǝla nǝmgana-be-a taktǝne.
- **Loktu kura kura.** Sauti larusabenzǝbe, biya burwoyebe, au album bǝlinzǝ yalla-be ngǝlaro suronzǝna.
- **Adin kuru al'ada.** Yir adinbe, gospel, sauti tayir, au sauti tǝlam burwoyenzǝ lan.
- **Cida kuru askǝr.** Band askǝrbe, sauti cidabe, au sauti loktu askǝrbe-a kǝltǝma.
- **Awo yalla tǝkǝna.** yanjugǝna, tadawa, kuru sawa ngǝla-a korowo awo sandi sǝbsǝ fanjǝ au yirjǝ ro.

### Jinsin sautiye lan taktǝne, sauti fal fal gǝna genyi

Ndarro burwonǝm nonǝmnyi ma, adǝnbe fal fal minti gǝname lan jarabtǝne kuru futu kambo cidin ro rui: big band kuru swing, doo-wop, Motown kuru soul, rock and roll burwoye, country kuru western, bluegrass, gospel kuru yir adinbe, Broadway show tunes, jazz standards, sauti Latin misallo boleros au mariachi, polka, folk, kuru classical. Awo kǝnǝgǝri au shi lǝgǝrma kutǝnabe gǝnatǝne; awo fǝskanzǝ badizǝnabe kolne.

### Fanjǝnzǝ kalkallo tǝdǝne

- **Ads bawo.** Alzheimer's Association ye ads-a fasar kutǝro banaajin gulzǝna. Sauti download tǝdǝna, CD, record, au cidaram ads bawo faidatǝne.
- **Awo fal kalkallo.** Speaker kǝmbu kura fal-a, au playlist dabbarrata gǝnata, kam ndusoye burwonzǝro.
- **Jeriwu indi gajere.** Jeriwu fal nafsu cistǝro, fal ye nǝmngalibe fǝletǝro.
- **Sunonzǝ ruwo.** Suno kambe-a kuru "sauti ngǝlaro suronzǝna" awo fanjǝrambe lan ruwo, yalla isǝna-a kuru cidama-a ye faidatǝnzaro.

## Cidawa sautiye 15 jarabtǝro

1. **Rokko fanne.** Kǝrǝngenni-a napne, awo gade bawo. Fanjǝ cidawa kalkal.
2. **Rokko yirne.** Nyi burone yirne. Sautibe lan yirko au cin lan yirko ye ngǝla.
3. **Mukko bǝkko au lǝgǝrko.** Ngurum bǝkko, tebur bǝkko, au drum gǝname au shaker faidatǝne.
4. **Biya lǝptǝram.** Mukko tǝne kuru kǝndǝga lan lǝgǝrne, au napkada lan biya de, mukko rokko lǝgǝrne.
5. **Biya scarf lan.** Scarf lǝptǝram sauti-a kǝlanzǝ lan lǝgǝrne, daada au napkada lan.
6. **Sauti-a kǝla manane.** Bowotǝne, jarabtǝne genyi: "Adǝ sauti biyarambe gai. Biyarabe-a kǝla wulsǝgǝne." "Sauti adǝ tǝkǝnǝm wa?" lan korone genyi.
7. **Album kalkal fanne.** Record ngǝlaro suronzǝna kalkal fǝletǝne, burwo-a daryero, kahawa-a rokko.
8. **Gǝnama albumbe rui.** Gǝnama record ngǝla-a kazǝmu, mota, kuru fǝska-a kǝla manabe ro ngǝla.
9. **Musical rui.** Film musical au concert loktunzǝbe ngawo kura kǝjiro. Dazǝne nafsu tǝro.
10. **Awo sauti nonzǝna yirne.** Kam piano au guitar kurun yirzǝna, yirko gǝreskǝ suronzayin. Ngawo-a, notǝ gǝname laa rokko yirko ye ngǝla.
11. **Cida sadin sauti-a rokko.** Sauti ngǝlaro suronzǝna fanne kazǝmu kapkǝram sadin au tebur dabbartǝram sadin."
12. **Sauti bǝriye.** Yimbi samma sauti nǝmngalibe fal lan burwone, adabe ro."
13. **Yir adinbe au sauti tayir yirne.** Sauti adinbenzǝbe fanne kuru rokko yirne.
14. **Jamawu yirrambero gaane.** NHS ye jamawu yirrambe nǝmngalibe kuru kǝndǝrambero faidajin gulzǝna. Jamawu yirrambe am dementia-a kǝla nzasobero na-a ndaran koro."
15. **Sauti cidi ro jamabe lan lene.** Concert jamabe gajere au band deyan. Kǝrǝngenni kǝnuwube-a napne kuru manǝmbe loktu samma lene.

Cidawa kǝla manabe kǝjiro, [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) jarabtǝne, nadǝ darinzǝ sa-a fǝletǝnǝm.

## Sauti-a loktǝbero kalkallo

Alzheimer's Association ye sauti faidatǝne nafsu manǝm tǝdǝro shawariyin: sauti nafsu cistǝma nafsu kǝjiro, kuru sauti nǝmngalibe loktu ngǝla nǝmgana-be nǝmngalibe fǝletǝro.

| Loktu | Jarabtǝne | Shawari |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinǝm | Sauti notǝna, nǝmngalibe | Yimbi samma sauti fal-a faidatǝne alamet gǝreskǝ ro waljin |
| Cida | Sauti nǝmngalibe | Sauti cida-a sawa ro tǝdǝro banaajin |
| Kǝmbu | Sauti awo bawo lǝptǝram | Lǝptǝram kolne, kǝmbu-a rokko gǝnyi |
| Kajiri darye | Sauti nafsu cistǝma, notǝna, nur ngǝla lan | [late-day restlessness](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) rui |
| Bǝne | Sauti lǝptǝram, gǝreskǝ | Sauti-a kuru nur-a rokko lǝptǝrne |
| Ngawo darye | Sauti ngǝlaro suronzǝna, lǝptǝram ro | Kǝrǝngenni-a napne kuru mukko tǝne |

## Shawariwa kǝji ngǝlaro

- **Sauti gade datǝne.** Alzheimer's Association ye windo-a kuru kǝnuwu-a zaktǝne, kuru TV-a datǝne shawariyin, sauti runzǝ-a faltǝro.
- **Sauti lǝptǝram gǝna kolne.** Sauti kǝji-a naptǝna, kura genyi. Kambo awo fanjǝram fanjǝnzǝ-a faidatǝna ma, mbeji-a kuru cidi-a rui; [hearing](/brain-health/hearing/) rui.
- **Sauti karǝgǝ kutǝnabe-a rui.** Sauti laa kǝnǝshi, kǝriwu, au batti kutǝro banaajin. Sauti kambo karǝgǝnza badizǝna ma, kǝndǝram yite kuru faltǝne.
- **Shi-a kolne burwonzǝro.** Dalandǝ, kambo sauti-a kartǝ kolne, kuru "a'a" nzǝ-a ye ngǝla ro gone.
- **Loktu samma dazǝne.** Minti uwu sauti fanjǝ cidawa ngǝla. Dazǝne kuru nafsu lan napkǝ ye ngǝla.

## Kulashi sauti-a kuru dementia-a kǝla awi guljin?

Dalil mbeji-a gana. [2025 Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) kulashi 30 cidawa sautiye mbeji-a cidi-a kǝla ruiwono, ngawonza jamawu lan fato kǝndǝrambe lan. Sandi cidawa adǝ-a karǝgǝ nǝmngalibe-a gǝname cistǝro banaajin tǝdǝna, amma karǝgǝ tǝwartǝ, kǝndǝram ngǝla, au hangal-a kuru tǝkǝne-a kǝla tasarru bayyine mbeji, kuru cidawa adǝ-a datǝna ngawo-a ye tasarru mbeji.

Andi sauti-a sawandin, dalilnzǝ awo kǝji, kǝskǝ bawo rokko loktu sawandoro, ngǝla kura kura wo. Cidawa jamabe ro, [care home group activities](/activities/care-home-groups/) rui; sauti-a foto-a kuru labar-a rokko faidatǝro, [reminiscence activities](/activities/reminiscence/) rui.
