Dementia burwoyen, am kada-a kǝndaramnzǝ buro-a alaktǝnza-a ro napcǝna. Cida cǝdin, cidawa ngǝla cǝdin, sawaanzan lezǝyin kuru kawunzǝye ngǝwunzǝ kǝlanzan rotǝyin. Cidawa dǝ ngawon faltǝnzǝro mbeji gǝnyi; faltǝ gana mbeji kǝla nzundo-a ro cǝdoro. Cidanzǝm dǝ kam bana sadǝma ro, kam kǝlanzǝbe cida: kam banazǝ sadǝma, kǝla faltǝnza kǝma, kuru sa kam dǝ cǝworo yeyi dǝn isǝma.

## Cida ngǝla ye dementia burwoyenro kalkal?

Cida ngǝla ye sa adǝben ngawon cidawa kam dǝ buron ra'ana dǝwo, amma gana nashartǝro sadǝna. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) shawari-a karta-a biskǝ-a, kalma matǝ, letǝ, bǝri, kulo, fure faltǝ, labar gultǝ, sura zakkǝ, musiki, diary ruwo, wowoni ruwo, album surotǝbe (scrapbooking), dewu-a adǝbe alawa-a-a bǝzǝna.

Abima faltǝna dǝ ngawon na cidabedǝ, cida dǝ gantǝnzǝ gǝnyi. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) fǝlezǝna kǝla am dementia burwoyen-a cidawa sandi ra'ana dǝn gaptǝro badijin, kuru misal sadǝna kam jamawu kura lan hangalnzǝ fasartin, amma jamawu gana lan ngǝla fando. Abima dunon tǝkkǝ dǝ kǝla manabe, kuru rokko faltǝne.

## Buro banasǝne, kuru futu banajin dǝ kǝla sadǝne

[Shawari Alzheimer's Association dementia burwoyebe](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage) ada-a cidawa-aro faidajin:

- **Tayiro koro.** "Awi ye karatu jamawunzǝ nashartǝro sadin?" tambu dǝ, hangalnǝmro tambun ngǝla.
- **Kǝla shi ye rakcǝna dǝn tǝmangǝne.** Tajirwa-a gǝnyi saa, kam dǝro jarabtǝro kolne, kuru kǝla dunonzǝn banane shi gantǝnzǝro gǝnyi.
- **Alama fal sadǝne.** Kalma au kǝla alama sadǝne ma'ananzǝ "kǝrma isǝnǝm ngǝla", misallo sa su fal biskǝ karta lan tǝwandinba.
- **Sa-a sa-a kǝla koro.** Banazǝ dǝ kalkal wa, kǝskǝ wa, au gana wa dǝ koro.

## Shawari cida-abe dementia burwoyen

### Cidawa-a nzundu-a rotǝne

- **Cida loktu-a fando.** Cida ngǝla satiyaben, jamawu yirobe au klob, kawube naptǝramnzǝ cǝdin. Kǝla tǝkkǝro waljin dǝ, cida kǝrtǝ au kǝla bǝlin kalkal.
- **Dewu kǝla faltǝne.** Kasuwu lezǝ dǝ dunoro waljin dǝ, Alzheimer's Association shawari sadǝna kam dǝro dewu satiyebe dǝ faltǝnǝmro kuru list dǝ ruzǝnǝmro.
- **Dewu de-a furno-a.** Resipi kam dǝ ra'ana dǝ rokko de. Baditǝro kawu dǝ, kare-a sammaa fǝletǝne futu tǝdǝnzǝ ruyinro.
- **Kulo.** Na farbe faltǝ, kǝskǝ au farawaa karne kuru cidanzǝ rokko cǝdǝne. List cida kulo satiyebe ruwo-a faidajin.
- **Cida mukkobe fando.** Kǝska cidawa, duwu, yirri sǝdǝ au model tǝdǝ dǝ nashartǝro waltǝyin futu misalnzǝ nashartǝro waljin-a nur ngǝla-a.

### Biskǝ nzundobe

- **Karta-a biskǝ-a biskǝne.** Biskǝ notǝna dǝ ngawon nashartǝro. Jamawu lan biskǝne kam ndasoma kǝla falro gǝnyiro, kuru lambar dǝ gana au bawo yaye.
- **Kalma matǝ kura-a ruwo-a jarabne.** [Word search](/games/word-search/) andeyen screen lan au ruwo lan biskǝtǝye, sa kǝla bǝlinzǝn.
- **Jigsaw puzzle cǝdǝne.** Lambar kǝskǝye nzundoro kalkal dǝ karne, dunoro gǝnyi. [Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) screen lan kǝrtǝro, kuru shawari andebe [puzzles for dementia](/activities/puzzles/) futu karne dǝ bayin.
- **Trivia nzundobero kǝlne.** Am laa-a jamawu trivia ra'ana. Jarabtǝro waljin dǝ, gade karne, au [kǝriwu tambu nashar-a](/activities/trivia/) andebe jarabne, jawawu-a mbeji amma lambar bawo.

Biskǝ adǝbe dalilnzǝ nzundo. Biskǝ ndasoma dementia kǝndoro au kǝrtǝro tǝdǝna dǝ fǝlezǝnyi; kuru karǝne [biskǝ hangalbe: kulashi awi manin](/brain-health/brain-games/).

### Ruwo, taktǝ-a kǝndaram fando-a

- **Diary rune.** Alzheimer's Association diary ruwo dǝ shawari fal ro gotǝna, kǝla hangal-a bayanzǝ ga-a ngawon. Kawube lan kalma gana-a kǝma.
- **Kitawu kǝndaramnzǝbe kǝlanzǝn kǝla rotǝne.** Sa burwoye dǝ sa ngǝla ye kam dǝ surotǝnzǝ karzǝno kuru labarnzǝ ruwo au gultǝ. [Cida-a taktǝbe](/activities/reminiscence/) rui.
- **Wowoni rune.** Wowoni tiyinza, sawa kura au kam cida buro-a ro ruwo dǝ sandi indi-aro maana mbeji.
- **Suro-a kǝla manane.** [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) suro yallanzǝbe biskǝ kalkalro faltǝyin, kuru [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) mana baditǝbe nashar-a cǝdi.

### Luwutǝ-a letǝ-a

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) shawari sadǝna luwutǝ notǝna dǝ fando, nandi indi ngǝla yiro waljinro. Sa kam dǝye ngǝla yiro waljin dǝn faltǝne kuru kǝrtǝro kolne.

- **Luwutǝ adǝbe kǝrtǝ.** Museum, fim kantǝge au suro fannǝbe, sa kǝla bǝlinzǝn.
- **Ingi lan letǝ.** NIA sa ingi lan na kǝla bǝlinzǝben shawari sadǝna.
- **Jamawu letǝbe-a bǝri klas-a.** [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) bǝri, tai chi, yoga, ingi lan letǝ-a jamawu letǝbe-a bǝzǝna, kuru sa mbejiya na dementia-ro kalkal sadǝna-a.
- **Jamawu yirobe-a memory café-a.** Na kǝla bǝlinzǝn am dementia-a kǝlanzan ro kǝrgata-a sawaanzan-a kǝltǝyin am gade kǝla adǝn kǝrgata-a.

## Faltǝ gana cidawa nzundoro rotǝbe

| Adǝ dunoro waljin dǝ | Adǝ jarabne |
| --- | --- |
| Na am ngǝwu au kǝla ngǝwu-a | Sa kǝla bǝlinzǝn lene, jamawu gana karne, naptǝram dǝye kǝnta lan napne |
| Su bǝlin taktǝ | Sa kulu am so sunzǝn bowone; jamawu-a sunzǝ tag lan yikkoro koro |
| Cida katap ngǝwu-a kǝla ga | Katap dǝ rune, au sammaa awo dǝ fǝletǝne |
| Kǝla ngǝri fandǝ | Luwutǝ kǝrtǝ, ngawo tusu, kawu adǝn cida gade gǝnyi |
| Biskǝ lan kǝla ga | Jamawu lan biskǝne, dokawu dǝ nashartǝne, au lambar dǝ kolne |
| Kalma matǝn tǝkkǝ | Sa kolne; alama nandi sadǝna dǝ manjin dǝn kalma fal bǝzǝne |

## Sa kǝrtǝnǝm yero

Cida fal kolne sa nzundon kozǝna kǝla tǝkkǝ cǝdin dǝ. Hangal tǝkkǝ sa-a sa-a, nzundu faln gaptǝ, au "wuma rakǝnyi" manǝ dǝ, mana ngǝla ro mbeji, dunotǝ gǝnyi. Ngawon jawawu dǝ cida adǝman kǝrtǝnzǝ. Sa kam dǝ banazǝ ngǝwu baditǝro-a katap ga-aro-a mbejiya, shawari [cida-a dementia dawuye](/activities/middle-stage/) kalkalro waljin. Mana banazǝbe ngǝwuro, [shawari mana-a](/caregiving/communication-tips/) rui, kuru shawari ngǝwuro [cida 101](/activities/ideas/) rui.

Am bana sadǝma-a ye banazǝ mbeji. Sa burwoye dǝ sa ngǝla ye banazǝm dǝ jamawu banazǝbe tǝdǝnǝm; [kǝla kǝndaramnzǝbe kam banazǝmabe](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) rui.
