Kalar cidawa cidawa halaktǝ nduli ye fal, kam Alzheimer-a au dementia gade-a zaktǝnaro yin raktǝ. Karatǝ mburo bawo, askǝrǝm mburo bawo, kuru jawabu kalkal bawo. Mukko cidi, kalarwa fǝlezayi, kuru shafi mana cidatǝ mbeji. Daliwu adǝye futu shafi kartǝnin, abi dawartǝnin, kuru futu kǝltǝ kalar cidatǝnin ro, cidawa dǝ sandi kǝrtǝn au jarabtǝ ro faltǝgǝnin ro, bayanzǝ.

## Ndayin kalar cidawa am dementia-a zaktǝna nguwuro ngǝla

Kalar cidawa mukkoro kare cidatǝ-a simmoro kare ruitǝ-a cin. Tebol kǝnza ro napcin au kursi windo kǝnza ro napcin raktǝ, kuru baditǝ-a dazǝtǝ-a yaye nduli. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ye zana dǝ karǝgǝ hangal bayantǝ-a sa adǝn kǝji fantǝ-a ro bayanzǝna, kuru nozǝna zana dǝn mburo bawo ro manazǝna.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ye shawari cin: kam dǝ kǝrma raktǝna dǝga cidawa kartǝ, kuru kǝndoramnzǝ faltǝna sa faltǝgǝ. Kalar cidawa faltǝgǝ nduli: shafi kura tǝma, krayon kura, kalar nguwu, au komputa lan kalar ro rǝptǝ.

## Shafi kura karta

Am kura ye sandi kǝrtǝn surowa raaza bawo. Adǝwa mata:

- **Kǝndaram dǝnia:** kǝsko fure kura-a, kǝldi marte-a, ngudowa dal kǝlanzǝn-a.
- **Kare noata:** shayi kǝskǝ, baisikil, tǝma fal, au fǝs.
- **Suro nduli kura:** mandala-a suro joometri kura kura-a na nguwu kura-a.
- **Zana au kǝndaram na dǝnia** kam dǝ na raana au zana ma raana ro nozana.

Shafi na gana gana-a jamaatǝ-a koltǝmni, adǝye cizǝtǝ hangal cidin. Shafi kura yitǝ ngǝla, kuru [cidawa dementia yitǝtǝ mbǝji](/activities/printable/) nde lan shafi ruwo kura-a mbeji.

## Abi dawartǝ

- **Kare cidatǝ kura-a mukkon tawatǝ nduli.** Krayon jumbo, makka kura, au pensil ruwo kura-a kalem gana-a kotǝ ngǝla.
- **Kalar nguwu bawo.** Kalar yaskǝ au degu ma ishin. Kalar kada hangal cidin.
- **Shafi dunno au kat.** Shafi gana tǝrǝyin kuru cidi. Kǝnza-a tepu lan rǝptǝ au clipboard faidatǝ.
- **Tebol asutǝ.** Kare gade-a cirtǝ, kuru sa fal ro shafi fal dawarnǝm.
- **Kare shafuyin au kaltǝgǝyin,** misallo: lenso au isponji, kalar nduli ro faltǝro.

## Tablet lan kalar cidawa

Golden Moments lan [cidawa kalar cidawa](/games/coloring/) dǝ kalar fal rǝptǝ-a kǝla shafi dǝn na fal rǝptǝ-a lan cidin. Mukko musko ngǝla mburo bawo, adǝye mukko kam dǝ dunno bawo ro waltǝna ma raktǝ. Kam dǝ-a kǝltǝ napnǝm, screen dǝ kǝltǝ ruiwo, kuru kalarwa dǝ shi kartǝ ro kolnǝm. Labarwa gade-a (notifications) zaktǝ, shafi dǝ ro hangalnzǝ napca.

## Futu kǝltǝ kalar cidatǝ

1. **Badiyaram burwo-a kǝltǝ badiyǝ.** Krayon dǝ shi-a kǝltǝ tǝ, au mukkonǝm shi mukkonzǝn dawarnǝm sa nguwu ro, kǝla kolnǝm.
2. **Kalarwa-a nawa-a lan mana bǝ.** "Kalar bulu adǝ shawa. Ndaran ingi adǝ gai ruima?" koro cidi, jarabtǝ bawo.
3. **Karta dǝ shawa, kalar layi dǝn napciya bawo.** "Kalar zǝmna shawa karta" shi "layi dǝn napkǝna" kotǝ ngǝla.
4. **Shi kǝnza ro kolnǝm.** Shafi sammaa cizǝnǝn mayo yaye, kolnǝm.
5. **Shi dazǝna sa dazǝnǝm.** Krayonwa dǝ dawarnǝm, kuru shayi au lele ro gulnǝm.

## Dementia faltǝna sa cidawa faltǝgǝ

Badiyaram burwon, am nguwu ye shafi kǝla kǝla-a raayin, kuru kalar-a suro-a kǝlanza ro cartin. Dawu dawun, shafi kura-a karta nguwu bawo-a faidajin. Dawu ngǝlaun, suro kura fal, shafi kalar nduli fal, au krayon tawatǝ kuru kǝla kǝnza ro zawar yitǝ jarabtǝ. Am laa ye shafi dazǝna dǝ rui kǝji, shima cidatǝ kotǝ.

Dawu yaye ro cidawa zana-a musko-a lan bayanna kura ro, [am dementia-a zaktǝnaro musko-a zana cidawa](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) kuru [am dementia-a zaktǝnaro cidawa zana](/activities/art-projects/) rui.

## Sa dazǝtǝ

Kam dǝ hangalnzǝ tǝdana, shafi dǝ tǝkkǝna, au lǝptǝna fǝlezǝna sa dazǝnǝm. Minti indi lan dazǝna yaye nasara, shi kǝji fanzǝna ma. Ngawo yaye ro waltǝ raktǝ, au shafi gade au cidawa gade [cidawa sim hangalbe](/activities/sensory/) gai jarabtǝ raktǝ.
