Batho ba tlholwa ba tlhoka go nna le ba bangwe, mme seo ga se fetoge fa dingwaga di ntse di oketsega. Dipatlisiso di golaganya go jewa ke bodutu le go ikgaoganya le ba bangwe mo dingwageng tsa moragonyana le kotsi e e kwa godimo ya dementia, gammogo le bolwetse jwa pelo le kgatelelo ya maikutlo. Dipatlisiso ga di ise di kgone go bolela gore ke bokae jwa kamano eo jo e leng sebako kgotsa ditlamorago. Tsebe eno e tlhalosa se bosupi bo se bontshang, ditsela tse di motlhofo tsa go nna o golagane le ba bangwe, le gore ke ka ntlha yang ditirwana tse di dirwang mmogo di le botlhokwa thata mo bathong ba ba tshelang le dementia.

## A bodutu le go itlhaola go amana le dementia?

Ee, dipatlisiso di le dintsi di bontsha gore go na le kamano, le fa go sa tlhaloganyesege gotlhelele gore ke efe e e bakang e nngwe. [2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) e tlhagisa go itlhaola mo bathong mo dingwageng tsa morago tsa botshelo jaaka nngwe ya mabaka a le 14 a kotsi a a ka fetolwang. [UCL's summary of the report](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) e kaya gore seno se amana le mo e ka nnang 5% ya dikgetsi tsa dementia mo lefatsheng lotlhe.

Se bosupi bo se bontshang:

- **Go kopana le batho go le gonnye, kotsi e ka nna kwa godimo.** Khomišene eno e tlhalosa ditshekatsheko di le pedi tse di golagantseng go kopana le batho ba bangwe ka sewelo le kotsi e e kwa godimo ya dementia. Nngwe e fitlhetse kotsi e e kwa godimo ka mo e ka nnang 57% mme e nngwe ka mo e ka nnang 18%.
- **Bodutu le jone.** Bodutu, ke maikutlo a go ikutlwa o kgaogane le ba bangwe, le jone bo ne jwa golaganngwa le kotsi e e kwa godimo ya dementia mo ditshekatshekong di le mmalwa.
- **Pono ya Surgeon General.** Ka Motsheganong 2023, US Surgeon General o ne a ntsha [advisory on loneliness and isolation](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf). E tlhalosa gore bodutu jo bo sa feleng le go itlhaola go ka oketsa kotsi ya dementia ka mo e ka nnang 50% mo bagolong. Gape e ne ya golaganya go se nne le dikamano tse di siameng le batho ba bangwe le kotsi e e kwa godimo ka 29% ya bolwetse jwa pelo le kotsi e e kwa godimo ka 32% ya setorouku.
- **Go tlwaelegile go le kana kang.** [National Academies report](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557972/) e fitlhetse gore mo e ka nnang 24% ya MaAmerika a a nang le dingwaga di le 65 le go feta a a nnang mo baaging ba bangwe a itlhaotse mo bathong.

Temoso ya botlhokwa: Lancet Commission e lemoga gore batho ba ka fokotsa go tsaya karolo mo ditirwaneng tsa loago mo legatong la ntlha la dementia, pele ga e tlhatlhobiwa. Seno se raya gore karolo nngwe ya kamano eno e ka tswa e le dementia e bakang gore motho a ikgogele morago, e seng fela go ikgogela morago go oketsa kotsi. Bontsi jo bogolo jwa bosupi jono bo tswa mo dipatlisisong tsa go ela tlhoko.

## Pharologano ke eng magareng ga go jewa ke bodutu le go ikgaoganya le batho?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/loneliness-and-social-isolation/loneliness-and-social-isolation-tips-staying-connected) e tlhalosa jaana. **Go ikgaoganya le batho** go kaya go nna le batho ba le mmalwa ba o ikgolaganyang le bone kgotsa ba o buisanang le bone gangwe le gape. **Go jewa ke bodutu** ke maikutlo a a utlwisang botlhoko a go ikutlwa o le nosi kgotsa o kgaogane le ba bangwe. Motho a ka nna a le nosi mme a itumetse, mme motho a ka ikutlwa a jewa ke bodutu le fa a le mo phaposing e e tletseng batho.

NIA e tlhalosa mabaka a a ka oketsang kotsi ya bobedi jwa tsona:

- Mathata a go utlwa kgotsa go bona
- Bothata jwa go tsamaya, kgotsa go se nne le sepalangwa
- Go nna o le nosi, kgotsa tatlhegelo e kgolo jaaka loso lwa molekane kgotsa go tlogela tiro
- Diphetogo mo kgopolong kgotsa mo tseleng ya go akanya
- Go tlhokomela motho yo o mo ratang yo o sa tsogang sentle
- Matshwenyego a madi kgotsa dikgwetlho tsa puo

Go utlwa ke ntlha e kgolo. NIA e tlhalosa gore batho ba ba sa utlweng sentle ba ka ikgogela morago ka gonne puisano e ka nna kgopisa kgotsa ya ba tlhabisa ditlhong. [Kaelo ya rona ya go utlwa le botsogo jwa boboko](/brain-health/hearing/) e akaretsa ditlhatlhobo tsa go utlwa le dithuso tsa go utlwa tse di rekwang kwa ntle ga lengolo la ngaka.

## Ditsela tse di mosola tsa go nna lo golagane

NIA e akantsha dikakanyo di le mmalwa. Tlhopha nngwe kgotsa pedi tse di go siametseng:

- **Rulaganya go ikgolaganya ka metlha.** Tlhoma nako e e tlhomameng letsatsi lengwe le lengwe kgotsa beke nngwe le nngwe ya go letsa, go romela molaetsa, imeile kgotsa karata kwa ba lelapa, ditsaleng kgotsa baagisanying.
- **Dirisa megala ya bidio kgotsa dibuela-kgolo tse di botlhale.** Fa thekenoloji e le ntšha mo go wena, dilaeborari di le dintsi tsa setšhaba le ditheo tsa baagi di na le dithuto. Bona [kaedi ya rona ya megala le dipuisano tsa bidio](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).
- **Tsena mo sengwe.** Tlelase, phuthego ya tumelo, setlhopha sa go tsamaya ka dinao kgotsa tiro ya boithaopo di ka go fa kamano le maikutlo a gore o na le maikaelelo.
- **Simolola gape se o neng o se rata** kgotsa ithute sengwe se sešwa le batho ba bangwe.
- **Itshidile mmele le ba bangwe.** Itshidile mmele le tsala kgotsa setlhopha. [Kaedi ya rona ya go itshidila mmele](/brain-health/exercise/) e na le dikgetho tse di bonolo.
- **Akanya ka go rua seruiwa sa lapeng** fa o ka kgona go se tlhokomela.
- **Dirisa ditirelo tsa mo gaeno.** Ditheo tsa bagodi, Area Agencies on Aging le Eldercare Locator (800-677-1116) di ka go kaela kwa mananeong le dipalangwa.

## Ke eng fa ditirwana tse lo di dirang mmogo di le botlhokwa mo bathong ba ba tshelang le dementia?

Mo mothong yo a setseng a tshela le dementia, go nna mmogo ga se ka ga go fokotsa kotsi, mme ke ka ga go itumelela, go ikutlwa a phuthulogile le go ikutlwa a amogelesega. The Lancet Commission e bolela gore ditirwana di thusa batho ba ba tshelang le dementia go tswelela ba itumelela dilo le go nna le lebaka la go dira sengwe. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) e lemoga gore, mo godimo ga go tokafatsa boleng jwa botshelo, ditirwana di ka fokotsa maitsholo a a jaaka go kgarakgatshega gongwe le gongwe kgotsa go tlhobaela. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e oketsa ka gore batho ba ba nang le bolwetse jwa ga Alzheimer ba tlhoka go nna matlhagatlhaga le go dira dilo tse ba di itumelelang.

Dikakanyo tse di dirang sentle:

1. **Lebang ditshwantsho mmogo.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) di na le dikakgelo tse di bulegileng, tse di senang dipotso tsa teko, tse di jaaka “Mpolelele ka...”. Bona [kaedi ya rona ya go gakologelwa](/activities/reminiscence/).
2. **Tshamekang lo le gaufi.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) e boloka ditshwantsho di bonala e bile ga e na sebadi sa nako kgotsa maduo, ka jalo ga go na kgatelelo. Kaedi ya rona ya [go tshameka mmogo](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) e na le dikgakololo.
3. **Dirisang ditshwantsho tsa lelapa.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) e fetola ditshwantsho tse o di tlhophang go nna tirwana ya go nyalanya, mme ditshwantsho di nna mo sedirisweng sa gago.
4. **Kopanyang batho.** [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) e siametse dikopano tsa lelapa le [ditlhopha tsa kwa mafelong a tlhokomelo](/activities/care-home-groups/).
5. **Abelanang mmino, ditiro tsa mo gae kgotsa tshingwana.** Bona [ditirwana tsa mmino](/activities/music/), [ditiro tsa mo gae](/activities/household-tasks/) le lenaane la rona le le tletseng la [dikakanyo tsa ditirwana](/activities/ideas/).
6. **Dirang gore maeto a nne motlhofo.** [Kaedi ya rona ya maeto](/activities/visiting/) e akaretsa maeto a makhutshwane, a a didimetseng a a utlwalang sentle mo go mongwe le mongwe.

Dirang dilo di le dinnye e bile di iketlile. Kamore e e didimetseng e e nang le motho a le mongwe kgotsa ba le babedi gantsi e dira botoka go gaisa setlhopha se segolo. Fa motho yo o mo tlhokomelang a lapile kgotsa a sa tlhole a kgatlhega, go emisa go siame. Go nna mmogo lo nwa tee le gone ke sephetho se se siameng.

Mo mothong yo o tshelang a le nosi le dementia, NIA e akantsha gore go batliwe motho yo o mo tshepang yo o ka mo etelang kgotsa a buang le ene ka mogala wa bidio ka metlha, a bo a nna motho yo go ka ikgolaganngwang le ene fa go na le tshoganyetso. E umaka gape memory cafe ya mo gaeno, e leng lefelo le le iketlileng la go kopana la batho ba ba nang le mathata a go gakologelwa le ba malapa a bone.

## Batlhokomedi le bone ba ka ikutlwa ba le bosi

NIA e tlhalosa go tlhokomela motho yo o mo ratang jaaka ntlha e e ka oketsang kotsi ya go kgaogana le ba bangwe. Ipeele nako ya ditsala tsa gago, ditlhopha tsa tshegetso le go ikhutsa. [Kaelo ya rona ya go itlhokomela fa o le motlhokomedi](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e na le dikakanyo tse di mosola.

## Buisana le ngaka

Fa wena kgotsa motho yo o mo tlhokomelang a ikutlwa a le nosi kgotsa a kgaogane le ba bangwe bontsi jwa nako, bolelela ngaka. NIA e rotloetsa batho go abelana ka diphetogo tse dikgolo mo botshelong, jaaka loso mo lelapeng, gore ngaka e tlhaloganye seemo sotlhe. Go ikutlwa o nyemile mooko dibeke di le mmalwa ka tatelano e ka nna kgatelelo ya maikutlo, e e ka alafiwang. Fa motho yo mogolwane a sa tswa go ikgogela morago mo ditsaleng kgotsa mo ditirwaneng tse a neng a di itumelela, phetogo eo le yone e tshwanetse go bolelelwa ngaka. Bala [ditshwao tsa pele le gore o ka bona ngaka leng](/brain-health/warning-signs/).

Tsebe eno ke tshedimosetso ya kakaretso, ga se kgakololo ya bongaka. Bona [tlhokomediso ya rona ya bongaka](/medical-disclaimer/).
