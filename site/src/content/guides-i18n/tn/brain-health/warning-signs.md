Go ka tshosa go lemoga diphetogo mo kgakologelong ya gago, kgotsa mo kgakologelong ya mongwe yo o mo ratang. Tsebe eno e ka go thusa go swetsa gore o bee nako ya go bona ngaka leng le gore o ka ipaakanyetsa jang. Ga e ka ke ya go bolelela gore a mongwe o na le dementia. **Ke moitseanape wa botsogo yo o nang le thupelo e e maleba fela yo o ka tlhomamisang gore motho o na le dementia**, mme maemo a mantsi a a bakang mathata a kgakologelo ga se dementia gotlhelele. Tsweetswee o se ka wa ikaega ka dipotso tsa mo inthaneteng kgotsa diteko tsa go itlhatlhoba.

## Ditshwao tsa ntlha tsa dementia ke dife?

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/10_signs) e tlhalosa ditshwao di le 10 tsa ntlha. E bapisa letshwao lengwe le lengwe le phetogo e e tlwaelegileng e e ka diragalang fa motho a ntse a gola, gore go nne motlhofo go di bapisa. Ka mafoko a rona:

| Letshwao le le tlhokang go elwa tlhoko | Se se tlwaelegileng fa motho a ntse a gola |
| --- | --- |
| Go lebala dilo ka tsela e e kgoreletsang botshelo jwa letsatsi le letsatsi, jaaka go lebala ditiragalo tsa bosheng kgotsa go botsa potso e le nngwe gangwe le gape | Ka dinako tse dingwe go lebala leina kgotsa nako ya kopano, mme wa bo wa e gopola moragonyana |
| Bothata jwa go rulaganya kgotsa go rarabolola mathata, jaaka go latela resepe e e tlwaelegileng kgotsa go sala morago dikoloto | Go dira phoso ka sewelo mo dikolotong tsa legae |
| Bothata jwa go fetsa ditiro tse di tlwaelegileng kwa gae, kwa tirong kgotsa fa o le kwa ntle | Ka dinako tse dingwe go tlhoka thuso ka go seta microwave kgotsa go rekota lenaneo la thelebišene |
| Go tlhoka go sala morago matlha, dipaka tsa ngwaga kgotsa gore o kwa kae | Go fosa letsatsi la beke, mme wa bo wa le gakologelwa |
| Bothata jo bosha jwa go lekanyetsa sekgala, go bala kgotsa go farologanya mebala | Diphetogo tsa pono tse di bakwang ke cataracts |
| Mathata a masha ka mafoko, jaaka go ema fa gare ga puisano kgotsa go bitsa dilo ka maina a a sa siamang | Ka dinako tse dingwe go thatafalelwa ke go bona lefoko le le siameng |
| Go baya dilo mo mafelong a a sa tlwaelegang mme o sa kgone go gopola gore o di beile kae | Ka dinako tse dingwe go baya dilo mo go sa siamang mme wa di bona fa o latela dikgato tsa gago |
| Go tsaya ditshwetso tse di sa siamang, jaaka ka madi kgotsa ka go itlhokomela | Ka dinako tse dingwe go tsaya tshwetso e e sa siamang |
| Go ikgogela morago mo dilong tse o di itumelelang, mo tirong kgotsa mo ditirwaneng tsa loago | Ka dinako tse dingwe go se ikutlwe o batla go ya tiragalong ya lelapa kgotsa ya loago |
| Diphetogo mo maikutlong kgotsa mo botho, jaaka go tlhakana tlhogo, go belaela, go tshoga kgotsa go tshwenyega | Go tlwaela go dira dilo ka tsela e le nngwe mme o tenege fa thulaganyo e fetoga |

Letswayo le le lengwe fela ga le reye gore motho o na le dementia. Se se botlhokwa ke **phetogo mo go se se tlwaelegileng mo mothong yoo**, segolobogolo fa e dira gore botshelo jwa letsatsi le letsatsi bo nne bokete.

## Go tsofala ka tlwaelo go farologana jang le dementia?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/memory-loss-and-forgetfulness/memory-problems-forgetfulness-and-aging) e re bagolo ba le bantsi ba lemoga gore ba gakologelwa dilo ka bonya, kgotsa ka dinako dingwe ba baya dilo mme ba se ka ba di fitlhela, kgotsa ba lebala go duela sekoloto. Gantsi tseno ke ditshupo tsa go lebala go go seng bogale. **Dementia ga se karolo e e tlwaelegileng ya go tsofala.** Mathata a a masisi a kgakologelo a dira gore ditirwana tsa letsatsi le letsatsi di nne thata, jaaka go kgweetsa, go dirisa mogala kgotsa go bona tsela ya go ya gae.

NIA e akantsha gore go buiwe le ngaka fa mongwe:

- A botsa dipotso tse di tshwanang gangwe le gape
- A timela mo mafelong a a neng a a itse sentle
- A nna le bothata jwa go latela diresepe kgotsa ditaelo
- A tlhakatlhakana thata ka nako, batho le mafelo
- A tlogela go itlhokomela, jaaka go ja go sa siama, go se tlhape kgotsa go itshwara ka tsela e e sa babalesegang

Batho bangwe ba na le **bothata jo bo seng bogale jwa go akanya le go gakologelwa (MCI)**. Seno se raya gore ba na le mathata a kgakologelo kgotsa a go akanya a mantsi go feta batho ba bangwe ba dingwaga tsa bone, le fa ba ntse ba kgona go dira dilo tsa letsatsi le letsatsi. NIA e re MCI e ka nna sesupo sa ntlha sa bolwetse jwa Alzheimer, mme ga se mongwe le mongwe yo o nang le MCI yo o felelang a nna le jone.

## Ke eng gape se se ka bakang mathata a kgakologelo?

Dilo di le dintsi ntle le dementia di ka ama kgakologelo, mme tse dingwe di ka tokafala ka kalafo. NIA e tlhalosa tse di latelang:

- **Mathata a thyroid, diphilo kgotsa sebete**
- **Ditlamorago tsa melemo**
- **Kgatelelo ya maikutlo le tlhobaelo**
- **Vithamine B12 e e kwa tlase** kgotsa go sa je dijo tse di nang le dikotla tse di lekaneng
- **Mathata a boroko**
- **Tiriso e e sa siamang ya nnotagi kgotsa diritibatsi**
- **Ditshwaetso, makgobokgobo a madi kgotsa dithuthubudu** mo bobokong
- **Kgobalo ya tlhogo**, jaaka go itaya tlhogo mo go amang boboko
- **Kgatelelo e kgolo ya maikutlo kgotsa kutlobotlhoko**, jaaka go tlogela tiro kgotsa go latlhegelwa ke molekane

Mathata a go utlwa le go bona le one a ka tsewa ka phoso jaaka tlhakatlhakano; bona [kaedi ya rona ya go utlwa](/brain-health/hearing/). Ke ka moo tlhatlhobo ya bongaka e leng botlhokwa. Go ya ka [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor), dingaka gantsi di kopa diteko tsa madi go tlhatlhoba mathata a thyroid, tlhaelo ya divithamine le ditshwaetso. [Boroko](/brain-health/sleep/) jo bo siameng le go alafiwa ga kgatelelo ya maikutlo le gone go molemo go bo umaka.

## Go tlhakana ga tlhaloganyo ka tshoganyetso ke boemo jwa potlako

**Go tlhakana ga tlhaloganyo mo go simologang ka bonako, mo diureng kgotsa mo malatsing, go tlhoka tlhokomelo ya bongaka ka bonako.** E ka nna delirium. [VA e tlhalosa delirium](https://www.va.gov/geriatrics/pages/delirium_when_should_i_worry_about_delirium.asp) e le boemo jwa potlako jwa bongaka mme e gakolola gore o letsetse modiri wa tlhokomelo ya botsogo wa motho yoo ka bonako kgotsa o ikgolaganye le ditirelo tsa tshoganyetso tsa mo lefelong la gago. Ditshwao di akaretsa:

- Go fetofetoga ga bokgoni jwa go akanya sentle
- Bothata jwa go tsepamisa mogopolo
- Go akanya mo go sa rulaganywang sentle
- Go otsela go sa tlwaelegang, kgotsa go se iketle le go tshwenyega go sa tlwaelegang

Seno se ka diragalela mongwe le mongwe, go akaretsa batho ba ba tshelang le dementia. Go ya ka [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), dibaki tse di tlwaelegileng di akaretsa ditshwaetso (ditshwaetso tsa tsela ya moroto di tlwaelegile mo bathong ba bagolo le mo bathong ba ba tshelang le dementia), setorouku, sukiri e e kwa tlase mo mading mo bathong ba ba nang le bolwetse jwa sukiri, kgobalo ya tlhogo le melemo mengwe. Fa o sa ntse o emetse thuso, nna le motho yoo mme o mo gomotse ka mafoko a makhutshwane, a a motlhofo. O se ka wa mmotsa dipotso tse dintsi, mme ela tlhoko melemo epe e a e nwang.

## Nka ipaakanyetsa jang kopano le ngaka?

Kopano e e siameng e simolola ka dintlha tse di kwadilweng sentle. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor) e akantsha gore o tle le:

1. **Lenaane la diphetogo** mo kgopolong, maikutlong le maitsholong, ka dikai tse di tlhamaletseng le gore di simolotse leng go le gonnye
2. **Mathata a botsogo a a fetileng le a gone jaanong**, go akaretsa le le fa e le mang mo lelapeng yo o kileng a nna le mathata a kgopolo
3. **Melemo yotlhe e e kwadilweng ke ngaka le e e rekwang kwa ntle ga lengwalo la ngaka**
4. **Divitamini kgotsa ditlaleletso dipe fela**
5. **Dipotso tsa gago**, tse di jaaka gore go tla dirwa diteko dife, di tsaya nako e kae le gore dipholo di tla tla leng

Gape go a thusa go tla le mongwe wa lelapa kgotsa tsala e e itseng motho yoo sentle. [NIA e tlhalosa](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-alzheimers-disease-diagnosed) gore dingaka di ka botsa motho yoo le mongwe wa lelapa ka botsogo, melemo, ditirwana tsa letsatsi le letsatsi le diphetogo mo maitsholong. Dingaka gape di ka:

- Dira diteko tsa kgopolo, go rarabolola mathata, tlhokomelo, go bala le puo
- Laela diteko tsa madi, motlhapo le tse dingwe go batla mabaka a mangwe
- Tlhatlhoba go kgobega marapo kgotsa maemo a mangwe a botsogo jwa tlhaloganyo
- Laela ditshwantsho tsa boboko tse di jaaka CT kgotsa MRI
- Romela kwa moitsaanaping, jaaka geriatrician, neurologist, geriatric psychiatrist kgotsa neuropsychologist

NIA e tlhalosa gore tlhatlhobo ya bokgoni jwa tlhaloganyo ke karolo e e tlhokegang ya Medicare Annual Wellness Visit, e e ka nnang nako e e siameng ya go bua ka dingongorego. Le fa e le diteko tse dikhutshwane tse dingaka di di dirisang ga di a lekana ka botsone go tlhatlhoba dementia. Ke kgato ya ntlha e e lebisang tlhatlhobong e e feletseng, mme go tlhatlhoba go ka tsaya go feta ketelo e le nngwe. NIA e gakolola gore batho ba ba nang le mathata a kgopolo ba boele kwa ngakeng morago ga dikgwedi di le 6 go ya go 12.

## Go bula kgang ka bopelonomi

Fa o tshwenyegile ka mongwe, tlhopha nako e e didimetseng e lo ka buang lo le nosi. Bua ka dilo tse di rileng tse o di lemogileng, o di tlhalose e le go tshwenyega ka boitekanelo jwa gagwe, e seng go mo tshwaya diphoso. Go akantsha gore a ye tlhatlhobong e e tlwaelegileng ya boitekanelo go ka utwala go sa mo tshose thata. Akaretsa motho yoo mo ditshwetsong mme o bue le ene ka tlotlo, jaaka mogolo yo a leng ene. [Dikakantsho tsa rona tsa puisano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) di ka thusa.

## Bua le ngaka

Fa o lemoga diphetogo tse disha mo kgakologelong, mo tseleng ya go akanya, mo maikutlong, kgotsa mo bokgoning jwa letsatsi le letsatsi, mme di tswelela di direga, dira nako ya go bona ngaka ya tlhokomelo ya motheo. Fa go na le tlhakatlhakano e e tlhagang ka tshoganyetso, ikgolaganye le ditirelo tsa tshoganyetso tsa mo lefelong la gago kgotsa le modiri wa tlhokomelo ya boitekanelo wa motho yo o mo tlhokomelang ka bonako. Go tlhatlhobiwa go sa le gale go ka thusa gore go bonwe mabaka a a ka alafiwang, mme fa e le dementia, go nna le nako e ntsi ya go rulaganya le go bona kemonokeng. Itlhokomele le wena mo nakong eno; bona [kaelo ya rona ya go itlhokomela ga batlhokomedi](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).

Tsebe eno ke tshedimosetso ya kakaretso, ga se kgakololo ya bongaka. Bona [tlhagiso ya rona ya bongaka](/medical-disclaimer/).
