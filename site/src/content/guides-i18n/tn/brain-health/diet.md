Gongwe o ka bo o badile gore dijo tse di siametseng boboko di ka thibela Alzheimer's. Setshwantsho sa nnete se lekanyeditswe. Mekgwa mengwe ya go ja e golagantswe le kotsi e e kwa tlase mo dipatlisisong tse di latelang batho mo lobakeng lo loleele, mme teko e e nonofileng go gaisa tsotlhe ga e a bona molemo o o bonalang. Tsebe eno e tlhalosa dilo tsoopedi, mme e bo e bua ka se gantsi se leng botlhokwa mo mothong yo o setseng a tshela le dementia: go ja le go nwa mo go lekaneng, le go itumelela dijo.

Tshedimosetso eno ke ya kakaretso, ga se kgakololo ya kalafi. Bua le ngaka kgotsa setsebi sa dijo se se kwadisitsweng pele o fetola dijo tsa gago kgotsa tsa motho yo mongwe. Bona [kitsiso ya kalafi](/medical-disclaimer/).

## Dijo tsa Mediterranean le MIND ke eng?

National Institute on Aging (NIA) e di tlhalosa jaana
([NIA on diet and Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-do-we-know-about-diet-and-prevention-alzheimers-disease)):

- **Dijo tsa Mediterranean:** maungo, merogo, mabele a a feletseng, dinawa, tlhapi le dijo tsa lewatle, le oli ya olive e ntsi, mme go jewa nama e khibidu, mae le dimonamone ka bonnye.
- **Dijo tsa MIND** (Mediterranean–DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay): ke motswako wa dijo tsa Mediterranean le dijo tsa DASH tsa kgatelelo ya madi. Di gatelela merogo, segolobogolo e metala ya matlhare; menokotshwai; mabele a a feletseng; dinawa; manoko; tlhapi bonnye gangwe ka beke; le oli ya olive. Di fokotsa nama e khibidu, dimonamone, tšhisi, botoro kgotsa margarine, le dijo tse di gadilweng kgotsa tsa bonako.

## A dijo tseno di fokotsa kotsi ya dementia?

Bosupi bo tlhakane. NIA e re dipatlisiso dingwe, mme e seng tsotlhe, tse di bonang se se diragalang mo bathong di golagantse dijo tsa Mediterranean le MIND le kotsi e e kwa tlase ya dementia fa di bapisiwa le dijo tsa mokgwa wa Bophirima tse di nang le nama e khibidu, mafura a a nonneng le sukiri e ntsi.

Lancet Commission ya 2024 e sekasekile patlisiso ka botlalo
([Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)):

- Tshekatsheko e nngwe ya dipatlisiso tse di latelang ditlhopha tsa batho e golagantse boleng jo bo kwa godimo jwa dijo le kotsi e e kwa tlase ya dementia ka diperesente di ka nna 20%.
- Tshekatsheko e kgolo ya ditlhopha di le tharo e golagantse go latela dijo tsa MIND thata le kotsi e e kwa tlase.
- Mme patlisiso ya kwa Sweden e e latetseng batho ba ba mo dingwageng tsa magareng ba le 28,025 mo dingwageng tse di ka nnang 20 ga e a bona kotsi e e kwa tlase mo dijong tseno kgotsa mo dijong tsa mokgwa wa Mediterranean.

Dipatlisiso tse di bonang se se diragalang ga di kgone go supa gore selo sengwe se bakile se sengwe. Batho ba ba jang jaana gantsi ba farologana ka ditsela tse dingwe, jaaka lotseno, thuto, go itshidila mmele le go fitlhelela kalafi. Commission e konokotse gore ga go na bosupi jo bo lekaneng jwa go re dijo tse di siameng di thibela dementia ka tlhamalalo, le fa go ja maungo le merogo e ntsi le go fokotsa dijo tse di fetotsweng thata mo difemeng (ultra-processed foods) go le molemo mo maemong a mantsi a boitekanelo mme go ama dikotsi tse di jaaka botenya, bolwetse jwa sukiri le kgatelelo e e kwa godimo ya madi. Ga e a tsenya dijo mo lenaaneng la [dikotsi di le 14](/brain-health/risk-factors/).

## Teko ya dijo tsa MIND ya 2023 e boneng?

Teko e e nonofileng go gaisa tsotlhe go fitlha jaanong e ne e le teko ya lotho e e gatisitsweng mo New England Journal of Medicine ka 2023
([MIND diet trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10513737/)):

- **Bomang:** bagolo ba le 604 ba dingwaga di le 65 kgotsa go feta kwa mafelong a Chicago le Boston, ba se na mathata a go akanya, ba na le losika lo lo gaufi lo lo nang le dementia, ba le boima jo bo feteletseng, mme ba simolotse ka dijo tse di sa siamang thata.
- **Se se dirilweng:** mo dingwageng di le tharo, halofo e ne ya latela dijo tsa MIND mme halofo ya tswelela ka dijo tsa yone tsa gale. Ditlhopha tsoopedi di ne tsa kopiwa go fokotsa dijo tsa tsone ka dikalori di ka nna 250 ka letsatsi mme tsa newa thuto ka gale ke ditsebi tsa dijo.
- **Maduo:** maduo a diteko tsa go akanya a ne a tokafala go le gonnye mo ditlhopheng tsotlhe. Pharologanyo fa gare ga ditlhopha e ne e le nnye mme **e se botlhokwa mo dipalopalong**. Ditshwantsho tsa boboko le tsone di ne tsa bontsha diphetogo tse di tshwanang mo ditlhopheng tsoopedi.
- **Boima:** ditlhopha tsoopedi di ne tsa latlhegelwa ke dikilogerama di ka nna 5 (mo e ka nnang diponto di le 11).

### Goreng teko e ka bo e sa bona pharologanyo?

Babatlisisi ba ne ba akantsha mabaka a le mmalwa. Setlhopha sa taolo se ka tswa se tokafaditse dijo tsa sone le sone, ka gonne se ne sa latlhegelwa ke boima jo bo tshwanang. Go boeletsa diteko go ka tokafatsa maduo ka bogone, ka gonne batho ba tlwaela diteko. Dingwaga di le tharo di ka tswa di le khutshwane. Bontsi jwa batsayakarolo ba ne ba rutegile thata e bile e le ba losika lwa Yuropa, ka jalo maduo a ka se ame mongwe le mongwe.

Ka jalo kakaretso e e ikanyegang ke gore: dijo tsa MIND ga di a fenya dijo tse di fokoditsweng dikalori ka kelotlhoko mo dingwageng di le tharo. Seo ga se reye gore go ja sentle ga go na mosola. Se raya gore ga go a tshwanela ga lebelelwa gore dijo dingwe di thibela dementia.

## Go tweng ka dijo dingwe le ditlaleletso?

NIA e re ga go na bosupi jwa gore go ja kgotsa go tila dijo dingwe go ka thibela Alzheimer's kgotsa go thibela go fokotsega ga bokgoni jwa go akanya ka ntlha ya dingwaga. Blueberries, merogo e metala ya matlhare le turmeric tsotlhe di kile tsa sekasekiwa, mme ga go a bonwa bosupi jo bo lekaneng. NIA e bile e re ga go na vitamin kgotsa tlaleletso e e bontshitsweng go thibela Alzheimer's, mme ga go na e e akantshiwang ka maikaelelo ao. NIA e tlhagisa gore dithoto tse di sa tlhomamisiwang tse di rekisiwang go thibela Alzheimer's di ka nna kotsi, tsa senya madi, kgotsa tsotlhe tsoopedi, mme di ka tsenana le melemo e o e newang ke ngaka. Ka jalo buisana le ngaka pele o leka sengwe
([NIA on preventing Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

## Go ja go ka nna ga boitekanelo le boitumelo jang?

Mo bagolong ba le bantsi, maikaelelo ke dijo tse di agang mmele, tse di tlwaelegileng le tse di monate, e seng moralo o o gagametseng:

- **Aga dijo ka dilo tse o di ratang** tsa mokgwa wa Mediterranean: merogo, maungo, dinawa, mabele a a feletseng, tlhapi le oli ya olive.
- **Tswelela ka dijo tse di ratiwang.** Boitumelo le keletso ya dijo di botlhokwa, segolobogolo mo botshelong jwa morago.
- **Ja le batho ba bangwe** fa go kgonega. Go ja mmogo go tlisa botsalano.
- **Apayang mmogo.** Go tlhatswa merogo kgotsa go tlhakanya pitsa ke tirwana e ntle ya go e dira mmogo; bona [ditirwana tsa mo gae](/activities/household-tasks/). Recipe ya lelapa e e ratiwang e ka tsosa [dikgakologelo](/activities/reminiscence/).

Bagolo ga ba a tshwanela go fokotsa dikalori kgotsa go leka go fokotsa boima ba mmele ba sa bue le ngaka, mme go itima dijo go na le dikotsi tse dingwe. Bona [kaelo ya go itima dijo](/brain-health/fasting/).

## O ka thusa jang motho yo o tshelang le dementia go ja sentle?

Mo bathong ba ba tshelang le dementia, go ja le go nwa **mo go lekaneng** gantsi ke bothata jo bogolo. Alzheimer's Association e re batho ba ka lebala go ja, ba akanya gore ba setse ba jele, kgotsa ba imelwa ke ditlhopho, mme phepo e e sa siamang e ka oketsa matshwao a boitshwaro le go baka go latlhegelwa ke boima
([Alzheimer's Association on food and eating](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating)). Dikakantsho tseno di tswa mo Alzheimer's Association, NIA le Alzheimer's Society:

1. **Naya dijo tse di tshwarwang ka menwana.** Dikarolwana tse dinnye tse di tshwarwang motlhofo, jaaka sandwich, koko, fish sticks, namune kgotsa broccoli e e bedisitsweng, di letla motho go iphepa a sa dirise foroko.
2. **Dirisa pharologanyo mo tafoleng.** Diphetogo tsa pono di ka dira gore go nne thata go farologanya dijo le poleiti. Dipoleiti kgotsa dikotlolo tse ditshweu mo mmetseng o o farologanang di ka thusa. Tila dipoleiti le matsela a tafole a a nang le dipaterone.
3. **Naya dijo di le nngwe kgotsa di le pedi ka nako.** Poleiti e e tletseng e ka imetsa motho.
4. **Dira nako ya dijo e ritibetse.** Tima TV, boloka tafole e le bonolo, mme o neye nako e ntsi. NIA e akantsha gore o se ka wa potlakisa kgotsa wa pateletsa.
5. **Naya dijo tse dinnye le diseneke gantsi.** NIA e akantsha dijo tse dinnye tse di dirwang gantsi le diseneke tse di nang le dikalori tse dintsi fa motho a sa je mo go lekaneng
([NIA tips for eating well](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/tips-caregivers-helping-people-alzheimers-disease-eat-well)).
6. **Thusa ka go nwa.** Naya dikopi tse dinnye tsa metsi kgotsa dino tse dingwe mo letsatsing, mmogo le dijo tse di nang le metsi a mantsi jaaka maungo, sopo, milkshake le smoothie. Fa motho a sa batle metsi, NIA e akantsha lero, metsi a a nang le tatso, broth, mašwi, kgotsa kofi kgotsa tee e e se nang caffeine.
7. **Lekola mogote.** Motho a ka se lemoge gore dijo kgotsa seno se bolelo thata.
8. **Fetola diforoko le dikopi.** Dikatlolo go na le dipoleiti, dikgaba tse di nang le matshwaro a magolo, le dikopi tse di nang le dikhurumelo di ka dira gore go ja go nne bonolo. O se ka wa tshwenyega ka phepa e e sa itekanela.
9. **Dira dijo di nne bonolo go tlhafuna le go kometsa.** Segolola dijo ka dikarolwana tse dinnye kgotsa naya dijo tse di bonolo. Fa motho a gotlhola kgotsa a kgamiwa ke dijo, bolelela ngaka ya gagwe.
10. **Tila go pateletsa motho go ja.** NIA e lemoga gore keletso ya dijo e fokotsega ka tlholego mo magatong a morago a Alzheimer's.

### Matshwao a o tshwanetseng go bolelela ngaka ka one

Go latlhegelwa ke boima, go gana dijo, letsapa kgotsa bokoa go ka nna matshwao a gore motho ga a je mo go lekaneng. Alzheimer's Society e re meno a a botlhoko kgotsa dentures, kgatelelo ya maikutlo, pipelo le diphetogo tsa melemo tsotlhe di ka fokotsa keletso ya dijo
([Alzheimer's Society on appetite](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/poor-appetite-dementia)). Matshwao a go tlhoka metsi a a umakilweng ke NIA a akaretsa molomo o o omeletseng, go tsewa ke sedidi, go itaya ga pelo ka bonako le go bona dilo tse di seyong
([NIA on common medical problems](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/common-medical-problems-alzheimers-disease-information-caregivers)).

## Bua le ngaka

Pele o dira diphetogo tse dikgolo mo dijong tsa gago kgotsa tsa motho yo mongwe, bua le ngaka kgotsa setsebi sa dijo se se kwadisitsweng, segolobogolo fa go na le bolwetse jwa sukiri, bolwetse jwa diphilo, mathata a go kometsa kgotsa go latlhegelwa ke boima bosheng. Mo mothong yo o tshelang le dementia, botsa gore o tshwanetse go leka go ja le go nwa bokae letsatsi le letsatsi, le gore setsebi sa dijo kgotsa ditlaleletso di ka thusa.

Batla thuso ya tshoganyetso fa motho a kgamiwa ke dijo, kgotsa a na le pherekano ya tshoganyetso, sedidi se se maswe kgotsa a idibala.
