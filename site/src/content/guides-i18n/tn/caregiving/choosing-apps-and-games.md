Mabenkele a di-app a tletse metshameko e e tshwailweng e le ya bagodi, ya kgopolo kgotsa ya dementia. Mengwe e dirilwe ka kelotlhoko. Mengwe e tsenya ditsholofetso tse di utlwalang jaaka tsa bongaka, dibadi tsa nako tse di fetolang motshameko go nna teko, kgotsa dipapatso le go kokoanya tshedimosetso. Tsebe eno e go naya tsela ya go sekaseka app nngwe le nngwe ka bowena. Ga re beye ditlhagiswa tse dingwe mo maemong, e bile ga re di sekaseke.

## Ke eng se ke tshwanetseng go se akanya pele ke tlhopha app ya dementia?

Akanya gore o batla app e dire eng. «Motshameko wa Mme» o ka raya dilo tse di farologaneng thata, mme tlhopho e e siameng mo maikaelelong mangwe gantsi ga e a siamela a mangwe.

| Maikaelelo | Se se kayang | Se o tshwanetseng go se batla |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tlhotlheletso e e bonolo | Go nyalanya, go rulaganya, diphazele tse di motlhofo, metshameko ya mafoko | Bothata jo bo ka fetolwang, ditshwantsho tse di nnang di bonala, go se nne le kgatelelo ya nako |
| Go gopola dilo tse di tlwaelegileng | Ditshwantsho tsa bogologolo, maele a a tlwaelegileng, mmino wa bonnyeng jwa bone | Ditshwantsho tsa gago, ditlhagiso tse di bulegileng, go se nne le dipotso tsa tlhatlhobo |
| Go ritibala | Ditshwantsho tse di bonya, medumo e e didimetseng, go hema, tlholego | Go motlhofo go simolola le go emisa, ga go na sepe se se phatsimang, ga go na dilo tse di gakgamatsang |
| Kamano | Go tshameka kgotsa go bua mmogo, lo le mmogo kgotsa ka bidio | E diretswe batho ba babedi, ditlhagiso tsa puisano |

Dipatlisiso ka ga «tlhotlheletso ya tlhaloganyo» e e rulagantsweng ya batho ba ba tshelang le dementia gantsi di akaretsa dikopano tsa setlhopha tse di etelelwang pele ke badiri ba ba katisitsweng, tse di nang le dipuisano, metshameko ya mafoko, diphazele, mmino le ditirwana tsa boitlhamelo. Tshekatsheko ya [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005562_can-cognitive-stimulation-benefit-people-dementia) e fitlheletse mosola o monnye ka palogare, o o neng o farologana mo dipatlisisong. Lenaneo leo la loago, le le farologaneng, ga se selo se le sengwe le app e e dirisiwang ke motho a le nosi, ka jalo tsaya app nngwe le nngwe e le tirwana e le nngwe e e abelanwang gareng ga tse dintsi.

## Nka itse jang gore app e siametse bokgoni jwa motho yoo?

Leba kafa ba tsibogelang ka gone, go na le go leba tshekatsheko kgotsa leina la legato. Batho ba babedi ba ba leng mo «legatong le le tshwanang» ba ka farologana thata mo ponong, kutlong, taolong ya diatla, puong le mo go ikutlweng ba phuthologile fa ba dirisa sekirini. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) e akantsha gore o gopole dilo tse ba neng ba di rata mo nakong e e fetileng le bokgoni jwa bone jwa jaanong, mme o tlhome mogopolo mo monateng go na le mo go atlega.

Ditshupo tsa gore e a ba siametse:

- ba simolola kgotsa ba tsaya karolo ntle le go tlhoka go tlhotlhelediwa thata;
- ba nyenya, ba ntsha maikutlo kgotsa ba anela polelo;
- diphoso ga di bonale di ba tshwenya;
- ba itumelela go emisa fa nako e fitlhile.

Ditshupo tsa gore ga e a ba siametse:

- ba boeletsa gangwe le gape «Ga ke kgone» kgotsa «Seno ke boeleele»;
- ba tlhakane tlhogo ka gore ba tobetse eng;
- kgopisego, ditlhong kgotsa go sutumetsa sedirisiwa kgakala;
- e dira fela fa wena o dira bontsi jwa dilo.

Go tshwenyega ke letshwao, ga se sengwe se o tshwanetseng go se pateletsa. Bona [se o ka se dirang fa ba gana](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Go bona dikakanyo tse di theilweng mo legatong kwa ntle ga di-app, bona ditirwana tsa [legato la ntlha](/activities/early-stage/) le tsa [legato la morago](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ke dikarolo dife tsa popego tse ke tshwanetseng go di batla kgotsa go di tila?

Batla popego e e ritibetseng, e e bonolo, e e siametseng batho ba bagolo, mme o tile sengwe le sengwe se se ka tlisang kgatelelo.

| Batla | Nna kelotlhoko ka |
| --- | --- |
| Ditshwantsho le mokwalo o mogolo, pharologanyo e e bonalang sentle | Ditshupo tse dinnye, disekirini tse di tletseng dilo |
| Tiro e le nngwe e e tlhaloganyesegang mo sekirining sengwe le sengwe | Dikonope tse dintsi, dimenyu, kgotsa ditaelo tsa dikgato tse dintsi |
| Ga go na sebadi sa nako, kgotsa sebadi sa nako se o ka se timang | Go balwa ga nako e e yang kwa tlase, meputso ya lebelo, «feta nako ya gago» |
| Ga go na maduo a dintlha, kgotsa tshedimosetso e e bonolo | Dintlha, matshelo, ditatelano tsa phenyo, maemo, «dingwaga tsa boboko» |
| Diphoso di tshwarwa ka tidimalo | Medumo ya temoso, matshwao a X a a khibidu, melaetsa ya «Ga go a siama» |
| Ditshwantsho tsa batho ba bagolo, tsa dilo tsa mmatota | Merero e e tshwanang le ya dikhathunu kgotsa ya bana |
| E a bereka le fa go sena inthanete, ga go tlhokege akhaonto go simolola | O tshwanetse go ikwadisa, go tsena, kgotsa go laolola diteng pele |
| Go motlhofo go ema nakwana le go emisa | Difensetere tse di itshimololang tse di kopang gore o tswelele o tshameka kgotsa o lekanyetse tiriswa |
| Ga go na dipapatso, tlhwatlhwa e e tlhaloganyesegang ya go duela gangwe fela kgotsa ya peeletso | Dipapatso, mabokoso a theko a diteng tse di sa itsiweng, dikgopotso tsa theko kgapetsakgapetsa |
| Go motlhofo gore motho wa bobedi a tsenelele | Ya motshameki a le mongwe fela, thulaganyo ya tlhodisano |

Dipapatso le dikgopotso tsa theko di motlhofo go di tobetsa ka phoso.

## Nka tlhatlhoba jang sephiri sa app?

Tlhatlhoba gore app e kokoanya eng, e e abelanwa le bomang, le gore a o ka e phimola. Pele o e tsenya:

1. **Bala tshobokanyo ya sephiri ya lebenkele la diapp.** [Data safety section](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) ya Google Play le [privacy information section](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102399) ya App Store di bontsha mefuta ya data e app e ka e kokoanyang le go e abelana ka yone. Ditlamo ka bobedi di bolela gore batlhami ke bone ba neelang tshedimosetso eno, ka jalo ke ntlha ya go simolola, e seng tlhatlhobo e e feletseng.
2. **Ipotse gore a e tlhoka akhaonto.** Fa app e bontsha ditshwantsho fela kgotsa metshameko e e motlhofo, go ka nna ga se ka ga tlhokega gore e itse leina la gago, imeile kgotsa letsatsi la botsalo.
3. **Tlhatlhoba ditetla.** A motshameko wa go nyalanya ditshwantsho ruri o tlhoka lefelo la gago, batho ba o ikgolaganang le bone, maekerofounu kgotsa laeborari yotlhe ya ditshwantsho tsa gago? Tima sengwe le sengwe se e sa se tlhokeng.
4. **Batla dipapatso le ditlhatlhobo tsa tiriso.** Didirisiwa tsa papatso le tsa go latedisa di ka romela data ka ga tsela e app e dirisiwang ka yone kwa ditlamong tse dingwe.
5. **Batlisisa gore ditshwantsho di ya kae.** Fa o tsenya ditshwantsho tsa lelapa, a di sala mo sedirising kgotsa di romelwa kwa sefareng? A o ka di tlosa?
6. **Tlhatlhoba go phimola.** Leba mo pholising ya sephiri kgotsa mo tshobokanyong ya data gore o ka phimola jang data le akhaonto. App e e reng data ga e ka ke ya phimolwa e tlhoka kelotlhoko e e oketsegileng, segolobogolo fa e boloka ditshwantsho kgotsa dintlha tsa boitekanelo.
7. **Bala pholisi ya sephiri** gore o bone dikarabo tse di utlwalang sentle ka ga se se kokoanngwang, lebaka, lobaka lo se bolokwang ka lone, le gore se abelanwa le bomang.

### A HIPAA e sireletsa tshedimosetso mo di-app tsa dementia?

Gantsi ga go a nna jalo, mme seo se gakgamatsa batho ba le bantsi. Lefapha la U.S. Department of Health and Human Services [le tlhalosa](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) gore HIPAA e dira mo mananeong a boitekanelo, bontsi jwa batlamedi ba tlhokomelo ya boitekanelo, mafelo a a rulaganyang tshedimosetso ya tlhokomelo ya boitekanelo, le badirisani ba bone ba kgwebo. Mo mabakeng a le mantsi ga e sireletse tshedimosetso e o e tsenyang mo app gore o e dirise wena, ntle le fa setheo se se akarediwang ke HIPAA kgotsa modirisani wa sone wa kgwebo e le sone se go fileng app eo. “App ya dementia” e e tswang mo lebenkeleng la di-app gantsi e ka nna kwa ntle ga HIPAA, le fa e bua ka boitekanelo.

### Ke eng se se sireletsang tshedimosetso ya boitekanelo mo diapp tse HIPAA e sa di akaretseng?

Molao wa Kitsiso ya Tlolo ya Tshireletsego ya Boitekanelo wa Federal Trade Commission o akaretsa bontsi jwa tsone. Ka 2024, FTC e ne ya [wetsa diphetogo](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule) tse di tlhalosang gore molao o dirisiwa mo diapp tsa boitekanelo le thekenoloji e e tshwanang le tsone tse di sa akarediwang ke HIPAA. “Tlolo ya tshireletsego” e akaretsa go senolwa ga tshedimosetso go go sa letlelelwang, e seng go thubiwa ga ditsamaiso tsa maranyane fela, mme dikhamphani di tshwanetse go itsise batho ba ba amegileng le FTC. Seo ke letlooa la tshireletsego morago ga gore sengwe se senyege, e seng tsholofetso ya gore app e kokoanya fela se e se tlhokang.

## O tshwanetse go bala jang se se buiwang ka dipatlisiso le bosupi?

Bala se se buiwang ka bosupi ka bonya, mme o belaele thata ditsholofetso tse dikgolo. Dipotso tse o ka di botsang:

- **Go ne ga ithutiwa eng tota?** Patlisiso ya lenaneo le le lengwe la go ikatisa ga e supe gore motshameko mongwe le mongwe mo app o dira ka tsela e e tshwanang.
- **Go ne ga ithutiwa bomang?** Dipoelo mo bathong ba bagolo ba ba itekanetseng di ka nna tsa se dire mo mothong yo o tshelang le dementia.
- **Go ne ga lekanyediwa eng?** Go tokafala mo motshamekong ka boone go farologane le diphetogo mo botshelong jwa letsatsi le letsatsi, mo puisano, mo maikutlong kgotsa mo boikemelong.
- **Patlisiso e nonofile go le kana kang?** Patlisiso e e laolwang ka go abela batho ditlhopha ka tšhono (RCT) e bapisa setlhopha se se dirisang app le setlhopha se se sa e diriseng. Ke tsela e e siameng ya go dira patlisiso, mme RCT e le nngwe e nnye kgotsa e khutshwane ga se bosupi. Batla dipatlisiso di le mmalwa, tse di dirilweng ke babatlisisi ba ba ikemetseng, tse di dumalanang.
- **A e bolela gore e fetola bolwetse?** Patlisiso e e bontshang tokafalo e nnye mo tekong ga se bosupi jwa gore app e fokotsa lebelo, e thibela kgotsa e alafa dementia.

Tsebe ya National Institute on Aging ka ga [cognitive health and older adults](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults) e tlhalosa teko e kgolo e e tshegeditsweng ka madi ke NIH ya katiso e e rulagantsweng ya tlhaloganyo, mme e tlhagisa babadi gore ba nne kelotlhoko ka dikopo tsa gore metshameko mengwe ya khomphiutha le ya mo inthaneteng e ka tokafatsa kgakologelo le go akanya. E re ga go ise go nne le bosupi jo bo lekaneng jwa gore di-app tsa katiso ya boboko tse di rekisiwang di na le tlhotlheletso e e tshwanang le katiso e e lekilweng mo tekong eo. O ka bala go feta mo kaeleng ya rona ya [metshameko ya boboko le bosupi](/brain-health/brain-games/) le [se dipatlisiso di se buang ka go thibela dementia](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).

### Sekai se se itsegeng sa polelo e e tsietsang ke sefe?

Ka Ferikgong 2016, FTC [e phatlalalitse tumalano ya tharabololo](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) le batlhami ba Lumosity, e leng lenaneo la “thapiso ya boboko”. FTC e ne ya bolela gore khamphani e ne e dirile ditsholofetso, kwa ntle ga bosupi jo bo lekaneng, tsa gore metshameko ya yone e ka diegisa go fokotsega ga tlhaloganyo go go amanang le botsofe le go sireletsa kgatlhanong le mathata a a seng magolo a tlhaloganyo, dementia le bolwetse jwa Alzheimer. Khamphani e ne ya dumela go duela $2 million mme e tshwanetse go nna le bosupi jwa saense jo bo nang le bokgoni le jo bo ka ikanngwang pele e ka dira ditsholofetso tse di ntseng jalo mo isagweng. Kgetsi eno ke kgopotso e e molemo ya gore puo ya papatso ga se bosupi.

### Ke mafoko afe a a tshwanetseng go go tlhagisa?

Nna kelotlhoko fa app kgotsa papatso e re:

- «e thibela», «e busetsa morago», «e emisa» kgotsa «e fodisa» dementia kgotsa bolwetse jwa ga Alzheimer;
- «e netefaditswe ke bongaka» kwantle ga go umaka patlisiso, gore ke bomang ba ba neng ba le mo go yone, le gore go ne go lekanyediwa eng;
- e go naya «dingwaga tsa boboko» kgotsa maduo a segopolo a a kayang ekete go ka dirwa tlhatlhobo ya bolwetse;
- e tla «louta», «godisa» kgotsa «thapisa» boboko;
- e dirisa dipolelo tsa batho jaaka bosupi.

Fa o sa tlhomamisega ka se se buiwang, botsa ngaka ya motho yo o mo tlhokomelang.

## Golden Moments e tsena fa kae?

Golden Moments e tlamela ka ditirwana tse di motlhofo tse lo di dirang mmogo, e seng teko ya kgakologelo, kalafi kgotsa kgakololo ya bongaka. Metshameko ya rona, e jaaka [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/), [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/), [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) le [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/), e diretswe go itumelela le go tlotla mmogo. Ga e tlhatlhobe, ga e alafe, ga e thibele kgotsa go lekanya dementia, e bile ga re dire dipego dipe ka ga kgakologelo kgotsa go akanya.

Gape re dira app e e sa duelelweng, [Golden Moments ya iPhone, iPad le Android](/app/). E na le motshameko o o bonolo wa go nyalanya ditshwantsho ka dithaele tse dikgolo, metshameko e e dirisang ditshwantsho tsa lelapa tse o di tlhophang, le dipakete tsa ditshwantsho tse di dirang le fa o se mo inthaneteng, ntle le akhaonto, dipapatso, sebadi sa nako kgotsa dintlha. Lenaane le le tshwanang la tekolo le a dira: leibole la sephiri la App Store la yone le re "Data Not Collected", mme [pholisi ya sephiri ya app](/app-privacy/) e tlhalosa gore dikhopi tsa ditshwantsho di bolokwa kae.

Re ka itumelela fa o ka re sekaseka go na le go re ikanya fela. Dirisa lenaane la tekolo mo tsebeng eno, bala [tsebe ya rona ya sephiri](/privacy/) le [tlhaloso ya rona ya gore ga se kgakololo ya bongaka](/medical-disclaimer/), mme o tlogele go dirisa sengwe le sengwe, go akaretsa le sa rona, se se sa siamelang motho yo o mo tlhokomelang.

## Tsela e e utlwalang ya go leka app e ntšha ke efe?

Le e leke mmogo ka dibeke di sekae mme lo lebe se se diragalang.

1. **Lo e baakanye pele** lo dirisa [kaelo ya rona ya go baakanya thebolete le founo](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/), mme lo simolole mo seemong se se motlhofo thata.
2. **Dirang gore dinako di nne dikhutshwane,** lo ele tlhoko menyebo, puisano le boiketlo, mme lo fele ka motsotso o monate.
3. **Lo e tlogele fa e baka kgoreletsego,** le fa e ka bo e na le dikatlholo tse di kwa godimo jang.
