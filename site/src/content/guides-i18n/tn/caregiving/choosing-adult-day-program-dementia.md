Lenaneo la letsatsi la bagolo le ka fetola sebaka le go thusa mo ditlwaelong tsa letsatsi. Le ka boela le fa motlhokomedi wa lelapa nako ya tiro, dithata kgotsa phomolo. Dilenaneo di farologana, mme setsha se se siameng motho a le mongwe se ka se siame yo mongwe. Dikgetho tsa motho le go ikutlwa ga gagwe ke tsone tse di tshwanetseng go laola tshwetso.

[Tlhaloso ya Alzheimer's Association ka disetsha tsa letsatsi la bagolo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/adult-day-centers) le [lenane la go tlhopha setsha](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-an-adult-day-center-ts.pdf) di gakolola gore o lekole ditirelo, badiri, dipapadi, ditefo le dipalangwa pele o ngwadisa. Tsebe e e thusa go rulaganya dipotso; ga e tlhophe kgotsa go nna setsha se se itseng.

## Ke ka tsela efe ya go tlhomamisa gore ke tshwanetse go leka lenaneo la letsatsi?

Simolola ka go botsa motho gore a ka itumela ka eng le gore o amogela thuso e e mofuta ofe. A o batla papadi ya setshaba, dijo le batho ba bangwe, kgotsa boteng jwa motho fa motlhokomedi a dira tiro, kgotsa sengwe se sengwe? Lenaneo ke tlhopho, e seng tlhokego.

Akanya ka dipaakanyo tse motho a batlang go di dira, ditlwaelo tsa gagwe le thuso e a e tlhokang mo letsatsing. Lebelela gore dinako tsa setsha, dipalangwa le dipapadi di kopana le ditlhoko tseno. Fa motho a sa rate batho ba le bantsi kgotsa a lapa fa a le mo sekgweng se se tshwerekang, botsa gore a go na kgetho e e khutsitseng kgotsa etelo e kgutshwane.

Lenaneo le ka thusa motlhokomedi go phomola kgotsa go tlhokomela tshwanelo tse dingwe. Seo se botlhokwa, mme go botlhokwa gape gore motho a ikutlwa jang. Bua ka ditlhokego tseno tsotlhe ka pulo mme o batle tshwetso e e amogelesegang go botlhe ba ba amegang.

## Ke ditirelo dife tse ke tshwanetseng go di botsa ka tsone?

Ditirelo di farologana. Disetsha tse dingwe di gatelela go nna le batho le dipapadi; tse dingwe di ka fanang ka dijo, dipalangwa, tlhokomelo ya mmele kgotsa ditirelo tsa boitekanelo. Botsa gore ke ditirelo dife di teng mo malatsing a motho a neng a tla go tla le gore badiri ba ka thusa ditlhoko tse motho a nang le tsone jaanong.

Tsenya tlhwatlhwa mo dipapading, e seng fela sekgala sa lenaneo. Botsa gore batsaya ba dira eng ka sebele, gore ba ka kgetha gore ba dire eng gore dipapadi di nne tse di tlwaelegileng jaaka setswakgolo, bonno, ditlhare kgotsa puisano. Motho ga a tlhoke go tsenela papadi nngwe le nngwe. Botsa gore a go na sefelo se se khutsitseng kgotsa nako ya go phomola.

Fa motho a tlhoka thuso ka dithelo, go sepelela, go ja kgotsa tlhokomelo ya mmele, botsa gore ke mang ka sebele yo o e fang le gore o na le tshwanelo efe. O se ke wa akanya gore lenaneo la letsatsi le fa tlhokomelo ya boitekanelo kgotsa le ka laola ditlhoko tsotlhe. Tlhomamisa ditaelo tse di amanang le boitekanelo le setlhopha sa tlhokomelo ya motho.

## Ke eng se nka se lebelelang mo etelong?

Rulaganya etelo mme, fa motho a batla, mo tlise. Lebelela gore badiri ba bua le batho ka tlotlo, ba fa batsaya nako ya go araba mme ba ba laletsa go tsenela ntle le kgatelelo. Batla dipapadi di ikaegileng ka dikgatlhego le dikgono tsa motho, e seng fela kalendara e e tletseng.

Botsa gore setsha se ithuta jang ka dikgetho tsa motho ka mongwe le gore se araba jang fa motho a tlhoka thuso, a tshwenyega kgotsa a batla go tsamaya. Tlhoma gore setsha se dira eng tshoganyetsong, gore malapa a amogela ditlhaloso jang le gore ke mang yo o ka bolelelwang ka tshwenyo.

Fa go kgonega, etela ka nako ya papadi kgotsa dijo. Lebelela gore sebaka se a utlwala go motho, gore go bonolo go tsamaya mo go sone le gore badiri ba a teng. Pampiri ya tshedimosetso kgotsa tsamaiso ya setsha e ka thusa go rulaganya dipotso, mme go bona letsatsi le le tlwaelegileng go ka go bontsha tse di oketsegileng.

## Ke dintlha dife tsa tirelo tse ke tshwanetseng go di tlhoka?

Botsa ka dinako, malatsi, ditefo, palo e e kwa tlase ya go tla, melao ya go tlogela le ditefo tse di oketsegileng. Botsa gore ke dijo kgotsa dijo tse nnye dife di akareditswe le gore a go ka thusiwa ka dijo tse di kgethegileng. Ditefo le ditirelo di farologana go ya ka lefelo, ka jalo kopa setsha se se tlhalose dikgwetlho tsa sone tse di kwadilweng.

Tlhoma gore a go na le sepelo, ditefo tsa yone le gore a e siame motho. Botsa ka dinako tsa go tsaya motho, thuso ya go nna mo koloing le go tswa, le se se diragalang fa mogaleki a diegile. Fa ngwana wa lelapa a tla go tsaya motho, akanya ka nako le ditefo tseo mo rulaganyong ya gago.

Botsa gore a setsha se ka fa thuso e motho a e tlhokang fa boitekanelo jwa gagwe kgotsa dikgono tsa letsatsi di fetoga. Fa setsha se sa kgone go fitlhelela ditlhoko tseo, botsa gore se a tsweletsa tsela efe kgotsa tirelo efe e nngwe. [Tataiso ya tlhokomelo ya kwa gae](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) e tlhalosa mofuta o mongwe wa thuso o o ka siamelang malapa a mangwe.

## Motho a ka leka lenaneo ka tsela e e sa patelelweng jang?

Botsa gore a setsha se na le etelo e kgutshwane ya tsebiso, dijo kgotsa papadi. Fa go kgonega, letla motho a tlhophe se a batlang go se leka le nako e a batlang go nna teng. Etelo ya ntlha e ka nna e sa tlwaelegang. Botsa motho morago gore o rategile eng, o sa rate eng kgotsa o ka fetola eng.

Fa motho a sa tlhomamisege, batla gore ke ka ntlha ya eng. Sepelo, modumo, batho ba ba sa tlwaelang kgotsa papadi e itlhophileng e ka nna ya bothata. O ka botsa setsha gore a go ka dirwa phetogo e nnye kgotsa gore o etele letsatsi le lengwe. O se ka wa dirisa boikutlo jwa molato kgotsa go gatelela motho gore a tsene.

Morago ga dietelo tse mmalwa, bua le motho le badiri ba setsha ka tsela e tiro e tsamaelang ka yone. Lenaneo le le siameng le ka tlhoka diphetogo mo nakong, mo dipapading kgotsa mo thusong. Fa motho a sa itumela kgotsa tirelo e sa siame, go siame go tlhatlhobang gape.

## Ke ka tsela efe ya go kopanya tiro le setsha?

Ka tumelo ya motho, abelana tshedimosetso e e thusang badiri go mo thusa: leina le a le batlang, tsela ya go bua, ditlwaelo, dikgatlhego le mofuta wa thuso o a o amogelang. Botsa gore setsha se sireletsa tshedimosetso eno jang le gore ditlhaloso di abelanwa jang le lelapa.

Botsa gore ke mang yo o tshwanetseng go bolelelwa fa motho a lwala, a sa tle kgotsa a tlhoka phetogo mo thusong. Boloka leano la thuso ya tshwanelo mo malatsing a setsha se tswetse kgotsa sepelo e sa tsamaye. Fa batho ba le bantsi ba thusa, dumelanang gore ke mang a tla laola sepelo le tshedimosetso ka nngwe.

Lenaneo la letsatsi la bagolo le ka nna karolo e le nngwe ya leano le lo lo pharologanyang la thuso. [Tataiso ya go itlhokomela ka motlhokomedi](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e akaretsa ditsela tsa go rulaganya diphomolo, mme [dipapadi tsa dihuto tsa tlhokomelo](/activities/care-home-groups/) di na le dikakanyo tsa dipapadi tse di bonolo tsa sehuto.

Lenaneo le le siameng ke le motho a batlang go le leka le le ka thusang ditlhoko tsa gagwe. Etela, botsa dipotso tse di tlhamaletseng mme o dire diphetogo go ya ka se motho a se ikutlwang.
