Khalefo e ka fetoga go goeletsa, go sunya kgotsa go kgoba, mme seo se ka tshosa batho ba ba gaufi. Boitshwaro bono bo ka nna bo sa rerwa. Ga bo bontshe gore motho o batla go utlwisa mongwe bothata. Simolang ka go dira gore maemo a nne a sireletsegile, morago lebelelang se motho a se tlhokang.

## Se ke tshwanetseng go se dira fa khalefo e fetoga bokgoba?

Emisa tiro, tlogela sebaka mme o dirise polelo e khutshwane e e khutsafalang. [Alzheimer's Association e eletsa badiriši ba tlhokomelo gore ba tsepamise maikutlo, ba bue ka bonya le go fokotsa dikgoboka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Fa o kotsing e e gaufi kgotsa motho a gobetse, isa batho kwa go sireletsegang mme o bolelele ditirelo tsa tshoganyetso tsa kwa lefelong la gago.

- **Emisa se se diragalang fa o ka kgona.** Fa go tlhapa, go apara, go etela kgotsa tiro e nngwe e le go mo galefisa, emisa. E ka emelela fa e se tlhokego ya go sireletsega kgotsa ya bophelo ka bonako.
- **Naya sebaka.** Tlogela sebaka mme o dire gore go nne le tsela e e bulegileng ya gore mongwe le mongwe a tsamaye. Se ka se thiba, se ntshe, se gateletse kgotsa se tshware motho ka tsela e e sa lebelelwang.
- **Buang ka lentswe le le tlhaela le mafoko a a khutshwane.** O ka re, “Ke bona gore o galefile. Ke tla go tlogelela sebaka.” Se ganetsane, se tshosetse kgotsa se kope tlhaloso.
- **Isa batho ba bangwe kwa thoko.** Ngwana, motsamaisi kgotsa mothusi a ka ya mo kamoreng e nngwe fa motho a ntse a kgodiega. Mongwe yo o khutsafetseng a bua a ka nna a thusa go feta batho ba le bantsi ba leka go thusa ka nako e le nngwe.
- **Tlogela fa go dirwa bogale jwa mmele.** Ya kwa lefelong le le sireletsegileng mme o kope thuso mo go mongwe. Se leke go tshwara motho ka matla.

[Alzheimer's Society e eletsa go tlogela sebaka, go se tshware ka matla kgotsa go mo gakgamatsa, le go tloga go batla thuso fa go direga bogale jwa mmele](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). [Alzheimer's Association e boela e eletsa go kopa tumelo, go fokotsa dikgoboka le go se ka wa mo thibela kgotsa go mo tshware ka matla](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Dirisa ditirelo tsa tshoganyetso tsa kwa lefelong la gago fa go na le kotsi e e gaufi; se emele gore modumo o fete fa motho a ka nna a gobala.

## Ke eng se se ka nnang le seabe mo boitshwarong jono?

Gangwe go na le dilo di le dintsi, mme motlhokomedi wa lelapa ga a kgone go tlhatlhoba sepe sa ntlha. Alzheimer's Association e bolela gore go ka nna le seabe ka bothata jwa mmele, kgatelelo ya tikologo le mathata a puisano.

Akanya ka se se diragetseng pele ga phetogo:

- A motho o lapile, o nyorogile, o tlhoka metsi, o tshwanetse go nna lefoifoi kgotsa o sa itumela?
- A tiro e ne e le thata, e ne e potlakile kgotsa e sa tlwaelega? A mongwe o ne a tswelela fa motho a bontsha kgatelelo?
- A go ne go na le modumo o o godileng, batho ba le bantsi ba bua, go tletse dilo kgotsa lefelo le le sa tlwaelegang?
- A motho o ne a na le bothata jwa go bona kgotsa go utlwa se se diragalang?
- A moriri, tlhokomelo, mothusi kgotsa bolwetse jwa motho jo bo fetogileng gaufi?

[Alzheimer's Society e eletsa go batla ditlwaelo le go akanya gore maemo a ne a ikutlwala jang go motho](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Dipotso tseno ke dikgopotso tsa go lebelela, e seng bosupi jwa gore sengwe se le sengwe se ne sa dira boitshwaro. Se leke go tlhatlhoba, go tshwara kgotsa go neela motho dihuduwana ka bowena.

## Re ka fokotsa jang go ya ga khutlo e nngwe?

Dirisa se o se boneng go fetola kopano e e latelang. Se se ka thusa, mme ga se kgone go tiisa gore boitshwaro bo ka se boe gape. Leka phetogo e le nngwe ka nako gore go nne bonolo go lemoga gore motho a ikutlwa a tlhaela kgatelelo.

- **Tlhopha nako e e khutsang.** Fa tiro e gantsi e sa tsamaya sentle ha motho a lapile, botsa gore a nako e nngwe e siama go feta.
- **Dira gore tikologo e nne bonolo.** Tima modumo o o neng o le morago, fokotsa palo ya batho mo kamoreng mme o bokelle dilo tse o di tlhokang pele lo simolola.
- **Tlhalosa kgato e le nngwe o bo o ema.** Batla go tswa pele ga motho, bolelela gore o dira eng, botsa pele o thusa mme o naye nako ya go araba.
- **Emisa fa motho a bontsha kgatelelo.** Go ganela, go tlhalela kwa morago, sefatlhego se se kgatelelwang kgotsa lentswe le le tsenang modumo di ka nna sesupo sa go emisa. Se tsamaisise tiro gore e nne botlhokwa go feta boikhutso jwa motho.
- **Abelana leano le le bonolo.** Fa batho ba le bantsi ba mo tlhokomelang, dumalanang ka matshwao a a raya “emisa,” polelo e e khutsafalang e e tshwanetseng go dirisiwa le gore a o bolelela mang fa tsela e fetoga.

Go tsaya ditiro tse di tshwanetseng, bona dikgetho tsa rona tsa [go tlhapa](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) le [go apara](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/). Tataiso ya [dikeletso tsa puisano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) e akaretsa mekgwa ya go bua le go reetsa ntle le go tsenya kgatelelo.

## Ke ka dira eng morago ga go dikologa?

Neela katlego le go boela mo tirong e e tlwaelegileng, e e sa tlhokeng matlapa, fa motho a batla seo. Se ko se tlhokomele, se ntshe molato kgotsa se otle. Alzheimer's Society e bolela gore motho a ka nna a sa ikutlwe a le botoka morago ga gore boitshwaro bo fete, le fa a sa gakologelwe se se diragetseng.

Fa o batla go tlhaloganya ditlwaelo, kwala kwa go wena ka bokhutshwane se se diragetseng pele, se o se boneng, nako e se tswetseng ka yone le se se thusitseng. Dira gore kwalo e nne ya therešo mme o e abelane le sehlopha sa tlhokomelo ya motho fa go siame. Se rekorde motho kgotsa go abelana ka dintlha tsa poifo ntle le tumelo.

Fa tiro e gantsi e tlisa kgatelelo, fetola nako kgotsa tsela, kgotsa kopa mongwe yo o tshepegang go thusa. O ka amogela go ganiwa mme o leke gape ka nako e nngwe. Lenaane la [go dira eng fa motho a re nnyaa](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) le na le ditsela tse dintsi tsa go emisa ntle le kgatelelo.

## Ke tshwanetse go batla keletso ya bongaka neng?

Bolelela ngaka ya motho ka bokgoba jo bo ncha, jo bo sa tlwaelegang, jo bo tsewang gape kgotsa jo bo oketsegang. [Alzheimer's Association e eletsa tlhatlhobo ya bongaka fa go na le diphetogo tsa boitshwaro, go sa kgathalesege fa di tlhaga ka bonako](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Botlhoko, bolwetse, boroko, tikologo le dibolaodi di ka nna di tlhokana go lebelelwa. Ngaka e ka tlhatlhoba maemo a motho.

Batla thuso ya bongaka ya tshoganyetso ka bonako fa motho a sa gakologelwe sentle ka tsela e e tsamaisang, a gobetse kgotsa a bonala a sa phutse. Fa wena kgotsa mongwe a le kotsing e e gaufi, bolelela ditirelo tsa tshoganyetso tsa kwa lefelong la gago. Se emele go bokelela dikwalo kgotsa go batla lebaka pele.

Tshireletso ya gago le yone e a botlhokwa. Bolelela leloko la lelapa le o le tshepang, ngaka kgotsa tirelo ya tshegetso ya bolwetse jwa go lebala ya kwa lefelong la gago fa dikgopotso tse di tsaya nako, kgotsa fa o ikutlwa o tshoga. Kopa leano le le sireletsang motho le batho ba ba mo potologileng. O tshwanetse go thusiwa; go dira tse o le nosi ga se tlhokego.
