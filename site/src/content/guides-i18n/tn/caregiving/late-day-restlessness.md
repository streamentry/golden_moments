Malapa a mantsi a lemoga selo se se tshwanang: dimoso di batla di ritibetse, mme fa thapama e ntse e tswelela, motho yo o tshelang ka dementia a ka nna a sa ritibala, a tshwenyega, a tlhakanyege tlhaloganyo kgotsa a tenegega bonolo. A ka nna a tsamayatsamaya, a kopa go “ya gae” le fa a le kwa gae, kgotsa a kgopisega motlhofo. Se gantsi se bidiwa sundowning.

Ga go na tharabololo e le nngwe fela, mme diphetogo mo thulaganyong ya letsatsi, mo phaposing le mo mofuteng wa ditirwana tse o di neelang di ka thusa gore dithapama di nne bonolo le go ritibala. Tsebe eno e phutha dikakanyo tse di mosola, tse e seng tsa bongaka, go tswa go National Institute on Aging le Alzheimer’s Association. Ga se kgakololo ya bongaka. Fa matshwao a le maswa, a tlhaga ka tshoganyetso kgotsa a nna maswe le go feta, bua le ngaka ya motho yoo.

## Sundowning ke eng?

Sundowning ke go sa iketla, go tshwenyega, go tenegega bonolo kgotsa go tlhakana tlhogo mo go simologang kgotsa mo go nnang maswe fa lesedi la letsatsi le nyelela, mo thapameng kgotsa mo maitseboeng a ntlha. [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) e bolela gore go lapa thata go ka oketsa go sa iketla kwa bokhutlong jwa letsatsi.

## Ke eng se se ka dirang gore go sa ritibale mo maitseboeng go nne maswe le go feta?

Dilo di le mmalwa di ka nna le seabe, gantsi mmogo. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/sleep-issues-sundowning) e umaka mabaka a a jaaka:

- letsapa morago ga letsatsi le le tletseng;
- tikologo e ntšha kgotsa e e tlhakanyang tlhogo;
- «nako ya mmele ya ka fa teng» e e sa tsamayeng sentle le nako, e e dirang gore motho yo o nang le dementia a ikutlwe a otsela motshegare mme a nne a tsogile bosigo;
- lesedi le le kwa tlase le meriti, tse di ka tlhakanyang tlhogo;
- go lemoga kgatelelo ya maikutlo kgotsa go tenegega ga batho ba ba mo dikologileng;
- go tlhoka boroko jo bonnyennyane, mo go tlwaelegileng mo bathong ba bagolo.

NIA e oketsa mabaka a go sa ritibale ka kakaretso, a a akaretsang botlhoko, go kgobega marapo kgotsa kgatelelo ya maikutlo, go se ikhutse go le kalo, go bipelwa, modumo o montsi thata kgotsa batho ba bantsi thata, le melemo mengwe kgotsa fa melemo e dirisana ka ditsela tse di sa siamang.

## Nka rulaganya jang letsatsi gore maitseboa a nne a ritibetse?

Sudusetsa dilo tse di tlhokang maiteko kwa pele mo letsatsing mme o dire gore thapama e nne bonolo e bile e ka solofelwa. Ka bobedi NIA le Alzheimer's Association ba akantsha thulaganyo e e tlwaelegileng ya letsatsi. Sekai sa letsatsi, se o ka se fetolang go dumalana le sa gago:

| Nako | Se se ka thusang |
| --- | --- |
| Moso | Dithulaganyo tsa go kopana le ba bangwe, go tlhapa, go ya kwa ntle le tirwana nngwe le nngwe e e tlhokang maiteko, fa batho ba le bantsi ba le matlhagatlhaga thata |
| Moragonyana mo mosong | Nako kwa ntle kgotsa fa letlhakoreng la letlhabaphefo le le nang le letsatsi; go tsamaya kgotsa tirwana nngwe |
| Motshegare | Dijô tsa motshegare tse dikgolo (Alzheimer's Association e akantsha dijo tsa motshegare tse dikgolo le dijo tsa maitseboa tse di bobebe) |
| Thapama ya ntlha | Ikhutse fa go tlhokega, mme o tile boroko jo boleele thapama |
| Go tloga mo bogareng jwa thapama go ya kwa thapama e e tseneletseng | Ditirwana tse di ritibetseng, tse di tlwaelegileng; seneke le seno; go se nne le kafeine |
| Pele ga phirimo ya letsatsi | Tshuba mabone mo ntlong mme o tswale digaretene pele ga lefifi le tsena |
| Maitseboa | Dijo tse di bobebe, mmino o o ritibetseng, dilo tse di sa tsosang maikutlo thata, thulaganyo e e tlhomameng ya nako ya go robala |

Dintlha di se kae tse di kgethegileng go tswa mo metsweding:

- **Lesedi la letsatsi.** NIA e akantsha go rulaganya nako kwa ntle kgotsa fa letlhakoreng la letlhabaphefo letsatsi lengwe le lengwe go bona lesedi la letsatsi.
- **Go tsamaya letsatsi le letsatsi, mme e seng go le gontsi thata.** Leka go nna matlhagatlhaga mo mmeleng letsatsi le letsatsi o sa rulaganye ditirwana di le dintsi thata, go bua NIA.
- **Dino tsa kwa bofelong jwa letsatsi.** Tila bojalwa le dino tse di nang le kafeine, jaaka kofi kgotsa kola, kwa bofelong jwa letsatsi (NIA). Alzheimer's Association e bile e umaka nikotine.
- **Boroko jwa motshegare.** Tila boroko jo boleele le go otsela thapama (NIA). Alzheimer's Association e akantsha go fokotsa boroko jwa motshegare fa go robala bosigo e le bothata.

Go bona go le gontsi ka go aga letsatsi le le tlhomameng, bona [ditirwana tsa thulaganyo ya letsatsi le letsatsi](/activities/daily-routine/).

## Nka dira jang gore legae le nne le ritibetse mo thapameng e e kwa bofelong?

Fokotsa modumo, oketsa lesedi mme o boloke dilo tse di tlwaelegileng gaufi.

- **Lesedi le le phatshimang le le lekanang.** Alzheimer's Association e gakolola gore legae le nne le bonesitswe sentle mo maitseboeng. Bonesa lefelo pele meriti e bonala.
- **Modumo o o kwa tlase.** Tima kgotsa fokotsa TV le radio, mme o fokotse baeti kgotsa ditiro tse dintsi tsa mo gae ka nako eno. Alzheimer's Association e akantsha go fokotsa dilo tse di ka tsosang maikutlo thata mo maitseboeng jaaka TV, ditiro tsa mo gae le mmino o o kwa godimo.
- **Dilo tse di tlwaelegileng tse di gomotsang.** NIA e akantsha mmino o o ritibatsang le go boloka dilo le ditshwantsho tse di rategang thata gaufi mo legaeng.
- **Tlhola boiketlo.** Alzheimer's Association e gakolola go tlhola gore a go na le botlhoko, tlala, lenyora, pipitlelo, senya se se tletseng, letsapa, ditshwaetso kgotsa go tlhotlheletsega ga letlalo, le go boloka kamore e le mo themperetšhareng e e siametseng boiketlo.
- **Maikutlo a gago.** Batho ba kgona go lemoga fa o na le kgatelelo ya maikutlo. NIA e akantsha gore o dire dilo ka bonya mme o leke go iketla fa matshwenyego a gago a ka tswa a oketsa kgatelelo.

## Ke ditirwana dife tse di didimetseng tse re ka di dirang thapama morago?

Tlhopha ditirwana tse di tlwaelegileng, tse di sa itlhaganediweng, le tse go leng motlhofo go di emisa. Alzheimer's Association e akantsha ditirwana tse di ritibatsang jaaka mmino o o didimetseng, dinepe kgotsa baesekopo e e rategang, le go tsamaya mmogo go ka thusa go fokotsa go sa ritibala. NIA e akantsha seneke kgotsa seno, lenaneo la TV le le rategang, mmino, go tsamaya, go bala, kgotsa tiro e e bonolo ya mo gae jaaka go mena diaparo tse di tlhatswitsweng.

Megopolo e o ka e lekang:

1. **Go mena diaparo tse di tlhatswitsweng mmogo.** NIA e akantsha mofuta ono wa tiro e e bonolo ya mo gae. Dithawulo di motlhofo go di tshwara, mme tiro eno e na le bokhutlo jo bo bonalang sentle.
2. **Tshameka mmino o o rategang.** Dipina tse di tlwaelegileng tsa dingwaga tsa bone tsa bonnye, ka modumo o o kwa tlase. Go opela mmogo ka bonolo le gone go siame. Megopolo e mengwe mo go [ditirwana tsa mmino](/activities/music/).
3. **Leba dinepe.** Alebamo ya dinepe, kgotsa dinepe di sekae tsa lelapa mo go [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ka dinepe di tlogetswe di bonala, di ka tlisa puisano e e didimetseng.
4. **Tsamaya sebaka se sekhutshwane.** Mo tshingwaneng, mo tikologong kgotsa mo phasejeng, pele ga lefifi le tsena.
5. **Abelanang seneke le seno.** Sengwe se se bonolo, se se bothitho e bile se se se nang kafine, lo le kwa tafole mmogo.
6. **Go nyalanya ditshwantsho ka bonolo.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ka dithaele di le nne mme ditshwantsho di bonala go motlhofo go simolola le go emisa.
7. **Go taka mebala.** [Coloring](/games/coloring/) mo sekirining kgotsa mo pampiring e e gatisitsweng, go se na maikaelelo a gore lo tshwanetse go fetsa.
8. **Go leba le go bua.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) di bontsha setshwantsho le potso e e bulegileng. Go leba fela go lekane.
9. **Khutso ya go hema.** [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ke khutso e e bonya, e e didimetseng e o e simololang le go e emisa ka bowena.
10. **Ditirwana tsa diatla.** Go tlhopha dikonopo kgotsa dikausu, go boreletsa kobo e e bonolo, kgotsa go tshwara selo se se tlwaelegileng. Bona [ditirwana tsa maikutlo a mmele](/activities/sensory/).

Tila metshameko e mesha, maeto a a tletseng dilo tse dintsi le sengwe le sengwe se se tlhokang go gakologelwa go le gontsi kgotsa go tsepamisa mogopolo thata mo thapama morago. Di bolokele moso.

## Ke tshwanetse go dira eng fa go sa ritibala go simolola?

Nna o ritibetse, mo tlhomamisetse, mme ka bonolo o mo gogele kwa sengwe se sele. NIA e akantsha gore o bue ka bonolo, o reetse dilo tse di mo tshwenyang, o tile dikgang, o mo tlhomamisetse gore o sireletsegile, mme o dirise go ama ka bonolo fa go ka thusa. Go tswa foo, leka go mo faposa ka sengwe: seneke, seno, mmino kgotsa go tsamaya ka maoto.

Alzheimer's Association e akantsha mafoko a a jaaka «O sireletsegile fano» le «Ke maswabi gore o tshwenyegile», mme e gakolola gore o oketse lesedi, o dire dilo ka bonya mme o fokotse dilo tse di ka mo tlhotlheletsang thata. O se ka wa mo tswalelela mo sekhutlong, wa mo pitlaganyetsa, wa mo thibela ka go mo tshwara, kgotsa wa ganetsana le ene.

Fa motho yo o mo tlhokomelang a simolola go nna bogale, NIA e gakolola gore o nne kgakala ka pabalesego go fitlha maitsholo ao a fela. Fa e le tshoganyetso, letsa 911 mme o tlhalose gore motho yoo o na le dementia. [Kaedi ya rona ya go araba bogale le maitsholo a a bogale](/caregiving/when-anger-becomes-aggression-with-dementia/) e akaretsa pabalesego ya ka bonako le se o ka se dirang morago ga moo.

Dikaedi tsa rona ka ga [tlhaeletsano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) le [se o ka se dirang fa a re nnyaa](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) di na le dintlha tse dingwe ka mafoko a a didimatsang.

## Nka lemoga jang dilo tse di bakang seno?

Boloka rekoto e khutshwane ya beke kgotsa tse pedi. Alzheimer's Association e akantsha gore o latelele se se diragalang pele ga ditiragalo tsa go sa ritibale fa letsatsi le phirima go lemoga dilo tse di bakang seno. Kwala nako e go sa ritibale go simolotseng ka yone, se se diragetseng mo oureng pele ga moo, boroko jwa motshegare, dijo le dino, baeti, modumo le lesedi. Isa rekoto eo kwa ngakeng. NIA e bolela gore go boloka rekoto ya diphetogo mo maitsholong go ka thusa batlhokomedi le baitseanape ba tlhokomelo ya botsogo go lemoga paterone.

## Nka ikgolaganya leng le ngaka ka ga sundowning?

Ikgolaganye le ngaka ya motho yo o mo tlhokomelang fa go sa ritibala go le gosha, go ntse maswe ka tshoganyetso, kgotsa go tla le diphetogo tse dingwe. NIA e bolela gore diphetogo tsa boitshwaro tse di tlang ka tshoganyetso kgotsa tse di fetogang ka bonako, segolobogolo morago ga tshwaetso kgotsa diphetogo tsa bosheng mo melemo, di tshwanetse go itsisiwe ngaka ka bonako.

Kopa ngaka gore e tlhatlhobe dilo tse di ka dirang gore maitseboa a nne thata. Alzheimer's Association e umaka mathata a mmele a jaaka ditshwaetso tsa ditsela tsa motlhapo, go sa kgone go itshwara, maoto a a sa ritibaleng le sleep apnea, mme e akantsha gore o botse gore ke dinako dife tsa letsatsi tse di siametseng molemo mongwe le mongwe. NIA e thathamisa botlhoko, go pipamala ga mala le ditlamorago tsa melemo kgotsa kafa melemo e ka dirisanang ka teng jaaka dingwe tsa dibaka tse di ka nnang teng tsa go tlhobaela.

Alzheimer's Association e bolela gore mekgwa e e sa diriseng melemo gantsi ke yone e e itshupelediwang pele mo mathateng a boroko a a amanang le Alzheimer's, le gore melemo ya go tlhobaela kwa bofelong jwa letsatsi e tshwanetse go sekasekwa fela fa mekgwa eo e sa bereka, morago ga go lekola dikotsi le melemo. Tshwetso eo ke ya ngaka. O se ka wa simolola, wa emisa kgotsa wa fetola molemo ope, thuso ya boroko kgotsa setsenngwa sa tlaleletso ntle le kgakololo ya ngaka.

## Nka kgona jang go itshokela maitseboa ke le nosi?

Thapama go ya maitseboa e ka nna nako e e thata thata mo letsatsing la motho yo o tlhokomelang motho yo o nang le dementia. Fa go kgonega, kopa leloko la lelapa, tsala kgotsa mothusi yo o duelang go go thusa ka nako eo ya thapama go ya maitseboa, malatsi a se kae mo bekeng, le fa e ka nna ura e le nngwe fela. Kaedi ya rona ya [go itlhokomela fa o tlhokomela yo mongwe](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e na le dikgetho tsa go ikhutsa le Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline ya mahala.
