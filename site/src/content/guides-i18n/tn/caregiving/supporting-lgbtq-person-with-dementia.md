Boitshupo jwa motho wa LGBTQ+ ke karolo e le nngwe ya gore ke mang. Botsa gore ke eng se se botlhokwa mo go ene, dirisa mafoko a a a tlhophang, mme o mo letle go swetsa gore ke bomang ba ba ka itseng dintlha tsa gagwe. O se ka wa tsaya gore boitshupo, molekane, tshegetso ya lelapa kgotsa dikeletso tsa sephiri tsa motho ke dife ka ntlha ya ponalo, direkoto tsa bogologolo kgotsa gore ke mang yo o etelang.

[Kaedi ya Alzheimer’s Society ya go tshegetsa motho wa LGBTQ+ yo o tshelang le dimantia](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/supporting-lgbtq-dementia) e akantsha go botsa ka dikeletso tsa motho le go tila go dira ditshwetso ka dikamano. [Metswedi ya dimantia ya LGBTQ+ ya Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) le yona e tlhalosa matshwenyego ka kgethololo le go batla tlhokomelo e e amogelang. Batho ba farologana; dirisa dikakanyo tse jaaka dintlha tsa puisano, e seng lenaane le le tlhalosang motho.

## Nka botsa jang ka leina, maemedi a puo le sephiri?

Tlhopha nako e e nang le sephiri e bile e sa itlhaganele. O ka botsa jaana: «Ke leina lefe le maemedi afe a puo a o batlang re a dirisa?» kgotsa «A go na le sengwe ka boitshupo kgotsa dikamano tsa gago se o batlang batho ba ba go tshegetsang ba se itse?» Motho a ka araba ka bontlhanngwe, a ka gana, kgotsa a ka bua ka gone moragonyana. Ga go tlhokege gore o botse dipotso tsa sephiri tse di sa amaneng le tlhokomelo ya gagwe.

Botsa gore ke mang yo o ka itseng eng. Mongwe a ka batla gore badiri ba amogele molekane wa gagwe, mme a sa batle gore ba lelapa le ba bantsi ba bolelelwe ka bong jwa gagwe jwa thobalano kgotsa boitshupo jwa bong. Motho a ka nna a rata mafoko a a farologaneng mo mafelong a a farologaneng. O se ka wa abelana dintlha tsa boitshupo le ba lelapa, baeti kgotsa badiri fela ka gonne o di itse.

Fa motho a batla gore tlhopho ya gagwe e kwalwe, botsa gore go kwalwe eng le gore ke mang yo o tshwanetseng go e bona. Tlhatlhoba gore motlamedi o tshwara jang leina le le farologaneng le le le mo direkotong tse dingwe, go akaretsa le gore a badiri ba ka dirisa leina le motho a le ratang mo dipuisanong. Nna o gopotse sephiri sa motho; ga se mongwe le mongwe yo o thusang ka tlhokomelo yo o tlhokang go itse sengwe le sengwe.

Ditlhopho di ka fetoga. Tlhatlhoba gape fa motho a kgona go bua se a se tlhophang, segolobogolo pele ga phetogo ya bonno kgotsa thulaganyo e ntšha ya tlhokomelo. Fa go sa tlhomama gore o batla go abelanwa eng, o se ka wa tsaya fela. Latela tlhopho e e tlhamaletseng e a kileng a e bua mme o kope thuso mo setseging se se nang le bokgoni mo lefelong la lona ka dipotso tse di amanang le taolo ya tlhokomelo kgotsa melao ya go abelana tshedimosetso.

## Ke bomang ba ba tshwanetseng go akarediwa mo dipuisanong tsa tlhokomelo?

Botsa motho gore o tshepa bomang le gore o batla gore ba nne teng. Batho ba ba mo tshegetsang ba ka akaretsa molekane wa lenyalo, molekane, tsala e e gaufi, wa lelapa kgotsa «lelapa le a le itlhophetseng»—batho ba ba mo tshegetsang jaaka lelapa le fa ba sa amana ka madi. Bangwe ba ikaega ka lelapa la kwa gae; ba bangwe ga ba dire jalo. Letla motho go tlhalosa dikamano tsa gagwe ka mafoko a gagwe.

Botsa dipotso tse di mosola: Ke bomang ba ba ka etelang? Badiri ba tshwanetse go letsa mang fa dithulaganyo di fetoga? Ke bomang ba ba ka amogelang kitsiso? Fa motlamedi a tlhoka tetla kgotsa dipampiri pele ga go abelana tshedimosetso, botsa gore o tlhoka eng mme o tlhalosetse motho ditlhopho. Go nna molekane kgotsa motshegetsi yo a itlhophetsweng ga go reye ka boyone gore o na le taolo ya semolao ya go dira ditshwetso; melao e farologana go ya ka lefelo.

Dirisa leina la kamano le motho a le ratang. Modiri wa tlhokomelo yo o bitsang molekane «tsala» kgotsa «wa lelapa» fa motho a batla gore a amogelwe jaaka molekane a ka baka kutlobotlhoko. Ntlha e khutshwane e e theilweng mo tetleng e ka bolelela badiri gore ba itsise batho jang le gore ke dintlha dife tse di ka abelanwang.

## Nka botsa motlamedi wa tlhokomelo eng?

Fa o bapisa tlhokomelo ya bonno, tshegetso ya kwa gae kgotsa ditirelo tsa motshegare, botsa motho gore ke dipotso dife tse a batlang go di botsa le gore a o batla gore o nne teng. [Kaedi ya SAGE ya ditirelo tse di amogelang](https://www.sageusa.org/resource/10-tips-for-finding-lgbt-affirming-services/) e bolela gore go abelana tshedimosetso ya sephiri le motlamedi go ka ikutlwa go le botlhoko. Kopa motlamedi go tlhalosa ditsela tsa gagwe tsa ga jaana ka mafoko a a motlhofo.

- Lo kwala jang leina le maemedi a puo a a rategang? Ke badiri bafe ba ba ka bonang tshedimosetso eo?
- Motho a ka tlhopha jang gore ke mang yo o mo etelang le go nna le nako ya sephiri le molekane kgotsa motshegetsi yo mongwe?
- Lo ithuta jang le go tlotla ditlhopho tsa motho ka kamore, diaparo le tlhokomelo ya gagwe?
- Badiri ba amogela katiso efe ka bagolo ba LGBTQ+ le dimantia?
- Monni kgotsa motshegetsi a ka tlhagisa jang ngongorego ka sephiri kgotsa go tlhoka tlotlo, mme go diragala eng morago ga moo?

[Kaedi ya SAGE le Human Rights Campaign Foundation ya tlhokomelo ya nako e telele e e akaretsang](https://www.sageusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/lei-consumer-guide-finding-lgbtq-inclusive-ltcc.pdf) e akantsha go tlhatlhoba melao e e kwadilweng ka maeto, dikamore le mekgwa ya badiri. E phasaladitswe ka 2021; e dirise jaaka dipotso tse o ka di botsang, e seng jaaka polelo ya molao wa ga jaana wa lefelo la lona. Melao le ditirelo di farologana go ya ka lefelo.

Letshwao, pampitshana kgotsa karabo e le nngwe e e gomotsang ga di ka ke tsa netefatsa gore tlhokomelo ya letsatsi le letsatsi e tla ikutlwa jang. Fa motho a batla go etela, ela tlhoko gore badiri ba mmitsa jang le gore ba araba jang molekane wa gagwe kgotsa batshegetsi ba a ba itlhophetseng. Kopa go bua le modiri ka sephiri fa seo se ka thusa motho go ikutlwa a sireletsegile.

## Nka tshegetsa motho yotlhe jang?

Botsa ka dilo tse motho a di ratang ka tlwaelo, mekgwa ya gagwe, puo, setso, tumelo, ditsala le baagi. Boitshupo jwa gagwe bo ka nna botlhokwa go buiwa ka jone, kgotsa a ka rata go fetsa nako ka dikarolo tse dingwe tsa botshelo. O se ka wa fetola ketelo nngwe le nngwe go nna puisano ka boitshupo kgotsa wa tsaya gore batho botlhe ba LGBTQ+ ba kile ba nna le maitemogelo a a tshwanang.

Batho bangwe ba kile ba lebana le go ganwa kgotsa kgethololo mme ba ka nna kelotlhoko ka ditirelo. Ba bangwe ba ka se tseye seo e le tshwenyego. Reetsa ntle le go kopa motho go tlhalosa kgotsa go netefatsa sengwe. Fa segopotso, senepe kgotsa polelo ka nako e e fetileng e utlwise botlhoko, botsa gore a o batla go emisa kgotsa go fetola setlhogo.

Fa o le molekane, tsala kgotsa wa lelapa, tshegetso ya gago le yona e botlhokwa. Botsa motho gore o batla gore o nne le seabe jang, mme o batle mokgatlho wa selegae wa dimantia kgotsa wa bagodi ba LGBTQ+ fa o tlhoka kgakololo. [Letlhomela la metsedi ya Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) le golaganya le metsedi ya baagi le batlhokomedi; se se leng teng se ikaegile ka kwa o nnang teng.

Go bona lenaane le le pharaletseng fa o bapisa magaeng a tlhokomelo, bona [go tlhopha tlhokomelo ya bonno ya dimantia](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/). Go bona thuso ya go rulaganya morago ga tshekatsheko, bona [dikgato tsa ntlha morago ga tshekatsheko ya dimantia](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
