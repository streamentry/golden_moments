Papadi ya go fedisa polelo e dirisa polelo e e tlwaelegileng e e emang pele fela ga lentswe la bofelo. Ka bontsi jwa batho ke tsela e e monate, e e sa tlhokeng go gatelela, ya go abelana nako. Papadi e a dirisega ka gore dipolelo ke tsa bogologolo, mme motho ga a tlhoke go gakologelwa sepe ka gompieno. Tlhaloso e e tlhalosa go e dira jang, go tlhopa maele jang, le go e dira ka bonolo.

## Papadi ya go fedisa polelo ke eng?

Ke papadi e e khutshwane e motho mongwe a balang karolo ya ntlha ya polelo, mme yo mongwe a tlatsa mafelelo. Mehlala e e jaaka "Ditiro di bua ka go feta..." le "Nonyane ya pele e tshwara..." Motho a ka bua mafelelo ka lentswe, a a supe, kgotsa a tlhophe go tswa mo mafelelong a mabedi kgotsa a mararo a o a neelang. Batho ba bangwe ba ikutlwa botoka fa e le bone ba balang halofo ya ntlha.

Golden Moments e neela se e le [papadi ya mahala ya go fedisa polelo](/games/finish-the-saying/) ka mongwalo o o kgolo. O ka e dira le ka ntle ga sekerini, ka go bua karolo ya ntlha mme o emele.

## Go dira ka dikgato

1. **Tlhopa nako e e khutsa** mme o nne mo go tlhomang gaufi le motho, mo go ba bonang.
2. **Bala karolo ya ntlha ka bonya,** ka lentswe le le monate, mme o emise. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) e eletsa go bua ka bonya le go dirisa dipolelo tse dikhutshwane, tse di bonolo.
3. **Emele.** Letla metsotso e mmalwa. Fa go se sepe se se tla, neela dikgetho tse pedi kgotsa tse tharo, e le nngwe e le ya go siama.
4. **Itumelela karabo nngwe le nngwe.** "Ee, ke yona" kgotsa "Ga ke ka ka akanya ka seo" e dira gore go nne bontle.
5. **Bua ka polelo.** Botsa gore e kaya eng go bone, kgotsa gore ba e utlweng kae. Mo go ntse go tlhoka boleng jwa nnete.
6. **Emisa pele ba tapa.** Dipolelo tse mmalwa le go tshega ke papadi e e feletseng.

## Go neela dikgetho ntle le go botsa dipotso

Mafelelo a dikgetho a ka dira gore papadi e nne bonolo, mme a ka ikutlwisa jaaka tlhatlhobo fa a neelwa jaaka tshegetso. Dira gore mafoko a nne a bonolo. Bua "A o batla go utlwa mafelelo kgotsa o tlhophe o le mongwe?" e seng "Ke ofe?" Neela dikgetho morago ga go emisa, e seng pele motho a bone sebaka sa go araba.

Fa motho a tshwenyega kgotsa a ikutlwa a sa tlhoke go itlhoboga, tlogela papadi mme o tswelele. [Ditlhaloso tsa rona tsa puisano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) di tlhalosa go fetola tsela ka go se dire ntlha e kgolo.

## Go tlhopa maele a a siamang motho

- **Dirisa maele a motho ka sebele.** Botsa lelapa gore motsadi kgotsa mogatsaagwe o ne a bua eng ka nako tsotlhe. Maele a bogologolo gantsi ke a bonolo go a itse.
- **Tlhopa dipolelo tse dikhutshwane, tsa letsatsi le letsatsi.** Dipolelo tse di ka tsa boleng jwa leratadima, ka kitchen, tshimo, lelapa le dinako tsa ngwaga di tlwaelegile, mme di bonolo go di latela.
- **Dira gore e nne ya bonolo.** Maele a a itshetlegang ka mafoko a bogologolo kgotsa a a nang le molaetsa a ka nna a tlhoafatsa. A boloke batho ba a ratang.
- **Lebelela puo.** Dipolelo tse dintsi ga di fetolelwe sentle. Dirisa dipolelo tse motho a di itseng ka puo ya gagwe, le fa o tlhoka go batla tse dinsha.

## Dipolelo ka dihuto

Sampole nngwe le nngwe e e tlasa fa e bontsha mafelelo mo mabatlaneng gore o itse. Bala karolo ya ntlha fela, mme o emele.

- **Leratadima:** Dipolelo tsa Setswana tse batho ba di itseng di tla go tlhaloganya go feta mehlala ya Sekgoa e e latelang.
- **Kitchen le dijo:** Dipolelo tsa Sekgoa tse di tlwaelegileng, jaaka "Too many cooks..." (spoil the broth), "Don't count your chickens..." (before they hatch), e le mehlala fela. Dirisa dipolelo tsa lelapa la gago.
- **Ntlo le lelapa:** Mehlala ya Sekgoa, jaaka "Home is where the heart..." (is), "Blood is thicker than..." (water), e le mehlala fela.
- **Dinako tsa ngwaga le tshimo:** Mehlala ya Sekgoa, jaaka "April showers bring..." (May flowers), "You reap what you..." (sow), e le mehlala fela.

## Go dirang fa motho a sa kgone go araba?

Batho ba bangwe ga ba kgone go bona mafoko gotlhe mo mothading wa bofelo. Se tlogele papadi. Bala polelo, itshegetse, mme o laletse karabo ka ditshupo, ka go tsotlhela tlhogo, kgotsa ka go tlhopha setshwantsho. Go abelana rhythm e e tlwaelegileng go tlhala le go dira nako e e abelanwang. Kopanya papadi le dipina, tee e e bothitho kgotsa go tlhatlhobelwa ka seatla, jaaka ka [ditiro tsa mothading wa bofelo](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ditsela tse dingwe tsa go dirisa maele

Maele a isa kwa puisanong ka botshelo jwa bogologolo jwa motho. O ka laletsa dipolelo ka dikai tse di tswang mo [dipotsong tsa go gakologelwa](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), kgotsa o dire papadi e e amanang le yone jaaka [dikarata tsa puisano](/games/conversation-cards/). Go bona dikakanyo tse dingwe tsa letsatsi, bala [ditiro tsa batho ba ba nang le dementia](/activities/ideas/).
