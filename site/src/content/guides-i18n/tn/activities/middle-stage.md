Legato la magareng la dementia gantsi ke lone le leleele, mme ke lone le ditirwana di simololang go tlhoka tshegetso ya nnete. Mafoko a nna thata, dikgato di tlhakana, mme go simolola sengwe go ka nna boima. Mme keletso ya go nna le mosola le go nna karolo gantsi e a sala. Ka go ipaakanya go le gonnye, motho wa legato leno a ka santse a itumelela dilo tse dintsi, segolobogolo ditiro tse di tlwaelegileng, mmino le ditshwantsho tse lo di dirang bapile.

## Ke ditirwana dife tse di dirang sentle mo magatong a magareng?

Ditirwana tse dikhutshwane, tse di tlwaelegileng tsa diatla, tse di kgaogantsweng ka dikgato tse di nosi, di dira sentle. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/middle-stage) e re ga go tlhokege go tlhama dilo tse disha: ditirwana di ka nna karolo ya botshelo jwa gale, jaaka go apaya mmogo, go lema, go reetsa mmino kgotsa go tsamaya.

[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) e re batho ba legato leno gantsi ba tlhoka go ikutlwa ba na le lebaka kgotsa ba tlhokomela sengwe, mme ba tlhoka thuso ya go simolola kgotsa go tswelela. Lenane la yone le akaretsa go mena diaparo, go tlhatswa dijana, go rulaganya dikausu, boitlosobodutu jo bo bonolo, go bina ka mmino o o tlwaelegileng, maeto a a kaelwang, go opela mmogo, botaki jo bo bonolo le go leba dialbamo.

## O baakanya tirwana jang gore e dire?

Bontsi jwa katlego bo mo go ipaakanyeng. Mekgwa eno e tswa mo [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) le [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/), mme [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e gakolola gore ditirwana di tsamaisane le se motho a kgonang go se dira:

1. **Tlosa dilo tse di sa tlhokegeng.** Tima TV, tswala kgoro mme o ntsha fela se tirwana e se tlhokang.
2. **Simolola ka bowena.** Simolola go mena kgotsa go tlhakanya mme o re, "A o ka nthusa?"
3. **Bontsha mme o eme.** Bontsha kgato e le nngwe; supa kgotsa kaela ka seatla.
4. **Naya kgato e le nngwe ka nako.** "Mena ka bogare" go bonolo go feta ditaelo tse dintsi.
5. **Naya ditlhopho di le pedi.** "Dithaole tse di pududu kgotsa tse ditshweu?" go bonolo go araba go feta potso e e bulegileng.
6. **Dira karolo e e thata ka bowena.** Wena lekanya, ene a tlhakanye. Wena tsenya tlhale mo naleng, ene a e phutholole.
7. **Naya lebaka.** "Dithaole tseno ke tsa dijo tsa Sontaga" go dira tiro e utlwe e le ya nnete.
8. **Mo letle a lebe fa a batla.** NIA e re go leba fela le gone go ka itumedisa.

## Dikakantsho tsa ditirwana tsa legato la magareng

### Ditiro tse di nang le mosola

- **Mena dithaole le matsela.** Dilo tse dinnye tse di sephara di bonolo. Seroto sa matsela a a bothitho se ka mennwa le go borelediwa.
- **Kopanya dikausu.** Simolola ka dipara di le mmalwa tse di farologaneng.
- **Tlhatswa kgotsa omisa dijana tse di sa thubege.** Metsi a a bothitho a a monate mo diatleng.
- **Baakanya tafole.** Naya mofuta o le mongwe wa selo ka nako mme o dire lefelo le le lengwe e le sekai.
- **Thusa ka dijo tsa maitseboa.** Tlhatswa ditapole, gagola lettuce kgotsa tlhakanya sekotlolo. Wena tshwara dithipa le mogote.
- **Swepa, phumola kgotsa phimola.** Lesela la tafole kgotsa lefielo le le bobebe.

Bona [ditirwana tsa mo gae](/activities/household-tasks/) go bona go feta.

### Mmino le motsamao

- **Opelang dipina tsa bogologolo.** Simolola ka bowena; batho ba le bantsi ba tsena mo dipineng tse ba di itseng. Bona [ditirwana tsa mmino](/activities/music/).
- **Binang ka kgololesego.** Swaya, opa diatla kgotsa tsamaya ka tsela e e monate. Go nna fatshe go siame.
- **Latlhelang balloon.** E tsamaya ka bonya mme go bonolo go e tshwara.
- **Tsamayang lo kaelwa.** Tsamayang mo tseleng e khutshwane e e tlwaelegileng mmogo.

### Ditshwantsho le dipale

- **Lebang alebamo.** Bua ka se o se bonang go na le go botsa dipotso: "Leba koloi eo. Mpolelele ka yone." Bona [ditirwana tsa dikgakologelo](/activities/reminiscence/).
- **Dirisang conversation cards.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) di kopanya setshwantsho le potso e e bulegileng e e se nang teko.
- **Lebang filimi ya bogologolo mmogo.** Tlhopha e e tlwaelegileng mme lo nne gaufi.

### Metshameko e e bonolo e e se nang kgatelelo

- **Picture pairs ka ditshwantsho di bonala.** [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) e dira gore e nne go lemoga, e seng go gakologelwa.
- **Find the same.** [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) e bontsha setshwantsho se le sengwe le ditlhopho di le pedi go ya go di le nne.
- **Sort together.** [Sort together](/games/sort-together/) e tshwana le go rulaganya diaparo mo skrining.
- **Picture bingo.** [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) e dira sentle mo malapeng le mo ditlhopheng.

Fa motshameko o simolola go baka kgobego ya marapo, emisa mme o dire sengwe se sele. Bona [kafa lo ka tshamekang mmogo](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).

## Go fetola go tlhoka boiketlo go nna tirwana

Boitshwaro jo bo sa iketleng kgotsa jo bo boeletswang gantsi bo na le lebaka. Motho yo o nnang a tsholola lae a ka tswa a batla tiro kgotsa go ikutlwa a na le mosola. Alzheimer's Association e akantsha go fetola maatla ao go nna tirwana ya go bapisa, go na le go a emisa.

| Se o se bonang | Se o ka se nayang |
| --- | --- |
| Go gotlha tafole | Lesela le le bongola le kopo ya go phimola |
| Dinao tse di sa iketleng | Mmino o o nang le morethetho wa go opa kgotsa go itaya |
| Go tsholola di-lae | Seroto sa diaparo kgotsa matsela a go mena |
| Go botsa potso e le nngwe gape | Gomotso, morago pina kgotsa tiro ya mmogo |
| Go tsamaya ka bonako | Loeto mmogo kgotsa tiro ya go rwala dilo |
| Go sa iketle maitseboa | Tiro e e tlwaelegileng mo leseding le le siameng; bona [go tlhoka boiketlo maitseboa](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |

## Re emise kgotsa re fetole leng?

Emisa fa o bona letsapa, kgobego ya marapo, ditlhong kgotsa bodutu, jaaka go tshikinyega, go sosobanya sefatlhego, go kgorometsa dilo kgotsa go re "Ga ke kgone." Alzheimer's Association e akantsha nako e nngwe ya letsatsi kgotsa tirwana e e bonolo. O se ka wa pateletsa. Go gana go ka tswa go bakwa ke poifo kgotsa tiro e e thata; bona [fa motho a re nnyaa](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

Go emisa pele ga nako go siame. Go nna mmogo le kopi ya tee le gone go siame. Fa bokgoni bo fetoga, [ditirwana tsa maemo a morago](/activities/late-stage/) di ka nna botoka, mme [ditirwana di le 101](/activities/ideas/) di na le dikakantsho tse dingwe.
