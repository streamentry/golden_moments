Mmino ke nngwe ya dilo tse di bonolo go abelanwa le motho yo o tshelang le dementia. Ga o kope dikarabo tse di siameng kgotsa dikgato tse dintsi. Pina e e tlwaelegileng e ka tlisa monyenyo, leoto le le itayang morethetho kgotsa mafoko a se kae, le fa motlotlo o le thata. Se se tlhokegang ke mmino o o siametseng motho yoo le nako e e didimetseng.

## Ke ditirwana dife tsa mmino tse di dirang sentle?

Go reetsa, go opela, go opa diatla, go swaya le go bua ka mmino tsotlhe di a thusa. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e akantsha go tshameka mmino, go bua ka pina le moopedi, go opela kgotsa go bina, le go ya kwa dikonsarateng fa go kgonega. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) e oketsa ka gore motsamao o oketsa boitumelo mme mmino o ka nna kgolagano fa mafoko a nna thata.

## O aga lenane la mmino jang?

Selo se se mosola go gaisa ke lenane la mmino le le nang le bokao mo mothong yoo. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) le yone e gatelela mmino wa bokao jwa motho.

### Simolola ka botshelo jwa gagwe

- **Bosha jwa gagwe.** Akanya ka se se neng se le mo radio, mo metshamekong le mo difiliming fa a ne a le monnye.
- **Dinako tse dikgolo.** Mmino wa lenyalo, motantsho wa ntlha kgotsa alebamo ya loeto lwa lelapa.
- **Tumelo le setso.** Dipina tsa kereke, gospel kgotsa dipina tsa puo ya ntlha.
- **Tiro le tirelo.** Mmino wa bo-kgwedi, dipina tsa tiro kgotsa mmino wa nako ya sesole.
- **Se lelapa le se gakologelwang.** Botsa bana, bana ba motho le ditsala gore o ne a reetsa eng.

### Akanya ka mefuta, e seng dipina fela

Fa o sa itse gore o simolole kae, leka metsotso e se kae ya mefuta e e farologaneng, mme o lebe karabo: big band, swing, doo-wop, Motown, soul, rock and roll ya bogologolo, country, gospel, dipina tsa Broadway, jazz, mmino wa Latin, polka, folk le classical. Boloka se se tlisang monyenyo kgotsa leoto le le itayang; tlogela se se tlisang go sosobanya sefatlhego.

### Dira gore go nne bonolo go tshameka

- **Go se na dipapatso.** Alzheimer's Association e tlhagisa gore dipapatso di ka tlhakanya tlhogo. Dirisa mmino o o downloadilweng, CD, direkoto kgotsa tirelo e e se nang dipapatso.
- **Sedirisiwa se le sengwe se se bonolo.** Sebui se se nang le konopo e kgolo kgotsa lenane le le baakantsweng.
- **Manane a le mabedi a makhutshwane.** Le le ritibatsang le le lengwe le le godisang maikutlo.
- **Leina le le kwadilweng.** Kwala leina la motho le "mmino o o ratiwang" mo sedirisiweng gore baeng le badiri ba kgone go o dirisa.

## Ditirwana tsa mmino di le 15

1. **Reetsang mmogo.** Nnang bapile lo se na sengwe se sele. Go reetsa fela ke tirwana e e feletseng.
2. **Opelang mmogo.** Simolola ka bowena. Go dumela ka fa tlase kgotsa go tshikinyetsa molomo go bala.
3. **Opa kgotsa itaya morethetho.** Opa mangole, itaya tafole kgotsa dirisa moropa o monnye.
4. **Binang ka bonya.** Tshwaranang diatla mme lo swaye, kgotsa lo bine lo ntse fatshe.
5. **Tshameka ka dikhafo.** Tsamaisa dikhafo tse di bobebe ka morethetho.
6. **Bua ka mmino.** Laletša, o se ka wa leka: "Pina eno e utlwala jaaka holo ya motantsho. Mpolelele ka metantsho." O se ka wa botsa "A o a gakologelwa pina eno?"
7. **Tshameka alebamo yotlhe.** Bula rekoto e a neng a e rata mme lo nwe kofi.
8. **Lebang dikhurumelo tsa direkoto.** Di ka tsosa motlotlo ka diaparo, dikoloi le difatlhego.
9. **Lebang musical.** Filimi ya mmino kgotsa konsarata ya motlha wa gagwe e ka nna monate. Emisang fa go tlhokega.
10. **Dirisa sedirisiwa se a se itseng.** Motho yo o neng a letsa piano kgotsa katara a ka itumelela go letsa ka bonolo.
11. **Mmino fa lo dira tiro.** Tshameka dipina fa lo mena diaparo kgotsa lo baakanya tafole.
12. **Pina ya mosong.** Simolola letsatsi ka pina e e tshwanang e e itumedisang.
13. **Opelang dipina tsa tumelo.** Tshameka mmino wa tumelo ya gagwe mme lo opela mmogo.
14. **Tsena mo setlhopheng sa go opela.** NHS e re ditlhopha tsa go opela di ka thusa maikutlo le boitekanelo. Botsa ka choir e e amogelang batho ba ba nang le dementia.
15. **Yang kwa mminong wa nnete.** Konsarata e khutshwane kgotsa setlhopha sa kwa ntle. Nnang gaufi le kgoro gore lo tsamaye fa lo batla.

Ka tirwana ya mafoko, leka [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/), e o ka nnang wa bontsha bokhutlo ka nako nngwe le nngwe.

## Bapisa mmino le nako

Alzheimer's Association e akantsha mmino o o ritibetseng go dira tikologo ya kagiso le pina e e matlhagatlhaga ya bosha go godisa maikutlo.

| Nako | Leka | Kgakololo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Go tsoga | Pina e e tlwaelegileng e e itumedisang | Pina e e tshwanang letsatsi le letsatsi e nna letshwao |
| Ditiro | Dipina tse di matlhagatlhaga | Mmino o dira gore tiro e nne botsalano |
| Dijo | Mmino o o bonolo wa didirisiwa | O se ka wa dira gore go nne thata go ja |
| Maitseboa | Mmino o o ritibetseng mo leseding le le siameng | Bona [go tlhoka boiketlo maitseboa](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |
| Go ya bolaong | Mmino o o bonya | Fokotsa lentswe le mabone mmogo |
| Legato la morago | Dipina tse a di ratang thata, ka bonya | Nna gaufi mme o tshware seatla |

## Dikgakololo tsa boitemogelo jo bo siameng

- **Tima modumo o mongwe.** Tima TV mme o tswale dikgoro gore mmino e nne one modumo.
- **Boloka lentswe le le bonolo.** Fa a dirisa didirisiwa tsa kutlo, tlhomamisa gore di beilwe; bona [kutlo](/brain-health/hearing/).
- **Leba dipina tse di tsosang bohutsana.** Fa pina e mo tshwenya, mo gomotse mme o e fetole.
- **Mo letle a etelele.** Fa go kgonega, motho a tlhophe mmino mme o amogele nnyaa.
- **Emisa nako nngwe le nngwe.** Metsotso e le metlhano ya mmino e lekane; go o tima mme lo nne ka tidimalo le gone go siame.

## Patlisiso e reng ka mmino le dementia?

Bosupi bo lekanyeditswe. [Tshekatsheko ya Cochrane ya 2025](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) e sekasekile dipatlisiso di le 30 tsa dikopano tsa mmino tse di rulagantsweng. E fitlhetse gore di ka fokotsa matshwao a kgatelelo ya maikutlo go le gonnye, mme ga go a bonwa phelelo e e tlhamaletseng mo kgobegong ya marapo, boitekanelong kgotsa kgakologelong, mme diphelelo ga di a tswelela morago ga dikopano.

Re abelana ka mmino ka gonne e le tsela e e monate e e se nang kgatelelo ya go fetsa nako mmogo, e seng kalafi. Mo ditlhopheng, bona [ditirwana tsa setlhopha sa tlhokomelo](/activities/care-home-groups/); go dirisa mmino le ditshwantsho, bona [ditirwana tsa dikgakologelo](/activities/reminiscence/).
