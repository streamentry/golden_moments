Dipuo tse le dintsi tsa dementia di akanya gore motho o mo dingwageng tsa bo-masome a supa kgotsa bo-masome a robedi. Mo motho yo o neng a bolelelwa a le dingwaga tsa bo-masome a manne kgotsa bo-masome a matlhano, letsatsi le letsatsi le fapana. A ka nna a sa ntse a dira, a na le kgwetlho ya ntlo, bana ba bagolo kgotsa motsadi yo o tlhokang tlhokomelo. Ditiro di tshwanetse go bona botshelo jono jwa batho ba bagolo. Tlhaloso e e tlhalosa se se fetogang, se o ka se lekang, le fa bagwera le bana ba bagolo ba ka batlang thuso.

## Early-onset dementia ke eng?

Early-onset dementia ke dementia e e simolang pele ga dingwaga tse 65, e e bidiwang gape younger-onset. [Alzheimer's Association blog](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) e tlhalosa Alzheimer jaaka ya bonako fa e ama motho yo o fa tlase ga dingwaga tse 65, mme e bolela gore go tlhokomela motho yo o monnye jaana go tla ka dikgwetlho tse di ikgethang. Dikgwetlho tsa tlhaloso di ka nna tse di tshwanang le tsa botshelo jwa morago, mme dikakanyo tsa motho, boikarabelo jwa gagwe le botshelo jwa gagwe jwa phuthego ga di tshwane.

Ka gore ga e tlwaelega, batho ba ka nna ba ema nako e telele pele ba bona tlhaloso, mme malapa a ka ikutlwa a se na motho yo ba ka buang le ene. Go itse gore batho ba bangwe ba na le maitemogelo a le mangwe go a thusa thata.

## Dira gore motho a nne motho yo o dirang

Batho ba bagolo ba nang le early-onset dementia gantsi ga ba batle go tshwarwa jaaka bana. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e eletsa gore ditiro di fetolwe go ya ka se motho a sa ntse a se kgona le se a se itumelelang. Mo motho yo o dingwageng tsa bo-masome a matlhano, seno se ka nna:

- **Dira kgatlhego e e tlwaelegileng,** jaaka go dira ka matlhare, metshameko, dikarata, phuthego ya dibuka kgotsa sekwaya, ka thuso e nnye kgotsa nako e kgutshwane.
- **Tsamaya kgotsa tlhaba ka baesekele le mogwera,** mo tseleng e ba e itseng sentle.
- **Apaya lelapa dijo,** motho a dire dikarolo tse a kgonang go di dira.
- **Ithaopa kgotsa thusa** mo tirong e ba e tlhophang, jaaka go tlhopha dimpho kgotsa go thusa mo ketsong ya sekolo.
- **Rulaganya leeto** go ya kwa lefelong le le botoka go ene, le mogwera le tshebetso e e bonolo.
- **Tsenya nako le bana ba bagolo, bommamogolo le bommangwane,** ka go ba tlhomela ntshe kgotsa tiro.

Letla motho a tlhophe. Go botsa "O batla go dira eng mosegare?" go a ikutlwisa botlhale go feta go mo tlhophela.

## Dirisa dipina le dikakanyo tsa dingwaga tsa gagwe

Dipina le ditshwantsho tsa dingwaga tsa motho tsa bo-masome a mabedi, bo-masome a mararo le bo-masome a manne di ka tsosa maikutlo a a monate le puisano e e bonolo. Tlhaloso ya rona ya [ditiro tsa dipina](/activities/music/) e tlhalosa gore o ka dira playlist jang, mme [ditiro tsa gakologelo](/activities/reminiscence/) di bontsha gore o ka abelana ditshwantsho le dipolelo ka tsela e e sa tlhatlhobeng. Mo papading e e nang le bogolo, leka [dikarata tsa puisano](/games/conversation-cards/), tse di nang le dikai tse di bulegileng ka tiro, ditsamaiso, bagwera le lelapa.

Dipapadi tsa ditshwantsho tse di dirilweng ka ditshwantsho tsa lelapa le tsone di ka dira sentle, ka gore ditshwantsho ke ba batho le dibaka tse di nang le botlhokwa mo go ene. [Dipapadi tsa ditshwantsho](/games/photo-pairs/) di boloka ditshwantsho mo browser ya sepaki seno.

## Tiro, go tsamaya ka koloi le botshelo jwa letsatsi le letsatsi

Batho le le bantsi ba nang le early-onset dementia ba sa ntse ba dira, ba tsamaya ka koloi kgotsa ba laola madi. Tseno ga se ditiro ka kakaretso, mme di tlhalosa se motho a kgonang go se dira. Bua le ngaka ya gagwe ka go tsamaya ka koloi le polokego mo tirong, mme o lebelele fa a batla thuso mo ditshelete kgotsa dikopano. Maikaelelo ke go sireletsa seriti, mme motho a nne a tsaya karolo fa a batla.

Go bona tataiso ya ditiro tsa letsatsi le letsatsi le go nna sentle, bala [letsatsi le le bonolo](/activities/daily-routine/) le [ditiro tsa ntlo jaaka ditiro](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Thuso ya bagwera le bana ba bagolo

Bagwera le bana ba bagolo gantsi ba ikutlwa ba le kgakala. Tlhokomelo e ka ikutlwa e le tiro e nngwe e e tlhokegang kgotsa go feta mo tirong le lelapeng. Alzheimer's Association e bolela gore batlhokomedi ba mo dikopanong tsa early-onset ba fitlhela di thusa ka dilo tsa tlhokomelo ya molao, le go ikutlwa ba utlwisisiwa. Se se bolela gape gore Association e dira dikopano tsa thuso tsa mahala ya batlhokomedi, go akaretsa dikopano tsa batho ba ba tlhokomelang motho yo o nang le early-onset dementia, mme tse tharo tsa tsone di kopana ka inthanete ka Labobedi le Laboraro.

Go botsa ka dikopano, letsetsa Association Helpline ya yone ya 24/7 ka 800.272.3900. [Tlhokomelo ya batlhokomedi ka bo bone](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e bua ka go phomola, tlhokomelo e e nakong le matshwao a go lapa ka go tlhoka matla.

## Dingwaga tsa tlhaloso di sa ntse di botlhokwa

Mothading wa dementia wa motho o sa ntse o tlhalosa se se thusang. Mo go simolang, batho le le bantsi ba ka dira ditiro tsa bone tsa tlwaelo ka diphetogo tse nnye, mme [ditiro tsa mothading wa pele](/activities/early-stage/) di na le dikakanyo tse dingwe. Mo go tswelelang, ditiro di nna tse di bonolo le tse di tlhotlheletsang dikutlo, jaaka go tlhalosa mo [ditiro tsa mothading wa bofelo](/activities/late-stage/). Fetola ditiro fa motho a fetoga, mme o botse gore o batla eng fa a kgona go re.
