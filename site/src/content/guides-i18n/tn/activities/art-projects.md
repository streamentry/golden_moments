Ditiro tsa bonono ke tsela e e itumedisang ya go itshwarelela nako le motho yo o nang le dementia. Di tlhotlheletsa diatla, matlho le dikutlo, mme di go fa sengwe se lo ka se buang ka sone. Tlhaloso e fa ditiro tse di nang le dikgato tse di bonolo, le tataiso ya go tlhopa dithoto ka tsela e e sireletsegileng. Mo melaong e e kwa tlase ya bonono jwa batho ba bagolo, jaaka go tlogela dithoto tsa bana, bala [bonono le ditiro tsa batho ba ba nang le dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Go simolola tiro ya bonono ope

1. **Itlhophe pele lo simolola.** Beela dithoto tse pedi kgotsa tse tharo, lesela le le sireletsang le lesela le le notileng.
2. **Bontsha kgato ya ntlha.** Tsenya borasi mo metsing, kgaola pampiri ya ntlha, kgotsa tlhatlhoba letsopa le le nnyane, mme o ba kgothaletse go dira jalo.
3. **Dirang ka ga mmogo.** Beela setshwantsho sa gago gaufi, gore tiro e nne ya ga mmogo, e seng ya go lebelelwa.
4. **Buang ka se lo se bonang.** Tlhalosang mebala, dipeakanyo le dibopeho, e seng go atlhola tiro.
5. **Emisang pele matla a fokola.** Metsotso e le lesome go ya go masome a mabedi e ka nna nako e e siameng.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) e bolela gore bonono ke tsela ya go bontsha maikutlo le go dira ka boitshepo le e seng ka bokgoni jwa bonono. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e eletsa gore ditiro di kgaolwe dikgato tse di kgonegang.

## Mebala ya metsi (watercolor wash)

Dirisa pampiri e e bokete ya mebala ya metsi, borasi jo bogolo jo bo boleta le mebala e mmalwa mo dikopong tse di tlhalosegileng. Nosetsa pampiri ka metsi a a itshekileng, mme o ba letle ba tshele mebala mo go yone. Tsikinya pampiri ka bonolo gore mebala e tsamaye. Setshwantsho se se ntseng jalo ke sa boitshwaro, mme ga se tlhoke morero.

- **Mo mothading wa pele:** Ba letle ba tlhophe mebala, mme ba penye naga.
- **Mo mothading wa bobedi:** Ba neele mebala e pedi kgotsa e tharo, mme lo nosetse ka bo lona.
- **Mo mothading wa bofelo:** Tshwarang pampiri fa ba tlhatlhobang ka sponge.

## Collage ya dimakasine

Bokang dikhasa tsa dimakasine tse di nang le ditshwantsho tse di tlhapileng tsa dimela, dijo, diphologolo, dikoloi kgotsa dileng. Gagolanyang ditshwantsho ka dikarolo tse dikgolo, e leng go nna bonolo go feta go gaola, mme lo di kgomaretse mo khateng e e tiileng. Collage e dirisiwa sentle jaaka tsela ya go simolola puisano, ka gore setshwantsho se sengwe le se sengwe se ka isa motho kgopolong. Se se siame motho yo o ratang go tlhopa le go kgetha.

## Letsopa le le boleta

Letsopa la go bopa le le boleta le ka tswengwa, le ka fenngwa, le ka tlhakanngwa ka diatla. Dirang sekotlhwana se se bonolo kgotsa tile e e tlhamaletseng. Letsopa le tlhotlheletsa kutlo ya go tshwara, ka jalo le ka thusa batho ba ba ratang go tshwara dilo. Dirang dikarolo tse dikgolo, mme lo seka lwa dirisa ditlhako tse di nang le matlhwatlhwa.

## Chalk pampiring e ntsho

Pampiri e ntsho e dira gore mebala e e bonolo e tlhabologe sentle. Dirisa chalk e kgolo e e nang le mebala e e bogale, mme motho a dire ditsela tse dikgolo kgotsa a tlhakanye ka monwana kgotsa ka tishu. Chalk e a tlhapa, ka jalo beela mo sesiceng, mme ka morago lo phuthe diatla. Tlhopa chalk e e boleta e e tlhalosang gore ga e na matshwao a go gakgamatsa.

## Go tlhatlhoba makhasi

Bokang makhasi a magolo mo tshimong, lo tlhape borasi jwa mebala e e ka tlhapiwang mo methapong ya one, mme lo tsikinye lekhasi mo pampiring. Le e tloše ka tlhoafalo, mme le itumelele setshwantsho. Tiro e ka simolola ka go tsamaya go bokela makhasi, mme e kopana sentle le [ditiro tsa ka ntle le tshimo](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Go baya masela le dimela

Tlhopa masela a a nang le mefuta ya go tshwara, kgotsa baya dimela tse mmalwa tse di mpsha mo sekotlhweng. Ke ditiro tsa dikutlo tse di nang le khatiso e e nnye. Di dirisiwa le batho ba mo mothading wa bofelo. Mo go tlhamaletseng, bona [ditiro tsa dikutlo tsa batho ba ba nang le dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Go tlhopa dithoto tsa bonono

Dithoto di tshwanetse go sireletsega le go itumedisa go feta boleng. Fa o tlhopa, lebelela:

- **Di sa tlhotlheleng le di tlhapiwa:** Batla dithoto tse di sa tlhotlheleng, mme o lebelele gore ditente di tlhapiwa.
- **Dibopiwa tse dikgolo:** Chalk e kgolo, borasi bo bo tenang le chalk e kgolo di a bonolega go tshwara.
- **Ga go na dikarolo tse dinnye:** Tlosa dipeene, dikhuduhudu, disetlhare le lerole la go tlhabologa tse di ka jewang.
- **Ga go na ditlhako tse di bogale:** Dirisa dibatsela tse di sireletsang kgotsa tsaya pampiri ka diatla.
- **Dienke:** Lebelela lesego (latex) le dienke tse di tlhokegang.
- **Botsema:** Mebala e e bogale mo pampiring e e tshweu kgotsa mo pampiring e ntsho e a bonala sentle.
- **Lenaane le le khutshwane:** Dithoto tse pedi kgotsa tse tharo di a lekana. Dikgetho tse dintsi di ka tlhoa.
- **Go tlosa ditlhaka ka bonolo:** Dirisa thaepe, lesela le le sa tshelang metsi le lesela le le notileng, gore tlhaka e se nne lebaka la go emisa.

## Go fetola ka mothading

Mo mothading wa pele, batho ba bantsi ba ka rulaganya le go fetsa tiro ka thuso e nnye. Mo mothading wa bobedi, neela dikgetho tse dinnye le tataiso e ntsi. Mo mothading wa bofelo, tsepamisa maikaelelo mo ditirong tsa dikutlo jaaka go gatelela letsopa kgotsa go tlhatlhoba ka sponge, mme o itumelele tsela e e dirwang e seng sephetho sa mafelelo.

Bala gape [ditiro tsa mothading wa pele](/activities/early-stage/), [ditiro tsa mothading wa bobedi](/activities/middle-stage/) le [ditiro tsa mothading wa bofelo](/activities/late-stage/). Fa motho a gana kgotsa a tshwenyega, kgaolo ya rona ya [fa motho a gana](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) e tlhalosa gore o ka itlhokomela jang.

## Dirisa tshwantsho le papadi

Morago ga letsatsi la go penta, leka [go kolora](/games/coloring/) mo tabeletong jaaka tiro e e bonolo, kgotsa [puzzle ya setshwantsho](/games/picture-puzzle/) jaaka tiro ya bofelo. Ditiro tseno di tshwara diatla ka nako e e siameng ntle le go kopa bokgoni jwa go ntlhofa.
