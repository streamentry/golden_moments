Fa o na le motho yo o mo ratang yo o nang le Alzheimer, gongwe o setse o lemogile gore dilo dingwe di tsamaya ka bonako, mme tse dingwe di santse di nna teng. Gongwe o ka lebala mogala wa mo mosong, mme a santse a opela mafoko otlhe a sefela, a phutha thaole jaaka a ntse a dira, kana a aba dikarata a sa akanye. Ditiro tse di siameng di agilwe mo dikgonong tseo tse di nnang teng. Tsebe eno e tlhalosa gone kaYone, mme e fa dikakanyo tsa kgato nngwe le nngwe le metshameko ya mahala ya setshaba se se e siametseng.

## A Alzheimer e farologane le dementia mo ditirong?

Ga e farologane thata. Dementia ke leina le legolo, mme bolwetse jwa Alzheimer ke lebaka le le tlwaelegileng thata la yone: [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) e re e bopa 60 go isa go 80 mo lekgolong la dikgetsi tsa dementia. Gaufi le tsotlhe tse di kwadilweng ka ditiro tsa dementia di dirisega mo go Alzheimer, go akaretsa [dikakanyo tsa rona tse 101 tsa ditiro](/activities/ideas/).

Se se bohlokwa go se itse ke tatelano e e tlwaelegileng ya diphetogo. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-alzheimers-disease) e tlhalosa gore Alzheimer pele e senya dikarolo tsa bobete tse di amanang le kgopolo, mme moragonyana e ama puo, go akanya le boitshwaro mo setshabeng. Alzheimer's Association e re sesupo se se tlwaelegileng sa ntlha ke bothata jwa go gopola tshedimosetso e e sa tswang go ithutwa. Mongwe le mongwe o farologane, mme batho ba bantsi ba na le mabaka a a tswakaneng, fela mokgwa ono o bopa se se tlwaelegileng go dira.

## Ke ditiro dife tse di siametseng Alzheimer go feta tse dingwe?

Ditiro tse di ikaegileng ka kitso ya bogologolo e e dirilweng thata gantsi di tsamaya sentle go feta tse di tlhokang gore sengwe se sešwa se ithutwe se bo se tshwarwe mo kgopolong.

- **Dikgono tse di dirilweng ka lobaka lo loleele.** Alzheimer's Association e bolela gore motho a ka tswelela a letsa dipina tse di bonolo tse a di ithutileng mo piano dingwaga tse di fetileng. Go loka, go tsentsha dikota tsa dikgong, go phetaphetanya dikarata, go kgotlhelela senkgwe le go phutha diaparo di tshwana.
- **Dipina tsa bogologolo le mmino o o itsegeng.** Batho ba bantsi ba santse ba itse mafoko otlhe a dipina tsa bofelelo jwa bonnye le dingwaga tsa bo-20, difela le dipina tsa lenyalo la bone. Bona [ditiro tsa mmino](/activities/music/).
- **Ditiro tsa letsatsi le letsatsi tse di nang le maikaelelo.** [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e kwala go tlhatswa dijana, go baya tafole, go kgetholola dikausu le go phutha diaparo mo gare ga ditiro tse batho ba ka di ratang. Go ikutlwa o le molemo go botlhokwa.
- **Go itse go na le go gopola.** Go supa tshwantsho se se tshwanang go bonolo go na le go se bitsa ka kgopolo. Ke ka moo metshameko ya ditshwantsho e e nang le tsotlhe tse di bonalang e dirang botoka go na le dipotso.
- **Maikutlo a mmele.** Go tshwara, monkgo, mogote, tatso le modumo di nna le bokao morago ga fa mafoko a setse a thata. Bona [ditiro tsa maikutlo a mmele](/activities/sensory/).

Se se tlwaelegileng go sa tsamaye sentle: sengwe le sengwe se se nang le oura, melao e mesha e e ithutwang, magato a mantsi a a tshwanetseng go gopolwa, kana dipotso tse di lekang kgopolo, jaaka "A o gopola gore motho yono ke mang?" Tseo di baya karolo e thata ya Alzheimer kwa pele.

## Ditiro tsa Alzheimer mo kgatong ya ntlha

Mo kgatong ya ntlha, [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/stages) e re motho a ka tswelela a otlolola koloi, a dira tiro le go tsaya karolo mo ditirong tsa kgolagano, mme a lemoga dipoelo tsa kgopolo tse di jaaka go lebala mafoko a a itsegeng kana mo dilo di leng teng. Tsela e e siameng ke go dirisana: botsa se a ratang go tswelela a se dira le thuso e a e ratang.

1. **Tswelela ka dilo tse o di ratang.** Golf, bridge, go roka mapatcha, koiri le dikgopolo tsa kereke, ka diphetogo tse dinnye jaaka dikgatlhego tse dinnye le dinako tse di kgutsitseng.
2. **Tshameka metshameko e e itsegeng ya dikarata le ya boto.** [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) e kwala dipotso, go batla mafoko, metshameko ya dikarata le ya boto mo kgatong ya ntlha. Tshamekang ka dithimi fa go bala dikoro go simolola go mo tshwenya, kana o tlogele dikoro ka ho feletseng.
3. **Apaya mmogo.** Mo tlogele a dire karolo e a e itseng sentle ka pelo, mme wena o bale magato.
4. **Simolola morero wa kanelo ya botshelo.** Dienepe, ditsamaiso tsa dijo le dikanelo mo bukeng ya go kopanya e a e etelelang pele. Bona [ditiro tsa go gopola](/activities/reminiscence/).
5. **Tswelang kwa ntle.** Resetorente e a e ratang mo nakong e e kgutsitseng, musiamo go ya go etela ka nakwana, kana go tsamaya mo phaakeng.
6. **Leka diphasele tse a di tlhophang.** Dipotso tsa mafoko tsa mokwalo o mogolo, go batla mafoko le diphasele tsa dikgetsi. Bona [diphasele tsa dementia](/activities/puzzles/).

Dikakanyo tse dingwe: [ditiro tsa kgato ya ntlha](/activities/early-stage/).

## Ditiro tsa Alzheimer mo kgatong ya bogare

Alzheimer's Association e tlhalosa kgato ya bogare e le yone gantsi e e leng telele go gaisa tse dingwe, mme e re e ka tshwarelela dingwaga di le dintsi. Mafoko a a tswakana, go befelwa go tla ka bonako, mme motho o tlhoka tlhokomelo e e oketsegileng. Alzheimer's Foundation of America e oketsa gore batho ba bantsi ba tlhoka thuso go simolola kana go tswelela ditiro. Seabe sa gago se fetoga go nna go baya dilo ka tlhamalalo, go bontsha go na le go tlhalosa, le go tsaya karolo e thata.

1. **Phutha, kgetholola le tshwanisa.** Dithaole, dikausu, dikgaba le dikonopo ka mmala. Ditiro tsa nnete, di dirwa mmogo.
2. **Opela le go tshameka ka mmino.** Opa diatla, kgakgamala kana bina mo khitšhing. Simolola go opela wena, mme o mo tlogele a tsene.
3. **Lebelela dibuka tsa dienepe.** Tlhalosa se o se bonang mme o mo laletse go anela dikanelo: "Koloi e ntle ruri. Mpolelele ka yone." Latela mabitso ape a a a dirisang.
4. **Tshameka metshameko e e bonolo ya ditshwantsho.** [Dipaera tsa ditshwantsho](/games/picture-pairs/) ka ditaele di le nne go isa go di le thataro le ditshwantsho tse di tlogetsweng di bonala, kana [Batla se se tshwanang](/games/find-the-same/) ka dikgetho tse pedi kana tse tharo.
5. **Fetsa dipolelwana tse di itsegeng.** "Motho ke motho ka..." Dipolelwana tse di ithutilweng bogologolo gantsi di tla bonolo. Leka [Fetsa polelwana](/games/finish-the-saying/), e o ka bontshang bofelo bope ka go kgoma gangwe.
6. **Nosetsa le go lema dimela.** Dipitsa tsa mo ntlong kana lobelo le le tsholositsweng, ka sekotlolo se sennye sa go nosetsa.
7. **Dira motshameko wa go tshwanisa ka dienepe tsa lelapa.** [Dipaera tsa dienepe](/games/photo-pairs/) di dirisa ditshwantsho tse o di tlhophang mo sedirisweng sa gago, mme ga di ke di se tlogele.

Dikakanyo tse dingwe: [ditiro tsa kgato ya bogare](/activities/middle-stage/).

## Ditiro tsa Alzheimer mo kgatong ya bofelo

Mo kgatong ya bofelo, Alzheimer's Association e re batho ba latlhegelwa ke bokgoni jwa go araba tikologo ya bone le go tswelela ka puisano, le fa ba ka tswelela ba bua mafoko kana dipolelwana. Gompieno tiro e kaya matshelo le kgolagano, e tliswa mo motho a kopanang gone.

1. **Letsa mmino o o ratwang** ka maswabi, TV e sa o kgoreletse.
2. **Gagabe diatla** ka lotshe le le nang le monkgo o o ratwang.
3. **Neela dilo tse di bonolo tsa go di tshwara:** kobo e e bothitho, sekobo sa fleece, lejwe le le boleta.
4. **Bala ka lentswe le le kwa godimo** dipesalema, direto kana kanelo e e itsegeng. Mokgwa wa yone o botlhokwa go gaisa bokao.
5. **Nna kwa ntle** mo moriting, kana gaufi le fensetere e e nang le dinonyane tse o ka di lebelelang.
6. **Lebelelang ditshwantsho tse dikgolo mmogo.** Lotlhware lwa letsatsi kana ntša e e tletseng sekirini sa tablet, mme wena o bolela se o se bonang.

Dikakanyo tse dingwe: [ditiro tsa kgato ya bofelo](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ke metshameko efe e e dirang mo motho yo o nang le Alzheimer?

Tlhopha metshameko e go se nang selo se se ka palelwang mo go yone. Motshameko mongwe le mongwe mo setšhabeng sena ke wa mahala, o dira mo browser mme ga o na sekhutlwane sa nako, dikoro kana go ikwadisa.

| [Dipaera tsa ditshwantsho](/games/picture-pairs/) | Tshwanisa ditshwantsho tse dikgolo; di ka nna di bonala | Kgato ya ntlha le ya bogare |
| [Batla se se tshwanang](/games/find-the-same/) | Kgoma tshwantsho se se tshwanang le se se kwa godimo | Kgato ya bogare le ya bofelo, ka thuso |

| [Pokano ya tshwantsho](/games/picture-puzzle/) | Fetolelana dikarolo tse 4 kana tse 9 gore di boele mo mafelong a tsone, tshwantsho se bonala | Kgato ya ntlha le ya bogare |
| [Dikarata tsa puisano](/games/conversation-cards/) | Tshwantsho le potso e e bulegileng ya go bua ka yone | Kgato nngwe le nngwe, le motho yo o ka bualang le ene |

Fa o ipotsa ka "metshameko ya kgopolo", bona [metshameko ya kgopolo ya batho ba bagolo ba ba nang le dementia](/activities/memory-games/).

## Mokgwa wa go dira tiro nngwe le nngwe go nna bonolo

Keletso ya NIA e fela mo mekgweng e mmalwa: tshwanisa tiro le se motho a ka se dirang, mo thuse go simolola, lebelela go bo go le teng ga kgalefo, netefatsa gore o ikutlwa a atlegile, mme o mo tlogele a lebelele fela fa e le gone se a se ratang.

- **Tlhopha nako ya letsatsi fa a le botoka,** mme o dire dikopano tse dikhutshwane.
- **Baya sengwe le sengwe pele** gore le kgone go simolola ka bonako.
- **Laletsa, o seka wa laela.** "A o ka nthusa?" e dira botoka go na le "Ke nako ya tiro."
- **Dira kgato ya ntlha wena** mme o mo tlogele a tsene.
- **Neela kgato e le nngwe le kgetho e le nngwe ka nako.**
- **Tlogela e dirwe ka mokgwa wa gagwe.** Dithaole tse di sa lekalekanang ga di na bothata.
- **Ema fa go santse go le monate.** Go emisa o santse o le gaufi ke dipoelo tse di siameng, e seng go palelwa. Fa a gana, bona [fa motho yo o nang le dementia a re nnyaa](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) e e bolela ka bonolo: tlhoma mogopolo mo boitumelong, e seng mo katlegong.
