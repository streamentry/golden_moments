Gakologelo ke tiro ya go bua mmogo ka bogologolo: ntlo ya bongwana, tiro ya ntlha, dijo tse di ratwang, dipina, dikeletso le malatsi a dikeletso. Bagolo ba le bantsi ba nang le dementia, puisano e e monate ka botshelo jwa bone ke e nngwe ya dikarolo tse di itumedisang tsa letsatsi. Sephiri ke go botsa ka tsela e e laletsang pale mme e seng e e tlhatlhobang gakologelo. Dipotso tse di nnang mo dihutong, ka dikarolo tsa tsone.

## Melao ya dipotso tsa gakologelo

- **Botsa dipotso tse di bulegileng.** "Mpolelele ka..." le "Go ne go le jang..." di laletsa pale. "A o gakologelwa...?" e batla karabo e e siameng.
- **Se ke wa tlhatlhoba.** Tlogela "Ke mang yo?", "Ke ngwaga ofe?" le "A o gakologelwa leina la...?"
- **Amogela karabo nngwe le nngwe.** Fa pale e fetoga kgotsa lefelo le tlhakanngwa, o itumelele mme o botse ka maikutlo.
- **Botsa potso e le nngwe ka nako.** Emele, reetsa, mme o latele ka potso e e bonolo.
- **Letla motho a tataye.** Fa a batla go bua ka sengwe se sengwe, tsamaya le ene.

Tataiso ya [National Institute on Aging ka puisano](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) e eletsa dipolelo tse dikhutshwane, lentswe le le khutsafetseng, le go nna le sepe. Melao e e tshwanetse go dirisiwa mo go one jaaka mo dilong tse dingwe.

## Ntlo le bongwana

- Mpolelele ka ntlo e o godileng mo go yone. Kitchen e ne e le jang?
- O ne o rata go dira eng kwa ntle fa o le monnye?
- Ke batho bafe ba o neng o tshameka le bone mo setlheng sa gago?
- Kamore ya gago e ne e le jang? A o ne o e abelana le mang?

## Tiro le go dira dilo

- Ke mofuta ofe wa tiro o o neng o o rata go feta?
- Mpolelele ka letsatsi la tiro leo le go dirileng gore o ikutlwe o itumetse.
- O ne o rata go dira kgotsa go lokisa eng ka diatla tsa gago?
- Ke mang yo o go rutileng go feta mo tirong ya gago?

## Dijo le go apaya

- Lelapa la gago le ne le ja eng ka Sontaga?
- Mpolelele ka sejo se mongwe wa lelapa la gago a neng a se apaya sentle thata.
- A o ne o na le lefelo le o neng o rata go reka tswii ka lone fa o le monnye?
- Ke monko ofe o go gakologelang gae?

Puisano ya dijo e kopana sentle le [ditiro tsa dikutlo](/activities/sensory/), le go ja go nnye fa go siame go ja.

## Dipina le go bina

- Ke dipina dife tseo o neng o di bina kgotsa o di binela fa o ne o le monnye?
- Ke moimbi kgotsa setlhopha sefe se o neng o se rata go feta?
- O ne o utlwa dipina kae mo malatsing ayo: gae, mo dikeletsong, kereke?

Go bona dipapadi tsa dipina le dikakanyo, bala [ditiro tsa dipina ba ba nang le dementia](/activities/music/).

## Dikeletso le dikeletso tsa setlhopha

- Letsatsi la go itumela le le neng le go tlhoma pelo go feta ke lefe fa o ntse o gola?
- Mpolelele ka phata ya matsalo e o e gakologelwang.
- Lelapa le ne le dira eng mo mosegareng wa ntwa?
- Lelapa la gago le ne le keletsa dinako tsa ngwaga jang?

Dikakanyo tse di tshwanetseng le letsatsi le le itseng la dikeletso, bala [ditiro tsa dikeletso ba ba nang le dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Batho le ba ba lelapa

- Mpolelele ka motho yo o go tshegisang go feta.
- Letsatsi la go nyalana la gago le ne le le jang?
- Mpolelele ka bana ba gago fa ba ne ba le bannye.
- Batsadi ba gago ba go bitsa eng?

Botsa dipotso tsa lelapa ka bonya. Fa motho a lela mongwe, latela tsela ya gagwe, mme o se ke wa mo kopa gore a gakologelwe dintlha tse di ka mo botlhokong.

## Dibaka le tlhaho

- Mpolelele ka lefelo le o ratang go le akanya.
- Maemo a loago a ne a le jang kwa o godileng teng?
- A o ne o na le tshimo kgotsa setlhare se o neng o se rata?
- Mpolelele ka leeto le o neng o le itumelela.

## Go dirisa ditshwantsho le dithoto

Ditshwantsho ke tsela e e tlwaelegileng ya go simolola. Tshwara setshwantsho mo go matlho a motho mme o re, "Mpolelele ka se." Tataiso ya [Alzheimer's Association ka ditiro](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) e eletsa go dirisa dithoto tse di tlwaelegileng, ditshwantsho le dipina go simolola puisano. Sebokwa sa gakologelo se se nang le dithoto tse mmalwa tsa botshelo jwa motho le sone se ka dira sentle; bona [ditiro tsa gakologelo](/activities/reminiscence/) go bona gore o ka se rulaganya jang.

## Fa gakologelo e le botlhoko

Dikakanyo tse dingwe di tlisa botlhoko, poifo kgotsa kgalefo. Fa motho a tshwenyega, se ke wa mo sundumelela. Bua sengwe se se monate jaaka, "Seo se bonala e le nako e e thata. A o batla go bua ka sengwe se se itumedisang?" Ebe o fetola setlhogo, o neele tee, kgotsa o bapale pina e a e ratang.

## Dirisa papadi kgotsa tsamaya

Morago ga seboka sa gakologelo, papadi e nnye e ka dira gore maikutlo a nne a bonolo. Leka [dikarata tsa puisano](/games/conversation-cards/) ya dikai tse di bulegileng tse dintsi, kgotsa [go fedisa polelo](/games/finish-the-saying/) ya dipolelo tse di tlwaelegileng go tswa mo bogweneng jwa motho. Go bona dikeletso tsa puisano ka kakaretso, bala [dikeletso tsa puisano mo batlhokomedi](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
