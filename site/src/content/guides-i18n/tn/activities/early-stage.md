Mo legatong la pele la dementia, batho ba le bantsi ba santse ba tshela jaaka pele. Ba ka nna ba bereka, ba dira boithaopo, ba bona ditsala mme ba tsamaisa bontsi jwa letsatsi la bone. Ditirwana gantsi ga di tlhoke go emisetswa; di tlhoka diphetogo di le mmalwa gore di tswelele di le monate. Seabe sa gago se gaufi le sa molekane go na le motlhokomedi: motho yo o tshegetsang, yo o rulaganyang mmogo le ene, mme a tsene fela fa a kopiwa.

## Ke ditirwana dife tse di siametseng dementia ya maemo a pele?

Ditirwana tse di siameng gantsi ke tse motho a setseng a di rata, di nolofaditswe go le gonnye. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) e umaka dikarata le metshameko ya boto, word search, go tsamaya, go bina, go lema, go rulaganya dipalesa, go anela dipale, go taka, mmino, go kwala jenale, makwalo, scrapbook, go apaya le ngwaetelo ya malatsi a boikhutso.

Se se fetogang gantsi ke tikologo, e seng tirwana. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) e re batho ba legato la pele ba ka simolola go ikgogela mo dilong tse ba neng ba di rata, jaaka motho yo o imelwang ke moletlo o mogolo mme a dira botoka mo setlhopheng se sennye. Buang ka se se simololang go nna thata mme lo se fetole mmogo.

## Botsa pele mme lo dumalane ka thuso

[Kaelo ya Alzheimer's Association ya legato la pele](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage) e akantsha mekgwa e e dirang le mo ditirwaneng:

- **Botsa ka tlhamalalo.** "Ke eng se se ka dirang gore book club e nne bonolo mo go wena?" go botoka go gopola ka bowena.
- **Dumela gore o ka kgona.** Fa go se na kotsi, mo letle a leke mme o mo kgothatse go na le go tsaya taolo.
- **Dumalanang ka letshwao.** Tlhophang lefoko kgotsa go dumela ka tlhogo go go rayang gore "go siame go tsena", jaaka fa leina le sa tle fa lo tshameka dikarata.
- **Leka gantsi.** Botsa gore selekanyo sa thuso se siame, se ntsi kgotsa se nnye thata.

## Dikakantsho tsa ditirwana mo legatong la pele

### Tswelela ka ditiro le boitlosobodutu

- **Boloka boikarabelo jo bo tlwaelegileng.** Boithaopo jwa beke le beke, choir kgotsa setlhopha di naya beke sebopego. Fa go nna kgatelelo, tiro e khutshwane kgotsa e e didimetseng e ka nna botoka.
- **Rulaganyang dijo mmogo.** Fa go reka go simolola go nna thata, Alzheimer's Association e akantsha go kopa motho go rulaganya menu le lenane la beke.
- **Apayang mme lo bake.** Latelang recipe e a e ratang. Baya metswako ka tatelano pele lo simolola gore dikgato di bonale.
- **Lemang.** Rulaganyang seroto, tlhopha dipeo kgotsa dimela mme lo dire mmogo. Lenane le le kwadilweng la ditiro tsa tshingwana le a thusa.
- **Tswelela ka botaki.** Go dira ka legong, go roka, go loga kgotsa go aga model go ka tswelela ka dipaterone tse di bonolo le lesedi le le siameng.

### Metshameko ya boitumelo

- **Tshamekang dikarata le metshameko ya boto.** Metshameko e e tlwaelegileng e bonolo. Tshamekang ka ditlhopha gore go se nne ope yo o mo pontsheng, mme lo se ka lwa tshwara maduo ka thata.
- **Leka word search ya ditlhaka tse dikgolo.** [Word search](/games/word-search/) e ka tshamekiwa mo skrining kgotsa ya gatisiwa.
- **Dirang puzzle.** Tlhopha palo ya dikarolo e e ritibatsang, e seng e e gwetlhang. [Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) ke mofuta o mokhutshwane wa skrini; bona le [dipuzzle tsa dementia](/activities/puzzles/).
- **Tsena trivia ka boitumelo.** Batho bangwe ba rata trivia ya setlhopha. Fa e simolola go nna teko, tlhopha sengwe se sele kgotsa dirisa [dipotso tse di bonolo tsa trivia](/activities/trivia/) tse di nang le dikarabo mme di se na maduo.

Metshameko fano ke ya boitumelo. Ga go na motshameko o o bontshitsweng go alafa kgotsa go fokotsa dementia; bala [brain games: se patlisiso e se buang](/brain-health/brain-games/).

### Kwala, akanya mme o boloke dipale

- **Boloka jenale.** Alzheimer's Association e akantsha jenale go thusa ka maikutlo morago ga go bolelelwa ka dementia. Mela e se kae ka letsatsi e lekane.
- **Etelela buka ya botshelo.** Legato la pele ke nako e ntle ya gore motho a tlhophe ditshwantsho tsa gagwe mme a kwale kgotsa a bue dipale tsa tsone. Bona [ditirwana tsa dikgakologelo](/activities/reminiscence/).
- **Kwala makwalo.** Lekwalo le le yang kwa setlogolong, tsaleng ya bogologolo kgotsa modirimmogo le ka nna botlhokwa.
- **Bua ka ditshwantsho.** [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) e fetola ditshwantsho tsa lelapa go nna motshameko, mme [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) di naya tshimologo ya motlotlo.

### Tsamayang mme lo tswe

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e akantsha go tswelela ka maeto a a tlwaelegileng fa lotlhe lo santse lo iketlile. A rulaganye mo nakong e motho a leng botoka ka yone mme o se ka wa a dira maleele thata.

- **Maeto a makhutshwane a setso.** Museum, matinee kgotsa pontsho ya botaki ka nako e e didimetseng.
- **Go thuma.** NIA e akantsha letamo mo nakong e e sa tlalang.
- **Ditlhopha tsa go tsamaya le dithuto tsa go bina.** [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) e umaka go bina, tai chi, yoga, go thuma le ditlhopha tsa go tsamaya, fa di le teng.
- **Ditlhopha tsa go opela le memory café.** Ditlhopha tse di ritibetseng tse batho ba ba tshelang le dementia le balekane ba bone ba ka kopanang le ba bangwe.

## Diphetogo tse dinnye tse di bolokang ditirwana di le monate

| Fa seno se simolola go nna thata | Leka seno |
| --- | --- |
| Mafelo a a tletseng kgotsa a modumo | Tsamaya ka dinako tse di didimetseng, tlhopha ditlhopha tse dinnye, nna kwa thoko |
| Go gakologelwa maina a mesha | Itsise batho ka maina nako nngwe le nngwe; dirisa name tags |
| Go latela leano la dikgato di le dintsi | Kwala dikgato kgotsa o baakanye tsotlhe ka tatelano pele |
| Go lapa | Maeto a makhutshwane, boikhutso morago, o se ka wa rulaganya sengwe se sele |
| Go sala morago motshameko | Tshamekang ka ditlhopha, nolofatsa melao kgotsa tlogelang maduo |
| Go batla mafoko | Naya nako; naya lefoko fela fa letshwao le lo dumalaneng ka lone le letla |

## Nako ya go fokotsa lobelo

Emisa kgotsa fetola tirwana fa e tlisa kgatelelo go feta boitumelo. Go tenega gantsi, go tila boitlosobodutu kgotsa go re "Ga ke tlhole ke kgona" go tlhoka motlotlo o o bonolo, e seng pateletso. Gantsi karabo ke mofuta o o nnye wa tirwana eo. Fa motho a tlhoka thuso e ntsi go simolola le go latela dikgato, [ditirwana tsa magato a magareng](/activities/middle-stage/) di ka nna botoka. Bona [dikgakololo tsa puisano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) le [ditirwana di le 101](/activities/ideas/).

Balekane le bone ba tlhoka tshegetso. Legato la pele ke nako e ntle ya go aga network ya thuso; bona [go itlhokomela ga motlhokomedi](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
