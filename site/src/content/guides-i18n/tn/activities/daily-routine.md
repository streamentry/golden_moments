Thulaganyo e e bonolo e naya letsatsi sebopego se se tlwaelegileng: go tsoga, dijo, tirwana e nnye, boikhutso le go ya bolaong mo dinakong tse di ka nnang tsa tshwana. E fokotsa ditshwetso le dimakatso, mme e go naya leano mo dinakong tse o neng o ka ipotsa gore go dirwe eng. Thulaganyo e e fa tlase ke sekai, e seng molao. E dirise e le tshimologo mme o e fetole go ya ka motho yo o mo tlhokomelang.

## Goreng thulaganyo ya letsatsi e thusa?

Thulaganyo e e tlhomameng e thusa ka gonne mekgwa e e tlwaelegileng e bonolo go e latela go feta e mesha. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) e re letsatsi le le rulagantsweng le fokotsa nako ya go tsaya ditshwetso mme le oketsa nako ya ditirwana tse di monate, mme dinako tse di tlhomameng tsa go tsoga le go ya bolaong di ka thusa ka mathata a boroko le go sa iketle ga maitseboa. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) e akantsha go tlhapa, go apara le go ja ka dinako tse di tshwanang letsatsi le letsatsi, mme [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) le yone e akantsha mekgwa e e tshwanang.

## Sekai sa letsatsi le le bonolo

Dinako ke dikai fela. Sutisa letsatsi lotlhe pele kgotsa morago go tsamaisana le morethetho wa motho.

| Nako | Se se diragalang | Dikakantsho | Mo letsatsing le le thata |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8:00 a.m. | Tsoga, tlhapa, apara | Thulaganyo e e tshwanang; baya diaparo ka tatelano | Boloka tlhokomelo e le bonolo; hempe e ntšha e lekane |
| 8:45 a.m. | Dijo tsa mo mosong | Kofi le pampiri e balelwa kwa godimo, kgotsa radio | Jang mmogo ka tidimalo, lo se ka lwa patelediwa go bua |
| 9:30 a.m. | Tirwana e kgolo | Mena diaparo, baka, lema, loeto lo lokhutshwane kgotsa go tsamaya | Mofuta o mokhutshwane, kgotsa mo letle a go lebe |
| 10:30 a.m. | Nako ya tidimalo | Mmino, alebamo ya ditshwantsho kgotsa go nna gaufi le fensetere | Ikhutse mo setulong se a se ratang |
| 11:00 a.m. | Thusa ka dijo tsa motshegare | Tlhatswa merogo, baakanya tafole | Wena apaya; ene a nne le wena |
| 12:00 p.m. | Dijo tsa motshegare le go phepafatsa | Phimola tafole, omisa dijana di le mmalwa | Tlogela ditiro tsa mo gae |
| 1:00 p.m. | Boikhutso jo bokhutshwane | Beela dinao kwa godimo, mmino o o bonolo | Go ntse jalo |
| 2:00 p.m. | Kwa ntle kgotsa tirwana | Tsamaya, nna mo porch, nosetsa dimela, latlhela balloon | Nna gaufi le fensetere e e nang le letsatsi |
| 3:00 p.m. | Loago kgotsa botaki | Ketelo, kitsokopanyo, [picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/), coloring, dikarata | Nwa le senwamaphiri mmogo |
| 4:30 p.m. | Nako ya go ritibala | Tswala digaretene, tshuba mabone, mena matsela | Mmino o o tlwaelegileng, modumo o o kwa tlase |
| 5:30 p.m. | Dijo tsa maitseboa | Thusa go tlhakanya kgotsa go baakanya tafole | Dijo tse di bonolo tse di tlwaelegileng |
| 6:30 p.m. | Nako ya maitseboa mmogo | Bua ka dikgakologelo ka dessert, dikarata, filimi e a e ratang, go sidila seatla | Botsalano jo bo didimetseng |

Sekai seno se latela sebopego sa moralo wa Alzheimer's Association wa magato a pele le a magareng, go tswa mo tlhokomelong ya mmele le dijo tsa mo mosong go ya mo ditirwaneng, maetong, mminong, boikhutsong, ketelong, dijo tsa maitseboa, dikgakologelo le go ipaakanyetsa go ya bolaong.

## O aga thulaganyo ya gago jang?

Simolola ka kafa motho a ntseng a tshela ka teng, e seng ka chate. Alzheimer's Association e akantsha gore pele o rulaganya o akanye ka:

- **Dilo tse a di ratang, tse a sa di rateng, maatla le kgatlhego.**
- **Kafa a neng a dirisa letsatsi ka teng.** Motho yo o tsogang pele a tsamaya le ntša o tshwanetse go tswelela a tsamaya pele. Motho yo o ratang go robala morago a ka se itumelele go simolola ka 8 a.m.
- **Nako e a leng botoka ka yone.** Baya go tlhapa, maeto le tirwana e kgolo mo nakong eo.
- **Nako e e lekaneng** ya dijo, go tlhapa le go apara, gore go se ka ga potlakisiwa.
- **Dinako tse di tlhomameng tsa go tsoga le go ya bolaong.**

Motswedi oo o akantsha motswako wa ditiro tsa mo gae, dijo, tlhokomelo ya mmele, botaki, go itshidila mmele, nako ya loago, ditirwana tsa tumelo le dinako tse di itlhagelang, jaaka tsala e etela.

### E leke beke, mme o e fetole

Boloka ntlha e bonolo letsatsi le letsatsi. Botsa gore ke ditirwana dife tse di dirileng sentle le gore goreng, gore a go ne go na le dilo di le dintsi kgotsa go se na sepe se se dirwang, le gore a maano a a itlhagelang a ne a itumedisa kgotsa a tshwenya. Boloka se se dirang mme o fetole se se sa direng.

### E dire gore e bonale

Fa motho a santse a kgona go bala, boto e tshweu kgotsa karata e kgolo e e kwadilweng e ka gomotsa. E boloke e le khutshwane: "Dijo tsa mo mosong. Tsamaya le Sarah. Dijo tsa motshegare. Ikhutse." Batho bangwe ba dira botoka fa ba sa itse dilo go sale gale; Family Caregiver Alliance e akantsha gore o se ka wa bua ka leano go fitlha pele ga tiragalo fa seo se baka poifo.

## O fetola thulaganyo jang?

### Tlogela sebaka sa go hema

O se ka wa tlatsa motsotso mongwe le mongwe. Alzheimer's Association e gatelela tekatekano fa gare ga tirwana le boikhutso, mme motho a ka tlhoka diphutso gantsi. Fa a lebega a boregile, a itlhokomolosa kgotsa a tenegile, fetola tirwana kgotsa ikhutse.

### Sireletsa maitseboa

Batho ba le bantsi ba nna ba sa iketla fa lesedi la letsatsi le fokotsega. NIA e akantsha nako kwa ntle kgotsa gaufi le fensetere letsatsi le letsatsi, go nna matlhagatlhaga ntle le go rulaganya thata, go tila caffeine le bojalwa maitseboa, le go thibela boroko jo boleele morago ga nako. Bona [go tlhoka boiketlo maitseboa](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) le [boroko](/brain-health/sleep/).

### E fetole fa ditlhoko di fetoga

- **Magato a pele:** rulaganyang mmogo. Motho a ka batla thulaganyo e e kwadilweng e a e tsamaisang ka boene.
- **Magato a magareng:** wena o etelela pele. Ditirwana tse dikhutshwane, boikhutso jo bontsi le ditiro tse di tlwaelegileng; bona [ditirwana tsa magato a magareng](/activities/middle-stage/).
- **Magato a morago:** thulaganyo e ikaegile ka boiketlo, dijo le tlhokomelo, e na le dinako tse dikhutshwane tsa mmino kgotsa go sidila diatla; bona [ditirwana tsa magato a morago](/activities/late-stage/).

### Fetola ditirwana, e seng foreimi

Boloka dijo, boikhutso le go ya bolaong di tlhomame, mme o fetole ditirwana tse di fa gare. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e akantsha ditirwana tse di farologaneng mo malatsing a a farologaneng. O ka baka ka Mosupologo, wa lema ka Labobedi mme wa leba ditshwantsho ka Laboraro. [Dikakantsho di le 101 tsa ditirwana](/activities/ideas/) le [ditirwana tsa mo gae](/activities/household-tasks/) di ka tlatsa diphatlha.

## Fa letsatsi le sa tsamaye sentle

Malatsi mangwe ga a latele leano lepe. Bolwetse, bosigo jo bo maswe, ketelo ya ngaka kgotsa phetogo ya maemo a bosa di ka senya tsotlhe. Tlogela leano, boloka dilo tsa motheo (dijo, dino, boikhutso, boiketlo) mme o leke gape ka moso. Fa motho a gana sengwe, bona [fa motho a re nnyaa](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Go ema le go hema ka bonya mmogo, jaaka [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/), go ka thusa lotlhe go simolola gape.

Thulaganyo ya gago le yone e botlhokwa. Ipeele diphutso mme o kope lelapa kgotsa tirelo ya boikhutso go go naya nako ya go ikhutsa. Bona [go itlhokomela ga motlhokomedi](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
