Bingo ke papadi e e tlwaelegileng, e e kopanyang batho ba le bantsi ba bagolo, mme dipalo di ka nna thata go latela ka dementia. Bingo ya ditshwantsho e dira tshwanelo e e bonolo e le tlwaelo, mme e fetola dipalo ka ditshwantsho tse dikgolo le tse di bonalang sentle. E dira mo gae, mo tirong ya letsatsi, kgotsa mo ntlong ya batlhokomedi. Tlhaloso e e bontsha go e bapala jang, go e fetola jang, le go dira gore tsepamo e nne mo boitumelong.

## Bingo ya ditshwantsho e dira jang

Mongwe le mongwe a na le karata e e nang le mmapa wa ditshwantsho, jaaka setsha sa tee, katse, rose, sekepe kgotsa baesekele. Mmereki o bontsha kgotsa o bitsa setshwantsho se le sengwe ka nako. Batshameki ba tshwantsha setshwantsho se se tshwanang mo karateng ya bone. Motho yo o tlatsang mola o o feletseng, kgotsa seteng sa setlhopha se se dumalaneng, a ka bitsa "Bingo," mme dihuto tse dintsi ga di tlhole gope.

O ka dirisa dikarata tse di tlhatlhobegang go tswa mo [ditirong tse di tlhatlhobegang tsa mahala](/activities/printable/), kgotsa [papadi ya bingo ya ditshwantsho](/games/picture-bingo/), e e nang le mmereki wa mo sekerineng wa dihuto. Tse di bereka sentle mo tabeletong e e tsholelediwang gore setlhopha yotlhe se e bone.

## Go baakanya kopano

- **Tlhopa setlhopha se se nnye.** Batho ba bane go ya go ba segolome ke boleng jo bo siameng. Dihuto tse dikgolo di thata go di latela, mme di ka ikutlwisa e le tse di sa lebelelweng.
- **Dira ditshwantsho tse dikgolo.** Dirisa mela e e bogale le sediriwa se se tlwaelegileng mo sekwere e nngwe le e nngwe. Tlogela dikarata tse di tletseng.
- **Beela batho sentle.** Beela motho yo mongwe le yo mongwe fa a ka bonang mmereki le karata ya gagwe, mme gaufi le mothusi fa a ka tlhoka thuso.
- **Dirisa dikgamano tse di bonolo.** Ditlhako tse dikgolo, dichelete kgotsa go tlhatlhoba monwana di siama go feta dipensele. Boloka tse dingwe.
- **Rulaganya kopano e e khutshwane.** Metsotso tse masome a mararo di a lekana gantsi. Emisa pele fa matla a fokola.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) e eletsa gore ditiro di fetolwe go ya ka se motho a sa ntse a se kgona, mme ka jalo bonolofatsa dikarata le melao fa go thusa.

## Go bitsa ditshwantsho jang

1. **Tsholetsa setshwantsho** kgotsa o se supe mo sekerineng pele o bua.
2. **Se bitse ka go bonala,** ka mafoko a a tlwaelegileng: "Setsha sa tee." Bua gangwe, ka bonya.
3. **Emele.** Neela batho nako ya go lebelela le go tshwantsha. O se ke wa bitsa setshwantsho se se latelang go fitlhela batho ba le bantsi ba bone sebaka.
4. **Tlhalosa, fa go thusa.** "Letlhare le le khubidu" kgotsa "se re se boneng mo picnic" di ka tsosa puo.
5. **Dira gore pace e nne bonya.** Mmereki yo o nnang jaaka khutsafalo o botlhokwa go feta yo o bitsang ka bonako.

## Go dira gore go tlhole go nne ga boikgethelo

Tlhodiso e ka nna bothata. Motho yo o latlhegelwang a ka ikutlwa a lekwa, mme motho yo o tshwenyegang ka go wina a ka tenega. Tse ke dikgetho tse dingwe:

- **Mongwe le mongwe o wina setshwantsho.** Motho yo mongwe le yo mongwe a bitse setshwantsho se a se ratang, mme setlhopha se se ananele.
- **Abelanang karata e e feletseng.** Kopa batho ba bue sengwe ka setshwantsho se le sengwe mo karateng ya bone.
- **Bapalang go fitlha setshwantsho se fela.** Bitsa setshwantsho sotlhe mo karateng, mme o simolele e nngwe, ntle le tshwanelo ya mongwe wa pele.
- **Dira gore mpho e nne nnye kgotsa o e tlogele.** Cookie kgotsa pina di dira sentle go feta trophy.

## Go fetola mo mothading wa bofelo

Mo batho ba ba fitlhelang go batla setshwantsho thata, dirisa karata e e nang le dikwere tse nne kgotsa tse tshela fela, kgotsa neela motho yo mongwe le yo mongwe karata e le nngwe ka nako. Letla mothusi a tshware karata fa motho a supa, kgotsa a tsamaisa tshupo ka bonya ka tumelo ya bone. Batho ba bangwe ba fitlhela go le bonolo go utlwa mmereki fela le go itumelela modumo wa mafoko.

Lebelela matshwao a go se itumele, jaaka go tlhoka go tlogela kgotsa go nna le sefahlego se se tlhomogileng, mme o emise fa a le teng. Go bona dikakanyo tse dingwe tsa ditiro tse di homileng, bala [ditiro tsa mothading wa bofelo](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo mo ntlong ya batlhokomedi le mo ditirong tsa letsatsi

Ditiro tsa setlhopha di dira sentle fa di rulagantswe go ya ka batho ba ba mo kamoreng. [Kgaolo ya rona ya ditiro tsa setlhopha mo ntlong ya batlhokomedi le mo ditirong tsa letsatsi](/activities/care-home-groups/) e tlhalosa go rulaganya kopano, go beela batho, le go tshwara batho ba ba batlang go tsamaya. Bingo ya ditshwantsho e dirisiwa sentle le pina kgotsa pale, le [go fedisa polelo](/activities/finish-the-saying/) jaaka papadi e e homileng morago mo kopanong.

## Bingo e ka dira eng le e e sa ka dirang

Bingo ya ditshwantsho e a kopanya, e bile e a itumedisa. E ka kopanya batho, ya neela lebaka la go lebelela ditshwantsho le go utlwa, e dire gore setlhopha se ikutlwe se le sentle. Ga e alafe dementia kgotsa go e dira gore e nne bonya. Sekaseka katlego ya yone ka ditiro tsa batho mo nakong ya hura, e seng ka gore ke mang yo o wintseng.
