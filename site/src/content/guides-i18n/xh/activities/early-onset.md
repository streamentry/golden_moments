Ulwazi oluninzi ngedementia luthatha ukuba umntu uneminyaka engamashumi asixhenxe okanye emashumi asibhozo. Kumntu ofunyaniswe enayo eneminyaka engamashumi amane okanye amashumi amahlanu, umfanekiso wemihla ngemihla uhluke. Usenokuba usenayo ingqesho, imali ayibolekileyo yendlu, abantwana abakwishumi elivisayo ekhaya okanye umzali ofuna ukuhlalwa naye. Imisebenzi kufuneka ivume obo bomi bobudala. Esi sikhokelo sichaza oko kutshintshayo, into onokuyizama, nalapho abalingane nabantwana abadala bangafumana inkxaso khona.

## Yintoni idementia eqala kwangethuba?

Idementia eqala kwangethuba yidementia eqala ngaphambi kweminyaka engama-65, ngamanye amaxesha ibizwa ngokuba yiqala ebutsheni. [Ibhlog ye-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) ichaza i-Alzheimer's njengeqala kwangethuba xa ichaphazela umntu oneminyaka engaphantsi kwengama-65, kwaye ithi ukunakekela umntu oneminyaka emincinci kangaka "kuza neengxaki ezizodwa." Iimpawu zingafana nezobomi basemva, kodwa ulindelo, uxanduva nobomi bomntu kuluntu azifani.

Ngenxa yokuba ayixhaphakanga kangako, abantu basenokulinda ixesha elide ukuze bafumane isigqibo, kwaye iintsapho zisenokuziva zingenaye umntu wokuthetha naye. Ukwazi ukuba abanye abantu bahlangabezana nokufanayo kudla ngokuba yinto elula.

## Gcina umntu omdala kumsebenzi

Abantu abadala abane-dementia eqala kwangethuba badla ngokungafuni ukuphathwa njengabantwana. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ikhuthaza ukuvumelanisa imisebenzi noko umntu asenako ukukwenza nokukonwabela. Kumntu osezintlanzini zamashumi amahlanu, oko kunokuthetha:

- **Gcina into yokonwaba eyaziwayo,** njengomsebenzi wamaplanga, ezemidlalo, amakhadi, iqela lokufunda iincwadi okanye ikwayara, ngoncedo oluncinci okanye iseshoni emfutshane.
- **Hamba okanye ukhwele ibhayisikile nomlingane,** kwindlela ayaziyo kakuhle.
- **Pheka isidlo sosapho,** umntu enza iinxalenye anokuzimamela.
- **Zithandele okanye uncede** kwindima ayikhethayo, njengokuhlela izipho okanye ukuncedisa kumsitho wesikolo.
- **Ceba uhambo** lokuya kwindawo ebalulekileyo kuye, unomlingane nomsebenzi olula.
- **Chitha ixesha nabazukulwana, abatshana okanye iintsana zabantwana,** nabelana ngomdlalo okanye iprojekthi.

Mvumele umntu akhethe. Ukubuza "Ungathanda ukwenza ntoni ngokuhlwa kwanamhlanje?" kuhlonipha ngakumbi kunokumkhethela.

## Sebenzisa umculo neentsomi zeminyaka yakhe

Umculo nemifanekiso yeminyaka yamashumi amabini, amashumi amathathu namashumi amane yomntu inokuvusa iimvakalelo ezifudumeleyo nengxoxo elula. Isikhokelo sethu se-[misebenzi yomculo](/activities/music/) sichaza indlela yokwakha uluhlu lweengoma, kwaye [imisebenzi yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) ibonisa indlela yokwabelana ngemifanekiso neentsomi ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo yovavanyo. Ukuze ufumane umdlalo ofanele abantu abadala ngakumbi, zama [amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/), anemibuzo evulekileyo ngomsebenzi, uhambo, abahlobo nosapho.

Iipeyi zemifanekiso eyenziwe ngeefoto zosapho nazo zinokusebenza kakuhle, kuba imifanekiso ngeyabantu neendawo ezibalulekileyo komntu. I-[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) igcina iifoto kwibrawuza kwisixhobo esithile.

## Umsebenzi, ukuqhuba nobomi bemihla ngemihla

Abantu abaninzi abane-dementia eqala kwangethuba basasebenza, baqhuba okanye bayilawula imali. Ezi asizizo imisebenzi ngomqondo oqhelekileyo, kodwa zichaphazela oko umntu anokukwenza. Thetha nogqirha wakhe ngokuqhuba nokhuseleko emsebenzini, uze ucinge ukuba ufuna uncedo ngemali okanye ngeendibano. Injongo kukukhusela isidima ngoxa umntu ehlala ebandakanyekile ngangoko efuna.

Ukuze ufumane isikhokelo ngemihla ngemihla nokuhlala usebenza, jonga [inkqubo yemihla ngemihla enobubele](/activities/daily-routine/) kunye [nemisebenzi yasekhaya njengemisebenzi](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Inkxaso yabalingane nabantwana abadala

Abalingane nabantwana abadala bahlala beziva bodwa. Ukunakekela kunokuvakala njengomsebenzi wesibini ngaphezu komsebenzi nosapho. Okubhalwe kwibhlog ye-Alzheimer's Association kuxela ukuba abanakekeli kumaqela edementia eqala kwangethuba bawafumana eluncedo kwiinkcukacha ezisebenzayo ezinje ngocwangciso lwezomthetho, nasekuziva kuqondwa nje. Kuthi kwakhona i-Association iqhuba amaqela enkxaso yabanakekeli ngaphandle kwentlawulo, kuquka amaqela abantu abanakekela umntu one-dementia eqala kwangethuba, kwaye amathathu awo adibana kwi-intanethi ngoLwesibini nangoLwesithathu.

Ukuze ubuze ngamaqela, biza kwi-Helpline ye-Association ekhoyo iiyure ezingama-24 ngemini, iintsuku ezisi-7 evekini, kwa-800.272.3900. [Isikhokelo sethu sokuzinakekela kwabanakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sigubungela ukuphumla, ukuphumla okuthutyiweyo nemiqondiso yokudinwa kakhulu.

## Izigaba zisabalulekile

Isigaba sedementia yomntu sisachaphazela into enceda. Ekuqaleni, uninzi lwabantu lunokwenza imisebenzi yalo eqhelekileyo ngotshintsho oluthile, kwaye isikhokelo sethu se-[misebenzi yesigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/) sinezinye iingcamango. Kamva, imisebenzi iba lula ngakumbi kwaye ichukumisa imvakalelo, njengoko kuchaziwe kwi-[misebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/). Tshintsha umsebenzi njengoko umntu etshintsha, uze umbuze ukuba ufuna ntoni naxa kunokwenzeka ukuba azixele.
