Uninzi lolwazi lwe-dementia lucinga ukuba umntu usemalunga neminyaka yama-70 okanye yama-80. Kumntu owafunyanwa eneminyaka yama-40 okanye yama-50, imeko yemihla ngemihla ingahluka. Usenokusasebenza, enemortgage, abantwana abasebancinane ekhaya, okanye umzali okufuneka amnakekele. Imisebenzi kufuneka imamele ubomi bomntu omdala. Eli nqaku lichaza izinto ezitshintshayo, izinto ezinokuzanywa, nendawo abalingani nabantwana abakhulileyo abanokufumana kuyo inkxaso.

## Yintoni i-dementia yokuqala?

I-early-onset dementia yi-dementia eqala phambi kweminyaka engama-65, ekwabizwa ngokuba yeyokuqala kwabanye. I-[ibhlog ye-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) ichaza i-Alzheimer's njengeyokuqala xa ichaphazela umntu ongaphantsi kwe-65, kwaye ichaza ukuba ukunakekela umntu osemncinci ngolo hlobo “kunemingeni ekhethekileyo.” Iimpawu zinokuba zifana nezo zaxa kuthe kwakamva ebomini, kodwa iimfuno zomntu, uxanduva lwakhe nobomi bakhe bentlalo azifani.

Ngenxa yokuba ayisoloko yenzeka, abantu banokulinda ixesha elide ukufumana isiqinisekiso, kwaye iintsapho zinokuzizwa ngathi azinamntu onokuthetha naye. Ukwazi ukuba abanye abantu bakwabelana ngaloo mava kurhoqo kunika ukukhululeka.

## Gcina umntu omdala kumsebenzi

Abantu abane-early-onset dementia abahlali befuna ukuphathwa njengabantwana. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) icebisa ukulungelelanisa imisebenzi nento umntu asakwazi ukuyenza nokuyonwabela. Kumntu oneminyaka yama-50, oko kunokubhekisa kwezi:

- **Gcina umdlalo owaziwayo,** njengokwenza umsebenzi wamaplanga, ibhola, amakhadi, iclub yeencwadi okanye ikwayala, ngoncedo oluncinci okanye ngexesha elifutshane.
- **Hamba okanye uhambe ngebhayisikile nomhlobo,** kwindlela awaziyo kakuhle.
- **Pheka isidlo sosapho,** umntu enza izinxalenye azinakayo.
- **Zithandele okanye uncede** kwindima akhethayo, njengokuhlela izipho okanye ukunceda kwisiganeko sesikolo.
- **Cwangcisa uhambo** eya kwindawo ebalulekileyo kuye, kunye nomhlobo kunye nengqiqo elula yexesha.
- **Chitha ixesha nabazukulwana, nabazukulwana bakabhuti okanye bakadade,** nidlala umdlalo okanye nenze iprojekthi ndawonye.

Mvumele umntu akhethe. Ukubuza ukuthi “Ungathanda ukwenza ntoni namhlanje emva kwemini?” kuhlonipha ngaphezu kokumkhethela.

## Sebenzisa umculo neenkumbulo zeminyaka yakhe

Umculo nemifanekiso evela kwiminyaka yakhe yama-20, ama-30 nama-40 inokuvusa imvakalelo efudumeleyo nencoko elula. Isikhokelo sethu [semisebenzi yomculo](/activities/music/) sichaza indlela yokwakha uluhlu lwemiculo, kwaye [imisebenzi yenkumbulo](/activities/reminiscence/) ibonisa indlela yokwabelana ngemifanekiso namabali ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo. Ukuze ufumane umdlalo ongaphezulu kakhulu, zama [amakhadi encoko](/games/conversation-cards/), anemibuzo evulekileyo malunga nomsebenzi, uhambo, abahlobo nosapho.

Iimpaira zemifanekiso eyenziwe ngeefoto zosapho nazo zinokusebenza kakuhle, kuba ezo mifanekiso ziquka abantu nezindawo ezibalulekileyo kumntu. [Iimpaira zemifanekiso](/games/photo-pairs/) zigcina iifoto kwi-browser kwesi sixhobo.

## Umsebenzi, ukuqhuba imoto nobomi bemihla ngemihla

Abantu abaninzi abane-early-onset dementia basaqhubeka besebenza, beqhuba imoto okanye belawula imali. Ezi azisiyo imisebenzi ngendlela eqhelekileyo, kodwa ziyachaphazela into umntu anokuyenza. Thetha nogqirha wakhe malunga nokuqhuba imoto nokhuseleko emsebenzini, kwaye cinga ukuba ufuna uncedo na ngemali okanye ngeeapointments. Injongo kukukhusela ukuzimela kwakhe ngelixa emgcina enxulumene nabanye njengoko efuna.

Ukuze ufumane isikhokelo ngemigangatho yemihla ngemihla nokuhlala uqhubekile, bona [umgangatho wemihla ngemihla olula](/activities/daily-routine/) kunye [nemisebenzi yasekhaya njengemisebenzi](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Inkxaso yabalingani nabantwana abakhulileyo

Abalingani nabantwana abakhulileyo rhoqo bazizwa bodwa. Ukunakekela kunokuzizwa njengomsebenzi wesibini ngaphezu komsebenzi nosapho. Ibhlog yeAlzheimer's Association ichaza ukuba abanakekeli kumaqela e-early-onset bayawafumana ancedile kwimiba yenkxaso esebenzayo, njengocwangciso lwezomthetho, nakwelokuziva beqondwa. Ichaza nokuba i-Association inamaqela enkxaso yabanakekeli angenamahala, kuquka namaqela abanakekela umntu one-early-onset dementia, kwaye amathathu kuwo ahlangana ngokwe-intanethi ngoLwesibini naseLwesithathu.

Ukuze ubuze malunga namaqela, bizela i-Helpline yeAssociation evulekileyo ngamaxesha onke (24/7) ku-800.272.3900. [Isikhokelo sethu sokuzinakekela](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sigubungela ukuphumla kwexeshana, ukuthatha umhlalaphi, kunye neempawu zokukhathala.

## Amaqondo asabalulekile

Inqanaba lesifo somntu lisabanga ukuba yintoni eyanceda. Ekuqaleni, uninzi lwabantu lunokwenza imisebenzi yabo yemihla ngemihla ngoguqulo oluncinci, kwaye [imisebenzi yesigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/) inezinye iingcamango. Kamva, imisebenzi iba lula kwaye ibe nemvakalelo, njengoko kuchazwe kwisikhokelo [semisebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/). Tshintsha umsebenzi njengoko umntu etshintsha, kwaye umbuze ukuba ufuna ntoni xa esakwazi ukuthetha.
