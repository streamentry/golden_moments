Abantu baninzi bafuna imidlalo yasimahla ye-intanethi ye-dementia ukuze benze into kunye engafuni ivenkile, ilogin okanye ikhadi lemali. Eyona midlalo ilungileyo ilula, isekwe kwimifanekiso, kwaye ilula ukuyeka. Kufuneka ibe lixesha elabelwanwayo, hayi uvavanyo. Eli phepha lichaza into okufanele uyijonge, indlela yokuseta umdlalo, nento le midlalo enokuyenza nengenakuyenza.

## Yintoni enenza umdlalo we-intanethi ufaneleke kwi-dementia?

Umdlalo ufanelekile kwabaninzi abahlala ne-dementia xa ubuza ukuqonda hayi inkumbulo. Ukudibanisa imifanekiso emibini efanayo, ukucofa umfanekiso ohambelana nesithunzi, okanye ukuwisa into kwibhasikithi elungileyo kuxhomekeke kwento amehlo abona ngoku. Imidlalo exhomekeke ekukhumbuleni umthetho, igama, inani okanye inyathelo langaphambili ihlala ibangela ukukhathazeka.

I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) icebisa imisebenzi esekelwe kwento umntu asakonwabela aze asakwazi ukuyenza, evumela ukuba aphumelele ngaphandle koncedo. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) icebisa ukwenza umsebenzi ulula endaweni yokuwuyeka xa amandla etshintsha.

## Imidlalo ye-Golden Moments onokuyivula kwi-browser

Umdlalo ngamnye ongezantsi uvula kwiwebhusayithi ngaphandle kokukhuphela. Uninzi lwawo udlalwa kwithebhulethi okanye kwikhompyuter; ezinye zingaprintwa.

- **[Imifanekiso emibini](/games/picture-pairs/).** Iipeyiri ezinkulu zemifanekiso ebonakalayo, ngaphandle kwexesha okanye amanqaku. Ilungile kumdlalo wokuqala.
- **[Fumana okufanayo](/games/find-the-same/).** Umfanekiso omnye phezulu, kwaye ofanayo phakathi kwezinketho ezimbini ukuya kwezine ezingezantsi.
- **[Ukudibanisa isithunzi](/games/shadow-match/).** Isithunzi esithambileyo sivela phezulu; cofa umfanekiso owenza lowo sithunzi.
- **[Hlela kunye](/games/sort-together/).** Cofa umfanekiso, uze ucofe ibhasikithi eliwukuba, njengekhitshi okanye igadi.
- **[Ipuzzle yomfanekiso](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Umfanekiso uqhekezwe waba ziqwenga, kwaye umfanekiso wonke usahlala ubonakala njengesikhokelo.
- **[Gqiba isaci](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Izaci ezaziwayo ezinelizwi lokugqibela elingekho; cofa ukhetho okanye ubonise isiphelo.
- **[Amakhadi encoko](/games/conversation-cards/).** Amakhadi omfanekiso anemibuzo evulekileyo yokuthetha, hayi yovavanyo. Ayaprintwa.
- **[Iimpaira zemifanekiso](/games/photo-pairs/).** Iipeyiri ezenziwe ngeefoto ozikhethayo kwisixhobo sakho. Iifoto zigcinwa kwi-browser kwesi sixhobo.
- **[Ukuphefumla ngokuzolileyo](/games/calm-breathing/).** Ukuphumla ngokuzolileyo nokuphefumla kunye. Wena uyayiqalisa, wena uyayimisa.

Imidlalo yamagama, ukugcwalisa ngemibala kunye nebingo yemifanekiso nayo ikhona njengemisebenzi yesiprinti. Jonga [imisebenzi yesiprinti ye-dementia yasimahla](/activities/printable/) ukuze ufumane ngakumbi.

## Indlela yokuseta iseshoni yomdlalo

1. **Khetha ixesha elithulileyo** xa umntu evuyile. Ntambama emva kwemini ingaba nzima kwabanye abantu, ngoko jonga iphethini yakho ngaphambili.
2. **Sebenzisa iskrini esikhulu.** Ithebhulethi kwisiseko, okanye ikhompyuter enebrawuza ekhulisiwe, ilula ukuyibona nokuyithinta.
3. **Vala ezinye iithebhu uvale izaziso.** Ii-pop-up ezingalindelwanga ziyoyisa kwaye zinokuphelisa iseshoni elungileyo.
4. **Hlala ecaleni komntu, hayi ngaphambi kwakhe.** Ukujonga ndawonye kuziva kungakumbi njengokuba nomntu, kungekho vavanyo.
5. **Qala ngomdlalo omnye imizuzu emihlanu ukuya kweshumi.** Vumela umntu athinte iskrini okanye acofe, ngelixa nina niluthatha ngamaxesha.
6. **Phelisa ngokulungileyo.** Gqiba iraundi, uze uthethe malunga nomfanekiso omnye okanye ucele ikomityi yeti.

## Ubumfihlo nokhuseleko kwizixhobo ezabelwanwayo

- **Iifoto zihlala kwisixhobo.** Iimpaira zemifanekiso zisebenzisa iifoto ozikhethayo, kwaye zigcinwa kwi-browser kwesi sixhobo. Jonga ukuba ngubani onokuvula ikhompyuter okanye ithebhulethi.
- **Gwema iikhonkco zangaphandle nezibhengezo.** Sebenzisa imidlalo ngqo kwiwebhusayithi, kwaye ungacofi kumabhena okanye kwizikhuthazo ezicela iinkcukacha zakho zobuqu.
- **Gcina izinto zokuhlawula nee-akhawunti zahlukile.** Ungashiyi umntu eyedwa kwiskrini esicela iphasiwedi okanye inombolo yekhadi.
- **Qaphela ukuhamba-hamba.** Gcina ithebhulethi ikude ukuba umntu usenokuhamba nayo ngaphandle kwekhaya.

Ukuze ufumane uluhlu olubanzi, bona [indlela yokukhetha ii-apps nemidlalo ye-dementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) kunye [nokuseta ithebhulethi okanye ifowuni](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Yintoni le midlalo enokuyenza nengenakuyenza

Imidlalo ye-intanethi inika abantu abane-dementia ithuba lokonwabela ixeshana, lokujonga imifanekiso ndawonye, nokuthatha inxaxheba kwinto ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Ayiyonyangi i-dementia, ayinciphisi isantya sayo kwaye ayithintelwa. Umzekelo odumileyo yi-[Federal Trade Commission ka-2016 ngokuchasene ne-Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) ngezithembiso zokuqeqesha ingqondo ezingakwazanga ukuxhaswa. Qaphela nayiphi na imidlalo, i-app okanye iwebhusayithi ethembisa inkumbulo engcono, ilinganisa ingqondo yakho okanye ithi ingasabalazisa ukuhla.

Sebenzisa ezi midlalo njengenye yenxalenye yemini ebandakanya nomculo, ukuhamba, incoko nokuphumla. Ukuze ufunde ngendlela yokwenza imidlalo ingabi nxunguphalo, bona [indlela yokudlala imidlalo nomntu one-dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) kunye [nemidlalo yenkumbulo engavavanyi](/activities/memory-games/).

## Xa kufuneka uyeke

Yeka xa umntu ebonakala ekhathele, enganyameli, ekhathazekile okanye engonwabanga. Yeka xa elahlekelwa ngumdla. Ukuyeka kwangethuba yimiphumo elungileyo. Ungabuya kwenye imini, okanye utshintshele kwincoko okanye ukuhamba.
