Iintsapho ezininzi zikhangela imidlalo yasimahla ye-intanethi yedementia kuba zifuna into yokwenza kunye engadingi stoko, ilogini okanye ikhadi lebhanki. Ukhetho olugqwesileyo lulula, lusekelwe kwimifanekiso, kwaye kulula ukulumisa. Kufuneka luvakale njengexesha elabelwanayo, hayi uvavanyo. Esi sikhokelo sichaza into ekufuneka uyikhangele, indlela yokulungiselela umdlalo, nento le midlalo enokuyenza nengenako ukuyenza.

## Yintoni eyenza umdlalo we-intanethi ufaneleke kwidementia?

Umdlalo ufanelekile kubantu abaninzi abaphila nedementia xa ucela ukwazi kunokukhumbula. Ukudibanisa imifanekiso emibini efanayo, ukuchukumisa umfanekiso ohambelana nesithunzi, okanye ukuwisela into kwitshotsha elifanelekileyo konke kuxhomekeke koko amehlo anokukubona ngoku. Imidlalo exhomekeke ekukhumbuleni umthetho, igama, inani okanye inyathelo langaphambili idla ngokubangela ukucaphuka.

I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ikhuthaza imisebenzi etsala kuko umntu asakonwabela nakukwazi, ayivumela ukuba aphumelele ngaphandle koncedo. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) icebisa ukwenza umsebenzi ube lula kunokuyeka xa amandla eguquka.

## Imidlalo ye-Golden Moments onokuyivula kwibrawuza

Umdlalo ngamnye ongezantsi uvulwa kwiwebhusayithi ngaphandle kokukopa. Uninzi ludlalwa kwitablet okanye kwikhompyuter; ezinye zinokushicilelwa.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Amabini amakhulu odibanisayo ngemifanekiso ibonakala, ngaphandle kwesibali-xesha okanye iskori. Mhle ngomdlalo wokuqala.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Umfanekiso omnye ngaphezulu, nofanayo phakathi kwezimbini ukuya kwezine ezingezantsi.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Isithunzi esithambileyo siyavela ngaphezulu; chukumisa umfanekiso oyenza.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Chukumisa umfanekiso, uze uchukumise itshotsha elifaneleyo, elinje ngekhitshi okanye igadi.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Umfanekiso usikwa ube ziziqwenga kwaye umfanekiso opheleleyo uhlala ubonakala njengesikhokelo.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Izaci ezaziwayo ngegama lokugqibela elingekhoyo; chukumisa ukhetho okanye bonisa isiphelo.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Amakhadi emifanekiso anemibuzo evulekileyo yokuthetha, hayi yokuvavanya. Ayashicilelwa nawo.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Amabini odibanisayo enziwe ngeefoto ozikhethayo kwisixhobo sakho. Iifoto zihlala kwibrawuza kwesixhobo.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Ukuphumla okucothayo nokuthe cwaka ukuphefumla kunye. Uyaqala, uyamisa.

Ukukhangela amagama, ukupeyinta kunye ne-bingo yemifanekiso nazo ziyafumaneka njengemisebenzi eshicilelwayo. Jonga [imisebenzi yedementia ashicilelwayo yasimahla](/activities/printable/) ukuze ufumane okungakumbi.

## Indlela yokulungiselela iseshoni yomdlalo

1. **Khetha ixesha elinokuthula** xa umntu ephumlile. Emva kwemini kuninzi lwabantu kunzima ngakumbi, ngoko jonga eyakho indlela kuqala.
2. **Sebenzisa isikrini esikhulu.** Itablet kwisima, okanye ikhompyuter enebrawuza ekhulisiweyo, kulula ukuyibona nokuyichukumisa.
3. **Vala ezinye iitebhu uze ucime izaziso.** Izibhengezo ezivela ngequbuliso ziyothusa kwaye zinokuphelisa iseshoni entle.
4. **Hlala ecaleni komntu, hayi malunga naye.** Ukubukela kunye kuvakala njengenkampani kunokuba yovavanyo.
5. **Qala ngomdlalo omnye imizuzu emihlanu ukuya kwishumi.** Mvumele umntu achukumise isikrini okanye alathise ngoxa nitshintshana.
6. **Gqiba ngomoya omhle.** Gqibezela umjikelo, uze uthethe ngomfanekiso omnye okanye ucebise indebe yeti.

## Ufihlo nokhuseleko kwizixhobo ezabelwanayo

- **Iifoto zihlala kwisixhobo.** I-Photo pairs isebenzisa iifoto ozikhethayo, kwaye zihlala kwibrawuza kwesixhobo. Jonga ukuba ngubani omnye onokuvula ikhompyuter okanye itablet.
- **Phepha iilingi zangaphandle nezibhengezo.** Sebenzisa imidlalo ngokuthe ngqo kwisayithi, kwaye ungachukumisi iibhanari okanye izicelo zeenkcukacha zobuqu.
- **Gcina iintlawulo neeakhawunti zahlukile.** Ungamshiyi umntu yedwa kwiscreen esicela iphasiwedi okanye inombolo yekhadi.
- **Qaphela ukuphaphazela.** Gcina itablet kude ukuba umntu usenokushiya indlu enayo.

Ukuze ufumane uluhlu olubanzi, jonga [indlela yokukhetha ii-app nemidlalo yedementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) kunye ne-[ukulungiselela itablet okanye ifowuni](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Into le midlalo enokuyenza nengenako ukuyenza

Imidlalo ye-intanethi inika abantu abaneedementia ithuba lokonwabela umzuzu, lokujonga imifanekiso kunye, nokuthatha inxaxheba kwinto ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Ayinyangi idementia, ayibambezeli, kwaye ayithintheli. Umzekelo owaziwa kakhulu yi-[isenzo se-Federal Trade Commission ngo-2016 ngokuchasene ne-Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) ngenxa yeempawu zoqeqesho lobuchopho ezingazixhasanga. Qaphela nawuphi na umdlalo, i-app okanye iwebhusayithi ethembisa imemori engcono, ebala iskori sobuchopho bakho okanye ethi inokubuyisela ukuhla.

Sebenzisa le midlalo njengenxalenye eyodwa yosuku olubandakanya umculo, uhambo, ingxoxo nokuphumla. Ukuze ufunde ngendlela yokwenza imidlalo ibe nokuphumla kunoxinzelelo, jonga [indlela yokudlala imidlalo nomntu one-dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) kunye ne-[imidlalo yememori engavavanyiyo](/activities/memory-games/).

## Xa ufanele umise

Misa xa umntu ebonakala edinwe, engaphumlanga, ecaphukile okanye engakhululekanga. Misa xa ephelelwa ngumdla. Ukumisa kwangoko sisiphumo esihle. Ungabuya nangomhla wesinye, okanye utshintshele kwingxoxo okanye uhambo endaweni yoko.
