Ukupeyinta imifanekiso yenye yeendlela ezilula zokuyila onokuzinika umntu ophila ne-Alzheimer's okanye elinye iqela ledementia. Akudingi kufunda, akudingi kukhumbula kwaye akukho mpendulo echanekileyo. Izandla ziyahamba, imibala iyaphuma, kukho umfanekiso onokuthethwa ngawo. Esi sikhokelo sichaza indlela yokukhetha amaphepha, into onokuyibeka, nendlela yokupeyinta kunye ngaphandle kokuvakalisa ngathi ngumsebenzi wabantwana okanye uvavanyo.

## Kutheni ukupeyinta kufanelekile kubantu abaninzi abaneedementia

Ukupeyinta kunika izandla into yokwenza namehlo into yokulandela. Kunokwenziwa uhleli etafileni okanye esitulweni ecaleni kwefestile, kwaye kulula ukukuthatha nokukubeka phantsi. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ichaza ubugcisa njengendlela yokubonisa iimvakalelo nokonwabela okwangoku, kwaye ithi akudingi buchule bobugcisa.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ikhuthaza ukukhetha imisebenzi ehambelana noko umntu asenako ukukwenza, nokuyivumelanisa njengoko amandla eguquka. Ukupeyinta kulula ukukuvumelanisa: umfanekiso omkhulu, iikhreyoni ezityebileyo, imibala emincinci, okanye isikrini sokuchukumisa umbala.

## Khetha imifanekiso yabantu abadala

Abantu abadala bahlala bengawathandi amaphepha abantwana. Khangela:

- **Imiboniso yendalo:** igadi enamaluloyo amakhulu, echibini elinesikhephe, iintaka egatyeni.
- **Izinto ezaziwayo:** iteapot, ibhayisikile, isiqhamo, umnqwazi.
- **Iinoyilo ezinkulu, ezilula:** ii-mandala neemilo ezinkulu eziqaqambileyo ezinamacandelo amakhulu ambalwa.
- **Imifanekiso yobugcisa okanye imihlaba** onokuthi umntu ayibone kwindawo okanye komzobi athanda kuye.

Phepha amaphepha anemimandla emincinci exineneyo, ebangela ukucaphuka ekuzaliseni. Amaphepha abantu abadala ashicilelwayo yindlela entle, kwaye [imisebenzi yethu yedementia ashicilelwayo yasimahla](/activities/printable/) ibandakanya amaphepha eprinti enkulu.

## Into onokuyibeka

- **Izixhobo ezityebileyo ezilula ukuzibamba.** Iikhreyoni ezinkulu, iimarker ezityebileyo, okanye iipensile zemibala ezinesibambo esibanzi zilula kunamapeni acekeceke.
- **Imibala emincinci.** Imibala emithathu okanye emine yanele. Ukhetho oluninzi lunokudiniwa.
- **Iphepha okanye ikhadibhodi eqinileyo.** Iphepha elincinci liyandwendwela likhukuleke. Bambela amakona ngetheyiphu okanye usebenzise iklibhodi.
- **Itafile ecocekileyo.** Susa ukungcola ubeke iphepha elinye ngexesha.
- **Into yokucwifa okanye yokuxuba,** njengelaphu okanye isiponji, ukuze kuvele isiphumo esithambileyo.

## Ukupeyinta kwitablet

I-[coloring game](/games/coloring/) kwi-Golden Moments isebenza ngokuchukumisa umbala uze uchukumise inxalenye yomfanekiso. Ayidingi kulawula izandla ngokucokisekileyo, ngoko inokufanela abantu abakhubazeke ekubambeni. Hlala ecaleni komntu ukuze nabelane ngesikrini, umvumele akhethe imibala. Cima izaziso ukuze umfanekiso ube lilo ugxininiso.

## Indlela yokupeyinta kunye

1. **Qalani umdweliso wokuqala kunye.** Bamba ikhreyoni kunye naye, okanye beka isandla sakho phezu kwesakhe imidweliso embalwa, uze umyeke.
2. **Thethani ngemibala nangeendawo.** "Ngumbala oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka omhle. Lo mbala wamanzi uwubone phi?" kumema ingxoxo ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo yovavanyo.
3. **Bulela ukhetho, hayi ucoceko.** "Ukhethe imibala efudumeleyo kakhulu" kuvakala kulungile kuno "Uhlale ngaphakathi kwemigca."
4. **Mvumele akhokele.** Ukuba ufuna ukuzoba kuyo yonke iphepha, mvumele.
5. **Misa xa emisa.** Beka phantsi iikhreyoni, uze ucebise indebe yeti okanye uhambo.

## Ukuvumelanisa njengoko idementia iguquka

Kwisigaba sokuqala, abantu bahlala bonwabela amaphepha anenkcukacha kwaye banokukhetha imibala neenoyilo zabo. Kwisigaba esiphakathi, imifanekiso emikhulu nokhetho oluncinci kuyanceda. Kwizigaba zokugqibela, zama imilo enye enkulu, iphepha elinemibala ethambileyo, okanye nje ukubamba ikhreyoni ngoxa uvuma ingoma. Abanye abantu bonwabela ukujonga umfanekiso ogqityiweyo ngaphezu kokuwenza.

Ukuze ufumane okungakumbi ngomsebenzi wokuyila kwisigaba ngasinye, jonga [ubugcisa nezinto zezandla zabantu abadala abaneedementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) kunye ne-[iiprojekthi zobugcisa zabantu abaneedementia](/activities/art-projects/).

## Xa ufanele umise

Misa ukuba umntu uyacaphuka, uyayityhala iphepha, okanye ubonakala edikiwe. Ukugqiba emva kwemizuzu emibini ngempumelelo ukuba wonwabile. Unokubuyela kamva, okanye uzame omnye umfanekiso okanye omnye umsebenzi onje nge-[sensory play](/activities/sensory/).
