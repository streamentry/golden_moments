Ukugcwalisa ngemibala ngomnye wemisebenzi elula yokunika umntu ohlala ne-Alzheimer's okanye enye iintlobo ze-dementia. Akufuni ukufunda, inkumbulo okanye impendulo echanileyo. Izandla ziyahamba, imibala iyavela, kwaye kukho umfanekiso wokuthetha ngawo. Eli phepha lichaza indlela yokukhetha amaphepha, izixhobo ezifanele ukuzilungiselela, kunye nendlela yokugcwalisa kunye ngaphandle kokwenza kube ngathi yinto yabantwana okanye yovavanyo.

## Kutheni ukugcwalisa kulungele abantu abaninzi abane-dementia

Ukugcwalisa kunika izandla into yokwenza namehlo into yokulandela. Kunokwenziwa uhleli etafileni okanye esitulweni ngasefestileni, kwaye kulula ukukhupha nokubeka phantsi. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ichaza ubugcisa njengendlela yokuvakalisa iimvakalelo nokonwabela okwangoku, kwaye icacisa ukuba akufuneki ube nobuchule bobugcisa.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) icebisa ukukhetha imisebenzi efanelana nento umntu asakwazi ukuyenza, kwaye ukuyilungelelanisa xa amandla etshintsha. Ukugcwalisa kulula ukulungelelanisa: umfanekiso omkhulu, iikrayoni ezijiyileyo, imibala embalwa, okanye iskrini sokucofa.

## Khetha imiklo yabantu abadala

Abantu abadala rhoqo bayangathandi imifanekiso yabantwana. Khangela:

- **Imiboniso yendalo:** igadi eneentyatyambo ezinkulu, ichibi elineboti, iintaka esihlahleni.
- **Izinto eziyaziwayo:** itoti yeti, ibhayisikile, isiqhamo, isigqoko.
- **Iipateni ezikhulu nezilula:** imandala nemilo ejikeleze ngokukhulu enamacandelo amakhulu ambalwa.
- **Iiprinti zobugcisa okanye imiboniso yendawo** umntu anokuyibona kwindawo ayithandayo okanye kumzobi wakhe.

Gwema amaphepha anezindawo ezincinci nezixinene, ezikhathaza ukuzigcwalisa. Amaphepha abantu abadala aprintwayo ayikhetho elilungileyo, kwaye [imisebenzi yesiprinti yasimahla ye-dementia](/activities/printable/) ethu iquka amaphepha anamagama amakhulu.

## Zintoni ezifanele ukubekwa phambili

- **Izixhobo ezinkulu nezibambeka kulula.** Iikrayoni ezinkulu, iimarikhi ezinkulu, okanye iipensile zemibala ezinesibambo esibanzi zilula kunezikrayoni ezincinci.
- **Ipaleti encinci.** Imibala emithathu okanye emine iyanele. Ukhetho olukhulu lunokuthi luxinzelele.
- **Iphepha okanye ikhadi eliqinileyo.** Iphepha elincinci liyadumba kwaye liyahamba. Bopha iikona ngetepu okanye sebenzisa iklipibhodi.
- **Itafile ecacileyo.** Susa okungenamsebenzi kwaye ubeke iphepha elinye ngexesha.
- **Into yokuxuba okanye yokuthoba,** njengelaphu okanye isiponji, ukuze kube nesiphumo esithambileyo.

## Ukugcwalisa kwithebhulethi

[Umdlalo wokugcwalisa ngemibala](/games/coloring/) kwiGolden Moments usebenza ngokucofa umbala emva koko ucofe icandelo lomfanekiso. Awufuni ulawulo oluchanekileyo lwesandla, ngoko unokufanelana nabantu abathe amandla esandla abo abuthathaka. Hlala ecaleni komntu ukuze nabelane ngeskrini, kwaye umvumele akhethe imibala. Vala izaziso ukuze umfanekiso ube ngowona mbeko.

## Indlela yokugcwalisa kunye

1. **Qala umgca wokuqala kunye.** Bamba ikrayoni kunye nabo, okanye ubeke isandla sakho phezu kwesandla sabo kwimigca embalwa, emva koko uvumele bayikhulule.
2. **Thetha ngemibala neendawo.** “Lelo libala leblu elihle kakhulu. Wakha wabona phi amanzi anombala onjalo?” iyavula incoko ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo.
3. **Ncoma izikhetho, hayi ukucoceka.** “Ukhethe imibala efudumeleyo kakuhle” yenza umntu aziveke engcono kunokuthi “Uhlale phakathi kwemigca.”
4. **Mvumele akhokele.** Ukuba befuna ukugcwalisa iphepha lonke ngemigca engazange ibekwe, mvumele.
5. **Yeka xa bemisa.** Beka phantsi iikrayoni, uze ucele ikomityi yeti okanye ukuhamba.

## Ukulungelelanisa njengoko i-dementia itshintsha

Esigxeni sokuqala, abantu bahlala bonwabela amaphepha anemiklo ecikideke kwaye bakwazi ukukhetha imibala neemilo zabo. Esigxeni esiphakathi, imifanekiso emikhulu nokhetho oluncinane luyanceda. Kumaxesha asemva, zama umfanekiso omkhulu omnye, iphepha lombala othambileyo, okanye nje ukubamba ikrayoni ngelixa uhlabela ingoma. Abanye abantu bonwabela ukujonga umfanekiso ogqityiweyo ngaphezu kokuwenza.

Ukuze ufumane okungakumbi ngemisebenzi yobugcisa ngesigaba ngasinye, bona [ubugcisa nezakhono zabantu abadala abane-dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) kunye [neeprojekthi zobugcisa zabantu abane-dementia](/activities/art-projects/).

## Xa kufuneka uyeke

Yeka ukuba umntu uyacaphuka, ushiya iphepha kude, okanye ubonakala ekhathele. Ukuyeka emva kwemizuzu eyi-2 kuyimpumelelo ukuba bonwabile. Ungabuya kamva, okanye uzame omnye umfanekiso okanye omnye umsebenzi onjenge[wemvakalelo](/activities/sensory/).
