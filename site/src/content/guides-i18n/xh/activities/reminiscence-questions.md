Ukukhumbula kukuthetha kunye ngexesha elidlulileyo: amakhaya obuntwana, imisebenzi yokuqala, ukutya okuthandwayo, iingoma, imitshato neeholide. Kubantu abaninzi abaneedementia, ingxoxo efudumeleyo ngobomi babo yenye yeenxalenye ezonwabisayo kakhulu zosuku. Into ebalulekileyo kukubuza ngendlela emema intsomi kwaye ingavavanyi imemori. Le mibuzo ihlelwe ngokwemixholo, kunye namacebiso endlela yokuyisebenzisa.

## Imithetho yemibuzo yokukhumbula

- **Buza imibuzo evulekileyo.** "Ndixelele ngoko..." kunye no "Kwakunjani..." kumema intsomi. "Uyakhumbula...?" kucela impendulo echanekileyo.
- **Ungavavanyi.** Phepha "Ngubani lo?", "Yayiliphi unyaka?" kunye no "Uyalikhumbula igama le...?"
- **Yamkela nantoni na ebuya.** Ukuba intsomi iyatshintsha okanye iindawo ziyadityaniswa, yonwabele uze ubuze ngeemvakalelo.
- **Sebenzisa umbuzo omnye ngexesha.** Linda, mamela uze ulandelise ngento elula.
- **Mvumele umntu akhokele.** Ukuba ufuna ukuthetha ngenye into, hamba naye.

[Isikhokelo se-National Institute on Aging malunga nonxibelelwano](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) sikhuthaza izivakalisi ezimfutshane, ilizwi elizolileyo, nomonde ngoxa umntu efumana amagama akhe. Le mithetho ibaluleke apha njengaphi na.

## Ikhaya nobuntwana

- Ndixelele ngendlu owakhulela kuyo. Ikhitshi lalinjani?
- Yintoni owawuthanda ukuyenza ngaphandle xa wawusemncinci?
- Ngoobani abantu owawudlala nabo esitratweni sakho?
- Igumbi lakho lokulala lalinjani? Wawulabelana nabani na?

## Umsebenzi nokugqibezela izinto

- Luhlobo luni lomsebenzi owawuyonwabela kakhulu?
- Ndixelele ngosuku emsebenzini olwakwenza waziqhenya.
- Yintoni owawuthanda ukuyenza okanye ukuyilungisa ngezandla zakho?
- Ngubani owakufundisa okuninzi ngomsebenzi wakho?

## Ukutya nokupheka

- Yintoni eyayityiwa ngusapho lwakho ngeCawa?
- Ndixelele ngesitya omnye wosapho lwakho owayesiphekela kakuhle ngokukodwa.
- Wawunayo indawo oyithandayo yokuya kuyo ukufumana into emnandi xa wawusemncinci?
- Aziphi iivumba ezikukhumbuza ikhaya?

Ingxoxo yokutya ihambelana kakuhle nesikhokelo se-[misebenzi echukumisa imvakalelo](/activities/sensory/), kunye nencasa encinci ukuba kukhuselekile ukuyinika.

## Umculo nokudanisa

- Zeziphi iingoma owawuzicula okanye uzidanisa xa wawusemncinci?
- Ngubani owawumthanda kakhulu umculi okanye iqela?
- Wawuwuvela phi umculo ngelo xesha: ekhaya, kwimidlalo yokudanisa, ecaweni?

Ukuze ufumane uluhlu lweengoma neengcamango zomculo, jonga [imisebenzi yomculo yabantu abaneedementia](/activities/music/).

## Iiholide nemibhiyozo

- Yeyiphi iholide owayithanda kakhulu ekukhuleni?
- Ndixelele ngomgcobo wokuzalwa owuthethayo.
- Yintoni eyayenziwa ngusapho ngexesha lasebusuku basehlotyeni?
- Usapho lwakho lwalubhiyozela njani amaxesha onyaka?

Ukuze ufumane iingcamango ezifanelekileyo kwiholide ethile, jonga [imisebenzi yeeholide yomntu one-dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Usapho nabahlobo

- Ndixelele ngomntu owakuhlekisa kakhulu.
- Imini yomtshato wakho yayinjani?
- Ndixelele ngabantwana bakho xa babemincinci.
- Abahlobo bakho babekubiza ngokuthini?

Buza imibuzo yosapho ngobunono. Ukuba umntu uzila umntu, landela ukhokelo lwakhe ungacingi ukuba akhumbule iinkcukacha anokuzifumana zibuhlungu.

## Iindawo nendalo

- Ndixelele ngendawo ofuna ukucinga ngayo.
- Imozulu yayinjani apho wawukhulela khona?
- Wawunayo igadi, okanye umthi owuthandayo?
- Ndixelele ngohambo owalonwabelayo.

## Ukusebenzisa iifoto nezinto

Imifanekiso yindawo yendalo yokuqala. Bamba ifoto ukuphakama kwamehlo uthi, "Ndixelele ngale." [Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngemisebenzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) sicebisa ukusebenzisa izinto ezaziwayo, iifoto nomculo ukuvula ingxoxo. Ibhokisi yememori enezinto ezimbalwa zobomi bomntu nayo inokusebenza kakuhle; jonga [imisebenzi yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) ukuze ubone indlela yokuyilungisa.

## Xa imemori ibuhlungu

Ezinye iimemori zizisa usizi, uloyiko okanye ingqumbo. Ukuba umntu uyacaphuka, ungatyhali. Thetha into efudumeleyo njengokuthi, "Kuvakala ngathi lalixesha lintsha. Ungathanda ukuthetha ngento emnandi ngakumbi?" Emva koko tshintsha umxholo, ubonelele ngendebe yeti, okanye uvuthe ingoma ayithandayo.
