Inkumbulo yindlela yokuthetha ndawonye ngexesha elidlulileyo: iikhaya zobuntwana, imisebenzi yokuqala, izinto ezithandwayo zokutya, iingoma, imitshato namaholide. Kwabaninzi abahlala ne-dementia, incoko efudumeleyo ngobomi babo ngenye yeenxalenye ezibalulekileyo zemini. Isihloko kukubuza ngendlela eyamkela ibali ingazange ivavanye inkumbulo. Le mibuzo iqulunqwe ngemixholo, kunye neengcebiso zokuyisebenzisa.

## Imigaqo yemibuzo yenkumbulo

- **Buza imibuzo evulekileyo.** “Ndixelele malunga...” kunye “Yayinjani...” zimema ibali. “Uyakhumbula...?” ibuza impendulo echanileyo.
- **Ungabuzi ngovavanyo.** Gwema “Ngubani huyu?”, “Yayiyeyiphi nyaka?” kunye “Uyakhumbula igama lo...?”
- **Yamkela nantoni na ebuya.** Ukuba ibali liyatshintsha okanye iindawo ziyaxutyushwa, yonwabela kwaye ubuze ngemvakalelo.
- **Sebenzisa umbuzo omnye ngexesha.** Linda, mamele, uze ulandele ngento elula.
- **Vumela umntu akhokele.** Ukuba befuna ukuthetha ngento engelinye ixesha, hamba nabo.

Isikhokelo se-[National Institute on Aging ngokuthetha](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) sicebisa iisentensi ezimfutshane, izwi elithambileyo, kunye nokunamathela xa umntu efumana amagama akhe. Ezi migaqo zibalulekile apha ngokufanayo nakwenye indawo.

## Ekhaya nobuntwana

- Ndixelele ngendlu owakhulela kuyo. Yayinjani ikhitshi?
- Wawuthanda ukwenza ntoni xa wawusemncinci?
- Ngabantu bobani owayedlala nabo kwisitrato sakho?
- Yayinjani igumbi lakho lokulala? Wawuyabelana negumbi nomntu na?

## Umsebenzi nokwenza izinto

- Wayiyithanda yeyiphi imisebenzi ngakumbi?
- Ndixelele ngemini emsebenzini eyakwenza uziqhenye.
- Wayethanda ukwenza okanye ukulungisa ntoni ngezandla zakho?
- Ngubani owakufundisa okuninzi ngomsebenzi wakho?

## Ukutya nokupheka

- Usapho lwakho lwalutya ntoni ngeCawe?
- Ndixelele ngesitya esithile esenziwa ngokukhethekileyo ngumntu wosapho lwakho.
- Ubenayo indawo oyithandayo yokuthenga into emnandi xa wawusemncinci na?
- Ziphi iivumba ezikukhumbuza ikhaya?

Incoko yokutya ihambelana kakuhle nesikhokelo se[semisebenzi yemvakalelo](/activities/sensory/), kunye nesidlo esincinci ukuba kukhuseleke ukusinikela.

## Umculo nokudanisa

- Ziziphi iingoma owayezicula okanye owayedanisa kuzo xa wawusemncinci?
- Ngubani owayethandwa ngaye ngakumbi, ngumculi okanye iqela?
- Wawuva umculo phi kwezo mini: ekhaya, kwiidanisi, ecaweni?

Ukuze ufumane iipleyliste nezingcamango zomculo, bona [imisebenzi yomculo yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/music/).

## Amaholide neziganeko

- Yayiliholide eliyithandayo xa ukhula?
- Ndixelele ngomhla wokuzalwa owukhumbulayo.
- Intsapho yakho yayenza ntoni ngobusuku behlobo?
- Intsapho yakho yayibhiyozela njani amaxesha onyaka?

Ukuze ufumane iingcamango ezihambelana neholide ethile, bona [imisebenzi yamaholide yomntu one-dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Usapho nabahlobo

- Ndixelele ngomntu owayekuhleka ngakumbi.
- Umtshato wakho wawumeka njani?
- Ndixelele ngoomntwana bakho xa babancinci.
- Abahlobo bakho babekubiza ntoni?

Buza imibuzo yosapho ngobumnene. Ukuba umntu ulila umntu alahlekelwe nguye, landela isihloko sakhe kwaye ungamcelisi ukukhumbula iinkcukacha ezinokuba ngubuhlungu kuye.

## Iindawo nendalo

- Ndixelele ngendawo oyithandayo ukucinga ngayo.
- Yayinjani imozulu apho wakhula khona?
- Wawunegadi okanye omnye umthi wakho owuthandayo na?
- Ndixelele ngohambo owalonwabela.

## Ukusebenzisa imifanekiso nezinto

Imifanekiso yindawo yokuqala yendlela. Bamba ifoto kumehlo ebonakala, uthi, “Ndixelele ngale nto.” I-[Alzheimer's Association ngesikhokelo sayo semisebenzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) icebisa ukusebenzisa izinto ezaziwayo, imifanekiso nomculo ukuqalisa incoko. Ibhokisi yenkumbulo enezinto ezimbalwa ezivela ebomini babo nayo inokusebenza kakuhle; bona [imisebenzi yenkumbulo](/activities/reminiscence/) ukuze ubone indlela yokuyilungiselela.

## Xa inkumbulo inobuhlungu

Ezinye iinkumbulo zizisa intlungu, uloyiko okanye umsindo. Ukuba umntu uyakhathazeka, musa ukucinezela. Thetha into enobubele njengo, “Kubonakala sasinzima lelo xesha. Ufuna ukuthetha ngento enovuyo?” Emva koko tshintsha umxholo, nika ikomityi yeti, okanye vula iculo abalithandayo.

## Dibanisa nomdlalo okanye ukuhamba

Emva kweseshoni yenkumbulo, umdlalo othambileyo unokugcina imo ikhanyisiwe.
