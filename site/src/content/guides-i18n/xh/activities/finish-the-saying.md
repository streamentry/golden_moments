Umdlalo wokugqiba isaci usebenzisa isibhalo esaziwayo esiyimisa ngaphambi kwegama lokugqibela. Kwabaninzi, ngumdlalo onomnandi onoxinzelelo olumbalwa wokwabelana ngexeshana. Umdlalo usebenza kuba amagama ayindala aziwa kakhulu, kwaye umntu akafuni ukukhumbula nto malunga nale mini. Eli phepha lichaza indlela yokudlala, ukukhetha iisaci, nendlela yokuwugcina ukhululekile.

## Yintoni umdlalo wokugqiba isaci?

Ngumdlalo omfutshane apho umntu omnye efunda inxalenye yokuqala yesaci omnye agqibe isiphelo. Imizekelo ibandakanya “Actions speak louder than...” kunye “The early bird catches the...” Umntu unokuthi isiphelo ngelizwi, akhombe kuso, okanye akhethe kwezimbini okanye ezintathu eziniwe. Abanye abantu bonwabela ukuba ngowefunda inxalenye yokuqala.

I-Golden Moments ikunika lo mdlalo wesaci [simahla](/games/finish-the-saying/) ngemibhalo emikhulu. Ungawudlala nangaphandle kwesikrini, usithi nje inxalenye yokuqala uze umlinde.

## Indlela yokudlala, ngamanyathelo

1. **Khetha ixesha elithulileyo** uhlale ngokulingana namehlo nomntu ojongene naye.
2. **Funda inxalenye yokuqala ngokuzolileyo,** ngelizwi elifudumeleyo, uze umise. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) icebisa ukuthetha ngokuzolileyo kwaye ngeesentensi ezimfutshane nezilula.
3. **Linda.** Nika imizuzwana emininzi. Ukuba akukho nto ivela, nika izaci ezimbini okanye ezintathu, enye kuzo ilungileyo.
4. **Bhiyozela nayiphi na impendulo.** “Ewe, yileyo,” okanye “Ndingazange ndicinge ngaloo nto,” kugcina into ilungile.
5. **Thetha ngesaci.** Buza ukuba lithetha ntoni kuye, okanye wayivephi kuqala. Apho kukho ixabiso lenyaniso.
6. **Yima phambi kokuba bakhathale.** Izaci ezimbalwa kunye nohleko sisenzo esipheleleyo.

## Ukunika ukhetho ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo

Ukhetho lweziphelo lunokwenza umdlalo ube lula, kodwa lunokuvakala njengovavanyo ukuba lunikwa njengomceli. Gcina ilizwi lilula. Thi “Ufuna ukuva isiphelo okanye ukhethe enye?” endaweni yokuthi “Yeyiphi na?” Nika ukhetho emva kokuthula, hayi ngaphambi kokuba umntu afumane ithuba lokuphendula.

Ukuba umntu uyakhathazeka okanye uziva ehlazekile, yeka umdlalo uze uqhubele phambili. [Iingcebiso zencoko](/caregiving/communication-tips/) zichaza indlela yokutshintsha inkqubo ngaphandle kokwenza ingxolo.

## Ukukhetha iisaci ezifanelana nomntu

- **Sebenzisa iisaci zomntu.** Buza usapho ukuba unyana okanye umyeni wakhe wayesoloko ethi ntoni. Iisaci zobuntwana zihlala zilula ukuzikhumbula.
- **Khetha iisaci ezimfutshane nezemihla ngemihla.** Izibhalo malunga nemozulu, ikhitshi, igadi, intsapho neminyaka ziqhelekile kwaye zilula ukuzilandela.
- **Yenza kube lula.** Iisaci ezixhomekeke kumagama amandulo okanye kwimfundiso zinokudideka. Zigcine kwabo bazonwabela zona.
- **Jonga ulwimi.** Iidiomu ezininzi aziguqukiswa. Sebenzisa iisaci umntu azaziyo ngolwimi lwakhe, nangaxa kufuneka ufumane ezintsha.

## Iisaci ngokwemixholo

Imizekelo elandelayo ingesiNgesi, kuba ibonisa uhlobo lwesaci. Iimbonakalo zesiphelo zibekwe phakathi kwemigangatho. Funda kuphela inxalenye yokuqala, uze ulinde. Sebenzisa iisaci zakho zesiXhosa xa ukwazi.

- **Imozulu:** "Rain before seven..." (ending: "fine by eleven"); "Every cloud has..." (a silver lining).
- **Ikhitshi nokutya:** "Too many cooks..." (spoil the broth); "Don't count your chickens..." (before they hatch).
- **Ikhaya nosapho:** "Home is where the heart..." (is); "Blood is thicker than..." (water).
- **Iminyaka negadi:** "April showers bring..." (May flowers); "You reap what you..." (sow).

## Xa umntu engakwazi ukuphendula?

Abanye abantu abakwazi ukufumana amagama ngokupheleleyo kwizigaba zokugqibela. Ungayeki isaci. Yifunde, mamele, uze wenze umqondiso okanye ukhethe umfanekiso. Ukwabelana ngohlobo oluqhelekileyo lwesaci kusangumzuzu wokwabelana. Yidibanise nomculo, isiselo esifudumeleyo, okanye ukuhlikihla isandla, njengoko kuchaziwe kwisikhokelo [semisebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ezinye iindlela zokusebenzisa iisaci

Iisaci zikhokelela kwincoko malunga nexesha elidlulileyo lomntu. Ungabiza amabali ngemibuzo ye[nkumbulo](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), okanye udlale omnye umdlalo onxulumeneyo njengo[makhadi encoko](/games/conversation-cards/). Ukuze ufumane iingcamango ezingaphezulu zemihla yonke, bona [imisebenzi yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/ideas/).
