Umdlalo wokugqibezela isaci usebenzisa intetho eqhelekileyo emiselwa ngaphambi kwegama layo lokugqibela. Kubantu abaninzi, yindlela emnandi, engenaxinzelelo yokwabelana ngexesha. Umdlalo usebenza kuba amagama ayindala kwaye aziwa kakuhle, kwaye umntu akafuni kukhumbula nto nganamhlanje. Esi sikhokelo sichaza indlela yokudlala, indlela yokukhetha izaci, nendlela yokugcina izinto zizolile.

## Umdlalo wokugqibezela isaci yintoni?

Ngumdlalo omfutshane apho omnye efunda inxalenye yokuqala yesaci omnye egqibezela isiphelo. Imizekelo ibandakanya "Izenzo zithetha ngaphezu kwamagama..." kunye "Intaka yasekuseni ibamba..." Umntu angathetha isiphelo ngokuvakalayo, alalathise, okanye akhethe phakathi kweziphelo ezimbini okanye ezintathu oziphayo. Abanye abantu bonwabela ukuba ngabo abafunda isiqingatha sokuqala.

I-Golden Moments inika oku njenge-[umdlalo wasimahla wokugqibezela isaci](/games/finish-the-saying/) onoonobumba abakhulu. Ungawudlala nangaphandle kwesikrini, ngokuthi nje isiqingatha sokuqala uze ulinde.

## Indlela yokudlala, inyathelo ngenyathelo

1. **Khetha ixesha elinokuthula** uhlale ngokulinganayo namehlo, ujonge umntu.
2. **Funda inxalenye yokuqala ngokucotha,** ngelizwi elifudumeleyo, uze ume. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) ikhuthaza ukuthetha ngokucotha nokusebenzisa izivakalisi ezimfutshane nezilula.
3. **Linda.** Yinike imizuzwana emininzi. Ukuba akukho nto ivelayo, nika iziphelo ezimbini okanye ezintathu, esinye sazo sichanekile.
4. **Vuyela nayiphi na impendulo.** "Ewe, yiyo leyo" okanye "Bendingazange ndicinge ngaloo nto" igcina izinto zinobubele.
5. **Thetha ngesaci.** Buza ukuba sithetha ntoni kuye, okanye sivela phi. Apha kulapho kukho ixabiso lokwenyani.
6. **Misa ngaphambi kokuba adinwe.** Izaci ezimbalwa nokuhleka yiseshoni epheleleyo.

## Ukunika ukhetho ngaphandle kokuvavanya

Iziphelo zokhetho ezininzi zingenza umdlalo ube lula, kodwa zingavakala njengovavanyo ukuba zinikezwa njengomceli-mngeni. Gcina ithoni ilula. Yithi "Ungathanda ukuva isiphelo okanye ukhethe esinye?" kunokuba uthi "Yeyiphi?" Nika ukhetho emva kokuma, hayi ngaphambi kokuba umntu abe nethuba lokuphendula.

Ukuba umntu uxhalabile okanye unentloni, shiya umdlalo uqhubeke. [Iingcebiso zethu zonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) zichaza indlela yokutshintsha ngaphandle kokwenza ingxolo.

## Ukukhetha izaci ezifanele umntu

- **Sebenzisa izaci zomntu ngokwakhe.** Buza usapho ukuba umzali okanye umlingane wayehlala ethini. Izaci zobuntwana zihlala zilula kakhulu.
- **Khetha izaci ezimfutshane, zemihla ngemihla.** Amabinzana ngemozulu, ikhitshi, igadi, usapho namaxesha onyaka ayaqhelekile kwaye alula ukuwalandela.
- **Gcina izinto zicacile.** Izaci ezixhomekeke kumagama amadala okanye kwisifundo zingadida. Zigcine abantu abazonwabelayo.
- **Khangela ulwimi.** Iindinyana ezininzi azihambi nolwimi. Sebenzisa izaci azaziyo umntu ngolwimi lwakhe, nokuba kufuneka uthi uzifumane ezintsha.

## Izaci ngokwemixholo

Umzekelo ngamnye ongezantsi ubonisa isiphelo ngaphakathi kwamakhonkco kuwe. Funda kuphela inxalenye yokuqala, uze ulinde.

- **Imozulu:** "Imvula ngaphambi kweyesixhenxe..." (isiphelo: "ihle ngeyeshumi elinanye"); "Ilifu ngalinye linayo..." (ucingo olungqwabalala).
- **Ikhitshi nokutya:** "Abapheki abaninzi kakhulu..." (bonakalisa umhluzi); "Musa ukubala amantshontsho..." (phambi kokuba aphume).
- **Ikhaya nosapho:** "Ikhaya yindawo apho intliziyo..." (iyiyo); "Igazi limhlophe ngaphezu..." (kwamanzi).
- **Amaxesha onyaka negadi:** "Imvula yeApreli izisa..." (iintyatyambo zeMeyi); "Uvuna into oyihlwayo..." (oyihlwayo).

## Kuthekani ngomntu ongakwaziyo ukuphendula?

Abanye abantu abakwazi kufumana magama konke kwizigaba zokugqibela. Musa ukuyeka isaci. Sifunde, uncume, uze umeme impendulo ngesimbo, ukunqwala, okanye ngokukhetha umfanekiso. Ukwabelana ngesigqi esaziwayo kusengumzuzu owabelwanayo. Hlanganisa umdlalo nomculo, isiselo esifudumeleyo, okanye ukuxuba izandla, njengakwi-[misebenzi yethu yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ezinye iindlela zokusebenzisa izaci

Izaci ziholela kakuhle kwingxoxo ngexesha elidlulileyo lomntu. Ungamema iintsomi ngemibuzo evela kwi-[imibuzo yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), okanye udlale umdlalo ohambelana nawo onje nge-[conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/). Ukuze ufumane ezinye iingcamango imini yonke, jonga [imisebenzi yabantu abaneedementia](/activities/ideas/).
