Esi sikhokelo seso sabalawuli bemisebenzi, amaqela okhathalelo lwememori nabakhokeli beenkqubo zemini zabantu abadala. Sithetha ngeendlela zeqela ezisebenzayo, ukulungiswa kwegumbi, ukulungelelanisa umsebenzi omnye kwizakhono ezixubeneyo, nokusebenzisa amakhadi ethu ebhingo anokuprintwa nemidlalo yesikrini esikhulu. Yonke into apha injolise kwintuthuzelo, unxibelelwano nokhetho. Akukho nto apha engovavanyo.

## Yintoni eyenza umsebenzi weqela usebenze kukhathalelo lwememori?

Umsebenzi weqela olungileyo uqhelekile, mfutshane, ulula ukungenelela kuwo nakuwuphi na umgangatho kwaye awunaxinzelelo. I-[Dementia Care Practice Recommendations](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1a0020aa-0bfb-47ac-a545-1dd8a3ee1444/alzheimers-dementia-care-practice-recommendations.pdf) ye-Alzheimer's Association ichaza ukubandakanyeka okunentsingiselo njengento exhasa umdla neemfuno zomntu kwaye ivumela ukhetho nempumelelo, nasekubeni i-dementia inzima kakhulu. Obo busuku busithi iinkqubo zakudala zidla ngokuxhomekeka kwimisitho emikhulu yeqela emnye okanye emibini ngemini apho abahlali badla ngokuba ngabangabandakanyekanga ngenkuthalo.

Oku kukhomba kwimigaqo embalwa esebenzayo:

- **Okuqhelekileyo kuphumelela kunokutsha.** Iingoma, amaqhalo, imifanekiso nezinto zeminyaka yobutsha yabahlali zilula ukungenelela kuzo.
- **Okufutshane kuphumelela kunokude.** Cwangcisa ixesha elilinganayo nolwazi iqela lakho elidla ngokulubonisa, kunye nendawo yokuyeka yemvelo embindini.
- **Ukwenza kuphumelela kunokubukela.** Nika umntu ngamnye into yokubamba, yokuchukumisa, yokukhetha okanye yokuthetha.
- **Amanqanaba amaninzi, umsebenzi omnye.** Yakha iindlela ezilula neziqinileyo zokuthatha inxaxheba.
- **Akukho baphumeleleyo, akukho manqaku.** Iindlela zokhuphiswano zishiya abanye bevakalelwa kukuba bahlulekile.

## Likhulu kangakanani ifuneka libe iqela?

Elincinane lidla ngokuba lula. I-Alzheimer's Association iyaqaphela ukuba umntu ofumanisa ukuba umbutho omkhulu uyamxinzelela usenokunxibelelana ngempumelelo ngakumbi eqeleni elincinane. Akukho nani linye elichanekileyo. Kuxhomekeke kubahlali, kwigumbi nakwinani labasebenzi neevolontiya ezikhoyo.

Uvavanyo oluluncedo: ngaba umntu ngamnye angakubona ubuso bomkhokeli, aveke ngokucacileyo kwaye afikelelwe ngumncedisi kwisithuba esimbalwa seesekhondi? Ukuba hayi, ahlulahlule iqela, yongeza abancedisi, okanye uye kwigumbi elincinane.

Kumsitho omkhulu, onjengekonsathi yeholide, hlalisa abasebenzi neevolontiya phakathi kwabahlali, hayi ecaleni kweendonga.

## Ndilungisa njani igumbi nokuhlala?

- **Umbuthano okanye imilo yesihlangu sehashe.** Iingcebiso zenkqubo zichaza umbuthano wokwabelana njengolona lungileyo kwindawo enkulu ngokwaneleyo ukuba wonke umntu ahlale emthanjeni, ngaphandle kwengxolo engasemva.
- **Umkhokeli kufutshane kwaye emgangathweni wamehlo.** Hlala endaweni yokuma xa unako.
- **Ukuva nokubona kuqala.** Hlalisa abantu abanokuva okuncinane kufutshane nomkhokeli, ecaleni labo elingcono. Qinisekisa ukuba iiglasi nezixhobo zokuviwa zinxitywe. I-Alzheimer's Association iyaqaphela ukuba iingxaki zokubona nokuva zinokwenza unxibelelwano lube nzima.
- **Ukukhanya kusuka ngasemva kwabahlali, hayi ngasemva kwakho.** Phepha ukuhlalisa nabani na ejongene nefestile eqaqambileyo.
- **Cima umabonwakude nerediyo.** I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukususa ingxolo ekhuphisanayo ngexesha lemisebenzi yomculo.
- **Incoko enye ngexesha.** Ukuthetha ngaxeshanye kunzima kubantu abaphila ne-dementia nabantu abanokuva okuncinane, njengoko i-Alzheimer's Association ibonisa kwi-[holiday guidance](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) yayo.
- **Iindlela ezicacileyo zokuphuma nekona ethe cwaka.** Nabani na ofuna ukumka kufuneka akwazi ukwenza njalo ngokulula, kunye nelungu lesiphaluka lokuhamba naye.

## Imibono yemisebenzi yeqela

### Ibhingo yemifanekiso

Ibhingo yemifanekiso ithatha indawo yeenombolo ngemifanekiso ecacileyo, enjengeteyipoti, inja okanye irosi, ukuze kungabikho mntu ukufuneka alandele inombolo ende okanye afunde ukushicilela okuncinane.

**Indlela yokuyiqhuba:**

1. Printa ikhadi elinye ngomntu ngamnye kwiphepha lethu le-[Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) usebenzisa iqhosha lokuprinta. Iphepha eliqinileyo lilula ukulisingatha. Lakhe ngeplastiki ukuze ulisebenzise kwakhona.
2. Nika iimaka ezinkulu, ezilula ukuzithatha, ezifana neetshiphusi zepoker, iziciko zebhotile okanye izikwere zekhadibhodi ezisikiweyo.
3. Sebenzisa umbizi wesikrini kumabonwakude okanye kwiprojekta ukuze wonke umntu abone umfanekiso njengoko uthetha igama lawo.
4. Nqumama emva komfanekiso ngamnye uze umeme isimvo esifutshane: “Ngaba kukho umntu onokusixelela ngenja ayaziyo?” okanye “Uqaphela ntoni ngalo?”
5. Qhubeka ubiza de wonke ikhadi lizaliswe. Wonke umntu uyagqiba.

**Ukuyilungelelanisa:** Sebenzisa izikwere ezimbalwa kubantu abafumanisa ukuba ikhadi elipheleleyo likhuphela kakhulu. Umncedisi unokukhomba umfanekiso ohambelanayo komntu ofuna oko. Abanye abantu bangakhetha ukubamba ikhadi lomfanekiso lombizi baze balibonise eqeleni.

### Ukucula kunye

Umculo ngomnye wemisebenzi yeqela eyonwabelwa kakhulu. I-Alzheimer's Association iyaqaphela ukuba nakwinqanaba lokugqibela, umntu usenokukwazi [ukubetha isingqisho okanye ukucula ingoma yobuntwana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music).

**Indlela yokuyiqhuba:**

- Khetha iingoma abahlali abakhulele kuzo, hayi ezintandokazi zomkhokeli. Buza iintsapho ukuba umntu ngamnye wayethanda ntoni.
- Sebenzisa umculo ongenayo imibhengezo, i-Association ithi inokudida.
- Printa amazwi eengoma ngoonobumba abakhulu kwabo bafundayo, kodwa ungakufuni.
- Nika izishekeshe, iingqoma zezandla okanye izikhafu zokuzigcagcela.
- Khuthaza ukubetha izandla nokushukuma, kwaye ugcine ivolyum ithuthuzelekile.

**Ukuyilungelelanisa:** Abanye abantu bacula onke amazwi, abanye bavuyisa ngokuvuma, abanye babetha unyawo, kwaye abanye bamamela nje. Zonke ezi zibalwa. Jonga i-[music activities guide](/activities/music/) yethu ukufumana okungakumbi.

### Amaqela okukhumbula

Iqela lokukhumbula likhenkceza ngomxholo, onjengeentsuku zesikolo, iimoto zokuqala, iikhitshi okanye ihlobo, kusetyenziswa iifoto, izinto nomculo. Injongo yincoko emnandi, hayi ukukhumbula ngokuchanekileyo.

**Indlela yokuyiqhuba:**

1. Khetha umxholo omnye uze uzise izinto ezimbalwa abantu abanokuzibamba: umxokozeli wesandla, iglavu yebhola, umnqwazi, uludwe lembewu.
2. Dlulisa into enye ngexesha. Buza imibuzo evulekileyo: “Ndixelele ngale nto,” “Uqaphela ntoni?” “Ngubani owasebenzisa into enjengale?”
3. Amkela yonke impendulo. Musa ukulungisa umhla okanye inyaniso.
4. Khokela incoko ukuya kwiinkumbulo ezifudumeleyo. Iingcebiso zenkqubo zithi abahlinzeki bokhathalelo kufuneka bakhokele ukukhumbula ukuya kwiinkumbulo ezintle kunezo ezinokubangela ukukhathazeka.

I-[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) inikela ngamakhadi emifanekiso anokuprintwa anezacelo ezivulekileyo ngomxholo asebenza kakuhle njengabaqalisi bencoko. Izimvo ezingakumbi zikwi-[reminiscence guide](/activities/reminiscence/) yethu.

### Gqibezela ikhalimo

Amaqhalo aqhelekileyo (“Umntu ngumntu nga…”) amema wonke umntu ukuba abize isiphelo kunye. Bonisa i-[Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) kwiscreen esikhulu uvumele iqela liphendule ngelizwi elinye. Kukho isacelo sokuthetha emva kweqhalo ngalinye. Ukuba akukho mntu uthetha isiphelo, sibonise uqhubeke. Akukho lungileyo okanye ukungalunganga.

### Ukufunwa kwamagama ngoonobumba abakhulu, kunye

Projekta i-[Word Search](/games/word-search/) enoonobumba abakhulu kwiscreen kwaye nifune amagama njengeqela, okanye uprinte iikopi zabo bonwabela ukusebenza bebodwa etafileni. Amagama aya ecaleni kuphela naphantsi.

### Intshukumo ethambileyo

Ukolula kwisitulo, ukudlulisa ibhola ethambileyo okanye ibhaluni, okanye ukudanisa esitulweni kumculo oqhelekileyo. Bonisa intshukumo nganye kunokuyichaza.

### Ukohlela nokuhambelanisa etafileni

Iitafile ezincinane zabantu abathathu okanye abane zingohlela imifanekiso, iziqwenga zendwangu okanye izitya zesitshetshe kunye. Kwiscreen, i-[Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ingadlalwa kunye nemifanekiso ishiyiwe ibonakala, kunye nomntu omnye echukumisa ngelixa itafile ithetha ngomfanekiso ngamnye.

## Ndiyisebenzisa njani imidlalo yenu kwiscreen esikhulu?

Imidlalo yethu isebenza kwibrawuza yewebhu, ngoko unokuyisebenzisa kumabonwakude okanye kwiprojekta ngaphandle kokufaka nantoni na.

1. Vula umdlalo kwilaptop okanye kwitablet exhunywe kwiscreen ngocingo okanye ngokukhanyisa.
2. Yenza ibrawuza ibe yiscreen esipheleleyo kwaye useke isandi kumgangatho othuthuzelekileyo.
3. Yiba nomkhokeli omnye owenza ukuchukumisa ngelixa iqela libiza ukhetho. Okanye dlulisa itablet kumhlali ofuna ithuba.
4. Yeka nanini xa iqela likulungele. Imidlalo ayinaxesha, manqaku okanye ukugqiba ngenkani.

I-[Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/), i-[Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/), i-[Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ne-[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) zidla ngokufanela amaqela kakhulu. Jonga zonke iindlela kwi-[games page](/games/) yethu.

## Ndilungelelanisa njani umsebenzi omnye kwizakhono ezixubeneyo?

Cwangcisa ubuncinane iindlela ezintathu zokungenelela kumsebenzi ngamnye.

| Umsebenzi | Ozimeleyo ngakumbi | Inkxaso ethile | Inkxaso eninzi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ibhingo yemifanekiso | Ufumana aze agqume imifanekiso yakhe | Umncedisi ukhomba; umntu ugqumela | Ubamba ikhadi lomfanekiso; uyamamela aze ayibukele |
| Ukucula kunye | Uyacula, ukhetha ingoma elandelayo | Uyavuma, ubetha izandla, usebenzisa isishekeshe | Uyamamela, ubamba isikhafu, ubetha isandla |
| Ukukhumbula | Uxela amabali | Uphendula ewe-hayi okanye ukhomba | Ubamba aziva into |
| Ukufunwa kwamagama | Usebenza ikopi yakhe eprintiweyo | Ufumana amagama kwiscreen esikhulu kunye nomncedisi | Uyamamela njengoko amagama efundwa |

Kubahlali abakwinqanaba lokugqibela, i-NHS iyaqaphela ukuba imisebenzi idla ngokufuna ukwenziwa lula nokugxila ngakumbi kwizivamvo, ezifana nomculo, izinto zokuchukumisa nokuxovulwa kwesandla. Umzuzu womntu omnye ngexesha okanye emva kweqela usenokubafanela ngcono kunokuba neqela ngokwalo. Jonga i-[late-stage activities](/activities/late-stage/).

## Abakhokeli bafanele bathethe njani ngexesha leqela?

- Zazise ngalo lonke ixesha, kwaye usebenzise igama lomntu ngamnye alithandayo.
- Thetha ngokucothayo, nika inyathelo linye ngexesha, kwaye ubonise phambi kokuba uxele.
- Buza izimemo ezivulekileyo (“Ndixelele ngoku...”) okanye ukhetho olulula, ungabuzi imibuzo yovavanyo (“Uyakhumbula...?”).
- Musa ukulungisa. Bulela abantu ngomnikelo wabo wonke.

Okungakumbi ngale nto kukwi-[communication tips](/caregiving/communication-tips/) yethu.

## Ukugqiba kakuhle, nokuvumela abantu bemke

Ukuyeka ngumphumo omhle. Jonga iimpawu zokuba umntu uyadinwa, uyaxhalaba okanye ukhathazekile, kwaye umncedisi anike uhambo okanye isitulo esithe cwaka kwenye indawo. Umntu ozelayo ngexesha lokucula usenokuba ethuthuzelekile nje.

Vala yonke iqela ngendlela enye, ngengoma eqhelekileyo okanye ngombulelo olula, ukuze isiphelo sivakale sizolile nesilindelekileyo. Emva koko, bhala oko kusebenzileyo nokuba ngubani wonwabele ntoni. Loo manqaku enza iseshoni elandelayo ibe ngcono.
