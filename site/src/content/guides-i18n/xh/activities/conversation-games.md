Abantu abaninzi bafuna umdlalo abangawudlala kunye ofana nencoko, hayi uvavanyo. Imibuzo ngamashumi amabini nemidlalo efana nayo yokuqikelela ingasebenza kakuhle, kuba imithetho yayo ilula kwaye incoko inokuya nakuphi na. Into ebalulekileyo kukuguqula iindima ezijwayelekileyo: umntu uthatha inxaxheba yokucinga impendulo, wena uqikelela. Eli phepha lichaza indlela yokudlala ezi midlalo ngobubele, kunye neemidlalo yencoko ezifanele abantu abane-dementia.

## Kutheni kuguqulwa imithetho eqhelekileyo?

Kwivavanyo eqhelekileyo, omnye ubuza kwaye omnye kufuneka azi. Oko kubeka umntu one-dementia kwindawo apho anokusilela, kwaye ukusilela kunokumkhathaza. Xa ekhetha into kwaye wena uqikelela, uba ngumcebi. Ubamba impendulo, ugqiba xa aza kuthi ewe okanye hayi, kwaye anokonwabela ukukujonga uzama.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) icebisa abanakekeli ukuba basebenzise iisentensi ezimfutshane nezilula, umbuzo omnye ngexesha, nelizwi elithambileyo nelinamonde. Ezo zinto zilungele imidlalo yokuqikelela.

## Indlela yokudlala imibuzo ngamashumi amabini ngobubele

1. **Khetha umxholo** ovela ebomini bomntu, njengekhitshi, igadi, izilwanyana okanye ulwandle.
2. **Vumela bacinge into** kwaye bayithethe ngelizwi elithambileyo, okanye bonise ikhadi lomfanekiso ukuze ungafuni ukuyiva.
3. **Buza umbuzo omnye ngexesha, owewu okanye owahayi.** “Ingaba yinto oyityayo?” “Ingaba ikhulu kunesipato?”
4. **Yamkela nayiphi na impendulo.** Ukuncuma, ukuhlwitha intloko, okanye ukuhleka, konke kubalwa.
5. **Qikelela ngelizwi elikhulu** kwaye wonwabele. Ukuba uqikelele kakuhle, bhiyozelani kunye.
6. **Nika izikhokelo** xa umntu ebonakala engasokwazi: “Kukho into ekhitshini.”
7. **Ungabali imibuzo** kwaye ungagcini manqaku.

Ukuba umntu akakwazi ukulandela imithetho, tshintshela kwinguqulelo elula: umbhalele izinto ezimbalwa, uze akhombe enye elungileyo.

## Zeziphi izinto ezilungileyo zokukhetha

- Isiqhamo, imifuno okanye iintyatyambo ezikuthandayo
- Isilwanyana sepulazi, isilwanyana sezoo okanye isilwanyana sasekhaya abasigcinayo
- Isixhobo, njengesando, irhawu okanye inaliti yokuthunga
- Into yefanitshala okanye yasekhaya
- Indawo abayaziyo kakuhle, njengepaki okanye idolophu
- Into yempahla abebeyithanda ukuyinxiba

Gwema amagama abantu, nto enokubangela isijwili, kunye nezinto ezinokuba yingozi okanye ezilula ukudideka ngazo.

## Imidlalo yencoko eyongezelelweyo

- **Eli okanye elo.** Nika izikhetho ezimbini ubuze ukuba ukhetha yiphi: iti okanye ikofu, izintaba okanye ulwandle, ingubo ebomvu okanye eluhlaza. Akukho mpendulo ilungileyo.
- **Ungakhetha ntoni.** Buza umbuzo olulwelwayo: “Ungakhetha ipikiniki okanye ukutya ngaphandle kwekhaya?”
- **Gqiba isaci.** Isiqendu esaziwayo ngokungagqitywanga. Zama [imidlalo yokugqiba isaci](/activities/finish-the-saying/) ukuze ufumane isikhokelo.
- **Thetha ngendawo.** Cela bachaze igumbi, idolophu okanye umbono abawuthandayo, kwaye ubacele ukuba nawe ucinge ngawo.
- **Imibuzo evulekileyo namakhadi.** [Amakhadi encoko](/games/conversation-cards/) anika imibuzo evulekileyo ngemixholo, anokuprintwa etafileni.

Ukuze ufumane iingcamango ezithe ngqo kwinkumbulo nakubomi, bona [imibuzo yenkumbulo](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Ukuze ufumane umdlalo wemibuzo onobubele, osebenzisa ukhetho ngaphandle kokubala amanqaku, bona [imibuzo elula yeqhinga kubantu abadala abane-dementia](/activities/trivia/).

## Xa umntu ekhathazeka?

Yima, unwebe, uthi, “Masidlale into enye.” Ukukhathazeka luphawu lokutshintsha umdlalo, hayi lokungcikiva. Isikhokelo sethu malunga [nendlela yokudlala imidlalo nomntu one-dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) sichaza indlela yokutshintsha imithetho ngokukhawuleza.

## Yintoni imidlalo enokuyenza nengenakuyenza

Imidlalo yencoko inomnandi kwaye inoluntu. Inokunceda abantu baxhumane kwaye babelane ngamabali. Ayiyonyango lwe-dementia, ayinciphisi isantya sayo, kwaye ayithintelwa. Gcina injongo ilula: ixesha elifudumeleyo kunye.
