Iintsapho ezininzi zifuna umdlalo wokudlala kunye ovakala njengengxoxo, hayi uvavanyo. Imibuzo engamashumi amabini nemidlalo efanayo yokuqikelela inokusebenza kakuhle, kuba imithetho ilula kwaye ingxoxo inokuya naphi na. Ubuchule kukuguqula indima eqhelekileyo: umntu ucinga impendulo, wena ubuza. Eli phepha lichaza indlela yokuqhuba le midlalo ngobunono, nemidlalo yengxoxo efanelekileyo kubantu abaneedementia.

## Kutheni siguqula imithetho eqhelekileyo?

Kuvavanyo oluqhelekileyo, omnye ubuza omnye kufuneka azi. Oko kubeka umntu one-dementia kwindawo apho anokusilela, nokusilela kunokucaphukisa. Xa yena ekhetha into wena uqikelela, yena yingcali. Ubambe impendulo, unyula ixesha lokuthi ewe okanye hayi, kwaye unokonwabela ukukubukela uzama.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) ikhuthaza abanakekeli ukuba basebenzise izivakalisi ezimfutshane nezilula, umbuzo omnye ngexesha, nethoni ezolileyo nenomonde. Ezo mikhwa ifanelana kakuhle nemidlalo yokuqikelela.

## Indlela yokudlala imibuzo engamashumi amabini, ngobunono

1. **Khetha umxholo** ovela ebomini bomntu, onje ngekhitshi, igadi, izilwanyana okanye unxweme.
2. **Mvumele acinge into** ayithethe ngokuthe cwaka, okanye abonise ikhadi lomfanekiso ukuze ungadingi ukuyiva.
3. **Buza umbuzo omnye ophendulwa ngokuthi ewe okanye hayi ngexesha.** "Yinto oyidlayo?" "Inkulu kunesihlangu?"
4. **Yamkela nayiphi na impendulo.** Ukunqwala, ukunyakazisa intloko, okanye ukuhleka konke kuyabala.
5. **Qikelela ngokuvakalayo** uze unandipheke. Ukuba uqikelele ngokuchanekileyo, nivuyiseni kunye.
6. **Nika izalathiso** xa umntu ebonakala etshixekile: "Yinto ekhitshini."
7. **Ungaze ubale imibuzo** kwaye ungaze ugcine iskori.

Ukuba umntu akakwazi ukulandela imithetho, tshintshela kwingxelo elula: wena uthi amagama ambalwa yena alathise elililo.

## Izinto ezilungileyo zokukhetha

- Isiqhamo, imifuno okanye intyatyambo athanda kakhulu
- Isilwanyana esivela emfama, kwindawo yezilwanyana okanye esaphila naso
- Isixhobo, njengesando, i-rake okanye inalidi yokuthunga
- Ifanitshala okanye into yasekhaya
- Indawo ayaziyo kakuhle, njengepaki okanye idolophu
- Isambatho athanda ukusinxiba

Phepha amagama abantu, nantoni na enokubangela usizi, nezinto ezinokuba yingozi okanye ezidityaniswa lula.

## Eminye imidlalo yengxoxo

- **Le okanye leya.** Nika iindlela ezimbini ubuze ukuba ikhetha eyiphi: iti okanye ikofu, iintaba okanye ulwandle, ilokhwe ebomvu okanye eblowu. Akukho mpendulo echanekileyo.
- **Ungakhetha ntoni.** Buza umbuzo omnene, olula: "Ungakhetha ukuba nepikniki okanye ukutya ngaphandle?"
- **Gqibezela isaci.** Intetho eyaziwayo ngesiphelo esishiyiweyo. Zama [imidlalo yethu yokugqibezela isaci](/activities/finish-the-saying/) ukuze ufumane isikhokelo.
- **Thiya indawo.** Mcele achaze igumbi, idolophu okanye imbonakalo athanda kuyo, ummemele ukuba nawe uyicinge.
- **Amakhadi emibuzo evulekileyo.** [Amakhadi ethu engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) anika imibuzo evulekileyo ngokwemixholo, enokushicilelwa etafileni.

Ukuze ufumane ezinye iingcamango ezitsala kwimemori nasebomini bomntu, jonga [imibuzo yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Ukuze ufumane ukonwaba kwezovavanyo okwenziwa ngobunono, jonga [imibuzo elula yabantu abadala abaneedementia](/activities/trivia/), esebenzisa ukhetho kwaye ingenaskori.

## Kuthekani ukuba umntu uyacaphuka?

Misa, uncume uthi into enjengale, "Masidlale enye into." Ukucaphuka luphawu lokutshintsha umdlalo, hayi lokutyhala ngakumbi. Isikhokelo sethu ku-[indlela yokudlala imidlalo nomntu one-dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) sichaza indlela yokutshintsha imithetho ngokukhawuleza.

## Into emidlalo enokuyenza nengenako ukuyenza

Imidlalo yengxoxo iyonwabisa kwaye inobudlelwane. Ingabanceda abantu ukuba baqhagamshelane babelane ngeentsomi. Ayiyonyangi idementia, ayiyibambezeli, kwaye ayiyithinteli. Gcina injongo ilula: ixesha elifudumeleyo kunye.
