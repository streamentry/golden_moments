I-bingo ngumdlalo oqhelekileyo nonobudlelwane kubantu abadala abaninzi, kodwa amanani anokuba nzima ukuwalandela kumntu ophila nedementia. I-bingo yemifanekiso igcina isigqi esifanayo esithambileyo ize itshintshe amanani ngemifanekiso emikhulu, ecacileyo. Isebenza ekhaya, kwiprogram yemini, okanye kwikhaya lokunakekela. Esi sikhokelo sichaza indlela yokudlala, indlela yokuyivumelanisa, nendlela yokugcina ugxininiso kuvuyo.

## Indlela esebenza ngayo i-bingo yemifanekiso

Umdlali ngamnye unekhadi elinegridi yemifanekiso, njengeteapot, ikati, irozi, isikhephe okanye ibhayisikile. Umlawuli ubonisa okanye ebiza umfanekiso omnye ngexesha. Abadlali baphawula umfanekiso ohambelana kwikhadi labo. Umdlali ophawule umqolo opheleleyo, okanye iseti evunyelwene ngayo liqela, angabiza "Bingo," kodwa amaqela amaninzi akuyeki ngokupheleleyo ukuphumelela.

Ungasebenzisa amakhadi ashicilelwayo kwi-[imisebenzi yethu eshicilelwayo yasimahla](/activities/printable/), okanye i-[picture bingo game](/games/picture-bingo/), equka umlawuli wesikrini weqela. Zombini zisebenza kakuhle kwitablet ephakanyiswe ukuze lonke iqela libone.

## Ukulungiselela iseshoni

- **Khetha iqela elincinci.** Abantu abane ukuya kwabasibhozo ngobungakanani obulungileyo. Amaqela amakhulu anzima ukuwalandela kwaye anokuvakala ekhawulezisa.
- **Yenza imifanekiso ibe mikhulu.** Sebenzisa imigca eqaqambileyo nento enye eyaziwayo kwisikwere ngasinye. Phepha amakhadi axineneyo.
- **Beka abantu kakuhle.** Beka umdlali ngamnye apho anokubona umlawuli nekhadi lakhe, kufutshane nomncedisi ukuba uyamfuna.
- **Sebenzisa iimpawu ezilula.** Iitshipsi ezinkulu, iingqekembe, okanye ukuchukumisa ngomnwe kusebenza ngcono kunamapensile. Gcina ezimbalwa eziseleyo.
- **Cwangcisa iseshoni emfutshane.** Imizuzu engamashumi amathathu idla ngokwanela. Misa kwangoko ukuba amandla ayehla.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ikhuthaza ukuvumelanisa imisebenzi noko umntu asenako ukukwenza, ngoko yenza amakhadi nemithetho ibe lula nanini na xa oko kunceda.

## Indlela yokubiza imifanekiso

1. **Phakamisa umfanekiso** okanye ulathise kuwo kwisikrini phambi kokuba uthethe.
2. **Yibize igama ngokucacileyo,** ngamagama alula: "Iteapot." Liphinde kanye, ngokucotha.
3. **Linda.** Nika abantu ixesha lokujonga nokuphawula. Ungabizi umfanekiso olandelayo de uninzi lwabantu lube nethuba.
4. **Chaza, ukuba kuyanceda.** "Intyatyambo ebomvu" okanye "leyo sasinayo kwipikniki" inokuvusa ingxoxo.
5. **Gcina isantya sithambile.** Umlawuli oqinisekileyo obalulekileyo ngaphezu komkhawulezayo.

## Ukwenza ukuphumelela kube kukhetho

Ukhuphiswano lunokuba yingxaki. Umntu oyilahlekelayo usenokuziva evavanywa, kwaye umntu onexhala lokuphumelela usenokuba nexhala. Ezi ziindlela ezinye:

- **Wonke umntu uphumelela umfanekiso.** Umdlali ngamnye ubiza umfanekiso omnye awuthandayo, kwaye iqela liyawuncoma.
- **Yabelana ngekhadi eligcweleyo.** Cela abadlali ukuba bathethe into ngomfanekiso omnye kwikhadi labo.
- **Dlalani ude umfanekiso uphele.** Biza yonke imifanekiso kwikhadi, uze uqale elinye, ngaphandle kokubiza ophumelele kuqala.
- **Gcina umvuzo mncinci okanye uwutsibe.** Ibhiskithi okanye ingoma isebenza ngcono kunekhaphu.

## Ukuvumelanisa kubantu abasezigabeni zokugqibela

Kubantu abafumana ukufumana umfanekiso kunzima, sebenzisa ikhadi elinezikwere ezine okanye ezintandathu kuphela, okanye unike umdlali ikhadi elinye ngexesha. Mvumele umncedisi abambe ikhadi ngoxa umntu elathisa, okanye uhambise itshipsi ngobunono ngemvume yakhe. Abanye abantu kulula ukuthi bamamele umlawuli bonwabele isandi samagama.

Qaphela imiqondiso yokungakhululeki, enjengokuhlehla okanye ukujuza ibhatyi, uze umise ukuba iyavela. Ukuze ufumane ezinye iingcamango ngemisebenzi ethe cwaka, yokuhlala, jonga [imisebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/).

## I-Bingo kumakhaya okunakekela nakwiiprogram zemini

Imisebenzi yeqela isebenza ngcono xa icwangciswe ngokwabantu abakwigumbi. [Isikhokelo sethu semisebenzi yeqela yamakhaya okunakekela neeprogram zemini](/activities/care-home-groups/) sigubungela indlela yokucwangcisa iseshoni, ukubeka abantu, nokubaphatha abo bafuna ukuhamba. I-bingo yemifanekiso ihambelana kakuhle nokucula okanye intsomi, nge-[finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) njengomdlalo onokuthula ngakumbi kamva kwiseshoni.

## Into i-bingo enokuyenza nengenako ukuyenza

I-bingo yemifanekiso inobudlelwane kwaye iyonwabisa. Inokudibanisa abantu, inike isizathu sokujonga imifanekiso nokumamela, yenze iqela livakale lifudumele. Ayinyangi idementia kwaye ayibambezeli inkqubo yayo. Linganisa impumelelo yayo ngendlela abantu ababonakala ngayo nabaziva ngayo kwiyure, hayi ngokuba ngubani ophumeleleyo.
