I-bingo ngumdlalo owaziwayo nonentlalo kwabaninzi abadala, kodwa amanani anokunzima ukulandela kumntu one-dementia. I-bingo yemifanekiso igcina imvakalelo efanayo yokuhamba kancinane, kodwa ithatha amanani ibe ngimifanekiso emikhulu necacileyo. Isebenza ekhaya, kwiprogram yemini, nakwindlu yonakalo. Eli nqaku lichaza indlela yokudlala, indlela yokuyilungelelanisa, nendlela yokugcina ingqwalasela ekonwabeni.

## Indlela i-bingo yemifanekiso esebenza ngayo

Umdlali ngamnye unekhadi elinegridi yemifanekiso, njengetoti yeti, ikati, irozi, iboti yesela okanye ibhayisikile. Umbizi ubonisa okanye abize umfanekiso omnye ngexesha. Abadlali baphawula umfanekiso ofanayo ekhadini labo. Umdlali ophawula umgca opheleleyo, okanye iseti evumelaniswe ngayo iqela, anokumemeza “Bingo,” kodwa amaqela amaninzi ayitsiba le nxaxheba yokuwina ngokupheleleyo.

Ungasebenzisa amakhadi aprintiweyo kwi[simisebenzi yesiprinti yasimahla](/activities/printable/) ethu, okanye [umdlalo we-bingo yemifanekiso](/games/picture-bingo/), oquka umbizi kwiskrini weqela. Zombini zisebenza kakuhle xa ithebhulethi ibanjiwe ukuze iqela lonke libone.

## Ukuseta iseshoni

- **Khetha iqela elincinane.** Abantu abane ukuya kwabasibhozo bayinani elifanelekileyo. Amaqela amakhulu anzima ukulandela kwaye anokuvakala ngathi akhawulezisiwe.
- **Yenza imifanekiso mikhulu.** Sebenzisa imigca ebanzi kunye nento enye eyaziwayo kwisikwere ngasinye. Gwema amakhadi anzima.
- **Hlala abantu kakuhle.** Beka umdlali ngamnye apho akwazi ukubona umbizi nekhadi lakhe, kwaye ufumaneke nomncedi ukuba kukho imfuneko.
- **Sebenzisa izixhobo zokuphawula ezilula.** Izinto ezinkulu, iikhowuni okanye ukuthinta ngomnwe kusebenza ngcono kuneepensile. Gcina ezingaphezulu ezimbalwa.
- **Cwangcisa iseshoni emfutshane.** Imizuzu engamashumi amathathu ihlala yaneleyo. Yeka kwangethuba ukuba amandla ehla.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) icebisa ukulungelelanisa imisebenzi kwinto umntu asakwazi ukuyenza, ngoko yenza amakhadi nemithetho ilula xa oko kunceda.

## Indlela yokubiza imifanekiso

1. **Phakamisa umfanekiso** okanye ubonise kwiskrini phambi kokuba uthethe.
2. **Yibize ngokucacileyo,** ngamagama alula: “Itoti yeti.” Phinda kanye, ngokuzolileyo.
3. **Linda.** Nika abantu ixesha lokujonga nokuphawula. Ungabizi omnye umfanekiso de uninzi lube nethuba.
4. **Chaza, ukuba kuyanceda.** “Irozi ebomvu” okanye “leyo yasepikniki esasiyithethayo” ingavusa incoko.
5. **Gcina isantya siphakathi.** Umbizi ozolileyo ubalulekile ngaphezu komntu okhawulezayo.

## Ukwenza ukuwina kube kukhetho

Ukukhuphisana kunokuba yingxaki. Umntu olahlekayo angazizwa evavanywa, kwaye umntu onexhala ngokuwina angazizwa ekhathazekile. Zezi ezinye iindlela:

- **Wonke umntu uwina umfanekiso.** Umdlali ngamnye ubiza umfanekiso omnye awuthandayo, kwaye iqela liyawuncoma.
- **Yabelana ngekhadi elipheleleyo.** Cela abadlali bathethe into malunga nomfanekiso omnye ekhadini labo.
- **Dlala de kuphele imifanekiso.** Biza yonke imifanekiso ekhadini, uze uqale enye, ngaphandle kokubeka ilungelo lomphumeleli wokuqala.
- **Gcina isipho sincinci okanye ungasibeki.** Ikuki okanye iculo lisebenza ngcono kunetrofi.

## Ukulungelelanisa kubantu basesigxeni sokugqibela

Kubantu abafumana kunzima ukufumana umfanekiso, sebenzisa ikhadi elinesikwere esine okanye esithandathu, okanye nika umdlali ngamnye ikhadi elinye ngexesha. Vumela umncedi abambe ikhadi ngelixa umntu ebonisa, okanye ahambise ngobumnene isiqwenga ngemvume yakhe. Abanye abantu bafumana kulula ukuphulaphula umbizi kuphela kwaye bonwabele izandi zamagama.

Jonga iimpawu zokungonwabi, njengokutsala umva okanye ukuqubisa ubuso, kwaye yeka xa ziveleyo. Ukuze ufumane iingcamango zemisebenzi ethe cwaka, ehleli phantsi, bona [imisebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/).

## I-bingo ezindlwini zonakalo nakwiiprogram zemini

Imisebenzi yeqela isebenza kakuhle xa icwangciswe ngokujikelezwe ngabantu begumbi. [Isikhokelo sethu seemisebenzi yeqela zezindlu zonakalo neeprogram zemini](/activities/care-home-groups/) siquka indlela yokucwangcisa iseshoni, ukubeka abantu, nokumelana nabantu abafuna ukuhamba. I-bingo yemifanekiso ikwahambisana kakuhle nengoma okanye ibali, kunye ne[gqiba isaci](/activities/finish-the-saying/) njengomdlalo othe cwaka kamva kwiseshoni.

## Yintoni i-bingo enokuyenza nengenakuyenza

I-bingo yemifanekiso ngumdlalo onentlalo nomnandi. Inokudibanisa abantu, inike isizathu sokujonga imifanekiso nokuphulaphula, kwaye yenze iqela livakale lifudumele. Ayiyonyangi i-dementia okanye ayinciphisi indlela yayo. Vavanya impumelelo yayo ngendlela abantu ababonakala ngayo kwaye bava ngayo ngexesha leyure, hayi ngokuba ngubani onqobayo.
