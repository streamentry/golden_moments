Iiprojekthi zobugcisa yindlela entle yokuchitha ixesha nomntu one-dementia. Zisebenzisa izandla, amehlo neemvakalelo, kwaye zinika into ongathetha ngayo. Eli phepha linika iiprojekthi ezithile ezinamanyathelo alula, kunye nesikhokelo sokukhetha izixhobo ngokukhuselekileyo. Malunga neenkokeli zobugcisa babantu abadala, ezinje ngokuphepha izinto zabantwana, jonga [ubugcisa nezinto zezandla zabantu abadala abaneedementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Indlela yokuqala nayiphi na iprojekthi yobugcisa

1. **Lungiselela ngaphambi kokuqala.** Beka izixhobo ezinye okanye ezibini, ishiti ekhuselayo nelaphu elimanzi.
2. **Bonisa inyathelo lokuqala.** Cwifa ibrashi, yaza umfanekiso wokuqala, okanye uxinzelele ibhulukhwe encinci yobumba, uze ummemele ukuba enze okufanayo.
3. **Sebenzani macala.** Gcina iprojekthi yakho ecaleni ukuze umsebenzi uvakale wabelwanwe, hayi ukhokelwe.
4. **Thethani ngaloo nto niyibonayo.** Chaza imibala, oyilo neemilo kunokuba uhlulele umsebenzi.
5. **Misani ngaphambi kokuba amandla ehle.** Imizuzu elishumi ukuya kuthi ga kwamashumi amabini ihlala ilixesha elifanelekileyo.

I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ithi ubugcisa yindlela yokubonisa imvakalelo nokuyila ngaphandle kokufuna ubuchule bobugcisa. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ikhuthaza ukwahlula imisebenzi ibe ngamanyathelo amancinci alawulekayo.

## Upeyinti wamanzi

Sebenzisa iphepha elinzima lepeyinti yamanzi, ibrashi enkuluethambileyo nemibala embalwa kwiikomityi ezingenzulu. Manzisa iphepha ngamanzi acocekileyo, uze umvumele athontsize umbala kulo. Beka iphepha ngokuthe tyaba ukuze imibala ibaleke. Isiphumo ngumfanekiso othambileyo, ongenasimo, ongafuni cebo.

- **Isigaba sokuqala:** Mvumele akhethe imibala aze apeyinte umhlaba.
- **Isigaba esiphakathi:** Mnike imibala emibini okanye emithathu wenze wena ukumanzisa.
- **Isigaba sokugqibela:** Bamba iphepha ngoxa eyithinta ngesiponji.

## Ukunamathelisa imifanekiso yeemagazini

Qokelela amaphepha eemagazini anezithombe eziqaqambileyo zeentyatyambo, ukutya, izilwanyana, iimoto okanye uhambo. Yaza imifanekiso ibe ziziqwenga ezinkulu, kulula kunokusika, uze uyinamathelise kwikhadibhodi. Ukunamathelisa kusebenza kakuhle njengesiqalo sengxoxo, kuba umfanekiso ngamnye ungakhokelela kwimfanelo. Kufanelekile kumntu othanda ukuhlela nokukhetha.

## Ubumba obuthambileyo

Ubumba obomisa emoyeni okanye ubumba lokwenza iimbonakalo obuthambileyo bunokutyabulwa, bujikwe kwaye buxinzelelwe. Yenza isitya esilula okanye intsimbi etyaba. Ubumba lubanga ukuchukumisa ngamandla, ngoko lunokuthomalalisa abantu abathanda ukubamba izinto. Gcina iziqwenga zinkulu, uze ubalekele izixhobo ezineziphelo ezibukhali.

## Ii-pastel zetshokhwe ephepheni elimnyama

Iphepha elimnyama lenza imibala ye-pastel ikhazimle. Sebenzisa ii-pastel ezinkulu ezityebileyo uvumele umntu enze imidweliso ebanzi okanye ayixube ngomnwe okanye ngetishu. Ii-pastel zenza uthuli, ngoko lungiselela kwitreyi uze ucime izandla emva koko. Khetha ii-pastel ezithambileyo ezibhalwe ukuba azinabungozi.

## Ukushicilela ngamagqabi

Qokelela amagqabi amakhulu egadini, ubrashe upeyinti ogxothekayo emithanjeni, uze utyabe igqabi ephepheni. Likhuphe ngononophelo uze uvuyele isishicilelo. Le projekthi iqhagamshelana kakuhle ne-[misebenzi yangaphandle nokulima](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), kwaye ingaqala ngokuhamba ukuqokelela amagqabi.

## Ukucwangcisa ilaphu neentyatyambo

Hlela iziqwenga zelaphu ngokweemvakalelo zokuchukumisa, okanye cwangcisa iintyatyambo ezintsha ezimbalwa kwivazi. Ezi ziiprojekthi ezichukumisa imvakalelo ezinoxinzelelo oluncinci kakhulu. Zikwafanelekile nakubantu abasezigabeni zokugqibela. Ukuze ufumane ezinye iingcamango, jonga [imisebenzi echukumisa imvakalelo yabantu abaneedementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Ukukhetha izixhobo zobugcisa

Izixhobo zibaluleke ngakumbi ekukhuseleni nasekonwabeni kunasemgangathweni. Xa ukhetha, cinga ngale:

- **Ezingenabungozi nezinokuhlanjwa:** Khangela amalebhile athi azinabungozi, uze ukhangele ukuba iipeyinti ziyahlanjwa.
- **Izibambo ezinkulu:** Iikhreyoni ezinkulu, iibrashi ezityebileyo nee-pastel ezinkulu kulula ukuzibamba.
- **Akukho malungu amancinci:** Phepha iibhidi, iiqhosha, ii-sequin ne-glitter ekhululekileyo enokuginyiswa.
- **Akukho zixhobo zibukhali:** Sebenzisa izikere zokhuseleko okanye ayaze iphepha ngesandla.
- **Ukungavani komzimba:** Khangela i-latex, nakuphi na ukuvela kwezikhumba okwaziwayo.
- **Umahluko:** Imibala eqaqambileyo kwiphepha elimhlophe elicocekileyo okanye elimnyama kulula ukuyibona.
- **Uluhlu olufutshane:** Izixhobo ezimbini okanye ezintathu zanele. Ukhetho oluninzi lunokudiniwa.
- **Ukucoca okulula:** Sebenzisa itreyi, ishiti yeplastiki nelaphu elimanzi, ukuze ukungcola kungabi sisizathu sokumisa.

## Ukuvumelanisa ngokwesigaba

Kwisigaba sokuqala, uninzi lwabantu lunokucwangcisa lugqibezele iprojekthi ngoncedo oluncinci. Kwisigaba esiphakathi, nika ukhetho oluncinci nesikhokelo esingakumbi. Kwisigaba sokugqibela, jonga ngakumbi iinkqubo ezichukumisa imvakalelo ezinje ngokuxinzelela ubumba okanye ukuthinta ipeyinti ngesiponji, uvuye ngomsebenzi kunokuba uvuyele ukugqitywa.

Ukuze ufumane ezinye iingcamango ngokwesigaba, jonga [isigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/), [isigaba esiphakathi](/activities/middle-stage/) kunye ne-[misebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/). Ukuba umntu uyala okanye uyadanduluka, isikhokelo sethu ku-[xa umntu esithi hayi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) sichaza indlela yokuhlehla.
