Iiprojekthi zobugcisa ngumsebenzi onomvuzo wokuchitha ixesha nomntu one-dementia. Zisebenzisa izandla, amehlo nezivakalisi, zibe nento yokuthetha ngayo. Eli phepha linika iiprojekthi ezithile ngamanyathelo alula, kunye nesikhokelo sokukhetha izixhobo ngokhuseleko. Ukuze ufumane iimigaqo zobugcisa obufanelekileyo kubantu abadala, njengokugwema izixhobo ezibonakala zingeabantwana, bona [ubugcisa nezakhono zabantu abadala abane-dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Indlela yokuqala nayiphi na iprojekthi yobugcisa

1. **Lungiselela phambi kokuqala.** Beka izixhobo ezimbini okanye ezintathu, ishiti elikhusela, nelaphu elinamanzi.
2. **Bonisa inyathelo lokuqala.** Cwina ibrashi, caphula isiqwenga sokuqala, okanye cinezela umqulu omncinci wobumba, kwaye bamemele ukuba benze okufanayo.
3. **Sebenza ecaleni.** Gcina iprojekthi yakho ikufuphi ukuze umsebenzi uziveke njengento yokwabelana, hayi ukujongwa.
4. **Thetha ngento oyibonayo.** Chaza imibala, izinto ezichukumisayo nezimo, endaweni yokugweba umsebenzi.
5. **Yima phambi kokuba amandla ehle.** Imizuzu elishumi ukuya kwamashumi amabini ihlala ilungile.

I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) iqaphela ukuba ubugcisa yindlela yokuvakalisa imvakalelo nokuba nobuchule ngaphandle kokufuna isakhono sobugcisa. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) icebisa ukwahlula imisebenzi ibe ngamanyathelo amancinci alawulekayo.

## Ukupeyinta ngemibala yamanzi

Sebenzisa iphepha elinzima lemibala yamanzi, ibrashi enkulu ethambileyo, kunye nemibala embalwa kwiindebe ezincinci. Manzisa iphepha ngamanzi acocekileyo, emva koko uvumele bawise imibala kulo. Tyekeza iphepha ngobunono ukuze imibala ihambe. Isiphumo sisifanekiso esithambileyo, esingacwangciswanga, esingafuni cwangciso.

- **Esigxeni sokuqala:** Bavumele bakhethe imibala bapeyinte umhlaba.
- **Esigxeni esiphakathi:** Nika imibala emibini okanye emithathu, wena ubamanzise iphepha.
- **Esigxeni sokugqibela:** Bamba iphepha ngelixa bedoba ngesiponji.

## Ikolaji yephepha lemagazini

Qokelela amaphepha emagazini anemifanekiso eqaqambileyo yeentyatyambo, ukutya, izilwanyana, iimoto okanye ukuhamba. Caphula imifanekiso ibe ziqwenga ezikhulu, kulula ukuyicaphula kunokuyisika, kwaye uyinamathisele kwiphepha elomeleleyo. Ikolaji isebenza kakuhle njengesiqalisi sencoko, kuba umfanekiso ngamnye unokukhokelela kwinkumbulo. Ilungele umntu othanda ukuhlela nokukhetha.

## Ubumba obuthambileyo

Ubumba obomileyo emoyeni okanye ubumba obuthambileyo bokumodela bunokucinezelwa, buqengqelwe, bucinezelwe. Yenza isitya esilula okanye ithayile ethe tyaba. Ubumba buzizwa kakhulu ngesandla, ngoko bunokutyhilisa abantu abathanda ukubamba izinto. Gcina iziqwenga zikhulu, kwaye ugweme izixhobo ezinamaphuma abukhali.

## Iipastel zetshoki kwiphepha elimnyama

Iphepha elimnyama lenza imibala ye-pastel ibe nkanyiso. Sebenzisa iipastel ezinkulu nezinqindi, uvumele umntu enze imigca ebanzi okanye axube ngomnwe okanye ngetishu. Iipastel zinothuli, ngoko zibeke kwitreyi kwaye uhlambe izandla emva koko. Khetha iipastel ezithambileyo ezibhalwe ukuba azinamakhemikhali.

## Ukuprinta ngamagqabi

Qokelela amagqabi amakhulu egadini, uthambise ipeyinti ehlanjwayo kwimithambo yegqabi, uze ucinezele igqabi phezu kwephepha. Liphakamise ngobunono, kwaye ubukele ukuprinta. Le projekthi idibana kakuhle [nemisebenzi yangaphandle nokulima](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), kwaye inokuqala ngokuhamba ukuqokelela amagqabi.

## Ukuhlela iindwangu neentyatyambo

Hlela iisampulu zelaphu ezinobunzima obahlukeneyo ngokuziva, okanye lungisa iintyatyambo ezimbalwa ezintsha embizeni. Ezi zi-projekthi zemvakalelo ezinoxinzelelo oluncinci. Zikwafanele abantu besigxeno sokugqibela. Ukuze ufumane ezinye iingcamango, bona [imisebenzi yemvakalelo yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Ukukhetha izixhobo zobugcisa

Izixhobo zibaluleke ngakumbi ekhuselekeni nasekulula kunasekuhleni kwazo. Xa ukhetha, cinga ngezi:

- **Ingenamakhemikhali nehlanjwayo:** Jonga amalebhile athi azinamakhemikhali kwaye uqinisekise ukuba iipeyinti ziyahlanjwa.
- **Izibambelelo ezinkulu:** Iikrayoni ezinkulu, iibrashi ezijiyileyo kunye neepastel ezinkulu zilula ukubamba.
- **Akukho macandelo amancinci:** Gwema iqhosha, iintsimbi, izinkonzo nomgubo weglitter ongathi utyiwe.
- **Akukho zixhobo ezibukhali:** Sebenzisa izikere ezikhuselekileyo okanye caphula iphepha ngesandla.
- **Ukungxamla:** Hlola ukuba akukho latex, nakwiimpawu zesikhumba ezaziwayo.
- **Ukwahlukana kwemibala:** Imibala eqaqambileyo kwiphepha elimhlophe okanye elimnyama iyabonakala lula.
- **Uluhlu olufutshane:** Izixhobo ezimbini okanye ezintathu zanele. Ukhetho olunyulu lunokuthi lumoyikise.
- **Ukucoca lula:** Sebenzisa itreyi, ishiti leplastiki, kunye nelaphu elinamanzi, ukuze ukungcola kungabi sisizathu sokuyeka.

## Ukulungelelanisa ngesigaba

Esigxeni sokuqala, uninzi lwabantu lunokucwangcisa kwaye lugqibe iprojekthi ngoncedo oluncinci. Esigxeni esiphakathi, nika ukhetho oluncinane kunye nokhokelo oluninzi. Esigxeni sokugqibela, gxininisa kwiinkqubo zemvakalelo ezifana nokucinezela ubumba okanye ukuthambisa ipeyinti ngesiponji, kwaye uvuyele isiphumo endaweni yokugqibela.

Ukuze ufumane iingcebiso ezithile ngesigaba, bona [imisebenzi yesigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/), [imisebenzi yesigaba esiphakathi](/activities/middle-stage/) kunye [nemisebenzi yesigaba sokugqibela](/activities/late-stage/). Ukuba umntu uyala okanye uqumba, isikhokelo sethu se[xa umntu ethi hayi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) sichaza indlela yokuthatha umgama.
