Ubugcisa nemisebenzi yezandla zinika abantu abaphila ne-dementia indlela yokuzibonakalisa engaxhomekekanga ekufumaneni amazwi alungileyo. I-Alzheimer's Association iyaqaphela ukuba iiprojekthi zobugcisa zinokuzisa [intliziyo yempumelelo nenjongo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) nethuba lokuzibonakalisa, kumnonopheli nakumntu one-dementia. Isitshixo kukugcina izinto zezabantu abadala, zilula kwaye zingenaxinzelelo. Eli phepha linika iiprojekthi ezithile, indlela yokuzilungiselela nendlela yokuzilungelelanisa xa izinto zitshintsha.

## Yintoni eyenza umsebenzi wobugcisa ube weyabantu abadala hayi weyabantwana?

Umsebenzi wobugcisa wabantu abadala usebenzisa izinto zokwenyani, izihloko zabantu abadala nethoni enentlonipho. I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukugcina iiprojekthi kumgangatho wabantu abadala nokuphepha nantoni na enokubonakala ingahloniphi okanye injengeyabantwana.

| Ivakala njengeyabantwana | Ivakala njengeyabantu abadala |
| --- | --- |
| Iglitha, izitikha zefowuni, amehlo angqukuva | Iphepha lepeyinti yamanzi, indwangu yengubo, iintyatyambo ezicudisiweyo |
| Amaphepha okukhulisa eekhathuni | Amaphepha okukhulisa eengadi, amaqhekeza engubo, iimoto okanye imihlaba |
| Ukuncoma “Umsebenzi omhle” kuphawu ngalunye | Incoko yokwenyani: “Obo bumnyama obuluhlaza buhle ecaleni kobuhlaza” |
| Umsebenzi ongenamsebenzi | Ikhadi lomhlobo, into emiswa embindini wetafile, isipho |
| Imiyalelo efundwa njengesifundo | Isimemo: “Ungandinceda ngale nto?” |

Cinga ngencasa yomntu ngokwakhe. Umntu owayethanda ukuthungela angakonwabela ukubamba indwangu. Injineli ethathe umhlala-phantsi isenokukhetha iseti yemodeli enamacandelo amakhulu okanye ukohlela izixhobo kwiprojekthi. Utitshala wangaphambili usenokonwabela ukwenza amakhadi.

## Kutheni kufuneka sigxile kwinkqubo hayi kwimveliso?

Kuba ixesha enikunye yinjongo. Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association se-[activities at home](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/5337a7c3-15c6-4b4d-a3a0-ca00a0839a6b/alzheimers-dementia-activities-at-home-ts.pdf) sithi gxila kwinkqubo, hayi kwisiphumo. Upeyinti “obuhlaza okwesibhakabhaka nje” usengumfanekiso opeyintiweyo. Ikholaji enemifanekiso emithathu isengumsebenzi wekholaji.

I-Association iyaqaphela nokuba igcisa lobugcisa lisenokukhathazeka ngenxa yotshintsho emsebenzini walo, ngelixa umntu ongengomgcisi anokonwabela ithuba elitsha lokuzibonakalisa. Ukuba umntu wayeyigcisa elikrelekrele, zama indlela eyahlukileyo kuleyo awayeyiqeqeshele, ukuze ivakale njengomdlalo hayi uthelekiso.

## Imibono yobugcisa nemisebenzi yezandla onokuyizama

Umbono ngamnye uquka indlela yokuwenza ube lula ukuba uba nzima.

1. **Ukuhlamba ngepeyinti yamanzi.** Manzisa iphepha lepeyinti yamanzi eliqinileyo uvumele umntu abeke umbala kulo ngebrashi enkulu. Imibala iyasasazeka ngokwayo, ngoko akukho siphumo sibi. *Kulula:* nika imibala emibini kuphela, okanye upeyinte ngamanzi kwiphepha elimnyama elibonisa iimpawu ezimanzi.
2. **Ikholaji yemagazini.** Sika kwangaphambili imifanekiso kwimagazini zokulima, zokupheka, iimoto, uhambo okanye imidlalo yakudala yomntu. Mvumele akhethe kwaye anamathelise imifanekiso kwikhadibhodi. *Kulula:* nika imifanekiso emine esikiweyo nentonga yegulu, kwaye ninamathelise kunye.
3. **Ukucwangcisa iintyatyambo.** I-Alzheimer's Foundation of America icebisa [ukucwangcisa iintyatyambo ezisikiweyo nemifuno emiselweyo](https://alzfdn.org/dementia-activity-gardening/) kwimiboniso elula yexesha lonyaka. Sebenzisa ivasi enzima, ezinzileyo kunye neziqu esele zinqunyiwe. *Kulula:* beka iziqu ezimbalwa kwindebe encinane, enye ngexesha.
4. **Amakhadi eentyatyambo ezicudisiweyo.** Cudisa iintyatyambo ezivela kwibhokisi kwiimbumba zencwadi iveki, uze uzinamathelise kwikhadibhodi etyonkiweyo. *Kulula:* ubeka igulu wena, yena acudisa intyatyambo ezantsi.
5. **Amaphepha okukhulisa abantu abadala.** Khetha amaphepha anemiqolo ecacileyo nezihloko zokwenyani. [Amaphepha ethu okukhulisa](/games/coloring/) angaprintwa nawo angakhuliswa kwiskrini ngokuchukumisa umbala, uze uchukumise inxalenye yomfanekiso. *Kulula:* iindawo ezinkulu, imibala embalwa, iipeni ezinkulu.
6. **Ubumba okanye intlama ethambileyo.** Qengqeleka, cinezela kwaye wenze ubume ngobumba bokuomisa emoyeni okanye intlama yokwenza iimodeli ethambileyo. Ukucinezela igqabi ebumbeni kwenza ithayile elula yomfanekiso. *Kulula:* ukuxovula nokuqengqeleka nje ngumsebenzi opheleleyo. Qaphela ukuba umntu akabeki bumba emlonyeni.
7. **Ukwenza amakhadi.** I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukulibonisa njengomsebenzi wokwenyani: “Siyathumela ikhadi lokuphila kwindwendwe ethile. Ungandinceda?” Ikhadibhodi esikiweyo kwangaphambili nezitikha ezimbalwa zeentyatyambo okanye izitampu zisebenza kakuhle.
8. **Ukwenza incwadi yokugcina.** Nibeke iikopi zeefoto zakudala, iipostkhadi okanye iindawo zamathikithi kumaphepha e-albhamu kunye. Thethani ngalinye ngokuhamba. Jonga i-[reminiscence guide](/activities/reminiscence/) yethu ukufumana iindlela ezithambileyo zokuthetha ngexesha elidlulileyo.
9. **Iibhodi zentsimbi nokuchukumisa.** Namathelisa iziqwenga zekhodurhoyi, ivelvethi, ibhegi yejute nesilika kwikhadibhodi eqinileyo. Ukuyenza ngumsebenzi; ukuyichukumisa kamva ngomnye. Izimvo ezingakumbi zikwi-[sensory activities guide](/activities/sensory/) yethu.
10. **Ukugqiba umthi ngobulula.** Kumntu owayethanda ukusebenza ngomthi, ukucola ibloko yomthi egudileyo okanye ukupeyinta iseti yendlu yeentaka kunokuvakala kuqhelekile. Susa izixhobo ezibukhali kwaye ujonge.
11. **Ukupeyinta kunye nomculo.** Beka irekhodi eyintandokazi uze upeyinte imilo okanye imibala ehambelana nemo. Oku kudibanisa imisebenzi emibini abantu abaninzi abayonwabelayo. Jonga i-[music activities](/activities/music/).

## Ndilungiselela njani iseshoni yobugcisa?

1. **Lungiselela kuqala.** Sika, nqumla kwaye uhlele yonke into phambi kokumema umntu. Ukulinda ngelixa ukhangela izikere kunokwenza ulahlekelwe lixesha elifanelekileyo.
2. **Yigcine incinane.** Imibala emibini okanye emithathu, ibrashi enye, iphepha elinye. Ukhetho olunyuluke kakhulu lunokuxinzelela.
3. **Khusela itafile.** Ilaphu lepulasitiki okanye ishiti endala kuthetha ukuba ukungcola akuyiyo inkxalabo.
4. **Hlalani icala ngecala.** Sebenza umsebenzi wakho kufutshane nowakhe. Kuvakala kwabelwana, hayi kukhokelwa.
5. **Mncede aqale.** I-Alzheimer's Association iyaqaphela ukuba usenokufuna ukuqala intshukumo yebrashi kunye nomntu. Xa kuqalile, abantu abaninzi baqhubeka.
6. **Thetha ngelixa usebenza.** Gxeka imibala neentsimbi, okanye uvumele ubugcisa bukhokelele kwibali. Phepha imibuzo yohlobo lovavanyo enjengethi “Yintoni le?”
7. **Vumela ixesha elininzi.** Iprojekthi ayifuni ukugqitywa ngokuhlala kanye.

## Ndiyilungelelanisa njani imisebenzi yobugcisa xa i-dementia itshintsha?

| Inqanaba | Oko kudla ngokusebenza | Oko kufuneka kulungiswe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lokuqala | Iiklasi, ukukhulisa ngeenkcukacha, iincwadi zokugcina, ukwenza amakhadi, ukupeyinta kwifoto | Nika ukhetho lweeprojekthi; yigcine ineentlalo |
| Eliphakathi | Ikholaji esikiweyo kwangaphambili, ukuhlamba ngepeyinti yamanzi, ukucwangcisa iintyatyambo, ukukhulisa iindawo ezinkulu | Inyathelo linye ngexesha; wena wenza ukusika nokunamathelisa |
| Lokugqibela | Imigca emikhulu yebrashi, ukuxovula ubumba, ukuchukumisa indwangu, ukukubukela upeyinta | Gxila kwizivamvo; hola isandla ngobunono ukuba kuyamkelekile |

I-NHS icebisa ukuba kumanqanaba amva, imisebenzi idla ngokwenziwa lula kwaye igxile kwizivamvo. Ukubukela omnye upeyinta, nokuthetha ngemibala, yindlela yokwenyani yokuthatha inxaxheba. Ukufumana izimvo ezingakumbi ngenqanaba ngalinye, jonga i-[activities for every stage](/activities/ideas/).

## Ukhuseleko ngezixhobo zobugcisa

- Khetha iipeyinti, iipeni namagulu angenabungozi.
- Gcina izikere, imela zobugcisa neegulu ezishushu kude nesandla. Yenza nokusika wena, kwangaphambili.
- Qaphela nabani na obeka ubumba, ipeyinti okanye izinto ezincinane emlonyeni.
- Sebenzisa iziqulatho ezizinzileyo, ezinzima zamanzi neevasi.

## Siyeka nini?

Yeka xa ingqalelo isuka okanye ukuphelelwa ngumonde kubonakala. I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuphelisa okanye ukutshintsha umsebenzi xa ixesha lengqalelo licutha okanye ukuphelelwa ngumonde kukhula. Beka iprojekthi kwindawo ekhuselekileyo kwaye unike into ephumlisayo. Unokubuyela kuyo ngomso, okanye ungabuyeli. Omabini kulungile.

## Bonisa umsebenzi, ukuba umntu uyafuna

Ukubethelela upeyinti kwifriji okanye ukubeka ikholaji kwisakhelo kunokuba yindlela ethe cwaka yokubonisa intlonipho. Buza kuqala. Abanye abantu bayakuthanda ukubona umsebenzi wabo eludongeni; abanye bangakhetha ukungawubonisi.
