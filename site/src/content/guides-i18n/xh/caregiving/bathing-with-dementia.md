Ukuhlamba yinto yobumfihlo. Kumntu ohlala nedementia, ukuhlamba nge-shawa okanye ebhafini kunokubonakala kubanda, kuveze, kudideke okanye kungakhuseli. Qala ngokubuza ukuba yintoni eya kwenza umsebenzi ube lula, emva koko ulungiselele indawo phambi kokunikela uncedo. Injongo kukuxhasa inkqubo yomntu ngokwakhe, hayi ukuguqula ukungavumi kube yimbambano.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokuhlamba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/bathing) siphawula ukuba ukufumana uncedo kumsebenzi osondele kakhulu emzimbeni kunokuba nzima. [Icebiso le-Dementia Australia malunga nokucoceka](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/hygiene) licebisa ukulandela izikhethelo zomntu, ukusebenzisa amanyathelo alulayo kunye nokubuza ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso afuna. Abantu bayahluka; zama kuphela utshintsho olufanelekile kulo mntu.

## Ndilungiselela njani phambi kokunikela uncedo?

Buza ngenkqubo yomntu ejwayelekileyo. Ngaba uthanda ibhafu okanye i-shawa, ekuseni okanye ngokuhlwa, uncedo olusuka kumntu othile, okanye ukuhlamba amanye amacala ngokwakhe? Landela izikhethelo ezaziwayo xa kunokwenzeka. Ukuba efuna ukhetho, nikela ezimbini ezicacileyo, njengokuthi “Ngaba ufuna i-shawa ngoku okanye emva kwesidlo sasekuseni?”

Phambi kokumema igumbi lokuhlambela:

- **Lungiselela izixhobo kuqala.** Beka iitawuli, iimpahla ezicocekileyo, isepha, ishampu kunye nelaphu lokuhlamba ngolandelelwano oluya kusetyenziswa ngalo. Oku kuthintela ukumshiya yedwa xa usenza ukukhangela izinto.
- **Yenza indawo ibe nethuthuzelo.** Hlola ukuba ifudumele ngokwaneleyo, ukhanyise indawo, uvale umnyango okanye iikhethini, kwaye ugcine umgangatho uomile ngangokunokwenzeka.
- **Hlola amanzi.** Lingisa ubushushu phambi kokuba umntu angene kwibhafu okanye kwi-shawa. Ungathembeli ekutheni angaqonda ukuba amanzi ashushu kakhulu.
- **Lungiselela izixhobo zokukhusela ubumfihlo.** Ityhawula enkulu, isambatho okanye ikumbi lebhafu linokunceda umntu ahlale egqunyiwe ngexesha lokuhamba phakathi kwamanyathelo.

Isikhokelo sethu [sokhuseleko ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sinolwazi olungakumbi malunga nokukhanya kwegumbi lokuhlambela, imiphezulu enokuzelela, kunye nokubuza ingcali eyaziyo malunga nezixhobo zokubamba okanye isitulo se-shawa.

## Ndingamncedisa njani ngaphandle kokuthatha yonke into?

Cela imvume ngaphambi kokumchukumisa okanye ukumncedisa kwindawo yakhe yobuqu. Chaza into oza kuyenza ngesivakalisi esifutshane, emva koko yima ulinde impendulo. Isignali elula efana nokuthi “Nantsi ilaphu lokuhlamba” icace ngaphezu kohlu olude lwemiyalelo.

Mvumele enze kuphela iinxalenye anokuzihlamba kwaye azifunayo. Ungamgquma ngetawuli kwaye ukhusele kuphela indawo ehlanjwayo. Ukuba ufuna, mnike ilaphu lokuhlamba okanye isepha ukuba abambe. Umntu usenokufuna ixesha elongezelelekileyo ukuqala okanye ukugqiba; yima endaweni yokukhawuleza kumanyathelo.

Ukuba umntu uyaphazamiseka, uyazihlutha okanye uthi hayi, yima. Mnike indawo kwaye uzame ukuqonda into engamkhathazayo. Igumbi lusenokubanda, amanzi asenokumvisa ngokungaqhelekanga, okanye umntu usenokufuna omnye umncedisi. Umncedisi amthandayo unokuba lula ukuba umntu wakhetha yena. Musa ukusebenzisa ukumangaliswa, ukuphoqa okanye uncedo lwemzimba ukugqiba ukuhlamba.

## Ndingazigcina njani iindlela eziqhelekileyo?

Gcina ixesha eliqhelekileyo emini kunye nolandelelwano lwamanyathelo xa esebenza kakuhle. Beka isepha kunye neetawuli ezaziwayo apho alindele khona, kwaye ulungiselele iimpahla ezicocekileyo ngaphambi kokuqala. Chaza inyathelo elinye ngexesha; ukuba isiyalelo esithethwayo asicacanga, bonisa ilaphu lokuhlamba okanye ubonise into elandelayo endaweni yokuphinda isichazo side. Ukuba ukuhlamba iinwele kubangela ukuxhalaba, yenze ngokwahlukileyo okanye yishiye enye imini. Ezi zinto ezincinci zinokwenza umsebenzi ube lula ukulandela ngaphandle kokuthatha indima yomntu.

## Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba ibhafu okanye i-shawa ipheleleyo iyinzima?

Ukuhlamba ngokupheleleyo akuyiyo kuphela indlela yokumnceda ajongeke ehlambekile. Ukuba umntu uzolile ngayo, zama ukuhlamba esityeni, usebenzisa ilaphu elifudumeleyo okanye uhlambe inxalenye enye ngexesha. Ukuhlamba iinwele kunokwenzeka omnye umhla ukuba yeyona nxalenye aziyikhathaleyo. Mgquma kwaye umbandakanye, kwaye yima ukuba nale ndlela ikhathaza.

Ukuba umntu alale ngokungavumi namhlanje, mvume kwaye uzame kwakamva. Ukuhlamba ngokuthula kwangomso kungcono kunokuzama ngoku ukwoyikisa. Isikhokelo sokuphendula xa umntu ethi hayi [sinezindlela ezininzi zokunikela uncedo ngaphandle koxinzelelo](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

## Ndingayenza njani indawo yokuhlambela ikhuseleke ngakumbi?

Gcina indlela eya ebhafini okanye kwi-shawa icace kwaye usebenzise izixhasi ezifanelekileyo kwiimfuno zomntu. Iindawo ezingazelelwayo, isitulo esizinzileyo okanye izixhobo zokubamba zinokunceda, kodwa izixhobo zifuna ukubekwa ngendlela efanelekileyo. Buza ingcali yezoqeqesho lomsebenzi okanye enye ingcali enelungelo ngaphambi kokufaka izixhobo zokubamba okanye ukwenza utshintsho lwesakhiwo. Musa ukuzisa isifudumezi esiphathekayo egumbini lokuhlambela, kwaye ulandele imiyalelo yanaliphi na isixhobo sombane.

Ukuba umntu akazinzanga okanye ewile, musa ukuzama ukumphakamisa okanye ukumhambisa ngendlela enokulimaza nina bobabini. Funa icebiso lobuchwephesha malunga nesixhobo kunye nolwaneleko olufunekayo. Gcina nasiphi na isicwangciso sokhuseleko esisekiweyo sihambelana nelungelo lomntu lobumfihlo nokhetho lwakhe.

## Nini ekufuneka ndicele icebiso lengcali?

Thetha nengcali yezempilo ukuba ukuhlamba kuba nzima ngenxa yobuhlungu obutsha, ukuwa, ingxaki yesikhumba okanye utshintsho kwikhono lomntu lokuzihlambela ngokhuseleko. Udokotela wamazinyo, udokotela, umongikazi okanye ingcali yezoqeqesho lomsebenzi banokunceda ngengxaki ethile. Ungasebenzisi ikhokelo jikelele ye-intanethi ukuthatha isigqibo sokuba umntu ufuna uncedo lwezandla ngokuthe ngqo.

Ukuba umntu akakwazi ukuhlamba kangangexesha elithile, musa ukumbeka ityala. Buza ukuba yeyiphi inxalenye enzima kakhulu kwaye ukuba utshintsho olulula lunokunceda. Umkhuba oqhelekileyo, ilaphu lokuhlamba okanye uncedo lomntu omthembayo lusenokwaneleka; ukuba akwaneli, buza iqela lonakekelo ngenkxaso.
