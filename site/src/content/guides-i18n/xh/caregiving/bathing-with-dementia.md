Ukuhlamba kuyimfihlo. Kumntu ophila ne-dementia, ishawa okanye ibhafu inokuvakala ibanda, ityhila umzimba, ididekisa okanye ingakhuselekanga. Qala ngokubuza ukuba yintoni enokwenza umsebenzi ube lula, uze ulungise igumbi phambi kokunikela uncedo. Injongo kukuxhasa umkhwa womntu, ungawenzi ukwala kube yingxabano.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokuhlamba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/bathing) siphawula ukuba ukufumana uncedo kumsebenzi wobuqu kunokuvakala kungathandeki. [Icebiso le-Dementia Australia ngococeko](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/hygiene) licebisa ukulandela oko umntu akuthandayo, ukusebenzisa amanyathelo alula nokubuza ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso afuna yona. Abantu bayahluka; zama kuphela utshintsho olufanelekileyo kulo mntu.

## Ndilungiselela njani phambi kokunikela uncedo?

Buza ngomkhwa womntu oqhelekileyo. Ingaba uthanda ishawa okanye ibhafu, ekuseni okanye ngokuhlwa, uncedo lomntu othile, okanye ukuhlamba ezinye iindawo yedwa? Landela izinto eziqhelekileyo apho unako. Ukuba ufuna ukhetho, nika ukhetho oluthathu lucace, njengokuthi “Ungathanda ishawa ngoku okanye emva kwesidlo sakusasa?”

Phambi kokummema kwigumbi lokuhlamba:

- **Qokelela izinto kuqala.** Beka iitawuli, impahla ehlambulukileyo, isepha, ishampu nelaphu lokuhlamba ngokulandelelana kokuzisebenzisa. Oku kuphepha ukushiya umntu yedwa ngoxa ufuna izinto.
- **Yenza igumbi lifudumale.** Jonga ukuba lifudumele ngokwaneleyo, cwebisa indawo, vala umnyango okanye iindonga zeefestile, uze ugcine umgangatho womile kangangoko.
- **Jonga amanzi.** Vavanya ubushushu phambi kokuba umntu angene kwibhafu okanye kwishawa. Musa ukuthembela kukwazi kwakhe ukuthi amanzi atshisa kakhulu.
- **Lungiselela izinto zobumfihlo.** Itawuli enkulu, ingubo yokuhlamba okanye ingubo yebhafu inokunceda umntu ahlale ebhaliwe ngexesha eshukuma phakathi kwamanyathelo.

[Isikhokelo sethu sokhuseleko lwasekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sinolwazi olungakumbi ngokukhanya kwigumbi lokuhlamba, iindawo ezibhuqayo nokubuza ingcali efanelekileyo ngezibambo zokubamba okanye isitulo seshawa.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngaphandle kokuthatha yonke into?

Cela imvume phambi kokuchukumisa umntu okanye ukuncedisa kwindawo yobuqu. Cacisa oko uzakukwenza ngezwi elifutshane, uze umise ukulinda impendulo. Isalathiso esizolileyo, njengokuthi “Nalu ilaphu lokuhlamba”, sicace ngaphezu koluhlu olude lwemiyalelo.

Mvumele ahlambe iindawo anokukwazi nanqwenelayo ukuzihlamba. Ungagcina itawuli kuye uze ubonise kuphela indawo ehlanjwayo. Ukuba uyathanda, mnike ilaphu lokuhlamba okanye isepha ukuba ayibambe. Umntu unokufuna ixesha elingakumbi ukuqala okanye ukugqiba; linda ungakhawuleziswa kumanyathelo.

Ukuba umntu uyaxhalabisa, uyatsalela umva okanye uthi hayi, yeka. Mnike indawo uzame ukuqonda ukuba yintoni engonwabisiyo. Igumbi lingabanda, amanzi angavakala engaqhelekanga, okanye umntu angafuna omnye umncedi. Umntu owaziwayo unokuvakala elula ukuba umntu umkhethile. Musa ukusebenzisa isiphithiphithi, amandla okanye ukukhokela ngomzimba ukuze ugqibe ukuhlamba.

## Ndingayigcina njani into eqhelekileyo?

Gcina ixesha losuku elisetyenzwayo nokulandelelana kwamanyathelo xa isebenza kakuhle. Beka isepha neetawuli eziqhelekileyo apho umntu azilindele khona, uze ulungise impahla ehlambulukileyo phambi kokuqala. Cacisa inyathelo elinye ngexesha; ukuba umyalelo ongathethwayo awucacanga, bonisa ilaphu lokuhlamba okanye ubonise into elandelayo endaweni yokuphinda inkcazelo ende. Ukuba ukuhlamba iinwele kubangela ukuxhalaba, yenza oko ngokwahlukileyo okanye ukushiye ngolunye usuku. Utshintsho olunje olufutshane lunokwenza umsebenzi ube lula ukulandela ngaphandle kokususa indima yomntu.

## Kuthiwa ntoni ukuba ibhafu epheleleyo okanye ishawa ibonakala inzima kakhulu?

Ibhafu epheleleyo ayiyo yodwa indlela yokunceda umntu azive ehlambulukile. Ukuba umntu uziva ekhululekile, zama ukuhlamba esinkini, usebenzisa ilaphu lokuhlamba elifudumeleyo okanye ukuhlamba indawo enye ngexesha. Ukuhlamba iinwele kunokwenzeka ngolunye usuku ukuba yeyona nxalenye angayithandiyo. Gcina umntu ebhaliwe kwaye ebandakanyekile, uze umise ukuba enye indlela nayo ibangela ixhala.

Ukuba umntu uyala namhlanje, yamkela uze uphinde uzame kamva. Ukuhlamba okuzolileyo ngomso kungcono kunokwenza umzamo wanamhlanje uyoyikise. [Isikhokelo sokuthi umntu athi hayi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) sinezinye iindlela zokunikela uncedo ngaphandle koxinzelelo.

## Ndingalenza njani igumbi lokuhlamba likhuseleke ngakumbi?

Gcina indlela eya kwibhafu okanye kwishawa icacile uze usebenzise izixhobo ezifanele iimfuno zomntu. Imigangatho engabhuqiyo, isitulo esizinzileyo okanye izibambo zokubamba zingakunceda, kodwa izixhobo zifuna ukubekwa ngokufanelekileyo. Buza ingcali yezonyango lomsebenzi okanye enye ingcali efanelekileyo phambi kokufaka izibambo okanye ukwenza utshintsho lwesakhiwo. Musa ukungenisa isitovu esihambayo kwigumbi lokuhlamba, uze ulandele imiyalelo yazo naziphi izixhobo zombane.

Ukuba umntu akazinzanga okanye uwile, musa ukuzama ukumphakamisa okanye ukumsusa ngendlela enokonzakalisa nawe. Funa icebiso lengcali ngezixhobo noncedo oludingekayo. Gcina nawuphi na isicwangciso sokhuseleko esimiselweyo sihambelana nelungelo lomntu lobumfihlo nelokhetho.

## Ndimele ndicele icebiso lengcali nini?

Thetha nengcali yezempilo ukuba ukuhlamba kuba nzima ngenxa yentlungu entsha, ukuwa, inkxalabo yolusu okanye utshintsho kukwazi komntu ukuhlamba ngokukhuselekileyo. Ugqirha wamazinyo, ugqirha, unesi okanye ingcali yezonyango lomsebenzi inokunceda ngengxaki ethile. Musa ukusebenzisa isikhokelo esiqhelekileyo se-intanethi ukugqiba ukuba umntu ufuna ukhathalelo ngezandla.

Ukuba umntu akakwazanga ukuhlamba ixesha elithile, musa ukumsola. Buza ukuba yeyiphi inxalenye enzima kakhulu nokuba utshintsho olunzinyana lungancedisa. Umkhwa oqhelekileyo, ilaphu lokuhlamba okanye uncedo lomntu othenjiweyo lunokwanela; ukuba akwanelanga, cela inkxaso kwiqela lokukhathalela.
