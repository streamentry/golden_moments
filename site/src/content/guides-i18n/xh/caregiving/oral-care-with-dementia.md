Unkathalo lomlomo lolomntu ngokwakhe. Umntu ophila ne-dementia usenokulibala umkhwa owaziwayo, afumanise ukuba ibrashi inzima ukuyisebenzisa okanye aziva engakhululekanga xa omnye umntu emnikela uncedo. Gcina umkhwa uthuthuzelekile kwaye uhloniphekile. Umntu kufuneka enze okuninzi okanokuba enokwenza kwaye afune ukwenza.

I-[Alzheimer's Association dental-care guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dental-care) kunye ne-[icebiso le-Dementia Australia malunga nonkathalo lwamazinyo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/dental-care) zicebisa ukusebenzisa umkhwa owaziwayo, imiyalelo emfutshane kunye noncedo lugqirha wamazinyo xa kuvela iingxaki. Eli phepha lolwazi jikelele, akuyiyo icebiso lamazinyo okanye icwangciso yonkathalo yomntu othile.

## Ndingawugcina njani umkhwa wonkathalo lwemihla ngemihla uqhelekile?

Sebenzisa ixesha nendawo eqhelekileyo komntu, ukuba zisasebenza. Beka ibrashi, ityhuthe lamazinyo kunye nethawula apho umntu alindele khona. Chaza into oyenzayo ngaphambi kokuqala, uze unike umqondiso omnye olula ngexesha. “Thatha ibrashi” ilula ukuyilandela kunokuba kube kukhokelo olude.

Nika umntu ixesha lokwenza inyathelo ngalinye. Ukuba anokuhlamba ngokwakhe, mvumele. Ukuba isikhumbuzo sanele, linda ngaphambi kokunika uncedo olongezelelweyo. Abanye abantu bafumana kulula ukuhlala esinkini okanye bebambe ibrashi enesibambiso abayibambe ngokukhululekileyo. Buza ugqirha wamazinyo ngokhetho ukuba ibrashi ejwayelekileyo inzima ukuyilawula.

Musayenze into yokuhlamba ibe sisivivane okanye ukuvavanywa. Ukuba umntu uthi hayi, uyazikhulula okanye uyakhathazeka, yima. Zama kwakhona kamva, tshintsha indawo okanye ucele icebiso kugqirha wamazinyo. Musa ukunyanzela ibrashi emlonyeni womntu okanye ukumbamba ukuze ugqibe umsebenzi.

Ukuba umkhwa owawusebenza ngaphambili uba nzima, qaphela inyathelo elinzima. Umntu usenokungaqondi ukuba enze ntoni ngebrashi, angathandi ityhuthe elitsha, adiniwe okanye abe nentlungu emlonyeni. Ezi zinto zinokwenzeka, azisiyo uxilongo. Inqaku elifutshane malunga nexesha lokuba ingxaki iqhuba linokunceda ugqirha wamazinyo axoxe ngamanyathelo alandelayo. Tshintsha inxalenye enye yomkhwa ngexesha, ukuze kucace ukuba umntu uthanda ntoni.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngelixa ndikhusela ubumfihlo?

Buza ngaphambi kokunceda, nokuba umntu kade efuna uncedo. Mxelele ukuba uzimisele ukwenza ntoni kwaye ulinde impendulo yakhe. Ukuba uvumile, vala umnyango, sebenzisa ithawula ukukhusela izambatho kwaye ukhulule kuphela indawo edingekayo. Vumelana ngobani umncedisi amkhululekileyo ngaye.

Ukuba umntu udinga uncedo olusebenzisa izandla, chaza inyathelo ngalinye ngaphambi kokulenza kwaye uyeke ukuba ubonakalisa uxinzelelo. Ugqirha wamazinyo unokubonisa umkhwa okhuselekileyo ngokweemfuno zomntu. Musa ukuzenzela ukukhokela ngokwasemzimbeni okanye usebenzise ubuchule ongabonisanga bona ngumntu oqeqeshiweyo.

## Yintoni endiyaziyo malunga namazinyo afakwayo?

Gcina amazinyo afakwayo kwindawo eqhelekileyo nemakiweyo komntu. Buza ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba kufuneka ahlanjwe njani agcinwe njani, kwaye ukuba ukuwanelisa kufuneka kujongwe na xa umntu akakhululeki. Ukuba amazinyo afakwayo ahlala kwindawo yonyango, phawula isikhongozeli sokugcina ukuze abasebenzi bawuqonde.

Musa ukunyanzela amazinyo afakwayo emlonyeni womntu ukuba uyanqaba okanye ubonakala ephethwe bubuhlungu. Amazinyo afakwayo angakhululekanga okanye angazinzanga anokwenza ukutya kube nzima; buza ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba makwenziwe ntoni. [Isikhokelo sethu sesidlo](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) sichaza ukulungiselela isidlo ngokuthuthuzela kunye nokuba zeziphi iingxaki zokutya ezifuna icebiso lengcali.

## Singalilungiselela njani uhambo lokuya kugqirha wamazinyo?

Bhukisha ixesha apho umntu adla kukuba uphumle. Xa umntu evumile, xelela ikliniki yamazinyo kwangaphambili ukuba angafuna ixesha elongezelelweyo, incazelo ethuthuzelayo okanye uncedo lomntu amthembileyo. Zisa uhlu lwamayeza olusemva kwexesha kunye nenqaku elifutshane malunga nento eye yaba nzima, njengokuhlamba, ukutya okanye ukunxiba amazinyo afakwayo.

Cela ithimba lamazinyo ukuba lichaze ukuba yintoni eyakwenzeka inyathelo ngalinye. Ilungu losapho elaziwayo linokunceda umntu azive ezinzile, kodwa umntu kufuneka aqhubeke enxulumene nencoko kunye nezigqibo. Ukuba ukuya kwiofisi eqhelekileyo kunzima, buza ugqirha wamazinyo ngokhetho lokutyelela olufikelelekayo kwindawo yakho.

## Abanye ababancedisi banokugcina njani umkhwa uqhelekile?

Ukuba ilungu losapho, umsebenzi wasekhaya okanye umsebenzi wendlu yokuhlala ncedisa, vumelana nomntu ngomkhwa okhululekileyo kuye. Sebenzisa indawo eqhelekileyo kunye nomqondiso omfutshane xa kunokwenzeka. Buza ukuba uyakhetha ukuba umntu othile ancedise ngamanye amaxesha.

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, yabelana kuphela ngeenkcukacha eziya kunceda umncedisi olandelayo: ixesha elikhethwayo, into anokuyenza ngokwakhe, yeyiphi inyathelo elinzima kunye nelixesha afuna ukuba uncedo lume ngalo. Gcina nasiphi na isiqwenga sencwadi siyimfihlo kwaye ulandele inkqubo eqhelekileyo yendawo yonyango. Umkhwa obhalwe phantsi yisikhokelo, hayi imvume yokuqhubeka xa umntu esithi hayi. Izikhethwa zinokutshintsha, ngoko buza kwakhona kwaye uzamkele. Ukuba iindlela ezininzi zibe nzima, chaza into oyibonileyo kwithimba lamazinyo kwaye ucele icebiso kulo mntu.

## Yeziphi iimpawu ekufanele ndizichaze kugqirha wamazinyo?

Intlungu yomlomo ayisoloko ilula ukuyichaza. Ukuthinta ubuso ngokuqhubekayo, ukala ukuhlamba, utshintsho ekutyeni okanye ukulala okuphazamisekileyo kunokuba zizibonakaliso, kodwa azifakazeli ukuba kukho ingxaki yamazinyo. Xelela ugqirha wamazinyo oko ukubonileyo kunye nelixesha okuqale ngalo. Ugqirha wamazinyo unokujonga intlungu, ingxaki yezicubu zomlomo, ukubola kwezinyo okanye amazinyo afakwayo angasalingani kakuhle.

Ukuba umntu une-ngxaki entsha yokuhlafuna okanye yokuginya, qhagamshelana nengcali yakhe yezempilo okanye ugqirha wamazinyo. Musa ukutshintsha ubume bokutya, ukusebenzisa amayeza omlomo okanye iindlela zokuginya ngokusekelwe kwinqaku eliqhelekileyo. Funa uncedo lwangxamu xa kukho intlungu enkulu, ukuvuvukala, ukopha okanye enye ingxaki yezonyango ekhawulezayo.

Injongo kukuba kubekho umkhwa umntu aluvumelayo, kunye noncedo oluncinci olufunekayo. Isikhumbuzo esiqhelekileyo, isikhundla esithuthuzelayo okanye ilixesha elilandelayo lokuzama linokwanela. Ukuba ingxaki iqhubeka, buza ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba luluphi uncedo olufanelekela lo mntu.
