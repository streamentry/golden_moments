Ukhathalelo lomlomo lubuntu bomntu. Umntu one-dementia angalibala inkqubo ayiqhelileyo, afumane ibrashi yamazinyo idinisa ukuyisebenzisa okanye azive engakhululekanga xa omnye umntu emnika uncedo. Gcina inkqubo ithe cwaka kwaye inentlonipho. Umntu makenze oko anokukwenza nakufunayo.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokhathalelo lwamazinyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dental-care) kunye [nesicebiso se-Dementia Australia sokhathalelo lwamazinyo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/dental-care) sicebisa ukusebenzisa inkqubo eqhelekileyo, imiyalelo emifutshane noncedo lukagqirha wamazinyo xa kuvela iingxaki. Eli phepha linolwazi jikelele, asilicebo lamazinyo okanye icebo lokhathalelo lomntu othile.

## Ndingayigcina njani ikhathalelo lomlomo losuku ngosuku iqhelekile?

Sebenzisa ixesha nendawo eqhelekileyo yomntu ukuba zisasebenza. Beka ibrashi yamazinyo, isithambiso samazinyo nethawula apho umntu ewalindele khona. Chaza into oyenzayo phambi kokuba uqale, uze unike isikhumbuzo esinye esilula ngexesha elinye. “Thatha ibrashi yamazinyo” kulula ukulandelwa kunoluhlu olude lwemiyalelo.

Nika umntu ixesha kwinyathelo ngalinye. Ukuba angahlamba yedwa, mvumele. Ukuba isikhumbuzo sanele, linda phambi kokunika uncedo olungakumbi. Abanye abantu bakufumana kulula ukuhlala ecaleni kwesinki okanye ukusebenzisa ibrashi enomphini anokuwubamba ngokukhululekileyo. Buza ugqirha wamazinyo ngoku khetho ukuba ibrashi eqhelekileyo inzima ukuyisebenzisa.

Musa ukuguqula ukuhlamba kube luvavanyo okanye ukhuphiswano. Ukuba umntu uthi hayi, atsalele ecaleni okanye axhalabe, yeka. Zama kwakhona kamva, tshintsha indawo okanye ubuze ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba ucebisa ntoni. Musa ukunyanzelisa ibrashi emlonyeni womntu okanye ukumbamba ukuze ugqibe umsebenzi.

Ukuba inkqubo eyayisebenza ngaphambili iba nzima, qaphela ukuba lelipi inyathelo elinzima. Umntu angabe engaqinisekanga ukuba enze ntoni ngebrashi, engayithandi isithambiso esitsha, edinwe okanye enokungakhululeki emlonyeni. Ezi zinokwenzeka, asiyondlela yokufumanisa. Inqaku elifutshane lokuba ubunzima buvela nini linokunceda ugqirha wamazinyo axoxe ngamanyathelo alandelayo. Tshintsha inxalenye enye yenkqubo ngexesha elinye ukuze kucace ukuba umntu uthanda ntoni.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngelixa ndikhusela imfihlo?

Buza phambi kokunceda, nokuba umntu ebesele ebufuna uncedo ngaphambili. Mxelele into ocinga ukuyenza uze ulinde impendulo yakhe. Ukuba uyavuma, gcina ucango luvaliwe, sebenzisa ithawula ukukhusela impahla uze ubonise kuphela indawo efunekayo. Vumelanani ukuba ngowuphi umncedisi umntu akhululeke naye.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo ngezandla, chaza inyathelo ngalinye phambi kokulenza uze uyeke ukuba ubonisa ukuxhalaba. Ugqirha wamazinyo angakubonisa inkqubo ekhuselekileyo ngokweemfuno zomntu. Musa ukuyila isikhokelo sokuzithintela okanye ukusebenzisa indlela ongabonisiwe ngayo yingcali efanelekileyo.

## Ndifanele ndazi ntoni ngamazinyo okufakelwa?

Gcina amazinyo okufakelwa kwindawo eqhelekileyo yomntu, ebonakala ngokucacileyo. Buza ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba kufuneka acocwe agcinwe njani, nokuba ukulingana kufuneka kujongwe ukuba umntu ubonakala engakhululekanga. Ukuba amazinyo okufakelwa anxitywa kwindawo yokhathalelo, phawula isitya sokugcina ukuze abasebenzi bakwazi ukumazi.

Musa ukunyanzelisa amazinyo okufakelwa emlonyeni womntu ukuba uyayala okanye ubonakala enentlungu. Amazinyo okufakelwa abhalisekileyo okanye angakhululekanga anokwenza ukutya kube nzima; buza ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba wenze ntoni. [Isikhokelo sethu samaxesha okutya](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) sigubungela ukulungiselela isidlo esithe cwaka nokuba kufuneka nini icebiso lengcali ngeengxaki zokutya.

## Singalungiselela njani utyelelo lukagqirha wamazinyo?

Bhukisha ngexesha apho umntu ehlala ephumle. Xa umntu evuma, xelela iofisi yamazinyo kwangaphambili ukuba angafuna ixesha elingakumbi, inkcazo ethe cwaka okanye uncedo lomntu amthembayo. Zisa uluhlu lwamayeza lwangoku nenqaku elifutshane ngoko kuye kwanzima, njengokuhlamba, ukuhlafuna okanye ukunxiba amazinyo okufakelwa.

Cela iqela lamazinyo ukuba lichaze oko kuya kwenzeka inyathelo ngalinye. Umhlobo oqhelekileyo unokunceda umntu azive ekhululekile, kodwa umntu makahlale eyinxalenye yencoko nezigqibo. Ukuba uhambo oluya kwi-ofisi eqhelekileyo lunzima, buza ugqirha wamazinyo ngokhetho lotyelelo olufikelelekayo kwindawo yakho.

## Abanye abancedisi banokugcina njani inkqubo ihambelana?

Ukuba ilungu losapho, umsebenzi wokhathalelo lwasekhaya okanye isebenzi sekhaya lokhathalelo siyanceda, vumelana nomntu ngenkqubo akhululekileyo ngayo. Sebenzisa indawo enye eqhelekileyo nesikhumbuzo esifutshane xa kunokwenzeka. Buza ukuba uthanda umntu othile ukuba amncede ngalo lonke ixesha.

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, yabelana kuphela ngeenkcukacha ezinokunceda umncedisi olandelayo: ixesha alikhethayo, oko anokukwenza yedwa, lelipi inyathelo elinzima nokuba ufuna uncedo luyeke nini. Gcina nawuphi na umbhalo uyimfihlo uze ulandele inkqubo eqhelekileyo yendawo yokhathalelo. Inkqubo ebhaliweyo sisikhokelo, ayiyonto evumela ukuqhubeka xa umntu ethi hayi. Izinto azithandayo zinokutshintsha, ngoko buza kwakhona uze uhlengahlengise. Ukuba iindlela ezininzi sezinzima, chaza oko ukuqaphelayo kwiqela lamazinyo uze ucele icebiso loo mntu.

## Zeziphi iimpawu ekufuneka ndizikhankanye kugqirha wamazinyo?

Intlungu yomlomo ayihlali ilula ukuyichaza. Ukutsala ubuso, ukwala ukuhlamba, utshintsho ekutyeni okanye ukuphazamiseka kwesiqaqa kunokuba zezikhokelo, kodwa aziqinisekisi ingxaki yamazinyo. Xelela ugqirha wamazinyo oko ukuqaphelileyo nokuba kwaqala nini. Ugqirha wamazinyo angajonga intlungu, iingxaki zeentsini, ukonakala kwamazinyo okanye amazinyo okufakelwa angasalingani kakuhle.

Ukuba umntu une-ngxaki entsha yokuhlafuna okanye yokugwinya, qhagamshelana nengcali yakhe yezempilo okanye ugqirha wamazinyo. Musa ukutshintsha ubume bokutya, ukusebenzisa unyango lomlomo okanye ukuzama iindlela zokugwinya ngokwenqaku jikelele. Funa uncedo olukhawulezileyo ngentlungu enzima, ukudumba, ukopha okanye enye ingxaki yezonyango ekhawulezileyo.

Injongo yinkqubo umntu anokuyamkela, enoncedo oluncinci olufunekayo. Isikhumbuzo esiqhelekileyo, indawo yokuhlala ekhululekileyo okanye ukuzama kwakhona kamva kunokwanela. Ukuba ingxaki iyaqhubeka, buza ugqirha wamazinyo ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso efanelekileyo kumntu.
