Inkqubo yamayeza inokutshintsha njengoko iimfuno zitshintsha. Umntu usenokufuna uncedo lokukhumbula isixa, lokufunda ilebheli okanye lokuphinda athenge iyeza elinencwadi yogqirha, ngelixa esafuna ukulawula ezinye iinxalenye ngokwakhe. Injongo kukuxhasa ukusetyenziswa okukhuselekileyo kungekho kuthatha ngaphezulu koko kufuneka.

I-[Alzheimer's Association medication-safety guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/medication-safety) icebisa ukusebenza kunye nogqirha kunye nofamasi, ukugcina uhlu olusemva kwexesha kunye nokuvumelana ngenkqubo. Eli phepha lolwazi jikelele. Landela imiyalelo evela kumbhali wencwadi nakufamasi malunga namayeza omntu.

## Ndingalibandakanya njani umntu kwinkqubo yamayeza?

Buza ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo luni. Usenokukhetha isikhumbuzo, uncedo lokuvula isitya okanye ilungu losapho elijonge uhlu. Ukuba umntu usenokulawula amayeza athile ngokwakhe, gcina esi sakhono simbonakalele. Inkqubo entsha yezikhumbuzo kufuneka ixoxwe naye kwaye ilungelelaniswe ukuba iziva ikhathazekile okanye idideka.

Sebenzisa ulwimi oluthe tye, olufanelekileyo kubantu abadala. Ukuba umntu uthatha iyeza ngexesha eliqhelekileyo, dibanisa isikhumbuzo nomkhwa owaziwayo kuphela ukuba oko kuhambelana nemiyalelo yofamasi. Nika umntu ixesha lokuphendula. Musamvavanya ngemibuzo, musamhlazisa ngokukhohlwa okanye musaxabane ngokuba sele uthathe isixa na.

Ukuba isikhumbuzo asisebenzi, buza ukuba yeyiphi inxalenye enzima. Ilebheli inokuba nzima ukuyifunda, isitya sinokuba nzima ukusivula okanye iyeza linokubangela imvakalelo engamnandanga. Ugqirha okanye ufamasi unokunceda ukuchonga ezinye iindlela. Musaqhubeka utshintsha inkqubo yamayeza ngokuqikelela.

## Yeyiphi inkcukacha kufuneka ibe kuluhlu lwamayeza?

Gcina uhlu olunye oluhlala luhlaziywa oluquka igama lephilisi ngalinye, isixa nexesha layo, kunye nesizathu sokuba ibhalelwe ukuba siyaziwa. Quka amayeza afumaneka ngaphandle kwencwadi yogqirha, iivithamini, amayeza esiko nezityalo kunye neenkonzo zokongeza. Bhala i-allergy nezinto eziye zabangela iziphumo ezingathandekiyo ngaphambili, kwaye ubhale namagama abasebenzi bezempilo abalawula unyango lomntu.

Zisa uhlu kwintlanganiso zonyango kwaye uhlaziye xa umbhali wencwadi etshintsha into. Ukuba umntu ubona ugqirha ngaphezu komnye, yabelana ngohlu nelungu ngalinye leqela lonyango kuphela njengoko umntu evumelene nalo. Gcina uhlu apho umntu nabancedisi abakhethiweyo bakwazi ukulufumana khona, kwaye ulukhusele njengolwazi lwempilo oluyimfihlo.

Buza ugqirha okanye ufamasi ukuba ajonge ukudibana okunokwenzeka xa kubhalwa iyeza elitsha. Ukuba umntu une-ngxaki entsha emva kotshintsho lwamayeza, bhala ukuba iqale nini kwaye unxibelelane nombhali wencwadi. Utshintsho kwindlela yokuziphatha lunokuba nezizathu ezininzi, ngoko musa ukuthatha ukuba iyeza linetyala ngaphandle kocebiso lwengcali.

## Ingaba isitya sezixa okanye isikhumbuzo siyancedisa?

Ikhalenda, ichati ebhaliweyo okanye umgcini wepilisi unokuncedisa abanye abantu. Isixhobo esilungileyo sixhomekeke kumayeza nakwizakhono zomntu. Buza ufamasi ukuba umgcini wepilisi ufanelekile na ngaphambi kokudlulisa amayeza kwiphakheji yawo yokuqala. Amanye amayeza afuna ukuphathwa okukhethekileyo okanye indawo yokugcina.

Vumelana ukuba ngubani ozakugcwalisa okanye ojonge umgcini, kwaye uya kuthintela njani izixa ezilityaliweyo okanye ezophindwayo. Ukuba abantu abaninzi bancedisa, sebenzisa indlela enye abayabelanayo yokurekhoda into enikiweyo. Ukuba umntu efuna ukulawula umgcini ngokwakhe, buza ufamasi ukuba oko kunokwenziwa njani ngokukhuselekileyo nangokusebenzayo.

Ukuba isixa silityaliwe, jonga imiyalelo ebhalwe kulo mayeza okanye ubize ufamasi ngaphambi kokugqiba into ozakuyenza. Musa ukuqikelela okanye uphinde isixa ngokusekelwe kwisikhokelo esiqhelekileyo.

## Yenzeni ukuba umntu uyala okanye unengxaki yokuginya?

Yima ubuze into eyenzekayo. Umntu usenokungathandi incasa, angaziva engakhoyo, adiniwe okanye angaqondi ukuba iyeza leliphi na elo. Nika incazelo ecacileyo kunye nexesha lokuphendula. Musa ukunyanzela iyeza emlonyeni wakhe okanye ukulifihla kwisidlo ngaphandle kwemvume kunye necebiso lengcali.

Ungazityumzi iithebhulethi, ungavuli ikepusi okanye uxube iyeza nokutya ngaphandle kokuba umbhali wencwadi okanye ufamasi eqinisekisa ukuba kukhuselekile. Amanye amayeza anokungasebenzi njengoko kulindelekile okanye angabangela umonakalo xa etshintshiwe. Ukuba ukuginya kunzima, buza ukuba kukho ukhetho lolwelo okanye elinye ifomu. Qhagamshelana negqirha okanye nofamasi endaweni yokutshintsha isixa ngokwakho.

## Mayigcinwe njani amayeza kwaye aphathwe njani?

Gcina amayeza ngokwemiyalelo ebhalwe kwilebheli nakwemiyalelo kafamasi. Ukuba umntu usenokuthatha isixa esongezelelweyo ngengozi, buza umntu nethimba lonyango ukuba ukugcinwa okanye uncedo lupi olufanelekileyo. Ikhabhathi evaliweyo inokunceda kwezinye iimeko, kodwa ukufikelela kunye nokuzimela nako kuyabalulekile. Hlola icwangciso njengoko izakhono zomntu zitshintsha.

Gcina amayeza aphelelwe lixesha okanye angasasetyenziswa ahlukile kwaye ubuze ufamasi indlela yokuwalahla ngokukhuselekileyo. Musa ukugcina amayeza amdala njengesigcinelo okanye wabelane ngeyeza phakathi kwabantu. Ukuba iyeza lilahlekile okanye kusenokuba kuphindwe isixa, qhagamshelana nofamasi, nesevisi yoncedo lwetyhefu okanye nenkonzo yezimo eziphuthumayo ukuze ufumane icebiso ngokukhawuleza.

## Ndifanele ndifune uncedo lwezonyango nini?

Qhagamshelana negqirha okanye nofamasi ukuba umntu une-ziphumo ezingathandekiyo ezikhathazayo, ubunzima bokuginya, izixa ezilityaliweyo rhoqo okanye utshintsho olukhawulezayo emva kweyeza elitsha. Cela imiyalelo ebhalwe ngokucacileyo. Musa ukuyeka okanye wehlise iyeza ngokwakho.

Ukuba ucinga ukuba umntu uthathe amayeza ngaphezulu kwesixa, biza isevisi yoncedo lwetyhefu kwindawo yakho okanye inkonzo yezimo eziphuthumayo ngoko nangoko. Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso, i-[NHS icebisa uncedo lwezonyango olungxamisekileyo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Sebenzisa inkonzo yezimo eziphuthumayo okanye inkqubo yonyango olungxamisekileyo endaweni ohlala kuyo.
