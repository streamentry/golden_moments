Inkqubo yamayeza inokutshintsha ngokutshintsha kweemfuno. Umntu angafuna uncedo ekukhumbuleni umthamo, ekufundeni ilebhile okanye ekuzaliseni kwakhona iyeza, ngoxa efuna ukuzilawula ezinye iinxalenye. Injongo kukuxhasa ukusetyenziswa okukhuselekileyo ngaphandle kokuthatha okungaphezulu kwesidingo.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokhuseleko lwamayeza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/medication-safety) sicebisa ukusebenza nodoktela noogqirha bamayeza, ukugcina uluhlu olusesikweni nokuvumelana ngenkqubo. Eli phepha linolwazi jikelele. Landela imiyalelo kagqirha obhale iyeza nokagqirha wamayeza ngamayeza omntu.

## Ndingamenza njani umntu abe yinxalenye yenkqubo yamayeza?

Buza ukuba ngoncedo olunjani umntu alufunayo. Angakhetha isikhumbuzo, uncedo lokuvula isitya okanye ilungu losapho elijonge uluhlu. Ukuba umntu angawalawula amanye amayeza ngokwakhe, gcina loo nto ingqondweni. Inkqubo entsha yezikhumbuzo mayixoxwe naye kwaye ilungiswe ukuba iziva ingenelela okanye ibhidanisa.

Sebenzisa ulwimi olucacileyo, lomntu omdala. Ukuba umntu usela iyeza ngexesha eliqhelekileyo, nxibelelanisa isikhumbuzo nenkqubo eqhelekileyo kuphela ukuba oko kuhambelana nemiyalelo kagqirha wamayeza. Nika umntu ixesha lokuphendula. Musa ukumbuza imibuzo yovavanyo, ukumbhidisa ngenxa yokulibala okanye ukuxabana ngokuba sele esele umthamo.

Ukuba isikhumbuzo asisebenzi, buza ukuba yeyiphi inxalenye enzima. Ilebhile ingabi nzima ukufundeka, isitya singabi nzima ukuvulwa okanye iyeza lingabangela imvakalelo engathandekiyo. Ugqirha okanye ugqirha wamayeza angancedisa ukufumana ukhetho. Musa ukutshintsha inkqubo yamayeza ngokuqikelela.

## Yiyiphi ingxelo ekufuneka ibe kuluhlu lwamayeza?

Gcina uluhlu olunye oluvuselelweyo olunegama leyeza ngalinye elibhaliweyo, umthamo walo nexesha, nesizathu sokubhalwa kwalo ukuba siyaziwa. Faka amayeza angabhalwanga, iivithamini, iimveliso zemifuno nezongezelelo. Yazi iialergy okanye iziphumo ezingathandekiyo zangaphambili namagama eengcali ezilawula ukhathalelo lomntu.

Zisa uluhlu kwiintlanganiso zezonyango uze uluvuselele xa ugqirha obhale iyeza etshintsha into. Ukuba umntu ubona ogqirha abangaphezu kumnye, yabelana noluhlu nelungu ngalinye leqela lokhathalelo kuphela njengoko umntu evuma. Gcina uluhlu kwindawo apho umntu nabancedisi abakhethiweyo banokuluqwalasela, uze uluzikhusele njengolwazi lwempilo oluyimfihlo.

Cela ugqirha okanye ugqirha wamayeza ukuba ajonge ukuxubana okungenzeka xa kubhalwa iyeza elitsha. Ukuba umntu une-ngxaki entsha emva kotshintsho lwamayeza, bhala ukuba yaqala nini uze uqhagamshelane nogqirha obhale iyeza. Utshintsho kwindlela yokuziphatha lunezizathu ezininzi, ngoko musa ukucinga ukuba iyeza lilo elinesizathu ngaphandle kwecebiso lengcali.

## Ibhokisi yamaphilisi okanye isikhumbuzo singanceda?

Ikhalenda, itshathi ebhaliweyo okanye ibhokisi yamaphilisi inokunceda abanye abantu. Isixhobo esifanelekileyo sixhomekeke kumayeza nakumandla omntu. Buza ugqirha wamayeza ukuba ibhokisi yamaphilisi ifanelekile phambi kokuhambisa iyeza kwiphakheji yalo yoqobo. Amanye amayeza adinga ukuphathwa okanye ukugcinwa ngokukhethekileyo.

Vumelanani ukuba ngubani oza kuzalisa okanye ajonge ibhokisi nokuba nigxeka njani imithamo eyeqiwe okanye ephindiweyo. Ukuba abantu abangaphezu komnye bayanceda, sebenzisani indlela enye yabelwanayo yokubhala oko kunikiweyo. Ukuba umntu efuna ukulawula ibhokisi ngokwakhe, buza ugqirha wamayeza ukuba ungayenza njani ikhuseleke isebenze.

Ukuba umthamo uyeqiwe, jonga imiyalelo ebhaliweyo yeso siyeza okanye utsalele ugqirha wamayeza phambi kokuba uthathe isigqibo. Musa ukuqikelela okanye ukuphinda umthamo ngokusekelwe kwisikhokelo esiqhelekileyo.

## Kuthekani ukuba umntu uyala okanye unengxaki yokugwinya?

Yeka uze ubuze ukuba kutheni. Umntu angabanganyanzelekiyo ngetyhefu yokunambitha, azive egula, adinwe okanye angaqondi ukuba iyeza lenzelwe ntoni. Nika inkcazo ecacileyo nexesha lokuphendula. Musa ukunyanzelisa iyeza emlonyeni womntu okanye ukulifihla ekutyeni ngaphandle kwemvume necebiso lengcali.

Ungaze uxebe ipilisi, uvule ikhapsule okanye uxube iyeza ekutyeni ngaphandle kokuba ugqirha obhale iyeza okanye ugqirha wamayeza ekuqinisekisile ukuba kukhuselekile. Amanye amayeza angasebenzi njengoko kuceliwe okanye angabangela ukonakala ukuba atshintshiwe. Ukuba ukugwinya kunzima, buza ukuba kukho into engamanzi okanye enye indlela yeyeza. Qhagamshelana nogqirha okanye nogqirha wamayeza endaweni yokutshintsha umthamo ngokwakho.

## Amayeza kufuneka agcinwe aze aphathwe njani?

Gcina amayeza ngokwelebhile nemiyalelo kagqirha wamayeza. Ukuba umntu angasela umthamo ongaphezulu ngengozi, buza umntu neqela lokhathalelo ukuba ngokugcinwa nokuxhaswa okufanelekileyo. Ikhabhathi etshixiweyo inokunceda kwezinye iimeko, kodwa ukufikelela nokuzimela nako kubalulekile. Phinda ujonge icebo njengoko amandla omntu etshintsha.

Gcina amayeza aphelelwe lixesha okanye abhalwe ukuba ayekwe ahlukile uze ubuze ugqirha wamayeza ukuba angachithwa njani ngokukhuselekileyo. Musa ukugcina amayeza amadala njengesixhobo sokuhlawula okanye ukwabelana ngamayeza phakathi kwabantu. Ukuba iyeza alifumaneki okanye umthamo unokuba uphindiwe, qhagamshelana nogqirha wamayeza, nenkonzo yetyhefu okanye neenkonzo zongxamiseko ukufumana icebiso elikhawulezileyo.

## Ndifanele ndifune uncedo lwezonyango nini?

Qhagamshelana nogqirha okanye nogqirha wamayeza ukuba umntu une-ziphumo ezingathandekiyo ezikhathazayo, ubunzima bokugwinya, imithamo eyeqiwe rhoqo okanye utshintsho olukhawulezileyo emva kweyeza elitsha. Cela imiyalelo ebhaliweyo ecacileyo. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukunciphisa iyeza ngokwakho.

Ukuba ucinga ukuba umntu utye amayeza amaninzi kakhulu, tsalela ngoko nangoko inkonzo yakho yasekhaya yetyhefu okanye iinkonzo zongxamiseko. Ukuba umntu ngequbuliso ubhidekile, [i-NHS icebisa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Sebenzisa iinkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni okanye inkqubo yokhathalelo olukhawulezileyo apho uhlala khona.

Ngeentlanganiso eziqhelekileyo, [isikhokelo sotyelelo lukagqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinokukunceda ulungiselele imibuzo noluhlu lwamayeza. [Isikhokelo sotyelelo esibhedlele](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) sigubungela ukwabelana ngolwazi lwamayeza neqela lokhathalelo.

Inkqubo ekhuselekileyo kakhulu yeyo umntu ayamkelayo neqela lokhathalelo elayiqondayo. Buza imibuzo, gcina uluhlu luvuselelwe uze ufumane icebiso lengcali phambi kokwenza utshintsho.
