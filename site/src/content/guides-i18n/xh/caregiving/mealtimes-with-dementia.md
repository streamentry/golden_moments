Isidlo sinokuba nzima ukulandela xa igumbi linengxolo, itafile icinezelekile okanye kukho ukhetho oluninzi ngaxeshanye. Ushintsho oluncinci kwindawo nakwisantya lunokwenza kube lula komntu ophila ne-dementia ukuba athathe inxaxheba. Qala ngeendlela zomntu zokutya kunye nezikhetho zakhe, kwaye ugcine oko kusebenza kakuhle.

Ukhokelo lwe-[Alzheimer's Association malunga nokutya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) kunye necebiso le-[Dementia Australia malunga nexesha lokutya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) zombini ziyacebisa indawo ethule, ukutya okwaziwayo kunye nexesha elaneleyo. Esi sikhokelo sigubungela indawo yesidlo kunye noncedo lwemihla ngemihla. Asiyonkqubo yezondlo okanye uvavanyo lokuginya.

## Sinokwenza njani isidlo sibe nentsebenzelwano yoluntu ngaphandle koxinzelelo?

Ukuba umntu uthanda ukuba nabantu, hlala udle kunye naye. Gcina incoko ilula kwaye umvumele agxile ekutyeni xa efuna. Yekela ukumlungisa indlela asebenzisa ngayo izixhobo okanye ukubuza imibuzo eyenza ukuba isidlo sibe luvavanyo. Ukuba itafile iba yingxolo okanye inxilisa, nciphisa inani labantu okanye ushukumele kwindawo ethule.

Xa ukupheka sekuye kwaba nzima, isidlo esilungiselelwe ngaphambili esaziwayo okanye uncedo kwinxalenye enye yomsebenzi lunokwenza isidlo silula ukusilawula. I-Dementia Australia icebisa ukutya okuziswa ekhaya okanye okulungiselelwe ngaphambili njengezinketho xa kufuneka. Buza ukuba yeyiphi ukutya umntu akuthandayo kunye nohlobo loncedo alungele ukulamkela; ukulula akumele kuthathe indawo yokhetho lomntu.

## Ndingalenza njani itafile ilule ukuyilandela?

Yenza isidlo sibonakale silula ngaphambi kokutshintsha into eyanikezelwayo.

- **Nciphisa ingxolo.** Vala umabonakude kwaye unciphise ezinye iingxolo ezingxabanayo. Hlala umntu kude nendlela yabantu ekhitshini ukuba oko kumncedisa agxile.
- **Susa izinto ezongezelelweyo.** Beka kuphela izixhobo nezinongo umntu aya kuzisebenzisa. Susa amalaphu omlomo, iimbali okanye ezinye izinto ezinokuphazamisa okanye ziqondwe njengokutya.
- **Sebenzisa umahluko ocacileyo.** Ipleyiti eyahluka kakuhle kwindawo ebekwe kuyo inokwenza ukutya kubonakale lula. Gwema iiphethini ezinzima kwiipleyiti nakwiilaphu.
- **Jonga ukukhanya nobushushu.** Qinisekisa ukuba umntu uyakwazi ukubona ipleyiti, kwaye uvavanye ukutya neziselo phambi kokukhonza ukuze zingabi shushu kakhulu.
- **Gcina inkqubo eyaziwayo.** Ukuba indawo, ixesha okanye ulungelelwaniso letafile lulula kumntu, zama ukulugcina lufanayo. Tshintsha kuphela into ebangela ingxaki.

## Yintoni ekufanele ndiyikhonze?

Qala ngokutya umntu akuqondayo kwaye akuthandayo. Nika ikhosi enye ngexesha okanye ukhetho phakathi kwento ezimbini eziqhelekileyo, endaweni yeplate egcwele izinto ezininzi ezahlukeneyo. Iinxalenye ezincinci zinokuphinda zenziwe ukuba umntu efuna okungakumbi.

Iintando zingatshintsha. Ukuba ukutya okuthandwa kakhulu kwaliwa, musa ukuxabana okanye ukuphikisana. Nika enye into eyaziwayo ekhethweni, vumela umntu aphumle, okanye zama kwakhona kamva. Isidlo akufuneki silandele ixesha elingaguqukiyo ukuba umntu udiniwe okanye ukhathazekile; gcina amaxesha esidlo apho asebenza khona, kwaye ulungelelanise icwangciso ngokweemfuno zomntu.

Ukuba ukupheka sekuye kwaba nzima, ilungu losapho lingancedisa ekulungiseleleni ukutya ngelixa umntu ejoyina kumsebenzi okhuselekileyo akuthandayo. Isikhokelo sethu [somsebenzi wasekhaya](/activities/household-tasks/) sinendlela zokulungelelanisa imisebenzi yasekhitshini ngaphandle kokuyenza uvavanyo.

## Ndingaxhasa njani ukuzimela komntu?

Vumela umntu adle ngendlela enolonwabo nekhuselekileyo kuye. Usenokukhetha isitya, icephe, ukutya okuthathwa ngeminwe okanye inxalenye encinci. Musa ukuthatha ngokukhawuleza ukulungisa ukuchitheka okanye ukuthatha umsebenzi omntu asakwazi ukuwenza. Nika uncedo xa lufunwa okanye lufuneka, kwaye umnike ixesha elaneleyo lesidlo.

Ukutya kunye kunokwenza isidlo sibe sentlalo, ukuba umntu uyathanda ukuba nabantu. Hlala kufuphi, gcina incoko ilula kwaye umvumele agxile ekutyeni xa efuna oko. Gwema imibuzo evavanya inkumbulo okanye izimvo malunga nokuba utye kangakanani. Ukuba umntu uyayeka, linda kwaye ubuze ukuba usele ukulungela okungakumbi.

## Yenzeni xa ixesha lokutya liba nzima?

Yima ujonge into eyenzekayo ngokusebenzayo. Igumbi lisenokuba nengxolo; ukutya kusenokungaziwa; umntu usenokufuna ukusebenzisa indlu yangasese, ukuphumla okanye ukutshintsha isikhundla. Zama utshintsho olunye ngexesha, njengokucoca itafile, ukunika amanzi okanye ukuhamba uye kwindawo ethule. Ukuba umntu uxinezelekile, yeka uze uzame kwakhona kamva endaweni yokumcinezela ukuba agqibe.

Ukuba umntu rhoqo edlula izidlo, esoloko ekhathazekile, ezama ngobunzima ukusebenzisa umlomo okanye izandla, okanye ene-tshintsho entsha kwinkanuko okanye ubunzima bomzimba, bhala oko ukubonayo uze uthethe negqirha okanye negcisa lezondlo. Ubunzima obutsha bokuhlafuna okanye ukuginya, ukukhwehlela rhoqo ngokutya okanye iziselo, okanye ukubhenca kufuna icebiso lengcali. Ingcali yentetho nokuginya inokuvavanya kwaye icebise iindlela ezikhuselekileyo. Musa ukutshintsha ubume bokutya, ukuqinisa iziselo okanye iindlela zokuncedisa ukutya ngokusekwe kuluhlu oluqhelekileyo.

Isidlo esinye esishiyeke sisodwa asicacisi ukuba kutheni umntu eyekile. Jonga iphethini kwiisidlo ezininzi: ukutya okuthile, ixesha, uxinzelelo lwengxolo okanye indawo abehlala kuyo ingasenza umsebenzi ube nzima. Ukuba ukutya okuthandwayo kukhe kwaba nzima ngequbuliso, jonga ukuba kukho intlungu okanye ukungakhululeki kwamazinyo, kwaye ubuze ingcali yezempilo endaweni yokuthatha ukuba umntu ukhetha nje.

Ngemibuzo malunga nempilo okanye ukutya okukhethekileyo, buza ingcali yezempilo yomntu. Musa ukuqalisa ukutya okunciphisayo okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ukuze utshintshe inkanuko yokutya ngaphandle kocebiso. [Isikhokelo sezondlo zobuchule bengqondo](/brain-health/diet/) sixoxa ngophando malunga nokutya kunye nengozi ye-dementia; sihlukile kwesi sikhokelo sesidlo sokusebenza.

## Ingaba isidlo kufuneka sihlale sityiwa etafileni?

Hayi. Ukuba ukuhlala etafileni kuyadinisa okanye kudideka, enye indawo ehlala ilungile inokusebenza ngcono, kuphela ukuba umntu usenokutya ngokukhuselekileyo. Isidlo esincinci, ukutya okulula okanye ukuphumla okwethutyana nako kunokufaneleka ngakumbi kunokuzama ukugqiba isidlo esipheleleyo ngexesha elicwangciswe ngaphambili.

Ukuba ukutya kube nzima kangangeentsuku ezininzi, okanye uxhalabele ukutya, iiziselo okanye ukuginya, qhagamshelana nengcali yezempilo. Funa uncedo lwangxamu ukuba kukho ingozi yokubhenca ngoku.
