Isidlo sinokunzima ukulandelwa xa igumbi lingxolo, itheyibhile igcwele okanye kukho ukhetho oluninzi ngaxeshanye. Utshintsho oluncinci kwindawo nakwisantya lunokwenza kube lula ukuba umntu one-dementia athathe inxaxheba. Qala kwizinto aqhelele kuzo nazithandayo, uqhubeke nento esele isebenza.

Zombini [isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngokutya nokutya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) kunye [nesicebiso se-Dementia Australia ngamaxesha okutya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) ziyacebisa indawo ethe cwaka, ukutya okuqhelekileyo nexesha elaneleyo. Esi sikhokelo sigubungela indawo yesidlo noncedo losuku ngosuku. Asiyondlela yokutya okunempilo okanye uvavanyo lokugwinya.

## Singasenza njani isidlo sibe yimibutho engenakucinezela?

Ukuba umntu uyakuthanda ukuba nenkampani, hlala utye naye. Gcina ncokola ilula uze umvumele agxile kwisidlo xa efuna. Musa ukulungisa indlela asebenzisa ngayo isixhobo okanye ukubuza imibuzo eguqula isidlo sibe luvavanyo. Ukuba itheyibhile iba lingxolo okanye ixakekile, nciphisa inani labantu okanye uye kwindawo ethe cwaka.

Xa ukupheka sele kudinisa, isidlo esilungisiweyo esiqhelekileyo okanye uncedo kwinxalenye enye yomsebenzi kunokwenza isidlo silawuleke lula. I-Dementia Australia icebisa ukutya okuziswa ekhaya okanye okulungisiweyo xa kufuneka. Buza ukuba ngawaphi amatyiwa umntu awathandayo nohlobo loncedo aya kulwamkela; ukulula kufuneka kushiye indawo yoko akuthandayo.

## Ndingayenza njani itheyibhile ilandeleke lula?

Yenza isidlo sibonakale silula phambi kokuba utshintshe oko kuphakelwayo.

- **Nciphisa ingxolo.** Vala umabonakude uze unciphise ezinye izandi ezikhuphisanayo. Hlalisa umntu kude nokuhamba-hamba kwekhitshi ukuba oko kumnceda ukuba agxile.
- **Susa izinto ezongezelelweyo.** Beka kuphela izixhobo nezinongo umntu aya kuzisebenzisa. Susa iinapkini, iintyatyambo okanye ezinye izinto ezinokuphazamisa okanye ezinokuthatyathwa njengokutya.
- **Sebenzisa umahluko ocacileyo.** Icwecwe elicacileyo elahlukileyo kwimethi okanye itheyibhile lunokwenza ukutya kubonakale lula. Phepha imizobo exakekileyo kwiiplate nakwizindlu zetafile.
- **Khangela ukukhanya nobushushu.** Qinisekisa ukuba umntu unokuliqonda icwecwe, uze uvavanye ukutya neziselo phambi kokuphakela ukuze zingabi shushu kakhulu.
- **Gcina inkqubo eqhelekileyo.** Ukuba indawo ethile, ixesha okanye indlela yokulungiselela itheyibhile iyamenzela, zama ukuyigcina ifana. Tshintsha kuphela oko kubangela ingxaki.

## Ndiphakele ntoni?

Qala ngokutya umntu akwaziyo aze akuthande. Phakela isidlo esinye ngexesha elinye okanye ukhetho phakathi kwezinto ezimbini ezaziwayo, endaweni yecwecwe elizele ngokutya okuninzi okwahlukeneyo. Izabelo ezincinci zinokuvuselelwa ukuba umntu efuna ngakumbi.

Izinto azithandayo zinokutshintsha. Ukuba ukutya akuthandayo kuyalelwa, phepha ukuxabana okanye ukunyanzelisa. Nika elinye ukhetho oluqhelekileyo, vumela umntu aphumle, okanye uzame kwakhona kamva. Isidlo akufuneki silandele ixesha elingaguquguqukiyo ukuba umntu udiniwe okanye uxhalabile; gcina amaxesha aqhelekileyo okutya apho esebenza khona, uze ulungelelanise icebo ngokwezidingo zomntu.

Ukuba ukupheka sele kunzima,ilungu losapho linokunceda ekulungiseleleni ngoxa umntu ejoyina kumsebenzi okhuselekileyo awonwabela. [Isikhokelo sethu ngemisebenzi yasekhaya](/activities/household-tasks/) sinezindlela zokuhlengahlengisa imisebenzi yasekhitshini ngaphandle kokuyiguqula ibe luvavanyo.

## Ndingaxhasa njani ukuzimela komntu?

Mvumele umntu atye ngendlela ekhululekileyo nekhuselekileyo kuye. Angathanda isitya esinzulu, icephe, ukutya okuthathwa ngeminwe okanye isabelo esincinci. Musa ukukhawuleza ukulungisa into eyakhukhulekileyo okanye ukuthatha umsebenzi umntu asenako ukuwenza. Nika uncedo xa lufunwa okanye lufuneka, uze unike ixesha elaneleyo lesidlo.

Ukutya kunye kunokwenza isidlo sive njengesentlalo, ukuba umntu uyakuthanda inkampani. Hlala ecaleni, gcina incoko ilula uze umvumele agxile ekutyeni xa efuna. Phepha imibuzo ehlola inkumbulo okanye izimvo ngokuba utye kangakanani. Ukuba umntu uyayeka, linda uze ubuze ukuba ukulungele ukufumana okungakumbi.

## Kuthekani ukuba amaxesha okutya aba yingxaki?

Yeka uze ukhangele isizathu esisebenzayo. Igumbi lingaba lingxolo; ukutya kungabi kungaqhelekanga; umntu angafuna ukuya endlini encinci, ukuphumla okanye ukutshintsha indawo ahleli kuyo. Zama utshintsho olunye ngexesha elinye, njengokucoca itheyibhile, ukunika amanzi okanye ukuya kwindawo ethe cwaka. Ukuba umntu uxhalabile, yeka uze uzame kwakhona kamva endaweni yokumcinezela ukuba agqibe.

Ukuba umntu rhoqo ugqitha izidlo, ubonakala engakhululekanga, enengxaki yokusebenzisa umlomo okanye izandla, okanye enotshintsho olutsha ekutyeni okanye kubunzima bomzimba, bhala oko uqaphelayo uze uthethe nogqirha okanye nogqirha wokutya. Ingxaki entsha yokuhlafuna okanye yokugwinya, ukukhohlela kuphindaphindwa ngokutya okanye isiselo, okanye ukuqhawula umqala kufuna icebiso lengcali. Ingcali yokuthetha nolwimi inokuvavanya ukugwinya iphinde icebise ngeendlela ezikhuselekileyo. Musa ukutshintsha ubume bokutya, ukwenza iziselo zibe nzima okanye ukuzama iindlela zokondla ngokusekelwe kuluhlu oluqhelekileyo.

Icwecwe elinye elingagqitywanga alichazi ukuba kutheni umntu eyekile. Khangela umkhwa kwizidlo ezininzi: ukutya okuthile, ixesha, inqanaba lengxolo okanye indawo yokuhlala kunokwenza umsebenzi ube nzima. Ukuba ukutya okuthandwayo okuqhelekileyo kuba nzima ngequbuliso, khangela intlungu okanye ukungakhululeki kwamazinyo uze ubuze ingcali yezempilo endaweni yokuthatha ukuba umntu uyakhetha nje.

Ngemibuzo yezonyango okanye ukutya okukhethekileyo, buza ingcali yezempilo yomntu. Musa ukuqalisa ukutya okuthintelayo okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ukutshintsha isilumko sokutya ngaphandle kwecebiso. [Isikhokelo sokutya sempilo yobuchopho](/brain-health/diet/) sixoxa ngophando lokutya nomngcipheko we-dementia; sahlukile kwesi sikhokelo sisebenzayo samaxesha okutya.

## Ingaba isidlo sifanele sitywe etafileni ngamaxesha onke?

Hayi. Ukuba ukuhlala etafileni kuyadinisa okanye kuyabhidanisa, enye indawo ekhululekileyo inokusebenza ngcono, kuphela ukuba umntu unokutya ngokukhuselekileyo. Isidlo esincinci, isitya esincinci okanye ukuphumla kunokulunga ngakumbi kunokuzama ukugqiba isidlo esipheleleyo ngokwexesha.

Ukuba ukutya sele kunzima kwiintsuku ezininzi, okanye uxhalabile ngokutya, iziselo okanye ukugwinya, qhagamshelana nengcali yezempilo. Funa uncedo lwengozi ukuba kukho ingozi yokuqhawula umqala ngoko nangoko.
