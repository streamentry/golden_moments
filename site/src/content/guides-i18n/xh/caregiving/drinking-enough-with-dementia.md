Umntu ophila ne-dementia usenokulibala ukusela okanye angaziva unxano. Isiselo esingabonakaliyo okanye esinzima ukusibamba sinokulahleka lula. Utshintsho oluncinci kumkhwa lunokwenza iziselo zamkelwe lula ngoxa umntu eqhubeka elawula.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngokutya nokusela](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) sicebisa ukunikela iindebe ezincinci zolwelo imini yonke kunye nokutya okunamanzi. [Icebiso le-Dementia Australia lesondlo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/nutrition-and-dementia) licebisa nokunika iziselo eziqhelekileyo nokufikelela lula. Eli phepha linikela iingcamango eziqhelekileyo, hayi umlinganiselo wolwelo wemihla ngemihla womntu othile.

## Ndingayenza njani into yokusela ibonakale ibe lula ukuyifikelela?

Beka isiselo umntu asithandayo apho anokusibona nokusifikelela. Sebenzisa indebe okanye ibhotile eqhelekileyo ekhululekileyo ukuyibamba. Ukuba umntu uthanda ubushushu, incasa okanye indebe ethile, yikhumbule. Ukhetho olunye olulula lunokuba lula ngaphezu kokumcela akhethe kwiziselo ezininzi.

Nika iziselo njengenxalenye yemikhwa umntu ayaziyo, njengesidlo, ukuphumla okanye uhambo ngaphandle. Indebe encinci ingavakala ingafuni kakhulu kunebhotile enkulu. Nika esinye isiselo kamva ngaphandle kokukwenza kube ngumvavanyo okanye isikhumbuzo sokuba umntu ukhohliwe.

Ukuba umntu unokuthulula okanye athwale isiselo ngokwakhe, mvumele. Ukuba ufuna uncedo, buza phambi kokuthulula okanye ukubeka indebe esandleni sakhe. Indebe entsha, umbhobho okanye indawo entsha inokuvakala ingaqhelekanga, ngoko tshintsha into enye ngexesha uze ugcine oko kusebenzela umntu.

Kuhambo, zisa isiselo umntu asithandayo uze usinike ngexesha lokuphumla okanye isidlo. Ukuba abantu abaliqela bayanceda, yabelana ngesiselo nendebe umntu azithandayo ngemvume yakhe. Gcina isiselo sifikeleleke, kodwa buza phambi kokusisusa okanye ukumnika sona.

## Ndingasinika ziziphi iziselo okanye ukutya?

Qala ngeziselo umntu azonwabelayo anokuzisela ngokukhuselekileyo. Amanzi ngokunye kokhetho; ubisi, isuphu, iismuthi okanye ezinye iziselo eziqhelekileyo zinokufanela into ayithandayo nesicwangciso sakhe sokhathalelo. Ezinye iziqhamo nokunye ukutya kuneamanzi nako. [I-Alzheimer's Association ibhala iziqhamo, isuphu, iimilkshake neesmuthi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) phakathi kokhetho olunamanzi amaninzi.

Akukho mfuneko yokunyanzelisa isiselo esinye ukuba umntu siyala. Nika enye into eqhelekileyo okanye uzame kamva. Buza ugqirha okanye ingcali yezondlo womntu phambi kokutshintsha ukutya okanye isicwangciso sokusela, ngakumbi ukuba umntu sele enemiyalelo yezonyango ngolwelo.

## Ndimele ndinciphise iziselo ukunciphisa ukuya endlini encinci?

Musa ukunciphisa ulwelo ukuphepha iingozi ngaphandle kokuba ingcali yezempilo inikele isicwangciso somntu. [I-Alzheimer's Association iyalumkisa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence) ukuba ukunciphisa ulwelo kunokubangela ukuphelelwa ngamanzi emzimbeni nezinye iingxaki. Umntu ofuna uncedo lokufumana okanye ukusebenzisa indlu encinci angaxhamla kolunye uhlobo lwenkxaso. [Isikhokelo sethu sokungabambi umchamo](/caregiving/toileting-and-incontinence-with-dementia/) sicubungula imikhwa yendlu encinci nobumfihlo.

Phalaza iminikelo imini yonke kunokucela umntu asele kakhulu kanye kanye. Landela nayiphi na imiyalelo yezonyango esele ikhona uze ubuze ugqirha ukuba lingakanani inani elifanelekileyo kumntu. Musa ukuthembela kwinani eliqhelekileyo le-intanethi; iimfuno zolwelo ziyahluka, kwaye abanye abantu banesicwangciso sezonyango solwelo.

## Ndimele ndiqaphele utshintsho luni?

Qaphela oko kwahlukileyo kwindlela yomntu eqhelekileyo. Angasela kancinci kunesiqhelo, ashiye isiselo asithandayo singachukunyiswanga okanye adinge uncedo olungakumbi kunangaphambili. Umlomo owomileyo, isiyezi, ukudinwa okungaqhelekanga okanye utshintsho lokuchama nazo ziizizathu zokubuza ingcali yezempilo. Ezi mpawu zinokuba nezinye izizathu, ngoko musa ukuzifumanisela ukuphelelwa ngamanzi emzimbeni.

Bhala oko ukuqapheleyo nokuba kwakuxa. Bandakanya ukuba umntu wayegula, esendaweni eshushu okanye enobunzima bokubamba okanye ukugwinya isiselo. [I-Alzheimer's Association iphawula ukuba iimpawu zokuphelelwa ngamanzi emzimbeni zinokuba nzima ukuziqaphela komntu one-dementia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster). Ukuba kukho utshintsho olukhawulezileyo okanye uxhalabile, qhagamshelana nogqirha okanye enye ingcali yezempilo.

## Kuthiwa ntoni ukuba ukusela kuye kwaba nzima?

Ukuba umntu uyakhohlela, uyaminxwa, ugcina ukutya okanye isiselo emlonyeni, okanye unobunzima obutsha bokugwinya, yeka uze ucele icebiso kwingcali yezempilo efanelekileyo. Musa ukugquma iziselo, ukutshintsha ukuxinana kokutya okanye ukuzama iindlela zokugwinya ngaphandle kokuba ingcali iluncomile olo hlelo kumntu. [Isikhokelo sethu sezidlo](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) sicubungula oko kufuneka ukwenze ngobunzima obutsha bokuhlafuna okanye bokugwinya.

Ukuba umntu une-mlomo owomileyo, amazinyo abuhlungu okanye amazinyo enziwe angakhululekanga, mbuze oko akuqapheleyo uze uqhagamshelane nogqirha wamazinyo okanye ugqirha. Intlungu yomlomo inokwenza ukusela kube nzima ngakumbi. Jonga [isikhokelo sethu sokhathalelo lomlomo](/caregiving/oral-care-with-dementia/) ngeendlela ezithambileyo zokuxhasa ukhathalelo lomlomo.

Ukudideka okuqubulayo kufuna uncedo lwezonyango olukhawulezileyo. [Isikhokelo se-NHS ngokudideka okuqubulayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) siyalela abantu ukuba bangazifumanisi ngokwabo. Sebenzisa iinkonzo zasekhaya zemeko eyingxamisekileyo okanye inkqubo yenkathalo ekhawulezileyo apho uhlala khona.

## Ndingazinika njani iziselo ngaphandle koxinzelelo?

Yenza isiphakamiso, uze unike umntu ixesha lokugqiba. Angakhetha ukusela kamva okanye akhethe indebe eyahlukileyo. Phepha ukulungisa, ukuthethathethana okanye ukunyanzelisa isiselo. Ukuba rhoqo uyala, jonga ukuba isiselo sifikeleleke lula na, ukuba uyayithanda incasa nobushushu, nokuba umntu uziva ekhululekile kwindawo.

Buza umntu ukuba yintoni emnceda uze wabelane ngezinto athanda ngazo nabanye abancedi kuphela ukuba uyavuma. Indebe eqhelekileyo, indawo ekhethiweyo okanye umzuzu othe cwaka unokwenza ukusela kube lula. Ukuba inkxalabo iyaqhubeka, buza ugqirha okanye ingcali yezondlo ngecebiso elithe ngqo kumntu.

Injongo kukwenza iziselo zifumaneke ngendlela umntu ayamkelayo. Nika ngobubele, hlonipha ukhetho lwakhe uze ufune icebiso lengcali xa iimfuno zitshintsha.
