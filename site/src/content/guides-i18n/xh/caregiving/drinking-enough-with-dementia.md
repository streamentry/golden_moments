Umntu ohlala nedementia unokukhohlwa ukuphuza okanye angaziva ukoma. Isiselo esingabonakali okanye esinzima ukusibamba sinokungaphawulwa lula. Utshintsho oluncinci kwindlela yemihla lunokwenza iziselo zamkeleke kakhulu, kwaye zigcine umntu elawula.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association malunga nokudla nokutya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) sicebisa ukunika iikomityi ezincinci zamanzi kwimini yonke kunye nokutya okunamanzi. [Icebiso le-Dementia Australia ngesondlo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/nutrition-and-dementia) lichaza nalo iziselo ezaziwayo kunye nokufikeleleka. Eli phepha linikezela ngeengcamango jikelele, hayi umda wamanzi wemihla kumntu othile.

## Ndingayenza njani isiselo sibonakale kwaye sifikeleleke?

Beka isiselo umntu asithandayo apho abona khona kwaye afikelele kuso. Sebenzisa ikomityi okanye ibhotile ejwayelekileyo elula ukuyibamba. Ukuba umntu uthanda ubushushu, incasa okanye ikomityi ethile, gcina oko engqondweni. Isinqumlo esincinci sinokulula kunokumbuza ukuba akhethe phakathi kweziselo ezininzi.

Nikela iziselo ngaphakathi kwezinto ezaziwayo umntu azenzayo, njengesidlo, ikhefu okanye ukuphuma. Ikomityi encinci inokuziva ingafuni kakhulu kunengilazi enkulu. Yinike esinye isiselo kamva ngaphandle kokuyenza ibe luvavanyo okanye isikhumbuzo sokuba ulibele.

Ukuba umntu unokuthela okanye athwale isiselo ngokwakhe, mvumele. Ukuba efuna uncedo, buza ngaphambi kokuthela okanye ukubeka ikomityi esandleni sakhe. Ikomityi entsha okanye indawo entsha inokungaziwa ngumntu, ngoko tshintsha into enye ngexesha kwaye ugcine oko kusebenza kumntu.

Xa uphuma nomntu, zisa isiselo azithandayo kwaye ukinikele ngexesha lekhefu okanye lesidlo. Ukuba abanye abantu bakunceda, yabelana ngesiselo nekomityi ethandwayo ngemvume yomntu. Gcina isiselo sifikeleleka, kodwa buza ngaphambi kokusihambisa okanye ukusinikela esandleni sakhe.

## Ziziphi iziselo okanye ukutya engingazinika?

Qala ngeziselo umntu azithandayo kwaye akwazi ukuzifaka kakhuselekileyo. Amanzi ngelinye ikhetho; ubisi, isuphu, iismoothie okanye ezinye iziselo ezaziwayo nazo zinokufanelana neenkhetho zakhe kunye necwangciso yonakekelo. Ezinye iziqhamo nezinye iintlobo zokutya nazo zinamanzi. [I-Alzheimer's Association idwelisa iziqhamo, isuphu, iimilkshake kunye neesmoothie](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) phakathi kweenkhetho ezinamanzi amaninzi.

Akukho mfuneko yokunyanzela isiselo esinye ukuba umntu asale. Nikela enye into ethandwayo okanye zama kamva. Buza udokotela okanye ingcali yezondlo ngaphambi kokutshintsha icwangciso yakhe yokutya okanye yeziselo, ingakumbi ukuba umntu esele enemiyalelo yonyango malunga namanzi.

## Ndingalinciphisa ngeziselo ukunciphisa ukuya endlwini yangasese?

Musa ukunciphisa amanzi ukuthintela iingozi ngaphandle kokuba ingcali yezempilo inike icwangciso yomntu ngamnye. [I-Alzheimer's Association ilumkisa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence) ukuba ukunciphisa iziselo kunokubangela ukomiswa ngamanzi nezinye iingxaki. Umntu ofuna uncedo lokufumana indlu yangasese okanye ukuyisebenzisa unokufuna uncedo oluhlukileyo. Isikhokelo sethu [sokulawula ukuchama nokuya endlwini yangasese](/caregiving/toileting-and-incontinence-with-dementia/) sigubungela iindlela zokuhamba kwendlu yangasese nemfihlo.

Sasaza unikezelo lwamanzi ngaphakathi kwemini endaweni yokucela umntu ukuba aphuze kakhulu ngexesha linye. Landela nawuphi na umyalelo wonyango okhoyo kwaye ubuze udokotela ukuba ngaliphi inani liyafaneleka kumntu. Musa ukuthembela kwinani elikwi-intanethi; iimfuno zamanzi ziyahluka, kwaye abanye abantu banecwangciso yonyango yamanzi.

## Yintoni ekufuneka ndiyiqaphele?

Qaphela into engafaniyo nendlela eqhelekileyo yomntu. Usenokuphuza kakhulu ngaphantsi kwesiqhelo, ayeke ukuphuza isiselo asithandayo okanye afune uncedo oluninzi ngaphezu kwangaphambili. Umlomo owomileyo, ukuzungulezelwa, ukudinwa okungaqhelekanga okanye utshintsho kwindlela yokuchama nakho zinokuba zizizathu zokuhlola nengcali yezempilo. Ezi mpawu zinokuba nezinye izizathu, ngoko musa ukuzixilonga ukomiswa ngamanzi ngokwakho.

Bhala phantsi oko uqaphelileyo kunye nexesha. Qwalasela ukuba umntu wayegulile, ebushushini okanye enobunzima ekubambeni okanye ekuginyeni isiselo. [I-Alzheimer's Association iphawula ukuba iimpawu zokomiswa ngamanzi zinokuba nzima ukuzibona kumntu onedementia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster). Ukuba kukho utshintsho olungazelelwanga okanye uxhalabile, qhagamshelana nodokotela okanye enye ingcali yezempilo.

## Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba ukuphuza kuye kwaba nzima?

Ukuba umntu ukhohlela, ukhwankqisa okanye ubambe ukutya okanye iziselo emlonyeni, okanye unobunzima obutsha bokuginya, yima ucele icebiso kwingcali eqeqeshiweyo yezempilo. Musa ukuxuba iziselo, ukutshintsha ukuqina kokutya okanye ukuzama iindlela zokuginya ngaphandle kokuba ingcali icebise icwangciso yomntu. Isikhokelo sethu [sexesha lesidlo](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) sichaza into yokwenza malunga nobunzima obutsha ekutyeni nasekuginyeni.

Ukuba umntu unomlomo owomileyo, amazinyo abuhlungu okanye izinyo lobuxoki elingalunganga, buza ukuba uziva njani, kwaye qhagamshelana nodokotela wamazinyo okanye ingcali yezempilo. Ubuhlungu emlonyeni lunokwenza ukuphuza kube nzima. Bona [isikhokelo sethu sokunakekela umlomo](/caregiving/oral-care-with-dementia/) ngeendlela ezithambileyo zokuxhasa unakekelo lomlomo.

Ukudideka okungazelelwanga kufuna uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko. [Isikhokelo se-NHS malunga nokudideka okungazelelwanga](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sichaza ukuba abantu mabangazixilongi. Sebenzisa iinkonzo zesiphuthuma okanye inkqubo yonyango lwangoko kwindawo ohlala kuyo.

## Ndingazinika njani iziselo ngaphandle koxinzelelo?

Nikela isiselo, uze unike umntu ixesha lokuthatha isigqibo. Usenokuphuza kamva okanye akhethe ikomityi eyahlukileyo. Musa ukumlungisa, ukumkhathaza ngokuxoxa okanye ukumphoqa ukuba aphuze. Ukuba edla ngokwala, jonga ukuba isiselo asifikeleleki na, ukuba akuthandi incasa okanye ubushushu, kunye nokuba umntu akazolanga kwindawo leyo.

Buza umntu ukuba yintoni ekunceda, kwaye wabelane ngezinto ezincedayo kuphela nabanye abancedayo, ngemvume yakhe. Ikomityi eyaziwayo, indawo ekhethwe ngumntu okanye ixesha elithe cwaka linokwenza ukuphuza kube lula. Ukuba ingxaki ihleli, buza udokotela okanye ingcali yezondlo icebiso elikhethekileyo kumntu.

Injongo kukuba iziselo zifikeleleke ngendlela umntu ayamkelayo. Nikela ngobubele, hlonipha ukhetho lwakhe kwaye ufumane icebiso lobuchwephesha xa iimfuno zitshintsha.
