Isilwanyana sinokuba yinxalenye yemihla ngemihla yomntu kunye nobomi bosapho. Ukutya, ukuhamba nesilwanyana okanye ukuhlala nesilwanyana kunokuba ngumsebenzi umntu afuna ukuqhubeka ewenza. I-dementia inokwenza ezinye izinto zibe nzima, kodwa oko akuthethi ukuba omnye umntu kufuneka athathe umsebenzi ngokuzenzekelayo.

I-[Alzheimer's Society's pet-care guidance](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/how-plan-your-pet-care-after-diagnosed-dementia) icebisa ukucwangcisa unkathalo lwesilwanyana kwangaphambili kwaye kusetyenziswe imikhwa okanye izikhumbuzi. I-[icebiso lomsebenzi le-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/activities-people-dementia) liquka izilwanyana phakathi koomsebenzi abanokuba nentsingiselo. Injongo apha luncedo olusebenzayo komntu nesilwanyana, ayisiyo ingxelo yokuba izilwanyana ziyayiphilisa i-dementia.

## Sinokwenza njani umkhwa wonkathalo lwesilwanyana ulule?

Qala ngokubuza umntu imisebenzi ayithandayo kwaye afuna ukuyigcina. Umntu omnye usenokuthanda ukondla ikati kodwa angathandi ukucoca indawo yalo; omnye usenokufuna ukuhamba nenja nelungu losapho. Gcina icwangciso ithe ngqo kumntu, kwisilwanyana nakuncedo olukhoyo.

- **Dibanisa unkathalo nomkhwa okhoyo.** Beka ukutya ngaphandle emva kwesidlo sakusasa, jonga isitya samanzi xa wenza iti, okanye uhambe nenja emva kwenkqubo yeredio ejwayelekileyo. Isignali esele ikhona kwimihla inokulula ukuyilandela kunokuba yishedyuli entsha.
- **Gcina izixhobo kwindawo enye eqhelekileyo.** Gcina ukutya kwesilwanyana, ilali okanye ibrashi kunye apho umntu alindele khona, ngelixa izinto ezinzima okanye iikhemikhali zibekwe ngokhuseleko. Ilebheli elula okanye isikhumbuzo esibhalwe phantsi siyancedisa ukuba umntu uyayifuna.
- **Yenza isikhumbuzo esinye sibonakale.** Isiqwenga sencwadi esimfutshane ecaleni kwezixhobo zesilwanyana okanye ingeniso kwikhalenda inokwanela. Gwema ukuzalisa indlu ngezikhumbuzo ezenza umntu azive ejongwa okanye elungiswa.
- **Yekela ixesha somsebenzi.** Ukuhamba okanye umkhwa wokondla kunokuthatha ixesha elide kunangaphambili. Musamkhawulezisi umntu xa ekonwabela ixesha abanalo kunye.

Ukuba abantu abaninzi bakwabelana ngonkathalo lwesilwanyana, sebenzisa uluhlu olufutshane olubonisa ukuba ngubani ophatha umsebenzi ngamnye ngalo mhla. Gcina kufuphi nezixhobo okanye usebenzise isikhumbuzo umntu asele eyithanda. Gwema ukudala ezinye izilumkiso okanye iinqaku xa isignali enye ecacileyo sanele.

[Isikhokelo somkhwa wemihla ngemihla](/activities/daily-routine/) sinezinye iingcamango zokudibanisa umsebenzi nenxalenye ethile yemini eqhelekileyo.

## Ziluncedo luni ezinokunikezelwa ngusapho okanye ngabahlobo?

Nika umsebenzi othile endaweni yesithembiso jikelele. Umhlobo unokuthenga ukutya kwesilwanyana ngenyanga nganye, unxibelelwano lwakho lunokujoyina ukuhamba, okanye ilungu losapho linokuya nesilwanyana kugqirha wezilwanyana. Ukuba umntu ufuna ukuqhubeka ekondla okanye ekhuhla isilwanyana, lungiselela uncedo ngokusekelwe kwaloo ndima endaweni yokuyisusa.

Vumelanani ngokuba ngubani oza kunceda ukuba umnakekeli oqhelekileyo akakho okanye umntu akaphilanga kakuhle. Xelela loo mncedisi ukuba izixhobo zikuphi, ukuba umntu uthanda luhlobo luphi lomkhwa kunye nokuba ubize bani ukuba isilwanyana sigula. Qinisekisa ukuba umncedisi uyakhululeka nesilwanyana kwaye nesilwanyana sikhululekile naye.

Ukuba umntu ufumana uncedo kumboneleli wonkathalo, buza ukuba unkathalo lwesilwanyana lunokudibana njani nokutyelela komncedisi. Imvume kunye nemithetho yasekhaya ibalulekile: musacinga ukuba umnakekeli ohlawulelwayo unokuhamba, ondle okanye aphathe isilwanyana njengenxalenye yomsebenzi wakhe. Vumelana ngomsebenzi nomntu, nomboneleli kunye nolungu losapho olunoxanduva lwesilwanyana.

## Sinokuqaphela njani xa kufuneka uncedo oluninzi?

Qaphela iziganeko ezithile endaweni yokugweba umntu ngenqanaba okanye ngamanqaku. Isitya esihlala singenanto, ukuhamba okuphosiweyo okushiya isilwanyana sikhathazekile, okanye umntu obonakala edinwe emva kokunyamekela isilwanyana usenokubonisa ukuba umsebenzi omnye ufuna uncedo oluninzi. Utshintsho kwindlela, kwisidlo okanye kwimbonakalo yesilwanyana nalo lulusizi lokuba unxibelelane negqirha wezilwanyana.

Thetha nomntu malunga nento ayiqaphelileyo. Usenokwamkela isikhumbuzo, ukuhamba ndawonye okanye umncedisi womsebenzi omnye ngelixa esagcina enye yonke icwangciso. Ukuba impilo, ukuhamba okanye ukhuseleko kuchaphazela ukukwazi kwakhe ukunyamekela isilwanyana, buza iqela lakhe lonkathalo okanye inkonzo yasekuhlaleni ukuba ikunceda uqwalasele izikhetho.

Musalinde ide ibe yingxaki ukuze ucinge ngonkathalo lwexesha lokungena. Isikhokelo [sokuhlala wedwa kunye ne-dementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/) sinezindlela zokuvumelana ngokujongwa ngamaxesha nongcedo oluzenzekayo ngelixa ugcina ukhetho lomntu phakathi kwento yonke.

## Sicwangcise njani utshintsho kwixesha elizayo?

Buza umntu ukuba ngubani amthembayo ukuba anyamekele isilwanyana ukuba usesibhedlele, ukuba akekho ekhaya okanye engasakwazi ukwenza umsebenzi othile. Bhala umkhwa oqhelekileyo wesilwanyana, ukutya, iinkcukacha zoqhagamshelwano lugqirha wezilwanyana kunye nabantu abavumelene ukuncedisa. Gcina olu lwazi apho umntu nabancedisi abathembekileyo banokulufumana khona, ngemvume yomntu.

Icwangciso inokutshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha. Yiphonononge xa imikhwa, ukuhamba okanye uncedo lomntu lutshintsha, okanye xa iimfuno zesilwanyana ziguquka. Ukuba kuyimfuneko ukuhamba, bandakanya umntu ekuthatheni isigqibo sokuba isilwanyana siya kuhlala phi kunye nendlela angahlala ngayo enxibelelwano xa oko kusebenza. Ukucwangciswa kwangaphambili kuvumela wonke umntu ixesha elingakumbi lokuqwalasela ukhetho olukhusela izifiso zomntu kunye nonkathalo lwesilwanyana.

## Yintoni ukuba ukutyelela isilwanyana okanye umsebenzi awukhululekanga?

Vumela umntu nesilwanyana bawumisele umgangatho. Nika ixesha elibanzi kuphela ukuba zombini zibonakala zikhululekile. Ukutyelela okufutshane, ukuhlala kufuphi okanye ukujonga umfanekiso wesilwanyana kunokwanela. Yima ukuba umntu uba nexhala, isilwanyana sizama ukuhamba kude okanye sibonakala sixinezelekile. [Iingcamango zemisebenzi](/activities/ideas/) kule sayithi zibandakanya ezinye iindlela ezaziwayo zokuchitha ixesha kunye.
