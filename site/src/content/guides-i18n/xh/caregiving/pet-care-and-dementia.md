Isilwanyana sinokuba yinxalenye yenkqubo yemihla ngemihla yomntu nobomi bosapho. Ukondla, ukuhamba okanye ukuhlala nje nesilwanyana kunokuba ngumsebenzi umntu afuna ukuqhubeka ngawo. I-dementia inokwenza eminye imisebenzi ibe nzima, kodwa oko akuthethi ukuba omnye umntu kufuneka athathe ngokuzenzekelayo.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Society sokhathalelo lwezilwanyana](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/how-plan-your-pet-care-after-diagnosed-dementia) sicebisa ukucwangcisa ukhathalelo lwesilwanyana kwangoko nokusebenzisa iinkqubo okanye izikhumbuzo. [Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia semisebenzi](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/activities-people-dementia) sibandakanya izilwanyana phakathi kwemisebenzi enokuba nentsingiselo. Injongo apha kukuxhasa ngokusebenzayo umntu nesilwanyana, ayikuthi izilwanyana zinyanga i-dementia.

## Singayenza njani inkqubo yokhathalelo lwesilwanyana ibe lula?

Qala ngokubuza umntu ukuba yimiphi imisebenzi ayonwabelayo nafuna ukuqhubeka nayo. Omnye umntu angathanda ukondla ikati kodwa ingenjalo ukucoca ibhokisi yayo; omnye angafuna ukuhamba nenja nelungu losapho. Gcina icebo likhethekile kumntu, esilwanyaneni nakwinkxaso ekhoyo.

- **Nxulumanisa ukhathalelo nenkqubo esele ikhona.** Beka ukutya emva kwesidlo sakusasa, jonga isitya samanzi xa uphekela iti, okanye uhambe nenja emva kwenkqubo yerediyo eqhelekileyo. Isikhumbuzo esele sisemini sinokulandelwa lula kunexesha elitsha.
- **Gcina izixhobo kwindawo enye eqhelekileyo.** Gcina ukutya kwesilwanyana, ityathanga okanye ibrashi kunye apho umntu ewalindele khona, ngoxa izinto ezinzima okanye iikhemikhali zigcinwa ngokukhuselekileyo. Ileyibhile okanye inqaku elilula linokunceda ukuba umntu liyafuna.
- **Yenza isikhumbuzo esinye sibonakale.** Inqaku elifutshane ecaleni kwezixhobo zesilwanyana okanye into kwikhalenda inokwanela. Phepha ukugcwalisa ikhaya ngezikhumbuzo ezenza umntu azive ejongwa okanye elungiswa.
- **Shiya ixesha lomsebenzi.** Ukuhamba okanye inkqubo yokondla inokuthatha ixesha elide kunangaphambili. Musa ukumkhawulezisa umntu ukuba uyakonwabela ixesha kunye.

Ukuba abantu abaninzi babelana ngokhathalelo lwesilwanyana, sebenzisa uluhlu olufutshane ukubonisa ukuba ngubani owenza umsebenzi ngamnye ngalo mhla. Lugcine ecaleni kwezixhobo okanye usebenzise isikhumbuzo umntu asele eluthanda. Phepha ukwenza izilumkiso okanye amanqaku angaphezulu xa isikhumbuzo esinye esicacileyo sanele.

[Isikhokelo senkqubo yemihla ngemihla](/activities/daily-routine/) sinezimvo ezingakumbi zokunxulumanisa umsebenzi nenxalenye eqhelekileyo yosuku.

## Yiloluphi uncedo lunokunikwa ngamalungu osapho okanye abahlobo?

Nika umsebenzi othile endaweni yesithembiso esijikelele. Umhlobo angathatha ukutya kwesilwanyana nyanga nganye, ummelwane angajoyina ekuhambeni, okanye ilungu losapho lingaya kwintlanganiso kagqirha wezilwanyana. Ukuba umntu efuna ukuqhubeka nokondla okanye ukuxubha isilwanyana, lungiselela uncedo ngokujikeleze eso sikhundla endaweni yokusisusa.

Vumelanani ukuba ngubani oya kunceda ukuba umnyamekeli oqhelekileyo akekho okanye umntu uyagula. Xelela loo mncedisi ukuba izixhobo ziphi, yeyiphi inkqubo umntu ayithandayo nokuba kuqhagamshelwana nabani ukuba isilwanyana sibonakala sigula. Qinisekisa ukuba umncedisi ukhululekile nesilwanyana nokuba isilwanyana sikhululekile naye.

Ukuba umntu ufumana inkxaso kumnikezeli wokhathalelo, buza ukuba ukhathalelo lwesilwanyana lungangena njani kutyelelo. Imvume nemigaqo yekhaya ibalulekile: musa ukucinga ukuba umnyamekeli ohlawulelwayo angahamba, ondle okanye aphathe isilwanyana njengenxalenye yomsebenzi wakhe. Vumelana ngomsebenzi nomntu, umnikezeli nelungu losapho elinoxanduva lwesilwanyana.

## Singazi njani ukuba kufuneka inkxaso engakumbi?

Qaphela iziganeko ezikhethekileyo endaweni yokugweba umntu ngenqanaba okanye ngamanqaku. Isitya esihlala singenanto, ukuhamba okuyeqiweyo okushiya isilwanyana sixhalabile, okanye umntu obonakala edinwe emva kokhathalela isilwanyana kunokubonisa ukuba umsebenzi omnye ufuna inkxaso engakumbi. Utshintsho kwindlela isilwanyana esiziphatha ngayo, kwisilumko sokutya okanye kwimbonakalo yaso nalo lunesizathu sokuqhagamshelana nogqirha waso wezilwanyana.

Thetha nomntu ngoko akuqaphelileyo. Angamkela isikhumbuzo, ukuhamba kunye okanye umncedisi womsebenzi omnye ngoxa egcina yonke enye inkqubo. Ukuba ingxaki yempilo, yokushukuma okanye yokhuseleko ichaphazela amandla omntu okhathalela isilwanyana, cela iqela lakhe lokhathalelo okanye inkonzo yenkxaso yasekuhlaleni ukuba ikunceda ufumane ukhetho.

Musa ukulinda ingxaki phambi kokucinga ngokhathalelo olungasemva. [Isikhokelo sokuhlala wedwa ne-dementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/) sinezindlela zokuvumelana ngokubukwa noncedo olusebenzayo ngoxa ukhetho lomntu lujikeleza.

## Singalungiselela njani utshintsho lwexesha elizayo?

Buza umntu ukuba ngubani anokumthemba ukuba akhathalele isilwanyana ukuba usesibhedlele, akekho ekhaya okanye engasakwazi ukwenza umsebenzi othile. Bhala inkqubo eqhelekileyo yesilwanyana, ukutya, iinkcukacha zonxibelelwano zikagqirha wezilwanyana nabantu abavumileyo ukunceda. Gcina ulwazi kwindawo apho umntu nabancedisi abathembekileyo banokulufumana khona, ngemvume yomntu.

Icebo lingatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha. Lijonge kwakhona xa inkqubo yomntu, ukushukuma okanye inkxaso kutshintsha, okanye ukuba iimfuno zesilwanyana ziyatshintsha. Ukuba ukufuduka kuyimfuneko, bandakanya umntu ekugqibeni ukuba isilwanyana siya kuhlala phi nokuba angaqhubeka njani ngonxibelelwano xa kunokwenzeka. Ukucwangcisa kwangoko kunika wonke umntu ixesha elingakumbi lokucinga ngokhetho olukhusela izinto umntu azifunayo nokhathalelo lwesilwanyana.

## Kuthekani ukuba utyelelo lwesilwanyana okanye umsebenzi awukhululekanga?

Vumela umntu nesilwanyana basethe isantya. Nika ixesha kunye kuphela ukuba zombini ziyakhululeka. Utyelelo olufutshane, ukuhlala ecaleni okanye ukujonga ifoto yesilwanyana kunokwanela. Yeka ukuba umntu uyaxhalaba, isilwanyana siyazama ukuhamba okanye sibonakala sixhalabile. [Izimvo zomsebenzi](/activities/ideas/) zesi sayithi zibandakanya ezinye iindlela eziqhelekileyo zokuchitha ixesha kunye.
