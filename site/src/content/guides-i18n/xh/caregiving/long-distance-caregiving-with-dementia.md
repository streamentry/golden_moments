Ukuhlala kude kunokwenza ukuba kube nzima ukwazi ukuba luluphi uncedo olufunekayo, kodwa umgama awususi zonke iindlela zokuthatha inxaxheba. Usenokukwazi ukulungiselela iinkonzo, ukuphatha umsebenzi umntu avumayo, okanye ukuxhasa umntu onyamekela imihla ngemihla. Qala ngokubuza umntu onedementia ukuba ufuna ntoni nokuba ufuna bani ababandakanywe.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokunyamekela ukude](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) sichaza iindima ezinjengokulungiselela izicelo zokubona ugqirha, ukujonga iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni, ukulungelelanisa nabancedisi abakufutshane nokunika umnyamekeli wemihla ngemihla ikhefu. [I-Family Caregiver Alliance ikwathi](https://www.caregiver.org/faq/how-can-i-help-with-caregiving-from-far-away/) ukufumana iinkonzo nokucwangcisa utyelelo kunokuba nzima usemgama. Esi sikhokelo sinika iingcinga zokucwangcisa ezibanzi; iinkonzo nemithetho yasekuhlaleni iyahluka.

## Ndingenza ntoni ndisemgama?

Khetha imisebenzi esebenzisekayo efanele oko umntu akufunayo noko unokukwenza ngokuthembeka. Inkxaso inokuquka:

- **Lungiselela izicelo zokubona ugqirha.** Buza ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo ekuqhagamsheleni kwiklinikhi, ekugcineni ikhalenda okanye ekucwangciseni ukuthutha.
- **Jonga iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni.** Jonga ukuhanjiswa kokutya, ukuthutha, inkxaso yasekhaya, iinkqubo zosuku zabantu abadala okanye amaqela abanyamekeli apho umntu ehlala khona. Qinisekisa iinkcukacha nomnikezeli; ukufumaneka kwahluka ngokwendawo.
- **Phatha amaphepha okanye iiodolo ezivunyiweyo.** Usenokunceda ngokulungiselela iifomu okanye ukulungiselela izinto ezifunekayo ukuba umntu uyacela. Iirekhodi zemali, inshorensi nezempilo zifuna ukulumkela okongezelelekileyo; sebenzisa [isikhokelo sethu soncedo lwemali nokhuseleko kwiimpazamo](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/) uze ujonge naliphi na igunya okanye imfuneko yemvume.
- **Yenza ixesha lomnyamekeli wasekuhlaleni.** Ngexesha lotyelelo, buza ukuba yeyiphi imisebenzi okanye ikhefu elinokunceda. Usenokulungiselela isidlo, ugqume isicelo esicwangcisiweyo okanye uchithe ixesha nomntu ngelixa umncedisi wasekuhlaleni ephumla.
- **Hlala uxhumene ngendlela umntu ayonwabelayo.** Buza ukuba uthanda ifowuni, umnxeba wevidiyo, ileta okanye utyelelo. Ifowuni inokuba yincoko kunokuba ngumvavanyo ngokutya, amayeza okanye inkumbulo.

[I-Alzheimer's Association ithi abantu abanika inkxaso usemgama basenokungakwazi ukugwebela iimfuno ngqo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage). Xa kunokwenzeka, qhagamshelana nabantu abambona umntu imihla ngemihla uze ulungiselele utyelelo kwindawo yomntu ngokwakhe. Loo mbono unokongeza ulwazi oluluncedo, kodwa aluyondawo yokubuza umntu okanye yokufumana uvavanyo lwengcali.

## Ndingalungelelanisa njani nabancedisi basekuhlaleni?

Qala ngokubuza umntu ukuba ufuna bani ababandakanywe nokuba ziziphi iindaba aziva ekhululekile ukwabelana ngazo. Usenokufuna isihlobo esinye silungelelanise izicelo zokubona ugqirha, ummelwane ajonge umba othile, okanye angafuni kujonga rhoqo konke konke. Musa ukongeza umntu kwincoko yokunyamekela nje kuba ehlala ekufutshane.

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, setha icebo elilula loqhagamshelwano nabantu abakhethayo:

- **Khetha umqhagamshelwana omnye oqhelekileyo.** Vumelanani ukuba ngubani oza kuthumela iindaba eziqhelekileyo nokuba rhoqo kangakanani.
- **Chaza umntu ophakathi.** Khetha ukuba ngubani onokujonga ukuba umncedisi wasekuhlaleni oqhelekileyo akafumaneki.
- **Yahlula iindaba eziqhelekileyo kwinkxalabo ekhawulezileyo.** Vumelanani ukuba umntu wasekuhlaleni uza kukufikelela njani ukuba ukhuseleko okanye impilo yomntu itshintsha ngesiquphe.
- **Gcina imisebenzi icacile.** Bhala phantsi ukuba ngubani oza kuphatha izicelo zokubona ugqirha, ukuthutha, iifowuni zeenkonzo okanye uncedo lwasekhaya. Buza ngaphambi kokutshintsha isicwangciso.

[I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa unxibelelwano rhoqo nosapho, abamelwane nabanikezeli bonyamekelo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ungabuza kwakhona umncedisi wasekuhlaleni ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso ayidingayo. Isiphakamiso esifutshane, esithile sisenokuba lula ukusisebenzisa kunokuthi "ndazise ukuba udinga nantoni na."

## Ulwazi luni ugqirha okanye umnikezeli wenkonzo anokwabelana nalo nam?

Buza ugqirha womntu okanye umnikezeli wenkonzo ukuba yeyiphi imvume okanye amaphepha ayafunayo phambi kokwabelana ngeendaba. [I-Alzheimer's Association ithi umnikezeli usenokufuna ukukhululwa phambi kokuxoxa ngonyamekelo nesihlobo esikude](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Iifomu nemithetho ziyahluka, ngoko buza umnikezeli endaweni yokucinga ukuba ubudlelwane losapho bukunika ukufikelela.

Hlonipha unqweno lomntu malunga nokuba ngubani ofumana ulwazi. Yabelana kuphela noko kufunekayo kumsebenzi ovunyiweyo, uze ugcine iinkcukacha zonxibelelwano okanye amanqaku engasese. Ukuba kuquka ixwebhu lomthetho okanye igunya elisesikweni, buza ingcali efanelekileyo kwindawo yomntu ukuba liquka ntoni. Esi sikhokelo asimiseli igunya lomthetho lomntu.

## Ndingalwenza njani utyelelo lube luncedo kuthi sobabini?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukuchitha ixesha njani. Shiya indawo yesidlo, ukuhamba, umculo okanye incoko ezolileyo, kungekhona izicelo zokubona ugqirha neengcambu zomsebenzi kuphela.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo ekulungelelaniseni, sebenzisa inxalenye yotyelelo ukudibana nabancedisi basekuhlaleni, ukuthetha nogqirha okanye ukujonga ukuba uququzelelo lusebenza na. Buza ngaphambi kokujonga iindawo zabucala okanye iirekhodi. Yabelana ngenkxalabo ngokuzolileyo uze ugxile kutshintsho oluthile, kungekhona ukugweba ukuba umntu umelana njani.

[I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukusebenzisa utyelelo ukuphinda uxhumane nokulungelelanisa nabanyamekeli basekuhlaleni](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ukuba uqaphela inkxalabo, xoxa ngayo nomntu nabantu abamthembileyo. Utyelelo olunye aluyokwazi ukumisela umfanekiso opheleleyo wempilo okanye amandla omntu.

## Kuthekani ukuba iimfuno okanye inkxaso yomntu zitshintsha?

Phonononga isicwangciso xa umntu ecela uncedo olwahlukileyo, umkhuba uba nzima okanye umncedisi wasekuhlaleni engasakwazi ukuqhubeka. Buza ukuba yintoni umntu afuna ukuqhubeka eyenza nokuba ngumsebenzi muphi ofuna ingqalelo ngoku. Ugqirha, usomashishini wezentlalo okanye inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yabantu abadala isenokunceda ukuchonga amanye amathuba. Ngolwazi ngoncedo lwasekuhlaleni, bona izikhokelo zethu [zonyamekelo lwasekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) kunye [nokuhlala wedwa nedementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/).

Qinisekisa ukuba kukho umntu wasekuhlaleni onokuphendula kwimeko ekhawulezileyo. [Isikhokelo sethu sokucwangcisa ongxamiseko](/caregiving/emergency-planning-dementia/) sinokunceda umntu nabancedisi bakhe abakhethiweyo ukubhala phantsi abaqhagamshelwana necebo elinye. Ngokungavumelani ngokuba ngubani oza kwenza ntoni, bona [iincoko zosapho ngeendima zonyamekelo](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/).

Umgama unokunciphisa uncedo lwezandla, kodwa awumiseli ukuba umntu ukhathalela kangakanani okanye unokunegalelo kwintoni. Gcina isicwangciso sisekelwe kwiminqweno yomntu, imisebenzi yokwenene nenkxaso ekhoyo ekufutshane.
