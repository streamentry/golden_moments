Ukuhlala kude kunokwenza kube nzima ukwazi ukuba uncedo luyafuneka, kodwa umgama awususi wonke amathuba okuthatha inxaxheba. Usenokuba nakho ukulungiselela iinkonzo, ukuthatha umsebenzi umntu awuvumelayo, okanye ukuxhasa umntu onakekela imihla ngemihla. Qala ngokubuza umntu onesifo sokulibala ukuba ufuna ntoni nokuba ufuna ubandakanye bani.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sonakekelo ukusuka kude](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) sichaza iindima ezinje ngokulungiselela amaxesha okubonwa, ukuphanda iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni, ukulungelelanisa nabancedi abakufuphi kunye nokunika umnakekeli wemihla ngemihla ikhefu. I-[Family Caregiver Alliance ikwaphawula](https://www.caregiver.org/faq/how-can-i-help-with-caregiving-from-far-away/) ukuba ukufumana iinkonzo kunye nokucwangcisa izityelelo kunokuba nzima ukusuka kude. Esi sikhokelo sinika iingcinga zokucwangcisa eziqhelekileyo; iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni nemithetho iyahluka.

## Yintoni endingayenza ukusuka kude?

Khetha imisebenzi esebenzayo efanelana nento umntu ayifunayo kunye nento onokuyenza ngokuthembeka. Uncedo lunokubandakanya:

- **Lungiselela amaxesha okubonwa.** Buza ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo lokuqhagamshelana nekliniki, ukugcina ikhalenda okanye ukucwangcisa uhambo.
- **Phanda iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni.** Khangela ukuhanjiswa kokutya, uhambo, uncedo lwasekhaya, iinkqubo zemini zabantu abadala okanye amaqela abanakekeli kwindawo umntu ahlala kuyo. Hlola iinkcukacha kumboneleli; ukufumaneka kuyahluka ngokwendawo.
- **Phatha amaxwebhu okanye iodolo evunyelwene ngazo.** Usenokunceda ukulungiselela iifom okanye ukulungiselela izixhobo xa umntu ecela oko. Iirekhodi zemali, zeinshurensi nezempilo zifuna ingqalelo ekhethekileyo; sebenzisa [isikhokelo sethu sosuku lwemali nokhuseleko kwiqhinga](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/) kwaye ujonge nayiphi na imfuneko yegunya okanye imvume.
- **Yenza ixesha lomnakekeli wasekuhlaleni.** Ngexesha lotyelelo, buza ukuba yeyiphi imisebenzi okanye ikhefu eyayiya kuncedo. Usenokulungiselela isidlo, uthathe indawo kwixesha lonyango olucwangciswe kwangaphambili okanye uchithe ixesha nomntu ngelixa umncedi wasekuhlaleni ephumla.
- **Qhubeka unxibelelene ngendlela umntu alonwabela ngayo.** Buza ukuba bakhetha ikholi yefowuni, ikholi yevidiyo, incwadi okanye ukundwendwela. Ikholi inokuba yincoko hayi uvavanyo malunga nokutya, iiyeza okanye inkumbulo.

I-[Alzheimer's Association iphawula ukuba abantu abanika uncedo ukusuka kude kusenokungabi lula ukuba bahlulele iimfuneko ngokuthe ngqo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage). Xa kunokwenzeka, qhagamshelana nabantu abambona umntu imihla ngemihla kwaye ucwangcise ukundwendwela kwindawo yomntu ngokwakhe. Lo mbono unokongeza ulwazi oluluncedo, kodwa awuthathi indawo yokubuza umntu okanye yovavanyo lwengcali.

## Ndinokulungelelanisa njani nabancedi basekuhlaleni?

Qala ngokubuza umntu ukuba ufuna ubandakanye bani kunye nezibhengezo abazizwa bekulungele ukwabelana ngazo. Usenokufuna elinye ilungu losapho lilungelelanise amaxesha okubonwa, omakhelwane ajonge ingxaki ethile, okanye ungabikho uhlolo lwemihla ngemihla. Musa ukongeza umntu kwincoko yonakekelo ngenxa nje yokuba uhlala kufuphi.

Ukuba umntu uvumile, lungiselela isicwangciso sonxibelelwano esilula nabantu abakhethileyo:

- **Khetha unxibelelwano olunye oluqhelekileyo.** Vumelana ngobani oza kuthumela iindaba eziqhelekileyo kunye nokuba rhoqo kangakanani.
- **Khankanya ilungiselelo lokugcina.** Chonga ubani onokujonga xa umncedi wasekuhlaleni eqhelekileyo engafumanekiyo.
- **Yahlula iindaba eziqhelekileyo kwiinkxalabo eziphuthumayo.** Vumelana ngendlela umntu wasekuhlaleni ekufanele akufikelele ngayo ukuba ukhuseleko okanye impilo yomntu itshintsha ngequbuliso.
- **Gcina imisebenzi icacile.** Bhala phantsi ubani oza kuphatha amaxesha okubonwa, uhambo, iikholi zeenkonzo okanye uncedo lwasekhaya. Buza ngaphambi kokutshintsha isicwangciso.

I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa unxibelelwano oluqhelekileyo nentsapho, nomakhelwane kunye nababoneleli bonakekelo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ungabuza nomncedi wasekuhlaleni ukuba ufuna nayiphi na inkxaso. Isiphakamiso esifutshane nesithile sinokusetyenziseka lula kunokuba ithi ngokubanzi “ndazise ukuba ufuna nantoni na.”

## Ngolwazi luphi ugqirha okanye umboneleli wenkonzo anokundinika?

Buza ugqirha womntu okanye umboneleli wenkonzo ukuba ifuna yimvume okanye amaxwebhu aphi ngaphambi kokwabelana ngeendaba. I-[Alzheimer's Association iphawula ukuba umboneleli usenokufuna ukukhululwa ngaphambi kokuxoxa ngonakekelo nosapho olukude](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Iifom nemithetho iyahluka, ngoko buza umboneleli endaweni yokucinga ukuba ubudlelwane bosapho buvumela ukufikelela.

Hlonipha izifiso zomntu malunga nabo bafumana ulwazi. Yabelana kuphela ngento efunekayo kumsebenzi ovunyelwene ngawo, kwaye ugcine iinkcukacha zonxibelelwano nezicatshulwa zisimfihlo. Ukuba kukho uxwebhu lomthetho okanye igunya elisemthethweni, buza ingcali eyaziyo kwindawo umntu ahlala kuyo ukuba lubandakanya ntoni. Esi sikhokelo asichazi igunya lomthetho lomntu.

## Ndingenza njani ukutyelela kube luncedo kuthi sobabini?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukuchitha ixesha lakhe njani. Shiya indawo yesidlo, ukuhamba, umculo okanye incoko ethe cwaka, hayi ukuba sisixhobo sokulungiselela amaxesha okubonwa nemisebenzi kuphela.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo lokulungelelanisa, sebenzisa ezinye zexesha lotyelelo ukudibana nabancedi basekuhlaleni, uthethe negcali yonyango okanye ujonge ukuba isicwangciso siyasebenza na. Buza ngaphambi kokuphonononga iindawo zabucala okanye amarekhodi. Yabelana ngeenkxalabo ngokuzola kwaye ugxile kutshintsho oluthile, hayi ekugwebeni indlela umntu akwazi ngayo ukuzikhathalela.

I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukusebenzisa izityelelo ukuqhagamshelana kwakhona nokulungelelanisa nabanakekeli basekuhlaleni](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ukuba uqaphela inkxalabo, xoxa ngayo nomntu kunye nabantu abamthembileyo. Ityelelo elinye alinakubonisa lonke ulwazi malunga nempilo okanye ukukwazi komntu.

## Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba iimfuneko zomntu okanye uncedo lutshintsha?

Phonononga isicwangciso xa umntu ecela uncedo oluhlukileyo, xa imikhwa ibonakala inzima okanye xa umncedi wasekuhlaleni engakwazi ukuqhubeka. Buza ukuba umntu ufuna ukuqhubeka nantoni nokuba ngumsebenzi muni ofuna ingqalelo ngoku. Ugqirha, ingcali yezentlalo okanye inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yokuguga inokunceda ukubona izinketho. Ukuze ufumane iinkcukacha zoncedo lwasekuhlaleni, bona izikhokelo zethu [zonakekelo lwasekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) kunye [nokuhlala yedwa xa unesifo sokulibala](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/).

Qinisekisa ukuba kukho umntu wasekuhlaleni onokuphendula kwimeko engxamisekileyo. Isikhokelo sethu [sesicwangciso sezimo eziphuthumayo](/caregiving/emergency-planning-dementia/) sinokunceda umntu nabancedi abakhethileyo babhale phantsi unxibelelwano kunye nesicwangciso sokugcina. Ukuze ufumane iincoko zokungavumelani ngokuba ngubani okufanele enze ntoni, bona [iincoko zosapho ngeendima zonakekelo](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/).

Umgama unokunciphisa uncedo ngezandla, kodwa awuthathi sigqibo ngokuba umntu uyakhathalela kangakanani okanye angathini na. Gcina isicwangciso sisekelwe kwizifiso zomntu, kwimisebenzi yokwenyani nakwinkxaso ekufuphi.
