Isiganeko esingxamisekileyo sinokuphazamisa imigaqo eqhelekileyo kwaye senze kube nzima kumntu ohlala nedementia ukuqonda ukuba yintoni eyenzekayo. Icwangciso inokunceda umntu nabancedisi bakhe benze ngoxinzelelo oluncinci lokugqibela. Kufuneka ilingane neemfuno zomntu, indawo ahlala kuyo kunye neengozi zasekuhlaleni.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokulungiselela iimeko ezingxamisekileyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster) sicebisa ukulungiselela ulwazi, izixhobo kunye nenkxaso yokuhlangabezana nemeko. [Isikhokelo se-FEMA sabantu abadala nabantu abakhubazekileyo](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_hm-preparedness-for-older-adults_english.pdf) siqaqambisa nonxibelelwano, uthutho, amayeza kunye nezixhobo zoncedo. Eli lolwazi jikelele lokucwangcisa; landela imiyalelo yasekuhlaleni yeemeko ezingxamisekileyo.

## Ndingalenza njani icwangciso elingana nomntu?

Buza umntu ukuba yintoni engamncedisa ukuba azive ekhuselekile kwaye ngubani amthembayo. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, mbandakanye ekukhetheni indawo yokudibanela, oonxibelelwano abangxamisekileyo kunye nezinto ezaziwayo zokuthatha. Gcina imiyalelo imfutshane necacileyo. Icwangciso inokutshintsha njengoko iinkqubo zomntu, impilo okanye ukuhamba kwakhe kutshintsha.

Chonga abantu abangaphezulu kwabanye abanokunceda. Umnakekeli oyintloko unokuba emsebenzini, ekude nekhaya okanye echatshazelwe yingxaki efanayo. Vumelana ukuba ngubani oza kuqhagamshelana nobani, ngubani onokubonelela ngothutho kwaye ngubani onokuhlola umntu ukuba izicwangciso zitshintsha. Nika ngamnye wabancedisi ikopi yolwazi afuna.

Qwalasela into eyayinokuphazamisa uncedo oluxhasa umntu. Ukuba uhlala kwiflethi, ufumana iinkqubo zemini okanye uncedo lwasekhaya, buza ukuba ezo nkonzo ziyiphatha njani ukuhanjiswa, ukungabikho kombane okanye ukuvalwa. Fumanisa ukuba ngubani oza kuqhagamshelana nosapho kwaye ukuba iimfuno zenkxaso zomntu ziya kufezekiswa njani.

## Yintoni ekufuneka ndiyigcine ilungele?

Lungiselela ikhithi encinane elula ukuyithwala. Quka iinkcukacha zonxibelelwano zangoku, uluhlu lwamayeza, ulwazi olubalulekileyo lwempilo, isichaji sefowuni, iiglasi okanye izixhobo zokuva ukuba zifunekayo, iimpahla ezifanelekileyo kwimozulu kunye nento ethuthuzelayo ayaziyo. Yongeza izixhobo zokuhamba okanye zonxibelelwano ezixhomekeke kuzo. Hlola ukuba izinto zihlala zilungile ngamaxesha athile.

Gcina amaxwebhu abalulekileyo kwindawo ofikelela kuyo ngokukhawuleza, kodwa khusela ulwazi oluyimfihlo. Buza umntu ukuba zeziphi izinto afuna ukuba zabelwane ngazo kwaye ngubani. Umncedisi usenokufuna igama lomntu, indlela akhetha ngayo ukunxibelelana, iinkcukacha zonxibelelwano ezibalulekileyo kunye nolwazi lwamayeza, kodwa akafuni wonke amarekhodi abucala.

Izinto ezona zisebenza zezo umntu azisebenzisayo ngempela. Ingubo ethandwayo, ifoto, incwadi okanye into encinci ayaziyo inokuthuthuzela ngakumbi kunezinto ezongezelelweyo angazaziyo. Gcina ikhithi ilula ukuze umntu okanye umncedisi akwazi ukuyiphakamisa ngokukhuselekileyo.

## Yintoni ekufuneka ibhalwe kwicwangciso ebhaliweyo?

Icwangciso emfutshane ilula ukwabelana ngayo nomncedisi wasemva kwesiganeko kunokuba yiseti ende yeenotsi. Ngemvume yomntu, quka:

- **Ubani oza kuqhagamshelana naye.** Dwelisa abancedisi abangundoqo nabasemva, kunye neenkcukacha zonxibelelwano zabo. Chaza ukuba ngubani oza kuhlaziya abanye ukuba indlu iyatshintsha.
- **Indlela yonxibelelwano.** Bhala igama elithandwayo lomntu, indlela aqonda ngayo ulwazi ngcono, kunye namagama okanye iinkqubo ezaziwayo ezinokumnceda azive ekhuselekile.
- **Indlela yokuhamba.** Rekhoda uhlobo lokuhamba kunye nezixhobo zokuhamba okanye zonxibelelwano umntu azixhomekeke kuzo. Buza umnikezi wenkonzo okanye iofisi yezimo eziphuthumayo yasekuhlaleni malunga neendlela ezifikelelekayo ngaphambi kwesiganeko.
- **Apho kufumaneka izinto ezibalulekileyo.** Bhala apho kugcinwa khona ikhithi kunye noluhlu lwamayeza lwangoku. Gcina amarekhodi abucala kwindawo ekhuselekileyo, kwaye wabelane kuphela ngeenkcukacha ezifunwa ngumncedisi ngamnye.
- **Eyenzayo inkonzo.** Ukuba umntu ufumana inkathalo ngaphandle kwekhaya, dwelisa igama lomntu oxhomekeke kuye kwaye ubonise apho kufumaneka khona icwangciso yesiphuthumo yenkonzo ngoku.

Qinisekisa ukuba umncedisi wasemva kwesiganeko uyayazi icwangciso kwaye uyayazi indlela yokuyisebenzisa. Gcina ikopi ebhaliweyo ilungele xa izibane okanye ifowuni zingasebenzi, kodwa gwema ukuthumela iinkcukacha ezibucala zempilo apho izihambi zinokuzifunda khona.

## Ndingazi njani malunga nokuhlala okanye ukuhanjiswa?

Funda apho imiyalelo yasekuhlaleni yeemeko ezingxamisekileyo ipapashwa khona kunye nendlela izilumkiso ezifikelela ngayo kwindlu. Chonga apho uya khona xa iingcebiso zikhuthaza ukuhanjiswa kunye nendlela umntu aya kufika ngayo apho. Ukuba umntu usebenzisa isihlalo seziyeleli, isihambi, ioksijini okanye ezinye izixhobo, buza umnikezi okanye iofisi ngoluhlobo lwezixhobo nendlela yokuzithutha.

Akukho mgaqo mnye wokuba umntu mhlala okanye ahambe. Isiganeko, iimfuno zomntu kunye neengcebiso zabasemagunyeni zonke ziyabaluleka. Buza iofisi yolawulo lweemeko eziphuthumayo yasekuhlaleni okanye umnikezi wonakekelo malunga nothutho olufikelelekayo kunye nendawo yokuhlala. Musa ukuthembela kolu lwazi ukuze wenze isigqibo ngexesha lesiganeko esithile.

Ukuba iinkonzo zokunakekelwa ngaphandle kwekhaya zibandakanyekile, cela icwangciso yayo ebhaliweyo yesiganeko. Fumanisa ukuba ngubani onoxanduva lomntu, indlela usapho oluya kufumana ngayo izaziso kunye nokuba kwenzeka ntoni ukuba izicwangciso zokuthatha umntu okanye zohambo zitshintsha. Isikhokelo [sokhuseleko ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) siquka iingozi zemihla ngemihla ngaphakathi nangaphandle kwekhaya.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu ngexesha lesiganeko?

Hlala nomntu ukuba kukhuselekile ukwenza oko. Chaza into eyenzekayo ngamagama amafutshane athuthuzelayo kwaye uphinde ulwazi ukuba kufuneka. Gcina iimpawu zemihla ngemihla apho kunokwenzeka, kwaye yazise abaphenduli ukuba umntu uthanda ukubizwa njani kunye nento ebancedayo ukuba bathethe naye.

Ukuba umntu uxinzelekile, nciphisa ingxolo eyongezelelweyo kwaye ulinganise inani labantu abathetha ngaxeshanye. Nika into ethandwayo ukuba ikhuselekile kwaye ifumaneka. Musa ukuxabana okanye ukuchaza into ende ngelixa wonke umntu ekhathazekile. Landela imiyalelo yabasebenzi bezimo eziphuthumayo nabasebenzi bonakekelo.

Xelela abasebenzi bezonyango ngayo nayiphi na imeko entsha yokuziphatha, unxibelelwano okanye impilo yomntu. [I-NHS ithi ukudideka okungazelelwe kufuna uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Musa ukucinga ukuba utshintsho olutsha okanye olunzima lulilungu nje ledementia.

## Ndingalugcina njani icwangciso luluncedo?

Jonga uluhlu lonxibelelwano, ulwazi lwamayeza kunye nezixhobo xa iilungiselelo zonakekelo ziyatshintsha. Hlola imihla yezinto ezigcinwayo kwaye uhlaziye icwangciso emva kokuhamba okanye kwimfuneko entsha yokuhamba. Buza umntu ukuba icwangciso isenokuthuthuzela na kwaye ufuna ukuba ngubani obandakanyeke.

Nabanakekeli bayadinga uncedo. Isikhokelo [sokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sinemibono yokucela abanye ukuba babelane ngenkxaso. Icwangciso elula enabancedisi abambalwa abathembekileyo inokusetyenziswa lula kunoluhlu olude olungakhange luqwalaselwe muntu.

Injongo kukulungela ukusebenza ngokukhawuleza, ngelixa umntu ehlala ebandakanyekile kwaye ethuthuzelekile. Landela imiyalelo yasekuhlaleni yeemeko ezingxamisekileyo kwaye ubuze ingcali ezifanelekileyo malunga neemfuno ezithile zomntu.
