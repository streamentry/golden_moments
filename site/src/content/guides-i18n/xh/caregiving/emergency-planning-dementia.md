Imeko eyingxamisekileyo inokuphazamisa imikhwa eqhelekileyo kwaye yenze kube nzima komntu ophila ne-dementia ukuqonda okwenzekayo. Isicwangciso sinokunceda umntu nabancedi bakhe benze ngoxinzelelo oluncinci lwemizuzu yokugqibela. Kufuneka sihambelane neemfuno zomntu, indawo ahlala kuyo neengozi zasekhaya kuloo ndawo.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokulungiselela imeko eyingxamisekileyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster) sicebisa ukulungiselela ulwazi, izinto nenkxaso yesibini. [Isikhokelo se-FEMA sabantu abadala nabantu abakhubazekileyo](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_hm-preparedness-for-older-adults_english.pdf) sigxininisa nonxibelelwano, uthutho, amayeza nezixhobo zokuxhasa. Olu lwazi lwenkqubo eqhelekileyo yokucwangcisa; landela imiyalelo yasekhaya yemeko eyingxamisekileyo.

## Ndingenza njani isicwangciso esifanele umntu?

Buza umntu ukuba yintoni enokumnceda azive ekhuselekile nokuba ngubani amthembileyo. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, mbandakanye ekukhetheni indawo yokudibana, oqhagamshelwano lwemeko eyingxamisekileyo nezinto eziqhelekileyo zokuzithatha. Gcina imiyalelo ifutshane kwaye icace. Isicwangciso sinokutshintsha njengoko imikhwa yomntu, impilo okanye ukuhamba kutshintsha.

Bhala abantu abangaphezu komnye abanokunceda. Umkhathaleli oyintloko unokuba emsebenzini, engekhoyo ekhaya okanye echatshazelwa yimeko enye eyingxamisekileyo. Vumelanani ukuba ngubani oza kuqhagamshelana nabani, ngubani onokunikela ukuhanjiswa nokuba ngubani onokujonga umntu ukuba izicwangciso zitshintsha. Nika umncedi ngamnye ikopi yolwazi alufunayo.

Cinga ngoko kunokuphazamisa inkxaso eqhelekileyo yomntu. Ukuba uhlala kwisitya, uya kwinkqubo yemini yabantu abadala okanye ufumana ukhathalelo lwasekhaya, buza ukuba loo nkonzo iyiphatha njani ukufuduswa, ukucinywa kombane okanye ukuvalwa. Fumanisa ukuba ngubani oza kuqhagamshelana nosapho nokuba iimfuno zenkxaso yomntu ziza kuhlangatyezwa njani.

## Ndimele ndilungiselele luphi ulwazi nezinto?

Yenza ingxowa encinci elula ukuyithwala. Bandakanya oqhagamshelwano lwemeko eyingxamisekileyo lwangoku, uluhlu lwamayeza, ulwazi olubalulekileyo lwempilo, itshaja yefowuni, izibuko ezongezelelweyo okanye izixhobo zokuva ukuba kuyimfuneko, impahla efanele imozulu nento eqhelekileyo yentuthuzelo. Yongeza izinto zokuhamba okanye zonxibelelwano umntu azithembele kuzo. Jonga ukuba izinto ziyalungiswa ngamaxesha ngamaxesha.

Gcina amaxwebhu abalulekileyo kwindawo onokuyifikelela ngokukhawuleza, kodwa khusela ulwazi lobuqu. Buza umntu ukuba zeziphi izinto afuna ukuba zabelwane ngazo nabani. Umncedi unokufuna igama lomntu, indlela athanda ukunxibelelana ngayo, iinkcukacha zoqhagamshelwano ezibalulekileyo nolwazi lwamayeza, kodwa akadingi zonke iirekhodi zobuqu.

Izinto ezilungileyo zezo umntu azisebenzisayo ngokwenene. Iswitshi ayithandayo, ifoto, incwadi okanye into encinci eqhelekileyo inokuthuthuzela ngakumbi kunezinto ezongezelelweyo angazaziyo. Gcina ingxowa ilula ngokwaneleyo ukuba umntu ayithwale okanye ayiphakamise ngokukhuselekileyo.

## Isicwangciso esibhaliweyo kufuneka sithi ntoni?

Isicwangciso esifutshane silula ukwabelana ngaso nomncedi wesibini kunoluhlu olude lwamanqaku. Ngemvume yomntu, bandakanya:

- **Ukuqhagamshelana nabani.** Bhala abancedi abaphambili nabesibini, abanikeli bokhathalelo lomntu nendlela yokufikelela kwabo bonke. Yithi ngubani oza kwazisa abanye ukuba usapho luyafuduka.
- **Ukunxibelelana njani.** Qaphela igama umntu alithandayo, indlela aqonda ngayo ulwazi ngokuhle namagama okanye imikhwa eqhelekileyo enokumnceda azive ekwazi apho ekhoyo.
- **Ukuhamba njani.** Bhala indlela yothutho nezixhobo zokuhamba okanye zonxibelelwano umntu azithembele kuzo. Buza umnikeli okanye i-ofisi yasekhaya yemeko eyingxamisekileyo ngeendlela ezifikelelekayo phambi kwemeko eyingxamisekileyo.
- **Apho ungafumana khona izinto ezibalulekileyo.** Qaphela apho kugcinwa ingxowa noluhlu lwamayeza lwangoku. Gcina iirekhodi zobuqu kwindawo ekhuselekileyo, uze wabelane kuphela ngeenkcukacha umncedi ngamnye azifunayo.
- **Okuza kwenziwa yinkonzo.** Ukuba umntu ufumana ukhathalelo ngaphandle kwekhaya, bhala uqhagamshelwano olubizwayo nalapho isicwangciso sangoku semeko eyingxamisekileyo yenkonzo sifumaneka khona.

Jonga ukuba umncedi wesibini uyazi ukuba isicwangciso sikuphi nendlela yokusisebenzisa. Gcina ikopi yephepha ifumaneka ukuba umbane okanye ifowuni iyasilela, kodwa phepha ukubeka iinkcukacha zempilo ezibuthathaka apho iindwendwe zingazifunda khona.

## Ndimele ndazi ntoni ngokufihlakala okanye ukufuduswa?

Funda ukuba imiyalelo yasekhaya yemeko eyingxamisekileyo ipapashwa phi nokuba izilumkiso zifikelela njani kwikhaya. Gqiba ukuba uya phi ukuba amagunya acebisa ukufuduswa, nokuba umntu uza kufika njani apho. Ukuba umntu usebenzisa isitulo esinamavili, umhambi, ioksijini okanye ezinye izixhobo, buza umnikeli ofanelekileyo ukuba bazithutha njani nokuba ziziphi izinto zokuxhasa ezifunekayo.

Akukho mthetho ofanayo wokuba umntu uhlala okanye uhamba. Imeko eyingxamisekileyo, iimfuno zomntu nemiyalelo evela kumagunya asekhaya konke kubalulekile. Buza i-ofisi yasekhaya yolawulo lweemeko eziyingxamisekileyo okanye umnikeli wokhathalelo ngendlela yokucwangcisa uthutho nendawo yokufihla ezifikelelekayo. Musa ukuthembela kwesi sikhokelo ukwenza isigqibo ngexesha lemeko eyingxamisekileyo ethile.

Ukuba indlu yokukhathalela okanye inkqubo yemini yabantu abadala ibandakanyekile, cela isicwangciso sayo esibhaliweyo semeko eyingxamisekileyo. Fumanisa ukuba ngubani oxhomekeke kumntu, usapho lufumana njani iindaba ezintsha nokuba kwenzeka ntoni ukuba izicwangciso zokuthatha okanye zohambo ziyatshintsha. [Isikhokelo sethu sokhuseleko lwasekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sicubungula iingozi zemihla ngemihla ngaphakathi nangaphandle kwekhaya.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu ngexesha lemeko eyingxamisekileyo?

Hlala nomntu ukuba kukhuselekile ukwenza njalo. Cacisa okwenzekayo ngamabinzana amafutshane, azolileyo uze uphinde ulwazi ukuba kuyimfuneko. Gcina imikhwa eqhelekileyo apho kusebenzayo, uze uxelele abaphendulayo ukuba umntu uthanda ukubizwa njani nokuba yintoni emnceda anxibelelane.

Ukuba umntu uyaxhalaba, nciphisa ingxolo eyongezelelweyo uze unciphise inani labantu abathetha ngaxeshanye. Nika into eqhelekileyo ukuba ikhuselekile kwaye ikhona. Phepha ukuxoxa okanye ukunika inkcazelo ende ngoxa wonke umntu esoxinzelelweni. Landela imiyalelo yabasebenzi bemeko eyingxamisekileyo nabasebenzi bokhathalelo.

Xelela abasebenzi bezonyango ngalo naliphi na utshintsho olukhawulezileyo kwindlela umntu aziphethe ngayo, unxibelelwano okanye impilo. [I-NHS ithi ukudideka okuqubulayo kufuna uncedo lwezonyango olukhawulezileyo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Musa ukucinga ukuba utshintsho olutsha okanye olunzima lulinxalenye nje ye-dementia.

## Ndingasigcina njani isicwangciso silusizo?

Phinda ujonge uluhlu loqhagamshelwano, ulwazi lwamayeza nezixhobo xa amalungiselelo okhathalelo etshintsha. Jonga imihla yezinto uze uhlaziye isicwangciso emva kokufuduka okanye imfuneko entsha yokuhamba. Buza umntu ukuba isicwangciso sisavakala sikhululekile nokuba ufuna ukubandakanya bani.

Abakhathali bafuna nenkxaso yesibini. [Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela komkhathaleli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sinengcamango zokucela abanye abantu ukuba babelane ngenkxaso. Isicwangciso esilula esinabancedi abathenjiweyo abambalwa singalula ukusebenzisa kunoluhlu olude lungavavanywanga ngumntu.

Injongo kukulungela ngokwaneleyo ukwenza, ngoxa umntu ebandakanyekile kwaye ekhululekile. Landela imiyalelo yasekhaya yemeko eyingxamisekileyo uze ubuze iingcali ezifanelekileyo ngeemfuno ezithe ngqo kumntu.
