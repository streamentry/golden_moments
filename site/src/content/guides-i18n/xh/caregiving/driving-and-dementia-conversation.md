Ukuqhuba kunokuba yinxalenye yobuqu bomntu, inkululeko nomkhwa wemihla ngemihla. Ukuthetha ngotshintsho lwexesha elizayo kunokuba nzima kuye wonke umntu. Ukuqala kwangoko kunokushiya indawo engakumbi yokuphulaphula, ukwenza ukhetho nokuzama ezinye iindlela zokuhamba. Eli phepha asiso uvavanyo lokuqhuba okanye isigqibo sokuba umntu othile ukhuselekile ukuqhuba.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngokuqhuba ne-dementia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving), [icebiso le-Dementia Australia ngokuqhuba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/driving-and-dementia) kunye ne-[isikhokelo se-NHTSA sabakhathali](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/older-drivers) zicebisa ukucwangcisa kwangaphambili nokubandakanya umntu. Ugqirha, ingcali yokubuyisela ukuqhuba negunya lasekhaya lamalayisensi zinokunceda ngemibuzo yomntu.

## Ndimele ndiqale incoko nini?

Qala phambi kokuba ingozi yokuqhuba inyanzele isigqibo esikhawulezileyo, ukuba kunokwenzeka. Khetha ixesha elizolileyo xa umntu engalungiseleli ukuya kwenye indawo okanye ebuya ohambweni olunoxinzelelo. Le ingaba yeyokuqala kwiincoko ezininzi, hayi isaziso esinye.

Buza umntu ukuba ukuqhuba kuthetha ntoni kuye. Kunokuba yindlela atyelela ngayo abahlobo, athenga ngayo ukutya, anceda ngayo usapho, asebenza ngayo okanye aziva ezimele ngayo. Ukuqonda loo ntsingiselo akusisombululi umbuzo wokhuseleko. Kubonisa ukuba uyaqonda into enokulahleka nokuba kutheni ukucwangcisa kubalulekile.

Bandakanya umntu ekukhetheni ngohambo lwexesha elizayo ngoxa efuna ukuthatha inxaxheba. Angakhetha umntu othile ukuba aqhube, indlela eqhelekileyo okanye ixesha elithile losuku. Bhala iindlela azithandayo uze uzizame phambi kosuku olunoxinzelelo, ukuba uyavuma.

## Ndingayiphakamisa njani inkxalabo ngaphandle kokuyenza ingxabano?

Thetha ngesehlo esithile kunokwenza isigwebo esibanzi. Umzekelo, chaza ukulahleka kwindlela eqhelekileyo, ukuphosa uphawu lokumisa okanye ukukrwela kwemoto kutshanje. Yithi oko ukubonileyo uze ubuze umntu oko akukhumbulayo okanye akuvayo. Phepha ukumbiza umqhubi ombi okanye ukuvavanya inkumbulo yakhe ngemibuzo.

Phulaphula ingcamango yomntu. Angaba uqaphele ukudideka, ukukhazimla, ukudinwa okanye enye ingxaki naye. Okanye angangavumelani nenkxalabo yakho. Musa ukuthatha incoko enye njengobungqina bokuba uyaqonda okanye akaqondi umngcipheko. Gcina ithoni ihlonipha uze ubuyele kumbandela kamva ukuba nawuphi na umntu uyaxhalaba.

I-Dementia Australia icebisa iincoko ezifutshane, ezibuyelelwayo nokugxila kwizinto ezomeleleyo zomntu nakwiindlela ezintle zokuhamba. Ilumkisa nokuba ukufihla izitshixo ngokufihlakeleyo okanye ukucima imoto kunokuvakala kungenabubele ngexesha lokucwangcisa kwangoko. Ukuba kukho inkxalabo enzulu yokhuseleko ekhawulezileyo, thatha amanyathelo okuphepha uhambo olulandelayo olunengozi uze ufune isikhokelo sengcali ngokukhawuleza.

## Yeyiphi inkcazelo enokunceda incoko yengcali?

Bhala iziganeko eziphathekayo nokuba zenzeka nini. Imizekelo inokubandakanya ukudideka kwisiphambuka, ukulahlekelwa yindlela eya kwindawo eqhelekileyo, ukungaqapheli iimpawu okanye umonakalo omtsha kwimoto. Bandakanya oko umntu akuthethileyo ngohambo nanawuphi na utshintsho lwempilo okanye lwamayeza afuna ukuxoxa ngalo.

Gcina amanqaku esekwe kwiinyaniso kwaye efihlakele. Musa ukurekhoda, ukulandela okanye ukuvavanya umntu ngokufihlakeleyo. Ukuba umntu uyavuma, ilungu losapho elibe semotweni lingabelana ngoko bakubonileyo. Inkcazelo ecacileyo inika ugqirha okanye ingcali into ethile yokuxoxa; ayifumanisi isizathu okanye ayigqibi ukufaneleka kokuqhuba.

Iimpawu zokulumkisa kwi-[isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving) ziizizathu zokufuna icebiso lengcali. Aziyilali indawo yovavanyo lomntu. Buza ugqirha womntu ngesalathiso kwingcali yezonyango lomsebenzi okanye enye ingcali efanelekileyo yokubuyisela ukuqhuba. Jonga imithetho yamalayisensi yasekhaya kwigunya lamalayisensi leleziko okanye lommandla.

## Singenza njani isicwangciso sohambo kunye?

Qala ngohambo umntu afuna ukulugcina: ukubona abahlobo, ukuthenga, amadinga, unqulo okanye imisebenzi eqhelekileyo. Kwenye nganye, xoxani ukuba ngubani onokuqhuba, ziziphi iindlela zokuhamba ezamkelekileyo nokuba yeyiphi inkxaso enokwenza uhambo lusebenze.

Iindlela zinokubandakanya ukuhanjiswa ngusapho okanye ngabahlobo, iiteksi okanye iinkonzo zokwabelana ngemoto, uthutho lukawonke-wonke, uthutho loluntu okanye ukuhanjiswa kwezinye iimpahla. Iinkonzo, iindleko nokufikeleleka ziyahluka ngokwendawo. Jonga okukhoyo ekhaya kunokucinga ukuba indlela enye isebenzela wonke umntu.

Zamani indlela okanye inkonzo kunye phambi kokuba ibe yeyodwa. Buza umntu ukuba ufuna umhlobo okanye ufuna ukuhamba ngokuzimela. [Isikhokelo sethu sokucwangcisa uhambo](/caregiving/traveling-with-dementia/) sinengcamango zokugcina uhambo luqhelekile nokushiya indawo yokutshintsha izicwangciso.

## Kuthiwa ntoni ukuba ndicinga ukuba uhambo olulandelayo lunokuba nengozi?

Thatha inkxalabo ngokunzulu uze ulungiselele enye indlela yokuhamba ngoxa ufuna icebiso elikhawulezileyo. Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso, [i-NHS icebisa uncedo lwezonyango olukhawulezileyo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/); sebenzisa iinkonzo zasekhaya zemeko eyingxamisekileyo. Utshintsho olukhawulezileyo kwindlela umntu aziphethe ngayo akufuneki lujongwe njengengxabano eqhelekileyo yokuqhuba.

Kwezinye iinkxalabo ezinzulu, qhagamshelana nogqirha womntu negunya lasekhaya lamalayisensi ukuze ufumane amanyathelo asebenza apho ahlala khona. Musa ukuthembela kwisithembiso sosapho okanye eli phepha ukugqiba iimfuno zomthetho. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, cacisa isicwangciso esikhawulezileyo kumntu uze umgcine ebandakanyekile kwinto eza kwenzeka.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu ukuba ukuqhuba kuphela?

Ukuyeka ukuqhuba kunokuvakala njengelahleko yokwenene. Yamkela oko ngokucacileyo. Qhubeka umema umntu kwiindawo nakwimisebenzi ebalulekileyo kuye. Nika ukhetho ngokuhanjiswa, ixesha nabahlobo kunokuphatha uhambo njengomsebenzi omlawulela wona.

Yabelana ngemisebenzi yokuqhuba nezalamane okanye nabahlobo ukuba umntu uyavuma. Buza iiarhente zoluntu ngothutho lwabantu abadala okanye abantu abakhubazekileyo. Jonga iindleko, ixesha, imithetho yokuthatha nokuba umhlobo uvumelekile. Iinkcukacha ezincinci zinokugqiba ukuba ukhetho luvakala luluncedo.

[Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela komkhathaleli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sicubungula ukucela abanye ukuba babelane ngenkxaso. Umntu kufuneka abe nelizwi elininzi kangangoko kwindlela ahlala ebandakanyekile ngayo kubomi bemihla ngemihla.

Injongo kukukhusela ukhuseleko ngoxa uhlonipha into ethethwa kukuqhuba kumntu. Qala incoko kwangoko, phulaphula, fumana icebiso lengcali uze nakhe isicwangciso sohambo kunye.
