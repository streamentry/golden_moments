Ukuqhuba kunokuba yinxalenye yomazisi womntu, inkululeko kunye nemigaqo yemihla ngemihla. Ukuthetha ngoshintsho oluzayo kunokunzima kwabo bonke. Ukuqala kwangethuba kunokunika indawo engakumbi yokuphulaphula, ukwenza ukhetho kunye nokuzama ezinye iindlela zokuhamba. Eli phepha alilikhethi uvavanyo lokuqhuba okanye isigqibo malunga nokuba umntu ukhuselekile na ekuqhubeni.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association malunga nedementia nokuqhuba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving), [icebiso le-Dementia Australia ngokuqhuba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/driving-and-dementia) kunye [nesikhokelo se-NHTSA sabanakekeli](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/older-drivers) zicebisa ukucwangcisa kwangaphambili nokubandakanya umntu. Udokotela, ingcali yokuqeqeshwa kokuqhuba kunye neofisi yezelayisensi yasekuhlaleni banokunceda ngemibuzo ethile.

## Ndiqala nini incoko?

Qala phambi kokuba isiganeko sokuqhuba sinyanzele isigqibo esingxamisekileyo, ukuba kunokwenzeka. Khetha ixesha elithe cwaka xa umntu engalungiseli ukuya kwenye indawo okanye ebuyela kuhambo olunoxinzelelo. Oku kunokuba yincoko yokuqala kwezininzi, hayi isaziso esinye.

Buza ukuba kuthetha ntoni kumntu ukuqhuba. Kunokuba kukuhamba kubahlobo, ukuthenga izinto, ukunceda usapho, ukwenza umsebenzi okanye ukuziva uzimele. Ukwamkela oko kuthetha akuyisombululi imibuzo yokhuseleko. Kubonisa ukuba uyayiqonda into oza kuyilahlekelwa kwaye ukuba ukucwangcisa kubalulekile.

Bandakanya umntu ekukhetheni iindlela zokuhamba ngexesha elifanelekileyo. Usenokuthanda umntu ethile ukuba aqhube, indlela ejwayelekileyo okanye ixesha elithile lemini. Bhala phantsi ezo zinto azithandayo kwaye uzame ukuzisebenzisa phambi kwemini enoxinzelelo, ukuba uvumelana nazo.

## Ndiyikhalazela njani inkxalabo ngaphandle kokuba yimpikiswano?

Thetha malunga nesiganeko esithile endaweni yokwenza ugxeko olubanzi. Umzekelo, chaza ukulahleka kwindlela eyaziwayo, ukungaboni isiginali yokuma okanye umonakalo wakutshanje kwimoto. Chaza into obuyibonileyo kwaye ubuze ukuba umntu ukhumbula ntoni okanye wayeziva njani. Gwema ukumthiya ngokuba ngumqhubi ombi okanye ukumvavanya ngemibuzo malunga nenkumbulo yakhe.

Phulaphula imbono yomntu. Usenokuba ubone udideko, ukukhanya okuqaqambayo, ukudinwa okanye enye ingxaki. Okanye usenokungavumi nenkxalabo yakho. Musa ukuthatha incoko enye njengobungqina bokuba uyayiqonda okanye akayiqondi ingozi. Gcina incoko ihloniphekile kwaye ubuyele kwisihloko kamva ukuba omnye wenu ukhathazekile.

I-Dementia Australia icebisa iincoko ezimfutshane eziphindwayo kunye nokugxila kwiimpawu zomntu kunye neendlela ezintle zokuhamba. Ikwalumkisa ukuba ukufihla izitshixo okanye ukucima imoto ngasese kunokubonakala njengento enobutshaba ngexesha lokucwangcisa kwangaphambili. Ukuba kukho ingozi enkulu ngoku, thatha amanyathelo okuphepha uhambo olulandelayo olunengozi kwaye ufune icebiso elikhawulezayo.

## Yintoni enokunceda ingxoxo engokobuchwephesha?

Bhala phantsi iziganeko ezithile kunye nexesha ezenzeka ngalo. Imizekelo inokubandakanya ukudideka kwindawo yokudlulela, ukulahleka kwendlela eyaziwayo, ukungaboni iimpawu okanye umonakalo omtsha kwimoto. Quka into umntu ayithethileyo ngohambo kunye notshintsho lwempilo okanye lwamayeza afuna ukuxoxwa ngawo.

Gcina iinotsi zinyanisekile kwaye zimfihlo. Musa ukurekhoda ngasese, ukulandela okanye ukuvavanya umntu. Ukuba umntu uvumile, ilungu losapho elwake laba semotweni linokuchaza into aliqaphelileyo. Inkcazelo ecacileyo imnika ingcali into ethile yokuxoxa; ayichazi isizathu sengxaki, kwaye ayichazi nokuba umntu unokuqhuba na.

[Iimpawu zokulumkisa kwisikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving) ziyizizathu zokufuna icebiso lobuchwephesha. Azithathi indawo yovavanyo olukhethekileyo komntu ngamnye. Buza udokotela womntu malunga nesalathiso sengcali yezoqeqesho lomsebenzi okanye enye ingcali eqeqeshiweyo yokuqeqeshwa kokuqhuba. Jonga imithetho yezelayisensi yasekuhlaleni kurhulumente wesifundazwe okanye kurhulumente wengingqi.

## Singalwenza njani uhambo ngokubambisana?

Qala ngohambo umntu afuna ukulugcina: ukutyelela abahlobo, ukuthenga, iintlanganiso zonyango, inkonzo yenkolo okanye imisebenzi ejwayelekileyo. Kowo nawuphi na, xoxani ukuba ngubani onokuqhuba, ukuba zeziphi iindlela zokuhamba eziyamkelekileyo kwaye yintoni enokwenza uhambo lusebenze.

Ukhetho lunokubandakanya ukukhwelwa ngabahlobo okanye usapho, iteksi okanye inkonzo yokwabelana ngerayidi, ezothutho zoluntu okanye uthutho lwasekuhlaleni olunikezela ngezinto ezithile. Ukufumaneka, iindleko kunye nokufikeleleka kuyahluka ngendawo. Jonga ukuba yintoni ekhoyo kwindawo oyihlala kuyo endaweni yokuthatha ukuba ukhetho olunye lunokusebenza kubo bonke abantu.

Zama indlela okanye inkonzo kunye phambi kokuba ibe lukhetho kuphela. Buza ukuba umntu ufuna umntu omhambisayo okanye ukuba ufuna ukuhamba yedwa. Isikhokelo sethu [sokucwangcisa uhambo](/caregiving/traveling-with-dementia/) sinemibono yokugcina uhambo luqhelekile nokushiya indawo yokutshintsha izicwangciso.

## Ndingenzani ukuba ukhuselekile ukuba uhambo olulandelayo lunengozi?

Thatha inkxalabo ngokungathi ibalulekile, kwaye cwangcisa enye indlela yokuhamba ngelixa ufuna icebiso elikhawulezayo. Ukuba umntu udideka ngokungazelelwe, [i-NHS icebisa ukufumana uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/); sebenzisa iinkonzo zesiphuthuma zasekuhlaleni. Utshintsho olukhawulezayo kwindlela umntu eziphatha ngayo akufuneki lubonwe njengengxabano eqhelekileyo ngokuqhuba.

Kwiinkxalabo ezinye ezinkulu zokhuseleko, qhagamshelana nodokotela womntu kunye neofisi yezelayisensi yasekuhlaleni malunga nezinyathelo ezisebenza kwindawo oyihlala kuyo. Ungathembeli kwisithembiso sentsapho okanye kule phepha ukuze ugqibe iimfuneko zomthetho. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, cacisa icwangciso ekhoyo kumntu kwaye umgcine ebandakanyeke kwinto eyenzekayo kamva.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu xa ukuqhuba kuphela?

Ukuyeka ukuqhuba kunokuziva njengelahleko yokwenene. Yamkela oko ngokucacileyo. Qhubeka umbiza kwiindawo nakwimisebenzi ebalulekileyo kuye. Nika ukhetho malunga nokukhwelwa, ixesha kunye nabahambi nabo, endaweni yokuphatha uhambo njengomsebenzi awenzelwa wona.

Yabelana ngemisebenzi yokuqhuba nabahlobo okanye abantu basapho ngemvume yomntu. Buza iiarhente zasekuhlaleni malunga nothutho lwabantu abadala okanye abantu abakhubazekileyo. Jonga indleko, ishedyuli, imigaqo yokukhwelwa kunye nokuba ukuba umhambi uyavumelwa na. Iinkcukacha ezincinci zinokuchaza ukuba ukhetho lube luncedo okanye akunjalo.

Isikhokelo [sokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) siquka ukucela abanye ukuba babelane ngenkxaso. Umntu kufuneka abe nelizwi elikhulu okusemandleni akhe ekuhlaleni kwakhe kwimihla ngemihla.

Injongo kukukhusela ukhuseleko ngelixa uhlonipha into ukuqhuba ethetha kumntu. Qala incoko kwangethuba, phulaphula, fumana icebiso lobuchwephesha kwaye wakhe icwangciso yohambo kunye.
