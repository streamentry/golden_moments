Abantu abane-dementia bayayiva intlungu, nokuba abakwazi ukuyichaza ngokucacileyo. Umntu angasebenzisa amagama ahlukileyo, ukunyikima okanye utshintsho lokuziphatha ukubonisa ukuba kukho into engalunganga. Umnyamekeli angaqaphela aze abelane ngezo tshintsho, kodwa makangaluqikeleli isizathu yedwa.

[Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia sentlungu](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/pain) sicebisa ukubuza ngokuthe ngqo nokufuna uvavanyo lukagqirha xa kusolwa intlungu. [I-Alzheimer's Association iyaqaphela nayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger) ukuba ukungakhululeki komzimba kunokube negalelo kutshintsho lokuziphatha. Esi sikhokelo sinikela ulwazi jikelele, asiluvavanyo lwentlungu okanye icebo lonyango.

## Umntu one-dementia angayiva intlungu angayithethi?

Ewe. Intlungu isenyani nokuba umntu akakwazi ukufumana amagama ayo. Angaba engayiqondi imibuzo ngenxalenye ethile yomzimba, okanye abe nobunzima bokuxelela omnye ukuba iyenzakala nini okanye njani. Musa ukucinga ukuba ukuthula kuthetha ukuba umntu ukhululekile.

Buza ngexesha elithe cwaka usebenzisa amagama awaziyo. “Ingaba ubuhlungu?” okanye “Ingaba oku kuyabuhlungisa?” kunokuba lula kunenkcazo ende. Abanye abantu bayawaqonda amagama athi “ukuvuthela,” “ukungakhululeki” okanye “ukuhlungisa.” Banike ixesha lokuphendula uze uyamkele impendulo yabo ngaphandle kokuyivavanya okanye ukuyilungisa.

Buza umbuzo omnye olula, uze uyeke. Umntu angaloba, enze isimbo okanye asebenzise isivakalisi esiqhelekileyo endaweni yokubiza inxalenye yomzimba. Phepha imibuzo ekhawulezileyo okanye ukumqhubela ekuthini ewe. Ukuba uthi hayi kodwa ubuqaphele utshintsho, yabelana nalo nengcali yezempilo endaweni yokuxabana ngokuba intlungu ikhona.

## Yeyiphi inguqu efanele iqatshelwe?

Khangela utshintsho kubuso, ekushukumeni nakwinkqubo eqhelekileyo yomntu. Angaqubuda, aqhwabe ubuso, alile, akhalaze, akhusele inxalenye yomzimba, ashukume kancinci, alale ngendlela eyahlukileyo okanye alahlekelwe lunomdla ekutyeni. Angathula ngokungaqhelekanga, adankwe okanye angaphumli.

Akukho namnye kwezi mpawu ungqina ukuba umntu unentlungu. Zinokuba nezizathu ezininzi. Utshintsho olunjengokwala isidlo lunokunxulumana nentlungu yomlomo, kodwa lunokuba nenye inkcazo. Yabelana noko ukubonayo nogqirha okanye umongikazi, uze ubavumele bavavanye isizathu.

## Ndingachaza njani oko ndikuqaphelileyo?

Bhala inyaniso ezimbalwa ukunceda ukuchaza utshintsho. Phawula into eyenzekileyo, yaqala nini, uyibona kangaphi nokuba yintoni eyayisenzeka ngelo xesha. Umzekelo, khankanya ukuba umntu uyatsala ubuso xa elapha, uphepha umsebenzi oqhelekileyo okanye utya ngaphantsi kunesiqhelo. Faka amagama omntu okanye izimbo zakhe.

Fanisa utshintsho nendlela eqhelekileyo yomntu, hayi neyomnye umntu. Umntu othe cwaka angabi nakuthi mpu xa enentlungu; omnye angabonisa ukungakhululeki ngokushukuma okanye ngokuthetha ngakumbi. Phepha ukwenza amanqaku entlungu okanye ukumisela ukuba indlela yokuziphatha inesizathu esinye. Injongo kukunika ingcali yezempilo ukuqwalaselwa okunengenelo.

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, yabelana namanqaku nelungu losapho, umongikazi okanye ugqirha ababandakanyekileyo ekhathalelweni lwakhe. Gcina ulwazi luyimfihlo uze udlulise kuphela oko kunceda umntu afumane ukhathalelo. [Isikhokelo sethu sotyelelo esibhedlele](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) sinolwazi olungakumbi ngokwabelana ngezinto ezikhethwayo zonxibelelwano nabasebenzi bebhedlele.

## Ndenze ntoni ukuba ndicinga ukuba umntu unentlungu?

Qhagamshelana nengcali yezempilo uze uchaze oko kutshintshileyo. Intlungu inokuba nezizathu ezininzi, kubandakanya ukonzakala kwakudala, i-arthritis, iingxaki zamazinyo, usulelo, ukuqhina okanye uxinzelelo lokuhlala kwindawo enye. Ugqirha angajonga impilo yomntu aze enze isigqibo ngovavanyo okanye unyango olufanelekileyo.

Musa ukuqala iyeza lentlungu, ukutshintsha umthamo okanye ukuyeka elinye iyeza ngokwewebhusayithi. Amanye amayeza anokuxubana okanye abengakhuselekanga kumntu onemeko ethile yempilo. Landela icebo lokhathalelo lomntu uze ubuze ugqirha obhale iyeza okanye ugqirha wamayeza xa kukho into engacacanga.

Ungancitshisa uxinzelelo ngoko nangoko ngoxa ufuna icebiso. Buza ukuba yintoni eya kumenza akhululeke ngakumbi, vumela umntu aphumle uze ungaNyanzeli ukushukuma okanye umsebenzi owenzakalisayo. Buza ingcali yezempilo phambi kokusebenzisa ubushushu, umkhenkce, ukuxovula okanye elinye unyango ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba kukhuselekile.

## Ingaba utshintsho lokuziphatha lunokubangelwa yenye into?

Ewe. Intlungu yenye yezizathu ezinokubakho zokuxhalaba, kodwa utshintsho lunokunxulumana nokudinwa, indawo ebhidekayo, indlala, usulelo, iziphumo zamayeza okanye enye ingxaki yempilo. Jonga into eyenzekileyo phambi kotshintsho nokuba iyaphinda, kodwa musa ukuzama ukuqonda isizathu sezonyango ngokwakho.

Ukuba umntu ngequbuliso ubhidekile ngakumbi okanye wahlukile kakhulu kunesiqhelo, xelela ingcali yezempilo ngoko nangoko. [I-NHS ithi ukubhideka kwequbuliso kufuna uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Sebenzisa iinkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni okanye inkqubo yokhathalelo olukhawulezileyo apho uhlala khona.

Ukuba utshintsho luyanyuka kancinci okanye luyaphinda, lungiselela utyelelo lukagqirha uze uzise amanqaku akho. [Isikhokelo sokulungiselela utyelelo lukagqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinendlela elula yokuhlela imibuzo nokuchaza utshintsho.

## Abancedisi bangaphendula njani ngendlela enentlonipho?

Mxelele umntu into oyenzayo phambi kokunceda. Buza imvume phambi kokumchukumisa okanye ukuhambisa into yempahla. Nika ukhetho oluncinci olunye, njengokuphumla ngoku okanye ukuhlala kwenye indawo, uze unike ixesha lempendulo. Ukuba uyala, yeka uze ubuze ingcali yezempilo ngenye indlela.

Buza umntu ukuba ngawaphi amagama okanye uhlobo loncedo alukhethayo. Ngemvume yakhe, yabelana ngolwazi olunceda nabanye abancedisi abathembekileyo ukuze umntu angabi nyanzelekanga ukuba achaze into enye ngokuphindaphindiweyo. Musa ukuthetha ngaye ngokungathi akakho.

Intlungu inokuba nzima ukuyiqaphela ukusuka ngaphandle. Ukubuza ngobubele, ukujonga utshintsho nokufumana icebiso lengcali kunokunceda umntu eve, ngaphandle kokuthatha uphawu njengofumaniso.
