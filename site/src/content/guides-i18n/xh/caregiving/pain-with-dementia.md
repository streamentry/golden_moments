Abantu abaphila ne-dementia bayayiva intlungu, nokuba abanakuyichaza ngokucacileyo. Umntu usenokusebenzisa amagama ahlukileyo, izenzo okanye utshintsho kwindlela yokuziphatha ukubonisa ukuba kukho into engalunganga. Umnakekeli unokuqaphela aze axelele ngolo tshintsho, kodwa akafanele uxilonge isizathu yedwa.

I-[Dementia Australia's pain guidance](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/pain) icebisa ukubuza ngokuthe ngqo kwaye kufunyanwe uvavanyo lukagqirha xa kukhathazeka intlungu. I-[Alzheimer's Association iphinda iphawule](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger) ukuba ukungakhululeki komzimba kunokuba negalelo kutshintsho kwindlela yokuziphatha. Esi sikhokelo sinolwazi jikelele, asiyo uvavanyo lwentlungu okanye icwangciso yonyango.

## Ingaba umntu ophila ne-dementia unokuziva intlungu engayithethi?

Ewe. Intlungu ikhona nokuba umntu akafumani amagama oyichazayo. Usenokungaqondi umbuzo malunga nenxalenye ethile yomzimba, okanye anokuba nengxaki yokuxelela umntu ukuba kuhlungu nini okanye kuhlungu njani. Musa ukuthatha ukuba ukuthula kuthetha ukuba umntu uthuthuzelekile.

Buza ngexesha elithuleyo usebenzisa amagama awaziyo. “Uyabuhlungu na?” okanye “Oku kubuhlungu na?” inokulula kunentshayelelo ende. Abanye abantu bayawazi amagama afana no “buhlungu,” “akuthuthuzelekanga” okanye “uyabuhlungu.” Nika ixesha lokuphendula kwaye wamkele impendulo yakhe ngaphandle kokumvavanya okanye ukumlungisa.

Buza umbuzo omnye olula, uze uyeke. Umntu usenokukhomba, akhombise ngezandla okanye asebenzise isibinzana esaziwayo endaweni yokubiza inxalenye yomzimba. Gwema imibuzo ekhawulezayo okanye ukumkhokelela kwimpendulo ethi ewe. Ukuba uthi hayi kodwa wena uqaphele utshintsho, yabelana ngalo negcisa lezempilo endaweni yokuxabana ngokuba intlungu ikhona na.

## Ngutshintsho luphi olufanelekileyo ukuba uqaphele?

Jonga utshintsho kwindlela umntu ajwayele ukuvakalisa ngayo, ukuhamba kwakhe nomkhwa wakhe. Usenokubonakala ebuhlungu ubuso bakhe buqumbe, akhale okanye akhusele inxalenye yomzimba, ahambe kancinci, alale ngendlela eyahlukileyo okanye alahlekelwe ngumdla wokutya. Usenokuba ngophaphathekileyo ngokungaqhelekanga, adakumbe okanye abe nomsindo ulula.

Azikho ezi mpawu ezibonisa ngokuqinisekileyo ukuba umntu uyabuhlungu. Zinokuba nezizathu ezininzi. Utshintsho olufana nokwala isidlo lunokuhambelana nentlungu yomlomo, kodwa lunokuba nenye inkcazelo. Yabelana ngoko ubona nogqirha okanye umongikazi, kwaye uvumele bona bavavanye isizathu.

## Ndingazichaza njani iimbono zam?

Bhala iinyani ezimbalwa ukucacisa utshintsho. Qaphela into eyenzekileyo, ukuba iqale nini, ukuba uyilubona rhoqo kangakanani kunye nokuba yayisenzeka ntoni ngelo xesha. Ngomzekelo, chaza ukuba umntu ubonakala ebuhlungu xa emi, ugwema umsebenzi awuqhelileyo okanye utya ngaphantsi kwesiqhelo. Faka amagama omntu ngokwakhe okanye iimpawu zakhe.

Thelekisa utshintsho nendlela yomntu eqhelekileyo, hayi nomntu omnye. Umntu othule angakhali kakhulu xa ebuhlungu; omnye usenokubonisa ukungakhululeki ngokuhamba okanye ngokuthetha ngakumbi. Gwema ukwenza amanqaku entlungu okanye ukugqiba ukuba utshintsho olunye lunesizathu esinye. Injongo kukunika ingcali yezempilo iimbono ezinceda.

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, yabelana ngezikhumbuzo nelungu losapho, umongikazi okanye ugqirha abathatha inxaxheba kunyango lwakhe. Gcina ulwazi lusemfihlweni kwaye udlulisele kuphela oko kunceda umntu afumane unyango. Isikhokelo sethu [sokutyelela isibhedlele](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) sibandakanya ngakumbi malunga nokwabelana ngokhetho lonxibelelwano nabasebenzi besibhedlele.

## Yenzeni ukuba ndicinga ukuba umntu usenentlungu?

Qhagamshelana negcisa lezempilo uze uchaze utshintsho olwenzekileyo. Intlungu inokubangelwa zizinto ezininzi, kubandakanya nokwenzakala kwakudala, i-arthritis, iingxaki zamazinyo, usulelo, ukuqina kwesisu okanye ingcinezelo yokuhlala kwindawo enye. Ingcali yonyango inokuhlola impilo yomntu ize igqibe ukuba yeyiphi uvavanyo okanye unyango olufanelekileyo.

Musa ukuqalisa iyeza lentlungu, ukutshintsha isixa okanye ukuyeka elinye iyeza ngokusekelwe kwiwebhusayithi. Ezinye iiyeza zinokudibana okanye zingakhuseleki kubantu abane-meko ezithile zempilo. Landela icwangciso yonyango lomntu kwaye ubuze umbhali wencwadi okanye ufamasi xa into ingacacanga.

Ungancitshisa nengcinezelo ekhoyo ngelixa ufuna icebiso. Buza ukuba yintoni eya kumthuthuzela, mvumele umntu aphumle kwaye ungaphikisani nentshukumo okanye umsebenzi obuhlungu. Hlola nengcali yezempilo ngaphambi kokusebenzisa ubushushu, iqhwa, ukuhlikihla okanye elinye unyango ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba luyakhuseleka na.

## Ingaba utshintsho kwindlela yokuziphatha lunokubangelwa yinto enye?

Ewe. Intlungu yenye yezinto ezinokubangela ukuxinezeleka, kodwa utshintsho lunokuhambelana nokudinwa, indawo edidekisayo, indlala, usulelo, iziphumo zamayeza okanye enye ingxaki yezempilo. Jonga into eyenzeke phambi kotshintsho nokuba iyaphinda na, kodwa musa ukuzama ukufumanisa isizathu sezonyango ngokwakho.

Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso okanye utshintsha kakhulu kwindlela yakhe eqhelekileyo, xelela ingcali yezempilo ngoko nangoko. I-[NHS ithi ukudideka okungalindelekanga kufuna uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Sebenzisa inkonzo yezimo eziphuthumayo okanye inkqubo yonyango olungxamisekileyo endaweni ohlala kuyo.

Ukuba utshintsho lucotha kancinci okanye luqhubeka luphinda, lungiselela ukutyelela ugqirha kwaye uzise iinqaku zakho. [Isikhokelo sokulungiselela ukutyelela ugqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinendlela elula yokubhala imibuzo nokuchaza utshintsho.

## Abancedisi banokuphendula njani ngendlela ehloniphayo?

Xelela umntu into oyenzayo ngaphambi kokumncedisa. Cela imvume ngaphambi kokumthinta okanye ukususa isambatho. Nika ukhetho olunye oluncinci, njengokuphumla ngoku okanye ukuhlala kwindawo eyahlukileyo, kwaye unike ixesha lokuphendula. Ukuba akavumi, yima uze ucele ingcali yezempilo enye indlela.

Buza umntu ukuba uthanda ukuthethwa ngayo ntoni okanye luhlobo luphi loncedo. Ngemvume yakhe, yabelana ngolwazi oluncedayo nabanye abancedisi abathembekileyo ukuze umntu angafuni ukucacisa into efanayo rhoqo. Musa ukuthetha ngabo ngathi abakho apha.

Intlungu inokuba nzima ukuyiqaphela ngaphandle. Ukubuza ngobumnene, ukujonga utshintsho nokufumana icebiso lengcali kunokunceda umntu aviwe ngaphandle kokuphatha uphawu njengoxilongo.
