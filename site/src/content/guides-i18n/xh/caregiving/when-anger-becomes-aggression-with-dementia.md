Umsindo unokuguquka ube kukukhwaza, ukutyhala okanye ukubetha, kwaye oko kunokoyikisa wonke umntu okufuphi. Ukuziphatha okunjalo kusenokungabi kokwenjongo. Akubonisi ukuba umntu uyakujolisa ukonzakalisa omnye. Qala ngokwenza imeko ikhuseleke ngakumbi, uze ukhangele into umntu anokuyidinga.

## Ndenze ntoni xa umsindo uba bubundlobongela?

Nqumamisa umsebenzi, hlehla kwaye usebenzise isivakalisi esifutshane nesithe cwaka. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa abanonopheli ukuba bagxininise kwiimvakalelo, bathethe ngokucothayo kwaye banciphise izinto ezidanisayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Ukuba usengozini ekhoyo ngoku okanye omnye umntu ophukileyo, yiya kwindawo ekhuselekileyo kwaye unxibelelane neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni.

- **Yeka okwenzekayo ukuba unako.** Ukuba ukuhlamba, ukunxiba, utyelelo okanye omnye umsebenzi ubonakala ukhathaza umntu, nqumamisa. Unokulinda ngaphandle kokuba kukho imfuneko ekhoyo ngoku yokhuseleko okanye yempilo.
- **Nika indawo.** Hlehla kwaye ugcine indlela ecacileyo yokuba nobabini nikwazi ukumka. Musa ukumngxamela, ukumvalela indlela, ukumcinezela okanye ukumchukumisa ngequbuliso.
- **Gcina ilizwi lisezantsi namazwi mafutshane.** Usenokuthi, “Ndiyabona ukuba uyakhathazeka. Ndiza kukunika indawo.” Musa ukuphikisana, ukoyikisa okanye ukufuna ukuchazelwa.
- **Susa abanye abantu.** Umntwana, undwendwe okanye umncedisi angaya kwelinye igumbi ngelixa umntu ezola. Umntu omnye othe cwaka othethayo usenokuba lula kunabantu abaninzi abazama ukunceda.
- **Hamba ukuba kwenzeka ubundlobongela bomzimba.** Yiya kwindawo ekhuselekileyo kwaye ucele omnye umntu aluncede. Musa ukuzama ukumbamba umntu ngenkani.

I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukuhlehla, ukugweba ukubamba ngenkani okanye ukuchukumisa ngequbuliso, nokumka ukuze ufune uncedo ukuba kwenzeka ubundlobongela bomzimba](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa nokucela imvume, ukunciphisa izinto ezivuselelayo nokungamngxameli okanye ukumbamba ngenkani umntu](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Sebenzisa inombolo yongxamiseko yendawo yakho xa kukho ingozi ekhoyo ngoku; musa ukulinda ukuba isiganeko sidlule ukuba umntu usenokonzakala.

## Yintoni enokuba negalelo kuko oko kuziphatha?

Kusenokubakho into engaphezu kwenye, kwaye ilungu losapho alinakwazi ukubona isizathu ngokwakhe. I-Alzheimer's Association idwelisa ukungaphatheki kakuhle komzimba, uxinzelelo lwendawo nengxaki zonxibelelwano phakathi kwezinto ezinokunegalelo.

Cinga ngoko kwenzeke kwangethuba ngaphambi kotshintsho:

- Ngaba umntu wayediniwe, enendlala, enxaniwe, eshushu okanye ebanda kakhulu, okanye engaphatheki kakuhle?
- Ngaba umsebenzi wawunzima, ungxamile okanye ungaqhelekanga? Ngaba umntu othile waqhubeka umntu esele ebonisa ukukhathazeka?
- Ngaba kwakukho izandi ezikhulu, abantu abaninzi bethetha, ukungacoceki okanye indawo engaziwayo?
- Ngaba umntu wayenengxaki yokubona okanye yokuva okwenzekayo?
- Ngaba iyeza, umkhwa, umnonopheli okanye imeko yempilo yatshintsha kutsha nje?

I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukukhangela iipateni nokucinga ukuba imeko yabonakala injani ngokwembono yomntu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Le mibuzo yeyokuqaphela, ayiyonto ibonisa ukuba into enye ibangele oko kuziphatha. Musa ukuzama ukuhlola, ukumbamba ngenkani okanye ukunika umntu amayeza ngokwakho.

## Ndingawunciphisa njani umngcipheko wesinye isiganeko?

Sebenzisa oko ukuqapheleyo ukulungisa unxibelelwano olulandelayo. Oku kusenokunceda, kodwa akukwazi ukuqinisekisa ukuba ukuziphatha akuyi kuphinda kwenzeke. Zama utshintsho olunye ngexesha ukuze kube lula ukuqaphela ukuba umntu uvakala ethuthuzelekile ngakumbi na.

- **Khetha ixesha elithe cwaka.** Ukuba umsebenzi udla ngokuhamba kakubi xa umntu ediniwe, jonga ukuba elinye ixesha liyasebenza ngcono na.
- **Yenza indawo ibe lula.** Cima ingxolo engasemva, nciphisa inani labantu egumbini kwaye uqokelele oko ukufunayo phambi kokuqala.
- **Cacisa inyathelo linye uze ulinde.** Sondela ngaphambili, xela okoukwenzayo, buza phambi kokunceda kwaye unike umntu ixesha lokuphendula.
- **Yeka xa kukho uphawu lokukhathazeka.** Ukwala, ukutsala emva, ubuso obuxineneyo okanye ilizwi eliphakamileyo kunokuba luphawu lokunqumama. Musa ukuthatha ukugqiba umsebenzi njengobaluleke ngakumbi kunentuthuzelo.
- **Yabelana ngecebo elilula.** Ukuba abantu abaninzi banonopha, vumelanani ngezinto ezibonisa “nqumama,” ibinzana elithe cwaka nokuba ngubani ofanele ukunxibelelana naye xa ipateni itshintsha.

Ukufumana izimvo zemisebenzi ethile, jonga izikhokelo zethu ze-[ukuhlamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) kunye ne-[ukunxiba](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/). Isikhokelo se-[communication tips](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sibonisa iindlela zokuthetha nokumamela ngaphandle kokongeza uxinzelelo.

## Ndingenza ntoni emva kokuba imeko izolile?

Nika umntu intuthuzelo kwaye ubuyele kumsebenzi oqhelekileyo, ongafuni kakhulu, ukuba umntu ukufuna oko. Musa ukukhalima, ukumtyhola okanye ukumtshutshisa. I-Alzheimer's Society iyaqaphela ukuba umntu usenokuqhubeka ekhathazekile emva kokuba ukuziphatha kudlulile, nokuba akayikhumbuli into eyenzekileyo.

Ukuba ufuna ukuqonda ipateni, yenza inqaku elifutshane lobuqu lokwenzeke kwangethuba, oko ukuqaphelileyo, ixesha elathatha nento ebonakala inceda. Gcina inqaku liyinyani kwaye ulabele neqela elinonophelayo lomntu xa kufanelekile. Phepha ukurekhoda umntu okanye ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha zobuqu ngaphandle kwemvume.

Ukuba umsebenzi udla ngokukhokelela ekukhathazekeni, tshintsha ixesha okanye indlela, okanye ucele omnye umntu omthembileyo anganceda. Unokwamkela ukwala uzame kamva. Isikhokelo sethu se-[what to do when someone says no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) sinikela ngeendlela ezingakumbi zokunqumama ngaphandle koxinzelelo.

## Ndifanele ndifune nini icebiso lezonyango?

Nxibelelana nogqirha womntu ngobundlobongela obutsha, obungequbuliso, obuphindaphindekayo okanye obenda bubi. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa uhlolo lwezonyango ngenxa yotshintsho lokuziphatha, ngakumbi xa lubonakala lungequbuliso](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Intlungu, ukugula, ukulala, indawo ekungqongileyo namayeza zonke zisenokufuna ukuphononongwa. Ugqirha unokuvavanya imeko yomntu.

Funa uncedo lwezonyango olungxamisekileyo lwasekuhlaleni ukuba umntu uyadideka ngequbuliso, wonzakele okanye ubonakala egula kakhulu. Ukuba wena okanye omnye umntu usengozini ekhoyo ngoku, nxibelelana neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. Musa ukulinda ukuqokelela amanqaku okanye ukuzama ukuqala ukuqonda isizathu.

Ukhuseleko lwakho lubalulekile nalo. Xelela ilungu losapho elithembekileyo, ugqirha okanye inkonzo yenkxaso ye-dementia yasekuhlaleni ukuba iziganeko zihlala zenzeka okanye uyoyika. Cela icebo likhusela umntu nabantu abamngqongileyo. Ufanele uncedwe; akunyanzelekanga ukuba ukulawule oku wedwa.
