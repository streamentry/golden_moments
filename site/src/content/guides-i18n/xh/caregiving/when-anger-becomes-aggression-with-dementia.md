Ukucaphuka kunokuguquka kube kukukhala, ukutyhala okanye ukubetha, kwaye oko kunokoyisa wonke umntu okufuphi. Ukuziphatha okunjalo akusiyo into yenziwa ngamabomu. Akuthethi ukuba umntu uhlose ukulimaza bani. Qala ngokwenza imeko ikhuseleke, emva koko ujonge into umntu asenokubaiyafuna.

## Yintoni ekufanele ndiyenze xa ukucaphuka kuguquka kubundlobongela?

Yima umsebenzi, ume ngasemva uthethe isivakalisi esifutshane nesizolileyo. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa abanakekeli ukuba bagxile kwiimvakalelo, bathethe ngokuthe chu kwaye banciphise into ezibaphazamisayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Ukuba usengozini okanye umntu ulimele, hamba uye kwindawo ekhuselekileyo kwaye uqhagamshelane neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni.

- **Yima into eyenzekayo xa unakho.** Ukuba ukuhlamba, ukugqoka, utyelelo okanye omnye umsebenzi usenza umntu acaphuke, yima. Oko kunokulinda ngaphandle kokuba kukho imfuneko ekhawulezayo yokhuseleko okanye yezempilo.
- **Nika indawo.** Yiba kude kancinci kwaye ushiye indlela ecacileyo yokuba nina nomnye nihambe. Musa ukuvala indlela, ukuxinezela, ukuchukumisa okanye ukubamba umntu ngokungazi.
- **Gcina ilizwi lishinxa kunye namagama amafutshane.** Ungathi, “Ndiyabona ukuba uyacaphuka. Ndiza kukunika indawo.” Musa ukuphikisana, ukoyikisa okanye ukufuna incazelo.
- **Hambisa abanye abantu.** Umntu omncinci, umtyeleli okanye umncedisi unokuya kwelinye igumbi ngelixa umntu ezola. Umntu omnye othetha ngokuzolileyo unokulula kunabantu abaninzi bezama ukunceda ngaxeshanye.
- **Shiya ukuba kukho ukuxhatshazwa ngokwasemzimbeni.** Fumana indawo ekhuselekileyo, ucele omnye umntu ukuba akuncede. Musa ukuzama ukubamba umntu.

I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukuba uyeke, ungasebenzisi ukubamba okanye ukuthinta ngokungazi, kwaye ushiye ukuze ufune uncedo ukuba kukho ubundlobongela ngokwasemzimbeni](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). I-[Alzheimer's Association ikwacebisa ukucela imvume, ukunciphisa ukungcangcazeka kunye nokungamjiki okanye ukumbamba umntu](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Sebenzisa inombolo yexesha lengxaki yendawo yakho xa kukho ingozi ekhoyo; ungalindi ukuba isiganeko siphele ukuba umntu usenokulimala.

## Yintoni enokuba nefuthe kwindlela yokuziphatha?

Kunokubakho izizathu ezingaphezulu kunye, kwaye ilungu losapho alinakuchaza isizathu. I-Alzheimer's Association ibala ukungonwabi kwemzimba, uxinzelelo lwendawo kunye neengxaki zoncoko njengezinto ezinokuba negalelo.

Cinga ngento eyenzekayo ngaphambi kotshintsho:

- Ngaba umntu udinwe, elambile, eyomileyo, eshushu okanye ebanda kakhulu, okanye engonwabanga?
- Ngaba umsebenzi wawunzima, uxhaphazile okanye awaziwa? Ngaba umntu uqhubeke nokwenza into emva kokuba esele ebonakalisa ukuxhalaba?
- Kwakukho iingxolo ezinkulu, abantu abaninzi bethetha, ukungacoceki okanye indawo engaziwayo?
- Ngaba umntu wayenengxaki yokubona okanye yokuva into eyayenzeka?
- Ngaba iyeza, umgaqo, umnakekeli okanye imeko yempilo itshintshile kutshanje?

I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukujonga iipatheni kunye nokucinga ukuba imeko yayiyinjani kumntu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Le mibuzo ngumgaqo wokujonga, ayisiyo ubungqina bokuba enye yezi zinto ibangele ukuziphatha okunjalo. Musa ukuzama ukuhlola umntu, ukumbamba okanye ukumnika iyeza ngokwakho.

## Ndingancitshisa njani amathuba esiganeko esitsha?

Sebenzisa oko ubonileyo ukulungisa udliwano-ndlebe olulandelayo. Oku kunokuncedisa, kodwa akunakuqinisekisa ukuba ukuziphatha okunjalo akuyi kuphinda. Zama utshintsho olunye ngexesha, ukuze kube lula ukubona ukuba umntu uziva ezola na.

- **Khetha ixesha elizolileyo.** Ukuba umsebenzi uhlala uhamba kakubi xa umntu edinwe, buza ukuba ixesha elahlukileyo lingasebenza ngcono na.
- **Yenza indawo ibe lula.** Cima ingxolo esemva, ncitshisa inani labantu egumbini kwaye uqokelele into oyidingayo ngaphambi kokuqala.
- **Chaza inyathelo elinye uze ulinde.** Sondela phambili, uthi okwenzayo, ubuze ngaphambi kokunceda kwaye unike ixesha lokuba umntu aphendule.
- **Yima xa kukho iimpawu zokungonwabi.** Ukwala, ukuhoya, ubuso obuqinileyo okanye ilizwi eliphezulu zinokuba sisignali sokuyima. Ukugqiba umsebenzi akusoze kubaluleke ngaphezu kokuzola komntu.
- **Yabelana ngecwangciso elula.** Ukuba abantu abaninzi bemnakekela, vumelanani ngeempawu ezithetha “yima”, igama elizolileyo lokusebenzisa kunye nomntu oza kuqhagamshelana naye ukuba iphethini iyatshintsha.

Ngemisebenzi ethile, bona izikhokelo zethu [zokuhlamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) kunye [nokugqoka](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/). Isikhokelo [sonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sigubungela iindlela zokuthetha nokuphulaphula ngaphandle koxinzelelo.

## Yintoni ekufanele ndiyenze emva kokuba imeko izole?

Nika induduzo kwaye ubuyele kumsebenzi oqhelekileyo, olula, ukuba umntu uyawufuna loo nto. Musa ukukhalaza, ukugweba okanye ukujezisa. I-Alzheimer's Society ithi umntu usenokuba nentlungu emva kokuba ukuziphatha kuphelile, nangona engakhumbuli ukuba kwenzeke ntoni.

Ukuba ufuna ukuqonda iphethini, bhala inqaku elifutshane elicacileyo: yenzeke ntoni ngaphambili, ubone ntoni, kwathatha ixesha elingakanani kwaye yayisincedisa ntoni. Gcina inqaku lingqinelana nenyani, yabelana ngalo nethimba lonakekelo lomntu xa kufanelekile. Gwema ukurekhoda umntu okanye ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha zabucala ngaphandle kwemvume.

Ukuba umsebenzi uhlala ukhokelela kwintlungu, tshintsha ixesha okanye indlela, okanye cela omnye umntu othembekileyo ukuba anceda. Ungamkela ukala kwaye uzame kamva. Isikhokelo sethu [sokuthini xa umntu esithi hayi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) sinezinye iindlela zokuyeka ngaphandle koxinzelelo.

## Ndifanele ndicele uncedo lwezonyango nini?

Qhagamshelana nogqirha womntu malunga nobundlobongela obutsha, obungxamisekileyo, obuphindaphindayo okanye obuya buba bubi. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuba kwenziwe uhlolo lwezonyango xa ukuziphatha kutshintsha, ngakumbi xa kuvela ngokungxamisekileyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Intlungu, ukugula, ubuthongo, indawo kunye neeyeza zonke zisenokufuna ukuphononongwa. Ingcali yezonyango inokuvavanya imeko yomntu.

Funa uncedo lwexesha lengxaki lwasekuhlaleni ukuba umntu ngequbuliso uba sisiphithiphithi, ulimele okanye ubonakala engaphilanga kakhulu. Ukuba wena okanye omnye umntu ukhusela ingozi ekhoyo, qhagamshelana neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. Musa ukulinda ukuqokelela amanqaku okanye ukuzama ukufumanisa isizathu kuqala.

Nokhuseleko lwakho lubalulekile. Xelela ilungu losapho ohlomekileyo, ingcali yezonyango okanye inkonzo yenkxaso yesifo sokulibala yasekuhlaleni ukuba iziganeko ziqhubeka okanye ukuba uyoyika. Cela icebiso lokukhusela umntu nabantu abakufuphi kwakhe. Ufanelwe luncedo; ukuphatha oku wedwa akusiyo imfuneko.
