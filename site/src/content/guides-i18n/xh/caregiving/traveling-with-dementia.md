Uhambo lusenokuhlala lukhetho xa umntu efuna ukuya kwaye icwangciso ilingana neemfuno zakhe. Utyelelo oluncinane kusapho lunokulula kunohambo olupakishwe ngokupheleleyo; uhambo lwemini lunokulungela ngaphezu kokuhlala ubusuku. Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ntoni kuhambo, emva koko yakha icwangciso enegumbi lokuphumla okanye lokutshintsha.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association malunga nokuhamba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) kunye [necebiso le-Dementia Australia ngokuhamba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) zombini zigxininisa ukucwangcisa ngokukhathalela ukonwabela komntu kunye neendlela zakhe. Eli phepha linikezela ngeengcebiso zokulungiselela ngokubanzi, kungekhona isigqibo sokuba ukuhamba kukhuselekile na kumntu othile.

## Siyigqiba njani ukuba uhambo lulungele thina?

Thetha ngohambo nomntu, usebenzisa amagama aqhelekileyo kunye nenkcazelo elula yokuba niya phi nokuba ubani oza kuba khona. Ukuba akafuni ukuhamba, yamkela oko. Ungacwangcisa isidlo, ifowuni okanye umsebenzi oqhelekileyo ekhaya endaweni yoko.

Cinga ngento umntu ayiphatha ngokulula ngoku. Qwalasela indlela aphendula ngayo kutshintsho lwemigaqo, ingxolo, izixeko ezinabantu abaninzi, imigama yokuhamba ngeenyawo, ukulinda kunye neegumbi ezingaziwayo. Ukuba iinxalenye ezininzi zohambo zinokuba nzima, zenze zilule okanye khetha enye indlela yokuchitha ixesha kunye. UDementia Australia icebisa ukuba ubuze ugqirha xa ungaqinisekanga ukuba uhambo lufanele iimfuno zempilo okanye zenkxaso zomntu.

Ukuba umntu unemiqondiso emitsha yempilo, ufuna uncedo lwamayeza, okanye iimfuno zokuhamba zinokutshintsha ngexesha lohambo, buza ingcali yezempilo efanelekileyo ngaphambi kokubhukisha. Ungayeki, utshintshe okanye ulungise ixesha lokuthatha amayeza amiselweyo ngaphandle kwecebiso lalowo wawabhalayo okanye likafarmasi.

## Luhlobo luni lohambo esifanele sicwangcise?

- **Khetha indawo eyaziwayo xa kusenokwenzeka.** Indawo umntu ayaziyo, okanye utyelelo kubantu abaziwayo, kunokuba neenguqu ezimbalwa kunendlela entsha kunye necwangciso yokujonga izinto ezininzi.
- **Gcina indlela ilula.** Khetha iinguqu ezimbalwa kwaye ugweme ukuqhagamshelana ngexesha elincinci. Jonga apho kukho iitoyilethi, izihlalo kunye neendawo ezithe cwaka ngaphambi kokuqala.
- **Hamba ngexesha elifanelekileyo lemini.** Cwangcisa ngokwamandla aqhelekileyo komntu kunye nomjikelo wakhe wemihla ngemihla. Gcina ixesha lokutya, amayeza, ukuphumla kunye nokuzilungiselela.
- **Gcina ishedyuli ilula.** Umsebenzi omnye omkhulu usenokwanela. Ungabhukisha yonke iyure; shiya indawo yokulibaziseka okanye yokubuyela ekhaya kwangethuba.
- **Buza ngoncedo ngaphambi kokuhamba.** Iinkampani zeenqwelomoya, iinkampani zezitimela, iihotele kunye neendawo zemisitho zinezinkonzo kunye nemithetho eyahlukeneyo. Qhagamshelana nazo kwangethuba ubuze ngoncedo lokuhamba, izihlalo, iitoyilethi ezifikelelekayo kunye nokufumana indawo echanekileyo.

Isikhokelo [sohambo oluzolileyo kumaholide](/activities/holidays/) sinemibono yotyelelo nemibutho yentsapho. Ukuba niza kudibana nezalamane, [isikhokelo sethu sotyelelo](/activities/visiting/) sigubungela incoko, utyelelo oluncinane kunye nendlela yokushiya ngaphandle koxinzelelo.

## Yintoni ekufanele siyipakishe?

Gcina ibhegi encinci yohambo enezinto ezibalulekileyo ezihlala kunye nomntu omthembekileyo, kungekhona kwiphakheji ebhalisiweyo.

- **Amayeza kunye neenkcukacha zempilo.** Thatha amayeza aqhelekileyo kwiphakheji yawo yokuqala, anele kuhambo olucwangciswe kunye nokulibala okufanelekileyo, kunye nohlu lwamayeza lwangoku. Jonga imigaqo yangoku yezothutho kunye neyendawo oya kuyo ngaphambi kokuhamba namayeza amiselweyo. Gcina iidosi kunye nexesha njengoko zalinywa.
- **Uxanduva kunye noqhagamshelwano.** Thwala iphepha lokuchonga umntu, idilesi yehotele, iinkcukacha zoqhagamshelwano lwexesha lengxaki kunye necwangciso ebhaliweyo yohambo. Ukuba kuncedo kwaye kuvunyelwene ngako, faka ikhadi elinenkcukacha zoqhagamshelwano lomnakekeli.
- **Izinto zentuthuzelo.** Phakamisa iziqaqa zamehlo, izixhobo zokuva kunye nebhetri okanye isichargi sazo, izidlo ezincinci ezaziwayo, amanzi, amaphepha okusula, ingubo elula kunye nenguo yokutshintsha. Into eqhelekileyo okanye ifoto inokwenza igumbi elingaziwayo libe lula ukuhlala kuo.
- **Iikopi zezinto ezibalulekileyo.** Gcina ikopi yephepha okanye edijithali yeetikithi, izibhukisho, isiqinisekiso kunye neenkcukacha zempilo ezifanelekileyo. Yabelana ngecwangciso nomntu othembekileyo ongahambi nawe.

Ubuninzi bolwazi oluza kuthwalwa luxhomekeke kohambo. Gcina iinkcukacha zomntu zikhuselekile kwaye uzabelane kuphela nabantu abazidingayo ukuze bancede.

## Singenza njani ukuba imini yohambo ibe lula?

Xelela umntu into eza kwenzeka emva koko, inyathelo elinye ngexesha. Nika ixesha elongezelelweyo lokulungiselela, ukubhalisa, ukusebenzisa itoyilethi kunye nokuhamba phakathi kweendawo. Ukuba icwangciso iyatshintsha, chaza inyathelo elilandelayo ngokulula endaweni yokuphinda icwangciso ende.

Hlala kunye kwiindawo ezinxunguphalayo okanye ezingaziwayo. Ukuba umntu udinwe okanye engonwabanga, yima kwindawo ethe cwaka, mnike amanzi okanye umsebenzi oqhelekileyo, kwaye nisagqiba ngokunye ukuba niyaqhubeka na. Ungamshiyi umntu yedwa kwindawo engaziwayo apho anokudideka okanye aphazamiseke khona.

Ukuba uhamba ngenqwelomoya okanye usebenzisa isikhululo esikhulu, buza umboneleli ukuba zeziphi iinkonzo ezikhoyo kunye nendlela yokuzicela. Iinkqubo zinokutshintsha, ngoko qinisekisa iinkcukacha ngokuthe ngqo kwinkampani yeenqwelomoya, kwisikhululo okanye kwindawo ngaphambi kohambo. Ungaxhomekeki kuluhlu lwakudala lwemigaqo yokhuseleko okanye yokufikelela yangoku.

## Yeyiphi icwangciso yokuhlehla esebenzayo?

Chonga kwangaphambili into oza kuyenza ukuba umntu ufuna ukushiya, uhambo lulibazeka okanye umntu ufuna ukuphumla. Khetha umntu omnye onokunceda ngefowuni. Gcina idilesi yehotele kunye neendlela zokubuya zilula ukuzifumana, kwaye wazi apho unokuhlala khona ngokuzolileyo okanye usebenzise itoyilethi.

Ukuba kufuneka ufutshanise uhambo, yenza njalo. Ukuphoswa kwendawo ethile okanye ukubuya kwangethuba akusiyo ukusilela. Ukuba uhambo luba nzima, khetha umsebenzi ozolileyo xa ufika ekhaya. [Isikhokelo sonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sinemibono yokuthetha ngokuzola kunye nokunika inkcazelo enye ngexesha.

## Kufuneka sixelele ihotele okanye umboneleli wohambo?

Ukuba umntu uvumile, xelela abasebenzi kuphela ezo zinto bazidingayo ukuze banike uncedo oluncedayo. Umzekelo, ungacela ihotele ukuba iqaphele imfuneko yokuhamba okanye ucele igumbi elifikelelekayo. Ukuba umntu akafuni ukuba uxilongo lwakhe lwabelwane ngalo, xoxani enye indlela yokucela uncedo ekhusela ubumfihlo bakhe.

Kuhambo olude, gcina ukutya kwakhe okuqhelekileyo, ukulala kunye nexesha lamayeza zisondele kwimeko eqhelekileyo njengoko icwangciso ivumela. Cwangcisa imini yokuqala engakhawuleziyo kwaye ushiye ixesha lokuphumla emva kokubuya. Iinguqu ezincinci nezaziwayo zinokwenza uhambo lube lulula ukuzolusa.
