Ukuhamba kusenokuhlala kuyinto enokwenzeka xa umntu efuna ukuhamba kwaye icebo lihambelana neemfuno zakhe. Utyelelo olufutshane kusapho lusenokulula kunohambo olugcweleyo lokubona iindawo; uhambo losuku lunokulunga ngakumbi kunokulala ngaphandle kwasekhaya. Buza ukuba umntu ufuna ntoni kuloo hambo, uze wenze icebo elishiya ithuba lokuma okanye lokutshintsha.

I-[Alzheimer's Association travel guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) kunye ne-[Dementia Australia's travel advice](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) zombini zigxininisa ukucwangcisa ngokuhambelana nentuthuzelo yomntu kunye nemikhwa yakhe. Esi sikhokelo sinikela ngezimvo ezilula zokulungiselela, asisosigqibo sokuba ukuhamba kukhuselekile na kumntu othile.

## Siya kwazi njani ukuba uhambo lulungile kuthi?

Thetha ngohambo nomntu, usebenzisa amazwi aqhelekileyo kunye nenkcazo elula yaphi apho niya khona nokuba ngubani oya kubakho. Ukuba akafuni ukuhamba, yamkela oko. Usenokucwangcisa isidlo, umnxeba okanye into eqhelekileyo ekhaya endaweni yoko.

Cinga ngoko umntu akwenza ngokulula ngoku. Cinga ngendlela aphendula ngayo xa kutshintsha imikhwa, ingxolo, abantu abaninzi, imigama yokuhamba ngeenyawo, ukulinda kunye namagumbi angawaziyo. Ukuba iinxalenye ezininzi zohambo zinokuba nzima, lwenze lube lula okanye khetha enye indlela yokuchitha ixesha ndawonye. I-Dementia Australia icebisa ukubuza ugqirha xa ungaqinisekanga ukuba uhambo luhambelana nempilo yomntu okanye neemfuno zakhe zenkxaso.

Ukuba umntu une-ngxaki entsha yempilo, ufuna uncedo ngamayeza, okanye une-mfuno zokuhamba ezinokutshintsha ngexesha lohambo, buza ingcali efanelekileyo yezempilo phambi kokubhukisha. Musa ukuyeka, ukutshintsha okanye ukuvuselela ixesha lamayeza abhalelwe uhambo ngaphandle kwengcebiso yomnikezi wawo okanye yekhemesti.

## Luhlobo luni lohambo esifanele ukulucwangcisa?

- **Khetha indawo eqhelekileyo xa kunokwenzeka.** Indawo umntu ayaziyo, okanye utyelelo lwabantu aziqhelileyo, lusenokuba nokutshintsha okuncinane kunendlela entsha kunye neshedyuli exinekileyo yokubona iindawo.
- **Gcina indlela ilula.** Khetha ukutshintsha okuncinane kwezithuthi kwaye ugweme ukudibana kwexesha elimxinwa. Khangela apho kukhona izindlu zangasese, izitulo neendawo ezithe cwaka phambi kokuba nihambe.
- **Hamba ngexesha elifanelekileyo losuku.** Cwangcisa ngokuhambelana namandla aqhelekileyo omntu nendlela aqhuba ngayo usuku lwakhe. Shiya ixesha lokutya, amayeza, ukuphumla nokulungela.
- **Gcina ishedyuli ilula.** Into enye ephambili isenokwaneliswa. Musa ukubhukisha yonke iyure; shiya indawo yokulibaziseka okanye yokubuya kwangethuba.
- **Buza ngoncedo phambi kokuba nihambe.** Iinkampani zeenqwelomoya, zoloyiko, iihotele neendawo zeminyhadala zinemisebenzi nemithetho eyahlukeneyo. Zinxibelelane nazo kwangethuba ukuze ubuze ngenkxaso yokuhamba, izitulo, izindlu zangasese ezifikelelekayo noncedo lokufumana indawo efanelekileyo.

I-[guide to calmer holidays](/activities/holidays/) inezimvo zotyelelo nokuhlangana kosapho. Ukuba nityelele izalamane, i-[visiting guide](/activities/visiting/) yethu ithetha ngencoko, utyelelo olufutshane nendlela yokuhamba ngaphandle koxinzelelo.

## Sipakisha ntoni?

Gcina ingxowa encinane yohambo enezinto ezibalulekileyo ezihlala nomhlobo omthembileyo, hayi kwimithwalo etyunjelwe.

- **Amayeza nolwazi lwempilo.** Thatha amayeza aqhelekileyo omntu kwisikhongozeli sawo sokuqala, anele uhambo olucetyiweyo kunye nokulibaziseka okufanelekileyo, kunye noludwe lwamayeza lwangoku. Khangela imithetho yangoku yothutho neyendawo oya kuyo phambi kokuhamba namayeza abhalelwe. Gcina izixa nexesha njengoko kubhaliwe.
- **Isazisi neenkcukacha zonxibelelwano.** Thatha isazisi somntu, idilesi yendawo enihlala kuyo, iinkcukacha zabantu onokunxibelelana nabo xa kukho ingxaki kunye noludwe lohambo olubhaliweyo. Apho kunceda kwaye kuvunyiwe, faka ikhadi elinenkcukacha zonxibelelwano zomnonopheli.
- **Izinto zentuthuzelo.** Pakisha iiglasi, izixhobo zokuviwa nebhetri okanye itshaja yazo, izinto ezincinane zokutya ezaziwayo, amanzi, iitishu, ingubo ebhityileyo neempahla zokutshintsha. Into eqhelekileyo okanye ifoto isenokwenza igumbi elingaziwayo lilula ukulazelela.
- **Iikopi zeenkcukacha ezibalulekileyo.** Gcina ikopi eprintiweyo okanye ye-digital yeethikithi, ukubhukisha, i-inshorensi nolwazi lwempilo olufanelekileyo. Yabelana ngoludwe lohambo nomntu omthembileyo ongahambi nani.

Ubungakanani bolwazi olufanele ukuthathwa buxhomekeke kuhambo. Gcina iinkcukacha zobuqu zikhuselekile kwaye wabelane nazo kuphela nabantu abazidingayo ukuze banikeze uncedo.

## Singalwenza njani usuku lohambo lube lula?

Xelela umntu ukuba kuza kwenzeka ntoni emva koko, inyathelo linye ngexesha. Nika ixesha elongezelelekileyo lokulungela, lokubhalisa, lokusebenzisa indlu yangasese nokuhamba phakathi kweendawo. Ukuba izicwangciso ziyatshintsha, cacisa inyathelo elilandelayo ngokulula kunokuphinda uludwe olude lohambo.

Hlalani nonke ndawonye kwiindawo ezixineneyo okanye ezingaziwayo. Ukuba umntu uyadinwa okanye akazoli, mani kwindawo ethe cwaka, mnike amanzi okanye into aqhelene nayo, nize nisenze isigqibo kunye sokuba niyaqhubeka na. Musa ukumshiya yedwa umntu kwindawo angayaziyo apho anokudideka okanye akhathazeke.

Ukuba uhamba ngenqwelomoya okanye usebenzisa isikhululo esikhulu, buza umhlinzeki ukuba ngowuphi uncedo olukhoyo nokuba lucelwa njani. Iinkqubo zinokutshintsha, ngoko qinisekisa iinkcukacha ngqo neenkampani zeenqwelomoya, isikhululo okanye indawo phambi kokuhamba. Musa ukuthembela kuludwe lwakudala lwemigaqo yangoku yokhuseleko okanye yokufikeleleka.

## Licebo elinjani eliluncedo lokuzenzela?

Nikhethe kwangaphambili ukuba nenza ntoni xa umntu efuna ukuhamba, xa uhambo lulibele okanye xa omnye efuna ukuphumla. Khetha umntu omnye onokunceda ngomnxeba. Gcina idilesi yendawo enihlala kuyo nendlela yokubuya ilula ukuyifumana, kwaye wazi apho ningahlala phantsi ngokuthula okanye nisebenzise indlu yangasese.

Ukuba kufuneka ufinyeze uhambo, yenza njalo. Ukungabi kho kwenye yeendawo ozibonayo okanye ukubuya kwangethuba akukokungaphumeleli. Ukuba uhambo luba nengxinezelelo, khetha umsebenzi othe cwaka xa ufika ekhaya. I-[communication guide](/caregiving/communication-tips/) inezimvo zokuthetha ngokuthula nokunika ulwazi olunye ngexesha.

## Ngaba kufuneka siyixelele ihotele okanye umhlinzeki wohambo?

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, xelela abasebenzi kuphela into abayidingayo ukuze banike uncedo oluluncedo. Ngokomzekelo, usenokucela ihotele iqaphele imfuno yokuhamba okanye ucele igumbi elifikelelekayo. Ukuba umntu akafuni ukuba isifo sakhe sixelelwe abanye, thethani ngenye indlela yokucela uncedo ekhusela ubumfihlo bakhe.

Kuhambo olude, gcina ukutya komntu okuqhelekileyo, ukulala nexesha lamayeza kusondele kokuqhelekileyo ngokokuvumela kweshedyuli. Cwangcisa usuku lokuqala olungabalekiyo kwaye ushiye ixesha lokuphumla emva kokubuya. Ukhetho olulula nolwaziwayo lunokwenza kube lula ukujayela uhambo.
