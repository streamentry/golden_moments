Izigqibo zemali ziphembelela ukuzimela nobomi bemihla ngemihla bomntu. Ukuba umsebenzi uba nzima, qala ngalo msebenzi endaweni yokucinga ukuba omnye umntu kufuneka athathe yonke into. Buza umntu ukuba ufuna uncedo olunjani uze umgcine ebandakanyiwe.

Esi sikhokelo sinika ulwazi olubanzi kubafundi base-U.S. Iindlela zebhanki nemithetho yomthetho ziyahluka ngokwendawo. [Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Society sokulawula imali](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) siquka iindlela zokuxhasa umntu ngelixa egcina indima yakhe. [I-Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) ichaza ukuba uncedo olungekho sesikweni, abaqhagamshelwana abathenjiweyo negunya lomthetho zizinto ezahlukeneyo.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngaphandle kokuthatha ulawulo?

Buza umntu ukuba ngumsebenzi muphi wemali angathanda uncedo ngawo. Usenokufuna ukuphonononga ityala kunye, ukucoca iposi, ukukhumbula umhla wokuhlawula okanye ukujonga isicelo esingalindelekanga. Umntu usenokufuna uncedo ngemisebenzi ethile kwaye akhethe ukuphatha eminye.

- **Buza phambi kokuba wenze.** Zama, "Ungathanda ukujonga eli tyala kunye?" Nika umntu ixesha lokugqiba.
- **Gxila kumsebenzi omnye.** Ukuba umntu uyavuma, phonononga ixabiso, umhla wokuhlawula neendlela zokuhlawula kunye. Mvumele enze ukhetho afuna ukulwenza.
- **Yenzani irekhodi elilula kunye.** Uluhlu olufutshane lwamatyala, imihla yokuhlawula okanye amanyathelo avunyiweyo lunokunceda nobabini ukubona oko sele kuphethwe.
- **Buza ibhanki ngeendlela zayo.** Isenokunika iindlela zokwenza iintlawulo okanye ukujonga umsebenzi ube lula. Buza ukuba nganye ivumela ntoni phambi kokutshintsha iakhawunti.
- **Jonga kwakhona kamva.** Izinto umntu azithandayo zisenokutshintsha. Gcina uncedo lulinganiselwe koko akufunayo nakudingayo ngoku.

I-CFPB ichaza inkxaso engekho sesikweni njengokuphonononga amatyala nokwenza ibhajethi nomntu ngelixa lo mntu esagunyazisa iintlawulo. Oko kunokwahluka ekuvumeleni omnye umntu ukuba akhuphe imali okanye enze iintlawulo. Musa ukwabelana ngepasiwedi, ii-PIN okanye iikhowudi zokhuseleko zexesha elinye njengendlela emfutshane.

## Umqhagamshelwana owathenjiweyo uvumela umntu ukuba enze ntoni?

E-United States, uphawu lomqhagamshelwana owathenjiweyo lunokuvumela ibhanki ukuba iqhagamshelane nomntu kwezinye iimeko, njengenkxalabo ngobuqhetseba obusenokubakho. I-CFPB ithi ukukhetha umqhagamshelwana owathenjiweyo akumniki ukufikelela kwiakhawunti okanye imvume yokuhambisa imali. Uphawu lusenokungafumaneki kuyo yonke iakhawunti, ngoko buza ibhanki okanye inkampani yezemali ukuba isebenza njani.

| Uhlobo loncedo | Okuthethwa ngako |
|---|---|
| Phonononga amatyala kunye | Umntu nomncedisi bajonga amatyala kunye; umntu uthatha isigqibo aze agunyazise iintlawulo. |
| Umqhagamshelwana owathenjiweyo | Ibhanki isenokuqhagamshelana nomntu otyunjiweyo kwezinye iimeko. Igama kuphela alinikeli ukufikelela kwiakhawunti. |
| Iakhawunti esesikweni okanye igunya lomthetho | Imvume yebhanki, igunya lommeli okanye uququzelelo olufanayo lusenokuvumela iintlawulo. Amandla noxanduva lwalo zixhomekeke kwiakhawunti nakumthetho wasekuhlaleni. |

Ngaphambi kokutshintsha ukufikelela okanye ubunini beakhawunti, buza ibhanki nengcali yomthetho efanelekileyo yasekuhlaleni ukuba ichaze iindlela neengozi. Ukufunyanwa kwedementia kuphela akukuxeleli ukuba ngemisebenzi yemali yeyiphi umntu anokuyiphatha okanye ukhetho analufunayo. Mgcine eyinxalenye yezigqibo ngokwamandla afuna ngawo. Ukuba ufuna umntu ukuba enze egameni lakhe, buza indlela yokuseta oko ngokusemthethweni endaweni yokusebenzisa ukungena komnye umntu.

## Zeziphi iinguqu ekufuneka ndizijonge?

Ityala elilahlekileyo okanye ukuthenga okungaqhelekanga kunokuba nezizathu ezahlukeneyo. Akungqini ukuba umntu akakwazi ukulawula imali okanye ukuba omnye umntu uye wamxhaphaza. Buza ukuba kwenzeke ntoni uze, ukuba umntu uyavuma, ujonge ingxelo okanye ityala elifanelekileyo kunye.

[I-CFPB idwelisa iimpawu ezinokubakho zokuxhatshazwa kwezemali](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), kuquka imali okanye impahla elahlekileyo, ukukhutshwa kwemali okungaqhelekanga ngesiquphe, amatyala angabhatalwanga, abantu abangaziwayo abongezwa kwiiakhawunti, okanye umntu olinganisa iifowuni notyelelo lwalo mntu. Qaphela iipatheni noguqulo kwesiqhelo salo mntu; musa ukuthatha uphawu olunye njengobungqina.

Ukuba umntu uthi imali ilahlekile, thatha inkxalabo ngokunzulu. [Isikhokelo sethu sokuphendula ukrokrelo](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) sinika iindlela zokujonga inkxalabo ngaphandle kokuyitshabalalisa. Buza umntu ngasese ukuba uqaphele ntoni nokuba ufuna kwenzeke ntoni emva koko. Phepha ukusola isihlobo, umnyamekeli okanye umsebenzi webhanki phambi kokujonga iinyaniso. Ukuba ukrokrela ukuba umncedisi othenjiweyo usenokuba ubandakanyekile, cela ingcebiso kwingcali eyahlukileyo okanye kwinkonzo yasekuhlaleni.

## Singawunciphisa njani umngcipheko wobuqhetseba?

Abaqhetseba basenokuzenza ibhanki, iofisi karhulumente, ishishini, umhlobo okanye isihlobo. Basenokuthi kukho ingxaki ekhawulezileyo baze banyanzelise intlawulo kwangoko. [Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokhuseleko lobuchwepheshe](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) sicebisa ukuyeka, ukujonga umqhagamshelwana nokufumana inkxaso phambi kokwabelana ngolwazi okanye ngemali.

- **Yeka xa umyalezo uvakala ukhawuleza.** Musa ukuthumela imali okanye ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha zeakhawunti ngelixa umntu ofowunayo okanye umyalezo uxinzelela.
- **Jonga usebenzisa umqhagamshelwana oselethenjiwe.** Vala ifowuni uze ufowune ibhanki kwinombolo ekwingxelo yamva nje okanye ekhadini, okanye usebenzise i-app yayo esesikweni. Musa ukusebenzisa ilinki okanye inombolo ethunyelwe ngumntu ofowunayo.
- **Gcina iinkcukacha zokhuseleko zisisithuba.** Musa ukuthumela iipasiwedi, iinkcukacha ezipheleleyo zeakhawunti okanye iikhowudi zokuqinisekisa ekuphenduleni umyalezo ongalindelekanga. [I-FTC icebisa ukuphelisa ifowuni engalindelekanga ecela ikhowudi uze uqhagamshelane nebhanki ngenombolo oyithembileyo](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Vumelana ngenkxaso nomntu.** Buza ukuba angathanda ukujonga nabani isicelo esimothusayo. Uphawu lomqhagamshelwana owathenjiweyo webhanki lusenokunceda unxibelelwano, kodwa alugunyazisi iintlawulo ngokwalo.

## Ndimele ndenze ntoni ukuba imali isenokuba iye kumqhetseba?

Qhagamshelana nebhanki, i-app yeentlawulo, umnikezeli wekhadi okanye inkampani esetyenziselwe ukuthumela imali kwangoko ngoko unokukwenza. Sebenzisa inombolo ekwikhadi okanye ekwingxelo, okanye i-app okanye iwebhusayithi esesikweni yombutho. Buza ukuba ingayimisa okanye ibuyise intlawulo. [Amanyathelo okubuyisela e-FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) achaza ukuba iindlela zixhomekeke kwindlela imali ethunyelwe ngayo; akukho ngqinisiso lokuba ingabuyiswa.

Gcina imiyalezo, iirisithi, imihla neenkcukacha zeakhawunti. E-United States, ungaxela ubuqhetseba ku-[FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Ukuba ukrokrela ukuba umntu uxhaphaza umntu omdala, [i-Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) ithi qhagamshelana ne-Adult Protective Services kwidabi lomntu. Ukuba umntu usengozini ekhoyo ngoku, qhagamshelana neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. Ngaphandle kwe-United States, sebenzisa iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni zokukhusela abathengi nokhuseleko.

Thetha ngaphandle kokusola. Ubuqhetseba bayilelwe ukulahlekisa abantu. [Isikhokelo se-CFPB soncedo ngobuqhetseba](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) sidwelisa iindlela zokuxela zase-U.S. kwaye sicebisa ukusebenzisa iiwebhusayithi neenombolo zefowuni ezithenjiweyo.

## Ndingafumana phi uncedo ngesigqibo esithile semali?

Buza ibhanki yomntu ukuba ngawaphi amathuluzi neemvume ezikhoyo. Ngexwebhu lomthetho okanye utshintsho lobunini beakhawunti, thetha nengcali efanelekileyo eyaziyo imithetho kwindawo apho umntu ahlala khona. Ukuba uxhalabile ngokuxhatshazwa okusenokubakho, sebenzisa inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yokhuseleko. Eli phepha alinakugqiba ukuba ngubani onokuba negunya elinjani okanye ukuba ukuthengiselana okuthile bubuqhetseba na.

[Isikhokelo sokungavumelani kosapho](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) sinika iindlela zokuxoxa ngenkxalabo enye ngexesha.
