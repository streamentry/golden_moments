Izigqibo zemali zichaphazela ukuzimela komntu kunye nobomi bemihla ngemihla. Ukuba umsebenzi uba nzima, qala ngalo msebenzi endaweni yokucinga ukuba omnye umntu kufuneka athathe konke. Buza umntu ukuba ufuna uncedo luluhlobo luni kwaye umgcine ebandakanyekile.

Esi sikhokelo sinika ulwazi jikelele kubafundi base-U.S. Ukhetho lwebhanki kunye nemithetho yomthetho kuyahluka ngokwendawo. I-[Alzheimer's Society isikhokelo sokulawula izimali](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) sigubungela iindlela zokuxhasa umntu ngelixa igcina indima yakhe. I-[Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) ichaza ukuba uncedo olungasemthethweni, unxibelelwano oluthembekileyo kunye negunya lomthetho zahlukile.

## Ndinokunika njani uncedo ngaphandle kokuthatha ulawulo?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna uncedo ngomsebenzi wemali oluphi. Usenokufuna ukujonga ibhili ndawonye, ukuhlela iposi, ukukhumbula umhla wokuhlawula okanye ukujonga isicelo esingalindelekanga. Umntu usenokufuna uncedo kwimisebenzi ethile kwaye akhethe ukuphatha ezinye ngokwakhe.

- **Buza ngaphambi kokwenza.** Zama ukuthi, “Ingaba ufuna sijonge le bhili kunye?” Nika umntu ixesha lokuthatha isigqibo.
- **Gxila kumsebenzi omnye.** Ukuba umntu uvumile, jonga kunye isixa, umhla wokuhlawula kunye neendlela zokuhlawula. Mvumele enze unyulo afuna ukulwenza.
- **Yenzani irekhodi elilula ndawonye.** Uluhlu olufutshane lwebhili, imihla yokuhlawula okanye amanyathelo avunyelwene ngawo lunokunceda nina sobabini nibone into eyenziwe.
- **Buza ibhanki ngeenketho zayo.** Usenokufumana iindlela zokwenza iintlawulo okanye zokujonga umsebenzi ziye zibe lula. Buza into ngayinye enikezelayo ngaphambi kokutshintsha i-akhawunti.
- **Jonga kwakhona kamva.** Izikhetho zomntu zinokutshintsha. Gcina uncedo lulinganiselwe kwento abayifunayo kwaye bayidinga ngoku.

I-CFPB ichaza uncedo olungasemthethweni njengokujonga iibhili kunye nokucwangcisa uhlahlo-lwabiwo-mali nomntu, ngelixa lowo mntu usagunyazisa iintengiselwano. Oko kunokwahluka ekuvumeleni omnye umntu ahoze imali okanye enze iintlawulo. Musa ukwabelana ngeenombolo zemfihlo, ii-PIN okanye iikhowudi zokhuseleko ezisetyenziswa kanye kuphela njengendlela emfutshane.

## Unxibelelwano oluthembekileyo lumvumela umntu ukuba enze ntoni?

Kwi-United States, isakhono sonxibelelwano oluthembekileyo sinokuvumela iziko lezemali liqhagamshelane nomntu ngeemeko ezithile, njengokukhathazeka ngokukhohliswa okunokwenzeka. I-CFPB ithi ukukhankanya unxibelelwano oluthembekileyo akumnikezeli ufikelelo kwiakhawunti okanye imvume yokuhambisa imali. Esi sakhono asinakufumaneka kuzo zonke ii-akhawunti, ngoko buza ibhanki okanye ifemu yezemali ukuba isebenza njani.

| Uhlobo loncedo | Kuthetha ntoni |
|---|---|
| Ukujonga iibhili ndawonye | Umntu nomncedi bajonga iibhili ndawonye; umntu uthatha isigqibo kwaye uvumela iintlawulo. |
| Unxibelelwano oluthembekileyo | Iziko lezemali lisenokuqhagamshelana nomntu okhankanyiweyo kwiimeko ezithile. Igama elodwa alinikezi ufikelelo kwiakhawunti. |
| Ufikelelo oluyimfuneko okanye igunya lomthetho | Imvume yebhanki, igunya lokuthetha egameni lomntu okanye enye imisebenzi efanayo inokuvumela iintengiselwano. Amandla nemisebenzi yayo axhomekeke kwiakhawunti nakumthetho wasekuhlaleni. |

Ngaphambi kokutshintsha ufikelelo lwe-akhawunti okanye umnini wayo, cela ibhanki kunye negcali esemthethweni yasekuhlaleni ukuba icacise ezi ndlela kunye neengozi. Isazisi sesifo ngokwaso asikuxeleli ukuba ngeephi imisebenzi yezimali umntu anokuyiphatha okanye ngeziphi izigqibo afuna ukuzenza. Mgcine ebandakanyekile kwizigqibo njengoko efuna. Ukuba bafuna umntu abenzele izinto, buza indlela yokuseta oko ngokomthetho endaweni yokusebenzisa ulwazi lokungena lomntu omnye.

## Yeziphi iitshintsho ekufuneka ndizijonge?

Ibhili eziphoswe okanye ukuthenga okungaqhelekanga kunokuba nezichazi-nto ezahlukeneyo. Akubonisi ukuba umntu akakwazi ukulawula imali okanye ukuba kukho omnye umntu owamkhohlisayo. Buza ukuba kwenzeke ntoni kwaye, ukuba umntu uvumile, jonga ukhutshiso okanye ibhili efanelekileyo ndawonye.

I-[CFPB idwelisa iimpawu ezinokubakho zokuhlukunyezwa ngezimali](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), kubandakanya imali okanye ipropathi elahlekayo, ukukhutshwa kwemali okungaqhelekanga ngequbuliso, iibhili ezingahlawulwanga, abantu abangaziwayo bongezwa kwii-akhawunti, okanye umntu oncitshisa iikholi nokundwendwela komntu. Qaphela iipatheni kunye notshintsho olungqinelana nento eqhelekileyo kulo mntu; musa ukuthatha impawu enye njengobungqina.

Ukuba umntu uthi imali ilahlekile, thatha inkxalabo ngokungathi sha. Isikhokelo sethu [sokuphendula ukukrokrela](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) sinika iindlela zokuhlola inkxalabo ngaphandle kokuyicothisa. Buza umntu ngasese ukuba ubone ntoni kwaye ufuna kwenzeke ntoni emva koko. Gwema ukumbeka icala ilungu losapho, umnakekeli okanye umsebenzi webhanki ngaphambi kokuhlola iinyani. Ukuba ukrokrela ukuba umncedi othembekileyo usenokuba nxulumanisiwe, buza ingcali ehlukileyo okanye inkonzo yasekuhlaleni ngecebiso.

## Singanciphisa njani umngcipheko wokukhohliswa?

Abakhohlisi banokuziphosa njengebhanki, iofisi karhulumente, ishishini, umhlobo okanye ilungu losapho. Banokuthi kukho ingxaki eyongxamisekileyo kwaye bacinezele intlawulo ngoko nangoko. [Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokhuseleko lwetekhnoloji](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) sicebisa ukuma, ukujonga unxibelelwano kwaye ufumane uncedo ngaphambi kokwabelana ngolwazi okanye ngemali.

- **Yima xa umyalezo uziva ungxamisekile.** Musa ukuthumela imali okanye ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha ze-akhawunti ngelixa umbizi okanye umyalezo ucinezela.
- **Hlola usebenzisa unxibelelwano omthembileyo kakade.** Vala ifowuni kwaye ubize ibhanki kwinombolo ekwikhadi okanye kwisitatimenti yakutshanje, okanye usebenzise i-app yayo esemthethweni. Musa ukusebenzisa ikhonkco okanye inombolo ethunyelwe ngumbizi.
- **Gcina iinkcukacha zokhuseleko zisimfihlo.** Musa ukuthumela iipasiwedi, zonke iinkcukacha ze-akhawunti okanye iikhowudi zokuqinisekisa ekuphenduleni umyalezo ongalindelekanga. I-[FTC icebisa ukuvala ikholi engalindelekanga ebuza ikhowudi kwaye uqhagamshelane nebhanki ngenombolo oyithembayo](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Vumelana ngoncedo kunye nomntu.** Buza ukuba bafuna ukujonga isicelo esimangalisayo kunye nobani. Isakhono sonxibelelwano oluthembekileyo sebhanki sinokunceda unxibelelwano, kodwa asigunyazisi iintengiselwano ngokwaso.

## Ndifanele ndenze ntoni ukuba imali isenokuba yifikele kumkhohlisi?

Qhagamshelana nebhanki, i-app yentlawulo, umnikezeli wekhadi okanye inkampani esetyenziswe ukuthumela imali ngoko nangoko. Sebenzisa inombolo ekhadini okanye kwisitatimenti, okanye i-app okanye iwebhusayithi esemthethweni yombutho. Buza ukuba ingayimisa okanye ibuyisele intlawulo. [Amanyathelo okubuyisela ye-FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) achaza ukuba ezi ndlela zixhomekeke kwindlela imali ethunyelwe ngayo; akukho siqinisekiso sokuba inokubuyiselwa.

Gcina imiyalezo, iirisithi, imihla kunye nezaziso ze-akhawunti. Kwi-United States, unokunika ingxelo ngokukhohliswa kwi-[FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Ukuba ukrokrela ukuba umntu usebenzisa kakubi umntu omdala, i-[Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) ithi qhagamshelana nenkonzo yoKhuseleko loMntu Omdala kwilizwe lomntu. Ukuba ukho umntu ozingozini ngoko nangoko, qhagamshelana neenkonzo zasekuhlaleni eziphuthumayo. Ngaphandle kwe-United States, sebenzisa iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni zokhuseleko lomthengi nokhuseleko.

Thetha ngaphandle kokugweba. Ukukhohliswa kwenziwe ukuba kukhohlise abantu. [Isikhokelo se-CFPB sosizo lokukhohliswa nobuqhetu](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) sidwelisa iindlela zokunika ingxelo e-U.S. kwaye sicebisa ukusebenzisa iiwebhusayithi neenombolo zomnxeba ezithembekileyo.

## Ndingafumana phi uncedo ngesigqibo esithile sezimali?

Buza ibhanki yomntu ukuba zeziphi izixhobo kunye nemvume ezikhoyo. Ngoxwebhu lomthetho okanye utshintsho lomnini we-akhawunti, thetha negcali esemthethweni eyaziyo imithetho yendawo umntu ahlala kuyo. Ukuba uxhalabile ngokuhlukunyezwa okunokubakho, sebenzisa inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yokhuseleko. Eli phepha alinakuthatha sigqibo ngegunya elifanele umntu okanye ukuba intengiselwano ethile yingqwalasela yokukhohliswa na.
