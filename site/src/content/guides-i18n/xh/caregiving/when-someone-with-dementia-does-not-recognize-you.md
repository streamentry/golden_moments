Ukuba umntu onesifo sokulibala akakuqondi ngoku, oko kunokubuhlungu kwaye kudideke. Unokuzibuza ukuba uthini okanye ukuba ufanele uchaze ubudlelwane bakho kwakhona. Imeko nganye yahlukile. Qala ngento enceda umntu azive ekhuselekile nekhululekile, kungekhona ngovavanyo lwento akhumbulayo.

I-[Alzheimer's Association isikhokelo malunga nokulibala nokudideka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/memory-loss-confusion) ibandakanya ukungaqondi usapho njengenye yeenguqu ezisenokwenzeka. I-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) ne-[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/how-dementia-changes-perception) zichaza ukuba umntu usenokudida omnye umntu amaziyo ngomnye. Eli lulwalathiso ngokubanzi; alinakuchaza ukuba kutheni utshintsho oluthile lwenzeka.

## Yintoni enokuba ngumba xa umntu engandiqondi?

Isifo sokulibala sinokuchaphazela inkumbulo kunye nendlela umntu etolika ngayo into abayibonayo. Umntu usenokukudida nelinye ilungu losapho, okanye angaqinisekanga ukuba niqhagamshelana njani. Oku akuchazi ngokuchanileyo ukuba umntu uziva njani ngawe. Gxininisa ekukhuseleni kwakhe kunye nasekuphenduleni kwakhe ngelo xesha.

Igumbi elikhanyiswe kakubi okanye elixakeke, ukungaboni kakuhle okanye ukungaviwa kakuhle, ukugula, iimpembelelo zeeyeza, indawo engaziwayo okanye utshintsho kumjikelo wemihla ngemihla nako kunokuchaphazela ukuqonda. Ezi zinto zinokuba negalelo, kodwa azinakuchaza isizathu. Ukuba utshintsho lutsha okanye lusoyikisa umntu ngokuqhubekayo, xelela ingcali yakhe yezonyango ukuba yenzeke ntoni kwaye ubuze ukuba kufuneka ahlolwe na.

Ukuba igumbi lidimazile okanye lixakeke, zama utshintsho olunye: vula ilayithi, nciphisa ingxolo okanye uhambele kwindawo eyaziwayo. Buza ukuba umntu ufuna iiglasi zakhe okanye izixhobo zokuva ukuba uyazisebenzisa. Ezi nyathelo zinokunceda, kodwa azizukuchaza zonke iimeko zokungaqondwa.

## Yintoni endinokuyithetha ngelo xesha?

Sondela apho umntu akubona khona, uthethe ngelizwi elizolileyo nelaziwayo. Zazisa igama lakho kunye nobudlelwane benu ngendlela elula, njengoko wawuya kuyenza ngolunye usuku: “Molo, ndinguAlex, yintombi yakho.” Emva koko yima kwaye uqaphele ukuba uphendula njani.

- **Ukuba uzolile,** qhubeka nencoko eqhelekileyo. Thetha ngento eyenzeka ngoku, okanye ubuze ukuba ufuna ukuhlala kunye.
- **Ukuba uyangqongqoza,** nika uqinisekiso olufutshane: “Ukhuselekile. Ndihleli nawe.” Mnike ixesha lokuphendula.
- **Ukuba uyoyika okanye ucela ukuba ushiye,** yima umke kwaye umnike indawo. Musa ukumkrazula ngamandla, ungaphindi uthi uyakwazi okanye uphinde ulwazi olumcaphukisayo.
- **Ukuba ukubiza ngegama elingelinye,** ungazazisa kanye ngaphandle kokuguqula into ibe sisilungiso. Ukuba uhlala ezola, tshintsha isihloko okanye umsebenzi awuthandayo.

Isikhokelo [sonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sincoma incoko emfutshane, ezolileyo kunye nokugwema imibuzo okanye iingxabano. Zama ukungabuzi ukuthi, “Undazi ubani?” Lo mbuzo unokuziva njengovavanyo kwaye awunakuncedisa umntu akuqonde.

## Ingaba umqondiso oqhelekileyo unokunceda?

Ngamanye amaxesha umqondiso unokunceda umntu akubeke, kodwa awuyi kusebenza kubo bonke. Zama umqondiso omnye ngexesha, kwaye yeka xa ungeza uxinzelelo.

- **Thetha igama lakho ngendlela eqhelekileyo.** Izwi eliqhelekileyo okanye intshayelelo emfutshane inokwanela.
- **Sebenzisa ifoto okanye into eyabelwanwayo.** Ukuba umntu uyonwabela iifoto, ningajonga enye kunye ngaphandle kokumcela ukuba abiza ilungu elithile. Isikhokelo sethu [sokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) sinemibono yokusebenzisa iifoto ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo.
- **Zama umsebenzi oqhelekileyo wabantu bobabini.** Ingoma eyaziwayo, isiselo okanye umsebenzi oqhelekileyo unokwenza ixesha libe lula. Landela impendulo yomntu; kulungile ukutshintsha isihloko okanye ukugqiba utyelelo.
- **Nciphisa ukudideka okusingqongileyo.** Qinisekisa ukuba igumbi linokukhanya ngokwaneleyo kwaye umntu unazo iiglasi okanye izixhobo zokuva ukuba uzisebenzisa. Indawo ethe cwaka inokuncedisa xa abantu abaninzi bethetha.

Ungaqhubeki ungeza imiqondiso ukuba umntu usahlala engakhululekanga. Inkcazelo ende okanye uluhlu lwemifanekiso lunokumoyikisa. Ukuba uhlala nje ngokuzolileyo kunokwanela; kwakhona kulungile ukuthatha ikhefu kwaye uzame kwakhona ngelinye ixesha.

## Ndingenza njani ukuba utyelelo lube lula?

Ukuba umntu uhlala kwindawo yokukhathalelwa okanye ufumana abancedi abaninzi, buza abantu abamaziyo indlela yakhe ngexesha lakhe ukuba xa ehlala ezola kakhulu. Ungatyelela ngexesha elithe cwaka, uhambe nomntu amaziyo okanye uzise into enye eyaziwayo. Ezi zinto zikhetho ekufanele uzicinge, zingasiyo ishedyuli ekufuneka yonke intsapho iyilandele.

Ngaphambi kotyelelo, chonga into eya kukuvumela nawe ukuba uzole. Unokucwangcisa umsebenzi omfutshane omnye, uzise incwadi yeefoto okanye uhlale nje kunye. Ukuba umntu akabonakali eyazi ukuba ungubani, usenokuphendula kwiimfuno zakhe ngelo xesha. Musa ukwenza utyelelo lulawulwe kukuba uthi igama lakho na.

Kuyanzima ukuziva udanile, udeliwe okanye ucaphukile. Thetha nomntu omthembileyo, umcebisi okanye iqela lenkxaso labanakekeli ukuba oku kukunzima kuwe. Isikhokelo [sokuzinakekela](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sinemibono yokucela abanye bakunike inkxaso.

## Ndicele uncedo lwezonyango nini?

Ukuba ukungaqondi umntu kutsha, kuphindaphindeka okanye kubangela ukungonwabi, xelela ugqirha womntu. Qaphela ukuba kwenzeka nini kwaye ukuba kwakukho ntoni enye ngelo xesha, njengotshintsho kwimpilo, ubuthongo, ukukhanya, ukuva, iyeza okanye indawo. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza ngokusekelwe kwinqaku eliqhelekileyo.

Ukudideka okungazelelwanga okanye utshintsho olukhawulezayo kwindlela umntu eziphatha ngayo okanye kwimpilo kufuna unyango lwexesha elingxamisekileyo. Isikhokelo se-[NHS malunga nokudideka okungazelelwanga](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi kufuneka ufumane uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko. Sebenzisa inkonzo yezempilo yoxanduva okanye yexesha lengxaki yasekuhlaleni apho umntu ahlala khona.

Ukungaqondwa kunokuba nzima kubo bobabini. Gcina amagama akho alula, nika umqondiso kuphela xa uncedo, kwaye vumela ukuzola komntu kube yiyo ekhokela ukuba kwenzeke ntoni kulandelayo.
