Ukuba umntu one-dementia akasakwazi, eso sihlandlo sisenokukwenza ubone ubuhlungu nokudideka. Usenokuzibuza ukuba uthethe ntoni okanye ukuba kufuneka uphinde uchaze ubudlelwane. Imeko nganye yahlukile. Qala ngoko kubonakala kunceda umntu azive ekhuselekile nethuthuzelekile, hayi ngovavanyo lwento ayikhumbulayo.

I-[Alzheimer's Association guide to memory loss and confusion](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/memory-loss-confusion) ibalela ukungabazi usapho phakathi kotshintsho olunokwenzeka. I-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) kunye ne-[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/how-dementia-changes-perception) zichaza ukuba umntu usenokubhidanisa umntu amaziyo nomnye umntu. Oku kukukhokela ngokubanzi; akunakukuxelela ukuba kutheni utshintsho oluthile lwenzeka.

## Kusenokwenzeka ntoni xa umntu engakwazi?

I-dementia inokuchaphazela inkumbulo nendlela umntu atolika ngayo oko akubonayo. Umntu usenokukubhidanisa nelinye ilungu losapho okanye angaqinisekanga ukuba nidibene njani. Oku akukuxeleli ncam ukuba uvakalelwa kanjani ngawe. Gxila kwintuthuzelo yakhe nendlela aphendula ngayo kweso sihlandlo.

Ukukwazi kunokuchaphazeleka nayikukhanya okungeyonganeno, ngeengxaki zokubona okanye zokuva, kukugula, ziziphumo zamayeza, yindawo engaziwayo okanye kukutshintsha kwemikhwa. Ezi zizinto ezinokubakho, hayi indlela yokuchonga isizathu. Ukuba utshintsho lutsha okanye luhlala lumkhathaza umntu, xelela ugqirha wakhe okwenzekileyo kwaye ubuze ukuba kufuneka ahlolwe na.

Ukuba igumbi lingenakukhanya okanye liphithene, zama utshintsho olunye: bhanyabhanya isibane, nciphisa ingxolo engasemva okanye yiya kwindawo eqhelekileyo. Buza ukuba umntu ufuna iiglasi okanye izixhobo zokuviwa ukuba uyazisebenzisa. La manyathelo asenokunceda, kodwa akayi kuchaza yonke imeko yokungakwazi.

## Ndingathetha ntoni ngelo xesha?

Sondela apho umntu enokukubona khona kwaye uthethe ngelizwi elithe cwaka, eliqhelekileyo. Yitsho igama lakho nobudlelwane benu ngokulula, njengoko ungamsebenzisa ngosuku olwahlukileyo: “Molo, ndinguAlex, intombi yakho.” Emva koko nqumama uqaphele indlela aphendula ngayo.

- **Ukuba ubonakala ekhululekile,** qhubeka nencoko eqhelekileyo. Thetha ngokwenzekayo ngoku, okanye ubuze ukuba angathanda ukuhlala nani na.
- **Ukuba ubonakala engaqinisekanga,** nika isiqinisekiso esifutshane: “Ukhuselekile. Ndikunye nawe.” Mnike ixesha lokuphendula.
- **Ukuba ubonakala eoyika okanye ekucela ukuba umke,** hlehla umnike indawo. Musa ukunyanzela ukwanga, ukunyanzela ukuba akwazi okanye ukuphindaphinda ulwazi olubonakala lumkhathaza.
- **Ukuba ukubiza ngelinye igama,** unokuzazisa kube kanye ngaphandle kokuyenza ibe kukulungisa. Ukuba uhlala ekhululekile, tshintshela kwisihloko okanye kumsebenzi awuthandayo.

I-[communication guide](/caregiving/communication-tips/) incoma ulwimi olufutshane, oluthe cwaka nokuphepha imibuzo yovavanyo okanye ukuphikisana. Zama ungabuzi, “Uyandikhumbula ukuba ndingubani?” Lo mbuzo usenokuvakala njengovavanyo kwaye usenokungamnceda umntu ukuba akwazi.

## Ngaba isikhumbuzo esaziwayo singanceda?

Ngamanye amaxesha isikhumbuzo sinokunceda umntu akubeke, kodwa asiyi kusebenza kuye wonke umntu. Zama isikhumbuzo esinye esithambileyo ngexesha, kwaye uyeke ukuba songeza uxinzelelo.

- **Yitsho igama lakho ngokwemvelo.** Ilizwi eliqhelekileyo okanye intshayelelo emfutshane isenokwanela.
- **Sebenzisa ifoto okanye into eyabelanwayo.** Ukuba umntu uyazonwabela iifoto, ninokuyijonga kunye ngaphandle kokumcela ukuba abize igama lomntu. I-[reminiscence guide](/activities/reminiscence/) yethu inezimvo zokusebenzisa iifoto ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo yovavanyo.
- **Zama umsebenzi oqhelekileyo enibabelana ngawo.** Ingoma eyaziwayo, isiselo okanye umsebenzi usenokwenza eso sihlandlo sibe lula. Landela indlela umntu aphendula ngayo; kulungile ukutshintsha isihloko okanye ukugqiba utyelelo.
- **Nciphisa ukudideka okungqonge wena.** Qinisekisa ukuba igumbi linokukhanya okwaneleyo nokuba umntu unazo iiglasi okanye izixhobo zokuviwa ukuba uyazisebenzisa. Indawo ethe cwaka isenokunceda xa abantu abaninzi bethetha.

Musa ukuqhubeka ungeza izikhumbuzo ukuba umntu uhlala engakhululekanga. Inkcazo ende okanye uthotho lweefoto lusenokumdinga. Ukubakho ngokuthe cwaka kusenokwanela; kulungile ukuphumla uzame kwakhona ngelinye ixesha.

## Ndingawenza njani utyelelo lube lula?

Ukuba umntu uhlala ekhaya lokhathalelo okanye ubona abancedisi abaninzi, buza abo bayaziyo imikhwa yakhe ukuba athe ukuvakala ethuthuzelekile ngaliphi ixesha. Usenokutyelela ngexesha elithe cwaka, uye nomntu amaziyo okanye uzise into enye eqhelekileyo. La ngamanyathelo onokuwacinga, hayi ishedyuli ekufuneka yalandelwa yiyo yonke imizi.

Phambi kotyelelo, nqwenela ukuba yintoni eya kuvakala ilula nakuwe. Usenokucwangcisa umsebenzi omnye ofutshane, uzise i-albhamu yeefoto okanye nihlale kunye nje. Ukuba umntu akubonakali azi ukuba ungubani, usenakho ukuphendula kwiimfuno zakhe kweso sihlandlo. Musa ukwenza utyelelo luxhomekeke ekubeni uyayitsho igama lakho.

Kuyaqondakala ukuvakalelwa kukudakumba, ukwaliwa okanye ukuphelelwa ngumonde. Thetha nomntu omthembileyo, umcebisi okanye iqela lenkxaso labanonopheli ukuba le nto ikuxinezele. I-[caregiver self-care guide](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ibandakanya iindlela zokucela inkxaso kwabanye.

## Ndifune nini uncedo lwezonyango?

Ukuba ukungabikho kokwaziwa yipateni entsha, kwenzeka rhoqo okanye kubangela ukukhathazeka, xelela ugqirha womntu. Bhala xa kwenzeka kunye nezinye izinto ezibekhona, ezifana notshintsho lwempilo, ukulala, ukukhanya, ukuva, amayeza okanye indawo ekungqongileyo. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ngenxa yomqulu jikelele.

Ukudideka kwangequbuliso okanye utshintsho olungequbuliso lokuziphatha okanye lwempilo kufuna ukhathalelo lwezonyango olungxamisekileyo. I-[NHS guidance on sudden confusion](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) ithi funa uncedo lwezonyango kwangoko. Sebenzisa inkqubo yongxamiseko okanye yokhathalelo olungxamisekileyo apho umntu ehlala khona.

Ukungaziwa kunokuba nzima kuwo omabini amacala. Gcina amazwi akho elula, nika isikhumbuzo kuphela xa sinceda kwaye vumela intuthuzelo yomntu ikhokele into eza kwenzeka emva koko.
