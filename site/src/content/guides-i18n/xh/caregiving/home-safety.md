Utshintsho lokhuseleko ekhaya kufuneka lusombulule ingxaki ebisenzeka kwenene kumntu, kungekhona ukwenza igumbi ngalinye libe luluhlu lwemiqathango. Qala ngegumbi elinye nenkxalabo enye: indlela emnyama eya endlwini yangasese, intambo ephakathi kwendlela, okanye ukungaqiniseki ngesixhobo sasekhitshini. Injongo kukwenza imikhuba yemihla ngemihla ibe lula ukuyiqhubeka.

[Uluhlu lwe-Alzheimer's Association lokhuseleko ekhaya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/home-safety) luquka iingozi ezikhitshini, kwindlu yangasese, kwiindawo zokulala, kwiindawo zokugcina nakwiindawo zangaphandle. Lukwathi utshintsho lufanele luxhase iimfuno zalo mntu nokuzimela kwakhe. [Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Society sezixhobo zasekhaya](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/what-equipment-improve-adapt-home-person-dementia) sichaza indlela utshintsho oluncinci olunokwenza ngayo imikhuba yemihla ngemihla ibe lula nekhuselekileyo. Akukho luhlu olufanela wonke umntu okanye wonke ikhaya.

## Ndiyiqala njani impendulo yokhuseleko ekhaya?

Khetha igumbi elinye apho into sele ihambe gwenxa okanye ivakala inzima. Ukuba umntu ufuna ukuthatha inxaxheba, hambani kunye nibuze ukuba yintoni ivakala inzima. Umnyamekeli usenokubona intambo ekhululekileyo; umntu usenokukhomba ukuba iswitshi yesibane inzima ukufikelela okanye isihlalo esaziwayo sisuswe. Zozibini iimbono zinceda ekukhetheni utshintsho olunengqiqo.

Bhala phantsi ingxaki ngamagama alula: “Indlela yangasemva imnyama emva kwesidlo sangokuhlwa,” “Ucango lwendlu yangasese lunzima ukulufumana ebusuku,” okanye “Into yokucoca ibekwe kufutshane nesinki.” Emva koko khetha inyathelo elinye elisebenzisekayo uze ubone ukuba liyanceda na. Akufuneki uthenge izixhobo kuyo yonke inkxalabo. Isibane esingcono, indlela ecociweyo okanye ukuhambisa into esetyenziswa rhoqo ukuyibeka ekufutshane kusenokwanela.

[Iingcebiso ze-Alzheimer's Society zokhuseleko ekhaya](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/tips-staying-safe-secure-home-dementia) ziquka imizekelo eyabelwana ngabantu abachatshazelwe yidementia, njengokugcina ulwazi oluluncedo lukwindawo elula ukuyifumana nokusebenzisa izibane zesensa ebusuku. Zithathe njengeengcinga zokuqwalasela, kungekhona imiyalelo ekumele ilandelwe ngumntu wonke.

## Ndimele ndijonge ntoni kwigumbi ngalinye?

### Iindawo zokungena, iikhoridi nezitepsi

- **Yenza indlela ibonakale lula.** Jonga ukuba iindawo zokungena, izitepsi nendlela phakathi kwegumbi lokulala nendlu yangasese zinesibane esizinzileyo. Isibane sasebusuku okanye isibane esinesenzi sentshukumo sisenokunceda ukuba umntu uvuka rhoqo emva kokuba kuqalile ubumnyama.
- **Coca iindlela zokuhamba.** Susa iintambo ezikhululekileyo, ukungacoceki nezinto ezilula ukukhubeka kuzo. Jonga ukuba iikhaphethi zilele ngokulinganayo; susa okanye uqinise nje enye ukuba ibangela ingxaki.
- **Jonga utshintsho lomgangatho.** Ukuba isitepsi okanye umqobo kunzima ukuqaphela, buza ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi okanye yokhuseleko lwasekhaya ukuba loluphi utshintsho olunokwenza ukubonakala kube ngcono ngaphandle kokwenza elinye icala lokukhubeka.
- **Gcina izinto ezaziwayo apho zinengqiqo.** Isihlalo, isibane okanye into esetyenziswa rhoqo isenokunceda umntu alandele umkhuba. Ukuhambisa yonke into ukwenza ibonakale icocekile kunokwenza indlela ingaziwa kangako.

### Ikhitshi

- **Phonononga ukusetyenziswa kwezixhobo.** Ukuba bekukho ingxaki ngesitovu okanye esinye isixhobo, jonga iindlela zokhuseleko ezenzelwe loo sixhobo. I-Alzheimer's Association idwelisa iimpawu zokuvala ngokuzenzekelayo nezixhobo zokhuseleko lwesitovu njengamathuba. Funa ingcebiso efanelekileyo ngaphambi kokutshintsha izixhobo zegesi okanye zombane.
- **Gcina amayeza nezinto ezingezozokutya zahlukile kukutya.** I-Association icebisa ukugcina amayeza ekhuselekile kunokuba abekwe kwikhawuntari. Ukuba umntu uphatha amayeza akhe, buza i-pharmacist okanye ingcali yempilo ngecebo elikhusela loo mkhuba.
- **Gcina izinto zokucoca neemveliso eziyingozi zikhuselekile.** Zigcine kwiingxowa zazo zokuqala, kude nokutya nangaphandle kokufikelelwa xa kuyimfuneko. Musa ukugalela iikhemikhali kwibhotile yesiselo okanye kwisitya sokutya.
- **Gcina izinto eziluncedo zasekhitshini zifikeleleka.** Ucwangciso olukhuselekileyo kufuneka lumvumele umntu aqhubeke nemisebenzi ayonwabelayo xa kunokwenzeka. Ukuba utshintsha ikhabhathi okanye isixhobo, chaza utshintsho uze ujonge ukuba lubangela mpambano na.

Ukuba inkxalabo ivela ngexesha lomsebenzi wokwabelana, [isikhokelo sethu semisebenzi yasekhaya](/activities/household-tasks/) sineengcinga zokulungelelanisa umsebenzi ngelixa umntu ehlala ebandakanyiwe.

### Indlu yangasese

- **Khanyisa indlela negumbi.** Jonga iswitshi, izibane zasebusuku nendlela eya endlwini yangasese. I-Alzheimer's Society icebisa isibane sasebusuku okanye isibane sesenzi xa sinceda umntu afumane igumbi.
- **Jonga iindawo ezityibilizayo nezixhasi.** Ukuba umgangatho okanye ibhafu iyatyibiliza, buza ingcali efanelekileyo ngomgangatho ongatyibilizi ofanelekileyo, ibha yokubamba okanye esinye isixhasi. Indawo echanekileyo ixhomekeke kumntu nakwindlu yangasese.
- **Gcina igumbi lilula ukuliqonda nokulisebenzisa.** Beka izinto zemihla ngemihla apho umntu azilindele khona. Ukuba unengxaki yokufumana indlu yangasese, uphawu olulula okanye ukugcina ucango lukhululekile kusenokunceda, ukuba kuyifanele indlu.
- **Musa ukungenisa isishushu esiphathwayo kwindlu yangasese.** Gcina izixhobo zombane kude neendawo ezimanzi uze ulandele imiyalelo yomenzi.

### Igumbi lokulala nendlela yasebusuku

- **Coca indlela esuka ebhedini ukuya endlwini yangasese.** Susa izinto emgangathweni uze uqiniseke ukuba umntu angafikelela esibaneni ngaphandle kokuwela igumbi elimnyama.
- **Jonga izishushu nezixhobo zokufudumeza.** Ukuba ingubo yombane, ipedi yokufudumeza okanye isishushu esiphathwayo siyasetyenziswa, phonononga indawo yaso nemiyalelo. Cela ingcebiso ukuba kukho ingozi yokuvutha okanye umntu akakwazi ukusisebenzisa ngokukhuselekileyo yedwa.
- **Yenza izinto zemihla ngemihla zifikeleleke.** Gcina iimpahla, iiglasi, ifowuni okanye ezinye izinto ezifunekayo kwiindawo ezaziwayo nasebude umntu anokufikelela kubo ngaphandle kokunyuka.

### Indawo yokuhlamba iimpahla, ukugcina nangaphandle

- **Khusela izinto zokucoca, amayeza nezixhobo.** Jonga iikhabhathi, iindawo zokuhlamba, iigaraji, iindlwana nezindlu ezingaphantsi komhlaba. Gcina iikhemikhali kwiingxowa zazo zokuqala uze ugcine izixhobo ezibukhali okanye oomatshini bekhuselekile xa kukho ingozi ethile.
- **Jonga izixhobo zokulumkisa.** Qinisekisa ukuba izilumkiso zomsi nezixhobo zokufumana irhasi ye-carbon monoxide ziyabakho kwaye ziyasebenza. Landela imiyalelo yazo yokuvavanya nokutshintsha iibhetri.
- **Gcina uluhlu lwabaqhagamshelwana abangxamisekileyo lulula ukufumana.** Quka inombolo yongxamiseko yasekuhlaleni, abaqhagamshelwana abaphambili balo mntu nayo nayiphi na inkcazelo anokuyidinga umncedi othenjiweyo. Musa ukubeka iinkcukacha zabucala apho undwendwe lunokuzibona.
- **Jonga indlela engaphandle.** Jonga ukukhanya okuphantsi, imigangatho engalinganiyo, iimbhobho okanye izixhobo ezisendleleni. Yenza utshintsho olunye ngexesha ukuze iindlela ezaziwayo zihlale zaziwa.

Ngemikhuba yasebusuku, [isikhokelo sokulala](/brain-health/sleep/) sinolwazi olungakumbi ngotshintsho lokulala nemikhuba yangokuhlwa eyaziwayo.

## Ndingayigcina njani inguqu ihlonipha?

Ukhuseleko nokuzimela kufuneka kusebenze kunye. Ukuba utshintsho luthintela umntu ukuba asebenzise igumbi okanye enze umsebenzi owaziwayo, yima uze ufune ukhetho olungathintela kangako. Ezinye iinkqubo zidwelisa iitshixo zeengozi ezithile; oko akuyomiyalelo ebanzi. Ngaphambi kokutshintsha ucango okanye ukuthintela indlela, funa ingcebiso yasekuhlaleni yokhuseleko lwasekhaya nolomlilo uze ucinge ukuba umntu uya kuphuma njani kwimeko yongxamiseko. Gcina indlela yokuphuma ikhuselekile.

Xa kunokwenzeka, chaza utshintsho ngaphambi kokulwenza uze unike umntu ixesha lokuluqhela. I-Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukucinga ukuba isixhobo siza kuba sikhuselekile ukusisebenzisa sodwa, ukuba sifuna ukufakwa, nokuba sisenokwenza omnye umsebenzi wasekhaya ube nzima. Buza umntu ukuba uqaphela ntoni emva kotshintsho. Ukuba ukhuphe igumbi okanye uyeka ukusebenzisa into ayixabisayo, lungisa icebo.

Phinda ujonge ikhaya xa imikhuba, ukuhamba okanye umbono zitshintsha, okanye emva kokusilela okuncinane. Igumbi elalisebenza kakuhle kwinyanga ephelileyo lisenokufuna isibane esahlukileyo okanye indlela ecocekileyo ngoku. Ungagcina uluhlu olufutshane lwento etshintshileyo nento esasebenza, endaweni yokuzama ukuyila kwakhona ikhaya lonke ngaxeshanye.

## Ngubani onokunceda ngokulungelelanisa ikhaya?

Ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi inokuhlola indlela umntu ahamba ngayo ekhaya uze ucebise izixhobo okanye utshintsho lwemisebenzi yemihla ngemihla. Kuxhomekeke kunkxalabo, ingcali yokuhambisa umzimba, umongikazi wesithili, i-pharmacist okanye ingcali yezindlu inokuncedakwa. I-Alzheimer's Society ithi ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi isenokufumaneka ngegqirha lakho, esibhedlele okanye ngenkonzo yentlalo yasekuhlaleni e-UK; iindlela ziyahluka kwezinye iindawo. Ngokufakwa okanye utshintsho lwezixhobo zegesi, zombane, zesakhiwo okanye zokhuseleko lomlilo, sebenzisa ingcali efanelekileyo yasekuhlaleni.

Ukuba uqeshile, buza umnini-ndlu okanye umnikezeli wezindlu ngaphambi kokwenza utshintsho oluhlala ngonaphakade. Inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yabantu abadala okanye yabakhubazekileyo isenokuchaza ukuba yeyiphi uvavanyo okanye inkxaso efumanekayo. Ukuba ingxaki ikhawuleza, qala ngaloo ngozi ithile uze ucele uncedo lwengcali ngayo nayiphi na into ongaqinisekanga ngendlela yokuyitshintsha ngokukhuselekileyo.

## Ndimele ndiphonononge ikhaya rhoqo kangakanani?

Liphonononge xa umsebenzi owaziwayo uba nzima, ukuhamba okanye umbono kutshintsha, okanye kukho ukusilela okuncinane. Qala ngegumbi okanye umkhuba oqhagamshelene noko kutshintsha. Akufuneki uwugqibe wonke umba kuluhlu; gcina oko kusebenzayo uze uphinde ujonge utshintsho olunye olusebenzisekayo ngexesha. Ukuba ukuwa sele kwenzekile, [isikhokelo sethu sokwenza emva kokuwa](/caregiving/responding-after-a-fall-with-dementia/) siquka amanyathelo akhawulezileyo nokulandelela.
