Utshintsho lokhuseleko ekhaya kufuneka lulungise ingxaki umntu aye wajongana nayo ngokwenene, kungabi kukuguqula wonke amagumbi abe luluhlu lwemida. Qala ngegumbi elinye kunye nenkxalabo enye: indlela emnyama eya egumbini lokuhlambela, intambo edlula kwindlela yokuhamba, okanye ukungaqiniseki malunga nesixhobo sasekhitshini. Injongo kukwenza imikhwa yemihla ngemihla ilula ukuba iqhubeke.

[Uluhlu lokhuseleko ekhaya lwe-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/home-safety) lugubungela iingozi kumakhitshi, kwiindawo zokuhlambela, kwiigumbi zokulala, kwiindawo zokugcina nakwiindawo zangaphandle. Likwathi utshintsho kufuneka luxhase iimfuneko zomntu nokuzimela kwakhe. [Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Society sezixhobo zekhaya](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/what-equipment-improve-adapt-home-person-dementia) sichaza indlela utshintsho oluncinci olunokwenza ngayo imikhwa yemihla ngemihla ibe lula nekhuselekile. Akukho luluhlu olufanelana nomntu nekhaya ngalinye.

## Ndiqala njani uphononongo lokhuseleko ekhaya?

Khetha igumbi elinye apho into sele iphazamise khona okanye ibonakala inzima. Ukuba umntu ufuna ukuthatha inxaxheba, hambani kunye nijonge ndawonye kwaye ubuze ukuba yintoni enzima. Umnakekeli unokuqaphela intambo ekhululekileyo; umntu usenokuchaza ukuba iswitshi yelambu inzima ukuyifikelela okanye isihlalo esaziwayo sitshintshile. Zombini iingqiqo ziyakunceda ukhethe utshintsho olunengqiqo.

Bhala phantsi ingxaki ngamagama alula: “Indlela ikhanyiswe kakubi emva kokutya kwasemini,” “Umnyango wasebhafini unzima ukuwufumana ebusuku,” okanye “Ucoceko ugcinwe ecaleni kwesinki.” Emva koko khetha inyathelo elinye elisebenzayo kwaye ubone ukuba linceda na. Akufuneki uthenge izixhobo ngenxa yayo yonke ingxaki. Ukukhanya okungcono, indlela ecocekileyo okanye ukubeka into esetyenziswa rhoqo kufikelela kulula kunokwanela.

[Iingcebiso zokhuseleko ekhaya ze-Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/tips-staying-safe-secure-home-dementia) zibandakanya imizekelo eyabelwanwa ngabantu abachaphazelekayo yisifo sokulibala, njengokugcina ulwazi oluluncedo lufikeleleka lula nokusebenzisa amalambu aziva izinto ebusuku. Zithathe njengeengcinga zokucinga ngazo, hayi njengemiyalelo abonke abantu kufuneka bayilandele.

## Ndifanele ndihlole ntoni kwigumbi ngalinye?

### Iindlela zokungena, iikhoridor nezitebhisi

- **Yenza indlela ibonakale kakuhle.** Hlola ukuba iindlela zokungena, izitebhisi kunye nendlela phakathi kwegumbi lokulala negumbi lokuhlambela zinokukhanya okuzinzileyo. Ilambu lasebusuku okanye elivula xa kukho intshukumo linokunceda ukuba umntu uhlala evuka emnyameni.
- **Cocekisa iindlela zokuhamba.** Susa iintambo ezikhululekileyo, into ezinqumlayo nezinto ezilula ukuwa ngazo. Hlola ukuba iikhaphethi zilele zithe tyaba; zisuse okanye uziqinise kuphela ukuba zibangela ingxaki.
- **Jonga utshintsho lwemilo yomgangatho.** Ukuba inyathelo okanye umqolo unzima ukuwubona, buza ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi okanye ingcali yokhuseleko ekhaya ukuba utshintsho luphi oluya kuphucula ukubonakala ngaphandle kokudala omnye umphetho ongawa kuwo.
- **Gcina izinto ezaziwayo apho zinengqiqo khona.** Isihlalo, ilambu okanye into esetyenziswa rhoqo kunokunceda umntu alandele umkhwa. Ukuhambisa yonke into ukuze igumbi libonakale licocekile kunokwenza indlela ingaziwa kakhulu.

### Ikhitshi

- **Hlola ukusetyenziswa kwezixhobo.** Ukuba kukho ingxaki nesitofu okanye nesinye isixhobo, khangela iindlela zokhuseleko ezenzelwe leso sixhobo kuphela. I-Alzheimer's Association idwelisa izixhobo zokuvala ngokuzenzekelayo kunye nezixhobo zokhuseleko lwesitofu njengezinto ezinokwenzeka. Fumana icebiso lengcali esemthethweni ngaphambi kokutshintsha izixhobo zegesi okanye zombane.
- **Gcina iiyeza nezinto ezingeziyo ukutya zide zibe kude nokutya.** I-Association icebisa ukugcina iiyeza ngokhuseleko endaweni yokuzishiya kwikhawuntali. Ukuba umntu uzilawula iiyeza zakhe, buza umfarmasi okanye ingcali yezempilo ukuba ikwenzele isicwangciso esikhusela loo mkhwa.
- **Gcina izinto zokucoca nezinto eziyingozi ngokhuseleko.** Zigcine kwizikhongozeli zazo zokuqala, kude nokutya kwaye kude nokufikelela xa kufuneka. Musa ukuthululela ikhemikhali kwisikhongozeli sesiselo okanye sokutya.
- **Gcina izixhobo zasekhitshini eziluncedo zifikeleleka.** Ulungelelwaniso oluphepheleyo kufuneka lunike umntu ithuba lokuqhubeka nemisebenzi ayithandayo xa kunokwenzeka. Ukuba utshintsha ikhabhathi okanye isixhobo, chaza utshintsho kwaye ujonge ukuba lidala na ukudideka.

Ukuba le nkxalabo ivela ngexesha lomsebenzi wendlu wabelwane, [isikhokelo semisebenzi yasekhaya](/activities/household-tasks/) sinamacebiso okulungelelanisa lo msebenzi ngelixa umntu ebandakanyekile.

### Igumbi lokuhlambela

- **Khanyisa indlela negumbi.** Hlola iswitshi, ukukhanya kwasebusuku kunye nendlela eya kwindlu yangasese. I-Alzheimer's Society icebisa ilambu lasebusuku okanye elivula ngokuva intshukumo xa linceda umntu afumane igumbi.
- **Hlola iindawo ezinyibilikisayo nezixhasi.** Ukuba umgangatho okanye ibhafu inyibilika, buza ingcali esemthethweni malunga nomphezulu ofanelekileyo ongatyibiliki, uhlaka lokubamba okanye olunye uxhaso. Indawo elungileyo ixhomekeke kumntu nakwigumbi lokuhlambela.
- **Gcina igumbi libonakala lula kwaye lisetyenziseke lula.** Beka izinto zemihla ngemihla apho umntu alindele khona. Ukuba unzima ukufumana indlu yangasese, uphawu olulula okanye ukugcina umnyango uvulekile kunokunceda, ukuba kufanelana nekhaya.
- **Musa ukuzisa isitovu esisongelekayo egumbini lokuhlambela.** Gcina izixhobo zombane kude neendawo ezimanzi kwaye ulandele imiyalelo yomvelisi.

### Igumbi lokulala nendlela yasebusuku

- **Cocekisa indlela ukusuka kwibhedi ukuya egumbini lokuhlambela.** Susa izinto ezisemgangathweni kwaye uqinisekise ukuba umntu unokufikelela kwilambu engadlulanga kwigumbi elimnyama.
- **Hlola izitovu nezixhobo zokufudumeza.** Ukuba kusetyenziswa ingubo yombane, ipadi yokufudumeza okanye isitovu esisongelekayo, hlola indawo yaso nemiyalelo yaso. Cela icebiso ukuba kukho umngcipheko wokutshiswa okanye umntu engakwazi ukusebenzisa loo nto yedwa ngokhuseleko.
- **Yenza izinto zemihla ngemihla zifikeleleke.** Gcina iimpahla, iiglasi, ifowuni okanye ezinye izinto ezifunekayo kwiindawo ezaziwayo nasebude umntu afikelela kuzo ngaphandle kokukhwela.

### Indawo yokuhlamba iimpahla, yokugcina nezangaphandle

- **Khusela izinto zokucoca, iiyeza nezixhobo.** Hlola iikhabhathi, iindawo zokuhlamba iimpahla, iigaraji, izindlu zokugcina nezindlu ezingaphantsi komhlaba. Gcina iikhemikhali kwizikhongozeli zazo zokuqala kwaye ugcine izixhobo ezibukhali okanye imishini iqinisekisiwe xa kukho ingozi ethile.
- **Hlola izixhobo nezikhalazi.** Qinisekisa ukuba izikhalazi zomsi kunye nezixhobo zokufumanisa i-carbon monoxide zikhona kwaye zisebenza. Landela imiyalelo yazo yokuvavanya nokutshintsha ibhetri.
- **Gcina uluhlu lwabantu oluqhagamshelana nabo ngexesha lengxaki lulula ukulufumana.** Quka inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo yasekuhlaleni, abantu abaphambili kumntu kunye nalo naluphi na ulwazi umncedi othembekileyo anokulufuna. Musa ukubeka iinkcukacha zabucala apho umtyeleli anokuzibona khona.
- **Hlola indlela ngaphandle.** Jonga ukukhanya okungalunganga, iindawo ezingalingananga, ipayipi okanye izixhobo ezisendleleni. Yenza utshintsho ngalinye ngexesha ukuze iindlela eziqhelekileyo zisaqondakala.

Ngemikhwa yasebusuku, [isikhokelo sokulala](/brain-health/sleep/) sinezinye iinkcukacha malunga notshintsho lokulala kunye neenkqubo zokuhlwa eziqhelekileyo.

## Ndigcina njani utshintsho lunembeko?

Ukhuseleko nokuzimela kufuneka kusebenze kunye. Ukuba utshintsho lumnqanda umntu ekusebenziseni igumbi okanye ekwenzeni umsebenzi wakhe ozaziwayo, yima ukhangele ukhetho olungathintiyo kakhulu. Ezinye iimbumbano zidwelisa izikhiye zoxanduva oluthile; oko akuyo imiyalelo ebanzi. Ngaphambi kokutshintsha umnyango okanye ukunciphisa indlela, fumana icebiso lokhuseleko lwekhaya nomlilo kwindawo oyihlala kuyo kwaye ucinge ukuba umntu uza kuphuma njani kwimeko yengxaki. Gcina indlela yokuphuma ekhuselekileyo ifikeleleka.

Xa kunokwenzeka, chaza utshintsho ngaphambi kokulwenza kwaye umnike umntu ixesha lokuzolelana nalo. I-Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukucinga ukuba izixhobo ziya kukhuselekela na ukusetyenziswa yedwa, ukuba zifuna ukufakwa na, kunye nokuba zinokwenza omnye umsebenzi wekhaya ube nzima na. Buza umntu ukuba ubona ntoni emva kotshintsho. Ukuba bayayiphepha igumbi okanye bayeka ukusebenzisa into abayixabisayo, lungisa isicwangciso.

Phinda uhlole ikhaya xa imikhwa, ukuhamba okanye ukubona kutshintsha, okanye emva kokuphepha ingozi encinci. Igumbi elisebenze kakuhle inyanga ephelileyo lisenokufuna ukukhanya okwahlukileyo okanye indlela ecacileyo ngoku. Ungagcina uluhlu olufutshane lwento etshintshileyo nento esasebenza, endaweni yokuyila ikhaya lonke ngexesha linye.

## Ngubani onokunceda ngolungelelwaniso lwekhaya?

Ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi inokuvavanya indlela umntu ahamba ngayo ekhaya kwaye icebise izixhobo okanye utshintsho lwemisebenzi yemihla ngemihla. Kuxhomekeke kwingxaki, ingcali yonyango lomzimba, umongikazi wesithili, ifarmasi okanye ingcali yezindlu nayo inokunceda. I-Alzheimer's Society iphawula ukuba ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi inokufumaneka ngokudlula kugqirha wasekuhlaleni, esibhedlele okanye kwinkonzo yonakekelo lwentlalo yasekuhlaleni e-UK; iindlela ziyahluka kwezinye iindawo. Ukuze ufakelwe okanye utshintsho kwezixhobo zegesi, zombane, zesakhiwo okanye zokhuseleko lomlilo, sebenzisa ingcali esemthethweni yasekuhlaleni.

Ukuba uqeshile indlu, buza umnikazi okanye umboneleli wendlu ngaphambi kokwenza utshintsho oluhlala ixesha elide. Inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yokuguga okanye yokukhubazeka inokuchaza ukuba luphi uvavanyo okanye uncedo olukhoyo. Ukuba ingxaki ingxamisekile, sombulula loo ngozi ithile kuqala kwaye ucele uncedo lwengcali kuyo nayiphi na into ongaqinisekanga ukuba ungayitshintsha njani ngokhuseleko.

## Ndifanele ndiphononongele ikhaya rhoqo kangakanani?

Phonononga xa umsebenzi oqhelekileyo usiba nzima, xa ukuhamba okanye ukubona kutshintsha, okanye xa kukho ingozi encinci eyaphela. Qala ngegumbi okanye ngomkhwa odibene notshintsho lolo. Akufuneki ugqibezele yonke into kuluhlu lokuhlola; gcina into esebenzayo kwaye uphinde uqwalasele utshintsho olunye oluzisebenzayo ngexesha. Ukuba sele kwenzeke ukuwa, [isikhokelo sokwenza emva kokuwa](/caregiving/responding-after-a-fall-with-dementia/) sigubungela amanyathelo akhawulezayo nokulandelela.
