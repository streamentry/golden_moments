Ukuxilongwa kwesifo sokulibala ngexesha lobomi bokusebenza kunokuvusa imibuzo malunga nengeniso, ubumfihlo, ubuntu bomntu kunye nokhuseleko. Abanye abantu bafuna ukugcina umsebenzi wabo wangoku, abanye bacinga ngotshintsho, kwaye abanye bakhetha ukushiya. Akukho ndlela enye efanelekileyo kubo bonke. Izifiso zakho, imisebenzi yakho, impilo yakho kunye nenkxaso zonke zibalulekile.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association malunga ne-Alzheimer's esiqalise kwangethuba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset) sixoxa malunga nomsebenzi, imali yentsapho kunye nezibonelelo zomqeshi. I-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead/work-and-dementia) ne-[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) zichaza iindlela zokuhlala emsebenzini, zokutshintsha indima okanye zokushiya. Imithetho yomsebenzi ihluka ngokwendawo, ngoko eli phepha linikezela ngemibuzo yokucinga ngayo, kungekhona icebiso lomthetho okanye lezemali.

## Ingaba uxilongo luthetha ukuba kufuneka ndiyeke umsebenzi?

Hayi. Uxilongo ngokwalo aluzigqibi ukuba ungakwazi na ukuqhubeka usebenza. I-[Alzheimer's Society ithi](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) isigqibo sixhomekeke kwimvakalelo yakho, kumsebenzi wakho kunye nemeko yakho yobuqu. Abanye abantu baqhubeka kwindima efanayo, abanye batshintsha imisebenzi okanye iiyure, kwaye abanye bayeka umsebenzi.

Cinga ngento esebenza kakuhle kunye nento eye yanzima. Ungaphawula imisebenzi ekuthi kulula ukuyenza, ukuba ukhathala okanye uphazanyiswa nini, nokuba kukho iimpazamo okanye iingxaki zokhuseleko ezithile. Imizekelo ethile inokunceda wena nengcali yakho ukuxoxa ngomsebenzi ngaphandle kokuguqula usuku olunye olunzima libe sisigwebo ngobuthathaka bakho.

Ukhuseleko nalo lubalulekile. Ukuba umsebenzi wakho ubandakanya ukuqhuba, ukusebenza ngemishini, ukunakekela abanye abantu okanye omnye umsebenzi onobungozi, buza ingcali yakho ukuba ingacinga njani ngobungozi. Imithetho yasekuhlaleni, imigaqo yobuchwephesha kunye nekhontrakthi yomsebenzi zingasebenza. Eli phepha alinakuchaza ukuba indima ethile ikhuselekile na okanye ukuba umqeshi wakho kufuneka enze ntoni.

## Ndingaxoxa njani nengcali yam ngomsebenzi?

Chaza imisebenzi ebandakanyeka emsebenzini wakho kunye neenguqu owazibonayo. Buza ukuba ingxaki yezempilo inokuchaphazela umsebenzi othile na, zeziphi iimpawu ekufuneka zikukhuthaze ukufuna uncedo, kunye nokuba omnye ungcali unokuqwalasela umsebenzi othile na. Ungathanda ukuzisa umntu omthembileyo, kodwa vumelanani ngendlela anokukuxhasa ngayo ngaphandle kokuthetha phezu kwakho.

[Isikhokelo sohambo lokuya kugqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinemibono yokulungiselela imibuzo kunye neenkcukacha. Ungacela ingcali yakho ukuba icacise into anokuyiqwalasela kunye neyo kufuneka iqwalaselwe ngokwahlukileyo kwindawo yomsebenzi okanye kuvavanyo lomsebenzi. Isikhokelo esibanzi malunga nesifo sokulibala asinakuthatha indawo yohlolo lwempilo kunye nokhuseleko lomntu ngamnye.

## Yeziphi iinguqu emsebenzini ezinokunceda?

Ukuba ufuna ukuqhubeka usebenza, buza ukuba zeziphi iinguqu ezinokwenzeka kwindima yakho. I-Dementia Australia icebisa ukusebenzisa umjikelo, ukugxila komsebenzi omnye ngexesha, ukubhala iinkcukacha kunye nokwenza isikhokelo esinenyathelo ngenyathelo semisebenzi eqhelekileyo. Ngokuxhomekeke kumsebenzi wakho, unokubuza ukuba kunokubakho imiyalelo ebhaliweyo, ikhalenda, ukunciphisa ukuphazanyiswa okanye ukuphumla okucwangciswe.

Thatha ezi zinto njengemibono yokuxoxa, hayi uluhlu oluqinisekileyo olunokusetyenziswa ngumqeshi wonke. Khetha utshintsho olunye olujongene nomsebenzi onzima. Ukuba wena nomqeshi wakho nizama, vumelanani ukuba niya kuluqwalasela njani kwaye ukuba luyancedisa na. Utshintsho olusebenza kwindima enye lunokungafaneleki kwenye.

## Ndifanele ndixelele umqeshi wam na?

Wena unelungelo lokukhetha ukuba ufuna ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha zakho zobuqu, ngaphandle kokuba umthetho wasekuhlaleni, ikhontrakthi okanye imfuneko yokhuseleko isebenza kwindima yakho. Imfuneko yahluka ngokwelizwe, ngorhulumente wesifundazo nangomsebenzi. Ngaphambi kokugqiba, jonga ikhontrakthi yakho, umgaqo womsebenzi kunye nesikhokelo sasekuhlaleni. Ukuba awuqinisekanga, thetha necebiso lengcali yezemisebenzi efanelekileyo, umeli wenyunyuzo okanye igqwetha elaziyo imithetho yakwindawo yakho.

Ukuba ugqibe ukuxelela umntu, qwalasela into ofuna ukuba ayazi kunye noncedo olufunayo. Unokuthanda ukuthetha nomphathi okanye nesebe labasebenzi kuqala. Buza ukuba iinkcukacha zakho ziyakuphathwa njani kunye nobani oza kuzifumana; musa ukucinga ukuba zonke iindawo zomsebenzi zinenkqubo efanayo yobumfihlo.

Ezinye iimisebenzi zibandakanya imisebenzi enobungozi. Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba ukhe wanqamla okanye watshintsha omnye wale misebenzi, qhagamshelana ngoko nangoko necebiso lengcali yakho yezonyango kunye nengcali yezemisebenzi efanelekileyo. Musa ukuthembela kwisikhokelo esiqhelekileyo ukugqiba ukuba umsebenzi othile ukhuselekile na.

## Yeziphi izibonelelo neemali ekufuneka ndizihlolisise?

Ngaphambi kokutshintsha iiyure zakho okanye ukushiya, hlola izibonelelo ozifumanayo kumqeshi wakho. Buza isebe labasebenzi okanye umphathi wezibonelelo malunga nemvume yokuphumla, izibonelelo zokukhubazeka okanye zengeniso, uncedo lwezempilo kunye neemhla zokugqiba. Cela imigaqo yangoku ngokubhaliweyo kwaye ugcine ikopi yolwazi olufumanayo.

Ukufaneleka, irhafu kunye nexesha kuxhomekeke kumqeshi wakho, kwipolisi yakho yesiqinisekiso okanye kwindawo. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuba uhlolisise izibonelelo zomqeshi kwaye ugcine isiqinisekiso esibhaliweyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset). Kwisigqibo sakho, thetha nengcali yezibonelelo okanye yezemali efanelekileyo enokuhlola amaxwebhu afanelekileyo kuwe. Musa ukutyikitya okanye ukuphelisa ukhuseleko ngokusekelwe kwinqaku eliqhelekileyo kuphela.

Usapho lwakho lunokuchaphazeka ngotshintsho lwengeniso okanye lweshedyuli. Ukuba ufuna, thetha nabantu obathembayo malunga nento ebaluleke ngakumbi kuwe kunye neenketho ofuna ukuziphanda. [Isikhokelo sokuqala emva kokuxilongwa](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) sigubungela inkxaso ebanzi kunye nemibuzo yokucwangcisa ikamva.

## Ndigqibe ukunciphisa iiyure okanye ukushiya?

Ukushiya umsebenzi kunokuba sisigqibo esilungileyo kwabanye abantu. Akuthethi ukuba udanile. Unokucinga ngengeniso, ukhathalelo lwempilo, usetyhini lwemihla ngemihla kunye nabantu oza kubabulela ukuba ubona. Buza ngenkxaso yasekuhlaleni ngaphambi kokwenza utshintsho, kwaye uzinike ixesha lokuthelekisa ezo nketho zibalulekileyo kuwe.

Umsebenzi yinxalenye nje yobomi. Ukuba uyayeka okanye unciphisa iiyure, khetha ezinye iimisebenzi okanye ubudlelwane obunokonwabisa xa ulungele. Amalungu entsapho anokunceda, kodwa isigqibo kufuneka sibandakanye nawe. [Isikhokelo sokuzinakekela](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sinemibono yokucela abanye bakwabelane ngenkxaso ngaphandle kokubeka yonke imithwalo kumntu omnye.
