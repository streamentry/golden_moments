Ufunyaniso lwe-dementia ngexesha lobomi bomsebenzi lunokuvusa imibuzo ngengeniso, ubumfihlo, ubuqu nokhuseleko. Abanye abantu bafuna ukugcina umsebenzi wabo wangoku, abanye bacinga ngotshintsho, kwaye abanye bakhetha ukuyeka. Akukho ndlela inye ifanele wonke umntu. Oko ukukhethayo, imisebenzi yakho, impilo yakho nenkxaso yakho zonke zibalulekile.

I-[Alzheimer's Association guide to younger-onset Alzheimer's](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset) ithetha ngomsebenzi, ngeemali zosapho nangezibonelelo zomqeshi. I-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead/work-and-dementia) kunye ne-[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) zichaza ukhetho lokuhlala emsebenzini, ukutshintsha indima okanye ukuyeka. Imithetho yomsebenzi iyahluka ngokwendawo, ngoko esi sikhokelo sinika imibuzo onokuyicinga, asisosicebiso sasemthethweni okanye semali.

## Ngaba ufunyaniso lithetha ukuba kufuneka ndiyeke ukusebenza?

Hayi. Ufunyaniso lodwa aluqinisekisi ukuba ungaqhubeka usebenza na. I-[Alzheimer's Society ithi](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) isigqibo sixhomekeke kwiimvakalelo zakho, kumsebenzi wakho nakwimeko yakho yobuqu. Abanye abantu baqhubeka nendima enye, abanye batshintsha imisebenzi okanye iiyure, kwaye abanye bayayeka umsebenzi.

Cinga ngoko kusebenzayo noko kuye kwaba nzima ngakumbi. Unokubhala ukuba yeyiphi imisebenzi evakala iqhelekile, xa udinwa okanye uphazamiseka, nokuba kuye kwabakho iimpazamo ezithile okanye iinkxalabo zokhuseleko. Imizekelo ethe ngqo inokukunceda wena nogqirha wakho nithethe ngomsebenzi ngaphandle kokuguqula usuku olunye olunzima lube sisigqibo ngezakhono zakho.

Ukhuseleko lubalulekile nalo. Ukuba umsebenzi wakho ubandakanya ukuqhuba, ukusebenzisa oomatshini, ukunakekela abanye abantu okanye omnye umsebenzi onokubeka ukhuseleko engozini, buza ugqirha wakho ukuba ungawuvavanya njani umngcipheko. Imithetho yasekuhlaleni, imigaqo yengcali neekhontrakthi zomsebenzi zisenokusebenza nazo. Eli phepha alinakukuxelela ukuba indima ethile ikhuselekile na okanye yintoni umqeshi wakho ekufuneka ayenze.

## Ndingathetha njani nogqirha wam ngomsebenzi?

Chaza imisebenzi ebandakanywa ngumsebenzi wakho notshintsho oluqaphelileyo. Buza ukuba inkxalabo yempilo inokuchaphazela umsebenzi othile, zeziphi iimpawu ezithetha ukuba kufuneka ufune uncedo, nokuba enye ingcali inokuvavanya umsebenzi womsebenzi. Usenokufuna ukuzisa umntu omthembileyo, kodwa nigqibe kunye ukuba angakuxhasa njani ngaphandle kokuthetha endaweni yakho.

I-[doctor-visit guide](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) inezindlela zokulungiselela imibuzo namanqaku. Unokucela ugqirha wakho achaze ukuba yintoni anokuyivavanya nokuba yintoni efuna uvavanyo oluhlukeneyo lwendawo yomsebenzi okanye lwezempilo yomsebenzi. Isikhokelo esijikelele se-dementia asinakuthatha indawo yovavanyo lomntu ngamnye lwempilo nokhuseleko.

## Ngawaphi utshintsho emsebenzini anokunceda?

Ukuba ufuna ukuqhubeka usebenza, buza ukuba ngawaphi utshintsho anokwenzeka kwindima yakho. I-Dementia Australia icebisa ukhetho olufana nokusebenzisa umkhwa, ukugxila kumsebenzi omnye ngexesha, ukubhala amanqaku nokwenza isikhokelo senyathelo ngenyathelo semisebenzi eqhelekileyo. Kuxhomekeke kumsebenzi wakho, usenokubuza nangemiyalelo ebhaliweyo, ikhalenda, ukuphazanyiswa okuncinane okanye ezokuphumla ezicwangcisiweyo.

Zithathe njengezimvo zokuthetha ngazo, hayi uludwe lokukhangela olunokusetyenziswa ngumqeshi wonke okanye olufanele ukusetyenziswa. Khetha utshintsho olunye olujongana nomsebenzi okubona unzima. Ukuba wena nomqeshi wakho niyalizama, vumelanani ukuba niya kulijonga njani nokuba liyanceda na. Utshintsho olusebenzela indima enye lusenokungafaneli enye.

## Ndimxelele na umqeshi wam?

Wena ugqiba ukuba yeyiphi ingcaciso yobuqu oza kwabelana ngayo, ngaphandle kokuba umthetho wasekuhlaleni, ikhontrakthi okanye imfuneko yokhuseleko isebenza kwindima yakho. Imfuneko ziyahluka ngelizwe, ngephondo nangomsebenzi. Phambi kokuthatha isigqibo, jonga ikhontrakthi yakho, umgaqo wendawo yomsebenzi nesikhokelo sasekuhlaleni. Ukuba awuqinisekanga, thetha nomcebisi wezemisebenzi onezakhono, ummeli wemanyano okanye igqwetha elibaziyo imithetho apho uhlala khona.

Ukuba ukhetha ukuxelela umntu, cinga ukuba ufuna aqonde ntoni nokuba yeyiphi inkxaso oyicelayo. Usenokukhetha ukuqala ngokuthetha nomphathi omnye omthembileyo okanye umntu wesebe lezabasebenzi. Buza ukuba ulwazi lwakho luza kuphathwa njani nokuba ngubani oza kulufumana; musa ukucinga ukuba zonke iindawo zomsebenzi zinenkqubo efanayo yobumfihlo.

Eminye imisebenzi ibandakanya imisebenzi enokuchaphazela ukhuseleko. Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba kufuneka unqumamise okanye utshintshe omnye wemisebenzi enjalo, nxibelelana nogqirha wakho nomcebisi wezemisebenzi wasekuhlaleni onezakhono ngokukhawuleza. Musa ukuthembela kwisikhokelo esijikelele ukugqiba ukuba umsebenzi othile ukhuselekile na.

## Zeziphi izibonelelo neemali ekufuneka ndizijonge?

Phambi kokutshintsha iiyure zakho okanye ukuyeka, phonononga izibonelelo ezikhoyo ngomqeshi wakho. Buza kwisebe lezabasebenzi okanye kumphathi wezibonelelo ngemvume, izibonelelo zokukhubazeka okanye zengeniso, ukhuseleko lwempilo nayo yonke imihla yokugqibela. Cela imithetho yangoku ibhaliwe kwaye ugcine ikopi yolwazi olufumanayo.

Ukufaneleka, irhafu nexesha kuxhomekeke kumqeshi wakho, kwi-inshorensi yakho nakwindawo. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuphonononga izibonelelo zomqeshi nokugcina isiqinisekiso esibhaliweyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset). Kwisigqibo sakho, thetha nomcebisi wezibonelelo okanye wezemali onezakhono onokuphonononga amaxwebhu asebenza kuwe. Phepha ukutyikitya okanye ukuphelisa ukhuseleko ngokusekwe kumqulu ophangaleleyo kuphela.

Usapho lwakho lusenokuchaphazeleka nalo kutshintsho lwengeniso okanye ishedyuli. Ukuba ufuna, thetha nabantu obathembileyo ngento ebaluleke kakhulu nokuba ngowuphi ukhetho ofuna ukulukhangela. I-[guide to first steps after a diagnosis](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) ithetha ngenkxaso ebanzi nemibuzo yokucwangcisela ikamva.

## Kuthekani ukuba ndigqiba ekubeni ndinciphise iiyure okanye ndiyeke?

Ukuyeka umsebenzi kunokuba sisigqibo esifanelekileyo kwabanye abantu. Akuthethi ukuba uhlulekile. Usenokufuna ukucinga ngengeniso, ukhuseleko lwempilo, umkhwa wemihla ngemihla nabantu oya kuphosa ukubabona. Buza ngenkxaso yasekuhlaleni phambi kokutshintsha, kwaye uzinike ixesha lokuthelekisa ukhetho olubalulekileyo kuwe.

Umsebenzi yinxalenye enye yobomi. Ukuba uyeka okanye unciphisa iiyure, khetha eminye imisebenzi okanye ubudlelwane obuvakala bunexabiso xa ukulungele. Amalungu osapho anokunikela uncedo, kodwa isigqibo kufuneka sibandakanye nawe. I-[caregiver self-care guide](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) inezimvo zokucela abanye babelane ngenkxaso ngaphandle kokubeka yonke imithwalo emntwini omnye.
