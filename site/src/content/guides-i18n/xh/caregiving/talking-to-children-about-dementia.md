Xa i-dementia itshintsha umntu othandwa ngumntwana, angaqaphela phambi kokuba umntu omdala aqale incoko. Umntwana angakhathazeka, axhome umsindo, ahlazeke, azive enetyala okanye angaqinisekanga ukuba aziphathe njani. Umntwana ngamnye nosapho ngalunye luyahluka. Ulwazi oluqondakalayo nenkxaso ezinzileyo zinokunceda umntwana aqonde oko kutshintshayo ngaphandle kokumcela ukuba athwale uxanduva lwabantu abadala.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokuthetha nabantwana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) sicebisa ukuphendula imibuzo ngenyaniso ngamagama abantwana abawaqondayo. [Isikhokelo sosapho se-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) sigxininisa kwanencoko ezithembekileyo, ukuthuthuzela nokukhusela indawo yomntu oselula yokuba ngumntwana. Sebenzisa ezi zimvo njengendawo yokuqala, ingeyonto ekufuneka ilandelwe ngumzi wonke.

## Ndiyiqala njani incoko ngedementia?

Qala ngotshintsho umntwana alubonileyo okanye umbuzo abewubuzile. Ungathi, "Uqaphele ukuba umakhulu uphinda imibuzo. Une-ngxaki ebizwa ngokuba yi-dementia echaphazela inkumbulo nokucinga kwakhe." Sebenzisa uxilongo lomntu kuphela ukuba luyaziwa. Ukuba usapho lusalinde uvavanyo, chaza utshintsho ngaphandle kokulubiza igama.

Khetha ixesha elithe cwaka apho unokuhlala encokweni. Sebenzisa izivakalisi ezimfutshane uze uyeke umntwana aphendule. Akufuneki uchaze yonke into ngaxeshanye. Buza oko seleekuqondayo noko afuna ukukwazi ngokulandelayo. Umntwana omncinci angaxhamla encwadi enemifanekiso okanye umzekelo olula; omdala angafuna iinkcukacha ezingakumbi okanye ixesha lokucinga yedwa.

Gcina umntu one-dementia ebandakanyekile xa efuna kwaye enokuthatha inxaxheba. Thetha nomntwana ngezinto azithandayo umntu phambi kokwabelana ngeenkcukacha zezonyango eziyimfihlo nabanye abantu. Umntwana angafuna umzali okanye umntu omdala othembekileyo asondele xa etyelela, okanye angakhetha ukungazixoxi ngoxilongo nabahlobo okanye esikolweni okwangoku.

## Ndimxelele ntoni umntwana ngedementia?

Chaza iinxalenye ezibalulekileyo ebomini bemihla ngemihla bomntwana. I-dementia sisifo esinokutshintsha indlela umntu akhumbula ngayo, acinga okanye aqhagamshelana ngayo. Ayosulelisi, kwaye umntwana akazange ayibangele. [I-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) iphawula ukuba abantwana banokuxhalela ukosulelwa yi-dementia okanye bazigxeke xa abantu abadala bexhalabile. Phendula loo mingcipheko ngokuthe ngqo.

Yiba ngenyaniso ngoko ukwaziyo nokungakwaziyo. Ungachaza oko usapho lukuqapheleyo, uncedo umntu alufumanayo namacebo anokuchaphazela inkqubo yomntwana. Phepha ukuthembisa ukuba umntu uya kuphila, ukuba akukho nto iya kutshintsha okanye ukuba uyazi ukuba utshintsho luya kwenzeka nini. Ukuba awuyazi impendulo, yitsho uze unikele ngokufumana ulwazi oluthembekileyo kunye.

Abantwana banokufuna ukuva inkcazo enye ngaphezu kwexesha elinye. Oku kuqhelekile. Shiya indawo yokuba umntwana abuze kwakhona kamva, uze ujonge oko akuqondileyo ngaphandle kokuguqula incoko ibe luvavanyo. Ukuba umntwana usebenzisa amanye amagama esifo, mamela kuqala uze unike ulwimi olucacileyo anokulusebenzisa ngobubele.

## Ndingazibuyisela njani imibuzo enzima?

Phendula inkxalabo engaphantsi kombuzo, uze unike impendulo eyinyaniso ecacileyo onokuyinika. Yigcine ifutshane kwaye ungongezi iinkcukacha ongazaziyo.

- **"Ndingasuleleka?"** Yitsho hayi: i-dementia ayosulelisi. Umntwana angaqhubeka echitha ixesha nomntu.
- **"Ingaba ndiyenzile le nto?"** Mthuthuzele ngokuba isifo asibangelwanga yinto ayithethileyo okanye ayenzileyo.
- **"Kutheni utatomkhulu endibiza ngelinye igama?"** Chaza ukuba i-dementia inokutshintsha indlela akhumbula ngayo amagama. Akusosiphoso somntwana, kwaye angabi akwenzi oko ukumkhathaza.
- **"Ndiya kuba ne-dementia nam?"** Musa ukuqikelela ikamva lomntu omnye. Umngcipheko wosapho uxhomekeke kuhlobo nembali. Ukuba kukho inkxalabo ethile ngohlobo olufunyenwe lufudula, buza ugqirha womntu ukuba ingcali yemfuza inganceda na.
- **"Uya kuphila?" okanye "Uya kufa?"** Musa ukuthembisa iziphumo okanye ixesha. Xelela umntwana oko iqela lokhathalelo likuthethileyo, ukuba usapho lufuna ukwabelana ngako, uze ucacise nini oya kuthetha kwakhona.
- **"Usandithanda?"** Musa ukumenza umntwana abe noxanduva lokungqina impendulo. Ungathi ubudlelwane buyabalulekileyo uze ufune indlela ekhululekileyo yokuchitha ixesha kunye ngoku.

Kulungile ukuyeka ukuba umbuzo uvakala mkhulu kakhulu. Xelela umntwana ukuba uya kubuyela nini kuwo, uze ukwenze oko. "Andikazi okwangoku" ethe cwaka, eyinyaniso inengenelo ngakumbi kunokuqikelela okuqinisekileyo.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngaphandle kokwenza umntwana abe ngumnyamekeli?

Nika unxibelelwano ngokukhetha. Ukuba umntwana nomntu bobabini bayafuna, banokuphulaphula umculo oqhelekileyo, bajonge iifoto, bakrole, bafunde okanye babelane ngomsebenzi olula wasekhaya. [Isikhokelo sethu sokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) kunye [nesikhokelo sethu somculo](/activities/music/) sinezimvo. Landela iimpendulo zomntu nezomntwana; kulungile ukuyeka, ukutshintsha umsebenzi okanye ukutyelela ngelinye ixesha.

Gcina uxanduva lokhathalelo nokhuseleko kubantu abadala. Umntwana angakhetha ukubeka isidlo esincinci okanye ukuzisa incwadi, kodwa akufanele abe noxanduva lokubeka esweni, amayeza, ukhathalelo lomntu, iintlanganiso okanye iingxaki. Musa ukuthi ukutyelela, ukunceda okanye ukuhlala usondele ngumsebenzi womntwana. [Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) silumkisa imizi ukuba ihloniphe imida yabantu abaselula nesidingo sabo sokuhlala bengabantwana.

Khusela ixesha eliyelomntwana yedwa. Gcina isikolo, ubuhlobo, izinto aziyonwabelayo neenkqubo zosapho zizinzile kangangoko. Ukuba ukhathalelo lutshintsha loo macebo, chaza isizathu uze ufune omnye umntu omdala onokunceda ukugcina umsebenzi okanye uhambo lomntwana. [Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela komnyamekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sigubungela iindlela zokucela abanye abantu abadala ukuba babelane ngenkxaso.

Phambi kokuxelela utitshala, umqeqeshi okanye omnye umntu omdala, bandakanya umntwana kwisigqibo ngokufanelekileyo. Angafuna umntu omnye othembekileyo azi, okanye angakhetha ukulinda. Ukuba wabelana ngolwazi, vumelanani ukuba nithini nokuba yintoni eyimfihlo.

## Ndifanele ndifune inkxaso engakumbi nini?

Buza ukuba umntwana uphila njani ngokuhamba kwexesha, hayi kuphela emva koxilongo. Abanye abantwana bathetha ngokukhululekileyo; abanye babonisa ukuxhalaba ngotshintsho ekulaleni, esikolweni, kubuhlobo okanye ekuziphatheni. Ezi nguqu zinokuba nezizathu ezininzi kwaye aziqinisekisi ukuba umntwana ufuna uxilongo. Mamela uze ubuze ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso enokunceda.

Ukuba umntwana ufuna umntu ongaphandle kosapho ukuba athethe naye, cinga ngotitshala othembekileyo, umcebisi wesikolo, ugqirha wabantwana okanye ingcali yempilo yengqondo yabantwana. Buza phambi kokuqhagamshelana nabasebenzi besikolo ngoxilongo xa unako; umntwana angafuna ukuba yinxalenye yaloo ncoko. Imizi echatshazelwe yi-dementia eqala kwangoko ingafuna nenkxaso ngomsebenzi, isikolo neendima zosapho ezitshintshayo.

Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba ungachaza njani utshintsho oluthile, buza iqela lokhathalelo lomntu okanye umbutho we-dementia ukufumana isikhokelo. Umntwana akafuni iimpendulo ezigqibeleleyo kuwe. Ufuna ulwazi oluthembekileyo, ithuba lokubuza kwakhona nabantu abadala abaqhubeka bebakho kuye.
