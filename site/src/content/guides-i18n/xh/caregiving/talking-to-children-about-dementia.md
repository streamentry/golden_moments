Xa i-dementia iguqula umntu umntwana amthandayo, umntwana usenokuyiqaphela ngaphambi kokuba umntu omdala aqale incoko. Umntwana usenokuba nexhala, umsindo, ihlazo, ukuziva enetyala okanye engazi ukuba kufuneka enze njani. Yonke intsapho nomntwana bayahluka. Ulwazi olucacileyo kunye noxhaso oluzinzileyo lunokunceda umntwana aqonde into eyenzekayo ngaphandle kokumbuza ukuba aphathe uxanduva lwabantu abadala.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association ngokuthetha nabantwana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) sicebisa ukuphendula imibuzo ngokuthembeka ngamagama abantwana abawaqondayo. [Isikhokelo sosapho se-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) sigxininisa nakwincoko ethembekileyo, ukuthuthuzela nokukhusela indawo yomntu oselula yokuba ngumntwana. Sebenzisa ezi ngcamango njengendawo yokuqala, hayi umbhalo oqinisekileyo wayo yonke intsapho.

## Ndiqala njani incoko malunga ne-dementia?

Qala ngotshintsho umntwana alubonileyo okanye umbuzo abuze wona. Ungathi: “Uqaphele ukuba uGogo uphinda imibuzo. Unesifo esibizwa ngokuba yi-dementia esichaphazela inkumbulo nengqiqo yakhe.” Sebenzisa igama elichanileyo lale mntu kuphela ukuba liyaziwa. Ukuba intsapho isalinde uvavanyo, chaza utshintsho ngaphandle kokulubiza ngegama.

Khetha ixesha elithuleyo xa unokuhlala nomntu kwincoko. Sebenzisa izivakalisi ezimfutshane kwaye uyeke ukuze umntwana aphendule. Akufuneki uchaze yonke inkcukacha ngaxeshanye. Buza ukuba uyaqonda ntoni kwaye ufuna ukwazi ntoni okulandelayo. Umntwana omncinci usenokuxhamla ngencwadi yemifanekiso okanye umzekelo olula; umntwana omdala usenokufuna inkcazelo engakumbi okanye ixesha lokuzicingela yedwa.

Gcina umntu one-dementia ebandakanyekile xa efuna ukuba njalo kwaye xa enokuthatha inxaxheba. Thetha nomntwana malunga nezifiso zomntu ngaphambi kokwabelana neenkcukacha zonyango zangasese nabanye abantu. Umntwana usenokufuna umzali okanye omdala owathembayo ngaphambili xa etyelela, okanye usenokuthanda ukungathethi ngoxilongo nabahlobo okanye esikolweni okwangoku.

## Yintoni endiza kuxelela umntwana malunga ne-dementia?

Chaza iinxalenye ezibalulekileyo kubomi bomntwana bemihla ngemihla. I-dementia sisifo esitshintsha indlela umntu akhumbula ngayo, acinga ngayo nokuthetha ngayo. Ayisiyo into esasazekayo, kwaye umntwana akayibangelanga. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) iphawula ukuba abantwana basenokoyika ukuba bayayifumana okanye bazibeke ityala xa abantu abadala bexinezelekile. Phendula ezo ndlela zokoyika ngokuthe ngqo.

Yiba nyanisekileyo malunga nento oyaziyo nento ongayaziyo. Unokuchaza into intsapho eyiqaphelileyo, uncedo umntu alufumanayo kunye nezicwangciso ezinokuchaphazela imikhwa yomntwana. Qwalasela ukungathembisi ukuba umntu uya kuphila kakuhle, ukuba akukho nto iya kutshintsha okanye ukuba uyazi ixesha utshintsho oluza kwenzeka ngalo. Ukuba awuyazi impendulo, yithi oko kwaye ucebise ukuba nifumane ulwazi olunokuthenjwa kunye.

Abantwana badinga ukuva inkcazelo efanayo ngaphezu kwesinye. Oku kuqhelekile. Shiya indawo umntwana angabuza kwakhona kamva, kwaye ujonge ukuba uyiqondile na ngaphandle kokuguqula incoko ibe luvavanyo. Ukuba umntwana usebenzisa amagama ahlukileyo kwesi sifo, mamela kuqala uze ucebise ngobumnene amagama acacileyo asebenzisayo.

## Ndingaziphendula njani imibuzo enzima?

Phendula uxinzelelo olusemva kombuzo, uze unike impendulo ethe ngqo kwaye ethembekileyo oyinikwayo. Gcina kufutshane kwaye ungazaliseli iinkcukacha ongazaziyo.

- **“Ndinokuyifumana na?”** Thi hayi: i-dementia ayisiyo into esasazekayo. Umntwana usenokuqhubeka echitha ixesha nomntu.
- **“Ingaba ndiyenzile oku kwenzeke?”** Mqinisekise ukuba isifo asibangelwanga yinto abe ikhe yathetha okanye yenza.
- **“Kutheni uTatomkhulu endibiza ngelinye igama?”** Chaza ukuba i-dementia isenokutshintsha indlela akhumbula ngayo amagama. Asiyo impazamo yomntwana, kwaye akenzi oko ukuze amcaphukise.
- **“Nam ndiya kuba ne-dementia na?”** Musa ukuqikelela malunga nekamva lomntu omnye. Umngcipheko wentsapho uxhomekeke kuhlobo nakwembali. Ukuba kukho inkxalabo ethile malunga nohlobo olufumana ngelifa, buza ugqirha womntu ukuba ingcali yezofuzo inokunceda na.
- **“Uya kuphila kakuhle na?” okanye “Uya kufa na?”** Musa ukuthembisa isiphumo okanye ixesha. Xelela umntwana oko iqela lonyango lakuthethayo, ukuba intsapho ifuna ukwabelana ngako, kwaye uchaze ukuba niza kuthetha kwakhona nini.
- **“Ungandithanda na?”** Musa ukwenza umntwana abe noxanduva lokubonisa impendulo. Ungathi unxulumano luyabalulekile kwaye ufumane indlela enemvakalelo elungileyo yokuchitha ixesha ndawonye ngoku.

Akulungile ukuyeka xa umbuzo ubonakala ukhulu kakhulu. Xelela umntwana ukuba uya kubuya nini kuwo, kwaye uwulandele ngokwenene. Ukuthi ngokuthuleleyo “Andikakwazi ukuyazi ngoku” ulusizo ngakumbi kunokuqikelela okuqinisekileyo.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngaphandle kokwenza umntwana abe ngumnakekeli?

Nika unxulumano ngokukhetha. Ukuba bobabini umntwana nomntu befuna, bangamamela umculo ojwayelekileyo, bajonge imifanekiso, badweba, bafunde okanye babelane ngomsebenzi olula wasekhaya. Isikhokelo sethu [sokukhumbula izinto zakudala](/activities/reminiscence/) nesikhokelo [somculo](/activities/music/) sinezingcamango. Landela impendulo yomntu nowomntwana; kulungile ukuyeka, ukutshintsha umsebenzi okanye ukutyelela ngelinye ixesha.

Gcina uxanduva lonyango nokhuseleko kubantu abadala. Umntwana usenokukhetha ukubeka isidlo esincinci okanye ukuzisa incwadi, kodwa akafanele abe noxanduva lokujonga, lwamayeza, lokunyamekelwa komntu, lweentlanganiso okanye lweengxaki ezikhawulezayo. Musa ukuqonda ukuba ukutyelela, ukunceda okanye ukuhlala kufuphi kuyimfanelo yomntwana. Isikhokelo se-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) silumkisa iintsapho ukuba zihloniphe imida yabantu abaselula nemfuneko yokuba bahlale besengabantwana.

Khusela ixesha elingokomntwana kuphela. Gcina isikolo, ubuhlobo, iindawo ezithandwa ngumntwana kunye nemikhwa yosapho izinzile ngangokunokwenzeka. Ukuba unonkathalo utshintsha ezo zicwangciso, chaza ukuba kutheni kwaye ufumane omnye umntu omdala onokunceda agcine umsebenzi okanye uhambo lomntwana. [Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela somnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sichaza iindlela zokucela abanye abantu abadala ukuba babelane ngoncedo.

Ngaphambi kokuxelela uthisha, umqeqeshi okanye omnye umntu omdala, mbandakanye umntwana kwisigqibo ngokufanelekileyo. Usenokufuna omnye umntu omthembileyo ukuba azi, okanye usenokuthanda ukulinda. Ukuba wabelana ngolwazi, vumelana ngento oza kuyithetha kunye nento ekufanele igcine iyimfihlo.

## Ndifanele ndifune uncedo oluninzi nini?

Buza ukuba umntwana usenjani ngokuhamba kwexesha, kungekhona kuphela emva kokuxilongwa. Abanye abantwana bathetha ngokukhululekileyo; abanye babonisa uxinezelo ngotshintsho ekulaleni, esikolweni, ebuhlobo okanye ekuziphatheni. Ezo ntshintsho zinokuba nezizathu ezininzi kwaye azifakazeli ukuba umntwana ufuna uxilongo. Mamela kwaye ubuze ukuba yintoni uncedo olunokumnceda.

Ukuba umntwana ufuna umntu ongaphandle kosapho athethe naye, qwalasela uthisha omthembileyo, umcebisi wesikolo okanye ingcali yempilo yengqondo yabantwana. Buza ngaphambi kokuqhagamshelana nabasebenzi besikolo malunga noxilongo xa unokwenza njalo; umntwana usenokufuna ukuba yinxalenye yale ncoko. Iintsapho ezichaphazelwe yi-dementia equla abantu abasebancinci nazo zisenokufuna uncedo ngomsebenzi, ngesikolo nangokutshintsha kweendima zosapho.

Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba uchaza njani utshintsho oluthile, buza iqela lonyango lomntu okanye umbutho wase-dementia ukuze ufumane icebiso. Umntwana akafuni iimpendulo ezigqibeleleyo ukuba zivela kuwe. Ufuna ulwazi oluthembekileyo, ithuba lokubuza kwakhona kunye nabantu abadala abaqhubeka bekhona kuye.
