Ukufunyanwa kwe-dementia kunokuzisa imibuzo ngempilo, ngobomi bemihla ngemihla, ngenkxaso nangekamva. Abantu baphendula ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo: abanye bafuna ixesha lokwamkela iindaba, abanye bafuna ukuqala ukucwangcisa. Akukho ndlela inye elungileyo yokuphendula okanye ixesha elinye elimiselweyo. Qala ngento ebalulekileyo kumntu ngoku, uze ubuyele kwimibuzo eyenye xa esele ekulungele.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sobomi emva kokufunyanwa](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) sikhokelela abantu kukhathalelo lokulandelela, kwinkxaso yasekhaya nasekucwangciseni ikamva. [Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia ngokunyamezela ukufunyanwa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) sigxininisa ukuba abantu baphendula ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo kwaye bangafuna inkxaso xa bethanda. Eli phepha liyindawo yokuqala, asiso isicwangciso somntu sempilo, somthetho okanye semali.

## Linyathelo lini eliluncedo lokuqala?

Khetha umbuzo omnye ongenza le veki ibe lula. Unokuba kukufumanisa ukuba ngubani oza kulungelelanisa ukhathalelo lokulandelela lomntu, ukubuza apho ungafumana khona ulwazi oluthembekileyo ngokufunyanwa kwakhe, okanye ukuchonga umntu amthembileyo angathetha naye. Umntu unokufuna ukukhokela, ukucela omnye ukuba amncede, okanye ukulinda phambi kokugqiba.

Kwidinga elilandelayo, bhala imibuzo embalwa nonke ukuba umntu uyafuna. Ungabuza ukuba ukufunyanwa kuthetha ntoni, yintoni engekacaci, nini ukulandelela, nokuba uqhagamshelana nabani ukuba kuvela inkxalabo entsha. Cela ugqirha acacise nawuphi na umbandela okanye isiphakamiso esingacacanga. [Isikhokelo sethu sotyelelo lukagqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinoluhlu olufutshane lokulungiselela.

Umntu akanyanzelekanga ukuba afunde yonke into ngedementia kanye kanye. Khetha ulwazi oluthembekileyo ngokufunyanwa akufumeneyo, ubhale imibuzo evela kamva. Kuphawulwe ngokulumkela izithembiso, ibango lokuphilisa okanye icebiso eliyalela wonke umntu ukuba alandele unyango okanye isicwangciso esinye.

## Singayifumana phi inkxaso?

Buza umntu ukuba luluphi uhlobo lwenkxaso olunokumnceda nokuba ufuna ukubandakanya bani. Umhlobo othenjiweyo okanye ilungu losapho lingaba yincoko elungileyo yokuqala. Abanye abantu bakhetha iqela lenkxaso, umcebisi, umbutho wasekhaya we-dementia okanye incoko neqela labo lokukhathalela. Ukhetho olufanelekileyo lungxamele umntu nokukhoyo kufuphi.

Inkxaso yomntu ofunyenweyo kunye nosapho nabahlobo. Umkhathaleli unokufuna ukuphumla okanye uncedo ngemisebenzi yemihla ngemihla; umntu unokufuna ulwazi okanye ithuba lokudibana nabanye abaphila ne-dementia. Ezi mfuno zinokwahluka. Buza ngaphambi kokulungiselela iqela, ukwenza idinga okanye ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha zobuqu.

[Isikhokelo sethu sokuzikhathalela komkhathaleli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sinengcamango zokucela abanye ukuba babelane ngenkxaso. Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo oluthe kratya emini, izikhokelo ze-[iinkqubo zemini zabantu abadala](/caregiving/choosing-adult-day-program-dementia/) nezoku[khathalelo ekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) zicacisa imibuzo onokuyibuza ngeenkonzo zasekhaya.

## Umntu angahlala njani eyinxalenye yezigqibo?

Ukufunyanwa kodwa akukuxeleli ukuba umntu unokugqiba ntoni okanye ufuna ukuthatha inxaxheba njani. Buza umntu ngokuthe ngqo. Mnike ixesha, cacisa ukhetho ngolwimi olulula uze ubuze ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso enokwenza incoko ibe lula. Ezinye izigqibo zinokuba lula ukuxoxwa kwindawo ethule okanye kwiincoko ezingaphezu kwenye.

Ukuba umntu ufuna ukucwangcisa ukhathalelo lwempilo lwexesha elizayo, imali okanye apho aza kuhlala khona, qala ngoko kubalulekileyo kuye nabo abathembileyo. [Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia soncwangciso](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) sicebisa ukuxoxa ngeminqweno nabantu abathenjiweyo neengcali ezifanelekileyo. Amaxwebhu asemthethweni nemithetho yokwenza izigqibo ayahluka ngokwendawo, ngoko buza ingcali efanelekileyo yasekhaya ngaphambi kokuthembela kwisikhokelo esiqhelekileyo. Ngoncedo lwemihla ngemihla ngeebhili nenkxalabo ngeenkohliso, jonga [isikhokelo sethu sokulawula imali ne-dementia](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/).

### Singabuza ntoni kwincoko yokuqala ngokhathalelo lwexesha elizayo?

Buza ukuba umntu ufuna ukuthetha ngoku, uze uzise umbandela omnye ngexesha. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa iincoko ezininzi ezincinci, ulwimi olulula nokuphulaphula iminqweno, iinkolelo nexabiso lomntu](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [I-Alzheimer's Society iphawula ukuba abantu bayahluka ngokukulungela ukucinga ngekamva](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Kulungile ukuphumla ubuye ngolunye usuku.

Ukuba umntu ufuna izikhokelo, zama enye yezi:

- "Zeziphi iinxalenye zosuku lwakho ongathanda ukuzigcina kakhulu?"
- "Ngubani ongathanda ukuba abandakanywe xa sithetha ngokhathalelo lwexesha elizayo?"
- "Yintoni ekunceda uzive uphumile kwaye uhlonitshwa xa ufuna uncedo?"
- "Ingaba ikhona into ofuna iqela lakho lokukhathalela ukuba lilazi ngoko kubalulekileyo kuwe?"
- "Ungathanda ndibhale oko ukuthethileyo, kwaye ngubani ofanele akubone?"

Phulaphula ungacelanga isigqibo ngokukhawuleza. [I-National Institute on Aging ichaza iincoko ezinentsingiselo ngexabiso neenkolelo njengesentloko kuncwangciso lokhathalelo lwexesha elizayo](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Ukuba umntu uyavuma, bhala amazwi akhe, umhla nabo babekhona. Cela imvume phambi kokwabelana ngenqaku. Aya manqaku anokukhokela iincoko zamva, kodwa akunamaxwebhu asemthethweni okanye imiyalelo yezonyango.

Musa ukuthatha izigqibo ngokusekwe kokufunyanwa kodwa okanye kwincoko enye. Iimpawu zomntu zinokutshintsha, kwaye angacela ukumisa okanye ukubuyela kumbandela kamva. Mgcine ebandakanyekile kwizigqibo ezimchaphazelayo ngokuthe tsolo njengoko efuna.

## Ngubani ofanele azi ngokufunyanwa?

Umntu uthatha isigqibo sokuba ingaba, nini kwaye njani ukuxelela abanye abantu, ngaphandle kokuba imeko ethile yokhuseleko okanye yomthetho idinga icebiso lengcali. Buza ukuba ufuna ukuxelela bani nokuba ufuna ukuba abo bantu bazi ntoni. Unokukhetha umntu omnye athembileyo kuqala, okanye angafuni ukuxelela bonke abantu.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo kwincoko, buza ukuba ufuna uthi ntoni nokuba ufuna ukubakho okanye hayi. Yabelana kuphela ngolwazi avume ukwabelana ngalo. [Isikhokelo sethu soonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sicubungula iincoko ezihloniphekileyo nosapho nabanye.

## Ubomi bemihla ngemihla bunokuqhubeka njani sisalungelelanisa?

Gcina izinto eziqhelekileyo nemisebenzi ebalulekileyo kumntu apho esenesiqhelo. Ukufunyanwa akuthethi ukuba onke amathanga, uhambo okanye indima yasekhaya kufuneka iyeke. Ukuba into iba nzima, buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukutshintsha ntoni. Utyelelo olufutshane, umculo owaziwayo, uncedo ngenyathelo elinye okanye ixesha elahlukileyo losuku lunokumfanela ngcono.

Umntu unokufuna nendawo yokwamkela iindaba ngaphandle kokuthetha ngezicwangciso. Qhubeka ubuza ngaphandle kokwenza yonke incoko ibe yintlanganiso yokhathalelo. Kulungile ukuphumla ekucwangciseni uchithe ixesha kunye ngendlela eqhelekileyo.

## Sifanele sibuze ingcali nini?

Buza ugqirha womntu imibuzo ethile ngokufunyanwa kwakhe, impilo, amayeza okanye iimpawu. Kwizigqibo zomthetho nezemali, thetha neengcali ezifanelekileyo ezazi imithetho apho umntu ahlala khona. Iwebhusayithi eqhelekileyo ayinakukuxelela ukuba yeyiphi unyango, uxwebhu okanye inkonzo efanelekileyo kumntu othile.

Ukudideka okuqubulayo okanye utshintsho olunye olukhawulezileyo lwempilo kudinga inkathalo yezonyango ekhawulezileyo. [Isikhokelo se-NHS ngokudideka okuqubulayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi fumana uncedo lwezonyango ngokukhawuleza. Sebenzisa inkqubo yasekhaya yemeko eyingxamisekileyo okanye yenkathalo ekhawulezileyo apho umntu ahlala khona; musa ukucinga ukuba utshintsho oluqubulayo lulinxalenye yemeko yakhe eqhelekileyo.

Ukuba ukucwangcisa kuvakala kunzima kakhulu, khetha umntu omnye othenjiweyo okanye inkonzo enye yokuncedisa ukwahlula imibuzo ibe yeyangoku neyakamva. Umntu angamisela isantya aze aqonde ukuba ufuna uncedo ngantoni. Ukufunyanwa lulwazi olubalulekileyo, kodwa akumchazi umntu ngamathanga, ngoqhagamshelwano lwakhe okanye ngobomi bakhe bemihla ngemihla.
