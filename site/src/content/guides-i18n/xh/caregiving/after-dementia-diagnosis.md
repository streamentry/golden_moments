Uxilongo lwe-dementia lunokuvelisa imibuzo malunga nempilo, ubomi bemihla ngemihla, inkxaso kunye nexesha elizayo. Abantu baphendula ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo: abanye bafuna ixesha lokuqonda le ndaba, ngelixa abanye befuna ukuqala ukucwangcisa. Akukho ndlela enye elungileyo okanye ixesha elimiselweyo. Qala ngento ebalulekileyo kumntu ngoku, kwaye ubuyele kwezinye imibuzo xa esele elungele.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association malunga nobomi emva kokuxilongwa](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) sikhomba abantu kulandelelo lonyango, kwinkxaso yasekuhlaleni kunye nocwangciso lwexesha elizayo. [Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia malunga nokumelana noxilongo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) sigxininisa ukuba abantu baphendula ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo kwaye banokufuna inkxaso xa befuna. Eli phepha ngumqalo nje, ayisiyo icwangciso yomntu yezempilo, yomthetho okanye yezemali.

## Yintoni inyathelo elinye elisebenzayo lokuqala?

Khetha umbuzo onokwenza ukuba eli veki libe lula. Unokuba ngowokufumanisa ukuba ngubani oza kulungelelanisa ukulandelwa kwempilo yomntu, ukubuza apho kufumaneka khona ulwazi oluthembekileyo malunga noxilongo lwakhe, okanye ukuchonga umntu amthembayo ukuba bathethe naye. Umntu usenokufuna ukuthatha inkokeli, acele umntu amncedise, okanye alinde ngaphambi kokugqiba.

Kwisibonakaliso sokutyelela okulandelayo, bhala imibuzo embalwa kunye, ukuba umntu uyafuna oko. Ungabuza ukuba uxilongo lunentsingiselo ntoni, yintoni engakaqinisekiswa, nini kufuneka ulandele, kunye nokuba ungaxhomana nabani xa kuvela enye inkxalabo entsha. Cela ingcali yezonyango ichaze nayiphi na igama okanye isincomo esingacacanga. Isikhokelo sethu [sokutyelela udokotela](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinoluhlu olufutshane lokulungiselela.

Umntu akafuneki ukufunda yonke into malunga ne-dementia ngaxeshanye. Khetha ulwazi oluthembekileyo malunga noxilongo akufumeneyo, kwaye ubhale imibuzo eyakuvela kamva. Qaphela izithembiso, amabango okuphilisa okanye iingcebiso ezithi wonke umntu makulandele unyango okanye icwangciso efanayo.

## Sifumana phi inkxaso?

Buza umntu ukuba luhlobo luphi uncedo oluya kumncedisa kwaye ufuna ukuba ubani abandakanyeke. Umhlobo okanye ilungu losapho elithembekileyo linokuba yincoko yokuqala elungileyo. Abanye abantu bakhetha iqela lenkxaso, umcebisi, umbutho wasekuhlaleni wedementia okanye incoko neqela labo lonyango. Ukhetho olufanelekileyo luxhomekeke kumntu nakwento efumaneka kufutshane.

Inkxaso yenzelwe umntu oxilwe kunye nosapho nabahlobo. Umnakekeli usenokufuna ukuphumla okanye uncedo lwemisebenzi yemihla ngemihla; umntu usenokufuna ulwazi okanye ithuba lokudibana nabanye abaphila nedementia. Ezi mfuno zingahluka. Buza ngaphambi kokulungiselela iqela, ukwenza uthethathethwano olutsha okanye ukwabelana ngeenkcukacha zomntu.

Isikhokelo sethu [sokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sinemibono yokucela abanye ukuba babelane ngenkxaso. Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo oluninzi emini, izikhokelo ezichaza [iinkqubo zemini zabantu abadala](/caregiving/choosing-adult-day-program-dementia/) kunye [nenkathalo yasekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) zichaza imibuzo okufanele uyibuze ngeenkonzo zasekuhlaleni.

## Umntu angahlala njani enxulumene nezigqibo?

Uxilongo ngokwalo aluchazi ukuba umntu unokuthatha siphi isigqibo okanye ukuba ufuna ukuthatha inxaxheba njani. Buza umntu ngqo. Mnike ixesha, chaza ukhetho ngolwimi olulula kwaye ubuze ukuba yiyiphi inkxaso eyakwenza ukuba incoko ibe lula. Ezinye iindidi zokhetho zingalula ukuxoxwa kwazo kwindawo ethe cwaka okanye kwiincoko ezingaphezu kwenye.

Ukuba umntu ufuna ukucwangcisela unyango lwempilo lwexesha elizayo, imali okanye indawo yokuhlala, qala ngento ebalulekileyo kuye kunye nabantu abamthembayo. Isikhokelo se-[Dementia Australia sokucwangcisa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) sicebisa ukuxoxa ngeminqweno nabantu abathembekileyo nabasebenzi abafanelekileyo. Imithetho yamaxwebhu asemthethweni kunye neemfuno zokwenza izigqibo ziyahluka kwindawo ngayinye, ngoko buza ingcali yasekuhlaleni enelungelo phambi kokuxhomekeka kwisikhokelo jikelele. Ukuba unemibuzo yemali yemihla ngemihla okanye ukukhathazeka ngobuqhetseba, bona [isikhokelo sethu sokulawula imali ngedementia](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/).

### Yintoni esinokuyibuza kwincoko yokuqala malunga nonyango lwexesha elizayo?

Buza ukuba umntu ufuna ukuthetha ngoku, emva koko uphakamise umbandela omnye ngexesha. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa iincoko ezimbalwa ezincinci, ulwimi olulula kunye nokumamela iminqweno, iinkolelo kunye nexabiso lomntu](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [I-Alzheimer's Society iphawula ukuba abantu bahluka ngokuzimisela kokucinga ngexesha elizayo](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Akukho nto ingalunganga ngokumisa kwaye ubuyele kwelinye ilanga.

Ukuba umntu efuna imibuzo engenza incoko iqhubeke, zama enye yale ilandelayo:

- “Zeziphi iinxalenye zemihla yakho ozithanda ngakumbi ukuzigcina?”
- “Ngubani ofuna ukuba abandakanyeke xa sithetha ngonyango lwexesha elizayo?”
- “Yintoni ekunceda uzive ukhululekile kwaye uhlonelwe xa ufuna uncedo?”
- “Kukho nto ofuna ukuba iqela lakho lonyangoliyazi malunga nento ebalulekileyo kuwe?”
- “Ungathanda ukuba ndibhale oko uthe wakuchaza, nokuba ngubani okufanele akubone?”

Phulaphula ungacelanga isigqibo kwangoko. [Isebe lase-National Institute on Aging lichaza iincoko ezinentsingiselo malunga nexabiso neminqweno njengento ephambili kwicwangciso yonyango lwangaphambili](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Ukuba umntu uvumile, bhala amazwi akhe, umhla kunye nabantu ababekho. Cela imvume ngaphambi kokwabelana ngale nqaku. Ezi zibhalo zinokukhokela iincoko zexesha elizayo, kodwa azisiyo amaxwebhu asemthethweni okanye iiodolo zonyango.

Musa ukwenza iingcinga ngokusekelwe kuxilongo kuphela okanye kwincoko enye. Iimfuno zomntu zinokutshintsha, kwaye angacela ukuba kuyekwe okanye ukubuyelwa kwisihloko kamva. Qhubeka umbandakanya kwizigqibo ezimchaphazelayo njengoko efuna.

## Ngubani okufanele azi malunga noxilongo?

Umntu uyagqiba ukuba uxelela abanye ukuba, nini nangaliphi indlela, ngaphandle kokuba imeko ethile yokhuseleko okanye yomthetho ifuna icebiso lobuchwephesha. Buza ukuba ngubani afuna ukumxelela nokuba ulwazi oluphi na afuna ukuba bazi. Unokukhetha umntu omnye amthembayo kuqala, okanye angafuni ukwabelana ngalo ngokubanzi.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo kwincoko, buza ukuba ufuna ukuba uthethe ntoni, kunye nokuba ufuna ukuba ukhona. Yabelana kuphela ngolwazi avumelene nokuba lwabelwane ngalo. Isikhokelo sethu [sonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) siquka iincoko ezihloniphekileyo nosapho nabanye abantu.

## Ngaba ukuphila kwemihla ngemihla kunokuqhubeka njani xa sisazolowana?

Gcina iinkqubo nemisebenzi ebalulekileyo kumntu apho usezizwa ukhululekile khona. Uxilongo akuthethi ukuba zonke izinto abazithandayo, ukuphuma okanye iindima zasekhaya kufuneka zime. Ukuba into ithe yanzima, buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukuyitshintsha ntoni. Ukuhamba kwexesha elifutshane, iculo elaziwayo, uncedo kwenye inyathelo okanye ixesha elahlukileyo lemini kunokumlungela ngakumbi.

Umntu usenokufuna indawo yokuqonda le ndaba ngaphandle kokuxoxa ngezicwangciso. Qhubeka uhlola ukuba ukhona, ungayiguqulisi yonke incoko ibe yintlanganiso yonakekelo. Kulungile ukuthatha ikhefu kwicwangciso kwaye uchithe ixesha ndawonye ngendlela eqhelekileyo.

## Nini ekufuneka sibuze ingcali?

Buza udokotela womntu imibuzo engqinelana noxilongo lwakhe, impilo yakhe, amayeza okanye iimpawu. Ngeziggibo zomthetho nezemali, thetha neengcali ezinelungelo ezikwazi imithetho yasekuhlaleni apho umntu ahlala khona. Iwebhusayithi jikelele ayinakukuxelela ukuba yeyiphi na unyango, ixwebhu okanye inkonzo elungele umntu othile.

Ukudideka okungalindelwanga okanye olunye utshintsho olukhawulezayo kwimpilo kufuna uncedo lwexesha elifanelekileyo. Isikhokelo se-[NHS malunga nokudideka okungalindelwanga](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi kufuneka ufumane uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko. Sebenzisa inkonzo yesiphuthuma okanye yonyango lwangoko yasekuhlaleni apho umntu ahlala khona; ungacingi ukuba utshintsho olukhawulezayo luyinxalenye yemeko yakhe eqhelekileyo.

Ukuba icwangciso ikhathaza kakhulu, khetha umntu omthembayo okanye inkonzo eya kukunceda uhlele imibuzo ibe ziimpawu eziphangaleyo nezo zinokulindela kamva. Umntu unokuseta isantya sakhe kwaye akhethe ukuba ufuna uncedo ngantoni. Uxilongo lubalulekile, kodwa aluchazi izithakazelo, ubudlelwane okanye ubomi bemihla ngemihla bomntu.
