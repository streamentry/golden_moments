Ukuhlala wedwa akuchazi ngokwako ukuba umntu udinga uncedo olungakanani. I-Alzheimer's Association ithi abantu abaninzi abane-Alzheimer's baqhubeka behlala bebodwa, ngakumbi kwinqanaba lokuqala, ngokulungiswa okulula, ukhuseleko nenkxaso evela kwabanye. Umbuzo oluncedo uthe ngqo: yintoni lo mntu afuna ukuqhubeka eyenza, kwaye yintoni enokwenza loo misebenzi ibe lula?

Qala loo ncoko xa kukho ixesha kwaye kungekho ngxaki ekhawulezileyo yokusombulula. Buza ukuba yintoni evakala kamnandi, yeyiphi imikhuba ebalulekileyo nokuba luluphi uhlobo loncedo umntu anokulwamkela. Isicwangciso esenziwe naye sinokulingana nobomi bakhe kunoluhlu lwemisebenzi elungiswe ngenxa yenkxalabo yomnye umntu.

## Yintoni enokwenza ubomi bemihla ngemihla bube lula?

Sebenzisa iinkxaso ezimbalwa ezaziwayo ezihambelana nemikhuba yalo mntu. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa izixhobo ezisebenzisekayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) ezinjengekhalenda, uluhlu nezikhumbuzo zeemfuno zemihla ngemihla.

- **Khetha indawo enye yezinto eziyimfuneko.** Gcina iitshixo, iiglasi, isingxobo semali nefowuni kwindawo enye evunyiweyo. Itreyi ekufutshane nomnyango okanye ishelufu enelebhile isenokwenza umkhuba ube lula ukuwulandela.
- **Sebenzisa ikhalenda enye okanye inkqubo enye yezikhumbuzo.** Khetha ikhalenda yodonga, ibhodi emhlophe, idayari yephepha okanye ikhalenda yefowuni umntu asele ethanda ukuyisebenzisa. Gcina izicelo zokubona ugqirha nemisebenzi eqhelekileyo kwindawo enye endaweni yokuzisasaza kwiiapp nenqaku ezininzi.
- **Yenza imikhuba ibonakale.** Ukuba umsebenzi uhlala ulibaleka, bhala inqaku elifutshane okanye ubeke into ekufuneka kuyo kwindawo eyaziwayo. Gcina amanyathelo emafutshane uze uwaphonononge kunye ukuba ayayeka ukunceda.
- **Yenza imisebenzi ephindaphindwayo ibe nokuqikelelwa.** Ukuhanjiswa kweempahla zokuthenga rhoqo, utyelelo lokucoca okanye uququzelelo lokutya kunokunciphisa inani lezigqibo umntu ekufuneka alawule yedwa.

[Isikhokelo sethu semikhuba yemihla ngemihla](/activities/daily-routine/) sinezindlela ezilula zokugcina usuku luqhelekile ngaphandle kokulwenza lube lukhuni. Injongo kukuxhasa ukhetho lwalo mntu, kungekhona ukuzalisa iyure nganye.

## Usapho nabahlobo banganceda njani ngokuluncedo?

Vumelanani ngenani elincinci labaqhagamshelwana rhoqo. Utyelelo olufutshane okanye ifowuni ngexesha elaziwayo linokuba luncedo ngakumbi kunabantu abaninzi abajonga ngokungalindelekanga. Buza umntu ukuba ufuna bani ababandakanywe nokuba umntu ngamnye angakwenza ntoni.

Yenza iminikelo ibe ngqo. “Ingaba kuza kunceda ukuba ndizise iimpahla zokuthenga ngoLwesine?” kulula ukuphendula kunokuthi “Ndazise ukuba udinga nantoni na.” Umntu othenjiweyo usenokunceda ngokuthenga, ukuthutha, ukutya, amatyala okanye ikhalenda yezicelo zokubona ugqirha. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukulungiselela uncedo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) ngemisebenzi yasekhaya, ukutya, ukuthutha nezikhumbuzo zamayeza xa ezo mfuno ziqala.

Ukuba abantu abaninzi bayanceda, vumelanani ukuba ngubani oza kuphatha umsebenzi ngamnye nokuba baza kudlulisa njani iindaba ezibalulekileyo. Gcina ulwazi lufutshane kubantu abakhethwe ngumntu, ngaphandle kokuba inkxalabo ekhawulezileyo yokhuseleko ifuna uncedo kwangoko. Ukuba uhlala kude, bona [isikhokelo sethu sokunyamekela ukude](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) ngeendlela zokulungelelanisa nabantu abakhethwe ngumntu. [Isikhokelo sonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sinolwazi olungakumbi ngokuxoxa ngenkxaso ngaphandle kokuyenza ibe ngumvavanyo.

## Umntu angaqhubeka njani ehlala exhumene ngelixa ehlala yedwa?

Khetha imisebenzi noqhagamshelwano umntu alonwabelayo. Kunokuba yifowuni yeveki, utyelelo lommelwane, isidlo senkqubo yoluntu, iklabhu eyaziwayo okanye ukuhamba nomntu athembileyo. Buza umntu ukuba zeziphi iindlela ezivakala zamkelekile; musa ukucinga ukuba abatyeleli okanye amaqela amaninzi ahlala engcono.

Ixesha lentlalo rhoqo linokwenza iveki ibe lula ukuyiqonda lize linike umntu into emlindelweyo. Ukuba uhambo oluya kumsebenzi luba nzima, cinga ngefowuni, ukuthutha ngumntu othenjiweyo okanye isiganeko esincinci esikufutshane nekhaya. [Isikhokelo sethu sonxibelelwano loluntu](/brain-health/social-connection/) siquka iindlela ezingenabo uxinzelelo zokugcina unxibelelwano.

## Siqaphela njani ukuba isicwangciso kufuneka sitshintshe?

Akukho luhlu lunye oluchaza ukuba umntu angahlala yedwa okanye hayi. Endaweni yoko, nikela ingqalelo kutshintsho oluthile ebomini bemihla ngemihla: umsebenzi umntu awayewenza kamnandi ngoku ubangela ingxaki ephindaphindwayo, ukutya kuyashiywa, izicelo zokubona ugqirha ziyalahleka, okanye uthi umsebenzi othile uvakala unzima ngakumbi.

Buza umntu ukuba uqaphele ntoni. Nika utshintsho olunye olusebenzisekayo uze ujonge ukuba luyanceda na. Ukuba kukho inkxalabo ephindaphindwayo ngokutya, amayeza, imali, ukuhamba okanye ukhuseleko lwasekhaya, buza umntu ukuba angathetha nogqirha wakhe okanye ingcali yenkxaso yentlalo. Uvavanyo olusesikweni lunokunceda ukuchonga iindlela zenkxaso; aluyinyanzelo kumntu ukuba amkele nganye isiphakamiso.

Ukuba umntu ubonakala engaqinisekanga ngoncedo, qala kancinane. Usenokukhetha ukuhanjiswa kweempahla zokuthenga ngeveki kunomnyamekeli ekhaya, okanye uncedo ngamaphepha kodwa hayi unyamekelo lomntu. Mbandakanye ekukhetheni umntu oza kunceda nokuba utyelelo lwenzeka nini. [Isikhokelo sokhuseleko ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) siquka utshintsho igumbi ngegumbi xa inkxalabo ikwikhaya ngokwalo.

## Kuthekani ukuba umntu akafuni uncedo?

Mamela isizathu ngaphambi kokunika esinye isicwangciso. Usenokuxhalaba ngemfihlo, iindleko, ukuphulukana nolawulo okanye ukuba nomntu ongaziwayo ekhaya. Buza ukuba luluphi uhlobo loncedo olunokuvakala lwamkelekile, uze ubone ukuba uvavanyo oluncinci luyasebenza. Abanye abantu bakhetha isikhumbuzo okanye umsebenzi wokwabelana; abanye bafuna inkxaso evela komnye umntu othenjiweyo kunenkonzo entsha.

Musa ukwenza ukujonga kube ngumvavanyo. Ifowuni inokuba yincoko, kungekhona uluhlu lwemibuzo ngokuba umntu utyile, uthathe iyeza okanye ukhumbule isicelo sokubona ugqirha. Ukuba kukho ingozi ekhoyo ngoku, qhagamshelana neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. Ngenkxalabo eqhubekayo, gcina umntu ebandakanyiwe uze ufune ingcebiso kwiqela lakhe lonyango okanye kwinkonzo yenkxaso yasekuhlaleni.

Ukuhlala wedwa kusenokuqhubeka kusebenza ngexube eliguqukayo lemikhuba, ubudlelwane noncedo olusebenzisekayo. Phonononga isicwangciso nomntu, gcina oko kuvakala kuluncedo uze ulungise iinxalenye ezingasafanelanga.
