Ukuhlala yedwa akuchazi ngokwaso ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo oluninzi kangakanani. I-Alzheimer's Association iphawula ukuba abantu abaninzi abanesifo se-Alzheimer baqhubeka bihlala bodwa, ngakumbi kwinqanaba lokuqala, ngoguquko oluncinci, izinyathelo zokhuseleko kunye noncedo kwabanye. Umbuzo ongcono ngokwenene ngowokuba: ngowaphi umsebenzi umntu efuna ukuwuqhubeka nawo, kwaye yintoni eyayenza ezo misebenzi zibe lula?

Qala le ncoko xa kukho ixesha kwaye kungekho ngxaki ekhoyo ekufuneka isonjululwe ngoko nangoko. Buza into eziva umntu ilula kuyo, ukuba yeyiphi imikhwa ebalulekileyo nohlobo lweluncedo umntu alulandelelayo. Isicwangciso esenziwa kunye naye sinokufaneleka ubomi bakhe ngakumbi kunoluhlu lwemisebenzi elungiswe ngokwenkathalo yabanye.

## Yintoni enokwenza ubomi bemihla ngemihla bube lula?

Sebenzisa ezinye zezixhasi ezaziwayo ezifanelana nemikhwa yomntu. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa izixhobo ezisebenzayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone), njengamakhalenda, uluhlu kunye nezikhumbuzo zeemfuneko zemihla ngemihla.

- **Khetha indawo enye yezinto eziyimfuneko.** Gcina izitshixo, iiglasi, isipaji semali kunye nefowuni kwindawo enye evunyelwene ngayo. Ibhodwe elingasemnyango okanye ishelufu elinolebheli linokwenza umkhwa ube lula ukuwulandela.
- **Sebenzisa ikhalenda okanye inkqubo yezikhumbuzo enye.** Khetha ikhalenda ludonga, ibhodi emhlophe, ijenali yephepha okanye ikhalenda yefowuni ethanda ukuyisebenzisa kakade. Gcina amaxesha okubonwa nemisebenzi eqhelekileyo kwindawo enye endaweni yokuyisasaza kwizicelo nakwiinothi ezininzi.
- **Yenza imikhwa ibonakale.** Ukuba umsebenzi ulityalwa rhoqo, bhala isicatshulwa esifutshane okanye ubeke into efunekayo kuwo kwindawo eyaziwayo. Gcina amanyathelo emfutshane kwaye uwaphonononge ndawonye xa ezingekho luncedo.
- **Yenza imisebenzi ephindaphindekayo ibe lula ukuyilindela.** Ukuhanjiswa kwezithengi okuqhelekileyo, ukundwendwelwa ngabasebenzi bekhaya okanye ulungiselelo lokutya kunokunciphisa inani lezigqibo umntu ayifuna ukuzilawula yedwa.

Isikhokelo sethu [semikhwa yemihla ngemihla](/activities/daily-routine/) sinezindlela ezilula zokugcina imini iyaziwa ngaphandle kokuyenza ibe lukhuni. Injongo kukuxhasa unyulo lomntu, hayi ukuzalisa yonke iyure.

## Iintsapho nabahlobo bangancedisa njani ngoncedo oluluncedo?

Vumelanani ngenani elincinci labantu abaqhagamshelana nabo rhoqo. Ukundwendwela okufutshane okanye ikholi ngexesha elaziwayo kunokuba luncedo ngakumbi kunokuba abantu abaninzi bajonge ngokungalindelekanga. Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukubandakanya bani nokuba ngumntu ngamnye unokwenza ntoni.

Nika izindululo ezithile. “Ingaba kuya kunceda ukuba ndizise izithengi NgoLwesine?” kulula ukuyiphendula kunokuba “Ndazise ukuba ufuna nantoni na.” Umntu othembekileyo angancedisa ngokuthenga, ukuhamba, ukutya, iimfuneko ezithengwayo okanye ikhalenda lamaxesha okubonwa. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukulungiselela uncedo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) ngemisebenzi yasekhaya, ukutya, ukuhamba kunye nezikhumbuzo zeeyeza xa ezo mfuneko zivela.

Ukuba abantu abaninzi bancedisa, vumelanani ukuba ngubani ozakuphatha umsebenzi ngamnye nendlela abazakuwuhambisa ngayo iindaba eziphambili. Gcina ulwazi lulinganiselwe kubantu umntu abakhethileyo, ngaphandle kokuba ingxaki yokhuseleko engxamisekileyo ifuna uncedo ngoko nangoko. Ukuba uhlala kude, bona [isikhokelo sonakekelo ukusuka kude](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) ngeendlela zokulungelelanisa abantu umntu abakhethileyo. Isikhokelo [sonxibelelwano](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sinezinye iinkcukacha malunga nokuxoxa ngoncedo ngaphandle kokuyenza isivivinyo.

## Umntu angahlala njani eqhagamshelekile ngelixa ehlala yedwa?

Khetha imisebenzi nonxibelelwano umntu alonwabela kuyo. Kunokuba yikholi yeveki nganye, undwendwelo lomakhelwane, isidlo sasekuhlaleni, iklabhu eyaziwayo okanye ukuhamba nomntu abamthembileyo. Buza umntu ukuba zeziphi ezo ndlela zimamkela; musa ukucinga ukuba abatyeleli abaninzi okanye amaqela akusoloko elungile.

Ixesha elihlala lenziwa ngokuhlala nabantu linokwenza iveki ibonakale ilula kwaye linike umntu into alindele ukuyibona. Ukuba uhambo oluya kwisenzo lunzima, cinga ngekholi, ukuhanjiswa ngumntu othembekileyo okanye umsitho omncinci ufuphi nekhaya. Isikhokelo sethu [sonxibelelwano lwentlalo](/brain-health/social-connection/) sibandakanya iindlela ezingacindezeli ukuhlala unxibelelene.

## Siyiqaphela njani ukuba isicwangciso kufuneka sitshintshe?

Akukho luluhlu lunye oluthi ukuba umntu unokuhlala yedwa na. Endaweni yoko, qwalasela utshintsho oluthile ebomini bemihla ngemihla: umsebenzi umntu awuphatha ngokuzola ngoku ubangela iingxaki eziphindaphindekayo, izidlo ziyaphoswa, amaxesha okubonwa ngugqirha aya ngamanye amaxesha alahleka, okanye bathi umsebenzi othile ubonakala unzima ngakumbi.

Buza umntu ukuba ubonile ntoni. Nika utshintsho olunye olusebenzayo kwaye ujonge ukuba luyancedo na. Ukuba kukho inkxalabo ephindaphindekayo ngokutya, ngeeyeza, ngemali, ngokuhamba okanye ngokhuseleko lwekhaya, buza umntu ukuba uyathetha na negqirha wakhe okanye ingcali yonakekelo lwentlalo. Uvavanyo olusemthethweni lunokunceda ukubona izinketho zoncedo; alufuni ukuba umntu avume kuso sonke isiphakamiso.

Ukuba umntu ebonakala engaqinisekanga ngoncedo, qalisa ngokuncinci. Usenokukhetha ukuhanjiswa kwezithengi evekini kunomnakekeli ekhaya, okanye uncedo ngamaxwebhu kodwa hayi unakekelo lomntu ngokwakhe. Mbandakanye ekukhetheni umntu oza kunceda nokuba ukundwendwela kuya kwenzeka nini. Isikhokelo [sokhuseleko lwekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sigubungela utshintsho ngegumbi ngegumbi xa inkxalabo ikhankanya ikhaya ngokwalo.

## Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba umntu engafuni uncedo?

Phulaphula isizathu phambi kokunika olunye uhlobo lwesicwangciso. Usenokuba nexhala ngobumfihlo, ngexabiso, ngokulahlekelwa kolawulo okanye ngokuba nomntu ongaziwayo ekhaya. Buza ukuba zeziphi iintlobo zoncedo ezinokwamkelwa, emva koko bona ukuba uvavanyo oluncinci luyasebenza na. Abanye abantu bakhetha isikhumbuzo okanye umsebenzi wabelwane; abanye bafuna inkxaso yomntu mnye othembekileyo endaweni yenkonzo entsha.

Gwema ukwenza ukujonga kube sivivinyo. Ikholi inokuba yincoko, hayi uluhlu lwemibuzo malunga nokuba umntu utyile na, uthathe iyeza na okanye akhumbule ixesha lokubonwa ngugqirha. Ukuba kukho ingozi ekhoyo ngoko nangoko, qhagamshelana neenkonzo ezikhawulezayo zasekuhlaleni. Ngeenkxalabo eziqhubekayo, mbandakanye umntu kwaye ufune icebiso kwiqela lonakekelo lakhe okanye kwinkonzo yoncedo yasekuhlaleni.

Ukuhlala yedwa kusenokuqhubeka kusebenza ngomxube otshintshayo wemikhwa, ubudlelwane kunye noncedo olusebenzayo. Phonononga isicwangciso kunye nomntu, gcina into eziva ilusizo kwaye ulungelelanise iinxalenye ezingasafaneleki.
