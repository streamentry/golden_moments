Xa kukho ilungu losapho elinesifo sokulibala, izalamane zinokungavumelana ngokhuseleko, ngonakekelo okanye ngekamva. Omnye umntu usenokubona iingxaki zemihla ngemihla; omnye unokundwendwela kancinci okanye akhumbule umntu njengoko wayenjalo ngaphambili. Imali, umgama, umsebenzi kunye neendima zosapho zinokongeza uxinzelelo. Ungqubano ubuhlungu, kodwa ngokwalo alubonisi ukuba komnye umntu akakhathali kangako.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association sezingxabano zosapho](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts) siphakamisa ukuphulaphula ngaphandle kokugxeka, ukuxoxa ngemisebenzi ethile yonakekelo kunye nokubuyela kwincoko xa iimfuneko zitshintsha. [I-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/news/how-do-i-navigate-changes-my-relationships) ikwaphawula ukuba iintsapho zinokungavumelana ngezigqibo zonakekelo kwaye zinokufuna uncedo lwangaphandle. Esi sikhokelo sinika indlela yokuthetha; asisombululi igunya lomthetho okanye asigqibi ukuba unakekelo lulungile na kumntu omnye.

## Sinokumgcina njani umntu esembindini?

Buza umntu ophila nesifo sokulibala ukuba yintoni ebalulekileyo kubo nokuba bafuna ukuthatha inxaxheba njani. Ukuxelwa ukuba unesifo akuthathi indawo yakhe kwiincoko malunga nobomi bakhe bemihla ngemihla. Buza ukuba bafuna ukubandakanya bani nolwazi abakulungele ukwabelana ngalo. Ukuba bafuna uncedo lokuveza unyulo lwabo, mnike ixesha kwaye nixoxe ngonyulo olunye ngexesha.

Isikhokelo [sokuqala emva kokuxelwa ukuba unesifo](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) sinamacebiso okubandakanya umntu ekucwangcisweni kwexesha elizayo. Ukuba umntu ukhethe umntu oza kumncedisa ekwenzeni izigqibo, mbandakanye loo mntu kwingxoxo ngelixa uhlonipha ubume bomntu kunye neziphelo zakhe. Ukuba iintsapho zingavumelani ngegunya lokwenza izigqibo ngokomthetho, buza ingcali yasekuhlaleni eyaziyo esi sihloko kunokuba uqikelele.

## Siqala njani incoko yosapho?

Khetha ixesha elizolileyo ngaphambi kwengxaki xa kunokwenzeka. Khetha isihloko esinye sale ncoko, njengokuba ngubani onokunika izihambi okanye indlela yokuhlela ixesha elilandelayo lokubonwa ngugqirha. Uluhlu olude lwezigqibo ezinzima lunokwenza kube nzima kubo bonke ukuphulaphula.

Ngaphambi kwale ncoko, bhala phantsi oko ubonileyo nokuba ucinga ntoni. Umzekelo, “UTata uphosile amaxesha amabini okubonwa ngugqirha kule nyanga” sisiqwalaselo esicacileyo. “UTata akasakwazi ukulawula nto” sisiphelo esibanzi esingangqinelani nezakhono zakhe okanye nezifiso zakhe.

Nika umntu ngamnye ithuba lokuchaza into axhalabele yona. Phulaphula ungayiphazamisi, ungagxeki, kwaye ungathelekisi ukuba ngubani onegalelo elikhulu. Umntu ohlala kude akasoze abone iimfuneko zemihla ngemihla; umntu othatha uninzi lonakekelo usenokuba udiniwe. Zombini iimvakalelo zibalulekile, nokuba zikhokelela kwiingcinga ezahlukeneyo.

## Sifanele ukuxoxa ntoni kwintlanganiso?

Gcina incoko isebenzayo. Uluhlu olufutshane lwezihloko lunokunceda usapho lushiye nenyathelo elicacileyo elilandelayo:

| Isihloko | Umbuzo wokuxoxa ngawo |
| --- | --- |
| Izifiso zomntu | Ngowaphi umsebenzi umntu afuna ukuqhubeka nawo? Ufuna ukubandakanya bani? |
| Okwenzekayo ngoku | Ngubani obone ntoni, kwaye nini? Yintoni efuna ingqiqo yengcali yonyango? |
| Imisebenzi kunye nemida | Ufuna uncedo oluphi? Ngubani onexesha nesakhono sokuthatha umsebenzi ngamnye? |
| Amanyathelo alandelayo | Ngubani oya kwenza ntoni, ngaliphi ixesha, kwaye nini usapho luza kubuyela kuhlolwa? |

Ukuba umba ufuna icebiso lezonyango, bhala umbuzo phantsi ubuze iqela lonakekelo. Isikhokelo [sokuya kugqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinokunceda ulungiselele loo ncoko. Iingxabano ngamaxwebhu omthetho, uncedo lwezimali okanye iikontraka zishiye kwiingcali ezinesakhono ezaziyo imithetho yasekuhlaleni.

## Sinokwahlula njani imisebenzi yonakekelo ngokulungileyo?

Ukulungileyo akuhlali kuthetha ukulingana. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukwenza uluhlu lomsebenzi oquka ixesha, imali kunye nomzamo ofuneka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts). Emva koko buza ukuba ngumntu ngamnye ukhetha ntoni kwaye unokuyenza ngokwenene ntoni.

Abanye abantu banokunceda ngokundwendwela rhoqo okanye ngonakekelo lomntu ngokwakhe. Abanye banokufaneleka ngakumbi ekulawuleni ukuhlelwa kwamaxesha okubonwa ngugqirha, ukuhanjiswa, ukuhanjiswa kokutya, amaxwebhu okanye ukuhlolwa kwveki nveki. Izalamane ezihlala kude zisenokuhlela iikholi, zikhangele iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni okanye zilungiselele izixhobo, ukuba umntu uyavuma. Isikhokelo [sonakekelo ukusuka kude](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) sinika isicwangciso esipheleleyo seendima, izibhengezo kunye nokundwendwela.

Beka imisebenzi evumelaneyo kwikhalenda eyabelwanwayo okanye kuluhlu olubhaliweyo. Yongeza ukuba ngubani onoxanduva, rhoqo kangakanani kunye nokuba ungaqhagamshelana nabani xa izicwangciso zitshintsha. Hlola olu luhlu xa impilo yomntu, ixesha lakhe lokusebenza okanye amandla okunakekela etshintsha. Isikhokelo [sokuzikhathalela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sichaza iindlela zokucela abanye abantu bakwabelane ngoncedo.

## Singenza ntoni xa sisangavumelani?

Buyela kwizinto ezaziwayo, kwizifiso zomntu nakumbuzo osele ungasombululekanga. Akunakwenzeka ukuba usombulule yonke into kwintlanganiso enye. Vumelana ngesenzo esinye esikhuselekileyo nesamkelekileyo okwangoku, emva koko ubeke ixesha lokuphinda uqwalasele isigqibo esikhulu.

Ukuba izalamane zingavumelani ngokuhamba kwendawo, yenza inyathelo lokuqala lokuqokelela ulwazi endaweni yokuvota ewe okanye hayi. Buza ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso engekho ekhaya, ukuba umntu uza kukhetha ntoni nokuba yeyiphi indlela usapho olufuna ukuyifunda. Ukundwendwela indawo yonakekelo okanye ukuthetha nomboneleli wonakekelo ekhaya kunokukhokela incoko ngaphandle kokubopha umntu ekuhambeni. [Isikhokelo sonakekelo lwendawo yokuhlala](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/) sineempendulo zokuthelekisa ezi ndlela.

Ukuba uxinzelelo luyaqhubeka, cela umntu wesithathu othembekileyo ukuba acebe. Kuxhomekeke kwingxaki, oko kunokuba yingcali yonyango yomntu, ingcali yezentlalo, umeluleki, umlamli okanye inkokeli yenkolo. Iqela lokuxhasa nalo lunokubanika izalamane indawo yokuthetha nabantu abajongene notshintsho olufanayo. Icebiso leengcali lubalulekile ngakumbi kwimibuzo yonyango, yomthetho okanye yezimali.

Ukuba ungqubano losapho luchaphazela ukhuseleko okanye unakekelo lomntu, qhagamshelana nenkonzo yasekuhlaleni eyaziyo ukuze ufumane uncedo. Musa ukushiya umntu esesembindini wengxabano. Ukuba kukho ingozi ekhoyo ngoko nangoko, sebenzisa iinkonzo ezikhawulezayo zasekuhlaleni.

Iintsapho azihlali zivumelana, kwaye incoko enye ayinakusombulula ingxaki. Qhubeka uphulaphula umntu, yenza izibophelelo ezicacileyo kwaye ucele uncedo phambi kokuba elinye ilungu losapho lithwale yonke into lodwa.
