Xa ilungu losapho linedementia, izalamane zinokungavumelani ngokhuseleko, ngokunyamekela okanye ngekamva. Omnye unokubona iingxaki zemihla ngemihla; omnye unokutyelela kancinane okanye akhumbule lo mntu njengoko wayenjalo ngaphambili. Imali, umgama, umsebenzi neendima zosapho ziyakwazi ukongeza uxinzelelo. Ungquzulwano luyabuhlungu, kodwa lungenabo ubungqina bokuba omnye akakhathali.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngongquzulwano losapho](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts) sicebisa ukumamela ngaphandle kokusola, ukuxoxa ngemisebenzi ethile yokunyamekela nokubuyela kwincoko xa iimfuno zitshintsha. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/news/how-do-i-navigate-changes-my-relationships) ikwathi usapho lunokungavumelani ngezigqibo zokunyamekela kwaye lungafuna uncedo olusuka ngaphandle. Esi sikhokelo sibonelela ngendlela yokuthetha; asisizisi sigqibo ngamandla asemthethweni, kwaye asigqibi ngokuba kuyintoni ukunyamekela okufanelekileyo kumntu omnye.

## Singamgcina kanjani umntu embindini?

Buza umntu onedementia ukuba yintoni ebalulekileyo kuye nokuba ufuna ukubandakanywa njani. Ukufunyanwa kwedementia akususi indawo yakhe kwiincoko ngobomi bakhe bemihla ngemihla. Buza ukuba ufuna bani abandakanywe nokuba yeyiphi inkcazelo ayiboniselayo ukuba yabelwane. Ukuba ufuna uncedo ekuboniseni into ayithandayo, mnike ixesha uze uxoxe ngolunye ukhetho ngexesha.

[Isikhokelo samanyathelo okuqala emva kokufunyanwa kwedementia](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) sineengcamango zokubandakanya umntu ekucwangciseni ikamva. Ukuba lo mntu ukhethe umntu oza kumnceda ngezigqibo, bandakanya lowo kwincoko ngelixa uhlonipha imfihlo yakhe nezinto azifunayo. Ukuba amalungu osapho engavumelani ngamandla asemthethweni okwenza izigqibo, buza ingcali efanelekileyo yasekuhlaleni endaweni yokuqikelela.

## Siqala njani incoko yosapho?

Khetha ixesha elizolileyo ngaphambi kobunzima xa kunokwenzeka. Nigqibe ngesihloko esinye sencoko, njengokuba ngubani onokuthutha okanye indlela yokucwangcisa isicelo esilandelayo sokubona ugqirha. Uluhlu olude lwezigqibo ezinzima lunokwenza kube nzima kubo bonke ukumamela.

Phambi kwencoko, bhala phantsi oko ukubonileyo nento oyicingayo. Umzekelo, “Utata akazange aye kwizicelo ezimbini zokubona ugqirha kule nyanga” yinto ebonwayo ethile. “Utata akasakwazi nto” ngamazwi ajikelezayo anokungabonisi bukho bakhe okanye into ayithandayo.

Nika ilungu ngalinye ithuba lokuchaza into elixhalabele ngayo. Mamela ngaphandle kokuphazamisa, ukusola okanye ukuthelekisa ukuba umntu ngamnye unegalelo elingakanani. Umntu ohlala kude unokungaziboni iimfuno zemihla ngemihla; umntu owenza uninzi lokunyamekela unokudinwa. Zozibini ezi ntlanga zinengqiqo, nokuba zikhokelela kwiimbono ezahlukeneyo.

## Yintoni esimele sixoxe ngayo entlanganisweni?

Gcina incoko isebenzisekile. Uluhlu olufutshane lwezinto ezixoxwayo lunokunceda usapho luphume lunenyathelo elilandelayo elicacileyo:

| Isihloko | Umbuzo wokuxoxwa |
| --- | --- |
| Unqweno lwalo mntu | Yintoni afuna ukuqhubeka ayenza? Ufuna bani abandakanywe? |
| Okwenzekayo ngoku | Yintoni ebonwe ngumntu, kwaye nini? Yintoni efuna ingxelo yegqirha? |
| Imisebenzi nemida | Luthini uncedo olufunekayo? Ngubani onexesha nokukwazi ukwenza umsebenzi ngamnye? |
| Amanyathelo alandelayo | Ngubani oza kwenza ntoni, ngaphambi kwexesha elithile, kwaye usapho luza kuphinda lujonge nini? |

Ukuba umba ufuna ingcebiso yezonyango, bhala phantsi umbuzo uze uwubuze iqela lonyango. [Isikhokelo sokutyelela ugqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinokunceda ekulungiseleleni leyo ncoko. Gcinela ingcali ezaziyo imithetho yasekuhlaleni ukungavumelani ngamaxwebhu asemthethweni, iinzuzo okanye iikontraki.

## Sinokuyabela njani imisebenzi yokunyamekela ngokufanelekileyo?

Ukufaneleka akuhlali kuthetha ukulingana. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukwenza uluhlu lwemisebenzi oluquka ixesha, imali nomgudu ofunekayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts). Emva koko buza ukuba umntu ngamnye uthanda ntoni, kwaye angayenza ntoni ngokwenyani.

Abanye abantu banokunceda ngokutyelela rhoqo okanye ngokunyamekela umntu emzimbeni. Abanye banokuba kwindawo engcono yokulungiselela izicelo zokubona ugqirha, ukuthutha, ukuhambisa ukutya, amaphepha okanye ukubuza rhoqo ngeveki. Isihlobo esihlala kude sisenokulungelelanisa iifowuni, sijonge iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni okanye silungiselele izinto ezifunekayo, ukuba umntu uyavuma. [Isikhokelo sokunyamekela ukude](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) sinesicwangciso esipheleleyo seendima, iindaba nokutyelela.

Beka imisebenzi evunyiweyo kwikhalenda enye ekwabelwanayo okanye kuluhlu olubhaliweyo. Quka ukuba ngubani onoxanduva, rhoqo kangakanani, nokuba ubani ongathethwa naye xa izicwangciso zitshintsha. Phonononga uluhlu ukuba impilo, ixesha lomsebenzi okanye amandla omntu okunyamekela ayatshintsha. [Isikhokelo sokuzinyamekela komnyamekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) siquka iindlela zokucela abanye ukuba babelane ngenkxaso.

## Sinokwenza ntoni ukuba asivumelani nangoku?

Buyela koko kwaziwayo, koko afuna lo mntu nakumbuzo ongekaphendulwa. Unokungazisombululi zonke izinto kwintlanganiso enye. Vumelanani ngenyathelo elinye elikhuselekileyo nelamkelekileyo ngoku, uze usete ixesha lokuphinda ujonge isigqibo esikhulu.

Ukuba izihlobo azivumelani ngokufuduka, yenza inyathelo lokuqala ukuqokelela ulwazi endaweni yokuvota ngo-ewe okanye hayi. Buza ukuba kusilele uncedo olunjani ekhaya, yintoni umntu angayithanda nokuba ngawaphi amanye amathuba usapho ofuna ukuwafunda. Ukutyelela ikhaya lokunyamekela okanye ukuthetha nomnikezeli wenkonzo yasekhaya kunokwazisa incoko ngaphandle kokumnyanzela umntu ukuba afudukele. [Isikhokelo sokunyamekelwa kwindawo yokuhlala](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/) sinemibuzo yokuthelekisa amanye amathuba.

Ukuba ingxwabangxwaba iyaqhubeka, cela umntu wesithathu oyithembileyo ukuba anceda. Ngokuxhomekeke kumba, kunokuba ngugqirha walo mntu, usomashishini wezentlalo, umcebisi, umlamli okanye inkokeli yenkolo. Iqela lenkxaso nalo linokunika izalamane indawo yokuthetha nabantu abajongene neenguqu ezifanayo. Ingcebiso yengcali ibaluleke kakhulu kwimibuzo yezonyango, yezomthetho okanye yeemali.

Ukuba ungquzulwano losapho luchaphazela ukhuseleko okanye ukunyamekelwa komntu, qhagamshelana nenkonzo efanelekileyo yasekuhlaleni ukuze ufumane uncedo. Musa ukumshiya umntu exinene embindini wengxabano. Ukuba kukho ingozi ekhoyo ngoku, sebenzisa iinkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni.

Usapho aluhlali luvumelana, kwaye incoko enye isenokungasisombululi isiganeko. Qhubeka umamela lo mntu, wenze izibophelelo ezicacileyo uze ucele uncedo ngaphambi kokuba ilungu elinye kufuneka lithwale yonke into lodwa.
