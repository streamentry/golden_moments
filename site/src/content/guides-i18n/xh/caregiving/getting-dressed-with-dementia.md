Ukuzinxiba sisinto esiyeyomntu ngamnye. Umntu ophila nesifo sokulibala usenokufumana ukukhetha, iqhosha nezixhobo zokuvala iimpahla, okanye ulandelelwano lwamanyathelo kunzima kunangaphambili. Usenokuba nentandokazi enamandla yeempahla ezaziwayo okanye isimbo esithile. Injongo kukuxhasa uvuyo nonyulo, hayi ukuthatha ulawulo okanye ukwenza ukuba inxibo ibonakale ngendlela ethile.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokunxiba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) kunye [necebiso le-Dementia Australia malunga nokuzinxiba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/getting-dressed) zicebisa ukunika ixesha elaneleyo, ukugcina unyulo lulula kunye nokuxhasa umntu ukwenza into anokuyenza. Eli phepha linika iingcinga eziqhelekileyo. Buza ugqirha malunga neengxaki ezintsha zokunxiba okanye ezinye iinkxalabo zempilo.

## Ndinokunika njani unyulo ngaphandle kokuthatha ulawulo?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukunxiba ntoni. Ukuba kunzima ukukhetha kwikhabhathi egcwele iimpahla, nika ukhetho olubini lweempahla ezaziwayo. Angakhetha phakathi kwebhulawuzi ebomvu nelinye elimhlophe, okanye phakathi kwesweta nekhadigani. Ukhetho lwababini lulapho uqala khona, asisiyo umthetho. Ukuba umntu uthanda ukukhetha kwiimpahla ezininzi, mlandele.

Gcina intandokazi yomntu eqondwa. Imibala ethandwayo, iintlobo zelaphu kunye neempahla ezaziwayo zinokwenza ukukhetha kube lula. Musa ukutshintsha isimbo sabo ngento ucinga ukuba ibukeka ilungile ngcono. Ukungafani kwenxibo akusiso isizathu sokungxabana. Ukuba umntu uzolile yaye iimpahla zifanelana nemozulu, vumela unyulo lwabo lume.

Xa uthenga iimpahla, cinga ngokuzolisa nangento umntu anokuyiphatha. Iintlobo zelaphu elithambileyo, iimilo ezaziwayo kunye nezixhobo zokuvula ezinokuvulwa zinokunceda. Iindawo zokudibanisa ezirhangqayo okanye ezilula zinokuba lula kwabanye abantu, kodwa uyilo olutsha lunokuziva lungaqhelekanga. Buza ngaphambi kokutshintsha iimpahla ezithandwayo okanye umkhuba oqhelekileyo.

## Ziziphi izinyathelo ezincinci ezenza ukunxiba kube lula?

Khetha ixesha xa kukho indawo yokuhamba ngocothe. Ukukhawuleza kunokwenza umsebenzi ube nzima ngakumbi. Indawo ethe cwaka nekhanyisiweyo, enempahla ebonakala kakuhle, inokunceda. Susa ezinye izinto kwindawo apho umntu anxiba khona, kwaye ubeke iimpahla ezikhethiweyo apho anokuzifikelela khona.

Beka iimpahla ngolandelelwano umntu afuna ukuzinxiba ngalo. Nika into enye ngexesha kwaye usebenzise isikhumbuzo esifutshane, njengokuthi “Nantsi ibhulawuzi lakho.” Umyalelo obanzi onjengokuthi “Zinxibe” unokuba nzima ukuwenza. Yima phakathi kwamanyathelo ukuze umntu abe nexesha lokuqonda aze aphendule.

Vumela umntu enze ingxenye nganye anokuyenza kwaye ayifunayo. Ukuba akaqinisekanga ukuba kulandela ntoni, nika isikhumbuzo okanye ubuze ukuba uncedo lunokuba luncedo na. Musa ukumfaka iimpahla ngamandla okanye ukuhambisa umzimba wakhe ngaphandle kwemvume. Ukuba inyathelo elinye liyacaphukisa, yima ubuze ukuba yintoni eyayenza ilula. Ngamanye amaxesha kwanele ukuzama kwakhona kamva.

## Ndikhusela njani ubume kunye nesidima?

Buza ngaphambi kokuhlala egumbini okanye ukunceda ngeempahla. Umntu usenokuzinxiba yedwa ezolile, okanye usenokuthanda umntu okufuphi kodwa engancedi ngezandla. Mlandele kwaso akucelayo. Ukuba uncedo luyafuneka, chaza into ocwangcisa ukuyenza kwaye uhlole ukuba useyazolile xa uqhubeka.

Gcina umntu egqunyiwe njengoko ekhetha ngako xa etshintsha. Vala umnyango okanye iikhethini, kwaye ugwema ukuxoxa ngeenkcukacha zabucala apho abanye abantu bayeva khona. Ukuba ilungu losapho okanye umnakekeli uyancedisa, buza ukuba loo mntu uyamkelekile na. Izifiso zinokutshintsha ukusuka kwenye imini ukuya kwenye, ngoko hlola rhoqo.

Izikhumbuzo zokutshintsha iimpahla zinokuziva zinamahloni. Nika ukhetho olucocekileyo nolaziwayo ngaphandle kokukhalaza okanye ukulungisa phambi kwabantu. Ukuba umntu ufuna ukunxiba inxibo ayithandayo rhoqo, iimpahla ezifanayo okanye ikopi inokunceda ukugcina unyulo lolaziwayo. Musa ukwenza ukutshintsha iimpahla kube sivivinyo senkumbulo okanye sokuzimela.

Isikhokelo [sokuhlamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) sinamanye amacebiso okukhusela ubume xa wenza omnye umsebenzi wonakekelo lomntu ngokwakhe.

## Zeziphi iimpahla nezihlangu eziyolisa?

Ukuzolisa kuxhomekeke kumntu. Iimpahla ezithambileyo, ezilula ukuzinxiba kunye nezaziwayo zinokukhethwa kunenxibo entsha enezixhobo ezininzi zokuvala. Khetha izihlangu umntu azifumanayo zilula kwaye ezinokubamba kakuhle. Iphepha lesiluleko se-[Alzheimer's Association sokunxiba](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/3e31dbc7-baf0-4f18-9c1b-7a8779af3d17/alzheimers-dementia-dressing-ts.pdf) lucebisa ukuhlola ukuba ubude bempahla bufanelekile ukunciphisa umngcipheko wokuwa.

Cinga ngobushushu bemini nangezicwangciso zayo. Umntu usenokuthanda ukunxiba amaleko eempahla, kodwa hlola ukuba uziva eshushu kakhulu okanye ebanda. Xa uphuma ngaphandle, zisa umaleko onokongezwa okanye ususwe. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) icebisa ukukhetha izihlangu ezilula kwaye ucinge ngemozulu. Ezi zinto ziyimibuzo yokuthetha nomntu, zingasiyo umgangatho wokunxiba.

## Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba ukuzinxiba sekuye kwaba nzima ngequbuliso?

Ukuba ukuzinxiba sekutshintshe ngokubonakalayo, buza umntu ukuba yintoni enzima kubo kwaye uthethe nogqirha wabo. I-Dementia Australia iphawula ukuba ukubona, iziphumo zamayeza okanye enye imeko yempilo nako kunokwenza ukunxiba kube nzima ngakumbi. Ugqirha unokunceda ukuhlola izizathu ezinokubakho. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza ngokusekelwe kwinqaku eliqhelekileyo.

Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso, funa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko kwiinkonzo ezikhawulezayo zasekuhlaleni. Isikhokelo se-[NHS sokudideka ngequbuliso](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi oku kufuna ingqalelo ekhawulezayo. Esi silumkiso sijongene nokudideka okungazelelwanga, hayi notshintsho olucothayo kumkhuba wokunxiba.

Qaphela ukuba yiphi inxalenye yomkhuba etshintshileyo: ukukhetha iimpahla, ukuzibona, ukusebenzisa isixhobo sokuvala okanye ukugcina ukulinganisela. Yabelana ngento oyibonileyo nomntu nogqirha wakhe, ukuba bavumile. Ingxaki entsha ngokwayo ayichazi isizathu sayo. Gwema ukucinga ukuba umntu uqinile ntloko okanye akakhathali.

Ukuba unceda ekhaya okanye kwindawo yonakekelo, buza umntu ukuba ufuna izifiso zakhe zabelwane nomnye umncedi na. Isicatshulwa esifutshane malunga neempahla ozithandayo, ulandelelwano abulukhethayo kunye nesikhumbuzo esisebenzayo unokunceda abanye balandele umkhuba ofanayo. Gcina esi sicatshulwa siyimfihlo kwaye usihlaziye xa izifiso zomntu zitshintsha.

Umntu uhlala elawula iimpahla zakhe njengoko efuna ukuba njalo. Nika kuphela uncedo amavumela ngalo, vumela ixesha kwaye uhloniphe unyulo olumzolisayo.
