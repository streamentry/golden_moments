Ukunxiba kuyinto yomntu ngamnye. Umntu ophila nedementia usenokufumana ukhetho, izinto zokutshixa iimpahla okanye ukulandelelana kwamanyathelo kunzima kunangaphambili. Usenokuba nomnqweno oqinileyo weempahla ezimaziyo okanye isimbo esithile. Injongo kukuxhasa intuthuzelo nokukhetha, kungekhona ukuthatha ulawulo okanye ukwenza impahla ibonakale ngendlela ethile.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngokunxiba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) kunye [nengcebiso ye-Dementia Australia ngokunxiba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/getting-dressed) zicebisa ukunika ixesha elaneleyo, ukugcina ukhetho lulula nokuxhasa umntu ukuba enze oko akwaziyo. Eli phepha linika iingcinga ezibanzi. Buza ugqirha ngobunzima obutsha bokunxiba okanye ezinye iingxaki zempilo.

## Ndingamnika kanjani ukhetho ngaphandle kokuthatha ulawulo?

Buza ukuba uthanda ukunxiba ntoni. Ukuba ukukhetha kwiwodrobhu epheleleyo kunzima, mnike ukhetho oluzizimbini lweempahla ezimaziyo. Usenokukhetha phakathi kwehempe eblowu neluhlaza, okanye phakathi kwejezi nekhadigani. Zimbini yindawo yokuqala, ayisosiqwenga somthetho. Ukuba umntu uthanda ukukhetha kwiimpahla ezininzi, landela into akhokelayo.

Gcina incasa yalo mntu eqhelekileyo ukhumbula. Imibala ayithandayo, iintsimbi neempahla ezimaziyo zinokwenza ukukhetha kuve kulula. Musa ukubuyisela isimbo sakhe ngento ocinga ukuba ibukeka ngcono. Impahla engahambelaniyo ayisosizathu sokuxabana. Ukuba umntu uzive kamnandi kwaye iimpahla zifanelana nemozulu, vumela ukhetho lwakhe lume.

Xa uthenga iimpahla, cinga ngentuthuzelo nokuba umntu angakwazi ntoni ukukwenza. Iintsimbi ezithambileyo, iimilo ezimaziyo neentsimbi azikwaziyo ukuvula zisenokunceda. I-elastiki okanye izitshixo ezilula zisenokuba lula kwabanye abantu, kodwa uyilo olutsha lusenokuvakala lungaqhelekanga. Buza ngaphambi kokutshintsha iimpahla azithandayo okanye umkhuba.

## Ngawaphi amanyathelo amancinci enza ukunxiba kube lula?

Khetha ixesha apho kukho indawo yokuhamba ngokucotha. Ukungxama kunokwenza umsebenzi uxinezele ngakumbi. Indawo ethe cwaka, ekhanyiswe kakuhle nezinxibo ezibonakala lula zisenokunceda. Susa ezinye izinto kwindawo apho umntu anxiba khona, ubeke iimpahla ezikhethiweyo ekufutshane.

Beka iimpahla ngokulandelelana awathanda ngalo umntu ukuzinxiba. Nika into enye ngexesha uze usebenzise isikhumbuzo esifutshane, njengo, “Nantsi ihempe yakho.” Umyalelo obanzi onje ngo, “Nxiba,” usenokuba nzima ukuwenza. Yima ngokufutshane phakathi kwamanyathelo ukuze umntu abe nexesha lokuqonda aze aphendule.

Mvumele umntu enze nayiphi na inxalenye ayaziyo kwaye ayifunayo. Ukuba akaqinisekanga ukuba kulandela ntoni, mnike isikhumbuzo okanye ubuze ukuba uncedo luza kuba luncedo. Musa ukutsala iimpahla emzimbeni wakhe okanye ukuhambisa umzimba wakhe ngaphandle kwemvume. Ukuba inyathelo elinye liyamkhathaza, yima ubuze ukuba yintoni eza kulenza lula. Ngamanye amaxesha ukuzama kwakhona kamva kwanele.

## Ndiyikhusela njani imfihlo nesidima?

Buza ngaphambi kokuhlala egumbini okanye ukunceda ngeempahla. Umntu usenokuziva ekhululekile ukunxiba yedwa, okanye usenokufuna umntu ekufutshane ngaphandle koncedo lwezandla. Landela oko akucelayo. Ukuba uncedo luyafuneka, chaza oko ucinga ukukwenza uze uqinisekise ukuba usaziva ekhululekile njengoko usenza.

Gcina umntu egqunyiwe ngokwamandla asethandayo ngelixa etshintsha. Vala ucango okanye iikhethini, uze ungaxoxi ngeenkcukacha zabucala apho abanye abantu bevayo. Ukuba ilungu losapho okanye umsebenzi wonyango uyanceda, buza ukuba lo mntu wamkelekile na. Izinto azithandayo zisenokutshintsha ngosuku nosuku, ngoko jonga rhoqo.

Izikhumbuzo zokutshintsha iimpahla zisenokuvakala zihlazisa. Mnike ukhetho oluhlambulukileyo nolwaziwayo ngaphandle kokukhalimela okanye ukulungisa phambi kwabantu. Ukuba umntu ufuna ukunxiba isimbo asithandayo rhoqo, iimpahla ezifanayo okanye ezinye ezifanayo zisenokunceda ukugcina ukhetho luqhelekile. Musa ukwenza ukutshintsha iimpahla kube ngumvavanyo wenkumbulo okanye wokuzimela.

[Isikhokelo sethu sokuhlamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) sineengcamango ezongezelelekileyo zokukhusela imfihlo ngexesha lomnye umsebenzi wokuzinyamekela.

## Ziziphi iimpahla neembadada ezinokuvakala zinentuthuzelo?

Intuthuzelo ixhomekeke kumntu. Iimpahla ezithambileyo, ezilula ukuzinxiba nezimaziyo zisenokuthandeka kunempahla entsha enezinto ezininzi zokutshixa. Khetha iibhutsi okanye izihlangu ezinentuthuzelo kumntu kwaye ezibambelelayo kakuhle. [Ipheyiphu ye-Alzheimer's Association ngokunxiba](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/3e31dbc7-baf0-4f18-9c1b-7a8779af3d17/alzheimers-dementia-dressing-ts.pdf) icebisa ukujonga ukuba ubude beempahla bufanelekile ukunciphisa ingozi yokwenzakala ngokukhubeka.

Cinga ngobushushu bosuku nezicwangciso. Umntu usenokonwabela ukunxiba iileya ezininzi, kodwa jonga ukuba uziva ebanda kakhulu okanye eshushu kakhulu. Ngexesha elingaphandle, thatha ileya enokongezwa okanye isuswe. [I-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) icebisa ukukhetha izihlangu ezinentuthuzelo nokucinga ngemozulu. Le yimiyalezo yencoko nomntu, kungekhona umbhalo ofanayo wokunxiba okanye umthetho wokunxiba.

## Kuthekani ukuba ukunxiba kuye kwaba nzima ngokutsha?

Ukuba ukunxiba kutshintshe ngokucacileyo, buza umntu ukuba yintoni evakala inzima uze uthethe nogqirha wakhe. I-Dementia Australia ithi umbono, iziphumo zamayeza okanye esinye isifo zisenokwenza ukunxiba kube nzima. Ugqirha unokunceda ukuhlola izizathu ezinokubakho. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza ngokwenqaku elibanzi.

Ukuba umntu udideka ngesiquphe, funa uncedo lwezonyango kwangoko ngeenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. [Isikhokelo se-NHS ngokudideka kwesiquphe](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi oku kufuna ingqwalasela ekhawulezileyo. Esi silumkiso sixa ukudideka kwesiquphe, kungekhona utshintsho olucotha kumkhuba wokunxiba.

Qaphela ukuba yeyiphi inxalenye yomkhuba etshintshileyo: ukukhetha iimpahla, ukuzibona, ukuphatha into yokutshixa okanye ukugcina ibhalansi. Yabelana noko ukubonileyo nalo mntu nogqirha wakhe, ukuba uyavuma. Ubunzima obutsha abuchazi isizathu sabo ngokwabo. Musa ukucinga ukuba umntu unenkani okanye akakhathali.

Ukuba unceda ekhaya okanye kwindawo yokunyamekela, buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukuba izinto azithandayo zabelwane nomnye umncedi na. Inqaku elifutshane ngeempahla azithandayo, ukulandelelana akuthandayo nesikhumbuzo esisebenzayo lunokunceda abanye balandele umkhuba ofanayo. Gcina inqaku lisisithuba sabucala kwaye uliqhubele phambili xa iminqweno yomntu itshintsha.

Umntu usahlala elawula iimpahla zakhe ngokwamandla afuna ukuzilawula ngawo. Nika kuphela uncedo analamkelayo, vumela ixesha uze uhlonipha ukhetho oluvakala lunentuthuzelo kuye.
