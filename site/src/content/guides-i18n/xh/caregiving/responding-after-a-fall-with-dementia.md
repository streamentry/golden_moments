Ukuwa kunokoyikisa umntu nabo nabani abasondeleyo. Hlala uzolile uze ugxile kukhuseleko olukhawulezileyo. Musa ukucinga ukuba umntu akenzakalanga ngenxa yokuba akakwazi ukuchaza oko kwenzekileyo okanye ukuthi buhlungu phi.

## Ndenze ntoni ngoko nangoko emva kokuwa?

Jonga ukuba umntu uvukile, ubonakala enzakele okanye egula, nokuba unokuphendula njengesiqhelo. Musa ukumkhawulezisa ukuba aqonde okanye ukuzama ukumphakamisa wedwa. [I-NHS ithi uncedo lwongxamiseko luyafuneka ukuba umntu usenokuba wenzakele entloko, emqolo, entanyeni okanye entanyeni yomlenze, une-ngozi enye enkulu, okanye akakwazi ukuvuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/).

- **Fonela iinkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni** ukuba enye yezo mpawu zikhulu isenokubakho. Landela imiyalelo yomnxibelelanisi.
- **Gcina umntu ekhululekile ehlaziyiwe** ngelixa ulindele uncedo. Musa ukumhambisa ukuba usola ukwenzakala okukhulu okanye awuqinisekanga ukuba kukhuselekile.
- **Hlala naye ukuba unako.** Sebenzisa amagama amafutshane, athe cwaka. Musa ukumbuza imibuzo ngoko kwenzekileyo okanye ukunyanzelisa ukuba ame.
- **Ukuba umntu unentlungu, wenzakele okanye uyagula kodwa akukho ngozi ikhawulezileyo,** qhagamshelana necebiso lezonyango elikhawulezileyo lasekuhlaleni okanye ingcali yakhe yezempilo ukufumana isikhokelo.

Musa ukuzama ukumphatha okanye ukumphakamisa umntu wedwa. Ukuba kukho icebo lokhathalelo lokuwa, ulilandele kuphela xa lilingana noko kusenzeka ngoku; funa uncedo lwongxamiseko ukuba umntu usenokuba wenzakele kakhulu.

## Kuthekani ukuba umntu ubonakala enokuvuka?

Musa ukumkhawulezisa umntu. Buza ukuba uziva njani nokuba ufuna uncedo. Ukuba ubonakala engenzakalanga kwaye angavuka esebenzisa inkxaso yakhe eqhelekileyo ngaphandle kokuphakanyiswa, nika ixesha uze uhlale usondele. Yeka ukuba uyaziva intlungu, uyajikeleza okanye akakwazi ukuphatha. Ukuba awuqinisekanga, buza ingcali yezempilo endaweni yokuzama ukumhambisa ongabonisiwe ukuba usenza njani.

[I-NHS icebisa ukuthatha ixesha nokuphumla emva kokuvuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Imiyalelo yayo yokuzinceda ingeyomntu oziva enako ukuvuka; asisosizathu sokutsala okanye ukuphatha umntu owileyo. Ukushukuma komntu, ukulinganisela kwakhe neemfuno zempilo ziyahluka.

## Ndiqhagamshelane nogqirha nokuba andiboni kwenzakala?

Ewe. [I-Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukuthetha nogqirha womntu emva kokuwa, nokuba awucingi ukuba wenzakele](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/blog/why-person-dementia-more-likely-have-fall-and-what-can-you-do-help). Ukuba uqaphele ukubetha entloko, intlungu, ukubhideka okutsha, ukuthamba, ukuphelelwa ngamandla okanye utshintsho kwindlela eqhelekileyo yomntu, nika ingxelo xa utsalela. Musa ukusebenzisa uluhlu lwewebhusayithi ukumisela ukuba uvavanyo lwezonyango alufuneki.

Ukuba kwakukho ukwenzakala entloko, landela icebiso lengcali okanye inkonzo yokhathalelo olukhawulezileyo lasekuhlaleni. [Isikhokelo se-NHS sokwenzakala entloko](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) sichaza iimpawu ezifuna ingqwalasela ekhawulezileyo. Ukuba umntu ngequbuliso ubhidekile, ozelwe kakhulu, uhlanza okanye ubonakala egula kakhulu, funa uncedo olukhawulezileyo endaweni yokulinda utyelelo oluqhelekileyo.

## Ulwazi luni olunokunceda ingcali yezempilo?

Emva kokuba iimfuno ezikhawulezileyo ziqwalaselwe, bhala ukuqaphela okumbalwa ukwabelana nako ngemvume yomntu:

- ukuba ukuwa kwenzeka nini nakuphi, ukuba kuyaziwa;
- oko umntu wayekwenza ngaphambi kwako;
- ukuba ukuwa kwabonwa kwaye umntu usenokuba wabetha entloko;
- ntoni intlungu, ukwenzakala okanye utshintsho olubone;
- naluphi na utshintsho lwakutshanje kwimpilo, ekushukumeni, enkqubeni okanye kumayeza olaziyo.

Chaza oko ukubonileyo endaweni yokuqikelela isizathu sokuwa. Inqaku linokuxhasa incoko; alifumanisi isizathu. Ukuba ukwenzakala okukhulu okanye utshintsho olukhawulezileyo lunokwenzeka, fumana uncedo kuqala endaweni yokugqiba irekhodi.

## Ndibuze ntoni ekulandeleleni?

Ugqirha angancedisa ukuthatha isigqibo sokuba umntu ufuna uvavanyo na nokuba kufuneka kujongwe ntoni emva koko. [I-NHS iphawula ukuba uvavanyo lokuwa lunokujonga amandla, ukubona, ukuva, amayeza noguqulo lwekhaya](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Ungabuza:

- Ingaba umntu ufuna uviwo emva kokuwa, nokuba akukho kwenzakala kucacileyo?
- Zeziphi iinguqu okanye iimpawu ekufuneka zikhokelele kuncedo olukhawulezileyo?
- Ingaba ukulinganisela, ukubona, ukuva, intlungu okanye amayeza kufuneka kuphononongwe?
- Ingaba inkonzo yokuwa okanye ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi inganceda ukuvavanya ukushukuma okanye izixhobo zasekhaya?
- Ngubani umntu okanye usapho ekufuneka aqhagamshelane naye ukuba kuwa kwakhona?

Xelela ugqirha oko ukwaziyo nokungakwaziyo. Ukuba ukuwa kwakungabonwanga, yitsho endaweni yokuqikelela ukuba kwenzeke njani. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza, ukuthenga isixhobo sokuhamba okanye ukufaka izixhobo ngokwewebhusayithi kuphela. Inkxaso efanelekileyo ixhomekeke kwimpilo yomntu, ekushukumeni, ekhaya nakwizinto azifunayo.

## Sinokucinga njani ngokuwa kwakhona?

Emva kokuba iinkxalabo zezonyango ezikhawulezileyo ziqwalaselwe, cinga ukuba yintoni eyayisenzeka kwindawo nakwinkqubo. Indlela egcwele izinto, utshintsho lokukhanya okanye indlela engaqhelekanga inokuba nobudlelwane, kodwa musa ukucinga ukuba ingozi enye yabangela ukuwa. Buza umntu ukuba uqaphele ntoni nokuba yintoni eya kwenza indawo ibe lula ukuyisebenzisa. Ukuba umntu akakhumbuli okanye akakwazi ukuchaza ukuwa, yabelana noko ukubonileyo ngaphandle kokumcinezela ngeenkcukacha. Phawula ixesha, indawo, izicathulo, ukukhanya, umsebenzi nawuphi na utshintsho kwindlela eqhelekileyo yokushukuma okanye yokuziphatha. Irekhodi elifutshane lunokunceda ugqirha akhangele imikhwa; alichazi isizathu.

[Isikhokelo sethu sokhuseleko lwekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sigubungela ukuhlolwa igumbi ngegumbi. Ukuba ukuwa kwenzeke ngexesha lokuhlamba, bona [isikhokelo sethu sokuhlamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). Ugqirha, ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi okanye inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yokuwa inokuvavanya iinkxalabo zomntu ngamnye icebise utshintsho olufanelekileyo. Musa ukufaka izixhobo okanye ukutshintsha iyeza elibhaliweyo ngokwelaa phepha kuphela.

Ukuwa akubonisi ngokwako oko umntu anokukwenza okanye angakwenzi. Gcina umntu ebandakanyekile kwizigqibo ngenkxaso, uze ulandele icebiso leqela lakhe lokhathalelo lasekuhlaleni.
