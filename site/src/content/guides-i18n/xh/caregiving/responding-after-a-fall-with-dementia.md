Ukuwa kunokoyikisa umntu nabani na osecaleni. Hlala utuliswe uze ugxile ekhuseleni okukhawulezayo. Ungaqikeleli ukuba umntu akenzakalanga ngenxa yokuba engakwazi ukuchaza into eyenzekileyo okanye ukuthi apha ebuhlungu.

## Yintoni endiyenzayo ngoko nangoko emva kokuwa?

Jonga ukuba umntu uyaphapheka, uvakala ukuba wenzakele okanye akaphilanga, kwaye usenokuphendula njengesiqhelo. Musa ukumkhawulezisa ukuba ame okanye uzame ukumphakamisa ngokwakho. I-[NHS ithi uncedo lwangxamu luyafuneka ukuba umntu usenokuba wenzakalise intloko, umqolo, intamo okanye i-hip, unokwenzakala okunye okunzima, okanye akakwazi ukuvuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/).

- **Biza inkonzo yezimo eziphuthumayo endaweni yakho** ukuba kukho enye yezi mpawu ezinzima. Landela imiyalelo yomthumeli wekholi.
- **Gcina umntu ekhululekile kwaye efudumele** ngelixa elindele uncedo. Musa ukumhambisa ukuba ucinga ukuba kukho ukwenzakala okunzima okanye awuqinisekanga ukuba kukhuselekile na.
- **Hlala naye ukuba unokwenza njalo.** Sebenzisa amagama amafutshane, azolileyo. Musa ukumbuza ngento eyenzekileyo okanye ukunyanzela ukuba eme.
- **Ukuba umntu usenentlungu, unokwenzakala okanye akaphilanga kodwa akukho ngozi ekhoyo ngoko nangoko,** qhagamshelana necebiso lonyango olungxamisekileyo endaweni yakho okanye negcisa lakhe lezempilo ukuze ufumane icebiso.

Musa ukuzama ukuthwala okanye ukuphakamisa umntu wedwa. Ukuba kukho icwangciso yokuwa, yilandele kuphela xa isihambelana noko kwenzekayo ngoku; fumana uncedo lwangxamu ukuba umntu usenokwenzakala kakhulu.

## Yintoni ukuba umntu ebonakala enokuvuka?

Musa ukumkhawulezisa. Buza ukuba uziva njani nokuba ufuna uncedo na. Ukuba ebonakala engenzakalanga kwaye enokuvuka ngokusebenzisa inkxaso yakhe ejwayelekileyo ngaphandle kokuphakanyiswa, mnike ixesha kwaye uhlale ufuphi. Yima ukuba uziva intlungu, uziva ukuba ujikajika okanye akakwazi ukuyilawula. Ukuba awuqinisekanga, buza ingcali yezempilo endaweni yokuzama ukuhambisa umntu ngendlela ongakhange ubonisiwe ngayo.

I-[NHS icebisa ukuthatha ixesha kunye nokuphumla emva kokuvuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Iingcebiso zayo zokuzinceda zezomntu oziva ekwazi ukuvuka; azisiyiyo isizathu sokutsala okanye ukuthwala umntu owayo. Iimfuno zomntu zokuhamba, ukulinganisa kunye nempilo ziyahluka.

## Ndifanele ndibize ugqirha nangona ndingabonanga kwenzakala?

Ewe. I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukuthetha negcisa lakhe lezempilo lokuqala (GP) emva kokuwa, nokuba ungacingi ukuba umntu wenzakele](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/blog/why-person-dementia-more-likely-have-fall-and-what-can-you-do-help). Ukuba uqaphele isibetho entloko, intlungu, ukudideka okutsha, ubuthathaka, ujikajika okanye utshintsho kwindlela eqhelekileyo yomntu, yichaze xa ubiza. Musa ukusebenzisa uluhlu lwewebhusayithi ukugweba ukuba uvavanyo lwezonyango alufuneki.

Ukuba kukho ukwenzakala entloko, landela icebiso lengcali okanye lenkonzo yonyango olungxamisekileyo endaweni yakho. I-[icebiso lokwenzakala entloko le-NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) ichaza iimpawu ezifuna ukunakekelwa ngokukhawuleza. Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso, ulele kakhulu, ugabha okanye ebonakala egula kakhulu, funa uncedo ngoko nangoko endaweni yokulinda ixesha lonyango lwesiqhelo.

## Yintoni engancedisa ingcali yezempilo?

Emva kokuba iimfuno ezikhawulezayo zixoxiwe, bhala iimbono ezimbalwa ukuze uzabelane nazo ngemvume yomntu:

- ixesha kunye nendawo yokuwa, ukuba liyaziwa;
- into umntu ayeyenza ngaphambi kokuwa;
- ukuba ukuwa kwabonwa na nokuba usenokuba uye wabetha intloko;
- yeyiphi intlungu, ukwenzakala okanye utshintsho olubonakeleyo;
- nayiphi na imiba yakutshanje yempilo, ukuhamba, umkhwa okanye amayeza ozaziyo.

Chaza oko ubonileyo endaweni yokuqikelela ukuba kutheni umntu ewile. Inqaku linokuxhasa incoko; alichazi isizathu. Ukuba ukwenzakala okunzima okanye utshintsho olukhawulezayo kunokwenzeka, funa uncedo kuqala endaweni yokugqiba inqaku elibhaliweyo.

## Ndifanele ndibuze ntoni kulandelelwano?

Ingcali yonyango inokunceda igqibe ukuba umntu udinga uvavanyo na nokuba yintoni okufanele ukhangelwe emva koko. I-[NHS iphawula ukuba uvavanyo lokuwa lunokujonga amandla, ukubona, ukuva, amayeza kunye noguqulo lwekhaya](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Ungabuza:

- Umntu udinga uhlolo emva kokuwa, nokuba akukho kwenzakala okubonakalayo na?
- Yeyiphi imitshintshi okanye iimpawu ezifanele zikhuthaze uncedo olungxamisekileyo?
- Ingaba ukulinganisa, ukubona, ukuva, intlungu okanye amayeza kufuneka kuphononongwe na?
- Ingaba inkonzo yokuwa okanye ingcali yezokusebenza inokunceda ekuvavanyeni ukuhamba okanye izixhobo zasekhaya?
- Ngubani umntu okanye usapho okufanele aqhagamshelane naye ukuba kwenzeka enye ingozi yokuwa?

Xelela ingcali oko uyaziyo kunye noko ungazi. Ukuba ukuwa kwayingabonwanga, yixelele njalo endaweni yokuchaza ukuba kwenzeke njani. Musa ukuyeka okanye utshintshe amayeza, uthenge uncedo lokuhamba okanye ufake izixhobo ngokusekelwe kwiphepha lewebhusayithi kuphela. Uncedo olufanelekileyo luxhomekeke kwimpilo yomntu, ekuhambeni kwakhe, ekhaya nakwizifiso zakhe.

## Sinokucinga njani ngokuwa okunye?

Emva kokuba iimfuno zonyango ezikhawulezayo zixoxiwe, cinga into eyayisenzeka kwindawo nakumkhwa. Indlela enengxowankulu, utshintsho lokukhanya okanye indlela engazange ihambe kuyo inokuba nendima, kodwa musa ukuqikelela ukuba into enye yingozi ebangele ukuwa. Buza umntu ukuba uqaphele ntoni nokuba yintoni eya kwenza indawo ibe lula ukuyisebenzisa. Ukuba umntu akakwazi ukukhumbula okanye ukuchaza ukuwa, yabelana ngoko ubonileyo ngaphandle kokumcinezela ngeenkcukacha. Qaphela ixesha, indawo, izihlangu, ukukhanya, umsebenzi kunye nanaliphi na utshintsho kwintshukumo okanye kwindlela yokuziphatha ejwayelekileyo. Inqaku elifutshane linokunceda ingcali ijonge iiphethini; alichazi isizathu.

Isikhokelo sethu [sokhuseleko lwekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sigubungela ukujonga igumbi negumbi. Ukuba ukuwa kwenzeke ngexesha lokuhlamba, jonga [isikhokelo sethu sokuhlamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). Ingcali yonyango, ingcali yezokusebenza okanye inkonzo yasekuhlaleni yokuwa inokuvavanya iingxaki ezithile kwaye income utshintsho olufanelekileyo. Musa ukufaka izixhobo okanye ukutshintsha amayeza anikwe ugqirha ngokusekelwe kweli phepha kuphela.

Ukuwa akubonisi ngokwaso into umntu anokuyenza okanye angayenzi. Mgcine ebandakanyekile kwizigqibo malunga noncedo, kwaye landela icebiso lethimba lakhe lonyango lasekuhlaleni.
