Ukuya esibhedlele kunokuphazamisa imikhuba eqhelekileyo kwaye kunzima ukukuqonda. Umntu ophila nedementia usenokufuna ixesha elingakumbi, inkcazelo noncedo ngemisebenzi yemihla ngemihla ngelixa ekhona. Ukucwangcisa oko uza kwabelana ngako nabasebenzi kunokumxhasa umntu nabantu abamncedayo.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sesibhedlele nokukhutshwa](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/d5afc74a-9d22-4a15-9c30-6315f6eb5f35/alzheimers-dementia-hospitalization-discharge-planning-ts.pdf) kunye [nesikhokelo se-Dementia Australia sokuya esibhedlele](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/going-hospital) zicebisa ukulungiselela ulwazi oluluncedo, izinto ezaziwayo nemibuzo yokulandelela. Imiyalelo yabasebenzi besibhedlele iza kuqala ngaphambi kwesi sikhokelo esibanzi.

## Ndingazilungiselela njani ukuya esibhedlele okucwangcisiweyo?

Ukuba ukuya kucwangcisiwe, buza umntu ukuba uyiqonda njani nokuba ufuna ukwazi ntoni. Mbandakanye ezigqibweni ngokwamandla afuna ngawo. Abanye abantu bakhetha ukuva ngokuya kwabo kwangaphambili; abanye bangaziva bexhalabile kancinane ngencazelo emfutshane nezolileyo ngaphambi nje kwexesha. Landela into ayithandayo umntu xa kunokwenzeka.

Buza isibhedlele ukuba luluphi ulwazi neempahla ekufuneka uzizise. Uluhlu lwamayeza lwangoku, iealerji ukuba ziyaziwa namagama neenombolo zeengcali zempilo eziqhelekileyo zisenokunceda abasebenzi. Zisa iiglasi, izixhobo zokuva, iimpahla ezaziwayo, izinto zokuhlamba nento ethuthuzelayo ukuba isibhedlele siyavuma. Gcina amaxwebhu asemthethweni okanye ezokhathalelo lwangaphambili nomntu kuphela ukuba ayafanelekile kwaye umntu okanye umenzi wesigqibo ogunyaziswe ukhetha ukuzizisa.

Cwangcisa ukuba ngubani onokuhamba nomntu nokuba usapho okanye abancedisi abathenjiweyo baza kwabelana njani ngeendaba. Ukuba omnye umntu akakwazi ukuhlala, cela omnye umntu othenjiweyo ukuba athathe ithuba. Umntu usenokufuna inkampani kwezinye iinxalenye zokutyelela kwaye afune imfihlo ngamanye amaxesha.

## Ziziphi iinkcukacha endimele ndabelane ngazo nabasebenzi besibhedlele?

Yabelana ngeenkcukacha ezinceda abasebenzi ukuba bathethe kwaye banyamekele lo mntu. Ngemvume yalo mntu, bhala inqaku elifutshane eliquka igama alithandayo, indlela athetha ngayo, yintoni emnceda aqonde, nayiphi na imikhuba okanye izinto ezithuthuzelayo ezibalulekileyo. Usenokukhankanya ukuba ubonisa njani iintlungu okanye ukukhathazeka ukuba usenokungazisebenzisi amagama ngayo.

Gcina inqaku lifutshane nelasese. Yabelana nalo namalungu eqela lonyango alidingayo kuphela. Buza abasebenzi ukuba bathanda ukufumana njani olu lwazi nokuba bafuna ukulugcina phi. Musa ukubeka iinkcukacha ezizinzulu kwibhodi ebonakalayo okanye ukwabelana nazo nabatyeleli abangazidingi.

Umntu kufuneka abe yinxalenye yeencoko xa kunokwenzeka. Cela abasebenzi bathethe naye ngqo, basebenzise amagama alula, bamvumele ixesha lokuphendula. Ukuba kufuneka wongeze into umntu anokungayikhumbuli, chaza oko ukuqapheleyo ngaphandle kokuqikelela isizathu.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngexesha lokuya kwigumbi lemfuneko?

Ukuba uhambo alulindelwanga, chaza oko kwenzekileyo, kwaqala nini, nento etshintshileyo kumkhuba oqhelekileyo walo mntu. Abasebenzi basenokubuza umbuzo ofanayo ngaphezu kwesihlandlo esinye. Inqaku elifutshane elibhaliweyo lisenokukunceda unike ingxelo efanayo ecacileyo kubantu abahlukeneyo.

Yazisa abasebenzi ukuba umntu unedementia nokuba yeyiphi inkxaso ehlala inceda. Buza ukuba ngubani oza kuba ngumqhagamshelwana oyintloko weendaba. Ukuba umntu uyalinda, ilizwi elaziwayo, into ethe cwaka yokujonga okanye iiglasi okanye izixhobo zakhe zokuva zisenokunceda, ukuba abasebenzi bathi zikhuselekile ukuzisebenzisa.

Landela imiyalelo yeqela lonyango. Phambi kokunceda ngokutya, isiselo, iyeza, ukuhamba okanye unyamekelo lomntu, jonga nabasebenzi ukuba yintoni ekhuselekileyo ngelo xesha. Uvavanyo okanye unyango lwesibhedlele lunokutshintsha oko umntu anokukwenza okwexeshana.

## Ndimele ndenze ntoni ukuba umntu ubonakala ehlukile ngesiquphe?

Xelela umongikazi okanye ugqirha kwangoko ukuba umntu udideka ngakumbi ngesiquphe okanye enza ngokwahlukileyo kakhulu kwesiqhelo. Musa ukucinga ukuba yidementia kuphela. [Isikhokelo se-NHS ngokudideka kwesiquphe](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi ukudideka kwesiquphe kufuna ingqalelo yezonyango kwangoko.

Yabelana ngexesha utshintsho olwaqala ngalo noko ukuqaphelileyo. Umntu usenokubonisa ukungakhululeki ngotshintsho lwenkangeleko, intshukumo, ukulala okanye ukutya nokuba akakwazi ukukuchaza. Cela iqela lonyango ukuba lihlole utshintsho endaweni yokuzama ukufumana isizathu ngokwakho.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu ngexesha lokuhlala esibhedlele?

Gcina imikhuba eqhelekileyo xa abasebenzi besithi ikhuselekile. Zisa umfanekiso owaziwayo okanye enye into ethuthuzelayo, ncitshisa ingxolo eyongezelelekileyo ukuba kunokwenzeka, uze uchaze inyathelo elinye ngexesha. Mvumele umntu enze oko anokukwenza nokukufunayo. Buza abasebenzi ngaphambi kokuhambisa izixhobo, ukutshintsha igumbi okanye ukunceda ngokutya nonyamekelo lomntu.

Umntu usenokufuna uncedo ekusebenziseni ibhatyi yokubiza, ekufumaneni indlu yangasese okanye ekuqondeni ishedyuli entsha. Xelela abasebenzi ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso ayidingayo rhoqo, ubuze ukuba ucela njani uncedo phakathi kotyelelo. Ukuba umntu ufuna ukuba omnye umntu ahlale naye, buza ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso yotyelelo okanye yobusuku esibhedlele esinokuyivumela.

Ukuhlala esibhedlele kunokubadinisa nabanyamekeli. Cwangcisa omnye umntu othenjiweyo athathe ishifti ukuba umntu uyavuma. Buza umqhagamshelwana wesibhedlele okanye usomashishini wezentlalo ngenkxaso yomntu nosapho ngelixa ukuhlala kuqhubeka.

## Ndimele ndijonge ntoni ngaphambi kokuba umntu aphume?

Cela isicwangciso sokukhutshwa esibhaliweyo. Jonga ukuba sichaza izicelo zokubona ugqirha ezilandelayo, imisebenzi yonyamekelo, naluphi na utshintsho lwamayeza nokuba ubani onokuqhagamshelwa ukuba kuvele ingxaki. Ukuba imiyalelo ayicacanga, cela iqela lokukhutshwa lichaze phambi kokuphuma.

Xoxani ngenkxaso eza kufuneka ekhaya. Cinga ukuba ngubani oza kunceda ngokutya, ukuhamba, ukuhlamba, izicelo zokubona ugqirha nayiphi na impahla entsha okanye iinkonzo zasekhaya. Yiba nenyaniso ngoko usapho nabahlobo abanokukunika. Buza umcwangcisi wokukhutshwa esibhedlele ngezinye iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni ukuba isicwangciso sivakala sinzima ukusiphatha.

Emva kokubuyela ekhaya, vumela ixesha lokuphumla nokuphinda uqhelane nemikhuba eqhelekileyo. Yabelana nesicwangciso esibhaliweyo kuphela nabantu umntu avumileyo ukubabandakanya. Ukuba umntu udideka ngesiquphe okanye egula kakhulu, funa uncedo lwezonyango olukhawulezileyo endaweni yokulinda ukulandelwa okuqhelekileyo.

Ukutyelela okuzolileyo akunakuhlala kunokwenzeka, ngakumbi kwimeko yongxamiseko. Ukulungiselela, unxibelelwano olucacileyo nesicwangciso sokukhutshwa esibhaliweyo zisenokuncedisa wonke umntu ukuba azi ukuba kulandela ntoni.
