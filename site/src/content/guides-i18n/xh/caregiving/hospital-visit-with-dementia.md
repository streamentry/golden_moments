Ukuya esibhedlele kunokuphazamisa imikhwa eyaziwayo kwaye kunzima ukuyiqonda. Umntu ophila nesifo sokulibala usenokufuna ixesha elongezelelweyo, inkcazelo kunye nokuncedwa kwimisebenzi yemihla ngemihla xa esendaweni leyo. Ukucwangciswa kwento oza kuyabelana ngayo nabasebenzi kunokuxhasa umntu nabo bamncedayo.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sesibhedlele nokukhutshwa](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/d5afc74a-9d22-4a15-9c30-6315f6eb5f35/alzheimers-dementia-hospitalization-discharge-planning-ts.pdf) kunye [nesikhokelo se-Dementia Australia sokuya esibhedlele](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/going-hospital) zicebisa ukulungiselela ulwazi oluluncedo, izinto ezaziwayo kunye nemibuzo yokulandelela. Imiyalelo yabasebenzi besibhedlele ibaluleke ngaphezu kwalesi sikhokelo jikelele.

## Ndilungiselela njani ukuya esibhedlele ngokuhlelweyo?

Ukuba ukuya esibhedlele kucwangciswe kwangaphambili, buza umntu ukuba uyaqonda ntoni ngayo nokuba ufuna ukwazi ntoni. Mbandakanye kwizigqibo njengoko efuna ngakumbi. Abanye abantu bakhetha ukuva ngayo kwangaphambili; abanye banokuba nexhala elincinci xa bechazelwe ngokufutshane nangokuzola kufuphi nexesha. Landela ukhetho lomntu xa kunokwenzeka.

Buza esibhedlele ukuba zeziphi iinkcukacha nezinto ekufuneka uzisise. Uluhlu oluhlaziywe lwamayeza, izinto anganyamezelanga nazo ukuba ziyaziwa kunye namagama nenombolo yabasebenzi bezempilo abahlala bebona umntu kunokunceda abasebenzi. Zisa iiglasi, izixhobo zokuva, iimpahla eziqhelekileyo, izinto zokuzicoca kunye nesinto esithuthuzelayo, ukuba isibhedlele siyavumela. Gcina amaxwebhu omthetho okanye amaxwebhu okunakekelwa kwangaphambili kunye nomntu kuphela ukuba ayafaneleka kwaye umntu okanye umntu onegunya lokwenza izigqibo ukhetha ukuwazisa.

Cwangcisa ukuba ngubani onokuhamba nomntu kunye nendlela iintsapho okanye abancedi ababethembekileyo abaza kwabelana ngayo ngezibhengezo. Ukuba omnye umntu akakwazi ukuhlala, cela omnye umntu wakho othembekileyo ukuba athathe ithuba. Umntu usenokufuna ukuba nomntu ecaleni kwexesha elinye lokuhlala esibhedlele kwaye afune ubumfihlo kwelinye ixesha.

## Zeziphi iinkcukacha ekufuneka ndizabelane nazo nabasebenzi besibhedlele?

Yabelana ngeenkcukacha ezinceda abasebenzi bathethe nalo mntu kwaye bamnakekele. Ngemvume yomntu, bhala isicatshulwa esifutshane esiquka igama abalithandayo, indlela abathetha ngayo, into ebancedayo baqonde, kunye nemikhwa okanye izinto zokuzola ezibalulekileyo. Ungakhankanya nendlela abakhombisa ngayo intlungu okanye uxinzelelo ukuba akasebenzisi amazwi ukuyichaza.

Gcina esi sicatshulwa sifutshane kwaye sisimfihlo. Yabelane ngaso kuphela nabalungu beqela lonakekelo abasifunayo. Buza abasebenzi ukuba bakhetha ukuwufumana njani lo lwazi kunye nokuba bafuna ugcinwe phi. Musa ukubeka iinkcukacha ezibucayi kwibhodi yoluntu okanye ukuzabelana nazo nabatyeleli abangazifuniyo.

Umntu kufuneka abe yinxalenye yezincoko xa kunokwenzeka. Cela abasebenzi bathethe naye ngokuthe ngqo, basebenzise amazwi alula kwaye bamnike ixesha lokuphendula. Ukuba kufuneka wongeze into umntu anokungayikhumbuli, chaza oko ukubonileyo ngaphandle kokuqikelela isizathu.

## Ndingancedisa njani ngexesha lokuya kwigumbi longxamiseko?

Ukuba uhambo luza ngequbuliso, chaza into eyenzekileyo, ukuba yaqala nini kunye nento etshintshileyo kwindlela umntu eqhelekile ngayo. Abasebenzi banokubuza umbuzo ofanayo ngaphezu kwekanye. Isicatshulwa esifutshane sinokukunceda unike ingxelo efanayo ecacileyo kubantu abahlukeneyo.

Yazisa abasebenzi ukuba umntu unesifo sokulibala kunye nendlela yoncedo elisoloko linceda ngayo. Buza ukuba ngubani oza kuba lunxibelelwano oluphambili lwezibhengezo. Ukuba umntu uyalinde, izwi elaziwayo, into ethile ethulileyo ukuba ayijonge okanye iiglasi zakhe okanye izixhobo zokuva zinokunceda, xa abasebenzi besithi ezi zinto zikhuselekile ukusetyenziswa.

Landela imiyalelo yeqela lezonyango. Phambi kokunceda ngokutya, isiselo, iyeza, ukuhamba okanye unakekelo lomntu ngokwakhe, hlola nabasebenzi malunga nento ekhuselekileyo ngelo xesha. Uvavanyo okanye unyango esibhedlele lunokutshintsha into umntu anokuyenza ixesha elithile.

## Ndenze ntoni ukuba umntu ebonakala ephuma kwesinye isimo ngequbuliso?

Xelela umongikazi okanye ugqirha ngoko nangoko ukuba umntu udideka ngakumbi ngequbuliso okanye uziphatha ngendlela eyahluka kakhulu kwendlela eqhelekileyo. Musa ukucinga ukuba yisifo sokulibala kuphela. [Isikhokelo se-NHS sokudideka ngequbuliso](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi ukudideka okungazelelwanga kufuna ingqalelo yonyango ngoko nangoko.

Yabelana ngexesha utshintsho oluqale ngalo kunye nento oyiqwalaselileyo. Umntu usenokubonisa ukungonwabi ngotshintsho lwebuso, ukuhamba, ukulala okanye ukutya, nokuba akakwazi ukuyichaza. Cela iqela lonakekelo luvavanye utshintsho kunokuba uzame ukulichaza ngokwakho.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu ngexesha lokuhlala esibhedlele?

Gcina imikhwa eyaziwayo xa abasebenzi besithi ikhuselekile. Zisa ifoto eyaziwayo okanye enye into ethuthuzelayo, ncitsha ingxolo eyongezelelweyo xa kunokwenzeka, kwaye chaza inyathelo elinye ngexesha. Vumela umntu enze into anokuyenza kwaye ayifunayo. Buza abasebenzi ngaphambi kokuhambisa izixhobo, ukutshintsha igumbi okanye ukunceda ngokutya nonakekelo lomntu ngokwakhe.

Umntu usenokufuna uncedo lokusebenzisa iqhosha lokubiza, lokufumana igumbi lokuhlambela okanye lokuqonda ishedyuli entsha. Xelela abasebenzi ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso adla ngokuyifuna, kwaye buza indlela yokucela uncedo phakathi kokundwendwela. Ukuba umntu ufuna umntu ahlale naye, buza ukuba isibhedlele siyavumela ntoni ngokundwendwela okanye ngokuhlala ebusuku.

Ukuhlala esibhedlele kunokudinisa nabanakekeli. Lungiselela omnye umntu wakho othembekileyo ukuba athathe ixesha xa umntu evumile. Buza unxibelelwano lwesibhedlele okanye ingcali yezentlalo malunga noncedo lomntu nosapho ngelixa ukuhlala kuqhubeka.

## Ndifanele ndihlole ntoni ngaphambi kokuba umntu ahambe?

Cela isicwangciso sokukhutshwa esibhaliweyo. Qinisekisa ukuba sichaza amaxesha okulandelela, imisebenzi yonakekelo, nayiphi na itshintsho leeyeza kunye nomntu okufuneka uqhagamshelane naye ukuba kuvela ingxaki. Ukuba imiyalelo ayicacanga, cela iqela lokukhupha liyichaze ngaphambi kokuhamba.

Xoxa ngento yoncedo oza kufuneka ekhaya. Cinga ngokuba ngubani oza kunceda ngokutya, ukuhamba, ukuhlamba, amaxesha okubonwa ngugqirha kunye nezixhobo ezintsha okanye iinkonzo zasekhaya. Yiba nyani malunga nento iintsapho nabahlobo abanokuyinika. Buza umcebisi wokukhutshwa esibhedlele malunga nezinye iinkonzo zasekuhlaleni ukuba isicwangciso sibonakala sinzima ukusilawula.

Emva kokubuya ekhaya, vumela ixesha lokuphumla kunye nokubuyela kwimikhwa eyaziwayo. Yabelana ngesicwangciso esibhaliweyo kuphela nabantu umntu avumelene nokubandakanya. Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso okanye agule kakhulu, funa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko kunokuba ulinde ukulandelelwa okuqhelekileyo.

Uhambo oluzolileyo akusoloko lunokwenzeka, ngakumbi kwingxaki eyongxamisekileyo. Ukulungiselela, unxibelelwano olucacileyo kunye nesicwangciso sokukhutshwa esibhaliweyo kusenokunceda bonke bazi into eza kwenzeka emva koko.
