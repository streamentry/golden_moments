Isityholo sokuba uthathe ingxowa, ususe ipaseki okanye watshintsha iinkcukacha zebhanki kunokukhathaza. Umntu onesifo sokulibala usenokulahla into, akhumbule ukuba uyibeke phi okanye angaqondi kakuhle okwenzekileyo. Kodwa isifo sokulibala asiboneki ngokuqinisekileyo ukuba isityholo asinyaniso. Qala ngokumamela nangokujonga iinyani.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association malunga nokukrokrela nokukholelwa kwizinto ezingekhoyo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/suspicions-delusions) sicebisa ukuhlala uzolile, unike impendulo emfutshane kwaye ungaphikisani. I-[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) icebisa nokuba ujonge inkxalabo endaweni yokuyeka ngenxa yokuba umntu unesifo sokulibala. Eli phepha linikezela ngeengcebiso zonxibelelwano ngokubanzi, kungekhona indlela yokuchonga ukuba isityholo esithile sinyaniso na. Ukuze ufumane inkcazelo eyahlukileyo malunga nokubona okanye ukuva izinto abanye abangazibonyo, bona [isikhokelo sethu sokubona izinto ezingekhoyo](/caregiving/hallucinations-with-dementia/).

## Yintoni endinokuyithetha xa umntu enokrokrela?

Phendula kwinkxalabo ngaphandle kokuvuma into ongazi ukuba iyinyani. Impendulo emfutshane nezinzileyo inokugcina incoko ivulekile. Umzekelo: “Uxhalabile ngokuba ingxowa yakho ilahlekile. Masikhangele kunye.” Emva koko yima kwaye uqaphele indlela umntu aphendula ngayo.

- **Mamela ngaphambi kokucacisa.** Buza ukuba yintoni engekhoyo okanye yintoni eyenze umntu akhathazeke. Usenokuba udanile ngaphezu kwento yokuba into ilahlekile.
- **Gcina impendulo yakho imfutshane.** Impendulo ende inokongeza iinkcukacha ezinzima ukuba zilandelwe kwaye inokwenza nina bobabini nicaphuke ngakumbi.
- **Gwema ukuphikisana okanye ukubuza imibuzo.** Musa ukufuna ukuba umntu akhumbule apho abeke khona into okanye ukubonisa ukuba akukhange uyithabathe.
- **Nika uncedo olusebenzayo.** Ukuba umntu uyafuna, jonganini kwiindawo eziqhelekileyo kunye okanye ubuze ukuba ufuna omnye umntu ukuba anceda.
- **Yima xa ukudana kunyuka.** Nika umntu indawo kwaye ubuyele kwinkxalabo kamva ukuba kukhuselekile ukwenza oko.

Isityholo sinokuziva sisibhengezo somntu. Zama ukwahlula uloyiko lomntu kwinto eyaziwayo ngelo nqanaba. Akufuneki wamkele ityala lento ongayenzanga, kwaye akufuneki unqande yonke impikiswano ngaloo mzuzu.

## Ingaba inkxalabo ingangento yokwenene elahlekileyo?

Ewe. Okokuqala jonga ukuba into isilahlekile ngokwenene na. Umntu usenokuba wayibeka kwindawo engaqhelekanga, okanye kukho umntu owayihambisa okanye into ibe ithathiwe. Musa ukucinywa ingxelo yokuba into ithathiwe, yokungakhathali okanye yokuxhatshazwa ngenxa yokuba umntu unesifo sokulibala.

Ngento elahlekileyo ebekwe ngendlela engaqhelekanga, jonganini kunye ukuba umntu uyafuna. Khangela kwiindawo azigcina kuzo ngokuqhelekileyo kwaye uhlaziye umjikelo wakutshanje. Ukuba into ibaluleke kakhulu, ibalulekile kokhuseleko okanye isalahlekile, xelela umntu ongaphezulu kwaye ubandakanye ingcali ethembekileyo okanye elinye ilungu losapho.

Ukuba imali okanye imisebenzi yasekhaya yabelwana, sebenzisa iirekhodi ezicacileyo eziyamkelekileyo komntu. Gcina iirisithi okanye amanqaku oshishino olwavunyelwaniweyo kwaye uchaze ngamagama alula. Buza ingcali yezemali okanye yomthetho yasekuhlaleni malunga nezivumelwano zemali okanye uxanduva lomthetho; esi sikhokelo asinakuvavanya ezo ngxaki.

Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba inkxalabo ichanile na, cela umntu ongathathi cala ukuba akuncede ukujonga. Khetha umntu omthembayo kuye, kwaye yabelana kuphela ngolwazi oluyimfuneko ukuqonda oko kwenzekileyo.

## Iindlela eziqhelekileyo zinokunceda njani kwizinto ezilahlekileyo?

Nika izinto ezilahleka rhoqo indawo eyaziwayo. Isitya sezitshixo, ithreyi yesipaseki okanye ibhokisi elinelebhile linokwenza kube lula ukufumana into kamva. I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukusebenzisa indawo eqhelekileyo yezinto](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) ezinje ngezitshixo okanye izibonakaliso. Buza umntu ukuba yeyiphi indawo eyenza ingqiqo kuye.

Tshintsha into enye ngexesha. Ukuhambisa izinto ngaphandle kokuchaza kunokwenza umntu angaqinisekanga ukuba izinto zikuphi. Ukuba into engabizi mali ihlala ilahleka, isipele sinokunceda kwezinye iikhaya, kodwa akufuneki sithathe indawo yokujonga ukuba oyiginali ithathiwe okanye ilahlekile.

Qinisekisa ukuba umntu unokubona nokuva ngokonwabileyo ngokunokwenzeka. I-Dementia Australia iqaphele ukuba ukungaboni okanye ukungaviwa kakuhle, ukugula, iimpembelelo zeeyeza, utshintsho lomjikelo okanye indawo egcwele ukuba kunokuba negalelo kutshintsho lokuqonda. Buza umntu ukuba indawo igcwele ingxolo okanye ayaziwa, kwaye ujonge ukuba unazo iiglasi okanye izixhobo zokuva ukuba uyazisebenzisa.

## Ndifanele ndicele uncedo lwezonyango okanye lwangaphandle nini?

Xelela ingcali yomntu ukuba ukrokrela kutsha, kuya kwanda okanye kubangela ukungonwabi. Qaphela ukuba kwenzeka nini, ukuba kwakukho ntoni ngelo xesha kunye nokuba kukho utshintsho kwimpilo yomntu, kwimigaqo, kwindawo okanye kwiiyeza. Musa ukuqala, ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza ngokusekelwe kwinqaku eliqhelekileyo.

Ukudideka okungazelelwanga okanye utshintsho olukhawulezayo kwindlela eziphatha ngayo okanye kwimpilo kufuna unyango lwexesha elingxamisekileyo. Isikhokelo se-[NHS malunga nokudideka okungazelelwanga](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi kufuneka ufumane uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko. Sebenzisa inkqubo yexesha lengxaki okanye yonyango olungxamisekileyo apho umntu ahlala khona.

Ukuba isityholo sinokubandakanya ukuntshontshwa, ukungakhathali okanye ukuxhatshazwa, qinisekisa ukuba umntu ukhuselekile kwaye ufune icebiso kwingcali ethembekileyo yasekuhlaleni okanye kwinkonzo yokhuseleko yabantu abadala. Ukuba kukho umntu ongozini ngoku, qhagamshelana neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. Musa ukuphanda inkxalabo enzima wedwa ukuba oko kunokubeka wena okanye umntu engozini.

## Ndingazinakanga njani?

Izityholo eziphindaphindekayo zinokuxinezela ubudlelwane. Thetha nomntu omthembileyo okanye nomcebisi, kwaye cela uncedo kwimisebenzi yokunakekela ukuba uziva ucinezelekile. [Isikhokelo sokungavumelani kwentsapho](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) sicebisa iindlela zokwabelana ngoxanduva, kwaye [isikhokelo sokuzinakekela](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sigubungela ukuphumla okufutshane kunye nenkxaso.

Gcina ugxininiso kwizinto ezinokuhlolwa, kwimvakalelo yomntu kunye neenkxaso ezifunekayo. Akufuneki ubonise ukunakekela kwakho kwincoko nganye. Ukuba inkxalabo ibuya rhoqo, cela iqela lonakekelo luncedise ekufumaneni indlela ehlonipha umntu kwaye ikhusele wonke umntu okhethekileyo.
