Isityholo sokuba uthathe isingxowa semali, wafudusa isingxowa sesandla okanye watshintsha iinkcukacha zebhanki sinokukhathaza. Umntu one-dementia usenokubeka into kwindawo engeyiyo, alibale ukuyifudusa okanye angaqondi kakuhle okwenzekileyo. Kodwa i-dementia ayibonisi ukuba isityholo asisiso inyani. Qala ngokumamela nokukhangela iinkcukacha.

I-[Alzheimer's Association guide to suspicion and delusions](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/suspicions-delusions) incoma ukuhlala uzolile, unike impendulo emfutshane kwaye ungaphikisani. I-[Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) icebisa nokujonga into ekhathazayo kunokuyiphoxa ngenxa yokuba umntu one-dementia. Eli phepha linika izimvo ngokubanzi zonxibelelwano, aliyondlela yokuchonga ukuba isityholo esithile sinyani na. Ukufumana isikhokelo esahlukileyo ngokubona okanye ukuva izinto abanye abangazivayo, jonga i-[guide to hallucinations and dementia](/caregiving/hallucinations-with-dementia/) yethu.

## Ndingathetha ntoni xa umntu ekrokrela?

Phendula kwinto ekhathazayo ngaphandle kokuvuma into ongayaziyo ukuba inyani. Impendulo emfutshane, ezinzileyo isenokunceda ukugcina incoko ivulekile. Ngokomzekelo: “Uyakhathazeka ukuba isingxowa semali yakho ilahlekile. Masiyikhangele kunye.” Emva koko nqumama uqaphele indlela umntu aphendula ngayo.

- **Mamela phambi kokuchaza.** Buza ukuba yintoni elahlekileyo okanye yintoni emkhathazileyo umntu. Usenokuba nentlungu ngaphezu kwento ngokwayo.
- **Gcina impendulo yakho ifutshane.** Ukuzikhusela okude kunokongeza iinkcukacha ezinzima ukuzilandela kwaye kwenze nobabini nikhathazeke ngakumbi.
- **Phepha ukuphikisana okanye ukubuza imibuzo yovavanyo.** Musa ukunyanzela ukuba umntu akhumbule apho wabeke khona into okanye angqine ukuba awuyithathanga.
- **Nika uncedo olusebenzayo.** Ukuba umntu ufuna, khangelani kunye kwiindawo eziqhelekileyo okanye ubuze ukuba angathanda ukuba omnye umntu anceda.
- **Yeka ukuba ukukhathazeka kuyanda.** Nika umntu indawo uze ubuyele kwinto ekhathazayo kamva ukuba kukhuselekile ukwenza njalo.

Isityholo sisenokuvakala sibobuqu. Zama ukwahlula uloyiko lomntu koko kwaziwayo ngesiganeko. Akunyanzelekanga ukuba wamkele ityala lento ongayenzanga, kwaye akufuneki usombulule yonke ingxabano ngelo xesha.

## Ngaba into ekhathazayo isenokuba kukulahleka kwento yokwenyani?

Ewe. Qala ukhangele ukuba into ilahlekile ngokwenene na. Umntu usenokuba uyibeke kwindawo engaqhelekanga, omnye umntu usenokuba uyifudusile okanye into isenokuba ithathiwe. Musa ukuyiphoxa ingxelo yobusela, yokungakhathali okanye yokuphathwa kakubi ngenxa yokuba umntu one-dementia.

Kwinto ebekwe kwindawo engeyiyo, khangelani kunye ukuba umntu uyafuna. Khangela iindawo ayigcina kuzo rhoqo uphinde ulandele umkhwa wakutshanje. Ukuba into ixabisekile, ibalulekile ekhuselekeni okanye ihleli ilahlekile, xelela umntu oko ukwaziyo kwaye ubandakanye ingcali ethembekileyo okanye elinye ilungu losapho.

Ukuba imali okanye imisebenzi yekhaya yabelwana ngayo, sebenzisa amarekhodi acacileyo avunywa ngumntu. Gcina iirisithi okanye amanqaku eentengiselwano ezivunyiweyo kwaye uzichaze ngamazwi alula. Buza ingcali efanelekileyo yasekuhlaleni ngolungiselelo lwemali okanye uxanduva lwasemthethweni; esi sikhokelo asinakuhlola ezo ngongoma.

Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba into ekhathazayo ichanekile, cela umntu ongathathi cala akunceda ukukhangela. Khetha umntu umntu amthembileyo kwaye wabelane kuphela ngenkcukacha ezifunekayo ukuqonda okwenzekileyo.

## Imikhwa eqhelekileyo inganceda njani ngezinto ezilahlekileyo?

Nika izinto ezidla ngokuhlala zilahleka indawo eqhelekileyo. Isitya seziphatho, itreyi yesingxowa semali okanye idrowa enelebhile isenokwenza kube lula ukufumana into kamva. I-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukusebenzisa indawo ehlala ifanayo yezinto](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) ezifana neziphatho okanye iiglasi. Buza umntu ukuba yeyiphi indawo evakala ifanelekile kuye.

Zama utshintsho olunye ngexesha. Ukufudusa izinto ngaphandle kokuchaza kunokwenza umntu angaqinisekanga ngokuthe ngqo ukuba zikuphi izinto. Ukuba into engabizi kakhulu ihlala ilahleka, enye eyongezelelweyo isenokunceda kwamanye amakhaya, kodwa akufuneki ithathe indawo yokukhangela ukuba eyokuqala ithathiwe okanye ibekwe kwindawo engeyiyo.

Qinisekisa ukuba umntu unokubona nokuva ngendlela ethuthuzelekileyo kangangoko kunokwenzeka. I-Dementia Australia iyaqaphela ukuba iingxaki zokubona okanye zokuva, ukugula, iziphumo zamayeza, umkhwa otshintshileyo okanye indawo ebuthathaka inokunegalelo kutshintsho lwendlela umntu abona ngayo izinto. Buza umntu ukuba igumbi livakala linengxolo okanye lingaqhelekanga na, kwaye ujonge ukuba unazo iiglasi okanye izixhobo zokuviwa ukuba uyazisebenzisa.

## Ndifanele ndicele nini uncedo lwezonyango okanye lwangaphandle?

Xelela ugqirha womntu ukuba ukukrokrela kutsha, kuyanda okanye kubangela ukukhathazeka. Bhala xa kwenzeka, kwakusenzeka ntoni nokuba kukho into etshintshileyo kwimpilo yomntu, kumkhwa, kwindawo ekungqongileyo okanye kumayeza. Musa ukuqala, ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ngenxa yomqulu jikelele.

Ukudideka kwangequbuliso okanye utshintsho olungequbuliso lokuziphatha okanye lwempilo kufuna ukhathalelo lwezonyango olungxamisekileyo. I-[NHS guidance on sudden confusion](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) ithi funa uncedo lwezonyango kwangoko. Sebenzisa inkqubo yongxamiseko okanye yokhathalelo olungxamisekileyo apho umntu ehlala khona.

Ukuba isityholo sinokubandakanya ubusela, ukungakhathali okanye ukuphathwa kakubi, qinisekisa ukuba umntu ukhuselekile kwaye ufune icebiso kwingcali ethembekileyo yasekuhlaleni okanye kwinkonzo yokukhusela abantu abadala. Ukuba nabani na usengozini ekhoyo ngoku, nxibelelana neenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. Musa ukuphanda wedwa inkxalabo enkulu ukuba oko kunokukubeka wena okanye umntu engozini.

## Ndingazinakekela njani?

Izityholo eziphindaphindekayo zingaxinzelela ubudlelwane. Thetha nomntu omthembileyo okanye umcebisi, kwaye ucele uncedo kwimisebenzi yokhathalelo ukuba uziva uxinezelekile. I-[family disagreement guide](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) icebisa iindlela zokwabelana ngoxanduva, kwaye i-[caregiver self-care guide](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ithetha ngokuphumla nenkxaso.

Hlala ugxile koko kunokukhangelwa, indlela umntu avakalelwa ngayo nenkxaso efunekayo. Akunyanzelekanga ukuba ubonise ukhathalelo lwakho kuyo yonke incoko. Ukuba into ekhathazayo iyabuya, cela iqela lokhathalelo likunceda ufumane indlela ehlonipha umntu kwaye ikhusela wonke umntu obandakanyekayo.
