Ukubona, ukuva, ukunuka, ukungcamla okanye ukuziva into abanye abayiboni kunokuba yinyani kumntu. Inokoyikisa, okanye ingangamkhathazi. Qala ngokuba umntu uziva njani nokuba ukho na ozingozini, kunokuba uzame ukubonisa ukuba into ethile yenzekile okanye ayizange yenzeke.

Into eyenzekileyo ingaba ngumbono ongekho, kodwa ingaba nangumbono ongachanekanga okanye ukuchonga okungachanekanga. Isithunzi sinokubonakala njengomntu; umntu owaziwayo unokuphoswa njengomnye umntu. Akufuneki ukuba ugqibe ukuba yeyiphi enzekayo. Chaza into umntu athetha ngayo kunye nento oyiqwalaselayo, kwaye ubuze ingcali yonyango ukuba uxhalabile.

## Ndenze ntoni ngelo xesha?

Hlala uzolile, buza ukuba umntu uziva njani, kwaye umnike isiqinisekiso ngaphandle kokuphikisana. I-[Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuhlola ukuba into eyenzekileyo ibangela intlungu okanye ingozi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/hallucinations). Ukuba umntu uzolile kwaye ukhuselekile, mhlawumbi akukho mfuneko yokumphazamisa.

- **Vuma imvakalelo.** Ungathi, “Andiboni mntu, kodwa ndiyaqonda ukuba oku kwesabisa. Ndikhona.” Oku kuthetha inyani kwaye kumazisa umntu ukuba umphatha ngembeko.
- **Gcina amazwi akho emfutshane.** Inkcazelo ende okanye ukulungisa okuphindaphindiweyo kunokunyusa uxinzelelo. Vumela umntu achaze into ayiqwalaselayo ukuba ufuna ukuyenza loo nto.
- **Nika utshintsho, ungaphoqi.** Buza ukuba bafuna ukuhlala kwenye indawo, bakhanyise elinye ilambu okanye bamamele ingoma eyaziwayo. Ukuya kwigumbi elikhanyisiweyo nelaziwayo kunokunceda; yeka ukuba umntu akafuni ukuhamba.
- **Hlala eduze ukuba bayafuna ukuba ukho.** Musa ukumthinta umntu ngequbuliso. Buza ngaphambi kokunika isandla okanye ukumanga.

Ukuba into eyenzekileyo ayinamngcipheko kwaye ayingamkhathazi, mlandele umntu. Musa ukwenza lo mzuzu ube sivivinyo okanye ukumcela ukuba abonise into ayiboneyo.

Abanye abantu bafumana into eyenzekileyo iyanelisa okanye iyaziwa. Ukuba umntu uzolile kwaye ukhuselekile, mhlawumbi akukho mfuneko yokutshintsha umxholo okanye ukumsa kwenye indawo. Buza ukuba bafuna ntoni; musa ukwenza yonke loo mizuzu ibe yingxoxo yezonyango. Ukuba esoyika, phendula kule mvakalelo nangona ungakwazi ukubona into efanayo.

## Ingaba into egumbini ingaphoswa ibe ngumntu okanye isandi na?

Hlola indawo kunye nomntu ukuba uyamkela loo ncedo. I-[Alzheimer's Society yahlula imibono engekho kwiimbono ezingachanekanga](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations), apho into yokwenyani okanye iphethini ibonwa ngendlela engachanekanga.

- **Phucula ukukhanya.** Vula ilambu kwaye ujonge izithunzi okanye ukuqaqamba okuvela kwizibuko nakumafestile.
- **Hlola izandi eziqhelekileyo.** Umabonakude, ifenitha okanye isixhobo sasekhaya sinokuvakala njengelizwi okanye iinyathelo. Yehlisa okanye uyicime kuphela ukuba umntu uyavuma koko.
- **Jonga iiphethini.** Ikhaphethi, ikhethini okanye iphepha lodonga elinemibala neemilo eninzi linokuphoswa njengokuhamba okanye njengento. Buza ukuba umntu ufuna ukujonga ukusuka kwenye indawo okanye ukutshintsha umbono.
- **Hlola izixhobo zokubona nokuva.** Ukuba umntu usebenzisa iiglasi okanye isixhobo sokuva, buza ukuba bafuna uncedo lokuhlola ukuba sikhona kwaye sisebenza njengesiqhelo.

Ukuba into eyenzekileyo yenzeka kwindawo efanayo, khangela into ephindaphindekayo: ukuqaqamba kwiglasi, iphethini enamandla phantsi, umabonakude oveliswa kwenye igumbi okanye ukukhanya okuphantsi ngexesha lokuhlwa. Hlola isandi sokwenyani okanye into yokwenyani phambi kokuyiphatha njengotshintsho lwembono. Ukuba uzama utshintsho lwendawo, lichaze kwaye ubuze ukuba lulungile na.

Zama utshintsho olunye oluncinci ngexesha. Musa ukuhlengahlengisa izinto zomntu okanye ukususa into abayixabisayo ngaphandle kokubuza. Umkhuba oqhelekileyo kunye nendawo ethe cwaka, ekhanyisiweyo inokuziva ilula ngakumbi.

## Ndifanele ndiqhagamshelane negqirha nini?

Qhagamshelana negqirha womntu malunga neemvakalelo ezintsha, eziphindaphindekayo, ezihlala ixesha elide okanye ezikhathazayo. Utshintsho lokubona okanye lokuva, ukugula, iiyeza nezinye izinto zinokuba negalelo. Ingcali yonyango inokuvavanya okunokuba kwenzeka nokuba kukho ukulandelelwa okufunekayo. Isikhokelo se-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) sicebisa nokuhlola izinto zomzimba, ezokuva nokubona, iiyeza kunye nendawo ezungezileyo.

Utshintsho olungazelelwanga yinto eyahlukileyo. I-[NHS ithi ukudideka ngequbuliso kufuna uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), kwaye i-[Alzheimer's Society icebisa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko xa imibono ibandakanya iimvakalelo ezimbini okanye ngaphezulu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations). Qhagamshelana neenkonzo zonyango ezikhawulezayo zasekuhlaleni ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso okanye umbono ubandakanya iimvakalelo ezimbini okanye ngaphezulu. Ukuba ukho umntu ozingozini ngoko nangoko, sebenzisa iinkonzo ezikhawulezayo zasekuhlaleni.

Musa ukuqala, ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza ngokwakho. Buza ogunyazisa iyeza okanye umfarmasi ukuba ahlole utshintsho lwakutshanje kunye neeyeza zonke umntu azisebenzisayo, kubandakanya nezo zithengwa ngaphandle kwencwadi yokuthenga. Isikhokelo [sokuya kugqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinokunceda ulungiselele imibuzo.

## Zeziphi iinkcukacha ezinokunceda kwixesha lokubonwa ngugqirha?

Ukuba umntu uvumile, bhala phantsi izinto ezifutshane oziqwalaselayo ukuze uzabelane neqela lonakekelo:

- oko bathe babona, bava okanye baziva, besebenzisa amazwi abo xa kunokwenzeka;
- nini nakweyiphi indawo kwenzeka, kunye nokuba yahlala ixesha elingakanani;
- ukuba umntu ebonakala esoyika, ezolile okanye engozini;
- indawo yayinjani, kubandakanya ukukhanya, izithunzi, izandi kunye nabantu ababekufuphi;
- nasiphi na isifo esitsha, utshintsho lwemvakalelo okanye utshintsho lweyeza olwaziwayo.

Sebenzisa amazwi omntu ngokwakhe kwaye uchaze into oyiqwalaselayo. Umzekelo, bhala “uthe kukho umntu ecaleni kwefestile” endaweni yokubiza le nto ngegama elinye. Yabelana ngezi nqaku ngemvume yomntu xa kunokwenzeka.

Ezi nqaku azichazi isizathu. Ukuba imvakalelo iqala ngequbuliso ngokudideka okanye ngnotshintsho olukhulu kukuqaphela, funa uncedo ngokukhawuleza endaweni yokulinda ukuqokelela irekhodi.
