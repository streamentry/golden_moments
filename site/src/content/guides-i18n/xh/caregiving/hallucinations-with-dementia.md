Ukubona, ukuva, ukuhogela, ukungcamla okanye ukuva into abanye abangayiqapheliyo kusenokuvakala kuyinyani kumntu. Kusenokoyikisa, okanye kusenokungamkhathazi. Qala ngendlela umntu avakalelwa ngayo nokuba ukho umntu ongakhuselekanga, endaweni yokuzama ukungqina oko kwenzekileyo okanye okungenzekanga.

Amava asenokuba kukubona izinto ezingekhoyo, kodwa angabuye abe kukungaqondi kakuhle okanye ukubona umntu omnye njengomnye. Ithunzi lisenokubonakala njengomntu; umntu owaziwayo usenokuthathwa njengomnye. Akunyanzelekanga ukuba ukhethe ukuba kwenzeka ntoni. Chaza oko umntu akuthethayo noko ukuqaphelayo, uze ubuze ugqirha ukuba uyakhathazeka.

## Ndimele ndenze ntoni ngelo xesha?

Hlala uzolile, buza ukuba umntu uvakalelwa njani, uze unike ukuthuthuzelwa ngaphandle kokuxabana. [I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuhlola ukuba amava ayakhathaza okanye ayingozi na](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/hallucinations). Ukuba umntu uziva kamnandi kwaye ukhuselekile, kusenokungabikho mfuneko yokuphazamisa.

- **Qonda imvakalelo.** Usenokuthi, "Andiboni mntu, kodwa ndiyabona ukuba oku kuyoyikisa. Ndikho apha." Oku kuyinyaniso kwaye kwazisa umntu ukuba umthatha ngokusisiseko.
- **Gcina amagama akho emafutshane.** Inkcazelo ende okanye ukulungisa okuphindaphindwayo kunokongeza uxinzelelo. Mvumele umntu achaze oko akuqapheleyo ukuba uyathanda.
- **Nika, ungenyanzeli, utshintsho.** Buza ukuba angathanda ukuhlala kwenye indawo, ukuvula esinye isibane okanye ukumamela ingoma emaziyo. Ukuya egumbini elikhanyiswe kakuhle nelaziwayo kusenokunceda; yeka ukuba umntu akafuni ukuhamba.
- **Hlala ukufutshane ukuba ufuna wena.** Musa ukumchukumisa ngesiquphe. Buza ngaphambi kokunika isandla okanye ukumkhumbatha.

Ukuba amava akanabungozi kwaye akakhathazi, landela into akhokelayo umntu. Musa ukwenza ixesha libe ngumvavanyo okanye ukumcela ukuba angqine oko akubonileyo.

Abanye abantu bayabona amava onwabisa okanye aqhelekileyo. Ukuba umntu uzolile kwaye ukhuselekile, kusenokungabikho mfuneko yokutshintsha umba okanye ukumsa kwenye indawo. Buza into afuna ukuyenza; musa ukwenza ixesha ngalinye elinjalo libe yincoko yezonyango. Ukuba uyoyika, phendula loo mvakalelo nokuba awukwazi ukubona into efanayo.

## Ingaba kukho into egumbini enokuthatyathwa ngumntu okanye isandi?

Jonga indawo nomntu ukuba wamkela loo nkxaso. [I-Alzheimer's Society yahlula ukubona izinto ezingekhoyo ekungaqondini kakuhle](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations), apho into yokwenene okanye umzobo ubonwa ngokungeyonyani.

- **Phucula isibane.** Vula isibane uze ujonge amathunzi okanye iindawo ezibonakalisayo zeejini neefestile.
- **Jonga imisindo eqhelekileyo.** Umabonakude, ifeni okanye isixhobo sasekhaya zisenokuvakala njengelizwi okanye izikhondo zezinyawo. Ncitshisa okanye uvale kuphela ukuba umntu uziva kamnandi ngoko.
- **Jonga imizobo.** Ikhapethi, ikhethini okanye iphepha lodonga elixakekileyo lisenokuthathwa njengentshukumo okanye into. Buza ukuba umntu angathanda ukujonga evela kwenye indawo okanye atshintshe imbonakalo.
- **Jonga izixhobo zovakalelo.** Ukuba umntu usebenzisa iiglasi okanye isixhobo sokuva, buza ukuba ungathanda ukunceda ujonge ukuba sikhona kwaye sisebenza njengesiqhelo.

Ukuba amava enzeka kwindawo enye, funa into ephindaphindwayo: umbono egalasini, umzobo oqinileyo womgangatho, umabonakude oviwa kwelinye igumbi okanye ukukhanya okuncinci ngokuhlwa. Jonga isandi okanye into yokwenene phambi kokuyithatha njengotshintsho lokuqonda. Ukuba uzama utshintsho lwendawo, lucacise uze ubuze ukuba luvakala ngcono na.

Zama utshintsho olunye oluncinci ngexesha. Musa ukulungisa izinto zalo mntu okanye ukususa into ayixabisayo ngaphandle kokubuza. Umkhuba owaziwayo nendawo ezolileyo, ekhanyiswe kakuhle isenokuvakala ilula.

## Ndimele ndiqhagamshelane nogqirha nini?

Qhagamshelana nogqirha walo mntu ngamava amatsha, aphindaphindekayo, ahlala ixesha elide okanye akhathazayo. Utshintsho kwiliso okanye ekuvaleni, ukugula, amayeza nezinye izinto zinokunegalelo. Ugqirha unokuhlola ukuba kusenokuba kwenzeka ntoni nokuba ukulandelwa kuyafuneka. [Isikhokelo se-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) sikwacebisa ukujonga izinto zomzimba, zovakalelo, zamayeza nezendawo.

Utshintsho lwesiquphe lwahlukile. [I-NHS ithi ukudideka kwesiquphe kufuna uncedo lwezonyango kwangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), kwaye [i-Alzheimer's Society icebisa uncedo lwezonyango kwangoko xa ukubona izinto ezingekhoyo kubandakanya iimvakalelo ezingaphezu kwenye](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations). Qhagamshelana neenkonzo zonyango ezikhawulezileyo zasekuhlaleni ukuba umntu udideka ngesiquphe okanye ukubona izinto ezingekhoyo kubandakanya iimvakalelo ezininzi. Ukuba umntu usengozini ekhoyo ngoku, sebenzisa iinkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni.

Musa ukuqala, ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza wedwa. Cela umtyibilizi weyeza okanye i-pharmacist ukuba ajonge utshintsho lwamva nje nawo onke amayeza asetyenziswa ngumntu, kuquka iimveliso ezithengiswa ngaphandle kwerisithi. [Isikhokelo sethu sokutyelela ugqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinokunceda ekulungiseleleni imibuzo.

## Ziziphi iinkcukacha ezinokunceda kwisicelo sokubona ugqirha?

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, bhala phantsi amanqaku amafutshane ukuze wabelane neqela lonyango:

- oko wathi ukubonile, ukuvile okanye ukuvakalelwe, usebenzisa amazwi akhe xa kunokwenzeka;
- nini nakweyiphi indawo kwenzeka, nokuba kuhlale ixesha elingakanani ngokufutshane;
- ukuba umntu ubonakale eyoyika, ezolile okanye esengozini;
- indlela igumbi elalinjalo ngayo, kuquka isibane, amathunzi, imisindo nokuba ngubani owayekufutshane;
- naluphi na ugulo, utshintsho lovakalelo okanye utshintsho lweyeza olwaziyo.

Sebenzisa amazwi omntu ngokwakhe uze uchaze oko ukuqaphelayo. Umzekelo, bhala "wathi umntu wayeseduze nefestile" endaweni yokubiza isiganeko ngegama. Yabelana ngamanqaku ngemvume yomntu xa kunokwenzeka.

La manqaku akafumani sizathu. Ukuba amava aqala ngesiquphe nokudideka okanye utshintsho olukhulu ekuvukeni, funa uncedo olukhawulezileyo endaweni yokulinda ukuqokelela irekhodi.
