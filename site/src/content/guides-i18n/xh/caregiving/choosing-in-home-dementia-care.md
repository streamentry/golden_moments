Ukufumana uncedo lwasekhaya kuqala kumntu, hayi kuluhlu lweenkonzo. Buza ukuba ufuna ukuqhubeka ezenzela ntoni kwaye luluphi uhlobo loncedo angalwamkelayo. Umnikeli unokuxhasa ubuhlobo, imisebenzi yemihla ngemihla, imisebenzi yasekhaya okanye ukhathalelo lwempilo olunobuchule. Umxube ofanelekileyo uxhomekeke kumntu, kwimisebenzi nakwinkxaso esele ikhona.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokhathalelo lwasekhaya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/in-home-care) kunye ne-[isishwankathelo se-Dementia Australia sokhathalelo lwasekhaya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/home-care) zichaza iindidi ezahlukeneyo zeenkonzo. [Uluhlu lwe-Association lokujonga umnikeli](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-in-home-care-professional-ts.pdf) lucebisa ukubuza ngoqeqesho, amava, abanobungqina, inkxaso yesibini neendleko. Iinkonzo, izikhundla nemithetho yenkxaso yemali ziyahluka ngokwendawo; eli phepha alimxhasi mnikeli kwaye aliyichazi ukufaneleka.

## Luluphi uncedo umntu alufunayo?

Qala ngemisebenzi ebalulekileyo kusuku lomntu. Angafuna umntu otyelelayo nothetha naye, uncedo ngokuthenga okanye ngokulungisa ukutya, okanye uncedo ngokuhlamba okanye ukunxiba. Ukhathalelo lwempilo olunobuchule, njengonesi okanye unyango lomzimba, yinkonzo eyahlukileyo kwaye isenokufuna ingcali enoqeshiso okanye ukwaziswa ngumnikeli wempilo.

Bhala ukuba kufuneka uncedo luni, lunceda nini nokuba umntu uthanda ntoni. Bandakanya oko anokukwenza ngaphandle koncedo nokuba ufuna ubumfihlo nini. Oku kunokugcina inkonzo igxile ekuxhaseni inkululeko kunokuthatha usuku lonke.

Ukuba umntu akaqinisekanga, qala ngemfuneko enye esebenzayo okanye utyelelo olufutshane apho olo khetho lukhoyo. Buza ukuba waziva njani emva koko. Angafuna ukutshintsha umsebenzi, ixesha okanye umntu omncedayo. Isicwangciso sokhathalelo sinokulungiswa njengoko iimfuno nezinto ezithandwayo zitshintsha.

## Ndimele ndibuze ntoni umnikeli phambi kokumqesha?

Biza abanikeli abambalwa uze ucacise imisebenzi umntu afuna uncedo ngayo. Buza ukuba banokuhlangabezana naloo mfuno, bakhona nini nokuba bakhe bakhathalela umntu one-dementia. Buza ukuba abasebenzi babo bafumana uqeqesho lwe-dementia luni nokuba baxhasa njani umntu odidekileyo, onexhala okanye odiniweyo.

Cacisa iinkcukacha ezichaphazela ubomi bemihla ngemihla: ngubani oza kuza, kangaphi, utyelelo ngalunye lwaneka ixesha elingakanani, kwenzeka ntoni ukuba umsebenzi uyagula, nokuba utsalela umnxeba nabani ngenxalabo. Ukuba umntu ufuna umncedi omnye xa kunokwenzeka, buza ukuba inkonzo inokunikela olo qhubeko.

Cela abanobungqina uze ubuze ukuba i-arhente ivavanya njani abasebenzi bayo. Fumanisa ukuba ngaba uvavanyo lwengxelo yobuqu, uqeqesho nezifundo ziyajongwa, nokuba ngowuphi umsebenzi umsebenzi avunyelwe ukuwenza. Imithetho namagama ezikhundla ayahluka ngokwendawo, ngoko qinisekisa nayiphi na imfuneko yengcali okanye yoqeshiso apho uhlala khona.

## Ndingaqinisekisa njani ukuba umntu nomnikeli bayahambelana?

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, cwangcisa intlanganiso ekhaya phambi kotyelelo lokuqala. Qaphela ukuba umnikeli uthetha nomntu ngokuthe ngqo, uphulaphula oko akuthandayo kwaye umnika ixesha lokuphendula. Buza umntu ukuba intlanganiso yavakala njani, ngendlela anokuphendula ngayo ekhululekile.

I-Alzheimer's Association icebisa ukuba omnye umntu othenjiweyo abekho ngexesha lodliwanondlebe ukuze nixoxe iimvakalelo zenu emva koko. Sebenzisa intlanganiso ukuxoxa ngotyelelo oluqhelekileyo: ukufika, ukubulisa, umsebenzi omiselweyo nendlela umntu anokucela ngayo ukuyeka okanye ukuphumla.

Ukuba kunokwenzeka, vumelanani ngotyelelo lokuqala olufutshane nilujonge kunye nomntu. Umnikeli unokubonakala eqeshiwe ephepheni kodwa ungahambelani nomntu ngobuqu. Hlonipha impendulo yomntu uze uxoxe ngezinye iindlela ukuba nasiphi na isihlandlo sinenkxalabo.

## Ndabelana nomncedi ngolwazi luni?

Yabelana kuphela ngeenkcukacha ezinceda umnikeli axhase umntu ngokukhuselekileyo nangembeko. Ngemvume yomntu, cacisa ukuba ufuna ukubizwa njani, yeyiphi imikhwa ebalulekileyo, yintoni enceda unxibelelwano nokuba ufuna ukwenza ntoni yedwa.

Ukuba imiyalelo yempilo ichaphazela umsebenzi, buza ugqirha womntu ukuba umnikeli ufuna ukwazi ntoni. Musa ukucela umsebenzi ongenaso isiqinisekiso ukuba enze imisebenzi yezonyango engaphandle koqeqesho okanye indima yakhe. Gcina ulwazi lobuqu lufihlakele, uze ujonge ukuba ngubani onokufikelela kuwo nawaphi na amanqaku abhaliweyo okanye iirekhodi zokhathalelo.

[Isikhokelo sokulungiselela utyelelo lukagqirha](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) sinokunceda ukulungelelanisa imibuzo yengcali yezempilo. [Isikhokelo sokhuseleko lwasekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sicubungula utshintsho lwasekhaya nokuba ubuza nini ingcali efanelekileyo.

## Ndingalujonga njani ulungiselelo ngokuhamba kwexesha?

Buza umntu emva kotyelelo. Buza ukuba yintoni eziva ikhululekile, abayonwabeleyo nento aza kuyitshintsha. Ukuba akasebenzisi mazwi amaninzi, qaphela ukuba uyamvuyela na umncedi, ukuba uziva ekhululekile na kwaye ufuna utyelelo luqhubeke. Ezi mpawu aziso sikolo; zisebenzise ukukhokela incoko.

Vumelana nomnikeli ngendlela yokwabelana ngeendaba eziqhelekileyo nokujongana neenkxalabo. Ukuba utyelelo luyarhoxiswa, yazi ukuba ngubani onokunikela inkxaso yesibini okanye umntu athanda ukwenza ntoni endaweni yoko. Phinda ujonge imisebenzi xa umkhwa wemihla ngemihla womntu, impilo okanye iimfuno zenkxaso zitshintsha.

Iindleko, amaxesha neenkonzo ezikhoyo ziyahluka. Cela umnikeli imiqathango ebhaliweyo, kubandakanya amaxabiso, ubuncinane bexesha lotyelelo, imithetho yokurhoxisa neentlawulo ezongezelelweyo. Ugqirha womntu, iinkonzo zasekhaya zabantu abadala okanye imibutho ye-dementia inokukucebisa apho ungajonga khona, kodwa thelekisa abanikeli wena nomntu.

Ukuba umntu uba nokudideka okuqubulayo okanye egula kakhulu, musa ukucinga ukuba utshintsho lubangelwa yi-dementia. [I-NHS icebisa uncedo lwezonyango olukhawulezileyo ngokudideka okuqubulayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Kwiinkxalabo eziqhubekayo zokhathalelo, thetha nogqirha okanye enye ingcali efanelekileyo.

Injongo yinkxaso ehambelana nobomi bomntu. Cacisa umsebenzi, jonga amava omnikeli nesicwangciso senkxaso yesibini, bandakanya umntu kwisigqibo ngasinye uze uphinde ujonge indlela ulungiselelo eliziva ngayo ngokuhamba kwexesha.
