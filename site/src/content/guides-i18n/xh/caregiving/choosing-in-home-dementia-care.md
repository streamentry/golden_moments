Ukufumana uncedo lwasekhaya kuqala ngomntu, hayi ngoluhlu lweenkonzo. Buza ukuba yintoni afuna ukuyenza ngokwakhe kunye nolo hlobo loncedo alwamkelayo. Umnikezi unokuxhasa ukuhlala nomntu nokuxoxa naye, imisebenzi yemihla ngemihla, imisebenzi yasekhaya okanye unakekelo lwezempilo oluqeqeshiweyo. Ixabiso elifanelekileyo lixhomekeke kumntu, kwimisebenzi nakwinkxaso ekhoyo sele.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association malunga nonakekelo lwasekhaya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/in-home-care) kunye [nesishwankathelo se-Dementia Australia malunga nonakekelo lwasekhaya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/home-care) zichaza iintlobo ezahlukeneyo zeenkonzo. [Uluhlu lombutho lwabanikezi benkonzo](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-in-home-care-professional-ts.pdf) lucebisa ukubuza malunga noqeqesho, amava, izimvo kunye neendleko. Iinkonzo, iziqinisekiso kunye nemigaqo yentlawulo ziyahluka ngendawo; eli phepha alincomi nabani umnikezi wenkonzo kwaye alichazi ukufaneleka.

## Ngoluhlobo luphi uncedo umntu alufunayo?

Qala ngemisebenzi ebalulekileyo kwimihla yomntu. Usenokufuna umntu ohlala nawo aze athethe naye, uncedo ngokuthenga nokulungiselela izidlo, okanye uncedo ngokuhlamba nokunxiba. Unakekelo lwezempilo oluqeqeshiweyo, njengolomongikazi okanye ulonyango lomzimba, luhlobo olwahlukileyo lwenkonzo kwaye lunokufuna ingcali enelayisensi okanye isalathiso somboneleli wezempilo.

Bhala phantsi uncedo olufunekayo, ixesha apho lunokuncedisa kunye nento umntu ayikhethayo. Quka nto anokuyenza ngaphandle koncedo kunye nexesha afuna ubumfihlo ngalo. Oku kunokugcina inkonzo igxile ekuxhaseni ukuzimela endaweni yokuthatha lonke usuku.

Ukuba umntu akaqinisekanga, qala ngoncedo olunye lwemfuneko yemihla okanye ngotyelelo olufutshane apho inkonzo ikhoyo. Buza ukuba wayeziva njani emva koko. Unokufuna ukutshintsha umsebenzi, ixesha okanye umntu oncedayo. Icwangciso yonakekelo inokulungiswa njengoko iimfuno nokhetho lutshintsha.

## Yimibuzo ipi ekufuneka ndiyibuze umnikezi ngaphambi kokuqesha?

Bizela abanikezi bambalwa kwaye uchaze imisebenzi umntu afuna uncedo ngayo. Buza ukuba bayakwazi ukuhlangabezana neemfuno ezo, bafumaneka nini kwaye bake bawakhe amava okunakekela umntu onedementia. Buza ukuba abasebenzi babo bafumana uqeqesho luni lwedementia kwaye bamxhasa njani umntu odidekileyo, exhalabile okanye odiniwe.

Cacisa iinkcukacha eziphembelela ubomi bemihla ngemihla: ubani oza kufika, rhoqo kangakanani, ixesha ngalinye lihlala ixesha elingakanani, kwenzeka ntoni ukuba umsebenzi ugulile, nokuba ngubani ofanele ukubizelwa ukuba kukho inkxalabo. Ukuba umntu ufuna umncedisi ofanayo ngamaxesha onke, buza ukuba inkonzo inokuqinisekisa ukuba loo mncedisi uyafana.

Cela izimvo kwaye ubuze ukuba inkonzo ikhetha njani abasebenzi bayo. Fumanisa ukuba kuhlolwa na imbali yabasebenzi, uqeqesho kunye neziqinisekiso, kwaye yimisebenzi ipi umsebenzi onokuyenza ngokomthetho. Imigaqo kunye neetayitile ziyahluka ngendawo, ngoko qinisekisa iimfuno zobuchwephesha okanye zelayisensi kwindawo oyihlala kuyo.

## Ndingakwazi njani ukuba umntu kunye nomnikezi bayafanelana?

Ukuba umntu uyavuma, hlela intlanganiso ekhaya ngaphambi kotyelelo lokuqala. Qaphela ukuba umnikezi uthetha nomntu ngqo, uyamphulaphula kwaye umnika ixesha lokuphendula. Buza umntu ukuba intlanganiso ivakale njani, ngendlela aziva ekhululekile ngayo ukuphendula.

Umbutho we-Alzheimer's ucebisa ukuba kubekho omnye umntu othembekileyo ekhoyo ngexesha lodliwano-ndlebe, ukuze kube lula ukuthetha ngemiboniso yakho emva koko. Sebenzisa intlanganiso ukuthetha ngotyelelo oluqhelekileyo: ukufika, ukwamkelwa, umsebenzi ocwangciswe kunye nendlela umntu anokucela ngayo ukuyeka okanye ukuphumla.

Ukuba kunokwenzeka, vumelana ukuba kubekho utyelelo olufutshane lokuqala kwaye ulujonge kunye nomntu. Umnikezi unokubonakala elungele iphepha, kodwa angafaneleki kakuhle nomntu omthile. Hlonipha impendulo yomntu kwaye uxoxe ngezinye ezikhoyo ukuba omnye wenu unenkxalabo.

## Ndingabelana ngantoni nomncedisi?

Yabelana kuphela ngeenkcukacha ezinceda umnikezi ukuba axhase umntu ngokukhuselekileyo nangentlonipho. Ngemvume yomntu, chaza indlela athanda ngayo ukubizwa, ukuba yiziphi iinkqubo ezibalulekileyo, into eyenza ukuba axoxe, kunye nento afuna ukuyenza ngokwakhe.

Ukuba izimiselo zempilo zichaphazela umsebenzi, buza umntu ngengcali yakhe yonyango ukuba umnikezi kufuneka azi ntoni. Musa ukucela umncedisi ongaqeqeshwanga ukuba enze imisebenzi yonyango engaphandle koqeqesho lwakhe okanye indima yakhe. Gcina iinkcukacha zomntu zimfihlo, kwaye ujonge ukuba ngubani onokufikelela kuwo nawuphi na umbhalo okanye irekhodi lonakekelo.

Isikhokelo [sokulungiselela izityelelo zodokotela](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) singakunceda ulungiselele imibuzo kwingcali yezempilo. Isikhokelo [sokhuseleko ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sichaza utshintsho kwindlu kunye nexesha apho kufuneka ubuze ingcali eyaziyo.

## Ndingalubukeza njani olu hlobo lwenkxaso ngokuhamba kwexesha?

Hlola umntu emva kotyelelo. Buza ukuba yintoni eyayiluncedo, yintoni awayithandayo kwaye yintoni angafuna ukuyitshintsha. Ukuba akathethi kakhulu, qwalasela ukuba uyamamkela na umncedisi, uziva ekhululekile na kwaye ufuna ukuba utyelelo luqhubeke. Ezi zinto azisiyo amanqaku; zisetyenziselwe ukukhokela incoko.

Vumelana nomnikezi indlela yokwabelana ngezaziso zamaxesha onke kunye nendlela yokuphatha iinkxalabo. Ukuba utyelelo luhlelwe kwakhona, yazi ukuba ngubani onokubonelela ngoncedo lokuxhasa okanye ukuba umntu ufuna ukwenza ntoni endaweni yalo. Jonga imisebenzi xa imihla yomntu, impilo yakhe okanye iimfuno zenkxaso zitshintsha.

Iindleko, izicwangciso kunye neenkonzo ezikhoyo ziyahluka. Cela umnikezi amaxwebhu abhaliweyo, aquka amaxabiso, ubude bomtyelelo oncinane, imigaqo yokurhoxisa kunye neentlawulo ezongezelelweyo. Udokotela womntu, iinkonzo zabantu abadala zasekuhlaleni okanye imibutho yedementia inokucebisa iindawo ezinokujongwa, kodwa wena nomntu nifanele nithelekise abanikezi ngokwenu.

Ukuba umntu udideka ngokukhawuleza okanye akwazi kakuhle ngaphezu koko, musa ukucinga ukuba utshintsho luvela kwidementia ngokuqinisekileyo. [I-NHS icebisa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) ngokudideka okungalindelwanga. Ngeenkxalabo zonakekelo ezihleli nje, thetha nodokotela okanye nengcali enelungelo eyaziyo.

Injongo kukuxhasa ubomi bomntu. Chaza umsebenzi, jonga amava omnikezi kunye necebo lokufumana uncedo, zibandakanye nomntu kwisigqibo ngasinye, kwaye uqwalasele indlela enemvakalelo ngayo olu hlobo lwenkxaso ngokuhamba kwexesha.
