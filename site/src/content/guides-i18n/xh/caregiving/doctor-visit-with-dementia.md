Utyelelo lodokotela lunokusombulula izinto ezininzi ngexesha elifutshane. Icwangciso emfutshane inokunceda umntu kunye nomxhasi wakhe bakhuphe imibuzo ebaluleke ngakumbi, baqonde iimpendulo kwaye bahambe nezinyathelo ezicacileyo. Umntu onedementia kufuneka ahlale eyinxalenye yencoko nezigqibo njengoko efuna.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokusebenza nodokotela](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/working-with-the-doctor) sicebisa ukubhala imibuzo phantsi, ukuchaza utshintsho oluthile kunye nokucela udokotela ahlaziye icwangciso. [Icebiso le-Dementia Australia malunga nokusebenza nodokotela](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/working-doctors) ligxininisa imvume, ukumamela umntu kunye nokwabelana ngolwazi ngokuzolileyo xa kufuneka. Eli phepha linikezela ngeengcebiso jikelele zokulungiselela, hayi ngecebiso lonyango.

## Ndilungiselela njani phambi kotyelelo?

Buza umntu ukuba yintoni afuna udokotela ayazi kakhulu. Bhalani uluhlu lwemibuzo kunye, kwaye ubeke ebalulekileyo kakhulu phambili. Ukuba uluhlu lude, buza kwiofisi ukuba ixesha elide okanye utyelelo olulandelayo luya kunceda na. Umntu unokuthanda ukuthwala inothi ebhaliweyo endaweni yokukhumbula yonke into ngexesha lotyelelo.

Chaza utshintsho ngeenkcukacha ezicacileyo. Chaza into eyenzekileyo, ukuba iqale nini, ukuba ivela rhoqo kangakanani kunye nto eyayisenzeka ngelo xesha. Umzekelo, “Uyeke ukuya kwiqela lomculo veki zonke kwinyanga edlulileyo” lunceda ngakumbi kunokuthi “Uyaba kakubi.” Quka umbono womntu ngalo mtshintsho kwaye ungayiguquli ingcinga ibe sisigqibo sonyango.

Zisa uluhlu lwamayeza onke, kuquka amayeza enziwe ngaphandle kwencwadi yonyango, iivithamini kunye neziyolisi. Umntu usenokufuna izibona zakhe, izixhobo zokuva okanye amagama abanye oodokotela bakhe. Buza kwiofisi ukuba zeziphi iifom okanye amarekhodi afunekayo, endaweni yokuqokelela ulwazi olungaphezu koko kufuneka ngotyelelo.

## Ndingambandakanya njani umntu ngexesha lotyelelo?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukuba ungene kutyelelo na. Usenokufuna ukuqhutywa kwaye athethe nodokotela yedwa, okanye angakwamkela njengomntu obhala amanqaku. Buza ukuba ufuna ukumncedisa njani ngaphambi kokuba utyelelo luqale.

Vumela umntu aphendule imibuzo yodokotela kuqala. Yongeza iinkcukacha kuphela xa efuna oko, okanye xa udokotela ebuza ulwazi umntu angakhumbuli. Thetha nomntu, hayi nodokotela kuphela. Ukuba efuna ixesha lokuphendula, mnike lo ixesha endaweni yokugcwalisa wonke umzuzwana.

Umntu usenokufuna ixesha labucala nodokotela malunga nento ekhathazayo kuye. Hlonipha esi sicelo. Ukuba unenkxalabo eyoyikisayo ukuyiphakamisa phambi komntu, buza ikliniki indlela yokwabelana ngenothi ebhaliweyo ngokuzolileyo. Chaza ngokucacileyo into obuyibonileyo kwaye ungacela ikliniki ukuba ifihle ulwazi okanye ithathe izigqibo ngaphandle kokubandakanya umntu.

## Ziziphi imibuzo enokunceda icwangciso icace?

Buza ukuba kukho into engacacanga ichazwe ngolwimi olulula. Umntu nomxhasi wakhe banokubuza:

- “Yintoni ekufuneka sigcine iliso kuyo, kwaye nini ekufuneka sikuqhagamshele?”
- “Ziziphi izinto ezikhoyo ekufuneka sizikhethe, kwaye zineenzuzo nezinto ezinokungalunganga?”
- “Yeyiphi inyathelo elilandelayo, kwaye ubani oza kulilungiselela?”
- “Kufuneka silandele nini?”

Ezi zimbuzo ngomzekelo, hayi uluhlu oluyimfuneko kutyelelo ngalunye. Umntu usenokuba nemibuzo eyahlukileyo okanye afune ukugxila kwinkxalabo enye. Buza udokotela ukuba acwangcise iinkcukacha eziphambili ngaphambi kokuba uhambe.

## Yintoni okufanele ndiyibhale emva kotyelelo?

Bhala isishwankathelo esifutshane sencazelo kadokotela, izenzo ezivumelaniweyo kunye nexesha lokulandela. Qaphela ukuba ngubani oza kwenza isenzo ngasinye, njengokuthetha neklinikhi okanye ukubhukisha olunye utyelelo. Ukuba umntu uvumile, yabelana ngeenkcukacha nelungu losapho okanye umncedisi ofunekayo.

Gcina iinkcukacha zempilo zimfihlo kwaye zilula ukuzifumana kumntu. Musa ukuzithumela ngeakhawunti ekwabelwana ngayo okanye kwiqela lemiyalezo ukuba umntu akavumelanga kuloo ndlela. Ukuba umyalelo ungacacanga xa usekhaya, mbizela ikliniki ucele ukucaciswa. Musa ukuqala, ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ngokusekwe kwinothi yakho ngaphandle kokubuza umbhali wamayeza.

Ngaphambi kokuba uhambe, jonga ukuba utyelelo olulandelayo okanye uvavanyo luyafuneka na kwaye ubani onokuqhagamshelwa ngemibuzo. Umntu usenokufuna ukugcina ikopi yocwangciso ebhaliweyo okanye akhethe umncedisi amthembayo ukuba ayigcine. Buza kunokuba ugqibe ngaye.

## Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba umntu udideka ngokungazelelwe?

Musa ukulinda utyelelo oluqhelekileyo ukuba umntu udideka ngokungazelelwe. [I-NHS icebisa ukufumana uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), kuba ukudideka okungazelelwe kunokuba nezizathu ezifuna ingqwalasela ekhawulezayo. Sebenzisa iinkonzo zesiphuthuma okanye inkqubo yonyango lwangoko kwindawo ohlala kuyo.

Utshintsho olukhula kancinci lunokuba yinto yokuxoxa nodokotela, kodwa lwahlukile kwisiphithiphithi esingazelelwanga. Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba utshintsho lusheshayo, funa uncedo lwenkonzo yezempilo ekhoyo ukuze ikukhokele, kungekhona ukuba uzame ukuzixilonga ngokwakho.

## Ndingaxhasa njani umntu ukuba utyelelo lubangela uxinzelelo?

Buza umntu ukuba yintoni eyenza utyelelo lube lula. Usenokuthanda ixesha elijwayelekileyo lonyango, ixesha elifutshane lokulinda okanye umntu omthembayo ahambe naye. Biza ikliniki ngaphambi kotyelelo ubuze ngemfuno zokufikelela, uncedo lokuva okanye ulwimi, kunye nokuba imibuzo inokuthunyelwa ngaphambi kotyelelo na.

Ukuba umntu unexhala, chaza icwangciso ngendlela ezolileyo nethe ngqo ehambelana nomntu. Unokuthatha into eyaziwayo okanye into yokwenza xa elinde, ukuba uyafuna oko. Buza ukuba ufuna isaziso esingakanani: abanye abantu bafuna ixesha lokulungiselela, ngelixa abanye bekhetha isikhumbuzo esifutshane kufutshane notyelelo. Sebenzisa incazelo ethembekileyo esebenza kuye.

Utyelelo oluhle lushiya umntu eva kwaye nezinyathelo ezilandelayo ziyaqondwa. Lungiselela kunye, gcina ubumfihlo kwaye ubuze ikliniki ukucacisa nantoni na engakacaci.
