Utyelelo lukagqirha lunokugubungela izinto ezininzi ngexesha elifutshane. Isicwangciso esifutshane sinokunceda umntu nomxhasi wakhe baphakamise imibuzo ebalulekileyo, baqonde iimpendulo kwaye baphume benamanyathelo alandelayo acacileyo. Umntu ophila ne-dementia kufuneka ahlale eyinxalenye yencoko nezigqibo ngokuthanda kwakhe.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association sokusebenza nogqirha](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/working-with-the-doctor) sicebisa ukubhala imibuzo, ukuqaphela utshintsho oluthile nokucela ugqirha aphindaphinde isicwangciso. [Icebiso le-Dementia Australia ngokusebenza nogqirha](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/working-doctors) ligxininisa imvume, ukuphulaphula umntu nokwabelana ngolwazi ngenkathalo xa kuyimfuneko. Eli phepha linikela amacebiso aqhelekileyo okulungiselela, hayi icebiso lezonyango.

## Ndingalungiselela njani phambi kwedinga?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ugqirha azi ntoni ngaphezu kwayo yonke into. Bhalani uluhlu olufutshane lweemibuzo kunye, uze ubeke owona mbuzo ubalulekileyo kuqala. Ukuba uluhlu lude, buza i-ofisi ukuba idinga elide okanye utyelelo lokulandelela lunokunceda. Umntu angakhetha ukuzisa inqaku elibhaliweyo kunokuba azame ukukhumbula yonke into ngexesha lotyelelo.

Chaza utshintsho ngamagama aphathekayo. Qaphela okwenzekileyo, kwaqala nini, kwenzeka kangaphi nokuba kwakusenzeka ntoni ngelo xesha. Umzekelo, “Uyekile ukuya kwiqela lomculo lveki nganye kwinyanga edlulileyo” luluncedo ngaphezu kokuthi “Uya ngokuba mbi.” Bandakanya ingcamango yomntu ngokwakhe ngotshintsho, uze ungawubeki ugqaliso njengokufunyanwa.

Zisa uluhlu lwamayeza angoku, kuquka amayeza anamaphepha kagqirha nangenawo, iivithamini nezongezelelo. Umntu angafuna nezibuko, izixhobo zokuvuma okanye amagama abanye abasebenzi bezempilo. Buza i-ofisi ukuba zeziphi iifomu okanye iirekhodi ezifunekayo kunokuqokelela ulwazi lobuqu olungakumbi kunoko utyelelo lufuna.

## Ndingambandakanya njani umntu ngexesha lotyelelo?

Buza umntu ukuba ufuna ukuba ungene kwidinga. Angafuna ukuhanjiswa kodwa athande ukuthetha nogqirha yedwa, okanye angakwamkela njengomntu obhala amanqaku. Buza ukuba ufuna ukuncedisa njani phambi kokuba utyelelo luqale.

Mvumele umntu aphendule imibuzo kagqirha kuqala. Yongeza iinkcukacha kuphela xa efuna, okanye xa ugqirha ecela ulwazi umntu angenakuluqondisisa. Thetha nomntu, hayi nje nogqirha. Ukuba efuna ixesha lokuphendula, linda kunokuzalisa wonke umnqweno wokuthula.

Umntu angafuna ixesha lakhe yedwa nogqirha ngenxalabo yobuqu. Hlonipha loo ngcelo. Ukuba unenkxalabo enzima ukuyiphakamisa phambi komntu, buza ikliniki ukuba unganikela njani inqaku elibhaliweyo ngobumfihlo. Cacisa oko ukubonileyo uze ungacebi ikliniki ifihle ulwazi okanye yenze izigqibo ngaphandle kokubandakanywa komntu.

## Yimiphi imibuzo enokunceda isicwangciso sicace?

Cela inkcazelo ngolwimi olulula ukuba into ayicacanga. Umntu nomxhasi banokubuza:

- Sifanele sijonge ntoni, kwaye sifanele sikuqhagamshele nini?
- Ziziphi iindlela ezikhoyo, kwaye ziyintoni izinto ezilungileyo nezingalunganga ezinokubakho?
- Linyathelo lini elilandelayo, kwaye ngubani oza kulilungiselela?
- Sifanele silandelele nini?

Le yimizekelo, ayisiyo luhlu olufuna ukugqitywa ekutyeleleni ngalinye. Umntu angabanayo eminye imibuzo okanye athande ukugxila kwinkxalabo enye. Cela ugqirha aphindaphinde amanqaku aphambili phambi kokuba uhambe.

## Ndimele ndibhale ntoni emva kwedinga?

Bhala isishwankathelo esifutshane senkcazelo kagqirha, amanyathelo avunyelweneyo nexesha lokulandelela. Qaphela ukuba ngubani oza kujongana nenyathelo ngalinye, njengokubiza ikliniki okanye ukubhukisha elinye idinga. Ukuba umntu uyavuma, yabelana ngeenkcukacha nelungu losapho okanye umncedi ozidingayo.

Gcina amanqaku empilo efihlakele ibe lula ukuba umntu awafumane. Phepha ukuwathumela ngeakhawunti yokwabelana okanye umyalezo weqela ukuba umntu akavumanga koko. Ukuba umyalelo awucaci xa ufika ekhaya, biza ikliniki ucele ukucaciswa. Musa ukuqala, ukumisa okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ngokwamanqaku akho ngaphandle kokubuza umbhali wamayeza.

Phambi kokuba uhambe, jonga ukuba utyelelo lokulandelela okanye uvavanyo kufuneka lucwangciselwe na nokuba utsalela umnxeba nabani ngemibuzo. Umntu angafuna ukugcina ikopi yesicwangciso esibhaliweyo okanye akhethe umncedi othenjiweyo ukuba asigcine. Buza kunokumgqibela.

## Kuthiwa ntoni ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso?

Musa ukulinda idinga eliqhelekileyo ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso. [I-NHS icebisa ukufumana uncedo lwezonyango ngokukhawuleza](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), kuba ukudideka okuqubulayo kunokuba nezizathu ezifuna ingqwalasela ekhawulezileyo. Sebenzisa iinkonzo zasekhaya zemeko eyingxamisekileyo okanye ulandele inkqubo yenkathalo ekhawulezileyo apho uhlala khona.

Utshintsho olungekhawulezanga lunokufaneleka ukuxoxwa nogqirha, kodwa luhlukile kukudideka okuqubulayo. Ukuba awuqinisekanga ukuba utshintsho lukhawuleza na, tsalela inkonzo yezonyango efanelekileyo ukuze ufumane isikhokelo kunokuzama ukuyifumanisa wena.

## Ndingamxhasa njani umntu ukuba amadinga aziva enoxinzelelo?

Buza ukuba yintoni enokwenza utyelelo lube lula. Umntu angakhetha ixesha ledinga eliqhelekileyo, ukulinda okuncinci okanye ukuhamba nomntu othenjiweyo. Biza kwangaphambili ukubuza ikliniki ngeemfuno zokufikelela, uncedo lokuva okanye lolwimi, nokuba imibuzo inokuthunyelwa phambi kotyelelo.

Ukuba umntu une-nkxalabo, cacisa isicwangciso ngendlela ezolileyo nethe ngqo ethandwa nguye. Zisa into eqhelekileyo okanye into yokwenza ngoxa ulindile, ukuba umntu uyayifuna. Buza ukuba ufuna ukuxelelwa kwangaphambili kangakanani: abanye abantu bafuna ixesha lokulungiselela, ngoxa abanye bethanda isikhumbuzo esifutshane kufuphi notyelelo. Sebenzisa inkcazelo enyanisekileyo esebenza kuye.

Utyelelo olulungileyo lushiya umntu eviwa, esazi amanyathelo alandelayo. Lungiselelani kunye, khusela ubumfihlo uze ucele ikliniki icacise nantoni na engekacaci.
