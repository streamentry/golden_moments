Iingxaki zokuya ntlango zinokuphazamisa umntu nabantu abamncedayo. Qwalasela iingozi njengemfuno yonkathalo, hayi ukungaphumeleli. Buza ukuba yeyiphi inkxaso eyonwabisayo, gcina umntu ebandakanyekile kwaye ukhusele ubumfihlo bakhe. Umntu usenokukwazi ukwenza amanyathelo athile yedwa okanye usenokufuna umncedisi awaziyo ekufutshane.

Isikhokelo se-[Alzheimer's Association sokungalawuli komzimba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence), icebiso le-[Dementia Australia malunga nokulawula umzimba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/continence) kunye nesikhokelo se-[NHS sokunakekela umntu one-dementia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/looking-after-someone/) zicebisa isidima, inkxaso elula kunye nocebiso lweengcali xa iingxaki ziqala. Eli phepha lolwazi jikelele, asiyo ukuxilongwa okanye icwangciso yonkathalo lomntu siqu.

## Ingaba ingxaki entsha yokuya ntlango ihlala ibangelwa yi-dementia?

Hayi. Ingxaki entsha okanye ekhula ngakumbi inokuba nezizathu ezininzi. I-dementia inokwenza kube nzima ukuqaphela imfuneko yokuya ntlango, ukufumana indlu yangasese okanye ukulungiselela iimpahla. Ezinye izizathu ziquka usulelo, ukuqina kwesisu, iziphumo zamayeza okanye ubunzima bokuhamba. Umnakekeli akakwazi ukuchonga isizathu ngengozi enye.

Qhagamshelana negcisa lezempilo xa utshintsho ekulaweni komchamo nesisu luqala. Ukuba kuluncedo, bhala ukuba utshintsho lwaqala nini, into oyibonileyo kunye nokuba umntu ukhankanye na intlungu okanye ukungakhululeki. Oku kunokunceda ingcali ibuze imibuzo egxilileyo. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha amayeza ngokusekelwe kwisikhokelo jikelele.

Ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso, funa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko ngenkonzo yezimo eziphuthumayo endaweni yakho. Icebiso le-[NHS malunga nokudideka ngequbuliso](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) lichaza ukuba ukudideka ngequbuliso kufuna ukuqwalaselwa ngoko nangoko. Utshintsho olucothayo kumkhwa wokuya ntlango lwahlukile, kodwa lusafanele ingxoxo yengcali.

## Ndingalenza njani indlu yangasese ifumaneke lula kwaye isetyenziseke?

Yenza indlela icace kwaye ibonakale lula. Ukukhanya kwasebusuku, indawo eyaneleyo yokuhamba kunye nendawo eqhelekileyo yezinto zemihla ngemihla zinokunceda. Igama elilula okanye umfanekiso kwisicango unokuba luncedo ukuba umntu uyawuqonda. Buza ngaphambi kokushiya umnyango wendlu yangasese uvulekile, kuba umntu usenokuthanda ubumfihlo.

Iimpahla ezilula kumntu ukuzinxiba zinokunciphisa amanyathelo angongezelelweyo. Gcina iimpahla eziqhelekileyo kufuphi, kwaye ugweme ukufaka into entsha yokunxiba okanye utshintsho lwendawo yokugcina izinto ukuba lunokuphazamisa umkhwa oqhelekileyo. Isikhokelo sethu [sokhuseleko lwekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sigubungela ukukhanya kunye nendlela ecacileyo eya kwindlu yangasese. Buza ingcali yezokusebenza okanye enye ingcali efanelekileyo ngaphambi kokutshintsha izixhobo zendlu yangasese okanye ukufaka izibambelelo.

Indlu yangasese kufuneka ibe yeyoyisekayo kwaye eqhelekileyo. Susa into ezinde ezinokuthathwa njengendlu yangasese okanye enye into. Gcina iswitshi yokukhanya ilula ukuyifumana. Tshintsha into enye ngexesha, kwaye uyigcine kuphela ukuba umntu uyakwamkela.

## Ndingathetha ntoni xa umntu usenokufuna ukuya ntlango?

Sebenzisa amagama abantu abadala aqhelekileyo kunye nethoni ezolileyo. Isicelo esifutshane esifana no “Ungathanda ukusebenzisa indlu yangasese?” sinika umntu ithuba lokuphendula. Gwema amagama abantwana, ukuhleka, izikhumbuzo zoluntu okanye ukubuza phambi kwabanye abantu ukuba oko kunokumhlazisa.

Abanye abantu bakhombisa imfuneko ngezenzo endaweni yamagama. Basenokuba nomsindo, bahambe ngokuphindaphinda, babambe izambatho okanye bahambe besiya kwigumbi eliqhelekileyo. Ezi mpawu aziyo ubungqina bokuba umntu udinga ukuya ntlango, ngoko nikeza uncedo ngobumnene kwaye uqaphele impendulo yakhe.

Ukuba ixesha lokuya ntlango ngexesha eliqhelekileyo lisebenza kumntu, linikeze njengenxalenye yemini. Umkhwa yisimemo, hayi umyalelo. Ukuba akavumi, musa ukuxabana okanye ukumnyanzela ukuba ahlale phantsi. Linda, mnike elinye ixesha ukuba efuna, kwaye ulandele iindlela ezijwayelekileyo zomntu endaweni yokumnyanzela ishedyuli eqinileyo.

## Ndingaphendula njani emva kwengozi?

Gcina izwi lakho lingenambandela kwaye ukhusele isidima somntu. Gwema ukugweba, ukukhalaza okanye ukumlungisa phambi kwabanye abantu. Isicelo esifutshane esifana no “Ndixelele into enokunceda” sivumela umntu akukhokele. Nika izambatho ezicocekileyo okanye indawo yabucala yokutshintsha, kodwa buza ngaphambi kokumncedisa.

Nika umntu ixesha lokwenza amanyathelo angasebenza. Usenokufuna ukutshintsha izambatho ngokwakhe, anomntu othembekileyo ekufutshane okanye ashiywe wedwa ixesha elincinci. Ukuba efuna uncedo lwesenzo, chaza ngamanyathelo amafutshane kwaye uqinisekise ukuba uyakhululeka. Musa ukumbamba okanye ukumngxamisa kumsebenzi wabucala.

Ungalungiselela utshintsho lwezambatho olucocekileyo okanye ingxowa engachazwanga ngaphandle kohambo ukuba umntu uyafuna oko. Buza ngaphambi kokugcina iimveliso zokulawula umzimba kwindawo eyabelwanwa ngayo okanye ukuxelela abanye abantu malunga nengozi. Ukuba iimveliso zokukhusela okanye iindwebu zingancedisa, buza ingcali yezempilo okanye umcebisi wokulawula umzimba ukuba zeziphi ezo khetho zilungele iimfuno nokhetho lomntu.

## Ndifanele ndinciphise iziselo okanye ndikhethe iimveliso zokulawula umzimba?

Musa ukunciphisa ilwelo lomntu ukuthintela iingozi ngaphandle kokuba ingcali yakhe yezempilo inike icebiso elithile. Umbutho we-Alzheimer's ulumkisa ukuba ukunciphisa ilwelo kunokukhokelela ekomisweni komzimba nakwezinye iingxaki. Abantu abaphila ne-dementia basenokufuna izikhumbuzo okanye uncedo lokusela ilwelo elaneleyo. Landela icwangciso yonkathalo yomntu ngamnye kuyo nayiphi na imida yolwelo.

Iipadi, iimpahla zokukhusela okanye amashiti angangeni manzi zizinto zokukhetha komntu siqu. Zingancedisa abanye abantu, kodwa akukho imveliso enye elungele wonke umntu. Thetha negcisa lezempilo okanye nomcebisi wokulawula umzimba malunga neenketho, ukuthuthuzela, ukwaneleka, iingxaki zesikhumba nendlela umntu afuna ukuzisebenzisa. Musa ukuzibeka kumntu ochasayo; buza ukuba yintoni eyamkelekayo kuye.

## Ndifanele ndicele uncedo lwengcali nini?

Buza ugqirha malunga neengxaki ezintsha, ezikhulayo okanye ezibuhlungu zokuya ntlango. Usulelo, ukuqina kwesisu, iziphumo zamayeza okanye enye ingxaki yezempilo inokufuna ukuqwalaselwa. Zisa incazelo emfutshane yoko kutshintsheleyo kunye nokuba kwenzeke nini. Umcebisi wokulawula umzimba unokunceda ngeendlela ezisebenzayo kwezinye iindawo; buza ugqirha ukuba ufumana njani uncedo lwasekuhlaleni.

Funa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko ukuba umntu udideka ngequbuliso. Kwezinye iingxaki zokulawula umzimba, qhagamshelana negcisa lezempilo ukuze ufumane icebiso endaweni yokuchonga isizathu ngokwakho. Musa ukuthintela amanzi, ukutshintsha amayeza okanye ukuqalisa unyango ngokusekelwe kwinqaku lewebhusayithi.

## Ndingabelana njani ngomsebenzi ngokuhloniphana?

Ukuba abantu abaninzi bancedisa, buza umntu ukuba yintoni akulungele ukuyabelana ngayo. Inqaku elibhaliweyo ngamagama akhethwayo, ngomkhwa nangohlobo loncedo linokuthintela ukucacisa okuphindaphindwayo. Yabelana kuphela ngoko umncedisi olandelayo akufunayo ukwazi kwaye uhlaziye inqaku ukuba izifiso zomntu ziyatshintsha.

Ukunceda ngokuya ntlango kunokukhathaza nabanakekeli. Buza umntu okanye inkonzo yonyango ethembekileyo ukuba ithathe ithuba, ukuba umntu uyavuma. [Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela somnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sibandakanya iindlela zokufumana inkxaso nokuphumla. Ukunyamekela okuhloniphayo kukhusela bobabini ubumfihlo bomntu kunye nokukwazi komnakekeli ukuqhubeka.

Injongo kukwenza uncedo lufumaneke ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Phendula ngoxolo, khusela ubumfihlo, gcina ukhetho lomntu kwaye ubuze ingcali malunga neengxaki ezintsha.
