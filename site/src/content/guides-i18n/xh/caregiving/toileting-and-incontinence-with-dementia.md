Iingxaki zendlu yangasese zinokumkhathaza umntu nabantu abamnceda. Phatha ingozi njengesidingo sokhathalelo, ingeyiyo ukusilela. Buza ukuba loluphi uncedo olukhululekileyo, gcina umntu ebandakanyekile uze ukhusele imfihlo yakhe. Umntu angakwazi ukwenza amanye amanyathelo yedwa okanye angafuna umncedisi aqhelene naye asondele.

[Isikhokelo se-Alzheimer's Association ngokungabambi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence), [isicebiso se-Dementia Australia ngokubamba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/continence) kunye [nesikhokelo se-NHS sokhathalelo lomntu one-dementia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/looking-after-someone/) zicebisa isidima, inkxaso elula necebiso lengcali xa iingxaki ziqala. Eli phepha linolwazi jikelele, aliyonto yoxilongo okanye icebo lokhathalelo lomntu.

## Ingaba ingxaki entsha yendlu yangasese ihlala ibangelwa yi-dementia?

Hayi. Ingxaki entsha okanye ephambana ngakumbi inokuba nezizathu ezininzi. I-dementia inokwenza kube nzima ukuqaphela imfuno yokuya, ukufumana indlu yangasese okanye ukulawula impahla. Ezinye izinto ezinokwenzeka zezi: usulelo, ukuqhina, iziphumo zamayeza okanye ubunzima bokushukuma. Umnyamekeli akanakwazi isizathu ngokuwa kwesinye.

Qhagamshelana nogqirha okanye enye ingcali yezempilo xa ulawulo lwephaphu okanye lwamathumbu lutshintsha. Ukuba kuluncedo, bhala ukuba utshintsho lwaqala nini, oko ukuqaphelileyo nokuba umntu ukhankanye intlungu okanye ukungakhululeki. Oku kunokunceda ingcali ibuze imibuzo ekhethekileyo. Musa ukuyeka okanye ukutshintsha iyeza ngokwesikhokelo jikelele.

Ukuba umntu ngequbuliso ubhidekile, funa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko ngeenkonzo zongxamiseko zasekuhlaleni. [Icebiso le-NHS ngokubhideka kwequbuliso](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) lichaza ukuba ukubhideka kwequbuliso kufuna ingqwalasela ekhawulezileyo. Utshintsho olucotha lweenkqubo zendlu yangasese lwahlukile, kodwa lusafanele incoko nengcali.

## Ndingayenza njani indlu yangasese ibe lula ukuyifumana nokuyisebenzisa?

Yenza indlela icace kwaye kube lula ukuyibona. Isibane sasebusuku, indawo eyaneleyo yokuhamba nendawo eqhelekileyo yezinto zemihla ngemihla zinokunceda. Igama elilula okanye umfanekiso emnyango unokuba luncedo ukuba umntu uyawubona. Buza phambi kokushiya ucango lwendlu yangasese luvuliwe, kuba umntu angakhetha imfihlo.

Impahla elula ukuyilawula ngumntu inokunciphisa amanyathelo angaphezulu. Gcina impahla eqhelekileyo isondele, uze uphephe isixhobo sokuncamathisela esitsha okanye utshintsho lokugcina ukuba senza inkqubo ingaqhelekanga. [Isikhokelo sethu sokhuseleko lwekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) sigubungela ukukhanya nendlela ecacileyo eya endlini yangasese. Buza ingcali yonyango lomsebenzi okanye enye ingcali efanelekileyo phambi kokutshintsha izixhobo zendlu yangasese okanye ukongeza iirelingi.

Igumbi kufuneka livakale liyimfihlo kwaye liqhelekile. Nciphisa ukuxinana komntu anokukuthatha njengendlu yangasese okanye enye into. Gcina iswitshi yesibane kulula ukuyifumana. Tshintsha into enye ngexesha elinye, uyigcine kuphela ukuba umntu ukhululekile ngayo.

## Ndingathi ntoni xa umntu esenokufuna ukuya endlini yangasese?

Sebenzisa amagama aqhelekileyo abantu abadala nelizwi elizolileyo. Isicelo esifutshane njengelithi “Ungathanda ukusebenzisa indlu yangasese?” sinika umntu ithuba lokuphendula. Phepha ulwimi lwabantwana abancinci, ukuhlekisa, izikhumbuzo phambi kwabantu okanye ukubuza phambi kwabanye abantu ukuba oko kunokumhlazisa.

Abanye abantu babonisa imfuno ngezenzo endaweni yamagama. Banokuphazamiseka, bahambe-hambe, batsale impahla okanye baye kwigumbi eliqhelekileyo. Ezi mpawu aziyonyaniso ukuba umntu ufuna indlu yangasese, ngoko nika uncedo ngobubele uze uqwalasele impendulo yakhe.

Ukuba utyelelo lwendlu yangasese ngexesha eliqhelekileyo lusebenzela umntu, lunike njengenxalenye yosuku. Inkqubo ngumbiza, ayilomyalelo. Ukuba uyala, musa ukuxabana okanye ukumnyanzelisa ukuba ahlale. Linda, nika elinye ixesha ukuba efuna, uze ulandele iindlela eziqhelekileyo zomntu endaweni yokumisela uluhlu olungqongqo.

## Ndingaphendula njani emva kwengozi?

Gcina ilizwi lakho lithe cwaka uze ukhusele isidima somntu. Phepha ukugxeka, ukunyanya okanye ukumlungisa phambi kwabanye. Isivakalisi esilula esithi “Ndixelele ukuba yintoni enokunceda” sivumela umntu ukuba akukhokele. Nika iimpahla ezicocekileyo okanye indawo yangasese yokutshintsha, kodwa buza phambi kokunceda.

Nika umntu ixesha lokwenza amanyathelo anokuwakwazi. Angafuna ukutshintsha impahla yedwa, abe nomntu othembekileyo osondele okanye ashiywe yedwa okomzuzwana. Ukuba ufuna uncedo olusebenzayo, luchaze ngamanyathelo amafutshane uze uqinisekise ukuba ukhululekile. Musa ukumbamba okanye ukumkhawulezisa kumsebenzi wangasese.

Ungalungiselela iimpahla ezicocekileyo zokutshintsha okanye ibhegi engabonakaliyo yohambo ukuba umntu uyafuna oko. Buza phambi kokugcina iimveliso zokubamba kwindawo ekwabelwana ngayo okanye ukuxelela abanye abantu ngengozi. Ukuba iimveliso zokukhusela okanye ibhedi enokukhusela zinokunceda, buza ingcali yezempilo okanye umcebisi wokubamba ukuba ngawaphi ukhetho olufanelekileyo kwiimfuno nezinto umntu azithandayo.

## Ndinciphise iziselo okanye ndikhethe iimveliso zokubamba?

Musa ukunciphisa iziselo zomntu ukuthintela iingozi ngaphandle kokuba ingcali yakhe yezempilo inike icebiso elithile. I-Alzheimer's Association ilumkisa ukuba ukunciphisa iziselo kunokukhokelela ekuphelelweni ngamanzi omzimba nakwezinye iingxaki. Abantu abane-dementia banokufuna izikhumbuzo okanye uncedo lokusela ngokwaneleyo. Landela icebo lomntu ngamnye ngemida yeziselo.

Iipedi, impahla yangaphantsi ekhuselayo okanye ibhedi engangeni manzi ngukhetho lomntu. Zinokunceda abanye abantu, kodwa akukho mveliso inye eyanelisa wonke umntu. Thetha nogqirha okanye umcebisi wokubamba ngokhetho, ukulula, ukulingana, iinkxalabo zolusu nendlela umntu afuna ukuzisebenzisa ngayo. Musa ukufaka imveliso kumntu oyalayo; buza ukuba yintoni eya kwamkeleka.

## Ndicele nini uncedo lwengcali?

Buza ugqirha ngeengxaki ezintsha, ezipheleleyo okanye ezibuhlungu zendlu yangasese. Usulelo, ukuqhina, iziphumo zeyeza okanye enye ingxaki yempilo inokufuna ingqwalasela. Zisa inkcazo emfutshane yoko kutshintshileyo nokuba kwenzeka nini. Umcebisi wokubamba unokunceda ngokhetho olusebenzayo kwezinye iindawo; buza ugqirha ukuba ungafumana njani inkxaso yasekuhlaleni.

Funa uncedo lwezonyango ngoko nangoko ukuba umntu ngequbuliso ubhidekile. Ngezinye iinkxalabo zokubamba, qhagamshelana nengcali yezempilo ukufumana icebiso endaweni yokufumanisa isizathu ngokwakho. Musa ukunciphisa iziselo, ukutshintsha amayeza okanye ukuqala unyango ngokwenqaku lewebhusayithi.

## Ndingabelana njani ngomsebenzi ngentlonipho?

Ukuba abantu abaninzi bayanceda, buza umntu ukuba ukhululekile ukwabelana ngantoni. Inqaku eliyimfihlo ngamagama akhethayo, inkqubo nohlobo loncedo lunokuthintela ukuchaza ngokuphindaphindiweyo. Yabelana kuphela noko umncedisi olandelayo ekufuneka akwazi, uze uvuselele inqaku ukuba izinto azifunayo zomntu zitshintsha.

Uncedo lwendlu yangasese lunokudinisa nabanyamekeli. Cela umntu othembekileyo okanye inkonzo yokhathalelo ukuba athathe ithuba ukuba umntu uyavuma. [Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela komnyamekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sibandakanya iindlela zokufuna inkxaso nokuphumla. Ukhathalelo olunentlonipho lukhusela imfihlo yomntu namandla omnyamekeli okuqhubeka.

Injongo kukwenza uncedo lufumaneke ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Phendula ngokuzola, khusela imfihlo, gcina ukhetho lomntu uze ubuze ingcali ngeengxaki ezintsha.
