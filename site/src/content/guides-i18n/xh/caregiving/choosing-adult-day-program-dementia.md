Inkqubo yemini yabantu abadala inokunikela utshintsho lweendawo nenkxaso yemikhwa yemihla ngemihla. Inganika nomkhathaleli wosapho ixesha lomsebenzi, lemisebenzi emincinci okanye lokuphumla. Iinkqubo ziyahluka, kwaye iziko elifanele omnye umntu lingasifanele omnye. Intuthuzelo neminqweno yomntu kufuneka kukhokele isigqibo.

[Isishwankathelo se-Alzheimer's Association samaziko emini abantu abadala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/adult-day-centers) kunye ne-[luhlu lwayo lokukhetha iziko](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-an-adult-day-center-ts.pdf) zicebisa ukujonga iinkonzo, abasebenzi, imisebenzi, iindleko noluthutho phambi kokubhalisa. Eli phepha likunceda ulungiselele imibuzo; alinikeli mlinganiselo okanye isiphakamiso seziko elithile.

## Ndingagqiba njani ukuba ndizame inkqubo yemini?

Qala ngokubuza umntu into anokuyonwabela nohlobo loncedo angalwamkelayo. Angafuna umsebenzi wentlalo, isidlo kunye nabanye, inkampani ngoxa umkhathaleli esebenza, okanye enye into. Inkqubo ngukhetho, ayisiyo imfuneko.

Cinga ngohambo umntu afuna ukulwenza, imikhwa yakhe eqhelekileyo noncedo analufunayo emini. Cinga ukuba iiyure zeziko, uthutho nemisebenzi ihambelana na naloo mfuno. Ukuba umntu akathandi amaqela okanye udinwa kwindawo enamabhizi, buza ukuba ikhona na indawo ethe cwaka okanye utyelelo olufutshane.

Inkqubo inokunceda nomkhathaleli aphumle okanye alawule ezinye izinto. Oko kubalulekile, kodwa namava omntu abalulekile. Xoxani ngazo zozibini iimfuno ngokuvulekileyo, nifune ilungiselelo elamkelekileyo kubo bonke ababandakanyekileyo.

## Ndimele ndibuze ngeenkonzo zini?

Iinkonzo ziyahluka. Amanye amaziko agxininisa ubuhlobo nemisebenzi; amanye angabonelela ngokutya, uthutho, ukhathalelo lobuqu okanye iinkonzo ezinxulumene nempilo. Buza ukuba ziziphi iinkonzo ezikhoyo ngeentsuku ekuya kuthi umntu azohlala kuzo nokuba abasebenzi banokuxhasa iimfuno umntu anazo ngoku.

Yazi ngemisebenzi, hayi nje isihloko sohlelo. Buza ukuba abathathi-nxaxheba benza ntoni ngokwenene, ukuba banokukhetha phakathi kweendlela, nokuba inkqubo ibandakanya izinto ezaziwayo ezithandwayo njengomculo, ubugcisa, ukulima okanye incoko. Umntu akanyanzelekanga ukuba abandakanyeke kuyo yonke imisebenzi. Buza ukuba indawo ethe cwaka okanye ixesha lokuphumla likhona.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo ngamayeza, ukuhamba, ukutya okanye ukhathalelo lobuqu, buza ngokuthe ngqo ukuba ngubani onikela loo nkxaso nokuba uqeqeshwe njani. Musa ukucinga ukuba inkqubo yemini inikela ukhathalelo lwezonyango okanye inokujongana nazo zonke iimfuno. Qinisekisa nayiphi na imiyalelo yezempilo neqela lokukhathalela lomntu.

## Ndingajonga ntoni ngexesha lotyelelo?

Cwangcisa utyelelo, uze ukuba umntu ufuna, mzise kunye. Qaphela ukuba abasebenzi bathetha ngembeko, banika abathathi-nxaxheba ixesha lokuphendula, kwaye bamema abantu ukuba babandakanyeke ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Funa imisebenzi ehambelana nezinto ezithandwa ngumntu namandla akhe, hayi nje ikhalenda eneminyaka emininzi.

Buza ukuba iziko liyifunda njani into ethandwa ngumntu ngamnye nokuba liphendula njani xa umntu edinwa, enexhala okanye efuna ukuhamba. Fumanisa oko iziko elikwenzayo kwimeko eyingxamisekileyo, ukuba usapho lufumana njani iindaba ezintsha, nokuba kuqhagamshelwana nabani ukuba kukho inkxalabo.

Ukuba kunokwenzeka, tyelela ngexesha lomsebenzi okanye isidlo. Qaphela ukuba indawo imvakalisa ukuba ikhululekile kumntu, ukuba kulula ukuhamba, nokuba abasebenzi bakhona na. Ibhrosha okanye uhambo lokubonisa lunokukunceda ulungiselele imibuzo, kodwa ukubona usuku oluqhelekileyo kunokukuxelela okungakumbi.

## Ziziphi iinkcukacha ezisebenzayo ekufuneka ndizijonge?

Buza ngeeyure, iintsuku, iintlawulo, ubuncinane bokuya, imithetho yokurhoxisa nayiphi na intlawulo eyongezelelweyo. Buza ukuba ziziphi izidlo okanye izidlo ezincinci ezibandakanyiweyo nokuba ukutya okukhethekileyo kunokuxhaswa. Iindleko neenkonzo ziyahluka ngokwendawo, ngoko cela iziko licacise imiqathango yalo ebhaliweyo.

Fumanisa ukuba uthutho lukhona, luyatyeba kangakanani nokuba luyamfanela umntu. Buza ngamaxesha okuthatha, uncedo lokunyuka nokuhla, nokuba kwenzeka ntoni ukuba umqhubi uyalibaziseka. Ukuba ilungu losapho liza kunikela ukuhamba, bandakanya eli xesha neendleko kwisicwangciso sakho.

Buza ukuba iziko linokunikela inkxaso umntu ayidingayo ukuba impilo okanye amandla akhe emihla ngemihla ayatshintsha. Ukuba iziko alikwazi ukuhlangabezana naloo mfuno, buza ukuba lisiphakamisa ntoni. [Isikhokelo sokhathalelo ekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) sicubungula olunye uhlobo lwenkxaso olunokufanela ezinye iintsapho.

## Umntu angayizama njani inkqubo ngaphandle koxinzelelo?

Buza ukuba iziko linikela utyelelo olufutshane lokwazisa, isidlo okanye umsebenzi. Mvumele umntu akhethe ukuba uzama ntoni nokuba uhlala ixesha elingakanani xa kunokwenzeka. Utyelelo lokuqala lunokuvakala lungaqhelekanga. Buza umntu emva koko ukuba ukuthandile ntoni, ungakuthandanga okanye ufuna ukutshintsha ntoni.

Ukuba umntu uyathandabuza, funa ukwazi ukuba kungoba kutheni. Uhambo, ingxolo, abantu abangaziwayo okanye umsebenzi othile unokuba yinkxalabo. Ungabuza iziko ukuba utshintsho oluncinci lunokwenzeka, okanye utyelele ngolunye usuku. Musa ukusebenzisa ityala okanye ukunyanzelisa ukwenza umntu aye.

Emva kotyelelo oluthile, thetha nomntu nabasebenzi beziko ngendlela esebenza ngayo le ndlela. Inkqubo elungileyo ingafuna ukulungiswa kwexesha, imisebenzi okanye inkxaso. Ukuba umntu usaxhalabile okanye inkonzo ayifanelekanga, kuyavakala ukuphinda ucinge.

## Ndingalungelelanisa njani neziko?

Ngemvume yomntu, yabelana ngolwazi olunceda abasebenzi bamxhase: igama analithandayo, indlela yakhe yokunxibelelana, imikhwa, izinto azithandayo nohlobo loncedo angalwamkelayo. Buza ukuba iziko ligcina njani olu lwazi luyimfihlo nokuba iindaba ezintsha zabelwana njani nosapho.

Buza ukuba kuqhagamshelwana nabani ukuba umntu uyagula, akabikho okanye ufuna utshintsho kwinkxaso. Gcina isicwangciso sokungeniswa xa inkqubo ivaliwe okanye uthutho lungekho. Ukuba abantu abaliqela bayanceda, vumelanani ukuba ngubani oza kujongana nohambo ngalunye nolwazi olutsha.

Inkqubo yemini yabantu abadala inokuba yinxalenye enye yesicwangciso esibanzi senkxaso. [Isikhokelo sokuzikhathalela komkhathaleli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) sibandakanya iindlela zokulungiselela ukuphumla, kwaye [imisebenzi yamaqela asezindlini zokukhathalela](/activities/care-home-groups/) inikela iingcamango zemisebenzi elula yeqela.

Inkqubo elungileyo yeyo umntu afuna ukuyizama kwaye inokuxhasa iimfuno zakhe. Tyelela, buza imibuzo ethe ngqo uze wenze utshintsho ngokuxhomekeke kwindlela umntu azive ngayo.
