Inkqubo yemini yabantu abadala inokunika utshintsho lwendawo kunye nenkxaso kwimigaqo yemihla ngemihla. Inokunika nomnakekeli wosapho ixesha lomsebenzi, izinto zokwenza ngaphandle kwekhaya okanye ukuphumla. Iinkqubo ziyahluka, kwaye isikhululo esilungele umntu omnye asenakulungela omnye. Iminqweno nentando yomntu kufuneka ibe yeyona nto ikhokela isigqibo.

[Isishwankathelo se-Alzheimer's Association sezikhululo zemini zabantu abadala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/adult-day-centers) kunye [noluhlu lokukhetha isikhululo](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-an-adult-day-center-ts.pdf) zicebisa ukuhlola iinkonzo, abasebenzi, imisebenzi, iindleko kunye nokuthuthwa ngaphambi kokubhalisa. Eli phepha likunceda ulungiselele imibuzo; alikhuphisani okanye alincomi isikhululo esithile.

## Ndingazi njani ukuba ndikhangele inkqubo yemini?

Qala ngokubuza umntu ukuba yintoni angathanda ukuyenza kwaye luhlobo luphi uncedo angayamkela. Usenokufuna imbambano yokuzonwabisa, isidlo kunye nabanye, okanye ukuba nomntu xa umnakekeli esebenza. Inkqubo yinto yokhetho, hayi imfuneko.

Cinga ngeendawo umntu azifuna ukuya kuzo, iinkqubo zakhe zemihla ngemihla kunye nenkxaso afuna emini. Qwalasela ukuba amaxesha esikhululo, ukuthuthwa kunye neemisebenzi zilingana nezo mfuno. Ukuba umntu akathandi amaqela okanye udinwa xa kukho ingxolo, buza ukuba kukho ukhetho olunekhwiet okanye ityelelo elifutshane.

Inkqubo inokunceda nomnakekeli aphumle okanye alawule ezinye izinto zakhe. Oko kubalulekile, kodwa nolwamkelo lomntu nalo lubalulekile. Xoxa ngezi zinto zombini ngokufanelekileyo kwaye ufumane isigqibo esamkelekileyo kubo bonke abanxulumeneyo.

## Zeziphi iinkonzo ekufuneka ndizibuze?

Iinkonzo ziyahluka. Ezinye izikhululo zigxile ekuhlaleni kunye nemisebenzi; ezinye zinokubonelela ngezidlo, ukuthuthwa, ukunakekelwa komntu okanye iinkonzo zempilo. Buza ukuba zeziphi iinkonzo ezikhoyo ngemihla umntu aya kuba ngayo kwaye ukuba abasebenzi bangalungiselela iimfuno zakhe zangoku.

Fumana ulwazi malunga nemisebenzi, hayi nje igama lesicwangciso. Buza ukuba abathathi-nxaxheba benza ntoni ngokwenene, ukuba banokukhetha phakathi kwezinto kunye nokuba inkqubo iquka izinto ezaziwayo ezinje ngomculo, ubugcisa, ukulima okanye incoko. Umntu akafuneki ukuthatha inxaxheba kuyo yonke imisebenzi. Buza ukuba kukho indawo ethe cwaka okanye ixesha lokuphumla.

Ukuba umntu ufuna uncedo malunga nonyango, ukuhamba, ukutya okanye unakekelo lomzimba, buza ngokucacileyo ukuba ngubani ophatha loo nkxaso kunye nokuba ufundile ntoni. Musa ukucinga ukuba inkqubo yemini inikela unyango okanye inokuphatha yonke imfuneko. Qinisekisa nawuphi na umyalelo wezempilo kunye neqela lonyango lomntu.

## Yintoni engajongwa ngexesha lotyelelo?

Cwangcisa ukutyelela kwaye, ukuba umntu uyafuna, mkhwelele. Qaphela ukuba abasebenzi bathetha ngentlonipho, banike abathathi-nxaxheba ixesha lokuphendula kwaye babamemele ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Jonga imisebenzi ehambelana nezithakazelo nezakhono zomntu, hayi nje ikhalenda enemisebenzi emininzi.

Buza ukuba isikhululo sifunda njani ngokhetho lomntu ngamnye nokuba siphendula njani xa umntu edinwa, exhalabile okanye efuna ukuhamba. Fumanisa ukuba isikhululo senza ntoni ngexesha lengxaki, indlela usapho olufumana ngayo izaziso nokuba ngubani ofanele ukuqhagamshelwa xa kukho inkxalabo.

Ukuba kunokwenzeka, tyelela ngexesha lomsebenzi okanye lesidlo. Qaphela ukuba indawo ilula kumntu, ukuba kulula ukuhamba kuyo kunye nokuba abasebenzi bafumaneka. Incwadana okanye uhambo lunokukunceda ulungiselele imibuzo, kodwa ukubona usuku oluqhelekileyo kunokukuxelela okungakumbi.

## Ziziphi iinkcukacha ezisebenzayo ekufuneka ndizihlolile?

Buza ngamaxesha, ngemihla, ngeentlawulo, ngeemfuno zokufika ubuncinane, ngemigaqo yokurhoxisa nangazo naziphi na iindleko ezongezelelweyo. Buza ukuba zeziphi izidlo okanye iziqhamo ezifakiwe kunye nokuba ukutya okukhethekileyo kunokuxhaswa na. Iindleko neenkonzo ziyahluka ngendawo, ngoko cela isikhululo sichaze imigaqo yaso ebhalwe phantsi.

Fumanisa ukuba kukho uthutho na, lubiza kangakanani kwaye ukuba lulungele umntu na. Buza ngamaxesha okuthatha, uncedo lokungena nokuphuma emotweni, nokuba kwenzeka ntoni xa umqhubi elibele. Ukuba usapho luzakubhekela iintsuku, faka leyo ixesha kunye nale ndleko kwicwangciso yakho.

Buza ukuba isikhululo sinokuhlangabezana noncedo umntu alufunayo ukuba impilo yakhe okanye izakhono zakhe zemihla ngemihla ziyatshintsha. Ukuba isikhululo asinakuhlangabezana neemfuno ezo, buza ukuba sincoma yiphi na enye indawo okanye enye indlela. [Isikhokelo sonakekelo lwasekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) sichaza olunye uhlobo lwenkxaso oluthi lulungele ezinye iintsapho.

## Umntu angazama njani inkqubo ngaphandle koxinzelelo?

Buza ukuba isikhululo sinikezela na ngotyelelo olufutshane, isidlo okanye umsebenzi wokwazisa. Mnike umntu ukhetho lokuzama nokuba uzakuhlala ixesha elingakanani xa kunokwenzeka. Utyelelo lokuqala lunokuziva lungajwayelekanga. Buza umntu emva koko ukuba yintoni awayithandayo, awayingathandayo okanye awayiguqula.

Ukuba umntu uyangazi, fumanisa isizathu. Ukuhamba, ingxolo, abantu abangajwayelekanga okanye umsebenzi othile unokuba yinkxalabo. Unokubuza isikhululo ukuba kukho utshintsho olusincinci olunokwenzeka okanye ukuba unokutyelela ngenye imini. Musa ukusebenzisa ukuziva unetyala okanye ukunyanzela ukuze umphe umntu ukuba aye.

Emva kwamatyelelo ambalwa, thetha nomntu nabasebenzi besikhululo malunga nendlela olu lungelelwano olusebenza ngayo. Inkqubo elungileyo ingafuna utshintsho kwixesha, kwimisebenzi okanye kwinkxaso. Ukuba umntu usahlala enexhala okanye ukuba inkonzo ayifanelanga, kuyinto eqondakalayo ukuphinda uqwalasele.

## Ndingasebenzisana njani nesikhululo?

Ngemvume yomntu, yabelana ngolwazi oluncedayo abasebenzi ukuba bamncedise: igama analithandayo, indlela athetha ngayo, iinkqubo zakhe, izithakazelo zakhe kunye nolo hlobo loncedo alwamkelayo. Buza ukuba isikhululo silugcina njani olu lwazi lungabonakali kwabanye nokuba zabelwa njani izaziso kusapho.

Buza ukuba ngubani oza kuqhagamshelana naye ukuba umntu ugulile, akhoyo okanye ufuna utshintsho kuncedo. Gcina icebo lokusebenza lokuba inkqubo ivalwe okanye uthutho lungafumaneki. Ukuba abantu abaninzi bayanceda, vumelana ukuba ngubani oza kuphatha ngamnye uthutho kunye nesaziso.

Inkqubo yemini inokuba yinxalenye yenkxaso ebanzi. Isikhokelo [sokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) siquka iindlela zokulungelelanisa iikhefu, kwaye [imisebenzi yamaqela ezindlu zonakekelo](/activities/care-home-groups/) inikezela ngeengcamango zemisebenzi yeqela elilula.

Inkqubo elungileyo kukuba umntu afune ukuyizama kwaye ikwazi ukuxhasa iimfuno zakhe. Tyelela, buza imibuzo ngqo kwaye wenze utshintsho ngokwendlela umntu azive ngayo.
