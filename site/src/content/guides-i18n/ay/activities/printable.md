Awisax juk'amp sum lurawix phayañ uta mesana mä laphimpi lapicerompi anattañakiwa. Liq'it laphinakax janiw cargador munaskiti, janiw contraseñanaks munaskiti, janiw pantallanaks munaskiti, ukat tumpasiwirisa jan ukax tama taypiris jasakiw apasiñataki. Aka laphinx sitioxankir inaki liq'iñ lurawinakatwa qhanañchi, kunjamsa sum uñjañatakisa katt'asiñatakis liq'iñasa, ukat utan lurt'at laphinakatwa parlapxaraktana kawkïrinaktix wilanakampi irpirinakampi wali apnaqapxi.

## Kuna inaki liq'iñ lurawinakas utji?

Pusi inaki liq'iñ lurawinak churapxsma. Kawkïr anat laphirus mantanma ukat **Print** botonan limt'am sum liq'it laphipsa katuqañataki.

| Liq'iñ lurawi | Kunas ukaxa | Kawkits aski |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Word Search](/games/word-search/) | Jach'a qillqampi aru thaqhaña, arunakax ampar tuqirus aynach tuqirus sarakiwa | Lapicerompi amuki pachanakataki; puzle anatt'awinak muniri jaqitaki |
| [Coloring](/games/coloring/) | Samichañ laphanaka liq'iñataki, jan ukax pantalla tuqi samichañataki | Samart'añataki, lurt'asiñ pachanakataki; kawkïr pachatakis yanapampi |
| [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) | Jamuqanaka aruskipäw qalltañatak tema tuqita, janiw yatxatäwikiti | Tumpasiñataki, jawsañataki, manq'añ pachanakataki, amtäw aruskipir tamanakataki |
| [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) | Jakhuñanakat sipansa jamuqanakani bingo tarjetanaka, pantalla tuqi jawsirimpi | Uñjañ utanakataki, urun tantachäwinakataki, wila masinak tantachasiñataki |

### Jach'a qillqampi aru thaqhaña

[Word Search](/games/word-search/) ukax jach'a qillqanakampi luratawa, ukat arunakax ampar tuqirus aynach tuqirus sarakiwa, janiw k'umu tuqirus jan ukax qhipa tuqirus sarkiti. Panpachan lurapxam: maynix lista taypit aru liyi, maynisti thaqharaki. Arunaka jikiñax ch'amakïspa ukhaxa, sapa arun nayrïr qillqap muyuntapxam, jan ukax listankir arunaka arupampi liyisax uka arunakat aruskipapxasmawa.

### Samichañ laphanaka

[Coloring](/games/coloring/) laphanakax liq'itasispawa plumonanakampi jan ukax lápiz de coloranakampi samichañataki, jan ukax mä tabletan samichasisparakiwa mä sami llimt'asa, ukxarux jamuqan mayjt'ir chiqa llimt'asa. Liq'it laphanakatakixa, thuru plumonanaka ukat juk'a saminaka mayaki churam. Uñjam jiwasan [amparampi lurawinaka uñt'ayaña](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) ukana kunjamtix samichawix jach'a jaqinakataki ukat sum samart'at lurasispa.

### Aruskipañ tarjetanaka

[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ukax mä qhana jamuqa mä jawillt'ir arumpi chikt'ayi, sañäni: "Aka tuqit parlt'ita..." jan ukax "¿Kunas uñjasta?" ukhamanaka. Jawillt'äwinakjam qillqatawa, janiw jiskt'äwjam yant'añatakikiti. Mä grupo liq'im, kharisum, ukat bolsar ucht'asim tumpañatakixa jan ukax teléfono jak'ar ucht'asim jawsañatakixa. Ukhamarakiw [nayra amtäwinaka](/activities/reminiscence/) tantachäwinakans wali aski.

### Jamuqampi bingo tarjetanaka

[Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) tarjetanakax jakhuñanakat sipansa jamuqanaka apnaqi. Sapa anatt'iritak mä tarjeta liq'im ukat pantalla tuqi jawsirx laptop, TV jan ukax proyectoran apnaqam. Taqini tarjetap phuqhanchapxani ukkam jawsaskakiñawa iwxt'apxsma, ukhamat jan khitis qhiparxiti. Uñjam [uñjañ utanakatak tama lurawinaka](/activities/care-home-groups/) kunjams mä tantachäwi apasiñapa.

## Kunjamsa lurawinakx liq'iñasa jasak apnaqañapataki?

Liq'iñ pachan juk'a mayjt'ayäwinakax wali askisiwa.

### Sum uñjañjam luram

- **Jach'a, qhana qillqa.** Kawkïr jach'a qillqampi laphir purapxi ukxampi liq'im. K'achachata jan ukax ampar qillqanaka janiw apnaqañakiti.
- **Qhana mayj mayj saminaka.** Ch'iyar tinta janq'u jan ukax crema papilana. Jan qhana uqi jan ukax saminaka manqhar uchañakiti.
- **Jan phillqir papila** ukax qhana k'ajata jisk'achayi.
- **Lentes ukat qhananak uñjam.** Alzheimer's Association ukax qhanañchiw uñjaña ukat ist'añ jan walt'äwinakax aruskipäwr ch'amakiyaspawa, ukat sapa kuti uñjayasiñ iwxt'i. Mä qhana, kusiskaña lamparasa yanapt'arakispawa.

### Jasak katt'asiñjam luram

- **K'añu papila jan ukax cartulina** ukax pampaki sayt'asi, jasakiw apthapiñataki, ukat walja kuti apnaqañatak sum sayt'i.
- **Mä tabla** jan ukax jan llust'ir tapetex laphir janiw llust'aykiti.
- **Thuru plumonanaka jan ukax lápices** ukax juk'amp jasakiw katthapiñataki jisk'a lapicerot sipansa.

### Wasitamp apnaqañjam luram

- **Laphinak plastificam** jan ukax qhana laphinakar mantanayam. Chhaqhayir plumón aru thaqhañanakarusa bingo tarjetanakarus apnaqam, ukatx phiskhursum.
- **Jan phillqir laphinaka ajllim** qhanax nayr ust'aychi ukhaxa, ukat tijerampi **tarjetan k'uchunakap murut'am** jan llamkt'asis kharsusiñataki.
- **Mä carpeta** munat laphinakataki katuqam, ukhamat jan sapa siman kipka laphiki liq'iskakiñataki.

### Jasak uywata uñjam

- **Sapa laphina mä lurawiki.** Mä aru thaqhañaki jan ukax mä jamuqaki, janiw walja lurawinaka mayachatañapakiti.
- **Walja ch'usa chiqanaka.** Sintipuni phuqht'at laphix llakisiwayaspawa.
- **Jan liyiñ iwxt'anaka ucham, kawkirutsjama.** Jan ukasti jupa pachpar uñacht'ayam kuna lurañsa.
- **Laphinaka jisk'aptayam.** Taqpach laphix sintïchi ukhaxa, chikatat khart'am jan ukax k'umt'am mä chiqaki uñstayañataki.

## Yaqha utan lurañ aski liq'itanaka

Janiw taqi aski liq'it laphinakas anatt'awikïkiti. Akanakax mä computadorampi ukat liq'irimpi jasak lurt'atawa.

### Jakäwipat uñt'ayañ laphi

Mä sapa laphina "nayax khitïtwa" sasa, mä fotompi, sutitix jupa sutinchayasiñ munki ukampi, kawkints jilsüna, irnaqäwipa, wila masinaka, munat manq'anaka, jayllinaka ukat kawkïrinakas kusisiyi jan ukax llakisiya ukampi. Dementia Australia ukax [memory book](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/visiting-someone-dementia) iwxt'i, ukanxa jakäwipat, munañanakapat ukat wakiskir jaqinakapata qillqt'atawa, tumpirinakaru ukat uñjirnakaru yanapt'añataki ukat aruskipäwinak qalltañataki.

Wali askisiw qhulla utanakana ukat machaq uñjañ chiqanakana. NIA ukax iwxt'iwa hospitalankir irpirinakarux [personal information sheet](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/taking-person-alzheimers-disease-hospital) churaña, kawkintix uruntat lurawipa, sutip satasïwi, munañanakapa ukat jan munañanakapa, llakisiyirinaka, ukat jan arumpi usut uñacht'ayirinakapa uñstayi.

### Urun lurawinakapa

Mä jach'a, jasak uruntat lurawi pachat pachampi, juk'a arunakampi ukat sapa chiqatak jisk'a jamuqampi: alwaw manq'a, sarnaqata, chika uru manq'a, samaraña, jayllinak ist'aña, jayp'u manq'a. Alzheimer's Association-an [daily care plan](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) ukax amtiw uñjat jaqin sum jikxatasiñ pachanakaparjama luraña, sartasiña ukat ikiñ urasanak sum uñjasa, ukat lurawimpi samart'awimpi chikachasa. Nayraqatan uñjañ chiqaru ucham, refrigerador patxaru, ukat mayjt'aykayakiñapawa. Uñjam jiwasan [daily routine guide](/activities/daily-routine/) yaqhanak yatxatañataki.

### Utanakataki ukat cajonanakatak jamuqanaka

NIA ukax qhana saminani sanalanaka jan ukax jasak jamuqanak [label the bathroom, bedroom and kitchen](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips) iwxt'i. Ukhamarakiw cajonanakatakisa estantenakatakis walïski: calzoncillo jamuqa cajonapan, vasunaka vasu uchañana. Liq'im, plastificam ukat cintampi lip'katam.

### Chikt'ir laphinaka jaljaña

Pä categoría jamuqanaka liq'im cartulinana, sañäni achunakampi panqaranaka jan ukax herramintanakampi phayañ yänaka. Kharisum ukat panpacha pä canastar jaljtapxam. Aka pantalla tuqinkir lurawisti [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) satawa.

### Jayllinak laphinaka

Munat jayllinakan arunakap jach'apuni qillqampi liq'im chikt'ata q'uchuñataki. Khitinakatix janiw sinti liyxapkiti ukanakas laphir katthapisax kusisipxaspawa.

### Wila masinakan fotopa laphi

Mä sapa laphina pusi jan ukax suxta jach'a wila masinakan fotopampi, sapa maynixa sutip manqhankani. Mesan uchasispa kawkïr jaqis apthapiñapataki ukat aruskipañataki. Sutinakaxa tumpirinakaru ukat irpirinakarux aruskipäwir yatiyañ yanapt'i, ukat janiw khitis "¿Khitïns akaxa?" sasin jiskt'askapuniti.

### Manq'a phayañ yatichawi tarjeta

Mä munat wila manq'a phayañ qillqata jach'a qillqampi liq'im. Panpacha phayañataki apnaqapxam, jan ukax nayra manq'anakat aruskipañatakiki.

## Kunjamsa mä liq'it laphix churasiñapa jan tarea uñtatïñapataki?

Chikancht'asiñ jamuw churasiñapa, janiw mä examen uñtatäkiti.

- **Qunt'asim ukat jumapachp qalltam.** "Aka aru thaqhañ lurasktha. ¿'Jardín' thaqhañ yanapt'itasmati?"
- **Panpachan irnaqt'apxam.** Turnonakampi, jan ukax maynix jiki ukat maynisti muyuntayi.
- **Janiw waliski jan ukax pantasiwa sañakiti.** Alzheimer's Association-an irpïripax [activities at home](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/5337a7c3-15c6-4b4d-a3a0-ca00a0839a6b/alzheimers-dementia-activities-at-home-ts.pdf) tuqinx iwxarakiwa kuna lurasiskis ukar chuym uchaña, janiw tukuyäwirukikiti, ukat kusisiñar chuym churaña, janiw atipjañarukikiti.
- **Laphinaka uñt'ayat jaytam.** Uka irpiri pachparakiw amti fotonaka, libronaka jan ukax nayra revistanaka jasak katuyasiñ chiqanakar uchaña. Liq'it tarjetanaka ukat foto laphanakas mesa patxaru uchañjamarakiwa.
- **Munam ukhax sayt'am.** Chikat samichat laphix mä sum jayp'uwa. Laphir apthapisax mä té umt'asiñasa walikiskiwa.
