Walja arunakan parliri jaqinakax, dementia ukax arunak jikxatañ jan ukax amuyañ mayjtʼkchi ukhaxa, yaqha kunjamaw arsupxaspa. Yaqhipanakax nayra yatiqat aruparuw parlapxaspa; yaqhipanakasti janiw ukhamäpkiti. Jilpachax wali aski luräwix jichhax kawkïr arump parlaskañ aski jikxatasi uk jisktʼaña, ukat jaqen munasiñaparjam sarantaña.

[Alzheimer's Society ukax jaqinakan aru munasiñapax mayjtʼasispawa sasaw si, ukat jaqex sapüru kawkïr arump parlaski uka arump parlxañ amti](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association ukax nayra arupar kuttʼañax mä arsuñ mayjtʼäwiw sasaw qhananchi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Aka mayjtʼäwinakax janiw taqinir uñjapkiti, ukat janiw jaqen kunjamäskis uk uñachtʼaykiti.

## ¿Kawkïr arump parlxañasajja?

Aka aruskipäwitakix kawkïr arump parlaskañ munki uk jisktʼam. Kawkïr tuqit parlañ munapkiti, kawkharutï jikxasipki ukat urun kunäjapki ukat sipansa, munasiñapax mayjtʼasispawa. Aru mayjtʼi ukhajja, janiw yantʼäwjam uñjañakiti jan ukax juma amuykta uka arumpikiw parlarakiñapa sas jan saytʼayañakiti.

Munaski uka aru yatsta ukhajja, uñtʼat arunak apnaqam ukat jaysañapatak suytʼam. Walja arunak jan yatkstaxa ukhajja, chiqaparuw yatiyam ukat ampar luqtäwinakampi, yänanakampi jan ukax jamuqanakampi yanaptʼam. [Alzheimer's Society ukan aru tuqit irnaqtʼäwipax](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) arsuñ yatiñax urut urur mayjtʼasispawa, ukat qaritäwi, usunta jan ukax usu ukamp jan waltʼayasispawa sasaw qhananchi.

## ¿Sapüru aruskipäwin kunas yanaptʼaspa?

Sapüru arsuñataki amtanak amtapxam, ukat jaqen aruparjam yanaptʼäw yapxatam:

- **Uñtʼat arunakampi sutinakamp apnaqam.** Sapüru yänanakataki, familianakataki ukat sapüru luräwinakataki kawkïr arunak apnaqaspa uk jaqer jan ukax confiykañ jaqir jisktʼam.
- **Arsuñamp chikax uñachtʼayarakim.** Mä tʼantar luqhtam, pä ajlliñ uñachtʼayam jan ukax mä uñtʼat jamuq jan ukax yä apnaqam. Askir ar jikxatañ chʼamäkchi ukhajja, ampar luqtʼäwix yanaptʼirakispawa.
- **Suytʼam.** Jaqejj istʼañapataki ukat jaysañapatak pacha churam. Sapa arsuñ janiw tukuyxañakiti jan ukax walja arunakan mayni pachpa jisktʼäw wasitampi janiw jisktʼañakiti.
- **Mä sapa amuytʼäw apnaqam.** Jaya arsuñax kawkïr arutï parlaski ukat chʼamäkaspawa. Jiskʼa arump arsum, samartʼam, ukat jukʼamp istʼañ munaspa janicha uk uñjam.
- **Istʼañ ukat uñjañ uñakipam.** Nayranak uñjañataki, istʼañ yanaptʼirinaka, suma qhana ukat samara chiqawjja aru amuyañax jasakïspawa.

Jaqejj ullañ munaspa ukhajja, wakiskir qillqatanakax jupan arupankiti janicha uk jisktʼam. Qullañataki amtañatakixa, iyawsäwitakixa jan ukax qullanakataki kamachinakatakixa, janiw jupan pachpa jaqukipir qillqatataki atinisipkiti; wakiskir yatiyäwinakax qullir irnaqirinakamp chiqapar uñakipatañapawa.

## ¿Jaqen munaski uka aru jan yatkta ukhajja?

Sapüru aruskipäwitakix mä confiykañ paya ar yatiri masip jan ukax familianakap mantir munaspa janicha uk jisktʼam. Janiw mayni jaqen arstʼiripäñap munaspa ukhama amuytʼamti; ampar luqtäwinakampi, jamuqanakampi jan ukax mayni yaqha arumpi chik parlir munaspa.

Qulliri jawstʼäwitak jan ukax chiqapa yatiyäw munasir yaqha aruskipäwitakixa, qullañ utar jan ukax yanaptʼir utanakar nayraqat jawsam. Jaqen munaski uka aru yatiyam ukat arstʼäw yanaptʼäw utjiti janicha uk jisktʼam. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services ukax qhanañchi](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) familianakasa masinakas wakiskir qullañ yatiyäwinak chiqapar jan arstʼapkaspa; ukatwa uka yanaptʼir uta ajlliñanakapampi markapan kamachinakapampi qhanañchaspa. Jaqew yanaptʼir jaqi jupanamp chik sarañ munaspa janicha uk ajlliñapataki, ukat qulliri irnaqiriw munasir aru yanaptʼäw wakichtʼaspa.

Jawstʼäwinxa, jaqeruw chiqapar parlañama ukat arstʼirirux parlasiñapatak suytʼam. Jisktʼäw amuyaspa ukat jupan pachpa arunakapamp jaysañapatak pacha utjaspa uk yatiqam. Jupat yanaptʼassta ukhajja, jupa munaski ukhakiw jukʼamp yatiyäw yapxatañama.

## ¿Kunawsatix qullañ yanaptʼ thaqhañäni?

Arsuñ jan waltʼäwinakax pachat pacharuw mayjtʼaspa, ukampis akatjamat mayjtʼäwix mayjawa. [NHS ukax maynejj akatjamat chʼamañchtʼata uñjasi ukhajja jankʼak qullañ yanaptʼ thaqhañ amti](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Akatjamat arsuñ chhaqtäwix dementiapakiw sasin janiw amuytʼañakiti. Jaqejj akatjamat chʼamañchtʼata uñjaschi, sinti usuntata jan ukax ikiñatak chʼamäkchi ukhajja, markaman jankʼaki qullañ yanaptʼir jawstʼawir jawsam.

Jukʼat jukʼat mayjtʼäwinakatakix, kuntï uñjsta uk qillqtʼam ukat jaqen qulliripar amuytʼäw mayim. Kawkïr arump parlaskäna, kunas arsuñapatak yanaptʼäna ukat qaritata jan ukax usuntatäkäna uk qillqtʼam. Akax aruskiptʼañatak qillqatawa, janiw qullañ amuytʼäwkiti. [Qulliri jawstʼäwitak irnaqtʼäwi](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) jisktʼäwinak wakichtʼañatak yanaptʼiristamwa.

## ¿Yaqha yanaptʼirinakar kuns yatiyasna?

Jaqen iyawsäwipampixa, jupanakamp chik chikañ munapk ukanakatak mä jiskʼa qillqat imapxam. Uka qillqatanxa arsuñataki ukat ullañataki kawkïr aru jan ukax kawkïr parlañ amtki uk, uñtʼat arunak jan ukax sutinak ukat aruskipäw amuyañapatak kunas yanaptʼi uk qillqatasispawa. Qullañ jawstʼäwinakar jan ukax uywäw yatiyäwinakar khitis mantir munaspa uk jisktʼam. Janiw yanaptʼañatak jan wakiskir jaqen yatiyäwinakap qillqañakiti, ukat khitinakas uka qillqat uñjapxaspa uk yatiyam. Aru tuqit qillqatax jaqinakarux jukʼamp samartʼat aruskipañ qalltañapatakiw yanaptʼi; ukampis janiw chʼama qullañ aruskipäwitak arstʼäwir lantikiti.

## ¿Kunjamsa jaqen ajlliñanakap yanaptʼsna?

Arux jaqen mayachasiñapatak mä tuqiwa, ukampis janiw taqpach jupäkiti. Sapüru luräwinakap, munasiñanakap, ullañ munasiñanakap ukat khitinakamp chik chikañ munaspa uk jisktʼam. Jaqex iyawsächi ukhajja, yaqha yanaptʼirinakatak yanaptʼir arunak jan ukax arsuñanak qillqtʼam ukat kunjams apnaqañapa uk qhanañcham. Qillqatanak imantatäñapawa ukat jupa iyawski ukakiw yatiyañama.

Jaqerux yantʼasiñapataki, yaqhanakatak arstʼañapataki jan ukax kuns amuyi uk uñachtʼayañapatak janiw saytʼayañakiti. Yaqhip aruskipäwinakax mä arun jasakïspawa, yaqhipanakasti mayni arunwa. Jaqen samara jikxatasiñapa, jupankankañ yatiyäwinakapa ukat ajlliñanakapaw jukʼamp wakisi, mä sapa arun qhiparañat sipansa.

Jiwasan [arsuñataki amtanakajax](/caregiving/communication-tips/) jiskʼa arump arsuña, istʼaña ukat jamuqanak apnaqañ tuqit yanaptʼi. Dementia amuytʼäwi tukuyatat qhiphar wakichtʼäwitak yanaptʼ thaqhaskta ukhajja, [dementia amuytʼäwi tukuyatat qhiphar nayrïr sarawinaka](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) uñakipam.
