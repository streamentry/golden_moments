Dementia bɛma ɛyɛ den sɛ obi bɛhwehwɛ asɛm, bɛte nkyerɛkyerɛ tenten, anaa obi bɛtie nneɛma a ɛreyɛ ɔfa ho. Eyi nkyerɛ sɛ obi no ayɛ ne ho sɛ ɔmpɛ sɛ ɔka ho asɛm bio. Sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nkɔmmɔ, sɛnea wobɛka, ne sɛnea wobɛtie no mu nsakrae akitikitiw betumi ama kasa nyɛ den na ɛma mo nyinaa adwene ayɛ mmerɛ.

## Dɛn ne nkɔmmɔ akwan a ɛho hia paa sɛ wobɛkasa ne obi a ɔwɔ dementia?

Hwehwɛ sɛ wɔte wo asɛm, ma nsɛm nyɛ den, na fa boasetɔ di. Sɛ wɔkae nneɛma abiɛsa nko a:

1. **Ma adan no nni nnyiyimu na hwɛ wɔn anim.** Yi TV no, fa wo ani nhyɛ wɔn ani so, na frɛ wɔn din.
2. **Ka biribi baako pɛ.** Nkyerɛkyerɛ tiaa, adwene baako, mmisa baako.
3. **Ma wɔn nneyɛe mmienu.** "Tii anaa kɔfi?" yɛ mmerɛ sen "Dɛn na wopɛ?"
4. **Twɛn.** Ma wɔn bere pii sen sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a ɛnyɛ den sɛ wobɛbua, na nnyɛ ntoatoa wɔn kasa.
5. **Nnyɛ nkɔmmɔ anaa nsɛmmisa.** Ma wɔn nneyɛe ne adwene a ɛwɔ mu, na nnyɛ nokware no ara.

## Ɛdeɛn na mɛhyɛ nkɔmmɔ ase?

Hyehyɛ nneɛma ma obi no betumi asi ne adwene wɔ wo so. [Family Caregiver Alliance nkyerɛkyerɛmu](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) kyerɛ sɛ wɔntɔ nneɛma a ɛma nnyiyimu kyerɛ ansa na: fa radio anaa TV, pe nwiinwii anaa ɔpon, anaa kɔ beaebi a ɛyɛ dwodwoo.

- **Fi anim bra.** Sɛ wobɛba wɔn akyiri anaa wɔn afa a, ebetumi ama wɔnsuro.
- **Ka sɛ wo ne hena.** "Papa, ɛyɛ Sarah, wo ba mmaa." Eyi gyina wɔn so sɛ wɔrehwehwɛ wo din.
- **Fa wo ho bɔ wɔn ase.** Sɛ wɔatena a, tena anaa bɔ mmɔden na wo ani nhyɛ wɔn ani so.
- **Hwɛ wo ani ne wo kasa mu.** [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) kyerɛ sɛ wohu wo kasa mu, wo deŋ, wo ani mu ne wo nneyɛe, na ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ woyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ de ahoɔyɛ.
- **Hwɛ sɛ wɔde wɔn ani ahwɛyiedeɛ ne ntini aboaboa.** Ɛyɛ den sɛ obi bɛtie nkɔmmɔ a ɔnhu anaa ɔnte yiye.

## Ɛdeɛn na mɛka nsɛm yɛ?

Fa nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, nkyerɛkyerɛ tiaa, ne deŋ a ɛyɛ dodo.

- **Adwene baako wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ biara mu.** "Momma yɛ wo ntama. Afei yɛbɛkɔ akyi." Nnyɛ nea wɔka mu nyinaa wɔ ahyɛ baako mu.
- **Fa din di dwuma, nnyɛ nkyerɛdin.** "Auntie Jean afrɛ" ye sen "Ɔfrɛ."
- **Ka deŋ a ɛyɛ dodo, nnyɛ deŋ kɛse.** Family Caregiver Alliance kyerɛ sɛ deŋ a ɛyɛ fɛfɛ sen deŋ a ɛkɔ soro.
- **Kyerɛkyerɛ bio, de nsɛm foforo kyerɛkyerɛ.** Sɛ wɔantee a, ka nsɛm no ara bio pɛ. Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie a, twɛn bere tiaa na fa nsɛm foforo ka.
- **Kyerɛ wɔn, na nnyɛ kasa nko.** Hyɛ nsuo no so, de mpanin mmienu bɔ mmɔden, anaa kyerɛ anansesɛm no ntam kan.
- **Ka kyerɛ wɔn, nnyɛ wɔn ho asɛm wɔ wɔn anim.** NIA kɔ ntam sɛ wobɛka ho asɛm sɛ obi no nni hɔ, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wobɛka wɔn sɛ abɔfra.

NIA kyerɛ sɛ nsɛmmisa a wɔbisa daa no mu bi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. Eyinom yɛ nhwɛso wɔ saa adwene yi mu:

| Mmom sɛ | Yɛ saa |
| --- | --- |
| "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ yɛdi anwummere?" | "Wopɛ akɔnnɔ anaa akokɔ?" |
| "Wo ho te sɛn?" | "Wo ho ayɛ wo sɛ wɔrehye?" |
| "Wohia aduane?" | "Anwummerɛdi bɛba mmiensa a ɛreba." |
| "Ɛnyɛ saa na wobɛyɛ." | "Momma yɛnyɛ saa." |
| "Wo kae yɛn kɔ ɛpɔ no ho?" | "Na mereteɛ yɛn kɔ ɛpɔ no ho. Ka me nea ɛyɛɛ wo ɛpɔ no ho." |
| "Mekaa kyerɛɛ wo dada." | Ka bio mmoa ne ɔbrɛ, sɛ wɔrebisa no mpɛn kan. |

## Ɛdeɛn na mɛtie yie?

Tie nkyerɛase ne adwene a ɛwɔ asɛm no mu, nnyɛ nsɛm nko.

- **Twɛn.** Ɛbarima ɔfa anaa ɔtre ɛbɛyɛ adwene a ɛreyɛ mmerɛ. Kan mmɛ dupɔn ansa na woreka.
- **Boa asɛm kɔkɔ sɛ wɔrekɔ so wɔ mmerɛ a wɔrehaw.** Family Caregiver Alliance kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ yie sɛ woboa ne nsɛm sɛ obi rehaw.
- **San ka nsɛm a wutiee no ho.** [Alzheimer's Association nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ kasa ho](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications) kyerɛ sɛ hwehwɛ nkyerɛase a ɛwɔ wɔn nsɛm mu, na ka no bio sɛ wotie yiye. "Wowɔ suro ho wɔ kar no ho anaa?"
- **Hwɛ wɔn ani ne wɔn nipadua.** NIA kyerɛ sɛ sɛnea kasa rebɛyɛ den no, wɔn ani ahyɛaseɛ betumi akyerɛ awerɛhow, abufuo anaa awerɛhyɛm yie sen nsɛm.
- **Fa nsɛm a wɔka no di sɛ ɛho hia.** Ɛwom sɛ nsɛm no fi nsɛm a ɛnyɛ nokware mu deɛ, nea wɔn adwene wɔ mu no yɛ nokware.

## Dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wɔka nsɛm a ɛnyɛ nokware?

Nnyɛ nsɛmmisa. Woremmɔ wo ho dwa, na obi no betumi ahu ne ho sɛ ɔwɔ ɔhaw anaa ɔsuro. Mmom, gyina nea ɔrewɔ so, na afei fa ahoɔyɛ san kɔ nsɛm foforo so.

Family Caregiver Alliance kyerɛ sɛ ɛho hia sɛ wobɔ nneɛma a ɔrewɔ ho mmɔden ansa na wosan kɔ akyire. Sɛ n'ɛyɛ sɛ mama no ka sɛ ɔrehwehwɛ mma ne ba nketewa afiri sukuu:

- Mmom sɛ: "Mama, wo mma akɔ sɔre. Ɛyɛ mfe aduanan a atwam."
- Yɛ saa: "Wɔ bere nyinaa na wo ara ne mmaa a wɔhwɛ ho yiye. Ka me nea ɛyɛ wo sɛ wokɔ hwehwɛ wɔn. Yɛbɛdidi ɔkɔm kan?"

Alzheimer's Association nso kyerɛ sɛ wonnyɛ nsɛmmisa wɔ nsɛm a wunnye no ho. Yiyi bere: sɛ nhwehwɛ a ɛnyɛ nokware no nnyɛ ɔhaw a, gyae ho.

## Dɛn na kasa a ɛyɛ dwodwo no yɛ wɔ dementia mu?

Ahia no sesa bere a ɛkɔ so, enti sesa sɛnea wobɛyɛ. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ mmere abiɛsa a ɛnyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ:

| Ɔkwan | Nea ɛboa |
| --- | --- |
| Ɛfiase | Mma nnyi sɛ wɔntumi nkasa. Fa wɔn ka nkasa mu, ka nsɛm kyerɛ wɔn ankasa, bisa wɔn nea wopɛ sɛ wɔboa wɔn, na fa ɛserɛ di dwuma sɛ ɛboa. |
| Ɛhyɛn | Nkasa baako baako wɔ beaebi a ɛnyɛ nnyaa. Ka nsɛm ntɛmntɛm, hwɛ wɔn ani daa, fa nsɛmmisa a wɔbɔ mmuae ka, de nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ anammɔn kyerɛ wɔn, na mma nteɛ wɔn anaa nsesa wɔn. |
| Akyiri | Fi anim bra, ka sɛ wo ne hena. Kyerɛ wɔn sɛ wɔnkyerɛ nneyɛe anaa nsɛnnennen. Fa ahoɔyɛ, nne, ahyɛaseɛ ne ahoɔfɛ di dwuma. Nnyɛ kasa sɛ wɔnni hɔ. |

Obi biara yɛ adwene a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, na obi koro betumi ayɛ ne ho yiye ne mmerɛ a ɛyɛ den. Di so twɛn sɛnea wɔte nnɛ, nnyɛ sɛnea wɔfrɛ wɔn.

## Ebia mfonini ne nneyɛe betumi aboa nkasa?

Yiw. Mfonini a yɛn nyinaa bɛhwɛ bom ma yɛn nyinaa yɛ anigye, na ɛma nkasa yɛ fɛfɛ.

- **Mfonini a ɛbɛma nkasa.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) de mfonini ne nsɛmmisa a ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔbɔ mmuae ba.
- **Abusua mfonini.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) sesa mfonini a wopaw Ɛyɛ akwan a ɛnyɛ den sɛ wobɛka, na ɛbɛma nsɛm pii aba.
- **Nsɛm a wɔka daa.** [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) de American nsɛm a wɔka daa ne nkasa mmoa a ɛboa ma wɔka ho nsɛm.
- **Nneyɛe a ɛde nkɔmmɔ ahyɛ ase.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ma wo nsa wɔ biribi a ɛbɛyɛ bere a wobɛka.

Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛtumi de nneyɛe ahyɛ abusua ase](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ne [nneyɛe a ɛkyerɛ nkaebɔ](/activities/reminiscence/) ma nsɛm pii. Sɛ wobɛkɔ sɛ wobɛhyia anaa wobɛfrɛ, kenkan [nkɔhyia](/activities/visiting/) ne [frɛ anaa vidio frɛ](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu.

## Dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ mʼoboɔ ho ba?

Ɛbɛba obi biara so. NIA kyerɛ sɛ sɛ wohu sɛ wʼoboɔ reba a, fa bere ka ho ma wo ho dwodwo. Kɔ foro a ɛwɔ akyiri, hwɛ ahoɔfɛ tenten, na san ba bere a wotumi. Wobetumi asan aka nkɔmmɔ no bio, na obi no betumi ano wo ahoɔyɛ a ɛyɛ dwodwo no. Sɛ ɔhaw no bɛba daa a, ɛkyerɛ sɛ wohia mmoa a ɛboro saa, na ɛnkyerɛ sɛ wo nni ho. Wo nkɔmmɔ ahoɔfɛ ho mmoa, [caregiver self-care](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ho nkyerɛkyerɛmu, wɔ hɔ ma wo.
