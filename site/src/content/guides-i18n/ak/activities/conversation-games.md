Nnipa bebree pɛ akyɛdeɛ a wɔbɛyɛ wɔ ne ho a ɛte sɛ nkasa, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔrebɔ wɔn ho ban. Nsɛmmisa aduonu ne nkasa a ɛte saa betumi ayɛ yiye, esiane sɛ mmara no yɛ mmerɛw na asɛm no betumi akɔ baabiara. Ɛho hia sɛ yɛsesa akwan a yɛdi so daa: obi a ɔwɔ mmuae no ne nea ɔbɛkyerɛ, na wo na wobisa. Saa nkyerɛkyerɛ yi kyerɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ wobetumi ayɛ saa akwan yi wɔ ahoɔden mu, ne nkasa akwan a ɛfata ma dementia yare nipa.

## Adeɛn na yɛsesa mmara a yɛdi so daa?

Wɔ akwan a wɔdi so daa mu no, obi bisa na obi foforo nim mmuae. Eyi de dementia yare nipa bɛba baabi a ɔbɛtumi adi kwa, na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɔnni nea ɔpɛ ho. Sɛ ɔpaw ade no na wo de nsɛmmisa a wobisa, ɔyɛ ɔbenkyerɛfo. Ɔwɔ mmuae no, ɔde ma wo na ɔbɛkyerɛ sɛ aane anaa daabi, na ɔbɛn sɛ wo ho bɛyɛ den a ɔhwɛ.

[Akyerɛyɛ Nyansahyɛ Ahyɛnsoɔ no](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) kyerɛ abusuafo sɛ wɔnnyɛ nsɛm tenten, wɔmmisa baako nkyɛn, na wɔnnkasa wɔ ahoɔden ne ɔbɔnsam mu. Saa nneyɛe yi fata nsɛmmisa akwan.

## Sɛdeɛ wobɛtumi aduru nsɛmmisa aduonu, nʼanigye mu

1. **Paw asɛmmisa bi** a ɛfiri obi nkwa mu, te sɛ efiedan, afuo, mmoa anaa ɛpono ho.
2. **Ma wɔnsusuw biribi** na wɔnka no bɔkɔɔ, anaa wɔnkyerɛ kaadi a ɛwɔ ɔwɛn, sɛnea wo ntumi nnyae nkɔ.
3. **Bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛyɛ aane anaa daabi, baako baako.** "Ɛyɛ biribi a wodi?" "Ɛyɛ kɛse sen akyɛkyɛ?"
4. **Gye mmuae biara.** Ɔkɔm, ntwitwa ti, anaa ɔseɛ, biara ye.
5. **Ka wʼadwene kyerɛ wo ara wɔ nsɛmmisa mu** na ma wo ani nnye. Sɛ wo kae yie a, nyɛ anigye, mo nyinaa nyɛ anigye.
6. **Ma nsɛnkyerɛnne** sɛ obi no te sɛ ɔnni kwan: "Ɛyɛ biribi a ɛwɔ efie kuku mu."
7. **Nnkan nsɛmmisa no** na nnkan nkontaabu.

Sɛ obi no ntumi ntu mmara no so a, sesa kɔ akwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw: ka nneɛma kakra kyerɛ no, na ɔbɛtumi ahyɛ nea ɛfata no so.

## Nneɛma pa a wobetumi apaw

- Aburoo a ɔpɛ, nkwadaa anaa nhwiren a ɔpɛ
- Aboa a ɛwɔ afuo, ɛwɔ bɔfa anaa aboa a wɔde di so no
- Ɔyɛkyɛ nneɛma, te sɛ hammer, rake anaa nsaw a wɔde dɔ
- Ade a wɔde di so efie, anaa efie nneɛma
- Beaeɛ a wonim yiye, te sɛ akyekyedeɛ anaa kurow
- Ntama a ɔpɛ paa sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ

Fa din a ɛfa nnipa ho, nneɛma a ebetumi ama awerɛhyɛ, ne nneɛma a ɛbɛyɛ basaa anaa ɛbɛyɛ yaw ntɛmntɛm.

## Nkasa akwan foforo

- **Eyi anaa eyi.** Ma wɔn nneɛma mmienu na bisa nea wopɛ paa: tii anaa kɔfi, bepɔw anaa ɔpɔtee, ntama tuntum anaa abiduru. Mmuae pa biara nni hɔ.
- **Wopɛ deɛ dɛn.** Bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne ɛyɛ anigye: "Wopɛ sɛ yɛbɛyɛ aduane wɔ bepɔw so anaa yɛbɛdi ɛwɔ adidibea?"
- **Ka asɛm a ɛwɔ asɛm no awiei hɔ.** Asɛm a wonim, a ɛwɔ awiei a wɔammfa nka hɔ. Sɔ hwɛ [nkasa ne asɛm a wɔka wɔ mpɛn kɛse] wɔ [yɛn asɛm a wɔka ano](/activities/finish-the-saying/) mu.
- **Frɛ beaeɛ.** Srɛ wɔn sɛ wɔnkyerɛ dan a wɔpɛ, kurow anaa ɛhunu a wɔpɛ, na ma wɔnka sɛ wobɛhu no nso.
- **Nsɛmmisa kaadi.** Yɛn [nkasa kaadi](/games/conversation-cards/) de nsɛmmisa a ɛnyɛ mmuae a wɔatoa so kyɛn sɛdeɛ wɔyɛ, na wɔbetumi ato so wɔ ɔtenaa so.

Sɛ wopɛ afotu bi a ɛfa adwene ne nkwa asɛm ho, hwɛ [nsɛmmisa a ɛfa asɛm a atwam so](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Sɛ wopɛ anigye a ɛte sɛ ɔbisa-nnuan wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, hwɛ [nsɛmmisa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ma mpanyin a wɔwɔ dementia](/activities/trivia/), a ɛde mmuae a wɔpaw ne ntwitwa nni nkontaabu so.

## Dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ obi no ayɛ basaa?

Gyae, di ano, na ka sɛ, "Momma yɛnyɛ biribi foforo." Basaa yɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsesa akwan no, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔbɔ nkɔ so. Yɛn [akwan a wɔfa so ne dementia yare nipa rekyɛ agyinamoa](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) kyerɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ wobetumi asesa mmara wɔ bere a ɛreba so.

## Nneɛma a agyinamoa betumi ayɛ ne nneɛma a ɛntumi nyɛ

Nkasa agyinamoa yɛ anigye na ɛma nnipa bɛka ho. Ebetumi aboa nnipa ma wɔn ho abɛka ho ne wɔn asɛm. Ɛntumi nyɛ dementia yare, ɛntumi nnyɛ no ntɛm, na ɛntumi nsiw ano. Ma akwan no nyɛ kwan a ɛyɛ ɔkwan koro: bere a ɛyɛ ahoɔden wɔ ne ho.
