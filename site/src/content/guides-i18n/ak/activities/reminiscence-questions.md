Nkae ho nkitahodie yɛ sɛ yɛbɛka asɛm wɔ bere a atwam ho: abusuafie mu, adwuma a obi yɛe kan, aduane a obi pɛ, nnwom, ahoma ne afahyɛ. Ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia, nkitahodie a wɔka wɔn ankasa nkwan ho no ɛboro so yɛ anigye a ɛsen biara wɔ da no mu. Ɛho hia sɛ wobisa wɔ akwan a ɛma wɔka asɛm, na ɛnyɛ akwan a ɛbɛhwehwɛ nkae. Yɛakyekyɛ nsɛmmisa yi ma mmerɛ, na yɛde nhyehyɛe bi aka ho.

## Nkae ho nsɛmmisa mmara

- **Bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛma obi kyerɛ asɛm.** "Ka kyerɛ me..." ne "Ɛteɛ sɛn..." ma obi kyerɛ asɛm. "Wokae...anaa?" hwehwɛ mmuae a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.
- **Nnyɛ sɔhwɛ.** Gyae "Ɔyɛ hena?", "Afe bɛn na ɛyɛ saa?" ne "Wukae din a ɛyɛ...?"
- **Gye biribiara a ɛba.** Sɛ asɛm no sesa anaasɛ wɔnhyɛ nkyerɛ mmeae yiye, di anigye wɔ so na bisa awerɛhoɔ ho asɛm.
- **Fa nsɛmmisa baako ara.** Twɛn, tie, na afa so bisa nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Ma obi no nkyerɛ kwan.** Sɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔka asɛm foforo, nkɔ wɔ akyi.

[Nkae ho nkitahodie a National Institute on Aging de ama no](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) kyerɛ sɛ wɔnfa nsɛm tiaa, ɛnsɛ nne nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ dɛ, na ɔbɔ mmɔden sɛ ɔbɛtwe ne ho ase ama obi no akɔ nsɛm a ɔpɛ. Saa mmara yi ho hia wɔ ha te sɛ wɔ baabi foforo.

## Fie ne mmerɛ a atwam

- Ka kyerɛ me fie a wotenaa wɔ mmerɛ. Dɛn na ɛwɔ ɔdan no mu?
- Dɛn na wodii wɔ abonten a wɔyɛ kakraa?
- Hena na mmoaa a wɔyɛ mmoa wɔ mo kwan so?
- Ɛte sɛn sɛ wo dan no te? Wo ne obi bɛn na wɔdaa?

## Adwuma ne adwuma a wɔyɛ

- Adwuma bɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ kɛse?
- Ka kyerɛ me da bi a wuyɛɛ adwuma na ɛmaa wo ani gyee wo ho.
- Dɛn na woyɛ wɔ wo nsa mu a wopɛe?
- Hena na ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ wo adwuma no yiye?

## Aduane ne aduane yɛ

- Dɛn na wo abusua didiɛ Kwesida?
- Ka kyerɛ me aduane bi a obi wɔ wo abusua mu yɛe yiye.
- Wɔ mmere a wo yɔnko no, ɛhe na wokɔ nea wopɛ?
- Nsɛ bɛn na ɛkae wo fie?

Aduane ho nkitahodie fata [ɔhoɔden ho adwuma](/activities/sensory/) mu, ne ade ketewa a ɛyɛ a ɛyɛ ɛdɛ sɛ wobɛma.

## Nnwom ne ɛbɔ

- Nnwom bɛn na woyɛɛ anigye so wɔ mmerɛ a wo yɔnko?
- Hena na ɔyɛ wo nnwom ayɛfo anaasɛ ɔsɔfoɔ dɔ?
- Ɛhe na wotee nnwom wɔ saa mmere no: fie, ɛbɔ, anaasɛ asɔredan?

Sɛ wopɛ nnwom a wobɛtie anaasɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ, hwɛ [nnwom adwuma ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia](/activities/music/).

## Afahyɛ ne ahoma

- Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ afahyɛ wɔ wo youth?
- Ka kyerɛ me ahoma a woyɛe a wokae.
- Dɛn na abusua no yɛe osuahu anwummere?
- Ɛte sɛn sɛ abusua no yɛ bere a ɛsakra?

Sɛ wopɛ adwuma a ɛfata afahyɛ bi, hwɛ [afahyɛ adwuma ma obi a ɔwɔ dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Abusua ne nnamfo

- Ka kyerɛ me obi a ɔmaa wo anigye kɛse.
- Ɛte sɛn wo awareɛ da?
- Ka kyerɛ me wo mmofra bere a wɔyɛ mmerante.
- Dɛn na wo nnamfo frɛ wo?

Bisa abusua nsɛmmisa a ɛyɛ dɛ. Sɛ obi no awu obi anaa ɔrewu, di so ani gye, na mma wɔnnhwehwɛ nsɛm a ɛde awerɛhoɔ ba.

## Mmeae ne abɔdeɛ

- Ka kyerɛ me beae a wopɛ sɛ woteɛ ho nkɔso.
- Ɛte sɛn ewiem wɔ baabi a wotenaa?
- Wowɔ akuraa anaa dua a wopɛ?
- Ka kyerɛ me akwantu bi a woyɛɛ anigye so.

## Nkyerɛ nʼahoɔden ne nneɛma

Mfoni yɛ nkitahodie a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. Fa mfoni no bɛn ani sɛ nʼani hyɛ mfoni no, na ka sɛ, "Ka kyerɛ me ho asɛm." [Alzheimer's Association kwan a ɛfa adwuma ho](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ka sɛ wobɛfa nneɛma a wodi kan, mfoni ne nnwom de ahyɛ nkitahodie ase. Nneɛma bi a ɛfiri obi no nkwan mu wɔ nkotodwe ketewa mu nso betumi ayɛ adwuma; hwɛ [nkae ho adwuma](/activities/reminiscence/) sɛ wopɛ sɛ wo ara wo nkotodwe no.

## Sɛ nkae no yɛ yaw

Nkae bi betumi ama awerɛhoɔ, suro anaasɛ abufuo ba. Sɛ obi no ayɛ awerɛhoɔ, nnyɛ no ntɛntɛn. Ka asɛm a ɛyɛ dɛ te sɛ, "Ɛte sɛ bere a ɛyɛ den na ɛyɛe. Wopɛ sɛ yɛka asɛm a ɛyɛ anigye?" Afei, sesa asɛm no, ma no nsa a ɔbɛnom, anaasɛ gyina nnwom a ɔpɛ so.

## Di adwuma anaa ɔkwantu bi ho

Nkae nkitahodie akyi no, adwuma ketewa a ɛyɛ dɛ betumi ama ahoɔden no nyinaa tena yiye. Sɛ wopɛ nsɛmmisa a ɛma obi kyerɛ asɛm, sɔ [nsɛmmisa kaadi](/games/conversation-cards/) hwɛ; sɛ wopɛ kasa a wɔka wɔ obi mmerɛ mu, sɔ [kasa a wɔka ho asɛm](/games/finish-the-saying/) hwɛ. Sɛ wopɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ ho nkitahodie a ɛsen saa, hwɛ [nkitahodie akwan ma obi a ɔwɔ dementia](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
