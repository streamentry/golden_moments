Nkaebɔ yɛ ɔkwan a yɛfa so ka asɛm ho bom fa nnansa a atwam ho: abofrahood fie, adwuma a wɔfi ase yɛ, aduane a obi pɛ, nnwom, ahoma ne afahyɛ. Ma nnipa bebree a wɔwɔ dementia, asɛmkasa a ɛfa wɔn ankasa nkwa ho wɔ anigye mu bɛyɛ biribi a ɛboro sɛ ɛyɛ anigye wɔ da no mu. Nea ɛho hia ne sɛ wobisa wɔ ɔkwan a ɛma obi kɔka asɛm, na ɛnyɛ ɔkwan a ɛbɛsɔ nkaebɔ hwɛ. Yɛakyekyɛ saa nsɛmmisa yi ho ma kɛkɛ, na yɛde nhwɛsoɔ bi ka ho sɛdeɛ wobɛfa so.

## Mmara a ɛfa nkaebɔ nsɛmmisa ho

- **Bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛmmue nni.** "Ka kyerɛ me..." ne "Ɛte sɛn..." ma obi kɔka asɛm. "Wo kae...?" hwehwɛ mmuae a ɛteɛ.
- **Nkyɛ sɛ wobisa sɛ wɔsɔhwɛ.** Fa "Hena ni yi?", "Afe bɛn na ɛyɛ saa?" ne "Wo kae din a ɛyɛ...?" ara.
- **Gye nea ɛba.** Sɛ asɛm no sesa anaa nkyɛn ne nkyɛn bɛfom, di no ani gye, na bisa nneɛma a ɔnee wɔ akoma mu.
- **Fa nsɛmmisa baako ase.** Tew mu, tie, na di akyi nsɛmmisa a ɛyɛ kakraa.
- **Ma obi nni akwan.** Sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka asɛm foforɔ, di akwan.

[Amerika National Institute on Aging nkyerɛkyerɛ ho asɛm kɔka](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) hyɛ nkuran sɛ ɛmfa mmara tiaa, nne a ɛyɛ mmerɛ na ɔtɛn, ne nkɔsoɔ bere a obi reshwehwɛ ne nsɛm. Saa mmara yi ho hia wɔ ha sɛ wɔ baabi foforɔ.

## Fie ne abofrahood

- Ka kyerɛ me fie a woawo wo wɔ no. Ɛte sɛn wɔ ɛdan no mu?
- Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ wɔ abɔnten bere a woyɛ abofra?
- Hena na mo nyinii wɔ wo kurom no mu?
- Ɛte sɛn wɔ wo dadekyɛn no? Wɔ hɔ na woka ho ne obi?

## Adwuma ne nneyɛe

- Adwuma bɛn na woyɛ a ɛdɔɔ wo koraa?
- Ka kyerɛ me ɛda bi a woyɛɛ adwuma no a ɛmaa wo ani gyei.
- Nneɛma bɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ anaa wosiesie wo nsa so?
- Hena na ɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ wo nneɛma pii wɔ wo adwuma ho?

## Aduane ne ɛyɛ aduane

- Dɛn na wo abusua didii Kwesida?
- Ka kyerɛ me aduane bi a obi wɔ wo abusua yɛeɛ yiye paa.
- Wɔ baabi bɛn na woyɛ ɛberɛ a woyɛ abofra na wonya nneɛma a ɛyɛ dɔ?
- Nhwɛso bɛn na ɛkae wo fie?

Aduane ho asɛm fata bra a ɛyɛ [nsɛnkyerɛnne ho adwuma](/activities/sensory/) ho kɔkɔbɔ, na ɛfata sɛ wobɛma aduane kakraa a ɛyɛ ɛnam bɛboa sɛ ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ.

## Nnwom ne ɛsɛe

- Nnwom bɛn na woyɛɛ anaa woyɛɛ ɛsɛe ho ɛberɛ a woyɛ abofra?
- Hena na wopɛ sɛ ɔyɛ dwom anaa ɔbɛyɛ ɔdwom a ɛdɔɔ wo koraa?
- Ɛberɛ hɔ nnwom bɛte wɔ mmere ho? Wɔ fie, wɔ ɛsɛe mu, anaa wɔ asɔredan?

Sɛ wopɛ nnwom nhyehyɛe anaa nnwom ho nhwɛsoɔ, hwɛ [nnwom ho adwuma ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia](/activities/music/).

## Afahyɛ ne ahurusi

- Afahyɛ bɛn na woyɛ anigye wɔ bere a woyɛ abofra?
- Ka kyerɛ me abɛnkyɛ ɔkwan bi a woyɛ a wokae.
- Dɛn na abusua no yɛe anwummere a ɛyɛ osee bere?
- Ɛte sɛn na wo abusua yɛ bere a bere sesa?

Sɛ wopɛ nhwɛsoɔ a ɛfata afahyɛ bi, hwɛ [afahyɛ ho adwuma ma obi a wɔwɔ dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Abusua ne nnamfo

- Ka kyerɛ me obi a ɔmaa wo anigye pii.
- Ɛte sɛn wɔ wo marryɛ da no?
- Ka kyerɛ me wo mmofra wɔ ɛberɛ a wɔyɛ mmerante.
- Dɛn na wo nnamfo frɛ wo?

Bisa abusua nsɛmmisa de ahoɔfɛ. Sɛ obi ayɛ awerɛhyɛm ma obi a ɔwuo, di ne kwan akyi na mma nsɛm a ɛyɛ yaw so bisa no.

## Baabi ne ɛwiase

- Ka kyerɛ me baabi bi a woyɛ anigye sɛ wobɛsusuw ho.
- Ɛte sɛn wɔ baabi a woyɛ abofra no ɛberɛ no?
- Wɔ wo fie a wɔwɔ ɔbɔnwan anaa dua bi a woyɛ anigye ho?
- Ka kyerɛ me akwantu bi a ɛdɔɔ wo.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne ne nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma

Nsɛnkyerɛnne yɛ asɛm a yɛfi ase ɛnam so. Fa ɔfoto bɔ ahunu ankɔnsa, na ka sɛ, "Ka kyerɛ me ho asɛm." [Alzheimer's Association nkyerɛkyerɛ ho adwuma](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) kyerɛ sɛ wɔde nneɛma a wɔnim, ɔfoto ne nnwom de hyɛ kasa ase. Ɛyɛ papa sɛ wobɛyɛ adekyeɛ kakraa a ɛfiri obi nkwa mu, na wobɛkɔ so; hwɛ [nkaebɔ adwuma](/activities/reminiscence/) sɛdeɛ wobɛyɛ no.

## Sɛ asɛm no yɛ yaw

Nkaebɔ bi bɛma awerɛhoɔ, suro anaa abufuw aba. Sɛ obi ayɛ abufuw, nkyɛ sɛ wobɛtwe no mu. Ka asɛm a ɛyɛ dɔ sɛ, "Ɛte sɛ ɛberɛ a ɛyɛ den na ɛbaa ho. Wopɛ sɛ yɛka ho asɛm a ɛyɛ anigye?" Afei sesa asɛm no, de tii bi ma no, anaa to dwom a ɔdɔ.

## Di ɔkwan a ɛyɛ anigye akyi

Wɔ nkaebɔ bere akyi, ɛyɛ papa sɛ wobɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ anigye, a ɛbɛma animguase no nyɛ yaw. Sɛ wopɛ nsɛmmisa a ɛma obi kasa bebree, fa [nsɛmmisa kaa](/games/conversation-cards/) ; anaa sɛ wopɛ kasa a wɔka wɔ abofrahood, fa [kasa no awiei a yɛka](/games/finish-the-saying/). Sɛ wopɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ ho asɛm a ɛfa kasa ho dodow, hwɛ [kasa ho nhyehyɛe ma obi a wɔwɔ dementia](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
