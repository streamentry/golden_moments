Sɛ wohwehwɛ "memory games for seniors" wɔ intanɛt so a, wobɛhu nnwom pii a wɔyɛɛ ma nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔbɔ won adwene ho dwuma: kaad a wɔsie, ntɛmntɛm a ɛreboa, nkwan ne mmuae a wɔbɛkyerɛ. Ma obi a ɔwɔ dementia no, saa nneɛma yi ma nnwom no ayɛ nsɔhwɛ wɔ adwene a ɛreyɛ den ma no ho. Wɔtumi de saa nnwom yi ayɛ no wɔ kwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. Saa krataa yi kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛyɛ no, na ɛma nnwom a ɛyɛ yie wɔ ɛberɛ biara mu.

## Adɛn nti na nkaebɔ nnwom a wɔyɛ kɔsi ɛma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia awerɛhyɛ?

Ɛde ne nyinaa gyina so sɛ ɛhyɛ nnipa sɛ wɔkora adwene mu ade foforo. Eyi ne nea Alzheimer's, a ɛyɛ dementia a ɛba pii, de ne ho hyɛ ase kane. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) ka sɛ Alzheimer's nhyehyɛe a ɛba ɛberɛ a ɛreba mfiase pii ne sɛ obi nni ho yie sɛ ɔbɛkae ade foforo a ɔsua. Wɔ Concentration a ɛyɛ tẽẽ mu no, wobɔ kaad mmienu, wukae baabi a wɔwɔ, na wode ho ahwehwɛ no ɔkyena bere sua. Eyi yɛ ade foforo a wɔkora mu na wɔkae.

Sɛ wo de ntɛmntɛm, mmuae anaa ɔbrɔfo a ɔbɔ nne ma mmuae a ɛnyɛ yie a, ɛbɛma obi kae nea ɔntumi nyɛ, na wɔnam ɔdɔfo a ɔdɔ no anim. Ebinom nso ntɛn wɔn ho sɛ wɔrenyɛ nnwom no bio.

Ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wɔhunu ade a wɔrehwɛ no, na ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ den pii. Ɛyɛ anigye sɛ wobɛka mfoni a wɔde bɛn no wɔ bere a abɛn abiɛ abɛn ne baanu wɔ hɔ. Enti kwan no ne sɛ wobɛkɔ so ayɛ mfoni a ɛne ne baanu tɛ, na wamfa nkaebɔ nhwehwɛ nni hɔ.

## Sɛnea wobɛyɛ nkaebɔ nnwom a ɛyɛ anigye?

Nsakrae yi boa wɔ kaad nnwom, nnwom a wɔde papa ayɛ ne nnwom a wɔde screen ayɛ nyinaa mu.

1. **Ma mfoni no nkɔ so hwɛ so.** Sɛ wohwɛ mfoni a wɔde bɛtoto ho ka ho, yɛ nnwom pa, na ɛbɛtumi ayɛ anigye pii.
2. **Fa nneɛma ketewa.** Fi mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa ase. Sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛ a na wɔn bɛpɛ ɛka, ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔka nea ɛbɛyɛ kɛse.
3. **Paw mfoni kɛse, a wɔbɛhunu ntɛm, a ɛyɛ mfoni a ɛfata nkuraa.** Te sɛ kapu, ɔsoroma, anaa ɔpɔnkɔ a ɛreforo. Ɛnyɛ anansesɛm mfoni, na ɛnyɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ dodo.
4. **Yɛ adwuma sɛ asafo.** "Yɛmfa nkɔhwehwɛ nnompɛ" ne nea ɛyɛ yie sen "wo bere". Monnyina ase na mmoa wɔn a mo ne wɔn yɛ adwuma.
5. **Gyae nsɛnkyerɛnne.** Obiara nhia sɛ ɔdi. Sɛ obi bisa a, "yɛayɛ no" kakra no na ɛdɔɔso.
6. **Mfa ntɛmntɛm nnhyɛ wo ho ntɛm.** Nni ntɛmntɛm biara, na mma wo ho nnyɛ ntɛm wɔ bere a ɔfrɛ wɔ mu.
7. **Ka mfoni no ho asɛm.** "Hwɛ ɔpɔnkɔ no. Wowɔ ɛberɛ a woyɛɛ ɔpɔnkɔ?" Nkasa no yɛ nea ɛyɛ yie kɛse.
8. **Gyina wɔ anigye mu.** Gyina bere a nnwom a wɔayɛ ama bi, bere a ɛreyɛ anigye ka.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ma afotuo a ɛwɔ saa ntease yi mu: fa nnwom a obi tumi yɛ ka ho, hwɛ sɛ ɔrenyɛ awerɛhyɛ, na ma ɔnya ahotoso sɛ ɔyɛ yie.

## Nkaebɔ ne mfoni a ɛyɛ anigye a wobɛsɔ hwɛ

### Picture pairs (mfoni a wɔhwɛ so ne wɔn baanu)

[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) yɛ nnwom a wɔde mfoni kɛse ne nneɛma 4 kosi 12 yɛ. Mfoni no tumi gyina hwɛ so, enti ɛyɛ nnwom a wɔhwehwɛ mfoni a ɛne ne baanu tɛ, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔkae baabi a wɔwɔ. Fi nneɛma anan ase. Sɛ obi a ɔwɔ mfitiase mu ani gye sɛ ɔbɛyɛ den kakra a, wobɛtumi asesa akɔ **Mfoni a wɔahyɛ ase**, nanso san de mfoni a wɔhwɛ bɛba bere a ɛrenyɛ anigye bio.

### Find the same

[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) de mfoni baako bɛba soro, na mfoni mmienu kosi anan bɛba ase. Tow mfoni a ɛtɔ ho no. Ɛyɛ nnwom a ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ mmerɛ wɔ saa baabi yi so, na ɛfata nnipa a ɛyɛ den ma wɔn sɛ wɔhwɛ nneɛma pii bom wɔ ɛberɛ a wɔrekɔ akyiri, titiriw wɔ ɛberɛ a obi wɔ wɔn akyi.

### Photo pairs (nnwom a wɔde wo abusua mfoni yɛ)

[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) de mfoni a woapaw sesa nnwom a wɔhwehwɛ mfoni ne baanu tɛ: ɔkraman no, afuom no, efie a ɛwɔ asuo no ho. Abusuafo a wohu ne mmeae a wodɔ, ɛboro mfoni a wɔde papa ayɛ, ɛma nkasa pii. Mfoni no gyina wo browser mu, na wɔrenkɔ baabi.

### Sort together

[Sort together](/games/sort-together/) srɛ wo sɛ wo de mfoni biara ka baabi a ɛfata, te sɛ ɛdan mu anaa afuom mu. Nhyehyɛe yi de nimdeɛ a wɔde kɔɔ so ma nnipa wɔ ɛwiase ho hwehwɛ, na ɛnyɛ adwene a ɛyɛ ntɛmntɛm nko ara.

### Picture puzzle

[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) tew mfoni ɔkɔ 4 anaa 9 mu. Tow ntikoro mmienu ma ɛkɔ baabi a ɛfata, kɔsi sɛ mfoni no bɛyɛ pii. Mfoni a ɛyɛ pii no gyina screen so sɛ kwan a wɔbɛfa so akɔ so.

### Kaad a wɔde gyina tabɔ so

Ɛnhia sɛ wowɔ screen. Fa kaad a ɛne ne baanu tɛ fi kaad mmienu mu anaa fa mfoni kaad nhyehyɛe. Hyehyɛ wɔn wɔ nkyɛn mmienu so a mfoni no hwɛ so, na monhwehwɛ mfoni a ɛne ne baanu tɛ mu bom. Akyiri no, fa ahyehyɛ kɔ tɔ anaa agyinasɛm so. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) de kaad nnwom ne tabɔ nnwom ka ho sɛ ɛyɛ ade a ɛboa ɛberɛ a ɛreba mfiase.

## Nkaebɔ nnwom boa dementia anaa?

Biribiara wɔ nkaebɔ nnwom mu a wɔasi sɛ ɛbɔ yare, ɛma ɔyare akɔ akyiri anaa ɛtwa dementia. [Cochrane nhwehwɛmu](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/) a ɛfa komputa brain training ho ma nnipa a wɔwɔ mfitiase nkaebɔ yare kaa sɛ nsɛm a wɔahu ntɛm kɛse, na enti wɔantumi amfa nkɔ sɛ ɛbɛtumi aba dementia ho amfa. Yɛn [brain games ne dementia](/brain-health/brain-games/) akwankyerɛ kyerɛ nea nhwehwɛmu kyerɛ ne nea ɛnkyerɛ.

Eyi nni kwan a ɛma wɔnnyae nnwom. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) kamfo sɛ wɔnhwɛ anigye, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ adeyɛ. Simma du mmienu a wɔhwehwɛ mfoni ne ɛka ho asɛm yɛ anigye a ɛyɛ nokware wɔ da mu.

## Wɔ nkaebɔ nnwom ho wɔ agyanom a wɔnwɔ dementia ho?

Sɛ obi a ɔnwɔ nkaebɔ ho ɔhaw nanso ɔpɛ nnwom a wɔde ntɛmntɛm, ntɛmntɛm ne nsɛnkyerɛnne ayɛ den, ɛnyɛ biribi a ɛyɛ bɔne. Nanso, nnkyɛ sɛ wobɛhwɛ sɛ nnwom no bɛbɔ no ban fi dementia ho; biribiara nni hɔ a wɔakyerɛ sɛ ɛbɛtumi ayɛ saa. Nnwom a ɛyɛ anigye a yɛakyerɛ wɔ soro yɛ yie wɔ bere a mmusuakuw nyinaa di nnwom bom: agyanom a wɔwɔ dementia ne mmofra a wɔnnyɛ wɔn mfe pii nyinaa tumi hwehwɛ mfoni a ɛne ne baanu tɛ sɛ asafo, na obiara nnhwɛ sɛ wɔsɔ no.

## Bere bɛn na wobɛgyae anaa asesa nnwom no?

Sɛ obi bɔ mmoto, de tablet no to akyi, ka sɛ "metumi nyɛ eyi," anaa ɔhyɛ ɔyare a ɛyɛ sɛ ɔnyɛ awerɛhyɛ no, ɛyɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne sɛ wobɛma ɛnyɛ den anaa wogyae. Hwehwɛ nneɛma kakraa, ma mfoni nkɔ so hwɛ so, sesa akɔ [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/), anaa wɔ mfoni hwɛ na nkasa. Sɛ wɔamma wɔnnwɛn nnwom no, ɛyɛ yie nso. Yɛn [sɛnea wobɛyɛ nnwom wɔ asafo mu](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) akwankyerɛ wɔ ɛberɛ ne mmoa ho nsɛm pii.
