Jigsaw a wɔatrɛw so wɔ adidibea a wɔdi aduane so no yɛ biribi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ mobɛkyekyɛ. Mobɛtumi atena ase wɔ ho a ɛnhia sɛ mokasa pii, mfonini no bɛma mo wɔ asɛm a mobɛka, na sɛ wobɛka ntwa a ɛto so no a, anigye ba. Nanso mfiri no hia sɛ ɛsesa sɛnea dementia sesa. Mfiri a ɛyɛ den bɛma ahoɔdɛn ne anigye a ɛwɔ awia biara bɛyɛ awerɛhow. Saa nsɛm yi kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛpaw ne wobɛhyehyɛ jigsaw, nsɛmfua nhwehwɛ ne online mfiri a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ne nea wobɛyɛ sɛ ɛyɛ den.

## Mfiri yɛ papa ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia anaa?

Nnipa dodow pɛ wɔn, titiriw wɔn a wɔyɛɛ mfiri ansa na yɛ. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) kyerɛ nsɛmfua nhwehwɛ, kaad mfiri ne apon mfiri sɛ ɛyɛ nneyɛe a ɛfata stage a ɛdi kan no, na [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ka online mfiri ne tablet mfiri sɛ akwan biako a ɛboa ma obi nya ne ho anigye.

Mfiri nyɛ akuwa. Mfiri biara nni nkɔso a wɔakyerɛ sɛ ɛbɔ dementia ho ban anaa ɛma ɛkɔ akyɛre. Yɛn [nsɛm a ɛfa apon mfiri ne dementia](/brain-health/brain-games/) guide no kyerɛ nhwehwɛmu no. Nea ɛma yɛyɛ mfiri ne sɛ ɛbɛyɛ anigye, ɛbɛma wo ne obi no ahyɛ bere a ɛyɛ anigye. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ka no yiye: fa anigye so, nnyɛ nkonimdi.

## Sɛnea wobɛpaw jigsaw ma obi a wɔwɔ dementia?

Di nea obi no pɛ ne nea mfiri a ɛdi kan no kɔɔ so, nnyɛ stage a wɔfrɛ no no so.

### Ntwa dodow

Ntwa dodow pɛpɛɛpɛ biara nni hɔ. Obi a wadi mfiri daa bɛtumi ahu anigye wɔ ntwa ɛha so ɛberɛ a ɛreba mfiri no, nanso obi foforo ani begyina sɛ wɔwɔ ntwa nsia pɛ. Akwan a ɛyɛ mfaso: sɛ mfiri a etwa to no yɛ adwuma ma wo a, paw ɛwɔ ntwa a ɛyɛ fã a ɛte sɛ mfitiase. Sɛ wɔwiee no ntɛm na wɔpɛɛ sɛ wɔbɛyɛ foforo a, fa ntwa bi kɔ soro kakra.

### Ntwa kɛse ne nneɛma a ɛtɔ so

- **Ntwa kɛse** no yɛ den sɛ wobɛhu, wobɛfa, na wobɛpɛn.
- **Ntwa a ɛyɛ den a ɛyɛ mmerɛ** no ntumi nnwane ntɛm na ɛnnyɛ ɛfɛ sɛ wɔbɛtwe.
- **Nsu a ɛnyɛ mmerɛ** tew ahoɔhyɛ a ɛba ɛfiri sradaa ne mframa.
- **Fa ntwa ketewa ntɔ** sɛ obi no betumi ahyɛ nneɛma wɔ ne ano, na eyi bɛba dementia ne akyi.

### Mfonini

- **Nnipa akura mu nneɛma.** Mmepɔn, akyɛdeɛ, kar a ɛyɛ kɔsɔ, afuo mmoa, kurow a ɛbɛn ɛpɔw, anaa agodie a wɔpɛ. Ɛnyɛ mmofra akɔnnɔ anaa nneɛma a ɛte sɛ mmofra akɔnnɔ.
- **Nsakrae a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.** Dwom kɔkɔɔ, bo bibiri ne ahabammono ahoɔfɛ yɛ mmerɛ sen ɛpɔw a ɛyɛ nsukyiri a ɛyɛ kɛse.
- **Nea ɛyɛ ho nkitahodi ne nea obi no ne ho ahyɛ.** Baabi a wɔtenaa, ɛberɛ a ɔduaa paa, anaa agodie a ɔdɔ no, ɛma mo nyinaa mubetumi akasa ho asɛm. Nkɔm bi bɛtumi ahwɛ dwumadi bi mu sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ntwa a wɔbɛsesa.

## Sɛnea mobɛyɛ jigsaw wɔ mo mu?

Hyehyɛ ho ma obi no atumi aduru anigye no ara.

1. **Paw baabi a ɛyɛ.** Hann a ɛyɛ, agyapade a ɛwɔ nkyɛn, ne pon anaa tire a mobɛtumi agyae mfiri no.
2. **Bue bobe no ho** a mobɛtumi ahwɛ.
3. **Trɛw ntwa no so** ansa na moreyɛ.
4. **Kyekyɛ nkɔnkɔnkɔ ntwa no** wo ara, anaa yɛ nkɔnkɔnkɔ no ansa na wo ma wɔn nea ɛwɔ mfimfini.
5. **Kyekyɛ ntwa a ɛwɔ ne ara ne ara ho** ayɛ nkyɛnsɛ anaa aplee kakraa.
6. **Ma ntwa biako biako** sɛ ɛdi nea ɛyɛ den. "Me sɛ ɛyɛ afie no ho ntwa."
7. **Kasa fa mfonini no ho,** nnyɛ mfiri no nkɔso. "Eyi te sɛ ɔgya no ne ɔbɔat a ɔwɔ no."
8. **Gyae no wɔ hɔ.** San nkɔ so nna pii. Anhyɛ wo ho.
9. **Gyae bere a ɛyɛ anigye,** ɛberɛ a ntwa bi aka ne baabi ɛfa so, bere a ɛreyɛ anigye.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kyerɛ sɛ mmoa obi no sɛ ɔrehyɛ ase, hwɛ awerɛhow, na ma ɔnhwɛ kwan so sɛ ɔpɛ saa sɛ ɔhwɛ nneɛma no. Sɛ wohwɛ sɛnea wode ntwa refa baabi no, na wokyerɛ baabi a ntwa bi bɛkɔ, ɛyɛ sɛ wɔrekɔ so ayɛ nea ɛma wo ne no nya nkitahodi.

## Online mfiri a ɛyɛ mmerɛ

Tablet mfiri wɔ mfaso bi a ɛyɛ nokware: ntwa nyinaa wɔ hɔ, mfonini kɛse a ɛma ɛhyɛ ekran no ma, na ɛnhia sɛ woyi awie no.

Yɛn [Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) a wɔnnɔ ho ka mfonini no ho ntwa 4 anaa 9. Fa ntwa mmienu sesa ho kɔsi sɛ mfonini no bɛyɛ pɛ. Mfonini a ɛyɛ pɛ no wɔ ekran so sɛ ɛbɛboa, bere nni hɔ, na wobetumi agyae wɔ bere biara. Ntwa anan ne nea ɛfata ma nnipa pii a wɔwɔ mfimfini stage, sɛ obi wɔ wɔn nkyɛn.

Sɛ wopɛ mfiri a ɛyɛ mmerɛ sen saa, sɔ [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) hwɛ, baabi a wobɛpɛ mfonini a ɛte sɛ deɛ ɛwɔ soro no, anaa [Sort together](/games/sort-together/), baabi a mfonini kɔ nkotodwe mu te sɛ afie anaa nkɔnkɔnkɔ.

## Nsɛmfua mfiri: nsɛmfua nhwehwɛ ne nkyerɛwde

Nsɛmfua mfiri fata nnipa a wɔdɔɔ no daa, titiriw wɔn a ɛyɛ dementia mfitiase.

- **Nsɛmfua nhwehwɛ a nkyerɛwde kɛse wɔ mu.** Yɛn [Word search](/games/word-search/) a wɔnnɔ ho ka nkyerɛwde kɛse ne nsɛmfua kakraa, a ɛkɔ ɔfa ne fam, nnyɛ ɛkɔ akyi anaa ɛkɔ ɔfa a ɛkɔ akyi. Fa so wɔ ekran so anaa fa twe.
- **Kenkan nsɛmfua no mu.** Obi bi kenkan nsɛm, na obi foforo hwehwɛ. Ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wɔbɛkyerɛ nkyerɛwde a ɛdi kan no nko.
- **Nkyerɛwde kɛse a wɔfa crosswords.** Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ papa wɔ mfitiase stage. Kenkan nkyerɛase no mu, kyekyɛ aburoo no, na mo nyinaa mmua mmuaeɛ no. Crosswords a ɛfa asɛm bi ho a ɛyɛ mmerɛ yɛ papa sen nea ɛyɛ den.
- **Ka nsɛmfua no ho asɛm.** "Afuo" bɛtumi ama mo nkɔ nkɔsoɔ wɔ ntomadan ho.

Sɛ wopɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne a wɔtwa so kɛse, ne sɛnea wobɛtwa no ama ɛyɛ mmerɛ hwɛ na ɛyɛ mmerɛ dua, hwɛ [dementia nneyɛe a wɔatwa so a wɔnnɔ ho ka](/activities/printable/).

## Mfiri ahorow a ɛfata stage biara

Stage no mu ahorow hyɛ nsɛm, na nna a ɛyɛ papa ne nna a ɛyɛ den yɛ ade a ɛdɔɔso.

| Stage | Mfiri a ɛfata | Wo asɛdeɛ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mfitiase | Jigsaw a wɔadwene ho no, a ntwa a ɛkakra sen dada, nkyerɛwde kɛse crosswords ne nsɛmfua nhwehwɛ, online mfiri a ntwa 9 wɔ mu | Obi a ɔboa: ma wɔapaw, boa sɛ wobisa |
| Mfimfini | Jigsaw a ntwa kakraa a ɛyɛ kɛse bɛyɛ dwano, online mfiri a ntwa 4, nsɛmfua nhwehwɛ a wɔka ho asɛm bom | Hyehyɛ, kyekyɛ ntwa, ma ntwa biako biako |
| Awieeɛ | Hwɛ mfonini a wɔawie no bom, Find the same a mfonini abien wɔ mu, ntwa kakraa a ɛyɛ kɛse a wɔbɛtumi asi | Fa kɔma wɔn; wo ho bɛyɛ mfiri no ara |

## Ɛberɛ bɛn na mobɛyɛ mfiri no ayɛ yie, anaa mogyae?

Hwɛ sɛ wɔrehyɛ ahome, wɔrekyerɛ ntwa, wɔka "Mentumi" bere a ɛdi so, anaa wɔhwɛ wo ho a ɛyɛ awerɛhow. Saa nneɛma yi kyerɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wosesa biribi, nnyɛ sɛ wobɔ mmɔden kɔ so.

- **Yɛ no kakra.** Fa ntwa a ɛkakra, anaa gyae ntwa kakraa a ɛto so ma wɔn.
- **Yɛ nea ɛkɔ so wo ara,** na frɛ wɔn sɛ wɔmmoa nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ no.
- **Kɔ biribi a ɛbɛn ho:** kyekyɛ ntwa wɔ nkyɛnsɛ, online mfiri a ntwa 4, anaa [coloring](/games/coloring/).
- **Gyae.** Gyae no hɔ, na nom tii. Wobetumi asan aba da foforo, anaa wɔrenban.

Sɛ mfiri no bɛyɛ sɛ ɛkaa wo ho asɛm a ɛyɛ den no, ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wogyae. Akwan pii wɔ hɔ a mobɛfa so akɔ bere a ɛyɛ anigye; yɛn [101 activity ideas](/activities/ideas/) yɛ baabi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wohwehwɛ.
