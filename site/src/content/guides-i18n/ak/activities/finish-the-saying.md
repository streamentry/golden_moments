Akasɛm twerɛ agodi kenkan akasɛm a wɔaka dedaw, na ɛkɔ ne nsɛmfua a ɛto so ansa na ɛrekɔ so. Ma nnipa dodow no, ɛyɛ agodi a ɛyɛ anigye, na ɛnhyɛ wɔn mmɔden kɛse mpo so. Agodi no yɛ yie efisɛ nsɛm no ayɛ tete na nnipa nim no yiye, na obi nhia sɛ ɔkae biribi fi ɛnnɛ ho. Saa krataa yi kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛtwe agodi no, sɛnea wobɛpaw akasɛm, ne sɛnea wobɛma ɛnyɛ den.

## Dɛn ne akasɛm twerɛ agodi?

Ɛyɛ agodi a ɛkɔ so ntɛm kakra. Obi bi kenkan akasɛm no ɛfã, na obi foforo ka ne nsɛmfua a ɛto so. Nhwehwɛmu te sɛ "Nsa a ɛkɔ so no..." ne "Ɔkɔ kɔsoa..." Obi no bɛka nsɛmfua no kyerɛ nne, anaa ɔde bɛkyerɛ, anaa ɔbɛpaw ne mu mmuaeɛ mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa a wode bɛma no. Nnipa bi pɛ sɛ wɔn ara wɔkenkan ɛfã no.

Golden Moments de [agodi a ɛyɛ akasɛm twerɛ a ɛnsa sika](/games/finish-the-saying/) ma, ne nsɛm ne kyerɛwde a ɛyɛ kɛse. Wobetumi nso de ayi so se ɛnyɛ ekran a, kyerɛ ɛfã no, na wo ara bɛtwɛn.

## Sɛnea wobɛtwe, anammɔn anammɔn

1. **Paw bere a ɛnyɛ nnipa dodow.** Tena ase a wobɛhwɛ obi no ani.
2. **Kenkan ɛfã no yie, de nsɛm a ɛyɛ dɛdɛ, na bɔ mmɔden.** [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) kyerɛ sɛ wɔnka yie na wɔde nsɛm tiaa ne nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
3. **Twɛn.** Ma ɛnhyɛ wo mmɔden. Sɛ biribiara nni hɔ a, de mmuaeɛ mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa ma, baako mu no yɛ nokware.
4. **Ma wɔn anigye bere a wobɛtɔ mmuaeɛ biara.** "Ɛyɛ no ara" anaa "Merensusuu saa ara" ma agodi no ye.
5. **Ka akasɛm no ho asɛm.** Bisa sɛ ɛdeɛn na ɛkyerɛ wɔn, anaa wɔtee wɔ he. Ha na ɛyɛ nea ɛboa paa.
6. **Gyae ansa na wɔabrɛ.** Akasɛm kakra ne ɛseɛ bi no ye ahyɛde mu.

## Sɛnea wɔbɛma nhyɛso a ɛnyɛ nsɛmmisa

Nsɛmfua a wɔde ma mmiɛnsa mu no boa agodi no sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛ, nanso sɛ wɔde sɛ ɛyɛ ɔsɔhwɛ a, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ nsɛmmisa. Fa tebea a ɛyɛ anigye. Ka sɛ "Wopɛ sɛ menka ɛfã no anaa wopɛ sɛ woapaw?" na ɛnyɛ sɛ "Hwan na ɛyɛ?" Ma nhyɛso no bere a wɔatwɛn, ɛnyɛ ansa na obi no abɔ mmuaeɛ.

Sɛ obi no ayɛ basaa anaa ɔwɔ awerɛhoɔ a, gyae agodi no na kɔ so. Yɛn [communication tips](/caregiving/communication-tips/) kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛsesa kwan a wofa so anaa wobɛma ɛnyɛ basabasa.

## Akasɛm a ɛfata obi

- **Fa obi no ankasa akasɛm.** Bisa abusua sɛ dɛn na ɔno anaa ne yere kasa kɔɔ so daa. Akasɛm a ɛfiri mmofra bere mu no ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛte.
- **Paw akasɛm a ɛyɛ tiaa na ɛfa nkwa a ɛda so.** Nsɛm a ɛfa mframa, afiadie, akuw, abusua ne bere ahodoɔ no yɛ dodow na ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛte.
- **Fa ɛnyɛ den.** Akasɛm a ɛfa nsɛm a ɛyɛ dedaw anaa nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ den no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ nsɛnnennen. Sie ma nnipa a wɔpɛ.
- **Hwɛ kasa no yie.** Akasɛm dodow nnyin nsɛm foforo mu. Fa akasɛm a obi no nim wɔ ne kasa mu, mpo sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wohwehwɛ akasɛm foforo.

## Akasɛm sɛnea ɛyɛ mu

Wɔ mmuaeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ no, ɛdɛ a ɛwɔ ase no yɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ sɛ wɔka kyerɛ wo. Kenkan ɛfã no, na ɛnyɛ nea wɔka wɔ ase, na twɛn.

- **Mframa:** "Osu a ɛdi kan..." (mmuaeɛ: "ɛbɛyɛ pɛ"); "Ɔpɛ biara wɔ..." (ɛyɛ anigye).
- **Aduane ne aduane a wɔdidi:** "Nnipa a ɛwɔ hɔ dodow..." (mmuaeɛ: "ɛbɛsɛe ɛdeɛ"); "Mfa wo nkɔkɔɔ nnɔ nni..." (mmuaeɛ: "ansa na ɛbɛfifi").
- **Fie ne abusua:** "Fie yɛ..." (mmuaeɛ: "ɛyɛ ɔkwan a yɛbɛbɔ"); "Mogya..." (mmuaeɛ: "ɛyɛ den sen nsu").
- **Bere ahodoɔ ne afuo:** "Osu a ɛba ɛbɛma..." (mmuaeɛ: "aba"); "Nea wobɛdua..." (mmuaeɛ: "ɛyɛ nea wɔde bɛdua").

Sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a, akasɛm a wɔde ma wo mu no yɛ akasɛm a wɔaka wɔ wo fie. Ɛnsɛ sɛ wode nsɛm a ɛwɔ kwan a ɛnyɛ pɛ nkɔ so.

## Sɛ obi no ntumi nka mmuaeɛ a?

Nnipa bi ntumi nkae nsɛmfua biara wɔ mmara a ɛdi kan no mu. Mma wo ho nnyae akasɛm no. Kenkan no, fa anigye ka, na frɛ mmuaeɛ de nkyerɛkyerɛ, sɛ wɔbɛma wo ntiso anaa wɔpaw mfonini. Ɛdɛ a wɔpɛ no nso ka nnipa abom. Fa agodi no ka nnwom, nsa a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, anaa wɔ nsa so asɛe, sɛnea yɛn [late-stage activities](/activities/late-stage/) kyerɛ.

## Akwan foforo a wobɛfa akasɛm so

Akasɛm boa mu kɔ ɛfã a ɛfa obi no dedaw ho ka. Wobetumi de [reminiscence questions](/activities/reminiscence-questions/) bisa ɛfã ho asɛm, anaa wobɛtumi de agodi a ɛfa ho te sɛ [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/). Sɛ wopɛ nsɛm foforo a ɛfa da no mu ho a, hwɛ [activities for people with dementia](/activities/ideas/).
