Bingo yɛ dwumadie a nnipa pii a wɔakɔ akyire, nkwaseafo, di so, a ɛyɛ anigye. Nanso nkaeɛ no ebia ɛnyɛ yie ma obi a dementia wɔ ne so. Picture bingo di nkɔso no kwan koro, na ɛde mfonini kɛseɛ a ɛda adi sesa nkaeɛ no. Wobɛtumi ayɛ no wɔ fie, wɔ da mu adwumayɛbea mu, anaa wɔ ayaresabea mu. Saa tɛkstɛ yi bɛkyerɛ sɛnea wobɛyɛ no, sɛnea wobɛsesa no, ne sɛnea wobɛma anigye ka dwumadie no ho.

## Sɛnea picture bingo yɛ adwuma

Obiara wɔ kaad a ɛwɔ mfonini nkyerɛwde mu, te sɛ ɔdan a wɔde tii nsuo, ɔberɛ, ɔpɛ, ɔpɔnkɔ anaa baesikɛl. Ɔfrɛfoɔ no bɛkyerɛ anaa ka mfonini biako biako. Nnipa de nkyerɛnkyerɛ bɔ mfonini a ɛtɔ so wɔ wɔn kaad no ano. Obi a ɔma ne nkyerɛwde ma ɔhyɛ ma ɛyɛ ɔsoro anaa nea kurupɔ no ahyɛ ase no, ɔtumi frɛ "Bingo," nanso nkurɔfoɔ pii ntumi nkɔ so de no ato so.

Wobɛtumi de [printable activities](/activities/printable/) kaad a ɛwɔ hɔ no, anaa [picture bingo game](/games/picture-bingo/) a ɛwɔ ɔfrɛfoɔ a ɛwɔ screen so ma nnipa kakraa no. Ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛde tablet bɛma nnipa nyinaa hwɛ.

## Sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ bere a mo bɛyɛ

- **Fa nnipa kakraa.** Nnipa anan kɔsi mmiako yɛ papa. Nnipa pii yɛ den sɛ wɔbɛdi akyi, na ebia ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wɔrehyɛ ntɛm.
- **Ma mfonini no nyɛ kɛseɛ.** Fa nsɛmfua a ɛda adi, na ma adwene biako wɔ sikwankyɛn biara mu. Gyae kaad a ɛyɛ den.
- **Hyehyɛ nnipa yie.** Ma obiara nyɛ ɔfrɛfoɔ no ne ne kaad no hwɛ yiye, na ma ɔboafoɔ bɛn wɔn sɛ wɔhia.
- **Fa nkyerɛwde a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.** Chip kɛseɛ, sika anaa nsɛnkyerɛnne a wɔde ntin ka bɔ papa sen ɔpɛn. Fa bi a ɛbɛdi akyi.
- **Yɛ bere tiaa.** Nkɔmhyɛ ahyɛ mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa, na ɛmfa ho. Gyae ntɛm sɛ ahoɔden bɔ fam.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kyerɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔnsesa dwumadie no sɛ nea obi tumi yɛ, enti sɛ wɔbɛyɛ kaad ne mmara no nyɛ mmerɛw a, nyɛ saa.

## Sɛnea wobɛfrɛ mfonini no

1. **Fa mfonini no to ɔpon ansa na wobɛka asɛm.** Anaa twe so wɔ screen no so.
2. **Ka din no yie,** de asɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw: "Ɔberɛ." Ka no bio pɛn, da adi.
3. **Twɛn.** Ma nnipa bere sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ na wɔde bɔ ano. Nfrɛ mfonini a edi so kosi sɛ nnipa pii anya bere.
4. **Kyerɛkyerɛ, sɛ ɛboa.** "Ɔpɛ a ɛyɛ fitaa" anaa "nea yɛnoe wɔ asaase nnwuma no" betumi ama nkɔmhyɛ ba.
5. **Ma dwumadie no nyɛ yie.** Ɔfrɛfoɔ a odwo no ho hia sen ɔfrɛfoɔ a ɔyɛ ntɛm.

## Sɛnea wobɛma nkonya yɛ nea wopɛ

Nkonya betumi ayɛ ɔhaw. Obi a ɔreto ase no betumi ahu sɛ wɔrehwɛ no, na obi a ɔsuro sɛ ɔbɛnya nkonya no betumi ayɛ akɔnnɔ. Yei ne ebinom a wɔbɛtumi de ayɛ:

- **Obiara nya mfonini biako.** Obiara frɛ mfonini biako a ɔpɛ, na nnipa no ani gye.
- **Kyerɛkyerɛ kaad a ɛma.** Srɛ nnipa sɛ wɔnkyerɛkyerɛ mfonini biako a ɛwɔ wɔn kaad so.
- **Di so kɔsi sɛ mfonini no awie.** Frɛ mfonini nyinaa a ɛwɔ kaad no so, na afi ase foforo, a obiara nnya nkonya sɛ ɔdi kan.
- **Fa mfaso ketewa anaa nnyae.** Kɔkɔ anaa nnwom yɛ papa sen ɔbɔadeɛ.

## Sɛnea wobɛsesa ma nnipa a wɔ mpɔtam akyi

Ma nnipa a wɔhwehwɛ mfonini no yɛ wɔn den no, fa kaad a ɛwɔ nsɛ anan anaa abien kɔsi nkaeɛ no nkyɛn. Anaa ma obiara nya kaad biako nkyɛn. Ma ɔboafoɔ kyɛ kaad no bere a ɔfrɛfoɔ no pene so, anaa sɛ wobɛtwe chip no ma wɔn bere a wɔapene so. Ebinom betumi ate ɔfrɛfoɔ no so de ahoɔfɛ, na wɔn ani gye sɛnea nsɛm no yɛ no.

Hwɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɔnnye, te sɛ sɛ wɔdwane anaa wɔfi ne ho, na gyae sɛ wohu saa. Sɛ wopɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ dwumadie a ɛnyɛ den a wɔtete wɔ fam a, hwɛ [late-stage activities](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo wɔ ayaresabea ne da mu adwumayɛbea mu

Dwumadie a nnipa yɛ bom yɛ papa sɛ wɔhyehyɛ no ho ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dan no mu. Yɛn [group activities guide for care homes and day programs](/activities/care-home-groups/) kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ bere a mo bɛyɛ, sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nnipa, ne sɛnea wobɛyɛ nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkɔ. Picture bingo fa nnwom anaa abakɔsɛm yɛ papa, na ɛfa [finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) yɛ dwumadie a ɛyɛ ntɛm bere a ɛreba akyiri wɔ bere no mu.

## Bingo nea ɛtumi yɛ ne nea ɛntumi nyɛ

Picture bingo ma nnipa di so, na ɛyɛ anigye. Ɛtumi bɔ nnipa abom, ma nnipa hwɛ mfonini ne tie nsɛm, na ɛma kurom yɛ hyew. Ɛntumi nyɛ dementia ayaresa anaa ɛnnyɛ nkaeɛ no nkɔso. Hwɛ sɛnea nnipa te ne sɛnea wɔn ho te bere a dwumadie no reyɛ, na ɛnyɛ sɛnea obi di so no.
