Sɛ obi a wodɔ no wɔ Alzheimer's yare a, aduru baabi wo ahu sɛ nneɛma bi kɔ ntɛm, nanso afoforo tena. Ebia ɔrenkae nkɔmhyɛ a wowɔ nnɛ anadwo, nanso ɔbɛka nnwom a ɛbɛyɛ mpɛn kɔkɔ no nsɛm nyinaa, anaasɛ ɔbɛboa ntadeɛ sɛnea ɔyɛ daa, anaasɛ ɔbɛdeɛ kaa ntoma a ɔnnhwɛ ho. Akɔnnɔ a ɛyɛ paa fam nneyɛe a ɛtena so no. Saa nkyekyerɛ yi kyerɛ adwene a ɛwɔ ho, na ɛde agoro a ɛbɛyɛ ma mfeasɛ biara ne Golden Moments agoro a ɛyɛ free a ɛfata ma mo nyinaa ka ho.

## Alzheimer's akɔnnɔ fa dementia akɔnnɔ ho nsonsonoe anaa?

Ɛnyɛ nsonsonoe kɛseɛ. Dementia yɛ din a yɛka ka nkaebɔ bebree ho, na Alzheimer's yɛ dementia a ɛba bebree: [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) ka sɛ ɛyɛ 60 kɔsi 80 pasent wɔ dementia nsɛm nyinaa mu. Nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw fa dementia akɔnnɔ ho nyinaa fa Alzheimer's so, ne yɛn [101 akɔnnɔ nsusuwii](/activities/ideas/) nso.

Nea ɛyɛ mfaso ne sɛnea nneɛma a ɛsesa no di kan. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-alzheimers-disease) kyerɛ sɛ Alzheimer's bɔ ɔdwene ne nkaebɔ ho ban a ɛtena adwene mu kane, na akyiri no ɛka kasa, ntease ne ɔkwan a ɔfa so bom nne ho. Alzheimer's Association ka sɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛba mfitiase bebree ne sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ obedi nsɛm foforo a ɔsua ho nkyɛ. Nnipa nyinaa nyɛ pɛ, na nnipa bebree wɔ nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛyɛ nnansa, nanso saa kwan yi kyerɛ nea ɛboa kɛse.

## Akɔnnɔ bɛn na ɛfata Alzheimer's sen biara?

Akɔnnɔ a ɛde nimdeɛ a wɔayɛ bere tenten ne nneyɛe a ɛyɛ no nyinaa di so no, sɛ ɛyɛ, tɔ so sen nneyɛe a ɛhia sɛ wɔsua foforo na wɔkora so.

- **Nneyɛe a wɔayɛ bere tenten.** Alzheimer's Association ka sɛ obi betumi aka nnwom a ɛyɛ kɔkɔ a ɔsuaa wɔ piano so mfeɛ bebree atɔ so. Kɔnnti, sanwi dua, ntadeɛ hwɛ, nkɔhwe ahoma ne ntoma a wɔboa ho no yɛ saa ara.
- **Nnwom a wɔdɔ ne nnwom a wɔayɛ bere tenten.** Nnipa bebree ka nnwom a wɔtenaa so bere a wɔyɛ mmerante ne wɔn mmofra, nnwom a wɔhyɛ mu anaasɛ nnwom a wɔyɛɛ wɔ wɔn aware da no nyinaa. Hwɛ [akɔnnɔ a ɛfa nnwom ho](/activities/music/).
- **Adwuma a ɛwɔ botae a wɔyɛ da biara.** [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kyerɛ nneɛma te sɛ ntɔso nkuku, aduane ahyehyɛ, ntoma a wɔhyehyɛ ne ntoma a wɔboaboa sɛ nneɛma a nnipa bebree ani gye. Ɛho hia sɛ wɔka ho sɛ wɔboa.
- **Hu mu anaa ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛkae.** Ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ obi bɛhwɛ ɔfa a ɛpɛ sen sɛ ɔbɛkae ne din. Ɛno nti mfonini a wɔhwɛ ne nnwinnade a ɛnyɛ nsɛmmisa yɛ adwuma sen nsɛmmisa.
- **Nneɛma a yɛtie ne sɛnea yɛte.** Nkɔnkɔnsɛm, ɛwɔ a ɛnnya, ɛhye, aduane ne nnwom sɛnea wɔte no yɛ nea ɛho hia bere tenten, bere a nsɛm no ayɛ den. Hwɛ [akɔnnɔ a ɛfa nneɛma a yɛtie ho](/activities/sensory/).

Nneɛma a ɛnyɛ yie kɛse ne: bere a wɔhwɛ, mmara foforo a ɛhia sɛ wɔsua, anaasɛ nneyɛe bebree a ɛhia sɛ wɔkae, anaasɛ nsɛmmisa a ɛsɔ nkaebɔ hwɛ, te sɛ "Wokae obi yi anaa?" Saa nneɛma yi de Alzheimer's akyɛde a ɛyɛ den no bɛba kɛse wɔ ananmu.

## Akɔnnɔ ma Alzheimer's mfeasɛ

Wɔ mfeasɛ no mu, [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/stages) ka sɛ obi betumi aka sɛ ɔbɛkɔ kar so, ayɛ adwuma ne akɔnnɔ a ɛfa afoforo ho ka, bere a ɔrehunu sɛ ɔrekae nsɛm, sɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ kɛse a wɔaka no ne nneɛma a wɔde wɔn hyɛ hɔ. Ɛnam ka ho a ɛyɛ papa so no, bisa nea ɔpɛ sɛ ɔtena so ne mmoa a ɔpɛ.

1. **Ma wɔnkɔ so nnwuma a wɔpɛ.** Gɔfu, bridge, kɔtɔn ntoma, ne asɔredan dwom ne asɔre nkuo, nanso ma ɛyɛ ntaa ne bere a ɛyɛ dwodwo a ɛnyɛ den.
2. **Yɛ kaad ne boɔd gemu a wɔawɔ wɔ fie.** [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) kyerɛ trivia, nsɛmfua a wobɛhwehwɛ, kaad gemu ne boɔd gemu ma mfeasɛ no. Yɛ no sɛ kuw, anaasɛ gyae sɛ wɔbɛhwehwɛ nkontaabu sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ɔhaw ma wɔn.
3. **Yɛ aduane bom.** Ma ɔnnyɛ aduane a ɔnim sɛ ɔbɛdi so, na wo ara wo kenkan nkyerɛwde.
4. **Fi ase nkaebɔ a ɛfa wɔn asetra ho.** Mfonini, aduane ne nkyerɛase a ɔde ahyehyɛ wɔ nkyerɛwde bi mu. Hwɛ [nkaebɔ a ɛfa ɔkwan a wɔfa so kae asetra ho](/activities/reminiscence/).
5. **Kɔ akɔnnɔ mu.** Aduane a ɔpɛ wɔ bere a ɛnyɛ dwodwo, dwumadie a ɛyɛ ntakra ma nkɔhwɛ, anaasɛ nantew wɔ akuraa mu.
6. **Yi nneɛma a wɔayi ho ankasa.** Nkaebɔ a ɛyɛ kɛse, nkontaabu a wɔtwa ahyɛ mu, ne nneɛma a wɔyɛ ho ahyehyɛ. Hwɛ [nneɛma a wɔyɛ ma Alzheimer's ho](/activities/puzzles/).

Nsusuwii a ɛboro saa: [mfeasɛ akɔnnɔ](/activities/early-stage/).

## Akɔnnɔ ma Alzheimer's mfeasɛ a ɛtwa mfinimfini

Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ mfeasɛ a ɛtwa mfinimfini no yɛ bere tenten a ɛboro so, na ɛnna mfeɛ bebree. Nsɛm bi ntwentwe, abufuw ba ntɛm, na ɔhwɛ nnipa ho paa. Alzheimer's Foundation of America ka sɛ nnipa bebree hia mmoa sɛ wɔbɛfi ase anaasɛ wɔbɛkɔ so nneyɛe. Wo dwuma sesa kɔ sɛ wobɛhyehyɛ nneɛma, wodi kan kyerɛ ɛmfa nkyerɛkyerɛ, na wobɛfa adwuma a ɛyɛ den no.

1. **Fa, hyehyɛ ne di so.** Aduane, ntoma, ahwɛnan, bɔtan a wɔhyehyɛ sɛnea ɛte. Ɛyɛ adwuma nyinaa a wɔyɛ ka ho.
2. **Yɛ nnwom na fa ho na wɔnsi.** Pɛ nsa, nyimpa anaasɛ yɛ kɛse wɔ ɔdan mu. Fi ase ka nnwom no ho, na ma wɔn nso nka ho.
3. **Hwɛ nkaebɔ nwoma.** Ka nea wohunu, na fre wɔn sɛ wɔnka nsɛm. Ka sɛ, "Ɛyɛ kar pa. Ka ho asɛm mma me." Di nsɛnkyerɛnne a wɔde ma so.
4. **Di mfonini a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.** [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) a mfonini anan kɔ nkyɛn anaasɛ [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) a ɛwɔ nkyerɛwde abien anaasɛ mmiɛnsa.
5. **Kyerɛ mmebusɛm a wɔaka dedaw.** "Apɔ a wotu da biara no..." Mmebusɛm a wɔkyerɛkyerɛɛ bere tenten no ba ntɛm. Sɔ [Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) hwɛ, faa baabi a ɔbɛyɛ no nyinaa ntwa.
6. **Gugu ne hwɛ nnua.** Nnua a wɔde wɔ fie anaasɛ ɔboɔde a ɛsen fam, na ɛwɔ nsuo bobɔ a ɛyɛ kakraa.
7. **Yɛ mfonini gemu fi fie mfonini mu.** [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) de mfonini a wopɛ a ɛwɔ wo nnwinnade so di dwuma, na wɔnnyi wɔn fi saa nnwinnade no so.

Nsusuwii a ɛboro saa: [mfinimfini akɔnnɔ](/activities/middle-stage/).

## Akɔnnɔ ma Alzheimer's awieeɛ

Wɔ awieeɛ no, Alzheimer's Association ka sɛ nnipa tu wɔn ahoɔden a wɔde gye wɔn ɛha ne sɛ wɔbɛka kasa, nanso wɔrebɛtumi aka nsɛmfua anaasɛ mmebusɛm bi. Akɔnnɔ yɛ ahotɔ ne ne ho ahobrɛaseɛ, a wɔde ba baabi a ɔda so ara.

1. **Gyina nnwom a ɔpɛ** ntɛm, a TV nni ho nkɔm.
2. **Hyɛ nsa so a wɔde nnuane a ɔpɛ ahyɛ.**
3. **Ma nneɛma a ɛyɛ nwini a ɔbɛkyere.** Kɔtɔn abɔnten a ɛyɛ hyew, fleece, anaasɛ ɔboulo a ɛyɛ hyew.
4. **Kenkan nkyerɛwde.** Mmɔdenbɔ, nnwom anaasɛ abakɔsɛm a wɔtenaa so. Nsɛnkyerɛnne no yɛ kɛse sen nea ɛkyerɛ.
5. **Tena wɔ baabi a ɛyɛ hyew.** Wɔ ntease a ɛwɔ dua ase, anaasɛ wɔ adekyeeɛ a nnomaa a wɔhwɛ wɔ so.
6. **Hwɛ mfonini kɛseɛ baako nnɔnhwerew.** Ɔpɛɛ anaasɛ abɛnkyi a ɛhyɛ tablet sɛrɛn ma, ne wo a wɔka nea wuhu.

Nsusuwii a ɛboro saa: [awieeɛ akɔnnɔ](/activities/late-stage/).

## Agoro bɛn na ɛyɛ ma obi a Alzheimer's yare wɔ?

Paw agoro a obiara ntumi nhyɛ ho ase. Agoro nyinaa a ɛwɔ saa baabi yi yɛ free, wɔyɛ no wɔ browser so, na ɛnni bere, ɔkyɛ anaa mmara a wobɛkɔ so.

| Agoro | Nea wobɛyɛ | Ɛfata saa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) | Di mfonini kɛseɛ kɔ nkyɛn; ebetumi asi kɔ anifere so | Mfeasɛ ne mfinimfini |
| [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) | Fa mfonini a ɛtɔ so ne nea ɛwɔ soro no | Mfinimfini ne awieeɛ, a boafo wɔ hɔ |
| [Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) | Paw mmebusɛm a wɔaka dedaw no awiei anaasɛ ma wɔda so | Mfeasɛ ne mfinimfini |
| [Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) | Sesa nkyɛmu 4 anaasɛ 9 de sane ba baabi a ɛfata, mfonini no da so | Mfeasɛ ne mfinimfini |
| [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) | Mfonini ne asɛmmisa a ɛma nkɔkɔbɔ | Mfeasɛ biara, sɛ obi wɔ hɔ a ɔbɛka ne no |

Sɛ wopɛ sɛnea wobɛtena, boafo a wobɛboa ne ɔkwan a wɔbɛfa so, ne bere a wɔbɛsisi, hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛyɛ agoro ne obi a dementia wɔ](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/). Sɛ wowɔ nsusuwii wɔ "agoro a ɛfa adwene ho", hwɛ [agoro a ɛfa adwene ho ma nkɔnkɔnsɛm a dementia wɔ](/activities/memory-games/).

## Sɛnea wobɛyɛ akɔnnɔ biara ayɛ yie

NIA ka nneyɛe kakraa: fa akɔnnɔ no kɔ onipa no nneyɛe ho, boa no ɔkwan a ɔbɛfi ase, hwɛ sɛ ne abufuw rebɛba, ma ɔnnya ahosɛpɛw a ɛyɛ, na ma ɔntena hwɛ sɛ ɔpɛ a.

- **Fa bere a ɔyɛ ye wɔ ne nkɔm mu,** na ma nkitahodi nnyɛ tenten.
- **Hyehyɛ nneɛma nyinaa ansa na wɔrehyɛ,** na woabɔ ho ntɛm.
- **Frɛ no, na nkyerɛ no nni.** "Wobetumi aboa me?" yɛ papa sen "Bere de akɔnnɔ."
- **Yɛ ɔkwan a ɛdi kan wo ankasa,** na ma ɔnnyɛ nso ka ho.
- **Ma ɔkwan biako ne nnwinnade biako nkɔ so.**
- **Ma wɔnyɛ no ne ɔkwan.** Sɛ aduane a ɛyɛ nkataa ne ɛyɛ.
- **Gyae bere a ɛda so ara yɛ anigye.** Ɛboro bere a ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wɔbɛgyae no, ɛnyɛ atirimuɔden. Sɛ wɔmpɛ, hwɛ [bere a obi a dementia wɔ ka dabi no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ka no ankasa sɛ: fa ani gyina anigye so, na ɛnyɛ nea wɔayɛ.
