Nnipa pii a wɔwɔ dementia wɔ mmere pii a wɔde no bɛhwɛ fie, di abusua ho, anaa hwɛ adwumadan ho yie. Sɛ wɔfrɛ wo sɛ wobɛboa wɔ fie adwuma ho a, ɛbɛka sɛ "wo ara wo ne fie yi mu hia" wɔ ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ den sɛ nneɛma foforo bi. Saa asɛmmisa yi kyerɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ yie, sɛdeɛ wobɛhyehyɛ, sɛdeɛ wobɛsesa wɔ nsakrae a ɛba mu so, ne sɛdeɛ wobɛhwɛ sɛ kitchin ne nsukoro fie no yɛ ahobammɔ.

## Adɛn nti na fie adwuma yɛ ahyɛdeɛ a ɛyɛ yie?

Fie adwuma yɛ yie ɛfiri sɛ wɔn ho yɛ adwene ne wɔn botaeɛ pefee. National Institute on Aging kyerɛ sɛ nneɛma te sɛ nsuo yɛ aduane, abɔde hyehyɛ, nsuo gu, fa ahyɛ ase, fa nkrataa hyehyɛ, nkyɛmu sohwɛ ne nkyɛmu nkyekyɛ wɔ [nnipa a wɔwɔ Alzheimer's betumi ani agye](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) mu da biara. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ adwuma te sɛ abɔde hyehyɛ betumi ama obi [ahu sɛ ɔyɛ fie no mu akyinnyeɛ a wɔpɛ](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities).

Adwuma nso wɔ ne botaeɛ a ɛma ɛyɛ kwan sɛ wobehyɛ ase. "Wobetumi aboa me wɔ nkyɛmu yi ho anaa?" yɛ ade a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛka "aane" kyerɛ sen "Wopɛ sɛ yɛyɛ ahyɛdeɛ bi anaa?"

## Dɛn na fie adwuma a ɛyɛ sɛ wobɔ mmɔden sɛ wobedi so?

Fi adwuma a ɔyɛe dedaw ahyɛ ase, na afei hyɛ no ɛberɛ a ɔwɔ nnɛ.

### Nkyɛmu ne mpan-mpan

- **Fawudeɛ ne nkyɛmu nketewa fawudeɛ.** Ma wɔn kwaduaba a nneɛma nketewa ne nea ɛyɛ hɔ sɛ ntakra. Nkyɛmu nni ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ yie a wɔde fa.
- **Nkyɛmu bɔ nsa kɔ.** Fa nsaa a ɛyɛ nhyehyɛe nhyɛ abɔnten. Sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛhu nkyɛmu a ɛyɛ bɔ, fa mmoa abien anaa abiasa a wɔn ahoɔfɛ yɛ nnaadaa ahyɛ so.
- **Sohwɛ ne nkyɛmu sohwɛ.** Nkyɛmu, mpan-mpan anaa abɔde mpan a wɔbɛyɛ nyinaa sohwɛ ne nkyɛmu sohwɛ. Eyi yɛ yie ɛberɛ a fawudeɛ abɛyɛ den nnɔnhwerew ho.

### Abɔnten ne kitchin

- **Abɔnten hyehyɛ.** Hyehyɛ ɔfa baako sɛ ɛyɛ nhwɛso, na ma obi no nsɔ wɔn ho. Sɛ ɛyɛ den no, fa nneɛma baako baako ma wɔn: "Ɛha yɛ fɔki ma abɔnten biara."
- **Nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma sohwɛ.** Fa nneɛma a wɔde di dwuma, a wɔaboboa anaa wɔayɛ mu, hyɛ nkɔ ntwɔ mu, na sohwɛ wɔn fa wɔn ho. Gyae nneɛma a ɛyɛ nwoma kɛse.
- **Nsuo ma nneɛma a wɔaboboa.** Nneɛma a wɔnnyae anaa nneɛma a ɛyɛ plastic ne nkyɛmu a ɛyɛ nsuo nni mu, ne nkyɛmu nhwɛ a ɛyɛ hɔ, betumi ayɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ ahobammɔ.
- **Abɔnten anaa nsɔhwɛ fi ho ntɛm.** Ɛyɛ sɛ wɔde mpan kakra ne nsuo a ɛnyɛ den bɔ fi ho ntɛm wɔ patio anaa kitchin sradeɛ. Yɛ eyi sɛ ɔwɔ den nkwa wɔ nantew mu.

### Fie mu

- **Ɛdwuma a wɔde kɔ hɔ ne nsɔhwɛ.** Ɛdwuma a ɛyɛ nkɔ wɔ nsoromma ne nkyɛmu a ɛyɛ den so, sɛ te sɛ nsɔhwɛ anaa dwetɛ a wɔde sɔ bɔɔk.
- **Nsɔ ahyɛdeɛ.** Ma wɔn nsɔ ahyɛdeɛ a ɛnyɛ ade ne nkrataa a ɛyɛ nkrataa ayɛ, na ma wɔn ahyɛ mu: nea wɔbɛkora, nea wɔbɛsan de ayɛ, nea wɔbɛbue akyiri. Kora nkrataa a ɛyɛ nokware ne sika a wɔde yɛ adwuma baabi foforo.
- **Nsɔ ahyɛdeɛ.** Hyɛ nneɛma a wɔde sɔ bɔɔk, nkɔfa ne nsuo a wɔde nsuo sɔ.
- **Nkyekyɛ nneɛma a wɔde sɔ nnɔnhwerew.** Hyehyɛ nneɛma a wɔde sɔ nnɔnhwerew ho sɛ bɔɔtɛ, nnwaa, ne nneɛma a wɔde sɔ ho. Ɛkɔ akwan a ɛnyɛ den.
- **Nsuo gu nnua so.** Nsuo ahyɛ a ɛyɛ nketewa ne nnua kakra a wobetumi ahyɛ ase.

### Aduane a nkɔ so wɔ nhwɛso ase

Aduane betumi asan asi ahoɔden. Ɛboa ɛberɛ a ɔbɛyɛ aduane ne ɔno ankasa. Nkyerɛkyerɛ a National Institute on Aging de ma kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ yie sɛ wɔma obi no mmoa nneɛma a wɔde yɛ aduane, na ɔbɛhwɛ, ɔbɛyɛ, ɔbɛkyekyɛ ne ɔbɛhwɛ nsuo, anaa ɔbɛka sɛ ɔbɛka aduane a ɔpɛ ma wɔn. Adwuma a ɛyɛ yie ni:

- nsuo a wɔbɛsuo mfuw anaa nhwɛso
- nkyekyɛ kɛnkɛn anaa bibiaa a wɔde yɛ nsuo
- nkyɛmu sohwɛ kɔkɔ
- nkyɛmu a wɔde bɛbo mfuw wɔ nnuro so
- nkyɛmu a wɔde ahyɛ so bɛbɔ brɛd a wɔde yɛ toast
- nneɛma a wɔde ahyɛ sukɔre ho bɛbɔ tray so
- nneɛma a wɔde ahyɛ pizza so

Sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛsusuw wɔ nsɔhwɛ ho a, Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ wobɛtumi asiesie nsɔhwɛ no na afei ahyɛ adwuma foforo, sɛ te sɛ nkyɛmu sohwɛ.

## Dɛn na mɛyɛ na adwuma no akɔ yie?

1. **Yi bere a ɛyɛ.** Fa bere a obi no ahome yiye, ne ne ho nni ahohia no. Nnipa pii no, eyi yɛ anɔpa.
2. **Yi beaeɛ no.** Adwuma baako, beaeɛ baako, na nneɛma foforo nnkɔ abɔnten so.
3. **Frɛ obi, nnyina no ahyɛde.** "Me hia obi a ɔbɛboa me wɔ nkyɛmu yi ho" yɛ ɛboa sen "Ɛberɛ a wɔde nkyɛmu bɛsohwɛ aba."
4. **Kyerɛ no ɔkwan wɔ ade no mu.** Sohwɛ nkyɛmu baako mprɛnsɛ, na twɛn kɔ sɛ wɔde ba so. Ɛboa sen sɛ wɔkyerɛkyerɛ.
5. **Ma nkyekyɛ biara ba.** Nsɛm a ɛdɔɔso pii bɛyɛ den. Twɛn kɔsi sɛ wɔawie nkyekyɛ baako ansa na woaka nkyekyɛ a edi hɔ kɔ.
6. **Yɛ adwuma ne wo ho.** Adwuma pɛ a wɔyɛ ne wo ho ma ɛyɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn, na ɛnyɛ nhwɛsoɔ.
7. **Kasa fa adwuma ho, na ɛnyɛ nea ɛba ho.** "Eyi yɛ mmerɛw paa" anaa "Ka kyerɛ me sɛdeɛ woyɛ no fa nkyɛmu kɔ abɔnten mu" ma nkɔkɔbɔ ba, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wobɛsɔ obi so.

## Dɛn na mɛyɛ sɛ mesesa adwuma ɛberɛ a ahoɔden sesa?

Ahoɔden sesa ɛberɛ ne ɛberɛ, ne ɛda biara. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ sɛ yadwuma sesa a, fa adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne nea wɔsan yɛ daa so.

| Sɛ eyi asɛe seesei | Yɛ eyi mmom |
| --- | --- |
| Nkyɛmu kɛse fawudeɛ | Nkyɛmu nketewa fawudeɛ anaa nkyɛmu sohwɛ |
| Abɔnten a ɛma mmerɛ | Hyɛ nkyɛmu baako ma obi biara wɔ nʼanammɔn |
| Nkrataa kyekyɛ nkyɛmu | Ɛpo nkyɛmu a wɔde hyɛ baako mu |
| Nneɛma a wɔde yɛ aduane | Nsuo gu, anaa hwɛ ne sɔ wɔ aduane yɛ no |
| Fie a ɛyɛ kɛse mu tɛ | Pe baabi bi a ɛyɛ nketewa |
| Adwuma biara a ɛyɛ den | Ntena wo ho ne me ho ka wo sɛ wɔyɛ adwuma no |

Hwɛ nso yɛ adwuma. National Institute on Aging kyerɛ sɛ obiara a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ nkyɛmu no yɛ eyi sɛ ɔyɛ ne ho. Ɛno yɛ kwan a ɛyɛ yie sɛ ɔbɛka ne ho.

## Dɛn na mɛyɛ sɛ mema adwuma no ayɛ ahobammɔ?

Ahobammɔ ho hia kɛse, nanso ɛkyerɛ kitchin mu kɛse. National Institute on Aging [nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa fie ahobammɔ ho ma wɔn a wɔhwɛ Alzheimer's ho](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips) mu ka sɛ:

- Hyɛ aboɔden a wɔde yɛ nsukoro ho anaa yi fi hɔ, nso nkyɛmu a wɔde ntwa ahyɛ ase, a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛte sɛ bɔɔtɛ.
- Hyɛ nnuro, nkɔfa ne nneɛma a wɔde hwe nkɔ wɔ beaeɛ a wɔde kyekyɛ.
- Fa nnuro kɔkɔ ne nnuro a wɔde sɔ wɔ ɔhyɛ so.
- Fa nkyerɛwde "Stop" anaa "Very hot" bɛhyɛ ɔfɔ, toaster ne iron ho, na hyɛ wɔn baabi a wɔntumi nhyew ogya.
- Yɛ nsuo a ɛwɔ hɔ ma ɛyɛ 120°F, na ɛbɛtumi akɔ tew mfiase tua.
- Yi aduaba ne nnwuma a ɛyɛ dwetɛ a ɛyɛ sɛ nea wɔtumi di ano, na ɛma nea wɔde yɛ ayɛ no nni ɔfa.

Ɛboa nso sɛ wodi ɔkwan a ɛyɛ nokware yi so:

- Gyina dan no mu bere a ɔfɔ, ɔhyew, anaa aboɔden a ɛyɛ hyɛnyɛ wɔ adwuma mu.
- Fa nneɛma a wɔnnyae de nkyekyɛ ne abɔde sohwɛ.
- Ma sradeɛ ne beaeɛ no nyɛ ɔfɔ na nyɛ tɛ bere a wɔrekyekyɛ anaa wɔrepɛ.
- Sɛ obi no de nneɛma dii dwuma dedaw a, Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ wɔyi nneɛma a ɛyɛ hyɛnyɛ fi adwumadan mu, na adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, te sɛ dwetɛ a wɔde sɔ dua, betumi aka hɔ.

## Dɛn na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wɔyɛ no "nnyiyɛ"?

Ma ɔnkɔ. Ntoma a wɔde mfiri hɔ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔsɛnsɛn ansa na ɔyɛ fa a, ɛyɛ ntoma a wɔde mfiri. Alzheimer's Association ka no pefee sɛ deɛ ɛho hia ne sɛ obi nya bere ne sɛ obi no ahu sɛ ɔboa, na ɛnyɛ sɛ nkyɛmu no yɛ yie pɛpɛɛpɛ. Sɛ ade bi hia sɛ wɔsan yɛ bio nokware a, twɛn kɔsi akyiri, na yɛ no berɛ a nhwɛ.

Sɛ wɔkasa ne wɔn ho bere a adwuma rekɔ so no, adwuma a ɛyɛ anigye betumi ayɛ sɛ ɛkaa obi no nsɛnkyerɛnne ho. "Medaase, woaboa me kɛse" yɛ asɛm a ɛtɔ so pii.

## Ɛberɛ bɛn na yɛbɛgyae?

Gyae anaa sesa ade ɛberɛ a wuhu sɛ obi no ayɛ ahohia, ahome, anaa ne ani nnye adwuma no. Ɛnyɛ nea ɛyɛ hunu sɛ wɔaforo ntɛm. Wobetumi:

- sesa nkyekyɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw
- kɔ ade a ɛma ahome, te sɛ nnwom anaa kɔfi kakra
- san bɔ bio ɔberɛ foforo anaa ɛda foforo
- ntena wo ho ne me ho ka

Sɛ obi no ka dabi a, mma wo nkɔ akyiri. Hwɛ [sɛdeɛ wobɛyɛ sɛ obi ka dabi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) ho nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.

## Adwuma kakra a ɛnyɛ den ma ɛberɛ a ɛtɔ ase

Sɛ nneɛma a wɔde nsa yɛ no nni hɔ a, wobetumi ayɛ nhyehyɛe ne ntwitwa wɔ ɛberɛ a wotena fam. [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) ma wobetumi atwe nsɛnkyerɛnne mu kɔ kitchin anaa ɔsase kɔkɔbɔ mu, na [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) yɛ ntwitwa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a ɛwɔ nsɛnkyerɛnne kɛse ne bere biara nni mu. [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) wɔ nsɛmmisa bue a ɛfa fie ne abusua ho, na ɛyɛ yie bom ne kɔfi wɔ adwuma awie akyi.

Sɛ wopɛ mmoa a wobɛhyehyɛ adwuma wɔ da mu, hwɛ yɛn [nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ ahome da mu](/activities/daily-routine/) nkyerɛwde. Sɛ wopɛ nsɛm foforo a ɛfa mmere ho, hwɛ [ahyɛdeɛ a wɔde yɛ mmere a ɛda so](/activities/middle-stage/) ho.
