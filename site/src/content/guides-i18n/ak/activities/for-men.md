Nnipa bebree bisa "nnwinnwen ma mmarima a wɔwɔ dementia" efisɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ a wɔde ma no nnyina ɔbarima a wɔrehwehwɛ ho ano yie. Ɛyɛ nsɛm a ɛfata sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ so. Nanso mmuae no nyɛ "nnwinnwen a ɛyɛ mmarima" nkɔnhyɛ. Ɛsɛ sɛ wofi ɔbarima yi ankasa ho fi ase: adwuma a ɔyɛe, nea ɔyɛe ne awoɔ a ɔde ne nkwanneɛ too ase, ne nea ɛma ɔhunu sɛ ɔboa. Anigyedeɛ yɛ obiara yiye. Mmarima pii dɔ ayɛ aduane, nnuan, ahoma anaa dwom, na mmaa pii nso dɔ kar ne baseball.

## Ɛdeɛn na metumi ahunu nnwinnwen a ɔbɛpɛ?

Fi ne abakɔsɛm ase. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ wɔsɛ sɛ wɔde [nhyehyɛe a ɛda so](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) too onipa no nneɛma a ɔpɛ, nneɛma a ɔmpɛ, ne ne tumi, ne anigyedeɛ so, ne sɛnea ɔhyehyɛɛ ne da ase. Bisa wo ankasa sɛ:

- **Dɛn na ne adwuma yɛ?** Office, afuo, adwumadan, adwumayɛ, asraafo, nkɔsoɔ, adwuma a ɛwɔ fie?
- **Dɛn na ɔyɛ wɔ ne ntɛm a ɔnni adwuma mu?** Nnuan, kaate, wood adwuma, nnwinnwen, asɛnnennen, dwom, aduane, kar a ɛwɔ ɔdan mu?
- **Dɛn ne nhyehyɛeɛ a ɔdi?** Kɔfi ne nkrataa adekyee? Ɔhwɛ ewiase ntɛm? Ɔsukuu kar no adwoma?
- **Nneɛma bɛn na ɔhyɛ ase ankasa a wɔrenka kyerɛ no?** Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ sɛ obi fi adwuma ase a wɔrennka kyerɛ no, te sɛ ɔfifiri fasɔ a, saa adwuma no betumi aba ne da biara nhyehyɛeɛ mu.
- **Dɛn na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔtwe ne ho afi?** Obiara ntoaa nnwinnwen a ɛfa sɛ wɔrehwɛ mpɔw anaa nnyigyei. Hwɛ bere a ɔyɛ anigye, a ɔsuro anaa ɔyɛ abufuw.

Kasa nso kɔ abusua ne nkɔfo a wɔyɛ mmɔden no nkyɛn. Wɔbɛtumi akae nneɛma a ɔdɔ no a ɛwɔ hɔ ansa na ɛnnyina da biara mu.

## Nnwinnwen a ɛfa adwuma a ɔyɛe ho

Alzheimer's Association de ɛyɛ kyerɛkyerɛ a ɛda so: ɔdwumadan a ɔyɛe wɔ office mu betumi ani agye sɛ ɔyɛ nneɛma a ɛfa nhyehyɛe ho, te sɛ sɛ ɔbɛde sika bɛka ne mu anaa ɔboa wɔ nkrataa a wɔde bɛkɔ mu anaa ɔyɛ asɛnkyerɛnne a ɔwɔ hɔ; ɔfuo a ɔyɛe wɔ afuo mu betumi ani agye sɛ ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ afuo mu. Saa adwuma yi ho nso yɛ adwuma a ɛwɔ nnipa pii adwuma mu.

| Adwuma a ɔyɛe ansa na ɔrenyɛ yei | Nnwinnwen | Nea ɛyɛ mmerɛ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office anaa dwumadie | Nkrataa a wɔbɛhyehyɛ wɔ faele mu, nsɛnkyerɛnne a wɔbɛgu so, nsɛm a wɔbɛhwɛ, desk drawer a wɔbɛhyehyɛ | Nsɛnkyerɛnne a wɔbɛhyehyɛ; nsɔnsɔn a wɔde bɛgu bokɔ mu |
| Adwuma a wɔde nsa yɛ anaa mechanic | Nkyɛn, bolt ne washers a ɛyɛ pa a wɔbɛhyehyɛ; bolt akɛse a wɔbɛsɔre nsa so; kɔm katalogu a wɔbɛhwɛ | Hyehyɛ wɔ kɛse so wɔ nkɔm mmienu mu |
| Kuayɛ anaa afuo | Nsu, nnua a wɔbɛdua, nhaban a wɔbɛyi, nkyerɛ aduaba | Ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ kɛse mu bere a wʼadwuma; kyɛ nkotoɔ a wɔde di aduaba |
| Asraafo | Ɛfaho a wɔbɛpo nnaprɛw, ɛhyehyɛ kabonan, nhwɛ mfonini a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ fi asraafo bere no mu | Tie dwom a ɛfa saa bere no ho |
| Dwadie anaa ɔboafoɔ | Ɛfa ahyɛ nnipa, de nnuanee bɛma wɔn, hyehyɛ kupɔn | Tena ɔpon ano sɛ ɔbɛka akyɛ nnipa |
| Nkyerɛkyerɛ | Kenkan nkrataa, hyehyɛ nkrataa, hyehyɛ mfonini sɛnea wɔkyerɛ | Hwɛ mfonini nkrataa mu wɔ abɔ mu |
| Akwantu anaa kwan so | Hwɛ mmɔdensɛm anaa kaa nhwɛsoɔ a ɛyɛ dada, kar a wɔbɛsa | Tena fie ɔpon ano na hwɛ kaa a ɛreforo |
| Fie ne abusua | Pete ntoma, hyehyɛ ɛfie, boa ma abɛ | Hyehyɛ pɛsɛmfoɔ; sɛɛ |

Asɛm bi, te sɛ asraafo, betumi de nkɔso koro wɔ akoma mu. Di ne kwan so, ma nkɔm a wɔka wɔ abakɔsɛm ho ayɛ fɛ, na sesa asɛm no nʼakwan mpɛ sɛ ɔnyɛ ɔyaw.

## Nnwinnwen a ɛfa anigyedeɛ ho

1. **Wood adwuma.** Sanding wood a ɛyɛ nwonwa anaa ntoma wɔ birdhouse. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ wɔyi nneɛma a ɛyɛ asiane ne nneɛma a wɔde yɛ adwuma afiri adwumahyɛ mu, sɛnea sanding betumi aka adwuma a ɛyɛ anigye na ɛyɛ ahobammɔ.
2. **Aduane.** Hyehyɛ tackle box a wɔayi hooks no, hwɛ aduane nkrataa, anaa tena nsuo ano bom.
3. **Kaa.** Sɔ kaa, tɔ chrome, hwɛ mfonini a ɛfa kaa a ɛyɛ dedaw ho, anaa kɔ akwantu sɛ ɔyɛ ɔkɔyɛ.
4. **Agorɔ.** Hwɛ agorɔ a ɛyɛ dedaw anaa nkɛntɛnso, kyerɛ bɔɔl a ɛyɛ nwonwa, kɔ golf bɔɔl wɔ carpet so, anaa hyehyɛ baseball nkyerɛwde.
5. **Kaa ne agorɔ.** Kaa a wɔdɔ ntɛm bi, dominoes anaa kaa a wɔbɛhwehwɛ ne nea ɛtoa so. Yɛ no dɛ na ɛnyɛ sɛ wobɛkyerɛw nkɔnkɔnkɔm.
6. **Dwom.** Dwom a ɛfa ne mmerante ne ne mmerante so ho, ɔdwomfoɔ a ɔdɔ, anaa nnwom a wɔbɛyɛ wɔ ɔsɔfo ase. Hwɛ yɛn [nnwinnwen ho adwuma a ɛfa dwom ho](/activities/music/).
7. **Ɛfie yɛ ne ahodoɔ.** Hohoro nkyene, sɔ aprɛ, sɛ ɔbɛmɔ nkɔm, anaa ka sɛnea ɔyɛɛ ne chili a ɛyɛ dɛ no, bere a wo ara wo yɛ.
8. **Nkrataa.** Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ onipa a ɔkenkan nkrataa daa betumi agye sɛ ɔbɛkenkan bio, mpo sɛ ɔntumi nte nsɛm no nyinaa mu. Kɔfi ne nkrataa betumi aka ne mmɛnkyɛn.
9. **Ɛfie yɛ.** Nnwinnwen a ɛyɛ nkyerɛ kɛse anaa ɛsɛ a ɛyɛ mmarima. Yi nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wobɛkyekye.
10. **Ɛboa biribi.** Didi anika, ma ɔnantwi anaa nsuo nnua. NIA kyerɛ sɛ [didi, sɔ anaa twɛn anika](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) yɛ nnwinnwen a nipa betumi agye.

## Ɛdeɛn na metumi ama nnwinnwen a ɛnnyɛ adwuma a wɔde ayɛ?

Kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ mmoa a ɛyɛ nokware. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ wɔbisa sɛ "Wobɛboa me?" na wɔde adwuma no ahyɛ botae ho. Hwɛ saa nkyerɛaseɛ yi:

- "Wopɛ sɛ yɛhyehyɛ eyi bolt yi?" (ɛte sɛ sɔhwɛ)
- "Mete bolt a wɔabɔ ne nneɛma so, na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛhyehyɛ ankɔ sɛ yɛbɛsiesie shelf. Wobɛtumi aboa me?" (ɛte sɛ adwuma)

Afei nso:

- **Fa nneɛma a ɛyɛ nokware di dwuma.** Nneɛma a ɛyɛ nokware, a wɔayi nʼakɛse a ɛyɛ asiane, katalogu a ɛyɛ nokware, mpɔtam a ɛyɛ nokware.
- **Yɛ adwuma a mmoa rebɛyɛ ho.** Nipa baanu a wɔrehwɛ adwuma baako ho ɛyɛ abusuabɔ. Ɔko a obi rehwɛ obiara ɛyɛ nhwɛso.
- **Mma wo ho nka nsɛm.** Sɛ bolt baako kɔ nkyɛn a ɛnsɛ sɛ ɛkɔ hɔ a, gyae. Ɛyɛ sɛ bere a mo wɔ mu ne ahoɔfɛ a ɔnam so ayɛ adwumayɛ no nyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Boa no afi ase.** Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ nnipa pii hwehwɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nneɛma, nanso wɔwɔ ɔhaw sɛ wɔbɛhyehyɛ na wɔafi ase. Yɛ adwuma a edi kan no mu bom.

## Ɛdeɛn na metumi ayɛ sɛ nneɛma sesa?

Sɛ dementia kɔ anim a, Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ wɔsɛ sɛ wɔde adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, a ɛsan ayɛ bio, na wɔsiesie sɛ onipa no bɛyɛ adwuma kakraa. Ɔbarima a ɔsaa enjin ansa na ɔrenyɛ yei betumi ani agye sɛ ɔkyere kaa ntwe a ɛyɛ nwonwa, ne ɔka ho asɛm, anaa ɔhwɛ sɛnea woyɛ adwuma. Ɛhwɛ adwuma nso yɛ ne ho akyinnyeɛ.

Sɛ ɔyɛ adwuma kɛse wɔ biribi mu a, adwuma a ɛfa ho nanso ɛyɛ foforo betumi ayɛ ne ho yie sen ne dadaw adwuma. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ ɔdwumayɛfoɔ a ɔyɛ nwoma betumi awer sɛ nneɛma a ɛsesa ne adwuma, bere a ɔdwumayɛfoɔ a ɔdɔ no betumi ani agye sɛ ɔbɛyɛ adwuma foforo. Saa ara na ɛte ma ɔyɛ dwadie a ɔyɛ adwuma so.

## Ahobammɔ wɔ adwuma a wɔde nsa yɛ mu

- Yi nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmoɔ, nkotoɔ, nnwa ne nsɛnkyerɛnne a nsoroma yɛ hyew.
- Fa mfuw, gasoline ne nnuro a ɛyɛ asiane ka mu.
- Sɛ ɔntumi nnantew bio a, fa kaa nkotoɔ no kɔ baabi a ɔrentumi nhu.
- Hwɛ adwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ adwumadan ne afuo mu nyinaa so.
- Yi nnwinnwen a ɛyɛ akɛse a wɔntumi nnye.

## Sɛ ɔka daabi a?

Gye daabi no toto ho. Daabi betumi ayɛ sɛ adwuma no ayɛ den, ɛyɛ ɔbofra, anaa ɛnyɛ ne ho ɛnnɛ. Alzheimer's Association ka sɛ wɔnnnye no. Yɛ adwuma foforo, bere foforo, anaa mo ne mo tenatena. Hwɛ [nea wɔyɛ sɛ wɔka daabi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).
