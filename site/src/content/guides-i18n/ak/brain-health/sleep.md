Ɔhyɛse ne adwene ahoɔden ka ho pefee, nanso sɛnea wɔka ho no ka ho ka ho yɛ nhwehwɛmu. Nhwehwɛmu bi kyerɛ sɛ ɔhyɛse a ɛyɛ tiaa ka ho sɛ dementia ba kɛse. Dementia nso de, ɛtwe ɔhyɛse afiri. Saa krataa yi kyerɛ nea nhwehwɛmu kyerɛ ne nea ɛnkyerɛ, de ɔhyɛse nhyehyɛe a ɛboa, na ɛka ɔhyɛse ɔhaw a ɛba dementia mu ho asɛm. Ɛnkyerɛ nhyehyɛe ho nkyerɛkyerɛ ho; ɛno ka ho kyerɛ dɔkota.

## Ɔhyɛse ka ho ne dementia ho?

Nhwehwɛmu bi a ɛtenaa ase kyerɛ sɛ ɔhyɛse a ɛyɛ tiaa ka ho sɛ dementia ba kɛse, nanso adwene no nnyina pefee.

- **UK nhwehwɛmu a ɛfa mfeɛ 25.** Nhwehwɛfo dii nnipa a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ 8,000 akyi wɔ [Whitehall II study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22354-2) mu. Nnipa a wɔdaa nnɔnhwerew enum anaa ɛba ase wɔ mfeɛ 50 anaa 60 no nyaa dementia ɔsɔre kɛse sen wɔn a wɔdaa nnɔnhwerew nson. Nnipa a wɔdaa nnɔnhwerew enum anaa ɛba ase wɔ mfeɛ 50, 60 ne 70 no nyaa dementia ɔsɔre bi a ɛyɛ 30% kɔ soro sen nnipa a wɔdaa nnɔnhwerew nson daa daa. Eyi yɛ nhwehwɛmu a wɔhwɛ nea ɛreba, enti ɛkyerɛ nkitahodie, nanso ɛnkyerɛ nea ɛde kɔ.
- **Lancet Commission adwene.** [2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) amfa ɔhyɛse nni nsɛnkyerɛnne 14 a yɛbɛtumi asesa mu no mu. Wɔbuu sɛ nsɛm no nyinaa nhyia. Biako ne "reverse causation": adwene a dementia de aba sesa mfeɛ pii ansa na wɔaka sɛ wɔwɔ yare, na ɛbetumi ayɛ ɔhyɛse ɔhaw. Nhwehwɛmu kɛse bi kyerɛɛ sɛ ɔhyɛse a ɛyɛ tenten ne dementia nkitahodie kɔɔ hɔ bere a wɔyii mfeɛ ɛnan a edi kan no fi hɔ.

Enti anadwo nketewa bi bere bi nnyɛ ɔhaw. Ɔhyɛse a ɛyɛ mmere nyinaa ne a ɛyɛ papa boa anigye, adwene a ɛyɛ yie ne ahobammɔ ɛnnɛ, enti ɛfata sɛ yɛpɛ no.

## Nea "adwene hohoro" nhwehwɛmu ka ho ne dɛn?

Wɔabɔ wo ho ban sɛ ɔhyɛse "hohoro" adwene. Eyi fi nhwehwɛmu nokware mu, nanso ɛrehyɛ ase.

- **Mmoaa mu.** Nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛe 2013 a [NIH ka](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/brain-may-flush-out-toxins-during-sleep) kyerɛɛ sɛ ɛnam adwene mu nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so no, adwene cells ntam mpɔtam kɔɔ so bɛyɛ 60% bere a mmoaa no dae. Nsuo tumi faa adwene mu ntɛmntɛm, wɔfrɛ no glymphatic system. Beta-amyloid, protein a ɛfa Alzheimer's ho, tumi firi adwene a mmoaa a ɛdaa no mu no ntɛmntɛm.
- **Nnipa mu.** Nhwehwɛmu ketewa a wɔyɛe 2018 ([biribiara wɔ hɔ](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1721694115)) hwɛɛ nnipa 20 a wɔyɛ yie, mfeɛ 22 kɔsi 72, bere a wɔdaa anadwo pa no ne bere a wɔnnaa anadwo biara no. Anadwo a wɔnnaa no akyi no, adwene scan no kyerɛɛ beta-amyloid sɛ ɛkɔɔ so bɛyɛ 5% wɔ asase a ɛka hippocampus, adwene ɔkwan a ɛho hia ma nkaeɛ, ho. Nhwehwɛfo no frɛɛ no kan adanse. Wɔanhu sɛ ɛnam ɔsɔre so anaa ɛnam sɛ amyloid no retu ntɛmntɛm ne ɛdɔɔ de bɛyɛ ade.
- **Ɛyɛ ɛkwan abien.** Lancet Commission ka sɛ amyloid a ɛboaboa mu betumi asesa ɔhyɛse wɔ nkulaseɛ mu.

Eyi boa ma yɛte nea nhwehwɛfo pɛ ɔhyɛse ho, nanso ɛnkyerɛ sɛ ɔhyɛse a ɛyɛ papa tumi bɔ Alzheimer's ban.

## Ɔhyɛse nhyehyɛe bɛn na ɛboa?

Nkwakoraa ɔhyɛse nnɔnhwerew 7 kɔsi 9 da biara, sɛ nnipa foforo. Eyi ka National Institute on Aging (NIA) [ho](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/sleep/sleep-and-older-adults). Ne nkyerɛkyerɛ ne yi:

- **Di nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ pɛ.** Fa hyɛ mpaneda ne sɔre bere koro, bere a wo ho nni mu da biara, ɛwom awo da mpo.
- **Yɛ adwuma a ɛma wo ho dwo.** Kenkan, tie nnwom a ɛma wo ho yɛ ahoɔdɛn, anaa fa nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew hohoro wo ho ansa na wo sɔre.
- **Ma ɔdan no ayɛ fɛ,** ɛnyɛ hyew anaa yɛ krɔnkrɔn.
- **Tew kɔfi ne sukuu nom awia ayɛ no.** Kɔfi, tii, soda ne kakaw betumi ama wo nnɔ.
- **Twe nsa afi nsã,** ɛwom sɛ ɛyɛ kakraa bi, sɛnea NIA kyerɛ.
- **Ɛnnyɛ aduane kɛse hɔ nnɔnhwerew 2 kɔsi 3 ansa na wosɔre, ne nsã a ɛyɛ kɛse awia nso.**
- **Yɛ nneyɛe ɔhyɛse daa daa, nanso ɛnnyɛ nnɔnhwerew 3 ansa na wosɔre.** Hwɛ yɛn [exercise guide](/brain-health/exercise/).
- **Ma agyinatuo ne mframa nnhyɛ sɔrebɔde mu.** NIA kyerɛ sɛ ɛnyɛ komputa, foon anaa TV wɔ hɔ.
- **Twe nnɔnhwerew ankasa anaa awia a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.**

## Ɔhyɛse ɔhaw wɔ dementia mu

Ɔhyɛse sesa dementia mu yɛ nneɛma a ɛba daa. [NIA kyerɛ](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/sleep/managing-sleep-problems-alzheimers-disease) sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ obi da ɔhyɛse pii anaa nketewa, ɔsɔre ɛnan pii anadwo, ne ɔda ɔhyɛse pii awia. Ɛka sundowning nso: ahometɔ, ɔkɔm anaa adwene ntɔ a ɛba anaa ɛyɛ kɛse bere a owia ne kwan.

NIA nkyerɛkyerɛ ma wɔn a wɔhwɛ obi ne eyi:

1. **Nya owia ne yaredwuma ntɛm.** Fi ase kɔ awia anaa tena ahuruw ano awia, na ma obi no nyɛ adwuma da biara. Owia a ɛyɛ hann betumi aboa ma nnipa adansedie nsesa.
2. **Hyehyɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ den ma anɔpa.** Fa anɔpa ho ma wɔ anɔpa, anaa di aduane kɛse wɔ awia.
3. **Ma ɔhyɛse nketewa, na ɛnnyɛ akyire.** Tew ɔhyɛse a ɛwɔ akyiri awia no.
4. **Ma anadwo nyɛ dɔ.** Hyɛ hann ase, tew nnwiriwiri, na gyina nnwom a ɛyɛ dɛ so sɛ obi no pɛ. Yɛn [late-day restlessness](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) krataa wɔ afotuo pii.
5. **Di sɔre nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ pɛ.** Kenkan krataa wɔ anadwo biara bere koro ne NIA nhwɛso biako. [Mmere nyinaa nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ pɛ](/activities/daily-routine/) boa nso.
6. **Tew kɔfi ne nsã.**
7. **Fa hann kakraa** wɔ adaka, sɔre dan ne asuo dan mu.

Ɛfa anadwo a obi sɔre ho no, NIA [ahobammɔ afotuo](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips) ka hann kakraa anaa hann a ɛbɔ sɛ ɛhyɛ ara, nkɔnkɔnkɔ wɔ asuo dan mu, ntaban a ɛyɛ nnɔnhwerew ne tiri ntam, ne aduru a wɔkora wɔ baabi a wɔbɛtumi akora.

Anadwo ɔkwan a ɛyɛ nketewa, a ɛyɛ dɛ, betumi aboa mo nyinaa adwo. Yɛ [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ahwehwɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛ anaa [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) kakraa a wɔnnyɛ hwee. Twe nneyɛe a ɛde ahoɔden ba sɔre ho, na gyae bere biara a obi no ayɛ ma no.

Ɔhyɛse a ɛyɛ mmerɛ tumi hwe obi a ɔhwɛ no ase nso. Yɛn [caregiver self-care guide](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) kyerɛ ɔkwan a wobetumi anya ɔhyɛse ne mmoa so.

## Sleep apnea ho asɛm bɛn?

Sleep apnea de ntrɛwmu nketewa bi ba ɔhyɛse bere mu, a ɛbaa anadwo pii. NIA kyerɛ sɛ sɛ wɔammu no a, ɛbetumi ama mogya kɔ so kɔ soro, ɔyare abɔdeɛ, anaa adwene ayera. Nsɛnkyerɛnne ne nne a ɛyɛ den kɛse ne ɔhyɛse a ɛda owia mu, nnipa pii nnim sɛ wɔwɔ yi. Lancet Commission kyerɛ nhwehwɛmu 11 a wɔhwehwɛɛ mu sɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ sleep apnea nyaa dementia ɔsɔre bi a ɛyɛ 43% kɔ soro. Nanso nhwehwɛmu bi nni ɔdɔ ho, enti nkitahodie no nni mu pefee.

Wɔbetumi ahu sleep apnea na wɔayɛ no. NIA kyerɛ sɛ CPAP mfidie yɛ adwuma bere nyinaa. Sɛ wo anaa obi a wohwɛ no nne gyigyigyi kɛse, ɔntumi nhome bere a ɔrehye, anaa ɔda owia mu nnɔnhwerew kɛse a, ka ho kyerɛ dɔkota.

## Ɔhyɛse aduru ho asɛm

Yɛnnhyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ ho ɔhyɛse aduru anaa nnuru ho. NIA kyerɛ sɛ ɔhyɛse aduru betumi aboa bere tiaa, nanso ɛnyɛ Insomnia aduru. Ɛka nso sɛ aduru bi a wɔde ma Alzheimer's nnipa a wɔn ɔhyɛse yɛ den no betumi de ahohwi, ɔyare abɔdeɛ ne adwene ayera ba. Nnyɛ hwee a ɛfa ɔhyɛse aduru biara ho, sɛ ɛyɛ nea wɔtɔn wɔ dwumadie mu a, ayaayaa anaa sesa ho, a wo ankasa kɔka ho kyerɛ dɔkota anaa adwumayɛfo a ɔtɔn aduru.

## Ka ho kyerɛ dɔkota

Hwɛ dɔkota sɛ wo anaa obi a wohwɛ no daa daa ahohoro, anaa ɔnnyɛ ɔhyɛse anadwo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ne nne gyigyigyi kɛse, anaa ntrɛwmu bi wɔ anadwo. Sɛ ɔhyɛse ɔhaw wɔ dementia mu toa so a, [NIA ka sɛ](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) wɔnhwehwɛ afotuo afiri akwankyerɛ. Ayaresa nsɛnkyerɛnne betumi ahu nneɛma te sɛ ɔyare, ɔhyɛse ɔyare foforo, yare anaa aduru ho nkyɛnne. Adwene ayera a ɛba ntɛmntɛm, anaa ɔhyɛse a ɛsesa ne adwene ayera ka ho, hwehwɛ ayaresa ntɛm. Hwɛ [ɛberɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ dɔkota](/brain-health/warning-signs/).

Saa krataa yi yɛ nsɛmfua a ɛfa asetena ho, nnyɛ ayaresa afotuo. Hwɛ yɛn [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/).
