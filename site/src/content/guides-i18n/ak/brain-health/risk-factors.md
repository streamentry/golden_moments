## Nsɛm 14 a ɛde dementia ba ye sɛn?

[2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0), a
adwumayɛfo a ɛyɛ dementia nhwehwɛmu 27 kyerɛw, buu ho dwum sɛ nsɛm 14 a
ebetumi asesa no ka dementia nnipa bɛyɛ 45% wɔ wiase so. Ɔpɔw a ɛwɔ ase yi
biara ne Commission no ho nsusuwii a ɛfa nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia a ɛkɔ so wɔ
wiase so ho, a wɔatwa no ayɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu. Nea ɛde ɔpɔw no nyinaa ba ne sɛ
nnipa pii wɔ asiane pii, enti ɛma nkyerɛkyerɛmu no nyinaa bɛka 45% mu, na
ɛnyɛ sɛ ɛboro saa.

## Ɛsɛ sɛ mete nkyerɛkyerɛmu yi ase sɛn?

Yɛ nteaseɛ. Commission no ankasa pɔtɔ no mu kyerɛ nsɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ wokae:

- **Wɔkyerɛ nnipa dodow, ɛnyɛ nnipa biako.** University College London
  [nkyerɛase a ɛfa pɔtɔ no ho](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors)
  kyerɛ sɛ asiane a wɔsesa wɔ nnipa dodow mu boa, nanso ɛnkyerɛ sɛ obiara
  rentumi nni dementia.
- **Wɔsusuw sɛ biribi de biribi ba.** Ɔkyerɛwfo no de nsɛm a nhwehwɛmu a ɛyɛ
  nokware kyerɛ no nko ara na wɔde kɔ mu, nanso wɔka sɛ ebia ɛka ho no nyinaa
  nyɛ nea ɛde ba.
- **Wɔyɛ wiase nkyerɛkyerɛmu.** Nsɛm a ɛwɔ hɔ ne nea ɛfiri nsase a wɔyɛ nkwa
  yiye mu, na nea ɛwɔ ɔman biara anaa kurom biara mu bɛyɛ ka.
- **Ɔpɔw ketewa nso wɔ mfaso.** Asiane a ɛka nnipa 2% wɔ wiase so ho nso ka
  nnipa dodow pii.

## Nsɛm bɛn na wɔde ka ho wɔ 2024 mu?

2024 abɔdeɛ no de nsɛm baako ka nsɛm 12 a wɔkyerɛɛ wɔ 2020 no ho:

- **LDL kolesterol a ɛdɔɔso wɔ nkwa mfimfini mu**, a ɛka nnipa bɛyɛ 7% biara.
- **Ani a ɛnyɛ yie a wɔnsesa wɔ nkwa a ɛtwa so mu**, a ɛka nnipa bɛyɛ 2% biara.

## Nsɛm bɛn na wɔntumi nsesa?

National Institute on Aging ka sɛ
[mmere ne Alzheimer's asiane a yɛnim sen biara](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease),
na obiara ntumi nsesa. Nnipa ahoɔden nso wɔ ho dwumadie; ahoɔden bi a ɛma
Alzheimer's ba, a wɔfrɛ no APOE, ne ɔkwan biako. NIA ka ho sɛ nkwagyeɛ nnim
mmere yiye sɛ wɔbɛtumi ate nea ɛde Alzheimer's ba wɔ nnipa pii mu ase. Lancet
Commission nso ka sɛ asiane a wɔbɛtumi asesa no bɛtumi aba fam, ɛnsɛ nea obi
ahoɔden asiane ye.

Ɛfa sɛ mmere ne ahoɔden wɔ dwumadie kɛse, nnipa pii a wɔnni nsɛm 14 no biara
nso wɔ dementia. Eyi nni obiara ho bɔne.

## Nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛe wɔ nsakrae a wɔde ma asiane yi ho no kyerɛ sɛn?

Ɛnyɛ nea ɛma asiane ne dementia bɛka ho no ara de kyerɛ sɛ nsakrae a wɔde ma
asiane no betumi aba dementia ase. Nhwɛso kakra a nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛe wɔ
nnipa so no ka ho:

- **Bɔdɔ.** Wɔ SPRINT MIND nhwehwɛmu no mu, bɔdɔ a wɔtwa ase kakra no
  boaa ma adwene a ɛyɛ mmerɛw nni kɛse. Nanso ɛnboa ma dementia a wɔbɛka
  ho no nni kɛse, ɛwom sɛ nhwehwɛmu no mpɛ sɛ ɛkyerɛ saa
  ([SPRINT MIND wɔ JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Tie aboa.** Lancet Commission kyerɛkyerɛ nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛe mfe baasa wɔ tie
  aboa ho a ɛnnyɛ adwene ho yiye ma nnipa nyinaa, nanso ɛboa nnipa a asiane
  wɔ so kɛse. Nhwehwɛmu a wɔdi so kɔ akyiri hwehwɛ nnipa so ka tie aboa ho
  sɛ ebetumi aboa ma asiane nnyɛ kɛse. Yɛn [tie ho kyerɛwsɛm](/brain-health/hearing/)
  wɔ nsɛm pii.
- **Nnwuma a ɛyɛ nnipa ahoɔden.** National Institute on Aging ka sɛ nhwehwɛmu
  a wɔyɛe wɔ nnipa so no nnkyerɛ sɛ nnwuma betumi aboa ma mild cognitive
  impairment anaa Alzheimer's nni hɔ anaa ɛmfa nkɔ akyiri ntɛn, nanso
  nhwehwɛmu bi kyerɛ sɛ ebetumi aboa ma nkɔso a mmere de ba no ntɛn
  ([NIA wɔ Alzheimer's ho](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Nhwehwɛmu a ɛtena ase de retwe dementia no ka sɛ ɛbɛyɛ den na ɛbɛyɛ ka, enti
nsɛm pii no, nhwehwɛmu a ɛyɛ nokware no bɛba nhwehwɛmu a wɔdi so kɔ akyiri
hwehwɛ nnipa so mfe pii. Saa nhwehwɛmu no boa, nanso ɛyɛ sɛ nhwehwɛmu a
wɔyɛ ayɛ no.

## Ɛfa nna ne aduane ho dɛn?

Commission no hwehwɛɛ nna ne aduane, nanso wammɛ wɔ nsɛm 14 no mu, ɛfiri sɛ
nhwehwɛmu no nnyɛ den na ɛnyɛ pɛ. Yɛn [nna ho kyerɛwsɛm](/brain-health/sleep/) ne
[aduane ho kyerɛwsɛm](/brain-health/diet/) kyerɛkyerɛ nea yɛnim.

## Kasa wɔ dɔkota nkyɛn

Afeha nhwɛsoɔ a wɔyɛ mfe biara yɛ bere a ɛfata sɛ wɔka saa nsɛm yi ho asɛm.
Wobetumi de saa kratafa yi kɔ na wɔabisa:

- Me mogya bɔkɔɔ, mogya shuga ne kolesterol nkontaabu te sɛn, na ebia biribiara
  hia ho hwehwɛ?
- Ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhwehwɛ me tie ne me ani anaa?
- Nnwuma a meyɛ no ye ma me na ɛyɛ mmerɛw anaa?
- Ebia me nnuro biara betumi ama me nkaekae anaa me adwene anyɛ yie?

Ansa na wosesa nnuro, aduane, nna ho nneyɛe, nnwuma anaa nnuro a wɔde ka
nneɛma ho a, kasa wɔ dɔkota nkyɛn kan. Sɛ wo anaa obi a wohwɛ no ho nkyerɛ
mmerɛ mmerɛ ntɛm, ne nkɔ a ɛyɛ den, anaa ne nsa nna baako a ɛyɛ den anaa
ɛyɛ den ka, frɛ 112.
