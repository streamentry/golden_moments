Nnipa hia nkitahodie, na saa nni nsakrae wɔ mfe mu. Nhwehwɛmu kyerɛ sɛ ahoɔfɛ ne ankasa abɔdeɛ wɔ nkwagyeɛ mfe mu ka dementia asiane ho, ne yare a ɛfa koma ho ne ɔyare a ɛfa adwene ho nso. Nhwehwɛmu no rentumi ka kyerɛ ankasa koraa sɛ dɛn kakra na nkitahodie yi kyerɛ nsɛm a ɛde ba. Saa kratafa yi kyerɛkyerɛ nhwehwɛmu no kyerɛ, akwan a yɛbɛfa so anka wo ho ne obi a wɔn ho ayɛ mmerɛ, ne nea enti na nneyɛe a yɛyɛ bom no hia paa ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia.

## Ahoɔfɛ ne ankasa abɔdeɛ ka dementia ho anaa?

Aane, nhwehwɛmu pii ka wɔn ho, nanso nsɛm no kwan mu nni ankasa mu koraa. [2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) bu nkwagyeɛ mu ankasa abɔdeɛ sɛ dementia asiane kwan a yebetumi asesa ɔha 14 mu. [UCL nsɛmmisa a ɛwɔ saa adesua no ho](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) ka sɛ ɛyɛ ɔha 5 wɔ dementia nsɛm wɔ wiase.

Nea nhwehwɛmu no kyerɛ ne yei:

- **Nkitahodie a ɛyɛ kakra, asiane a ɛkɔ soro.** Commission no kyerɛkyerɛ nhwehwɛmu abien a ɛkyerɛ sɛ nkitahodie a ɛnyɛ daa ka dementia asiane ho. Biako kyerɛɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔha 57 kɔ soro, na biako nso kyerɛɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔha 18.
- **Ahoɔfɛ nso.** Ahoɔfɛ, a ɛyɛ tebea a obi twe ne ho fi nnipa ho, ka dementia asiane ho wɔ nhwehwɛmu pii mu.
- **US Surgeon General adwene.** Afe 2023, bere a ɛyɛ May, US Surgeon General de [nkyerɛwee a ɛfa ahoɔfɛ ne ankasa abɔdeɛ ho](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) bae. Ɛka sɛ ahoɔfɛ ne ankasa abɔdeɛ a ɛtenenee betumi ama dementia asiane kɔ soro kakra ɔha 50 wɔ mmofra a wɔwɔ mfe mu. Ɛka nso sɛ nkitahodie a ɛyɛ bɔne ka akoma yare asiane ɔha 29 ne ɔyare a ɛfa mogya ho asiane ɔha 32 ho.
- **Ɛdɔɔso sɛn.** [Nhwehwɛmu a National Academies ayɛ](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557972/) hunu sɛ nnipa a wɔwɔ mfe 65 kɔ soro a wɔte wɔn efie mu wɔ America, ɛyɛ ɔha 24 wɔn mu na wɔayi wɔn afi nnipa ho.

Ɛho hia sɛ yɛkaa ho asɛm: Lancet Commission kyerɛ sɛ nnipa betumi atwe wɔn ho fi nneyɛe a wɔyɛ bom ho wɔ dementia mfiase bere a wɔnnhunu no. Enti, ebia nkitahodie a ɛsakra no ka dementia ho, na ɛnyɛ sɛ nkitahodie nko ara de dementia ba. Nhwehwɛmu a ɛwɔ hɔ no bebree yɛ nhwɛso a wɔhwɛ, na ɛnyɛ nhwehwɛmu a wɔyɛ ara.

## Ahoɔfɛ ne ankasa abɔdeɛ kyerɛ dɛn?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/loneliness-and-social-isolation/loneliness-and-social-isolation-tips-staying-connected) kyerɛkyerɛ saa nsɛm yi kɛkɛ. **Ankasa abɔdeɛ** kyerɛ sɛ obi wɔ nnipa a ɔbɛtɔ nkitahodie wɔ mpɛnsa nni, anaa nnipa a ɔbɛtɔ nkitahodie wɔ ne ho nni nkɔso. **Ahoɔfɛ** yɛ adwene a ɛhaw a obi a wɔayi no afi nnipa ho anaa ɔyɛ ɔdɔɔso kɛse. Obi betumi ateɛ ne ho ankasa anigye, na obi nso betumi ayɛ ahoɔfɛ wɔ nnipa pii ntam.

NIA kyerɛ nneɛma a ɛma ankasa abɔdeɛ ne ahoɔfɛ nyinaa ba:

- Tie anaa ani haw
- Nnantwie haw, anaa ɔnni akwantuo
- Ɔteɛ ne ho ankasa, anaa ɔyaw kɛse a ɛba sɛ ɔkunu wu anaa ɔda adwuma so
- Adwene anaa nteaseɛ nsakrae
- Ɔdwen obi a ɔyare no
- Sika ho haw anaa kasa ho nsɛnkyerɛnne

Tie ho haw paa. NIA ka sɛ nnipa a wɔantie yiye betumi atwe wɔn ho fi nnipa ho, efisɛ nkɔmmɔ bɛyɛ den anaa ɛbɛyɛ wɔn ahohia. [Yɛn tie ne adwene yiye adesua](/brain-health/hearing/) kyerɛkyerɛ tie ntwahyɛ ne tie mmoa a wɔtɔn wɔ bi a ɛnyɛ ayaresabea mu.

## Akwan a yɛbɛfa so anka nkitahodie ho

NIA hyɛ nsusuiɛ bebree. Yiɛ baako anaa mmienu a ɛfata wo:

- **Hyɛ bere a wobɛfrɛ wo nnipa.** Bere a wobɛfrɛ anaa woakyerɛw ntrɛwmu, anaa woasoma kaa mmusua, nnamfo anaa nnipa a mo te ase bi wɔ mo nkurow.
- **Fa video frɛ anaa smart speaker.** Sɛ wɔrenhu technology no, sukuu a ɛwɔ library anaa community center bebree wɔ hɔ. Hwɛ [yɛn adesua a ɛfa frɛ ne video frɛ ho](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).
- **Fa nea ɛwɔ hɔ ka ho.** Sukuu, asɔredan nkɔmhyɛ, nantew klub anaa adwumayɛ a ɛyɛ boafoɔ de wo nkitahodie ne nea ɛma wo ho yɛ den.
- **San di ahobrɛase a wodii kan pɛ anaa sua ahobrɛase foforo** a wobɛyɛ ne nnipa.
- **Nantew nnipa ne ho.** Nantew ne nnamfo anaa kurom bi. [Yɛn nantew adesua](/brain-health/exercise/) wɔ akwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Susuw aboa ho** sɛ wobetumi aboa ne so.
- **Fa mmoa a ɛwɔ mmusua ho.** Nkurow mmusua, Area Agencies on Aging ne Eldercare Locator (800-677-1116) betumi akyerɛ wo nhyehyɛe ne akwantuo ho nkɔso.

## Adeɛn na nneyɛe a yɛyɛ bom hia wɔ dementia mu?

Ma obi a ɔwɔ dementia, nkitahodie yɛ anigyeɛ, ahomegyeɛ ne sɛ wɔpene so, na ɛnyɛ sɛ ɛbɛtew asiane. Lancet Commission ka sɛ nneyɛe boa nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia ma wɔnya anigyeɛ ne botae. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ka sɛ, ɛfiri sɛ nneyɛe ma nkwa a ɛwɔ hɔ ye, betumi atew nneyɛe a ɛte sɛ nantew anaa ahosɛeɛ nso. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ka nso sɛ Alzheimer's yare nnipa hia sɛ wɔyɛ adwuma na wɔyɛ nea wɔpɛ.

Nneɛma a ɛyɛ yie ne yei:

1. **Hwɛ mfonini bom.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) de nsɛmmisa a ɛnyɛ nsɔhwɛ ma, te sɛ "Ka me ho asɛm..." Hwɛ [yɛn reminiscence adesua](/activities/reminiscence/).
2. **Yɛ no bom wɔ ahyɛnsoɔ.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ma mfonini hwɛ hɔ, na ɛnni bere anaa mmuae, enti ɛnhaw obi. Yɛn adesua a ɛfa [sɛnea wobɛyɛ bom](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ho wɔ mmoa.
3. **Fa mmusua mfonini.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) sesa mfonini a woapaw sɛ wobɛyɛ agyinasɛm, na mfonini no ntena wo fon so.
4. **Fa nnipa bɛboa.** [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) yɛ adwuma wɔ mmusua nhyiam ne [ayaresabea nnipa a wɔyɛ ɔdɔɔso ho](/activities/care-home-groups/).
5. **Fa nnwom, adwuma fie anaa turo di.** Hwɛ [nnwom nneyɛe](/activities/music/), [fie adwuma](/activities/household-tasks/) ne yɛn [nneyɛe ho nsusuiɛ a ɛdɔɔso](/activities/ideas/).
6. **Ma akyɛde ayɛ ahoɔdɛn.** Yɛn [akyɛde adesua](/activities/visiting/) kyerɛkyerɛ akyɛde a ɛyɛ tebea a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, a ɛma obiara ani gye.

Ma ɛnyɛ kɛse na ɛnyɛ den. Efie a ɛdwodwoo ne nnipa baako anaa baanu betumi aboa sen nnipa pii a wɔboaboa. Sɛ ɔnnipa no ayɛ den anaa ɔnnim nneyɛe no bio a, ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wogyae. Sɛ mo te ase nsa kɔm tee tii no, ɛyɛ nea ɛsɛe.

Ma obi a ɔte ne ho ankasa wɔ dementia mu, NIA ka sɛ hwehwɛ obi a wotumi trust a ɔbɛtumi aba anaa ɔbɛfrɛ wo video so daa, na ɔbɛyɛ ɔmmoa a wɔfrɛ no wɔ ntɛmpɔ a ɛho hia mu. Ɛka nso kurom no mu kafe a wɔma nnipa a wɔwɔ nkaebɔ wɔ adwene mu ne wɔn mmusua ba, baabi a ɛyɛ anigyeɛ ne ahomegyeɛ.

## Wɔn a wɔboa wɔn nso ahoɔfɛ ka wɔn ho

NIA kyerɛ sɛ sɛ wobɔ obi a wɔyare no mmoa no, ɛma wowɔ ahoɔfɛ asiane. Hyɛ bere ma wo ankasa nnamfo, nnipa a wɔboa wɔn ne ntɔnsɛm. [Yɛn wo ho ahobammɔ adesua](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) wɔ akwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.

## Kasa kyerɛ dɔkota

Sɛ wo anaa obi a woboa no nya ahoɔfɛ anaa ɔwɔ ankasa mu bere pii a, ka kyerɛ dɔkota. NIA trɛw sɛ nnipa ka asetra nsakrae kɛse, te sɛ owu wɔ abusua mu, ma dɔkota ahu nea ɛreba ankɔsɛ. Sɛ adwene brɛ nnawɔtwe pii a, ebia ɛyɛ animuonyam ɔyare, na ɛtumi yɛ yiye. Sɛ okunu no firi ne nnamfo anaa adwuma a ɔdɔ so a ɛnyɛ den no ho a, kyerɛ dɔkota saa nsakraeɛ no. Hwɛ [nkɛntɛnso ne bere a ɛfata sɛ wokɔ dɔkota nkyɛn](/brain-health/warning-signs/).

Kratafa yi yɛ amanneɛ a ɛyɛ ade, ɛnyɛ ayaresa afotu. Hwɛ [yɛn ayaresa nhyɛso](/medical-disclaimer/).
