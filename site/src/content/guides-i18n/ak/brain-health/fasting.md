## Intermittent fasting siw dementia ano anaa?

Nsɛm pa biara nni hɔ sɛ intermittent fasting siw dementia ano anaa ɛma ɛdi
ntɛm. National Institute on Aging (NIA) kyerɛ sɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ nni hɔ sɛ
wɔbɛsrɛ obiara sɛ ɔntew aduane anaa ɔnnidi wɔ fasting ho, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔkɔ so
hwehwɛ ne nea ɛyɛ adwuma ne ne nea ɛnyɛ adwuma, titiriw wɔ mpanyin ho
([NIA on calorie restriction and fasting](https://www.nia.nih.gov/news/calorie-restriction-and-fasting-diets-what-do-we-know)).

### Adeɛn a nhwehwɛmu no yɛ kakra

NIA kyerɛ ademuɔ bi a ɛma saa:

- **Nhwehwɛmu bebree wɔ mmoa so.** Nhwehwɛmu a wɔayɛ wɔ mmoa, te sɛ nkwadaa,
  ntoma, nkɔkɔɔ ne mframa so no ama nsɛm a ɛyɛ anigye, nanso nsɛm yi nyinaa
  remfa nnipa ho. Ɛwɔ mmoa mu deɛ, nhwehwɛmu no mu ano aduru sɛ ɛbɛyɛ nkɔsoɔ
  ma nkwa tenten, nanso ɛwɔ nkɔsoɔ a ɛnyɛ mmoa so.
- **Nnipa mu nhwehwɛmu ankyɛ.** Bebree ankyɛ bere mpo bɛyɛ bosome anaa bosome
  abien.
- **Nnipa mu nhwehwɛmu bebree ka sɛ wɔbɛtew ahoyeden,** na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ
  adwene anaa dementia so. Nnipa a wɔwɔ ahoyeden kɛse no ne deɛ wɔsɔ hwɛ.
- **Mpanyin nkɔ ntɛm wɔ nhwehwɛmu mu.** Nhwehwɛmu bebree gyinaa mmofra anaa
  nnipa a wɔwɔ mfe 60 ase.

Nhwehwɛmu a ɛyɛ 2026 systematic review hwehwɛɛ intermittent fasting wɔ nnipa a
wɔwɔ mfe 60 anaa ɛboro saa so. Ɛhunuu sɛ nsɛm wɔ saa mfe yi mu ka ho kakra, na
nhwehwɛmu no yɛ nketewa, ɛnkɔɔ so tenten, na ɛnyɛ pɛ. Nsɛm a ɛfa adwene ne
ntease ho firi nhwehwɛmu bi mu, na ɛnyɛ nhwehwɛmu bebree a wɔde ne nsɛm
bom. Review no kyerɛ sɛ nhwehwɛmu bi kyerɛɛ sɛ nnidi bere tiaa anaa fasting
tenten ne nea ɛma nsɛm yɛ sɛ ɛsɔ, na nkɔsoɔ ne mpanyin nhyehyɛe ka sɛ wɔnhwɛ
yie ([2026 review in Nutrients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13165003/)).

Nkɔmhyɛ bi ka sɛ nipa ɔhyɛ ne ho ma ɔhyɛ nkyene sɛ ɔbɛdi ne sradeɛ wɔ fasting
bere, na eyi betumi ayɛ adeɛ wɔ adwene so, nanso ɛyɛ adwene a wɔreyɛ nhwehwɛmu
ho. NIA kyerɛ sɛ nimdeɛ wɔ eyi ho nni hɔ pefee.

## Asiane bɛn na ɛwɔ fasting ma mpanyin?

Ɛnyɛ nea ɛnyɛ hwee ma nnipa bebree a wɔwɔ mfe mu sɛ wobɛyɛ fasting. Asiane a
ɛbɛba ne:

- **Nnuane a ɛnyɛ wɔ hwehwɛ no.** Sɛ wɔdi kakra sen nea ɔhyɛ mu ho hia no,
  nnuane bɛpɛ, na ahoɔden bɛtew, na ɛbɛma ɔyare kɔ ahyɛ fam.
- **Nnompe ne apɔw a ɛpɛ.** Nhwehwɛmu bi a NIA boa ma a ɛfa bosome abien ho
  wɔ mmofra ne nnipa a wɔwɔ mfe mu ne mfe a ɛdi kan no mu no kyerɛɛ sɛ
  nnompe ne nipa a ɛyɛ den nnyinii kakra.
- **Sradeɛ a ɛwɔ mogya mu bɛyɛ fam wɔ aduro a wɔfa so yɛ diabetes ho.** Nnipa a
  wɔfa insulin anaa diabetes aduro foforo so betumi anya sradeɛ a ɛwɔ mogya mu
  ayɛ fam sɛ wɔpɛ nnuane anaa wɔdi bere a ɛwɔ hɔ no ankɔ so, sɛnea
  [National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/preventing-problems/low-blood-glucose-hypoglycemia)
  kyerɛ. Sradeɛ a ɛyɛ fam betumi ama ahoyeden tew, adwene nhyɛ mu, ne ɔhaw.
- **Nsuo ayera.** Sɛ wɔpɛ nnuane ne nsuo nso, obi betumi ayera.
- **Ahoyeden tew ne sɛ wobɛtɔ.** Mayo Clinic kyerɛ sɛ intermittent fasting betumi
  ama nnipa ayɛ mmerɛ kɛse ne ahoyeden tew na wobetumi atɔ, na ɛnyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ
  yie ma nnipa a wɔwɔ asiane kɛse sɛ wɔbɛpɛ nnyinom ne sɛ wɔbɛtɔ
  ([Mayo Clinic on intermittent fasting](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/nutrition-and-healthy-eating/expert-answers/intermittent-fasting/faq-20441303)).
- **Nneɛma foforo a ɛba.** Mayo Clinic ka mpɔtam ahyɛ, adwene a ɛsesa, ne
  sɛ wɔbɛyɛ kɛse bɔ. Ɛka nso sɛ nsɛm bi kyerɛ sɛ fasting a ɛyɛ dɔnhwerɛ 16 ne
  nnidi bere dɔnhwerɛ 8 betumi ama akokoduru yare asiane kɔ so sen nnidi bere
  foforo. Saa nsɛm yi nnyɛ pefee.

## Adeɛn nti na ɛho hia kɛse sɛ wodi yie wɔ dementia ho?

Ɛda a wɔwɔ dementia so no, ɔhaw a ɛba ara ne sɛ wɔrenni **nneɛma** a ɛfata ne
nsuo a ɛfata, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔdi bebree. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ nnipa a
wɔwɔ dementia wɔ ɔkwan a ɛreyɛ mfe a ɛdi so anaa ɔkwan a ɛreyɛ mfe a ɛdi
akyiri mu no, wɔn ho betumi ahyɛ wɔn ma nneɛma a wɔbɛpaw, wɔnkae sɛ wɔadi,
anaa wɔbɛtee sɛ wɔadi dedaw. Ɛka nso sɛ nnuane a ɛnyɛ yie betumi ama
ɛyɛ den ma adwene a ɛsesa, na ɛma nnipa ayɛ kɛse bɔ
([Alzheimer's Association on food and eating](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating)).

Alzheimer's Society kyerɛ sɛ sɛ obi nni nneɛma a ɛfata a, ebetumi ama wayɛ kɛse
bɔ, nnompe ne apɔw ntew, ahoyeden tew, mmerɛ ne ahoyeden tew, na ɛma nipa
ayɛ mmerɛ kɛse na ɛnyɛ den sɛ ɔbɛsane ayare
([Alzheimer's Society on appetite](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/poor-appetite-dementia)).

**Eyi nti, fasting nnyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mma obi a wɔwɔ dementia so.** Nnuane a
wɔnnidi betumi aba nnuane a wɔnnidi daa. Biribiara a ɛbɛsesa sɛnea obi a wɔwɔ
dementia di no, ɛsɛ sɛ dɔkotafo anaa dietitian a wɔwɔ mmara kyerɛ kwan.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne bɛn na ɛkyerɛ sɛ wodi anaa wonom nsuo yie?

Hwɛ:

- **Nnuane pɛ**, anaa ntadeɛ rehyɛ wo so mmerɛ.
- **Wɔpo nneɛma anaa wɔnni aduane.** Anaa wɔkora nneɛma wɔ wɔn anofa mu.
- **Ahoyeden tew anaa mmerɛ.**
- **Ɛyɛ den sɛ ɛbɛpɛ**, a ɛbɛba sɛ nsuo ayera ne nneɛma a ɛnnyɛ mu ka mmerɛ.
- **Nsuo ayera nsɛnkyerɛnne.** NIA kyerɛ sɛ sradeɛ ahyɛ fam, ntorɔ, sradeɛ a
  ɛnyɛ yie a ɛdi mpɛn, ne ahunahuna, na dementia ankasa betumi ama saa nneɛma
  ba
  ([NIA on common medical problems](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/common-medical-problems-alzheimers-disease-information-caregivers)).

Aduane a obi nnidi yie betumi aba nneɛma bebree mu: anoyɛ a ɛwɔ anofa, nnan a
ɛnyɛ yie anaa nnan a wɔde ayɛ hyɛ mu, aduro foforo, adwene ɔyare, ntorɔ, nnuane
a ɛnkɔ so, anaa sɛnea anim ne ahoɔ tɔ no nso sesa. Ka saa nneɛma yi kyerɛ
nipa no dɔkotafo. Wɔbɛhwehwɛ nea ɛde saa ba, na wɔbɛbɛka dietitian anaa
nnuane a wɔde bɔ ho ho.

## Wobetumi aboa obi adi ne anom nsuo yie sɛn?

- **Ma nnuane ketewa ne ntɛntɛn, na ɛnyɛ nnuane kɛse abiɛsa.**
- **Ma nneɛma a wɔpɛ,** na mma wo ani nnye sɛ wɔpɛ no sesa. Sɛ wɔpɛ aduane
  kakra a, nnuane a ɛyɛ dɔɔ, te sɛ nkwan a wɔde nkɔm ne mfuw ɛyɛ, nso ɛyɛ
  papa; bisa dɔkotafo sɛ ɛwɔ ɔyare ho ɔhaw a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtew nkɔm ho.
- **Ma nsuo bɛn wo nkyɛn,** na ma wɔn nsuokuruwa ketewa wɔ da mu. Nnuane te sɛ
  nsuo a wɔayɛ wɔ nnɔbae, nkyene, nsuo mfuw ne mfuw a wɔayɛ wɔ mu nso kyerɛ.
- **Didi bom sɛ wobetumi.** Ɛdɛn na ɛyɛ dɔ wɔ nnuane mu? Ɛyɛ nkɔmmɔ ne asɛm,
  ɛnyɛ adwuma a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ ntɛm.
- **Ma nneɛma a wɔbɛdi ne nea ɛyɛ mmɛtɛɛ.** Sɛ nsa ayɛ den sɛ wɔbɛdi nneɛma
  mu a, nnuane te sɛ sandwich, mfuw ne nkyene anaa nkukuo nkyene ɛyɛ.

Yɛn [nneɛma a wɔdi guide](/brain-health/diet/) wɔ nneɛma foforo a wobetumi
ahwehwɛ ma nnuane. Nneɛma a wobɛyɛ bom nso betumi ayɛ adwuma a wɔyɛ bom
([household tasks](/activities/household-tasks/)).

## Kasa kyerɛ dɔkotafo

Ansa na woyɛ fasting nhyehyɛe biara, kasa kyerɛ wo dɔkotafo anaa dietitian a
wɔwɔ mmara, titiriw sɛ wofa aduro a ɛyɛ sradeɛ anaa ntease ho aduro so, sɛ
wo ahoɔden atew kakra nnansa yi, anaa wo ho hwee. Ɛda a wɔwɔ dementia so no,
bisa wɔn dɔkotafo sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔdi ne wɔnom nsuo bɛn da biara, sɛ mfa fasting
nnyɛ.

Hwehwɛ ayaresa ntɛm sɛ adwene sesa ntɛm, wo ho bɔ, anaa nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛkyerɛ
sɛ sradeɛ a ɛwɔ mogya mu ayɛ fam sɛ ɛnyɛ yie no.
