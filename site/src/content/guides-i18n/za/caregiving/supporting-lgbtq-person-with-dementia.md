Swnghhingz LGBTQ+ dwg cib faen gij boux ndeu dwg bouxvunz yienghlawz. Cam coh gij de rin raeuj youqgvenq, yiengh gij vah de raeuj, caeuq hawj de raeuj dwg bouxlawz ndaej rox gij saehgih sim'gvang. Mbouj ndei caengz swnghhingz, banhdoih, banghceh bienh'ok roxnaeuz swnghhingz sim'gvang boux ndeu, ciuhdoih moqfaex, gij cuengh loih roxnaeuz boux raeuj caengz.

[Canj'iq banghceh boux LGBTQ+ youq bingh cingqmingz cij Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/supporting-lgbtq-dementia) ciuq cam gij swnghhingz boux de, caeuq mbouj ndei caengz gvanhhi. [Cienzwnj banghceh bingh cingqmingz LGBTQ+ cij Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) cungj gangj daengz gij dauqlaeng doenghdaeng yawjnaek caeuq ra banghceh yienghaih. Gak bouxvunz mbouj doengz; yiengh gij swhsienh neix guh gij saehgih gangj doxgap, mbouj dwg cibfaenh yienghlawz dinghyinx boux ndeu.

## Gou yienghlawz cam daihmingz, hoengzmingz caeuq sim'gvang?

Raeuj seiz boux mbouj miz, mbouj daemz. Mwngz cimh ndaej cam: “Mwngz sij gou yiengh daihmingz caeuq hoengzmingz maz?” roxnaeuz “Miz gijmaz doenghdaeng swnghhingz roxnaeuz gvanhhi, mwngz sij aeu boux banghceh mwngz rox mbouj?” Boux de ndaej daep cibfaen, mbouj daep, roxnaeuz gangj daiq. Mwngz mbouj aeu cam gij saehgih sim'gvang caep mbouj gvanhdaengz banghceh de.

Cam coh bouxlawz ndaej rox gijmaz. Miz boux cimh sij aeu boux gunghcoz nyinhceh banhdoih de, dwg mbouj sij aeu cinh'gyah gvaq rox daengz swnghhingz singz roxnaeuz swnghhingz singzbyez de. Boux ndeu cungj cimh ndaej aeu yiengh gij vah mbouj doengz youq doihgingz mbouj doengz. Mbouj aeu cang saehgih swnghhingz gvaq cinh'gyah, boux raeuj caengz roxnaeuz gunghcoz, cij yinvei mwngz rox.

Danghnaeuz boux de sij aeu gijlok swnghhingz ndeu, cam coh aeu swh gijmaz caeuq bouxlawz ndaej yawj. Ciemzsi danjveih banghceh cujciz daihmingz caep mbouj doengz gij cuengh giz, bauhgvaz gunghcoz ndaej yiengh daihmingz boux de aeu youq seiz gangjvah mbouj. Swngh cinbu sim'gvang boux de; mbouj dwg boux banghceh cung aeu rox cungj cibfaen.

Swnghhingz cimh ndaej gaij. Dang boux de ndaej biaujsi raeuj de, cam daiq, daiqbiet youq seiz raeuj duh roxnaeuz gaij an'bai banghceh moq. Danghnaeuz mbouj roxcingz de sij aeu cang gijmaz, mbouj ndei caengz. Cien gij swnghhingz roxcingz de guhqmaenh gaenj, caeuq cam boux cizyiz youq dieg mbouj gvaq doenghdaeng gienz banghceh roxnaeuz cwngci cang saehgih.

## Bouxlawz aeu canyaz youq seiz gangj saehgih banghceh?

Cam coh boux de, de sinqnyangz caeuq sij aeu bouxlawz canyaz. Mangka banghceh de cimh ndaej bauhgvaz fuqi, banhdoih, bingzyouj cinz, cinh'gyah roxnaeuz “bienh'ok boux de raeuj”—dwg boux banghceh de yiengh bienh'ok, hixmbouj dwg cinh'gyah sengli. Miz boux ij cinh'gyah sengli; miz boux mbouj. Hawj boux de yiengh vah bonjfaenh de gangj gvanhhi.

Cam gij saehgih ngoenzlaeng: Bouxlawz ndaej raeuj caengz? Dang geiqhua gaij, gunghcoz aeu cien bouxlawz? Bouxlawz ndaej daengz saehgih moq? Danghnaeuz danjveih aeu yunghcin roxnaeuz swhgiq cingq ndaej cang saehgih, cam de aeu gijmaz caeuq gaiqmingz raeuj dah boux de rox. Dwg banhdoih roxnaeuz boux banghceh de raeuj mbouj yienghgaiq miz gienz fa'tdingh youq goengfaz; cwngci gak dieg mbouj doengz.

Yiengh mingzcih gvanhhi boux de aeu. Danghnaeuz gunghcoz banghceh cieng banhdoih dwg “bingzyouj” roxnaeuz “cinh'gyah” seiz boux de sij aeu nyinhceh dwg banhdoih, cimh ndaej hawj de mbouj suenq. Cib geiq bat swh aeu yunghcin ndaej rox gunghcoz yienghlawz gaijsauh boux, caeuq gij saehgih maz ndaej cang.

## Gou ndaej cam danjveih banghceh gijmaz?

Seiz biujgeiq ranz banghceh, banghceh youq ranz roxnaeuz fuzva ngoenz, cam coh boux de sij aeu cam gij saehgih maz caeuq sij aeu mwngz raeuj mbouj. [Canj'iq cimh ra fuzva yienghaih cij SAGE](https://www.sageusa.org/resource/10-tips-for-finding-lgbt-affirming-services/) gangj, cang saehgih sim'gvang gvaq danjveih banghceh cimh ndaej hawj boux gamjgaenj mbouj onjdingh. Cingz danjveih yiengh vah ganjdan gaiqmingz guhfa cingq seizneix.

- Mwngz yienghlawz gijlok daihmingz caeuq hoengzmingz boux aeu? Boux gunghcoz maz ndaej yawj gij saehgih neix?
- Boux ndeu yienghlawz raeuj dwg bouxlawz ndaej raeuj caengz, caeuq miz seizgan sim'gvang caeuq banhdoih roxnaeuz boux banghceh gizneix?
- Mwngz yienghlawz rox caeuq cingqcingj swnghhingz boux de doenghdaeng an'bai fangzcienz, aen'fuk caeuq goenghoh bonjfaenh?
- Gunghcoz daengz peiynyangh maz doenghdaeng boux loih LGBTQ+ caeuq bingh cingqmingz?
- Boux ranz roxnaeuz boux banghceh yienghlawz cingz saehgih dauqlaeng doenghdaeng sim'gvang roxnaeuz mbouj cingqcingj, caeuq roengzlaeng cang yienghlawz?

[Canj'iq doenghdaeng seizgan cangq banghceh yienghaih cij SAGE caeuq Human Rights Campaign Foundation](https://www.sageusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/lei-consumer-guide-finding-lgbtq-inclusive-ltcc.pdf) ciuq ciemz cwngci swhsienh doenghdaeng raeuj caengz, an'bai fangzcienz caeuq guhfa gunghcoz. Cienj 2021 nienz fatbouh; yiengh guh gij saehgih cam, mbouj dwg gangj goengfaz dieg mbouj seizneix. Cwngci caeuq fuzva gak dieg mbouj doengz.

Cib bouh biauqci, cezfangj roxnaeuz cib geiq daep an'onj mbouj ndaej baozceng banghceh ngoenzlaeng dwg yienghlawz. Danghnaeuz boux de sij aeu raeuj caengz, liuzsim gunghcoz yienghlawz ciengzhoj de, caeuq yienghlawz daepyingh banhdoih roxnaeuz boux banghceh de raeuj. Dang aeu, cingz gangjvah sim'gvang caeuq gunghcoz ndeu, hawj boux de gamjgaenj onjdingh.

## Gou yienghlawz banghceh caengz bouxvunz?

Cam coh gij aih'hoz ngoenzlaeng, gwihdingh, vahyenz, vwnva, sinqngyangz, bingzyouj caeuq shehquj boux de. Swnghhingz cimh youqgvenq aeu gangj, roxnaeuz de cimh ningz aeu seizgan youq faen ngoenzlaeng gizneix. Mbouj ndei bienq gak cung raeuj caengz dwg gangj swnghhingz, caeuq mbouj ndei caengz cungj boux LGBTQ+ miz gingqyam doengzyiengh.

Miz boux guhqmaenh daengz miuzsawz roxnaeuz yawjnaek, sojyij cimh ndaej daengz fuzva seiz cingjgwz. Gizneix boux mbouj yawj dwg gij saehgih dauqlaeng. Cingqting gungh, mbouj aeu boux de gaiqmingz roxnaeuz cingsiz gijmaz. Danghnaeuz gij geiqok, ndawdoengh roxnaeuz gusiz doenghdaeng guhqlaeng hawj de mbouj suenq, cam de sij dingz mbouj roxnaeuz daenj gij hujdai gizneix.

Danghnaeuz mwngz dwg banhdoih, bingzyouj roxnaeuz cinh'gyah, banghceh mwngz bonjfaenh cung youqgvenq. Cam coh boux de sij aeu mwngz yienghlawz canyaz, caeuq dang aeu cingz coj, ra cuzci bingh cingqmingz roxnaeuz cuzci loih LGBTQ+ youq dieg mbouj. [Yieb cienzwnj cij Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) miz lienzciep daengz shehquj caeuq cienzwnj boux goenghoh; gij miz youq gijmaz ij gij mwngz youq gizlawz.

Doenghdaeng cibfaenh biujgeiq raeuj gvangq seiz biujgeiq ranz banghceh, yawj [raeuj ranz banghceh bingh cingqmingz yienghlawz](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/). Doenghdaeng banghceh guh geiqhua roengz buhdoz, yawj [buhdoz daih'it roengz buhdoz bingh cingqmingz](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
