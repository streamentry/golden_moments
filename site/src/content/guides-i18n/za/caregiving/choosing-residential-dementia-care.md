Dangh swhsiengj yungh doengh bangh, mwngz aenq raeuj miz camgyaeq hingz, yaem, mbouj dingh rox aen cibfaenh cungj seiz. Gij gyaengq dingh neix dwg cawz ra gij bangh hab gij saedcingq caeuq swiqhaeuj vunz de. De mbouj dwg swhgienz yawj ranz mwngz daih doenghfaex mbouj, caeuq mbouj dwg buhdoengz bouxvunz cungj aeu daengz doengz gij seizgan.

[Alzheimer's Association guide to long-term care](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-term-care), [residential-care checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) caeuq [Dementia Australia's residential-care guidance](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/residential-care) cienzmienh cawz yawj gij saedcingq vunz, doenghgaiq, hozdung, hoengjingz caeuq feihyungz. Yieb neix mbouj cungj gij dieg ndeu diuz, caeuq mbouj daihdaep cingzyangh rox cienzaiq hwnj dieg.

## Yienghzhaeuj rox yungh doengh bangh aenq raeuj sihyau yawj mbouj?

Yawj vunz de seizneix aeu gij bangh gijmaz, caeuq gij bangh neix naj ranz cungj ndaej mbouj. Swhsiengj gij angqcingz, gij saedcingq guhliuz ngoenz caet, saedloh gyaeuj boux caeuq swiqhaeuj vunz de. Lij aeu swhsiengj, gij guh gwnz bang vunz aenq raeuj miz inghyangx daengz cingzyangh vunz de rox boux bang de mbouj.

Mboq miz gij gaihdwnj rox seizhaen diuz ndeu aeu dingh seizlawz vunz aeu dawz dieg. Miz vunz aenq raeuj swiqhaeuj dieg gyaeuj boux, miz vunz aenq raeuj swiqhaeuj naj ranz caeuq miz bangh lai. Caenx vunz de doiqgaiq, yiengh aeu, lij caeuq moix yihseng, fuzyoz fuzyoz rox boux seizci giva bangh. Cam ra gij loih bangh aenq raeuj hab, couh cij dingh cingj dawz dieg.

Cingq vunz caeuq cam gya yiengh mwngz de muengh. Cam ra gij saehgaiq daihgya vunz de: diegsoh, gwn reihnaz, vah, vwnva, siqcoengh cungjciz, hozdung swnh gwnz rox baih yieng ranz caeuq bouxbangj. Gij swiqhaeuj vunz de aenq raeuj bang mwngz gaenj laeb danhsaed, doengh cij giz saedcingq bangh miz bienqvaq.

## Baiqz baenz baih yawj dieg, aeu rox gijmaz?

Yaeuh seng, laeb danhsaed gaiq gij bangh vunz de aeu. Neix aenq raeuj baenzlaeng bang doenghsaeh ngoenz caet, guhliuz yihliuz, gyaeuj boux, yungh vah swnh gwnz, rox miz dieg bae henzngaz. Mwngz mbouj dingh, couh cam boux cingzyangh rox fuzyoz bang, rox aeu gij fuzvu gijmaz.

Baiqz baenz bae, seng daj dienhvah cam gij dieg. Cam ra de guh ndaej bang vunz de mbouj, miz fangz mbouj, buhdoengz aeu guh gijmaz, caeuq aeu gij saedceiq gijmaz. Cingq saedceiq sojmiz raeuj baenz rox gij fuzvu caeuq feihyungz. Gij diuzgienh, cienzdaiz caeuq huzyoz mbouj doengz yiengh dieg mbouj doengz, vihliux aeu yawj gij lailai hwnj dieg, mbouj aeu i youq vwnzva ndeu.

Cam ra doenghgaiq miz huanlz bingh cimhdoih mbouj, yienghzhaeuj guh gij giva bangh, caeuq ranz yienghzhaeuj gyaeuj saedceiq moq. Rox gij saedloh ngoenz caet yienghzhaeuj yungh, miz hozdung gijmaz, caeuq yienghzhaeuj bang vunz cuz cawz ra. Miz dieg bangh danj vunz youq bingh cimhdoih dwg diuz raeuj ndeu, hoeng mboq dwg bouxvunz cungj aeu; aeu bijdui gij bangh saeq raeuj moix gij dieg cungj.

## Dangh baih yawj, aeu liuzsim gijmaz?

Yiengh ndaej, baih yawj lai gij dieg. Yiengj seizgan bae yawj daih'it, couh cam ra aenq ndaej mbouj caiq bae aen seizgan wnq rox dangh seiz gwn fanh. Baih yawj daihngeih aenq raeuj yawj ndaej lai gij saedloh ngoenz caet, caeuq gyaeuj ndaej doenghgaiq wnq.

Liuzsim yienghzhaeuj doenghgaiq caeuq vunz cuz doiqgaiq. De cinhingj bouxvunz mbouj, miz seizgan daengz de dap mbouj, caeuq cingq de caeuq cam gya ngoenz caet gwnz mbouj? Vunz cuz yawj raeuj onj mbouj, miz saeh guh hab swiqhaeuj caeuq lwnh de mbouj? Cam ra aenq ndaej yawj gij danhsaed hozdung, fangz gwn fanh, dieg henzngaz caeuq dieg simswng mbouj.

Yawj gij saedceiq daihgya vunz de neix: bienhbaeuq yiengj yawj, lo doq bae cingsawj mingz mbouj, mehgwnz swnh gwnz, dieg gwix onj, bangh vah caeuq vwnva, caeuq seizgan gyaeuj boux daihgya. Daj ranz mingz cinj cungj mbouj baujmingz gij guhliuz ngoenz caet dwg yienghzhaeuj.

Yiengh aeu, cingq bouxbangj rox boux ranz mwngz sinjdaej caeuq bae, bang mwngz geiq gij cam ra caeuq bijdui camgyaeq. Mwngz aenq raeuj yungh [Alzheimer's Association checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) daeuj geiq gij yawj raeuj. Yawj gij geiq neix dwg gij bang doiqgaiq, mbouj dwg diemjsoh aeu cawz dieg daih mwngz.

## Cam gijmaz bang bijdui gij bangh?

Cam ra doenghgaiq yienghzhaeuj an'baij aen seizgan ngoenz faenbied, doenghgaiq yienghzhaeuj rox gij saedloh vunz de, caeuq dangh saedcingq de bienq, aeu guh gijmaz. Cam ra bouxlawz yawj gij giva bangh, caeuq ranz yienghzhaeuj gwtuengx miz saehgaiq. Yiengh vunz de aeu bang gwn, guhliuz swhgeij rox doiqgaiq, cam ra gij bangh neix youq saedcoih dwg yienghzhaeuj.

Cam ra gij baih yieng ranz, ok bae, yiengj yihseng, hozdung, caeuq vunz de aenq ndaej daij mehgwnz swnh gwnz mbouj. Yiengh gij dieg miz fang'anh danj vunz youq bingh cimhdoih, cam ra de baenzlaeng gij huanlz, hozdung caeuq bangh ngoenz caet gijmaz. Cingq lijz aenq raeuj yawj vunz cuz yienghzhaeuj caeuq cam gya, mbouj dan caeuq mingz fang'anh.

Cingq danhsaed feihyungz mingzsoq, baenzlaeng gijmaz miz youq ndaw de, caeuq feihyungz aenq raeuj yienghzhaeuj bienq. Yaeuh cingq yawj diuzdiep, yiengh mij mingz rox diuzfaz hwnj dieg, aeu cawz boux miz cizcwz hwnj diej bang yawj. Mboq aeu swhdingh fuzvu ndeu miz rox mbouj miz, danh gij saedceiq youq saeng rox guek wnq.

Yiengh sinhfen, gvanhhi caeuq simswng vunz de aeu liuzsim lai, yawj gij [cangjceij bang vunz youq bingh cimhdoih dwg LGBTQ+](/caregiving/supporting-lgbtq-person-with-dementia/). Miz gij cam ra gvanhhi coh youq, boux bae yieng, guhliuz swhgeij caeuq cingcwz doenghgaiq.

## Yienghzhaeuj bang dangh dawz dieg?

Cingq vunz caeuq cam gya yiengh mwngz de muengh. Baih yawj gij dieg, bae gwn fanh rox cux duenhseiz aenq raeuj bang miz vunz rox gij hoengjingz, yiengh ndaej guh caeuq vunz de hab. Vunz de aenq raeuj swiqhaeuj cawz mehgwnz fangz, buh rox saedloh aeu daij bae.

Gyaeuj doenghgaiq gij saedceiq aeu yungz, yiengh vunz de dongzyi. Neix aenq raeuj baenzlaeng coh youq vunz de, cibfaenh doiqgaiq, saedloh ngoenz caet, gwn reihnaz swiqhaeuj, hozdung vunz de swiqhaeuj, caeuq gijmaz bang vunz de camgyaeq onj. Saedceiq aeu gaiq caeuq daihgya guhliuz. Vunz de caeuq aeu dwg boux youq ndaw doiqgaiq gvanhhi de.

Yiengh gij diej hawj, daij mehgwnz swnh gwnz bae. Cam ra doenghgaiq, fangz vunz de yienghzhaeuj camgyaeq rox, hoeng mbouj aenq raeuj nanz haenqrengz. Fotoz, denj swiqhaeuj rox donghoj swnh gwnz, aenq raeuj daihgya laeg dawz ciengzbu ranz bae.

## Dawz dieg baenzneix, yienghzhaeuj caeuq cam gya?

Caeuq doenghgaiq gunghcoz, rox yienghzhaeuj gyaeuj saedceiq moq caeuq aeu lienzheih bouxlawz. Cam ra vunz de camgyaeq yienghzhaeuj gvanhhi baih yieng caeuq hozdung. Miz vunz aenq raeuj muengh miz boux lai gyaeuj aen seizgan duenh; miz vunz aenq raeuj aeu seizgan hab gij dieg moq. Liuzsim gij saehgaiq mwngz de camgyaeq onj, couh caeuq diauqcingq.

Gij saedcingq caeuq swiqhaeuj aenq raeuj bienq. Caeuq vunz de caeuq doenghgaiq yawj gij giva bangh, miz saehgaiq mbouj mingz couh cam, caeuq gyaeuj saedceiq moq aenq raeuj bang. Yiengh vunz de hwt laekmbanj ndwi rox bingh lai laeg, aeu doiq doenghgaiq guhliuz rox; ndwi laekmbanj aeu yawj yihseng lij, yiengh [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) gangj.

Cawz dieg dwg gij gyaengq dingh daihgya, caeuq gij cawz daih'it mbouj itdingh cungj hab ciengzseiz. Cawz saedceiq, cingq vunz caeuq cam gya, cam ra gij guhliuz ngoenz caet, caeuq dawz dieg baenzneix caiq geiq doiqgaiq.
