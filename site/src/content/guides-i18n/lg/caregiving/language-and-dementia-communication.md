Abantu aboogera ennimi ezisukka mu lumu bayinza okwogeraganya mu ngeri ey’enjawulo nga dementia ekyusa engeri gye bafunamu ebigambo oba gye bategeeramu. Abantu abamu batandika okukozesa olulimi lwe baayiga emabega; abalala tebakikola. Eddaala erisooka erisinga okuyamba kwe kubuuza olulimi oluwulira nga lwe lukwatagana obulungi kati, oluvannyuma n’ogoberera omuntu ky’ayagala.

[Alzheimer's Society egamba nti okwagala kw’olulimi kuyinza okwawukana era ekuwa amagezi okukozesa olulimi omuntu lw’atera okwogeramu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association era eraga okudda ku lulimi omuntu lwe yazalibwa nalwo ng’emu ku nkyukakyuka eziyinza okubaawo mu kwogeraganya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Enkyukakyuka zino tezituuka ku buli muntu era tezitubuulira mutendera omuntu gw’alimu.

## Lulimi ki lwe nsaanidde okukozesa?

Buuza omuntu olulimi lw’ayagala okukozesa mu mboozi eno. Ky’ayagala kiyinza okukyuka okusinziira ku nsonga eyogerwako, ekifo oba ekiseera ky’olunaku. Bw’akyusa ennimi, tokitwala ng’okugezesebwa oba okumuwaliriza okweyongera mu lulimi lwe mutegeera.

Bw’oba oyogera olulimi lw’ayagala, kozesa ebigambo ebimanyiddwa era omuwe obudde okuddamu. Bw’oba omanyi ebigambo bitono byokka, yogera mazima era kozesa n’obubonero bw’emikono, ebintu oba ebifaananyi. [Eby’obulagirizi ebikwata ku lulimi ebya Alzheimer's Society](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) biraga nti obusobozi mu lulimi buyinza okwawukana okuva ku lunaku okudda ku lulala era buyinza okukosebwa obukoowu, obulumi oba obulwadde.

## Ki ekiyinza okuyamba mu mboozi eya bulijjo?

Jjukira amagezi agakwata ku kwogeraganya bulijjo, era oyongereko obuyambi obukwatagana n’olulimi lw’omuntu:

- **Kozesa ebigambo n’amannya agamanyiddwa.** Buuza omuntu oba omuntu gwe yeesiga ebigambo bye bakozesa ku bintu ebya bulijjo, ab’eŋŋanda n’emirimu egikolebwa bulijjo.
- **Laga era oyogere.** Laga ku kikopo n’olugalo, yimusawo eby’okulondako bibiri, oba kozesa ekifaananyi oba ekintu ekimanyiddwa. Akabonero k’omukono kayinza okuyamba ekigambo ekituufu bwe kiba kizibu okufuna.
- **Linda.** Wa omuntu obudde okuwuliriza n’okuddamu. Tomumalirako buli sentensi oba okuddamu ekibuuzo mu nnimi nnyingi omulundi gumu.
- **Yogera ku kintu kimu ku mulundi.** Okunnyonnyola okuwanvu kuyinza okuba okukalu mu lulimi lwonna. Kozesa sentensi ennyimpi, wummulamu katono, olwo olabe oba ayagala okuwulira ebisingawo.
- **Kebera okuwulira n’okulaba.** Endabirwamu, ebyuma ebiyamba okuwulira, ekitangaala ekimala n’awabadde awatali kuleekaana biyinza okwanguyiza okugoberera olulimi.

Omuntu bw’aba ayagala okusoma, buuza oba amawulire amakulu agawandiikiddwa galiwo mu lulimi lwe. Teweesiga kuvvuunula kwa byuma okw’otoma ku kusalawo okw’ebyobulamu, okukkiriza oba ku bulagirizi bw’eddagala; kakasa amawulire amakulu ng’oyita mu abo abakola ku bulamu.

## Nnaakola ki bwe mba soogera lulimi omuntu lw’ayagala?

Buuza oba ayagala mukwano gwe oba ow’eŋŋanda ayogera ennimi ebbiri okwegattako ku mboozi eya bulijjo. Toteebereza nti omuntu ayagala omulala okumuvvuunulira; ayinza okwagala obubonero bw’emikono, ebifaananyi oba olulimi olulala lwe babiri lwe bagatta.

Ku nnonda y’okulaba omusawo oba ku mboozi endala eyeetaaga amawulire amatuufu, kuba essimu ku ddwaaliro oba ku kitongole ky’obulamu nga tonnagenda. Bategeeze olulimi omuntu lw’ayagala era obuuze obuyambi bw’omuvvuunuzi obuliwo. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services ennyonnyola](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) nti ab’eŋŋanda n’emikwano bayinza obutasobola kuvvuunula bulungi mawulire makulu agakwata ku bulamu; ekitongole kisobola okunnyonnyola eby’okulondako byakyo n’ebisaanyizo by’omu kitundu. Omuntu asobola okusalawo oba ayagala omuntu amuwagire okumuwerekerako, ate ng’abo abakola ku bulamu bateekateeka obuyambi bw’olulimi bwe kiba kyetaagisa.

Mu kulaba omusawo, yogera n’omuntu butereevu era omuwe obudde omuvvuunuzi okwogera. Kakasa nti omuntu ategedde ekibuuzo era nti alina akakisa okuddamu mu bigambo bye. Bw’oba omuwagira, yongerako obubaka obulala bwe kiba kyetaagisa era ng’akikkirizza.

## Ddi lwe nsaanidde okunoonya amagezi g’abasawo?

Obuzibu mu lulimi buyinza okukyuka mpolampola, naye enkyukakyuka ey’amangu ya njawulo. [NHS ekuwa amagezi okufuna obuyambi bw’abasawo amangu ago omuntu bw’afuuka omubuzabuze mangu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Tolowooza nti okulemererwa okwogeraganya okw’amangu kitundu kya dementia. Omuntu bw’afuuka omubuzabuze mangu, n’alwala nnyo oba n’aba muzibu okuzuukusa, tuukirira ebitongole by’ebyobulamu eby’amangu ebiri mu kitundu kyo.

Ku nkyukakyuka ezibaawo mpolampola, wandiika by’olaba era obuuze omusawo w’omuntu amagezi. Wandiika olulimi lwe yali akozesa, ekyamuyamba okwogeraganya n’obanga yali mukoowu oba mulwadde. Kino kuba kunnyonnyola kw’osobola okwogerako, so si kwe kusalawo obulwadde. [Omukutu gwaffe ogw’okwetegekera okulaba omusawo](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) gusobola okukuyamba okweteekateeka ebibuuzo.

## Nnyinza ntya okutegeeza abalala abamuyamba?

Ng’omuntu akkirizza, teekawo akawandiiko akampi ak’abantu b’ayagala okubeeramu. Kayinza okulaga olulimi oba enjogera gy’ayagala mu kwogera ne mu kusoma, ebigambo oba amannya agamanyiddwa, n’ebimuyamba okugoberera emboozi. Buuza oba ayagala omuntu yenna ow’enjawulo okubeera mu nnonda z’okulaba omusawo oba mu mawulire agakwata ku kulabirirwa. Weewale okuwandiika ebikwata ku bulamu bwe eby’ekyama ebitetaagisa mu kumuyamba, era omutegeeze ani asobola okulaba akawandiiko ako. Akawandiiko akakwata ku lulimi kayamba abantu okutandika emboozi mu ngeri esingako okubeera ennyangu; tekadda mu kifo ky’omuvvuunuzi mu mboozi enzibu ey’ebyobulamu.

## Nnyinza ntya okuwagira by’omuntu by’ayagala?

Olulimi kitundu ku ngeri omuntu gy’akolaganamu n’abalala, naye si kye kimutegeeza yenna. Buuza ku mirimu gye egya bulijjo, ku by’ayagala, ku bye ayagala okusoma n’abantu b’ayagala okubeeramu. Omuntu bw’akkiriza, wandiika ebigambo oba ebigambo ebitonotono ebiyinza okuyamba abalala abamuyamba era obannyonnyole engeri y’okubikozesaamu. Kuuma ebiwandiiko mu kyama era ogabane ebyo byokka omuntu by’akkirizza okugabana.

Towaliriza omuntu kwetendeka, kuvvuunulira balala oba okulaga nti ategedde. Emboozi ezimu ziyinza okubeera ennyangu mu lulimi olumu ate endala mu lulala. Omuntu okuwulira obulungi, eby’ekyama bye n’eby’ayagala bye bisinga obukulu okusinga okugoberera olulimi lumu.

[Amagezi gaffe ku kwogeraganya](/caregiving/communication-tips/) galaga engeri y’okwogera mu ngeri ennyangu, okuwuliriza n’okukozesa ebifaananyi.
