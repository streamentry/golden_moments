Dementia waxay ka dhigi kartaa mid ka adag helitaanka erayada, raacidda weedh dheer, ama ka xidhidda buuqa hareeraha. Midkoodna macnaheedu maaha in qofku joojiyay inuu rabo inuu xiriir yeesho. Isbeddelo yaryar oo ku saabsan sida aad wadahadalka u diyaarisid, sida aad u hadlayso iyo sida aad u dhegeysato ayaa wadahadalka u fududayn kara oo u deggenaan kara labadiinaba.

## Waa maxay talooyinka ugu muhiimsan marka la hadlayo qof ku nool dementia?

Hel feejignaantooda, ka dhig mid fudud, oo samir yeelo. Haddii aad xasuusato shan shay oo keliya:

1. **Qolka aamusii oo wejiga u jeedi.** Damii TV-ga, fadhiiso heerka indhaha, oo isticmaal magaciisa.
2. **Hal shay mar sheeg.** Weedho gaaban, hal fikrad, hal su’aal.
3. **Soo bandhig doorasho laba ah.** “Shaah mise qaxwo?” way ka fududahay “Maxaad rabtaa?”
4. **Sug.** Sii wakhti jawaabta ka badan inta dabiiciga ah, oo isku day inaadan hadalkooda dhex-gelin.
5. **Weligaa ha dood, ha imtixaanin.** U jawaab sida ay dareemayaan, ma aha in xaqiiqooyinku sax yihiin.

## Sidee u bilowdaa wadahadal?

Wax u diyaari si qofku diiradda kugu saaro. [Hagaha Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) wuxuu ku talinayaa in marka hore la yareeyo buuqa: damii raadiyaha ama TV-ga, xidh daahyada ama albaabka, ama u guuri meel aamusan.

- **Ka soo dhawow hore.** In dhabarka ama dhinaca laga yimaado way ka cabsi gelin kartaa.
- **Sheeg yaad tahay.** “Haye aabo, waa Hodan, gabadhaadii.” Tani waxay ka badbaadinaysaa inuu raadiyo magacaaga.
- **U dhaaf heerkiisa.** Haddii uu fadhiyo, fadhiiso ama jilba joog si indhahaagu u siman yihiin.
- **Hubi wajigaaga iyo codkaaga.** [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) waxay ku talisaa inaad ka feejignaato codkaaga, heerkiisa, muuqaalka wejigaaga iyo luqadda jidhkaaga, oo aad hiigsato hab diiran oo toos ah.
- **Hubi in muraayadaha iyo qalabka caawiya maqalka ay ku jiraan.** Way adag tahay in la raaco wadahadal aan si buuxda loo arki ama loo maqli karin.

## Sidee u dhigaa waxa aan sheego?

Isticmaal erayo fudud, weedho gaaban iyo cod deggan oo hoose.

- **Hal fikrad weedh kasta.** “Aan jaakadaada xidhno. Kadibna banaanka aan aadno.” Ma aha labadaba hal mar.
- **Isticmaal magacyo, ma aha “isagu” ama “iyadu”.** “Habaryar Faadumo way wacday” way ka cadcad tahay “Way wacday.”
- **Cod hoose, ma aha mid sare.** Family Caregiver Alliance waxay soo jeedisaa cod hoose halkii mid kor loo qaaday.
- **Ku celi, kadibna dib u dhig.** Haddii aanay fahmin, ku celi isla erayada hal mar. Haddii aysan shaqayn, sug daqiiqad oo isku day erayo kale.
- **Tus iyo sheeg.** Tilmaan koobka, kor u qaad labada siwiitar, ama tus tallaabada ugu horreysa.
- **La hadal iyaga, ha ka hadlin.** NIA waxay uga digtaa ka hadalka qofka sida inaanu halkaas joogin, iyo “hadal carruur ah”.

NIA waxay soo jeedisaa noocyo ka fudud oo ka mid ah su’aalaha caadiga ah. Waa kuwan tusaalayaal ku saabsan ujeeddadaas:

| Halkii | Isku day |
| --- | --- |
| “Maxaad rabtaa casho?” | “Ma rabtaa kalluun mise digaag?” |
| “Sidee tahay dareenkaaga?” | “Ma dareemaysaa daal?” |
| “Ma gaajaysan tahay?” | “Casho waxay diyaar noqonaysaa shan daqiiqo gudahood.” |
| “Sidaas ma aha sida loo sameeyo.” | “Aan sidan isku daynee.” |
| “Ma xasuusataa safarkeenii harada?” | “Waxaan ka fikirayay safarkeenii harada. Ii sheeg waxa aad ka jeclaatay.” |
| “Horeba waan kuu sheegay.” | Mar kale ka jawaab si naxariis leh, sidii markii ugu horreysay. |

## Sidee u dhegeystaa si fiican?

U dhegeyso macnaha iyo dareenka, ma aha oo keliya erayada.

- **Sug.** Aamusnaanta inta badan waa wakhti fekerka ah. Si tartiib ah ilaa toban tiri intaadan soo dhex gelin.
- **Soo jeedi eray keliya haddii ay ku dhegaan.** Family Caregiver Alliance waxay leedahay waa fiican tahay in erayo la soo jeediyo marka qof ku dhibtoonayo.
- **Hubi waxa aad maqashay.** [Hagaha Alzheimer's Association ee wada xiriirka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications) wuxuu soo jeedinayaa inaad raadiso macnaha ka dambeeya erayadooda oo aad ku celiso si aad u hubiso inaad fahantay. “Waxaad ka welwelsan tahay baabuurka?”
- **Fiiri wajigooda iyo jidhkooda.** Marka hadalku sii adkaado, NIA waxay xustaa, muuqaalka wejigu wuxuu muujin karaa murugo, xanaaq ama quus si ka cad erayada.
- **Welwelka si dhab ah u qaado.** Xitaa haddii welwelku ku salaysan yahay xaqiiqo khaldan, dareenku waa dhab.

## Maxaa dhaca haddii ay sheegaan wax aan run ahayn?

Ha dood. Aad uguma badan tahay inaad guuleysato, qofkuna wuxuu ku dhammaan karaa inuu dareemo ceeb ama cabsi. Taa beddelkeeda, ku xidhiidh dareenka, kadibna si jilicsan u sii wad.

Family Caregiver Alliance waxay soo jeedisaa aqoonsiga dareenka ka hor intaadan kaga leexan. Tusaale ahaan, haddii hooyadaa ay ku adkaysato inay u baahan tahay inay carruurta dugsiga ka soo qaado:

- Halkii: “Hooyo, carruurtaadu way weynaadeen. Taasi waxay ahayd afartan sano ka hor.”
- Isku day: “Weligaa waxaad ahayd hooyo daryeel badan. Ii sheeg sida aad u soo qaadi jirtay. Ma qaadanaynaa wax yar oo cunto ah marka hore?”

Alzheimer's Association waxay sidoo kale ku talisaa in aan loo dood-gelin hadallo aad ku raacsaneyn. Dooro wakhtiyadaada: haddii aaminaad khaldan aysan waxyeello lahayn, iska daa.

## Sidee isu beddelaa wadahadalka marka dementia sii socoto?

Baahiyuhu waqti ka dib way isbeddelaan, sidaas darteed hagaaji habkaaga. Alzheimer's Association waxay sharraxdaa saddex marxaladood oo ballaaran:

| Marxalad | Waxa caawiya |
| --- | --- |
| Hore | Ha u malaynin inaanay xiriiri karin. Ku dar wadahadalada, si toos ah ula hadal, weydii taageerada ay rabaan, oo isticmaal kaftan haddii uu caawiyo. |
| Dhexe | Wadahadal laba qof ah meel aamusan. Si tartiib ah hadal, indho-isku-eegid hay, isticmaal su’aalo haa-ama-maya, sii tilmaamo tallaabo-tallaabo ah, oo ha dhaliilin ama ha sixin. |
| Dambe | Ka soo dhawow hore oo sheeg yaad tahay. Ku dhiirrigeli tilmaamidda iyo tilmaamaha gacanta. Isticmaal taabasho, dhawaaqyo, muuqaallo iyo urta. Weligaa ha ku hadlin sidii inaanay joogin. |

Qof kastaa waa kala duwan yahay, qofka isku midna wuxuu yeelan karaa maalmo wanaagsan iyo kuwo adag. U jawaab sida ay hadda yihiin, ma aha calaamad.

## Ma fududayn karaan sawirrada iyo waxqabadyadu wadahadalka?

Haa. Sawir la wadaago ayaa culeyska ka qaada oo labadiinaba siiya wax aad eegtaan oo aad tilmaamtaan.

- **Sawirro hadal-bilow ah.** [Kaararka Wadahadalka](/games/conversation-cards/) waxay bixiyaan sawirro leh su’aalo furan oo aan weligood imtixaanin.
- **Sawirrada qoyska.** [Lammaanaha Sawirrada Qoyska](/games/photo-pairs/) wuxuu u beddelaa sawirrada aad dooratay ciyaar isku-mid ah oo jilicsan, taas oo inta badan soo kicisa sheekooyin.
- **Maahmaahyada la yaqaan.** [Dhammaystir Hadalka](/games/finish-the-saying/) wuxuu isticmaalaa maahmaahyo Mareykan ah oo caan ah (Ingiriisi), kadibna wuxuu soo jeedinayaa su’aal wadahadal.
- **Ciyaaraha isku-midka ah sidii gadaal.** [Lammaanaha Sawirrada](/games/picture-pairs/) wuxuu gacmahaaga siinayaa wax ay sameeyaan intaad sheekaynayso.

Eeg [sida loo wada ciyaaro ciyaaraha](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) iyo [waxqabadyada xusuus-wadaagga](/activities/reminiscence/) wax dheeraad ah. Booqashooyinka iyo wicitaannada, akhri hagayaasheenna [booqashada](/activities/visiting/) iyo [wicitaannada telefoonka iyo fiidiyowga](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).

## Maxaa dhaca haddii aan lumiyo samirka?

Qof walba way ku dhacdaa. NIA waxay soo jeedisaa in haddii aad xanaaqdo ama ku daalo, aad wakhti u qaadato inaad degganaato. U gal qol kale, qaado dhawr neefsasho oo gaabis ah, oo soo noqo marka aad karto. Had iyo jeer waad dib u bilaabi kartaa wadahadalka, qofkuna wuu ka jawaabi karaa niyaddaada deggan. Haddii xanaaqa iyo daalku noqdaan kuwo joogto ah, taasi waa calaamad ah inaad u baahan tahay taageero dheeraad ah, ma aha inaad guuldarraysanayso. Hagaheenna [naftaada daryeelka daryeelaha](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) wuxuu leeyahay meelo aad u leexato.
