Go'aannada lacagta waxay saameeyaan madax-bannaanaanta qofka iyo nolosha maalinlaha ah. Haddii shuqul uu adkaado, ka bilow shuqulkaas halkii aad ka male-awaali lahayd in qof kale wax walba la wareegi qaado. Weydii qofka caawimada uu doonayo oo ka qayb geli.

Bogganku wuxuu siinayaa macluumaad guud oo loogu talagalay akhristayaasha Mareykanka. Ikhtiyaarrada bangiyada iyo xeerarka sharciga way ka duwan yihiin meel kasta. [Alzheimer's Society hagaheeda maaraynta lacagta](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) wuxuu daboolayaa siyaabaha loo taageero qofka iyada oo doorkiisa la ilaalinayo. [Hay'adda Ilaalinta Maaliyadda Macaamiisha (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) waxay sharaxaysaa in caawimada qaab aan rasmi ahayn, qof la aamino iyo awoodda sharciga ah ay kala duwan yihiin.

## Sidee ayaan u caawin karaa iyada oo aan wax la qabsan?

Weydii qofka shuqulka lacagta uu doonayo caawimo. Waxay rabi karaan in ay biil wada eegaan, warqadaha la kala saaro, taariikhda la bixinayo ama baadho codsi lama filaan ah. Qof ayaa laga yaabaa inuu doonayo caawimo shuqullo qaar ah, qaarna uu doorbido inuu si gaar ah u maamulo.

- **Hadda weydii.** Isku day, "Ma doonaysaa inaan biilkan wada eegno?" Siiso waqti uu go'aan ku gaaro.
- **Hal shuqul ku xidh.** Haddii uu oggolaado, eeg qaddarka, taariikhda ladhaafo iyo ikhtiyaarada bixinta wada jir. Ka dhig qofku inuu go'aanka qaado.
- **Hal liis oo fudud samee.** Liiska biilasha, taariikhaha ladhaafo ama tallaabooyinka la isku raacay ayaa caawin kara labadiinba inaad aragto waxa la maareeyay.
- **Bangiga weydii ikhtiyaaraadkiisa.** Waxa laga yaabaa inuu bixiyo siyaabo lagu bixiyo lacag ama lagu hubiyo hawlaha akoonka ayaa sahlaya. Weydii waxa ikhtiyaar kasta oggolaanayo ka hor intaadan akoonka beddelin.
- **Mar dambe dib u fiirso.** Doorbidka qofku wuu is beddeli karaa. Caawinta ku ekaanshaha shuqullada uu hadda doonayo iyo u baahan yahay.

CFPB waxay sharaxaysaa caawimada aan rasmi ahayn sida dib u eegista biilasha iyo miisaaniyadda la wada qabanayo, marka qofku weli awoodo oo ogolaado hawlaha. Taasi waxay ka duwan tahay in qof kale uu lacag ka qaado ama lacag bixiyo. Ha wadaagin ereyada sirta ah, PIN-ka ama koodhadhka ammaanka mar kasta si gaaban looga fogaado.

## Maxay qof la aamino ah u oggolaan karaa?

Dalka Mareykanka, astaanta qof la aamino waxay oggolaan kartaa in bangi ama hay'ad maaliyadeed ay la xiriirto qof la magacaabay xaalado gaar ah, sida walaac ku saabsan khiyaano suurtagal ah. CFPB waxay sheegtaa in magacaabista qof la aamino aanay siin qofkaas gelitaan akoonka ama ogolaansho lacag u dhaqaajiso. Astaantan ma laga yaabo inay ku jirto akoon kasta, markaa weydii bangiga ama hay'adda maaliyadda sida ay u shaqeyso.

| Nooca caawimada | Macnaha |
|---|---|
| Biilasha wada eeg | Qofka iyo caawiyaha wada eegaan biilasha; qofku wuu go'aaminayaa oo ogolaanayaa bixinta. |
| Qof la aamino | Hay'ad maaliyadeed ayaa wicitaan u samayn karta qofka la magacaabay xaalado gaar ah. Magaca keligiis ma siinayo gelitaan akoon. |
| Ogolaansho rasmi ah ee akoonka ama sharciga | Ogolaansho bangi ama awoodda sharciga ah ee lagu sameeyo wareejin ayaa oggolaan karta ka-jaridda. Awooddooda iyo waajibaadkooda waxay ku xidhan yihiin akoonka iyo sharciga maxalliga ah. |

Ka hor intaadan beddelin helitaanka akoonka ama milkiilaha, weydii bangiga iyo xirfad-yahan sharci oo maxalli ah inay sharxaan ikhtiyaarada iyo khataraha. Ogaanshaha keligiis ma sheegayo shuqullada maaliyadda ee qofku qaban karo ama go'aannada uu doonayo inuu qaado. Ku dhowow qofka qaybta go'aannada inta uu doonayo. Haddii uu doonayo qof wax u qabta, weydii sida loo dejin karo si sharci ah, halkii laga isticmaali lahaa magaca gelitaanka qof kale.

## Maxay isbeddelo ah ayaa lagu eegi karaa?

Biil dhacay ama iibsasho aan caadi ahayn ayaa laga yaabaa inay leedahay sharaxaad kala duwan. Taasi ma caddeynayso in qofku aanu awoodin inuu maareeyo lacagta ama in qof uu xadgudbiyay. Weydii waxa dhacay, oo haddii qofku oggolaado, eeg warbixinta ama biilka wada jir.

[CFPB waxay liistay calaamado suurtagal ah oo xadgudubka maaliyadda](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), oo ay ku jiraan lacag ama hanti maqan, qaadasho lama filaan ah oo aan caadi ahayn, biilal aan la bixin, dad aan la aqoon oo lagu daro akoonada, ama qof xakameynaya wicitaannada iyo booqashooyinka qofka. Eeg qaababka iyo isbeddelada ka duwan waxa caadiga u ah qofkan; ha u qaadan calaamad keliya caddeyn.

Haddii qofku sheego in lacag maqan tahay, welwelkaas si dhab ah u qaad. [Hagahayaga ka jawaabidda shakiga](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) wuxuu leeyahay siyaabo lagu hubiyo welwel iyada oo aan la diidin. Si gaar ah u weydii qofka waxa uu arkay iyo waxa uu doonayo inuu dhaco marka xigta. Ka fogow in aad eedeysid qaraabo, daryeel ama shaqaale bangi ka hor intaadan xaqiiqda hubin. Haddii aad u malaynayso in caawiye la aamino uu ku lug leeyahay, weydii la-talin ama adeeg maxalli ah oo kale.

## Sidee ayaan u yareyn karnaa khatarta khiyaanada?

Khiyaanoyinku waxay ku dhaari karaan inay yihiin bangi, xafiis dowladeed, ganacsi, saaxiib ama qaraabo. Waxay sheegi karaan in uu jiro xaalad degdeg ah oo ay weyddiistaan bixin degdeg ah. [Alzheimer's Association hagaheeda amniga tignoolajiyada](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) wuxuu soo jeedinayaa in la joogsado, la hubiyo xiriirka, oo caawimo la helo ka hor intaan macluumaad ama lacag la wadaagin.

- **Farriin degdeg ah yihiin markay dareento, joogso.** Ha dirin lacag ama ha wadaagin macluumaadka akoonka intaad cadaadis ku jirto wicitaan ama farriin.
- **Xiriir la aamino ah isticmaal oo hubi.** Gooye oo la xiriir bangiga lambarka ku qoran kaarka ama biilka dhowaan, ama isticmaal app-ka rasmiga ah. Ha isticmaalin link ama lambar uu soo diray wicitaanka.
- **Macluumaadka amniga ilaali.** Ha u dirin erayga sirta ah, faahfaahinta buuxda ee akoonka ama koodhadhka ammaanka farriin aan la filayn ku jawaabid. [FTC waxay talo siisay in la joojiyo wicitaanka aan la filayn ee weydiinaya kood, oo bangiga lagu xiriiro lambar la aamino](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Caawimada qofka la heshiiyo.** Weydii qofka cidda uu doonayo in uu la hubiyo codsi lama filaan ah. Astaanta qof la aamino ee bangigu wuxuu caawin karaa xiriirka, laakiin ma oggolaanayso wicitaan keliya.

## Maxaan sameeyaa haddii lacag u gudbay khiyaano?

La xiriir bangiga, app-ka lacag bixinta, kaarka ama shirkadda loo isticmaalay lacagta si ugu dhakhsaha badan. Isticmaal lambar ku qoran kaarka ama biilka, ama websaydka ama app-ka rasmiga ah ee hay'adda. Weydii haddii ay joojin ama soo celin karaan bixinta. [Tallaabooyinka soo kabashada ee FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) waxay sharaxayaan in ikhtiyaaradu ku xidhan yihiin sida lacagta loo diray; ma jiro dammaanad ah in la soo kabsan karo.

Ilaali fariimaha, rasiidhadhka, taariikhaha iyo ogaysiisyada akoonka. Mareykanka, khiyaanada waxaad ku warbixin kartaa [FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Haddii aad u malaynayso in qof uu xadgudbiyo qofka waayeelka ah, [Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) waxay sheegtaa in la xiriiro Adult Protective Services ee gobolka qofku deganyahay. Haddii qof khatar hadda ku jirto, la xiriir adeegyada degdegga ah ee maxalliga ah. Ka baxsan Mareykanka, isticmaal adeegyada ilaalinta macaamiisha iyo ilaalinta maxalliga ah.

Ku hadal iyada oo aan eedeyn lahayn. Khiyaanooyinku waxay loogu talagalay inay qof khaldaan. [Hagaha CFPB ee caawimada khiyaanada iyo nasiibda xumada](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) wuxuu liistaa khiyaarada warbixinta Mareykanka wuxuuna talo siinayaa in la isticmaalo websaydyo iyo lambarro la aamino.

## Meesha laga helo caawimo go'aan maaliyadeed gaar ah?

Weydii bangiga qofku aqaadka iyo ogolaanshaha ay leeyihiin. Dukumentiyada sharciga ama isbeddelka milkiilaha akoonka, la xiriir xirfad-yahan la aqoon oo ogaan sharciga meesha qofku deganyahay. Haddii aad welwelsan tahay in la xad-gudbi karo, isticmaal adeeg ilaalin maxalli ah. Boggani ma go'aamin karo awoodda ay qof u leeyahay ama in wareejin gaar ah yahay khiyaano.

[Hagaha tallaabooyinka ka dib ogaanshaha](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) wuxuu caawin karaa qofka iyo qoyska inay doortaan su'aal qorsheynta ah oo ay wax ka qabtaan. [Hagaha khilaafaadka qoyska](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) wuxuu bixiyaa siyaabo lagu wada hadlo hal arrin mar keliya.
