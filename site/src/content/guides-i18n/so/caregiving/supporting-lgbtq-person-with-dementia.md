Aqoonsiga qofka LGBTQ+ waa qayb ka mid ah cidda uu yahay. Weydii waxa u muhiimka ah, isticmaal ereyada ay doortaan, oo u oggolow inay go'aamiyaan cidda ogaan karta faahfaahinta gaarka ah. Ha ka malayn aqoonsiga qofka, lammaanaha, taageerada qoyska ama rabitaankooda asturnaanta muuqaalka, diiwaannadii hore ama cidda soo booqata.

[Hagaha Alzheimer's Society ee ku saabsan taageeridda qofka LGBTQ+ ee la nool waallida](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/supporting-lgbtq-dementia) wuxuu ku talinayaa in la weydiiyo rabitaanka qofka lagana fogaado in la malo xiriirrada. [Ilaha waallida ee LGBTQ+ ee Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) waxay sidoo kale sharxayaan walaacyada ku saabsan takoorka iyo helitaanka daryeel soo dhawayn leh. Dadku way kala duwan yihiin; u isticmaal fikradahan inay noqdaan kuwo kiciya wada hadal, ee ma aha liis qeexaya qof.

## Sideen ku weydiin karaa magaca, magac-u-yaallada iyo asturnaanta?

Dooro wakhti gaar ah oo aan degdeg lahayn. Waxaad weydiin kartaa, “Magacee iyo magac-u-yaallee ayaad rabtaa inaan isticmaalno?” ama “Ma jiraa wax ku saabsan aqoonsigaaga ama xiriirradaada oo aad rabto in dadka ku taageeraya ay ogaadaan?” Qofku wuxuu si qayb ah uga jawaabi karaa, wuu diidi karaa, ama dib buu ugu soo noqon karaa arrinta. Uma baahnid inaad weydiiso su'aalo gaar ah oo aan khusayn daryeelkooda.

Weydii cidda ogaan karta waxa. Qof ayaa laga yaabaa inuu rabo in shaqaaluhu aqoonsadaan lammaanihiisa, laakiin inuusan rabin in qoyska ballaaran loo sheego jihadiisa galmada ama aqoonsigiisa jinsiga. Qofku wuxuu sidoo kale doorbidi karaa ereyo kala duwan meelo kala duwan. Ha la wadaagin faahfaahinta aqoonsiga qaraabada, booqdayaasha ama shaqaalaha sababtoo ah adiga ayaad ogtahay.

Haddii qofku rabo in doorbidid la diiwaangeliyo, weydii waxa la qorayo iyo cidda arki lahayd. Hubi sida bixiyuhu u maareeyo magaca ka duwan diiwaannada kale, oo ay ku jirto in shaqaaluhu isticmaali karaan magaca qofku doorbido marka ay la hadlaan. Maskaxda ku hay asturnaanta qofka; qof kasta oo ka caawiya daryeelka uma baahna inuu ogaado faahfaahin kasta.

Dookhyadu way is beddeli karaan. Mar kale hubi marka qofku muujin karo doorasho, gaar ahaan ka hor guuritaan ama qorshe daryeel oo cusub. Haddii aysan caddayn waxa ay rabaan in la wadaago, ha u malayn. Raac doorbid kasta oo cad oo ay hore u sheegeen, oo weydii xirfadle deegaanka ah oo aqoon u leh si aad uga hesho caawimo su'aalaha ku saabsan awoodda go'aanka daryeelka ama xeerarka wadaagista macluumaadka.

## Yaa lagu dari karaa wada hadallada ku saabsan daryeelka?

Weydii qofka cidda ay aaminsan yihiin iyo cidda ay rabaan in lagu daro. Shabakaddooda taageero waxaa ku jiri kara lammaane, saaxiib dhow, qaraabo ama “qoys la doortay”—dad u taageera sidii qoys inkastoo aysan qaraabo ahayn. Dadka qaar waxay ku tiirsan yihiin qoyska ay ka soo jeedaan; qaar kalena ma tiirsana. U oggolow qofka inuu ku sharaxo xiriirradiisa ereyadiisa.

Weydii su'aalo wax ku ool ah: Yaa soo booqan kara? Yaa ay tahay in shaqaaluhu u soo wacaan haddii qorshayaashu is beddelaan? Yaa heli kara warbixin? Haddii bixiyuhu u baahan yahay oggolaansho ama waraaqo ka hor inta aan la wadaagin macluumaad, weydii waxa loo baahan yahay oo u sharax fursadaha qofka. Noqoshada lammaane ama taageere la doortay si toos ah uma siinayso awood sharci oo go'aan qaadasho; xeerarku way ku kala duwan yihiin goobaha.

Isticmaal magaca xiriirka ee qofku doorbido. Shaqaale daryeel oo u yeera lammaane “saaxiib” ama “qaraabo” marka qofku rabo in loo aqoonsado lammaane waxay keeni kartaa murugo. Qoraal gaaban oo ku salaysan oggolaansho ayaa shaqaalaha u sheegi kara sida loo soo bandhigo dadka iyo faahfaahinta la wadaagi karo.

## Maxaan ka weydiin karaa bixiyaha daryeelka?

Markaad is barbar dhigeyso daryeelka hoyga, taageerada guriga lagu bixiyo ama adeegyada maalinta dadka waaweyn, weydii qofka su'aalaha ay rabaan inay keenaan iyo in ay rabaan inaad halkaas joogto. [Hagaha SAGE ee adeegyada taageerada leh](https://www.sageusa.org/resource/10-tips-for-finding-lgbt-affirming-services/) wuxuu xusayaa in la wadaagista macluumaadka gaarka ah ee bixiyaha ay dareemi karto nuglaansho. Weydii bixiyaha inuu si cad u sharaxo hab-dhaqankiisa hadda.

- Sideed u diiwaangelisaan magaca la doorbidayo iyo magac-u-yaallada? Shaqaaleyaa arki kara macluumaadkaas?
- Sidee qofku u dooran karaa cidda soo booqata oo uu u heli karaa wakhti gaar ah oo uu la qaato lammaane ama taageere kale?
- Sideed u barataan oo u ixtiraamtaan dookhyada qofka ee ku saabsan qolalka, dharka iyo daryeelka shaqsiga ah?
- Tababar noocee ah ayay shaqaaluhu ka helaan dadka waaweyn ee LGBTQ+ iyo waallida?
- Sidee qofka deggan ama taageeruhu u soo gudbin karaa walaac ku saabsan asturnaanta ama ixtiraam la'aanta, maxaase dhacaya marka xigta?

[Hagaha SAGE iyo Human Rights Campaign Foundation ee daryeelka muddada dheer ee loo dhan yahay](https://www.sageusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/lei-consumer-guide-finding-lgbtq-inclusive-ltcc.pdf) wuxuu soo jeedinayaa in la hubiyo siyaasadaha qoran ee ku saabsan booqashada, qolalka iyo hab-dhaqanka shaqaalaha. Waxaa la daabacay 2021; u isticmaal su'aalo la weydiinayo, ee ha u qaadanin bayaan ku saabsan sharciga deegaanka ee hadda jira. Siyaasadaha iyo adeegyadu way ku kala duwan yihiin meelaha.

Astaanta, buug-yaraha ama hal jawaab oo lagu kalsoonaan karo ma dammaanad qaadi karaan sida daryeelka maalinlaha ahi dareemi doono. Haddii qofku rabo inuu booqdo, fiiri sida shaqaaluhu ugu yeeraan iyo sida ay uga jawaabaan lammaanihiisa ama taageerayaashiisa la doortay. Weydiiso inaad si gaar ah ula hadasho shaqaale haddii taasi ka caawin karto qofka inuu dareemo badbaado.

## Sideen u taageeri karaa qofka oo dhan?

Weydii waxyaabaha caadiga ah ee qofka xiiseeya, jadwalkiisa, luqaddiisa, dhaqankiisa, diintiisa, asxaabtiisa iyo bulshadiisa. Aqoonsigiisu wuxuu noqon karaa mid muhiim ah in laga hadlo, ama waxay doorbidi karaan inay waqtiga ku bixiyaan qaybaha kale ee nolosha. Ha ka dhigin booqasho kasta wada hadal ku saabsan aqoonsiga ama ha u malayn in qof kasta oo LGBTQ+ ah uu lahaa waayo-aragnimo isku mid ah.

Dadka qaar waxay la kulmeen diidmo ama takoor, waxaana laga yaabaa inay ka digtoonaadaan adeegyada. Kuwa kale laga yaabee inaysan arrintan u arkin walaac. Dhageyso adigoon qofka ka codsanin inuu sharaxo ama cadeeyo waxba. Haddii xusuus, sawir ama sheeko ku saabsan wixii la soo dhaafay ay dhibayso, weydii inay rabaan inay joojiyaan ama mawduuca beddelaan.

Haddii aad tahay lammaane, saaxiib ama qoys, taageeradaada adiguna waa muhiim. Weydii qofka sida ay rabaan inaad uga qayb qaadato, oo raadi urur deegaanka ah oo ka shaqeeya waallida ama gabowga LGBTQ+ haddii aad u baahan tahay talo. [Bogga ilaha ee Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) wuxuu xiriiriyayaa ilaha bulshada iyo daryeelayaasha; waxa la heli karo waxay ku xiran yihiin meesha aad ku nooshahay.

Si aad u hesho liis ballaaran oo hubin ah marka aad is barbar dhigeyso guryaha daryeelka, eeg [doorashada daryeelka hoyga ee waallida](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/). Si aad u hesho caawimo qorshaynta ka dib ogaanshaha, eeg [tallaabooyinka ugu horreeya ka dib ogaanshaha waallida](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
