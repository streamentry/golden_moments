Mashruucyada farshaxanku waa hab wanaagsan oo lagu qaato waqti la qaatay qofka qaba dementia. Waxay shaqaysiinaan gacmaha, indhaha iyo dareenka, waxayna kuu siinayaan wax aad ka wada hadli karto. Bogan waxaa ku jira mashruucyo gaar ah oo leh tallaabooyin fudud, iyo tilmaamo lagu doorto alaabta farshaxanka si badbaadada leh. Mabaadiida farshaxanka dadka waaweyn, sida in laga fogaado alaabta carruurta, eeg [farshaxanka iyo garashada dadka waaweyn ee qaba dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Sida loo bilaabo mashruuc kasta oo farshaxan

1. **Diyaari wax kasta ka hor.** Hal ama laba alaab ku dhig, xaashi ilaaliya iyo maro qoyan.
2. **Tus tallaabada koowaad.** Buroshka ku dhoob, sawirka koowaad jarjar, ama dhoobo yar cadaadi, kadibna ku martiqaad inay sidaas sameeyaan.
3. **Ku shaqee agtooda.** Mashruucaaga ku hay agtooda si waxqabadku u dareemo wadajir, ha ahaado mid la kormeerayo.
4. **Ka wada hadal waxa aad aragto.** Sharax midabada, taabashada iyo qaabka, halkii aad ku xukumi lahayd shaqada.
5. **Jooji ka hor inta aanu tamarta hoos u dhicin.** Toban ilaa labaatan daqiiqo ayaa inta badan ah dherer wanaagsan.

[Ururka Alzheimer's](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) wuxuu xusayaa in farshaxanku yahay hab lagu muujiyo dareen oo lagu farxo abuurista iyadoon xirfad farshaxan loo baahnayn. [Machadka Qaranka ee Da'da](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) wuxuu talinayaa in waxqabadyada loo jebiyo tallaabooyin yaryar oo la maamuli karo.

## Dhaqidda biyo-midabka

Isticmaal warqad biyo-midabka culus ah, buroshka weyn ee jilicsan, iyo dhowr midab oo koobbo yaryar ku jira. Warqadda biyo nadiif ah ku qoyo, kadibna u ogolow inay midabka ku daadiyaan. Warqadda si tartiib ah u jeexjeexi si midabadu u socdaan. Natiijadu waa sawir jilicsan oo aan qorshe u baahnayn.

- **Marxalada hore:** U ogolow inay midabada doortaan oo ay dhul-muuqaal rinjiyeeyaan.
- **Marxalada dhexe:** Siiso laba ama saddex midab, oo adiga ayaad qoyskiisa samee.
- **Marxalada dambe:** Warqadda qabo intaad sifuufka ku dhoobinayso.

## Isku-darka sawirada joornaalka

Ka soo qaado bogagga joornaalka oo leh sawiro ifaya oo ubaxyo, cunto, xayawaan, gaadiid ama safar ku saabsan. Sawirada jeexjeexi qaybo waaweyn, taas oo ka fudud jarista, kadibna ku dheji warqad adag. Isku-darka sawirada wuxuu wanaagsan yahay sida bilow wada hadal, maxaa yeelay sawir walba wuxuu hor keeni karaa xusuus. Waxay ku habboon tahay qof jecel inuu kala soocaa oo doorto.

## Dhoobo jilicsan

Dhoobada hawada ku engegta ama dhoobada jilicsan ee la hagaajin karo waa la cadaadin karaa, la fidin karaa, la rujin karaa. Samee koob fudud ama tiile balaadhan. Dhoobadu waxay leedahay taabasho xooga leh, sidaas darteed waxay nasiin kartaa dadka jecel inay wax taabtaan. Qaybaha weyn ha ahaadeen, oo ka fogow qalabka leh dhibco fiiqan.

## Shaaqada midabka ee warqad madow ku dul taal

Warqadda madow waxay ka dhigaysaa midabyada shaaqada iftiin leh. Isticmaal shaaqo weyn oo midabyo badan leh, oo u ogolow qofka inuu jaranjarro balaadhan sameeyo ama uu ku dhex qasto farta ama maro. Shaaqadu waa boodbood, sidaas darteed ku diyaari saxan, kadibna gacmaha ka tirtir markaa dambe. Dooro shaaqo jilicsan oo calaamad lagu saaray in aanay sun lahayn.

## Daabacaadda caleemaha

Ka soo ururi caleemo waaweyn beerta, rinjiga la dhaqi karo ku burush jirridaha, kadibna caleemaha ku riix warqadda. Si taxadar leh uga qaad, kadibna ka faraxso sawirka. Mashruucan wuxuu si wanaagsan ula xidhiidhaa [waxqabadyada dibadda iyo beerta](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), wuxuuna ka bilaabi karaa socod loo ururinayo caleemo.

## Dharka iyo habaynta ubaxa

Kala soo dhig maro dharka ah oo lagu taaban karo taabasho, ama ku habee dhowr ubax cusub weel. Kuwani waa mashruucyo dareen ku salaysan oo aan cadaadis badan lahayn. Waxay sidoo kale ku habboonaadaan dadka marxalada dambe. Fikrado dheeraad ah, eeg [waxqabadyada dareenka ee dadka qaba dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Sida loo doorto alaabta farshaxanka

Alaabtu waxay muhiim u tahay badbaadada iyo raaxada marka loo eego tayada. Markaad dooranayso, tixgeli:

- **Aan sun lahayn oo la dhaqi karo:** Raadso calaamado ku qoran aan sun lahayn, oo hubi in rangagu la dhaqi karo.
- **Qabow weyn:** Shamaasho weyn, buroshyo qaro weyn iyo shaaqo jajab ah ayaa fudud in la qabto.
- **Qaybo yaryar lahayn:** Ka fogow jiiga, batoonka, dhaladaha iyo dahabka yar-yar ee la liqi karo.
- **Qalab fiiqan lahayn:** Isticmaal shariiqyo badbaado ah, ama warqad ku jaraa gacmaha.
- **Xasaasiyad:** Hubi laastikada, iyo xasaasiyad kasta oo jirka ku saabsan.
- **Iftiin iyo midab:** Midabro cad oo ku dul taal warqad cad ama warqad madow ayaa fudud in la arko.
- **Liis gaaban:** Saddex ilaa laba alaab ayaa ku filan. Doorashooyin badan ayaa dadka qaar ka qaldi kara.
- **Nadiifinta fudud:** Isticmaal saxan, xaashi plastik ah iyo maro qoyan, si wasakhdu u noqon sabab lagu joojiyo.

## La qabsiga marxalada

Marxalada hore, dadka badankood way qorsheeyn karaan oo dhammaystiri karaan mashruuc gacan yar la siiyo. Marxalada dhexe, doorashooyin yar iyo hagid badan u bixi. Marxalada dambe, diiradda saar habka dareenka ku salaysan sida dhoobo cadaadinta ama sifuufka ku dhoobinta, oo ku raaxayso natiijada halkii aad ku eegi lahayd dhammaystirka.

Fikrado dheeraad ah oo marxala kasta ku saabsan, eeg [waxqabadyada marxalada hore](/activities/early-stage/), [marxalada dhexe](/activities/middle-stage/) iyo [waxqabadyada marxalada dambe](/activities/late-stage/). Haddii qofku diido ama uu niyad-jabo, hagaha [marka qofku yidhaahdo maya](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) wuxuu sharxayaa sida loo dib u dhigo.

## Isku day ciyaar

Kadib kalfadhi rinjiyeyn, isku day [midabbada](/games/coloring/) tablet-ka oo ah waxqabad aad u degan, ama [picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) oo ah waxqabad dhammaystir. Labadaba gacmaha ku hayaan shuqul aan u baahnayn xirfad nax ah.
