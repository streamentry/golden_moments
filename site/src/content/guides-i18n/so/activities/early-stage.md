Marxaladda hore ee dementia, dad badan weli way ku nool yihiin inta badan sidii hore. Waxaa laga yaabaa inay shaqeeyaan, mutadawicnimo sameeyaan, saaxiibo arkaan oo ay maamulaan inta badan maalintooda. Waxqabadyada inta badan looma baahna in la beddelo; waxay u baahan yihiin dhawr hagaajin si ay u sii ahaadaan kuwo lagu raaxaysto. Doorkaagu wuxuu ka sii dhow yahay lammaane ama saaxiib ka badan daryeele: qof taageera, la qorshaysta oo gala keliya marka la weydiiyo.

## Waxqabadyo noocee ah ayaa wanaagsan dementia marxaladda hore?

Waxqabadyada ugu wanaagsan marxaladda hore inta badan waa kuwa qofku hore u jeclaa, oo yar la fududeeyay. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) waxay taxdaa fikrado sida ciyaaraha kaararka iyo miiska, raadinta erayada (word finds), socodka, qoobka ka ciyaarka, beerista, habaynta ubaxyada, sheeko-sheegidda, rinjiyeynta, muusikada, qorista buug-yare, qorista warqado, buugaagta xusuusta (scrapbooking), karinta iyo caadooyinka dabaaldegyada.

Waxa isbeddela inta badan waa goobta, ma aha waxqabadka. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) waxay sheegtaa in dadka marxaladda hore ay bilaabi karaan inay ka noqdaan waxyaabihii ay ku raaxaysan jireen, waxayna tusaale u soo qaadataa qof ka dareema culeys xaflad weyn oo ka fiicnaata koox yar. Si furan uga hadal waxa adag, oo wada hagaaji.

## Marka hore weydii, oo isku raaca sida loo caawinayo

[Hagidda marxaladda hore ee Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage) waxay soo jeedisaa dhawr caado oo waxqabadyada sidoo kale quseeya:

- **Si toos ah u weydii.** “Maxaa kuu fududeyn kara kooxda buugaagta?” wuxuu helaa jawaab ka wanaagsan hadal-qiyaas.
- **U qaado inuu awoodo.** Haddii khatar badbaado aysan jirin, u daa qofka inuu isku dayo, oo dhiirrigelin sii halkii aad gacanta ku qaadi lahayd.
- **Isku raaca calaamad.** Dooro eray gaar ah ama madax luliddo oo macnaheedu yahay “waa fiican tahay inaad gasho,” tusaale ahaan marka magac aanu soo dhicin inta ciyaarta kaararka la ciyaarayo.
- **Si joogto ah u hubi.** Weydii in inta caawimo ee la helayo ay sax tahay, aad u badan tahay mise aad u yar tahay.

## Fikrado waxqabad oo loogu talagalay marxaladda hore

### Sii wad doorarka iyo hiwaayadaha

- **Sii wad ballan joogto ah.** Mutadawicnimo toddobaadle ah, koox heeso ama koox kale ayaa toddobaadka siisa qaab. Haddii ay cadaadis noqoto, door gaagaaban ama aamusan ayaa laga yaabaa inuu ka habboonaado.
- **Wada qorshee cuntooyinka.** Haddii wax-soo-iibsigu noqday mid keenaya niyad-jab, Alzheimer's Association waxay soo jeedisaa in qofka lagu weydiiyo inuu kaa caawiyo qorshaynta liiska cuntada toddobaadka iyo qorista liiska wax la iibsanayo.
- **Karso oo dubo.** Wada raac cunto-karis la jecel yahay. Marka hore u kala dhig maaddooyinka si isku xigta, si tallaabooyinku u muuqdaan.
- **Beer.** Qorshee goob la beerayo, dooro abuur ama dhir oo wada shaqeeya. Liis qoran oo hawlaha beerta ee toddobaadka ayaa caawisa.
- **Sii wad gacan-sameyn.** Shaqada alwaaxda, tolidda dharka, tolidda dunta (knitting) ama samaynta qaabab (model) waa sii socon karaan iyadoo la isticmaalayo qaabab fudud iyo iftiin wanaagsan.

### Ciyaaro raaxo ah

- **Ciyaar kaararka iyo ciyaaraha miiska.** Ciyaaraha la yaqaan ayaa ugu fudud. Ka ciyaar kooxo ahaan si qofna xaalad adag lagu hayn, dhibcahana si dabacsan u hay ama ha hayn.
- **Isku day raadinta erayada ee far waaweyn.** [Raadinta erayada](/games/word-search/) waxaa lagu ciyaari karaa shaashadda ama waa la daabici karaa wakhti aamusan.
- **Samee halxiraalayaal jigsaw.** Dooro tiro jajabyo ah oo nasasho leh, ma aha mid ku adag. [Halxiraalaha sawirka](/games/picture-puzzle/) waa nooc gaaban oo shaashadda ah, hagaheenna [halxiraalayaasha dementia](/activities/puzzles/) wuxuu ka hadlaa sida loo doorto.
- **Ku biir su’aalo cayaareed oo madadaalo ah.** Dadka qaar waxay jecel yihiin fiid su’aalo kooxeed. Haddii ay bilaabato inay u egtahay imtixaan, dooro wax kale, ama isku day [su’aalaheenna fudud](/activities/trivia/), kuwaas oo la socda doorashooyin jawaab iyo dhibco la’aan.

Ciyaaruhu halkan waa raaxo. Ciyaar lama muujin inay daaweyso ama dib u dhigto dementia; wax dheeraad ah ka akhri [ciyaaraha maskaxda: waxa cilmi-baarisu sheegto](/brain-health/brain-games/).

### Qor, ka fikir oo diiwaan geli

- **Qor buug-yare.** Alzheimer's Association waxay soo jeedisaa qorista buug-yare inay tahay hab loo maareeyo dareenka kadib markii dementia lagu ogaado. Dhawr xariiq maalintii way ku filan yihiin.
- **Hogaamiyo buug sheekada nolosha.** Marxaladda hore waa wakhti wanaagsan in qofku doorto sawirradiisa oo qoro ama ku sheego sheekooyinka ka dambeeya. Eeg [waxqabadyada xusuus-wadaagga](/activities/reminiscence/).
- **Qor warqado.** Warqad loo qoray ilmo-ilmeed, saaxiib duug ah ama shaqo-wadaag hore waxay u noqon kartaa mid macno leh labada dhinac.
- **Ka sheekee sawirrada.** [Lammaanaha sawirrada qoyska](/games/photo-pairs/) wuxuu u beddelaa sawirrada qoyska ciyaar isku-mid ah oo jilicsan, [kaararka wadahadalka](/games/conversation-cards/) na waxay bixiyaan meelo fudud oo hadalka laga bilaabo.

### Bax oo dhaqaaq

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) waxay soo jeedisaa in la sii wado bixitaannada la yaqaan inta aad labadiinaba raaxo ku jirtaan. U qorshee wakhtiga maalinta ee qofka ugu fiican, oo ha u daynin inay aad u dheeraadaan.

- **Safarro gaagaaban oo dhaqan ah.** Matxaf, riwaayad galab ah ama bandhig farshaxan, ugu fiican wakhti aamusan.
- **Dabaasha.** NIA waxay soo jeedisaa barkad wakhti maalinta ah oo aan mashquul badnayn.
- **Kooxaha socodka iyo fasalada qoob-ka-ciyaarka.** [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) waxay xustaa qoob-ka-ciyaar, tai chi, yoga, dabaasho iyo kooxaha socodka, iyo fadhiyo dementia u habboon meesha ay jiraan.
- **Kooxaha heesaha iyo kaafeeyada xusuusta (memory cafés).** Kooxo dabacsan oo dadka ku nool dementia iyo lammaanahooda ay kula kulmi karaan dad kale oo xaalad isku mid ah ku jira.

## Isbeddelo yaryar oo waxqabadyada ka dhigaya kuwo la ku raaxaysto

| Haddii tani ay sii adkaanayso | Isku day tan |
| --- | --- |
| Meelo mashquul badan ama buuq leh | Tag wakhtiyo aamusan, dooro kooxo yar yar, fadhiiso xagga qolka |
| Xasuusashada magacyo cusub | Mar kasta magaca ku soo bandhig dadka; weydii kooxaha inay isticmaalaan kaararka magaca |
| Raacidda qorshayaal tallaabo badan leh | Qor tallaabooyinka, ama marka hore wax walba si isku xigta u kala dhig |
| Daalka | Bixitaanno gaagaaban, nasasho kadib, maalintaas wax kale ha qorshayn |
| Dabagalka ciyaarta | Ka ciyaar kooxo ahaan, fududee xeerarka, ama ka tag dhibcaynta |
| Helitaanka erayada | Sii wakhti; soo jeedi eray keliya haddii calaamaddiinna la isku raacay ay sidaas tiraahdo |

## Goorta la dhimayo

Jooji ama beddel waxqabadka marka uu keeno cadaadis ka badan raaxo. Niyad-jab soo noqnoqda, ka fogaanshaha hiwaayad ama odhanta “mar dambe ma awoodo” waxay mudan yihiin wadahadal jilicsan, ma aha riixid. Inta badan jawaabku waa nooc yar oo isla waxqabadkaas ah. Marka qofku u baahdo caawimo dheeraad ah bilaabidda iyo raacidda tallaabooyinka, fikradaha [waxqabadyada marxaladda dhexe](/activities/middle-stage/) ayaa laga yaabaa inay ka habboonaadaan. Wax dheeraad ah oo ku saabsan wadahadal taageero leh, eeg [talooyinka wada xiriirka](/caregiving/communication-tips/), fikrado badan oo kalena eeg [101 waxqabad](/activities/ideas/).

Lammaanayaashuna waxay u baahan yihiin taageero. Marxaladda hore waa wakhti wanaagsan in aad dhisto shabakadaada caawimada; eeg [daryeelaha naftiisa daryeelka](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
