Marxaladda dhexe ee dementia inta badan waa tan ugu dheer, waana marka waxqabadyadu bilaabaan inay u baahdaan taageero dhab ah. Erayadu si fudud uma yimaadaan, tallaabooyinku way isku dhex jiraan, bilaabidda wax kasta waxay dareemi kartaa inay adag tahay. Laakiin rabitaanka ah in la noqdo qof wax tara oo ka qayb qaata inta badan wuu sii jiraa. Diyaarin yar, qof ku jira marxaladda dhexe wuxuu weli ku raaxaysan karaa wax yaab leh, gaar ahaan hawlo la yaqaan, muusik iyo sawirro oo dhinac dhinac lagu sameeyo.

## Waa maxay waxqabadyada si fiican uga shaqeeya dementia marxaladda dhexe?

Waxqabadyo gaagaaban, la yaqaan oo gacmaha lagu sameeyo oo loo kala jaray tallaabooyin keliya ayaa ugu wanaagsan. [Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/middle-stage) waxay tidhaahdaa looma baahna inaad hal-abuurto waxyaabo cusub oo la sameeyo: waxqabadyadu waxay noqon karaan qayb ka mid ah nolosha caadiga ah, sida cashada wada karinta, beerid, muusik dhegaysi ama socod.

[Alzheimer’s Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) waxay xustaa in dadka marxaladdan ku jira inta badan ay aad ugu baahan yihiin inay dareemaan ujeedo ama inay daryeelaan wax, iyo inay u baahan yihiin caawimo bilaabidda ama sii wadista waxqabadyada. Liiskeeda marxaladda dhexe waxaa ku jira laablaabidda dharka, weel dhaqidda, sharaabaadka kala-saaridda, jimicsi fudud, qoob ka ciyaarid muusik la yaqaan, socod la hagayo, wada heesid, rinjiyayn fudud iyo fiirinta albamka sawirrada.

## Sidee u diyaariyaa waxqabad si uu u shaqeeyo?

Waxqabadyada badankood waxay ku guulaystaan ama ku fashilmaan diyaarinta. Caadooyinkan, ee laga soo qaaday [Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) iyo [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/), ayaa farqiga ugu weyn sameeya:

1. **Nadiifi meesha.** Dami TV-ga, xidh albaabka oo kaliya soo saar waxa waxqabadku u baahan yahay.
2. **Adigu ka bilow.** Bilow laablaabid ama wareejin oo ku martiqaad: “Ma i caawin kartaa tan?”
3. **Muuji, ka dibna sug.** Tus hal tallaabo. Tilmaam ama ku hagaaji gacantaada, tusaale ahaan inaad tusto meesha saxanku aado.
4. **Hal tallaabo mar kasta sii.** “Badh ka laablaab” way ka fudud tahay “laablaab kuwan oo midab midab isku dul saar.”
5. **Soo jeedi laba doorasho, ma aha su’aalo furan.** “Tuwaalada buluugga ah mise kuwa cadcad?” way ka fudud tahay in laga jawaabo “Maxaad rabtaa inaad samayso?”
6. **Qaybta adag adigu samee.** Adigu cabbir, isagu wareeji. Adigu irbadda geli, isagu tidic duub.
7. **Sabab sii.** “Maryahan waa cuntada caawa ee qoyska” waxay ka dhigtaa hawsha mid run ah.
8. **U daa inuu daawado haddii uu doorbido.** [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) waxay xustaa in daawashada keliya ay sidoo kale noqon karto mid raaxo leh.

## Fikrado waxqabad oo marxaladda dhexe ah

### Hawlo wax tara

- **Laablaab tuwaalo iyo maro yaryar.** Waxyaabaha yaryar ee fidsan ayaa ugu fudud. Baasket maro yar oo diiran waa la laablaabi karaa, la siman karaa, mar kale na la laablaabi karaa.
- **Isku mid dhig sharaabaadka.** Ka bilow dhawr lammaane oo aad u kala duwan. Kala-saarista midabka keliya sidoo kale way fiicantahay.
- **Dhaq ama qalaji weel aan jabin.** Biyo diiran oo saabuun leh gacmaha way ku macaan yihiin. Qalajinta marka la fadhiyo cagaha way ka fududtahay.
- **Miiska dhig.** Hal nooc oo shay ah mar kasta sii, oo hal meel laftaada dhig si aad u tusaale ahaato.
- **Caawi cashada.** Dhaq baradho, jeex salaad ama wareeji baaquli. Adigu maamul mindiyaha iyo kulaylka.
- **Xaaq, boodh tir ama tirtir.** Maro miiska ah, xaaqin fudud oo barandada ah.

Ka eeg [hawlaha guriga](/activities/household-tasks/) wax badan oo dheeraad ah.

### Muusik iyo dhaqdhaqaaq

- **Heesa heeso hore la jeclaa.** Adigu ka bilow heesidda; dad badan ayaa ku biira heeso ay sannado badan yaqaaneen. Ka eeg [waxqabadyada muusikada](/activities/music/).
- **Si xor ah u qoob ka ciyaar.** Ruxruxo, sacab ama dhaqaaq sida ay fiicantahay. Fadhiga way fiicantahay.
- **Tuur baaloon.** Si gaabis ah ayuu u dhaqaaqaa oo way fududtahay in kursiga laga qabto.
- **Ku socod hage.** Ku socod wareeg gaaban oo la yaqaan, gacan gacan ku haysta haddii taasi caawinayso.

### Sawirro iyo sheekooyin

- **Ku fiiri albam sawirro ah.** Ka faallee waxa aad aragto halkii aad su’aalo weydiin lahayd: “Eeg gaarigaas. Ii sheeg wax ku saabsan.” Ka eeg [waxqabadyada xusuusta](/activities/reminiscence/).
- **Isticmaal kaararka wadahadalka.** [Kaararka wadahadalka](/games/conversation-cards/) waxay sawir ku isku daraan martiqaad furan oo aan imtixaan ahayn.
- **Wada daawo filim hore.** Dooro mid la yaqaan, agtiisa joog oo dhig nasasho.

### Ciyaaro fudud oo cadaadis la’aan ah

- **Lammaanaha sawirrada iyadoo sawirradu muuqdaan.** [Lammaanaha sawirrada](/games/picture-pairs/) wuxuu u daayaa sawirrada inay muuqdaan, sidaas darteed waa ku saabsan dareenka, ma aha xusuusta.
- **Raadi kan isku mid ah.** [Raadi kan isku mid ah](/games/find-the-same/) wuxuu muujiyaa hal sawir iyo kaliya laba ilaa afar doorasho.
- **Wada kala saar.** [Wada kala saar](/games/sort-together/) waa sida dharka loo kala saaro shaashadda: taabo sawir, ka dibna baasketkiisa.
- **Bingo sawir.** [Bingo sawir](/games/picture-bingo/) wuxuu si fiican ugu shaqeeyaa kulamada qoyska iyo kooxaha.

Haddii ciyaar ay bilowdo inay keento niyadjab, jooji oo samee wax kale. Ka eeg [sida wada loo ciyaaro](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).

## Beddelidda degganaan la’aanta waxqabad

Dabeecadda aan degganayn ama isku-celcelinta ah inta badan waxay leedahay sabab. Family Caregiver Alliance waxay xustaa in qof si joogto ah armaajada u madhiya laga yaabo inuu isku dayayo inuu buuxiyo baahida ah inuu dareemo mashquul iyo wax soo saar. Alzheimer’s Association waxay soo jeedisaa in tamartaas loo jiheeyo waxqabad ku habboon, halkii la joojin lahaa.

| Waxa aad aragto | Wax aad soo jeedin karto |
| --- | --- |
| Miiska oo uu isku xoqayo ama ku ficaanayo | Maro qoyan iyo codsi ah inuu miiska tirtiro |
| Cagaha oo aan degganayn ama garaacaya | Muusik leh garaac cad oo la garaaco ama lagu sacbiyo |
| Khasnado ama armaajooyin madhiin | Baasket dhar ama maro yar oo la laablaabo oo la kala saaro |
| Isla su’aasha mar kale weydiin | Xasillooni, ka dibna heeso ama hawl la wadaago |
| Hareeraha socod | Socod wadajir ah, ama hawl ku lug leh wax qaadid |
| Degganaan la’aanta galabta dambe | Hawl deggan oo la yaqaan iftiin wanaagsan; ka eeg [degganaan la’aanta galabta](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |

## Goorta la joojiyo ama la beddelo jihada

Jooji marka aad aragto calaamadaha daalka, niyadjabka, xishoodka ama caajiska, sida dhaqdhaqaaq aan joogsan, wejiga rururin, wax iska durkid ama odhanka “ma awoodo.” Alzheimer’s Association waxay soo jeedisaa inaad mar kale isku daydo waqti kale oo maalinta ah ama waxqabadka fududayso. Ha qasbin. Diidmadu waxay ka iman kartaa cabsi ama hawsha oo aad u adag; ka eeg [markay yiraahdaan maya](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

Goor hore dhammaystirku waa fiican yahay. Sidoo kale wada fadhiisashada koob shaah ah. Marka awoodaha sii beddelmaan, fikradaha ku jira [waxqabadyada marxaladda dambe](/activities/late-stage/) ayaa laga yaabaa inay ka sii habboonaadaan, [101 waxqabad](/activities/ideas/) wuxuuna leeyahay wax badan oo dheeraad ah oo la tijaabiyo.
