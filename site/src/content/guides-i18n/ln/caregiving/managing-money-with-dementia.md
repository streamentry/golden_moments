Mikano ya mbongo etali bonsomi ya moto mpe bomoi na ye ya mokolo na mokolo. Soki mosala moko ekómi mpasi, bandá na mosala yango na esika ya kokanisa ete moto mosusu asengeli kozwa nyonso. Tuna moto lisalisi nini alingi mpe tiká ye azala na mosala.

Mokanda oyo epesi sango ya mobimba mpo na batángi ya États-Unis. Makambo oyo banki epesi mpe mibeko ekeseni selon esika. [Mokanda ya Alzheimer's Society mpo na kokamba mbongo](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) elakisi ndenge ya kosunga moto wana kobatela eteni na ye. [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) elimboli ete lisalisi ya pete, moto ya kotyela motema mpe ndingisa ya mibeko ezali makambo ekeseni.

## Ndenge nini nakoki kopesa lisalisi kozanga kozwa nyonso?

Tuna moto mosala ya mbongo nini alingaki ozala lisalisi na yango. Akoki kolinga kotala faktire elongo, kobongisa mikanda, kokanisa mokolo ya kofuta to kotala bosengi ya mbongo oyo ayebaki te. Moto akoki kolinga lisalisi mpo na misala mosusu mpe kolinga kosala misusu ye moko.

- **Tuna liboso ya kosala.** Meka koloba: « Olingi tótala faktire oyo elongo? » Pesá moto ntango ya kozwa mokano.
- **Tyá likebi na mosala moko.** Soki moto andimi, talá motuya, mokolo ya kofuta mpe ndenge ya kofuta elongo. Tiká ye azwa mikano oyo alingi kozwa.
- **Salá elongo mokanda ya pete.** Liste mokuse ya bafaktire, mikolo ya kofuta to makambo oyo boyokani ekoki kosalisa bino mibale bómona nini esili kosalema.
- **Tuna banki nini bikoki ezali.** Ekoki kozala na ndenge ya kosala kofuta to kotala misala ya kɔnti na pete. Tuna nini likoki moko na moko epesi nzela ya kosala liboso ya kobongola kɔnti.
- **Talá lisusu sima.** Makambo oyo moto alingi ekoki kobongwana. Batelá lisalisi na oyo alingi mpe azali na posa na yango sikoyo.

CFPB elobi ete lisalisi ya pete ezali kotala bafaktire mpe kobongisa mbongo elongo na moto wana, kasi moto yango azali kaka kopesa ndingisa mpo na mbongo. Yango ekoki kozala ndenge mosusu koleka kopesa moto mosusu nzela ya kobimisa mbongo to kofuta. Kokabolaka te babandɛ, ba code PIN to ba code ya libateli ya mbala moko lokola nzela ya mokuse.

## Ndingisa nini moto ya kotyela motema azali na yango?

Na États-Unis, likoki ya moto ya kotyela motema ekoki kopesa ebongiseli ya mbongo nzela ya kobenga moto moko na makambo mosusu, lokola soki ezali na mitungisi mpo na lokuta oyo ekoki kozala. CFPB elobi ete kotya nkombo ya moto ya kotyela motema epesi ye te nzela ya kokɔta na kɔnti to ndingisa ya kotinda mbongo. Likoki yango ekoki kozala te na bakɔnti nyonso, yango wana tuna banki to ebongiseli ya mbongo ndenge esalaka.

| Lolenge ya lisalisi | Nini elimboli |
|---|---|
| Kotala bafaktire elongo | Moto mpe mosalisi batalaka bafaktire elongo; moto azwaka mokano mpe apesi ndingisa ya kofuta. |
| Moto ya kotyela motema | Ebongiseli ya mbongo ekoki kobenga moto oyo atyami nkombo na makambo mosusu. Nkombo yango moko epesi te nzela ya kokɔta na kɔnti. |
| Ndingisa ya kɔnti to ya mibeko | Ndingisa ya banki, procuration to ebongiseli mosusu ekoki kopesa nzela ya kosala mbongo. Makoki mpe mikumba na yango etaleli kɔnti mpe mibeko ya esika. |

Liboso ya kobongola nzela ya kokɔta na kɔnti to ya kozala nkolo ya kɔnti, tuna banki mpe moto ya mayele ya mibeko ya esika bálimbola makambo oyo ezali mpe makama na yango. Kozala na bokɔnɔ yango moko te elobi misala nini ya mbongo moto akoki kosala to mikano nini alingi kozwa. Tiká ye azala na eteni ya mikano ndenge alingi. Soki alingi moto mosusu asala na esika na ye, tuna ndenge ya kosala yango na mibeko na esika ya kosalela kokɔta ya moto mosusu.

## Mbongwana nini nasengeli kotala?

Faktire oyo efutami te to kosomba oyo ezali ndenge mosusu ekoki kozala na bantina ndenge na ndenge. Yango ezali kolakisa te ete moto azali lisusu na makoki te ya kokamba mbongo to ete moto mosusu asali ye mabe mpo na mbongo. Tuna nini esalemaki mpe, soki moto andimi, talá elongo relevé to faktire oyo etali likambo yango.

[CFPB etángi bilembo oyo ekoki kolakisa kosalela moto na ndenge ya mabe mpo na mbongo](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), lokola mbongo to biloko oyo ezangi, kobimisa mbongo ya mbala moko oyo ezali ndenge ya sika, bafaktire oyo efutami te, bato ya sika oyo bakɔtisami na bakɔnti, to moto oyo azali kopekisa babengi mpe bakendeke ya moto. Talá ndenge makambo ezali kobongwana longwa na oyo ezalaka momesano mpo na moto oyo; komonaka te elembo moko lokola ndimbola.

Soki moto alobi ete mbongo ezangi, ndima mitungisi na ye na motema malamu. [Mokanda na biso mpo na koyanola na mitungisi](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) epesi ndenge ya kotala likambo kozanga koboya yango. Tuna moto na kimya nini amoni mpe alingi nini esalema sima. Kofundaka te ndeko, mobateli to mosali ya banki liboso ya kotala makambo malamumalamu. Soki okanisi ete mosalisi ya kotyela motema akoki kozala na kati, luka toli epai ya moto mosusu ya mayele to lisungi ya esika.

## Ndenge nini tokoki kokitisa likama ya lokuta?

Bakosi lokuta bakoki komilakisa lokola banki, biro ya leta, mombongo, moninga to ndeko. Bakoki koloba ete likama ezali mpe kotinda yo ofuta mbango. [Mokanda ya Alzheimer’s Association mpo na libateli na teknolozi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) epesi toli ya kotelama, kotala moto oyo azali kobenga mpe koluka lisalisi liboso ya kokabola sango to mbongo.

- **Telama soki nsango emonani lokola ya mbangu mingi.** Kotindaka te mbongo to kokabola sango ya kɔnti ntango moto azali kobenga to nsango ezali kotinda yo na makasi.
- **Talá na nzela ya boyokani oyo otyeli motema.** Kanga mobengi mpe benga banki na nimero oyo ezali na relevé to na karte ya sika, to salelá application na yango ya mikolo. Kosalelaka te lien to nimero oyo mobengi atindi.
- **Batela makambo ya libateli na sekele.** Kotindaka te babandɛ, makambo nyonso ya kɔnti to ba code ya bondimi na eyano ya nsango oyo oyebaki te. [FTC epesi toli ya kosukisa mobengi ya sika oyo azali kosenga code mpe kobenga banki na nimero oyo otyeli motema](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Yokaná na moto mpo na lisalisi.** Tuna nani alingaki kotala elongo bosengi ya mbongo oyo ekamwisi ye. Likoki ya moto ya kotyela motema na banki ekoki kosalisa na boyokani, kasi yango moko epesi te ndingisa ya kosala mbongo.

## Nasengeli kosala nini soki mbongo ekoki kozala etindami epai ya mokosi lokuta?

Benga banki, application ya kofuta, kompanyi ya karte to esika oyo osalelaki mpo na kotinda mbongo nokinoki. Salelá nimero oyo ezali na karte to na relevé, to application to site internet ya mikolo ya ebongiseli yango. Tuna soki bakoki kopekisa to kozongisa kofuta yango. [Matambe ya FTC mpo na kozonga sima ya lokuta](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) elimboli ete makambo oyo ekoki kosalema etaleli ndenge mbongo etindamaki; ezali na ndimisami te ete mbongo ekoki kozonga.

Bombá bansango, barisiti, mikolo mpe mayebisi ya kɔnti. Na États-Unis, okoki koyebisa lokuta epai ya [FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Soki okanisi ete moto moko azali kosalela mobange na ndenge ya mabe mpo na mbongo, [Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) elobi ete benga Adult Protective Services na etúká ya moto yango. Soki moto azali na likama ya mbangu sikoyo, benga misala ya likama ya esika. Na libándá ya États-Unis, salelá misala ya esika mpo na kobatela basaleli mpe kobatela bato.

Loba kozanga kopamela moto. Lokuta esalemaka mpo na kokosa bato. [Mokanda ya CFPB mpo na lisungi na makambo ya lokuta mpe kanyaka](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) epesi ndenge ya koyebisa makambo na États-Unis mpe epesi toli ya kosalela basite mpe banimero oyo otyeli motema.

## Wapi nakoki kozwa lisalisi mpo na mokano ya mbongo ya sikisiki?

Tuna banki ya moto bisaleli mpe ndingisa nini ezali. Mpo na mokanda ya mibeko to mbongwana ya kozala nkolo ya kɔnti, solola na moto ya mayele oyo ayebi mibeko ya esika moto afandi. Soki ozali na mitungisi mpo na kosalela moto na ndenge ya mabe mpo na mbongo, salelá lisungi ya esika mpo na kobatela bato. Lokasa oyo ekoki te kopona ndingisa nini moto asengeli kozwa to koloba soki mbongo moko ezali lokuta.

[Mokanda mpo na matambe ya liboso sima ya koyeba bokɔnɔ](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) ekoki kosalisa moto mpe libota bápona likambo moko ya kobongisa. [Mokanda mpo na koyokana te na libota](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) epesi ndenge ya kolobela likambo moko na mbala moko.
