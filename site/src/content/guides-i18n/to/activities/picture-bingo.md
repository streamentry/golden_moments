‘Oku ta‘alo ‘a e pingo ‘e he tokolahi ‘o e kakai motu‘a ‘i he fakafe‘iloaki, ka ‘oku lahi ‘a e ngaahi nomba ‘oku faingata‘a ki he tangata ‘oku mahaki ‘i he dementia. Ko e pingo fakatātā ‘oku tauhi ‘a e laʻi mālie tatau, ‘o fakaliliu ‘a e ngaahi nomba ki he ngaahi fakatātā lalahi mo mahino. ‘E lava ke fai ia ‘i he fale, ‘i he potu ‘o e ‘aho, pe ‘i he fale tokanga. Ko e tohi‘ata ni ‘oku fakamatala ‘a e founga ke ta‘alo, ‘a e founga ke liliu ai, pea mo e founga ke tauhi ‘a e mahu‘inga ‘i he fiefia.

## Founga ‘o e pingo fakatātā

‘Oku ‘i ai ha kāate ‘a e taha kotoa ‘oku ‘i ai ha ‘ulu‘ulu ‘o e ngaahi fakatātā, hangē ko e ipu teaa, ha pusi, ha tōtō, ha vaka ‘i he tahi, pe ha pasikala. Ko e tokotaha ui ‘oku fakahā pe ui ‘a e fakatātā ‘e taha ‘i he taimi. ‘Oku tohi ‘e he kakai ‘a e fakatātā tatau ‘i honau kāate. Ko e tangata ‘oku ne tōʻaki ha laine kakato, pe ‘a e fakatātā ‘oku fakafepaki‘aki ‘e he kulupu, ‘e lava ke ne ui ‘a e “Pingo”, ka ‘oku taʻofi ‘e he ni‘ihi ‘a e toʻotoʻa kotoa.

‘E lava ke ke ngāue ‘aki ‘a e kāate pepa mei he [ngaahi ngaue ta‘etotoʻu](/activities/printable/), pe ko e [ta‘alo pingo fakatātā](/games/picture-bingo/), ‘oku kau ai ‘a e tokotaha ui ‘i he skrini ki he kulupu. ‘Oku lelei ‘a e ngaahi founga ‘e ua ‘i ha tapalēti ‘oku hā ki he kulupu kotoa.

## Teuteu ki ha fakataha

- **Filifili ha kulupu si‘isi‘i.** Ko e fā ki he valu ‘oku lelei ‘a e lahi. Ko e kulupu lalahi ange ‘oku faingata‘a ke muimui ki ai pea ‘e ongo ‘oku vave.
- **Ngāue ‘aki ‘a e fakatātā lalahi.** Ngāue ‘aki ‘a e laine mālohi mo ha me‘a ‘oku ‘ilo‘i ‘i he potukiʻi ‘e taha. ‘Oua na‘a ‘ai ‘a e ngaahi kāate ‘oku kaunga.
- **Tuku ‘a e kakai ‘i ha feitu‘u lelei.** Tuku ‘a e taha kotoa ‘i ha feitu‘u ‘oku lava ke ne sio ki he tangata ‘oku ui mo ho‘ana kāate, pea ofi ki ha tokoni kapau ‘oku fie ma‘u ia.
- **Ngāue ‘aki ‘a e ngaahi fakatātā mālie.** Ko e ngaahi tuʻa lalahi, ngaahi paʻanga, pe ha paʻi nima ‘oku lelei ange ‘i he peni. Tauhi ‘a e ngaahi me‘a fakamui ‘e ni‘ihi.
- **Teuteu ha fakataha nounou.** Ko e taimi ‘e tolu ‘i he hongofulu miniti ‘oku lahi. Taʻofi vave kapau ‘oku mavahe ‘a e mālohi.

‘Oku fakaafe ‘e he [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ke fakalelei ‘a e ngaahi ngaue ki he me‘a ‘oku lava ‘e he tangata ke ne fai, koe‘uhi ke fakasi‘isi‘i ‘a e kāate mo e ngaahi tafa‘aki ‘i he taimi ‘oku fie ai.

## Founga ke ui ai ‘a e ngaahi fakatātā

1. **Fakahā ‘a e fakatātā** pe tō ki ai ‘i he skrini ‘i mu‘a ke ke lea.
2. **Lea hingoa ia ‘i he lea mālie,** ‘i he ngaahi lea faingofua: “Ipu teaa.” Toe lea ‘ai ‘e taha, mālie.
3. **Tatali.** Foaki ha taimi ki he kakai ke nau sio mo tōʻaki. ‘Oua na‘a ui ‘a e fakatātā hoko mai ka‘ai ‘oku ‘osi ha taimi ki he tokolahi.
4. **Fakamatala, kapau ‘oku tokoni.** “‘A e tōtō ‘uli‘uli” pe “‘a e me‘a naʻa tau ‘i ai ‘i he ‘efiafi‘ā” ‘e lava ke fakaʻaʻi ai ‘a e talanoa.
5. **Tauhi ‘a e vave ‘oku mālie.** Ko e tokotaha ui ‘oku mālie ‘oku mahu‘inga ange ‘i he vave.

## Fakafiemālie ke fili ‘a e tangata

‘Oku lava ke ‘ai ‘e he tauhi‘aki ha palopalema. Ko e tangata ‘oku ‘ikai ke ne ikuna ‘e lava ke ne ongo‘i ‘oku tōmaki‘i, pea ko e tangata ‘oku mamahi ki he ikuna ‘e lava ke ne tu‘u mālohi. Ko e ngaahi filifili ni:

- **Ko e kotoa ‘oku ikuna ‘i ha fakatātā.** Ko e taha kotoa ‘oku ui ‘a e fakatātā ‘e taha ‘oku ne ‘ofa ai, pea ‘oku fakafiefia ‘e he kulupu ia.
- **Vahevahe ha kāate kakato.** Kole ke nau lea ‘a e me‘a ki he fakatātā ‘e taha ‘i honau kāate.
- **Ta‘alo ‘o a‘u ki he taimi ‘oku mavahe ai ‘a e fakatātā.** Ui ‘a e fakatātā kotoa ‘i he kāate, pea kamata ha kāate kehe, ta‘e‘ai ha tu‘unga ki he ikuna ‘uluaki.
- **Tauhi ‘a e ‘ōfa‘i nounou pe ta‘e‘ai.** Ko ha kuki pe ha hiva ‘oku lelei ange ‘i he ko e tōfoi.

## Fakalelei ki he kakai ‘i he sitepu ki mui

Ki he kakai ‘oku faingata‘a ke ‘ilo‘i ha fakatātā, ngāue ‘aki ha kāate ‘oku ‘i ai ‘a e ngaahi potukiʻi ‘e fā pe ono, pe foaki ha kāate ‘e taha ‘i he taimi. Tuku ha tokoni ke ne tuku ‘a e kāate ‘i he taimi ‘oku ne tōʻaki ai ‘a e tangata, pe liliu mālie ‘a e tōʻaki ‘i he tuʻunga ‘a e tangata. ‘Oku ni‘ihi ‘oku faingofua ange ke nau fanongo pe ui ‘a e tangata ‘oku ui, pea fiefia ‘i he le‘o ‘o e lea.

‘Oku ‘ai ke ke sio ki he fakafeʻātungia‘i, hangē ko e liliu ‘a e ‘ulu pe ‘ikai ke fiefia, pea taʻofi kapau ‘oku hā ‘a e ‘uhinga ko ia. Ki he ngaahi fakakaukau kehe ki he ngaahi ngaue mālie ‘oku nofo ai, vakai ki he [ngaahi ngaue ‘i he sitepu ki mui](/activities/late-stage/).

## Pingo ‘i he fale tokanga mo e ngaahi polokalama ‘aho

‘Oku lelei taha ‘a e ngaahi ngaue kulupu kapau ‘oku fakapalani ki he kakai ‘oku ‘i he loki. Ko e [tohi‘ata ki he ngaahi ngaue kulupu ki he fale tokanga mo e ngaahi polokalama ‘aho](/activities/care-home-groups/) ‘oku kau ai ‘a e founga ke palani ai ha fakataha, tuku ‘a e kakai ‘i honau ngaahi feitu‘u, pea mo e founga ke tokanga ai ki he kau ‘oku fie ‘alu. Ko e pingo fakatātā ‘oku ‘ikai tatau ‘i ai ‘a e hiva pe ‘a e talatalanoa, pea mo e [finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) ko ha ta‘alo mālie ange ‘i hono fakaʻosi ‘o e fakataha.

## Ko e hā ‘oku lava ‘e he pingo, pea ko e hā ‘oku ta‘elava

‘Oku fakafeohi‘aki mo fiefia ‘a e pingo fakatātā. ‘E lava ke ne fakataha ‘a e kakai, foaki ha ‘uhinga ke sio mo fanongo ki he ngaahi fakatātā, pea ke ‘ai ke mafana ‘a e kulupu. ‘Oku ‘ikai ke ne mo‘ui‘ia pe taʻofi ‘a e dementia. Fakafuofua ‘a e ola ‘aki ‘a e founga ‘oku nau sio ai pea ongo‘i ai ‘i he houa, ‘ikai ko e ‘ko e hā ‘oku ikuna ai.
