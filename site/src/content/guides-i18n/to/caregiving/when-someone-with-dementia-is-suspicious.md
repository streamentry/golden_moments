Ko e tuhu ʻoku ke kaihaʻa ʻa e pa'osi, ʻave ʻa e fakamaʻu, pe liliu ʻa e fakamatala ʻo e paʻanga ʻe lava ke mamahi. ʻE lava ke ʻave ʻe he taha ʻa e meʻa, ʻo ngalo ʻene fakahoko, pe ngaʻunu ʻa e ngaahi me'a kuo hoko. Ka ʻikai ʻe lava ke fakapapau'i ʻe he dementia ʻoku loi ʻa e tuhu. Kamata ʻaki ʻa e fanongo mo e vakai ki he mo'oni.

Ko e [tohi ʻa e Alzheimer's Association ki he manavahē mo e fakakaukau hala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/suspicions-delusions) ʻoku fakahinohino ke tuʻu mālie, lea ʻaki ha fakamatala nounou, pea ʻoua ʻe fekaunaʻi. Ko e [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) ʻoku nau fakahinohino foki ke vakai ki he tokanga kae ʻoua ʻe tukuaki ia koeʻuhi ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e dementia ʻa e taha. ʻOku ʻikai ko e fakahinohino ki he fakatonutonu pe ʻoku mo'oni ʻa e tuhu makehe; ko e fakakaukau ʻeni ki he fetuʻutaki lahi. Ko e fakamatala ki he sio pe fanongo ʻo e me'a ʻoku ʻikai ʻi ai ʻa e kakai kehe, vakai ki he [fakahinohino ki he fakaʻuluaki mo e kiʻi loi ʻo e dementia](/caregiving/hallucinations-with-dementia/).

## Ko e hā te u lea ʻaki kapau ʻoku manavahē ʻa e taha?

Tali ki he tokanga ʻoʻikai ke fakafeʻiloaki ʻaki ha me'a ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa ʻe koe ko e mo'oni. Ko ha lea nounou pea mālie ʻe lava ke tokoni ke kei fe'iloaki pe ko e talanoa. Hangē ko e lea: "ʻOku ke manavahē ʻoku ʻalu ho pulú. Ta tau sivi fakataha." Hili ia, tuku ha vahaʻa, pea vakai ki he founga ʻoku tali ai ʻa e taha.

- **Fanongo ʻi mu'a ʻi he fakamatala.** Fehuʻi ki he me'a ʻoku ʻalu ai ʻa e meʻa, pe ko e hā ʻoku manavahē ai ʻa e taha. ʻE lava ke ne ongoʻi ʻaʻa ʻi he tokanga ko e me'a pē ʻoku ʻalu.
- **Tauhi ho tali ke nounou.** Ko e fakamatala lōloa ʻe lava ke fakahoko ai ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala ʻoku faingataʻa ke muimui ki ai, pea ko ia ʻe lava ke ne ʻai koe mo e taha ke mamahi ange.
- **ʻOua ʻe fekaunaʻi pe fehuʻi.** ʻOua ʻe fiemaʻu ke manatu ʻa e taha ki he feituʻu ʻo e me'a, pe ke fakamaʻu ʻoku ʻikai ʻaʻau ʻa e kaihaʻa.
- **Foaki ʻa e tokoni fakaʻofa.** Kapau ʻoku loto ʻa e taha, vakai fakataha ki he feituʻu ʻoku faʻa ngaue ai ʻa e ngaahi me'a. Pe fehuʻi kapau ʻoku loto ia ke ʻi ai ha taha kehe ke tokoni.
- **Taʻofi kapau ʻoku tupu ʻa e mamahi.** Foaki ha vahaʻa ki he taha pea toe foki ki he tokanga ʻi ha taimi kehe kapau ʻoku malu ke fai ia.

ʻE lava ke mamahi ʻa e tuhu ʻo fakahoko ʻaki ha fakaʻuli. Feinga ke vahevahe ʻa e manavahē ʻo e taha mei he me'a ʻoku ʻiloa ʻo e me'a kuo hoko. ʻOku ʻikai ke fiemaʻu ke ke ʻave ʻa e fatongia ʻo e ngaahi me'a kotoa; pea ʻoku ʻikai ke fiemaʻu ke ke fakamaʻu ʻa e ngaahi fakalaulauloto kotoa ʻi he taimi ko iá.

## ʻOku lava nai ke hoko ʻa e tokanga ʻi he meʻa ʻoku ʻave moʻoni?

ʻIo. Kamata ʻaki ʻa e fakamatala ʻoku ʻave moʻoni ʻa e me'a pe ʻikai. ʻE lava ke ne ʻai ʻa e meʻa ʻi ha feituʻu ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa, ʻe lava ke liliu ia ʻe ha taha kehe, pe ʻe lava ke ʻave ʻa e meʻa. ʻOua ʻe fakaʻaʻau ha lipooti ʻo e kaihaʻa, ʻuli, pe kovi koeʻuhi ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e dementia ʻa e taha.

Ki ha meʻa kuo ngalo, vakai fakataha kapau ʻoku loto ʻa e taha. Vakai ki he ngaahi feituʻu ʻoku faʻa tauhi ai ia, pea toutou ʻeke ʻa e ngaue ʻo e ʻaho. Kapau ʻoku mahuʻinga pe ʻoku ʻikai ke kumi ʻa e meʻa, fakahā ki he taha ʻa e me'a ʻoku ke ʻiloa, pea fakakau ha tokotaha falala pe kāinga kehe.

Kapau ʻoku fakahoko ʻa e paʻanga pe ngaue ʻo e fale ʻaki ʻa e fāmili, tauhi ha ngaahi tohi mahino ʻoku tali ʻe he taha. Tauhi ʻa e fakamatala ʻo e teleaki pe tohi ki he ngaahi fefakatau'aki kuo fakafeangai, pea fakamatala ia ʻi he lea faingofua. Fehuʻi ki ha tokotaha ngaue ʻoku ʻiloa ʻi he lao ʻo e feituʻu ki he ngaahi tuʻutuʻuni ʻo e paʻanga pe fatongia lao; ʻe ʻikai lava ke fakahoko ʻe he fakahinohino ni ʻa e ngaahi me'a ko iá.

## ʻE fēfē hono tokoni ʻa e ngaahi founga faʻa ʻiloa ki he meʻa kuo ngalo?

Foaki ha feituʻu faʻa ʻiloa ki he ngaahi me'a ʻoku faʻa ngalo. Ko ha tuʻi ki he kī, ha tray ki he kato, pe ha loki ʻoku ʻiloa ʻe lava ke tokoni ke faingofua ange ke kumi ʻa e me'a ʻi ha taimi kehe. Ko e [Alzheimer's Society ʻoku fakahinohino ke fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e feituʻu faʻa tatau](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) ki he ngaahi me'a hangē ko e kī pe sipesipa-mata. Fehuʻi ki he taha pe ko e feituʻu fē ʻoku mahuʻinga kiate ia.

Feinga ke liliu ha me'a ʻe taha ʻi he taimi. ʻOku ʻikai ko e founga lelei ke liliu ʻa e ngaahi koloa ʻo ʻikai ha fakamatala, koeʻuhi ʻe lava ke ne ʻai ʻa e taha ke ʻikai ke ne ʻiloa ʻa e feituʻu ʻo e ngaahi me'a. Kapau ʻoku faʻa ngalo ʻa ha me'a ʻoku ʻikai mahuʻinga pau, ko ha tokoni fo'ou ʻe lava ke tokoni ʻi he ngaahi fale, ka ʻe ʻikai ke ne supu ʻa e vakai pe ʻoku kaihaʻa pe ʻoku ngalo ʻa e me'a tonu.

Tokanga ke ʻi he founga lelei ange ʻa e vakai mo e fanongo ʻa e taha. Ko e Dementia Australia ʻoku nau fakamatala ʻe lava ke tupu ʻa e liliu ʻi he sio pe fanongo, mahaki, vaitaʻa, liliu ʻi he founga ngaue, pe feituʻu ʻoku mafatukituki ke tokoni ki he liliu ʻi he ongoʻi. Fehuʻi ki he taha kapau ʻoku lahi ʻaupito ʻa e leʻo ʻi he feituʻu, pea vakai kapau ʻoku ʻi ai hono sipesipa-mata pe ʻu fanongo kapau ʻoku ne ngaue ʻaki.

## Ko e hā ʻa e taimi ke kole ai ʻa e tokoni mo'ui pe fakaʻatamai?

Fakahā ki he tokotaha mo'ui ʻa e taha kapau ko e manavahē ko ha me'a foʻou, ʻoku toe hoko ʻo lahi ange, pe ʻoku ʻomi ʻaki ha mamahi. Tohi ʻa e taimi ʻoku hoko ai, ʻa e me'a ʻoku hoko ʻi he taimi ko iá, pea mo e me'a ʻoku liliu ʻi he mo'ui, feituʻu, mohe, mo e vaitaʻa. ʻOua ʻe taʻofi pe liliu ʻa e vaitaʻa ʻi he fakahinohino ʻo ha ʻu tohi ʻoku ʻikai ko e tokotaha mo'ui.

Ko e [fakahinohino ʻa e NHS ki he fakalongolongo vave](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) ʻoku pehē ke kumi ki he tokoni mo'ui ʻi he taimi ko iá. Ko e liliu vave ʻi he founga pe mo'ui ʻoku fiemaʻu ʻa e tokoni fakavavevave. Ko e tokoni fakavavevave ʻo e feituʻu ʻoku nofo ai ʻa e taha ʻe lava ke ke fakaʻaongaʻi.

Kapau ko e tuhu ʻe lava ke kau ai ʻa e kaihaʻa, ʻuli, pe kovi, fakapapau'i ʻoku malu ʻa e taha pea kumi ki he fakakaukau mei ha tokotaha falala ʻoku ʻi he feituʻu pe ko ha kautaha tokanga ki he kakai ʻoku ngaue ʻi he malu. Kapau ʻoku ʻi he tuʻutāmaki fakavavevave ha taha, fetuʻutaki ki he tokoni fakavavevave ʻo e feituʻu. ʻOua ʻe fakahoko ʻa e ngaue fakatokanga mamafa ʻe koe pē kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha tuʻutāmaki ki hoʻo malu pe ʻo e taha.

## ʻE fēfē hono tokanga ki ho kakai?

Ko e tuhu toutou ʻe lava ke ʻai ke mamahi ʻa e fetuʻutaki. Talanoa mo ha taha ʻoku ke falala ki ai pe ko ha tokotaha fakalotolahi, pea kole ha tokoni ki he ngaahi ngaue tokanga kapau ʻoku ke ongoʻi ʻoku mafatukituki koe. Ko e [fakahinohino ki he fetuʻutaki fakamatakali](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) ʻoku fakahinohino ki he founga ke vahevahe ai ʻa e fatongia, pea ko e [fakahinohino ki he tokanga ki hoʻo kakai](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ʻoku kau ai ʻa e fakalaulauloto mo e tokoni.

Tuku ho tokanga ki he me'a ʻe lava ke vakai ki ai, ʻa e ongoʻi ʻo e taha, mo e tokoni ʻoku fiemaʻu. ʻOku ʻikai ke fiemaʻu ke ke fakamoʻoni ho tokanga ʻi he fetuʻutaki kotoa. Kapau ʻoku toe hoko ʻa e tokanga, kole ki he kau tokanga ke tokoni ke ʻilo ha founga ʻoku tali ai ʻa e taha pea malu ai ʻa e kakai kotoa ʻoku ʻi ai.
