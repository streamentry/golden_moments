ʻOku lava pe ke hoko ʻa e fononga ko ha filifili ʻi he taimi ʻoku loto ai ʻa e taha ke ō pea ʻoku fe'unga ʻa e palani mo ʻene ngaahi fiema'u. Ko ha fakafiefia nounou ki he fāmili ʻe faingofua ange ʻi ha malaʻe ʻi ha ʻaho kotoa, pea ko ha ʻaho fononga ʻe lelei ange ʻi ha ʻahiahi lahi. Fehuʻi ki he taha ʻoku ʻi he fakakaukau ʻo ʻene fiema'u mei he fononga, pea toki langa ha palani ʻoku ʻi ai ha feituʻu ke taʻofi pe liliu ai.

Ko e [tohinoa ʻa e Alzheimer's Association ki he fononga](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) mo e [fakahinohino ʻa Dementia Australia ki he fononga](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) ʻoku nau fakamamafa ʻa e palani ʻoku tokanga ki he loto-lelei mo e ngaahi founga ʻa e taha. ʻOku ʻikai ko ha fakahinohino ki he mo'ui ʻoku palani ʻaki ha faka'ilonga ʻo e fononga ʻoku haohaoa ki ha taha makehe; ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi fakakaukau lahi ʻoku ʻaonga.

## ʻE fēfē hono fakakaukau ʻi ha fononga ʻoku totonu pe ʻikai?

Talanoa ki he fononga mo e taha, ʻaki ha ngaahi lea ʻoku ʻiloa pea mo ha fakamatala nounou ʻo e feituʻu ʻoku mou ō ki ai mo ʻe ʻi ai ʻa e ʻe nofo mo kimoutolu. Kapau ʻoku ʻikai ke loto ʻa e taha ke ō, tali ia. Ko e ʻu me'a ʻe lava ke fai ʻi he fale, hangē ko ha kai, ha telefoni pe ha me'a ʻoku ʻiloa, ʻe lava pe ko ia.

Fakakaukau ki he me'a ʻoku lava ke faʻa fakahoko ʻe he taha ʻi he taimi ni. Fakakaukau ki he founga ʻene tali ki he ngaahi liliu ʻo e founga, longoʻa, kakai lahi, mamaʻo ʻo e fe'alu'aki, mo e loki ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa. Kapau ʻe faingata'a ʻa e ngaahi konga lahi ʻo e fononga, fakafaingofua ia pe filifili ha founga kehe ke fakaʻaʻau ai ha taimi fakataha. Ko e Dementia Australia ʻoku nau fakaʻaongaʻi ke fehuʻi ki he tokotaha mo'ui kapau ʻoku ʻikai ke ke ʻiloa pe ʻoku fe'unga ʻa e fononga mo e fiema'u ʻo e mo'ui mo e tokoni.

Kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha ongoʻi mo'ui fo'ou, ʻoku fiema'u tokoni ʻi he vaitaʻa, pe ʻoku liliu ʻene ngaue ʻa e sino ʻi he fononga, fehuʻi ki he tokotaha mo'ui ʻoku fe'unga ki he meʻa ni ki mu'a pe ʻoku tuku ai ha tu'utu'uni. ʻOua ʻe taʻofi, liliu, pe tuku ke fakahoko ʻa e vaitaʻa kuo tuʻutuʻuni ʻo ʻikai ha lea mei he taha ʻoku ne tuku ia pe he pharmacist.

## ko e hā ʻa e ʻano ʻo e fononga ʻoku totonu ke ta palani?

- **Filifili ha feituʻu ʻoku ʻiloa ʻi he taimi ʻoku lava ai.** Ko ha feituʻu ʻoku ʻiloa ʻe taha, pe ko ha fakatokanga mo e kakai ʻoku ʻiloa, ʻe lava ke ʻi ai ha liliu siʻisiʻi ange ʻi ha hala fo'ou mo ha palani lahi ʻo e malaʻe.
- **Tauhi ʻa e hala ʻoku faingofua.** Filifili ha ʻu fakalilifu ʻoku siʻisiʻi ange ʻa e ʻalu, kaekehe ko e ngaahi tafa'aki ʻoku tuʻu ʻi he fakatapui ʻe lelei ange. Vakai ʻi mu'a ki he ʻu falemahaki, nofoʻanga, mo e potu filo ʻoku ʻiloa ʻe lava ke tuʻu ai.
- **Fononga ʻi he taimi lelei ʻo e ʻaho.** Palani ʻaki ʻa e nusipu ʻo e taha ʻi he ʻaho. Tuku ha taimi ki he kai, vaitaʻa, mālōlō, mo e teuteu.
- **Tauhi ʻa e palani kiʻi nounou.** Ko ha ngāue lahi pe ʻe fe'unga. ʻOua ʻe tohi ʻa e taimi kotoa; tuku ha vahaʻa ki he taimi tuʻutuʻuni ʻoku tōkiʻi pe foki vave.
- **Fehuʻi ki he tokoni ʻi mu'a ke ʻalu.** ʻOku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi tokoni mo e tuʻutuʻuni kehekehe ʻi he kautaha vakapuna, kautaha tuʻu, falenoho, mo e feituʻu fakatahatahá. Fetuʻutaki kinautolu ʻi he taimi kimuʻa ke fehuʻi ki he tokoni ki he sino, nofoʻanga, falemahaki ʻoku ʻiloa, mo e tokoni ke ʻilo ai ʻa e feituʻu totonu.

Ko e [fakahinohino ki he ngaahi fakaʻosi fakaʻaho ʻoku fakalelei](/activities/holidays/) ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi fakakaukau ki he ngaahi asi mo e fakatahatahá. Kapau ʻoku ke feʻiloaki mo e ngaahi kāinga, ko e [fakahinohino ki he asi](/activities/visiting/) ʻoku kapau ʻa e lea fakataha, asi nounou, mo e founga ke ʻalu ai ʻo ʻikai ha fakamamafa.

## Ko e hā ʻa e me'a ke ʻave?

Tauhi ha kato iiki ʻo e ngaahi me'a mahu'inga ke nofo mo ha kaungāʻapi ʻoku falala ki ai, ʻo ʻikai ʻave ia ʻi he kato ʻoku tuku ki he ʻave ʻi he vaka.

- **Vaitaʻa mo e fakamatala ki he mo'ui.** Ave ʻa e vaitaʻa ʻoku faʻa ngaue ʻi he ʻave ʻa e taha ʻi hono tāpuaki kuo ʻi ai, pea mo e fakamatala ʻoku ʻi ai ʻi he taimi ni. Vakai ki he lao ʻo e fononga mo e feituʻu ʻe ō ki ai ʻi mu'a ke ʻave ʻa e vaitaʻa tuʻutuʻuni. Tauhi ʻa e taimi mo e tokanga ʻoku tuʻutuʻuni.
- **Fakamatala fakamatala mo e fetuʻutaki.** Ave ʻa e faka'ilonga ʻo e taha, feituʻu ʻo e nofo, fetuʻutaki ki he fakatokanga, mo ha tohi ʻo e palani fononga. Kapau ʻoku ʻaonga pea kuo fakafeangai ai, ʻe lava ke ʻi ai ha kāti ʻo e tokanga ʻoku ʻalu mo e taha ke tokoni.
- **Me'a ʻo e fiemalie.** Ave ʻa e nifo, faʻahinga telefoni, pe lama'i ngaahi meʻa, ʻi he taimi ʻo e lōngoa, kai ʻoku ʻiloa, vai, tisu, kofu ʻoku mafana, mo ha kofu fo'ou. Ko ha me'a ʻoku ʻiloa pe ko ha ata ʻe lava ke tokoni ke faingofua ange ai ha feituʻu ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa.
- **Kopi ʻo e fakamatala mahu'inga.** Tauhi ʻa e kopi ʻo e tikite, fakatonutonu, ʻinisiua, mo e fakamatala mo'ui ʻoku mahu'inga, ʻi he tohi pe ʻi he kompiuta. Fakahoko ʻa e palani ki ha taha ʻoku falala ʻoku ʻikai ke fononga mo koe.

Ko e lahi ʻo e fakamatala ke ʻave ʻoku fakatatau ki he fononga. Tauhi ʻa e fakamatala taʻeʻiloa ʻi he malu, pea fakahoko ia pē ki he kakai ʻoku fiema'u ia ke tokoni.

## ʻE fēfē hono faingofua ʻa e ʻaho fononga?

Fakahā ki he taha ʻa e meʻa ʻe hoko hokó, ʻi he laumālie ʻe taha. Tuku ha taimi lahi ki hono teuteu, ʻalu ki he laine, ʻalu ki he falemahaki, mo ʻalu mei he feituʻu ki he feituʻu. Kapau ʻoku liliu ʻa e palani, fakamatala nounou ʻa e ʻaho hoko hokó ʻi ha lea faingofua ʻi hono fakatoloi ʻa e palani kotoa.

Tauhi ʻa e ngāue ʻi he feituʻu fakatahatahá pe ʻoku faingata'a. Kapau ʻoku mālōlō pe ʻoku mamahi ʻa e taha, ʻalu ki ha feituʻu ʻoku filo, foaki ha vai pe ha ngāue ʻoku ʻiloa, pea fakakaukau fakataha pe ʻe kei ʻalu. ʻOua ʻe tuku ʻa e taha pe ʻi ha feituʻu ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa ʻo ʻikai ke ne ʻilo ʻa e ʻalu ʻa e ngaahi me'a.

Kapau ʻoku mou fononga ʻaki ʻa e vakapuna pe ko ha feituʻu lahi, fehuʻi ki he kautaha ʻa e tokoni ʻoku lava pea mo e founga ke kole ai. Ko e ngaahi founga ʻoku liliu ʻi he ngaahi ʻaho, ko ia fakamaau'i ʻa e fakamatala ʻi he kautaha, feituʻu, pe asi ʻi mu'a ke ʻalu. ʻOua ʻe falala ki ha tohi tuʻu kuo ʻosi maʻu ki he lao ʻo e malu pe ʻo e tokoni hono ʻaʻahi.

## Ko e hā ha palani fakamuimui ʻoku ʻaonga?

Fakakaukau ʻi mu'a ʻi he me'a te ke fai kapau ʻe loto ʻa e taha ke ʻalu, ʻoku fakatoloi ʻa e fononga, pe ʻoku mālōlō ha taha. Filifili ha tokotaha fetuʻutaki ʻe lava ke tokoni ʻi he telefoni. Tauhi ʻa e ʻadresi ʻo e nofoʻanga mo e hala foki ʻoku faingofua ke ʻilo, pea ʻilo ʻa e feituʻu ʻoku lava ai ke nofo mālié pe ʻalu ki he falemahaki.

Kapau ʻoku totonu ke ke fakanounouʻi ʻa e fononga, fai ia. ʻOku ʻikai ko ha fakatōmala ʻa e tuku ha sai ʻoku ʻikai ʻalu pe foki vave. Kapau ʻoku faingata'a ʻa e fononga, filifili ha ngāue ʻoku mālōlō ange ʻi he taimi ʻoku ke foki ai ki ʻapi. Ko e [fakahinohino ki he fetuʻutaki](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi fakakaukau ki he lea mālie mo ʻoange ha fakamatala ʻe taha ʻi he taimi kotoa.

## Totonu ke tau fakahā ki he hotele pe ki he kau tokoni fononga?

Kapau ʻoku tali ʻe he taha, fakahā ki he kau ngāue pe ʻoku nau fiema'u ke ʻilo ke nau tokoni lelei. Hangē ko ʻeni, ʻe lava ke ke kole ke tohi ʻe he hotele ʻa e fiema'u ki he sino, pe kole ha loki ʻoku faingofua ke ʻalu ki ai. Kapau ʻoku ʻikai loto ʻa e taha ke fakahā ʻa e fakamatala ʻo ʻene mo'ui, fakakaukau ki ha founga kehe ke kole ai ʻa e tokoni ʻoku tauhi ai ʻa e malu ʻo ʻene fakamatala.

Kapau ko ha fononga lahi ange, tauhi ʻa e kai, mohe, mo e taimi vaitaʻa ʻa e taha ʻi he founga ʻoku ofi ki he founga ʻoku ʻiloa ʻi he palani. Palani ha ʻaho ʻuluaki ʻoku ʻikai vave, pea tuku ha taimi ke mālōlō ʻi hono foki mai. Ko e ngaahi filifili ʻoku ʻiloa pe ʻoku iiki ʻe lava ke tokoni ke faingofua ange ʻa e fakatatau.
