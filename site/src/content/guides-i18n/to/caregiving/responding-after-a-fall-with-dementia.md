ʻE lava ke manavahē ʻaupito ʻa e hinga ki he tokotaha mo e niʻihi ʻoku ʻi ai. Malolo mo fakatokanga ki he malu ʻaki ʻaʻaku. ʻOua ʻe fakaʻuhinga ʻe ʻikai ke fasi ʻa e tokotaha koeʻuhi ʻoku ʻikai lava ke ne fakamatala ʻa e meʻa kuo hoko pe ko e feituʻu ʻoku mamahi ai.

## Ko e hā ʻa e meʻa ke fai muʻa ʻi he hinga?

Sivi pe ʻoku ʻi he loto-ʻaʻana ʻa e tokotaha, pe ʻoku ne fasi pe ʻoku ʻikai lelei, pea mo e lava ke ne tali hangē ko e taimi kotoa. ʻOua ʻe vave ke tuʻu hake ia pe toʻo ia ʻaʻaku. [ʻOku pehē ʻe he NHS ʻoku fiema'u ʻa e tokoni fakavave kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha manavahē ʻo e fasi ʻo e ʻulu, tua, faʻa pe kiʻi ʻulu-kuʻu, pe ha fasi lahi kehe, pe kapau ʻoku ʻikai lava ke tuʻu ʻa e tokotaha](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/).

- **Fetuʻutaki ki he tokoni fakavave** kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha ʻaʻaʻa ʻo e ngaahi fakaʻilo mamafa ko iá. Muimui ʻi he ngaahi fakatotonu ʻa e kau tokoni.
- **Tauhi ʻa e tokotaha ʻi he fiemālie mo mafana** ʻi he taimi ʻoku ke kumi ai ʻa e tokoni. ʻOua ʻe toʻo ia kapau ʻoku ke manavahē ʻa e fasi lahi pe ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa ʻoku malu.
- **Tuʻu ofi kapau ʻoku lava.** Fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e lea nounou mo mahino. ʻOua ʻe fehuʻi ki he meʻa kuo hoko pe fakamālohi ʻa e tokotaha ke ne tuʻu.
- **Kapau ʻoku mamahi, fasi pe ʻoku ʻikai lelei ka ʻoku ʻikai ha ngaahi meʻa ʻoku ʻi he taimi ni,** fetuʻutaki ki he tokoni moʻui ʻo e feituʻu pe ki he kau faʻu moʻui ʻa e tokotaha ke nau tataki.

ʻOua ʻe feinga ke toʻo pe ʻave ʻa e tokotaha ʻaʻaku pē. Kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha palani-tokanga ki he hinga, muimui ki ai pea kapau ʻoku fe'unga mo e meʻa ʻoku hoko ʻi he taimi ni; kumi ʻa e tokoni fakavave kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha manavahē ʻo e fasi lahi.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga kapau ʻoku hangē ʻoku lava ke tuʻu ʻa e tokotaha?

ʻOua ʻe vave ki he tokotaha. Fehuʻi pe ko e hā ʻene haʻisia pe ʻoku fie tokoni. Kapau ʻoku hangē ʻoku ʻikai ke ne mamahi pea ʻoku lava ke tuʻu ʻaki ʻene tokoni ʻaʻane ʻikai ko e toʻo-hake, foaki ha taimi pea nofo ofi. Kapau ʻoku mamahi pe ʻoku ʻikai lava ke ne fai, ʻoua ʻe feinga ke tuʻu hake. Kapau ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa ʻa e ngāue, fehuʻi ki ha kau faʻu moʻui ʻi he ʻikai ke ke feinga ha liliu ʻaki ha founga ʻoku ʻikai ke ke ʻiloa.

ʻOku fakahā ʻe he [NHS ʻoku fiema'u ke malolo mo mohu ʻi hono tuʻu hake](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Ko e tataki ʻo e tokoni ʻi he tokotaha ʻoku lava ke tuʻu ʻi hono ʻaʻane; ʻoku ʻikai ko ha fakamaʻu ke toʻo pe ʻave ha tokotaha kuo hinga. ʻOku ʻi ai ha ngaahi tokanga kehe ʻi he mālohi, malu pe moʻui ʻa e tokotaha.

## ʻE totonu nai ke u fetuʻutaki ki ha tokotaha moʻui neongo ʻe ʻikai ke u mamata ki ha fasi?

ʻIo. ʻOku fakatokanga ʻe he [Alzheimer's Society ke talanoa ki he GP ʻa e tokotaha hili ʻa e hinga, neongo ʻe ʻikai ke ke manatu ʻoku mamahi](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/blog/why-person-dementia-more-likely-have-fall-and-what-can-you-do-help). Kapau kuo ke mamata ki ha ʻaʻa ʻo e ʻulu, mamahi, ʻuʻuʻu foʻou, malolo-ʻaʻaʻa, ʻuʻuʻu ʻo e ʻulu, pe ha liliu mei he founga ʻo e tokotaha, fakahā ia ʻi he taimi ʻoku ke telefoni ai. ʻOua ʻe fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e lisi ʻi he initaneti ke fakamaau ʻoku ʻikai fiema'u ha sivi moʻui.

Kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha fasi ʻo e ʻulu, muimui ʻi he fakatotonu mei ha kau faʻu moʻui pe ko ha tokoni fakavave-ngaahi. Ko e [tohi ʻa e NHS ki he fasi ʻo e ʻulu](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) ʻoku fakahā ai ʻa e ngaahi fakaʻilo ʻoku fiema'u ha tokoni vave. Kapau ʻoku vave ʻaupito ʻa e ʻuʻuʻu pe ʻuʻuʻu-hake, tuʻu-ʻi, kiʻi ʻuʻuʻu fakavave, pe ʻoku ʻikai lelei fakafokifā, kumi ʻa e tokoni vave ʻoua ʻe fakatali ki ha taimi fakataha ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e taimi.

## Ko e hā ʻa e fakamatala ʻe lava ke tokoni ki he kau faʻu moʻui?

Hili ʻa e ngaahi fiemaʻu vave, tohi ʻa e ngaahi sivi nounou ke fakahā ki he kau faʻu moʻui ʻa e tokotaha, pea ko e loto ʻa e tokotaha ia:

- ʻa e taimi mo e feituʻu ʻo e hinga, kapau ʻoku ʻiloa;
- ʻa e meʻa ne fai ʻe he tokotaha kimuʻa pe ʻi he hinga;
- pe ko e hinga ne mamata ki ai ʻa ha tokotaha, pe ko e ʻulu kuo fasi;
- ʻa e mamahi, fasi, pe liliu kuo ke mamata ki ai;
- ʻa e liliu foʻou ʻi he moʻui, malolo, founga-moʻui pe mohu ʻoku ke ʻiloa.

Fakahā ʻa e meʻa kuo ke mamata ki ai, ʻoua ʻe fakaʻuhinga ʻa e ʻuhinga ʻo e hinga. Ko ha tohi nounou ʻe lava ke tokoni ki he talanoa, ka ʻoku ʻikai ko e fakamaau ʻo e ʻuhinga. Kapau ʻoku lava ke hoko ha fasi lahi pe ha liliu vave, ʻoku totonu ke tokoni muʻa ʻi he tohi.

## Ko e hā ʻa e ngaahi fehuʻi ʻe totonu ke u ʻeke ʻi he muimui?

ʻE lava ke tokoni ʻe he kau faʻu moʻui ke nau fakakaukau pe ʻoku fiema'u ha sivi ʻi he hinga ni, pea ko e hā ʻa e meʻa ke vakai ki ai hili ai. [ʻOku pehē ʻe he NHS ʻe lava ke kau ʻi he sivi ʻo e hinga ʻa e malohi, mata, telinga, mohu mo e ngaahi liliu ʻi he fale](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). ʻE lava ke ke fehuʻi:

- ʻOku fiema'u nai ʻa e tokotaha ke sivi hili ʻa e hinga ni, neongo ʻikai ʻi ai ha fasi ʻoku ʻaʻaʻa?
- ʻOku ʻi ai nai ha ngaahi liliu pe fakaʻilo ʻe totonu ke ʻomi ai ʻa e tokoni vave?
- ʻE totonu nai ke vakai ki he malu-ʻi-tuʻu, mata, telinga, mamahi pe mohu?
- ʻE lava nai ke tokoni ha kautaha fakatuʻuloa-hinga pe ko ha tokotaha ngāue-moʻui ke sivi ʻa e malava ʻi he ngāue pe ʻa e meʻa ki he fale?
- Ko hai ʻe totonu ke fetuʻutaki ki ai ʻa e tokotaha pe ʻa e fāmili kapau ʻe toe hoko ha hinga?

Fakahā ki he kau faʻu moʻui ʻa e meʻa ʻoku ke ʻiloa mo ʻoku ʻikai ke ke ʻiloa. Kapau na'e ʻikai ha tokotaha ne mamata ki he hinga, fakahā ia ʻoua ʻe fakaʻuhinga ʻa e founga ne hoko ai. ʻOua ʻe toʻo mohu, ngāue ʻaki ha meʻa-tokoni fakaʻalu, pe fakatu'u ʻa e meʻa-tokoni ʻi he tohi pē. Ko e tokoni ʻoku totonu ʻoku tuʻu ʻi he moʻui ʻa e tokotaha, malava, fale, mo e fili ʻa e tokotaha.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke tau fakakaukau ai ki ha hinga foʻou?

Hili ʻa e ngaahi palopalema vave ʻo e moʻui, fakakaukau ki he meʻa ne hoko ʻi he feituʻu mo e founga ʻaho. Ko ha hala ʻoku fu'u ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi meʻa, ko ha liliu ʻo e maama, pe ko ha hala ʻoku ʻikai ʻiloa ʻe lava ke fakafeʻunga, ka ʻoua ʻe fakaʻuhinga ʻoku ʻi ai ha meʻa pe ʻe taha ne fakatupu ʻa e hinga. Fehuʻi ki he tokotaha ʻa e meʻa kuo ne sio ki ai pe ko e hā ʻe faingofua ange ai ke ne ngāue ʻaki ʻa e feituʻu. Kapau ʻoku ʻikai lava ke ne manatu pe fakamatala ʻa e hinga, fakahā ʻa e meʻa kuo ke mamata ki ai ʻoua ʻe fakamālohi ke ne fakamatala ʻa e ngaahi meʻa lahi. Tohi ʻa e taimi, feituʻu, tafa-vaʻe, maama, ngāue mo ha liliu mei he founga ʻo ʻene fe-ʻalu pe ngāue. Ko ha lekooti nounou ʻe lava ke tokoni ki he kau faʻu moʻui ke nau sivi ʻa e ngaahi kakai, ka ʻikai ke fakaʻiloa ʻe ia ʻa e ʻuhinga.

Ko e [tohi ʻo e malu ʻi he fale](/caregiving/home-safety/) ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e sivi ʻi he potu kotoa. Kapau na'e hoko ʻa e hinga ʻi he taimi ʻo e kaukau, vakai ki he [tohi ʻo e kaukau](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). Ko e fakatotonu, ko e tauhi-ngāue, pe ko e kau tokoni ʻo e hinga ʻi ho feituʻu ʻe lava ke nau sivi ʻa e ngaahi palopalema makehe pea fakanofo ʻa e ngaahi liliu ʻoku fe'unga. ʻOua ʻe fakatu'u ʻa e meʻa-tokoni pe liliu ha mohu ʻaki ʻa e tohi ni pē.

ʻOku ʻikai ko e hinga pē ʻe fakahā ʻe ia ʻa e meʻa ʻe lava pe ʻe ʻikai ke lava ʻe he tokotaha. Tauhi ia ke ne kau ʻi he filifili ʻo e tokoni, pea muimui ʻi he fakatotonu ʻo e kau tokanga ʻi ho feituʻu.
