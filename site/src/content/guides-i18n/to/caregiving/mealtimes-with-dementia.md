ʻE faingataʻa ange ke muimui ʻi he kai kapau ʻoku longolongo ʻa e potu, ʻoku fakatoloi ʻa e laumata pe ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi filifili lahi ʻi he taimi tatai. Ko ha ngaahi liliu iiki ʻi he setingi mo e vave ʻe lava ke tokoni ke faingofua ange ki he tokotaha ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e dementia ke ne kau. Kamataʻaki ʻa e ngaahi anga-fakamatala mo e filifili ʻa e tokotaha, pea tauhi ʻa e meʻa ʻoku ngāue lelei.

ʻOku fakaʻaongaʻi ʻe he [ʻaofi ʻa e Alzheimer's Association ki he meʻakai mo e kai](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) mo e [tohi-fakaako ʻa Dementia Australia ki he taimi kai](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) ʻa e potu melino, meʻakai ʻoku ʻiloa mo ha taimi lahi. Ko e tohi ni ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e potu kai mo e tokoni ʻi he ʻaho ʻaho. ʻOku ʻikai ko ha palani meʻakai pe ko ha vakaiʻi ʻo e kai mo e fakamaau.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke hoko ai ʻa e kai ko ha taimi fakataha ʻo ʻikai ha mamafa?

Kapau ʻoku fiemālie ʻa e tokotaha ʻi he kau fakataha, nofo mo kai mo ia. Tauhi ʻa e talanoa faingofua pea tuku ke ne tokanga ki he kai ʻi he taimi ʻoku ne fie ai. ʻOua ʻe fakatonutonu ʻene founga ʻo ʻai ʻa e meʻa-tutu pe fehuʻi ʻe fakafolofola ʻa e kai ko ha sivi. Kapau ʻoku fakalahi ʻa e longoa pe ko e fakaloloa, fakaʻiiki ʻa e kau fakataha pe fetuʻutaki ki ha potu longolongo ange.

Kapau kuo faingataʻa ʻa e kuki, ko ha meʻakai kuo teuteu pe tokoni ki ha vahe ʻo e ngāue ʻe lava ke faingofua ange ai ʻa e kai. ʻOku fokotuʻu ʻe Dementia Australia ʻa e meʻakai ʻoku ʻomi ki he fale pe kuo teuteu ko ha ngaahi filifili kapau ʻoku fie ai. Fehuʻi ki he meʻakai ʻoku ʻofa ai ʻa e tokotaha mo e tokoni ʻoku ne loto ki ai; ʻe totonu pe ke tuku ʻa e faingofua ke ʻi ai ʻa e fili ʻa e tokotaha.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke faingofua ange ai ʻa e laumata ki he tokotaha?

Fai ke faingofua ʻa e kai ʻi he vakai ʻi mui ai ʻi he liliu ʻo e meʻakai.

- **Fakaʻiiki ʻa e longoa.** Tāpuni ʻa e telefisoni pea fakaʻiiki ʻa e ngaahi longoa ʻe fakatoloi. Nofoʻi ʻa e tokotaha mamaʻo mei he ʻalu ʻo e kiʻi kiteni kapau ʻe tokoni ia ke ne tokanga.
- **Hikihiki ʻa e ngaahi meʻa kehe.** Tuku pe ʻa e ngaahi meʻa-tutu mo e ngaahi meʻa-kakala ʻoku ʻaonga ki he tokotaha. Fakahoko ʻa e ngaahi kupesi, ngaahi mohuʻa pe meʻa kehe ʻoku ʻikai ke ne fakatoloi pe ʻoku lava ke hange ko e meʻakai.
- **Fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e kongokongo mahino.** Ko e pleti halaila ʻoku kehe mei he meʻa-fakaʻaho pe laumata ʻe lava ke faingofua ange ai ke sio ki he meʻakai. ʻOua ʻe fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e kupesi pe ngaahi tuʻunga-tupu ʻoku fakatoloi.
- **Vakai ki he maama mo e vevela.** Fakapapau ʻoku lava ke sio ʻa e tokotaha ki he pleti, pea sivi ʻa e meʻakai mo e inu ki mu'a ʻi he fakatoka ke ʻoua ʻe mafana lahi.
- **Tauhi ʻa e founga ʻo e kai ʻoku ʻiloa.** Kapau ʻoku melino ha potu, taimi pe founga ʻo e laumata, feinga ke tauhi ia ʻi he taimi tatau. Liliu pe ʻa e meʻa ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e palopalema.

## Ko e hā ʻoku totonu ke u foaki?

Kamata ʻaki ʻa e meʻakai ʻoku ʻiloa mo ʻofa ai ʻa e tokotaha. Foaki ha koesi ʻe taha ʻi he taimi, pe ha filifili ʻe ua ʻo e meʻa ʻoku ʻiloa, ka ʻikai ha pleti kakato ʻo e ngaahi meʻakai kehekehe. ʻE lava ke toe foaki ʻa e ngaahi vahe iiki kapau ʻoku loto ai ʻa e tokotaha ki ha toe.

ʻOku lava ke liliu ʻa e ngaahi mahuʻinga. Kapau ʻoku ʻikai talia ha meʻakai ʻoku ʻofa ai, ʻoua ʻe fekeʻikeʻi pe fakamālohi. Foaki ha filifili ʻe taha ʻoku ʻiloa, tuku ke malolō ʻa e tokotaha, pe feinga toe ʻi ha taimi kehe. ʻOku ʻikai ke fiema'u ha taimi kai tōtōlaga kapau ʻoku mahaʻaki pe loto-ngaʻunu ʻa e tokotaha; tauhi ʻa e taimi kai tuʻumaʻu ʻi he potu ʻoku ngāue ai, pea fetuʻutaki ʻa e palani ki he ngaahi fiemaʻu ʻo e tokotaha.

Kapau kuo faingataʻa ʻa e kuki, ʻe lava ke tokoni ʻa e kau fāmili ʻi he teuteu ʻoku ʻi he taimi ʻoku kau ai ʻa e tokotaha ʻi ha ngāue ʻoku haohaoa mo ʻofa ai ʻaʻane. Ko e [tohi ʻo e ngaahi ngāue ʻi he fale](/activities/household-tasks/) ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi founga ke fetuʻutaki ai ʻa e ngaahi ngāue ʻi he kiteni ʻo ʻikai ha fakamaau.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke tokoni ai ki he tuʻu-ʻaʻana ʻa e tokotaha?

Fakaʻatā ʻa e tokotaha ke kai ʻi he founga ʻoku melino pea haohaoa maʻana. ʻE lava ke fie ʻa e ipu, sipuni, meʻakai ʻoku ʻai ʻaki ʻa e nima pe vahe iiki. ʻOua ʻe vave ke fakatonutonu ʻa e fakatoloi pe fakamaʻu ʻa e ngāue ʻoku ʻi ai ʻene malava. Foaki ʻa e tokoni kapau ʻoku fie ai pe fiema'u, pea ʻoange ha taimi lahi ki he kai.

Ko e kai fakataha ʻe lava ke hoko ai ʻa e kai ko ha taimi fakafeʻiloaki, kapau ʻoku fiemālie ʻa e tokotaha ki he kau fakataha. Nofo ofi, tauhi ʻa e talanoa faingofua pea tuku ke ne tokanga ki he kai ʻi he taimi ʻoku ne fie ai. ʻOua ʻe fehuʻi ʻoku fakatupu ʻa e fakakaukau ki he manatu pe lea ʻo e meʻa ʻoku kai ai. Kapau ʻoku ne ʻuʻuʻu, fakaʻatā ke ne fuʻu taimi pea fehuʻi ʻa e loto ke ne toe kai.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga kapau ʻoku faingataʻa ai ʻa e taimi kai?

Ngaʻunu pea sivi ʻa e ʻuhinga ʻo e faingataʻa. ʻE lava ke hoko ʻa e potu ʻo mamafa, ʻoku ʻikai ke ʻiloa ʻa e meʻakai, pe ʻoku fie ʻa e tokotaha ke ʻalu ki he falemahakí, malolo pe liliu ʻene tuʻunga. Feinga ki ha liliu ʻe taha ʻi he taimi tatau, hangē ko e hikihiki ʻo e laumata, foaki ʻa e vai, pe fetuʻutaki ki ha potu melino ange. Kapau ʻoku loto-mamafa ʻa e tokotaha, tuku ʻo toe feinga ʻi ha taimi kehe ʻi he ʻikai ke fakamālohi ke ne fakaʻosi.

Kapau ʻoku tuʻo lahi ʻa e tokotaha ʻi he kai pe ʻoku ʻikai ke fiemālie, ʻoku faingataʻa ʻene ngutu pe nima, pe ʻoku liliu ʻa e fie kai pe kuʻi ʻoku ʻikai ke tatau, tohi ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku ke mamata ki ai pea talanoa mo e fakafoki pe ko e kau tokoni ʻo e kai. ʻE lava ke fakatotoloi ʻe he kau faʻu ʻo e leʻo mo e lea ʻa e fakamamafa ʻo e kai pea fakahā ʻa e ngaahi founga haohaoa. Ko e fakafokifā ʻo e lea pe ʻo e faʻa ʻohi ʻo e kai pe inu, pe ʻo e fakatoloi, ʻe fiema'u ha tokoni ʻi he kau faʻu ʻoku ʻiloa. ʻOua ʻe liliu ʻa e tuʻunga ʻo e meʻakai, fakalahi ʻa e inu pe feinga ki ha founga fafanga ʻi he ngaahi tohi lisi lahi.

ʻOku ʻikai ko e kai ʻe ʻaʻa ki he ʻuhinga ʻo e meʻakai kuo tuku. Vakai ki ha mahinoʻi ʻi he ngaahi kai kehekehe: ʻe lava ʻa e meʻakai pe taimi pe leʻo pe nofo ʻoku faingataʻa ai ʻa e ngāue. Kapau ʻoku faingataʻa ʻa e meʻakai ʻoku ʻofa ai ʻa e tokotaha, sivi ʻa e mamahi pe mamahi ʻi he nifo pea fehuʻi ki ha kau faʻu ʻoku ʻikai ke fakaʻuhinga ko e tokotaha ʻoku fakamālohi pē.

Ko e fehuʻi fekau'aki mo e moʻui pe ko ha kai makehe, fehuʻi ki he kau faʻu moʻui ʻa e tokotaha. ʻOua ʻe kamata ha kai ʻoku fakatoloi pe liliu ha vaiʻa ke liliu ai ʻa e fie kai ʻi he ʻikai ha fakatotonu. Ko e [tohi ʻo e kai ki he moʻui-ʻatamai](/brain-health/diet/) ʻoku talanoa ai ki he fakatotoloʻi ʻo e kai mo e fakalahi ʻo e dementia; ʻoku vahe ia mei he tohi ni ʻo e taimi kai fakaʻaho.

## ʻE totonu nai ke kai ʻa e meʻakai ʻi he laumata ʻi he taimi kotoa?

ʻIkai. Kapau ʻoku faingataʻa pe ʻoku fakalaka ʻa e nofo ki he laumata, ʻe lava ke lelei ange ha potu kehe ʻoku melino, pe ʻoku ʻikai ha faingataʻa, ʻo tuku ke ne kai ʻi he founga ʻoku haohaoa maʻana. Ko e kai iiki, ʻaisi pe fakatuʻuloa ʻe lava ke lelei ange ʻi he feinga ke fakaʻosi ha kai kakato ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakatoloi ai.

Kapau kuo faingataʻa ʻa e kai ʻi he ngaahi ʻaho, pe ʻoku ke ongo mamafa ki he meʻakai, vai pe ngutu, fetuʻutaki ki ha kau faʻu moʻui. Kumi ʻa e tokoni vave ʻi he fakaʻamu ʻo e tuʻunga ʻo e kai.

## Fakamatala

Ko e tohi ni ʻoku fakahoko ʻi he ngaahi fakamatala lahi ʻo e kau faʻu ʻo e meʻakai pe moʻui. Ke ʻoua ʻe fakamatala ki he kau faʻu moʻui pe ngaahi fehuʻi ʻe lava ke fakahoko ha tokoni tatau.
