ʻE faingataʻa ange ke muimui ʻi he kai ʻi ha potu longoa, ha laumata ʻoku fakatoloi, pe ha ngaahi filifili lahi ʻi he taimi tatau. ʻE lava ke tokoni ʻa e ngaahi liliu iiki ʻi he setingi mo e vave ke faingofua ange ai ke kau ʻa e tokotaha ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e dementia. Kamataʻaki ʻi he ngaahi anga mo e filifili ʻa e tokotaha, pea tauhi ʻa e meʻa ʻoku ngāue lelei.

ʻOku fokotuʻu ʻe he [tohi ʻa e Alzheimer's Association ki he meʻakai mo e kai](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) mo e [fakatotonu ʻa Dementia Australia ki he taimi kai](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) ʻa e setingi melino, meʻakai ʻoku ʻiloa mo ha taimi lahi. ʻOku fakamatala ʻa e tohi ni ki he feituʻu kai mo e tokoni ʻi he ʻaho ʻaho. ʻOku ʻikai ko ha palani ʻo e kai-meʻakai pe ko ha fakamaau ki he ngutu-kai.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke hoko ai ʻa e kai ko ha taimi fakataha ʻo ʻikai ha mamafa?

Kapau ʻoku fiemālie ʻa e tokotaha ki he kau fakataha, nofo ʻo kai mo ia. Tauhi ʻa e talanoa faingofua pea tuku ke ne tokanga ki he kai ʻi he taimi ʻoku ne fie ai. ʻOua ʻe fakatonutonu hono founga ʻo e fakaʻaongaʻi ʻo e meʻa-tutu pe fehuʻi ʻoku fakafeʻaʻaki ai ʻa e kai ko ha sivi. Kapau ʻoku longoa pe mamafa ʻa e laumata, fakasiʻisiʻi ʻa e kakai pe fetuʻutaki ki ha potu longolongo ange.

Kapau kuo mamahi ʻa e kuki, ko ha meʻakai kuo teuteu pe tokoni ki ha vahe ʻo e ngāue ʻe lava ke faingofua ange ai ʻa e taimi kai. ʻOku fokotuʻu ʻe Dementia Australia ʻa e meʻakai ʻoku ʻomi ki he fale pe kuo teuteu ko ha ngaahi filifili kapau ʻoku fiemaʻu. Fehuʻi ki he ngaahi meʻakai ʻoku ʻofa ai ʻa e tokotaha mo e founga tokoni ʻoku ne loto ki ai; ʻoku totonu pe ke tuku ʻa e faingofua ke ʻi ai ʻa e feinga ki he mahuʻinga ʻa e tokotaha.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke faingofua ange ai ʻa e laumata ki he tokotaha?

Fakafaingofua ʻa e kai ke hangē ʻoku faingofua ia, ʻi mui ai ʻi he liliu ʻo e meʻa ʻoku foaki.

- **Fakaʻiiki ʻa e longoa.** Tāpuni ʻa e telefisoni pea fakaʻiiki ʻa e ngaahi longoa ʻe taha ki he tahi. Nofoʻi ʻa e tokotaha mamaʻo mei he ʻalu ʻo e kiteni kapau ʻe tokoni ia ke ne tokanga.
- **Hikihiki ʻa e ngaahi meʻa kehe.** Tuku pe ʻa e ngaahi meʻa-tutu mo e ngaahi meʻa-tō ʻoku fiema'u ʻe he tokotaha. Hikihiki ʻa e ngaahi maile, ngaahi mohuʻa pe meʻa kehe ʻoku lava ke fakatoloi pe hange ko e meʻakai.
- **Fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e lanu ʻoku mahino ʻi he kehe.** Ko e pleti halaila ʻoku kehe mei he laumata pe ko e kiʻi ʻakau ʻe lava ke faingofua ange ai ke sio ki he meʻakai. ʻOua ʻe fakaʻaongaʻi ʻa e ngaahi mahaʻi ʻoku fakatoloi ʻi he pleti pe ʻi he kiʻi mata.
- **Vakai ki he maama mo e vevela.** Fakapapau ʻoku lava ke sio ʻa e tokotaha ki he pleti, pea sivi ʻa e meʻakai mo e inu ki muʻa ʻi he foaki ke ʻoua ʻe mafana lahi.
- **Tauhi ʻa e founga ʻo e kai ʻoku ʻiloa.** Kapau ʻoku melino ha feituʻu, ha taimi pe ha founga ʻo e laumata, feinga ke tauhi ia ʻi he taimi tatau. Liliu pe ʻa e meʻa ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e palopalema.

## Ko e hā ʻoku totonu ke u foaki?

Kamata ʻaki ʻa e meʻakai ʻoku ʻiloa mo ʻofa ai ʻa e tokotaha. Foaki ha koesi ʻe taha ʻi he taimi, pe ha filifili ʻe ua ʻo e meʻa ʻoku ʻiloa, ka ʻikai ha pleti kakato ʻo e ngaahi meʻakai kehekehe. ʻE lava ke foaki toe ʻa e ngaahi vahe iiki kapau ʻoku fie ai ʻa e tokotaha.

ʻOku lava ke liliu ʻa e ngaahi mahuʻinga. Kapau ʻoku ʻikai talia ha meʻakai ʻoku ʻofa ai, ʻoua ʻe fekeʻikeʻi pe fakamālohi. Foaki ha filifili ʻe taha ʻoku ʻiloa, tuku ke malolo ʻa e tokotaha, pe feinga toe ʻi ha taimi kehe. ʻE ʻikai ke fiema'u ke muimui ʻi ha taimi kai fakalelei kapau ʻoku mafatukituki pe ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e loto-mamafa ʻa e tokotaha; tauhi ʻa e taimi kai tuʻumaʻu kapau ʻoku ngāue ia, pea fetuʻutaki ʻa e palani ki he ngaahi fiemaʻu ʻo e tokotaha.

Kapau kuo faingataʻa ʻa e kuki, ʻe lava ke tokoni ʻa e mēmipa ʻo e fāmili ʻi he teuteu ʻi he taimi ʻoku kau ai ʻa e tokotaha ʻi ha ngāue ʻoku haohaoa mo ʻofa ai ʻaʻane. Ko e [tohi ʻo e ngaahi ngāue ʻi he fale](/activities/household-tasks/) ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e ngaahi founga ke fetuʻutaki ai ʻa e ngaahi ngāue ʻi he kiteni ʻo ʻikai ha sivi.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga ke tokoni ai ki he tuʻu-ʻaʻana ʻa e tokotaha?

Fakaʻatā ʻa e tokotaha ke kai ʻi he founga ʻoku melino pea haohaoa maʻana. ʻE lava ke fie ʻa e ipu, sipuni, meʻakai ʻoku ʻai ʻaki ʻa e nima pe vahe iiki. ʻOua ʻe vave ke fakatonutonu ʻa e ʻasi pe fakahoko ʻa e ngāue ʻoku ʻi ai ʻene malava. Foaki ʻa e tokoni kapau ʻoku fie ai pe fiemaʻu, pea ʻoange ha taimi lahi ki he kai.

Ko e kai fakataha ʻe lava ke hoko ai ʻa e kai ko ha taimi fakafeʻiloaki, kapau ʻoku fiemālie ʻa e tokotaha ki he kau fakataha. Nofo ofi, tauhi ʻa e talanoa faingofua, pea tuku ke ne tokanga ki he kai ʻi he taimi ʻoku ne fie ai. ʻOua ʻe fehuʻi ʻoku fakatupu ʻa e fakakaukau ki he manatu, pe ko e ngaahi lea ki he meʻa ʻoku ne kai ai. Kapau ʻoku ne ʻuʻuʻu, fakaʻatā ke ne fuʻu taimi pea fehuʻi ʻa e loto ke ne toe kai.

## Ko e hā ʻa e founga kapau ʻoku faingataʻa ai ʻa e taimi kai?

Ngaʻunu pea sivi ʻa e ʻuhinga ʻo e faingataʻa. ʻE lava ke hoko ʻa e potu ko e longoa, ʻoku ʻikai ke ʻiloa ʻa e meʻakai, pe ʻoku fie ʻa e tokotaha ke ʻalu ki he falemahaki, malolo pe liliu ʻene tuʻunga. Feinga ki ha liliu ʻe taha ʻi he taimi tatau, hangē ko e hikihiki ʻo e laumata, foaki ʻa e vai, pe fetuʻutaki ki ha potu longolongo ange. Kapau ʻoku mamahi ʻa e tokotaha, tuku ʻo toe feinga ʻi ha taimi kehe ʻi he ʻikai ke fakamālohi ke ne fakaʻosi.

Kapau ʻoku tuʻo lahi ʻa e tokotaha ʻi he kai, ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e mamahi, ʻoku faingataʻa ʻene ngutu pe nima, pe ʻoku liliu ʻa e fie-kai pe ʻa e mamafa, tohi ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku ke mamata ki ai pea talanoa mo e fakafoki pe ko e kau faʻu ʻo e kai. Ko e ʻuʻuʻu ʻi he tō-kai pe ʻi he inu, fakaʻatu ia ki ha kau faʻu moʻui pe ki he kau faʻu ʻo e kai ke nau fakatotonu. ʻOua ʻe liliu ʻa e tuʻunga ʻo e meʻakai, fakamafatu ʻa e inu pe feinga ki ha founga fafanga ʻi he ngaahi tohi lisi lahi.

ʻOku ʻikai ko e kai ʻe taha kuo tuku ʻoku fakamatala ki he ʻuhinga ʻo e tokotaha kuo ʻosi. Vakai ki ha mahino ʻi he ngaahi kai kehekehe: ʻe lava ʻa e meʻakai, taimi, leʻo, pe nofo ʻi he laumata ke faingataʻa ai ʻa e ngāue. Kapau ʻoku faingataʻa ʻa e meʻakai ʻoku ʻofa ai ʻa e tokotaha, sivi pe ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e mamahi pe mamahi ʻi he nifo pea fehuʻi ki ha kau faʻu moʻui ʻi he ʻikai ke fakaʻuhinga ko e tokotaha ʻoku ʻi ai ʻa e fakamamahi pē.

Ko e fehuʻi fekau'aki mo e moʻui pe ko ha kai makehe, fehuʻi ki he kau faʻu moʻui ʻa e tokotaha. ʻOua ʻe kamata ha kai ʻoku fakatoloi pe liliu ha vaiʻa ke liliu ai ʻa e fie-kai ʻi he ʻikai ha fakatotonu. Ko e [tohi ʻo e kai ki he moʻui-ʻatamai](/brain-health/diet/) ʻoku talanoa ai ki he fakatotoloʻi ʻo e kai mo e faingamālie ʻo e dementia; ʻoku vahe ia mei he tohi ni ʻo e taimi kai ʻi he ʻaho ʻaho.

## ʻE totonu nai ke kai ʻa e meʻakai ʻi he laumata ʻi he taimi kotoa?

ʻIkai. Kapau ʻoku faingataʻa pe ʻoku fakaʻalu ʻa e nofo ki he laumata, ʻe lava ke lelei ange ha potu kehe ʻoku melino, ʻi he ʻikai ke fakatupu ʻa e fakamamahi ʻi he kai. Ko e kai iiki, ko ha kai-kiʻi pe ko ha fakamalolo ʻe lava ke lelei ange ʻi he feinga ke fakaʻosi ha kai kakato ʻi he taimi ʻoku fakatoloi ai.

Kapau kuo faingataʻa ʻa e kai ʻi he ngaahi ʻaho, pe ʻoku ke ongo mamafa ki he meʻakai, vai pe ngutu, fetuʻutaki ki ha kau faʻu moʻui. Kumi ʻa e tokoni fakavave ki he ʻuʻuʻu ʻo e kai-meʻakai ʻi he tō-kai pe ʻi he ʻuʻuʻu ʻo e tokotaha.
