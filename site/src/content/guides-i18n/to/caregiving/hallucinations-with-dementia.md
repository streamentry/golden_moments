Ko e sio, fanongo, ʻongo, tō, pe ongo ki ha meʻa ʻoku ʻikai ke sioʻi ʻe he kakai kehe ʻe lava ke moʻoni ʻaupito ki he tokotaha. ʻE lava ke manavahē ia, pe ʻe ʻikai ke ne mamahi. Kamata ʻaki ʻa e founga ʻoku ongoʻi ai ʻa e tokotaha mo kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha taha ʻoku ʻi he kovi, ʻo ʻikai ko e feinga ke fakamoʻoni pe ko e hā ʻa e meʻa kuo hoko.

ʻE lava ke hoko ʻa e meʻa ʻe taha ko e hallucination, ka ʻe lava foki ke ko e sio hala pe misidentification. Ko e ʻata ʻe lava ke hangē ko ha tangata; ko ha kakai ʻoku ʻiloa ʻe lava ke hala ai ʻa e ʻilo. ʻOku ʻikai ke fiemaʻu ke ke fakamaʻu pe ko e hā ʻoku hokó. Fakamatala ʻa e me'a ʻoku lea ai ʻa e tokotaha mo e me'a ʻoku ke sio ki ai, pea fehuʻi ki ha taha tokanga fakatōketā kapau ʻoku ke manavahē.

## Ko e hā ʻoku totonu ke u fai ʻi he taimi ni?

Tauhi ʻa e loto-mālie, fehuʻi pe ko e hā ʻa e ongoʻi ʻo e tokotaha, pea foaki ha fakalotolahi ʻi he taʻe-fekauʻaki. Ko e [Alzheimer's Association ʻoku fakahā ke vakai pe ʻoku mamahi pe ʻoku ʻi ai ha kovi ʻi he meʻa ko iá](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/hallucinations). Kapau ʻoku loto-lelei mo malu ʻa e tokotaha, ʻe ʻikai fiemaʻu ke fakapōpōʻuli ʻa e taimi.

- **Fakaʻilo ʻa e ongoʻi.** ʻE lava ke ke pehē, "ʻOku ʻikai ke u sio ki ha taha, ka ʻoku ou ʻiloa ʻoku mamahi ʻaupito ʻa e meʻa ni. ʻOku ou ʻi heni." Ko e meʻa ni ʻoku moʻoni pea ʻoku ʻomi ai ki he tokotaha ʻa e ongoʻi ʻoku ke fakakaukau ki ai ʻaupito.
- **Tauhi ʻa e lea siʻisiʻi.** Ko ha fakamatala lahi pe ko e fakatonutonu toe hokó ʻe lava ke fakalahi ai ʻa e mamahi. Tuku ke fakamatala ʻe he tokotaha ʻa e meʻa ʻoku ne sioʻi kapau ʻoku loto ia.
- **Foaki, ʻoua ʻe fakamaʻu, ha liliu.** Fehuʻi pe ʻoku loto ia ke nofo ʻi ha feituʻu kehe, toʻo ha maama kehe, pe fanongo ki ha hiva ʻoku ʻiloa. ʻE lava ke tokoni ke ʻalu ki ha potu maama mo ʻiloa; tuku ʻi he taimi ʻoku ʻikai loto ai ʻa e tokotaha ke ʻalu.
- **Tuʻu ofi kapau ʻoku loto ke ke ʻi ai.** ʻOua ʻe taʻofi vave ʻa e tokotaha. Fehuʻi muʻa ki ha nima pe ʻofa.

Kapau ʻoku ʻikai ha kovi pe mamahi ʻa e meʻa ko iá, muimui ki he ʻuluaki ʻa e tokotaha. ʻOua ʻe liliu ʻa e taimi ko iá ko ha tesi pe fekau ke ne fakamoʻoni ʻa e me'a kuo ne sioʻi.

ʻE lava ke ʻi ai ha tokotaha ʻoku ʻofa pe ʻiloa ʻa e meʻa ko iá. Kapau ʻoku mālie pea malu ʻa e tokotaha, ʻe ʻikai fiemaʻu ke liliu ʻa e kaveinga pe ʻave ia ki ha feituʻu kehe. Fehuʻi ʻa e me'a ʻoku loto ai; ʻoua ʻe liliu ʻa e taimi kotoa ko ha talanoa fakatōketā. Kapau ʻoku manavahē ia, tali ʻa e ongoʻi ko iá neongo ʻoku ʻikai ke ke sioʻi ʻa e me'a tatau.

## ʻOku ʻi ai nai ha meʻa ʻi he potu ʻoku hala ke fakatokanga ko ha tangata pe leo?

Vakai ki he feituʻu mo e tokotaha kapau ʻoku ne loto ki he tokoni ko iá. Ko e [Alzheimer's Society ʻoku ʻiloa ai ʻa e hallucinations mei he misperceptions](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations), ʻa ia ʻoku sio hala ai ha meʻa moʻoni pe ngaahi tāʻanga.

- **Fakalelei ʻa e maama.** Toʻo ha lamp pea vakai ki he ngaahi ʻata pe ngaahi tāʻanga mei he ngaahi sipeki mo e ngaahi matapā.
- **Vakai ki he ngaahi leo ʻoku ʻiloa.** Ko e telefisoni, fanga-matangi, pe ngaahi meʻa-ngāue ʻe lava ke hangē ko e leo pe ʻaʻaʻa. Toʻo hifo pe tāpuni pē kapau ʻoku malu ʻa e tokotaha ki ai.
- **Vakai ki he ngaahi tuʻunga.** Ko ha tuʻunga ʻi he kāpeti, kafu, pe pepa ʻoku ʻi ai ʻe lava ke hangē ko e fekaʻi pe meʻa. Fehuʻi pe ʻoku loto ia ke sio mei ha feituʻu kehe pe liliu ʻa e sio.
- **Vakai ki he ngaahi meʻa tokoni ʻi he sensa.** Kapau ʻoku ʻaʻusia ʻe he tokotaha ʻa e sipeki pe fanongo, fehuʻi pe ʻoku loto ia ke tokoni ke sivi pe ʻoku ʻi ai pea ngāue ʻo hangē ko e ʻuhinga ʻo e ʻaho.

Kapau ʻoku hoko ʻa e meʻa ʻi he feituʻu tatau, kumi ha meʻa ʻoku toe hokó: ko ha tuʻunga ʻi he sipeki, ko ha tuʻunga ʻi he papa, ko ha telefisoni ʻoku fanongo ai mei ha potu kehe, pe ko e maama siʻisiʻi ʻi he afiafi. Sivi pe ʻi ai ha leo moʻoni pe meʻa moʻoni muʻa ʻi he fakakaukau ʻoku liliu ai ʻa e ʻilo. Kapau ʻoku ke feinga ki ha liliu ʻo e feituʻu, fakamahino pea fehuʻi pe ʻoku mālie ange.

Feinga ʻaki ha liliu siʻisiʻi ʻe taha ʻi he taimi. ʻOua ʻe liliu ʻa e ngaahi koloa ʻa e tokotaha pe toʻo ha meʻa ʻoku ne taukaveʻi ʻo ʻikai ke fehuʻi. Ko ha ngāue ʻoku ʻiloa mo ha potu mālie mo maama ʻe lava ke mālie ange.

## Ko e hā ʻa e taimi ke u fetuʻutaki ai mo ha tokotaha fakatōketā?

Fetuʻutaki ki he tokotaha fakatōketā ʻo e tokotaha ʻi he ngaahi meʻa ʻoku foʻou, toe hokó, lōloa, pe ʻoku mamahi. ʻE lava ke tokoni ʻa e liliu ʻo e mata pe fanongo, mahaki, ngaahi meʻa-inu, mo e ngaahi meʻa kehe. ʻE lava ke vakai ʻe he tokotaha fakatōketā pe ko e hā ʻoku lava ke hoko, pea pe ʻoku fiemaʻu nai ha muimui. Ko e [talanoa ʻa Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) ʻoku fakaʻaongaʻi foki ke vakai ki he ngaahi tokoni ʻi he sino, sensa, meʻa-inu, mo e feituʻu.

Ko e liliu vave ʻoku kehe. Ko e [NHS ʻoku pehē ʻe fiemaʻu ʻa e tokoni fakatōketā vave ʻi he fakalilifu vave](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), pea ko e [Alzheimer's Society ʻoku fakahā ʻe fiemaʻu ʻa e tokoni fakatōketā vave kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha ngaahi sēnisi lahi ʻi he hallucination](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations). Fetuʻutaki ki ha tokoni fakatōketā vave ʻi hoʻo feituʻu kapau ʻoku vave ʻa e fakalilifu ʻa e tokotaha pe ʻoku ʻi ai ha ngaahi sēnisi lahi. Kapau ʻoku ʻi ai ha taha ʻoku ʻi he mamahi vave, ngāue ʻaki ʻa e ngaahi tokoni fakavave ʻo ho feituʻu.

ʻOua ʻe kamata, tuku, pe liliu ha meʻa-inu ʻiho tafa'aki pē. Fehuʻi ki he fakatau pe ki he fale-meʻa-inu ke vakai ki he ngaahi liliu foʻou mo e ngaahi meʻa-inu kotoa ʻoku ngāue ʻaki ʻe he tokotaha, kau ai ʻa e ngaahi meʻa ʻoku fakatau ʻe he kakai ʻo ʻikai ha tohi. Ko e [talanoa ki he ʻaʻahi ki he tokotaha fakatōketā](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ʻe lava ke tokoni ke teuteu ʻa e ngaahi fehuʻi.

## Ko e ngaahi fakamatala fēfē ʻe tokoni ʻi he fakataha?

Kapau ʻoku loto ʻa e tokotaha, tohi ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala siʻisiʻi ke vahevahe mo e kau tokanga:

- ʻa e me'a kuo pehē ʻe ia kuo ne sio, fanongo, pe ongoʻi, ʻaki ʻene ngaahi lea ʻi he taimi ʻoku lava ai;
- ʻi he taimi mo e feituʻu ʻi ai, pea ʻa e loloa ʻo e taimi ko iá;
- pe na'e hā ʻoku manavahē, mālie, pe ʻi he kovi ʻa e tokotaha;
- ko e feituʻu ʻoku hangē ko e maama, ngaahi ʻata, ngaahi leo, mo e kakai ʻoku ofi;
- ʻa e mahaki fo'ou, liliu ʻo e sensa, pe liliu ʻo e meʻa-inu ʻoku ke ʻiloa.

Ngāue ʻaki ʻa e ngaahi lea ʻa e tokotaha pe ʻoku lava, pea fakamatala ʻa e me'a kuo ke mamata ki ai. Hangē ko e fakamatala, tohi ʻa e "pehē ʻe ia ʻoku ʻi ai ha tangata ofi ki he matapā" ʻi he ʻikai ko e fakahingoa ʻo e meʻa ko iá. Ko e ngaahi fakamatala ni ʻoku ʻikai ko e fakamoʻoni ʻo e ʻuhinga. Kapau ʻoku kamata vave ʻa e meʻa ʻi he fakalilifu pe liliu lahi ʻo e fakatokanga, kumi ha tokoni vave ʻi he ʻikai ko e tatali ke ʻosi ʻa e fakamatala.

Ko e [talanoa ki he ʻuluaki ʻo e afiafi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) ʻoku fakamatala ki he maama ʻi he afiafi mo e ngāue fakaʻaho. Ko e ngaahi fekau ʻo e meʻa ʻoku ʻalu mei ai ʻoku ʻi he [talanoa ki he tokotaha ʻoku ʻi ai ha manavahē](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/).
