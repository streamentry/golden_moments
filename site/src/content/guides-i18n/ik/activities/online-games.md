Amatchiat ilagiit uuktuqtuat akiḷḷaan online piqutinik dementiaqaqtunun, piqpagniq atautchikun piusuktuaq niuviġviŋmik, login-mik suli credit card-mik piiḷḷutik. Payaŋitchuat piqutit aglaktaqaqtut, nutqaġnaqtuallu. Atautchikun quviasuutiŋasuaq, uuktuutigñatun piiññaitchuq. Una maliguagak uqautigivaa suna piusuktuat, qanuktuallu piqutiq aullaqtisaġlugu, sunallu piqutit makua pisuŋagaat suli pisuŋiññagaat.

## Sumik online piqutiq dementiaqaqtumun nalaunŋaruq?

Piqutiq amatchianik dementiaqaqtunun nalaunŋaruq iḷitchuġiniq apiqsruutiŋagumi, itqaumaġniŋñaitchuaq. Malġuk atausiik aglak nalaunŋiḷḷugik, aglak taqqiq nalaunŋaġaa aktuuġlugu, suli sunik qaġġutmun ilaŋŋaġmun iḷḷiḷḷugu — makua iñuum tautuktait atuqpagait. Piqutit itqaumaġniqmik maliguagak, taiguagak, nalunaqutaaġniqmik suli sivunġani piqpagniqmik apiqsruutit qiñiqsiññiqsaqtuq.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) uqautivai piqujaksraq iñuum quviagiyaat suli pisuŋagaat atuġlugit, ikayuġnaiġñatigullu nalaunŋaruq. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) uqautivuq piqujaksraq payatchiliġlugu, nutqaġnaiġñagu pisuŋagniat allakkaŋagumi.

## Golden Moments piqutit browser-mi aullaqtisaġnaktat

Atausiq piqutiq makua iluqaisa website-miñ aullaqtuq iḷḷiḷḷaġnaiġñaitchuq. Amatchiat tablet-mi suli nalauttaaqsaġvigmi piŋagaat; ilai aglaġviŋmun nuutauŋasaaqtuq.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Aŋiḷḷuat aglaktaat nalaunŋiḷḷugit aglallu tautukkun iḷḷatiqaqtut, timer-lu nalunaqutaaġniqlu piiññaitchuq. Sivulliqmun piqutmun nakuuruq.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Atausiq aglak qulaani, atausiillu taamna malġuknit sisamanullu akiani.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Nalunaitchuaq taqqiq qulaani tautuktuq; aglak taamna sanivaŋa aktuuġlugu.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Aglak aktuuġlugu, tavra qaġġutiq ilaŋŋaġaa, siku iglu niġivik suli nautchiivik.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Aglak aviktuaq, iḷuatŋalu tautukkun iḷḷatiqaqtuq maliguaqsaun.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Iḷitchuġiruat uqausiit kinguliq taiguaq piiññaitchuq; tikiġiñ aktuuġlugu suli naatchiġiñ tautuktilugu.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Aglaktaqaqtut uqausiġniġmun apiqqutauruat, uuktuutiginaiġñaitchuat. Aglaġviŋmullu nuutauŋasaaqtuq.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Nalaunŋiḷḷutit aglaktanik tikiġivinit savaaqsraġmiñ. Aglaktaqsi browser-mi taamnaġmi savaaqsraġmi iḷḷatiqaqtut.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Sukatchiaq nalunaitchuaq aniqsaaġniq atautchikun. Iḷiññiq aullaqtitpiñ nutqaġlugulu.

Word search, coloring, picture bingo-lu aglaġviŋmun piqutit atuŋasaaqtuq. Taiguġilugu [free printable dementia activities](/activities/printable/) ilisaġniq.

## Qanuktuallu piqutiq aullaqtisaġlugu

1. **Nalunaitchuaq piviuttaq tikiġiñ** iñuum aniqsaġumi. Unnuami qiñiġniq qiñiqsaunasuq ilaanun, taimani iḷisimavutin qimilguuġlugu.
2. **Aŋiḷḷuaq savaaqsraq atuġlugu.** Tablet nakuuruġmi suli nalauttaaqsaġvik browser zoom-iruq tautuktauŋaruq aktuutaunaruqlu.
3. **Allat tabs umiktuġilugit nutaqqutit quliatŋullu qiḷiutquġiñ.** Tusaatchiat pop-up-t qiñiqsiññiqsaqtuq nakuuruq piqpagniqlu nutqaġtitaasaaqtuq.
4. **Iñuum saniani aquviġiñ, akiananiññaitchuaq.** Atautchikun tautukniq ilagiġniġnatuq, uuktuutigñatun piiññaitchuq.
5. **Atausiq piqutiq aullaqtitqugu tallimat qulit-lu minutes.** Iñuum savaaqsraq aktuuġlugu suli uqautilugu pisigilugu, atautchikunlu simmiktuġlusi.
6. **Nakuuruġmi nutqaġiñ.** Atausiq round naatchiġiñ, tavra atausiq aglak uqautigilugu suli tea-lik niuġilugu.

## Iñuuniaġniq suli annautiniq savaaqsraġni allanun atuqtauŋani

- **Aglaktaqsi savaaqsraġmi iḷḷatiqaqtut.** Photo pairs aglaktaqsi tikiġivinit atuqpagait, browser-mi taamnaġmi savaaqsraġmi iḷḷatiqaqtut. Kina allat nalauttaaqsaġvik suli tablet aullaqtisaŋagaat qimilguuġlugu.
- **External link-t ads-lu piiññaitchut.** Piqutit website-miñ atuġilugit, banner-lu uqausiillu iñuuniaġutit apiqsruit atuuġnaiġñagitki.
- **Akiḷḷiutit account-lu allami iḷḷatiġilugit.** Iñuŋ inna uniagutilaaŋiññagu screen-mi password suli card number apiqsruŋani.
- **Aullaqtiŋasugiññiq qimilguuġlugu.** Tablet tainna piviutigiñ iñuum iglumun aullaqtisaŋagumi.

Tautukniq ilisaġniq taiguġilugu [how to choose dementia apps and games](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) suli [setting up a tablet or phone](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Sumik piqutit makua pisuŋagaat suli pisuŋiññagaat

Online piqutit dementiaqaqtunut quviasuġniqmik piviuttaqaqtitkaat, aglaktaqsi atautchikun tautukkaġlugit, piqpagniqmullu ilaunŋaruq qiñiqsiññiġnaiġñatigun. Dementia naipiqtittiyaŋitchait, sukatchiyaŋitchait, suli annautitchuŋitchait. Nalunaitchuaq nalunaitchuaq [Federal Trade Commission-m 2016-mi Lumosity-mun piḷḷuata](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) brain-training uqausiŋi nalunaiqsauŋiḷḷugit. Qimilguuġilugu piqutiq, app suli website itqaumaġniq nakuuruġniqsauq, isumaġniq nalunaqutaaġniqsauq suli sukatchiniq utqiaġniqsauq uqausiqaqtuq.

Piqutit makua atuġilugit uvluġmi ilaŋat, atuutit, pisuaqtuutit, uqausiġniq aniqsaaġniqlu ilagalugit. Piqutit nalunaitchuakun piqpagniq taiguġniġumaguuk, taiguġilugu [how to play games with someone who has dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) suli [memory games that do not test](/activities/memory-games/).

## Qanuq nutqaġniq

Nutqaġiñ iñuum aniqsaŋŋiġumi, aulaniŋŋiġumi, qiñiqsaŋŋiġumi suli nalunaitchuŋŋiġumi. Nutqaġiñ piusuiññiġumi. Nutqaġniq sivulliqpaŋŋuŋaniqsauq. Uvlumi allami utlautiŋasaaqtuq, suli uqausiġniqmullu pisuaqtuutmunlu simmiktuŋasaaqtuq.
