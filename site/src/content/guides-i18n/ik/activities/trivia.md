Apiqqutit atuġauvaktut katimmani, uvluakunlu iḷagiit niġiusimmi atuġivagaat. Nalunaġviŋiññik inuk atauchimmiḷu kiunniqtuġlutik savaktaŋat, iñuk itqaumaniġmiḷuk nalupqisimaġmiḷu. Saimaġnaqtuat, unipkaat tusaayugaaġlugit nalaunŋaġaat. Ataani 50-nik apiqqutinik kiunniutinik uqautchiññiḷḷu, kinguliqsuġlugu qanuq savaktaksraŋat.

## Qanuq apiqqutit nalaunŋaġniŋat iñuŋmun itqaumaniġmiḷuŋmun?

Taimani ilitaqtaŋanik itqaumasuġlugit ilitchuġilugillu, kiunniq atausiq quviasuġlugu.

1. **Apiqsruġit taimaniġmiḷi.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) uqaqtuq, Alzheimer's-kut sivulliġmi nalunaiqsautaa nutaat ilitaqsimaniŋit itqaumaniq sapiġḷuaqtuq. Atuutit, uqausiit, iñuuniaġniq taimani ilitaqtaŋat nalaunŋaġaat.
2. **Kiunniutinik piŋasuutinik aitchuġiñ.** Atausiŋŋuuruŋniḷḷu malġuŋniḷḷu nalautchiġniq aŋiḷḷuaguuq iññi aullaqtilliqsuġnaitchuanit.
3. **Apiqqutit nipiquvlugit aŋiḷḷuaqtuġlugillu aglaġiñ.** Aglaŋiqsivik aŋiḷḷuaq uvvalu tablet-kuaq ikayuutausuuq tusaayumiḷuŋnun.
4. **Kiuŋit uqallautigiḷugit.** Kiñaliqaa ilisimaniŋitpat, quviasuġlugu uqallautigiñ suli. “Iḷisimavat!” uqaqtuŋŋitchutin.
5. **Nalunaġviŋit piŋitchut.** Iñuŋnun uvvalu katimmanun. Kisimik katimmapak nalunaiġivagaat piqataagun.
6. **Inuk ataasiq apiqsriŋitchuaq.** Kiuŋit kiñaliqammin aglaqtut. Atausiġmiḷu, atautchikun kiunniqtuġlusi: “Piano-guruŋa iḷumun. Iḷvich suma iḷitchuġiviuk?”
7. **Uqautchiñiq atuġlugu.** Kiuŋit aullaqtiutaat, naatchiutaaŋitpat. Unipkaat piŋasuutaat.
8. **Qitiġmi savakłusi.** Quliŋnik apiqqutinik unipkaanik akuliŋani nalaunŋaaq.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) uqaqtuq: nalupqisimaniq qiñiġlugu, iñuk nalaunŋaruq iḷitchuġilugu. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) apiqqutit nalunaiqsaaŋat sivulliġmi suli. Quliŋmi suli, malġuŋnik kiunniutinik atuġlutin uqautchiñiñmun tiguġlugit. Kinguliġmi, “una uvvalu una” apiqqutinik suliqsuġit.

## “Una uvvalu una” apiqqutit kiunniġmik iḷḷatiŋŋitchuat

Ukua nalaunŋaġaat suli tamaini, kinguliġmilu, kiunniq iḷḷatiŋŋitchuq. Apiqsruġit, tusaaġlugit, iḷisimaġuvin kiunniġlutin.

- Kapi uvvalu tii?
- Tariuq uvvalu iġġit?
- Qimmiq uvvalu qitunġaq?
- Auyaq uvvalu ukiak?
- Pie uvvalu cake?
- Mumigniq uvvalu qiñigniq?
- Siqiñiq uvvalu siqiñiq taġium?
- Sila iglum uvvalu kingunaa iglum?
- Radio uvvalu records?
- Pancakes uvvalu manniit?
- Roses uvvalu daisies?
- Aullaaq train-mi uvvalu kamik-mi?

Uqallautinik aglaŋiññik, atuġlugu [Uqallautit aglaŋit](/games/conversation-cards/).

## Qanuq apiqqutit savaktaksraŋat katimmani?

1. **Iñuŋnik aquppitigiñ takusinnaġlugit tusaaġlugillu.** Tusaayun ikayuqtit, irgutinik atuġlugit, TV-mik kamitlugu.
2. **Uqallautigiḷugu aullaqtit.** “Apiqqutinik taimaniġmiḷi pigikpiñ, iḷisimagupsi iññi uvamnin.”
3. **Apiqqutiq ataasiq malġuġniqtiqługu kiunniutit aŋiḷḷuaqtuġlugillu.**
4. **Kiuŋit katimmanit tamatkiumaniñ aglaġlugit.** Kiuŋit nalaunŋaġaat uqaqtigilugit tamaŋmun tusaayuglugit.
5. **Kiuŋit tamaŋmun uqallautigiñ,** kinguliqsuġlugu uqautchiñiq katimmun.
6. **Iñuŋnik maliġlugu.** Apiqqutiq ataasiq qulit-nik minutes-nik unipkaanik aullaqtippat, taima.
7. **Atuutimik uvvalu “una uvvalu una”-mik naatchiġlugu,** tamaŋmik nalaunŋaruġlugu.

Allanik katimmaqatigiinik, takulugu [katimmanun savaktuat](/activities/care-home-groups/).

## Qanuq allanik savaktaksraŋat

Iñuk nalupqisimaġmi qiñiġuvin, nipaiġmi uvvalu “iḷisimaiññiaġa” uqaqtuġmi malġuġniqtiq, aŋiḷḷuaŋaruaq: malġuŋnik kiunniutinik, atautchikun kiunniqtuġlusi, uvvalu “una uvvalu una”-mun suliqsuġit. Katimmaqatigiik apiqqutiq atauchimi uqallautigigumiŋ uvlaakun, taamna nalaunŋaaq. Kiuŋit iḷḷatiŋŋitchuat piŋasuutaaŋitpat. Atautchikun nalaunŋaruġlugit piŋasuutaat.
