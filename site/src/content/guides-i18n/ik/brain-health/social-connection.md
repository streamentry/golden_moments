Iñuit iñuiḷḷasiñiq piksraqtuat, taamnalu allannguqtuq utuqqaniġmi. Qimilġuutit uqaġaat aliŋasruniḷiq avataaniññiḷḷu utuqqami iñuuniaq atautchimukpaktuk anŋuivlutik isumalluamik pisuġniaqtuamik, uummatillu aŋalatchiaŋanik iviġavluġniġmillu. Qimilġuutit suli nalunaiqsuġniaqtuat qanuq atautchimukpaktuat. Una makpiġaaq uqaqtuq qanuq qimilġuutit nalunaiqsaat, qanuq iñuiḷḷasiñiqsraq, sumiklu atautchikun savaat piḷḷugiḷḷugiit isumalluamik iñuuruanun.

## Aliŋasruniḷiq avataaniññiḷḷu atautchimukpaktuak isumalluamun?

Ii, amigaitchuat qimilġuutit atautchimukpaktuat uqaġaat, aglaan nalunaiqsimaŋitchuq. [2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) uqaġaa avataaniññiq utuqqami iñuuniaq atauhiq 14-nit savautiksaġniŋaniññit. [UCL-m uqautigaa](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) imaatun 5%-mik nunami isumalluaniq.

Qimilġuutit nalunaiqsaat:

- **Iñuiḷḷasiñiq ikitqiaq, anŋuivluq.** Commission-mi uqaqtuq malġuk qimilġuutinik atautchimuktunik ikitqiatun iñuiḷḷasiñiq isumalluamun. Atausiq nalunaiqsaa imaatun 57%-mik anŋuivluq, aipaa imaatun 18%-mik.
- **Aliŋasruniḷiġlu.** Aliŋasruniḷiq, iviġavluġniq avataaniññiġmi, atautchimukpaktuq isumalluamik pisuġniaqtuamun.
- **Surgeon General-m isumaa.** May 2023-mi, U.S. Surgeon General [uqautigigaa aliŋasruniḷiq avataaniññiḷḷu](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf). Uqaqtuq aliŋasruniḷiq avataaniññiḷḷu akuniuġniq anŋuivlugisuktuak imaatun 50%-mik isumalluamik utuqqani. Atautchikun nalunaiqsaa iñuiḷḷasiñiq ikitqiaq anŋuivlugisuktuq imaatun 29%-mik uummatim aŋalatchiaŋanik, 32%-miglu stroke-mik.
- **Qaffiŋñiq.** [National Academies-m uqautigaa](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557972/) imaatun 24%-nik 65-nik ukiuġniaqtuanik avataaniŋŋuat.

Nalunaiqsuq: Lancet Commission-mi uqaqtuq iñuit ikitqisigisugaat iñuiḷḷasiñiq isumalluat aullaqtilluat nalunaiqsimanġitkumiŋa. Taamna uqaqtuq ilaŋat atautchimukpaktuat isumalluat pisuktait avataaniññiq, avataaniññiġmiñallaŋŋitkumiŋa. Atausiulluat qimilġuutit taimaiḷḷuat.

## Suna alġaqsraq aliŋasruniḷiq avataaniññiḷḷu akunninganni?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/loneliness-and-social-isolation/loneliness-and-social-isolation-tips-staying-connected) ima uqaqtuq. **Avataaniññiq** uqaqtuq ikitqianik iñuŋnik uqautigiyaġniq. **Aliŋasruniḷiq** uqaqtuq iviġavluġniq aliŋaruŋniḷḷu. Iñuk iñuŋniaqtuq atausiġmik quviasuġlunilu, iñuġlu aliŋaruŋniaqtuq iñuŋni iḷagilluni.

NIA-m uqautigai sunich anŋuivlugit:

- Tusaallavik tautullaviḷḷu aŋalatchiaq
- Aullaqiḷḷutiŋniq sapinaniq, aullaasuutimlu piŋił̣ḷutiq
- Iñuŋniq atausiġmik, allallu aŋalatchiat (nuliam tuquŋniq, savaktuiññiq)
- Isuma iḷisimaruaq allanguqtuq
- Iñuŋmik aŋalatchiamik munariñiq
- Maniŋmik sapinaniq uqausiġmullu sapinaniq

Tusaallavik piḷḷugiruq. NIA-m uqaqtuq tusaallavik aŋalatchiaq avataaniññiqtiġumiñaqtuq uqaqatigiñiq sapinami. [Tusaallavik niġrutitlu makpiġaaq](/brain-health/hearing/) uqaqtuq tusaallavik qimilġuusiq.

## Qanuq iñuiḷḷasiñiqsraq

NIA-m pitqusiġai savaktuat. Tainnaġmik malġugnigluuniit piŋ:

- **Pinasuarusiq uqautigiñiq.** Pisuqtaq uvlumi uvlumiluuniit uqaqatigiñiq, uqaluk, qillaut, aglaalluuniit iḷaŋnun, iñuqaġnun, nunaaqqataġnunlu.
- **Video-kkun uqaqatigiñiq.** Nutaaq qanuq atuġniq, ataramik atuġvik uumaniqsuq ikayuutinik. Tautukkik [uqaqatigiñiq makpiġaaq](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).
- **Iḷagiñiq.** Ilihaġvik, ukpiġvik, pisuutaġvik, ikayuuġviklu iñuiḷḷasiñiqtitsisuq.
- **Savaaq utaqqiq aullaqtiñiq** nutaaq savaaq iḷaŋnun iḷḷugu.
- **Aullaaqtuġniq iḷaŋnun.** Timimun savaaq iñuqaġmun iḷḷugu. [Aullaaqtuġniq makpiġaaq](/brain-health/exercise/) uqaqtuq.
- **Niviġġiaq** munarigisukkuviñ.
- **Nunami ikayuutit.** Utuqqat ikayuutiviat, Area Agencies on Aging, Eldercare Locator-lu (800-677-1116) ikayuġniaġaatin.

## Sumik atautchikun savaat piḷḷugiḷḷugiit isumalluami?

Iñuum isumalluamik, iñuiḷḷasiñiq quviasuŋniq, tuttummiq, iḷagiñiḷḷu piḷḷugiḷḷugiit, anŋuivluginiq pinani. Lancet Commission-mi uqaqtuq savaat ikayuutausuut quviasuŋniq piḷḷugu. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) uqaqtuq savaat ikayuġumiñaqtut iñuuniaġniq, aglaan ikayuġumiñaqtut iñuuniaq qanuq (pisuutaġniq, iviġavluġniq). [NIA-m](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) uqaqtuq iñuit Alzheimer-mik aullaaqtuġniaqtut quviasuktaminniklu savaġniaqtut.

Savaat nakuuruat:

1. **Agiġviit tautukkatigiñiq.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) apiqqutait nalautchaiḷḷugit, “Uqautigiginga…”. Tautukkik [uqaluktuat makpiġaaq](/activities/reminiscence/).
2. **Atautchikun piñiq.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) agiġviit tautuktuksraŋit, nalunaiqsautaiḷḷu, sivunniutaiḷḷu. Tautukkik [qanuq atautchikun piñiq](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).
3. **Iḷaŋnun agiġviit atuġlugit.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) agiġviich piŋasut savaaqtiksraqtuq, agiġviillu pisuktut uvani qaluktuutiḷḷutit.
4. **Iñuich katitñiq.** [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) nakuuruq iḷagiit katinngani [iñuich katimmaniḷḷu](/activities/care-home-groups/).
5. **Atuġvik, savaat, nautchiaq.** Tautukkik [atuutit](/activities/music/), [igluŋmi savaat](/activities/household-tasks/), tamaitalu [savaat](/activities/ideas/).
6. **Pulaaqtaġniq ikayuġlugu.** [Pulaaqtaġniq makpiġaaq](/activities/visiting/) uqaqtuq naatchitkaluaq, nipitaiḷḷu pulaaqtaġniq.

Mikiruŋniaqtuq tuttummiq. Nipitaiḷḷu iglu atauhiq malġugnigluuniit iñuich nakuuruaqtuq iñugiaktuanit. Iñuk taqtuq piñigisukkuŋił̣ḷuni, nutqaqtitñiq nakuuruq. Atautchikun aquviñiq kaffimik imiqł̣ugu nakuuruq.

Iñuum isumalluamik atausiġmik iñuuruamun, NIA-m pitqusiġaa paqitñiq iñuŋmik atqunnaqtuamik pulaaqtaġumiñaqtuamik uqaqatigiġumiñaqtuamiglu, ikayuuġumiñaqtuamiglu. Uqaqtuġlu memory cafe-mik, tuttummiq katitvik iñuŋnun isumalluamik iḷaŋŋuanullu.

## Munarirut aliŋaruŋniaqtuġlu

NIA-m uqaqtuq iñuŋmik munariñiq avataaniññiqtiġumiñaqtuq. Pisuġlutin iñuqaġnun, ikayuutiqatignun, taaqtivigmunlu. [Munariñiq makpiġaaq](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) uqaqtuq ikayuutinik.

## Uqautigilugu taakti

Iḷisiluuniit munarigiviḷḷu aliŋaruŋniq avataaniññiġlu akuniuġniq, uqautigilugu taakti. NIA-m pitqusiġaa uqautigiñiq allanguġniŋit (tuquŋniq iḷagiitni), taaktim nalupqisukł̣ugu. Iviġavluġniq akuniuġniq iviġavluġniġmiñaqtuq, ikayuutauniaqtuaq. Utuqqaq avataaniññiqsiruq, taamna uqautigisaġniaqtuq taaktimun. Tautukkik [nalunaitchuat aullaqtilluat taaktimullu](/brain-health/warning-signs/).

Una makpiġaaq nalunaitchuq, taaktim uqautinani. Tautukkik [taaktim uqautiniŋa](/medical-disclaimer/).
