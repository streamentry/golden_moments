"Elekere ole ka dọkịta na-anọ?" Nkeji iri ka e mesịrị: "Elekere ole ka dọkịta na-anọ?" Ịnụ otu ajụjụ ugboro ugboro bụ otu n'ime akụkụ kacha ewe ike nke ilekọta onye na-ebi ná dementia. Ọ bụ ihe a na-ahụkarị, a naghị eme ya n'ihi ebumnuche, e nwekwara ụzọ dị jụụ isi zaghachi nke na-eme ka ụbọchị dị mfe maka unu abụọ.

## Gịnị mere onye nwere dementia ji ajụ otu ajụjụ ugboro ugboro?

N'ọtụtụ ọnọdụ n'ihi ncheta. Dementia na-emetụta ncheta mkpụmkpụ, ya mere onye ahụ na-adịghị echeta na ọ jụrụ, ma ọ bụ na-echeghị azịza ahụ. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/alzheimers-today-volume-17-number-3/navigating-difficult-behaviors/) na-akọwa ịjụ ajụjụ ọtụtụ ugboro dị ka ihe a na-ahụkarị ma a na-atụ anya ya.

Ajụjụ ahụ na-ebukwa mmetụta mgbe ụfọdụ. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) na-akọwa na ajụjụ a na-ajụ ugboro ugboro nwere ike igosi nchegbu, arịrịọ maka enyemaka, ma ọ bụ egwu na enweghị nchekwa, na onye ahụ na-achọkarị nkasi obi na ihe a maara. "Mgbe anyị na-aga?" nwere ike ịpụta n'ezie "Egwu na-atụ m ịga." Ịdị ya ka ọ nweghị ihe ọ na-eme, egwu, mkpọtụ na mgbanwe n'usoro ụbọchị nwere ike ịkpalite ya, ajụjụ a na-ajụ ugboro ugboro nwekwara ike igosi mkpa, dị ka mkpa ịga ụlọ mposi ([Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/)).

## Gịnị ka m kwesịrị ikwu?

Zaa n'udo na n'ụzọ mkpụmkpụ, dị ka ọ bụ nke mbụ.

- **Mee ka azịza ahụ dị mkpụmkpụ ma bụrụ otu.** "Dọkịta na-anọ n'elekere abụọ. Aga m anọnyere gị." Azịza na-agbanwe agbanwe adịghị mfe ịnata.
- **Zaa mmetụta ahụ.** "Ị na-eche banyere dọkịta. Ọ bụ n'ehihie taa, m ga-ebukwa anyị. Ọ nweghị ihe ị ga-eme."
- **Jiri olu dị ọkụ obi na imetụ aka dị nro,** ma ọ bụrụ na ọ masịrị ya imetụ aka. Alzheimer's Association na-adụ ọdụ ịnọ jụụ ma ghara iji ihe ezi uche ma ọ bụ ịrụ ụka.
- **Nye azịza ọ na-achọ,** ugboro ole ọ dị mkpa. Alzheimer's Association na-ekwu ka ị kwughachi ya ugboro ole ọ na-ajụ.

## Gịnị ka m kwesịrị izere?

- **"I jụwo m nke ahụ."** [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) na-adụ ọdụ megide igosi na ọ jụụrụ ugbu a. Ọ nweghị ike inyere ya aka icheta, ọ nwekwara ike ịdị ka a na-abara ya mba.
- **Nkọwa ogologo.** Ọtụtụ okwu na-enye ọtụtụ ihe a ga-echefu.
- **Ịgbapụ ume na-egosi iwe, ịtụgharị anya ma ọ bụ olu siri ike.** [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) na-adụ ọdụ ndidi na ịghara igosi iwe. Nke ahụ siri ike; lee akụkụ banyere izu ike n'okpuru.

## Ngwa dị mfe nke na-azaa ajụjụ maka gị

Mgbe onye ahụ ka nwere ike ịgụ ihe, azịza e dere nke ọ na-ahụ na-enyekarị aka karịa nke a na-ekwu.

| Ngwa | Otu esi eji ya |
| --- | --- |
| Ndetu n'mkpụrụedemede buru ibu | "Dọkịta taa n'elekere 2 nke ehihie. Ada ga-akwọ ụgbọ." Tinye ya ebe ọ na-elekarị |
| Bọọdụ ọcha | Atụmatụ taa n'ahịrị atọ ma ọ bụ anọ; hichapụ ma dee ọzọ kwa ụtụtụ |
| Elekere buru ibu nwere ụbọchị na ụbọchị nke ọnwa | Na-enyere aka n'"Kedu ụbọchị taa?" na "Oge eruola?" |
| Kalenda | Dee akara n'elu ụbọchị ọ bụla gara aga; kee okirikiri gburugburu ụbọchị nleta ọzọ |
| Foto ndị a maara | Foto nke onye na-abịa nwere ike ịzaa "Onye na-abịa leta?" |

Echiche ndị a sitere na [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) na [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf). Gosi ndetu ahụ nwayọọ ("Ka anyị lee bọọdụ ahụ ọnụ") kama ịsị "Edere m ya maka gị."

## Gwa ha banyere ihe nso oge ha

Ikwu banyere oge nleta ma ọ bụ njem ụbọchị ole na ole tupu oge nwere ike ịghọ awa nke ajụjụ nchegbu. Family Caregiver Alliance na Alzheimer's Los Angeles abụọ na-atụ aro ikwu banyere atụmatụ, karịsịa nke na-akpata nrụgide dị ka nleta dọkịta ma ọ bụ ịsa ahụ, naanị nso oge ha ga-eme. Wee kwuo ihe ga-eme ọzọ, otu nzọụkwụ n'otu oge.

## Ọrụ nwere ike ịkpali uche n'ebe ọzọ

Ozugbo ị zara ma nabata mmetụta ahụ, ọrụ dị jụụ, na-atọ ụtọ nwere ike inye aka, karịsịa ma ọ bụrụ na ịdị ya ka ọ nweghị ihe ọ na-eme (boredom) bụ ihe dị n'azụ ajụjụ ndị ahụ. Isi mmalite ọ bụla dị n'elu na-atụ aro ichee uche n'ihe onye ahụ masịrị.

1. **Obere egwuregwu dị mfe ọnụ.** [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) na-eme ka foto ndị ahụ na-anọ ebe a na-ahụ ha, ya mere ọ dịghị ihe a ga-echeta.
2. **Ọrụ a maara.** Ịpịkọta akwa nhicha, ịhazi sọks ma ọ bụ ịdọba tebụl. [Sort together](/games/sort-together/) bụ ụdị ya n'elu ihuenyo.
3. **Kwuo banyere ihe ọ hụrụ n'anya.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) na-enye ajụjụ mepere emepe na-enweghị azịza ziri ezi.
4. **Egwu, ije ma ọ bụ nri ntakịrị.** National Institute on Aging depụtara ndị a n'etiti ụzọ dị mfe isi chee uche n'ebe ọzọ.
5. **Nkwụsị dị jụụ.** Nọdụ ọnụ, lee n'èzí windo, ma ọ bụ nwaa oge [iku ume nwayọọ](/games/calm-breathing/) ọnụ.

Ọ bụrụ na ha alaghachi n'ajụjụ ahụ, zaa ya ọzọ, n'obi ọma. Nke ahụ dị mma.

## Lekwasị anya n'ụkpụrụ

Alzheimer's Association na-atụ aro ịtụle ma ajụjụ ndị ahụ na-abịa n'oge ụfọdụ n'ụbọchị, gburugburu ndị mmadụ ụfọdụ ma ọ bụ n'ebe ụfọdụ. Ọ bụrụ na ha na-amalite mgbe niile tupu ndị ọbịa abịa, ma ọ bụ n'ehihie ka anyanwụ na-agbada, ị nwere ike ịhazi tupu oge: ndetu banyere nleta, ọrụ a maara dị njikere, ụlọ dị jụụ karị. Nduzi anyị banyere [ọrụ dị jụụ maka ịgbụ mgbede](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) nwere ike inye aka ma ọ bụrụ na mgbede bụ oge kacha ọ siri ike.

## Lekọta onwe gị

Ịzaa otu ajụjụ ugboro iri abụọ na-ewe ike. Ọ bụrụ na ị na-eche na ndidi gị na-agwụ ma onye ahụ nọ n'ebe nchekwa, banye n'ime ụlọ ọzọ nkeji ole na ole wee kuo ume, dị ka [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf) na-atụ aro. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) na-agba ndị na-elekọta ume ka ha rịọ enyemaka ma tụlee nlekọta izu ike (respite care). Ihe ndị ọzọ dị n'[nduzi ilekọta onwe gị maka ndị na-elekọta](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) anyị.

## Oge ole ka m kwesịrị ikpọ dọkịta?

Kpọọ dọkịta ha ngwa ngwa ma ọ bụrụ na ajụjụ, mgbagwoju anya ma ọ bụ omume gbanwere na mberede ma ọ bụ ka njọ nke ukwuu n'ime awa ma ọ bụ ụbọchị. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) na-ekwu na omume na-abịa na mberede ma ọ bụ na-agbanwe ngwa ngwa, karịsịa mgbe ọrịa ma ọ bụ mgbanwe ọgwụ gasịrị, kwesịrị ịgwa dọkịta ozugbo. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/delirium) na-akọwa na ọrịa, ọgwụ, enweghị mmiri zuru ezu n'ahụ, mgbu ma ọ bụ afọ ịkpọchi nwere ike ịkpata delirium, mgbagwoju anya na mberede, siri ike nke chọrọ ọgwụgwọ ngwa ngwa. Kpọọ nọmba mberede nke mpaghara gị ma ọ bụrụ na onye ọ bụla nọ n'ihe egwu.
