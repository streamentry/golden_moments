Tablet nwere ike ịbụ ezigbo ụzọ isi kerịta foto, egwu na egwuregwu dị mfe. Ọ nwekwara ike ịgbagwoju anya: otu mgbe a pịrị ihe n'enweghị ebumnuche meghee ngwa ọzọ, ọkwa pụta, okwu pere mpe, ma ọ bụ ihuenyo adaba n'ọchịchịrị n'etiti egwuregwu. Obere nhazi site n'aka gị na-edozi ọtụtụ n'ime ya.

Nzọụkwụ ndị a na-eji ntọala dị n'ime iPhone, iPad na Android. Aha menu nwere ike ịgbanwe n'etiti ụdị sọftụwia na ndị na-emepụta ngwaọrụ, ya mere ọ bụrụ na ihe dị iche, soro ibe Apple ma ọ bụ Google jikọtara maka ngwaọrụ gị. Mee nhazi ahụ n'onwe gị, tupu unu nọdụ ọnụ.

## Onye nwere dementia ọ̀ kwesịrị iji tablet ma ọ bụ ekwentị?

Ọ dabere n'onye ahụ. Ọtụtụ ndị na-enwe obi ụtọ iji tablet mgbe onye nọ n'akụkụ ha, ọbụna ma ọ bụrụ na ha agaghị ejikwa ya naanị ha. Malite site n'iji ya ọnụ, n'otu ngwa meghere, nkeji ole na ole. Ọ bụrụ na ọ na-eweta obi ụtọ na mkparịta ụka, gaa n'ihu. Ọ bụrụ na ọ na-eweta mgbagwoju anya ma ọ bụ iwe, album foto, ibe e bipụtara ma ọ bụ egwu nwere ike ịdabara ha nke ọma. [Ọrụ anyị a ga-ebipụta](/activities/printable/) bụ otu nhọrọ ọzọ.

Tablet na-adịkarị mfe karịa ekwentị: ihuenyo na foto buru ibu, ọ bụghịkwa ụzọ nzọpụta mberede nke onye ahụ.

## Kedu ka m ga-esi kpọchie tablet ma ọ bụ ekwentị n'otu ngwa?

Jiri Guided Access n'iPhone na iPad, ma ọ bụ app pinning na Android. Ha abụọ na-eme ka ihuenyo nọrọ n'otu ngwa ruo mgbe ị tinyere koodu, ka ịpị ma ọ bụ ịkụ aka n'enweghị ebumnuche ghara imeghe ihe ọzọ.

### iPad na iPhone: Guided Access

Site na [ntụziaka Guided Access nke Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111795):

1. Meghee **Settings**, pịa **Accessibility**, wee pịa **Guided Access** ma gbanye ya.
2. N'okpuru **Passcode Settings**, tọọ passcode Guided Access. Ị nwekwara ike ikwe ka Face ID ma ọ bụ Touch ID.
3. Nhọrọ: n'okpuru **Display Auto-Lock**, họrọ ka ihuenyo si mechie ngwa ngwa n'oge ọrụ ahụ, ka o ghara ịdaba n'ọchịchịrị n'etiti egwuregwu.
4. Meghee ngwa ịchọrọ iji.
5. Pịa ugboro atọ bọtịn dị n'akụkụ (iPhone), bọtịn dị n'elu (iPad), ma ọ bụ bọtịn Home n'ngwaọrụ ochie.
6. Nhọrọ: kee okirikiri gburugburu akụkụ ihuenyo ọ bụla ịchọrọ ka ọ ghara ịza imetụ aka. Pịa **Options** (ma ọ bụ **Session Settings**) iji gbanyụọ bọtịn ụda ma ọ bụ kwụsị ihuenyo ịtụgharị.
7. Pịa **Start**.

Iji kwụsị oge ahụ, pịa ugboro atọ otu bọtịn ahụ ọzọ, tinye passcode, wee pịa **End**. Apple na-ekwu na n'ụfọdụ ụdị sọftụwia ochie ị ga-apị ugboro abụọ kama.

**Dị mkpa:** Apple na-ekwu na Crash Detection na Emergency Services adịghị n'oge Guided Access. Ahapụla Guided Access na-agba ọkụ n'ekwentị onye ahụ nwere ike ịchọ maka oku mberede.

### Android: app pinning

Site na [enyemaka Android nke Google banyere ịkpọchi ihuenyo n'otu ngwa](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9455138?hl=en):

1. Meghee ngwa **Settings**.
2. Pịa **Security** (ma ọ bụ **Security & location**), wee **Advanced**, wee **App pinning**. Ọ bụrụ na ị chọtaghị ya, lelee ibe enyemaka nke onye mepụtara ngwaọrụ gị.
3. Gbanye **App pinning**. A ga-achọ PIN gị, ụkpụrụ ma ọ bụ paswọọdụ iji wepụ ya.
4. Meghee ngwa ịchọrọ. Swaịp elu ruo etiti ihuenyo wee jide iji mepee Overview (ma ọ bụ pịa Overview site na nhazi bọtịn 3).
5. N'elu ihe ngosi ngwa ahụ, pịa akara ngwa ahụ, wee pịa **Pin**.

Iji wepụ ya site na nhazi gesture, swaịp elu wee jide. Site na nhazi bọtịn 3, metụ ma jide Back na Overview. Site na nhazi bọtịn 2, metụ ma jide Back na Home. Wee tinye PIN gị, ụkpụrụ ma ọ bụ paswọọdụ.

## E nwere ụdị dị mfe maka iPhone ma ọ bụ iPad niile?

Ee. Assistive Access nke Apple na-anọchi ihuenyo ụlọ nkịtị n'ihuenyo dị mfe karị. [Apple na-akọwa ya](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-iphone/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ios) dị ka nke e mere ka iPhone dị mfe ka ndị nwere nsogbu echiche jiri ya n'onwe ha, nwere ihe buru ibu n'ihuenyo na njirimara lekwasịrị anya karị. Apple na-enye [otu njirimara ahụ n'iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-ipad/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ipados).

- **Onye na-atọ ya.** Apple na-atụ aro ka onye ntụkwasị obi na-enyere aka, dị ka onye ezinụlọ ma ọ bụ onye na-elekọta nke maara onye ahụ, nyere aka họrọ ngwa na njirimara.
- **Ebe a ga-amalite.** Gaa na **Settings > Accessibility > Assistive Access**, pịa **Set Up Assistive Access**, wee **Continue**.
- **Ngwa.** Ị na-ahọrọ ngwa ndị ga-apụta. Ụfọdụ ngwa Apple nwere ụdị dị mfe pụrụ iche, a pụkwara itinye ọtụtụ ngwa ndị ọzọ n'ụdị ha na-adịkarị.
- **Ọdịdị.** Họrọ **Rows** (ndepụta dị mfe ịgụ) ma ọ bụ **Grid** (ihe buru ibu na-ekwusi ike foto na akara).
- **Passcode.** Ị na-atọ passcode Assistive Access dị iche iji banye, pụọ ma ọ bụ gbanwee ntọala. Apple na-atụ aro itọ Apple Account mgbake n'oge nhazi, n'ihi na n'ejighị ya, ichefu passcode pụtara ịtọgharịa ngwaọrụ ahụ.

Assistive Access nwere ike ịdabara onye na-eji ngwaọrụ nke ya kwa ụbọchị. Guided Access ma ọ bụ app pinning dị ngwa karịa maka oge egwuregwu e kerịtara.

## Kedu ka m ga-esi mee ka okwu na foto buru ibu?

Mee ka okwu na nha ihuenyo buru ibu, wee lelee na bọtịn ka dabara n'ihuenyo.

**iPhone na iPad.** Dị ka [ntụziaka nha okwu nke Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102453) siri kwuo, gaa na **Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size** wee dọta slider. Maka okwu karịrị nke ahụ buru ibu, gaa na **Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text**. Apple na-ekwu na okwu buru ibu nke ukwuu nwere ike ime ka ụfọdụ bọtịn siere ike ịpị, ya mere gbanwee ma ọ bụrụ na ọ dị mkpa.

**Android.** Dị ka [enyemaka ntọala okwu na ihuenyo nke Google](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/11183305?hl=en) siri kwuo, meghee **Settings** wee chọọ **Font size**, wee kwaga slider ahụ. Chọọ **Display size** iji mee ka ihe niile dị n'ihuenyo buru ibu. Ị nwekwara ike ịgbanye **Bold text** n'okpuru **Accessibility > Display size and text**. Ụfọdụ ngwa anaghị agbaso ntọala ndị a.

N'egwuregwu dị ka [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) na [Find the Same](/games/find-the-same/), ị nwekwara ike ịhọrọ obere mpempe ma ọ bụ nhọrọ ole na ole, nke na-eme ka ihe dị mfe n'ihuenyo.

## Kedu ka m ga-esi gbochie ọkwa na pop-ups?

Gbanyụọ ọkwa maka ngwa ndị ịchọghị, jiri Do Not Disturb n'oge ọrụ.

**iPhone na iPad.** Gaa na **Settings > Notifications**, pịa ngwa n'okpuru **Notification Style**, wee gbanyụọ **Allow Notifications** ([ntụziaka ntọala ọkwa nke Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios)). Iji mee ka oku, ọkwa na ọkwa dị jụụ ruo oge, meghee Control Center wee gbanye **Do Not Disturb** site na bọtịn **Focus** ([enyemaka Do Not Disturb nke Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105112)). Ị nwere ike ịhazi oge n'okpuru **Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb > Set a Schedule**.

**Android.** Swaịp ala site n'elu ihuenyo wee pịa **Do Not Disturb**, ma ọ bụ gaa na **Settings > Modes > Do Not Disturb** ([enyemaka Do Not Disturb nke Google](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9069335?hl=en)). N'okpuru **Notification filters** ị nwere ike ịhọrọ ndị mmadụ na ngwa ndị ka nwere ike ịbata. Ntọala dị iche site n'onye mepụtara ngwaọrụ.

## Kedu ka m ga-esi mee ihuenyo ụlọ dị mfe?

Debe ngwa ole na ole a maara n'otu ihuenyo wee wepụ ndị ọzọ n'anya. Ọ nweghị otu ntọala maka nke a; ọ bụ iwepụ ihe ndị na-ejuputa ihuenyo.

- **Họrọ ngwa atọ ruo isii.** Dị ka ihe atụ, foto, egwu, ngwa oku vidiyo na otu egwuregwu.
- **Tinye ha n'ihuenyo mbụ,** n'otu ebe oge ọ bụla, wee kwaga ihe niile ọzọ n'ihuenyo nke abụọ ma ọ bụ n'ime folda.
- **Jiri ihe ndabere dị mfe, nwere ọdịiche agba doro anya,** dị ka otu agba ma ọ bụ otu foto a maara, ka akara pụta ìhè.
- **Hichapụ ngwa ndị ịchọghị.** Obere ngwa pụtara obere ohere mgbagwoju anya na obere data a na-achịkọta.
- **Tụlee Assistive Access** n'ngwaọrụ Apple ma ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ka ngwaọrụ ahụ mee nke a maka gị.

## Gịnị banyere ụda?

Tọọ ụda dị mma tupu ị nyefee ngwaọrụ ahụ. Site na Guided Access, ị nwere ike ịgbanyụ bọtịn ụda ka ọ ghara ịgbanwe n'etiti ọrụ. Lelee na ihe enyemaka maka ịnụ ihe dị n'ntị ma na-arụ ọrụ.

## Ebee ka tablet kwesịrị ibi?

Nye ya ihe e debere ya na ebe a na-ejiri ya ọkụ mgbe niile.

- **Ihe e debere ya ma ọ bụ mkpuchi** nke na-ejide tablet ka ọ guzo n'elu tebụl pụtara na ọ dịghị onye kwesịrị ijide ya, igosi aka dịkwa mfe.
- **Otu ebe a na-ejiri ya ọkụ** nke na-adịghị agagharị, ọ bụrụ na o kwere omume ihe e debere ya na-ejide ọkụ n'elu tebụl n'ime ụlọ ị na-eji kwa ụbọchị. Tablet na-enwe ọkụ mgbe niile ma na-anọ n'otu ebe.
- **Belata ìhè na-egbu anya.** Debe ihuenyo n'ebe windo na-egbu ìhè na-adịghị agbanye ya.

## Ogologo oge ole n'ihuenyo ka dị mma?

Ọ nweghị ọnụ ọgụgụ nkeji ziri ezi. Gbalịa maka oge mkpụmkpụ, e kerịtara dị ka otu akụkụ nke ụbọchị zuru ezu nke gụnyere mmegharị, egwu, ọrụ ụlọ, mkparịta ụka na oge n'èzí. N'ihi na mgbede nwere ọtụtụ ọrụ nwere ike ịgbakwunye enweghị izu ike n'ịgbụ mgbede, tụlee idebe ọrụ ihuenyo n'oge mbụ n'ụbọchị. Lee [ọrụ dị jụụ maka ịgbụ mgbede](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Iji tablet ọnụ, kama ịkpọ ya onye na-elekọta nwa, na-eme ya ihe mmekọrịta. Nduzi anyị banyere [ịkpọ egwuregwu ọnụ](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) na-akọwa otu esi eme ya.

## Kedu ka m ga-esi chekwaa nzuzo ya n'ngwaọrụ ahụ?

Belata ihe ngwa nwere ike ịchịkọta, ekwela chee na iwu nzuzo ahụike kpuchiri ya. U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) [na-akọwa](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) na iwu HIPAA anaghị echekwa ozi ahụike e chekwara n'ekwentị na tablet onwe onye ma ọ bụ nke a na-enweta site na ha n'ozuzu. N'ọtụtụ ọnọdụ, HIPAA anaghịkwa echekwa data e tinyere n'ngwa maka ojiji onwe onye belụsọ ma ngwa ahụ sitere n'aka onye na-enye nlekọta ahụike nwere ikike, atụmatụ ahụike ma ọ bụ otu n'ime ndị ha na-arụkọ ọrụ.

HHS na-atụ aro:

- izere ngwa ndị na-adịghị mkpa ma ọ bụ ndị a na-enweghị ebumnuche, karịsịa ndị "n'efu";
- ịghara inye ngwa ohere n'ebe ị nọ belụsọ ma ha chọrọ ya n'ezie, dị ka maps.

Tụlekwa:

- **Jiri passcode** n'ngwaọrụ ahụ, dekwa ya ebe nchekwa.
- **Lelee ikike** maka foto, igwefoto, microfon, ndị nkwurịta okwu na ebe ị nọ, gbanyụọ nke ọ bụla ngwa na-achọghị.
- **Lelee ihe ngwa na-achịkọta** tupu itinye ya. [Nduzi ịhọrọ ngwa na egwuregwu dementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) anyị nwere ndepụta nzuzo.
- **Lezie anya n'foto ezinụlọ.** Kerịta naanị foto onye ahụ ga-enwe obi ụtọ na ha, mara kwa ebe ngwa na-edebe ha. N'[Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), foto ndị ị họọrọ na-anọ n'ihe nchọgharị gị.
- **Gbanyụọ ịzụta n'ime ngwa** ma ọ bụ wepụ nkọwa ịkwụ ụgwọ n'akaụntụ onye ahụ na-eji.
