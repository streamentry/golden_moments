Nkaeɛ ne asɛm a yɛka ho wɔ nkɔ ho: fie a wɔtenaa mmere mu, adwuma a wɔyɛe firi mfiase, aduane a wɔpɛ, dwom, ayɛyie ne mmere a wɔyɛ anigye. Ma nnipa pii a wɔwɔ dementia, asɛm a ɛyɛ nyansa firi wɔn nkwa ho ne bere a ɛyɛ anigye pii wɔ da no mu. Nea ɛho hia ne sɛ wobisa wɔ ɔkwan a ɛma obi ka asɛm na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔsɔ nkaeɛ no hwɛ. Nsɛmmisa yi ka ho wɔ nsɛm mu, na nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛboa ka ho.

## Mmara a ɛfa nsɛmmisa a ɛfa nkaeɛ ho

- **Bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛmma obi ka asɛm.** "Ka ho asɛm..." ne "Ɛte sɛ dɛn..." ma obi ka asɛm. "Wo hu...?" bisa mmuae a ɛteɛ.
- **Nnsɔ hwɛ.** Gyae "Hwan ne saa?", "Afe bɛn na ɛyɛ saa?" ne "Wo kae din no?"
- **Gye asɛm biara a ɛba.** Sɛ asɛm no sesa anaa wɔn baabi kɔ ntam a, di anigye na bisa nneɛma a ɛwɔ adwene mu.
- **Fa nsɛmmisa baako baako.** Gyina, tie, na afei bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.
- **Ma obi no ara hwɛ kwan.** Sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka asɛm foforɔ a, kɔ ne nkyɛn.

[National Institute on Aging's guidance on communicating](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) ka sɛ wɔnkasa nkɔsoɔ, wɔnnkasa kɔkɔɔkɔɔ, na wɔngyina sɛ obi no rehwehwɛ n'asɛm. Mmara yi ho hia wɔ ha sen wɔ baabi biara.

## Fie ne mmofra

- Ka me ho asɛm fie a wɔtenaa wo mmere mu. Ɛdeɛn na ɛte wɔ kitchen mu?
- Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ wɔ abɔnten bere a wo yɛ ketewa?
- Hena na mmofra a wɔn ne wo tenaa nkuraase so?
- Ɛdeɛn na wo mpɔtam kɔsi sɛ ɛte? Wo ne obi bɛtena ho anaa?

## Adwuma ne nneɛma a wɔyɛ

- Adwuma bɛn na woyɛɛ a wɔpɛ sen biara?
- Ka adwuma bi a ɛmaa wo ani gyee wɔ adwuma mu ho asɛm.
- Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ woyɛ de wo nsa, wɔ wo mmere?
- Hena na wokɔ akyiri nkyerɛkyerɛ adwuma ho sen obiara?

## Aduane ne aduane a wɔyɛ

- Dɛn na wo abusua didii adekyee?
- Ka aduane bi a obi wɔ wɔ wo abusua mu a wɔyɛ yiye ho asɛm.
- Ɛwɔ baabi a wukɔ kɔtɔ aduane wɔ mmere a wo yɛ ketewa?
- Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ kɛse a ɛka ho asɛm fie?

Asɛm a ɛfa aduane ka ho yɛ adwuma bi a ɛfa [sensory activities](/activities/sensory/) ho, na sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛw a, ɛbɛyɛ yiye sɛ wobɛma obi a ɔpɛ nnuan kakraa.

## Dwom ne ayɛyie

- Dwom bɛn na wo dwom anaa wotenaa ahoɔfɛ wɔ mmere a wo yɛ ketewa?
- Hena na wo dwom bɛyɛ ɔdɛfoɔ a wopɛ sen biara?
- Ɛwɔ baabi bɛn na wotee dwom wɔ saa mmere no: fie, ayɛyie, asɔredan?

Wɔ playlist ne dwom ho nkyerɛkyerɛ mu, hwɛ [music activities for people with dementia](/activities/music/).

## Ayɛyie ne mmere a wɔyɛ anigye

- Ayɛyie bɛn na wopɛ sen biara wɔ wo mmere?
- Ka ɔhaw a wokae wɔ ayɛyie a wɔyɛe.
- Ɛdeɛn na abusua no yɛ wɔ anwummere a ɛyɛ ɔhyew?
- Sɛnea abusua no yɛ ayɛyie mmere a ɛsesa no ho?

Sɛ wopɛ adwuma a ɛfa ayɛyie biako ho, hwɛ [holiday activities for someone with dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Abusua ne nnamfo

- Ka obi a ɔmaa wo ani gyee sen biara ho asɛm.
- Ɛdeɛn na wo awareɛ da te?
- Ka wo mmofra ho asɛm bere a wɔyɛ ketewa.
- Dɛn na nnamfo no frɛɛ wo?

Bisa abusua nsɛmmisa no ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw. Sɛ obi no awu obi, di n'akwan na nnsɛnkyerɛ nsɛm a ɛbɛma ɔhaw.

## Baabi ne nature

- Ka baabi a wopɛ sɛ wosusuw ho sen biara ho asɛm.
- Ɛdeɛn na ewim te wɔ baabi a wotenaa?
- Wo wɔ afuom anaa dua a wopɛ?
- Ka akwantuo bi a woyɛɛ a wopɛ ho asɛm.

## Mfonini ne nneɛma

Mfonini yɛ asɛm a ɛfiri ankasa. Fa mfonini ba ɛboɔ wo ani, na ka sɛ, "Ka eyi ho asɛm." [Alzheimer's Association guidance on activities](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ka sɛ wobɛde nneɛma a wonim, mfonini ne dwom bɛbue asɛm. Adekyeɛ a ɛwɔ nneɛma kakraa a ɛfiri obi nkwa mu nso betumi ayɛ yiye; hwɛ [reminiscence activities](/activities/reminiscence/) ma nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa sɛnea wobɛyɛ ho.

## Sɛ nkaeɛ no ɔhaw

Nkaeɛ bi betumi ama awerɛhoɔ, suro anaa abufuw. Sɛ obi no ɔboɔ, mma wo ho nkɔ so. Ka asɛm a ɛyɛ nyansa sɛ, "Ɛte sɛ bere a ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛtra. Wopɛ sɛ yɛka asɛm a ɛyɛ anigye?" Afei sesa asɛm no, de nnuan kɔ, anaa tie dwom a ɔpɛ.

## Di adwuma a ɛyɛ nkɔ so anaa nantew

Nkaeɛ no akyi, nantew a ɛyɛ mmerɛw betumi ama anigye atena hɔ. Sɛ wopɛ, yɛ [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) a ɛwɔ nsɛmmisa a ɛbue asɛm, anaa [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) a ɛfa asɛm a ɛyɛ tete ho. Nsɛm a ɛfa nkitahodie a ɛyɛ nnipa ne wɔn ho, hwɛ [communication tips for someone with dementia](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
