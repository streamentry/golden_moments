Nnipa pii hwehwɛ nnwomkɔ ahoɔfɛ ma dementia efisɛ wɔpɛ adwuma a wobetumi de ayɛ bom a ɛnhia sitoa, akontaa anaa credit card. Nneɛma a ɛyɛ yiye ne nea ɛyɛ mmerɛw, ɛfa mfonini ho, na ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛgyae. Ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ anigye a wɔne wɔn di, ɛnyɛ asɛnka. Saa kratafa yi kyerɛ nea wɔhwehwɛ, sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nnwomkɔ, ne nea nnwomkɔ yi betumi ayɛ ne nea ɛntumi nyɛ.

## Dɛn na ɛma nnwomkɔ fata dementia?

Nnwomkɔ fata nnipa pii a wɔwɔ dementia sɛ ɛde hu ho ara na ɛnyɛ ɛde nkaebɔ. Mfonini a wɔde hyɛ ho (mfonini a ɛte sɛ), sɛ wobɛto mfonini a ɛfa "shadow" ho, anaa sɛ wɔbɛhyɛ nneɛma wɔ basket a ɛfata mu, yɛ adwuma wɔ ani a ɔhu so. Nnwomkɔ a ɛde mmara, asɛm, nkontaabu anaa ntoaso a obi kaeɛ ho ho hia no mma ɔhaw ba.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) kyerɛ adwuma a ɛfa nneɛma a obi pɛ ne nea ɔbetumi ayɛ ho, na ɛma ɔnya nkonim ankasa. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ka sɛ wɔsesa adwuma sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ abilities sesa, na wɔnnkɔ so gyae.

## Golden Moments nnwomkɔ a wobetumi abue wɔ browser mu

Nnwomkɔ a ɛwɔ ase yi biara bue firi websaet so ɛmmehia download. Ɛbɛyɛ ɛfa tablet anaa komputa, na ebinom betumi asa nso.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Mfonini a ɛyɛ kɛse ne nea ɛtɔ mu, mfonini no hunu, na biribiara nni timer anaa nkontaabu. Ɛyɛ fɛ ma nnwomkɔ a ɛdi kan.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Picture biako wɔ soro, na ɛnam no bɛn mu, wobetumi ahu nea ɛtɔ mu wɔ nneɛma mmienu kosi mmiɛnsa a wɔde ahyɛ fam.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Ɛnam ntuɔ a ɛyɛ fitaa a ɛba soro, tɔ mfonini a ɛyɛ ntuɔ no.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Tɔ mfonini, afei tɔ basket a ɛfata, te sɛ aburoo anaa afuom.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Wɔtwa mfonini bi mu nkyɛn, na mfonini nyinaa hunu sɛ ɛyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Asɛm a wonim a ɛnna ne awiei ase; tɔ mmuae anaa ka awiei no.

## Sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nnwomkɔ bere

1. **Paw bere a ɛyɛ dwodwoo** bere a obi no ahome yie. Anwummere a ɛyɛ akyire betumi ayɛ den ma ebinom, enti hwɛ wo ho ansa.
2. **Fa screen a ɛyɛ kɛse.** Tablet a wɔde ayɛ stand anaa komputa a wɔde browser zoom ano yɛ mmerɛw sɛ obi bɛhu na obi bɛtɔ.
3. **Gyae tabs foforɔ na tu notifications.** Pop-up a ɛba ntɛmntɛm ma obi ayɛ ɔfrɛ, na ɛbetumi asɛe bere a ɛyɛ.
4. **Fa wo ho ntɛ obi no ho, na nnyɛ sɛ wo ne no gu nkyɛn.** Sɛ wohwɛ bom no, ɛbɛyɛ sɛ ɔwɔ wo nkyɛn sen sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma a wɔhwɛ.
5. **Fi ase nnwomkɔ baako mma simma mmienu kosi mmiɛnsa.** Ma obi no ntɔ screen no anaa ɔnkyerɛ, na mmom mo nyinaa mmɔ ne nnu.
6. **Gyae wɔ anigye mu.** Wie ɔsɛ baako, na ka mfonini baako ho asɛm anaa ka sɛ mo nnom tea.

## Ahobammɔ ne privacy wɔ device a afoforɔ nso di dwuma so

- **Mfonini gyina device no so.** Photo pairs de mfonini a wopaw di dwuma, na ɛgyina browser mu wɔ saa device no so. Hwehwɛ nnipa a wɔbɛtumi abue komputa anaa tablet no.
- **Ntwe links a ɛfiri baabi foforɔ ne ads.** Fa nnwomkɔ no firi websaet no so, na nnyɛ sɛ wobɛtɔ banners anaa prompts a ɛbisa wo ho nsɛm.
- **Hyɛ payments ne akontaa afa ho.** Nnyɛ sɛ wɔde obi a ɔwɔ screen a ɛbisa password anaa card number nko.
- **Hwɛ sɛ obi bɛkɔ.** Kora tablet no akyɛ sɛ obi bɛtu fie kɔ akɔ no nya.

## Nneɛma a nnwomkɔ yi betumi ayɛ ne nneɛma a ɛntumi nyɛ

Nnwomkɔ a ɛwɔ intaneti ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia nya anigye bere tiaa, hwɛ mfonini wɔ ho, na wɔn ho ayɛ wɔ ade a ɛnhia ahohia. Ɛntumi nyɛ dementia ayaresa, ɛnntew ne kɔ so, anaa ɛnntumi nkɔ so. Ɛbɛyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ [Federal Trade Commission's 2016 action against Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) a ɛfa nkɔsoɔ a wɔkaa a wɔantumi anhwɛ ho. Fa ho ɛnhwɛ ntɛntɛn wɔ nnwomkɔ, app anaa websaet a ɛka sɛ ɛbɛma adwene ayɛ yie, ɛbɛhwɛ wʼadwene anaa ɛbɛsesa sɛnea ɔyɛ den ankasa.

Fa nnwomkɔ yi yɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ ɛka nnwom, nantew, asɛm ne ahome ho. Ma nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a ɛfa nnwomkɔ ho, hwɛ [how to play games with someone who has dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ne [memory games that do not test](/activities/memory-games/).

## Bere a wɔbɛgyae

Gyae sɛ obi no ahome, ɔyɛ ɔboɔ, ɔnni anigye, anaa ɔnhwɛ ade bio. Gyae ntɛm a ɛyɛ anigye. Wobetumi aba bio da foforɔ, anaa wosesa kɔ asɛm ka anaa nantew.
