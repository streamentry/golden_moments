Nhwɛsoɔ adwuma yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ anigye de tena ase wɔ obi a ɔwɔ dementia nkyɛn. Ɛma nsa, ani ne asɛnka ayɛ adwuma, na ɛma yɛn nsɛm a yɛbɛka. Saa kratafa yi de adwuma a ɛwɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ pefee ne nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛboa wɔn a wɔpaw nneɛma ahyɛ nsow a ɛyɛ ɔhonam. Sɛ wopɛ nhwɛsoɔ ho ahunmuɔ a ɛyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ wɔ mu, te sɛ sɛ wɔnnye nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmofra, hwɛ [nhwɛsoɔ ne adwuma a wɔyɛ ma nnipa akyɛde](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Sɛnea wobɛfi ase nhwɛsoɔ adwuma biara

1. **Hyehyɛ ne ansa na wofi ase.** Hyehyɛ nneɛma baako anaa mmienu, ntoma a ɛbɔ ɛse, ne nsuo mu guan a ɛyɛ nwini.
2. **Kyerɛ nea ɛdi kan.** Sɔ brɔsɛ wɔ nsuo mu, tiatia picture a ɛdi kan, anaa tɔ dɔteɛ ketewa, na ka kyerɛ wɔn sɛ wɔmmɔ saa bere no.
3. **Yɛ adwuma ne wɔn ho.** Fa wo nhwɛsoɔ adwuma bi bɛn wo, na ɛma adwuma no nyɛ adwuma a wɔhwɛ, na ɛnyɛ adwuma a wɔde ahwɛ.
4. **Ka nea wuhu.** Ka mmala, nneɛma a wɔhwɛ ne sɛnea ɛte, na ɛnyɛ sɛ wɔbɛbu adwuma no atɛn.
5. **Gyae ansa na ahoɔden no bɛtew.** Nnwam a ɛyɛ mmienu kɔsi mmiensa bere a ɛyɛ yiye.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ka sɛ nhwɛsoɔ yɛ ɔkwan a obi betumi de ne tebea kyerɛ na wɔnhia nhwɛsoɔ ahoɔden. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) nso ka sɛ wɔmfa adwuma nkɛse ntoa nkyɛnkyɛn ntɛm.

## Nsuo nhwɛso

Fa pampa a ɛyɛ den a wɔde nsuo nhwɛso, brɔsɛ a ɛyɛ kɛse, ne mmala bi a wɔde wɔ kɔkɔɔ ntɛm mu. Gugu pampa no nsuo a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ, afei ma wɔnnɛn mmala no ma ɛntɔ so. Yɛ wɔn ahyɛ mmɔho gyinabea, na mmala no bɛfa ne ho. Ɛbɛyɛ picture a ɛnyɛ mmara biara, a ɛyɛ ahoɔfɛ.

- **Asetena a ɛdi kan:** Ma wɔnnye mmala no na wɔntɔ nkɔ osuo.
- **Asetena a ɛtɔ mu:** Ma mmala abien anaa mmiensa, na wo ara wo ka nsuo no.
- **Asetena a ɛtɔ so:** Kyere pampa no ma wɔnsɛ brɔsɛ wɔ so.

## Magazine collage

Boa magazine mpɔn a ɛwɔ ahoɔfɛ ne nnua, aburodɔ, mmoa, kar anaa akwantu. Tew nkɔsoɔ no sɛ nkyɛn kɛse, ɛyɛ sɛ wɔde tew, ɛse sɛ wɔtwa, na fa ɔdaa ahyɛ card so. Collage yɛ ɔkwan a ɛma nsɛm bɛfi ase, efisɛ picture biara betumi aka asɛm bi. Ɛfata obi a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔpaw nneɛma ne ɔhwɛ.

## Dɔteɛ a ɛyɛ nwini

Dɔteɛ a ɛyɛ nwini anaa dɔteɛ a wɔde hyehyɛ ho betumi wɔ nsa mu, wobetumi asɛe, asɔ ne so, na wɔ kɔ so. Fa ɔdaa anaa tanka ketewa yɛ. Dɔteɛ yɛ adwuma a ɛma nsa ka ho, na ɛbɛma obi a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkɔ nneɛma so no ahome. Hyɛ nkyɛn kɛse, na mma nneɛma a wɔn mpɔtam yɛ ɔkwan tiaa.

## Chalk pastels wɔ pampa a ɛyɛ nwini so

Pampa a ɛyɛ nwini ma mmala a ɛyɛ nwini no hyerɛn. Fa chalk pastels a ɛyɛ kɛse de, na ma obi ntwa ahyɛ mmɔho kɛse anaa de ne nsa anaa tissue ba nhwɛ. Pastels yɛ mframa, enti sie wɔ tray so, na akyiri no hohoro nsa. Paw pastels a ɛyɛ nwini a wɔka sɛ ɛnyɛ toksik.

## Nhahan nhwɛso

Boa nhahan a ɛyɛ kɛse fi afuom, de nsu a wɔsa ne ho mmɔ dan mmɛnsa so, na tɔ so de nhahan no ma wɔ pampa so. Yɛ nhwɛ ye, na yi nhahan no firi pampa no so. Ɛnsɛɛ sɛ wo ho bɛyɛ anigye wɔ nea wɔayɛ no ho. Ɛbɛyɛ adwuma a ɛfa [afuom ne aduaba adwuma](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) ho, na ɛbɛfi ase ne tu kɔ nhahan boaboa.

## Ntoma ne nnua ahyehyɛ

Hyehyɛ ntoma a ɛwɔ ahoɔfɛ ne nneɛma a ɛyɛ sɛ nsa a wobɛtumi asɔ ne so, anaa hyehyɛ nnua kakraa wɔ dua mu. Ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛde ahoɔden kakraa na ɛda so adwuma a ɛwɔ ahoɔden ketewa. Ɛfata nnipa a wɔwɔ asetena a ɛyɛ den. Ma nneɛma a ɛwɔ ahyehyɛde a ɛboa ma nhwɛsoɔ ahyɛ no ho nkyerɛkyerɛ, [asɛnka adwuma ho nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ ahoɔfɛ ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Sɛnea wobɛpaw nhwɛsoɔ nneɛma

Nneɛma a wopaw ho hia sen wɔ ahobammɔ ne ahoɔfɛ mu sen nea ɛyɛ den. Bere a wopaw no, hwɛ:

- **Toksik nni ne sɛ wobetumi asa:** Hwɛ sɛ ɛwɔ asɛm sɛ ɛnyɛ toksik, na hwɛ sɛ nsuo nhwɛso no betumi asa.
- **Nkyɛn kɛse:** Crayons a ɛyɛ kɛse, brɔsɛ a ɛyɛ den, ne pastels a ɛyɛ kɛse yɛ mmerɛw sɛ obi bɛkyere.
- **Nneɛma ketewa nni mu:** Fi nkɔnkɔm, nkwan, ne glitter a ɛbɛyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛduane.
- **Nneɛma a ɛyɛ akyin nni mu:** Fa sikoo a ɛyɛ ahobammɔ anaa tɔ pampa a wo nsa so.
- **Akɔnnɔ:** Hwɛ latex ne nsa a ɛbɛyɛ akyin anaa obi a ɔwɔ asɛm bi wɔ ne honam so.
- **Ahoɔfɛ a ɛyɛ pefee:** Mmala a ɛyɛ den wɔ pampa a ɛyɛ fitaa anaa a ɛyɛ nwini so yɛ mmerɛw sɛ obi bɛhwɛ.
- **Nneɛma kakraa:** Nneɛma mmienu anaa mmiensa na ɛfata. Nneɛma pii bɛma obi ayɛ den sɛ ɔbɛpaw.
- **Ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛpepɛ:** Fa tray, ntoma a wɔde ahyɛ mu ne tanka a wɔde nsuo bɔ ho, na ahuhuro nyɛ ɔkwan a wɔde gyae adwuma.

## Sɛnea asetena no ne nkɔso ho

Asetena a ɛdi kan no, obi pii betumi ahyehyɛ na awie adwuma wɔ ɔboafo kakraa ase. Asetena a ɛtɔ mu no, de nneɛma a wobɛpaw ho sua ne nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛboa. Asetena a ɛtɔ so no, fa adwuma a ɛde wɔn ho ahyɛ mu, te sɛ dɔteɛ a wɔsɛe anaa wɔde nsuo a wɔsa ne ho mmɔ dan dua so, na anigye wɔ nea ɛba awiei mu, nyɛ nea wobɛtumi ayɛ pefee.

Ma nsɛm a ɛfa asetena biara ho, hwɛ [asetena a ɛdi kan](/activities/early-stage/), [asetena a ɛwɔ mfiase mmiensa](/activities/middle-stage/) ne [asetena a ɛtɔ so](/activities/late-stage/). Sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔnpɛ anaa ɔbɔ ɔhaw, [akwankyerɛ a ɛfa sɛnea wobɛma obi ayɛ adwuma bere a ɔka sɛ ɔnpɛ](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) betumi aboa wo sɛ wobɛtu ne ho akyi.

## Di adwuma a ɛyɛ nkɔ so

Nhwɛsoɔ no akyi, sɛ wopɛ sɛ mmom bɛyɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ dwodwoo, yɛ [coloring](/games/coloring/) wɔ tablet so, anaa [picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) sɛ adwuma a ɛbɛdi nsɛm wɔ awiei. Ɛnyinaa ma nsa di adwuma ntɛm, na ɛnhia ahoɔden a ɛyɛ den.
