Bingo yɛ adwuma a nnipa pii a wɔawɔ mfeɛ pii ani gye, nanso nkontaabu betumi ayɛ den ma obi a wɔwɔ dementia. Picture bingo de adwuma no ntam ara, nanso ɛde mfonini kɛse ne pefee sɛ nkontaabu. Ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ fie, wɔ nnipa a wɔbɛhyiam ɛda biara, anaa wɔ agyaadwa a wɔdi nnipa so. Saa kratafa yi kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛyɛ no, sɛnea wobɛsesa no, ne sɛnea wobɛma ɛnyɛ anigye ho ayɛ kyerɛwee.

## Sɛnea picture bingo yɛ adwuma

Obiara wɔ card a ɛwɔ mfonini a wɔahyehyɛ wɔ mu, te sɛ aduane, ɔpɛkyɛ, nkyɛnkyɛn, akwantu ntoma anaa ɔkɔnkɔ. Ɔfrɛfoɔ no kyerɛ anaa ka picture biako biako. Nnipa de mfonini a ɛtɔ mu no ka ho wɔ wɔn card so. Obi a ɔma nsɛ ntoatoaso anaa nea ɛnam so ahyehyɛ no betumi aka "Bingo," nanso nnipa pii nni nkonim biara.

Wobetumi de kaad a wɔde prente yɛ firi [nneɛma a wɔde ahyɛ a ɛnni tua](/activities/printable/) mu, anaa [picture bingo game](/games/picture-bingo/), a ɛwɔ ɔfrɛfoɔ a ɛwɔ screen so ma nnipa a wɔboaboa. Adwuma abien yi yɛ yiye sɛ wobɛde tablet bɛma nnipa nyinaa ahu.

## Sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nkɔm

- **Paw nnipa a ɛyɛ kakraa.** Nnipa anan kɔsi mmiɛnsa yɛ tebea a ɛyɛ yiye. Nnipa a wɔyɛ pii yɛ den sɛ wobɛdi akyi, na ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wɔrehyɛ ntɛm.
- **Ma mfonini no yɛ kɛse.** Fa ntwerɛ a ɛyɛ den ne nneɛma baako a wohu wɔ sikwa biara mu. Gyae card a ɛyɛ nkɔnkɔm.
- **Hyehyɛ nnipa yiye.** Fa obiara wɔ beaeɛ a ɔbɛhu ɔfrɛfoɔ ne ne card, na ɛbɛn boafoɔ sɛ ɔhia.
- **Fa nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw de di dwuma.** Crayons, sika anaa tɔ nsa ka sen pencils. Fa nneɛma bi a aka ka ho.
- **Hyehyɛ nkɔm a ɛyɛ tenten.** Simma abiɛsa ne ɔtɔ mu na ɛboro. Gyae ntɛm sɛ ahoɔden tew.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ka sɛ wɔnsesa adwuma sɛnea obi no betumi ayɛ, enti sesa card ne mmara no sɛnea ɛbɛboa.

## Sɛnea wobɛfrɛ mfonini no

1. **Fa picture no kyerɛ anaa tɔ screen no so ansa na woreka.**
2. **Ka din no pefee,** de asɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw: "Tii." San ka bio koraa, na ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ dodo.
3. **Ntwɛn.** Ma nnipa bere sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ na wɔatwe mu. Nfrɛ picture a ɛdi so kɔsi sɛ nnipa dodow bi anya bere.
4. **Kyerɛkyerɛ, sɛ ɛboa.** "Ɔbɔbɔ kɛse a ɛyɛ fitaa" anaa "eyi a yɛdii wɔ ahoma no" betumi ama asɛm ba.
5. **Ma nnɔ no ntɛmntɛm.** Ɔfrɛfoɔ a ɔyɛ ahoɔdɛn yɛ hia sen ɔfrɛfoɔ a ɔyɛ ntɛm.

## Nkonim nyɛ ɔpɛ

Ɔpɔnkɔ betumi ayɛ ɔhaw. Obi a ɔrekɔ akyiri betumi ahu sɛ wɔrehwɛ no, na obi a ɔsuro sɛ ɔbɛdi nkonim nso betumi ayɛ den. Yei yɛ nneɛma a wobetumi ayɛ:

- **Obiara nya picture biako.** Obiara frɛ picture biako a ɔpɛ, na nnipa nyinaa ahwɛ na wɔhwɛ.
- **Ka picture bi wɔ card no so ho asɛm.** Bisa nnipa sɛ wɔnka asɛm ho ɔfa picture biako a ɛwɔ wɔn card so ho.
- **Tɔ kɔsi sɛ picture no nyinaa aba awie.** Frɛ picture nyinaa a ɛwɔ card so, afei fi ase foforɔ, a obiara nni kwan sɛ ɔbɛdi nkonim kan.
- **Ma nkɔmhyɛ no nyɛ kɛse anaa nnyɛ.** Asɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw anaa dwom yɛ mmerɛw sen nneɛma a ɛyɛ den.

## Sɛnea wɔbɛyɛ mfonini a ɛboro ne tebea mu

Sɛ obi ahu sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔhwehwɛ mfonini, fa card a ɛwɔ nkɔnkɔm anaa nea ɛwɔ mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa, anaa ma obiara nya card baako pɛɛ. Ma boafoɔ mmɔ card no bere a obi no rebɔ n'asɛde, anaa ɛnam ne kwan so de nnwɛn no kɔ baabi a ɛfata. Nnipa bi pɛ sɛ wɔtie ɔfrɛfoɔ no kɛse sen sɛ wɔbɛhwehwɛ mfonini no, na ɛma wɔn ani gye.

Hwɛ sɛ obi bɛda so nyɛ den anaa ɔbɛyi ne ho, te sɛ ɔyi ne ho firi wo anaa ɔbɔ ɔhyɛ, na gyae sɛ ɛbɛba. Ma adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wɔtena fie ho nkyerɛkyerɛ, hwɛ [late-stage activities](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo wɔ agyaadwa ne nnipa a wɔhyiam ɛda biara

Nnipa a wɔboa ho adwuma yɛ yiye sɛ wɔhyehyɛ no ho wɔ nnipa a wɔwɔ dan no mu. [Group activities guide for care homes and day programs](/activities/care-home-groups/) kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nkɔm, sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nnipa, ne sɛnea wobɛyɛ nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkɔ. Picture bingo nso fata dwom a wɔbɛdwom anaa abakɔsɛm, ne [finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) sɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobetumi de ayɛ nkɔm akyiri.

## Nneɛma a bingo betumi ayɛ ne nneɛma a ɛntumi nyɛ

Picture bingo yɛ adwuma a nnipa bom yɛ, na ɛyɛ anigye. Ɛbetumi ama nnipa aboa ho, ama wɔnhwɛ mfonini ne ntie, na ɛma kuw no ayɛ hɔ. Ɛntumi nyɛ dementia ayaresa anaa ɛnntew ne kɔ so. Fa nea ɛbaa no mu ne sɛnea nnipa te ne sɛnea wɔte wɔ dwannimma no mu ho sɛ ɛyɛ yiye, na ɛnyɛ nea nkonim bɛyɛ.
