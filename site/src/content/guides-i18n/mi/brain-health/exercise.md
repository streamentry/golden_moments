Ko te nekeneke tētahi o ngā tikanga e tino rangahaua ana i te rangahau hauora roro. E hono ana ngā
rangahau ki te tūraru mate wareware iti ake, engari kāore anō kia whakaatuhia e ngā whakamātautau he
aukati te mahi tinana i te mate wareware. Ko te mea e mārama ana ko te nekeneke e āwhina ana i te
kaha, te taurite, te ngākau me te moe i ngā wā katoa. Ko tēnei whārangi e whakaatu ana he aha te kī
a te rangahau, he aha te nui o te mahi e tohutohungia ana mō ngā pakeke, me ngā huarahi ngāwari ki te
nekeneke me tētahi kei te mate wareware.

## Ka iti ake te tūraru mate wareware i te mahi tinana?

E kī te rangahau ko ngā tāngata e mahi tinana ana he iti ake te tūponotanga ka pā mai te mate wareware
ki a rātou, engari he taunakitanga mātakitaki ēnei, he iti ngā taunakitanga whakamātautau. Ko te
[pūrongo 2024 a te Lancet Commission mō te mate wareware](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)
e whakarārangi ana i te kore mahi tinana hei tētahi o ngā 14 tūraru ka taea te whakarerekē. I te
whakakotahitanga, ko ēnei 14 tūraru te take o tata ki te 45% o ngā keehi mate wareware o te ao i tana tauira.

Anei te āhua o te taunakitanga:

- **He maha ngā rangahau mātakitaki e tohu ana i tētahi huarahi.** I arotakehia e te Commission ngā
  rangahau 58, ā, kitea ana ko ngā tāngata kaha ki te mahi he tūraru tata ki te 20% iti ake mō te mate
  wareware. Ko te rerekētanga nui i waenga i ngā tāngata he tino kore mahi tonu me ērā i mahi iti rawa.
- **Ka rere ngā hononga ki ngā taha e rua.** E kī te Commission ko te kore mahi ka piki pea te tūraru.
  E kī anō ia ko ngā panonitanga o te roro o te mate wareware, e tīmata ana ngā tau i mua i te whakatau,
  ka whakaiti pea i te mahi o ngā tāngata.
- **He rerekē ngā whakamātautau.** I roto i tētahi whakamātautau, 945 ngā pakeke te toharite o te pakeke
  tata ki te 78 ngā tau, i tohua ki te mahi tinana ā-tiaki e rua ia wiki mō te rima tau, ki tētahi rōpū
  whakataurite rānei. I te mutunga, kāore i rerekē mārama ngā pūkenga whakaaro i waenga i ngā rōpū. He
  maha ngā tāngata o te rōpū whakataurite i nekeneke anō, nā reira he uaua te pānui i te hua.

E whakamārama ana te [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)
i tēnei māmā. Kāore anō kia whakaatuhia tētahi hononga mārama i te mahi tinana me te aukati i te
Alzheimer's, ā, kāore he taunakitanga whakamātautau nui hei kī ka aukati, ka whakaroa rānei te mahi
tinana i te mate. Ka whakaiti tonu te mahi tinana i te tūraru o te hinga, o te pōuri, o te mate huka,
o te pēhanga toto teitei me te toke. Ko ētahi o ēnei raruraru he hononga anō ki te heke o te whakaaro.

Ko te whakaaro pono: nekeneke mō te pai ake o tō oranga i tēnei rā. Ko ngā painga roro o muri ake he
painga pea anō, ehara he oati.

## E hia te mahi tinana e hiahiatia ana e ngā pakeke?

E kī te [aratohu a te CDC mō ngā pakeke e 65 ngā tau me runga ake](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/older-adults.html)
me e toru ngā momo mahi ia wiki:

| Momo | Te nui ia wiki | Tauira |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aerobic | Iti rawa 150 meneti o te mahi ōrite, pērā i te 30 meneti i ngā rā e 5 | Te hīkoi tere, te kanikani, te aerobics wai |
| Te whakapakari uaua | Iti rawa e rua ngā rā, e whakamahi ana i ngā uaua matua katoa | Te whakapiki taimaha iti, kāneti hupa, ngā whītau whakaroa |
| Taurite | Ia wā | Te hīkoi waewae ki te waewae, te whakaara mai i tētahi nōhanga |

Ko ēnei he whāinga, ehara he paenga kia uru atu. E kī te CDC mēnā kāore koe e eke ki ēnei, me mahi
koe ki te taumata e taea ana e tō kaha me tō hauora. He pai iti atu i te kore.

## Huarahi ngāwari ki te nekeneke mō ngā pakeke

- **Hīkoi me tētahi hoa.** He hīkoi ia rā me tētahi hoa, tētahi hāpori, tētahi rōpū hīkoi, ka tāpiri i
  te kōrero me te haumaru.
- **Mahi nōhanga.** Ko te whakaara ringa i te nōhanga, te porotaka waewae, te hīkoi i tōna wāhi, me te
  yoga tūru, ka whakapakari i te kaha me te kore tūraru taurite.
- **Kanikani i te kautaki.** Whakatangihia ngā waiata o te tangata i tōna taiohitanga me ōna rua tekau, ka
  hātepe, ka tāpuhi, ka hīkoi rānei. Hei tauira, [te aratohu mahi waiata](/activities/music/).
- **Kaha me ngā taonga kāinga.** Whakapiki i ngā pauwharau wai kī, i ngā kāneti hupa i a koe e mātakitaki
  ana i tōu whakaaturanga pai.
- **Māra, mahi kāinga.** Ko te whakamākū i ngā tipu, te rake, te tīmata, te pōro kākahu, ka tatau katoa.
  Titiro ki [ngā mahi māra ki waho](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) me [ngā mahi kāinga](/activities/household-tasks/).
- **Mahi wai.** E kī te NIA ko ngā akomanga aerobics wai tētahi kōwhiringa mō ngā tāngata e uaua ana ki
  te mahi tinana ki uta nā ngā hononga.

## Me pēhea te mahi tinana me tētahi kei te mate wareware

Mō tētahi e noho ana me te mate wareware, ko te whāinga ko te ngākau pai, te ngahau me te pupuri i ngā
pūkenga o ia rā, ehara i te eke ki tētahi nama. E kī te NIA he āwhina te mahi tinana ki te pupuri i ngā
uaua, ngā hononga me te ngākau o tētahi kei te Alzheimer's i te āhua pai, ā, ka pai ake anō te ahua o te
ngākau me te moe. Me pātai ki tō rātou ringatoi i mua i te tīmata i tētahi hōtaka hou. Ko ngā tohutohu mai
i [te aratohu kaitiaki a te NIA](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) ko:

1. **Hīkoi tahi ia rā.** Whiriwhiria tētahi ara haumaru, mōhio, kāore he taupoki. Mēnā ka hīkoi mokemoke
   te tangata, he pai te whakamau i tētahi mauroto tohu (ID) me tō nama waea.
2. **Whakamātau ngā ataata mahi tinana mō ngā pakeke.** Whai tahi, i te nōhanga, i te tū rānei.
3. **Whakapōkia ngā wā poto.** He pai ake pea ngā "whakangungu iti" maha i tētahi roa.
4. **Tāpirihia ngā waiata.** Ka mahi ngā waiata mōhio i te nekeneke hei mahi, ehara hei mahi noa. Kanikani
   ina pai ki te tangata.
5. **Hoatu wai me ngā hū pai.** Tukuna te wai, te hupa i te wā me muri, ā, kōwhiria ngā kākahu mārama me
   ngā hū e tika ana mō te mahi tinana.

Mēnā uaua te hīkoi, ka tohu te [aratohu tiaki a te NIA](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf)
i ngā kōwhiringa nōhanga. Ko ēnei ko ngā mahi whakaroa tūru, te yoga tūru, te tuku pēkana pīni, te tuku pōpi
ngāwari, pōhīnāhi rānei, ngā whītau whakaroa, te whakapiki taonga kāinga māmā, me ngā mīhini pēti. Ngā mahi
māmā pērā i te tahi me te whakamaroke ka tatau anō.

Me whakaaro pono mō te nekeneke e taea ana. Kāore i kitea e te Lancet Commission he taunakitanga ka whakaiti
te mahi tinana i ngā panonitanga whanonga pērā i te pōharatanga. Nekeneke tahi nā te mea he pai ki a koe,
ehara hei whakatika i tētahi ahiahi uaua. Mēnā ka kī te tangata kāo, he pai tērā. Ko tā mātou aratohu mō
[te wā e kī kāo ai rātou](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) he huarahi ngāwari ki te whakamātau anō ā muri ake.
Ko te mutu moata, ko te noho tahi ki waho, he hua pai anō tērā.

Whai muri i te nekeneke, ka āwhina tētahi wā hā mārie pērā i [te Hā Mārie](/games/calm-breathing/) ki te
whakamārie i a kōrua. Mō tētahi mahi nōhanga me te nekeneke ringa ngāwari, [te Whakarōpū Tahi](/games/sort-together/)
me [te Bingo Whakaahua](/games/picture-bingo/) he pai i te tēpu.

## Te taurite me te haumaru hinga

He mea noa te hinga, he mea nui hoki. E kī te [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/falls/data-research/facts-stats/index.html)
ko neke atu i te kotahi i te whā o ngā pakeke ka hinga ia tau, engari iti iho i te haurua e kōrero ana ki tō
rātou ringatoi. Ka rua pea te tūponotanga o te hinga anō ina hinga kotahi. Ko ngā tūraru e rārangi ana te CDC:

- Te ngoikoretanga o ngā waewae me ngā tārere
- Ngā raruraru hīkoi me te taurite
- Ētahi rongoā, tae atu ngā rongoā moe me ētahi rongoā pōuri
- Ngā raruraru tirohanga
- Te mamae o ngā waewae, ngā hū kino
- Ngā raru kāinga pērā i te raruraru o ngā taonga, ngā whāriki takahi me ngā hikoi kua pakaru

Hei nekeneke haumaru ake:

- **Whakamahia he nōhanga pakari** kāore he wīra, ā, whakatairangitia he tauta, he pou ki te wāhi ka taea
  e koe te toro mō ngā mahi tū.
- **Whakamāmāa te wāhi** i ngā whāriki, ngā taura me ngā taonga tuatahi.
- **Haere māhorahora ina whakaara**, ā, mutu mēnā he pōrangi, he ngoikore te tū te tangata.
- **Mau hū tautoko**, kāore he tōkena, he kōpare pāwhe.
- **Mahi i te taurite tata ki te tautoko.** Ko te whakaara mai i te nōhanga me te hīkoi waewae ki te waewae
  he tauira a te CDC mō ngā mahi taurite.

E tohutohu ana te CDC me tiki koe i te ringatoi tonu mēnā he pakeke te tangata ka hinga, ka pā te upoko,
otirā mēnā he kai rongoā whakaheke toto (blood thinners) rātou.

## Kōrero ki tētahi ringatoi hauora

Me pātai ki tētahi ringatoi i mua i te tīmata i tētahi hōtaka mahi tinana hou, otirā mō tētahi he raruraru
ngākau, he raruraru hononga, he raruraru tārere, he mate wareware rānei. Ka taea e tētahi ringatoi, e tētahi
tohunga rongoā tinana (physical therapist) te whakaatu i ngā mahi haumaru, me te tirotiro mēnā ka pā ngā
rongoā ki te taurite. Kōrerohia ki tō ringatoi mō ia hinga, ahakoa kāore i pā kino. Mēnā ka mamae, ka kino rānei
tētahi mea i te wā mahi tinana, whakamutua. Mō te mamae nui o te uma, te uaua o te manawa, te pōhēhē ohorere,
tonoa he āwhina ohotata, waea ki te nama ohotata ā-rohe.

Ko tēnei whārangi he kōrero whānui, ehara i te tohutohu hauora. Tirohia tā mātou
[whakapāhatanga hauora](/medical-disclaimer/). Mō te whakaaro whānui ake, pānuihia
[te kōrero a te rangahau mō ngā tūraru mate wareware](/brain-health/risk-factors/) me
[te mea ka taea te aukati i te mate wareware](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).
