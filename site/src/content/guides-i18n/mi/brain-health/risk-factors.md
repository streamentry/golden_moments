He tūraru tētahi mea e hono ana ki te tūponotanga teitei o tētahi āhuatanga. Ehara i te take i ngā wā katoa,
ā, ehara i te mea ka pā mai te mate wareware ki a koe mēnā he tūraru tō. Ko tēnei whārangi e rārangi ana i ngā
tūraru mate wareware 14 e kī ana te Lancet Commission 2024 ka taea pea te whakarerekē, ngā mea e hono ana ki ngā
rangahau, me ngā mea ka taea e kōrero ai ki tō ringatoi.

Mō te whakaaro whānui ake, tīmatahia me
[Ka taea te aukati i te mate wareware? Ngā kitenga rangahau](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).
He kōrero whānui tēnei, ehara i te tohutohu hauora; tirohia tā mātou
[whakapāhatanga hauora](/medical-disclaimer/).

## He aha ngā tūraru 14 mō te mate wareware?

Nā te [Lancet Commission 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0), he rōpū o 27 ngā kairangahau
mate wareware, i tātari tata ki te 45% o ngā keehi mate wareware o te ao ka hono ki ngā tūraru 14 ka taea pea te
whakarerekē. Ko te ōrau mō ia tūraru kei raro nei, ko te tātaritanga a te Commission mō te wāhanga o ngā keehi o
te ao e hono ana ki a ia, kua whakapōtaetia ki ngā tau katoa. Ka tūtaki ngā ōrau, nā te mea he maha ngā tāngata
he tūraru maha i te wā kotahi, nā reira ka tapa ki te 45% engari kāore e nui ake.

## He aha te titiro ki ngā ōrau?

Mā te tupato. Ko te pūrongo a te Commission e whai whakaaro ana ki ēnei kōrero:

- **He tātaritanga mō ngā taupori, ehara i te tangata takitahi.** Ko te
  [whakarāpopototanga a University College London mō te pūrongo](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors)
  e kī ana ka mahi te whakaiti tūraru i te taumata taupori, ā, kāore e oati ka karo tētahi takitahi i te mate wareware.
- **E whakaaro ana he take, he pānga.** I whakauru anake e ngā kaituhi ngā tūraru he taunakitanga whakaaro, engari
  e kī ana he wāhanga anake pea ētahi hononga.
- **He ōrau mō te ao.** Ko te nuinga o ngā raraunga i ahu mai i ngā whenua whiwhi moni nui, ā, he rerekē pea te
  wāhanga tūturu mō tētahi whenua, mō tētahi hapori rānei.
- **He iti ngā ōrau ka nui tonu te pānga.** Ko tētahi tūraru e hono ana ki te 2% o ngā keehi o te ao, he tino nui
  tonu ngā tāngata e pā ana.

## Ko ēhea ngā tūraru i āpitihia i te 2024?

I tāpiri te pūrongo 2024 i ngā tūraru e rua ki ngā 12 i whakaingoahia i te 2020:

- **LDL kōhuru teitei i te haurua o te oranga**, e hono ana ki tata ki te 7% o ngā keehi.
- **Ngoikoretanga tirohanga kāore i maimoatia i te oranga pakeke**, e hono ana ki tata ki te 2% o ngā keehi.

## Ko ēhea ngā tūraru ka kore e taea te whakarerekē?

E kī te National Institute on Aging
[ko te pakeke te tūraru nui e mōhiotia ana mō te Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease),
ā, kāore e taea te whakarerekē. He wāhi anō ngā ira. Ko tētahi ira e tino mōhiotia ana e whakaawe ana i te tūraru o
te Alzheimer's ko APOE. E tāpiri ana te NIA kāore anō kia mōhio nui ngā kairangahau he aha te take o te Alzheimer's
i te nuinga o te tangata. E kī te Lancet Commission ka taea te whakaiti i ngā tūraru ka taea pea te whakarerekē,
ahakoa te tūraru ira o te tangata.

Nā te nui o te wāhi o te pakeke me ngā ira, he maha ngā tāngata kāore he tūraru 14 e pā tonu ana te mate wareware ki
a rātou. Ehara tērā i te hē a tētahi.

## He aha te kī o ngā whakamātautau mō te whakarerekē i ēnei tūraru?

Ko te hono i tētahi tūraru ki te mate wareware, ehara i te mea kua whakaatuhia ka whakaiti te whakarerekē i taua
tūraru i te tūponotanga o te mate wareware. Anei ētahi tauira o te tūnga o ngā taunakitanga whakamātautau:

- **Pēhanga toto.** I te whakamātautau SPRINT MIND, he mea nui i whakaiti te whāinga pēhanga toto iti ake i te
  **raruraru whakaaro iti** (mild cognitive impairment). Kāore i whakaiti nui te mate wareware tūmanako, ahakoa pea
  he iti rawa te whakamātautau hei whakaatu i tērā
  ([SPRINT MIND i te JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Awhi whakarongo.** E whakamārama ana te Lancet Commission i tētahi whakamātautau awhi whakarongo e toru tau,
  kāore i kitea he pānga nui ki te whakaaro, engari he painga i tētahi rōpū iti i tūraru teitei ake. Ko ngā rangahau
  e whai ana i ngā tāngata i roto i te wā e hono ana te whakamahi awhi whakarongo ki te tūraru iti ake. Titiro ki tō
  mātou [aratohu whakarongo](/brain-health/hearing/).
- **Mahi tinana.** E kī te National Institute on Aging kāore anō kia rahi ngā taunakitanga whakamātautau hei kī ka
  aukati, ka whakaroa rānei te mahi tinana i te raruraru whakaaro iti, i te Alzheimer's, ahakoa e kī ana ētahi
  rangahau pea ka āwhina i te heke e pā ana ki te pakeke
  ([NIA mō te aukati i te Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Ko ngā whakamātautau roa e tatari ana kia puta te mate wareware he puhoi, he utu nui, nā reira mō te nuinga o ngā
tūraru ko ngā taunakitanga pai rawa ka ahu mai tonu i ngā rangahau e whai ana i ngā tāngata mō ngā tau. He painga
taua taunakitanga, engari he iti ake i te whakamātautau.

## He aha mō te moe me te kai?

Ka tirotiro te Commission i te moe me te kai, engari kāore i tāpirihia ki te rārangi, nā te mea he iti, he kore
raupapa rawa ngā taunakitanga. Ko ā mātou aratohu mō [te moe](/brain-health/sleep/) me [te kai](/brain-health/diet/)
e whakamārama ana i ngā mea kua mōhiotia.

## Kōrero ki tētahi ringatoi hauora

Ko te haumanu tau-tau he wā pai hei whakaara i ēnei kaupapa. Ka taea e koe te kawe mai i tēnei rārangi, ka pātai:

- He aha ōku nama pēhanga toto, huka toto me te cholesterol, ā, me tirotiro ētahi?
- Me tirotiro ōku taringa me ōku karu?
- He haumaru, he nui rawa rānei te mahi tinana e mahia ana e au?
- Ka pā ōku rongoā ki te maharatanga, ki te whakaaro rānei?

I mua i te whakarerekē i ngā rongoā, i te kai, i te kore kai, i te mahi tinana, i ngā matūkai rānei, kōrerohia tō
ringatoi i mua. Mēnā kei a koe, kei tētahi e tiaki ana koe, he pōuri-ā-wā te mate wareware, te ngoikoretanga o tētahi
taha, he raruraru kōrero rānei, tonoa he āwhina ohotata, waea ki te nama ohotata ā-rohe.
