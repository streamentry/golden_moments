Ko te kite i ngā rerekētanga o tō mahara, o te mahara o tētahi aroha, he mea whakawehi. Ka āwhina tēnei whārangi ki te whakatau he wā tika ki te whakarite whakatau ki te tākuta, me pēhea te whakarite mō taua wā. Kāore e taea e tēnei te kī mēnā he mate wareware tētahi. **Ko te tohunga hauora tōtika anake ka taea te whakatau i te mate wareware**, he maha hoki ngā mate e raru ai te mahara, ehara i te mate wareware. Tēnā, kaua e whakawhirinaki ki ngā pātai ipurangi, ki ngā whakamātautau ā-tōna anō.

## He aha ngā tohu timatanga o te mate wareware?

E whakamārama ana te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/10_signs) i ngā tohu timatanga e 10. Ka whakatata ia tohu ki tētahi rerekētanga pakeke noa hei whakataurite. Ā mātou ake kupu:

| Tohu hei tirotiro | He noa ake i te pakeke |
| --- | --- |
| Te wareware ka whakararu i te oranga o ia rā, pēnei i te wareware i ngā take tata nei, i te pātai i te mea ano me te mea ano | Ā, ētahi wā ka wareware i tētahi ingoa, i tētahi toronga, ka maumahara anō ina muri mai |
| Te uaua ki te whakamahere, ki te whakaoti raruraru, pēnei i te whai i tētahi tohutao kai mōhio, i te whai i ngā pire | Ētahi wā he hapa ki ngā pire o te whare |
| Te uaua ki te oti i ngā mahi mōhio i te kāinga, i te mahi, i waho rānei | Ētahi wā e hiahia ai āwhina ki te whakarite i te ōwhanga kai, ki te hopu i tētahi pouaka whakaata |
| Te wareware i ngā rā, i ngā wā o te tau, i te wāhi e tū ana koe | Te hē o te rā o te wiki, ā, ka whakatika anō ina mahara |
| Ngā raruraru hou ki te whakatau tawhiti, ki te pānui, ki te wehewehe i ngā tae | Ngā rerekētanga kite nā ngā mate karu |
| Ngā raruraru hou ki ngā kupu, pēnei i te mutu i waenga i te kōrerorero, i te karanga i ngā mea ki te ingoa hē | Ētahi wā e uaua ai te kimi i te kupu tika |
| Te whakatakoto i ngā mea ki ngā wāhi rerekē, me te kore e hoki anō ki ngā tūtaki | Te wareware i ngā mea ētahi wā, ā, ka kitea mā te hoki anō ki ngā tūtaki |
| Te whakaaro kino, pēnei i ngā moni, i te tiaki i a koe anō | Te whakatau kino ētahi wā |
| Te wehe i ngā hobi, i te mahi, i ngā mahi pāpori | Ētahi wā kāore e pai ki te haere ki tētahi hui whānau, pāpori rānei |
| Ngā rerekētanga o te ngākau, o te ahua, pēnei i te pōhēhē, i te whakapaetanga, i te wehi, i te māharahara | He tikanga mahi tūturu, ā, he pukuriri ina whakararu te tikanga |

Kotahi tohu anake, kāore e tohu ana i te mate wareware. Ko te mea nui ko te **rerekētanga mai i te āhua noa o taua tangata**, otirā tētahi e uaua ai te oranga o ia rā.

## He aha te rerekētanga o te pakeke noa me te mate wareware?

E kī ana te [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/memory-loss-and-forgetfulness/memory-problems-forgetfulness-and-aging) he maha ngā pakeke e kite ana he uaua ake te whakahoki mahara i ētahi mea, ā, ētahi wā he wareware i ētahi mea, i tētahi pire rānei. He tohu iti noa ēnei o te wareware. **Ehara te mate wareware i te āhuatanga noa o te pakeke.** Ko ngā raruraru mahara nui ka uaua ai ngā mahi o ia rā, pēnei i te taraiwa, te whakamahi i te waea, me te kimi i te huarahi ki te kāinga.

E tūtohu ana te NIA kia kōrero ki tētahi tākuta mēnā:

- E pātai tonu ana i ngā pātai ano
- Ka ngaro i ngā wāhi i mōhio pai ai rātou ki mua
- He uaua ki te whai i ngā tohutao kai, i ngā tohutohu
- Ka nui ake te pōhēhē mō te wā, ngā tāngata me ngā wāhi
- Kāore e tiaki i a rātou anō, pēnei i te kai kino, te kore horoi, te whanonga morearea

He tangata kei a ia te **raruraru whakaaro iti (MCI)**. Ko te tikanga he nui ake ana raruraru mahara, whakaaro rānei i ō rātou pakeke, engari e mahi tonu ana i te oranga o ia rā. E kī ana te NIA he tohu timatanga pea te MCI mō te Alzheimer's, engari kāore ngā katoa e whai MCI ana e pā ki taua mate.

## He aha atu ka puta ai ngā raruraru mahara?

He maha ngā mea i tua atu i te mate wareware e pā ana ki te mahara, ā, ētahi ka pai ake ina rongoātia. E whakarārangi ana te NIA:

- **Ngā raruraru o te tairoidi, o te whatukuri, o te ate**
- **Ngā pānga kino o ngā rongoā**
- **Te pōuri me te māharahara**
- **Te iti o te huaora B12**, te kore kai hauora kia pai ai
- **Ngā raruraru moe**
- **Te whakamahi kino waipiro, tarukino rānei**
- **Ngā mate, ngā toto whakakī, ngā puku** i roto i te roro
- **Te whara ki te mahunga**, pēnei i te whara roro
- **Te pehanga nui, te pōuri nui**, pēnei i te whakatā mahi, i te ngaronga o tētahi hoa rangatira

Ka pōhēhētia pea ngā raruraru rongo me te kite he pōhēhē; tirohia tā mātou [aratohu rongo](/brain-health/hearing/). Koia e nui ai te tirotiro hauora. E kī ana te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor), he maha ngā wā e tono ai ngā tākuta i ngā whakamātautau toto hei tirotiro i ngā raruraru o te tairoidi, i te iti o ngā huaora me te mate. Me kōrero hoki mō te [moe pai](/brain-health/sleep/) me te rongoā i te pōuri.

## Ko te pōhēhē tere he ohotata

**Ko te pōhēhē ka tere mai, ka puta i ngā hāora, i ngā rā rānei, me hiahia te āwhina hauora tere.** Pea he pōrearea tere o te whakaaro tēnei. E whakamārama ana [te VA i te pōrearea tere](https://www.va.gov/geriatrics/pages/delirium_when_should_i_worry_about_delirium.asp) hei ohotata hauora, ā, e tohutohu ana kia waea tonu atu ki te kaiwhakarato hauora o te tangata, ki tō nama ohotata o tō takiwā rānei. Ko ngā tohu:

- Ngā piki, ngā heke o te kaha ki te whakaaro mārama
- Te uaua ki te whakarongo, ki te aro
- Te whakaaro whakaraerae
- Te moe nui rawa, te pōrearea nui rawa, te whakaohooho i te ngākau

Ka pā tēnei ki te katoa, tae atu ki ngā tāngata kua noho ana me te mate wareware. E kī ana te [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), ko ngā take noa ko ngā mate (he mate i ngā ara mimi he mea noa i ngā pakeke me ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana), te whētuki, te iti rawa o te huka toto i ngā tāngata e mate huka ana, te whara ki te mahunga, me ētahi rongoā. I a koe e tatari ana ki te āwhina, noho tahi ki te tangata, whakamārie ia me ngā kupu poto, māmā. Kaua e pātai he maha ngā pātai, ā, tuhia ngā rongoā katoa e inu ana ia.

## Me pēhea taku whakarite mō te toronga ki te tākuta?

Ka tīmata tētahi toronga pai ki ngā tuhituhi pai. E tūtohu ana te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor) kia kawea mai:

1. **He rārangi o ngā rerekētanga** o te mahara, o te ngākau me te whanonga, me ngā tauira mārama, me te wā i tīmata ai, tata noa
2. **Ngā raruraru hauora o mua me o nāianei**, tae atu ki te hītori whānau o ngā raruraru mahara
3. **Ngā rongoā tāpiri katoa me ngā rongoā ka hokona noa atu**
4. **Ngā huaora, ngā tāpiringa katoa**
5. **Ō pātai**, pēnei i ngā whakamātautau ka mahia, te roa o te wā e kiia ana, me te wā ka puta ai ngā hua

He pai hoki te kawe mai i tētahi mema whānau, i tētahi hoa rānei e mōhio pai ana ki te tangata. E whakamārama ana te [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-alzheimers-disease-diagnosed) ka pātai pea ngā tākuta ki te tangata me tētahi mema whānau mō te hauora, ngā rongoā, ngā mahi o ia rā me ngā rerekētanga o te whanonga. Ka mahi pea hoki ngā tākuta:

- Te whakahaere whakamātautau mō te mahara, te whakaoti raruraru, te aro, te tatau me te reo
- Te tono whakamātautau toto, mimi me ētahi atu hei rapu i ētahi atu take
- Te tirotiro mō te pōuri, mō ētahi atu āhuatanga hauora hinengaro
- Te tono tirotiro roro pēnei i te CT, i te MRI
- Te tuku ki tētahi tohunga motuhake, pēnei i te tākuta kaumātua, te tākuta roro, te tākuta hinengaro kaumātua, te tohunga hinengaro roro rānei

E kī ana te NIA he wāhi here te aromatawai whakaaro i te Medicare Annual Wellness Visit, ā, he wā pai pea tērā hei whakaara i ngā māharahara. Ahakoa ngā whakamātautau poto e whakamahia ana e ngā tākuta, kāore e ora anake hei whakatau i te mate wareware. He tīmatanga anake ēnei ki tētahi aromatawai whānui, ā, he maha pea ngā toronga ka tika ai te whakatau. E tohutohu ana te NIA kia hoki anō ngā tāngata e raruraru ana te mahara ki te tākuta i ia 6 ki te 12 marama.

## Te whakaara i te kaupapa me te atawhai

Mēnā kei te māharahara koe mō tētahi atu, kōwhiria tētahi wā mārie, ngākau tūmataiti. Kōrero mō ngā mea motuhake kua kitea e koe, he mea hāngai hei āwangawanga mō tō hauora, ehara i te whakahē. Ko te tūtohu i tētahi tirotiro hauora whānui ka iti ake pea te whakamataku. Whakauruhia te tangata ki ngā whakatau, ā, kōrero ki a ia hei pākeke e tika ana. Ka āwhina pea tā mātou [tohutohu whakawhitiwhiti kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/).

## Kōrero ki tētahi tākuta

Mēnā ka kite koe i ngā rerekētanga o te mahara, o te whakaaro, o te ngākau, o ngā kaha o ia rā, he hou, ā, e mau tonu ana, whakaritea he toronga ki tētahi tākuta tuatahi. Mō te pōhēhē tere, waea atu ki tō nama ohotata o tō takiwā, ki te kaiwhakarato hauora o te tangata rānei i tērā tonu. Ka tirotirohia moata, ka kitea ngā take ka taea te rongoa, ā, mēnā he mate wareware, he wā roa ake ki te whakamahere me te rapu tautoko. Tiakina hoki tō whaiaro i tēnei wā; tirohia tā mātou [aratohu tiaki whaiaro mō te kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).

He mōhiohio whānui tēnei whārangi, ehara i te tohutohu hauora. Tirohia tā mātou [whakapūtanga hauora](/medical-disclaimer/).
