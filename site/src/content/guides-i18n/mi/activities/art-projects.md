He mahi toi he huarahi whai hua hei whakapau wā ki tētahi tangata e mate wareware ana. Ka whakahihiri ngā ringa, ngā kanohi me ngā rongo, ā, ka whai kōrero hoki koe. Kei tēnei whārangi ngā kaupapa takitahi me ngā tohutohu māmā, me ngā ārahi mō te whiriwhiri rauemi haumaru. Mō ngā mātāpono o te toi pakeke, pērā i te karo i ngā rauemi tamariki, tirohia [te toi me ngā mahi ringa mō ngā pakeke e mate wareware ana](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Me pēhea te tīmata i ngā mahi toi katoa

1. **Whakaritea i mua i te tīmatanga.** Whakatakotoria kotahi, rua rauemi, he kākahu whakamarumaru me tētahi kākahu kōpeke.
2. **Whakaatuhia te takahanga tuatahi.** Kūmahia te paraihe, rīpoatahia te pepa tuatahi, rānei kōmamatia he kōpuku uku iti, ā, tonohia rātou kia pēnei anō.
3. **Mahia ngātahi.** Kia tata tō mahi ki a koe, kia rite ai te mahi ki tētahi mahi tahi, ehara i te mahi mātakitaki.
4. **Kōrerohia ngā mea e kitea ana.** Whakaahuatia ngā tae, ngā kakano me ngā hanga, kaua e whakawā i te mahi.
5. **Whakamutua i mua i te hekenga o te kaha.** He pai te roa o tekau ki te rua tekau meneti.

Ka kī te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) he huarahi te toi hei whakaputa i te whakaaro me te rongo, ā, he auaha tonu, me te kore e hiahia he pūkenga toi. Ka tohutohu te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kia wāwāhia ngā mahi ki ngā takahanga iti, ngā takahanga e whakahaere ai.

## Pēnga peita wai

Whakamahia te pepa peita wai taimaha, he paraihe māeneene nui, me ētahi tae ki ngā kapu papaku. Whakamākukuhia te pepa ki te wai ma, ā, tukua te tangata kia tuku i te tae ki runga. Hūrirutia māmāhia te pepa kia rere ngā tae. Ko te hua he whakaahua māeneene, ātahanga, e kore e hiahia he mahere.

- **Wāhanga tuatahi:** Tukua rātou kia kōwhiri i ngā tae, ā, peitahia he whenua.
- **Wāhanga waenga:** Hōmai rua, toru tae, ā, mahia e koe te whakamākuku.
- **Wāhanga whakamutunga:** Pupuria e koe te pepa, ā, ka pātōtō rātou ki te pūngāwhā.

## Kōlāji ā-moheni

Kohia ngā whārangi moheni he whakaahua kanapa o ngā puāwai, o ngā kai, o ngā kararehe, o ngā waka, o ngā haerenga. Whatiwhatia ngā whakaahua ki ngā wāhanga nui, he māmā atu tēnei i te tārai, ā, pania ki tētahi kāri pakari. He pai te kōlāji hei tīmatanga kōrero, nā te mea ka arahi tētahi whakaahua ki tētahi maharatanga. He pai mō te tangata e pai ana ki te whiriwhiri me te whakarōpū.

## Uku māeneene

Ka taea te pēhi, te rōrō me te pēhi i te uku maroke-i-te-hau, i te uku whakahāngai rānei. Hangaia he kōpuku māmā, he tāera papatahi rānei. He uku te uku e nui ana te whakapā, nā reira ka mārie ki ngā tāngata e pai ana ki te pupuri i ngā mea. Whakanuia ngā wāhanga nui, ā, karohia ngā taputapu he koi ō te pito.

## Pēkana ki runga pepa pouri

Ka rongo ngā tae pēkana ki te pepa pouri. Whakamahia ngā pēkana nui, taratahi, ā, tukua te tangata kia tuhi i ngā rārangi whānui, kia whakakōpani rānei ki te maihi o te ringa, ki te tihou rānei. He ngōtahi ngā pēkana, nō reira whakaritea ki runga tēpu iti, ā, muru ō ringa i muri. Kōwhiria ngā pēkana māeneene kua tohua hei kore paitini.

## Tā rau

Kohia ngā rau nui mai i te māra, pania te peita wai ka horoi ki ngā uaua, ā, pēhia te rau ki runga pepa. Tangohia māmāhia te rau, ā, whakanuia te tā. Ka hono tēnei kaupapa ki ngā [mahi māra me te taiao](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), ā, ka tīmata pea ki tētahi hīkoi kohi rau.

## Ngā kākahu me te whakarite puāwai

Whakarōpūhia ngā papa kākahu he rerekē te kakano ki te pā, ā, whakarītea rānei he puāwai hou ki te oko. He kaupapa ā-rongo ēnei me te iti rawa o te pēhanga. He pai hoki mō ngā tāngata i ngā wāhanga whakamutunga. Hei whakaaro anō, tirohia [ngā mahi ā-rongo mō ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana](/activities/sensory/).

## Te whiriwhiri rauemi toi

He nui ake te hiranga o ngā rauemi mō te haumarutanga me te whakamārietanga i te kounga. Ina whiriwhiri koe, whakaarohia:

- **Kore paitini, ka taea te horoi:** Rapua ngā tapanga e kī ana he kore paitini, ā, tirohia he horoi ngā peita.
- **Ringa nui:** He māmā te pupuri i ngā pēniti nui, ngā paraihe matotoru me ngā pēkana nui.
- **Kaua he wāhi iti:** Karohia ngā pēti, ngā pātene, ngā tuhipoka me te parakiwa wāhi-kē ka taea te horomia.
- **Kaua he taputapu koi:** Whakamahia he kuri haumaru, ā, whatiwhatia te pepa ki te ringa.
- **Ngā mate:** Tirohia mō te rapa, me ngā mate kiri e mōhiotia ana.
- **Rōnaki:** He māmā te kite i ngā tae kaha ki runga pepa mā, ki runga pepa pouri rānei.
- **Rārangi poto:** E rua, e toru ngā rauemi he nui. Ka whakapōhēhē ngā kōwhiringa he nui rawa.
- **Ngāwari te horoi:** Whakamahia he tīpare, he pepa kirihou me tētahi kākahu kōpeke, kia kaua te raru ki te ātete ka mutu te mahi.

## Te urutau ki te wāhanga

I te wāhanga tuatahi, ka taea e te nuinga te whakamahere, te oti i tētahi kaupapa me te āwhina iti. I te wāhanga waenga, tukua he kōwhiringa iti ake me ngā ārahi nui ake. I te wāhanga whakamutunga, arohaina ngā tukanga ā-rongo, pērā i te pēhi uku, te pūngāwhā tā peita, ā, whakanuia te hua, ehara i te oti.

Hei mōhiotanga anō mō ngā mahi ā-wāhanga, tirohia [te wāhanga tuatahi](/activities/early-stage/), [te wāhanga waenga](/activities/middle-stage/) me [ngā mahi wāhanga whakamutunga](/activities/late-stage/). Mēnā ka whakakorehia, ka pōrearea rānei te tangata, ka whakamārama [te aratohu mō te kī kore](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) me pēhea te hoki whakamuri.

## Whakahono ki tētahi kēmu

Whai muri i te wā peita, whakamātauria te [kēmu whakakara](/games/coloring/) i runga papaaho mō tētahi wā mārie, he [kēmu whakaahua wāwāhia](/games/picture-puzzle/) rānei hei mutunga. Ka whakapaea ngā ringa e rua, ā, kāore e hiahia he pūkenga ringa whaitua.
