Ko ngā mahi ā-rongo e whakamahi ana i ngā rongo e rima: te pā, te haunga, te reka, te oro, te kitenga. Kāore e hiahia te mahara, ngā tikanga, ngā whakautu tika, nō reira ka taea i ngā wāhanga katoa. He nui rawa atu ō rātou painga ki muri ake. Ka kī te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) i te wāhanga whakamutunga, ka tirohia te ao mā ngā rongo, ā, ka taea te whakaatu i te manaaki mā te pā, te oro, te kitenga, te reka me te haunga. He iti ngā tirotiro haumaru ka harikoa ai te katoa.

## He aha ngā mahi ā-rongo mō te mate wareware?

Ko ngā mahi ā-rongo he wheako māmā e hangaia ana i runga i tētahi rongo kotahi i te wā, pēnei i te pupuri i te papanga kākahu, te hongi i ngā otaota, te whakarongo ki te ua. Ka kī te [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) he nui ake te aro o ngā mahi wāhanga whakamutunga ki ngā rongo, ā, ka whakahua i te waiata, ngā mea hei pā, hei whakapupuri, me te whakamomi ringa. Ka kī te [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ka nui pea te hiahia o ngā tāngata i te wāhanga whakamutunga ki te pupuri i tētahi mea, hei whakamarie.

## Ngā whakaaro ā-rongo, rongo ki te rongo

### Pā

- **Whakamomi ringa.** Whakamahanatia he hinu iti ki ō ringa, ka mimiti te nekeneke mai i te kōkōwhi ki ngā maihao. Ka kī te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) me whakamomi ki te hinu kāore he kakara mō te whakamarie.
- **Kete kakahu.** He tapawhā nui o te pōtae, te kakahu aurora, te kōtiro, te satini me te huruhuru hipi hei pā, hei pāwhiri, hei kōrero mō.
- **Tauera mahana.** He tauera mahana, mākū mō ngā ringa i te rā makariri. Whakamātaua te pāmahana ki tō kōkōwhi anō i mua.
- **Apuron mahi, paraikete takiwā-waewae.** He pātene nui, he rīpene, he here, he papatipu kua tuia pakari.
- **Pāwhiri me te whakamāmā.** He kete tauera horoi, he tauera kai mā hei pāwhiri, hei tuku, hei tuku anō.
- **Tētahi mea māmā hei pupuri.** He paraikete pūoro, he urunga, he kararehe māmā tōtika, e hoatu ana noa, kāore rawa he mea hei tākaro.

### Haunga

- **Otaota hou.** Miria he rau mint, rosemary, basil, lavender ki waenga i ō maihao, ā, tukua atu.
- **Haunga kīhini.** Te kawhe e rīnoa ana, te parāoa e tōtō ana, te hinamona, te ārani e tīhaehae ana i tētahi wāhi tata.
- **Puāwai.** Kawea mai ētahi pua hou mai i te māra, i te toa hoko rānei.
- **Tētahi hua mōhio.** Te hopi, te mirimiri i ngā rā tawhito, mēnā ka pai ki te kiri.

### Reka

- **He reka pai.** He kōtahi iti o tētahi mea ka aroha ai rātou, pēnei i te aporo, te pudding, te aihikirīmi, i tētahi kakano ka taea e rātou te whakahaere haumaru.
- **Āwhina i te parapara.** Tō i ngā huarākau, i te kirīmu iti i a koutou e parapara tahi ana.
- **Kai tōtikanga.** Te kōhua i te raumati, te haina wera i te ngahuru, i tētahi pāmahana haumaru.

### Oro

- **Waiata pai.** Ngā waiata mōhio i te reo māmā; tirohia [ngā mahi pūoro](/activities/music/).
- **Oro o te taiao.** Te ua, ngā ngaru, te waiata a ngā manu. Ka kī te NIA he rongo mārie ētahi tāngata ki te pūoro, te oro hāngū me ngā oro o te taiao.
- **Tō reo.** Pānui ā-reo, hīmene, kōrero pūrākau rānei.
- **Taonga pūoro māmā.** He pahū, he tangitangi, he pere hei tākaro ngātahi me te waiata.

### Kitenga

- **Mātakitaki matapihi.** He kōhao manu ki waho i te matapihi, ngā tamariki e hoki kāinga ana, te hukarere e heke ana.
- **He kōhao ika, he ataata māmā.** He nekeneke ngāwari kāore he oro nui.
- **He whakaahua nui.** Tētahi whakaahua mārama i te wā kotahi, ka pupuri ki te taumata kanohi.
- **Tae me te tauira.** Te whakarōpū i ngā rā tae kanapa, ngā pua, ngā tauera kai tae, te whārangi tae māmā mai i tā mātou [whārangi tae](/games/coloring/).
- **Ki waho.** Ka kī te NHS he māra ā-rongo me ngā tipu haunga, ngā kiko, ngā wai. Tirohia [ngā mahi ki waho me te māra](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Me pēhea te hanga pouaka ā-rongo?

1. **Kōwhiria he kaupapa** e hono ana ki te oranga o te tangata: te takutai, te māra, te kīhini, te wāhi mahi, te whakapaipai.
2. **Kōwhiria e rima, e ono ngā mea nui, haumaru,** pēnei i tētahi anga moana nui, tētahi koko rākau, tētahi kākahu māmā, tētahi pēke lavender maroke kua tuia, tētahi poro rākau māmā.
3. **Tirohia ia taonga** mō ngā wāhanga iti, ngā tapatapa koi, ngā miro kāore i pakari, me ngā mea ka pakaru.
4. **Tangohia kotahi taonga i te wā.** Hoatu, whakamāramatia, ā, waiho te tangata kia tūhura.
5. **Whakamautia te pouaka i muri iho** kia mau tonu tōna wātea, ā, tirohia anō ngā taonga i mua i te wā e whai ake nei.

## Te haumaru i te tuatahi

He ngāwari ngā mahi ā-rongo, engari he mea nui ētahi tūpono. Ka pōhēhē pea ētahi tāngata whai mate wareware i ngā mea kāore he kai hei kai, ā, ka uaua pea te horomi i te wā roa; ka whakatupato te NIA ko ngā raru horomi o te wāhanga whakamutunga ka piki ai te tūpono o te whakakai.

| Tūpono | Me pēhea |
| --- | --- |
| Whakakai | Whakamahia ngā taonga nui anake e kore e taea te horomi. Kaua he pātene, he kirīkiri, he moni, he mātā, he taonga iti kua tuku noa ki te pouaka. Hoatu kai anake i ngā momo kai e kī ana te rōpū tiaki he haumaru. |
| Ngā mea he rite ki te kai | Ka kī te NIA me katia ngā hinu, ngā hopi, ngā hāmpu me te whakakakara, ka rite pea ki te kai, ki te haunga kai, me te whakakore i ngā hua hangahanga. Whakamarautia ngā kōaro ā-rongo i muri i te whakamahi. |
| Aleji | Tirohia ngā aleji ki ngā nati, ki te rātusi, ki te huruhuru hipi, ki ngā tipu, ki ngā kakara i mua i te tīmata. |
| Kakara kaha | Karo ngā kakara kaha me ngā hinu kakara, otirā ina raru te hāwhe, ina raru manawa rānei. He pai ake ngā haunga māmā, taiao. |
| Kiri | Tirohia mēnā he mamae, he wehe, he ngoikore te kiri i mua i te whakamomi. Whakamahia te pēhi ngāwari, ā, whakamutua mēnā ka tīkuka ngā kanohi. |
| Pāmahana | Whakamātaua ngā tauera mahana me ngā inu ki tō kōkōwhi anō i mua. |
| Taonga koi, paitini rānei | Kaua he taputapu koi, he karaihe, he rauemi toi paitini. |

## Ka āwhina rānei te aromatherapy?

Tera pea kāore i te kitea, mā te ine. I titiro tētahi [arotake Cochrane mō te aromatherapy mō te mate wareware](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) ki te 13 rangahau me te 708 tangata, katoa i ngā whare tiaki, ā, kāore i kitea he taunakitanga whakapūmau o te painga. He iti ngā rangahau, he rerekē ngā hua, he iti hoki te pūrongo mō ngā pāpātanga taha. Mēnā e pai ana te tangata ki tētahi haunga māmā, mōhio, pēnei i te lavender o te māra, he take pai tērā hei tiritiri. Ehara i te rongoā.

## Me pēhea te tuku i te mahi ā-rongo

- **Kōwhiria he wā mārie** ka katia te pouaka whakaata.
- **Haere mai i te mua,** kōrero i tō ingoa, ā, pātai, kī rānei ki a rātou i mua i te pā: "He lotion tōku. Kei te pai koe kia whakamomi ahau i ōku ringa?"
- **Hoatu kotahi mea i te wā** kia kaua e rahi rawa.
- **Mātakitakina te urupare.** He pai te kanohi māmā, ngā ringa mārie, te kata. Ko te whakahokia atu, te kāmaru, te pōrearea he tohu kia mutu.
- **Whakapōtoa.** Ko ngā meneti e rima mārie he wāhanga pai.

He pai te whakamutu me te manawa mārie tahi, pēnei i [te whakangā mārie](/games/calm-breathing/). Mō ētahi atu whakaaro mō te moenga, tirohia [ngā mahi wāhanga whakamutunga](/activities/late-stage/); mō te pōuritanga o te ahiahi, tirohia [te whakapōrearea i te ahiahi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
