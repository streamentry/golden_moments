Ko te nuinga o ngā kōrero mō te mate wareware e whakaaro ana he 70, he 80 ngā tau o te tangata. Mō tētahi i takea i āna tau 40, i āna tau 50 rānei, he rerekē te āhua o ia rā. Tērā pea he mahi tāna, he nama whare, he taitamariki kei te kāinga, he mātua hei tiaki. Me whakaae ngā mahi ki taua oranga pakeke. He aratohu tēnei whārangi mō ngā mea e rerekē ana, ngā mea hei whakamātau, me te wāhi ka taea e ngā hoa me ngā tamariki pakeke te kite tautoko.

## He aha te mate wareware ā-moata?

Ko te mate wareware ā-moata he mate wareware e tīmata ana i mua i te 65 tau te pakeke, ka kīia hoki he rangatahi-ā-moata. Ka kī te [pukapuka blog a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) he rangatahi-ā-moata te Alzheimer's ina pā ki tētahi kei raro i te 65, ā, ka kī ia he “wero motuhake” te tiaki i tētahi pēnei. He rite pea ngā tohu ki ngā tohu o ngā tau muri ake, engari ko ngā whakaaro, ngā haepapa me te oranga pāpori o te tangata, kāore i rite.

Nō te mea he iti ake, he roa pea te wā e tatari ai te tangata mō te whakatau, ā, he pēhea pea te whānau i te rongo kāore he tangata hei kōrero. Ko te mōhio he tangata anō e pēnei ana tōna wheako, he mea whakamārie.

## Whakamaua te pakeke i roto i te mahi

Ko ngā tangata pakeke e mate wareware ā-moata ana, kāore e pai kia whakaarahia rite tamariki. Ka tohutohu te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kia urutaua ngā mahi ki ngā mea e taea tonu e te tangata, e harikoa tonu ai ia. Mō tētahi tangata i āna tau 50, he pēnei pea:

- **Whakamaua tētahi hobi mōhio,** pērā i te mahi rākau, te tākaro, ngā kāri, he karapu pukapuka, he rōpū waiata, me tētahi āwhina iti, rānei he wā poto ake.
- **Hīkoi, paihikara rānei me tētahi hoa,** i tētahi huarahi e mōhio pai ana rātou.
- **Tunu kai whānau,** ka mahi te tangata i ngā wāhanga e taea ana e ia.
- **Kia whakaūkui, kia āwhina rānei** i tētahi tūranga e kōwhiria ana e rātou, pērā i te whakarōpū koha, te āwhina i tētahi huihuinga kura.
- **Whakamaheretia he haerenga** ki tētahi wāhi e whai take ana ki a rātou, me tētahi hoa me tētahi hōtaka māmā.
- **Whakapaua he wā ki ngā mokopuna, ngā iramutu, ngā irapōua,** me te tākaro, me tētahi kaupapa ngātahi.

Waiho te tangata kia kōwhiri. He tika ake te pātai, "He aha tāu e pai ai ki te mahi i tēnei ahiahi?" i te whakatau mō rātou.

## Whakamahia ngā waiata me ngā maharatanga nō ō rātou ake tau

Ka puta mai te whakaaro mahana me te kōrero māmā mai i ngā waiata me ngā whakaahua nō ngā tau rua tekau, toru tekau, whā tekau o te tangata. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mahi waiata](/activities/music/) e whakamārama ana me pēhea te hanga i tētahi rārangi waiata, ā, ko [ngā mahi maumahara](/activities/reminiscence/) e whakaatu ana me pēhea te tiritiri i ngā whakaahua me ngā kōrero kāore he pātai whakamātau. Hei kēmu pakeke ake, whakamātauria ngā [kāri kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/), me ngā akiaki tuwhera mō te mahi, te haerenga, ngā hoa me te whānau.

Ka pai pea ngā [rōpū whakaahua rite](/games/photo-pairs/) i hangaia mai i ngā whakaahua whānau, nā te mea he tangata me ngā wāhi e whai take ana ki te tangata ngā whakaahua. Ka noho ngā whakaahua ki te pūtirotiro i taua taputapu.

## Te mahi, te taraiwa me te oranga o ia rā

He maha ngā tāngata e mate wareware ā-moata ana kei te mahi tonu, kei te taraiwa tonu, kei te whakahaere moni tonu rānei. Ehara ēnei i te mahi i te tikanga noa, engari he whakahāngai i ngā mea e taea e te tangata. Kōrerohia tō rātou tākuta mō te taraiwa me te haumaru i te mahi, ā, whakaarohia mēnā e hiahia ana rātou ki te āwhina i ngā pūtea, ngā hui rānei. Ko te whāinga kia tiakina te mana, ā, kia noho tonu te tangata ki te mahi tōna wā e hiahia ai ia.

Hei aratohu mō ngā hōtaka ia rā me te noho kaha, tirohia [he hōtaka ia rā māmā](/activities/daily-routine/) me [ngā mahi whare hei mahi](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Tautoko mō ngā hoa me ngā tamariki pakeke

He maha ngā wā ka mokemoke ngā hoa me ngā tamariki pakeke. He pērā te tiaki ki tētahi mahi tuarua, i runga i te mahi me te whānau. Ka kī te pukapuka blog a te Alzheimer's Association, he mea whai hua ngā rōpū mō ngā rangatahi-ā-moata ki ngā take whaiaro pērā i te whakamahere ture, ā, ki te mōhio kua mārama tētahi ki a rātou. Ka kī anō he rōpū tautoko kaiwhakamaru koreutu tā te Association, tae atu ngā rōpū mō ngā tangata e tiaki ana i tētahi e mate wareware ā-moata ana, ā, e toru o ērā e hui ana ā-ipurangi i ngā Tūrei me ngā Wenerei.

Hei pātai mō ngā rōpū, waea ki te Pūkōrero 24/7 a te Association ki te 800.272.3900. Ka whakaatu [te aratohu manaaki ake a te kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) i te okiokinga, te whakatā, me ngā tohu o te ngenge nui.

## He nui tonu ngā wāhanga

Ko te wāhanga o te mate wareware o te tangata e whai tonu ana ki ngā mea e āwhina ai. I te tīmatanga, ka taea e te nuinga te mahi i ā rātou mahi noa me ētahi panonitanga, ā, he aratohu [ngā mahi wāhanga tuatahi](/activities/early-stage/) he whakaaro anō. I muri atu, ka māmā, ka ahua-rongo ake ngā mahi, pērā i te kī i [ngā mahi wāhanga whakamutunga](/activities/late-stage/). Panonitia te mahi ina panonitia te tangata, ā, pātaihia he aha tā rātou e hiahia ai, ina taea e rātou te kī.
