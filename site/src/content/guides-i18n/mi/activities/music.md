Ko te waiata tētahi o ngā mea māmā rawa atu ka taea e koe te tiritiri ki tētahi tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware. Kāore ia e tono whakautu tika, kāore he tikanga whai. Ka taea e tētahi waiata mōhio te whakaata mai i tētahi ataata, i tētahi waewae e tōtō ana, i ētahi kupu e waiatahia ana tahi, ahakoa he uaua te kōrero i tērā rā. Ko tāu anake e hiahia ana he waiata tika mō tēnei tangata, me tētahi wā mārie hei whakatangi.

## He aha ngā mahi waiata e pai ana mō te hunga whai mate wareware?

Ko te whakarongo, te waiata, te pātete, te nekeneke me te kōrero mō te waiata he mea pai katoa. Ka kī te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) me whakatangi waiata, kōrero mō te waiata me te kaiwaiata, waiata, kanikani rānei ki ngā waiata e mōhiotia ana, me te haere ki ngā konohete, ngā kaupapa waiata ā-hapori. Ka tāpiri te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) he pai ake te nekeneke pēnei i te pātete, te kanikani, ā, he huarahi hono te waiata ina uaua ngā kupu.

## Me pēhea te hanga rārangi waiata whaiaro?

Ko te mea whai painga rawa ka taea e koe te hanga, he rārangi waiata e whai tikanga ana mō tēnei tangata. Ka whakaatu hoki te [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) i ngā waiata e whai tikanga whaiaro ana. Anei tētahi tikanga māmā mō te hanga i tētahi.

### Tīmata mai i tōna oranga

- **Ngā tau taiohi me ngā rua tekau.** Whakaaro ki ngā mea i runga i te reo irirangi, i ngā kanikani, i ngā kiriata i a ia tamariki.
- **Ngā wā nui.** Te waiata i tōna marenatanga, tōna kanikani tuatahi, tētahi pukaemi haerenga whānau pai.
- **Whakapono me te ahurea.** Ngā himene, te waiata rongopai, te waiata tapu, me ngā waiata i tōna reo tuatahi.
- **Mahi me te mahi tūmatanui.** Ngā roopū pīkari, ngā waiata mahi, te waiata e hono ana ki tētahi wā i te ope taua.
- **Te mea e maumahara ana te whānau.** Pātai ki ōna teina, ōna tamariki, ōna hoa tawhito he aha ā rātou i tākaro ai, i waiata ai i ngā wā katoa.

### Whakaarohia ngā momo, kāore ko ngā waiata anake

Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio ki te tīmatanga, whakamātauria ētahi meneti o ia momo, ā, mātakitahia te whakautu: te pīkī nui me te kiwi, te doo-wop, te Motown me te wairua, te rōkiroi moata, te kantri me te uwana, te bluegrass, te rongopai me ngā himene, ngā waiata whakaaturanga Broadway, ngā waiata jazz tuku iho, te waiata Latina pēnei i ngā bolero, ngā mariachi, te polka, te waiata iwi me te waiata karāhipi. Pupuri i te mea ka kata, ka pātete rānei te waewae; whakamutua te mea ka kōmuri te kanohi.

### Me whakamāmā te whakatangi

- **Kāore he panuku.** Ka kī te Alzheimer's Association he pōhēhē ngā panuku ki te tangata. Whakamahia ngā waiata kua tikiria mai, ngā pūoro CD, ngā rēkōta, te ratonga kāore he panuku rānei.
- **Kotahi pūoro māmā.** He pāpaka me te pātene nui kotahi, he rārangi kua tiakina, kia taea e te tangata katoa te tīmata.
- **Rārangi e rua poto.** He rārangi mārie hei whakatau, he rārangi hihiri hei whakaara i te wairua.
- **Tohua.** Tuhia te ingoa o te tangata me "te waiata pai" ki runga i te pūoro, kia taea ai e te whānau manuhiri me ngā kaimahi tiaki te whakamahi.

## 15 mahi waiata hei whakamātau

1. **Whakarongo tahi.** Noho tahi, kāore he mea anō e haere ana. Ko te whakarongo he mahi tino oti.
2. **Waiata tahi.** Tīmatahia mā koe anō te waiata. Ka pai te hīmene, te kōrero noa i ngā kupu hoki.
3. **Pātete, tōtō rānei i te pēke.** Pato i ngā pekenga, tōtō i te tēpu, whakamahia rānei he pūoro ringa iti, he tangitangi rānei.
4. **Kanikani mārie.** Piri ngā ringa, nekeneke i te kīhini, kanikani rānei i te nohoanga, ngā ringa e nekeneke ngātahi ana.
5. **Kanikani me ngā kākahu māmā.** Wiri i ngā kākahu māmā i te tōtō o te waiata, e tū ana, e noho ana rānei.
6. **Kōrero mō te waiata.** Tono, kaua e whakamātau: "He rite tēnei ki te whare kanikani. Kōrerohia mai ngā kanikani." Kaua e pātai "Kei te maumahara koe ki tēnei waiata?"
7. **Whakatangi tētahi pukaemi katoa.** Whakatangi tētahi rēkōta i aroha ai ia, tīmatanga ki te mutunga, me te tī.
8. **Tirohia ngā uhi pukaemi.** Ko ngā uhi rēkōta tawhito he pai mō te kōrero mō ngā kākahu, ngā waka me ngā kanohi.
9. **Mātakitaki tētahi whakaari waiata.** He whakaari waiata, he konohete rānei nō tōna wā ka pai mō te pō. Whakamutua mō ngā okiokinga.
10. **Whakatangi tētahi pūoro e mōhio ana ia.** He kaitākaro piana, he kaitā kītā o mua pea ka pai ki te whakatangi māmā. Ā muri ake, he pai noa te tiki i ētahi nota tahi.
11. **Waiata i te mahi.** Whakatangi waiata pai i te wā e takahi ana i ngā kākahu horoi, e whakarite ana i te tēpu.
12. **Waiata ata.** Tīmatahia ia rā me te waiata hari ōna tonu hei wāhanga o te whakahaerenga.
13. **Waiata himene, waiata tapu rānei.** Whakatangia ngā waiata o tōna whakapono, waiatahia tahi.
14. **Uru ki tētahi rōpū waiata.** Ka kī te NHS he pai ngā rōpū waiata mō te ngākau me te oranga. Pātaihia ā-rohe mō ngā roopū waiata e mōhio ana ki te mate wareware.
15. **Haere ki te waiata ora.** He konohete poto ā-hapori, he roopū pīkari ā-waho rānei. Noho tata ki te kuwaha, ka taea te wehe i te wā e pai ai koe.

Mō tētahi mahi kupu māmā me ngā kīwaha mōhiotia, whakamātauria [whakaotihia te kīwaha](/games/finish-the-saying/), ka taea tonu te whakaatu i te mutunga.

## Te whakarite i te waiata ki te wā

Ka kī te Alzheimer's Association me whakamahi te waiata hei hanga i te āhuatanga e hiahiatia ana e koe: waiata mārie mō te āhuatanga marino, me tētahi waiata hihiri nō te taiohitanga o tētahi hei whakaara i te wairua.

| Te wā | Whakamātau | Tohutohu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Te whakaara | He waiata mōhio, hari | Ko te waiata ōna ia ata he tohu ngāwari |
| Ngā mahi | Ngā waiata pai hihiri | Ka pai te waiata hei hoa mō te mahi |
| Ngā kai | He waiata ngāwari, kāore he kupu | Kia iti te reo kia kaua e whakataetae ki te kai |
| Te ahiahi | Waiata mārie, mōhio i te rama pai | Tirohia [te whakapōrearea i te ahiahi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |
| Te moenga | Waiata mōmō, ngāwari | Whakaitia te reo me ngā rama i te wā kotahi |
| Wāhanga whakamutunga | Ōna waiata e tino aroha ana, he tino mārie | Noho tata, pupuri i tētahi ringa |

## Ngā tohutohu mō te wheako pai

- **Whakakorea ētahi atu haruru.** Ka kī te Alzheimer's Association me kati ngā matapihi me ngā tatau, me whakakore te pouaka whakaata kia waiata anake te haruru.
- **Whakamārie te reo.** Me pai te waiata ki te taringa, kāore e tino nui. Mēnā he ngā awhina rongo tā te tangata, me mahi, me mahi tika; tirohia [te rongo](/brain-health/hearing/).
- **Tirohia ngā mahara pōuri.** Ko ētahi waiata e hāpai ake ai te pōuritanga, te pakanga, te ngaronga rānei. Mēnā ka pōuri te tangata i tētahi waiata, āwhinatia, whakarerekētia rānei.
- **Waiho kia arahi ia.** Ina taea, waiho kia kōwhiri te tangata i te waiata, ā, whakaaetia te kāore.
- **Whakamutua i ngā wā katoa.** Ko ngā meneti e rima o te waiata he mahi pai. Ko te whakamutu, te noho mārie tahi rānei he pai hoki.

## He aha ngā rangahau mō te waiata me te mate wareware?

He iti te taunakitanga. I titiro te [Arotake Cochrane 2025](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) ki 30 rangahau o ngā huihuinga waiata whakaritea, he nuinga ki ngā rōpū i ngā whare kaumātua. I kitea e rātou pea he iti rawa te ngāwari o ēnei huihuinga i ngā tohu pōuri i whakataurite ki te tiaki i ia rā, engari kāore he pāpātanga marama ki te whakaōhooho, ki te oranga pai, ki te whakaaro me te mahara, ā, kāore he pāpātanga i mau i muri i te mutunga o ngā huihuinga.

Ka tiritiri mātou i te waiata he mea pai, he iti te pēhi, hei whakapau wā tahi, ehara i te rongoā. Mō ngā wāhi rōpū, tirohia [ngā mahi rōpū o te whare kaumātua](/activities/care-home-groups/); mō te whakamahi i te waiata i te taha o ngā pikitia me ngā kōrero, tirohia [ngā mahi hoki ki ngā mahara o mua](/activities/reminiscence/).
