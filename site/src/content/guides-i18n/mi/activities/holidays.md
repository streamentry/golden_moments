Ka mahara pai pea ngā hararei, engari ka hanga anō te haruru, te tini tangata, me ngā panoni i te rā noa. Mō te tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware, he iti, he ngāwari, he whakahaere ki ngā mea ka taea tonu e ia te pai ai ngā whakanuitanga pai rawa. Ka pai ēnei whakaaro mō te Thanksgiving, te Kirihimete, te Hanukkah, te Tau Hou me ngā rā whānau katoa.

## Me pēhea e whakarite ai i tētahi hararei mārie?

Tīmatahia mai i te tikanga o te tangata, ā, whakaritea te rā mā reira. E kī te [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/6-steps-to-make-thanksgiving-dementia-friendly/) me whakanui i te whakanuitanga i te ata, nā te mea ka piki ake te pōhēhē me te ohotata i te ahiahi me te pō (ka kīa pea ko te “sundowning”). He pai ake te kai tina, te parakuihi rānei i te tina pō.

- **Whakarea ki te rahi tika.** He hui iti, ngāwari ake, he manuhiri e tae mai ana i ngā rōpū iti, he pai ake tēnā i tētahi tēpu e whakakīkī ana.
- **Tohaina ngā mahi.** Tonoa ngā whanaunga kia kawe i tētahi kai, kia hoko, kia noho ki te tangata i a koe e tunu ana. Whakamāramatia te mea e āwhina ai.
- **Whakamāmāhia ngā whakapaipai.** E kī te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) he pōhēhē pea ngā rama e mirimiri ana, ā, ko ngā reka, ngā huariki hanga e whakamahia ana hei whakapaipai, he pōhēhē pea ko te kai.
- **Whiriwhiria ngā mea ka whakarerea.** Ki te kaha ngā tikanga ki te pēhanga, ki te tūraru rānei, he pai kia whakarerea, kia whakamātau i tētahi mea mārie kē.

## He aha ngā mahi hararei ka taea e mātou te mahi ngātahi?

Whiriwhiria ngā mahi e tika ana ki ngā mea ka taea e te tangata i tēnei rā. Ko te whai wāhi ki te rā he mea nui atu i te hua o te mahi.

1. **Whakarite i te tēpu ngātahi.** Whakatakotoria ngā pereti i te tuatahi, ā, ko ngā tōwera kai, ko ngā kapu muri mai. Ko te whakapaki tōwera kai he mahi pai ki te noho iho.
2. **Horoi, whakarōpū huawai.** Te horoi i ngā rīwai, te whatiwhati i ngā pini kākahu i te tēpu kāinga, he mea haumaru, he mea mōhio mō te nuinga o ngā tāngata i tunu i mua.
3. **Kohu, whakakotahi, whakatakoto.** Te kohu i te kōwae, te whakakotahi i te parāoa māmā, te whakatakoto pihikete ki te pereti, ka kaua ngā ringa e noho ki te kōhua me ngā mata.
4. **Whakapaki koha.** Whakarite mōkihi me te kōwae i mua, ā, tukua te tangata kia pōtiti, kia whakaōrite. Ehara i te mea kia pai rawa te whakapaki.
5. **Whakapaipai i ētahi mea.** Whiriwhiria ngā mea whakapaipai e kore e pakaru, me tētahi wāhi iti, pēnei i te karaone, te rākau iti.
6. **Hanga toi māmā.** Ngā mekameka pepa, te kōlāge rau, he kāri hararei me ngā tāmi, he pai. Ko tā mātou [arataki toi me te mahi ringa](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) he whakaaro atu anō.
7. **Waiata, whakarongo.** Ngā kāreti mōhio me ngā waiata hararei he māmā te uru, ahakoa he iti te kōrero o te tangata i tēnei wā. Titiro ki tā mātou [arataki waiata](/activities/music/) mō te hanga rārangi waiata.
8. **Titiro ki ngā whakaahua hararei tawhito.** Whakaputaina ngā pukapuka o ngā hākarihanga o mua, ā, tonoa ngā kōrero (“Korerotia mai mō tēnei tēpu”) kaua e pātai ko wai tēnei. Ka whakamārama tā mātou [arataki whakamahara](/activities/reminiscence/) pēnei.
9. **Tākaro rōpū mārie.** Ka tā tere te [pingo whakaahua](/games/picture-bingo/) mō ngā pakeke katoa, ā, ka hoatu ngā [kāri kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/) i tētahi take kōrero mō te katoa.
10. **Tā kara ngātahi.** Ka taea te tā ngā [whārangi kara](/games/coloring/) mō ngā tamariki me ngā kaumātua hei tiritiri i te tēpu.
11. **Mātakitakina he kiriata hararei pai.** He kiriata mōhio he okiokinga, ā, he pai anake te mātakitaki i tētahi wāhanga.
12. **Taraiwa poto ki te mātakitaki rama.** He haerenga mārie i ngā whare kua whakapaipaia he māmā ake i tētahi hākari whakatōpuatanga.

Mēnā he nui te kai i te oranga o te tangata, ko tā mātou arataki [ngā mahi kāinga hei mahi](/activities/household-tasks/) he tikanga anō mō ngā mahi kāuta haumaru, whakarewa.

## Me pēhea te whakarite i ngā manuhiri?

Whakamōhiotia ngā manuhiri i mua i te taenga mai, otirā ngā whanaunga kāore anō kia tae mai mō te wā roa. E kī te Alzheimer's Association me whakamārama he panoni i te mahara me te whanonga, nā te mate, ehara i te tangata.

- **Tuku i ētahi tohutohu māmā:** kōrero kotahi-kotahi, hoatu wā ki te whakaoti i te whakaaro, kaua e whakatika, kaua e pātai pātai, ā, whakautu ki te tōna o te tangata mēnā he uaua ki a rātou te mārama ki ngā kupu.
- **Kīa he aha ka āwhina, he aha ka pōrearea.** Mēnā ka pōrearea ngā reo nui, te whakawhāitia, ētahi kaupapa rānei, kīa atu.
- **Whakamōhiotia ngā manuhiri i mua.** Whakaaturia ki te tangata ngā whakaahua o ngā tāngata e haere mai ana, ā, kōrerotia kotahi, rua pūrākau, whakaritea rānei he kōrero iti ā-waea, ā-ataata rānei i mua i te rā.

Ka whakamāramatia e ā mātou [tohutohu kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) he kōrero whānui atu.

## He aha ina nui rawa?

Whakaritea he rūma mārie i mua i te taenga mai o ngā manuhiri, kei tua atu i te haruru, me ngā mea whakamārie pēnei i tētahi kākahu moe pai, i tētahi kākahu mahana rānei, i tētahi mahi māmā. Tukuna wawe, tukuna ki te nuinga o ngā wā: “Me noho tātou ki tētahi wāhi mārie mō tētahi wā?”

Ko te wehe i te tēpu, te moe moata, te whakarere wāhanga o te rā he hua pai, ehara i te hē. Ina pōuri te tangata, he reo mārie, he waiata mōhio, he hīkoi ki tētahi atu rūma he pai atu i te whakamārama. Ko tā mātou arataki [ohotata o te ahiahi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) he whakaaro anō mō ngā pō.

## Me pēhea e whai wāhi ai ngā whānau tawhiti?

Ka taea e ngā kōrero ā-ataata ngā whanaunga te uru atu me te kore e hāpai i te hui. E kī te Alzheimer's Association me whakarite ngā kōrerorero ki tētahi hanganga, hei tauira ko te waiata i ngā waiata tau, te tohatoha i ngā whakaahua o ngā hui o mua, te tunu tahi i tētahi tohutunu, te mātakitaki tahi i tētahi kiriata hararei. Kia poto ngā kōrerorero, ā, tonoa ngā tāngata kia kōrero kotahi-kotahi, nā te mea he uaua te whai i ngā kōrero e kōrerohia ana i te wā kotahi. Ko tā mātou arataki [waea, kōrero ā-ataata](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) he whakaaro mō ia kaupapa.

Ki te noho te tangata i te wāhi maumahara, tirohia te kaupapa toro, ā, tonoa ngā kaimahi kia āwhina ki te whakarite i te kōrerorero. Ko tā mātou arataki [toro i tētahi kei te wāhi maumahara](/activities/visiting/) e whakaatu ana he aha te mea ka mau, he pēhea te wehe pai.

## He aha ngā koha e pai ana mō te tangata e mate wareware ana?

Ko ngā koha māmā, haumaru ka taea e rātou te whakahoa i tēnei wā te mea pai rawa: tētahi kai pai, he kākahu moe māmā, he waiata mōhio, he kalenda pukapuka nui te tuhi, he pukapuka whakaahua o te whānau. E kī te Alzheimer's Association me karo i ngā taputapu whakararu, i ngā hangarau uaua, me ngā tākaro papatipu uaua.

Ko te koha ka rite tonu ki te whiwhi. Ko te pupuri pihikete i tunua e te tangata, ko te whakapaki i tētahi koha i hokona e koe, ka āhei rātou ki te whai wāhi ki te wā. Ko tētahi atu whakaaro ko te tākaro whakaahua whānau: ko [pōtaki rite](/games/photo-pairs/) ka huri i ētahi whakaahua i kōwhiria e koe hei tākaro takirua mārie i te paetukutuku.

## Tiakina anō a koe

He tāpiri mahi te hararei ki te tiaki. Tukuna kia whakarite tētahi atu te hākari, te tunu i te kukuru, mēnā ka kī atu ki ngā hui uaua, ā, kia whai okiokinga i te wā e noho ana ētahi atu ki te tangata. Mēnā kei te māuiui koe, kei te māuiui rānei te tangata e tiakina ana e koe, he ringa ringa i te huarahi, he kōrerorero ā-ataata he toro pai tonu. Ko tā mātou arataki [tiaki i a koe anō](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) he tautoko anō.
