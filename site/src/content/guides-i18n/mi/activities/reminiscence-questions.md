Ko te maumahara te mahi kōrero tahi mō te wā o mua: ngā kāinga o te tamarikitanga, ngā mahi tuatahi, ngā kai pai, ngā waiata, ngā marena me ngā hararei. Mō te tokomaha o ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana, he kōrero mārie mō tōna ake oranga tētahi o ngā wāhanga harikoa rawa o te rā. Ko te mea nui, me pātai i tētahi āhua e akiaki ai i te pūrākau, kaua e whakamātau i te maumahara. He rōpū pātai ēnei i whakarōpūngia ā-kaupapa, me ngā tohutohu mō te whakamahi.

## Ngā ture mō ngā pātai maumahara

- **Pātaia ngā pātai tuwhera.** “Korerohia mai…” me “He pēhea te āhua o…” e akiaki ana i tētahi pūrākau. “Kei te maumahara koe…?” e pātai ana mō te whakautu tika.
- **Kaua rawa e whakamātau.** Kaua e pātai “Ko wai tēnei?”, “He aha te tau i tērā?” rānei “Kei te maumahara koe ki te ingoa o…?”
- **Whakaaetia he aha rawa te hoki mai.** Ki te rerekē te pūrākau, ki te whakakotahi ngā wāhi, harikoa ki a ia, ā, pātaihia mō ngā kare-ā-roto.
- **Kotahi pātai i te wā.** Tatari, whakarongo, ā, whai atu ki tētahi pātai māmā.
- **Waiho te tangata kia arahi.** Ki te hiahia rātou ki te kōrero mō tētahi atu mea, haere tahi ki a rātou.

Ka tohutohu te [aratohu a te National Institute on Aging mō te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) ki ngā rerenga poto, te reo mārie, me te manawanui i te wā e kimi ana te tangata i ōna kupu. He nui ēnei tikanga i konei.

## Te kāinga me te tamarikitanga

- Korerohia mai mō te whare i tipu ai koe. He pēhea te kāinga?
- He aha ngā mahi i pai ai koe ki te mahi ki waho i a koe i te iti?
- Ko wai ngā tāngata i tākaro ai koe i tō huarahi?
- He pēhea tō rūma moe? I tiritahi koe ki tētahi?

## Mahi me te mahi i ngā mea

- He aha te mahi i pai rawa ai koe?
- Korerohia mai mō tētahi rā mahi i whakahīhī ai koe.
- He aha ngā mea i pai ai koe ki te hanga, ki te whakatika ā-ringa?
- Ko wai te tangata i akona ai koe mō ō mahi?

## Kai me te tunu kai

- He aha te kai a tō whānau i te Rātapu?
- Korerohia mai mō tētahi rihi i tunua pai rawa e tētahi o tō whānau.
- I a koe e tamariki ana, he wāhi pai anō tā koe i tono ai ki tētahi kai?
- He aha ngā kakara e maumahara ai koe ki te kāinga?

Ka hono te kai ki te [mahi ā-rongo](/activities/sensory/), me tētahi kai iti, ina haumaru te tuku.

## Waiata me te kanikani

- He aha ngā waiata i waiata ai, i kanikani ai koe i tō wā tamariki?
- Ko wai tō kaiwaiata, tō rōpū pai rawa?
- I rongo koe ki te waiata i ēhea wāhi i aua rā: i te kāinga, i ngā kanikani, i te whare karakia?

Mō ngā rārangi waiata me ngā aratohu waiata, tirohia [ngā mahi waiata mō ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana](/activities/music/).

## Hararei me ngā whakanuitanga

- He aha tō hararei pai i a koe e tipu ana?
- Korerohia mai mō tētahi hākari whānau e maumahara ana koe.
- He aha tā te whānau i mahi ai i tētahi pō raumati?
- Me pēhea te whakanui a tō whānau i ngā tau?

Hei whakaaro mō ngā mahi e pai ana mō tētahi hararei motuhake, tirohia [ngā mahi hararei mō tētahi e mate wareware ana](/activities/holidays/).

## Whānau me ngā hoa

- Korerohia mai mō te tangata i tino katakata ai koe.
- He pēhea te rā marena?
- Korerohia mai mō ō tamariki i te wā e iti ana rātou.
- Ko ēhea ngā ingoa i kīia ai koe e ō hoa?

Pātai mārie mō te whānau. Ki te pōuri te tangata mō tētahi, whai i tana ara, ā, kaua e pātai kia maumahara ia ki ngā taipitopito pāmamae pea.

## Ngā wāhi me te taiao

- Korerohia mai mō tētahi wāhi e aroha ana koe ki te whakaaro atu.
- He pēhea te āhua o te rangi i te wāhi i tipu ai koe?
- He māra tāu, he rākau pai rānei?
- Korerohia mai mō tētahi haerenga i pai ai koe.

## Te whakamahi whakaahua me ngā taonga

Ko ngā whakaahua he take pai mō te tīmatanga. Pūruatia tētahi whakaahua ki te taumata o ngā kanohi, ā, kīa, "Korerohia mai mō tēnei." Ka kī te [aratohu mahi a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) me whakamahi ngā taonga, ngā whakaahua me ngā waiata e mōhio ana te tangata hei tīmata kōrero. He pai te pouaka maumahara me ētahi taonga nō te oranga o te tangata; tirohia [ngā mahi maumahara](/activities/reminiscence/) mō te hanga.

## Ina mamae te maumahara

Ko ētahi maumahara ka puta ai te pouri, te wehi, te riri. Ina pōrearea te tangata, kaua e pēhanga. Kīa atu he kupu mahana, pērā i, "He uaua tērā wā. Kei te pai koe ki te kōrero mō tētahi mea harikoa ake?" Kātahi ka huri te kaupapa, hōmai he tī, whakatangihia he waiata pai.

## Hono ki tētahi kēmu, ki tētahi hīkoi rānei

Whai muri i te hui maumahara, ka pai tētahi kēmu māmā hei whakamārama i te ngākau. Whakamātauria ngā [kāri kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/) mō ngā akiaki tuwhera atu, rānei [whakaoti te kīwaha](/games/finish-the-saying/) mō ngā kīanga mōhio nō te tamarikitanga o te tangata. Hei aratohu kōrero whānui, tirohia [ngā aratohu kōrero mō tētahi tangata e mate wareware ana](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
