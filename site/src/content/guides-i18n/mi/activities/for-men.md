He rite te nuinga o te tangata ki te kimi i ngā “mahi mō ngā tāne e mate wareware ana” nā te mea kāore ngā whakaaro noa e rite ana ki te tangata i a koe. He tika tērā āwangawanga. Engari ko te whakautu, ehara i te rārangi mahi “tāne”. Ko te tīmatanga, ko tēnei tāne tonu: he aha tana mahi, he aha tāna mahi i ngā rangi whakatā, he aha ngā mea i kaha ai ia ki te whai take. He rerekē ngā hiahia o ia tangata. He tini ngā tāne e aroha ana ki te tunu kai, te māra, te peita, te waiata, ā, he tini wāhine e aroha ana ki ngā motuka me te pōro whutupaoro.

## Me pēhea e kitea ai ngā mahi e pai ai ia?

Tīmatahia mai i tana kōrero o tōna oranga. E kī te Alzheimer's Association me hanga te [mahere o ia rā](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) i runga i ngā pai, ngā kino, ngā pūkenga me ngā hiahia o te tangata, me te āhua o tana whakahaere i tōna rā i mua. Pātai ki a koe anō:

- **He aha tana mahi?** Tari, pāmu, wheketere, tōtika, hōia, hoko, whakaako, taraiwa, te kāinga?
- **He aha tāna mahi i ana wā waiwai?** Hī ika, kāri, mahi rākau, hākinakina, karakia, waiata, tunu kai, he motuka kei te whare taputapu?
- **He aha ngā tikanga o tana rā?** Te kawhe me te nūpepa ia ata? Te tirotiro i te huarere? Te horoi motuka i ngā Rātapu?
- **He aha tāna i tīmata tonu i a ia anō?** E kī te Association, ina tīmata te tangata i ngā mahi kāore i tono, pēnei i te tātā i te papa, ka noho pea aua mahi hei wāhanga o te tikanga.
- **He aha tana e karo ana?** He pai ki ētahi te mātakitaki hākinakina; he raruraru ki ētahi te tere me te haruru. Titiro ki te wā e harikoa ai ia, e māuiui ai, e pōuri ai rānei.

Kōrerotia hoki ngā whānau me ngā hoa tawhito. He mahara pea ō rātou ki ngā hobi kua ngaro i te oranga o ia rā.

## Ngā whakaaro mahi i ahu mai i te mahi o mua

Ka whakaatu te Alzheimer's Association i tētahi tauira mārama: he kaimahi tari o mua pea ka pai ki te whakarite moni, ki te hanga tuhinga mahi rānei, ā, he kaiahuwhenua, he kaitiaki māra rānei pea ka pai ki te mahi i te māra. He rite tēnei whakaaro ki ngā mahi maha.

| Mahi o mua | Whakaaro mahi | Tauira ngāwari ake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tari, pakihi | Whakarite pepa ki ngā kōnae, tuhi ki ngā pūkete, arotake i tētahi rārangi, whakarite i te tuwhera o te tēpu | Whakakohi i ngā emepu; tuku ngā mēra ki tētahi pouaka |
| Ngā mahi ā-ringa, ngā makanika | Whakarite i ngā nati, ngā rivete me ngā ringi ma; whakatūturu i ngā nati nui ki te ringa; tirotiro i ngā kātaloha taputapu | Whakarite ā-rahi ki ngā kapu e rua |
| Pāmu, whakapaipai whenua | Whakamākū, tāwhiti i ngā rākau, kohikohi rau, tirotiro i te māra | Noho ki waho i a koe e mahi ana; pupuri i ngā paita kākano |
| Hōia | Whakamā i ngā hū, whakaraupapa i te tuhinga, tirotiro whakaahua o te mahi hōia mēnā he pai ki a ia | Whakarongo ki ngā waiata o taua wā |
| Hoko, ratonga kiritaki | Mihi ki ngā manuhiri, tuku kai iti, whakarite pūtea hoko | Noho ki te tatau mō te mihi |
| Whakaako | Pānui ā-waha, whakarite pukapuka, whakarite whakaahua ā-kaupapa | Titiro ki tētahi pukapuka whakaahua tahi |
| Taraiwa, waka | Titiro ki ngā mapi, ngā maheni motuka tawhito, horoi i te motuka | Mātakitaki motuka e haere ana mai i te paepae |
| Kāinga me te whānau | Tātā kākahu, whakarite i te tēpu, āwhina i te kai ahiahi | Whakamāmā i ngā tōwera; haramai |

Ko ētahi maharatanga, pēnei i te mahi hōia, ka pūtea pea he kare ā-roto kaha. Whaia tōna arataki, kia pai te kōrero mō ngā wā o mua, ā, whakarerea te kaupapa mārie mēnā he pōuri ia.

## Ngā whakaaro mahi i ahu mai i ngā hobi

1. **Mahi rākau.** Te whakapai i tētahi poraka rākau, te peita i tētahi kōhanga manu. E kī te Alzheimer's Association me tango ngā rauemi me ngā taputapu koi i te wāhi mahi, kia pai, kia haumaru te mahi pēnei i te tātā.
2. **Hī ika.** Te whakarite i tētahi pouaka hī ika kua tangohia ngā matau, te pānui i ngā tuhinga hī ika, te noho ki te taha roto wai tahi.
3. **Motuka.** Te horoi motuka, te whakapaipai i te konumohe, te titiro i ngā whakaahua motuka tawhito, te haere i te motuka hei pāhihi.
4. **Hākinakina.** Te mātakitaki i tētahi tākaro tawhito, ngā wāhanga matua rānei, te whiu i te paōro māmā, te whakatakoto i ngā paōro gōfa ki runga whāriki, te whakarite i ngā kāri pōro.
5. **Kāri me ngā tākaro.** He ringa kāri māmā i tētahi tākaro mōhio, he tākaro domino, he huri kāri kimi tōrite rānei. Whakamāmāhia, whakarerea te tatau.
6. **Waiata.** Ngā waiata o ana tau taiohi me ana tau rua tekau, he rōpū pai, he himene rānei. Titiro ki tā mātou [arataki mahi waiata](/activities/music/).
7. **Tunu me te kaurī.** Te horoi huawai, te whakakohu i te kōhua, te hanga pātī, te whakamārama i tana tunu hanu mīmī i a koe e tunu ana.
8. **Te nūpepa.** E kī te Alzheimer's Association ka pai pea tētahi tangata i pānui tonu i te nūpepa ki te mahi pēnei, ahakoa kāore e mārama katoa ki ngā kōrero. Ka taea te pupuri te kawhe me te nūpepa hei wāhanga o te ata.
9. **Hanga.** Ngā tauira nui, ngā pūnaha hanga māmā mō ngā pakeke. Whiriwhiria ngā wāhanga e māmā ai te mau.
10. **Te manaaki i tētahi mea.** Te whāngai i te mokai, te whakakī i te kōpu manu, te whakarite i ngā rākau. E whakarārangi ana te NIA i te [whāngai, te tiaki i te huruhuru, te hīkoi i te mokai rānei](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) hei mahi pea ka pai ki ngā tāngata.

## Me pēhea te tuku mahi kia kaua e pēhea he mahi pōkai?

Whakaaturia hei āwhina tūturu. E kī te Alzheimer's Association me pātai “Tēnā koe, ka taea e koe te āwhina ahau?” me hono ngā mahi ki tētahi take. Whakatauritea:

- “Me whakarite e koe ēnei rivete?” (he rite ki te whakamātautau)
- “He pēke rivete ranu tāku, me whakarite ēnei i mua i te whakatika i te kapa. Ka taea e koe te āwhina?” (he rite ki te mahi)

Ētahi atu tohutohu:

- **Whakamahia ngā rauemi pakeke.** Ngā taputapu tūturu kua tangohia ngā tuaiwi koi, ngā kātaloha tūturu, ngā emepu tūturu.
- **Mahi ki te taha.** Ko ngā tāngata e rua e mahi ana i tētahi mahi he hoa tahi. Ko tētahi e mātakitaki ana i tētahi, he tirotiro tērā.
- **Kaua e whakatika.** Ki te tomo ngā rivete ki te kapu hē, waiho kia pēnā. Ko te mea nui ko te wā tahi me te whakaaro he tangata whai hua ia.
- **Āwhina ia kia tīmata.** E kī te Alzheimer's Association he nui ngā tāngata e hiahia tonu ana ki te mahi engari he uaua ki a rātou te whakarite me te tīmata. Mahia te tuatahi ngātahi.

## Me pēhea te urutau ina rerekē ngā mea?

Ka rerekē te mate wareware, ā, e kī te Alzheimer's Association me whakauru ngā mahi māmā, ngā mahi auau ake, ā, me whakarite kia iti ake tana mahi. Ko te tangata i hanga i ngā miihini i mua, ka pai pea ki a ia te pupuri i te rīnoa ma, te kōrero mō ngā motuka, te mātakitaki i a koe e mahi ana. Ko te mātakitaki, he mahi tonu.

Ina pai rawa ia ki tētahi mahi, he pai pea te mahi e hono ana ki tana mahi o mua, ehara i te mahi tonu. E kī te Association, ko te kaitoi ngaio ka pōrearea pea nā ngā panoni i āna mahi, ā, ko te kaitoi ā-kāinga ka pai ki tētahi huarahi hou. He rite tērā ki te kaimahi rākau tohungatanga nui.

## Te haumaru o ngā mahi ā-mahi

- Tangohia ngā taputapu hiko, ngā tuaiwi, ngā ngira me ngā mati he kiko koi.
- Katia te wai peita, te hinu, me ngā matūkai.
- Kia kore e kitea ngā kī motuka ina kore e taraiwa tonu ia.
- Mātakitakina ngā mahi katoa o te wāhi mahi me te māra.
- Whiriwhiria ngā wāhanga nui e kore e taea te horoi.

## He aha ina kī kore ia?

Whakaarohia. He pēhanga pea te mahi, he tamariki pea te āhua, he kore tika rānei i tēnei rā. E kī te Alzheimer's Association kia kaua e akiaki. Whakamātauria tētahi mahi kē, tētahi wā kē, mēnā rānei kia noho tahi kē. Titiro ki [he aha te mahi ina kī kore rātou](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).
