Ko te whakakara tētahi o ngā mahi auaha māmā rawa hei tuku ki tētahi tangata e noho ana me te Alzheimer's, me tētahi momo mate wareware atu rānei. Kāore e hiahia he pānui, he mahara, he whakautu tika. Ka neke ngā ringa, ka puta ngā tae, ā, he whakaahua hei kōrero. Ko tēnei whārangi e whakaatu ana me pēhea te kōwhiri i ngā whārangi, he aha ngā mea hei whakarite, me pēhea te whakakara tahi kia kaua e ahua tamariki, e ahua whakamātautau rānei.

## Te take e pai ai te whakakara mō te tokomaha e mate wareware ana

Ka whai mahi ngā ringa, ka whai titiro ngā kanohi i te whakakara. Ka taea te mahi e noho ana ki tētahi tēpu, ki tētahi tūru ki te taha o te matapihi, ā, ka māmā te tango, te tuku. Ka kī te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) he huarahi te toi hei whakaputa i ngā whakaaro, te koa ki te wā o nāianei, ā, ka kī kāore e hiahia he pūkenga toi.

Ka tohutohu te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kia kōwhiria ngā mahi e rite ana ki ngā mea e taea tonu e te tangata, ā, kia urutaua i ngā wā e rerekē ana ngā pūkenga. He māmā te urutau i te whakakara: he whakaahua nui ake, he pēniti matotoru ake, he tae iti ake, he mata pāwhi rānei.

## Kōwhiria he hoahoa pakeke

He tino kino ki ngā pakeke ngā pikitia tamariki. Rapua:

- **Ngā whakaahua o te taiao:** he māra me ngā puāwai nui, he roto me tētahi poti, he manu i runga manga.
- **Ngā taonga mōhio:** he tīpoti, he paihikara, he hua, he potae.
- **Ngā hoahoa nui, māmā:** ngā mandala me ngā hoahoa rōpū ahurei he wāhanga nui ruarua.
- **Ngā tā toi, ngā whenua rānei** ka mōhio pea te tangata nō tētahi wāhi pai, nō tētahi kaitoi pai rānei.

Karohia ngā whārangi he wāhi iti, he tōmairangi, he uaua ki te whakakī. He pai ngā whārangi pakeke ka taea te tā, ā, kei ā mātou [mahi mate wareware ā-taiwhanga kore utu](/activities/printable/) ngā whārangi tuhi nui.

## Ngā mea hei whakarite

- **Ngā taputapu matotoru, māmā ki te pupuri.** Ko ngā pēniti whakakara nui, ngā tohu matotoru, me ngā pēniti tae he hono mānui he māmā atu i ngā pene iti.
- **He kōwhiringa tae iti.** Ko ngā tae e toru, e whā he nui. He nui rawa ngā kōwhiringa ka whakapōhēhē.
- **Pepa pakari, kāri pakari rānei.** Ka rari, ka heke te pepa mānanana. Tāpiria ngā koki ki ngā kokonga, whakamahia rānei he papa kōwhiri.
- **He tēpu mārama.** Tangohia ngā mea kua poke. Whakaritea he whārangi kotahi i te wā.
- **Tētahi mea hei kūmaha, hei whakakōtahi,** pērā i tētahi kākahu, tētahi pūngāwhā, mō tētahi āhua ngāwari ake.

## Te whakakara i runga taipapa

Ko te [kēmu whakakara](/games/coloring/) i runga i a Golden Moments e mahi ana mā te pāwhi i tētahi tae, ā, ka pāwhi i tētahi wāhanga o te whakaahua. Kāore e hiahia he pūkenga ringa nanakia, nō reira ka pai pea mō ngā tāngata kua ngoikore te pupuri. Noho ki te taha o te tangata kia taea ai te tiritiri i te mata, ā, waiho kia kōwhiri ia i ngā tae. Whakakorehia ngā pānui kia noho tonu te whakaahua hei aronga.

## Me pēhea te whakakara tahi

1. **Tīmatatia te rārangi tuatahi tahi.** Pupuria te pēniti ngātahi, rānei whakamaruhia tō ringa ki runga i tō rātou mō ētahi rārangi, kātahi ka tuku.
2. **Kōrerohia ngā tae me ngā wāhi.** "He ātahanga tērā kahurangi. I kite koe i te wai pērā te tae?" ka whakaara i te kōrero, kāore he pātai whakamātau.
3. **Whakanuia ngā kōwhiringa, kaua te māturuturu.** "He tae mahana rawa atu tāu i kōwhiri ai" he pai ake i te "Kua noho koe ki roto i ngā rārangi."
4. **Waiho kia arahi rātou.** Mēnā e hiahia ana rātou ki te tā i te whārangi katoa, tukua.
5. **Whakamutua i te wā e mutu ai rātou.** Whakatakotoria iho ngā pēniti, ā, tūtohua he tī, he hīkoi rānei.

## Te urutau i te whanaketanga o te mate wareware

I te wāhanga tuatahi, he maha ngā tāngata e pai ana ki ngā whārangi hōhonu, ā, ka taea e rātou te kōwhiri i ō rātou ake tae me ngā hoahoa. I te wāhanga waenga, he pai ake ngā whakaahua nui me ngā kōwhiringa iti. I ngā wāhanga whakamutunga, whakamātauria tētahi hanga nui kotahi, tētahi pepa tae māeneene, rānei te purihia noa tētahi pēniti i te wā e waiata ana koe i tētahi waiata. He tangata ka pai ake ki te titiro ki tētahi whakaahua kua oti, i te mahi i tētahi.

Hei kōrero anō mō ngā mahi auaha i ia wāhanga, tirohia [ngā mahi toi me te ringa mō ngā pakeke e mate wareware ana](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) me [ngā kaupapa toi mō ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana](/activities/art-projects/).

## Te wā e mutu ai

Whakamutua mēnā ka pōrearea te tangata, ka pēhi ia i te pepa ki tawhiti, ka ahua ngenge rānei. He angitu tonu mēnā ka koa ia ki ngā meneti e rua. Ka taea te hoki mai ā muri ake, ki te whakamātau whakaahua kē, rānei he mahi kē pērā i te [mahi ā-rongo](/activities/sensory/).
