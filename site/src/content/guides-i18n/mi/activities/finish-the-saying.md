Ko te kēmu whakaoti kīwaha he kīanga mōhio e mutu ana i mua tonu i tōna kupu whakamutunga. Mō te nuinga, he huarahi pai, iti te pēhanga, hei tiritiri i tētahi wā. Ka mahi te kēmu nā te mea he tawhito, he mōhio ngā kupu, ā, kāore e hiahia te tangata ki te maumahara i tētahi mea mō tēnei rā. Kei tēnei aratohu me pēhea te tākaro, me pēhea te kōwhiri i ngā kīanga, me pēhea te pupuri i te mārie.

## He aha te kēmu whakaoti kīwaha?

He kēmu poto tēnei, ka pānui tētahi tangata i te wāhanga tuatahi o te kīanga, ā, ka whakakī tētahi atu i te mutunga. Hei tauira, “Ehara taku toa i te toa takitahi, engari…” Ka kī pākahoa te tangata i te mutunga, ka tohu ki a ia, rānei ka kōwhiri i tētahi o ngā mutunga e rua, e toru ka hōmai e koe. He pai ki ētahi te pānui i te haurua tuatahi.

Ka tukuna e Golden Moments tēnei hei [kēmu whakaoti kīwaha koreutu](/games/finish-the-saying/) me ngā tuhinga nui. Ka taea hoki te tākaro kāore he papaaho, mā te kī i te haurua tuatahi, ā, mā te tatari.

## Me pēhea te tākaro, i ia takahanga

1. **Kōwhiria he wā mārie** ā, noho ki te taumata o ngā kanohi, ki tō aroaro o te tangata.
2. **Pānuitia te wāhanga tuatahi mārie,** ki tētahi reo mahana, ā, whakatārewa. Ka whakatau te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) kia kōrero mārie, me ngā rerenga poto, māmā.
3. **Tatari.** Hōmai he wā, he hēkona e whiti ana. Ina kore he mea e puta, hōmai rua, toru mutunga, kotahi tonu te tika.
4. **Whakanuia ngā whakautu katoa.** "Āe, koirā tērā" rānei "Kāore ahau i whakaaro ki tērā" ka pupuri i te harikoa.
5. **Kōrerohia te kīanga.** Pātaia he aha tōna tikanga ki a rātou, ko wai i rongo ai rātou. Koirā te wāhi o te uara pono.
6. **Whakamutua i mua i te ngenge.** He hātepe mōhio, he kata, he kī katoa te huihuinga.

## Te hōmai kōwhiringa, kaua e whakamātau

Ka māmā ake te kēmu ina hōmai he kōwhiringa mutunga, engari ka pēnei pea he whakamātautau mēnā ka hōmaitia hei wero. Kia mārie te reo. Kīa "Me whakarongo tātou ki te mutunga, me whiriwhiri rānei koe?" kāore ko "Ko tēhea?" Hōmaihia ngā kōwhiringa i muri i te tatari, kaua i mua i te wā e whai wāhi ai te tangata ki te whakautu.

Ki te pōuri, ki te whakamā te tangata, whakakorea te kēmu, ka neke ki tētahi atu mea. Ko tā mātou [aratohu kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) e whakamārama ana me pēhea te whakarerekē i te ara kāore he rongo nui.

## Te kōwhiri i ngā kīanga e pai ana ki te tangata

- **Whakamahia ngā kīanga nō te tangata.** Pātaihia te whānau he aha tā tō rātou mātua, tā tō rātou hoa rānei i kī tonu ai. Ko ngā kīanga nō te tamarikitanga he māmā rawa te mōhio.
- **Kōwhiria ngā kīanga poto, o ia rā.** Ko ngā kīanga mō te huarere, te kāinga me te kautanga, te whānau me ngā wā o te tau he tino mōhio, he māmā te whai.
- **Whakamāmāhia.** Ko ngā whakataukī e whakawhirinaki ana ki ngā kupu tawhito, ki tētahi kaupapa whakaako, he pōhēhē pea. Waiho mō ngā tāngata e harikoa ana ki a rātou.
- **Tirohia te reo.** He maha ngā kīwaha kāore e taea te whakamāori. Whakamahia ngā kīanga e mōhio ana te tangata i tōna ake reo, ahakoa me kimi kīanga hou.

## Ngā kīanga ā-kaupapa

I raro nei, ka whakaatu ngā tauira i te mutunga i roto i ngā pūkoro. Pānuitia anake te wāhanga tuatahi, kātahi tatari.

- **Huarere:** “Ka tāwhiri, ka…” (mutunga: “ka ua”); “Ehara taku toa i te…” (mutunga: “toa takitini”). Ko ngā tauira e rua ērā, e mōhio ana ngā whānau.
- **Kai me te tunu kai:** “Ko te kai a te rangatira…” (mutunga: “he kōrero”).
- **Kāinga me te whānau:** “Ka pū te ruha…” (mutunga: “ka hao te rangatahi”).
- **Wā me te māra:** “Whatungarongaro te tangata…” (mutunga: “toitū te whenua”).

## He aha mēnā kāore e taea e te tangata te whakautu?

Ētahi tāngata kāore e kitea he kupu i ngā wāhanga whakamutunga. Kaua e whakarerea te kīanga. Pānuitia, menemene, ā, tono he whakautu mā te tohu, te tūtū, rānei mā te kōwhiri i tētahi whakaahua. Ka taea tonu te whakaatu he wā tahi mā te rhythm mōhio. Whakahonohia te kēmu ki te waiata, ki te inu wera, ki te paoa ringa, pērā i ā mātou [mahi wāhanga whakamutunga](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ētahi atu huarahi o te whakamahi kīanga

Ka hono pai ngā kīanga ki te kōrero mō te oranga o mua o te tangata. Ka taea te akiaki i ngā pūrākau mā ngā akiaki nō [ngā pātai maumahara](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), mā te tākaro i tētahi kēmu e hono ana, pērā i ngā [kāri kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/). Mō ētahi atu whakaaro puta noa i te rā, tirohia [ngā mahi mō ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana](/activities/ideas/).
