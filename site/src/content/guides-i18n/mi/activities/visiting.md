Ko te toro ki tētahi aroha ana koe i te whare tiaki maharatanga, i te whare kaumātua, i te hōhipera rānei, he maha ngā whakaaro ka ara ake. Ka whakaaro pea koe he aha te kōrero, he aha te kawe, ā, he hua anō rānei te toro mēnā kāore ia e maharatia. He hua tōna. E kī ana te National Institute on Aging, ka pai pinepine ngā tāngata e whai Alzheimer's ki te [whai hoa](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease), ahakoa kāore e maharatia ko wai te manuhiri. Ko te wharangi nei he tohutohu whaihua mō te wā i mua, i te wā, ā, i muri i te toro, me ngā tohu motuhake mō ngā noho hōhipera.

## Me pēhea taku whakarite mō te toro?

He iti te whakamahere, ka māmā ake te toro mō tātou katoa.

- **Kōwhiria te wā pai.** Ka kī a Dementia Australia me toro i te wā e tino mataara ai te tangata, ko te nuinga o te wā he ata, he tata ki te awatea. Ka kī te Family Caregiver Alliance me ako i te hōtaka o te wāhi, ā, me toro i te [wā ātaahua kāore he mahi](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/residential-care-options-visiting-someone-dementia-care/), kaua i te wā mahi.
- **Pātai ki ngā kaimahi.** Pātaihia me pēhea te tangata i tēnei rā, ā, tirohia ngā ture mō ngā kai, ngā kararehe whare, ngā haerenga ki waho.
- **Whakakīa he kete toro iti.** Kotahi, e rua rānei ngā mea hei tuku atu he rite (tirohia te rārangi i raro nei).
- **Whakamaherehia ētahi kaupapa kōrero.** Kāore pea te tangata e whai kōrero hou, nō reira kawea tāu: ngā kōrero whānau, te āhuarangi, tētahi kēmu pōro, tētahi pakiwaitara whakakata.
- **Whakaritea ō hiahia anō.** Ka pai pea ētahi toro, ka noho puku, ka uaua pea ētahi. He mea noa ēnei katoa.

## He aha taku kawe?

Kawea he mea hei tuku atu, kaua he mea hei whakamātau. Ko ngā kōwhiringa pai ko:

- **He pukaemi whakaahua iti** me ngā whakaahua nui, mārama. Tuhia he tohu ki muri mō ngā kaimahi.
- **He rārangi waiata pai** i runga i te waea, me ngā pūkahakaha ngāwari, he kaiwhakarea iti rānei.
- **Tētahi mea māmā hei pupuri,** pērā i te paraikete mōhio, te kakahu pūngāwari, te koti rānei.
- **He hinu ringa** mō te whakapaki ringa māmā, mēnā e pai ana ki te tangata te pā.
- **He makasini, he pukapuka pikitia rānei** mō tētahi hiahia tawhito, pērā i te māra, ngā motokā, ngā manu, te tunu kai.
- **He kai pai,** mēnā ka kī ngā kaimahi he haumaru.
- **Ngā mea mōhio mō te rūma.** Ka kī te Family Caregiver Alliance he pūtiki, he tipu, he whakaahua whānau me ngā toi, he pōta nui o ngā whakaahua whānau o mua.
- **He papatipi,** hei titiro ki ngā whakaahua whānau, hei tākaro kēmu māmā ngātahi rānei.

## He aha ā tātou mahi ngātahi i te wā toro?

Ka pai te mahi ngātahi ki te kaupapa kōrero. Ka kī a Dementia Australia me titiro ki ngā whakaahua, he mahi toi māmā, te whakatō tipu, te waiata, te tiritiri kai, te hīkoi i te tuawhenua. He atu anō ngā whakaaro:

1. **Titiro ki ngā whakaahua.** Tohua ngā mea e kitea ana e koe: "Titiro ki taua pōtae." "He ātaahua tēnei takutai." Waiho kia arahi te tangata. Kaua e pātai "Ko wai tēnei?"
2. **Tākaro waiata, waiata hoki.** Ka whakaora ngā waiata mōhio i te ngākau, ā, ka hanga he wā ngātahi, ahakoa he uaua te kōrero.
3. **Hīkoi poto.** Ka kī te Family Caregiver Alliance me hīkoi haere i te wāhi, otirā i te māra mēnā he māra. Ka tatau hoki te hīkoi i runga i te tūru wīra.
4. **Horoi ringa.** He hinu iti me ngā pā mārie ki ngā ringa ka taea te whakamārie.
5. **Āwhina ki te whakapaipai.** Ka kī a Dementia Australia me kati te makawe, peita ngā rārangi ringa, āwhina rānei i te tangata ki tana kākahu pai, ina pai ki a ia.
6. **Tākaro kēmu māmā.** Ka kī te Family Caregiver Alliance me kāri, dominoes, whatu rānei i tākaro ai te tangata. Ki runga i te papatipi, ka taea te tākaro [Ngā takirua pikitia](/games/picture-pairs/) me ngā whakaahua kei te kitea tonu, kāore he wā. Ka huri [Ngā takirua whakaahua](/games/photo-pairs/) i ngā whakaahua ka whiriwhiria e koe ki tētahi kēmu whakataurite, ā, ka noho ngā whakaahua ki tō kaitirotiro.
7. **Mātakitaki tahi i tētahi mea.** Ka kī te Family Caregiver Alliance he pai ngā whakaaturanga ngahau me ngā kiriata tawhito. Ahakoa kāore e mārama te tangata ki te kōrero, ka taea e koe te noho tata, te whakawhiti wā.
8. **Pānui kōrero.** He rotarota poto, he wāhanga pai, he reta nā tētahi whānau.
9. **Noho tahi noa iho.** Te pupuri ringa, te noho puku, he toro tika tērā. E kī ana te Alzheimer's Association he pai kāore e mōhio ki te kōrero, ā, ko tō taenga mai te mea nui rawa.

Ka āwhina te [Kāri kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/) ina kore he kōrero ka puta. He whakaahua, he pātai tuwhera kei ia kāri.

## Me pēhea taku kōrero i te wā toro?

- **Whakamōhio atu i a koe ia wā.** "Kia ora Pāpā, ko Mark tēnei, tō tama." E kī ana te NIA me whakamōhio poto ngā manuhiri i a rātou ake hei tīmata kōrerorero.
- **Tata mai i te mua,** ā, noho ki te taumata kanohi.
- **Kaua e whakatika, kaua e tautohe.** E kī ana te NIA kaua e whakatika te tangata mēnā he hē tana kōrero, mēnā he wareware rānei.
- **Whakaroa.** Ina uaua te kimi kupu, hoatu he wā roa.
- **Whakautu ki ngā kare-ā-roto.** Mēnā he pōuri, he manukanuka rānei te tangata, whakaaetia mēnā ka huri te kaupapa.
- **Kaua e whakamahi pātai whakamātau** pērā i te "Kei te maumahara koe ki ahau?" me te "He aha tāu kai i te awatea?"

Tirohia ō mātou [tohutohu kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) hei kōrero anō.

## He aha taku mahi ina kī ia "Kei te pīrangi au ki te hoki ki te kāinga"?

Whakautu ki te kare-ā-roto kei muri i ngā kupu (ko tā mātou [aratohu mō te wā e pātai tonu ana te tangata kia hoki ki te kāinga](/caregiving/asking-to-go-home/) he roa atu te kōrero). Kī te Family Caregiver Alliance ko te "Kei te pīrangi au ki te hoki ki te kāinga" te nuinga o te wā he tikanga mō te "Kāore au i te pai", ā, ka whakaaro ki ngā whakautu pērā i te "Kōrerohia mai tō kāinga" me te "He aha tō mea pai ki te kāinga?" Kī a Dementia Australia ko te "kāinga" he wā, he wāhi rānei i whakaaro ai te tangata he haumaru, pērā i tōna tamarikitanga.

Ngā mea ka ngana ai koe:

- te whakaatu i te kare-ā-roto: "He rite ki te mea kei te ngaro koe i tō kāinga."
- te whakarato i te whakamārie mā te pā, mēnā e pai ana
- te titiro ki ngā whakaahua, te kōrero mō te kāinga whānau
- te hāpai i tētahi hīkoi, tētahi inu, tētahi kai rānei

Kaua e tautohe, kaua e whakamārama he aha i kore ai e taea te wehe. Kāore pea e āwhina, he nui pinepine te pōuritanga ka puta.

## He roa pēhea te toro?

He pai ake te poto, te auau. E kī ana te Family Caregiver Alliance kāore pinepine te tangata e maharatia mēnā i noho koe e te rima meneti, e te rima hāora rānei, ā, e kī ana he pai pea ngā toro poto e toru ia wiki i tētahi toro roa kotahi. Kī a Dementia Australia kāore he tatau tika mō te maha o ngā toro, te roa rānei.

I ngā wiki tuatahi i muri i te neke ki te whare tiaki maharatanga, e kī ana te Family Caregiver Alliance me toro auau, kātahi ka ngāwari haere ki tētahi tikanga auau. Pātaihia ngā kaimahi he aha ā rātou e kite ana.

## Me pēhea taku wehe kāore e pōuri ai ia?

Ko te wehe pea te wāhanga uaua rawa. Kī a Dementia Australia me:

- **Kīia tō roa e taea ai te noho i tōu taenga mai.** "Ka taea e au te noho mō tētahi hāora, kātahi au ka haere ki te hokomaha."
- **Whakamahia tētahi mahi hei mutunga māori.** "Ina oti tēnei waiata, ka haere au."
- **Whakatauria ki te kai, ki te mahi rānei,** ā, tonoa ngā kaimahi kia āwhina i te tangata kia noho ki tētahi atu mahi.
- **Whakapōkaitia te whakamutunga, ā, kia ōrite ia wā.** Ko te tuaropi, te whakapāha, te "noho noa tētahi wā iti atu" ka uaua ake ngā wehenga kei mua.

I muri i tō wehenga, he māori te pōuri. Whakaōrangia tō tinana. Mēnā kua pōuri te tangata, ka taea e ngā kaimahi te āwhina ki te whakamārie i muri tata mai.

## Te toro ki tētahi e whai mate wareware ana i te hōhipera

He pōkākā, he rerekē, he tini tangata ngaro te hōhipera, ā, he uaua rawa tērā mō te tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware. E kī ana te NIA ki te [ārahi mō te kawe i tētahi e whai Alzheimer's ki te hōhipera](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/taking-person-alzheimers-disease-hospital) me:

- **Whai tētahi e noho tahi ana me te tangata** mō te nuinga o te wā: tētahi whānau, tētahi hoa pono, tētahi kaitiaki utu rānei.
- **Hoatu ki ngā kaimahi he pepa mōhio mō te tangata** me tōna ritenga, tōna ingoa e pai ana, ngā mea e pai ana, ngā mea kāore e pai ana, ngā mea ka whakaoho pea, me ngā tohu mamae kāore e kōrerohia. Tukuna ngā kape ki te rūma, ki te wāhi noho o ngā tapuhi.
- **Āwhinatia te kai me te inu.** Ka iti haere te kai o ngā kaumātua i te hōhipera. Whakatuwheratia ngā kōhinu, āwhinatia te kai, kainga tahi, hoatu he inu.
- **Kawea ngā mea whakamārie** pērā i ngā whakaahua, ngā taonga wairua.
- **Whakaitia te haruru.** Whakakatia te pouaka whakaata, whakaitia te reo o te waea me te reo o te whare kōrero.
- **Whakamātau i ngā tikanga whakamārie,** pērā i te puoro ngāwari, te pānui, te inoi, te waiata, te titiro ki ngā whakaahua tawhito.
- **Whakahihiko te nekeneke** i te mea haumaru, me te whakaaetanga o ngā kaimahi.
- **Kīia te tākuta i te wā tonu mēnā he tino kino ake, he rerekē rānei te tangata.** E whakamārama ana te NIA ko te pōhēhē tino ohorere, e kīia ana ko te delirium, ka puta mai i ngā raru pērā i te whakawaiū, te pāhare, te kore wai, te pāhua rongoā rānei, ā, me te tirotiro hauora.

He toro poto, mārie me tētahi kanohi mōhio me tētahi waiata pai pea te wāhanga whakamārie rawa o tētahi rā hōhipera.

## Te kawe tamariki i te toro

Ka mōwhiti te whakaaro o ngā tamariki. Whakareritea rātou i mua, me ngā kupu māmā, pono, whakaritea te toro poto, ā, hoatu he mahi, pērā i te whakaatu i tētahi toi, i te whakawhiti i tētahi whakaahua. E kī ana te NIA ka taea e ngā tamariki me ngā tāngata e whai Alzheimer's te ngahau ngātahi i ngā mahi māmā, pērā i te titiro ki te pukaemi whakaahua, te whakarongo ki te puoro. Kī a Dementia Australia me kawe he kete iti o ngā kai reka, o ngā mea mārie hei whakamahi i ngā tamariki iti.

## Me tiaki anō koe

He ngenge, he hohe te toro, ina nui te rerekētanga o te tangata. He pai te toro iti ake mēnā e hiahia ana koe, te kawe i tētahi hoa hei tautoko, te wehe moata i tētahi rā uaua. Hei whakaaro mō te tiaki i a koe anō, tirohia te [tiaki whaiaro mō ngā kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/). Mēnā kei tawhiti koe, tirohia tā mātou aratohu mō ngā [mahi waea me te waea ataata](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).
