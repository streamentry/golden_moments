He maha ngā whānau e rapu ana i ngā kēmu ipurangi koreutu mō te mate wareware, nā te mea e hiahia ana rātou ki tētahi mea hei tahi, e kore e hiahia ai he toa, he takiuru, he kāri utu. Ko ngā kōwhiringa pai he whakaahua, he māmā, ā, he māmā ki te mutu. Me he wā tahi, ehara i te whakamātautau. Kei tēnei aratohu me pēhea te rapu, me pēhea te whakarite i tētahi kēmu, ā, he aha ngā mea ka taea e ēnei kēmu, ka kore e taea.

## He aha te mea e pai ai he kēmu ipurangi mō te mate wareware?

Ka pai te kēmu mō te nuinga o ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana, ina tono ia ki te mōhio ki te whakaahua, ehara i te maumahara. Ko te hono i ngā whakaahua ōrite e rua, te pāwhi i te whakaahua e rite ana ki tētahi ātārangi, te tuku i tētahi mea ki te kete tika, ka whakawhirinaki katoa ki ngā mea e kitea ana e ngā kanohi i tēnei wā. Ko ngā kēmu e whakawhirinaki ana ki te maumahara i tētahi ture, kupu, tau, nekehanga o mua rānei, he pai ki te whakapōrearea.

Ka akiaki te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) i ngā mahi e hanga ana i ngā mea e harikoa tonu ana te tangata, e taea tonu e ia, ā, e āhei ai ia ki te angitu me te kore āwhina. Ka kī te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) kia māmāhia te mahi, kaua e whakarerea, ina panonitia ngā pūkenga.

## Ngā kēmu Golden Moments ka taea te whakatuwhera i te pūtirotiro

Ka whakatuwhera ia kēmu i raro nei i te paetukutuku, kāore he tango. Ko te nuinga ka taea te tākaro i runga papaaho, rorohiko rānei; ko ētahi ka taea hoki te tā.

- **[Ngā rōpū whakaahua rite](/games/picture-pairs/).** He rārangi whakaahua rite nui, me ngā whakaahua e kitea ana, kāore he wā, kāore he kaute. He pai mō te kēmu tuatahi.
- **[Kimihia te ōrite](/games/find-the-same/).** Kotahi whakaahua kei runga, ā, te whakaahua ōrite i waenga i te rua ki te whā kōwhiringa kei raro.
- **[Whakarite atarangi](/games/shadow-match/).** Ka puta tētahi atarangi māmā ki runga; pāwhia te whakaahua e hanga ana i taua atarangi.
- **[Whakarōpū ngātahi](/games/sort-together/).** Pāwhia tētahi whakaahua, kātahi te kete e tika ana mōna, pērā i te kāinga tunu kai, te māra.
- **[Whakaahua wāwāhia](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Ka wāwāhia tētahi whakaahua ki ngā wāhanga, ā, ka mau tonu te whakaahua katoa hei arataki.
- **[Whakaoti te kīwaha](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Ngā kīanga e mōhiotia ana me te kupu whakamutunga kua ngaro; pāwhia tētahi kōwhiringa, kia whakaatu rānei i te mutunga.
- **[Kāri kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/).** Kāri whakaahua me ngā akiaki tuwhera mō te kōrero, ehara i te whakamātautau. Ka taea hoki te tā.
- **[Ngā rōpū whakaahua o te whānau](/games/photo-pairs/).** Ngā rārangi rite i hangaia mai i ngā whakaahua e kōwhiria ana e koe i runga i tō taputapu. Ka noho ngā whakaahua ki te pūtirotiro i taua taputapu.
- **[Manawa mārie](/games/calm-breathing/).** He wā mārie, hōhonu hei hā tahi. Tīmatatia e koe, mutua hoki e koe.

Ka taea hoki ngā kimi kupu, te whakakara me te pingo whakaahua hei mahi tā. Tirohia [ngā mahi mate wareware ā-taiwhanga kore utu](/activities/printable/) mō ētahi atu.

## Me pēhea te whakarite i tētahi wā tākaro

1. **Kōwhiria he wā mārie** ina okioki te tangata. He uaua pea te ahiahi mō ētahi, nō reira titiro ki tō anō tauira i mua.
2. **Whakamahia he mata nui.** He pai ake te kite me te pāwhi i tētahi papaaho ki runga tū, rānei he rorohiko kua whakanuia te whakaahua.
3. **Katia ētahi atu rā, whakakorea ngā pānui.** Ko ngā pānui ohorere ka ohorere, ka pau pea te wā pai.
4. **Noho ki te taha o te tangata, kaua ki tōna aroaro.** He pai ake te mātakitaki tahi, he āhua hoa, kāore he āhua whakamātautau.
5. **Tīmatatia ki tētahi kēmu mō ngā meneti e rima ki te tekau.** Waiho kia pāwhi, kia tohu rānei te tangata i te mata, ā, whakamahia ngā wā takahi.
6. **Whakamutua i tētahi wā pai.** Oti tētahi takahanga, kātahi kōrerohia tētahi whakaahua, kī atu kia inu tī.

## Te haumaru me te whāiti mō ngā taputapu tahi

- **Ka noho ngā whakaahua ki te taputapu.** Ka whakamahia e ngā rōpū whakaahua ngā whakaahua e kōwhiria ana e koe, ā, ka noho ki te pūtirotiro i taua taputapu. Tirohia ko wai anō ka taea te whakatuwhera i te rorohiko, i te taputapu rānei.
- **Karohia ngā hononga o waho me ngā pānui.** Whakamahia ngā kēmu i te paetukutuku tonu, ā, kaua e pāwhi i ngā pānui, i ngā akiaki e tono ana i ngā kōrero whaiaro.
- **Whakawehea ngā utu me ngā kaute.** Kaua e waiho te tangata anake ki tētahi mata e tono ana i tētahi kupuhipa, i tētahi nama kāri.
- **Mātakitaki mō te haere ngaro.** Kia mamao te papaaho i te ringa mēnā ka haere atu te tangata i te whare me tērā.

Hei rārangi whakarite whānui, tirohia [me pēhea te kōwhiri i ngā taupānga me ngā kēmu mate wareware](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) me [te whakarite i tētahi papaaho, i tētahi waea rānei](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Ko te mea e taea e ēnei kēmu, e kore e taea

Ka hoatu e ngā kēmu ipurangi ki ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana te wā ki te harikoa i tētahi wā, ki te titiro ngātahi ki ngā whakaahua, ki te whai wāhi ki tētahi mea kāore he pēhanga. Kāore rātou e rongoā i te mate wareware, e whakaroki, e ārai rānei. Tētahi tauira e kīa nuitia ana ko te [whakahē a te Federal Trade Commission i Lumosity i te 2016](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) mō ngā kīanga whakangungu hinengaro kāore e taea te whakamana. Kia tūpato ki tētahi kēmu, taupānga, paetukutuku rānei e kī ana e pai ake ana te maumahara, e whakatau ana i tō hinengaro, e kī ana ka hurihia te heke.

Whakamahia ēnei kēmu hei wāhanga o te rā e whai ana i te waiata, te hīkoi, te kōrero me te okiokinga. Hei pānui mō te whakarite i ngā kēmu kia māmā, kaua e pēhanga, tirohia [te tākaro kēmu me tētahi e mate wareware ana](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) me [ngā kēmu maumahara kāore e whakamātau](/activities/memory-games/).

## Ina mutu

Mutua ina ahua ngenge te tangata, ina kore whakatā, ina pōrearea, ina ahua pōhēhē rānei. Mutua ina ngaro tana hiahia. Ko te mutu moata he hua pai. Ka taea te hoki mai i tētahi rā kē, ka huri rānei ki te kōrero, ki te hīkoi rānei.
