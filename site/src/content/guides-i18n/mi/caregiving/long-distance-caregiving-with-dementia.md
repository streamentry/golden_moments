Ka uaua pea te mōhio he aha te āwhina e hiahiatia ana i te tawhiti, engari kāore te tawhiti e tango i ngā ara katoa o te whai wāhi. Ka taea e koe te āwhina ki ngā ratonga, te whakaoti i tētahi mahi kua whakaaetia e te tangata, te tautoko i te tangata e tiaki ana i a ia ia rā. Tīmatahia mā te pātai ki te tangata e mate wareware ana he aha tāna e hiahia ana, ko wai hoki tana e hiahia ana kia whakaurua.

E kī te [aratohu tiaki tawhiti a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) ka taea e koe te whakarite toro, te rapu ratonga ā-rohe, te whakarite me te tāngata tiaki ā-rohe, te tuku i tētahi whakatā mōna. E kī anō te [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/faq/how-can-i-help-with-caregiving-from-far-away/) me uaua pea te rapu ratonga, te whakamahere toro i tawhiti. He whakaaro whānui tēnei aratohu; he rerekē ngā ratonga, ngā ture ā-rohe.

## He aha tāku e taea ai i tawhiti?

Kōwhiria ngā mahi ngāwari e tika ana ki ngā hiahia o te tangata, ki tā koe e taea ai i runga i te pono. Ka taea te tautoko mā:

- **Whakarite toro.** Pātai mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te āwhina ki te whakapā atu ki tētahi kāinga hauora, ki te whakahaere maramataka, ki te whakarite waka.
- **Rapua ratonga ā-rohe.** Titiro mō te tuku kai, te waka, te tautoko kāinga, ngā hōtaka rā ā-rā mō ngā pakeke, ngā rōpū kaitiaki rānei i te wāhi e noho ana te tangata. Whakamātauhia ngā taipitopito me te kaiwhakarato; he rerekē te wātea ā-rohe.
- **Whakahaere pepa, tono kua whakaaetia.** Ka taea te whakarite i ngā puka, te whakarite rauemi mēnā ka pātai te tangata. Ko ngā rekoata pūtea, ā-inihua, ā-hauora e hiahia ana ki te tiaki motuhake; whaia tā mātou [aratohu mō te tautoko moni me te haumaru tinihanga](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/), ā, tirohia ngā herenga mana me ngā whakaaetanga.
- **Whakatau wā mō te kaitiaki ā-rohe.** I tētahi toro, pātai ki tētahi kaitiaki ā-rohe he aha te tautoko e hiahia ana ia, he wā whakatā rānei. Akene he tuku kai, he mahi tahi i tētahi haerenga tākuta, he noho ki te tangata i te wā e okioki ana te kaiāwhina.
- **Pupuria te tūhono i te āhua e pai ana ki te tangata.** Pātai mēnā he pai ki a ia te waea, te waea ataata, te reta, te toro. He kōrerorero te waea, ehara i te whakamātautau mō ngā kai, ngā rongoā, te maumahara.

E kī te [Alzheimer's Association e kore pea e taea e ngā tāngata e tautoko ana i tawhiti te whakatau i ngā hiahia tōtika](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage). Ina taea, whakapā ki ngā tāngata e kite ana i te tangata ia rā, me te whakarite toro ki te taiao o te tangata anō. He kōrero whai hua tēnā, engari ehara i te whakakapi i te pātai ki te tangata, i te aromatawai ngaio rānei.

## Me pēhea e whakarite ai ahau me ngā kaiāwhina ā-rohe?

Pātai tuatahi ki te tangata ko wai tāna e hiahia kia whakaurua, me ngā kōrero hei tohatoha e pai ana ia. Akene he hiahia tāna kia whakahaere tētahi whanaunga i ngā tūtakitanga, kia titiro tētahi hoa tata ki tētahi take motuhake, kāore rānei he toronga auau. Kaua e tāpiri tētahi ki tētahi kōrerorero tiaki i te mea he tata ia.

Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, whakaritea he mahere whakapā māmā me ngā tāngata kua kōwhiria e ia:

- **Kōwhiria tētahi whakapā auau.** Whakaaetia ko wai ka tuku i ngā kōrero noa, me te auau.
- **Tapaina he kaitiaki hei whakakapi.** Whakatauhia ko wai ka tūtaki mēnā kāore te kaiāwhina ā-rohe e wātea ana.
- **Wehea ngā kōrero noa mai i ngā take ohotata.** Whakaaetia me pēhea e whakapā mai ai tētahi ā-rohe ki a koe mēnā ka rerekē wawe te haumaru, te hauora rānei o te tangata.
- **Pupuria ngā mahi mārama.** Tuhia ko wai ka whakahaere i ngā tūtakitanga, ngā waka, ngā tono ratonga, ngā mahi kāinga. Whakapātaritari i mua i te rerekē i te mahere.

E kī te [Alzheimer's Association me whai tūhono auau ki te whānau, ngā hoa noho tata, ngā kaiwhakarato tiaki](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ka taea hoki te pātai ki te kaiāwhina ā-rohe he aha tāna e hiahia ana. He pai ake pea te tono poto, pono ki te whakamahi, i te kupu whānui “Whakamōhiotia mai mēnā e hiahia ana koe i tētahi mea.”

## He aha ngā kōrero ka taea e te tākuta, te kaiwhakarato ratonga te tuku mai ki ahau?

Pātai ki te tākuta o te tangata, ki te kaiwhakarato ratonga he aha te whakaaetanga, te tuhinga e hiahiatia i mua i te tuku kōrero. E kī te [Alzheimer's Association me hiahia pea tētahi tuku whakaaetanga e te kaiwhakarato kia kōrerotia te tiaki ki tētahi whanaunga tawhiti](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). He rerekē ngā āhua me ngā ture, nō reira pātai ki te kaiwhakarato, kaua e whakaaro he mana tonu ki te whānau whakapapa anake.

Whakaarohia ko wai ka whiwhi kōrero mō te tangata. Tohaina anake ngā kōrero e hiahiatia mō te mahi kua whakaaetia, pupuria ngā taipitopito whakapā me ngā tuhinga ngā mea motuhake. Mēnā he tuhinga ture, he mana ōkawa e uru ana, pātai ki tētahi tohunga pono i te wāhi o te tangata he aha tāna e uru ana. Kāore tēnei aratohu e whakatau i te mana ture o tētahi.

## Me pēhea e whai hua ai te toro mō tātou tahi?

Pātai ki te tangata he pēhea tāna e hiahia ai kia whakapau te wā. Waiho he wāhi mō te kai, te hīkoi, te waiata, te kōrerorero mārie, ehara ko ngā tūtakitanga me ngā mahi anake.

Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te āwhina i te whakahaere, whakamahia tētahi wāhi o te toro ki te tūtaki ki ngā kaiāwhina ā-rohe, ki te kōrero ki tētahi kaimahi hauora, ki te tirotiro mēnā he pai tētahi whakaritenga. Pātai i mua i te tirotiro i ngā wāhi motuhake, i ngā rekoata. Tohaina ngā āwangawanga mārie, ā, aro ki tētahi rerekētanga motuhake, ehara i te whakawā mō te nui o tana kaha ki te tiaki.

E kī te [Alzheimer's Association me whakamahi ngā toro ki te whakatata anō, ki te whakarite me ngā kaitiaki ā-rohe](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Mēnā ka kite koe i tētahi āwangawanga, kōrerohia me te tangata me ngā tāngata e whakawhirinaki ana ia. Kāore e taea e te toro kotahi te whakaatu i te whānui o te hauora, o te kaha o tētahi.

## He aha mēnā ka rerekē ngā hiahia o te tangata, ngā tautoko rānei?

Arotakehia te mahere ina tono te tangata ki tētahi āwhina rerekē, ina uaua tētahi ritenga, ina kāore e taea e tētahi kaiāwhina ā-rohe te haere tonu. Pātai he aha tāna e hiahia ana kia mahi tonu ia, ā, ko tēhea mahi e hiahia ana kia titiro ki tēnei wā. He āwhina pea tētahi tākuta, tētahi kaimahi hapori, tētahi ratonga pakeke ā-rohe ki te tautuhi i ngā kōwhiringa. Mō ngā kōrero mō te āwhina ā-rohe, tirohia ā mātou aratohu mō te [tiaki kāinga](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) me te [noho mokemoke me te mate wareware](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/).

Me whai tētahi tangata ā-rohe e taea ai te urupare ki te āhuatanga ohotata. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mahere ohotata](/caregiving/emergency-planning-dementia/) ka āwhina i te tangata me ngā kaiāwhina kua kōwhiria e ia ki te tuhi i ngā whakapā me tētahi mahere whakahoki. Mō ngā tautohetohe mō te ko wai me te aha e mahi ai, tirohia [ngā kōrerorero whānau mō ngā tūranga tiaki](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/).

Ka whakaiti pea te tawhiti i te āwhina ā-ringa, engari kāore e whakatau i te nui o te aroha, ā, i te āhua o te āwhina ka taea e tētahi. Kia pūmau te mahere ki ngā hiahia o te tangata, ngā mahi tūturu, me te tautoko e wātea ana i tōna taha.
