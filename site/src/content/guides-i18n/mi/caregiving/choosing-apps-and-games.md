He maha ngā tākaro kua tapaina mō ngā kaumātua, mō te mahara, mō te mate wareware kei ngā toa taupānga. He pai te hanga o ētahi. Ko ētahi atu he kerēme ka rite ki te rongoā, he taima e huri ai te tākaro hei whakamātautau, he panuku, he kohinga raraunga rānei. Ka whakaatu tēnei whārangi i tētahi huarahi hei whakatau mō tētahi taupānga. Kāore mātou e whakarārangi, e arotake i ētahi atu hua.

## He aha tāku e whakatau ai i mua i te whiriwhiri taupānga mate wareware?

Whakatauhia he aha tāu e hiahia ai kia mahi te taupānga. Ko “he tākaro mā Mama” he tino rerekē ngā tikanga, ā, ko te kōwhiringa pai mō tētahi whāinga he kōwhiringa kino mō tētahi atu.

| Whāinga | He pēhea te āhua | He aha te rapu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whakaohooho mārie | Te takirua, te whakarōpū, ngā pūtiki māmā, ngā tākaro kupu | Te hūnga o te uaua ka taea te urutau, ngā whakaahua e kitea tonu ana, kāore he pehanga taima |
| Whakamahara | Ngā whakaahua tawhito, ngā whakataukī mōhio, ngā waiata o tō rātou taiohi | Ō whakaahua ake, ngā tohutohu tuwhera, kāore he pātai whakamātau |
| Mārie | Ngā whakaahua puhoi, ngā oro klanga, te hā, te taiao | Māmā te tīmata, te mutu, kāore he mea e rama ana, kāore he mea ohorere |
| Hononga | Te tākaro, te kōrero tahi, ā-kanohi, ā-ataata rānei | Hangaia mō ngā tāngata e rua, ngā tohutohu kōrero |

Ko te rangahau mō te “whakaohooho whakaaro” whakahaere mō ngā tāngata e mate wareware ana, he rōpū hui tonu e arahia ana e ngā kaimahi kua whakangungu, me te kōrerorero, ngā tākaro kupu, ngā pūtiki, te waiata me ngā mahi auaha. I kitea e te [arotake Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005562_can-cognitive-stimulation-benefit-people-dementia) he iti ngā painga i te toharite, he rerekē i waenga i ngā rangahau. He rerekē taua hōtaka pāpori, ā, he momo rerekē ki te taupānga takitahi, nā reira me titiro ki ngā taupānga katoa hei mahi tahi kotahi i waenga i te maha.

## Me pēhea taku mōhio mēnā e tika ana tētahi taupānga ki ngā āheinga o te tangata?

Titiro me pēhea te urupare a te tangata, kāore e whakawhirinaki ki te whakatau, ki te tapanga wāhanga rānei. Ko ngā tāngata e rua i te “wāhanga ōrite” he rerekē rawa ā-kite, ā-rongo, ā-ringa, ā-reo, ā-māmā ki ngā mata. E tūtohu ana te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) kia mahara ki ngā hiahia o mua me ngā āheinga o nāianei, ā, kia arohaehae ki te harikoa, ehara i te tutukitanga.

Ko ngā tohu o te tika pai:

- ka tīmata, ka uru mai rānei me te kore akiaki nui;
- ka kata, ka whakaputa kōrero, ka kōrero pūrākau;
- kāore ngā hapa e pēhi ana ki a rātou;
- ka pai ki te mutu ina tae ki te wā.

Ko ngā tohu kāore e tika:

- “Kāore au e taea” me “he kuare tēnei” e hoki tonu ana;
- te pōhēhē mō te aha me pāwhiri;
- te pukuriri, te whakamā, te tuku atu i te taputapu;
- ka mahi anake mēnā ka mahi koe i te nuinga katoa.

Ko te pōuritanga he tohu, ehara i te mea hei pehi. Titiro ki [te mahi ina kī kāo rātou](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Mō ngā whakaaro wāhanga i tua atu i ngā taupānga, titiro ki ngā mahi [wāhanga timatanga](/activities/early-stage/) me [wāhanga whakamutunga](/activities/late-stage/).

## He aha ngā āhuatanga hoahoa me rapu, me karo rānei?

Rapua te hoahoa mārie, māmā, mō te pakeke, ā, karo i ngā mea ka piki ai te pehanga.

| Rapua | Kia tūpato ki |
| --- | --- |
| Ngā whakaahua me te kupu nui, te rerekē nui o te māmā | Ngā tohu iti, ngā mata whakararu |
| Kotahi mahi mārama i ia mata | Ngā pātene maha, ngā rārangi kōwhiringa, ngā tohutohu maha ngā hītepe |
| Kāore he taima, he taima ka taea te whakakore | Ngā kaunoa, ngā utu tere, “pipiri i tō wā” |
| Kāore he kaute, he whakautu mārie | Ngā pūtake, ngā ora, ngā rārangi toa, ngā “pakeke o te roro” |
| Ngā hapa e whakahaerehia mārie | Ngā tohu haruru, ngā tohu X whero, ngā karere “Hē” |
| Ngā whakaahua pākeke, o te ao tūturu | Ngā kaupapa kāmutu, tamariki |
| Mahi i te kore hono, kāore e hiahiatia he kaute hei tīmata | Me rēhita, me uru ki te kaute, me tango ihirangi tuatahi |
| Māmā te whakatārewa me te mutu | Ngā matapihi ara ka pā ki te whakahau kia haere tonu, kia arotake i te taupānga |
| Kāore he panuku, he utu kotahi mārama, he utu ā-wā rānei | Ngā panuku, ngā pouaka taonga, ngā tohutohu hoko auau |
| Māmā mō tētahi atu tangata ki te uru | Kotahi kaitākaro anake, he hoahoa whakataetae |

He māmā te pāwhiri hē i ngā panuku me ngā tohutohu hoko.

## Me pēhea taku tirotiro i te tūmataiti o tētahi taupānga?

Tirohia he aha ngā mea e kohia ana e te taupānga, ko wai e tohatoha ana, ā, mēnā ka taea e koe te muku. I mua i te whakauru:

1. **Pānuihia te whakarāpopototanga tūmataiti a te toa.** Ko te [wāhanga Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) a Google Play me te [wāhanga privacy information](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102399) a te App Store e whakarārangi ana i ngā momo raraunga ka kohia, ka tohatohahia pea e te taupānga. E kī ana ngā kamupene e rua ko ngā kaihanga ake e hoatu ana i ēnei kōrero, nō reira he tīmatanga anake, ehara i te arotake.
2. **Pātaihia mēnā e hiahiatia ana he kaute.** Mēnā he whakaahua, he tākaro māmā anake te taupānga, kāore pea he take ka mōhio ai ki tō ingoa, tō īmēra, tō rā whānau.
3. **Tirohia ngā whakaaetanga.** E hiahia tūturu ana te tākaro takirua ki te wāhi, ngā whakapā, te pūkoro, te whare pukapuka whakaahua katoa? Whakakorehia ngā mea e hiahiatia ana.
4. **Rapua ngā panuku me te tātaritanga.** Ka taea e ngā taputapu panuku, aroturuki te tuku raraunga mō te whakamahinga o te taupānga ki ētahi atu kamupene.
5. **Kimihia kei hea ngā whakaahua.** Mēnā ka tāpiri koe i ngā whakaahua whānau, ka noho ki te taputapu, ka tukuna ki tētahi wāhi rorohiko rānei? Ka taea e koe te muku?
6. **Tirohia te muku.** Rapua i te kaupapa here tūmataiti, i te whakarāpopototanga raraunga, me pēhea te muku i ngā raraunga me te kaute. Me tino tūpato koe ki te taupānga e kī ana kāore e taea te muku i ngā raraunga, otirā mēnā kei a ia ngā whakaahua, ngā kōrero hauora.
7. **Pānuihia te kaupapa here tūmataiti** mō ngā whakautu mārama mō ngā mea e kohia ana, he aha, mō te roa o te wā, me ko wai ka tohatohatia.

### Ka tiakina e te HIPAA ngā mōhiohio i ngā taupānga mate wareware?

Kāore te nuinga, he mea whakaohooho i ngā tāngata maha. E [whakamārama ana](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) te U.S. Department of Health and Human Services e pā ana te HIPAA ki ngā kaupapa hauora, ki te nuinga o ngā kaiwhakarato hauora, ki ngā kaiwhakahaere raraunga hauora me ō rātou hoa mahi. I te nuinga o te wā, kāore e tiakina e ia ngā raraunga ka urunga e koe ki tētahi taupānga mō tō whaiaro, mēnā karekau i tukuna e tētahi kaupapa kua whakaūngia, e tōna hoa mahi rānei. He taupānga “mate wareware” mai i te toa taupānga, he mea noa kei waho i te HIPAA, ahakoa ka kōrero mō te hauora.

### He aha ka tiaki i ngā raraunga hauora i ngā taupānga kāore i te HIPAA?

E uru ana te Health Breach Notification Rule a te Federal Trade Commission ki te maha o ēnei. I te 2024 i [whakaoti te FTC i ngā panonitanga](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule) e mārama ai he pā te ture ki ngā taupānga hauora me ngā hangarau rite kāore i te HIPAA. Ko te “takahi” he whakapuaki kore whakaaetia, ehara i te hakinga anake, ā, me whakamōhio ngā kamupene ki ngā tāngata pānga me te FTC. He āruhe tērā i muri i te hē, ehara i te oati ka kohi noa te taupānga i ngā mea e hiahiatia ana.

## Me pēhea taku pānui i ngā kerēme mō te rangahau me ngā taunakitanga?

Pānuihia ngā kerēme taunakitanga mā te puhoi, ā, kia tino whakapae ki ngā kupu nui rawa. Ngā pātai hei pātai:

- **He aha tonu i rangahaua?** Ehara i te mea ka whakaatu te rangahau o tētahi hōtaka whakangungu kotahi kua rite ngā tākaro katoa o te taupānga ki te mahi.
- **Ko wai i rangahaua?** Kāore pea ngā hua i ngā pakeke hauora e pā ki te tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware.
- **He aha i ine ai?** He rerekē te piki ake i te tākaro ake ki ngā rerekētanga o te oranga o ia rā, te kōrero, te ngākau, te motuhake.
- **He kaha pēhea te rangahau?** Ka whakataurite te whakamātautau whakahaere matapōkere (RCT) i tētahi rōpū e whakamahi ana i te taupānga ki tētahi rōpū karekau. He hoahoa pai, engari kāore he taunakitanga mai i tētahi RCT iti, poto rānei. Rapua ngā rangahau maha, nā ngā kaitirotiro motuhake, e ōrite ana.
- **E kī rānei ka whakarerekē i te mate?** Ko te rangahau e whakaatu ana i ngā pikinga iti i tētahi whakamātautau, kāore he taunakitanga mai he aukati, he ārai, he rongoā rānei i te mate wareware e te taupānga.

Ko te whārangi a te National Institute on Aging mō [te hauora whakaaro me ngā kaumātua](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults) e whakaatu ana i tētahi whakamātautau nui a te NIH mō te whakangungu whakaaro whakahaere, ā, ka whakatūpato ki ngā kerēme mō ētahi tākaro rorohiko, ipurangi rānei ka whakapai ai i te mahara me te whakaaro. E kī ana karekau anō he taunakitanga nui kia rite ngā taupānga whakangungu roro hokohoko ki te whakangungu i whakamātauria i taua whakamātautau. Ka taea te pānui atu i tā mātou aratohu mō [ngā tākaro roro me ngā taunakitanga](/brain-health/brain-games/) me [te rangahau mō te ārai i te mate wareware](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).

### He aha tētahi tauira tūmatanui o tētahi kerēme whakapohehe?

I te Kohi-tātea 2016, i [whakapuaki te FTC i tētahi whakataunga](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) me ngā kaihanga o Lumosity, he hōtaka “whakangungu roro”. E kī ana te FTC i kī te kamupene, kāore he taunakitanga whai take, ka taea e āna tākaro te whakaroa i te heke whakaaro nā te pakeke, ā, ka tiaki i te raruraru whakaaro iti, i te mate wareware me te Alzheimer's. I whakaae te kamupene ki te utu $2 miriona, ā, me whai taunakitanga pūtaiao tika, pūmau i mua i ngā kerēme pēnei i āpōpō. He whakamahara whai hua tēnei keehi mō te kupu hokohoko, ehara ia i te taunakitanga.

### He aha ngā kupu tohu tūpato?

Kia tūpato ina kī tētahi taupānga, tētahi panuku rānei he:

- “ārai”, “whakahoki”, “aukati” rānei “whakaora” i te mate wareware, i te Alzheimer's;
- “kua whakamātauhia ki te taha haumanu” engari kāore e whakaingoatia te rangahau, ko wai i uru, he aha i ine ai;
- hoatu he “pakeke o te roro”, he kaute mahara e whakaatu ana i te whakatau;
- ka “whakakoi”, “whakanui” rānei “whakangungu” i te roro;
- whakamahi i ngā whakaaturanga hei taunakitanga.

Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio ki tētahi kerēme, pātaihia te tākuta o te tangata.

## Ka noho pēhea a Golden Moments?

He mahi tahi māmā tā Golden Moments, ehara i te whakamātautau mahara, i te whakaora, i te tohutohu hauora. Ko ā mātou tākaro, pēnei i [Ngā Takirua Whakaahua](/games/picture-pairs/), [Ngā Kāri Kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/), [Ngā Takirua Whakaahua Whānau](/games/photo-pairs/), [Pingo Whakaahua](/games/picture-bingo/) me [Te Hā Mārie](/games/calm-breathing/), he mea hanga mō te harikoa me te kōrero. Karekau e tātari, e rongoā, e aukati, e ine i te mate wareware, ā, kāore mātou e kī mō te mahara, mō te whakaaro.

He taupānga koreutu hoki tā mātou, [Golden Moments mō te iPhone, iPad me te Android](/app/). He takirua whakaahua mārie me ngā tāera nui, he tākaro me ngā whakaahua whānau ka whiriwhiria e koe, me ngā kohinga whakaahua ka mahi i te kore hono, karekau he kaute, he panuku, he taima, he kaute. Ka rite tonu te rārangi arotake nei: ko tōna tapanga tūmataiti i te App Store e pānui ana “Kāore Raraunga i Kohia”, ā, e whakamārama ana te [kaupapa tūmataiti o te taupānga](/app-privacy/) kei hea ngā kāpī whakaahua e puritia ana.

He pai ake ki a mātou kia whakawā koe i a mātou, i te whakapono ki a mātou. Whakamahia te rārangi arotake o tēnei whārangi, pānuihia tā mātou [whārangi tūmataiti](/privacy/) me te [whakapūtanga hauora](/medical-disclaimer/), ā, whakamutua te whakamahi i ētahi mea, tae atu ki ā mātou, mēnā kāore e tika ana ki te tangata e tiakina ana e koe.

## He aha te huarahi whaiwhakaaro ki te whakamātau taupānga hou?

Whakamātau tahi mō ētahi wiki e rua, ā, titiro he aha ka puta.

1. **Whakaritea tuatahi** mā tā mātou [aratohu whakarite taputapu me te waea](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/), ā, tīmata i te taumata māmā rawa.
2. **Kia poto ngā wā,** kitea ngā kata, te kōrero me te mārie, ā, whakamutua i tētahi wā pai.
3. **Whakarerea mēnā ka pukuriri,** ahakoa te pai o te arotake.
