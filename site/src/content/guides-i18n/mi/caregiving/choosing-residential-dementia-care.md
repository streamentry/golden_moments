Ko te whakaaro ki te whare manaaki ka whakaoho pea i te manawa tau, i te pouri, i te rangirua, i ētahi kare ā-roto maha rānei i te wā kotahi. Ko te tikanga o tēnei whakataunga, he kimi tautoko e hāngai ana ki ngā hiahia me ngā manakohanga o te tangata. Ehara tēnei i te whakamātautau mō te nui o te aroha o te whānau, ehara hoki i te hipanga kua whakaritea ka tae atu te katoa i te wā kotahi.

E kī ana te [Alzheimer's Association guide to long-term care](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-term-care), tana [residential-care checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) me te [Dementia Australia's residential-care guidance](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/residential-care), me titiro ki ngā hiahia o te tangata, ki ngā kaimahi, ki ngā ngohe, ki te taiao me ngā utu. Kāore tēnei whārangi e taunaki ana i tētahi whare motuhake, kāore hoki e whakakapi i ngā tohutohu ngaio, ā-pūtea rānei o tō takiwā.

## Me pēhea taku mōhio mēnā he pai te whakaaro ki te whare manaaki?

Tirohia he aha ngā tautoko e hiahiatia ana e te tangata ināianei, ā, mēnā ka taea aua tautoko te tuku ki te kāinga. Whakaarohia te haumarutanga o te tangata, ōna hiahia tiaki o ia rā, tōna oranga pāpori me ōna manakohanga. Whakaarohia hoki mēnā kei te pā ngā hiahia o te mahi tiaki ki te hauora o te tangata, ki te hauora rānei o ngā tāngata e tautoko ana i a ia.

Kāore he wā kotahi, he rārangi arowhai rānei e whakatau ana i te wā me neke te tangata. Ka pai pea ki tētahi tangata te noho ki tētahi hapori, ka hiahia rānei ia ki te noho ki te kāinga me ētahi atu āwhina. Kōrerohia ki te tangata, ā, ki te whai hua, ki tōna tākuta, ki tana kaimahi toko i te ora, ki tana kairuruku tiaki rānei. Pātaihia he aha ngā momo tautoko ka hāngai pea i mua i te whakatau ki te neke.

Whakaurua te tangata ki ngā whakataunga e hiahiatia ana e ia. Pātaihia he aha ngā mea nui ki a ia: ko te wāhi, ko ngā kai, ko te reo, ko te ahurea, ko ngā tikanga whakapono, ko ngā ngohe e taunga ana ki a ia, ko ngā haerenga mai rānei a te whānau me ngā hoa. Ka āwhina pea ōna manakohanga ki te whakawhāiti i te rārangi, ahakoa kei te huri ētahi hiahia tiaki.

## He aha ngā mea me mōhio au i mua i te toro i ngā whare?

Tuatahi, whakaritea he rārangi poto mō ngā tautoko e hiahiatia ana e te tangata. Tērā pea ko te āwhina ki ngā mahi o ia rā, ko te mahi tapuhi, ko te hononga pāpori, ko te reo e taunga ana ki a ia, ko tētahi wāhi rānei ki te noho ki waho. Mēnā kāore koe i te tino mōhio, pātaihia ki tētahi mātanga hauora, tiaki rānei he aha ngā ratonga ka hiahiatia pea.

Waea atu ki ngā whare i mua i te toro. Pātaihia mēnā ka taea e rātou te tautoko i ngā hiahia o te tangata, mēnā he rūma wātea, he aha ngā mahi ka whai ake, ā, he aha ngā mōhiohio e hiahiatia ana e rātou. Tonoa he kōrero ā-tuhi mō ngā ratonga me ngā utu. He rerekē ngā ture mō te āhei, te pūtea me te raihana i ia takiwā, nō reira tirohia ngā puna kōrero o tō takiwā, kaua e whakawhirinaki ki tētahi tuhinga whānui noa.

Pātaihia mēnā kua whakangungua ngā kaimahi mō te tiaki mate wareware, me pēhea te hanga mahere tiaki, ā, me pēhea te tuku kōrero hou a te whānau. Mōhiohia me pēhea te whakahaere i ngā mahinga o ia rā, he aha ngā ngohe e tukuna ana, ā, me pēhea te tautoko i ngā kainoho ki te kōwhiri. Ko te wāhanga tiaki mate wareware motuhake tētahi kōwhiringa, engari ehara i te mea me whai te katoa; whakatauritea ngā tautoko tūturu e tukuna ana e ia wāhi.

## He aha ngā mea me mātakitaki i te wā o te haerenga?

Me toro atu ki ētahi whare maha mēnā ka taea. Whakaritea he wā mō te haere tuatahi, kātahi pātaihia mēnā ka taea te toro anō i tētahi wā rerekē, i te wā kai rānei. Mā te haerenga tuarua ka kite pea koe i te mahinga o ia rā, ā, ka tutaki pea koe ki ētahi atu kaimahi.

Tirohia te āhua o te kōrero a ngā kaimahi ki ngā kainoho. Ka kōrero whakaute rātou ki te tangata, ka hoatu wā kia whakautu, ka whakauru hoki i a rātou ki ngā kōwhiringa noa o ia rā? Kei te āhua whakamarie ngā kainoho, ā, he mea ā rātou hei mahi e hāngai ana ki ō rātou ngākau nuitanga me ō rātou āheinga? Pātaihia mēnā ka taea e koe te kite i ngā hōtaka ngohe, ngā wāhi kai, ngā wāhi o waho me ngā wāhi tūmataiti.

Kimihia ngā āhuatanga e whai take ana ki tēnei tangata: ngā tohu māmā te mārama, ngā ara mārama ki te wharepaku, ngā taonga e taunga ana, ngā wāhi mārie, te tautoko reo me te tautoko ahurea, me ngā wāhi e taea ai te kite i ō rātou hoa aroha. Ehara te tomokanga ataahua anake i te whakaatu i te āhua o te tiaki o ia rā.

Mauria mai he hoa, he whanaunga rānei e whakawhirinaki ana koe ki a ia, mēnā ka āwhina tērā ki a koe ki te maumahara ki ngā pātai me te whakataurite i ō whakaaro ā muri ake. Ka taea e koe te whakamahi i te [Alzheimer's Association checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) hei tuhi i āu i kite ai. Whakaarohia ō tuhipoka hei tīmatanga kōrero, kaua hei kaute e kōwhiri ana i te whare māu.

## He aha ngā pātai hei āwhina i te whakataurite i te tiaki?

Pātaihia me pēhea te mahi a ngā kaimahi i ngā wā rerekē o te rā, me pēhea rātou e ako ai i ngā mahinga o te tangata, ā, ka aha ina huri ōna hiahia. Pātaihia ko wai e arotake ana i ngā mahere tiaki, ā, me pēhea te whakapuaki māharahara a te whānau. Mēnā ka hiahia te tangata ki te āwhina mō te kai, te tiaki whaiaro, te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero rānei, pātaihia he pēhea taua tautoko i roto i te mahi tūturu.

Pātaihia mō ngā haerenga mai a te whānau, ngā haerenga ki waho, ngā wā tākuta, ngā ngohe, me te āhei o te tangata ki te mau mai i ētahi taonga e taunga ana ki a ia. Mēnā he hōtaka motuhake mō te mate wareware tā te whare, pātaihia he aha te whakangungu, ngā ngohe me te tautoko o ia rā kei roto. Tonoa he tauira mō te āhua o te whakauru i ngā kainoho ki ngā kōwhiringa, kaua e whakawhirinaki ki te ingoa o te hōtaka anake.

Tonoa he whakamārama taipitopito mō ngā utu, he aha ngā mea kei roto, ā, me pēhea pea te huri o ngā utu. Pānuihia ngā kirimana āta, ā, rapua he tohutohu ngaio o tō takiwā mēnā kāore i te mārama ngā tikanga ā-pūtea, ā-ture rānei. Kaua e whakaaro ka utua, ka wātea rānei tētahi ratonga i runga anō i ngā kōrero nō tētahi atu rohe, whenua rānei.

Mēnā me whai whakaaro motuhake ki te tuakiri, ki ngā hononga, ki te tūmataiti rānei o te tangata, tirohia tā mātou [guide to supporting an LGBTQ+ person with dementia](/caregiving/supporting-lgbtq-person-with-dementia/). Kei reira ētahi pātai mō ngā ingoa e manakohia ana, ngā manuhiri, te tiaki whaiaro me ngā kaupapa here a ngā kaiwhakarato.

## Me pēhea taku āwhina i te wā o te neke?

Whakaurua te tangata ki ngā kōwhiringa e hiahiatia ana e ia. Mā te toro ki te whare, te kai ki reira, te noho wā poto rānei mō te whakamāmā i te kaitiaki, ka āwhina pea i ētahi tāngata kia taunga ki te taiao, mēnā e wātea ana, ā, e pai ana ki a rātou. Ka hiahia pea te tangata ki te kōwhiri i tētahi taonga mō te rūma, i ētahi kākahu, i tētahi mahinga rānei hei mau atu.

Tuhia ki ngā kaimahi ētahi mōhiohio whai hua, me te whakaae a te tangata. Tērā pea ko tōna ingoa e manakohia ana, tōna āhua whakawhitiwhiti kōrero, ōna mahinga o ia rā, ngā kai e pai ana ki a ia, ngā ngohe e ngahau ana ki a ia, me ngā mea e āwhina ana i a ia kia tau te noho. Kia poto, kia hāngai hoki ēnei kōrero ki te tiaki. Me noho tonu te tangata hei wāhanga o ngā kōrero mōna.

Mauria mai ētahi taonga e taunga ana ki a ia, mēnā ka whakaaetia e te whare. Pātaihia ki ngā kaimahi me pēhea te whakarite i te rūma kia mōhio te tangata ki tōna āhua, me te kore e uaua te neke haere. He nui ake pea te tikanga o tētahi whakaahua, o tētahi paraikete tino pai, o tētahi karaka e taunga ana, i te ngana ki te hanga anō i te kāinga katoa.

## Me pēhea taku whai wāhi tonu i muri i te neke?

Mahia tahi me ngā kaimahi kia mōhio koe me pēhea te tuku kōrero hou, ā, ko wai hei whakapā atu. Pātaihia ki te tangata he pēhea ōna whakaaro mō ngā haerenga mai me ngā ngohe. Ka hiahia pea ētahi tāngata ki ngā haerenga poto auau; ko ētahi atu ka hiahia pea ki te wā kia taunga. Tirohia ngā mea e āhua pai ana ki a rātou, ā, whakarerekē ngātahi.

Ka huri pea ngā hiahia tiaki me ngā manakohanga. Arotakehia te mahere tiaki me te tangata me ngā kaimahi, pātaihia mēnā kāore tētahi mea i te mārama, ā, tuaritia he kōrero hou ka āwhina pea. Mēnā ka rangirua ohorere te tangata, ka tino māuiui rānei, whakamōhiotia wawe te rōpū tiaki; e ai ki te [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), me whai aro hauora tonu te rangirua ohorere.

He whakataunga nui te kōwhiri whare, ā, kāore pea te kōwhiringa tuatahi e noho hei mea tino tika mō ake tonu. Kohia he mōhiohio, whakaurua te tangata, pātaihia mō te tiaki o ia rā, ā, kia tuwhera tonu te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero i muri i te neke.
