Ko te tautuhinga mate wareware i te wā e mahi ana ai te tangata ka ara ake ai ngā pātai mō te moni, te tūmataiti, te tuakiri me te haumaru. Ka pai pea ki tētahi kia mau tonu tana mahi o nāianei, ka whakaaro pea tētahi ki te panoni, ā, ka whiriwhiri pea tētahi ki te wehe. Kāore he ara kotahi e tika ana mō te katoa. He mea nui ōu whakapono, ngā mahi, te hauora me te tautoko.

Ko te [aratohu a Alzheimer's Association mō te Alzheimer's i te wā iti](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset) e kōrero ana mō te mahi, ngā pūtea whānau, me ngā painga kaituku mahi. Ko te [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead/work-and-dementia) me te [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) e whakaatu ana i ngā kōwhiringa mō te noho ki te mahi, te panoni i tētahi tūranga, te wehe rānei. He rerekē ngā ture mahi i ia wāhi, nō reira he pātai hei whakaaro ēnei aratohu, ehara i te tohutohu ā-ture, ā-pūtea rānei.

## Ka mutu rānei taku mahi nā te tautuhinga?

Kāo. Ko te tautuhinga anake kāore e whakatau mēnā ka taea e koe te mahi tonu. Ko te [Alzheimer's Society e kī ana](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) he whakatau tēnei e whakawhirinaki ana ki ō whakaaro, ki tō mahi, ki tō āhuatanga whaiaro. Ko ētahi ka mahi tonu i taua tūranga anō, ko ētahi ka panoni i ngā mahi, i ngā hāora rānei, ā, ko ētahi ka wehe i te mahi.

Whakaarohia he aha kei te pai, he aha kua uaua ake. Ka taea te tuhi he mahi e mōhio ana koe, ina ngenge koe, ina kōhōhā koe, mēnā kua pā mai tētahi hē motuhake, tētahi raruraru haumaru rānei. Ko ngā kaupapa tūturu ka āwhina koe, me tō tākuta ki te kōrero mō te mahi, kāore e huri tētahi rā uaua hei whakatau mō tō kaha.

He mea nui hoki te haumaru. Mēnā ko tō mahi he tuku waka, he whakahaere miihini, he tiaki i ētahi atu, he mahi haumaru kē rānei, pātai ki tō tākuta mēnā me pēhea te aromatawai i te tūraru. Ka whai pea ngā ture ā-rohe, ngā ture ngaio me ngā kirimana mahi. Kāore tēnei wharangi e kī mai mēnā he haumaru tētahi tūranga motuhake, mēnā he aha ā tō kaituku mahi me mahi.

## Me pēhea e kōrero ai ki tōku tākuta mō te mahi?

Whakamāramatia ngā mahi e uru ana ki tō mahi me ngā panonitanga kua kitea e koe. Pātai mēnā ka pā tētahi raru hauora ki tētahi mahi motuhake, he aha ngā tohu e tika ai kia rapu āwhina, mēnā ka taea e tētahi atu kaimahi ngaio te aromatawai i tētahi mahi. Ka pai pea te kawe mai i tētahi tangata whakapono, engari whakaaetia tahi me pēhea e tautoko ai ia kāore e kōrero ki runga i a koe.

Ko te [aratohu toro-tākuta](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) he huarahi hei whakarite pātai me ngā tuhipoka. Ka taea e koe te tono ki tō tākuta kia whakamārama he aha ka taea e ia te aromatawai, he aha ka hiahia ki tētahi aromatawai wāhi mahi, aromatawai mahi ohotata rānei. Kāore e taea e te aratohu mō te mate wareware whānui te whakakapi i te aromatawai hauora, haumaru takitahi.

## He aha ngā panonitanga mahi ka āwhina pea?

Ki te hiahia koe ki te mahi tonu, pātai he aha ngā panonitanga ka taea i tō tūranga. Ko te Dementia Australia e tohutohu ana i ngā kōwhiringa pēnei i te whakamahi i tētahi tikanga, te aro ki tētahi mahi kotahi i te wā, te tuhi tuhipoka, me te hanga aratohu ā-tahi mō ngā mahi noa. Ka taea hoki, i runga i tō mahi, te pātai mō ngā tohutohu tuhituhi, he maramataka, he iti ake ngā raru, he wā okioki kua whakaritea.

Whakaarohia ēnei hei kaupapa hei kōrerohia, ehara i te rārangi e tika ana kia whai ai ngā kaituku mahi katoa. Kōwhiria tētahi panonitanga e whakatika ana i tētahi mahi e uaua ana ki a koe. Mēnā ka whakamātau koe me tō kaituku mahi, whakaaetia me pēhea e arotake ai, mēnā he āwhina. He pai pea tētahi panonitanga mō tētahi mahi, kāore pea e pai mō tētahi atu.

## Me kī atu ki tōku kaituku mahi rānei?

Ko koe te kaiwhakatau he mōhiotanga whaiaro ka tohaina, engari mēnā e pā ana te ture ā-rohe, te kirimana, te tikanga haumaru ki tō tūranga. He rerekē ngā whakaritenga i ia whenua, ā-rohe, mahi. I mua i te whakatau, tirohia tō kirimana, te kaupapa here o te wāhi mahi, ngā aratohu ā-rohe. Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio, kōrerohia ki tētahi kaitohutohu mahi pono, ki te kaiwhakahaere uniana, ki te roia rānei e mōhio ana ki ngā ture o te wāhi e noho ana koe.

Mēnā ka whiriwhiri koe ki te kī atu ki tētahi, whakaarohia he aha tāu e hiahia ai ia kia mōhio, he tautoko he aha tāu e tono ana. Ka pai pea ki a koe te kōrero ki tētahi kaiwhakahaere pono kotahi, ki tētahi tangata HR i mua. Pātai he aha te tiaki o ō mōhiotanga, ko wai ka whiwhi; kaua e whakaaro he rite te tukanga tūmataiti o ngā wāhi mahi katoa.

Ko ētahi mahi he tūraru haumaru. Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio mēnā me tārewa, me panoni rānei tētahi o ērā mahi, whakapā tere atu ki tō tākuta, ki tētahi kaitohutohu mahi pono ā-rohe. Kaua e whirinaki ki tētahi aratohu whānui hei whakatau mēnā he haumaru tētahi mahi motuhake.

## He aha ngā painga me ngā pūtea me tirotiro?

I mua i te panoni i ō hāora, i te wehe rānei, arotakehia ngā painga ka taea mai i tō kaituku mahi. Pātai ki te HR, ki te kaiwhakahaere painga mō te wehe, ngā painga hunga hauā, ngā painga moni, ngā āwhina hauora, me ngā wā katoa. Tonoa ngā ture o nāianei ā-tuhinga, ā, pupuri he kape o ngā mōhiotanga e whiwhi ana koe.

Ko te whiwhinga, ngā tāke, me te wā e pā ana ki tō kaituku mahi, ki tō kaupapa inihua, ki te wāhi. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e tohutohu ana kia arotakehia ngā painga kaituku mahi, kia pupuri i te whakamanatanga tuhinga](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset). Mō tō whakatau ake, kōrerohia ki tētahi kaitohutohu painga, pūtea pono e taea ana te arotake i ngā tuhinga e pā ana ki a koe. Kia kaua e hainatia, e whakamutua rānei te whakaruruhau i runga anake i tētahi tuhinga whānui.

He pā pea te panonitanga o te moni, o te hōtaka ki tō whānau. Mēnā e hiahia ana koe, kōrerohia ki ngā tāngata e whakapono ana koe mō ngā mea nui ki a koe, ō kōwhiringa e hiahia ana koe ki te tūhura. Ko te [aratohu o ngā tīmatanga i muri i te tautuhinga](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) e kapi ana i te tautoko whānui me ngā pātai anamata.

## Me pēhea mēnā ka whakaiti hāora, ka wehe rānei au?

Ko te wehe mahi he whiringa tika mō ētahi tāngata. Ehara tērā i te hinganga. Me whakaaro pea koe ki te moni, ki te whakaruruhau hauora, ki te nekehanga o ia rā, me ngā tāngata ka ngaro i a koe. Tirohia ngā kōwhiringa ā-rohe tautoko i mua i te panonitanga, ā, hoatu he wā ki a koe ki te whakataurite i ngā kōwhiringa e whai take ana ki a koe.

Ko te mahi he wāhanga noa o te oranga. Mēnā ka mutu, ka whakaiti hāora rānei koe, kōwhiria ētahi atu mahi, ētahi atu hononga e whai hua ana ina rite koe. Ka taea e te whānau te tautoko, engari me uru koe ki te whakatau. Ko te [aratohu hauora-whaiaro kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) he whakaaro mō te tono atu ki ētahi atu kia tuari te tautoko, kāore e kawea katoa ana e tētahi tangata anake.
