He nui ngā take ka kōrerotia i roto i te toro rata poto. Ko tētahi mahere poto ka āwhina i te tangata me tōna kaitiaki ki te whakaara i ngā pātai e nui ana, ki te mārama ki ngā whakautu, ā, ki te wehe me ngā mahi e mārama ana. Me noho te tangata e mate wareware ana hei wāhanga o te kōrerorero me ngā whakatau i te nui e pai ana ki a ia.

Ko te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Association mō te mahi tahi me te rata](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/working-with-the-doctor) e tohutohu ana kia tuhia ngā pātai, kia tuhia ngā panoni motuhake, ā, kia tonoa te rata kia whakahoki i te mahere. Ko te [aratohu a Dementia Australia mō te mahi tahi me ngā rata](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/working-doctors) e whakanui ana i te whakaaetanga, i te whakarongo ki te tangata, me te tuku mōhiotanga mūnga mēnā e hiahiatia ana. Ko tēnei whārangi he tohutohu whānui whakareri, ehara i te tohutohu hauora.

## Me pēhea e whakareri i mua i te toro?

Pātai ki te tangata he aha tāna e hiahia ai kia mōhio te rata. Tuhia he rārangi poto pātai tahi, me te mea nui rawa ki te tuatahi. Mēnā he roa te rārangi, pātai ki te tari mēnā he toro roa ake, he toro whai muri rānei e āwhina ai. He pai pea ki te tangata te kawe i tētahi tuhinga i te kore e maumahara i ngā mea katoa i te toro.

Whakamāramatia ngā panoni ā-tinana. Tuhia he aha te mea i tupu, he aha te wā i tīmata ai, he auau pēhea te puta, he aha te mea e pā ana i taua wā. Hei tauira, “Kua mutu tana haere ki te rōpū waiata i ngā wiki katoa o te marama kua hipa” he pai ake i te “Kei te kino haere ia”. Whakauruhia te whakaaro o te tangata mō te panoni, ā, kaua e tuhi he whakaaro hei arotake hauora.

Kawea he rārangi rongoā o nāianei, tae atu ngā rongoā tohu, ngā rongoā kore tohu, ngā huaora me ngā whakapakari. Ka taea e te tangata te kawe i ngā karaihe kanohi, ngā taputapu rongo rānei, te ingoa o ētahi atu rata. Pātai ki te tari he pepa, he rekoata e hiahiatia ana, kaua e kohikohi i ngā mōhiotanga whaiaro atu i te nui e hiahiatia ana e te toro.

## Me pēhea e uru ai te tangata i te toro?

Pātai mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata kia uru koe ki te toro. Akene ka hiahia ia ki te tūnga whakahaere, ka pai ki a ia te kōrero tōna anake ki te rata, akene ka pai ki a ia te tuhi tuhinga e koe. Pātai he pēhea tōna hiahia kia āwhina koe i mua i te tīmatanga o te toro.

Waiho kia whakautu te tangata i ngā pātai a te rata i te tuatahi. Tāpirihia ngā taipitopito ina hiahia ia kia tāpiri koe, ina pātai rānei te rata mō ngā mōhiotanga kāore pea e maumahara ai ia. Kōrero ki te tangata, ehara ki te rata anake. Mēnā he wā tōna whakautu, tatari, kaua e whakakī i ngā wā noho katoa.

Akene ka hiahia te tangata ki te wā mōna anake ki te rata mō tētahi take whaiaro. Whakaūtia taua hiahia. Mēnā he manukanuka tōu e uaua ana te kōrero i mua i te tangata, pātai ki te haumanu mēnā he pēhea te tuku tuhinga matatapu. Kia mārama he aha tāu i kite ai, kaua e tono ki te haumanu kia huna i ngā mōhiotanga, kia whakatau rānei me te kore e uru te tangata.

## He pātai hei whakamārama i te mahere?

Pātai mō tētahi whakamāramatanga māmā mēnā he rerekē tētahi mea. Ka pātai pea te tangata me te kaitiaki:

- He aha ngā mea me mātakitaki, āhea me whakapā atu ki a koe?
- He aha ngā kōwhiringa e wātea ana, he aha ngā painga me ngā raru tūturu?
- He aha te mahi e whai ake nei, ko wai e whakarite?
- Āhea me whai toro anō?

Ehara ēnei i te rārangi me whakaoti i ia toro. Akene he pātai anō tā te tangata, ka pai rānei ki a ia te arotahi ki tētahi take. Tonoa te rata kia whakahoki i ngā kaupapa matua i mua i tō wehenga.

## He aha ngā mea me tuhi i muri i te toro?

Tuhia he whakarāpopototanga poto o te whakamārama a te rata, ngā mahi kua whakaaetia, me te wā whai muri. Tuhia ko wai ka mahi i ia mahi, pēnei i te karanga ki te haumanu, te whakarite toro anō. Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, tukuna ngā taipitopito ki te whānau, ki te kaiāwhina e hiahia ana ki a rātou.

Pupuri ngā tuhinga hauora i te wāhi matatapu, māmā ki te tangata te kite. Kaua e tuku mā tētahi pūkete tiritahi, mā tētahi rōpū karere rānei, mēnā kāore anō te tangata kia whakaae. Mēnā he tohutohu kāore i mārama ina hoki koe ki te kāinga, karanga ki te haumanu, pātai mō te whakamārama. Kaua e tīmata, e whakamutu, e whakarerekē i ngā rongoā mā ōu ake tuhinga anake; me tirotiro i te kaituku rongoā.

I mua i tō wehenga, tirohia mēnā me whakarite he toro whai muri, he whakamātautau rānei, ā, ko wai hei whakapā atu mō ngā pātai. Akene ka hiahia te tangata ki te pupuri i tētahi kape o te mahere tuhi, ki te whiri i tētahi kaiāwhina pono hei pupuri. Pātai, kaua e whakatau mōna.

## Me aha mēnā ka pōrearea ohorere te tangata?

Kaua e tatari mō te toro whakaaro i te wā ka pōrearea ohorere te tangata. Ko te [NHS e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora i taua wā tonu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), nā te mea he take pōrearea ohorere ka hiahia ki te āwhina ohotata. Whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata ā-rohe, whai rānei i te tukanga tiaki ohotata i tōu rohe.

He mea pai anō te kōrero mō te panoni haere ā-iti ā-iti ki te rata, engari he rerekē tērā ki te pōrearea ohorere. Mēnā kāore koe i mōhio mēnā he ohotata te panoni, whakapā ki tētahi ratonga hauora pono hei arahi, kaua e whakatau tōna take ā-ake.

## Me pēhea e tautoko ai i te tangata mēnā he raru ngā toro?

Pātai he aha ka māmā ake te toro. Akene ka pai ki te tangata te wā toro e mōhio ana ia, te tatari poto, te hoa pono hei haere tahi. Waea ki te haumanu ki te pātai mō ngā hiahia uru, te tautoko rongo, reo rānei, me mēnā ka taea te tuku pātai i mua i te toro.

Mēnā e āwangawanga ana te tangata, whakamāramatia te mahere ki tētahi āhua mārie, tōtika e pai ana ki a ia. Kawea he taonga mōhio, he mahi hei mahi i te wā e tatari ana, mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata. Pātai he aha tāna wā whakamōhio e pai ana ki a ia: he tangata e hiahia ana ki te wā whakarite, he tangata rānei e pai ana ki te whakamahara poto e tata ana ki te toro. Whakamahia he whakamārama pono e pai ana ki a ia.

He toro pai ka whakarongo ki te tangata, ka mārama ngā mahi e whai ake nei. Whakarite tahi, tiakina te matatapu, ā, tonoa te haumanu kia whakamārama i ngā mea e noho ana te tino māramatanga.
