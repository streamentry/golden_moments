He ohotata ka whakararu i ngā tikanga o ia rā, ā, ka uaua ake mō te tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware ki te mārama he aha e tupu ana. He mahere ka āwhina i te tangata me ōna kaiāwhina ki te mahi me te iti o te pēhi i te wā whakamutunga. Me whai te mahere i ngā hiahia o te tangata, te wāhi e noho ai ia, me ngā matepā ā-rohe o taua wāhi.

Ko te [aratohu whakareri ohotata a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster) e tohutohu ana kia whakarite i ngā mōhiotanga, ngā taonga, me te tautoko whakahoki. Ko te [aratohu a FEMA mō ngā pakeke me ngā tāngata hauātanga](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_hm-preparedness-for-older-adults_english.pdf) e whakanui ana i te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero, te waka, ngā rongoā me ngā taputapu āwhina. He mōhiotanga whānui tēnei mō te whakamahere; whai i ngā tohutohu ohotata ā-rohe.

## Me pēhea e hanga ai he mahere e tika ana mō te tangata?

Pātai ki te tangata he aha ka āwhina i a ia kia haumaru ake, ko wai hoki tāna e whakawhirinaki ai. Mēnā ka taea, whakauruhia ia ki te kōwhiri i te wāhi hui, ngā whakapā ohotata, me ngā taonga mōhio hei kawe. Whakamāmāhia ngā tohutohu poto, mārama. Ka panoni pea te mahere ina panoni ngā mahi, te hauora, te nekeneke o te tangata.

Rārangitia neke atu i tētahi tangata e taea ana te āwhina. Akene kei te mahi te kaitiaki matua, kei tawhiti, kei te pāngia anō e taua ohotata. Whakaaetia ko wai ka whakapā ki a wai, ko wai hei waka, ko wai hei tirotiro i te tangata mēnā ka panoni ngā mahere. Hoatu ki ia kaiāwhina tētahi kape o ngā mōhiotanga e hiahia ana ia.

Whakaarohia he aha ka whakararu i te tautoko o te tangata. Mēnā e noho ana ia i te whare pūtahitanga, e haere ana ki tētahi hōtaka rā pakeke, e whai ana i te manaaki ā-kāinga, pātai he pēhea te whakahaere a taua ratonga i ngā whakawhitinga wāhi, ngā pānga o te hiko, ngā katinga. Kimihia ko wai ka whakapā ki te whānau, me pēhea e tutuki ai ngā hiahia tautoko o te tangata.

## He aha ngā mōhiotanga me ngā taonga ka tiakina mā?

Hangaia he kete iti, ngāwari te kawe. Whakauruhia ngā whakapā ohotata o nāianei, he rārangi rongoā, ngā mōhiotanga hauora matua, he chārā waea, ngā karaihe kanohi, ngā taputapu rongo mēnā e hiahiatia ana, ngā kākahu e pai ana mō te huarere, me tētahi taonga mārie. Tāpirihia ngā taputapu nekeneke, whakawhitiwhiti kōrero e whakawhirinaki ai te tangata. Tirohia ngā taonga kia hou tonu, ā, i ētahi wā.

Pupuri ngā pepa matua i te wāhi ka taea te tiki tere, engari tiakina ngā mōhiotanga whaiaro. Pātai ki te tangata ko ngā taonga e pai ana kia tohaina, ko wai hoki. Ka hiahia pea te kaiāwhina i te ingoa o te tangata, te huarahi pai ki te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero, ngā whakapā matua, ngā mōhiotanga rongoā, engari kāore ia e hiahia ki ngā rekoata whaiaro katoa.

Ko ngā taonga pai ko ērā e whakamahia ana e tēnei tangata. He kākahu ngākau pai, he whakaahua, he pukapuka, he taonga iti mōhio ka tino whakamārie atu i ngā taonga hou kāore ia e mōhio ai. Whakaritea te kete kia māmā kia taea e tētahi te mau, te hāpai i runga i te haumaru.

## He aha ka tuhia ki te tuhinga mahere?

He poto te mahere ka māmā ki te tuku ki tētahi kaiāwhina tuarua, ehara i te rārangi tuhinga roa. Me te whakaaetanga o te tangata, whakauruhia:

- **Ko wai ka whakapā.** Rārangitia ngā kaiāwhina matua me ngā tuarua, ngā kaiwhakarato tiaki o te tangata me te huarahi whakapā ki ia tangata. Kīa ko wai ka whakamōhio ki ētahi atu mēnā ka neke te whare.
- **Me pēhea te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero.** Tuhia te ingoa e pai ana ki te tangata, me pēhea tōna māramatanga ki ngā mōhiotanga, ngā kupu me ngā tikanga e āwhina ai ki te whakamārama i a ia.
- **Me pēhea te haere.** Tuhia te huarahi waka me ngā taputapu nekeneke, whakawhitiwhiti kōrero e whakawhirinaki ai te tangata. Pātai ki te kaiwhakarato, ki te tari ohotata ā-rohe mō ngā huarahi e wātea ana, i mua i tētahi ohotata.
- **Ka kitea ngā mea matua.** Tuhia ka pūmau te kete me te rārangi rongoā. Pupuri ngā mōhiotanga matatapu i tētahi wāhi haumaru, ā, tukuna anake ngā taipitopito e hiahia ana ia kaiāwhina.
- **Ka aha te ratonga.** Mēnā he tautoko e tukuna ana ki waho o te kāinga, rārangitia te ingoa o te kaiwhakapā, ā, ko ngā wāhi e kitea ai te mahere ohotata o te ratonga.

Whakarite kia mōhio tētahi kaiāwhina tuarua ki te wāhi o te mahere me te āhua o te whakamahi. Kia wātea tētahi kape tuhinga mēnā ka ngaro te hiko, te waea. Engari kaua e whakairi mōhiotanga hauora matatapu ki te wāhi e kite ai ngā manuhiri.

## He aha ka mōhio au mō te noho haumaru, te whakawhiti wāhi?

Kimihia ngā wāhi e tukuna ai ngā tohutohu ohotata ā-rohe, me pēhea e tae atu ai ngā whakamōhiotanga ki te whare. Whakatauria ka haere ki hea mēnā ka tohutohutia te whakawhiti wāhi e ngā mana, me pēhea te tangata e haere ai. Mēnā te tangata e whakamahi ana i te tūru wīra, i te hīkoi, i te hāora, ētahi atu taputapu rānei, pātai ki te kaiwhakarato tōna tikanga, me ētahi atu taputapu tautoko e hiahiatia ana.

Kāore he ture whānui mēnā me noho, me wehe rānei. Ko te ohotata, ngā hiahia o te tangata, me ngā tohutohu a ngā mana ā-rohe he mea nui katoa. Pātai ki te tari whakahaere ohotata ā-rohe, ki te kaiwhakarato tiaki mēnā he pai te haere waka me te nohoanga whakaruruhau mō te tangata. Kaua e whakawhirinaki ki tēnei aratohu ki te whakatau i tētahi ohotata motuhake.

Mēnā he whare tiaki, he hōtaka rā pakeke kei te uru, tonoa tana mahere ohotata tuhituhi. Kimihia ko wai te kawenga mō te tangata, me pēhea e whiwhi ai te whānau i ngā pānui, me he aha ka mahia mēnā ka panoni ngā mahere haere, tiki rānei. Ko tā mātou [aratohu haumarutanga kāinga](/caregiving/home-safety/) e uhi ana i ngā matepā o ia rā i roto, i waho o te kāinga.

## He aha ka taea e au ki te tautoko i te tangata i tētahi ohotata?

Noho tata ki te tangata mēnā he haumaru. Whakamāramatia he aha e tupu ana i ngā kupu poto, mārie, whakahokia anō te mōhiotanga mēnā e hiahiatia ana. Haere tahi ki te tangata ina taea, ngā tikanga o ia rā, ā, whakamārama ki ngā kaiurungi ohotata me pēhea te tangata e hiahia ai kia kōrerotia tōna ingoa, me he aha te mea e āwhina ai ia ki te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero.

Mēnā ka pōuri te tangata, whakaitihia ngā haruru, ngā tāngata e kōrero ana i te wā kotahi. Tukuna he taonga mōhio mēnā he haumaru, he wātea. Kaua e tautohetohe, kaua e whakamārama roa i te wā e pēhia ana te katoa. Whai i ngā tohutohu a ngā kaiurungi ohotata me ngā kaimahi tiaki.

Kīa ki ngā kaimahi hauora mō ngā panoni ohorere i te whanonga, i te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero, i te hauora o te tangata. Ko te [NHS e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora ohotata mō te pōrearea ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Kaua e whakaaro he panoni hou, he panoni nui rānei he wāhanga noa o te mate wareware.

## Me pēhea e pai ai te mahere?

Arotahia te rārangi whakapā, ngā mōhiotanga rongoā, me ngā taputapu i te wā ka panoni ngā whakaritenga tiaki. Tirohia ngā rā o ngā taonga, whakahoutia te mahere i muri i te neke, i tētahi hiahia nekeneke hou. Pātai ki te tangata mēnā e māmā tonu ana te mahere ki a ia, ko wai hoki e hiahia ana ia kia uru.

Ka hiahia anō ngā kaitiaki ki te tautoko. Ko te [aratohu manaaki i a koe anō](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) he whakaaro mō te tono i ētahi atu kia tohatoha i te tautoko. He māmā ake te mahere māmā me ētahi kaiāwhina pono i te rārangi roa kāore anō kia arotakehia e tētahi.

Ko te whāinga kia rite ai ki te mahi, me te pupuri i te tangata e whai wāhi ana, e māmā ana. Whai i ngā tohutohu ohotata ā-rohe, ā, pātai ki ngā tāngata ngaio mō ngā hiahia motuhake o te tangata.
