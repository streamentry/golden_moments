Ko te noho mokemoke, ehara i te mea e whakaatu ana he aha te nui o te āwhina e hiahia ana te tangata. E kī te Alzheimer's Association he maha ngā tāngata e mau tonu ana i te noho mokemoke, otirā i te wāhanga tuatahi, me ngā whakarerekētanga māmā, ngā tupato haumaru, me te tautoko a ētahi atu. Ko te pātai pūmau ake: he aha tā tēnei tangata e hiahia ana kia haere tonu, ā, he aha ka māmā ai aua mahi?

Tīmatahia taua kōrerorero ina whai wā, ina kore he raru tōmuri hei whakatika. Pātai he aha ngā mea e pai ana, he aha ngā ritenga e whai take ana, ā, he aha te momo āwhina e pai ana ki te tangata ki te whiwhi. He mahere i hangaia me ia, he pai ake te tūtaki ki tana oranga i tētahi rārangi mahi i hangaia mō ētahi atu.

## He aha ngā mea ka māmā ai te oranga o ia rā?

Whakamahia ētahi taonga tautoko mōhio e rite ana ki ngā ritenga o te tangata. E kī te [Alzheimer's Association e whakamahi ana i ngā taputapu āwhina pono](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) pēnei i ngā maramataka, ngā rārangi, ngā whakamaumahara mō ngā hiahia o ia rā.

- **Kōwhiria tētahi wāhi mō ngā taonga matua.** Pupuria ngā kī, ngā karu kōwae, te pēke moni, te waea i tētahi wāhi kua whakaaetia. He kōpaki iti tata ki te tatau, he papa tohu, ka āwhina pea i te ritenga kia māmā ake te whai.
- **Whakamahia tētahi maramataka, tētahi whakamaumahara kotahi.** Kōwhiria tētahi maramataka pakitara, tētahi papa tuhi, tētahi pukapuka ringa, tētahi maramataka waea rānei e pai ana ki te tangata kē te whakamahi. Pupuria ngā toronga me ngā mahi ā-wā i tētahi wāhi kotahi, kaua e whānui ki ngā taupānga me ngā tuhinga maha.
- **Whakaatuhia ngā ritenga.** Mēnā he wareware auau tētahi mahi, tuhia he tuhinga poto, ā, whakanohoa te taonga e hiahiatia ana ki tētahi wāhi mōhio. Whakaitia ngā mahi, ā, arotakehia tahi mēnā kāore e āwhina tonu.
- **Whakamāmāhia ngā mahi auau.** He tuku hokohoko, he toro kāinga, he whakarite kai ka whakaiti i te maha o ngā whiringa e hiahia ai te tangata ki te whakahaere mokemoke.

Ko tā tātou [aratohu mō ngā ritenga o ia rā](/activities/daily-routine/) he ara māmā hei pupuri i tētahi rā mōhio, kāore e tino pakeke. Ko te whāinga he tautoko i ngā whiringa a te tangata, ehara i te whakakī i ia haora.

## Me pēhea e hoatu ai te whānau me ngā hoa i te āwhina whai hua?

Whakaritea he rārangi iti o ngā whakapā pūmau. He toro, he waea i tētahi wā mōhio he pai ake i ngā tangata maha e haere ana i ngā wā kāore e whakatakoto ana. Pātai ko wai te hiahia ia kia whakaurua, ā, he aha tā ia tangata e taea ai.

Hanga ngā tono he mea pūmau. “Ka pai pea mēnā ka mau he kai ki a koe i te Rāpare?” he māmā ake te whakautu i tēnei i “Kī mai mēnā e hiahia ana koe ki tētahi mea.” He āwhina tētahi tangata pono ki ngā mahi hokohoko, te waka, ngā kai, ngā pire, ngā maramataka tūtakitanga. E kī te [Alzheimer's Association me whakarite āwhina](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) mō ngā mahi kāinga, ngā kai, te waka, me ngā whakamaumahara rongoā ina hiahiatia aua hiahia.

Mēnā he maha ngā tāngata e āwhina ana, whakaaetia ko wai ka mahi i ia mahi, me pēhea e tuku ai ngā kōrero matua. Pupuria ngā kōrero ki ngā tāngata kua kōwhiria e te tangata, engari mēnā he raru haumaru ohotata. Mēnā he tawhiti koe, tirohia tā mātou [aratohu tiaki tawhiti](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) mō ngā ara whakarite me te tangata e kōwhiria ana e ia. Ko te [aratohu whakawhiti kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) he kōrero anō mō te kōrero mō te tautoko kāore e hurihia hei whakamātautau.

## Me pēhea e pupuri tūhono ai te tangata e noho mokemoke ana?

Kōwhiria ngā mahi me te whakapā e pai ana ki te tangata. Ka taea he waea wiki, he toro a tētahi hoa tata, he hui hapori, he rōpū mōhio, he hīkoi me tētahi e whakawhirinaki ana ki a ia. Pātai ko ēhea ngā mea e pai ana ki a ia; kaua e whakaaro he pai rawa atu te maha o ngā manuhiri, ngā rōpū.

Ko te hāpai i te wā pāpori tūturu ka māmā ake te wiki te mōhio, ā, ka hoatu tētahi mea e tatari ai ia. Mēnā he uaua te haere ki tētahi mahi, whakaarohia he waea, he waka mai i tētahi e whakawhirinaki ana ki a ia, he hui iti tata ki te kāinga. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mō te tūhono pāpori](/brain-health/social-connection/) he whakaaro iti, māmā mō te pupuri whakapā.

## Me pēhea e mōhio ai mēnā me whakarerekē te mahere?

Kāore he rārangi kotahi e whakatau mēnā ka taea te tangata te noho mokemoke. Engari, me aro ki ngā rerekētanga motuhake i te oranga o ia rā: he mahi i pai tōna mahi i mua nei kua uaua ki a ia, kua ngaro ngā kai, kua ngaro ngā tūtakitanga, kua kī ia he uaua ētahi mahi.

Pātai ki te tangata he aha tāna i kite ai. Tukuna kotahi whakarerekētanga ngāwari, ā, whakamātau mēnā he pai. Mēnā he tūraru auau mō ngā kai, ngā rongoā, ngā moni, te nuku, ngā take kāinga, pātai ki te tangata mēnā ka kōrero ia ki tana tākuta, ki tētahi kaimahi tiaki hapori rānei. He aromatawai ōkawa ka āwhina ki te whakatakoto i ngā whiringa tautoko; kāore e hiahia te tangata ki te whakaae ki ngā tohutohu katoa.

Mēnā e māharahara ana te tangata ki te āwhina, tīmatahia he iti. He pai pea tana kaupapa hoko kai ā-wiki i te kaiāwhina kāinga, he āwhina ki ngā tuhinga engari kāore ki te tiaki tinana. Whakauruhia ia ki te whiriwhi i te tangata ka āwhina, me te wā o ngā tūtakitanga. Ko te [aratohu kāinga](/caregiving/home-safety/) e whakaatu ana i ngā whakarerekētanga ā-rūma, ina ko te take e pā ana ki te kāinga tonu.

## He aha mēnā kāore te tangata e hiahia ana ki te āwhina?

Whakarongo ki te take i mua i te hoatu i tētahi mahere anō. Akene e māharahara ia ki te mana motuhake, ki te moni, ki te pupuri i te mana, ki te tangata ohorere kei tōna kāinga. Pātai he aha te āwhina e pai ana ki a ia, kātahi whakamātauhia he whakamātau iti. He pai pea ki ētahi he whakamaumahara, he mahi tahi; he pai ki ētahi tētahi tangata pono kotahi, kāore he ratonga hou.

Kaua e huri ngā tūhono hei whakamātautau. Ka taea he kōrerorero te waea, kāore he rārangi pātai mō te kai, te rongoā, te maumahara o te tūtakitanga. Mēnā he tūraru tonu i te kāinga, whakapā ki ngā ratonga ohotata o tō rohe. Mō ngā take haere tonu, pupuria te tangata e uru ana, ā, rapua he tohutohu mai i tōna rōpū tiaki, i te ratonga tautoko ā-rohe rānei.

Ka taea te noho mokemoke me te mahi tonu i te āhua o ngā ritenga, ngā whanaungatanga, ngā āwhina ā-ringa e rerekē ana. Arotakehia te mahere tahi me te tangata, pupuria ngā mea e whai hua ana, ā, whakarerekētia ngā wāhanga kāore e tika tonu ana.
