Ka pāmamae pea te whakapae kua tahaetia e koe tētahi peeke, kua nukuhia e koe tētahi pēke, kua whakarerekētia e koe tētahi mōhiotanga pēke rānei. Ka wareware pea te tangata e mate wareware ana ki te wāhi i whakanohoa ai tētahi taonga, ka wareware rānei ia kua nukuhia, ka he rānei tana mārama ki te tupu. Engari kāore e whakamātauria e te mate wareware he teka te whakapae. Whakarongo tuatahi, tirohia ngā mea pono.

Ko te [aratohu a Alzheimer's Association mō te whakapae me te pōhēhē](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/suspicions-delusions) e whakahau ana kia noho mārie, kia whakahoki poto, kia kaua e tautohetohe. Ko te [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) hoki e tohutohu ana kia tirohia te māharahara, kaua e whakakorea nā te mea he mate wareware tō te tangata. Ko tēnei wharangi he whakaaro whānui mō te whakawhitiwhiti kōrero, ehara i te whakatau mēnā he pono tētahi whakapae takitahi. Mō ngā tohu kia kite, kia rongo i ngā mea kāore e kitea e ētahi atu, tirohia tā mātou [aratohu mō te kite me te rongo me te mate wareware](/caregiving/hallucinations-with-dementia/).

## He aha taku kōrero ina whakapae mai te tangata?

Whakautu ki te māharahara kāore e whakaae ana ki tētahi mea kāore koe e mōhio he pono. Ka āwhina pea tētahi whakautu poto, mārie kia tuwhera tonu te kōrero. Hei tauira, “Kei te māharahara koe kua ngaro tōu tāwhiti. Tirohia tahi tāua.” Ka taka tētahi wā, titiro ki te urupare o te tangata.

- **Whakarongo i mua i te whakamārama.** Pātai he aha te taonga kua ngaro, he aha rānei i māharahara ai ia. Ka pā pea tana pōuritanga ki tētahi mea atu i te taonga anake.
- **Whakapoto tō kōrero.** Ko te whakahoki roa ka tāpiri pea i ngā kōrero uaua te whai, ā, ka pōrearea tonu koutou.
- **Kaua e tautohetohe, kaua e pātai.** Kaua e tono kia maumahara te tangata ki te wāhi i whakanohoa ai e ia tētahi taonga, kaua e tono kia whakaatu koe kāore koe i tahae.
- **Hoatu he āwhina ngāwari.** Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata, tirohia tahi i ngā wāhi mōhio, pātai rānei mēnā e pai ana ki a ia tētahi atu tangata hei āwhina.
- **Whakamutua mēnā ka piki te pōuri.** Hoatu he wāhi ki te tangata, hoki ki te māharahara ā muri ake nei mēnā he haumaru.

He pā pea te whakapae ki a koe. Whakaarohia te wehi o te tangata, me te mea e mōhiotia ana mō te tupu. Kāore koe e tika ana kia whakaae ki te hara kāore koe i mahi. Kāore koe e tika ana kia whakaotia ngā whakahē katoa i taua wā.

## He pono rānei te māharahara mō tētahi taonga kua ngaro?

Ae. Tuatahi, tirohia mēnā kua ngaro tōtika te taonga. Pea kua whakanohoa e te tangata ki tētahi wāhi rerekē, kua nukuhia pea e tētahi, kua tahaetia pea te taonga. Kaua e whakahāwea i te pūrongo mō te tahae, te whakaraerae, te kino nā te mea he mate wareware tō te tangata.

Mō tētahi taonga kua ngaro noa, tirohia tahi, mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata. Tirohia ngā wāhi e rongo ai ia e rite ana ki te tikanga o ia rā, whakahokia ngā mahi o ngā rā kua hipa. Mēnā he nui te utu o te taonga, he mea matua mō te haumaru, kua ngaro tonu rānei, kōrerohia te tangata ki ngā mea e mōhiotia ana e koe, ka whakauru i tētahi kaimahi ngaio pono, i tētahi whānau atu rānei.

Mēnā he kōtahi ngā moni, ngā mahi kāinga, whakamahia ngā pūrongo mārama e whakaaetia ana e te tangata. Pupuri i ngā rīhi, i ngā tuhipoka mō ngā hokonga kua whakaaetia, whakamārama ki ngā kupu māmā. Pātai ki tētahi kaimahi ā-rohe whai mōhiotanga mō ngā whakaritenga pūtea, ngā herenga ture; kāore e taea e te aratohu nei te aromatawai i aua take.

Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio mēnā he tika te māharahara, tonoa tētahi tangata kore kōtahi hei āwhina ki te tirotiro. Kōwhiria tētahi tangata e whakapono ana te tangata, ā, whakaatuhia ngā mōhiotanga e tika ana kia mārama he aha i tupu.

## He pēhea ngā tikanga mōhio e āwhina ai ki ngā taonga ngaro?

Hoatu ki ngā taonga e ngaro pinga ana tētahi wāhi mōhio. He pōwhiri mō ngā kī, he pae mō te pēke, he kāpata tapanga rānei hei āwhina i te kimi i tētahi taonga ā muri mai. Ko te [Alzheimer's Society e tohutohu ana kia whakamahia tētahi wāhi tūturu mō ngā taonga pēnei i ngā kī, ngā karaihe](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions). Pātai ki te tangata he wāhi e mārama ana ki a ia.

Kotahi te panonitanga i te wā. Ko te nukunuku i ngā taonga me te kore e whakamārama ka hōhā ai te tangata mō te wāhi o ngā taonga. Mēnā he iti te utu o tētahi taonga ka ngaro pinga, ka āwhina pea te taonga rite i ētahi kāinga, engari kāore e whakakapi i te tirotiro mēnā kua tahaetia, kua ngaro rānei te taonga taketake.

Whakarite kia kite, kia rongo mārie te tangata i tōna ao. Ko te Dementia Australia e kī ana ka pāngia pea te tirohanga, te rongo, te mate, te pānga o ngā rongoā, te panonitanga o te hōtaka, te taiao e pōrearea ana ki ngā panonitanga o te mahara o te tirohanga. Pātai ki te tangata mēnā he haruru, he rerekē te rūma, me tirotiro mēnā kei a ia ana karaihe, ana taputapu rongo mēnā he whakamahinga tāna.

## Ina hea ka tono āwhina hauora, āwhina o waho rānei?

Kōrerohia ki te kaimahi hauora o te tangata mēnā he hou te whakapae, ka hoki mai, ka raru rānei. Tuhituhia te wā e tupu ai, he aha kē i tupu i taua wā, mēnā he panonitanga i te hauora, i te hōtaka, i te taiao, i ngā rongoā. Kaua e tīmata, e whakamutu, e panoni rānei i ngā rongoā i runga i tētahi tuhinga whānui.

Ko te pōhēhē ohorere, te panonitanga ohorere o te whanonga, o te hauora me tono āwhina ohotata hauora. Ko te [aratohu NHS mō te pōhēhē ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora i taua wā tonu. Whakamahia te pūnaha ohotata, ngā ratonga ohotata-iti o te rohe e noho ana te tangata.

Mēnā ka pā te whakapae ki te tahae, ki te whakaraerae, ki te kino rānei, whakarite kia haumaru te tangata, ā, rapu tohutohu mai i tētahi kaitohutohu pono ā-rohe, ratonga haumaru pāpori pakeke rānei. Mēnā kei te raru tata tētahi, whakapā atu ki ngā ratonga ohotata. Kaua e tirotiro i te raru taimaha anake mēnā ka whakawhiti i a koe, i te tangata rānei ki te morearea.

## Me pēhea e tiaki ai i a koe anō?

Ko ngā whakapae e hoki mai ana ka pāmamae pea te hononga. Kōrerohia ki tētahi tangata e whakapono ana koe, ki tētahi kaitohutohu, ā, tono āwhina mō ngā mahi tiaki mēnā e taimaha ana koe. Ko te [aratohu mō ngā tautohetohe whānau](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) e kōrero ana i ngā ara o te tuari i ngā haepapa, ā, ko te [aratohu hauora-whaiaro kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e kapi ana i te okioki me te tautoko.

Kia noho ki ngā mea ka taea te tirotiro, ki te āhua o te tangata, ki te tautoko e hiahiatia ana. Kāore koe e tika ana kia whakaatu i tō tiakitanga i ia kōrero. Mēnā ka hoki mai tonu tētahi māharahara, tonoa ki te tira tiaki he āwhina ki te kimi i tētahi ara e whakaute ana i te tangata, e tiaki ana i te katoa.
