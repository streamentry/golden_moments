Ina panoni te mate wareware tētahi tangata e aroha ana te tamaiti, ka kite pea te tamaiti i mua i te tīmatanga o te kōrerorero a te pakeke. Ka mataku pea te tamaiti, ka riri, ka whakamā, ka whakahē pea i a ia anō, ka kore mōhio he aha te mahi. He rerekē ia tamaiti, ia whānau. Ka āwhina te mōhiotanga mārama me te tautoko pūmau ki te tamaiti kia mārama ai ia ki ngā panoni, kaua e tono i a ia he haepapa pakeke.

Ko te [aratohu kōrero ki ngā tamariki a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) e tohutohu ana kia whakautu i ngā pātai mā te pono, ki ngā kupu e mārama ai ngā tamariki. Ko te [aratohu whānau a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) e whakanui ana i ngā kōrerorero pono, i te whakamārama, me te tiaki i te wāhi o te tamaiti hei tamaiti. Whakamahia ēnei whakaaro hei tīmatanga, kaua hei kōrero hāngai mō ia whānau.

## Me pēhea e tīmata ai te kōrerorero mō te mate wareware?

Tīmatahia mā tētahi panoni kua kitea e te tamaiti, mā tētahi pātai kua pātai mai ia. Hei tauira, “Kua kite koe he maha ngā pātai a Kuia? He mate tōna e kīa nei ko te mate wareware, e pā ana ki tōna maharatanga me tōna whakaaro.” Whakamahia te whakatau tūturu o te tangata anake mēnā kua mōhiotia. Mēnā kei te tatari te whānau mō te aromātai, whakamāramatia te panoni kaua e whakaingoa.

Kōwhiria he wā mārie, ka noho tahi koe ki te kōrerorero. Whakamahia ngā rerenga poto, whakatārewa kia whakautu te tamaiti. Kāore koe e tika kia whakamārama i ngā taipitopito katoa i te wā kotahi. Pātai he aha tāna i mōhio kē, he aha tāna e hiahia ana ki te mōhio ā muri ake. Ko te tamaiti iti pea ka pai ki tētahi pukapuka whakaahua, ki tētahi tauira māmā; ko te tamaiti kaumātua pea e hiahia ana ki te kōrero nui ake, ki te wā motuhake hei whakaaro.

Me whai wāhi tonu te tangata e mate wareware ana ina hiahia ia, ina taea e ia te whai wāhi. Kōrerohia ki te tamaiti ngā mahi a te tangata i mua i te kōrero i ngā kōrero hauora tūmataiti ki ētahi atu. Akene ka hiahia te tamaiti kia noho tētahi mātua, tētahi pakeke pono tata ina toro atu ia; akene ka pai ki a ia kaua e kōrero mō te whakatau ki ngā hoa, ki te kura i tēnei wā.

## He aha ngā mea me kī ki te tamaiti mō te mate wareware?

Whakamāramatia ngā wāhanga e whai take ana ki te oranga o te tamaiti. He mate te mate wareware e panoni ai te maharatanga, te whakaaro, te whakawhiti kōrero a te tangata. Kāore e hāwera, kāore hoki te tamaiti i hanga i a ia. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) e kī ana ka māharahara pea ngā tamariki kei te hopu i te mate wareware, ka whakahē pea i a rātou anō ina pōuri ngā pakeke. Whakahokia atu ēnei manaaki mā te tika.

Me pono koe mō tāu e mōhio ana, mō tāu kāore e mōhio ana. Akene ka whakamārama koe i ā rātou i kite ai, he aha te āwhina e whiwhi ai te tangata, he aha ngā mahere ka pā ki te tikanga o te tamaiti. Kaua e kī ka pai ake te tangata, kaua e kī kāore he panoni, kaua hoki e kī e mōhio ana koe ki te wā o te panoni. Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio ki tētahi whakautu, kīa mai, ā, tonoa te kimi mōhiotanga pono tahi.

He tika kia kōrero anō ngā tamariki i te whakamārama anō. He āhua noa tērā. Waiho he wā mō te tamaiti ki te pātai anō ā muri ake, tirohia mēnā kua mārama ia, kaua e huri te kōrerorero hei whakamātautau. Mēnā he kupu rerekē tāna mō te mate, whakarongo tuatahi, ā, tukuna mārie ngā kupu mārama hei whakamahi mā ia.

## Me pēhea e whakautu ai i ngā pātai uaua?

Whakautua te manukanuka kei raro i te pātai, ā, hoatu te whakautu mārama, pono rawa atu ka taea e koe. Kia poto, kaua e whakakī i ngā taipitopito kāore koe e mōhio ana.

- **“Ka taea e au te hopu?”** Kīa atu kāo: kāore te mate wareware e hāwera. Ka taea e te tamaiti te noho tonu me te tangata.
- **“Nāku i hanga tēnei?”** Whakamāramatia ki a rātou kāore te mate i tupu nā tētahi kōrero, mahi rānei a rātou.
- **“He aha i kī ai a Koro i ahau ki tētahi ingoa kē?”** Whakamāramatia ka panoni te mate wareware i te whakamaharatanga a te tangata i ngā ingoa. Ehara i te he o te tamaiti, ā, kāore pea ia e mahi nei kia pōuri ai rātou.
- **“Ka mate au i te mate wareware hoki?”** Kaua e whakaaro mō te ahunga o tētahi tangata. Ka whirinaki te tūponotanga whānau ki te momo me te hītori. Mēnā he māharahara motuhake mō te momo tuku iho, pātai ki te takuta mēnā ka taea e tētahi mātanga ira te āwhina.
- **“Ka ora ia? Ka mate rānei ia?”** Kaua e oati i tētahi huarahi, i tētahi wā. Kīa atu ki te tamaiti he aha tā te tīma tiaki i kī mai, mēnā e hiahia ana te whānau ki te tohatoha, ā, whakamāramatia ina kōrero anō koe.
- **“He aroha tonu ia ki ahau?”** Kaua e hāpai i te tamaiti ki te whakamātau i te whakautu. Ka taea e koe te kī he mea nui te whanaungatanga, ā, rapua tētahi huarahi mārie ki te noho tahi i tēnei wā.

He pai te whakamutu mēnā he nui rawa te pātai. Kīa atu ki te tamaiti āhea koe ka hoki mai ki taua kaupapa, ā, whakaaturia. He pono, he “Kāore anō au kia mōhio” te mea pai ake i te whakaaro pūmanawa.

## Me pēhea e āwhina ai kaua e hanga te tamaiti hei kaitiaki?

Hoatu he hononga mā te kōwhiringa. Mēnā e pai ana te tamaiti me te tangata, ka taea e rāua te whakarongo ki ngā waiata mōhio, te mātakitaki i ngā whakaahua, te tuhi, te pānui, te tohatoha i tētahi mahi kāinga iti. Ko tā mātou [aratohu whakamaharatanga](/activities/reminiscence/) me te [aratohu waiata](/activities/music/) he whakaaro. Whai i ngā urupare a te tangata, a te tamaiti; he pai te whakamutu, te panoni i te mahi, te toro anō i tētahi wā kē.

Me pupuri e ngā pakeke te haepapa mō te tiaki me te haumaru. Ka taea e te tamaiti te whiriwhiri ki te whakapuaki i tētahi kai iti, ki te kawe i tētahi pukapuka, engari kaua e tika kia haepapa ia mō te mātakitaki, ngā rongoā, ngā manaaki whaiaro, ngā toro, ngā ohotata. Kaua e kī he haepapa o te tamaiti te toro, te āwhina, te noho tata. Ko te [aratohu Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) e tohutohu ana ki ngā whānau kia whakaute i ngā rohe o ngā rangatahi me tā rātou hiahia kia noho tonu hei tamariki.

Tiakina te wā mō te tamaiti anake. Pupuri i te kura, ngā hoa, ngā mahi ā-ringa, ngā tikanga whānau kia mārie tonu te noho. Mēnā ka panoni nei ngā mahere e tiaki ana, whakamāramatia he aha, rapua tētahi atu pakeke e āwhina ai kia pupuri i te mahi, i te haerenga a te tamaiti. Ko tā mātou [aratohu manaaki whaiaro](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e whakaatu ana i ngā huarahi ki te tono i ētahi atu pakeke kia tohatoha i te tautoko.

I mua i te kōrero ki tētahi kaiako, tētahi kaiako tākaro, tētahi atu pakeke, whakauruhia te tamaiti ki te whakatau mō te wā e tika ana. Akene ka hiahia ia kia mōhio tētahi pakeke pono kotahi, akene kāore ia i te hiahia kia kōrero i tēnei wā. Mēnā ka whakaatu koe i ngā mōhiotanga, whakaaetia he aha te kī, he aha ngā mea me noho tūmataiti.

## Āhea me rapu tautoko anō?

Pātai he pēhea te tamaiti i ngā wā roa, ehara i te wā tonu i muri i te whakatau. Ka kōrero pūmanawa ētahi tamariki; ka whakaatu ētahi i te pōuri mā ngā panoni i te moe, i te kura, i ngā hoa, i te whanonga. Ko ēnei panoni he maha ngā take, ehara i te taunakitanga me whai whakatau hauora te tamaiti. Whakarongo, pātai he aha te tautoko e āwhina ai.

Mēnā e hiahia ana te tamaiti ki tētahi tangata i waho o te whānau hei kōrerorero, whakaarohia tētahi kaiako pono, tētahi kaitohutohu kura, tētahi takuta tamariki, tētahi kaimahi hauora hinengaro tamariki. Pātai i mua i te whakapā ki ngā kaimahi kura mō te whakatau mēnā ka taea; akene ka hiahia te tamaiti kia whai wāhi ki taua kōrerorero. Ko ngā whānau e pāngia ana e te mate wareware i te wā iti o te pakeketanga ka hiahia pea tautoko mō te mahi, te kura, ngā panoni i ngā tūranga whānau.

Mēnā kāore koe e mōhio ki te whakamārama i tētahi panoni motuhake, pātai ki te tīma tiaki o te tangata, ki tētahi whakahaere mate wareware rānei mō te aratohu. Kāore e hiahiatia e te tamaiti he whakautu kia tino pai mai i a koe. Me mōhiotanga pono, he wā anō mō te pātai, me ngā pakeke kei te hoki mai mōna.
