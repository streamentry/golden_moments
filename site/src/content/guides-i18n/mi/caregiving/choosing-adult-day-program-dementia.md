Ka taea e tētahi hōtaka rā pakeke te whakarerekē i te āhuatanga, te tautoko i ngā mahi o ia rā. Ka taea hoki e te kaitiaki whānau te whai wā mō ngā mahi, ngā hoko, te okiokinga rānei. He rerekē ngā hōtaka, nō reira ehara i te mea ka pai tētahi pokapū ki tētahi tangata, ka pai pea ki tētahi atu. Ko te mōhio me ngā whiringa o te tangata me arahi te whakatau.

Ko te [tirohanga a te Alzheimer's Association mō ngā pokapū rā pakeke](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/adult-day-centers) me tōna [rārangi mō te kōwhiri pokapū](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-an-adult-day-center-ts.pdf) e whakahau ana kia tirohia ngā ratonga, ngā kaimahi, ngā mahi, te utu me te waka i mua i te urunga. Ko tēnei whārangi e āwhina ki te whakarite pātai; kāore ia e aromatawai, e tūtohu rānei i tētahi pokapū motuhake.

## Me pēhea e whakatau ai mēnā me tirotiro he hōtaka rā?

Tīmata mā te pātai ki te tangata he aha ka pai ki a ia, he momo āwhina e pai ana ki a ia. Akene he mahi pāpori tāna e hiahia ana, he kai me ētahi atu, he hoa e noho ana i a koe e mahi ana, he mea anō. He hōtaka tētahi kōwhiringa, ehara i te herenga.

Whakaarohia ngā haerenga e hiahia ana te tangata, āna tikanga o ia rā, ā, te āwhina e hiahia ana i te rā. Tirohia mēnā ka tika te wā, te waka, ngā mahi o te pokapū ki ēnei hiahia. Mēnā kāore e pai ana ki a ia ngā rōpū nui, te ngenge ki ngā wāhi pōrearea rānei, pātai mēnā he wāhi ngāwari, he toro poto rānei.

Ka āwhina pea te hōtaka ki te kaitiaki kia okioki, kia whakahaere i ētahi atu kawenga. He mea nui tērā, engari he mea nui anō te wheako o te tangata. Kōrerorero i ngā hiahia e rua, kimihia he whakaritenga e pai ana ki ngā tāngata katoa e uru ana.

## He aha ngā ratonga me pātai?

He rerekē ngā ratonga. Ko ētahi pokapū e arotahi ana ki te hoa-rangatira me ngā mahi; ko ētahi he kai, he waka, he manaaki whaiaro, he ratonga hauora hoki. Pātai he aha ngā ratonga e wātea ana i ngā rā e haere ai te tangata, ā, mēnā ka taea e ngā kaimahi te tautoko i ngā hiahia o te tangata i tēnei wā.

Kimihia ngā mahi, kaua ko te rārangi wā anake. Pātai he aha ngā mahi e mahia ai e ngā mema, mēnā ka taea te kōwhiri i waenga i ngā mahi, ā, mēnā kei roto ngā mea whaiaro pēnei i te waiata, te toi, te māra, te kōrerorero. Kāore e herea te tangata kia uru ki ngā mahi katoa. Pātai mēnā he wāhi ngāwari, he wā okiokinga rānei.

Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te āwhina i te rongoā, te nekeneke, te kai, te manaaki whaiaro rānei, pātai ko wai e tuku ana taua āwhina, he aha tāna whakangungu. Kaua e whakaaro he tiaki hauora tā te hōtaka rā, he taea e ia ngā hiahia katoa. Whakamaua ngā tohutohu hauora me te rōpū maimoatanga o te tangata.

## He aha ngā mea hei tirotiro i te toro?

Whakaritea he toro, ā, mēnā e pai ana ki te tangata, haere tahi ki a ia. Tirohia mēnā e kōrero whakaute ana ngā kaimahi, mēnā ka hoatu wā ki ngā mema ki te whakautu, mēnā ka karanga ki te uru me te kore e akiaki. Rapua ngā mahi e rite ana ki ngā mariu me ngā āheitanga o te tangata, ehara ki te mahere e nui ana anake.

Pātai he pēhea te pokapū e mōhio ai ki ngā mariu o ia tangata, he aha tana whakautu mēnā ka mauiui, ka manukanuka, ka hiahia rānei ki te wehe. Kimihia he aha tana mahi i te wā ohotata, he pēhea te whakamōhio ki ngā whānau, ko wai hei whakapā atu mēnā he manukanuka.

Mēnā ka taea, toro i te wā mahi, i te kai rānei. Tirohia mēnā he whakamārie te wāhi ki te tangata, he māmā te neke haere, he wātea ngā kaimahi. Ka āwhina pea te pukapuka kōrero ki te whakarite pātai, engari he nui ake pea te mōhio ka kite koe i tētahi rā noa.

## He aha ngā mōhiotanga whaiaro hei tirotiro?

Pātai mō ngā hāora, ngā rā, ngā utu, te tapeke tae mai iti, ngā ture whakakore me ngā utu anō. Pātai he aha ngā kai kua whakauruhia, mēnā ka taea te tautoko i te kai motuhake. He rerekē ngā utu me ngā ratonga i ia rohe, nō reira tonoa te pokapū kia whakamārama i āna tikanga tuhi.

Kimihia mēnā he waka, he utu anō, mēnā he pai ki te tangata. Pātai mō ngā wā tiki, te āwhina i te eke, te heke, me he aha ka mahia mēnā ka roa te kaiwaka. Mēnā ka hoatu waka e tētahi o te whānau, whakauruhia taua wā me te utu ki tō mahere.

Pātai mēnā ka taea e te pokapū te tautoko i ngā hiahia o te tangata mēnā ka panoni tana hauora, ōna mahi o ia rā. Mēnā kāore e taea e te pokapū ēnei hiahia, pātai he tohutohu tuku, he whakaaetanga kē rānei ka tūtohu ia. Ko te [aratohu manaaki ā-kāinga](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) he momo tautoko anō ka pai ki ētahi whānau.

## He pēhea te whakamātau i te hōtaka me te kore e akiaki?

Pātai mēnā he toro poto whakamōhio, he kai, he mahi rānei tā te pokapū. Waiho kia kōwhiri te tangata he aha tāna e whakamātau ai, ā, he aha te roa o tana noho i reira ina taea. He ngāwari kore pea te toro tuatahi. Pātai ki te tangata i muri he aha tāna i pai ai, he aha i kore ai, he aha tāna e whakarerekē ai.

Ki te whakamuri te tangata, kimihia he aha. Akene ko te waka, te haruru, ngā tāngata kē, tētahi mahi motuhake te take. Pātai ki te pokapū mēnā he panoni iti ka taea, nē rānei ka toro i tētahi rā kē. Kaua e whakamahi i te whakapōuri, i te akiaki hei whakauru i te tangata.

Whai muri i ētahi toro, kōrerorero ki te tangata me ngā kaimahi o te pokapū mō te āhuatanga o te whakaritenga. He pai te hōtaka ka hiahia pea ki ngā panoni o te wā, ngā mahi, te tautoko. Ki te noho pōuri tonu te tangata, ki te kore e tika te ratonga, he tika kia whakaarohia anō.

## Me pēhea e whakarite ai ki te pokapū?

Ina whakaaetia e te tangata, tukuna he mōhiotanga e āwhina ai ngā kaimahi ki te tautoko i a ia: tōna ingoa e pai ana, tōna āhua kōrero, ōna tikanga, ōna mahi pai, te momo āwhina e whakaaetia ana e ia. Pātai he pēhea te pupuri a te pokapū i ēnei mōhiotanga ā-muna, me pēhea te tuku kōrero ki te whānau.

Pātai ko wai e whakapā atu mēnā ka mate te tangata, ka kore e tae mai, ka hiahia rānei ki te panoni i te tautoko. Kia whai mahere tuarua mō ngā rā e kati ai te pokapū, ā, ina kore te waka e wātea. Mēnā he maha ngā tāngata e āwhina ana, whakaaetia ko wai e kawe ana i ia waka, i ia kōrero hou.

Ka taea e tētahi hōtaka rā pakeke te noho hei wāhanga o te mahere tautoko whānui. Ko te [aratohu manaaki i a koe anō](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) he whakaaro mō te whakarite okiokinga, ā, ko ngā [mahi mō ngā rōpū o te whare tiaki](/activities/care-home-groups/) he whakaaro mō ngā mahi rōpū māmā.

Ko te hōtaka tika ko te mea e pai ana te tangata ki te whakamātau, ā, ka taea e tautoko ai ōna hiahia. Toro, pātai tika, ā, whakarerekētia ngā mea i runga i te whakaaro o te tangata.
