Ko te kite, te rongo, te hongi, te whakarō, te tātō, te whakaaro rānei ki tētahi mea e kore e kitea e ētahi atu, ka tino pono pea ki te tangata. Ka whakamataku pea, ka kore rānei e pōuri. Tīmatahia ki te āhua o te tangata, me mēnā he tūpono tētahi, kaua ki te whakamātau kia whakaatu he aha i puta, he aha kāore i puta.

Ka taea he kitenga whakapohehe, ka taea hoki he whakapohehe ake, he whakaaro he tangata tētahi mea kāore. Ka taea te whakaaro he tangata te ātārangi; ka taea hoki te whakaaro he tangata tētahi tangata e mōhio ana koe. Kāore koe e hiahia ki te whakatau he aha te mea e tupu ana. Whakaahuatia he aha tā te tangata e kī ana, he aha tāu e kite ana, ā, pātai ki tētahi kaimahi hauora mēnā e māharahara ana koe.

## He aha tāku mahi i taua wā tonu?

Noho mārie, pātai ki te tangata he aha tōna āhua, ā, hoatu he whakamarie kāore e tautohetohe. E kī ana te [Alzheimer's Association me aromatawai mēnā he pōrearea, he whakararu te wheako](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/hallucinations). Mēnā he mārie, he haumaru te tangata, kāore pea e hiahia ki te whakararu.

- **Whakaaetia te kare.** Tērā pea tāu kī, “Kāore au e kite i tētahi tangata, engari ka mōhio au he whakawehi tēnei. Kei konei au.” He pono tēnei, ā, ka mōhio te tangata he tino whakaaro nui tāu ki a ia.
- **Whakapoto ō kupu.** He whakamārama roa, he whakatika tonu ka nui ai te pēhanga. Waiho kia whakaahua te tangata i ā rātou mea e kite ana, mēnā e hiahia ana rātou.
- **Hoatu he whiringa, kaua e akiaki i te nekeneke.** Pātai mēnā e hiahia ana rātou ki te noho i tētahi wāhi kē, ki te whakaā i tētahi rama anō, ki te whakarongo ki tētahi waiata mōhio rānei. Ko te hoki ki tētahi rūma mārama, mōhio he pai pea; whakamutua mēnā kāore te tangata e hiahia ki te nuku.
- **Noho tata mēnā e hiahia ana ia kia tata koe.** Kaua e pā ohotata ki te tangata. Pātai i mua i te hoatu ringa, i te awhi rānei.

Mēnā he kore kino te wheako, ehara i te mea whakararu, whaia te arataki a te tangata. Kaua e hurihia te wā hei whakamātautau, kaua e tono kia whakaatu rātou he tika tā rātou i kite ai.

Ka pai pea te wheako ki tētahi tangata, ki tētahi mōhio rānei. Mēnā e mārie ana te tangata, e haumaru ana, kāore pea e hiahia ki te whakarerekē i te kaupapa, ki te kawe rānei ki tētahi wāhi kē. Pātai he aha tāna e hiahia ana; kaua e huri ia wā ki te kōrerorero hauora. Mēnā he wehi ia, whakahokia tōna ngākau ki taua wehi, ahakoa kāore koe e kite i taua mea.

## Akene he mea i te rūma ka whakapohehe ki te tangata, ki te haruru?

Tirohia te taiao me te tangata mēnā e pai ana ki a ia taua āwhina. E kī ana te [Alzheimer's Society e wehewehe ana i ngā kitenga whakapohehe i ngā whakapohehe](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations), ina kitea he mea tūturu, he tauira tūturu, he hē te titiro.

- **Whakapaia te rama.** Whakaaraia he rama, tirohia ngā atarangi me ngā whakaata mai i ngā matapihi me ngā karaihe.
- **Tirohia ngā haruru noa.** He pouaka whakaata, he hau, he taputapu hiko pea he rite ki te reo, ki ngā hikoi. Whakaitea, whakakorea rānei mēnā e pai ana te tangata.
- **Titiro ki ngā tauira.** He whāriki, he kōtaha, he pepa pakitara pea ka whakapohehe hei nekeneke, hei mea. Pātai mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te titiro mai i tētahi wāhi kē, ki te whakarerekē i te tirohanga.
- **Tirohia ngā taputapu kitenga, rongo.** Mēnā he karu, he pūkoro taringa tō te tangata, pātai mēnā e hiahia ana ia ki te āwhina kia tirotirohia ka wātea, ka mahi pai.

Mēnā ka puta te wheako i taua wāhi tonu, rapua tētahi āhuatanga ka hoki mai: he atarangi i te karaihe, he tauira kaha i te papa, he pouaka whakaata i tētahi rūma kē e rongo ana, he rama iti i te hāora o te pō. Tirohia tētahi haruru, mea rānei kua tino puta mai i mua i te whakaaro he rerekē te kitenga o te tangata. Mēnā ka whakamātau koe i tētahi rerekētanga taiao, whakamāramatia, ā, pātai mēnā he pai ake ki a ia.

Whakamātau i tētahi rerekētanga iti i te wā kotahi. Kaua e nekehia ngā taonga o te tangata, kaua e tangohia tētahi mea e whai wāhi ana ki a ia me te kore pātai. He tikanga mōhio, he wāhi mārama, mārie ka māmā pea.

## Āhea me whakapā atu ki tētahi tākuta?

Whakapā ki te tākuta o te tangata mō ngā wheako hou, ngā wheako e hoki mai ana, ngā wheako roa, ngā wheako whakararu rānei. Ka taea e ngā rerekētanga o te kanohi, o te rongo, o ngā mate, o ngā rongoā, o ētahi atu take te pānga. Ka taea e te kaimahi hauora te aromatawai he aha pea kei te tupu, ā, mēnā e hiahia ana tētahi whai ake. E kī anō te [aratohu a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) me tirotiro ngā take tinana, ngā take rongo, ngā take rongoā, ngā take taiao.

He rerekē te rerekētanga tere. E kī te [NHS me whai āwhina hauora tere mō te whakapōuri tere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), ā, e kī te [Alzheimer's Society me whai āwhina hauora tere ina rongo ki te kitenga o ngā rongo maha](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations). Whakapā ki ngā ratonga hauora ohotata o tō rohe mēnā ka whakapōuri tere te tangata, mēnā he rongo maha te kitenga. Mēnā he mōrearea tonu te tangata, whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata o tō rohe.

Kaua e tīmata, e whakamutu, e whakarerekē i tētahi rongoā i tō anō ringa. Tonoa te kaiwhakaatu rongoā, te kaiwhakarite rongoā rānei kia arotake i ngā rerekētanga o nā tata nei, me ngā rongoā katoa e whakamahia ana e te tangata, tae atu ki ngā rongoā hokohoko. Ka āwhina te [aratohu mō te toronga ki te tākuta](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ki te whakarite i ngā pātai.

## He aha ngā kōrero ka āwhina i te toronga?

Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, tuhia he kōrero poto hei tohaina ki te rōpū tiaki:

- he aha tāna i kī ai i kite, i rongo, i whakaaro rānei, ki ōna ake kupu i te nuinga;
- āhea, ā, kei hea i puta ai, tae atu ki te roa o te wā;
- mēnā i wehi te tangata, i mārie rānei, i tūraru rānei;
- he aha te āhua o te rūma, tae atu ki te rama, ngā atarangi, ngā haruru, ko wai i tata;
- he mate tata, he rerekētanga kanohi, rongo, rongoā rānei e mōhio ana koe.

Whakamahia ngā kupu o te tangata, whakaaturia he aha tāu i kite ai. Hei tauira, tuhia “i kī ia he tangata i te taha o te matapihi” hei utu mō te whakaingoa i te wheako. Tohaina ngā tuhinga ki te whakaaetanga o te tangata ina taea.

Ehara ēnei tuhinga i te whakatau i te take. Mēnā ka tīmata ohotata te wheako me te whakapōuri, me te rerekē nui o te mōhio, rapua āwhina ohotata, kaua e tatari ki te kohi tuhinga.

Ko te [aratohu mō te ngākau kore mārie i te ahiahi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) e whakaatu ana i te rama o te ahiahi me te ritenga. Mō ngā whakapae mō ngā taonga ngaro, tirohia [te whakaaro whakapae ki te tangata mate wareware](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/).
