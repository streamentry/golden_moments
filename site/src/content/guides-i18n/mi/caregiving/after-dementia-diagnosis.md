Ka puta pea ngā pātai mō te hauora, te oranga o ia rā, te tautoko me te anamata i muri i te whakatau mate wareware. He rerekē ngā whakautu a te tangata: ko tētahi e hiahia ana he wā ki te whakaaro nui ki te kōrero, ko tētahi e hiahia ana te whakamahere tōmua. Kāore he whakautu kotahi tika, kāore hoki he wā kotahi. Tīmatahia mā ngā mea e nui ana ki te tangata i tēnei wā, ā, hoki ki ētahi atu pātai ina rite ia.

Ko te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Association mō te oranga i muri i te whakatau](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) e tohu ana ki te manaaki whai muri, ki te tautoko ā-rohe me te whakamahere ā muri. Ko te [aratohu a Dementia Australia mō te whakaaro nui ki te whakatau](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) e whakanui ana i te rerekētanga o ngā whakautu, ā, ka taea te rapu tautoko ina hiahia te tangata. Ko tēnei whārangi he tīmatanga anake, ehara i te mahere hauora, ture, pūtea rānei mō te tangata.

## He aha te mahi tuatahi e whai hua ai?

Kōwhiria tētahi pātai ka whakamāmā i tēnei wiki. Ko tēnei pea ko te kimi ko wai e whakahaere ana i te manaaki whai muri a te tangata, te pātai ki hea ka tika ai te mōhiotanga pono mō tana whakatau, te kimi rānei i tētahi tangata e whakawhirinaki ai ia. Akene ka hiahia te tangata kia arahina e ia, kia āwhina mai tētahi atu, kia tatari rānei i mua i te whakatau.

Mō te haerenga ki te rata e whai ake nei, tuhia ētahi pātai tahi ki te tangata mēnā e hiahia ana ia. Ka taea te pātai he aha te tikanga o te whakatau, he aha ngā mea kāore anō kia mārama, kia hoki mai ai ināianei, me te wā ki whakapā atu ai ina puta mai he manukanuka hou. Tonoa te kaimahi hauora kia whakamārama i ngā kupu, i ngā tohutohu e kāore e mārama ana. Ko tā mātou [aratohu haere ki te rata](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) he rārangi whakarite poto.

Kāore e tika kia ako katoa te tangata mō te mate wareware i te wā kotahi. Kōwhiria he mōhiotanga pono mō te whakatau i whiwhi ai ia, ā, tuhia ngā pātai ka puta ā muri ake nei. Whai tūpato ki ngā kupu whakaū, ki ngā kōrero mō te rongoā, ki ngā tohutohu e kī ana ki te katoa me whai i te mahere kotahi.

## Ka kitea pēhea te tautoko?

Pātai ki te tangata he aha te tautoko e pai ana ki a ia, ko wai hoki tāna e hiahia ai ki te whai wāhi. He hoa pono, he whanaunga rānei tētahi kōrerorero tuatahi pai. Ko ētahi kei te pai ki tētahi rōpū tautoko, tētahi kaitohutohu, tētahi whakahaere mate wareware ā-rohe, tētahi kōrerorero me tōna rōpū hauora rānei. Ko te whiringa tika ka whakawhirinaki ki te tangata me ngā mea e wātea ana ki tō takiwā.

He tautoko mō te tangata kua whakatauhia, mō ngā whānau, mō ngā hoa hoki. Ka hiahia pea te kaitiaki i te wā whakatārewa, i te āwhina i ngā mahi o ia rā; ka hiahia pea te tangata i ngā mōhiotanga, i te wā hoki ki te tūtaki ki ētahi atu e noho ana me te mate wareware. He rerekē aua hiahia. Pātai i mua i te whakarite rōpū, i te whakaritenga haerenga rānei, i te tuku kōrero whaiaro rānei.

Ko tā mātou [aratohu manaaki i a koe anō](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) he whakaaro mō te tono i ētahi atu kia tohatoha i te tautoko. Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te āwhina nui atu i te rā, ko ngā aratohu mō ngā [hōtaka rā pakeke](/caregiving/choosing-adult-day-program-dementia/) me te [manaaki ā-kāinga](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) e whakamārama ana i ngā pātai hei pātai mō ngā ratonga ā-rohe.

## Me pēhea e noho ai te tangata hei wāhanga o ngā whiringa?

Ehara i te mea ko te whakatau anake e kī ana he aha ka taea e te tangata te whakatau, he aha rānei tāna e hiahia ai ki te whai wāhi. Pātai tika ki te tangata. Hoatu he wā ki a ia, whakamāramatia te whiriwhiri ki te reo māmā, ā, pātai he aha te tautoko e āwhina ai te kōrerorero. Mēnā e pai ana ki a ia he kōrerorero ki tētahi wāhi noho ngākau, ki ētahi kōrerorero maha rānei, he pai pea tērā.

Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te whakamahere mō te hauora ā muri, ngā pūtea, ngā wāhi noho rānei, me tīmata mai i ngā mea e nui ana ki a ia me ngā tāngata e whakawhirinaki ana ia. Ko te [aratohu whakamahere a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) e tohutohu ana kia kōrero ki ngā tāngata pono me ngā kaimahi e whai hāngai ana. He rerekē ngā ture tuhinga me ngā ture whakatau i ia wāhi, nō reira pātai ki te kaimahi ture, pūtea rānei o tō rohe i mua i te whakawhirinaki ki tētahi aratohu whānui. Mō ngā āwhina pūtea o ia rā me ngā tinihanga, tirohia tā mātou [aratohu whakahaere moni me te mate wareware](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/).

### He aha ā tātou pātai i te kōrerorero tuatahi mō te manaaki ā muri?

Pātai mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te kōrero i tēnei wā, kātahi ka whakaara i tētahi kaupapa anake i te wā kotahi. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e tohutohu ana ki ngā kōrerorero iti maha, te reo māmā me te whakarongo ki ngā hiahia, ngā whakapono me ngā uara o te tangata](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). Ko te [Alzheimer's Society e kī ana he rerekē ngā tangata i tō rātou pai ki te whakaaro whakamua](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). He pai te whakatārewa, te hoki ki te kaupapa i tētahi rā anō.

Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki ētahi kupu tohutohu, whakamātauria tētahi o ēnei:

- “He aha ngā wāhanga o tō rā e tino pai ana ki a koe kia mau tonu?”
- “Ko wai te hiahia kia uru mai ina kōrero tātou mō te manaaki ā muri?”
- “He aha ngā mea e āwhina ai koe kia ngākau mārie, kia whakahonoretia ai koe ina hiahia koe ki te āwhina?”
- “He aha tāu e hiahia kia mōhio mai tō rōpū hauora mō ngā mea e whai take ana ki a koe?”
- “Me tuhi e au tāu i kī mai ai, ā, ko wai e tika kia kite?”

Whakarongo me te kore e tono whakatau i taua wā tonu. Ko te [National Institute on Aging e whakamārama ana ko ngā kōrerorero whaitikanga mō ngā uara me ngā whiringa te matua o te whakamahere manaaki whakamua](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, tuhia āna kupu, te rā, me ko wai i reira. Pātai ki a ia i mua i te tuku i taua tuhinga. Ka āwhina ēnei tuhinga i ngā kōrerorero ā muri, engari ehara i te tuhinga ture, i te ota hauora rānei.

Kaua e whakatau i runga anake i te whakatau, i tētahi kōrerorero kotahi rānei. Ka huri pea ngā whiringa a te tangata, ā, ka taea e ia te tono kia tārewahia, kia hoki mai ki taua kaupapa ā muri. Kia noho ia hei wāhanga o ngā whiringa e pā ana ki a ia, kia kaha tonu tana whai wāhi.

## Ko wai me mōhio ki te whakatau?

Ko te tangata te whakatau mēnā, āhea, me pēhea e kōrero ai ki ētahi atu, engari mēnā he take haumaru, ā-ture rānei e hiahia ana ki te tohutohu ngaio, he rerekē te āhua. Pātai ko wai tāna e hiahia kia kōrero ki a rātou, he aha tāna e hiahia kia mōhio ai rātou. Ka kōwhiri pea ia i tētahi tangata pono tuatahi, ka hiahia rānei kia kaua e kōrero whānui.

Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te āwhina i tētahi kōrerorero, pātai he aha tāna e hiahia ai kia kōrero koe, me hiahia rānei ia kia tū ia i reira. Tukuna ngā mōhiotanga kua whakaae ia kia tohaina. Ko tā mātou [aratohu kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) e whakaatu ana i ngā kōrerorero whakaute ki te whānau me ētahi atu.

## Me pēhea e haere tonu ai te oranga ā-rā i a mātou e urutau ana?

Kia pēnei tonu ngā tikanga me ngā mahi e whai take ana ki te tangata i ngā wāhi e māmā tonu ai ia. Kāore te whakatau e kī me mutu ngā hobi, ngā haerenga, ngā mahi kāinga katoa. Mēnā he uaua ētahi mahi, pātai he aha tāna e hiahia kia rerekē. He poto ake te haerenga, he waiata mōhio, he āwhina ki tētahi mahi kotahi, he wā rerekē o te rā pea ka pai ake ki a ia.

Ka hiahia pea te tangata i te wā ki te whakaaro ki te pānga o te whakatau, kāore ngā mahere. Kia tirohia tonu, engari kaua e hurihia ia kōrerorero ki tētahi hui manaaki. He tika kia whakatārewa i te whakamahere, ā, kia noho tahi i tētahi āhua ōkawa.

## Āhea me pātai ki tētahi ngaio?

Pātai ki te kaimahi hauora o te tangata mō ngā pātai e pā ana ki tōna whakatau, ōna hauora, ōna rongoā, ōna tohu rānei. Mō ngā whiringa ture me ngā pūtea, kōrero ki ngā tāngata ngaio e mōhio ana ki ngā ture o te wāhi e noho ana te tangata. Kāore he pae ipurangi e kī mai ko tēhea maimoatanga, tuhinga, ratonga rānei e tika ana mō tētahi tangata.

Ko te pōrearea ohorere, ko tētahi atu rerekētanga ohorere o te hauora, me tono āwhina hauora ohotata. Ko te [aratohu a te NHS mō te pōrearea ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora i taua wā tonu. Whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata ā-rohe, ngā ratonga ohotata hauora rānei o tō rohe; kaua e whakaaro he rerekētanga ohorere he wāhanga o te āhua noa o te tangata.

Mēnā he uaua te whakamahere, kōwhiria tētahi tangata pono, tētahi ratonga rānei hei āwhina ki te whakarōpū i ngā pātai ki ngā mea ināianei me ngā mea ā muri ake nei. Ka taea e te tangata te whakatakoto i te tere, ā, ka whakatau ia he aha ana e hiahia ai te āwhina. He mōhiotanga nui te whakatau, engari ehara i te mea e whakamārama ai ngā hiahia, ngā whanaungatanga, ngā mahi o ia rā o te tangata.
