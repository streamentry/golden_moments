Ka uaua pea te whai i te kai mēnā he haruru te rūma, he maha ngā mea i te tēpu, he maha ngā kōwhiringa i te wā kotahi. Ka māmā ake te tuku i te tangata kia whai wāhi mā ngā whakarerekētanga iti ki te horopaki me te tere. Tīmatahia mai i ngā tikanga me ngā mahi a te tangata, ā, kia mau tonu ngā mea e pai ana kē.

E tohu ana te [aratohu kai a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) me te [tohutohu wā kai a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) ki te wāhi mārie, ki ngā kai e mōhiotia ana, ki te wā tika. Ko tēnei aratohu e titiro ana ki te horopaki o te kai me te tautoko ia rā. Ehara ia i te mahere kai, ehara hoki i te arotake o te horomi.

## Me pēhea e whai hoa ai te kai, kaua e pēhi ana?

Mēnā e pai ana te tangata ki te whai hoa, noho ki te kai tahi ki a ia. Kia māmā tonu te kōrerorero, ā, tukuna ia kia aro ki te kai ina hiahia ia. Kaua e whakatika i tana huarahi whakamahi tīpaki, kaua hoki e pātai i ngā pātai ka huri te kai hei whakamātautau. Ina haruru, ina kikī te tēpu, whakaitia te maha o te tangata, neke rānei ki tētahi wāhi noho puku ake.

Ina hōhā te tunu kai, he kai kua rite kē, he āwhina ki tētahi wāhanga o te mahi, ka māmā ake te kai. Ka kī a Dementia Australia he kai ka tukuna mai ki te kāinga, he kōwhiringa pea ina hiahiatia. Pātai he aha ngā kai e pai ana ki a ia, he momo āwhina e whakaae ai ia. Ahakoa he māmā te kai kua rite, me waiho tonu he wāhi mō te whiriwhiri a te tangata.

## Me pēhea e māmā ai te tēpu ki te whai?

Me whakarite te tēpu kia māmā te titiro i mua i te panoni i ngā kai e hoatu ana.

- **Whakaitia te haruru.** Whakakorea te pouaka whakaata, whakaitia ētahi atu oro e whakararu ana. Mēnā ka āwhina, noho ki tētahi wāhi kāore e pōkaikaha ana te haere o te tangata i te kāinga kai.
- **Tangohia ngā mea kē.** Whakapuakina anake ngā tīpaki me ngā horoi mangai e whakamahia ana e te tangata. Neke ngā puāwai, ētahi atu taonga rānei ka whakararu, ka whakaarotia pea he kai.
- **Whakamahia te rerekētanga mārama.** He pereti mā, he rerekē ki te whāriki, ka māmā ake te kite i te kai. Kaua e whakamahia ngā tauira nui ki ngā pereti me ngā kāpeti tēpu.
- **Tirohia te marama me te pāmahana.** Kia kite te tangata i te pereti, ā, whakamātauria te kai me ngā inu i mua i te tuku, kia kaua e wera rawa.
- **Pupuri i te tikanga mōhio.** Mēnā he wāhi, he wā, he whakaritenga tēpu e pai ana ki a ia, kia pēnei tonu. Whakarerekētia anake te mea e raru ana.

## He aha ngā kai hei tuku?

Tīmatahia ki ngā kai e mōhio ana te tangata, e pai ana ki a ia. Hoatu tētahi kai i te wā kotahi, he kōwhiringa rua ngā kai e mōhiotia ana, kaua e hoatu he pereti kī ngā kai motuhake maha. Ka taea te whakahoki mai i ngā wāhanga iti mēnā ka hiahia te tangata ki te kai atu.

Ka rerekē pea ngā hiahia. Mēnā ka whakakahore ia i tana kai pai, kaua e tautohe, kaua e tohe. Hoatu tētahi atu kōwhiringa e mōhiotia ana, tukuna ia kia whakatā, whakamātau anō ā muri ake. Kāore he mahere pakari me whai i ngā wā kai ina ngenge, ina pōuri rānei te tangata; whai i ngā wā kai e pai ana, ā, urutau ki ngā hiahia o te tangata.

Mēnā kua uaua te tunu kai, ka āwhina pea tētahi o te whānau i te whakarite kai, ā, ka uru atu te tangata ki tētahi mahi haumaru e pai ana ki a ia. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mahi kāinga](/activities/household-tasks/) he huarahi hei urutau i ngā mahi kāinga kia kaua e huri hei whakamātautau.

## Me pēhea e tautoko ai i te motuhake o te tangata?

Tukuna te tangata kia kai i te huarahi e pai ana, e haumaru ana ki a ia. Akene he pai ki a ia he pōkai, he kai ka kainga ki te ringa, he wāhi iti rānei. Kaua e whakatika wawe i te horo, kaua hoki e tango i tētahi mahi e taea tonu e ia. Hoatu āwhina ina hiahiatia, ā, tukuna he wā nui mō te kai.

Ka māmā te kai ki te tangata ina kai tahi, mēnā e pai ana ki a ia te hoa. Noho tata, kia māmā tonu te kōrerorero, waiho kia aro ia ki te kai ina hiahia ia. Kaua e pātai i ngā pātai mātai mahara, kaua e kōrero mō te nui o tāna kai. Ina whakaatu ia i te tatari, tatari, ā, pātai mēnā kua hiahia ia ki tētahi wāhanga anō.

## Ka aha mēnā ka uaua ngā wā kai?

Whakatārewa, rapua tētahi take ngāwari. Akene he haruru te rūma; akene he kai ehara i te mea e mōhio ana ia ki a ia; akene me haere ia ki te wharepaku, me okioki, me neke rānei. Whakamātau i tētahi panoni i te wā kotahi, pēnei i te whakamārama i te tēpu, i te tuku wai, i te neke ki te wāhi noho puku ake. Ina raruraru te tangata, whakamutua, ka ngana anō ā muri ake. Kaua e pēhi kia oti te kai.

Ina kai iti te tangata i ngā wā maha, ina uaua tana mahi ki te mangai, ki ngā ringa rānei, ina rerekē te hiahia kai, te taumaha rānei, tuhia ngā tohu e kite ana koe, kōrero ki tētahi takuta, ki tētahi kaimātanga kai. Ngā raruraru hou o te ngau, o te horomi, ngā rerekētanga i te kai, i te inu, me te whakapōrearea, me tono tohutohu ngaio. Ka taea e tētahi kaimātanga kōrero me te horomi te aromātai, te whakaatu i ngā huarahi haumaru. Kaua e panoni i te momo kai, kaua e whakaiti i ngā wai, kaua e ngana ki ngā tikanga whangai i tētahi raarangi whānui.

Kāore he kai kotahi e whakamārama ai he aha te tangata i whakakore ai. Titiro mō te tauira puta noa i ngā wā kai: he kai motuhake, he wā, he haruru, he tūnga noho, ka uaua pea te mahi. Mēnā he kai pai kua uaua ohorere te kai, tirohia mēnā kei te mamae, kei te raru niho rānei, ka pātai ki tētahi kaimahi hauora, kāore e whakaaro he hē noa te tangata.

Mō ngā pātai hauora, mō tētahi kai motuhake, pātai ki te kaimahi hauora o te tangata. Kaua e tīmata i tētahi kai whakahaere, kaua e panoni i ngā rongoā hei whakarerekē i te hiahia kai, me te kore tohutohu. Ko te [aratohu kai hauora-roro](/brain-health/diet/) e kōrero ana mō ngā rangahau mō te kai me te tūponotanga o te mate wareware; he wehe tēnei i tēnei aratohu wā kai ngāwari.

## Me kai tonu i te tēpu te kai?

Kāo. Mēnā he uaua, he pōuri rānei te noho ki te tēpu, akene he pai ake tētahi atu wāhi noho, ahakoa me kai haumaru te tangata. He kai iti, he kai poto, he pūtau kai pea he pai ake i te whakamātau kia oti te kai katoa i runga i te wā kua whakaritea.

Mēnā kua uaua te kai i ngā rā maha, mēnā kei te māharahara koe mō te kai, mō ngā wai, mō te horomi rānei, whakapā atu ki tētahi kaimahi hauora. Rapua āwhina ohotata mō tētahi raru tere tonu o te kai ki te korokoro.
