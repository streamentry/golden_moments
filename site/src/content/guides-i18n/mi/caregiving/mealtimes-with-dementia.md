Ka uaua pea te whai i te kai mēnā he haruru te rūma, he maha ngā mea i te tēpu, he maha ngā kōwhiringa i te wā kotahi. Ko ētahi whakarerekētanga iti ki te horopaki me te tere ka māmā ake te tuku i te tangata kia whai wāhi. Tīmatahia mai i ngā tikanga me ngā mataaho o te tangata, ā, kia mau tonu ngā mea e pai ana kē.

Ko te [aratohu kai me te kai a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) me te [tohutohu wā kai a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) e tohu ana ki te wāhi mārie, ngā kai e mōhiotia ana me te wā ausika. Ko tēnei aratohu e titiro ana ki te horopaki o te kai me te tautoko ia rā. Ehara ia i te mahere kai, i te arotake horomi.

## Me pēhea e whai hoa ai te kai, kāore e pēhi ana?

Mēnā e pai ana te tangata ki te whai hoa, noho me kai tahi ki a ia. Kia māmā tonu te kōrerorero, ā, tukuna ia kia aro ki te kai ina hiahia ia. Kaua e whakatika i tana huarahi whakamahi tīpaki, kaua hoki e pātai pātai ka huri te kai hei whakamātautau. Ina haruru, ina ngau-kore te tēpu, whakaitia te maha o te tangata, neke rānei ki tētahi wāhi noho puku ake.

Ina hōhā te tunu kai, he kai kua rite kē, he āwhina ki tētahi wāhanga o te mahi, ka māmā ake te kai. Ka kī a Dementia Australia he kai kua rite, he kai ka tukuna mai ki te kāinga, he kōwhiringa pea ina hiahiatia. Pātai he aha ngā kai e pai ana ki a ia, he momo āwhina e whakaae ai ia; ahakoa he māmā te āwhina, me waiho tonu he wāhi mō te pōhiri o te tangata.

## Me pēhea e māmā ai te tēpu ki te whai?

Me whakarite te kai kia māmā te titiro i mua i te whakarerekē i ngā kai e hoatu ana.

- **Whakaitia te haruru.** Whakakorea te pouaka whakaata, whakaitia ētahi atu oro e whakararu ana. Noho ki tētahi wāhi kua tawhiti atu i te haerenga o te kāinga kai, mēnā ka āwhina ia kia kaha ai tana aro.
- **Tangohia ngā mea kē.** Whakapuakina anake ngā tīpaki me ngā tāra e whakamahia ana e te tangata. Neke ngā horoi kākahu, ngā puāwai, ētahi atu taonga rānei ka whakararu, ka hē pea te mahara he kai.
- **Whakamahia te rerekētanga mārama.** He pereti mā, he rerekē ki te whāriki ka māmā ake te kai te kitea. Kaua e whakamahia he pereti, he kākahu tēpu e whai tauira nui ana.
- **Tirohia te marama me te pāmahana.** Kia kite te tangata i te pereti, ā, whakamātauria te kai me ngā inu i mua i te tuku kia kaua e wera rawa.
- **Pupuri i te tikanga mōhio.** Mēnā he wāhi, he wā, he whakaritenga tēpu e pai ana ki a ia, kia pēnei tonu. Whakarerekētia anake te mea e raru ana.

## He aha ngā kai hei tuku?

Tīmatahia ki ngā kai e mōhio ana te tangata, e pai ana ki a ia. Hoatu tētahi kai, tētahi kōwhiringa rua o ngā kai e mōhiotia ana, kaua e hoatu he tēpu kī ngā kai motuhake maha. Ka taea te whakahou i ngā wāhanga iti mēnā ka hiahia te tangata ki te kai atu.

Ka rerekē pea ngā hiahia. Mēnā ka whakakahore ia i tana kai pai, kaua e tautohe, kaua e tohe. Hoatu tētahi atu kōwhiringa e mōhiotia ana, tukuna ia kia whakatā, whakamātau anō ā muri ake. Kāore he mahere pakari me whai i te wā kai ina ngenge, ina pōuri rānei te tangata; kia tū tonu ngā wā kai ina pai ana, ā, urutau ki ngā hiahia o te tangata.

Mēnā kua uaua te tunu kai, ka āwhina pea tētahi o te whānau i te whakarite kai, ā, ka uru atu te tangata ki tētahi mahi haumaru e pai ana ki a ia. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mahi kāinga](/activities/household-tasks/) he huarahi hei urutau i ngā mahi kāinga kia kaua e huri hei whakamātautau.

## Me pēhea e tautoko ai i te motuhake o te tangata?

Tukuna te tangata kia kai i te huarahi e pai ana, e haumaru ana ki a ia. Ka pai pea ki a ia he pereti, he pōkai, he kai ringa, he wāhi iti rānei. Kaua e whakatika wawe i te horo, kaua hoki e tango i tētahi mahi e taea tonu e ia. Hoatu āwhina ina hiahiatia, ina tika, ā, tukuna he wā nui mō te kai.

Ka māmā te kai ki te tangata ina kai tahi, mēnā e pai ana ki a ia te hoa. Noho tata, kia māmā tonu te kōrerorero, waiho kia aro ia ki te kai ina hiahia ia. Kaua e pātai pātai ka whakamātau i te mahara, kaua e kōrero mō te nui o tāna kai. Ina tū ia, tatari, pātai mēnā kua rite ia ki tētahi wāhanga anō.

## Ka aha mēnā ka pōkaikaha ngā wā kai?

Whakatārewa, ka rapu i tētahi take ngāwari. He haruru pea te rūma; he kai pea kāore ia i mōhio; pea me haere ia ki te wharepaku, me okioki, me huri tūru rānei. Whakamātau i tētahi urutanga i te wā kotahi, pēnei i te whakamārama i te tēpu, i te tuku wai, i te neke ki te wāhi noho puku ake. Ina raruraru te tangata, whakamutua, ka ngana anō ā muri ake; kaua e pēhi kia oti te kai.

Ina kai pōhara te tangata i ngā wā maha, ina raru tana mahi ki te mangai, ki ngā ringa rānei, ina rerekē te hiahia kai, te taumaha rānei, tuhia ngā tohu e kite ana koe, kōrero ki tētahi takuta, ki tētahi kaimātanga kai. Ngā raruraru hou o te ngau, o te horomi, ngā kohu i te kai, i te inu, me te whakapōrearea, me tono tohutohu ngaio. Ka taea e tētahi kaimātanga kōrero me te horomi te aromātai, me te whakaatu i ngā huarahi haumaru. Kaua e panoni i te momo kai, e whakaitia ngā wai, e ngana ki ngā tikanga whangai mai i tētahi raarangi whānui.

Kāore he kai kotahi kua kainga kē e whakamārama ai he aha te tangata i whakakore ai. Titiro mō te tauira puta noa i ngā kai: he kai motuhake, he wā, he taumaha o te haruru, he tūnga noho, ka uaua pea te mahi. Mēnā he kai pai kua uaua ohorere te kai, tirohia mēnā kei te mamae, kei te raru niho rānei, ka pātai ki tētahi kaimahi hauora, kāore e whakaaro he hē noa te tangata.

Mō ngā pātai hauora, mō tētahi kai motuhake, pātai ki te kaimahi hauora o te tangata. Kaua e tīmata i tētahi kai whakahaere, kaua e panoni i ngā rongoā hei whakarerekē i te hiahia kai, me te kore tohutohu. Ko te [aratohu kai hauora-roro](/brain-health/diet/) e kōrero ana mō ngā rangahau mō te kai me te tūponotanga o te mate wareware; he wehe tēnei i tēnei aratohu wā kai ngāwari.

## Me kai tonu te kai i te tēpu?

Kāo. Mēnā he uaua, he pōuri rānei te noho ki te tēpu, akene ka pai ake tētahi atu wāhi noho, ahakoa me kai haumaru te tangata. He kai iti, he kai roa, he pūtahi rānei ka pai ake i te whakamātau kia oti he kai katoa i runga i te wā kua whakaritea.

Mēnā kua uaua te kai i ngā rā maha, mēnā kei te māharahara koe mō te kai, mō ngā wai, mō te horomi rānei, whakapā atu ki tētahi kaimahi hauora. Rapua āwhina ohotata mō te raru tere tonu o te kai ki te korokoro.
