Ka taea tonu te haere ina pai te tangata ki te haere, ā, ina tika te mahere ki āna hiahia. He māmā pea te toro ki te whānau i te haerenga iti, i te haerenga nui kīkī. He pai atu pea te haerenga rā kotahi i te noho pō. Pātai ki te tangata he aha tāna e hiahia ana mai i te haerenga, kātahi ka hanga he mahere me te wāhi hei tārewa, hei whakarerekē rānei.

Ko te [aratohu haerenga a Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) me ngā [tohutohu haerenga a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) e whakanui ana i te whakarite e pā ana ki te oranga me ngā tikanga o te tangata. Ko tēnei aratohu he whakaaro whānui mō te whakarite, ehara i te whakatau mēnā he haumaru te haere mō tētahi tangata takitahi.

## Me pēhea mātou e whakatau ai mēnā he tika te haerenga?

Kōrerohia te haerenga ki te tangata, me ngā kupu e mōhiotia ana e ia, me tētahi whakamāramatanga ngāwari o te wāhi e haere ana koutou, ko wai hoki kei reira. Ki te kore ia e hiahia ki te haere, whakaaetia tērā. Ka taea pea te whakarite he kai, he waea, he mahi e mōhiotia ana i te kāinga.

Whakaarohia ngā mea e whakahaere pai ana te tangata i tēnei wā. Titiro ki tana urupare ki ngā panonitanga o te tikanga, te haruru, te huihuinga tangata, ngā tawhiti hikoi, te tatari, me ngā rūma kāore ia i mōhio ki aua rūma. Ki te ōrite pea ngā wāhi maha o te haerenga e uaua ana, whakamāmāahia, kōwhiria rānei tētahi atu huarahi hei whakapau wā tahi ai. Ko te tohutoro a Dementia Australia kia pātai ki tētahi tākuta ina kāore koe e mārama mēnā e tika ana te haerenga mō te hauora o te tangata, mō āna hiahia āwhina rānei.

Mēnā he raruraru hauora hou te tangata, he hiahia āwhina mō ngā rongoā, he hiahia nekehanga kei te panoni pea i te haerenga, pātai ki te kaimahi hauora e tika ana i mua i te whakarite. Kaua e whakamutu, e panoni, e whakanuku rānei i ngā rongoā kua tohua mō te haerenga, mēnā kāore he tohutohu mai i te kaituku rongoā, i te kaihoko rongoā rānei.

## He momo haerenga pēhea me whakarite?

- **Kōwhiria he wāhi e mōhiotia ana ina taea.** He wāhi e mōhiotia ana e te tangata, he toronga ki ngā tāngata e mōhiotia ana, pea he iti ake ngā panonitanga i te huarahi hou me te haerenga whakahihiri.
- **Whakamāmāa te huarahi.** Whiriwhiria he iti ake ngā whakawhiti, kaua e whakatata ngā hononga tere. Tirohia ngā wharepaku, ngā nohoanga me ngā wāhi mārie i mua i te wehenga.
- **Haere i te wā pai o te rā.** Whakamahere i runga i te kaha me te rhythm o te rā o te tangata. Waiho he wā mō ngā kai, ngā rongoā, te okioki me te whakarite.
- **Whakamāmāa te hōtaka.** Kotahi pea te mahi matua e tika ana. Kaua e whakahāngai i ia haora; waiho he wā mō ngā whakaroa, mō te hoki wawe rānei.
- **Pātai mō te āwhina i mua i te haere.** He rerekē ngā ratonga me ngā ture a ngā kamupene rererangi, ngā kamupene tereina, ngā hōtera me ngā wāhi huihuinga. Whakapā atu moata ki te pātai mō te āwhina nekehanga, ngā nohoanga, ngā wharepaku ka taea te uru, me te āwhina ki te kimi i te wāhi tika.

Ka taea pea e te [aratohu mō ngā hararei mārie](/activities/holidays/) te whakaatu i ngā whakaaro mō ngā toro me ngā hui whānau. Mēnā kei te tūtaki koe ki ngā whānaunga, ko tā mātou [aratohu toro](/activities/visiting/) e kapi ana i te kōrero, ngā toro poto, me te haere atu me te kore e pēhia.

## He aha ngā mea me kawe?

Kawea he kete iti kei a koe ngā taonga matua e noho ana ki te hoa kaitiaki pono, kaua ki roto i te pēke kawe i whakaputaina.

- **Ngā rongoā me ngā mōhiotanga hauora.** Kawea ngā rongoā o ia rā o te tangata i tōna pūtea taketake, he nui ki te haerenga kua whakaritea me tētahi wā roa hei tūpono, me te rārangi rongoā hou. Tirohia ngā ture o nāianei mō te kawe waka me ngā rongoā tārewa i mua i te haere. Pupuri i ngā tātai rongoā me ngā wā i tohua e te kaiwhakaaro rongoā.
- **Tuakiri me ngā whakapā.** Kawea te tuakiri o te tangata, te wāhi noho, ngā whakapā ohotata, me te mahere tuhituhi o te haerenga. Ina āwhina, ina whakaaetia, whakauruhia he kāri me ngā taipitopito whakapā o te kaitiaki.
- **Ngā taonga whakamārie.** Kawea he karaihe, he taputapu rongo me ō rātou pākaru, ō rātou hiko rānei, he kai e mōhiotia ana, he wai, he pepa papapaku, he kākahu mārama, me he kākahu rerekē. He taonga e mōhiotia ana, he whakaahua rānei, ka māmā pea te noho ki te rūma rerekē.
- **Ngā kape o ngā mōhiotanga matua.** Pupuri i tētahi kape pepa, kape matihiko rānei o ngā tiki, ngā pukapuka, ngā inihua me ngā mōhiotanga hauora e hāngai ana. Tohaina te mahere haerenga ki tētahi tangata pono kāore e haere tahi ana me koe.

He rerekē te nui o ngā mōhiotanga e kawea ana, i runga i te haerenga. Pupuri i ngā taipitopito whaiaro i te haumaru, ā, tukuna ki ngā tāngata e hiahia ana ki a rātou anake hei āwhina.

## Me pēhea e māmā ai te rā haerenga?

Kīia atu ki te tangata he aha ka tupu i muri mai, ia mahi kotahi i te wā. Hoatu he wā kē hei whakarite, hei tūtaki, hei whakamahi i te wharepaku, hei nekehanga hoki i waenga i ngā wāhi. Ina panoni ngā mahere, whakamāramahia te mahi e whai ake nei ngāwari, kaua e whakahoki i te hōtaka roa.

Noho tahi i ngā wāhi pōhēhē, i ngā wāhi kāore koe i mōhio ki aua wāhi. Ina ngenge, ina pōhēhē rānei te tangata, tārewa ki tētahi wāhi mārie, hoatu he wai, he mahi e mōhiotia ana rānei, ā, whakatau tahi mēnā me haere tonu. Kaua e waiho te tangata anake ki tētahi wāhi kāore ia i mōhio ki aua wāhi, kei pōhēhē, kei raru rānei ia.

Ki te rere rererangi koe, ki te whare tereina nui rānei, pātai ki te kaiwhakarato he aha ngā āwhina e wātea ana me te pēhea te tono. He panoni ngā tikanga, no reira whakapūmau ngā taipitopito ki te kamupene rererangi, te whare tereina, te wāhi rānei i mua i te haerenga. Kaua e whakawiri ki te rārangi tikanga tawhito mō ngā ture haumaru, uru hoki o nāianei.

## He aha te mahere tuarua e whai hua ana?

Whakaritea i mua he aha tāu e mahi ai mēnā ka hiahia te tangata ki te wehe, mēnā ka roa te haerenga, mēnā ka hiahia tētahi ki te okioki. Kōwhiria tētahi tangata whakapā hei āwhina mā te waea. Māmā ki te kimi i te wāhi noho me te huarahi hoki, ā, kia mōhio koe ki te wāhi e taea ai te noho mārie, te whakamahi i te wharepaku rānei.

Mēnā me whakapoto e koe te haerenga, mahia. Ehara te toronga kāore i kitea, te hoki wawe rānei i te hinga. Mēnā ka pēhia te haerenga, kōwhiria tētahi mahi mārie ina hoki mai ki te kāinga. Ka āwhina te [aratohu whakawhitiwhiti kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) me ngā whakaaro mō te kōrero mārie me te whakawhiti kōrero kotahi kōrero i te wā.

## Me kōrero ki te hōtera, ki te kaiwhakarato haerenga rānei?

Mēnā e whakaaetia ana e te tangata, kōrerohia ngā kaimahi i ngā mōhiotanga e hiahia ana rātou hei āwhina pai. Hei tauira, pātai ki te hōtera kia tuhia he hiahia nekehanga, kia tonoa rānei he rūma e uru ai te tangata. Mēnā kāore te tangata e hiahia kia kōrerohia tana mate, kōrerohia he huarahi kē hei tono āwhina e tiaki ana i tōna tūmataiti.

Mō te haerenga roa, kia tata ki te tikanga o ia rā ngā kai, te moe me te wā rongoā o te tangata, i te mea e taea ana e te hōtaka. Whakamahere i tētahi rā tuatahi kāore e tere ana, ā, waiho he wā hei okioki i muri i tō hokinga mai. Ko ngā whiriwhiringa iti, e mōhiotia ana, ka māmā ai te urutau.
