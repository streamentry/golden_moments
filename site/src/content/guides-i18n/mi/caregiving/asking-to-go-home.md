“Kei te hiahia au ki te hoki ki te kāinga.” He kōrero whakamamae tēnei ki te nuinga o ngā whānau, otirā ina noho te tangata e tiakina ana e koe i tōna ake rūma noho. He mea noa, ka tupu i ngā wāhanga katoa o te mate wareware, ā, he huarahi mārie, whaihua hei whakautu. Ko tēnei whārangi e whakaatu ana he aha ngā kupu nei e rite ai te tikanga, he aha te kī, ngā mahi e āwhina ai i taua wā, me pēhea te noho haumaru.

## He aha te tangata e mate wareware ana e tono tonu ana kia hoki ki te kāinga?

Ko te nuinga o te wā, “kāinga” he kare nui ake i te wāhi. E whakamārama ana te Alzheimer's Society hei kare mārie me te haumaru, he kāinga o mua rānei, ehara i te whare tonu. Ka puta pea te ngangau ki ngā rūma mōhio nā te mate wareware, ā, ka whakaaro pea te tangata kei tētahi wā o mua o tōna oranga e noho ana ia, ina ko te kāinga he whare tamariki, he wāhi i whakatupu ai i āna tamariki.

Ka tohu pea ngā kupu nei he raru kē kei te puta i tēnei wā tonu: kei te hiakai rānei, kei te matewai, kei te hiahia ki te wharepaku, kei te mamae, kei te wera, kei te makariri rawa, kei te ngenge, kei te mokemoke, kei te wehi, kua rahi te haruru ki a ia. E whakarārangi ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-agitation-aggression-and-sundowning) i te mamae, i te haruru, i te mokemoke, i ngā rerekētanga o te tikanga me ngā rongoā hei take pōrearea.

## He aha taku kī ina tono ia kia hoki ki te kāinga?

Whakautu ki te kare, ehara i ngā kupu. E tohutohu ana te [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) kia kaua e tarai ki te whakapono ki a ia he hē tāna: he pono āna kare, nō reira whakautu ki te mārie me te whakamārie i te tuatahi, ā, muri iho hurihia atu mārie.

Ngā kupu e pai ana i te nuinga o te wā:

- **Kīa te kare.** “He rite ki te mea kei te ngaro tō kāinga.”
- **Whakamārie.** “Kei te haumaru koe i konei, ā, kei te noho tahi au me koe.” Ka tūtohu te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation) i ngā kupu māmā, “Kei te haumaru koe i konei.”
- **Tono mahara.** “Korerohia mai mō tō kāinga. He pēhea tō whare kai?” Ko te pātai mō te wā tawhiti he nui ake te mārie i ngā pātai mō tēnei rā.
- **Tukuna tētahi mahi tahi.** “Me inu tī tātou i mua,” “Ka āwhina mai koe ki ēnei tauera?”

He reo mahana me te ringa ki te ringa, mēnā e pai ana ki a ia te pāwhiri, ka nui ake te kī i ngā kupu.

## He aha ngā mea me karo?

- **Te tohe, te whakamārama.** “Kei konei koe e noho ana i tēnei wā” me “Ko tēnei tō kāinga mō ngā tau 40” e tono ana kia whakaae ki tētahi mea he uaua ki te mate wareware te whakaae, ā, ka rite pea ki te kī he hē tāna whakaaro.
- **Te whakamātau pātai.** “Kāore koe e maumahara?” me “He aha te wāhitau?” ka piki ai te pehanga.
- **Te aukati i te kuaha i te riri.** Mēnā ka haere atu ia, noho mārie, haere tahi me ia. He hīkoi poto tahi ka mārie pea te hiahia.
- **Te whakatere.** Hoatu he wā ki a ia kia kōrero mō tōna kāinga i mua i tō huri i te kaupapa.

## Ngā mahi ka āwhina i taua wā

Ā muri i tō whakaae ki te kare, ka taea e tētahi mahi mārie, mōhio te hurihia atu te aro, kāore e whakakorehia tana kare.

1. **Titiro ki ngā whakaahua o te kāinga me te whānau.** Huria ngā whārangi, whakaputa kōrero (“Titiro ki taua māra”) kaua e pātai pātai. I runga i te waea, i te taputapu, ka huri te [Ngā Takirua Whakaahua Whānau](/games/photo-pairs/) i ō whakaahua whānau ake ki tētahi tākaro rite mārie, ā, ka noho ngā whakaahua ki tō taputapu.
2. **Kōrero mō te kāinga.** Ka tuku ngā [Kāri Kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/) i ngā tohutohu tuwhera mō ngā whare, ngā whare kai, ngā hoa noho tata me ngā hararei, kāore he whakautu tika.
3. **Tākaro i ngā waiata e pai ana ki a rātou.** Ka whakarārangi te National Institute on Aging i ngā waiata mārie hei huarahi whakamārie i te pōrearea. Titiro ki ā mātou [mahi waiata](/activities/music/).
4. **Kai iti, inu rānei.** Ka rite pea te hiakai me te matewai ki te pōrearea. He kapu tī, he kai pai hoki he tikanga iti o te noho ki te kāinga.
5. **Hīkoi, noho ki waho rānei.** Ka āwhina te nekeneke me te rangi mārama, otirā i te wāhanga moata o te rā.
6. **Tukua he mahi mōhio.** Te whakapaki i ngā kākahu, te whakarōpū i ngā hāmene, te whakamākū i ngā tipu ka whakahoki mai te kare o te whakahaere kāinga.
7. **Whakatere iho i ngā mea katoa.** Whakakatia te pouaka whakaata, whakaitia te haruru me te rama kanapa, ā, whakamātau he wā hā mārie tahi me [Te Hā Mārie](/games/calm-breathing/).

Mēnā kāore tētahi whakaaro e pai, whakamātau anō tētahi atu ā muri ake. He hua pai hoki te noho puku tahi.

## Tirohia tētahi take ka taea e koe te whakatika

| Tirohia | Whakamātau |
| --- | --- |
| Hiakai, matewai | Tukuna he inu me tētahi kai iti |
| Hiahia ki te wharepaku | Tukuna te haere ki te wharepaku, mārie, kaua e whakatūma |
| Mamae, ngākau kino | Titiro ki ngā tohu pēnei i te kōtire, i te pupuri i tētahi wāhi tinana, i te kore whakaputa para, ā, kōrerohia ki tō tākuta |
| Kua wera rawa, kua makariri rawa | Whakarerekētia tētahi paparanga kākahu, te pāmahana o te rūma |
| Haruru, tāhua tangata | Neke ki tētahi rūma klanga ake, whakakatia te pouaka whakaata |
| Ngenge, kua rahi rawa te whakaohooho | Whakatā, whakapōuri te rūma, tākaro waiata ngāwari |
| Ahiahi | Titiro ki te wāhanga pōrearea i te ahiahi kei raro |

E tūtohu ana te Alzheimer's Society kia pupuri he rārangi tuhi poto mō te wā e tono ai, pēnei i mua i te kai, i muri i ngā manuhiri, i te pō. Ka māmā ake te whakamahere mēnā ka kitea he tauira.

## He pai rānei te kī i tētahi teka atawhai?

He tautohetohe tūturu tēnei, ā, he rerekē te whakaaro o ngā whānau atawhai. E kī ana te Alzheimer's Society karekau he whakautu tika kotahi: ka whakaruru pea ētahi tāngata i ngā teka, nā reira whakatauhia he aha te pai rawa mō te tangata, ā, kia pūmau. I te Penn Memory Center, ko te whakaaro a te kaiwhakahaere takirua, ko te pono te tikanga matua, ā, ka whakaaetia he teka atawhai anake ina kāore e taea e te tangata tētahi pono motuhake te whakaaro, ā, kaua rawa e whakahāwea i tana ao.

He maha ngā kaitiaki e kite ana he ara waenga e pai ana: te whakamārie pono me te huri atu i te tuatahi (“Ka noho tonu tātou i konei i tēnei pō, ā, kei konei tonu au me koe”), kāore e tohe mō te wāhi o te kāinga.

## Ka pēhea mēnā ka puta ia ahiahi, ia pō?

Ko te pōrearea e tīmata ana, e piki ana rānei i te ahiahi, ka kīia ko te pōrearea i te ahiahi. E tūtohu ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-agitation-aggression-and-sundowning) he tikanga tūturu, he rā, he mahi ia rā, he moe iti i te ahiahi, karekau he kawhe, he waipiro rānei i te mutunga o te rā. Ka āwhina hoki te whakahuri i ngā rama i mua i te pō. Ka nui ake ngā kōrero i tā mātou [aratohu mō ngā mahi mārie i te ahiahi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

## Te pupuri haumaru i a rātou mēnā ka ngana ki te wehe

Ko te tono kia hoki ki te kāinga ka arahi pea ki te haere kore whāinga. E tātari ana te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering) e ono i ngā tekau o ngā tāngata e noho ana me te mate wareware ka haere kore whāinga kotahi wā, neke atu rānei.

**Whakaritea i mua:**

- He pareti ID, he pareti hauora rānei me tōna ingoa me tō nama waea, me ngā tapanga i ōna kākahu. E kōrero ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-alzheimers-behaviors-wandering-and) mō te kaupapa MedicAlert me te kaupapa Safe and Found a te Alzheimer's Association, tērā pea he utu tā rātou.
- He tohu kuaha, he whaitara rānei, me ngā rakau kati kei runga, kei raro rānei. Kaua rawa e kati te tangata i roto i te rūma anake.
- Tētahi whakaahua tata hou, me te rārangi o ngā wāhi ka haere pea ia, pēnei i tōna kāinga o mua, i tōna wāhi mahi.
- Kōrerohia ō hoa noho tata pono, ā, hoatu ō nama ki a rātou.

**Mēnā ka ngaro ia:** tirohia ngā wāhi haumaru tata, ā, whakapā tere atu ki ngā pirihimana ā-rohe, ki ngā ratonga ohotata rānei. Kaua e tatari he wā kua whakatauhia i mua i te pūrongo. He tuhinga i te kōrero a te [U.S. Department of Justice mō ngā rapunga tangata ngaro](https://www.justice.gov/usdoj-media/usao/media/1134031/dl?inline=) i te 2021 e kī ana kāore e tika kia whakaaetia e ngā ratonga ture tētahi wā tatari kua whakatauhia; e whakarārangi ana hoki i ngā tohu pēnei i te mate wareware hei take tūraru nui. He rerekē ngā tikanga pūrongo ā-rohe. Kīa ngā kaiāwhina he mate wareware tōna, hoatu te wāhi whakamutunga i kitea ai ia me tētahi whakaahua hou, ā, whai i ō rātou tohutohu. Mēnā he kaiāwhina anō, tonoa kia noho tata ki te waea. Kaua e rapu i ngā wāhi morearea anake.

## Ā hea ahau waea ai ki tētahi tākuta?

Waea tere atu ki tana tākuta mēnā ka tīmata ohorere te tono, te pōhēhē rānei, mēnā ka nui rawa te kino i ngā hāora, i ngā rā rānei. E kī ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) me tirotiro tere te whanonga ohorere, tere rānei te huri, otirā i muri i tētahi mate, i te huringa rongoā. Ka pōrearea pea te pōhēhē tere, e kī ana te [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) he mea e hiahia ana ki te āwhina tere; he take noa ngā mate i ngā ara mimi ki ngā pakeke.

He pai hoki te kōrero mō tētahi tauira tūturu i te toronga e whai ake nei, nā te mea ka taea e te mamae, te pōuri iti, ngā raruraru moe, ngā pānga kino o te rongoā te whai wāhi.
