He tūmataiti te manaaki waha. Ka wareware pea te tangata e mate wareware ana i tētahi tikanga mōhio, ka ngenge pea ia ki te whakamahi i te horoi niho, ka kore mārie pea ia ina tuku mai tētahi atu āwhina ki a ia. Kia mārie, kia whakaute i te tikanga. Me mahi te tangata i ana mea e taea ana, e hiahia ana hoki.

Ko te [aratohu manaaki niho a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dental-care) me te [tohutohu manaaki niho a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/dental-care) e tohutohu ana kia whai i tētahi tikanga mōhio, i ngā tohutohu poto, i te āwhina a te tohunga niho ina raru. He tohutohu whānui tēnei whārangi, ehara i te tohutohu niho, ehara hoki i te mahere tiaki mō tētahi tangata takitahi.

## Me pēhea e pupuri ai i te manaaki waha ia rā, he mōhio?

Whai i te wā me te wāhi o te tangata, mēnā e mahi tonu ēnei. Whakaritea te horoi niho, te pēhi niho me te tauera ki te wāhi e tatari ai ia. Whakamārama i tāu e mahi ana i mua i te tīmatanga, katahi tuku i tētahi tohu ngāwari i te wā kotahi. “Hopua te hepa horoi” he māmā ki te whai i tētahi tohutohu roa.

Hoatu ki te tangata he wā ki te mahi i ia wāhanga. Mēnā ka taea e ia te horoi ake, tukuna ia. Mēnā he tohu anake te hiahia, tatari i mua i te tuku āwhina anō. He pai pea ki ētahi te noho ki te tahoa horoi, te whakamahi i te hepa he hīpoki ka taea te pupuri pai. Pātai ki te tohunga niho mō ngā kōwhiringa mēnā he uaua te whakamahi i te hepa mōhio.

Kaua e huri te horoi ki te whakamātautau, ki te tauwhainga. Mēnā ka kī kāo te tangata, ka kumea atu tōna mangai, ka pōuri, whakatārewa. Ngana anō ā muri ake, panoni te tūnga, ā, tono tohutohu ki te tohunga niho. Kaua e pēhi i te hepa ki roto i te mangai o te tangata, kaua hoki e pupuri i a ia kia oti te mahi.

Mēnā kua uaua tētahi tikanga i pai ai i mua, titiro ki te wāhanga e uaua ana. Pea kāore te tangata e mārama he aha te mahi ki te hepa, kāore e pai ki tētahi pēhi hou, kua ngenge pea, he mamae pea i te mangai. He whakaaro anake ēnei, ehara i te whakatau. He pai te tuhi poto mō te wā e raru ai, hei āwhina i te tohunga niho ki te kōrero mō ngā mahi e whai ake nei. Panoni tētahi wāhanga anake i te wā kotahi, kia mārama ai he aha ka pai ki te tangata.

## Me pēhea e āwhina ai i te wā e tiaki ana i te tūmataiti?

Pātai i mua i te āwhina, ahakoa kua hiahia āwhina kē te tangata i mua. Kōrerohia he aha tāu e whakaaro ana ki te mahi, ka tatari ki tana whakautu. Mēnā ka whakaae ia, katia te tatau, whakamahia he tauera hei tiaki i ngā kākahu, whakaarakia te wāhi e hiahiatia anake. Whakaaetia ko wai te kaiāwhina e pai ana ki a ia.

Mēnā me āwhina te tangata ki te mahi ā-ringa, whakamāramatia ia wāhanga i mua i te mahi, ā, whakamutua mēnā ka whakaatu ia i te pōuri. Ka taea e tētahi tohunga niho te whakaatu i tētahi tikanga haumaru mō ngā hiahia o te tangata. Kaua e ngana ki te tohutohu ringa me te kore whakangungu mai i tētahi kaimahi mātanga.

## He aha ngā mea me mōhio mō ngā niho hanga?

Pupuri ngā niho hanga ki te wāhi mōhio o te tangata, kua tohua mārama. Pātai ki te tohunga niho me pēhea te horoi, te pupuri, me mēnā me tirotiro te whakanoho ina pōuri pea te tangata. Mēnā ka whakamahia ngā niho hanga i tētahi wāhi tiaki, tohua te kete rokohanga kia mōhio ai ngā kaimahi.

Kaua e pēhi ngā niho hanga ki te mangai o te tangata mēnā ka whakakahore ia, mēnā ka kite koe he mamae kei a ia. Ko ngā niho hanga kāore e noho pai ana, he uaua pea te kai; pātai ki te tohunga niho he aha te mahi.

Mēnā he wā tika ki te tangata kia kai i te kai i tētahi wā noho, kaua e tino tohe ki ngā niho hanga i ngā wā katoa. Whakamāramatia te take ki te whānau, ki te kaitiaki, ā, kia mārama ai ki ngā tikanga kai o taua tangata.

## Me pēhea e whakarite ai mō te toro niho?

Whakaritea te toro i te wā e okioki ana te tangata. Ina whakaae ia, kōrerohia ki te tari niho i mua, me tono wā roa ake pea te tangata, he whakamārama mārie, he āwhina rānei i tētahi e whakawhirinaki ana ia. Kawea he rārangi rongoā o nāianei me tētahi tuhinga poto mō ngā mea kua uaua, pēnei i te horoi, te ngau, te whakamau i ngā niho hanga.

Tonoa te tīma niho kia whakamārama mai i ia wāhanga o ngā mea ka tupu. Ka āwhina tētahi hoa mōhio ki te tangata kia noho pai ai ia, engari me noho tonu te tangata ki te kōrerorero, ki ngā whiriwhiri. Mēnā he uaua te haere ki te tari tūturu, pātai ki te tohunga niho mō ngā kōwhiringa toro wātea i tō takiwā.

## Me pēhea e pupuri ai i te tikanga taurite e ngā kaiāwhina kē?

Mēnā he mema whānau, he kaimahi manaaki kāinga, he kaimahi whare manaaki e āwhina ana, whakaaetia ki te tangata he tikanga e pai ana ki a rātou. Whakamahia te wāhi mōhio me te tohu poto ina taea. Pātai mēnā he pai ki a ia tētahi tangata motuhake ki te āwhina ia wā.

Mēnā ka whakaae te tangata, tukuna anake ngā mōhiotanga ka āwhina i te kaiāwhina e whai ake nei: te wā pai, ko ēhea mea ka taea e ia te mahi, ko ēhea wāhanga e uaua ana, āhea ia e hiahia ai kia mutu te āwhina. Pupuri i tētahi tuhinga motuhake, whai i te tukanga o te wāhi tiaki. Ko tētahi tikanga tuhi he aratohu, ehara i te whakaaetanga kia haere tonu ina kī kāo te tangata. Ka panoni pea ngā hiahia, nō reira pātai anō, urutau. Mēnā kua uaua ngā ara maha, whakamaramatia ki te tīma niho tā koe i kite ai, ā, tonoa he tohutohu mō taua tangata.

## He aha ngā tohu hei kōrero ki te tohunga niho?

He uaua te whakamārama i te mamae o te mangai. Ko te kumea i te kanohi, te kāo ki te horoi, ngā panoni i te kai, te moe whakararu, he tohu pea enei, engari kāore he taunakitanga he raru niho. Kōrerohia ki te tohunga niho he aha tāu i kite ai, he aha te wā i tīmata ai. Ka titiro te tohunga niho mō te mamae, ngā raruraru kiko, te pirau o te niho, me ngā niho hanga kāore e noho pai ana.

Mēnā he raru hou te ngau, te horomi, whakapā ki tō kaimahi hauora, ki tō tohunga niho rānei. Kaua e panoni i te momo kai, kaua e whakamahi i ngā rongoā waha, kaua hoki e ngana ki ngā tikanga horomi mai i tētahi tuhinga whānui. Rapua āwhina tere mō te mamae nui, te whakaheke toto, te pokenga, me tētahi atu raru hauora tere tonu.

Ko te whāinga he tikanga e whai wāhi ai te tangata, me te āwhina iti rawa e hiahiatia ana. He whakamaharatanga mōhio, he tūnga noho pai, he whakamātau anō i tētahi wā kē pea he pai. Mēnā ka pūmau tonu te raru, pātai ki te tohunga niho he tautoko e tika ana mō te tangata.

Ko te [aratohu kai](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) e whakaatu ana i te whakarite kai mārie me te wā e tika ai te tohutohu ngaio mō ngā raruraru kai. Mēnā kei te raru ngā niho hanga ki te kai, whakamahia taua aratohu hei tīmatanga.
