Ko te taraiwa he wāhanga o te tuakiri, o te mana motuhake me te tikanga o ia rā o te tangata. Ko te kōrero mō ngā panoni anamata he uaua mō te katoa. Mā te tīmata moata e nui ake ai te wā ki te whakarongo, ki te whakatau, ki te whakamātau i ētahi atu huarahi haere. Ehara tēnei whārangi i te aromatawai taraiwa, i te whakatau mēnā he haumaru tētahi tangata takitahi ki te taraiwa.

Ko te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Association mō te mate wareware me te taraiwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving), te [tohutohu taraiwa a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/driving-and-dementia) me te [aratohu NHTSA mō ngā kaitiaki](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/older-drivers) e tohutohu ana kia whakamahere moata, kia whakauru te tangata. Ka āwhina te rata, te kaimahi whakaora taraiwa, me te tari raihana ā-rohe ki ngā pātai takitahi.

## Āhea me tīmata te kōrerorero?

Tīmatahia i mua i tētahi huihuinga taraiwa e whakamanahia ai te whakatau ohotata, mēnā ka taea. Kōwhiria he wā mārie, mēnā kāore te tangata e whakareri ana kia haere, kua hoki mai rānei i tētahi haerenga whakararu. Akene he tuatahi anake tēnei o ētahi kōrerorero maha, kāore he pānui kotahi.

Pātai he aha te tikanga o te taraiwa ki te tangata. Akene he huarahi ki ngā hoa, ki te hoko kai, ki te āwhina i te whānau, ki te mahi, ki te mana motuhake. Ko te mōhio ki taua tikanga kāore e whakatau i te take haumaru. Engari e whakaatu ana kua mārama koe ki te mea ka ngaro, ā, he take te mahere.

Whakauruhia te tangata ki ngā whiringa haerenga anamata i a ia e hiahia ana ki te whai wāhi. Akene ka pai ki a ia tētahi tangata motuhake ki te taraiwa, he huarahi mōhio, he wā o te rā. Tuhia ngā kōwhiringa e pai ana ki a ia, whakamātauria i mua i tētahi rā whakararu, mēnā e whakaae ana ia.

## Me pēhea e whakarewa ai i tētahi manukanuka kāore he tautohetohe?

Kōrero mō tētahi take motuhake, kaua mō te whakatau whānui. Hei tauira, whakamārama i te ngaro i tētahi huarahi mōhio, te warewareta i tētahi tohu tauārai, tētahi pākaru hou ki te waka. Kōrero he aha tāu i kite ai, ā, pātai he aha tana maumahara, he aha tōna whakaaro. Kaua e kī he taraiwa kino ia, kaua hoki e whakamātau i tōna maumahara mā ngā pātai.

Whakarongo ki te tirohanga o te tangata. Akene kua kite ia i te pōrearea, i te whakarerekē, i te ngenge, i tētahi raru kē rānei. Akene kāore ia e whakaae ki tō manukanuka. Kaua e whakaaro he kōrerorero kotahi he tohu kua mārama, kāore rānei i mārama ki te tūraru. Kia whakarangatira te reo, hoki anō ki te kaupapa ā muri mēnā he pōuri tētahi o koutou.

Ka kī a Dementia Australia kia poto, kia whakahoki-hoki ngā kōrerorero, kia arotahi ki ngā kaha o te tangata me ngā huarahi haere pai. Ka kī anō ia ko te huna i ngā kī, ko te whakakore i te waka, ka pai pea te āhua whakakino i te wā whakamahere moata. Mēnā he manukanuka haumaru tino nui, mahia he mahi hei ārai i te haerenga tūraru e whai ake nei, ā, rapua he arataki ngaio tere.

## He aha ngā mōhiotanga ka āwhina i te kōrerorero ngaio?

Tuhia ngā hūnga pūmau, ā, te wā i tupu ai. Hei tauira, te pōrearea i tētahi kōkonga, te ngaro i tētahi huarahi mōhio ki tētahi wāhi, te warewareta i ngā tohu, te pākaru hou ki te waka. Tāpirihia he aha tā te tangata i kōrero mō te haerenga, ngā panoni hauora, ngā rongoā rānei e hiahia ana ia ki te kōrero.

Waiho ngā tuhinga pono, matatapu. Kaua e hopu muna, e whai, e whakamātau rānei i te tangata. Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, ka taea e tētahi o te whānau i noho i te waka te tuku i tāna i kite ai. He mārama te whakamārama pūmau ka hoatu ki te rata, ki te tohunga tētahi mea motuhake hei kōrero; kāore e whakatau i te take, kāore hoki e whakatau i te aromatawai taraiwa.

Ko ngā tohu tūraru i roto i te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving) he take hei rapu i te tohutohu ngaio. Kāore ēnei e whakakapi i te aromatawai takitahi. Pātai ki te rata mēnā ka taea he tuku ki tētahi kaimahi whakaora mahi, ki tētahi tohunga whakaora taraiwa kē rānei. Tirohia ngā ture raihana ā-rohe ki te tari raihana ā-kāwanatanga, ā-rohe rānei.

## Me pēhea e whakamahere ai i te haerenga tahi?

Tīmatahia ki ngā haerenga e pai ana kia mau tonu te tangata: te toro ki ngā hoa, te hoko kai, ngā rata, te karakia, ngā mahi tūturu. Mō ia haerenga, whakaaetia ko wai ka taraiwa, he huarahi e pai ana, he āwhina e māmā ai te haerenga.

Ko ngā kōwhiringa ka taea pea he waka mai i te whānau, i ngā hoa, he takihi, he tono waka, he waka tūmatanui, he waka hapori, he tuku mā ētahi hokonga. He rerekē te wātea, te utu, me te urutanga ki ia wāhi. Tirohia he aha kei te wātea ā-rohe, kaua e whakaaro ka mahi tētahi kōwhiringa mā te katoa.

Whakamātauria tētahi huarahi, tētahi ratonga tahi i mua i te whakaaro he kōwhiringa anake. Pātai mēnā e pirangi ana te tangata ki tētahi hoa, mēnā he hāpai ia i a ia anō. Ko tā mātou [aratohu whakamahere haerenga](/caregiving/traveling-with-dementia/) he whakaaro mō te mau tonu i ngā haerenga mārama, me te wāhi mō te whakarerekē i ngā mahere.

## He aha mēnā ka whakaaro koe he uaua te haerenga e whai ake nei?

Whakaarohia te tino tūraru, whakaarahia tētahi atu huarahi haere i a koe e rapu ana i te tohutohu tere. Mēnā ka pōrearea ohorere te tangata, ka kī te [NHS me tono āwhina hauora i taua wā tonu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/); whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata ā-rohe. Kaua e whakaaro he mahi noa te panoni ohorere i te tangata he tautohetohe taraiwa noa.

Mō ētahi atu manukanuka nui, whakapā ki te rata o te tangata me te tari raihana ā-rohe mō ngā mahi e tika ana i tōna wāhi noho. Kaua e whakawhirinaki ki tētahi kupu whakaū a te whānau, ki tēnei whārangi hei whakatau i ngā ture ā-ture. Mēnā e taea, whakamāramatia te mahere tere ki te tangata, ā, whakaurua tonu ia ki ngā mea e whai ake nei.

## Me pēhea e tautoko ai i te tangata mēnā kua mutu te taraiwa?

He tino mea ngaro te whakamutu taraiwa. Whakaaetia tērā mārama. Kia haere tonu te karanga ki te tangata ki ngā wāhi me ngā mahi e whai take ana ki a ia. Hoatu he kōwhiringa mō ngā waka, ngā wā me ngā hoa, kaua e whakahaere i te haerenga hei mahi māna.

Tohatohea ngā kawenga taraiwa ki ngā whanaunga, ki ngā hoa mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata. Pātai ki ngā whakahaere hapori mō ngā waka mō ngā pakeke, mō ngā tāngata hauātanga. Tirohia te utu, te wā, ngā ture tiki, me mēnā ka whakaaetia he hoa. Ko ngā kōrero iti ka whakatau mēnā he pai ki a koe tētahi kōwhiringa.

Ko te [aratohu manaaki i a koe anō](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) e uhi ana i te tono i ētahi atu kia tohatoha i te tautoko. Me whai te tangata i te nui o te mana ki te whakaaro he pēhea tōna noho hononga ki te oranga ā-rā.

Ko te whāinga kia tiakina te haumarutanga, me te whakaute i te tikanga o te taraiwa ki te tangata. Tīmatahia te kōrerorero moata, whakarongo, whiwhi i te arataki ngaio, ā, hanga he mahere haerenga tahi.
