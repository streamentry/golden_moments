Ina kāore tētahi e mate wareware ana e mōhio ana ki a koe, he pōuri, he raruraru te wheako. Ka whakaaro pea koe he aha te kōrero, me whakamārama anō rānei te whanaungatanga. He rerekē ia āhuatanga. Me tīmata ki te mea e āwhina ana te tangata kia haumaru, kia mārie, kaua ki te whakamātau i tana maumahara.

Ko te [aratohu mō te mahara ngaro me te pōhēhē a Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/memory-loss-confusion) e whai wāhi ana ki te kore e mōhio ki te whānau hei panonitanga ka pā pea. Ko te [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) me te [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/how-dementia-changes-perception) e whakamārama ana ka pōhēhē pea te tangata ki tētahi tangata kē i tētahi tangata e mōhiotia ana e ia. He aratohu whānui tēnei; kāore e taea e tēnei te whakamārama he aha te panonitanga o tēnei.

## He aha pea e tupu ana ina kāore te tangata e mōhio ana ki ahau?

Ka pāngia e te mate wareware te maumahara me te āhua o te whakamāori a te tangata i ana e kite ana. Ka pōhēhē pea ia ki tētahi whanaunga kē, ka kore rānei e mārama he aha tō whanaungatanga. Ehara tēnei i te whakaaturanga tino tika o tana whakaaro ki a koe. Arohia tana mārietanga me tana urupare i taua wā.

Ka pāngia hoki te mōhio e te rauemi kāore e kitea pai, te raruraru kanohi, te raruraru rongo, te mate, te pānga o ngā rongoā, te wāhi rerekē, te panonitanga o te hōtaka rānei. He take pea ēnei, kāore he tohu hei whakatau i te mate. Mēnā he hou te panonitanga, mēnā kei te pōuri tonu te tangata, kōrerohia tōna tākuta he aha i tupu, ā, pātai mēnā me tirohia ia.

Mēnā he pōuri, he kāhoa te rūma, whakarerekētia kotahi te mea: whakahuatia he rama, whakaitia te haruru o muri, neke rānei ki tētahi wāhi e mōhiotia ana. Pātai mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki ana karaihe, ki ana taputapu rongo mēnā he whakamahinga tāna. Ka āwhina pea ēnei mahi, engari kāore e whakamārama katoa i ia wā o te kore e mōhio.

## He aha te kōrero ka taea i te wā tonu?

Whakatata atu ki te wāhi e kite ai te tangata i a koe, kōrero mārie, me te reo e mōhiotia ana. Whakamōhio i tō ingoa, i tō whanaungatanga māmā, pēnei i tō whakanui i a ia i tētahi rā kē. Ka taka tētahi wā, titiro ki tana urupare.

- **Mēnā he ngākau marino ia,** haere tonu ki tētahi kōrero noa. Kōrero mō te mea e tupu ana i tēnei wā, pātai rānei mēnā e hiahia ana ia ki te noho tahi.
- **Mēnā he whakaaro ia,** hoatu he whakamārie poto: “Kei te ora koe. Kei konei ahau me koe.” Hoatu he wā ki a ia hei whakautu.
- **Mēnā he wehi ia, mēnā ka tono ia kia wehe koe,** whakamuri, hoatu he wāhi. Kaua e pēhi he kōmuri, kaua e tohe kia mōhio ia, kaua e whakahoki i ngā kōrero e pōrearea ana ki a ia.
- **Mēnā ka karanga ia i tētahi atu ingoa ki a koe,** ka taea e koe te whakamōhio i tō ingoa kotahi anake, kaua e hurihia hei whakatika. Mēnā kei te mārie tonu ia, neke ki tētahi kaupapa, mahi rānei e pai ana ki a ia.

Ko te [aratohu whakawhitiwhiti kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) e whakahau ana kia whakamahia ngā kupu poto, mārie, kia kaua e pātai, e tautohetohe. Kia kaua e pātai, “Kei te maumahara koe ko wai au?” Ka pēhia pea te pātai, ā, kāore pea e āwhina i te tangata kia mōhio ki a koe.

## Ka taea e tētahi tohu mōhio te āwhina?

Ina tohu tētahi tohu, ka āwhina pea i te tangata kia mōhio ki a koe, engari kāore e pai mō te katoa. Whakamātauria tētahi tohu marino i te wā kotahi, ā, whakamutua mēnā ka pēhi.

- **Kīia tō ingoa ngāwari.** He reo mōhio, he whakamōhiotanga poto pea te mea anake e hiahiatia ana.
- **Whakamahia he whakaahua, he taonga tahi.** Mēnā he pai ki te tangata ngā whakaahua, ka taea e koutou te titiro tahi ki tētahi, kaua e tono kia ingoatia e ia tētahi tangata. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mahara](/activities/reminiscence/) he whakaaro mō te whakamahi i ngā whakaahua kaua e pātai.
- **Whakamātauria tētahi mahi noa tahi.** He waiata, he inu, he mahi e mōhiotia ana pea ka māmā ai te wā. Whai i te urupare o te tangata; he pai te huri i te kaupapa, te whakaoti rānei i te toro.
- **Whakaiti te raru huri noa i a koe.** Tirohia kei te marama te rūma, kei a ia āna karaihe, ana taputapu rongo mēnā he whakamahinga tāna. Ka āwhina pea tētahi wāhi mārie mēnā he maha ngā tāngata e kōrero ana.

Kaua e tāpiri tonu i ngā tohu mēnā kei te pōhēhē tonu te tangata. He nui rawa te whakamārama roa, te whakaaturanga whakaahua rānei hei whakapūtaiao i a ia. Ahakoa, he pai te noho mārie ki a ia; ka pai hoki te tango i tētahi wā ki te whakamātau anō i tētahi atu wā.

## Me pēhea e māmā ai ngā toro?

Mēnā e noho ana te tangata i tētahi whare manaaki, mēnā he maha ngā kaiāwhina e tata ana ki a ia, pātai ki ngā tāngata e mōhio ana ki tana hōtaka he aha te wā e māmā ai ia. Ka taea te toro i te wā mārie, te haere tahi me tētahi e mōhio ana ia, te mauria mai rānei i tētahi taonga e mōhiotia ana. Ko ēnei he kōwhiringa hei whakaaro, ehara i te hōtaka me whai e ia whānau katoa.

I mua i te toro, whakaarohia he aha ka māmā ki a koe anō. Ka taea te whakarite he mahi poto kotahi, te mauria mai he pukaemi whakaahua, te noho tahi anō rānei. Mēnā kāore te tangata e mōhio ana ki a koe, ka taea tonu e koe te whakautu ki ana hiahia i taua wā. Kaua e whakawhirinaki te toro ki te kī mai o tōna ingoa.

He mea tika te pōuri, te whakahāwea, te pōharatanga. Kōrero ki tētahi e whakapono ana koe, ki tētahi kaitohutohu, ki tētahi rōpū tautoko kaitiaki mēnā e taimaha ana te wheako ki a koe. Ko te [aratohu hauora-whaiaro kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) he whakaaro mō te tono āwhina a ētahi atu.

## Ina hea ka tono āwhina hauora?

Mēnā ko te kore e mōhio ki tētahi he hōtaka hou, he nui te tupu, he raru rānei, kōrerohia ki te tākuta o te tangata. Tuhituhia te wā e tupu ai, me he aha kē i tupu i taua wā, pēnei i te panonitanga o te hauora, te moe, te rama, te rongo, ngā rongoā, te taiao. Kaua e tārewa, e panoni rānei i ngā rongoā i runga i tētahi tuhinga whānui.

Ko te pōhēhē ohorere, te panonitanga ohorere o te whanonga, o te hauora me tono āwhina ohotata hauora. Ko te [aratohu NHS mō te pōhēhē ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora i taua wā tonu. Whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata, ngā ratonga ohotata-iti rānei o te rohe e noho ana te tangata.

Ko te kore e mōhiotia e tētahi he mea uaua mō ngā tāngata e rua. Whakamāmāa ō kupu, hoatu he tohu ina āwhina anake, ā, waiho kia arahina e te mārietanga o te tangata te whakatau.
