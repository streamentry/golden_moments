Ka mamae ngā tāngata e noho ana me te mate wareware, ahakoa kāore e taea e rātou te whakamārama mārie. Ka whakamahi pea te tangata i ētahi kupu kē, i ngā kīnaki, i ngā panoni whanonga hei whakaatu he raru tōna. Ka kite, ka kōrero mai hoki te kaitiaki i ngā panoni, engari kaua e whakatau te take mā tōna anake.

Ko te [aratohu mamae a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/pain) e tohutohu ana kia pātai tika, kia whai i te aromātai a te takuta ina whakaarohia he mamae. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e kī ana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger) he pea te mamae tinana ki ngā panoni whanonga. He aratohu whānui tēnei whārangi, ehara i te aromātai mamae, ehara hoki i te mahere maimoatanga.

## Ka mamae te tangata ahakoa kāore e kōrero?

Ae. He mamae tonu ahakoa kāore e kitea e te tangata ngā kupu mō tāna mamae. Akene kāore ia e mārama ki te pātai mō tētahi wāhanga tinana motuhake, he uaua pea ki a ia te kī mai āhea, pēhea rānei e mamae ai. Kaua e whakaaro he marino te tangata ina noho puku.

Pātai i tētahi wā mārie, mā ngā kupu e mōhio ana ia. “Kei te mamae koe?” “He mamae tēnei?” he māmā ake pea i tētahi whakamārama roa. Ka mōhio pea ētahi ki “mamae”, “kāore e pai”, “ngangau”. Hoatu he wā ki te whakautu, ā, whakaaetia tana whakautu kaua e whakamātau, kaua e whakatika.

Pātai i tētahi pātai ngāwari, ka tatari. Ka tohu pea te tangata, ka whakaatu mā tētahi kupu mōhio, kāore e kī i te wāhanga tinana. Kaua e pātai tere, kaua e aratakina ki te kī ae. Mēnā ka kī kāo ia engari kua kite koe i tētahi panoni, whakaatu te panoni ki tētahi kaimahi hauora, kaua e tautohe mēnā kei te mamae.

## He aha ngā panoni hei tirotiro?

Titiro ki ngā panoni mai i te tikanga noa o te tangata i te kanohi, i te nekeneke, i ngā mahi ia rā. Akene ka kōkiri ia, ka pōhēhē, ka tangi, ka tiaki i tētahi wāhanga tinana, ka nekeneke iti ake, ka moe rerekē, ka iti ake tana hiahia ki te kai. Ka tino noho puku pea ia, ka pukuriri, ka kore e whakatā rānei.

Kāore tētahi o ēnei tohu e whakaatu he mamae kei te tangata. He maha ngā take. Ko te whakakahore i te kai he hononga pea ki te mamae o te mangai, engari he whakamārama anō pea tōna. Whakaatuhia ā-kōrero ki te takuta, ki te nēehi, ā, waiho kia aromātai rātou i te take.

## Me pēhea e whakamārama ai i ā koe i kite ai?

Tuhia he mōhiotanga māmā hei āwhina i te whakamārama i te panoni. Tuhia he aha i tupu, nō hea i tīmata ai, he aha te auau o tōna kitenga, he aha te mea e tupu ana i taua wā. Hei tauira, whakahuatia mēnā ka kōkiri te tangata i a ia e tū ana, ka karo i tētahi mahi tawhito, ka iti ake tana kai i te tikanga noa. Whakaurua ngā kupu me ngā kīnaki o te tangata anō.

Whakatairitea te panoni ki te tikanga noa o te tangata, ehara i tētahi atu tangata. He tangata noho puku pea ia kāore e hamama ina mamae; he tangata anō pea ka whakaatu i te pōuri mā te nekeneke, mā te kōrero nui. Kaua e hanga he tauine mamae, kaua e whakatau he kotahi te take o te whanonga. Ko te whāinga kia whai kōrero whai hua ai te kaimahi hauora.

Mēnā he whakaaetanga tā te tangata, tukuna ngā tuhinga ki te whānau, ki te nēehi, ki te takuta e tautoko ana i a ia. Kia noho pūmau te mōhiotanga, tukuna anake ngā mea e āwhina ai te tangata kia whai tiaki. Ko te [aratohu toro hōhipera](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) he kōrero anō mō te tuku i ngā whiringa kōrero ki ngā kaimahi hōhipera.

## Me pēhea mēnā ka whakaaro koe kei te mamae pea te tangata?

Whakapā ki tētahi kaimahi hauora, whakamāramatia he aha te panoni. He maha ngā take o te mamae, tae atu ki ngā whara onamata, te arthritis, ngā raru niho, te pirau, te kōrenga o te puku, te pēhanga mai i te noho i tētahi tūnga. Ka aromātai te kaimahi hauora i te hauora o te tangata, ka whakatau he aromātai, he maimoatanga e tika ana.

Kaua e tīmata i tētahi rongoā mamae, kaua e panoni i te rahi, kaua e whakamutu tētahi atu rongoā i runga i tētahi paetukutuku. He whakararu pea ētahi rongoā, he kino pea mō te tangata me ētahi āhuatanga hauora motuhake. Whai i te mahere tiaki o te tangata, pātai ki te kaituku tohutohu, ki te tohunga rongoā ina rerekē tētahi mea.

Ka taea hoki te whakaiti i te pēhanga tere i a koe e rapu tohutohu ana. Pātai he aha ka mārie ake ki a ia, tukuna te tangata kia okioki, kaua e tohe ki tētahi nekeneke, ki tētahi mahi e mamae ana. Whakapā ki tētahi kaimahi hauora i mua i te whakamahi i te wera, te mātao, te mirimiri, i tētahi maimoatanga kē mēnā kāore koe e mōhio ki tōna haumarutanga.

## Ka pā mai te panoni whanonga i tētahi atu take?

Ae. Ko te mamae tētahi take pea o te pōhēhē, o te whakararu, engari ka pā mai pea ngā panoni i te ngenge, i te taiao poauau, i te hiakai, i ngā pānga rongoā, i tētahi atu raru hauora. Titiro ki te mea i tupu i mua i te panoni, mēnā ka hoki mai, kaua e ngana ki te whakatau te take hauora mā koe anake.

Mēnā kua pōuri ohorere te tangata, kua rerekē rānei i tōna āhua noa, kōrero ki tētahi kaimahi hauora i taua wā tonu. Ko te [NHS e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora tere mō te pōuri ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata, ngā tukanga āwhina ohotata o tō rohe.

Mēnā he māhoe, he hoki mai te panoni, whakaritea he toro takuta, kawea ō tuhinga. Ko te [aratohu mō te whakarite toro takuta](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) he tikanga māmā hei rārangi pātai, hei whakamārama i ngā panoni.

## Me pēhea e whai whakaaro pai ai ngā kaiāwhina?

Kōrerohia te tangata he aha tāu e mahi ana i mua i te āwhina. Pātai whakaaetanga i mua i te pā ki a ia, i te nuku i tētahi kākahu. Hoatu tētahi kōwhiringa iti, pēnei i te okioki i tēnei wā, i te noho i tētahi wāhi kē, ā, hoatu he wā mō te whakautu. Mēnā ka kī kāo ia, whakatārewa, pātai ki tētahi kaimahi hauora mō tētahi huarahi kē.

Pātai ki te tangata he kupu, he momo āwhina e pai ana ki a ia. Me tōna whakaaetanga, tukuna he mōhiotanga whai hua ki ētahi atu kaiāwhina e whakawhirinaki ana, kia kaua ai ia e whakamārama i te mea anō i ngā wā maha. Kaua e kōrero mō ngā tāngata me te mea kāore rātou i konei.

He uaua ki te kite i te mamae mai i waho. Ko te pātai mārie, te mātakitaki i te panoni, te tono tohutohu ngaio ka āwhina i te tangata kia rongo ai ki a ia, kaua e whakaaro he tohu tēnei he whakatau.
