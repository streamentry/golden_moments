Ka tupu pea te riri hei tārukuruku, hei werohanga, hei patunga rānei, ā, he mea whakawehi tēnei mō te katoa e tata ana. He pea ehara i te mahi whakaaro. Ehara i te tohu e whakaaro ana te tangata ki te whara i tētahi. Me whakarite tuatahi te haumaru o te wāhi, katahi ka titiro ki ngā hiahia o te tangata.

## He aha taku mahi ina ka panoni te riri ki te pūkaha?

Whakatārewa i te mahi, whakamuri, kōrero i tētahi rongo poto, mārie. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e tohutohu ana kia aro ki ngā kare-a-roto, kia kōrero māhorahora, kia whakaiti i ngā whakararu](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Mēnā kei te raru tata koe, kei te whara rānei tētahi, neke ki tētahi wāhi haumaru, whakapā atu ki ngā ratonga ohotata o tō rohe.

- **Whakamutua te mahi e tupu ana mēnā ka taea.** Mēnā ko te horoi, te whakakākahu, te toro, te mahi kē rānei e pōrearea ana te tangata, whakatārewa. Ka taea te waiho mō muri mai, engari mēnā he raru haumaru, hauora rānei e tino hiahiatia ana.
- **Hoatu he wāhi.** Whakamuri, waiho he ara mārama mō tēnei ki tēnei kia wehe. Kaua e rārangi, e aukati, e huihui tata, e pā ohorere ki te tangata.
- **Whakaiti tō reo, whakapoto ō kupu.** Hei tauira, “Kei te kite ahau kua pōrearea koe. Ka hoatu e ahau he wāhi mōu.” Kaua e tautohetohe, e whakawehi, e tono whakamārama.
- **Neke atu ētahi atu.** Tētahi tamaiti, tētahi manuhiri, tētahi āwhina rānei ka haere ki tētahi atu rūma i te wā e mārie ai te tangata. He pai atu te kōrero a tētahi tangata mārie i te kōrero a te maha e ngana ana ki te āwhina.
- **Wehe mēnā he pāhuatanga.** Haere ki tētahi wāhi haumaru, tono āwhina i tētahi atu. Kaua e ngana ki te herea te tangata.

Ko te [Alzheimer's Society e tohutohu ana kia whakamuri, kia kaua e herea, kia kaua e pā ohorere, kia wehe ki te rapu āwhina mēnā he pāhuatanga](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e tohutohu ana kia tono whakaaetanga, kia whakaiti i te whakaoho, kia kaua e kōpiki, e herea te tangata](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Whakamahia te nama ohotata o tō rohe ina tata te raru; kaua e tatari kia mutu te pūkahatanga mēnā he mea ka whara pea tētahi.

## He aha ngā take e pā ana ki te whanonga?

He nui pea ngā take, ā, kāore e taea e te whānau te tohu i te take. Ko te Alzheimer's Association e whakaatu ana i te rawaka tinana, te pēhanga taiao, me ngā raruraru whakawhitiwhiti kōrero hei take pea.

Whakaarohia he aha i tupu i mua tata atu i te panonitanga:

- I ngenge rānei te tangata, i matekai, i matewai, i ngēhi rawa rānei, i pōhēhē rānei?
- He uaua, he tere rawa, he rerekē rānei tētahi mahi? I haere tonu rānei tētahi i muri i te kitenga o te pōrearea?
- He haruru kaha rānei, he tangata maha e kōrero ana, he pōharatanga rānei, he wāhi rerekē rānei?
- I raru rānei te tangata ki te kite, ki te rongo i te tupu?
- I panoni rānei tētahi rongoā, tētahi tikanga, tētahi kaitiaki, tētahi āhuatanga hauora i tata ake nei?

Ko te [Alzheimer's Society e tohutohu ana kia rapu tauira, kia whakaaro ki te āhua o te wheako ki te tirohanga o te tangata](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Ko ēnei pātai he āwhina mō te titiro, ehara i te taunakitanga he take kotahi i whakatupu i te whanonga. Kaua e tirotiro, e herea, e whakamārie rongoā te tangata mā koe anō.

## Me pēhea e iti ai te tūpono o tētahi pūkahatanga anō?

Whakamahia ngā mea i kitea e koe hei panoni i te wā whakawhitiwhiti kōrero e whai ake nei. Ka āwhina pea tēnei, engari kāore e taea te whakapūmau kāore anō he pūkaha. Whakamātauria tētahi panonitanga kotahi i te wā, kia māmā ai te mōhio mēnā kei te mārie ake te tangata.

- **Kōwhiria he wā mārie ake.** Mēnā he kino te nuinga o te wā o tētahi mahi ina ngenge te tangata, pātai mēnā he pai ake tētahi wā kē.
- **Whakamāmāa te taiao.** Whakakorea te haruru o muri, whakaitia te maha o ngā tāngata i te rūma, kohia ngā mea e hiahiatia ana i mua i te timatanga.
- **Whakamāramatia tētahi mahi, ka tatari.** Haere mai mai i mua, kōrero he aha tāu e mahi ana, pātai mēnā e pai ana ki te tangata te āwhina, ā, hoatu he wā hei whakautu.
- **Whakamutu ina kitea te pōrearea.** Ko te whakakahore, te pehi atu, he kanohi pukuriri, he reo nui ake rānei he tohu hei whakatārewa. Kaua e whakaaro he nui ake te mahi o te whakaoti i te mahi i te oranga o te tangata.
- **Whakaritea he mahere māmā.** Mēnā he maha ngā kaitiaki, whakaaetia ngā tohu e kī ana “whakatārewa”, he kupu mārie hei whakamahi, ā, ko wai te tangata hei whakapā mēnā ka panoni te tauira.

Mō ngā mahi takitahi, tirohia ō mātou aratohu mō te [horoi](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) me te [whakakākahu](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/). Ka whakaatu te [aratohu whakawhitiwhiti kōrero](/caregiving/communication-tips/) i ngā ara o te kōrero me te whakarongo kāore e whakanuku ana.

## He aha taku mahi i muri i te mārietanga o te wā?

Whakamārietia, whakahoki ki tētahi mahi māmā, kāore e uaua ana, mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata. Kaua e whakahē, e whiu, e whakaatu hara. Ko te Alzheimer's Society e kī ana ka pōuri tonu pea te tangata i muri i te pūkahatanga, ahakoa kāore ia e maumahara ki te tupu.

Mēnā e hiahia ana koe ki te mārama ki tētahi tauira, tuhituhia he tuhipoka poto, motuhake e pā ana ki te wā i tupu ai, he aha tāu i kite ai, he roa pēhea, me he aha i āwhina. Kia pono te tuhipoka, tukuna ki te rōpū tiaki o te tangata mēnā e tika ana. Kaua e hopu i te tangata, kaua e tuku i ngā kōrero whaiaro kāore he whakaaetanga.

Mēnā he kino te mahi ki te tangata i te nuinga o te wā, whakarerekētia te wā, te huarahi rānei, tonoa rānei tētahi atu tangata whakapono hei āwhina. Ka taea te whakaae ki te kore, ā, ngana anō ā muri ake nei. Ko te [aratohu mō te mea ina kī mai te tangata kāore](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) e whai ana i ētahi atu huarahi hei whakatārewa kāore e pēhi ana.

## Ina pēhea te whakapā ki tētahi tākuta?

Whakapā ki te tākuta o te tangata mō te pūkaha hou, ohorere, whakaarotau, kino haere rānei. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e tohutohu ana kia tirohia e te tākuta ngā panonitanga whanonga, otirā ina ohorere mai](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Me arotake pea te mamae, te mate, te moe, te taiao, me ngā rongoā. Ka aromatawai te kaimahi hauora i te āhuatanga o te tangata.

Rapu āwhina ohotata ā-hauora mēnā ka ohorere te tangata ka pōhēhē, ka whara, ka rite ki te mate tata. Mēnā kei te raru tata koe, tētahi atu rānei, whakapā atu ki ngā ratonga ohotata o tō rohe. Kaua e tatari kia kohia ngā tuhipoka, kia mōhiotia ai te take i mua.

Ko tō haumaru hoki he mea nui. Kīia ki tētahi whānau, tētahi kaimahi hauora, tētahi ratonga tautoko mate wareware ā-rohe mēnā ka pūkahatia ki te wā roa, mēnā kei te wehi koe. Tonoa he mahere hei tiaki i te tangata me ngā tāngata e noho tata ana. He tika tō tono āwhina; ehara i te mea me mahi anake koe.
