Ko te kimi āwhina ā-kāinga ka tīmata mai i te tangata, ehara i te rārangi ratonga. Pātai he aha tāna e hiahia ana kia pupuri tonu ia mā tōna ake ringa, ā, he momo āwhina e whakaae ana ia. Ka tuku pea te kaiwhakarato i te hoa kōrero, i ngā mahi kāinga, i te tiaki hauora pakari. He rerekē te whakaritenga e tika ana i runga i te tangata, ngā mahi, me te tautoko e wātea ana.

Ko te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Association mō te manaaki ā-kāinga](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/in-home-care) me te [tirohanga a Dementia Australia mō te manaaki ā-kāinga](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/home-care) e whakamārama ana i ngā momo ratonga rerekē. Ko te [rārangi kaiwhakarato a te Association](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-in-home-care-professional-ts.pdf) e tohutohu ana kia pātai mō te whakangungu, te wheako, ngā tohu tuku, te tautoko whakahoki me te utu. He rerekē ngā ratonga, ngā tohu me ngā pūtea i ia rohe; kāore tēnei whārangi e whakamana i tētahi kaiwhakarato, e whakamārama i te āheinga rānei.

## He aha te āwhina e hiahia ana te tangata?

Tīmatahia ki ngā mahi e whai take ana i te rā o te tangata. Akene he tangata hei kōrero, he hoa mō ngā hokonga, mō te whakarite kai, ā, mō te horoi, te whakakākahu rānei. Ko te tiaki hauora pakari, pēnei i te whakaora ā-tapuhi, ā-tinana rānei, he momo ratonga rerekē e hiahia pea he kaimahi raihana, he tuku mai i tētahi kaimahi hauora.

Tuhia he āwhina e hiahiatia ana, i te wā e whai hua ai, ā, he aha te mea e pai ana ki te tangata. Whakauruhia ngā mahi e taea e ia te mahi mā tōna ake, me te wā e hiahia ai ia ki te mūnga. Mā tēnei e arotahi ai te ratonga ki te tautoko i te motuhake, kaua ki te whakahaere i te rā katoa.

Mēnā he whakaaro nui kē tōna, tīmatahia mā tētahi hiahia ngāwari, mā tētahi toro poto rānei mēnā he wātea. Pātai he pēhea tōna noho i muri. Ka hiahia pea ia ki te whakarerekē i te mahi, i te wā, i te tangata e āwhina ana. Ka whakarerekēhia te mahere tautoko ina panoni ngā hiahia me ngā mariu.

## He aha ngā mea me pātai ki te kaiwhakarato i mua i te whakamahi?

Waea ki ētahi kaiwhakarato, whakamārama i ngā mahi e hiahia ana te tangata ki te tautoko. Pātai mēnā ka taea e rātou te tutuki i ērā hiahia, he aha ō rātou wā wātea, mēnā kua manaaki rātou i tētahi tangata e mate wareware ana. Pātai he aha te whakangungu mate wareware a ō rātou kaimahi, me pēhea te tautoko i te tangata kua pōrearea, kua manukanuka, kua ngenge rānei.

Whakamārama i ngā taipitopito e pā ana ki te oranga o ia rā: ko wai ka tae mai, he aha te maha o ngā toro, he aha te roa o ia toro, me he aha ka mahia mēnā ka mate te kaimahi, ko wai hei karanga mō te manukanuka. Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te kaimahi ōna anō i ngā wā katoa, pātai mēnā ka taea e te ratonga te tautoko i tērā.

Tonoa ngā tohu tuku me te āhua o te kaiwhakarato e tirotiro ana i ngā kaimahi. Tirohia mēnā ka tirohia te whakangungu, ngā tohu me ngā ture mahi. He rerekē ngā ture me ngā ingoa mahi i ia wāhi, nō reira whakamaua ngā herenga ngaio, raihana rānei i tō rohe.

## Me pēhea e tirotiro ai mēnā e rite ana te tangata me te kaiwhakarato?

Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, whakaritea he hui i te kāinga i mua i te toro tuatahi. Tirohia mēnā ka kōrero tōtika te kaiwhakarato ki te tangata, ka whakarongo ki āna hiahia, ka hoatu wā ki a ia ki te whakautu. Pātai ki te tangata he pēhea tōna whakaaro ki te hui, i tētahi āhua e taea ai e ia te whakautu ngākau mārie.

Ko te Alzheimer's Association e tohutohu ana kia whai tētahi tangata pono kē i te uiuinga, kia kōrero ai koe i ōu whakaaro i muri. Whakamahia te hui hei kōrero mō tētahi toro tūturu: te taenga, te mihi, te mahi kua whakaritea, me pēhea e taea ai e te tangata te tono kia mutu, kia okioki rānei.

Mēnā e taea, whakaritea he toro tuatahi poto, ā, whakawhitiwhiti kōrero ki te tangata. He pai pea te kaiwhakarato i te pepa, engari kāore pea he hoa tino pai mō te tangata. Whakaute i te urupare o te tangata, kōrerohia he whiringa kē mēnā he awangawanga kei tētahi o koutou.

## He aha ngā kōrero hei tuku ki te kaiāwhina?

Tukua ngā taipitopito anake e āwhina ai te kaiwhakarato ki te tautoko i te tangata kia haumaru, kia whakaute. Me te whakaaetanga a te tangata, whakamāramatia he pēhea tāna e pai ai kia kīia ai tōna ingoa, ngā mahi e tika ana ki a ia, ngā mea e āwhina ai ia ki te whakawhiti kōrero, me ngā mahi e hiahia ana ia ki te mahi mā tōna ake.

Mēnā he tohutohu hauora e pā ana ki te mahi, pātai ki te rata o te tangata he aha te mea e tika kia mōhio ai te kaiwhakarato. Kaua e tono i tētahi kaimahi kāore he raihana ki te mahi hauora kei waho i tōna whakangungu, i tōna tūranga rānei. Pupuri i ngā mōhiotanga whaiaro, tirohia ko wai e taea ana te uru ki ngā tuhinga kōrero, ngā tuhinga tiaki rānei.

Ko te [aratohu mō te haere ki te rata](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ka āwhina ki te whakarite pātai mō te tangata hauora. Ko te [aratohu haumarutanga kāinga](/caregiving/home-safety/) e uhi ana i ngā panoni o te kāinga me te wā e tika ai te pātai ki te kaimahi ngaio.

## Me pēhea e arotake ai te whakaritenga i te wā?

Tirohia te tangata i muri i ngā toro. Pātai he aha te mea i pai ai, he aha tāna i pai ai, he aha tāna e whakarerekē ai. Mēnā he iti āna kōrero, mātakitaki mēnā e manaaki ana ia i te kaiāwhina, mēnā he ngākau mārie tōna, mēnā e hiahia ana ia kia haere tonu te toro. Ko ēnei kitenga ehara i te whika; whakamahia hei ārahi i tētahi kōrerorero.

Whakaaetia me te kaiwhakarato me pēhea te tuku i ngā kōrero auau, me pēhea te whakahaere i ngā awangawanga. Mēnā ka whakakorea tētahi toro, mōhio ko wai ka tuku tautoko whakahoki, he aha rānei tā te tangata e hiahia ai ki te mahi hei utu. Arotahia ngā mahi i te wā ka panoni te mahi kāinga o te tangata, tana hauora, ōna hiahia tautoko.

He rerekē ngā utu, ngā wā me ngā ratonga e wātea ana. Pātai ki te kaiwhakarato mō ngā tuhinga tuhi, tae atu ngā utu haora, te wā iti o te toro, ngā ture whakakore, me ngā utu anō. Ka taea e te rata o te tangata, ngā ratonga pakeke ā-rohe, ngā whakahaere mate wareware te tohutohu ki ngā wāhi hei titiro, engari whakatauritea ngā kaiwhakarato mā tōu ake whakaaro, mā te tangata.

Mēnā ka pōrearea ohorere te tangata, ka nui ake te ngoikore, kaua e whakaaro he mate wareware te take. Ko te [NHS e kī ana me tono āwhina hauora ohotata mō te pōrearea ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Mō ngā manukanuka tiaki haere tonu, kōrero ki te rata, ki tētahi kaimahi ngaio kē rānei.

Ko te whāinga he tautoko e pai ana ki te oranga o te tangata. Whakamāramatia te mahi, tirohia te wheako o te kaiwhakarato me tana mahere tuarua, whakaurua te tangata ki ia whakatau, ā, arotahia he pēhea tōna noho i te wā.
