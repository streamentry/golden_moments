Ka wareware pea te tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware ki te inu, ka kore pea e mōhio ki te matewai. He inu e kore e kitea, e uaua rānei te puri, ka ngāwari te warewaretia. Ko ngā panoni iti ki te tikanga o ia rā ka māmā ake te inu, ā, ka mau tonu te mana whakahaere o te tangata.

Ko te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Association mō te kai me te kai](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) e tohutohu ana kia hoatu he ipu iti wai i ngā wā katoa o te rā, me ngā kai he wai kei roto. Ko te [tohutohu kai a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/nutrition-and-dementia) e kī ana he inu whānau, he māmā te urupare. Ko tēnei whārangi he whakaaro whānui, ehara i te whāinga wai ā-rā mō tētahi tangata takitahi.

## Me pēhea e kitea ai, e tika ai te inu?

Whakanohoia he inu e pai ana te tangata ki te wāhi e kitea ai, e tata ai ki a ia. Whakamahia he ipu, he pēke rānei e whānau ana ki te pupuri. Mēnā he pai ki a ia te mahana, te reka, te ipu rānei, kia mahara ki taua mea. He māmā pea te kōwhiringa kotahi i te tono i a ia kia kōwhiri i ngā momo inu maha.

Hoatu inu hei wāhanga o ngā tikanga e mōhio ana te tangata, pēnei i te kai, te okiokinga, te haerenga ki waho. He māmā ake te ipu iti i te karaihe nui. Hoatu anō i muri, kaua e waiho hei whakamātautau, hei whakamahara i kua warewarehia e ia.

Mēnā ka taea e te tangata te hoatu, te kawe i te inu mā tōna ake ringa, waiho kia mahi. Mēnā e hiahia ana ia ki te āwhina, pātai i mua i te hoatu, i te whakanoho i te ipu ki tōna ringa. He rerekē te ipu hou, te hono inu, te wāhi hou; panoni kotahi mea i te wā, ā, pupuri te mea e pai ana ki a ia.

Mō te haerenga, kawea he inu e pai ana ki te tangata, hoatu ā te okiokinga, te kai rānei. Mēnā he maha ngā kaiāwhina, whakaaetia me tana inu me tana ipu pai. Waiho te inu kia tata, engari pātai i mua i te nuku, i te hoatu ki a ia.

## He aha ngā inu, kai hei hoatu?

Tīmatahia ki ngā inu e pai ana ki a ia, e tika ana mō ia. Ko te wai tētahi kōwhiringa; ko te miraka, te hupa, ngā smoothie, ētahi atu inu whānau, ka taea pea te whakauru ki ōna mariu me tana mahere tiaki. He wai kei roto i ētahi huarākau me ētahi atu kai. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e whakarārangi ana i te huarākau, te hupa, ngā miraka me ngā smoothie](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) hei kōwhiringa he nui te wai.

Kaua e herea te tangata ki tētahi inu mēnā ka whakakahore ia. Hoatu tētahi atu kōwhiringa whānau, whakamātau i muri. Pātai ki te rata, ki te kaimahi kai i mua i te whakarerekē i tāna mahere kai, inu, ina whai tohutohu hauora ia mō ngā wai.

## Me whakaiti inu hei whakaiti i ngā haerenga ki te wharepaku?

Kaua e whakaiti wai hei ārai aitua, mēnā kāore he mahere takitahi kua hoatu e te kaimahi hauora. Ko te [kōrero a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence) e whakatūpato ana ka taea e te whakaiti wai te arahi ki te whakaheke wai me ētahi atu raru. Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te āwhina ki te kimi, ki te whakamahi i te wharepaku, he pai pea he momo tautoko rerekē. Ko tā mātou [aratohu whakahaere mimi](/caregiving/toileting-and-incontinence-with-dementia/) e uhi ana i ngā tikanga wharepaku me te matatapu.

Whakamaheretia ngā tono i te rā, kaua e tono kia inu nui tahi. Whai i ngā tohutohu hauora o nāianei, pātai ki te rata he aha te nui e tika ana mō te tangata. Kaua e whakawhirinaki ki tētahi tau whānui i te ipurangi; he rerekē ngā hiahia wai, he tangata e whai mahere wai hauora.

## He aha ngā panoni e tirohia?

Mātakitakina ngā rerekētanga mai i te tikanga o te tangata. Akene he iti rawa tana inu i tōna auau, kāore e kai ana i tana inu pai, he nui ake te āwhina e hiahia ana ia i mua. He waha maroke, te wirinaki, te ngenge ohorere, ngā panoni o te mimi, he take pea hei tirotiro me tētahi kaimahi hauora. He ake pea ēnei tohu, nō reira kaua koe e tohu i te matewai mā tōu ake whakaaro.

Tuhia he aha te mea i kite ai koe, he aha te wā. Tuhia mēnā kua mate te tangata, kei te wera he wāhi, mēnā he uaua te pupuri, te horomi rānei i te inu. Ko te [Alzheimer's Association e kī ana he uaua te kite i ngā tohu matewai i te tangata e mate wareware ana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster). Mēnā he panoni ohorere, mēnā kei te manukanuka koe, whakapā ki te rata, ki tētahi kaimahi hauora kē rānei.

## He aha mēnā kua uaua te inu?

Mēnā ka pawa, ka kōhi, ka pupuri kai, inu rānei i tana waha, mēnā he raru hou ki te horomi, whakamutua, pātai ki tētahi kaimahi hauora pono mō te tohutohu. Kaua e whakarerekē i te mātotoru o te inu, i te momo kai, kaua e whakamātau i ngā tikanga horomi, mēnā kāore he kaimahi ngaio kua tohutohu i taua mahere mō te tangata. Ko tā mātou [aratohu kai](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) e uhi ana i ngā mahi mō ngā raru nganga me ngā raru horomi hou.

Mēnā he waha maroke, he niho mamae, he niho hanga e kore e mārie ana te tangata, pātai ki a ia he aha tāna e mōhio ana, ā, whakapā ki te tohunga niho, ki te rata rānei. Ka taea e te mamae waha te whakaraerae i te inu. Mō ngā āwhina mārie mō te tiaki waha, tirohia tā mātou [aratohu tiaki waha](/caregiving/oral-care-with-dementia/).

Ko te pōrearea ohorere me te āwhina hauora ohotata tonu. Ko te [aratohu NHS mō te pōrearea ohorere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) e kī ana kia kaua te tangata e tohu tōna mate mā ōna ake whakaaro. Whakamahia ngā ratonga ohotata ā-rohe, te tukanga tiaki ohotata rānei i tō rohe.

## Me pēhea e hoatu ai i ngā inu me te kore e akiaki?

Hoatu te tono, ā, hoatu ki te tangata he wā ki te whakatau. Ka hiahia pea ia ki te inu ā muri, ki te kōwhiri i tētahi ipu kē. Kaua e whakatika, e whawhai, e akiaki i te inu. Mēnā he maha ngā wā ka whakakahore ia, tirohia mēnā he uaua te tiki i te inu, mēnā he pai ki a ia te reka, te mahana, ā, mēnā e mahana ana te tangata i taua wāhi.

Pātai ki te tangata he aha te mea e āwhina ai, ā, whakaaetia kia tohaina ngā kaupapa pai ki ētahi atu kaiāwhina mēnā ka whakaaetia e ia. He ipu whānau, he wāhi kua whiriwhiria, he wā mārino pea ka māmā ake te inu. Mēnā ka roa tonu te raru, pātai ki te rata, ki te kaimahi kai mō ngā tohutohu motuhake mō te tangata.

Ko te whāinga kia wātea ngā inu i tētahi āhua e whakaaetia ana e te tangata. Tono mārie, whakaarohia tōna mana, ā, whai tohutohu ngaio ina panoni ngā hiahia.
