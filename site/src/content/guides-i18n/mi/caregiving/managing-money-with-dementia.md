Ka whai pānga ngā whakataunga moni ki te motuhake o te tangata me tōna oranga o ia rā. Ina uaua tētahi mahi, tīmatahia ki taua mahi, kaua e whakaaro me tango e tētahi atu te katoa. Pātai ki te tangata he aha te āwhina e hiahia ana ia, ā, whakaurua ia.

Ko tēnei aratohu he kōrero whānui mā ngā kaipānui o te United States. He rerekē ngā kōwhiringa peeke me ngā ture ā-rohe. E whakaatu ana te [aratohu a te Alzheimer's Society mō te whakahaere pūtea](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) ngā huarahi ki te tautoko i te tangata me te tiaki i tōna tūranga. E whakamārama ana te [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) he rerekē te āwhina ā-āhua, te hoa whakapā pono, me te mana ture.

## Me pēhea e hoatu ai te āwhina kāore e tango ana i te mana?

Pātai ki te tangata he aha te mahi moni e hiahia ana ia kia āwhina. Akene he hiahia tāna kia arotake tahi i tētahi pire, kia whakarite i ngā pukapuka, kia maumahara i tētahi rā utu, kia tirotiro i tētahi tono kāore i tūmanakohia. He pai ki tētahi kia āwhina ki ētahi mahi, ā, kia mahi ia ki ētahi atu.

- **Pātai i mua i te mahi.** Kīa, “Kei te pai ki a koe te arotake i tēnei pire tahi?” Hoatu he wā ki te tangata kia whakatau.
- **Whakaarotia tētahi mahi.** Mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, arotakehia tahi te moni, te rā utu, ngā whiringa utu. Waiho kia mahia e ia ngā whiriwhiringa e hiahia ana ia ki te whiriwhi.
- **Hangaia tētahi rārangi māmā tahi.** He rārangi poto o ngā pire, ngā rā utu, ngā mahi kua whakaaetia ka āwhina pea kia kite tahi koutou i ngā mea kua oti.
- **Pātai ki te peeke mō āna whiringa.** Akene ka whai ia ngā huarahi hei utu, hei tirotiro mahi, ā, ka māmā ake. Pātai he aha ia whiringa e whakaae ana i mua i te whakarerekē i tētahi pūkete.
- **Haere tahi anō ā muri ake.** Ka rerekē pea ngā whakaaro o te tangata. Pupuria te āwhina ki tā rātou e hiahia ana, ki tā ia e hiahia ana i tēnei wā.

E kī te CFPB ko te āwhina ā-āhua he arotake pire, he whakahaere moni me tētahi atu i a ia e whakaae ana ki ngā utu. He rerekē tērā i te whakaae kia tango moni tētahi atu, kia utu rānei. Kaua e tuku kupu huna, PIN, ngā waehere kotahi-wā hei ara poto.

## He aha ngā mea ka whakaaetia e te hoa whakapā pono?

I te United States, ka taea e te ahuatanga hoa whakapā pono kia whakapā atu te whakahaere pūtea ki tētahi tangata i ētahi āhuatanga, pēnei i te māharahara ki tētahi tinihanga pea. E kī te CFPB ehara tēnei i te mea ka whiwhi taua tangata ki te pūkete, ki te whakaaetanga kia nekeneke moni. Akene kāore tēnei āhuatanga i ngā pūkete katoa, nō reira pātai ki te whakahaere he pēhea tāna mahi.

| Momo āwhina | Te tikanga |
|---|---|
| Arotake pire tahi | Ka tirotiro te tangata me te kaiāwhina i ngā pire tahi; ka whiriwhiri te tangata, ka whakaae hoki ki ngā utu. |
| Hoa whakapā pono | Ka taea e te whakahaere pūtea te whakapā atu ki te tangata kua ingoatia i ētahi āhuatanga. Ko te ingoa anake kāore e hoatu te urunga ki te pūkete. |
| Urunga pūkete, mana ture | He mana peeke, he tuhinga mana, he whakaritenga pēnei ka āhei ki ngā whakawhiti moni. Ko ōna mana me ōna herenga e whakawhirinaki ana ki te pūkete me te ture ā-rohe. |

I mua i te whakarerekē i te urunga pūkete, i te rangatiratanga o te pūkete, pātai ki te peeke, ki tētahi tohunga ture pono i te rohe kia whakamārama i ngā whiringa me ngā tūraru. Kāore e whakaatu te tohu anake i ngā mahi pūtea e taea e te tangata te whakahaere, i ngā whiriwhiringa e hiahia ana ia ki te whakatau. Kia mau tonu ia hei wāhanga o ngā whakataunga i te nui e hiahia ana ia. Mēnā e hiahia ana ia kia mahi tētahi tangata mōna, pātai me pēhea e whakarite ai i raro i te ture, kaua e whakamahi i te ingoa uru o tētahi atu.

## He aha ngā rerekētanga hei tirotiro?

Ko te pire kua ngaro, te hokonga ohorere rānei, he whakamāramatanga pea tōna. Ehara i te taunakitanga e kāore e taea e te tangata te whakahaere moni, kāore rānei e whakamahia ana e tētahi. Pātai he aha i pā, ā, mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata, tirohia tahi te tuhinga, te pire rānei e hāngai ana.

E whakarārangi ana te [CFPB i ngā tohu pea o te whakamahi kino pūtea](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), tae atu ki ngā moni me ngā taonga kua ngaro, ngā tango ohorere kāore i te auau, ngā pire kāore i utua, ngā tāngata hou kua tāpirihia ki ngā pūkete, me tētahi e whakaiti ana i ngā waea, ngā toro a te tangata. Kimihia ngā tauira me ngā rerekētanga mai i ngā mea e noho ana ki te tangata; kaua e whakaaro he tohu kotahi he taunakitanga.

Mēnā e kī ana te tangata kua ngaro ana moni, whakaarohia te kaupapa ki te mea. Ko tā mātou [aratohu mō te whakautu ki te whakapae](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) he huarahi hei tirotiro i tētahi āwangawanga me te kore whakakahore. Pātai ki te tangata ā-motuhake he aha tāna i kite ai, he aha tāna e hiahia ana kia puta ā muri ake. Karo i te whakapae ki tētahi whanaunga, ki tētahi kaiāwhina, ki tētahi kaimahi peeke i mua i te tirotiro i ngā pono. Mēnā e whakapae ana koe ka uru pea tētahi kaiāwhina pono, pātai ki tētahi tohunga motuhake, ki tētahi ratonga ā-rohe rānei mō te tohutohu.

## Me pēhea e whakaiti ai te mōrearea tinihanga?

Ka tinihanga pea ngā kaitinihanga e whakaatu ana he peeke, he tari kāwanatanga, he pakihi, he hoa, he whanaunga rānei. Ka kī pea he ohotata, ka pēhi ki te utu wawe. E whakaatu ana te [aratohu hangarau haumaru a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) me whakaroa, me tirotiro i te whakapā, me whai tautoko i mua i te tuku kōrero, i te moni rānei.

- **Whakaroa ina ohotata te karere.** Kaua e tuku moni, kaua e tuku kōrero pūkete i te wā e pēhia ana koe e te kaikaranga, e te karere.
- **Tirohia mā tētahi whakapā kua whakawhirinaki koe ki a ia.** Whakamutua te waea, waeatia te peeke ki te nama kei te kāri, kei te tuhinga rānei, kei te taupānga ā-umanga rānei. Kaua e whakamahi i te hono, i te nama i tukuna mai e te kaikaranga.
- **Kia noho huna ngā taipitopito haumaru.** Kaua e tuku kupu huna, ngā taipitopito pūkete katoa, ngā waehere whakamana ki te karere kāore i tūmanakohia. E kī te [FTC me mutu te waea kāore i tūmanakohia e tono ana i tētahi waehere, me whakapā ki te peeke mā tētahi nama e whakawhirinaki ana koe](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Whakaae ki te tautoko me te tangata.** Pātai ki wai ka pātai koe mēnā he tono ohorere. He mahi te kaupapa ā-peeke whakapā pono hei āwhina i te whakawhiti kōrero, engari kāore e whakaaetia ai ngā mahi.

## He aha tāku mahi mēnā kua tuku moni pea ki tētahi kaitinihanga?

Whakapā wawe ki te peeke, ki te kaikāri, ki te ratonga utu, ki te kamupene i whakamahia hei tuku moni. Whakamahia tētahi nama i runga i te kāri, i te tuhinga, ki te pae tukutuku, ki te taupānga ā-umanga rānei. Pātai mēnā ka taea e rātou te whakamutu, te whakahoki i te utu. E whakamārama ana te [aratohu whakaoranga a te FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) he rerekē ngā kōwhiringa i runga i te huarahi i tukuna ai te moni; kāore he whakarite ka whakahokia.

Tiakina ngā karere, ngā rēhita, ngā rā, ngā pānui pūkete. I te United States, ka taea te whakaripoti i te tinihanga ki te [FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Mēnā e whakapae ana koe kei te whakamāuiui tētahi tangata i tētahi kaumātua, e kī ana te [Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) me whakapā ki ngā Ratonga Tiaki Pakeke o te kāinga o te tangata. Mēnā he mōrearea tonu te tangata, whakapā ki ngā ratonga ohotata o tō rohe. Ki waho o te United States, whakamahia ngā ratonga tiaki kaihoko, tiaki haumaru ā-rohe.

Kōrerohia kāore he whakapātaritari. He whakaaro tinihanga kia whakapōkaitia te tangata. E whakaatu ana te [aratohu CFPB mō te āwhina tinihanga me te tinihanga pūtea](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) ngā kōwhiringa ripoti ā-US, me te tohutohu ki te whakamahi i ngā paetukutuku pono, ngā nama waea pono.

## Ko ēhea ngā āwhina mō tētahi whakataunga pūtea motuhake?

Pātai ki te peeke o te tangata he aha ngā taputapu me ngā mana e wātea ana. Mō tētahi tuhinga ture, mō te whakarerekē i te rangatiratanga o te pūkete, kōrero ki tētahi tohunga pono e mōhio ana ki ngā ture o te wāhi e noho ai te tangata. Mēnā kei te māharahara koe mō te tukinotanga pea, whakamahia tētahi ratonga tiaki ā-rohe. Kāore tēnei whārangi e whakatau he mana e tika ana mō tētahi, kāore rānei he tinihanga tētahi whakawhitinga moni.

Ka āwhina te [aratohu mō ngā mahi tuatahi i muri i te whakatau](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) ki te tangata me te whānau ki te whiriwhiri i tētahi pātai whakamahere hei whakatika. Ko te [aratohu tautohetohe whānau](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) e whakaatu ana i ngā huarahi ki te kōrero mō tētahi take kotahi i te wā.
