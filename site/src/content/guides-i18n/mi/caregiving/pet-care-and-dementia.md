Ka waia pea te kararehe ki te tikanga ia rā o te tangata, ki te whānau. Ko te whangai, te hīkoi, te noho tata noa atu ki te kararehe he mahi ka hiahia pea te tangata ki te pupuri tonu. Ka uaua pea ētahi mahi ina pā mai te mate wareware, engari ehara tērā i te tikanga me tango aunoa e tētahi atu.

Ko te [aratohu manaaki kararehe a te Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/how-plan-your-pet-care-after-diagnosed-dementia) e tohutohu ana kia whakarite wawe, kia whakamahi i ngā tikanga me ngā whakamahara. Ko te [aratohu mahi a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/activities-people-dementia) e whakauru ana i ngā kararehe ki ngā mahi he whai tikanga pea. Ko te whāinga ko te tautoko whai hua mō te tangata me te kararehe, ehara i te kī he whakaora te kararehe i te mate wareware.

## Me pēhea e māmā ai te tikanga manaaki kararehe?

Tīmatahia mā te pātai ki te tangata ko ēhea mahi e pai ana ki a ia, e hiahia ana hoki ki te pupuri. Akene he pai ki a tētahi te whangai i te ngeru, engari kāore e pai ki a ia te horoi i te pouaka ngeru; ko tētahi atu, he pai ki a ia te hīkoi i te kuri me tētahi mema o te whānau. Me whai i te mahere te tangata, te kararehe, me ngā āwhina e wātea ana.

- **Hono te manaaki ki tētahi tikanga kua mōhiotia.** Whakatakotoria te kai i muri i te parakuihi, tirohia te kōmete wai i te tīmata i te tī, kātahi hīkoi te kuri i muri i tētahi hōtaka reo irirangi e mōhiotia ana. Ka māmā pea tētahi tohu kua tūtaki kē ki te rā, i tētahi wā hou.
- **Pupuri ngā rauemi i tētahi wāhi mōhio.** Whakaputaina te kai, te here, te kōmiti o te kararehe ki tētahi wāhi e kitea ana e te tangata, engari whakaritea ngā taonga taumaha, ngā kemikara ki tētahi wāhi haumaru. Ka āwhina he tapanga, he tuhinga iti mēnā ka hiahia te tangata.
- **Whakaataahia tētahi whakamahara.** He tuhinga poto i te taha o ngā rauemi kararehe, he urupā maramataka pea te mea e rawe ana. Kaua e whakakī i te kāinga i ngā whakamahara ka whakaaro te tangata he tirohia ia, he whakatikangia ia.
- **Waiho he wā mō te mahi.** He roa pea te hīkoi, te whangai i tēnei wā i tērā wā. Kaua e wawe te tohe i te tangata mēnā e pai ana tana wā tahi me te kararehe.

Mēnā he maha ngā tāngata e tiaki ana i te kararehe, whakamahia he rārangi poto hei whakaatu ko wai e mahi ana i ēnei mahi i tēnei rā. Tiakina ki te wāhi rauemi, ki tētahi whakamahara e pai ana ki te tangata. Kaua e hanga he whakaoho, he tuhinga kē ina rawe tētahi tohu mārama.

## He aha ngā āwhina ka taea e te whānau, e ngā hoa?

Tukuna tētahi mahi motuhake, kāore he kupu whānui. Ka taea e tētahi hoa te tiki kai kararehe ia marama, ka taea e tētahi tata te hīkoi tahi, ka taea e tētahi mema o te whānau te haere ki te tohunga kararehe. Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata ki te whangai, ki te horoi i te kararehe, whakaritea te āwhina e pā ana ki taua tūranga, kaua e tango atu.

Whakaaetia ko wai ka āwhina mēnā kāore te kaitiaki matua i te kāinga, mēnā kua mate te tangata. Kōrerohia ki taua kaiāwhina ko ēhea ngā rauemi, he aha te tikanga e pai ana ki te tangata, ko wai te tangata hei whakapā mēnā he mate te kararehe. Me mōhio te kaiāwhina he pai ki a ia te kararehe, he pai hoki te kararehe ki a ia.

Mēnā he kaiāwhina tiaki hauora tō te tangata, pātai me pēhea e uru ai te manaaki kararehe ki ngā toro. Whakaaetanga me ngā tikanga o te kāinga he mea nui: kaua e whakaaro ka taea e te kaimahi utu te hīkoi, te whangai, te whakahaere i tētahi kararehe ko te wāhanga o tana mahi. Whakaaetia te mahi ki te tangata, ki te kaiwhakarato, ki te mema o te whānau e haepapa ana mō te kararehe.

## Me pēhea e mōhio ai mēnā he nui ake te tautoko e hiahiatia ana?

Titiro ki ngā mahi motuhake, kaua e whakawā i te tangata mā te taumata, mā te tatau. He pakaru kai e kore e kī, he hīkoi kua warea e pōuri ai te kararehe, he tangata e ngenge ana i muri i te tiaki i te kararehe, he tohu pea enei kei te hiahia tētahi mahi ki te tautoko nui atu. Ko te panoni i te whanonga, te hiakai, te āhua o te kararehe he take hei whakapā ki tana tohunga kararehe.

Kōrerohia ki te tangata he aha tāu i kite ai. Akene ka pai ki a ia he whakamahara, he hīkoi tahi, he kaiāwhina mō tētahi mahi, ā, ka mau tonu ki te nuinga o te tikanga. Mēnā he raru hauora, o te nekeneke, o te haumaru rānei e pā ana ki tōna kaha ki te tiaki i te kararehe, pātai ki tōna tīma tiaki, ki tētahi ratonga tautoko ā-rohe ki te whakarite kōwhiringa.

Kaua e tatari ki te raru ka whakaaro ki te tiaki ohotata. Ko te [aratohu noho takitahi me te mate wareware](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/) he huarahi ki te whakaae ki ngā tirotiro me te āwhina mārama, me te pupuri i ngā whiringa o te tangata hei matua.

## Me pēhea e whakamahere ai mō ngā panoni o āpōpō?

Pātai ki te tangata ko wai ka whakawhirinaki ia ki te tiaki i te kararehe mēnā kei te hōhipera ia, kei tawhiti i te kāinga, kāore e taea e ia tētahi mahi. Tuhia te tikanga noa o te kararehe, tana kai, ngā taipitopito o tana tohunga kararehe, me ngā tāngata kua whakaae ki te āwhina. Pupuri i ngā mōhiotanga ki tētahi wāhi e kitea ana e te tangata me ngā kaiāwhina pono, me te whakaaetanga a te tangata.

Ka panoni pea te mahere i te wā. Arotahia i ngā wā ka panoni te tikanga, te nekeneke, te tautoko o te tangata, mēnā ka panoni ngā hiahia o te kararehe. Mēnā me neke, whakaurua te tangata ki te whiriwhiri ko ēhea wāhi e noho ai te kararehe, me pēhea e pūmau ai tana hononga ki a rātou ina taea. Ko te whakarite wawe e whai wā ai ngā tāngata katoa ki te whakaaro mō ngā kōwhiringa e tiaki ana i ngā hiahia o te tangata me te manaaki o te kararehe.

## Ka aha mēnā kāore e pai te toro, te mahi ki te kararehe?

Waiho kia whakatakoto te tangata me te kararehe i te tere. Hoatu he wā tahi mēnā he pai ki a rāua. He poto te toro, he noho tata, he tirotiro i tētahi whakaahua o te kararehe pea he rawe. Whakamutua mēnā ka uaua te tangata, ka neke atu te kararehe, mēnā ka pēhi pea te kararehe. Ko ngā [kaupapa mahi](/activities/ideas/) o te pae e whakaatu ana i ētahi atu huarahi mōhio ki te tahatahi tahi.
