Ka rārangi pea te haere ki te hōhipera i ngā ritenga mōhio, ā, he uaua pea te mārama. Ka hiahia pea te tangata e noho ana me te mate wareware ki te wā roa ake, ki te whakamāramatanga, ki te āwhina ki ngā mahi o ia rā i a ia e noho ana i reira. Ko te whakarite i ngā mea hei tohatoha ki ngā kaimahi ka tautoko i te tangata, me ngā tāngata e āwhina ana ia.

E kī ana te [aratohu hōhipera me te whakaputanga a te Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/d5afc74a-9d22-4a15-9c30-6315f6eb5f35/alzheimers-dementia-hospitalization-discharge-planning-ts.pdf), me te [aratohu a Dementia Australia mō te haere ki te hōhipera](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/going-hospital), me whakarite he kōrero whai hua, he taonga mōhio, he pātai whai muri. Ko ngā tohutohu a ngā kaimahi hōhipera te tino whakaarotia i te aratohu whānui nei.

## Me pēhea e whakareri ai mō te haerenga hōhipera kua whakaritea?

Mēnā kua whakaritea te haerenga, pātai ki te tangata he aha tāna e mārama ai mō taua mea, he aha ngā kōrero e hiahia ana ia kia mōhio. Whakauruhia ia ki ngā whakataunga e hiahia ana ia. He pai ake ki ētahi kia rongo i te haerenga i mua tonu; he pai pea ki ētahi he whakamārama poto, mārie i tata ki te wā. Whaia te whiriwhiri a te tangata ina taea.

Pātai ki te hōhipera he aha ngā kōrero me ngā taonga hei kawe. He rārangi rongoā o nā tata nei, ngā mate whakapāhā mēnā e mōhio ana koe, me ngā ingoa me ngā nama o ngā kaimahi hauora pūmau, ka āwhina pea ki ngā kaimahi. Kawea he karu kōwae, he pūkoro taringa, he kākahu mōhio, he taonga horoi, me tētahi taonga mōhio mēnā ka whakaaetia e te hōhipera. Ko ngā tuhinga ture, ngā tuhinga whakatakoto mō te tiaki i mua, me kawe anake mēnā he whai take, ā, mēnā ka whiriwhiria e te tangata, e te kaiwhakatau mana whakaaetanga.

Whakaaetia ko wai ka haere tahi ki te tangata, me pēhea e tohaina ai ngā kōrero e te whānau me ngā kaiāwhina pono. Mēnā kāore e taea e tētahi te noho, pātai ki tētahi atu tangata pono kia tū i tētahi wā. Akene e hiahia ana te tangata kia whai hoa i ētahi wāhanga o te haerenga, kia noho mokemoke rānei i ētahi atu wāhanga.

## He aha ngā kōrero hei tohaina ki ngā kaimahi hōhipera?

Tohaina ngā kōrero e āwhina ai ngā kaimahi ki te whakawhiti kōrero, ki te tiaki i tēnei tangata. Me te whakaaetanga o te tangata, tuhia he tuhinga poto e whai ana i tōna ingoa e pai ai ia, i pēhea tana whakawhiti kōrero, he aha ngā mea e āwhina ai ia ki te mārama, me ngā ritenga, ngā taonga mōhio e whai take ana. Ka taea hoki te whakahua i te āhua o tana whakaatu i te mamae, i te whakapōuri, mēnā kāore ia e whakamahi kupu mō tērā.

Kia poto te tuhinga, kia tūmataiti hoki. Tohaina ki ngā mema o te rōpū tiaki e hiahia ana ki a ia. Pātai ki ngā kaimahi he pēhea te pai ki a rātou te whiwhi i ēnei kōrero, me te wāhi e puritia ai. Kaua e tuku kōrero whaiaro ki tētahi pae pānui tūmatanui, ki ngā manuhiri e kore e hiahia ki a rātou.

Me whai wāhi te tangata ki ngā kōrerorero ina taea. Pātai ki ngā kaimahi kia kōrero tōtika ki a ia, kia whakamahia ngā kupu māmā, kia hoatu he wā mō te whakautu. Mēnā me tāpiri tētahi kōrero e kore pea e maumahara ai te tangata, whakamāramatia he aha tāu i kite ai, kaua e arohaehae i te take.

## Me pēhea e āwhina ai i te haerenga ki te rūma ohotata?

Mēnā he tere, he kore i tūmanakohia te haerenga, whakaahuatia he aha i puta, he aha te wā i tīmata ai, ā, he aha i rerekē i te āhua o te tangata i ia wā. Akene ka pātai ngā kaimahi i te pātai kotahi neke atu i te wā kotahi. He tuhinga poto ka āwhina i a koe ki te kōrero i te kōrero ōrite ki ngā tāngata rerekē.

Whakamōhiotia ki ngā kaimahi he mate wareware te tangata, me he aha ngā tautoko e āwhina ai ia. Pātai ko wai te kaiwhakapā matua mō ngā kōrero hou. Mēnā kei te tatari te tangata, he reo mōhio, he taonga ngāwari hei titiro, ngā karu kōwae, ngā pūkoro taringa rānei ka āwhina pea, mēnā ka kī ngā kaimahi he haumaru ērā.

Whaia ngā tohutohu a te rōpū hauora. I mua i te āwhina ki te kai, te inu, te rongoā, te hīkoi, te tiaki tinana, tirohia ngā kaimahi he aha te mea haumaru i tērā wā. He rerekē pea ngā mahi e taea e te tangata mō tētahi wā nā ngā whakamātautanga, ngā maimoatanga hōhipera.

## He aha tāku mahi mēnā he rerekē wawe te tangata?

Kōrerohia wawe ki te tapuhi, ki te tākuta mēnā kua rerekē wawe te tangata, kua rerekē rawa atu i tōna āhua o ia rā. Kaua e whakaaro he mate wareware anake tēnei. E kī te [aratohu NHS mō te whakapōuri tere](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) me whai āwhina hauora tere mō te whakapōuri tere.

Tohaina te wā i tīmata ai te rerekētanga, me he aha tāu i kite ai. Akene ka whakaatu te tangata i te whakahē i ōna mahi kanohi, ōna nekeneke, ōna moe, ōna kai, ahakoa kāore e taea e ia te whakamārama. Tonoa te rōpū tiaki ki te aromatawai i te rerekētanga, kaua e whakaaro ki te whakatau mōna anake.

## Me pēhea e tautoko ai i te tangata i tana noho hōhipera?

Kia mau tonu ngā ritenga mōhio ina kī ngā kaimahi he haumaru. Kawea he whakaahua mōhio, he taonga whakamārie rānei, whakaitea te haruru ina taea, ā, whakamāramatia kotahi mahi i te wā. Waiho kia mahi te tangata i ngā mahi e taea ana e ia, e hiahia ana ia. Pātai ki ngā kaimahi i mua i te nuku i ngā taputapu, i te whakarerekē i te rūma, i te āwhina ki te kai, ki ngā mahi tiaki tinana.

Akene ka hiahia te tangata ki te āwhina ki te whakamahi i te pātene karanga, ki te kimi i te wharepaku, ki te mārama i tētahi kaupapa hou. Kōrerohia ki ngā kaimahi he āwhina e hiahia ana ia i te nuinga, ā, pātai he pēhea te tono āwhina i waenga i ngā toronga. Mēnā e hiahia ana te tangata kia noho tētahi ki a ia, pātai he aha ngā toronga, ngā noho pō ka whakaaetia e te hōhipera.

Ka ngaro pea te kaha o ngā kaitiaki i ngā noho hōhipera. Whakaritea tētahi atu tangata pono kia whakakapi i tētahi wā, mēnā e whakaae ana te tangata. Pātai ki te kaimahi whakapā hōhipera, ki te kaimahi hapori mō ngā tautoko mō te tangata me te whānau i te wā e haere tonu ana te noho.

## He aha ngā mea hei tirotiro i mua i te wehenga o te tangata?

Tonoa he tuhinga whakaputanga tuhi. Tirohia he mea whakamārama ana ngā toronga whai muri, ngā mahi tiaki, ngā rerekētanga rongoā, me te tangata hei whakapā mēnā ka raru. Mēnā he rerekē ngā tohutohu, pātai ki te tīma whakaputanga kia whakamāramatia i mua i te wehe.

Kōrerohia he tautoko e hiahiatia ki te kāinga. Whakaarohia ko wai ka āwhina ki ngā kai, te nuku, te horoi, ngā toronga, ngā taputapu hou, ngā ratonga kāinga hou rānei. Kia pono ki ngā mea ka taea e te whānau me ngā hoa. Pātai ki te kaiwhakahaere whakaputanga hōhipera mō ētahi atu ratonga ā-rohe mēnā he uaua te mahere ki te whakahaere.

I muri i te hokinga ki te kāinga, tukuna he wā ki te okioki, ki te hoki ki ngā ritenga mōhio. Tohaina te mahere tuhi ki ngā tāngata anake e whakaae ana te tangata kia whakaurua. Mēnā ka rerekē wawe te tangata, ka mate nui rānei, rapua āwhina ohotata hauora, kaua e tatari ki te toronga whai muri ā-ritenga.

Ehara i te mea ka kaha ai te haere mārie ki te hōhipera, otirā i te wā ohotata. Ko te whakarite, te whakawhiti kōrero mārama, me te mahere tuhi ka āwhina tonu ki te katoa ki te mōhio he aha ka whai ake.
