“Ko te tākuta te wā hea?” Tekau meneti i muri mai: “Ko te tākuta te wā hea?” Ko te rongo i te pātai ano, ano tonu, tētahi o ngā wāhanga tino ngenge o te tiaki i tētahi e noho ana me te mate wareware. He tino noa tēnei, karekau i te mea kia kī ai, ā, he huarahi mārie hei whakautu e māmā ake ai te rā mō koutou tahi.

## He aha te tangata e mate wareware ana e pātai ana i te pātai ano, ano tonu?

Te nuinga nā te mahara. Ka pā te mate wareware ki te mahara poto, nō reira he maha ngā wā karekau te tangata e maumahara kua pātai ia, karekau rānei e maumahara ki te whakautu. E whakamārama ana te [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/alzheimers-today-volume-17-number-3/navigating-difficult-behaviors/) i te pātai maha ake i te wā he mea noa, he mea e tūmanakohia ana.

He kare anō kei te pātai. E whakamārama ana te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) he pātai ano pea he māharahara, he tono āwhina, he māharahara, he kore whakamarumaru rānei, ā, he rapu mārie, mōhio tonu te tangata. “Āhea tātou haere ai?” pea te tikanga, “Kei te māharahara au mō te haere.” Ko te whakahoha, te wehi, te haruru me ngā rerekētanga o te tikanga ka whakaoho i tēnei, ā, ka tohu pea te pātai ano i tētahi hiahia, pēnei i te wharepaku ([Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/)).

## He aha taku kī?

Whakautu mārie, poto, me te mea he wā tuatahi.

- **Kia poto, kia ōrite te whakautu.** “Ka tae te tākuta i te rua karaka. Ka noho tahi au me koe.” He māmā ake te whakaaro ki te whakautu ōrite.
- **Whakautu ki te kare.** “Kei te whakaaro koe mō te tākuta. Ko tēnei ahiahi, mā au koe e kawe. Karekau he mea hei mahia e koe.”
- **Whakamahia te reo mahana me te pāwhiri ngāwari,** mēnā e pai ana ki a rātou te pāwhiri. E tohutohu ana te Alzheimer's Association kia noho mārie, kaua e whakamahi i te whaitake, i te tohe.
- **Hoatu te whakautu e rapua ana rātou,** ia wā e hiahiatia ana. E kī ana te Alzheimer's Association kia whakahoki i te whakautu ia wā e pātai ana rātou.

## He aha ngā mea me karo?

- **“Kua pātai koe i tērā.”** E tohutohu ana te [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) kia kaua e whakahua kua pātai noa iho rātou. Karekau e āwhina i a rātou ki te maumahara, ā, ka rite pea ki te whakahāwea.
- **Ngā whakamārama roa.** He nui ake ngā kupu, he nui ake ngā mea hei wareware.
- **Te hā, te whakahuri kanohi, te reo koi.** E tohutohu ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) kia manawanui, kia kaua e whakaatu te pōrearea. He uaua tērā; titiro ki te wāhanga whakatā kei raro.

## Ngā taputapu māmā e whakautu ana i te pātai mō koe

I te wā e taea ana e te tangata te pānui, he nui ake te āwhina o te whakautu tuhi ka kitea e ia, i te whakautu kōrero.

| Taputapu | Me pēhea te whakamahi |
| --- | --- |
| He pepa tuhi nui | “Tākuta i tēnei rā i te 2 i te ahiahi. Ka taraiwa a Mary.” Tāpiria ki te wāhi e tino titiro ai rātou |
| He papapaki | Te mahere o tēnei rā i ngā rārangi e toru, e whā; horoia, tuhia anō ia ata |
| He karaka nui me te rā me te rā o te marama | Ka āwhina ki te “He aha te rā?” me te “Kua tae anō te wā?” |
| He maramataka | Tohua te rā kua pahemo; porohitahia te toronga e whai ake nei |
| Ngā whakaahua mōhio | Ko tētahi whakaahua o te tangata e haere mai ana ka whakautu i te “Ko wai te manuhiri?” |

He ahu mai ēnei whakaaro i te [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) me te [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf). Tohua mārie te pepa (“Me titiro tahi tātou ki te papapaki”), kaua e kī “I tuhi au mōu.”

## Kōrero mō ngā take tata ki te wā

Ko te kōrero mō tētahi toronga, tētahi haerenga, ngā rā i mua ka huri pea ki ngā hāora o ngā pātai māharahara. E tūtohu ana te Family Caregiver Alliance me te Alzheimer's Los Angeles kia kōrero mō ngā mahere, otirā ngā mea e pehanga ana pēnei i ngā toronga tākuta, te horoi, i tata noa iho i te wā e tū ai. Muri iho, kīa mai he aha ka whai ake, kotahi hīkoi i te wā.

## Ngā mahi ka huri i te aro

Ā muri i tō whakautu, tō whakaae ki te kare, ka āwhina tētahi mahi mārie, harikoa, otirā mēnā ko te ngenge kei muri i ngā pātai. E tūtohu ia puna i runga ake nei kia hurihia atu ki tētahi mea e pai ana ki te tangata.

1. **Tētahi tākaro poto, māmā tahi.** Ka whakaatu tonu te [Ngā Takirua Whakaahua](/games/picture-pairs/) i ngā whakaahua, nō reira karekau he mea hei maumahara.
2. **He mahi mōhio.** Te pekepeke tauera, te whakarōpū tōkena, te whakarite i te tēpu. Ko [Whakarōpū Tahi](/games/sort-together/) he putanga i runga i te mata.
3. **Korero mō tētahi mea e aroha ana rātou.** Ka tuku ngā [Kāri Kōrero](/games/conversation-cards/) i ngā tohutohu tuwhera, karekau he whakautu tika.
4. **Waiata, hīkoi, kai iti rānei.** Ka whakarārangi te National Institute on Aging i ēnei hei huarahi māmā ki te huri atu.
5. **Tētahi wā puku.** Noho tahi, titiro ki waho i te matapihi, whakamātau rānei i tētahi wā [Te Hā Mārie](/games/calm-breathing/) tahi.

Mēnā ka hoki anō rātou ki te pātai, whakautu anō, mārie. He pai tērā.

## Rapua ngā tauira

E tūtohu ana te Alzheimer's Association kia kite mēnā ka puta ngā pātai i ētahi wā o te rā, i ētahi tāngata, i ētahi wāhi rānei. Mēnā ka tīmata tonu i mua i te taenga mai o ngā manuhiri, i te ahiahi rānei, ka taea te whakamahere i mua: he pepa mō te toronga, he mahi mōhio kua rite, he rūma klanga ake. Ka āwhina pea tā mātou aratohu mō [ngā mahi mārie i te ahiahi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) mēnā he uaua rawa ngā ahiahi.

## Tiakina tō whaiaro

He ngenge te whakautu i te pātai ano mō te rua tekau pea. Mēnā kei te ngaro tō manawanui, ā, he haumaru te tangata, tomo ki tētahi atu rūma mō ētahi meneti, hā, pērā i te tūtohu a [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf). E whakahau ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) i ngā kaitiaki kia tono āwhina, kia whakaaro ki te manaaki whakatā. He atu ngā whakaaro i tā mātou [aratohu tiaki whaiaro mō te kaitiaki](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).

## Ā hea ahau waea ai ki tētahi tākuta?

Waea tere atu ki tana tākuta mēnā ka ohorere te pātai, te pōhēhē, te huringa whanonga, mēnā ka tino kino i ngā hāora, i ngā rā rānei. E kī ana te [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) me whakamōhio tere ki tētahi tākuta ngā whanonga ohorere, tere rānei te huri, otirā i muri i tētahi mate, i te huringa rongoā. E whakamārama ana [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/delirium) ka pōrearea pea te mate, te wai iti, te mamae, te kore para i te pōhēhē tere, he pōhēhē ohorere, kino e hiahia ana ki te rongoā tere. Waea atu ki tō nama ohotata o tō takiwā mēnā he tangata kei te tūraru.
