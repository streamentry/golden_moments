Ulendo ukhoza kukhalabe wotheka ngati munthuyo akufuna kupita ndipo dongosololi likugwirizana ndi zosowa zake. Kuchezera achibale kwa kanthawi kungakhale kosavuta kuposa ulendo wodzaza ndi zochitika zambiri; ulendo wa tsiku limodzi ukhoza kukhala wabwino kuposa kugona kutali. Funsani zimene munthuyo akufuna pa ulendowo, kenako konzani dongosolo lomwe limalola kupuma kapena kusintha zinthu.

[Alzheimer's Association travel guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) ndi [Dementia Australia's travel advice](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) onse amatsindika kufunika kokonzekera mogwirizana ndi kutonthoza kwa munthuyo ndi zizolowezi zake. Upangiri uwu umapereka malingaliro wamba okonzekera, osati chiganizo chakuti ulendo ndi wotetezeka kwa munthu wina aliyense.

## Kodi tingadziwe bwanji ngati ulendo ndi woyenera kwa ife?

Kambiranani za ulendowo ndi munthuyo, pogwiritsa ntchito mawu odziwika komanso kufotokozera kosavuta za komwe mukupita ndi amene adzakhale komweko. Ngati sakufuna kupita, vomerezani zimenezo. M’malo mwake, mukhoza kukonza chakudya, kuyimba foni kapena kuchita zinazake zodziwika kunyumba.

Ganizirani zimene munthuyo amatha kuchita mosavuta panopa. Onani momwe amachitira zinthu zikasintha pa zizolowezi zake, phokoso, kuchuluka kwa anthu, mtunda woyenda, kudikira komanso zipinda zosadziwika. Ngati mbali zambiri za ulendowo zingaoneke kuti zingakhale zovuta, pangani ulendowo kukhala wosavuta kapena sankhani njira ina yokhalira limodzi. Dementia Australia imalangiza kufunsa dokotala mukakhala kuti simukudziwa ngati ulendowo ukugwirizana ndi thanzi kapena chithandizo chomwe munthuyo amafunikira.

Ngati munthuyo ali ndi vuto latsopano la thanzi, amafunikira thandizo pa mankhwala, kapena ali ndi zosowa za mayendedwe zomwe zingasinthe paulendo, funsani katswiri woyenera wa zaumoyo musanasungitse ulendo. Musasiye, musasinthe kapena musasunthire nthawi ya mankhwala omwe analembedwa popanda malangizo kuchokera kwa amene anawalemba kapena wogulitsa mankhwala.

## Kodi tingakonze ulendo wotani?

- **Sankhani malo odziwika ngati n’kotheka.** Malo amene munthuyo amawadziwa, kapena kucheza ndi anthu odziwika, kungaphatikizepo kusintha kochepa kusiyana ndi njira yatsopano ndi ndondomeko yodzaza ndi maulendo ambiri.
- **Sungani njira kukhala yosavuta.** Sankhani njira yokhala ndi kusintha kochepa ndipo pewani kulumikizana kothamanga. Onani komwe kuli zimbudzi, mipando ndi malo abata musananyamuke.
- **Yendani pa nthawi yabwino ya tsiku.** Konzani mogwirizana ndi mphamvu za munthuyo ndi momwe tsiku lake limayendera. Siyani nthawi ya zakudya, mankhwala, kupuma ndi kukonzekera.
- **Sungani ndondomeko kukhala yopepuka.** Chochitika chimodzi chachikulu chikhoza kukhala chokwanira. Pewani kusungitsa zochitika ola lililonse; siyani mpata wa kuchedwa kapena kubwerera msanga.
- **Funsani za chithandizo musanapite.** Makampani a ndege, masitima, mahotela ndi malo ena ali ndi ntchito ndi malamulo osiyanasiyana. Lumikizanani nawo msanga kuti mufunse za chithandizo pa mayendedwe, malo okhala, zimbudzi zofikira mosavuta komanso thandizo lopeza malo oyenera.

[Upangiri wa maholide abata](/activities/holidays/) uli ndi malingaliro a maulendo ndi misonkhano ya banja. Ngati mukukumana ndi achibale, [upangiri wathu wochezera](/activities/visiting/) ukufotokoza za kukambirana, maulendo aafupi komanso momwe mungachokere popanda kukakamiza.

## Kodi tiyenera kunyamula chiyani?

Sungani chikwama chaching’ono cha ulendo chokhala ndi zinthu zofunika zomwe zimakhala ndi munthu wodalirika, osati m’katundu wopita pansi pa ndege.

- **Mankhwala ndi zambiri za thanzi.** Nyamulani mankhwala omwe munthuyo amagwiritsa ntchito nthawi zonse ali m’zotengera zawo zoyambirira, okwanira ulendo womwe mwakonza komanso kuchedwa koyenera, ndiponso mndandanda wamakono wa mankhwala. Onani malamulo amakono a mayendedwe ndi komwe mukupita musanayende ndi mankhwala olembedwa ndi dokotala. Sungani mlingo ndi nthawi monga momwe zinalembedwera.
- **Chiphaso ndi anthu olumikizirana nawo.** Nyamulani chiphaso cha munthuyo, adilesi ya komwe mukukhala, anthu oti mulumikizane nawo pakagwa vuto komanso ndondomeko yolembedwa ya ulendo. Ngati kuli kothandiza ndipo mwagwirizana, phatikizani khadi lokhala ndi njira yolumikizirana ndi munthu wosamalira.
- **Zinthu zothandiza kumutonthoza.** Nyamulani magalasi, zida zothandizira kumva ndi mabatire ake kapena chochapira, zokhwasula-khwasula zodziwika, madzi, mapepala opukutira, chovala chopepuka ndi zovala zosinthira. Chinthu chodziwika kapena chithunzi chikhoza kuthandiza kuti chipinda chosadziwika chikhale chosavuta kuzolowera.
- **Makope a zambiri zofunika.** Sungani kope la pepala kapena la pa digito la matikiti, zosungitsa malo, inshuwaransi ndi zambiri zokhudza thanzi zomwe zikufunika. Gawani ndondomeko ya ulendo ndi munthu wodalirika amene sakuyenda nanu.

Kuchuluka kwa zomwe munganyamule kumadalira ulendowo. Sungani zambiri za munthuyo motetezeka ndipo mugawane ndi anthu omwe akufunika kuziwona kuti apereke chithandizo chokha.

## Kodi tingachite bwanji kuti tsiku loyenda likhale losavuta?

Uzani munthuyo zomwe zichitike pambuyo pake, chimodzi ndi chimodzi. Perekani nthawi yowonjezera yokonzekera, kulowa, kugwiritsa ntchito chimbudzi ndi kusuntha kuchokera pamalo ena kupita ena. Ngati dongosolo lasintha, fotokozani mosavuta zomwe zichitike kenako m’malo mobwereza ndondomeko yonse yaitali.

Khalani pamodzi m’malo odzaza kapena osadziwika. Ngati munthuyo watopa kapena wasokonezeka, imani pamalo abata, m’patseni madzi kapena chinachake chodziwika chochita, ndipo ganizirani limodzi ngati mupitiriza. Musasiye munthuyo yekha pamalo osadziwika kumene angasokonezeke kapena kuvutika.

Ngati mukukwera ndege kapena mukugwiritsa ntchito siteshoni yaikulu, funsani amene akukuthandizani za chithandizo chomwe chilipo komanso momwe mungachipemphe. Njira zimatha kusintha, choncho tsimikizirani zambiri mwachindunji ndi kampani ya ndege, siteshoni kapena malo musanayende. Musadalire mndandanda wakale pa malamulo amakono achitetezo kapena ofikira mosavuta.

## Kodi dongosolo lothandizira ndi lotani?

Gwirizanani pasadakhale zomwe mudzachite ngati munthuyo akufuna kuchoka, ulendo wachedwa kapena wina akufunika kupuma. Sankhani munthu mmodzi wolumikizirana naye amene angathandize pa foni. Sungani adilesi ya komwe mukukhala ndi njira yobwerera kuti zikhale zosavuta kuzipeza, ndipo dziwani komwe mungakhale pansi mwabata kapena kugwiritsa ntchito chimbudzi.

Ngati mukufunika kufupikitsa ulendowo, chitani zimenezo. Kuphonya chochitika china kapena kubwerera msanga si kulephera. Ngati ulendo wakhala wovuta, sankhani chochitika chabata mukafika kunyumba. [Upangiri wa kulankhulana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) uli ndi malingaliro a momwe mungalankhulire modekha komanso kupereka chidziwitso chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi.

## Kodi tiyenera kuuza anthu a ku hotelo kapena oyendetsa?

Ngati munthuyo wavomereza, uzani ogwira ntchito zomwe akufunika kudziwa zokha kuti apereke chithandizo chothandiza. Mwachitsanzo, mukhoza kupempha hotelo kuti izindikire chosowa cha mayendedwe kapena kupempha chipinda chofikira mosavuta. Ngati munthuyo sakufuna kuti matenda ake afotokozedwe, kambiranani njira ina yopemphera thandizo yomwe imateteza chinsinsi chake.

Pa ulendo wautali, sungani zakudya za munthuyo, nthawi yogona ndi yomwera mankhwala pafupi kwambiri ndi momwe zimakhalira nthawi zonse, malinga ndi momwe ndondomeko ilili. Konzani tsiku loyamba lopanda kuthamanga ndipo siyani nthawi yopumira mutabwerera. Zosankha zazing’ono zodziwika zingathandize kuti ulendowo ukhale wosavuta kuzolowera.
