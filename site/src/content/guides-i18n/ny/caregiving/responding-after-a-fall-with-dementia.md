Kugwa kungakhale kochititsa mantha kwa munthuyo komanso kwa aliyense amene ali pafupi. Khalani odekha ndipo ganizirani kwambiri za chitetezo cha panthawiyo. Musaganize kuti munthuyo sanavulale chifukwa sangathe kufotokoza zomwe zachitika kapena kunena pomwe akumva kupweteka.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuchita chiyani munthu atangogwa?

Yang'anani ngati munthuyo ali maso, akuoneka kuti wavulala kapena sakumva bwino, komanso ngati angayankhe monga momwe amachitira nthawi zonse. Musamuthamangitse kuyimirira kapena kuyesa kumunyamula nokha. [NHS imanena kuti thandizo ladzidzidzi limafunika ngati munthu angakhale wavulala mutu, msana, khosi kapena chiuno, wavulala kwambiri, kapena sangathe kudzuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/).

- **Imbani foni ku ntchito zothandizira mwadzidzidzi za kwanuko** ngati chimodzi mwa zizindikiro zazikuluzo chingakhalepo. Tsatirani malangizo a amene walandira kuyimbako.
- **Musungeni munthuyo momasuka komanso mofunda** pamene mukudikira thandizo. Musamusunthe ngati mukuganiza kuti wavulala kwambiri kapena simukutsimikiza ngati n’kotetezeka.
- **Khalani naye pafupi ngati mungathe.** Gwiritsani ntchito mawu achidule komanso odekha. Musamufunse mafunso ambiri okhudza zomwe zachitika kapena kumukakamiza kuti ayimirire.
- **Ngati munthuyo akumva kupweteka, wavulala kapena sakumva bwino koma palibe ngozi ya nthawi yomweyo,** funsani malangizo a zachipatala mwadzidzidzi a kwanuko kapena katswiri wa zaumoyo wake.

Musayese kunyamula kapena kukweza munthuyo nokha. Ngati pali ndondomeko yosamalira pa nkhani ya kugwa, itsatireni pokhapokha ngati ikugwirizana ndi zomwe zikuchitika panthawiyo; pezani thandizo ladzidzidzi ngati munthuyo angakhale wavulala kwambiri.

## Bwanji ngati munthuyo akuoneka kuti angathe kudzuka?

Musamuthamangitse munthuyo. Mufunseni mmene akumvera komanso ngati akufuna thandizo. Ngati akuoneka kuti sanavulale ndipo angathe kudzuka pogwiritsa ntchito chithandizo chimene amagwiritsa ntchito nthawi zonse popanda kunyamulidwa, mupatseni nthawi ndipo khalani pafupi. Lekani ngati akumva kupweteka, akumva chizungulire kapena sangathe kuchita zimenezo. Ngati simukutsimikiza, funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo m’malo moyesa kumuthandiza kusuntha m’njira imene simunaphunzitsidwe.

[NHS imalangiza kuti munthu atenge nthawi ndi kupuma atadzuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Malangizo ake odzithandizira ndi a munthu amene akumva kuti angathe kudzuka; si chifukwa chokokera kapena kunyamula munthu amene wagwa. Kutha kuyenda, kulinganiza thupi ndi zofunikira zaumoyo zimasiyana munthu ndi munthu.

## Kodi ndiyenera kulankhula ndi dokotala ngakhale sindikuona kuvulala?

Inde. [Alzheimer's Society imalimbikitsa kulankhula ndi GP wa munthuyo munthu akagwa, ngakhale simukuganiza kuti wavulala](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/blog/why-person-dementia-more-likely-have-fall-and-what-can-you-do-help). Ngati munaona kuti wagunda mutu, akumva kupweteka, wasokonezeka maganizo mwatsopano, wafooka, akumva chizungulire kapena wasintha kuchokera mmene amakhalira nthawi zonse, fotokozani zimenezo mukaimba foni. Musagwiritse ntchito mndandanda wa pa intaneti kuganiza kuti kuunikidwa kwa zachipatala sikufunika.

Ngati panali kuvulala kwa mutu, tsatirani malangizo a dokotala kapena a ntchito zachipatala mwadzidzidzi za kwanuko. [Malangizo a NHS okhudza kuvulala kwa mutu](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) akufotokoza zizindikiro zomwe zimafuna thandizo mwachangu. Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka maganizo mwadzidzidzi, akugona kwambiri, akusanza kapena akuoneka kuti akudwala kwambiri mwadzidzidzi, funani thandizo mwachangu m’malo modikira nthawi yokumana ndi dokotala yachizolowezi.

## Kodi ndi mfundo ziti zomwe zingathandize katswiri wa zaumoyo?

Zofunika mwamsanga zikakonzedwa, lembani zinthu zochepa zomwe munaona kuti mugawane, munthuyo akavomereza:

- nthawi ndi malo amene kugwako kunachitikira, ngati mukudziwa;
- zomwe munthuyo anali kuchita kugwa kusanachitike;
- ngati panali wina amene anaona kugwako komanso ngati munthuyo angakhale anagunda mutu;
- kupweteka, kuvulala kapena kusintha kulikonse komwe munaona;
- kusintha kulikonse kwaposachedwa pa thanzi, kayendedwe, zizolowezi kapena mankhwala komwe mukudziwa.

Fotokozani zomwe munaona m’malo mongoganiza chifukwa chake munthuyo wagwa. Zolemba zingathandize pa zokambirana; sizimazindikira chomwe chayambitsa. Ngati pali kuthekera kwa kuvulala kwakukulu kapena kusintha kwadzidzidzi, funani thandizo kaye musanalembe zonse.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufunsa chiyani pa kutsatira?

Dokotala kapena katswiri wa zaumoyo angathandize kusankha ngati munthuyo akufunika kuunikidwa komanso zomwe muyenera kuyang’anira pambuyo pake. [NHS imanena kuti kuunika kwa kugwa kungaphatikizepo kuyang’ana mphamvu, maso, makutu, mankhwala ndi kusintha kwa panyumba](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Mungafunse:

- Kodi munthuyo akufunika kuunikidwa atagwa uku, ngakhale palibe kuvulala koonekera?
- Ndi kusintha kotani kapena zizindikiro ziti zomwe ziyenera kuchititsa kufuna thandizo mwachangu?
- Kodi kulinganiza thupi, kuona, kumva, kupweteka kapena mankhwala ziyenera kuunikidwanso?
- Kodi ntchito yothandizira anthu amene amagwa kapena katswiri wa occupational therapist angathandize kuunika kayendedwe kapena zipangizo zapanyumba?
- Kodi munthuyo kapena banja liyenera kulankhula ndi ndani ngati atagwanso?

Uzani katswiri wa zaumoyo zomwe mukudziwa komanso zomwe simukudziwa. Ngati palibe amene anaona kugwako, nenani zimenezo m’malo mongoganiza momwe zinachitikira. Musasiye kapena kusintha mankhwala, kugula chothandizira kuyenda kapena kuyika zipangizo potengera tsamba la pa intaneti lokha. Thandizo loyenera limadalira thanzi la munthuyo, kayendedwe, nyumba komanso zofuna zake.

## Kodi tingaganizire bwanji za kugwa kwina?

Nkhawa zaumoyo mwamsanga zikakonzedwa, ganizirani zomwe zinkachitika pamalopo komanso pa zizolowezi. Njira yodzaza ndi zinthu, kusintha kwa kuwala kapena njira yosadziwika kungakhale kofunikira, koma musaganize kuti choopsa chimodzi ndicho chayambitsa kugwa. Funsani munthuyo zomwe anaona komanso zomwe zingapangitse malowo kukhala osavuta kugwiritsa ntchito. Ngati munthuyo sangakumbukire kapena kufotokoza kugwako, gawani zomwe munaona popanda kumukakamiza kuti afotokoze zambiri. Lembani nthawi, malo, nsapato, kuwala, zomwe amachita komanso kusintha kulikonse kuchokera mmene amayendera kapena mmene amachitira nthawi zonse. Zolemba zazifupi zingathandize katswiri wa zaumoyo kuona ngati pali njira zobwerezabwereza; sizimazindikira chomwe chayambitsa.

[Kalozera wathu wa chitetezo panyumba](/caregiving/home-safety/) akufotokoza zinthu zoyenera kuyang’anira m’chipinda chilichonse. Ngati kugwako kunachitika panthawi yosamba, onani [kalozera wathu wosamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). Dokotala, katswiri wa occupational therapist kapena ntchito yothandizira anthu amene amagwa ya kwanuko angathe kuunika nkhawa za munthu payekha ndikupereka malingaliro a kusintha koyenera. Musayike zipangizo kapena kusintha mankhwala olembedwa ndi dokotala potengera tsamba ili lokha.

Kugwa pa kokha sikusonyeza zomwe munthuyo angathe kapena sangakwanitse kuchita. Pitirizani kumuphatikiza pa zisankho zokhudza thandizo, ndipo tsatirani malangizo a gulu losamalira la kwanuko.
