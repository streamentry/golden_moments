Kukhala yekha, pa kokha, sikunena kuchuluka kwa thandizo limene munthu amafunikira. Alzheimer's Association imanena kuti anthu ambiri amene ali ndi Alzheimer's amapitiriza kukhala okha, makamaka kumayambiriro kwa matendawa, mothandizidwa ndi kusintha kochepa, njira zodzitetezera, ndi thandizo lochokera kwa ena. Funso lothandiza kwambiri ndi ili: munthuyu akufuna kupitiriza kuchita chiyani, ndipo ndi chiyani chingapangitse ntchito zimenezo kukhala zosavuta?

Yambani kukambirana zimenezi pamene pali nthawi ndipo palibe vuto lofunika kuthetsedwa mwachangu. Funsani zomwe zimamupangitsa kukhala omasuka, mʼchitidwe umene ndi wofunika kwa iye, ndi mtundu wa thandizo limene angalandire mosangalala. Dongosolo limene lapangidwa limodzi ndi iye lingagwirizane bwino ndi moyo wake kuposa mndandanda wa ntchito wokonzedwa chifukwa cha nkhawa za wina.

## Ndi chiyani chingathandize pa moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku?

Gwiritsani ntchito zinthu zochepa zodziwika zomwe zikugwirizana ndi mʼchitidwe wa munthuyo. [Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa zinthu zothandiza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) monga makalendala, mndandanda, ndi zikumbutso za zosowa za tsiku ndi tsiku.

- **Sankhani malo amodzi osungiramo zinthu zofunika.** Sungani makiyi, magalasi, chikwama, ndi foni pamalo amodzi ogwirizana. Thireyi pafupi ndi khomo kapena shelufu yolembedwapo ingathandize kuti mʼchitidwewo ukhale wosavuta kutsatira.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito kalendala imodzi kapena njira imodzi yozikumbutsira.** Sankhani kalendala ya pakhoma, bolodi loyera, kabuku ka tsiku ndi tsiku, kapena kalendala ya pa foni imene munthuyo amakonda kale kugwiritsa ntchito. Sungani nthawi zokumana ndi anthu komanso zochitika zokhazikika pamalo amodzi, osati kuzimwaza mʼma app ndi zolemba zambiri.
- **Onetsani mʼchitidwe poyera.** Ngati ntchito ina imaiwalika kawirikawiri, lembani uthenga waufupi kapena ikani chinthu chofunikira pa ntchitoyo pamalo odziwika. Sungani njira zake kukhala zazifupi ndipo onaninso limodzi ngati sizikuthandizanso.
- **Pangani ntchito zobwerezabwereza kukhala zodziwikiratu.** Kutumizidwa kwa zinthu zogula nthawi zonse, ulendo wa munthu wothandiza ntchito zapakhomo, kapena kukonza chakudya kungachepetse zisankho zimene munthuyo ayenera kupanga ali yekha.

Kalozera wathu wa [mʼchitidwe wa tsiku ndi tsiku](/activities/daily-routine/) uli ndi njira zosavuta zosungira tsiku kukhala lodziwika popanda kulipanga kukhala lokhwima kwambiri. Cholinga chake ndi kuthandiza zosankha za munthuyo, osati kudzaza ola lililonse.

## Kodi achibale ndi anzawo angapereke bwanji thandizo lothandiza?

Gwirizanani pa njira zochepa komanso zokhazikika zoyankhulirana. Ulendo waufupi kapena foni pa nthawi yodziwika ingakhale yothandiza kwambiri kuposa anthu ambiri kufunsa mosakonzekera. Funsani munthuyo amene akufuna kuti azikhudzidwa komanso zimene munthu aliyense angachite.

Perekani thandizo mwachindunji. “Kodi zingakuthandizeni ndikakubweretserani zinthu zogula Lachinayi?” ndi funso losavuta kuyankha kuposa “Mudziwitse ngati mukufuna chilichonse.” Munthu wodalirika angathandize pa kugula zinthu, mayendedwe, chakudya, mabilu, kapena kalendala ya nthawi zokumana ndi anthu. [Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kukonza thandizo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) pa ntchito zapakhomo, chakudya, mayendedwe, ndi zikumbutso za mankhwala pamene zosowa zimenezo zabuka.

Ngati pali anthu angapo amene akuthandiza, gwirizanani amene adzasamalira ntchito iliyonse komanso momwe adzafotokozere nkhani zofunika. Sungani zambiri kwa anthu amene munthuyo wasankha okha, kupatula pamene pali nkhawa ya chitetezo yofunika thandizo mwachangu. Ngati mukukhala kutali, onani kalozera wathu wa [kusamalira munthu muli kutali](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) kuti muone njira zogwirizanirana ndi anthu amene munthuyo wasankha. [Kalozera wa kulankhulana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ali ndi malangizo ena okhudza kukambirana za thandizo popanda kuchipanga kukhala mayeso.

## Kodi munthu angapitirize bwanji kulumikizana ndi ena pamene akukhala yekha?

Sankhani zochitika ndi njira zolumikizirana zimene munthuyo amasangalala nazo. Zingakhale foni ya mlungu uliwonse, ulendo wa mnansi, chakudya cha mʼdera, gulu lodziwika, kapena kuyenda ndi munthu amene amamudalira. Funsani munthuyo njira zomwe akufuna; musaganize kuti alendo ambiri kapena magulu ambiri nthawi zonse ndi abwino.

Nthawi yolumikizana ndi ena nthawi zonse ingathandize kuti sabata ikhale yosavuta kuizindikira ndipo ingamupatse munthuyo chinthu choti aziyembekezera. Ngati kupita ku chochitika kwavuta kukhala kovuta, lingalirani foni, kukwera ndi munthu wodalirika, kapena chochitika chachingʼono chapafupi ndi kunyumba. Kalozera wathu wa [kulumikizana ndi ena](/brain-health/social-connection/) uli ndi njira zosavuta komanso zopanda kukakamiza zokhalira olumikizana.

## Kodi tingazindikire bwanji kuti dongosolo likufunika kusinthidwa?

Palibe mndandanda umodzi umene umatsimikiza ngati munthu angakhale yekha. Mʼmalo mwake, samalani kusintha kwenikweni pa moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku: ntchito imene munthuyo ankatha kuchita mosavuta tsopano ikumuvuta mobwerezabwereza, chakudya chikuphonyedwa, nthawi zokumana ndi anthu zikuiwalika, kapena iye akunena kuti ntchito inayake ikukhala yovuta.

Funsani munthuyo zimene wazindikira. Perekani kusintha kumodzi kothandiza ndipo onani ngati kukuthandiza. Ngati pali nkhawa zobwerezabwereza zokhudza chakudya, mankhwala, ndalama, kuyenda, kapena chitetezo chapakhomo, funsani munthuyo ngati angalankhule ndi dokotala wake kapena katswiri wa chisamaliro cha anthu. Kuunika mozama kungathandize kudziwa njira za thandizo zomwe zilipo; sizikutanthauza kuti munthuyo avomereze malingaliro onse.

Ngati munthuyo akuoneka kuti sakutsimikiza za thandizo, yambani pangʼono. Angakonde kutumizidwa kwa zinthu zogula mlungu uliwonse kusiyana ndi kukhala ndi wosamalira mʼnyumba, kapena thandizo pa zolemba koma osati pa chisamaliro cha munthu. Muphatikizeni posankha munthu amene adzathandize komanso nthawi imene maulendo adzachitikire. [Kalozera wa chitetezo chapakhomo](/caregiving/home-safety/) akufotokoza za kusintha chipinda ndi chipinda pamene nkhawa ikukhudza nyumba yomwe.

## Bwanji ngati munthuyo sakufuna thandizo?

Mvetserani chifukwa chake musanapereke dongosolo lina. Angakhale akuda nkhawa za chinsinsi, mtengo, kutaya ulamuliro, kapena kukhala ndi munthu wosadziwika mʼnyumba. Funsani mtundu wa thandizo umene ungakhale wovomerezeka kwa iye, kenaka onani ngati kuyesa pangʼono kungathandize. Anthu ena amakonda chikumbutso kapena ntchito yogwirira limodzi; ena amafuna thandizo kuchokera kwa munthu mmodzi wodalirika osati ntchito yatsopano.

Pewani kusintha njira zoyangʼanirana kukhala mayeso. Foni ingakhale kukambirana, osati mndandanda wa mafunso okhudza ngati munthuyo wadya, wamwa mankhwala, kapena wakumbukira nthawi yokumana ndi munthu. Ngati pali ngozi yofunika thandizo pomwepo, lumikizanani ndi ntchito zadzidzidzi za mʼdera lanu. Pa nkhawa zopitilira, muphatikizeni munthuyo ndipo funsani malangizo kwa gulu lake la chisamaliro kapena ntchito yothandiza ya mʼdera lanu.

Kukhala yekha kungapitirize kutheka ndi kusintha kwa mʼchitidwe, maubale, ndi thandizo lothandiza. Onaninso dongosolo limodzi ndi munthuyo, sungani zomwe zikuthandiza, ndipo sinthani zomwe sizikugwirizananso ndi momwe zinthu zilili.
