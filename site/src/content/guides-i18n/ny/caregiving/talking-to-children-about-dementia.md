Pamene dementia ikusintha munthu amene mwana amamukonda, mwina mwana ndi amene amawona poyamba asanalankhule mulu. Mwana angamve nkhawa, mkwiyo, manyazi, kulakwa, kapena kusatsimikiza mmene ayenera kuchitira. Mwana aliyense ndi banja lililonse n’zosiyana. Chidziwitso chomveka ndi chithandizo chokhazikika zingathandize mwana kumvetsetsa zimene zikusintha popanda kum’patsa udindo wa akulu.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer's Association pa kulankhula ndi ana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) amalimbikitsa kuyankha mafunso moona m’mawu amene ana amamvetsetsa. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia a m’banja](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) nawonso amalimbikitsa kulankhulana moona, kutsimikizira, ndi kuteteza malo a mnyamata kapena mtsikana kukhala mwana. Gwiritsani izi ngati poyambira, osati malangizo a banja lililonse.

## Kodi ndimayamba bwanji kulankhula za dementia?

Yambani ndi kusintha kumene mwana wawona kapena funso limene wafunsa. Mukhoza kunena kuti, “Wawona kuti Agogo akubwereza mafunso. Ali ndi matenda otchedwa dementia amene amasokoneza kukumbukira ndi kuganiza kwake.” Gwiritsani matenda enieni a munthuyo ngati amadziwika. Ngati banja likudikirabe kuyezetsa, fotokozani kusintha popanda kumupatsa dzina la matenda.

Sankhani nthawi yabata pamene mungathe kukhalabe ndi kulankhulanako. Gwiritsani ziganizo zazifupi ndipo yimani pang’ono mwana asanayankhe. Simuyenera kufotokoza zonse nthawi imodzi. Funsani zimene kale akudziwa ndi zimene akufuna kudziwa kenako. Mwana waching’ono angathandizidwe ndi buku la zithunzi kapena chitsanzo chosavuta; mwana wamkulu angafune zambiri kapena nthawi yakeyake yoganiza.

Mulole munthu amene ali ndi dementia atenge nawo mbali akafuna ndipo akhoza kutero. Lankhulani ndi mwana za zokonda za munthuyo musanauze ena za zachinsinsi zaumoyo. Mwana angafune kholo kapena munthu wokhulupirika kukhala pafupi akamayendera, kapena angafune kusalankhula za matendawa ndi anzake kapena kusukulu panopa.

## Kodi ndikani ndinene chiyani kwa mwana za dementia?

Fotokozani zimene zimakhudza moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku wa mwana. Dementia ndi matenda amene angasinthe mmene munthu amakumbukira, amaganizira kapena amalankhulira. Si yotengera, ndipo mwana sanayibweretse. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) imanena kuti ana angakhale ndi nkhawa yoti angayitengere kapena kuimba mlandu akakhala kuti akulu akumva chisoni. Yankhani mantha amenewo molunjika.

Khalani oona pa zimene mukudziwa ndi zimene simukudziwa. Mukhoza kufotokoza zimene banja lawona, thandizo limene munthuyo amalandira ndi mapulani amene angasinthe nthawi ya mwana. Musalonjeze kuti munthuyo adzachira, kuti palibe chisintha, kapena kuti mukudziwa pamene kusintha kudzachitikira. Ngati simukudziwa yankho, nenani motero ndipo perekani kuti mufunefune chidziwitso chodalirika pamodzi.

Ana angafunikire kumvanso kufotokoza komweko kangapo. Izi n’zachibadwa. Apezereni malo oti afunse mafunso ena pambuyo pake, ndipo onetsetsani zimene amvetsetsa popanda kupanga zokambiranazo mayeso. Ngati mwana akugwiritsa mawu osiyana pa matendawa, mvereni poyamba kenako m’patse mwaulemu mawu omveka amene angagwiritse ntchito.

## Kodi ndimayankha bwanji mafunso ovuta?

Yankhani nkhawa imene ili pansi pa funso, kenako perekani yankho lomveka ndi loona limene mungathe. Khalani achidule ndipo musawonjezere zimene simukudziwa.

- **“Kodi ndingayitengere?”** Nenani ayi: dementia si yotengera. Mwana akhoza kupitiriza kukhala ndi munthuyo.
- **“Kodi ndinayibweretsa?”** Mutsimikizireni kuti matendawa sanayambike chifukwa cha chinthu chimene ananena kapena kuchita.
- **“Chifukwa chiyani agogo amanditcha dzina lina?”** Fotokozani kuti dementia ingasinthe mmene amakumbukira mayina. Si chifukwa cha mwana, ndipo mwina sakuchita kuti akhumudwitse.
- **“Kodi inenso ndidzakhala ndi dementia?”** Musaganizire za tsogolo la munthu mmodzi. Kuopsa kwa m’banja kumadalira mtundu ndi mbiri. Ngati pali nkhawa inayake pa mtundu wotengera, funsani dokotala wa munthuyo ngati katswiri wa ma genetics angathandize.
- **“Kodi adzachira?” kapena “Kodi adzafa?”** Musalonjeze zotsatira kapena nthawi. Uzeni mwana zimene gulu la chisamaliro lanena, ngati banja likufuna kugawana, ndipo fotokozani pamene mudzakambiranenso.
- **“Kodi akadali kundikonda?”** Musayike mwana kukhala ndi udindo wotsimikizira yankho. Mukhoza kunena kuti ubale ndi wofunika ndipo fufuzani njira yabata yoti mukhale limodzi pano.

N’zabwino kuyimitsa ngati funso likuwoneka lalikulu. Muuzeni mwana pamene mudzabwerera kufunsoli, ndipo chitani zimene mwanena. Kungoti “Sindikudziwa panopa” mokhazikika ndi koona kumathandiza kuposa kuyerekeza molimba mtima.

## Kodi ndimathandiza bwanji popanda kupanga mwana kukhala msamaliri?

Perekani kulumikizana mwa kusankha kwake. Ngati mwana ndi munthuyo akufuna, angamve nyimbo zodziwika, aone zithunzi, ajambule, awerengere, kapena achite chinthu chosavuta cha panyumba. Malangizo athu a [kumbukiro](/activities/reminiscence/) ndi [nyimbo](/activities/music/) ali ndi malingaliro. Tsatirani mmene munthuyo ndi mwana akumvera; n’zabwino kusiya, kusintha ntchito, kapena kupita nthawi ina.

Sungani udindo wa chisamaliro ndi chitetezo kwa akulu. Mwana akhoza kusankha kuika chakudya poyera kapena kubweretsa buku, koma sayenera kukhala ndi udindo woyang’anira, mankhwala, chisamaliro chaumwini, maulendo kuchipatala, kapena ngozi. Musatanthauze kuti kupita, kuthandiza kapena kukhala pafupi ndi udindo wa mwana. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) amachenjeza mabanja kulemekeza malire a achichepere ndi kufunika kwawo kukhala ana.

Tetezani nthawi ya mwana yokha. Sungani sukulu, ubwenzi, masewera ndi nthawi za banja mokhazikika momwe mungathere. Ngati chisamaliro chikusintha mapulani amenewo, fotokozani chifukwa chake ndipo fufuzani mkulu wina amene angathandize kuti mwana apite ku ntchito kapena paulendo wake. Malangizo a [kusamalira yekha wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ali ndi njira zopupangira ena akulu kuthandiza.

Musanaudze mphunzitsi, mphunzitsi wa masewera kapena mkulu wina, phatikizani mwana pa chisankho chimenechi momwe kungatheke. Mwina akufuna munthu mmodzi wokhulupirika adziwe, kapena akufuna kudikira. Ngati mugawana chidziwitso, vomerezani zimene munganene ndi zimene ziyenera kukhalabe zachinsinsi.

## Kodi ndikani nditafunefune chithandizo china?

Funsani za mmene mwana akumvera pakapita nthawi, osati nthawi yomweyo matenda atadziwika. Ana ena amalankhula momasuka; ena amaonetsa mavuto mwa kusintha tulo, sukulu, ubwenzi, kapena khalidwe. Zosintha izi zingakhale ndi zifukwa zambiri ndipo sizitanthauza kuti mwana akufunika kuyezetsedwa matenda. Mvereni ndipo funsani thandizo lomwe lingathandize.

Ngati mwana akufuna kulankhula ndi munthu wina osati m’banja, ganizirani mphunzitsi wokhulupirika, mlangizi wa kusukulu, dokotala wa ana, kapena katswiri wa thanzi la maganizo a ana. Funsani musanalankhule ndi anthu a kusukulu za matendawa ngati mungathe; mwina mwana akufuna kutenga nawo mbali pokambirana. Mabanja okhudzidwa ndi dementia yoyambira msinkhu waching’ono angafunikirenso thandizo pa ntchito, sukulu, ndi kusintha maudindo m’banja.

Ngati simukutsimikiza mmene mungafotokozere kusintha kwina, funsani gulu la chisamaliro cha munthuyo kapena bungwe la dementia kuti likupatseni malangizo. Mwana safunika kuyankha koyenera kuchokera kwa inu. Akufuna chidziwitso choona, mwayi wofunsanso mafunso, ndi akulu amene akupitiriza kuoneka kwa iye.
