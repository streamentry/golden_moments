Chakudya chikhoza kukhala chovuta kuchitsatira pamene mʼchipindamo muli phokoso, pa tebulo pali zinthu zambiri kapena pali zosankha zingapo pa nthawi imodzi. Kusintha pangʼono malo komanso kayendedwe ka nthawi kungathandize kuti munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala atenge nawo mbali mosavuta. Yambani ndi zizolowezi ndi zokonda za munthuyo, ndipo sungani zomwe zikuyenda bwino kale.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer's Association okhudza chakudya ndi kadyedwe](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) komanso [malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza nthawi ya chakudya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/eating-and-dementia) onse amalimbikitsa malo abata, zakudya zodziwika komanso nthawi yokwanira. Buku ili likufotokoza za malo odyera komanso thandizo la tsiku ndi tsiku. Silili dongosolo la zakudya zopatsa thanzi kapena kuunika kwa vuto la kumeza.

## Kodi tingathandize bwanji kuti chakudya chikhale nthawi yocheza popanda kuwonjezera kukakamizidwa?

Ngati munthuyo amasangalala kukhala ndi ena, khalani naye pamodzi ndikudya naye. Sungani kucheza kukhala kosavuta ndipo muloleni kuika maganizo pa chakudyacho akafuna kutero. Pewani kumukonza mmene akugwiritsira ntchito chipangizo chodyera kapena kumufunsa mafunso amene amasintha chakudyacho kukhala mayeso. Ngati pa tebulo pakhala phokoso kapena chisokonezo, chepetsani chiwerengero cha anthu kapena pitani kumalo abata.

Pamene kuphika kwakhala kotopetsa, chakudya chodziwika chokonzekera kale kapena thandizo pa gawo limodzi la ntchitoyo chingathandize kuti chakudyacho chikhale chosavuta kuchikonza. Dementia Australia imatchula zakudya zobweretsedwa kunyumba kapena zokonzekera kale ngati njira zina zikafunika. Funsani zakudya zimene munthuyo amakonda ndi mtundu wa thandizo limene angavomereze; kusavuta kwa zinthu kuyenerabe kusiya malo a zokonda zake.

## Kodi ndingathandize bwanji kuti tebulo likhale losavuta kulitsatira?

Pangani chakudyacho kuwoneka chosavuta musanasinthe zimene zaperekedwa.

- **Chepetsani phokoso.** Zimitsani kanema wawayilesi ndipo chepetsani phokoso lina lililonse losokoneza. Khazikani munthuyo kutali ndi kumene anthu akudutsadutsa kukhitchini ngati zimenezo zingamuthandize kuika maganizo.
- **Chotsani zinthu zosafunikira.** Ikani pa tebulo zipangizo zodyera ndi zokometsera zokha zimene munthuyo adzagwiritse ntchito. Chotsani matewera, maluwa kapena zinthu zina zimene zingasokoneze kapena zingatengedwe molakwika ngati chakudya.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito kusiyana kooneka bwino.** Mbale yosavuta yosiyana ndi nsalu ya pa tebulo kapena tebulo lokha ingathandize kuti chakudya chioneke mosavuta. Pewani mapangidwe ovuta pa mbale ndi nsalu.
- **Onani kuwala ndi kutentha.** Onetsetsani kuti munthuyo akuona mbale bwino, ndipo yesani chakudya ndi zakumwa musanapereke kuti zisakhale zotentha kwambiri.
- **Sungani chizolowezi chodziwika.** Ngati malo enaake, nthawi kapena kakonzedwe ka tebulo kamamupangitsa kukhala omasuka, yesani kuzisunga mofanana. Sinthani pokhapokha ngati pali chinachake chimene chikubweretsa vuto.

## Kodi ndipereke zakudya zotani?

Yambani ndi zakudya zimene munthuyo amazidziwa komanso amazikonda. Perekani chakudya chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi kapena sankhani pakati pa zinthu ziwiri zodziwika, mʼmalo mopereka mbale yodzaza ndi zakudya zambiri zosiyanasiyana. Mungawonjezere zakudya pangʼonopangʼono ngati munthuyo akufuna zina.

Zokonda zingasinthe. Ngati chakudya chimene amachikonda chakana, pewani kukangana kapena kumuika. Perekani njira ina yodziwika, muloleni munthuyo apume pangʼono, kapena yesaninso nthawi ina. Chakudya sichiyenera kutsatira ndondomeko yokhwima ngati munthuyo watopa kapena sakusangalala; sungani nthawi zanthawi zonse za chakudya pamene zikuyenda bwino, ndipo sinthani ndondomekoyo mogwirizana ndi zosowa za munthuyo.

Ngati kuphika kwavuta, munthu wa mʼbanja angathandize kukonza chakudyacho pamene munthuyo akutenga nawo mbali pa ntchito yotetezeka imene amaisangalala nayo. Buku lathu la [ntchito zapakhomo](/activities/household-tasks/) lili ndi njira zosinthira ntchito za kukhitchini popanda kuzisintha kukhala mayeso.

## Kodi ndingathandize bwanji kuti munthuyo azitha kudziyimira pawokha?

Lolani munthuyo adye mʼnjira imene akumva bwino komanso motetezeka. Angafune mbale, supuni, zakudya zodyedwa ndi manja kapena chakudya chochepa. Musafulumire kumukonza akataya chakudya kapena kumuchitira ntchito imene angakwanitsebe. Perekani thandizo pamene likufunika kapena akulifuna, ndipo patsani nthawi yokwanira ya chakudyacho.

Kudya pamodzi kungathandize kuti chakudya chikhale nthawi yocheza, ngati munthuyo amasangalala kukhala ndi ena. Khalani pafupi, sungani kucheza kukhala kosavuta ndipo muloleni munthuyo kuika maganizo pa kudya akafuna kutero. Pewani mafunso amene amayesa kukumbukira kapena mawu okhudza kuchuluka kwa zimene wadya. Ngati munthuyo waima kaye, dikirani ndipo mufunseni ngati ali wokonzeka kudyanso.

## Bwanji ngati nthawi ya chakudya ikhala yovuta?

Imani kaye ndipo fufuzani chifukwa chenicheni. Mʼchipindamo mungakhale phokoso; chakudyacho chingakhale chosadziwika; munthuyo angafunikire kupita kuchimbudzi, kupuma kapena kusintha momwe wakhala. Yesani kusintha chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi, monga kuchotsa zinthu pa tebulo, kupereka madzi kapena kupita kumalo abata. Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka, lekani ndipo yesaninso nthawi ina mʼmalo momuumiriza kuti amalize.

Ngati munthuyo nthawi zambiri amalumphitsa chakudya, akuoneka kuti sakumva bwino, akuvutika kugwiritsa ntchito pakamwa kapena manja, kapena pali kusintha kwatsopano pa chidwi chofuna kudya kapena kulemera kwa thupi, lembani zimene mwaona ndipo lankhulani ndi dokotala kapena katswiri wa zakudya. Vuto latsopano la kutafula kapena kumeza, kutsokomola mobwerezabwereza podzya kapena pomwa, kapena kutsamwitsidwa ndi chakudya kumafunika malangizo a akatswiri. Katswiri wa zolankhula ndi kumeza angathe kuunika vuto la kumeza ndikupereka malangizo a njira zotetezeka. Musasinthe kapangidwe ka chakudya, musawonjezere makulidwe a zakumwa kapena kuyesa njira zodyetsera potengera mndandanda wa malangizo wamba.

Mbale imodzi yosamalizidwa siimafotokoza chifukwa chake munthuyo waleka. Yangʼanani ngati pali chizolowezi pa zakudya zosiyanasiyana: chakudya china, nthawi, kuchuluka kwa phokoso kapena mmene wakhalira kungakhale kukuvutitsa ntchitoyo. Ngati chakudya chodziwika chimene amachikonda mwadzidzidzi chayamba kumuvuta, onani ngati pali ululu kapena vuto la mano ndipo funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo mʼmalo moganiza kuti munthuyo akungosankha.

Pa mafunso a zaumoyo kapena zakudya zapadera, funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo wa munthuyo. Musayambe zakudya zoletsa zinthu zambiri kapena kusintha mankhwala kuti musinthe chidwi chofuna kudya popanda malangizo. [Buku la zakudya pa thanzi la ubongo](/brain-health/diet/) limafotokoza kafukufuku wokhudza zakudya ndi chiopsezo cha matenda a kuiwala; ndilosiyana ndi buku ili la malangizo a nthawi ya chakudya.

## Kodi chakudya chiyenera kudyedwa pa tebulo nthawi zonse?

Ayi. Ngati kukhala pa tebulo kukutopetsa kapena kukusokoneza, malo ena abwino angakhale oyenera, bola ngati munthuyo angadye motetezeka. Chakudya chochepa, chotupitsa kapena kupuma pangʼono kungakhale koyenera kuposa kuyesa kumaliza chakudya chonse pa nthawi yokhazikika.

Ngati kudya kwakhala kovuta kwa masiku angapo, kapena mukuda nkhawa ndi zakudya, zakumwa kapena kumeza, lankhulani ndi katswiri wa zaumoyo. Fufuzani thandizo ladzidzidzi ngati pali vuto lomwe likuchitika pomwepo la kutsamwitsidwa ndi chakudya.
