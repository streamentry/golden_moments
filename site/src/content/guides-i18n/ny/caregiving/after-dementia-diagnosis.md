Kuzindikira matenda a kuyiwala kungabweretse mafunso okhudza zaumoyo, moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku, chithandizo komanso zamtsogolo. Anthu amalandira nkhaniyi mosiyanasiyana: ena amafuna nthawi kuti aganizire zimene amvazo, pamene ena amafuna kuyamba kukonzekera. Palibe njira imodzi yokha yolondola kapena nthawi yofanana kwa onse. Yambani ndi zimene zili zofunika kwa munthuyo panopa, ndipo mubwererenso ku mafunso ena pamene ali wokonzeka.

[Upangiri wa Alzheimer's Association wokhudza moyo pambuyo pozindikira matenda](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) umathandiza anthu kudziwa za chithandizo chotsatira, chithandizo cha m’dera lawo komanso kukonzekera zamtsogolo. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza kulandira nkhani yozindikira matenda](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) akutsindika kuti anthu amalandira nkhaniyi mosiyanasiyana ndipo angafunefune chithandizo pamene akuchifuna. Tsambali ndi poyambira chabe, osati ndondomeko ya zaumoyo, zamalamulo kapena zachuma yopangidwira munthu payekha.

## Kodi chinthu chothandiza choyamba ndi chiti?

Sankhani funso limodzi limene lingapangitse sabata ino kukhala yosavuta. Lingakhale kudziwa amene adzayang’anire chithandizo chotsatira cha munthuyo, kufunsa komwe mungapeze uthenga wodalirika wokhudza matenda ake, kapena kudziwa munthu amene amamukhulupirira kuti akambirane naye. Munthuyo angafune kutsogolera, kupempha wina kuti amuthandize kapena kudikira asanapange chisankho.

Pa ulendo wotsatira, lembani mafunso ochepa limodzi ngati munthuyo akufuna. Mukhoza kufunsa kuti kuzindikira matendawa kukutanthauza chiyani, ndi ziti zomwe sizikudziwika bwinobwino, mudzabwerenso liti komanso muyenera kulankhula ndi ndani ngati padzabuka nkhawa ina yatsopano. Pemphani katswiri wa zaumoyo kuti afotokoze mawu aliwonse kapena malangizo aliwonse omwe sakumveka bwino. [Upangiri wathu wokhudza ulendo wokaonana ndi dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) uli ndi mndandanda wafupikitsa wokonzekera.

Munthuyo sakuyenera kuphunzira chilichonse chokhudza matenda a kuyiwala nthawi imodzi. Sankhani uthenga wodalirika wokhudza matenda amene apezeka nawo, ndipo lembani mafunso omwe angabwere pambuyo pake. Samalani ndi malonjezo, zonena zoti pali chithandizo chochiritsira kapena malangizo omwe amauza aliyense kutsatira chithandizo kapena ndondomeko yomweyo.

## Kodi tingapeze kuti chithandizo?

Funsani munthuyo kuti ndi chithandizo chotani chomwe chingamuthandize komanso ndani amene angafune kuti atenge nawo mbali. Mnzake kapena wachibale amene amamukhulupirira akhoza kukhala munthu woyamba kukambirana naye. Ena amafuna gulu lothandizirana, mlangizi, bungwe la m’dera lothandiza anthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuyiwala kapena kukambirana ndi gulu lawo la zaumoyo. Njira yoyenera imadalira munthuyo komanso zimene zilipo pafupi nawo.

Chithandizo ndi cha munthu amene wapezeka ndi matendawa komanso a m’banja ndi anzake. Munthu amene amasamalira angafune kupumula kapena thandizo pa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku; munthuyo angafune kudziwa zambiri kapena mwayi wokumana ndi ena amene ali ndi matenda a kuyiwala. Zosowa zimenezi zingasiyane. Funsani musanakonzere gulu, musanapange nthawi yokumana kapena musanagawane nkhani zaumwini.

[Upangiri wathu wokhudza kudzisamalira kwa munthu wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) uli ndi malingaliro ofunsira ena kuti agawane nawo chithandizo. Ngati munthuyo angafune thandizo lochulukirapo masana, maupangiri okhudza [mapulogalamu a masana a anthu akuluakulu](/caregiving/choosing-adult-day-program-dementia/) ndi [chisamaliro chapakhomo](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) akufotokoza mafunso oti mufunse okhudza ntchito zomwe zilipo m’dera lanu.

## Kodi munthuyo angapitirize bwanji kutenga nawo mbali pa zisankho?

Kuzindikira matendawa kokha sikukuuzani zimene munthuyo angathe kusankha kapena momwe akufunira kutenga nawo mbali. Funsani munthuyo mwachindunji. Mupatseni nthawi, fotokozani chisankhocho m’mawu osavuta ndipo funsani kuti ndi chithandizo chotani chomwe chingapangitse kukambiranako kukhala kosavuta. Zisankho zina zingakhale zosavuta kukambirana pamalo abata kapena pa zokambirana zingapo.

Ngati munthuyo akufuna kukonzekera zaumoyo wamtsogolo, zachuma kapena malo okhala, yambani ndi zimene zili zofunika kwa iye komanso amene amamukhulupirira. [Upangiri wokonzekera wa Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) umalimbikitsa kukambirana zokhumba ndi anthu odalirika komanso akatswiri oyenerera. Zolemba zamalamulo ndi malamulo okhudza kupanga zisankho zimasiyana malinga ndi dera, choncho funsani katswiri woyenerera wa m’dera lanu musanadalire upangiri wamba. Kuti mupeze thandizo pa kulipira mabilu a tsiku ndi tsiku komanso nkhawa zokhudza chinyengo, onani [upangiri wathu wokhudza kasamalidwe ka ndalama mukakhala ndi matenda a kuyiwala](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/).

### Kodi tingafunse chiyani pa zokambirana zoyamba zokhudza chisamaliro chamtsogolo?

Funsani ngati munthuyo akufuna kukambirana panopa, kenako kambiranani nkhani imodzi pa nthawi imodzi. [Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa zokambirana zing’onozing’ono zingapo, mawu osavuta komanso kumvetsera zokhumba, zikhulupiriro ndi zimene zili zofunika kwa munthuyo](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [Alzheimer's Society imanena kuti anthu amasiyana pa momwe amamvera kukhala okonzeka kuganizira zamtsogolo](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Ndibwino kuima kaye ndikubwereranso tsiku lina.

Ngati munthuyo akufuna malingaliro oyambira, yesani limodzi mwa mafunsowa:

- „Ndi mbali ziti za tsiku lanu zomwe mungafune kuti zipitirire?“
- „Ndani amene mungafune kuti atenge nawo mbali tikamakambirana za chisamaliro chamtsogolo?“
- „Ndi chiyani chimene chimakuthandizani kumva bwino komanso kulemekezedwa mukafuna thandizo?“
- „Kodi pali chilichonse chimene mungafune kuti gulu lanu la zaumoyo lidziwe zokhudza zimene zili zofunika kwa inu?“
- „Kodi mungafune kuti ndilembe zimene mwanena, komanso ndani ayenera kuziona?“

Mvetserani popanda kufuna kuti apange chisankho nthawi yomweyo. [National Institute on Aging imafotokoza kuti zokambirana zatanthauzo zokhudza zimene munthu amaona kuti n’zofunika komanso zokonda zake ndizofunikira kwambiri pokonzekera chisamaliro chamtsogolo](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Ngati munthuyo wavomera, lembani mawu ake, tsiku komanso amene analipo. Funsani chilolezo musanagawane zimene mwalembazo. Zolemba zimenezi zingathandize pa zokambirana zamtsogolo, koma si zolemba zamalamulo kapena malangizo a zaumoyo.

Musaganizire zinthu potengera kuzindikira matendawa kokha kapena zokambirana kamodzi zokha. Zokonda za munthuyo zingasinthe, ndipo angapemphe kuima kaye kapena kubwereranso ku nkhaniyo pambuyo pake. Pitirizani kumuphatikiza pa zisankho zomwe zimamukhudza monga momwe iye akufunira.

## Kodi ndani ayenera kudziwa za matendawa?

Munthuyo ndi amene amasankha ngati, liti komanso momwe angauzire anthu ena, pokhapokha ngati pa nkhani inayake ya chitetezo kapena yamalamulo pakufunika malangizo a akatswiri. Funsani amene akufuna awadziwitse komanso zimene akufuna anthuwo adziwe. Angasankhe kuuza munthu mmodzi wokhulupirika poyamba, kapena angasankhe kusauza anthu ambiri.

Ngati munthuyo akufuna thandizo pokambirana, funsani zimene angafune kuti munene komanso ngati akufuna kuti akhalepo. Gawanani zokha uthenga umene mwagwirizana nawo kuti ugawidwe. [Upangiri wathu wokhudza kulankhulana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ukufotokoza za kukambirana mwaulemu ndi a m’banja ndi ena.

## Kodi moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku ungapitirire bwanji pamene tikusintha?

Pitirizani ndi zizolowezi ndi zochitika zomwe zili zofunika kwa munthuyo ngati zikadali zomuyenera. Kuzindikira matendawa sikutanthauza kuti zokonda zonse, maulendo kapena maudindo apakhomo ayenera kutha. Ngati china chake chayamba kukhala chovuta, funsani zimene munthuyo angafune kusintha. Kucheza kwaufupi, nyimbo zodziwika, thandizo pa gawo limodzi kapena nthawi ina ya tsiku kungamuyenerere bwino.

Munthuyo angafunenso malo oti aganizire nkhaniyi popanda kukambirana za ndondomeko. Pitirizani kufunsa momwe alili popanda kusandutsa kukambirana kulikonse kukhala msonkhano wa chisamaliro. Ndibwino kupuma pa kukonzekera ndikukhala naye nthawi pamodzi m’njira wamba.

## Kodi tiyenera kufunsa katswiri liti?

Funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo wa munthuyo mafunso okhudza matenda amene apezeka nawo, zaumoyo wake, mankhwala kapena zizindikiro zake. Pa nkhani za malamulo ndi zachuma, lankhulani ndi akatswiri oyenerera omwe akudziwa malamulo a kumene munthuyo akukhala. Tsamba la pa intaneti la uthenga wamba silingakuuzeni kuti ndi chithandizo chiti, chikalata chiti kapena ntchito iti yomwe ili yoyenera kwa munthu winawake.

Kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kwa maganizo kapena kusintha kwina kwaumoyo komwe kwachitika mwadzidzidzi kumafuna thandizo la zaumoyo mwachangu. [Malangizo a NHS okhudza kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kwa maganizo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) akuti mupeze thandizo la zaumoyo nthawi yomweyo. Gwiritsani ntchito njira ya chithandizo chadzidzidzi kapena chachangu ya m’dera limene munthuyo akukhala; musaganize kuti kusintha kwadzidzidzi ndi mbali ya momwe alili nthawi zonse.

Ngati kukonzekera kukuoneka ngati kolemera, sankhani munthu mmodzi wodalirika kapena ntchito imodzi yothandizira kugawa mafunso kukhala a panopa ndi a mtsogolo. Munthuyo akhoza kusankha mayendedwe ake ndipo angasankhe zimene akufuna kuthandizidwa. Kuzindikira matendawa ndi uthenga wofunika, koma sikutanthauzira zokonda za munthuyo, ubale wake kapena moyo wake wa tsiku ndi tsiku.
