Kunenedwa kuti munatenga chikwama, munasuntha kachikwama kapena munasintha zambiri za kubanki kungakhale kopweteka. Munthu amene ali ndi vuto la kuiwala angayike chinthu pamalo olakwika, angaiwale kuti anachisuntha kapena angasamvetse bwino zimene zinachitika. Koma vuto la kuiwala silikusonyeza kuti zonena zake ndi zabodza. Yambani ndi kumvetsera ndi kufufuza zoona.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer’s Association pa kukaikira ndi maganizo olakwika](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/suspicions-delusions) amalimbikitsa kukhala odekha, kupereka yankho lalifupi ndipo osakangana. [Alzheimer’s Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) imalangizanso kufufuza nkhawa imeneyi m’malo mongoinyalanyaza chifukwa chakuti munthuyo ali ndi vuto la kuiwala. Tsambali likupereka malingaliro wamba a momwe mungalankhulire, osati njira yotsimikizira ngati zonena zinazake ndi zoona. Kuti mupeze malangizo osiyana okhudza kuona kapena kumva zinthu zimene ena sakuziona, onani [malangizo athu okhudza kuona zinthu zomwe palibe ndi vuto la kuiwala](/caregiving/hallucinations-with-dementia/).

## Kodi ndinganene chiyani munthu akakukaikirani?

Yankhani pa nkhawa yake popanda kuvomereza chinthu chimene simukudziwa ngati n’choona. Yankho lalifupi komanso lokhazikika lingathandize kuti kukambiranaku kupitirire. Mwachitsanzo: “Muli ndi nkhawa kuti chikwama chanu chasowa. Tiyeni tifufuze pamodzi.” Kenako imani pang’ono ndipo onani mmene munthuyo akuyankhira.

- **Mvetserani musanafotokoze.** Funsani kuti ndi chinthu chiti chasowa kapena n’chiyani chamupangitsa kukhala ndi nkhawa. Angakhale akukhudzidwa ndi zinthu zambiri kuposa chinthucho chokha.
- **Yankhani mwachidule.** Kufotokoza kwatali kungawonjezere zambiri zomwe n’zovuta kuzitsatira ndipo kungakukhumudwitseni nonsenu.
- **Pewani kukangana kapena kumufunsa mafunso ovuta.** Musamukakamize kuti akumbukire kumene anaika chinthu kapena kutsimikizira kuti simunatenge.
- **Perekani thandizo lothandiza.** Ngati munthuyo akufuna, fufuzani pamodzi m’malo odziwika kapena mufunseni ngati angafune munthu wina athandize.
- **Lekani ngati kukhumudwa kukukulirakulira.** Mpatseni munthuyo mpata pang’ono ndipo bwereraniko ku nkhaniyo pambuyo pake ngati n’kotheka kuchita zimenezo motetezeka.

Zoneneza zimenezi zingapweteke. Yesani kusiyanitsa mantha a munthuyo ndi zimene mukudziwa zokhudza zimene zinachitika. Simuyenera kuvomera mlandu wa chinthu chimene simunachite, ndipo simuyenera kuthetsa kusamvana kulikonse pa nthawiyo.

## Kodi nkhawayi ingakhale yokhudza chinthu chimene chasowadi?

Inde. Yambani ndi kufufuza ngati chinthucho chasowadi. Munthuyo angakhale anachiika pamalo achilendo, munthu wina angakhale anachisuntha kapena chinthucho chikhoza kukhala chitabedwa. Musanyoze nkhani ya kuba, kunyalanyaza kapena kuvulazidwa chifukwa chakuti munthuyo ali ndi vuto la kuiwala.

Ngati chinthu chayikidwa pamalo olakwika, fufuzani pamodzi ngati munthuyo akufuna. Yang’anani m’malo amene amachiikamo kawirikawiri ndipo tsatirani zimene amachita posachedwa. Ngati chinthucho ndi chamtengo wapatali, n’chofunika pa chitetezo kapena sichikupezeka, m’fotokozereni munthuyo zimene mukudziwa ndipo m’phatikizepo akatswiri odalirika kapena munthu wina m’banja.

Ngati mumagawana ndalama kapena ntchito zapakhomo, gwiritsani ntchito zolemba zomveka bwino zimene munthuyo amazivomereza. Sungani malisiti kapena zolemba za zomwe mwagwirizana ndipo muwafotokozere m’njira yosavuta. Funsani akatswiri oyenerera a m’dera lanu pa nkhani za ndalama kapena udindo walamulo; malangizo awa sangawunikire nkhani zimenezo.

Ngati simukutsimikiza kuti nkhawayi ndi yolondola, pemphani munthu wosalowerera mbali kuti athandize kufufuza. Sankhani munthu amene munthuyo amamudalira ndipo gawani zambiri zokhazo zomwe zikufunika kuti mumvetsetse zimene zinachitika.

## Kodi zizolowezi zodziwika zingathandize bwanji pa zinthu zosowa?

Perekani zinthu zomwe zimasowa kawirikawiri malo odziwika. Mbiya ya makiyi, thireyi ya chikwama kapena kabati yolembedwa chizindikiro zingathandize kupeza chinthu mosavuta pambuyo pake. [Alzheimer’s Society imalimbikitsa kugwiritsa ntchito malo amodzi odziwika pa zinthu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/delusions) monga makiyi kapena magalasi. Funsani munthuyo kuti ndi malo ati amene akumva kuti ndi abwino kwa iye.

Yesani kusintha kumodzi pa nthawi. Kusuntha zinthu popanda kufotokoza kungapangitse munthuyo kukhala wosatsimikiza kumene zinthu zili. Ngati chinthu chotsika mtengo chimasowa kawirikawiri, kukhala ndi china chofanana kungathandize m’mabanja ena, koma siziyenera kulowa m’malo mwa kufufuza ngati choyambirira chinabedwa kapena chinayikidwa pamalo olakwika.

Onetsetsani kuti munthuyo akuona ndi kumva bwino momwe zingathekere. Dementia Australia imanena kuti mavuto a kuona kapena kumva, matenda, zotsatira za mankhwala, kusintha kwa zizolowezi kapena malo osokoneza angathandize pa kusintha kwa mmene munthu amaonera zinthu. Funsani munthuyo ngati chipindacho chili ndi phokoso kwambiri kapena sichikumveka bwino kwa iye, ndipo onetsetsani kuti ali ndi magalasi ake kapena zida zothandizira kumva ngati amazigwiritsa ntchito.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufunsa thandizo lachipatala kapena lakunja liti?

Uzani dokotala wa munthuyo ngati kukaikira ndi kwatsopano, kukuchuluka kapena kukumvetsa chisoni. Lembani nthawi imene zikuchitika, zimene zinkachitika komanso ngati pali chilichonse chasintha pa umoyo, zizolowezi, malo okhala kapena mankhwala a munthuyo. Musayambe, musasiye kapena musasinthe mankhwala potengera nkhani ya wamba yokha.

Chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa kapena kusintha kwadzidzidzi pa khalidwe kapena umoyo kumafuna thandizo lachipatala mwamsanga. [Malangizo a NHS pa chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) amati mupeze thandizo lachipatala nthawi yomweyo. Gwiritsani ntchito njira za thandizo ladzidzidzi kapena zachangu za m’dera limene munthuyo akukhala.

Ngati zoneneza zingakhudze kuba, kunyalanyaza kapena kuvulazidwa, onetsetsani kuti munthuyo ali otetezeka ndipo funsani malangizo kwa akatswiri odalirika a m’dera lanu kapena ku ntchito za chitetezo cha akulu. Ngati munthu aliyense ali pa ngozi nthawi yomweyo, funsani ntchito zadzidzidzi za m’dera lanu. Musafufuze nokha nkhani yaikulu ngati kuchita zimenezo kungayike inu kapena munthuyo pa ngozi.

## Kodi ndingadzisamalire bwanji?

Zoneneza mobwerezabwereza zingalemeretse ubale wanu. Lankhulani ndi munthu amene mumamudalira kapena wothandizira pa nkhani za maganizo, ndipo pemphani thandizo pa ntchito zosamalira ngati mukumva kuti mwalemedwa. [Malangizo pa kusagwirizana m’banja](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/) akupereka njira zogawana maudindo, ndipo [malangizo pa kudzisamalira kwa wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) akufotokoza za kupuma ndi chithandizo.

Ganizirani pa zimene zingafufuzidwe, mmene munthuyo akumvera komanso thandizo lomwe likufunika. Simuyenera kutsimikizira chisamaliro chanu pa kukambirana kulikonse. Ngati nkhawa ikubwererabwerera, pemphani gulu losamalira kuti likuthandizeni kupeza njira yomwe imalemekeza munthuyo ndikutchinjiriza onse okhudzidwa.
