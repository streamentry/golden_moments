Munthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwala akhoza kuiwala kumwa kapena osaona ludzu. Chakumwa chomwe sichikuoneka kapena chovuta kuchigwira chingakhale chophweka kuchiphonya. Kusintha pangʼono pa zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku kungathandize kuti zakumwa zikhale zosavuta kulandira, nʼkumalola munthuyo kukhala ndi ulamuliro.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer's Association pa chakudya ndi kumwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) akusonyeza kupereka makapu angʼonoangʼono a zakumwa tsiku lonse komanso zakudya zokhala ndi madzi. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia pa zakudya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/nutrition-and-dementia) amalimbikitsanso zakumwa zodziwika bwino komanso kuziika pomwe nʼzosavuta kuzifikira. Tsambali likupereka malingaliro wamba, osati mulingo wa madzi wa tsiku ndi tsiku wa munthu winawake.

## Kodi ndingachite bwanji kuti chakumwa chikhale chosavuta kuchiona ndi kuchifikira?

Ikani chakumwa chomwe munthuyo amakonda pomwe angachione ndi kuchifikira. Gwiritsani ntchito kapu kapena botolo lodziwika bwino lomwe ndi losavuta kuligwira. Ngati munthuyo amakonda kutentha, kakomedwe kapena kapu inayake, kumbukirani zimenezo. Kusankha kumodzi kochepa kungakhale kosavuta kuposa kumufunsa kuti asankhe kuchokera mu zakumwa zambiri.

Perekani zakumwa ngati mbali ya zochitika zomwe munthuyo amazidziwa kale, monga nthawi ya chakudya, kupuma kapena kuyenda panja. Kapu yaingʼono ingawoneke yosalemedwa kuposa galasi lalikulu. Perekani chakumwa china pambuyo pake popanda kuchisintha kukhala mayeso kapena chikumbutso choti munthuyo waiwala.

Ngati munthuyo angathe kudzithira kapena kunyamula chakumwa pa yekha, muloleni. Ngati akufuna thandizo, mufunseni musanathire kapena kumuika kapu mʼdzanja. Kapu yatsopano, chitoliro kapena malo atsopano zingaoneke zosazolowereka, choncho sinthani chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi ndipo sungani zomwe zimamuthandiza munthuyo.

Mukamatuluka, nyamulani chakumwa chomwe munthuyo amakonda ndipo mupereke pa nthawi yopuma kapena ya chakudya. Ngati anthu angapo akuthandiza, gawani nawo zakumwa ndi kapu zomwe munthuyo amakonda, atavomera. Sungani chakumwacho pafupi, koma mufunseni musanachisunthire kapena kumupatsa.

## Kodi ndingapereke zakumwa kapena zakudya zotani?

Yambani ndi zakumwa zomwe munthuyo amasangalala nazo komanso zomwe angamwe motetezeka. Madzi ndi njira imodzi; mkaka, msuzi, zakumwa zosakaniza kapena zakumwa zina zodziwika bwino zingakhalenso zoyenera malinga ndi zokonda zake ndi dongosolo lake la chisamaliro. Zipatso zina ndi zakudya zina zimakhalanso ndi madzi. [Alzheimer's Association imatchula zipatso, msuzi, zakumwa za mkaka ndi zakumwa zosakaniza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) pakati pa zinthu zokhala ndi madzi ambiri.

Sikoyenera kumukakamiza kumwa chakumwa chimodzi ngati wakana. Perekani chisankho china chodziwika bwino kapena yesaninso pambuyo pake. Funsani dokotala kapena katswiri wa zakudya wa munthuyo musanasinthe zakudya kapena dongosolo la zakumwa, makamaka ngati munthuyo ali kale ndi malangizo achipatala okhudza zakumwa.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuchepetsa zakumwa kuti ndichepetse kupita ku chimbudzi?

Musamachepetse zakumwa pofuna kupewa ngozi pokhapokha katswiri wa zaumoyo atapereka dongosolo loyenera munthuyo. [Alzheimer's Association imachenjeza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence) kuti kuchepetsa zakumwa kungayambitse kuchepa kwa madzi mʼthupi ndi mavuto ena. Munthu amene akufunika thandizo kuti apeze kapena agwiritse ntchito chimbudzi angapindule ndi chithandizo cha mtundu wina. [Tsamba lathu lokhudza kudzikodzera](/caregiving/toileting-and-incontinence-with-dementia/) likufotokoza za nthawi za kuchimbudzi ndi ulemu.

Gawani nthawi zopereka zakumwa tsiku lonse mʼmalo mofunsa munthuyo kumwa zambiri nthawi imodzi. Tsatirani malangizo aliwonse achipatala omwe alipo ndipo funsani dokotala kuchuluka koyenera kwa munthuyo. Musadalire nambala wamba yochokera pa intaneti; zosowa za madzi zimasiyana, ndipo anthu ena ali ndi dongosolo la zakumwa lochokera kwa dokotala.

## Kodi ndi kusintha kotani komwe ndiyenera kuyangʼana?

Yangʼanani zomwe zasiyana ndi zomwe munthuyo amachita nthawi zonse. Angayambe kumwa zochepa kwambiri kuposa mmene amachitira, kusiya chakumwa chomwe amakonda kapena kufunika thandizo lochulukirapo kuposa kale. Kamuwa kouma, chizungulire, kutopa kosazolowereka kapena kusintha pa kukodza kungakhalenso zifukwa zofunsira katswiri wa zaumoyo. Zizindikirozi zingakhale ndi zifukwa zina, choncho musadziwire nokha kuti ndi kuchepa kwa madzi mʼthupi.

Lembani zomwe mwaona komanso nthawi yomwe zachitika. Phatikizani ngati munthuyo anali kudwala, anali pamalo otentha kapena anali kuvutika kugwira kapena kumeza chakumwa. [Alzheimer's Association imanena kuti zizindikiro za kuchepa kwa madzi mʼthupi zingakhale zovuta kuziona mwa munthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster). Ngati pakhala kusintha kodzidzimutsa kapena mukuda nkhawa, lankhulani ndi dokotala kapena katswiri wina wa zaumoyo.

## Bwanji ngati kumwa kwakhala kovuta?

Ngati munthuyo akutsokomola, akutsamwitsidwa, akusunga chakudya kapena chakumwa mkamwa, kapena ali ndi vuto latsopano la kumeza, imitsani ndipo funsani malangizo kwa katswiri woyenerera wa zaumoyo. Musamakhuthalitse zakumwa, kusintha mawonekedwe a chakudya kapena kuyesa njira za kumeza pokhapokha katswiri atalangiza dongosolo limenelo kwa munthuyo. [Tsamba lathu lokhudza nthawi ya chakudya](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) likufotokoza zoyenera kuchita ngati pakhala vuto latsopano la kutafuna kapena kumeza.

Ngati munthuyo ali ndi kamuwa kouma, mano opweteka kapena mano oikapo omwe sakumukhala bwino, mufunseni zomwe akuona ndipo lankhulani ndi dokotala wa mano kapena dokotala. Kupweteka mkamwa kungapangitse kumwa kukhala kovuta. Onani [tsamba lathu lokhudza chisamaliro cha mkamwa](/caregiving/oral-care-with-dementia/) kuti mupeze njira zofatsa zothandizira kusamalira mkamwa.

Chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa chimafuna thandizo lachipatala mwamsanga. [Malangizo a NHS pa chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) amalangiza anthu kuti asadziwire okha. Gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zadzidzidzi kapena njira yothandizira mwachangu komwe mukukhala.

## Kodi ndingapereke bwanji zakumwa popanda kukakamiza?

Perekani chakumwa, kenako mupatseni munthuyo nthawi yosankha. Angafune kumwa pambuyo pake kapena kusankha kapu ina. Pewani kukonza zolakwika, kukakamiza kapena kumuwumiriza kumwa. Ngati nthawi zambiri amakana, onani ngati chakumwa chili chosavuta kuchifikira, ngati amakonda kakomedwe ndi kutentha kwake, komanso ngati munthuyo akupeza bwino pamalopo.

Funsani munthuyo zomwe zimamuthandiza ndipo gawani zomwe mumadziwa ndi anthu ena omwe akuthandiza pokhapokha atavomera. Kapu yodziwika bwino, malo omwe wasankha kapena nthawi yachete zingathandize kuti kumwa kukhale kosavuta. Ngati nkhawayi ikupitirira, funsani dokotala kapena katswiri wa zakudya kuti akupatseni malangizo oyenera munthuyo.

Cholinga chake ndi kuika zakumwa pafupi mʼnjira yomwe munthuyo angavomere. Perekani mofatsa, lemekezani chisankho chake ndipo pezani malangizo a katswiri pamene zosowa zasintha.
