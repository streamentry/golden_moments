Kukhala kutali kungapangitse kuti zikhale zovuta kudziwa thandizo limene likufunika, koma kutalikirana sikutanthauza kuti palibe njira iliyonse yothandizira. Mungathe kukonza chithandizo, kugwira ntchito imene munthuyo wavomereza, kapena kuthandiza munthu amene amamusamalira tsiku ndi tsiku. Yambani pofunsa munthu ali ndi matenda a kuiwala zimene akufuna komanso amene akufuna kuti akhale nawo.

[Buku la Alzheimer's Association lokhudza kusamalira muli kutali](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) limafotokoza maudindo monga kukonza nthawi yokaonana ndi adotolo, kufufuza za chithandizo chapafupi, kugwirizana ndi othandiza amene ali pafupi, komanso kupatsa wosamalira wa tsiku ndi tsiku mpata wopuma. [Family Caregiver Alliance imanenanso](https://www.caregiver.org/faq/how-can-i-help-with-caregiving-from-far-away/) kuti kupeza chithandizo ndi kukonzekera maulendo kungakhale kovuta muli kutali. Buku ili limapereka malingaliro wamba okonzekera; chithandizo chapafupi ndi malamulo zimasiyana malinga ndi dera.

## Kodi ndingachite chiyani ndili kutali?

Sankhani ntchito zothandiza zimene zikugwirizana ndi zimene munthuyo akufuna komanso zimene mungathe kuchita modalirika. Thandizo lingaphatikizepo:

- **Konzani nthawi yokaonana ndi adotolo.** Funsani ngati munthuyo akufuna thandizo lolumikizana ndi chipatala, kusunga kalendala, kapena kukonza mayendedwe.
- **Fufuzani za chithandizo chapafupi.** Yang'anani za kubweretsa chakudya, mayendedwe, thandizo la kunyumba, mapulogalamu a masana a akulu, kapena magulu a osamalira komwe munthuyo amakhala. Tsimikizirani zambiri ndi amene akupereka chithandizocho; kupezeka kwake kumasiyana malinga ndi dera.
- **Thandizani pa zikalata kapena kuyitanitsa zinthu zimene mwagwirizana.** Mungathandize kukonza mafomu kapena kuyitanitsa zinthu zofunika ngati munthuyo akupemphani. Zokhudza ndalama, inshuwalansi, ndi zolemba zaumoyo zimafunika kusamala kwenikweni; gwiritsani ntchito buku lathu la [thandizo la ndalama ndi chitetezo ku chinyengo](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/) ndipo tsimikizirani ufulu kapena chilolezo chilichonse chimene chikufunika.
- **Patsani wosamalira wa pafupi mpata.** Paulendo wanu, funsani kuti ndi ntchito iti kapena mpata uti ungamuthandize. Mungakonze chakudya, kuthandiza pa nthawi yokonzekera yokaonana ndi dokotala, kapena kukhala ndi munthuyo pamene wothandiza wa pafupi akupuma.
- **Khalani olumikizana m'njira imene munthuyo amasangalala nayo.** Funsani ngati amakonda kuyimba foni, kuyankhulana pa kanema, kalata, kapena ulendo. Kuyimba foni kungakhale kukambirana, osati kuyesa kumufunsa za chakudya, mankhwala, kapena kukumbukira.

[Alzheimer's Association imanena kuti anthu amene amapereka thandizo ali kutali sangadziwe bwinobwino zosowa za munthuyo mwachindunji](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage). Ngati n'kotheka, lankhulani ndi anthu amene amaona munthuyo tsiku ndi tsiku ndipo konzekerani ulendo wopita kumalo kumene munthuyo amakhala. Kudziwako kungawonjezere uthenga wothandiza, koma sikulowa m'malo mofunsa munthuyo kapena kupeza kuwunika kwa katswiri.

## Kodi ndingagwirizane bwanji ndi othandiza apafupi?

Choyamba funsani munthuyo kuti akufuna ndani azikhala nawo komanso uthenga uti umene angamasuke kugawana. Angafune kuti wachibale m'modzi azikonza nthawi zokaonana ndi adotolo, mnansi aziyang'ana nkhani ina yake, kapena asamafune kuti azipatsidwa malipoti nthawi zonse. Musawonjezere munthu pa zokambirana za chisamaliro chifukwa choti amakhala pafupi.

Ngati munthuyo avomera, konzani dongosolo losavuta lolumikizirana ndi anthu amene wasankha:

- **Sankhani munthu m'modzi wolandira uthenga wanthawi zonse.** Gwirizanani kuti ndani azitumiza uthenga wanthawi zonse komanso kangati.
- **Tchulani munthu wina woti athandize pakafunika.** Sankhani amene angayang'ane ngati wothandiza wa pafupi wamba palibe.
- **Lekani uthenga wanthawi zonse ndi nkhawa zofunika mwachangu.** Gwirizanani momwe munthu wa pafupi angakudziwitsireni ngati chitetezo kapena thanzi la munthuyo lasintha mwadzidzidzi.
- **Sungani ntchito kukhala zomveka.** Lembani kuti ndani azikonza nthawi yokaonana ndi adotolo, mayendedwe, mafoni ofunsa chithandizo, kapena thandizo la panyumba. Funsani musanasinthe dongosololi.

[Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kulankhulana pafupipafupi ndi achibale, anansi, komanso opereka chithandizo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Mungafunsenso wothandiza wa pafupi thandizo limene akufuna. Thandizo lachindunji komanso lofupika lingakhale losavuta kulilandira kusiyana ndi kungonena kuti “udziwitse ngati ukufunika chilichonse.”

## Kodi dokotala kapena wopereka chithandizo angandiuze uthenga uti?

Funsani dokotala wa munthuyo kapena wopereka chithandizo kuti amafuna chilolezo kapena zikalata zotani asanagawire uthenga. [Alzheimer's Association imanena kuti wopereka chithandizo angafune chilolezo cholembedwa asanalankhule za chisamaliro ndi wachibale amene ali kutali](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Mafomu ndi malamulo zimasiyana, choncho funsani wopereka chithandizo m'malo mongoganiza kuti ubale wa m'banja umakupatsani mwayi wodziwa zonse.

Lemekezani zimene munthuyo akufuna pa nkhani ya amene ayenera kulandira uthenga. Gawani zokhazo zimene zikufunika pa ntchito imene mwagwirizana, ndipo sungani manambala olumikizirana kapena zolemba mwachinsinsi. Ngati pali chikalata cha malamulo kapena ufulu wokhazikika, funsani katswiri woyenerera m'dera limene munthuyo amakhala kuti akufotokozereni zimene chimakhudza. Buku ili silitsimikiza ufulu wa aliyense pa malamulo.

## Kodi ndingapangitse bwanji ulendo kukhala wothandiza kwa tonse?

Funsani munthuyo mmene angafunire kugwiritsa ntchito nthawiyo. Siyani mpata wodyera limodzi, kuyenda, kumvetsera nyimbo, kapena kukambirana modekha, osati nthawi zokaonana ndi adotolo ndi zinthu zina zokha.

Ngati munthuyo akufuna thandizo lokonza zinthu, gwiritsani ntchito mbali ina ya ulendowo kukumana ndi othandiza apafupi, kulankhula ndi katswiri wa zaumoyo, kapena kuona ngati dongosolo lina likuyenda bwino. Funsani musanayambe kuyang'ana malo aumwini kapena zolemba. Gawanani nkhawa modekha ndipo yang'anani pa kusintha kwenikweni, osati kuweruza mmene wina akukwanitsira.

[Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kugwiritsa ntchito maulendo kuti mulumikizanenso komanso mugwirizane ndi osamalira apafupi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ngati mwaona chinachake chokudetsani nkhawa, kambiranani ndi munthuyo komanso anthu amene amawakhulupirira. Ulendo umodzi sungasonyeze chithunzi chonse cha thanzi kapena luso la munthu.

## Bwanji ngati zosowa za munthuyo kapena thandizo lasintha?

Onaninso dongosololi pamene munthuyo wapempha thandizo losiyana, ntchito ina ya tsiku ndi tsiku yayamba kukhala yovuta, kapena wothandiza wa pafupi sangathe kupitiriza. Funsani zimene munthuyo akufuna kupitiriza kuchita komanso ntchito iti ikufunika chisamaliro tsopano. Dokotala, wogwira ntchito za chisamaliro cha anthu, kapena bungwe la chithandizo cha akulu m'deralo angathandize kupeza njira zomwe zilipo. Kuti mudziwe zambiri za thandizo lapafupi, onani mabuku athu okhudza [chisamaliro cha kunyumba](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) ndi [kukhala paokha ndi matenda a kuiwala](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/).

Onetsetsani kuti pali munthu wa pafupi amene angayankhe pakachitika vuto lofunika mwachangu. Buku lathu la [kukonzekera zadzidzidzi](/caregiving/emergency-planning-dementia/) lingathandize munthuyo ndi othandiza amene wasankha kulemba manambala olumikizirana komanso dongosolo lina lothandizira. Pa nkhani za kusagwirizana pa amene ayenera kugwira ntchito iti, onani [kukambirana kwa m'banja za maudindo a chisamaliro](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/).

Kutalikirana kungachepetse thandizo limene mungapereke pamaso pa munthu, koma sikutanthauza kuchuluka kwa chikondi cha munthu kapena zimene angathandize. Sungani dongosololi mogwirizana ndi zimene munthuyo akufuna, ntchito zenizeni, komanso thandizo limene lilipo pafupi.
