M'banja mwanu mukakhala munthu yemwe ali ndi dementia, achibale akhoza kusagwirizana pa nkhani za chitetezo, chisamaliro kapena zam'tsogolo. Munthu wina angaone zovuta za tsiku ndi tsiku; wina angamachezere mochepa kapena kumakumbukira munthuyo mmene analili kale. Ndalama, mtunda, ntchito komanso maudindo a m'banja zingawonjezere nkhawa. Mikangano imapweteka, koma pa yokha siimasonyeza kuti wina amasamalira pang'ono.

[Alzheimer's Association guide to family conflicts](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts) imalimbikitsa kumvetsera popanda kuimbana mlandu, kukambirana ntchito zenizeni za chisamaliro ndikubwereranso ku zokambiranazo pamene zosowa zasintha. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/news/how-do-i-navigate-changes-my-relationships) imanenanso kuti mabanja akhoza kusagwirizana pa zisankho za chisamaliro ndipo angafunike thandizo lakunja. Chitsogozo ichi chimapereka njira yokambirana; sichithetsa nkhani za ulamuliro wa zamalamulo kapena kusankha chisamaliro choyenera munthu wina.

## Kodi tingamuike bwanji munthuyo pakati pa zonse?

Mufunseni munthu amene ali ndi dementia zomwe zimamukhudza komanso mmene akufunira kutenga nawo mbali. Kudziwika kuti ali ndi dementia sikumachotsa malo ake pa zokambirana zokhudza moyo wake wa tsiku ndi tsiku. Mufunseni kuti akufuna ndani atenge nawo mbali komanso uthenga uti womwe angamasuke kugawana. Ngati akufuna thandizo pofotokoza zomwe akufuna, mupatseni nthawi ndipo kambiranani chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi.

[Chitsogozo cha njira zoyamba pambuyo pozindikira matenda](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) chili ndi malingaliro othandiza kumuphatikiza munthuyo pa kukonzekera zam'tsogolo. Ngati munthuyo wasankha munthu woti amuthandize popanga zisankho, muphatikizeni munthuyo pa zokambirana pomwe mukulemekeza chinsinsi ndi zokhumba za munthuyo. Ngati anthu a m'banja akusagwirizana pa ulamuliro wa zamalamulo popanga zisankho, funsani katswiri woyenerera wa m'dera lanu m'malo mongoganizira.

## Kodi tingayambe bwanji kukambirana kwa banja?

Sankhani nthawi yabata musanakhale pamavuto akulu, ngati n'kotheka. Sankhani nkhani imodzi yokha yokambirana, monga yemwe angapereke mayendedwe kapena mmene mungakonzekere ulendo wotsatira wa kwa dokotala. Mndandanda wautali wa zisankho zovuta ungapangitse kuti zikhale zovuta kwa aliyense kumvetsera.

Musanakambirane, lembani zomwe mwaona komanso zomwe mukungoganizira. Mwachitsanzo, “Bambo anaphonya maulendo awiri a kwa dokotala mwezi uno” ndi chinthu china chake chomwe mwaona. “Bambo sangathe kuchita chilichonse” ndi ganizo lalikulu lomwe silingasonyeze bwino zomwe angakwanitse kapena zomwe amakonda.

Patsani aliyense mwayi wofotokoza zomwe zikumudetsa nkhawa. Mvetserani popanda kudula mawu, kuimbana mlandu kapena kuyerekezera kuchuluka kwa zomwe aliyense amapereka. Munthu amene amakhala kutali sangawaone zosowa za tsiku ndi tsiku; munthu amene amapereka chisamaliro chachikulu akhoza kukhala wotopa kwambiri. Zokumana nazo zonsezi n'zofunika, ngakhale zitabweretsa maganizo osiyana.

## Kodi tiyenera kukambirana chiyani pa msonkhanowo?

Sungani zokambiranazo kukhala zothandiza. Ndondomeko yaifupi ingathandize banja kuchoka ndi njira yotsatira yomveka bwino:

| Nkhani | Funso lofunika kukambirana |
| --- | --- |
| Zokhumba za munthuyo | Munthuyo akufuna kupitiriza kuchita chiyani? Akufuna ndani atenge nawo mbali? |
| Zomwe zikuchitika panopa | Kodi wina anaona chiyani, ndipo liti? Kodi ndi chiyani chomwe chikufunika maganizo a dokotala? |
| Ntchito ndi malire | Kodi thandizo lotani likufunika? Ndani ali ndi nthawi komanso kuthekera kogwira ntchito iliyonse? |
| Njira zotsatira | Ndani achite chiyani, liti, ndipo banja lidzawonananso liti? |

Ngati nkhani ina ikufunika malangizo a zaumoyo, lembani funsolo ndipo mufunseni gulu la chisamaliro. [Chitsogozo cha ulendo wa kwa dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) chingathandize kukonzekera zokambiranazo. Siyani kusagwirizana pa zikalata za zamalamulo, mapindu kapena mapangano kwa akatswiri oyenerera omwe akudziwa malamulo a m'dera lanu.

## Kodi tingagawane bwanji ntchito za chisamaliro mwachilungamo?

Chilungamo sichitanthauza nthawi zonse kuti aliyense achite zofanana. [Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kupanga mndandanda wa ntchito womwe ukuphatikizapo nthawi, ndalama ndi khama lomwe likufunika](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/resolving-family-conflicts). Kenako funsani zomwe aliyense angakonde komanso zomwe angakwanitse kuchita moona.

Anthu ena angathandize pa maulendo okhazikika kapena chisamaliro cha pa munthu. Ena angakhale oyenera kukonza maulendo a kwa dokotala, mayendedwe, kubweretsa chakudya, zolemba kapena kulankhulana mlungu uliwonse. Wachibale amene amakhala kutali angathandizenso kugwirizanitsa mafoni, kufufuza ntchito za m'dera lanu kapena kukonza zofunika, ngati munthuyo wavomereza. [Chitsogozo chathu cha chisamaliro mukakhala kutali](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) chimapereka dongosolo lonse la maudindo, kupereka uthenga ndi maulendo.

Ikani ntchito zomwe mwagwirizana mu kalendala imodzi yogawana kapena pa mndandanda wolembedwa. Phatikizani yemwe ali ndi udindo, kangati komanso yemwe mungalumikizane naye ngati mapulani asintha. Onaninso mndandandawo ngati thanzi la wina, ndandanda ya ntchito kapena kuthekera kwake kosamalira kwasintha. [Chitsogozo cha kudzisamalira kwa wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) chimafotokoza njira zopempha anthu ena kuti agawane nawo thandizo.

## Kodi tingachite chiyani ngati tikusagwirizanabe?

Bwererani ku zomwe zikudziwika, zomwe munthuyo akufuna komanso funso lomwe silinathetsedwe. Simungathe kuthetsa chilichonse pa msonkhano umodzi. Gwirizanani pa chinthu chimodzi chomwe ndi chotetezeka komanso chovomerezeka panopa, kenako ikani nthawi yoti muwonenso chiganizo chachikulu.

Ngati achibale akusagwirizana pa nkhani yosamuka, pangani sitepe yoyamba kukhala kusonkhanitsa uthenga m'malo mochita mavoti a inde kapena ayi. Funsani kuti ndi thandizo liti lomwe likusowa kunyumba, munthuyo angakonde chiyani komanso ndi njira ziti zomwe banja likufuna kuphunzira. Kuchezera nyumba yosamalira kapena kukambirana ndi wopereka chisamaliro kunyumba kungathandize pa zokambirana popanda kumukakamiza munthuyo kusamuka. [Chitsogozo cha chisamaliro cha nyumba yosamalira](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/) chili ndi mafunso ofananizira njira zosiyanasiyana.

Ngati mikangano ikupitirira, pemphani munthu wina wodalirika kuti athandize. Kutengera ndi nkhaniyo, akhoza kukhala dokotala wa munthuyo, wothandiza pa za anthu, wolangiza, mkhalapakati kapena mtsogoleri wachipembedzo. Gulu lothandizana lingaperekenso malo oti achibale alankhule ndi anthu omwe akukumana ndi kusintha kofanana. Malangizo a akatswiri ndi ofunika kwambiri pa mafunso a zaumoyo, zamalamulo kapena zachuma.

Ngati mikangano ya m'banja ikukhudza chitetezo kapena chisamaliro cha munthuyo, funsani ntchito yoyenerera ya m'dera lanu kuti ikuthandizeni. Musasiye munthuyo pakati pa mkangano. Ngati pali ngozi yachangu, gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zadzidzidzi za m'dera lanu.

Mabanja sangagwirizane nthawi zonse, ndipo kukambirana kamodzi sikungathetse nkhaniyo. Pitirizani kumvetsera munthuyo, pangani malonjezo omveka bwino ndipo pemphani thandizo wachibale mmodzi asananyamule zonse yekha.
