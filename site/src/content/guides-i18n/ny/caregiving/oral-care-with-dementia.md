Kusamalira mkamwa ndi nkhani yaumwini. Munthu amene akukhala ndi vuto la kuiwalika angaiwale chizolowezi chake chodziwika, angaone kuti kugwiritsa ntchito burashi ya mano n'kotopetsa kapena kusamva bwino munthu wina akamuthandiza. Sungani chizolowezi kukhala modekha komanso mwaulemu. Munthuyo ayenera kuchita zimene angathe komanso zimene akufuna kuchita.

[Alzheimer's Association dental-care guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dental-care) ndi [Dementia Australia's dental-care advice](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/dental-care) amalimbikitsa kugwiritsa ntchito chizolowezi chodziwika, malangizo achidule ndi thandizo la dokotala wa mano mavuto akabuka. Tsambali ndi chidziwitso cha anthu onse, osati malangizo a mano kapena ndondomeko yosamalira munthu wina wake.

## Kodi ndingasunge bwanji chisamaliro cha mkamwa cha tsiku ndi tsiku kukhala chodziwika?

Gwiritsani ntchito nthawi ndi malo omwe munthuyo amazolowera ngati zikugwirabe ntchito. Ikani burashi ya mano, mankhwala otsukira mano ndi thaulo pomwe munthuyo amayembekezera kuzipeza. Fotokozani zimene mukuchita musanayambe, kenako perekani chizindikiro chimodzi chosavuta panthawi imodzi. “Tenga burashi ya mano” n'zosavuta kumvera kusiyana ndi malangizo ambiri nthawi imodzi.

Patsani munthuyo nthawi yochitira sitepe iliyonse. Ngati angathe kutsuka yekha, muloleni. Ngati kukumbutsa kokha n'kokwanira, dikirani musanapereke thandizo lina. Anthu ena amaona kuti n'kosavuta kukhala pa sinki kapena kugwiritsa ntchito burashi yokhala ndi chogwirira chomwe angachigwire mosavuta. Funsani dokotala wa mano za njira zina ngati burashi yanthawi zonse ndi yovuta kugwiritsa ntchito.

Musasandutse kutsuka mano kukhala mayeso kapena mpikisano. Ngati munthuyo akuti ayi, akubwerera m'mbuyo kapena wakwiya, imani. Yesaninso nthawi ina, sinthani malo kapena funsani dokotala wa mano upangiri. Musakakamize burashi mkamwa mwa munthu kapena kumugwira kuti amalize ntchitoyo.

Ngati chizolowezi chomwe chinkagwira ntchito kale chayamba kuvuta, onani sitepe yomwe ikuvuta. Munthuyo sangadziwe choti achite ndi burashi, sangakonde mankhwala atsopano otsukira mano, angakhale atatopa kapena kumva kusamva bwino mkamwa. Izi ndi zotheka, osati kudziwa kuti ndi vuto linalake. Kulemba mwachidule za nthawi yomwe vutolo limachitika kungathandize dokotala wa mano kukambirana zoti achite potsatira. Sinthani chinthu chimodzi panthawi imodzi kuti zikhale zomveka zimene munthuyo amakonda.

## Kodi ndingathandize bwanji ndikuteteza ulemu wake?

Funsani musanathandize, ngakhale munthuyo wakhala akufunika thandizo m'mbuyomu. Muuzeni zimene mukufuna kuchita ndipo dikirani yankho lake. Ngati wavomera, tsekani chitseko, gwiritsani ntchito thaulo kuteteza zovala zake ndipo onetsani gawo lofunika lokha. Gwirizanani kuti ndi ndani amene angamuthandize yemwe munthuyo amasangalala naye.

Ngati munthuyo akufunika thandizo la manja, fotokozani sitepe iliyonse musanayichite ndipo imani ngati akuonetsa kuti akuvutika. Dokotala wa mano angasonyeze njira yotetezeka yosamalirira zosowa za munthuyo. Musayesere njira yothandizira ndi manja kapena kugwiritsa ntchito njira yomwe simunaphunzitsidwe ndi katswiri woyenerera.

## Kodi ndiyenera kudziwa chiyani za mano ochotsa?

Sungani mano ochotsa pamalo omwe munthuyo amazolowera, omwe amawoneka bwino. Funsani dokotala wa mano momwe angawatsukire ndi kuwasungira, komanso ngati kukula kwake kuyenera kuyang'anidwa ngati munthuyo akuoneka kuti sakumva bwino. Ngati mano ochotsa amavalidwa kumalo osamalirako anthu, lembani chizindikiro pa chotengeracho kuti ogwira ntchito azitha kuchizindikira.

Musakakamize mano ochotsa mkamwa mwa munthuyo ngati akukana kapena akuoneka ngati akumva ululu. Mano ochotsa omwe sakukwanira bwino kapena sakumukomera angapangitse kudya kukhala kovuta; funsani dokotala wa mano zoti achite. [mealtime guide](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) yathu ikufotokoza za kukonzekera chakudya modekha komanso nthawi yomwe nkhawa za kudya zimafunika malangizo a katswiri.

## Kodi tingakonzekere bwanji ulendo wokaonana ndi dokotala wa mano?

Sungani nthawi yomwe munthuyo nthawi zambiri amakhala atapuma. Munthuyo akavomera, uzani ofesi ya mano pasadakhale kuti angafunike nthawi yochulukirapo, kufotokozera modekha kapena thandizo la munthu amene amamukhulupirira. Bweretsani mndandanda wa mankhwala omwe akumwa panopa ndi cholemba chachifupi cha zimene zayamba kuvuta, monga kutsuka mano, kutafuna kapena kuvala mano ochotsa.

Pemphani gulu la mano kuti lifotokoze zimene zichitike sitepe imodzi panthawi imodzi. Munthu wodziwika angathandize munthuyo kukhala womasuka, koma munthuyo ayenera kukhalabe mbali ya zokambirana ndi zosankha. Ngati kupita ku ofesi yanthawi zonse kuli kovuta, funsani dokotala wa mano za njira zopezeka za maulendo m'dera lanu.

## Kodi anthu ena othandiza angasunge bwanji chizolowezi chimodzi?

Ngati wachibale, wothandiza panyumba kapena ogwira ntchito kumalo osamalirako akuthandiza, gwirizanani ndi munthuyo pa chizolowezi chomwe akumasuka nacho. Gwiritsani ntchito malo omwewo odziwika ndi chizindikiro chachifupi chomwecho ngati n'kotheka. Funsani ngati angakonde munthu winawake kuti azimuthandiza nthawi iliyonse.

Ngati munthuyo wavomera, gawani zokhazo zomwe zingathandize wothandiza wotsatira: nthawi yomwe amakonda, zimene angachite yekha, sitepe yomwe ndi yovuta komanso nthawi yomwe akufuna kuti thandizo lisiyidwe. Sungani cholemba chilichonse mwachinsinsi ndipo tsatirani njira yanthawi zonse ya malo osamalirako. Chizolowezi cholembedwa ndi chitsogozo, osati chilolezo chopitiriza pamene munthuyo akunena kuti ayi. Zokonda zingasinthe, choncho funsaninso ndikusintha. Ngati njira zingapo zakhala zovuta, fotokozani zimene mwaona ku gulu la mano ndipo funsani upangiri woyenera munthuyo.

## Kodi ndi zizindikiro ziti zomwe ndiyenera kuuza dokotala wa mano?

Ululu wa mkamwa si nthawi zonse wosavuta kufotokoza. Kukodzola nkhope, kukana kutsuka mano, kusintha pa kudya kapena kusowa tulo kungakhale zizindikiro, koma sizikutanthauza kuti pali vuto la mano. Uzani dokotala wa mano zimene mwaona komanso nthawi yomwe zinayambira. Dokotala wa mano angayang'ane ululu, mavuto a nkhama, mano owola kapena mano ochotsa omwe sakukwanira bwino.

Ngati munthu ali ndi vuto latsopano potafuna kapena pomeza, funsani katswiri wake wa zaumoyo kapena dokotala wa mano. Musasinthe kapangidwe ka chakudya, musagwiritse ntchito mankhwala a mkamwa kapena kuyesa njira zopomeza potengera nkhani ya anthu onse. Fufuzani thandizo mwachangu pa ululu waukulu, kutupa, kutuluka magazi kapena vuto lina lililonse lachipatala lomwe likufunika chisamaliro mwachangu.

Cholinga chake ndi chizolowezi chomwe munthuyo angavomereze, ndi thandizo lochepa momwe zingathekere. Kukumbutsa kodziwika, kukhala pamalo abwino kapena kuyesanso nthawi ina kungakhale kokwanira. Ngati vuto likupitirira, funsani dokotala wa mano thandizo loyenera pa munthuyo.
