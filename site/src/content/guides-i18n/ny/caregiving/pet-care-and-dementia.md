Chiweto chikhoza kukhala mbali ya moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku wa munthu komanso banja lake. Kudyetsa, kuyenda kapena kungokhala pansi ndi chiweto kungakhalenso ntchito imene munthuyo akufuna kupitiriza kuchita. Matenda a kuiwala angapangitse ntchito zina kukhala zovuta, koma izi sizikutanthauza kuti munthu wina ayenera kungoyamba kuzichita yekha.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer's Society okhudza kusamalira chiweto](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/how-plan-your-pet-care-after-diagnosed-dementia) amalimbikitsa kukonzekera msanga za kusamalira chiweto komanso kugwiritsa ntchito chizolowezi kapena zikumbutso. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza zochitika](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/activities-people-dementia) amatchulanso ziweto pakati pa zochitika zimene zingakhale zatanthauzo. Cholinga apa ndi kupereka chithandizo chothandiza kwa munthuyo ndi chiweto, osati kunena kuti ziweto zimachiza matenda a kuiwala.

## Kodi tingapangitse bwanji chizolowezi chosamalira chiweto kukhala chosavuta?

Yambani pofunsa munthuyo ntchito zimene amasangalala nazo komanso zimene akufuna kupitiriza kuchita. Munthu wina angakonde kudyetsa mphaka koma osati kuyeretsa bokosi lake; wina angafune kuyenda ndi galu limodzi ndi wachibale. Sungani pulaniyo kuti ikhale yogwirizana ndi munthuyo, chiweto komanso thandizo lomwe lilipo.

- **Gwirizanitsani kusamalira chiweto ndi chizolowezi chomwe chilipo kale.** Ikani chakudya mutatha kadzutsa, yang’anani mbale ya madzi mukamapanga tiyi, kapena yendetsani galu mutatha pulogalamu yodziwika ya pawailesi. Chizindikiro chomwe chili kale mbali ya tsikulo chingakhale chosavuta kutsatira kusiyana ndi ndondomeko yatsopano.
- **Sungani zinthu pamalo amodzi odziwika.** Sungani chakudya cha chiweto, chingwe choyendera kapena burashi pamodzi pamalo amene munthuyo amayembekezera, ndipo sungani zinthu zolemera kapena mankhwala motetezeka. Chizindikiro chosavuta kapena uthenga ungathandize ngati munthuyo akufuna.
- **Ikani chikumbutso chimodzi pamalo oonekera.** Uthenga waufupi pafupi ndi zinthu za chiweto kapena cholembera pa kalendala chikhoza kukhala chokwanira. Pewani kudzaza nyumba ndi zikumbutso zomwe zingapangitse munthuyo kumva ngati akuyang’aniridwa kapena kukonzedwa nthawi zonse.
- **Perekani nthawi yokwanira pa ntchitoyo.** Kuyenda kapena kudyetsa chiweto kungatenge nthawi yaitali kuposa kale. Musamukakamize munthuyo kuchita mofulumira ngati akusangalala ndi nthawiyo pamodzi ndi chiweto chake.

Ngati anthu angapo akusamalira chiweto limodzi, gwiritsani ntchito mndandanda waufupi wosonyeza amene akugwira ntchito iliyonse tsikulo. Uikeni pafupi ndi zinthu za chiweto kapena gwiritsani ntchito chikumbutso chimene munthuyo amachikonda kale. Pewani kupanga ma alamu kapena mauthenga owonjezera ngati chizindikiro chimodzi chomveka chili chokwanira.

[Buku lothandizira pa chizolowezi cha tsiku ndi tsiku](/activities/daily-routine/) lili ndi malingaliro ena ogwirizanitsa ntchito ndi mbali yodziwika ya tsiku.

## Kodi achibale kapena anzanu angathandize bwanji?

Perekani thandizo pa ntchito inayake osati lonjezo lachidziwitso. Mnzanu angagule chakudya cha chiweto mwezi uliwonse, mnansi angayende nawo limodzi, kapena wachibale angapite nawo ku dokotala wa ziweto. Ngati munthuyo akufuna kupitiriza kudyetsa kapena kupukuta chiweto, konzani thandizo mozungulira udindowo m’malo mowuchotsa.

Gwirizanani amene adzathandize ngati wosamalira wa nthawi zonse palibe kapena munthuyo akudwala. Muuzeni wothandizayo kumene kuli zinthu za chiweto, chizolowezi chimene munthuyo amakonda komanso amene angalumikizane naye ngati chiweto chikuwoneka chodwala. Onetsetsani kuti wothandizayo akumasuka ndi chiwetocho komanso kuti chiwetocho chikumasuka naye.

Ngati munthuyo amathandizidwa ndi wosamalira, funsani momwe kusamalira chiweto kungagwirizane ndi maulendo awo. Chilolezo ndi malamulo a pakhomo ndi ofunika: musaganize kuti wosamalira wolipidwa angayendetse, kudyetsa kapena kusamalira chiweto monga mbali ya ntchito yake. Gwirizanani ntchitoyo ndi munthuyo, wothandizirayo komanso wachibale amene ali ndi udindo pa chiwetocho.

## Kodi tingadziwe bwanji kuti pakufunika thandizo lina?

Onani zinthu zinazake zimene zachitika m’malo mowaweruza munthuyo potengera gawo la matenda kapena chiwerengero. Mbale yomwe imakhala yopanda chakudya mobwerezabwereza, kuyenda komwe kwaphonyedwa ndipo chiweto chikusokonezeka, kapena munthu amene akuwoneka wotopa kwambiri atatha kusamalira chiweto, zingasonyeze kuti ntchito ina ikufunika thandizo lina. Kusintha kwa khalidwe la chiweto, mmene chimadyera kapena maonekedwe ake ndi chifukwa chomwe mungalumikizanirane ndi dokotala wake wa ziweto.

Lankhulani ndi munthuyo za zimene waona. Angalandire chikumbutso, kuyenda limodzi kapena wothandiza pa ntchito imodzi pomwe akusunga chizolowezi china chonse. Ngati vuto la thanzi, kuyenda kapena chitetezo likukhudza kuthekera kwa munthuyo kusamalira chiweto, funsani gulu lake losamalira kapena ntchito yothandiza ya m’dera lanu kuti muthandizike kupeza njira zoyenera.

Musadikire mpaka mavuto atabuka kuti muyambe kuganiza za thandizo la zosunga. [Buku lothandizira pa kukhala wekha ndi matenda a kuiwala](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/) lili ndi njira zogwirizanira zoyang’anirana ndi thandizo lothandiza pomwe zosankha za munthuyo zikukhala zofunika.

## Kodi tiyenera kukonzekera bwanji kusintha kwa mtsogolo?

Funsani munthuyo kuti ndi ndani amene angamukhulupirire kusamalira chiweto ngati atagonekedwa m’chipatala, atachoka pakhomo kapena ngati sangathenso kuchita ntchito inayake. Lembani chizolowezi cha chiweto, chakudya chake, nambala ya dokotala wa ziweto komanso anthu amene avomera kuthandiza. Sungani uthengawu pamalo amene munthuyo ndi anthu odalirika angaupeze, ndi chilolezo cha munthuyo.

Pulani ikhoza kusintha pakapita nthawi. Iunikenso pamene chizolowezi cha munthuyo, mmene amayendera kapena thandizo lake lasintha, kapena ngati zosowa za chiweto zasintha. Ngati kusamuka kukufunika, muphatikizepo munthuyo posankha kumene chiweto chidzakhale komanso momwe angapitirizire kulumikizana nacho ngati zili zotheka. Kukonzekera msanga kumapatsa aliyense nthawi yambiri yoganizira njira zomwe zingateteze zosankha za munthuyo ndi chisamaliro cha chiweto.

## Bwanji ngati kukaona chiweto kapena kuchita nawo ntchito sikuli kwabwino?

Lolani munthuyo ndi chiwetocho apite pa liwiro lawo. Perekani nthawi yocheza limodzi pokhapokha onse akuoneka omasuka. Kucheza kwakanthawi kochepa, kukhala pafupi kapena kuyang’ana chithunzi cha chiweto kungakhale kokwanira. Lekani ngati munthuyo akuyamba kusakhazikika, chiweto chikuyesa kuchoka kapena chiweto chikuwoneka chokhumudwa. [Malingaliro a zochitika](/activities/ideas/) a patsambali ali ndi njira zina zodziwika zochezera limodzi.
