Mavuto okhudza chimbudzi angakhale ovuta kwa munthuyo komanso kwa anthu amene akumuthandiza. Onani ngozi ngati chosowa cha chisamaliro, osati kulephera. Funsani mtundu wa thandizo umene ukumveka bwino kwa munthuyo, musiyeni akhale nawo pa zomwe zikuchitika ndipo tetezani chinsinsi chake. Munthuyo angathe kuchita zina mwa yekha kapena angafune wothandiza wodziwika bwino kukhala pafupi.

Malangizo a [Alzheimer's Association pa kulephera kudziletsa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence), [upangiri wa Dementia Australia pa kudziletsa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/continence) ndi [malangizo a NHS pa kusamalira munthu amene ali ndi vuto la kuiwala](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/looking-after-someone/) amalimbikitsa ulemu, thandizo losavuta komanso malangizo a katswiri mavuto akayamba. Tsambali ndi uthenga wamba, osati njira yozindikira matenda kapena ndondomeko ya chisamaliro cha munthu payekha.

## Kodi vuto latsopano la kuchimbudzi nthawi zonse limayamba chifukwa cha vuto la kuiwala?

Ayi. Vuto latsopano kapena lomwe likukulirakulira lingakhale ndi zifukwa zingapo. Vuto la kuiwala lingapangitse kukhala kovuta kuzindikira kufunika kopita kuchimbudzi, kupeza chimbudzi kapena kusamalira zovala. Zifukwa zina zingaphatikizepo matenda, kudzimbidwa, zotsatira za mankhwala kapena kuvutika kuyenda. Wosamalira sangadziwe chomwe chayambitsa vutolo kuchokera pa ngozi imodzi yokha.

Lumikizanani ndi dokotala kapena katswiri wina wa zaumoyo pamene kudziletsa kwa mkodzo kapena chimbudzi kwasintha. Ngati kuli kothandiza, lembani nthawi imene kusinthaku kunayambira, zomwe munaona komanso ngati munthuyo ananenapo za ululu kapena kusamva bwino. Izi zingathandize katswiriyo kufunsa mafunso oyenera. Musasiye kapena kusintha mankhwala potengera malangizo a tsamba ili lokha.

Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka maganizo mwadzidzidzi, funsani thandizo la zachipatala nthawi yomweyo kudzera mu ntchito za mwadzidzidzi za m'dera lanu. [Malangizo a NHS pa kusokonezeka kwa maganizo mwadzidzidzi](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) akufotokoza kuti kusokonezeka kwa maganizo mwadzidzidzi kumafuna chisamaliro chachangu. Kusintha kwa pang'onopang'ono pa zizolowezi za kuchimbudzi n'kosiyana, koma kukufunikabe kukambirana ndi katswiri.

## Kodi ndingachite bwanji kuti chimbudzi chikhale chosavuta kuchipeza ndi kuchigwiritsa ntchito?

Pangani njira kukhala yomveka komanso yosavuta kuiona. Nyali yausiku, malo okwanira oyendamo komanso kuika zinthu za tsiku ndi tsiku m'malo odziwika kungathandize. Mawu osavuta kapena chithunzi pa chitseko zingakhale zothandiza ngati munthuyo akuzizindikira. Funsani musanasiye chitseko cha chimbudzi chili chotsegula, chifukwa munthuyo angakonde chinsinsi.

Zovala zimene munthuyo angazivule ndi kuzivala mosavuta zingachepetse masitepe owonjezera. Sungani zovala za nthawi zonse pafupi, ndipo pewani mabatani atsopano kapena kusintha malo osungiramo zinthu ngati zimapangitsa chizolowezi kukhala chosadziwika bwino. [Malangizo athu pa chitetezo cha panyumba](/caregiving/home-safety/) akufotokoza za kuwala ndi njira yomveka yopita kuchimbudzi. Funsani katswiri wa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku (occupational therapist) kapena katswiri wina woyenerera musanasinthe zipangizo za m'chimbudzi kapena kuwonjezera zothandizira zogwirira.

Chipindacho chiyenera kumveka chachinsinsi komanso chodziwika bwino. Chotsani zinthu zosafunikira zimene munthuyo angazisokoneze ndi chimbudzi kapena chinthu china. Onetsetsani kuti switchi ya kuwala ili yosavuta kuipeza. Sinthani chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi, ndipo musunge kusinthako kokha ngati munthuyo akumasuka nako.

## Kodi ndinganene chiyani ngati munthuyo angafunikire kupita kuchimbudzi?

Gwiritsani ntchito mawu wamba a anthu akuluakulu komanso mawu odekha. Funso lalifupi monga “Kodi mungakonde kupita kuchimbudzi?” limapatsa munthuyo mpata woyankha. Pewani kuyankhula ngati kwa mwana, kunyoza, kukumbutsa pamaso pa anthu kapena kufunsa pamaso pa ena ngati zingamuchititse manyazi.

Anthu ena amaonetsa chosowa chawo pogwiritsa ntchito zochita m'malo mwa mawu. Angakhale osakhazikika, kuyendayenda, kukoka zovala kapena kupita kuchipinda chodziwika. Zizindikirozi sizikutanthauza kuti munthuyo akufunika kupita kuchimbudzi, choncho mupatseni thandizo mofatsa ndipo mvetserani mayankho ake.

Ngati kupita kuchimbudzi pa nthawi yodziwika bwino kumagwira ntchito kwa munthuyo, mupempheni monga gawo la tsiku lake. Chizolowezi ndi pempho, osati lamulo. Ngati akana, musatsutsane naye kapena kumukakamiza kukhala pansi. Dikirani, mupemphenso nthawi ina ngati akufuna, ndipo tsatirani zizolowezi zake m'malo mokakamiza ndondomeko yokhwima.

## Kodi ndingatani ngozi itachitika?

Sungani mawu anu odekha ndipo tetezani ulemu wa munthuyo. Pewani kumudzudzula, kusonyeza kunyansidwa kapena kumuwongolera pamaso pa ena. Mawu osavuta ngati “Ndiuzeni zomwe zingathandize” amapatsa munthuyo mwayi wokutsogolerani. Mupatseni zovala zoyera kapena malo achinsinsi oti asinthire, koma funsani musanamuthandize.

Mupatseni munthuyo nthawi yochita zomwe angakwanitse. Angafune kusintha zovala yekha, kukhala ndi munthu wodalirika pafupi kapena kukhala yekha kwa kanthawi. Ngati akufuna thandizo, fotokozani mu masitepe achidule ndipo onetsetsani kuti akumasuka. Musamugwire kapena kumufulumizitsa pa ntchito yachinsinsi.

Mukhoza kukonzekeratu zovala zoyera zosinthira kapena thumba losaonekera poyenda naye ngati munthuyo akufuna zimenezo. Funsani musanasunge zinthu zothandizira kudziletsa pamalo omwe anthu ambiri amawagwiritsa ntchito kapena kuuza anthu ena za ngoziyo. Ngati zinthu zodzitetezera kapena zofunda pabedi zingathandize, funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo kapena alangizi pa nkhani za kudziletsa kuti akuuzeni zomwe zikugwirizana ndi zosowa ndi zokonda za munthuyo.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuchepetsa zakumwa kapena kusankha zinthu zothandizira kudziletsa?

Musachepetse zakumwa za munthuyo pofuna kupewa ngozi pokhapokha ngati katswiri wawo wa zaumoyo wapereka malangizo enieni. Alzheimer's Association imachenjeza kuti kuchepetsa zakumwa kungayambitse kuuma kwa thupi ndi mavuto ena. Anthu amene akukhala ndi vuto la kuiwala angafunikenso zikumbutso kapena thandizo kuti amwe mokwanira. Tsatirani ndondomeko ya chisamaliro yogwirizana ndi munthuyo pa nkhani ya malire aliwonse a zakumwa.

Ma pad, zovala zamkati zodzitetezera kapena zofunda pabedi zosalowa madzi ndi zisankho zaumwini. Zingathandize anthu ena, koma palibe chinthu chimodzi chokha chomwe chimayenera aliyense. Kambiranani ndi dokotala kapena alangizi pa nkhani za kudziletsa za zosankha, kusamasuka, kukula kwake, nkhani za khungu komanso momwe munthuyo akufunira kuzigwiritsa ntchito. Musamuvazitse chinthu munthuyo akakana; funsani zomwe zingamveke bwino kwa iye.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufunsa liti thandizo kwa katswiri?

Funsani dokotala za mavuto atsopano, omwe akukulirakulira kapena owawa okhudza chimbudzi. Matenda, kudzimbidwa, zotsatira za mankhwala kapena vuto lina la thanzi zingafunike chisamaliro. Bweretsani kufotokoza kwachidule kwa zomwe zasintha komanso nthawi yake. Alangizi pa nkhani za kudziletsa angathandizenso ndi njira zothandiza m'madera ena; funsani dokotala momwe mungapezere thandizo la m'dera lanu.

Funsani thandizo la zachipatala nthawi yomweyo ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka maganizo mwadzidzidzi. Pa nkhani zina zokhudza kudziletsa, lumikizanani ndi katswiri wa zaumoyo kuti akupatseni malangizo m'malo modziwira nokha chomwe chayambitsa vutolo. Musachepetse zakumwa, kusintha mankhwala kapena kuyamba chithandizo potengera nkhani ya pa webusayiti.

## Kodi tingagawane bwanji ntchitoyi mwaulemu?

Ngati anthu angapo akuthandiza, funsani munthuyo zomwe akumasuka kugawana. Chidziwitso chachidule chachinsinsi chokhudza mawu omwe amakonda, chizolowezi chake komanso mtundu wa thandizo angachepetse kufunika kofotokozanso mobwerezabwereza. Gawanani zokhazo zomwe wothandiza wotsatira akuyenera kudziwa, ndipo sinthani chidziwitsocho ngati zokhumba za munthuyo zasintha.

Thandizo la kuchimbudzi lingakhale lotopetsa kwa osamalira nawonso. Funsani munthu wodalirika kapena bungwe la chisamaliro kuti alowe m'malo ngati munthuyo wavomera. [Malangizo pa kudzisamalira kwa wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ali ndi njira zofunira thandizo ndi kupuma. Chisamaliro chaulemu chimatanthauza kuteteza chinsinsi cha munthuyo komanso kuthekera kwa wosamalira kupitiriza kusamalira.

Cholinga chake ndi kupereka thandizo popanda kukakamiza. Yankhani modekha, tetezani chinsinsi, sungani zisankho za munthuyo ndipo funsani katswiri za mavuto atsopano.
