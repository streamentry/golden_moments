“Ndikufuna kupita kwawo.” Kwa mabanja ambiri awa ndi mawu ovuta kwambiri kumva, makamaka pamene munthu amene mumamusamalira wakhala m’chipinda chake chochezera. Izi zimachitika kawirikawiri, zingachitike pa gawo lililonse la matenda a kuiwalika, ndipo pali njira zodekha komanso zothandiza zoyankhira. Tsamba ili likufotokoza zimene mawuwa nthawi zambiri amatanthauza, zoti munene, zochitika zimene zingathandize pa nthawiyo, ndi momwe mungakhalire otetezeka.

## N’chifukwa chiyani munthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwalika amapitiriza kufuna kupita kwawo?

Nthawi zambiri, “kwawo” ndi kumverera koposa malo. Alzheimer's Society imafotokoza kuti ndi kumva kukhala omasuka ndi otetezeka, kapena kunyumba ya kalekale, osati nyumba yeniyeniyo. Matenda a kuiwalika angapangitse zipinda zodziwika kuoneka zachilendo, ndipo munthuyo angamaone ngati akukhala mu nthawi yakale ya moyo wake, pamene kwawo kunali nyumba ya ubwana kapena malo amene analerera ana ake.

Mawu amenewa angakhalenso chizindikiro kuti pali china chake cholakwika pa nthawiyo: ali ndi njala, ludzu, akufuna kupita ku chimbudzi, akumva kupweteka, akutentha kapena akuzizira, watopa, ali yekhayekha, akuchita mantha, kapena wasokonezedwa ndi phokoso. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-agitation-aggression-and-sundowning) imatchula kupweteka, phokoso, kusungulumwa, kusintha kwa ndondomeko ya tsiku ndi mankhwala pakati pa zomwe zingayambitse kusakhazikika.

## Kodi ndinenepo chiyani akamafunsa kuti apite kwawo?

Yankhani momwe akumvera, osati mawu enieniwo. [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) imalangiza kuti musayese kumutsimikizira munthuyo kuti walakwitsa: momwe akumvera ndi koona, choncho yankhani poyamba ndi kumutonthoza ndi kumutsimikizira, kenako mosamala sinthani nkhani.

Mawu amene nthawi zambiri amathandiza:

- **Tchulani momwe akumvera.** “Zikuoneka ngati mukusowa kwanu.”
- **Mutsimikizireni.** “Muli otetezeka kuno, ndipo ndili nanu.” [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation) imalimbikitsa mawu osavuta akuti “Muli otetezeka kuno.”
- **Muitanireni kukumbukira.** “Tandifotokozereni za kwanu. Khitchini yanu inali bwanji?” Kumufunsa za nthawi zakale nthawi zambiri kumakhala kosavuta kuposa mafunso okhudza lero.
- **Mupatseni choti muchite limodzi.** “Tiyeni timwe tiyi kaye,” kapena “Mungandithandize kupinda matawulo awa?”

Mawu ofewa ndi kugwira dzanja, ngati amakonda kugwiridwa, zinganene zambiri kuposa mawu okha.

## Kodi ndiyenera kupewa chiyani?

- **Kutsutsana kapena kufotokoza kwambiri.** “Mumakhala kuno tsopano” kapena “Iyi yakhala nyumba yanu kwa zaka 40” kumamupempha kuvomereza chinthu chimene matenda a kuiwalika amachipangitsa kukhala chovuta kuvomereza, ndipo zingamupangitse kumva ngati akuuza kuti walakwitsa.
- **Kumufunsa mafunso ngati mayeso.** “Simukukumbukira?” kapena “Adilesi ndi iti?” kumawonjezera kukakamizidwa.
- **Kutsekereza chitseko mwaukali.** Ngati akuyamba kutuluka, khalani odekha ndipo yendani naye. Kuyenda pang’ono limodzi nthawi zambiri kumachepetsa chikhumbocho.
- **Kuthamangira.** Mupatseni nthawi yolankhula za kwawo musanasinthe nkhani.

## Zochitika zimene zingathandize pa nthawiyo

Mukavomereza momwe akumvera, ntchito yodekha komanso yodziwika ingathandize kusintha maganizo popanda kunyalanyaza zomwe akumva.

1. **Onani zithunzi za kwawo ndi banja.** Tembenuzani masamba ndi kunena mawu monga (“Onani munda uja”) m’malo mofunsa mafunso. Pa foni kapena tabuleti, [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) imasintha zithunzi za banja lanu kukhala masewera osavuta ofananitsa, ndipo zithunzizo zimakhala pa chipangizo chanu.
2. **Lankhulani za kwawo.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) imapereka mafunso otseguka okhudza nyumba, makhitchini, anansi ndi maholide, popanda mayankho olondola kapena olakwika.
3. **Imbani nyimbo zimene amakonda.** National Institute on Aging imatchula nyimbo zotsitsimula pakati pa njira zochepetsera kusakhazikika. Onani [zochitika za nyimbo](/activities/music/).
4. **Mupatseni chakudya chochepa kapena chakumwa.** Njala ndi ludzu zingawonekere ngati kusakhazikika. Kapu ya tiyi kapena chakudya chomwe amakonda zingakhalenso njira yaying’ono yomupangitsa kumva kuti ali kwawo.
5. **Pitani kokayenda kapena khalani panja.** Kuyenda ndi kuwala kwa masana kumathandiza, makamaka masana.
6. **Mupatseni ntchito yodziwika.** Kupinda zovala, kusanja mipeni ndi mafoloko kapena kuthirira zomera kungabwezeretse kumverera kwa kusamalira nyumba.
7. **Chepetsani zonse.** Zimitsani TV, chepetsani phokoso ndi kuwala kowala kwambiri, ndipo yesani kupuma modekha limodzi ndi [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).

Ngati lingaliro lina silikuyenda bwino, yesani lina nthawi ina. Kukhala chete limodzi ndi zotsatira zabwino nazonso.

Alzheimer's Society imalimbikitsa kulemba mwachidule nthawi imene kufunsako kumachitika, monga asanadye, pambuyo pa alendo, kapena dzuwa likamalowa. Zizolowezi zimathandiza kukonzekera pasadakhale.

## Kodi n’zabwino kunena zabodza zazing’ono?

Iyi ndi nkhani imene anthu amakambirana kwambiri, ndipo mabanja achifundo amafika pa maganizo osiyana. Alzheimer's Society imanena kuti palibe yankho limodzi lokha loyenera: zonena zosiyana ndi zoona zingapangitse ena kukhala okayikira kwambiri, choncho sankhani chimene chili chabwino kwa munthuyo ndipo khalani osasinthasintha. Ku Penn Memory Center, maganizo a mtsogoleri wake ndi akuti choonadi ndicho chiyenera kukhala choyamba ndipo kunena zosiyana pang’ono ndi zoona kungakhale kovomerezeka pokhapokha ngati munthuyo sangathe kumvetsetsa choonadi china, ndipo simuyenera kunyoza mmene akuonera zinthu.

Osamalira ambiri amaona kuti njira yapakati imagwira ntchito: kumutonthoza moona mtima ndi kusintha nkhani poyamba (“Tikhalabe kuno usiku uno, ndipo ndili pambali panu”), popanda kutsutsana naye za komwe kwawo kuli.

## Nanga zikachitika madzulo aliwonse?

Kusakhazikika kumene kumayamba kapena kukulirakulira madzulo nthawi zambiri kumatchedwa sundowning. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-agitation-aggression-and-sundowning) imalimbikitsa ndondomeko yokhazikika, kuwala kwa dzuwa ndi zochitika tsiku lililonse, kuchepetsa kugona masana mochedwa, komanso kupewa khofi kapena mowa mochedwa masana. Kuyatsa magetsi dzuwa lisanalowe kungathandizenso. Kalozera wathu wa [zochitika zodekha madzulo](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) uli ndi zambiri.

## Kumusunga otetezeka ngati ayesa kuchoka

Kufuna kupita kwawo kungayambitse kuyendayenda popanda cholinga. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering) imanena kuti anthu asanu ndi mmodzi mwa khumi aliwonse amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwalika adzayendayenda popanda cholinga kamodzi kokha.

**Konzani pasadakhale:**

- Chizindikiro cha dzina kapena zachipatala pa mkono chokhala ndi dzina lake ndi nambala yanu ya foni, komanso zilembo pa zovala zake. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-alzheimers-behaviors-wandering-and) imatchula pulogalamu ya MedicAlert ndi Alzheimer's Association Safe and Found, yomwe ingafune ndalama.
- Kulira kwa chitseko kapena alamu, ndi maloko oikidwa pamwamba kapena pansi. Musamutsekere yekha m’nyumba.
- Chithunzi chaposachedwa cha nkhope yake, ndi mndandanda wa malo amene angapite, monga nyumba kapena malo antchito akale.
- Uzani anansi odalirika, ndipo muwapatse nambala yanu ya foni.

**Ngati wasowa:** yang’anani malo otetezeka apafupi pamene mukulumikizana ndi apolisi kapena othandiza mwadzidzidzi a m’dera lanu mwamsanga. Musadikire mphindi zinazake musananene. Nkhani ya mu 2021 ya [U.S. Department of Justice journal article on missing-person investigations](https://www.justice.gov/usdoj-media/usao/media/1134031/dl?inline=) imanena kuti mabungwe a malamulo sayenera kufuna nthawi yokhazikika yodikira; imatchulanso zizindikiro zofanana ndi za matenda a kuiwalika monga nkhani ya chiopsezo chachikulu. Njira zonenera za m’dera lanu zingasiyane. Uzani oyankha kuti munthuyo ali ndi matenda a kuiwalika, gawani malo omaliza kumuwona ndi chithunzi chaposachedwa, ndipo tsatirani malangizo awo. Ngati pali wina woti athandize, mupempheni akhale pafupi ndi foni. Musafufuze malo oopsa nokha.

## Kodi ndi liti pamene ndiyenera kuyimbira dokotala foni?

Imbani foni kwa dokotala wake mwamsanga ngati kufunsa kapena kusokonezeka kwachitika mwadzidzidzi, kapena kukula kwambiri pa maola kapena masiku. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) imanena kuti khalidwe losintha mwadzidzidzi kapena mofulumira, makamaka pambuyo pa matenda kapena kusintha mankhwala, liyenera kuyang’anidwa nthawi yomweyo. Kusokonezeka kwadzidzidzi kungakhale delirium, komwe [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) imanena kuti kumafuna chithandizo mwachangu; matenda a m’njira ya mkodzo ndi chifukwa chodziwika mwa anthu achikulire.

Zingathandizenso kufotokoza za zizolowezi zimene zikupitirira pa ulendo wotsatira wa kuchipatala, popeza kupweteka, kusowa mtendere, mavuto a kugona kapena zotsatira za mankhwala zitha kukhudza.
