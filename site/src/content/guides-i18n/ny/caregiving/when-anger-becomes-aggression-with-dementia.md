Kukwiya kungasanduke kufuula, kukankha kapena kumenya, ndipo zimenezi zingachititse mantha kwa aliyense amene ali pafupi. Khalidweli silingakhale lachifuniro. Sizikutanthauza kuti munthuyo akufuna kuvulaza wina. Yambani ndi kupanga kuti zinthu zikhale zotetezeka, kenako yesani kuona zimene munthuyo angafune.

## Kodi ndichite chiyani kukwiya kukasanduka zinthu mwaukali?

Siyani zomwe mukuchita, bwererani m'mbuyo ndipo nenani chiganizo chachifupi mofatsa. [Alzheimer's Association imalangiza anthu osamalira kuti aike maganizo pa mmene munthuyo akumvera, alankhule pang'onopang'ono komanso achepetse zosokoneza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Ngati muli pangozi yomwe ili pafupi kapena wina wavulala, pitani kumalo otetezeka ndipo lankhulani ndi ntchito zothandiza mwadzidzidzi za m'dera lanu.

- **Siyani zomwe zikuchitika ngati mungathe.** Ngati kusamba, kuvala zovala, ulendo wa alendo kapena ntchito ina ikuoneka kuti ikumukwiyitsa munthuyo, siyani. Zingadikire pokhapokha ngati pali zofunika zachangu zokhudza chitetezo kapena thanzi.
- **Mupatseni mpata.** Bwererani m'mbuyo ndipo siyani njira yoti aliyense athe kuchokapo. Musamupanikize pakona, musamutseke njira, musamuyandikire kwambiri kapena kumukhudza modzidzimutsa.
- **Lankhulani mofatsa komanso mwachidule.** Mungathe kunena kuti, “Ndikuona kuti mwakhumudwa. Ndikupatsani mpata.” Musatsutsane naye, musamuopseze kapena kumukakamiza kufotokoza.
- **Chotsani anthu ena pamalopo.** Mwana, mlendo kapena wothandizira angapite kuchipinda china pamene munthuyo akukhazikika mtima. Munthu mmodzi wolankhula mofatsa angakhale bwino kusiyana ndi anthu ambiri omwe akuyesera kuthandiza nthawi imodzi.
- **Chokani ngati pachitika chiwawa.** Pitani kumalo otetezeka ndipo funsani munthu wina kuti akuthandizeni. Musayesere kumugwira mwamphamvu munthuyo.

[Alzheimer's Society imalimbikitsa kubwerera m'mbuyo, kupewa kumugwira mwamphamvu kapena kumukhudza modzidzimutsa, komanso kuchoka kukafunafuna thandizo ngati pachitika chiwawa](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). [Alzheimer's Association imalangizanso kupempha chilolezo, kuchepetsa zosokoneza komanso kusamupanikiza pakona kapena kumugwira mwamphamvu](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Gwiritsani ntchito nambala yothandizira mwadzidzidzi ya m'dera lanu pakakhala ngozi yomwe ili pafupi; musadikire kuti vutolo lidutse ngati wina angavulale.

## Kodi ndi chiyani chomwe chingakhale chikuthandizira khalidweli?

Pakhoza kukhala zinthu zingapo, ndipo wachibale sangadziwe chifukwa chenicheni. Alzheimer's Association imatchula kusamva bwino m'thupi, kupsinjika kwa malo komanso mavuto olankhulana monga zina mwa zinthu zomwe zingathandizire.

Ganizirani zomwe zinachitika nthawi yaying'ono vutoli lisanafike:

- Kodi munthuyo anali atatopa, ndi njala, ndi ludzu, akutentha kapena kuzizira, kapena osasangalala?
- Kodi ntchito inali yovuta, yothamanga kapena yosadziwika bwino? Kodi wina anapitiriza ngakhale munthuyo akuonetsa kuti akuvutika?
- Kodi panali phokoso lalikulu, anthu ambiri akulankhula, zinthu zosokoneza kapena malo osadziwika?
- Kodi munthuyo anali ndi vuto loona kapena kumva zomwe zinkachitika?
- Kodi mankhwala, chizolowezi, wosamalira kapena thanzi lasintha posachedwa?

[Alzheimer's Society imalimbikitsa kufufuza machitidwe obwerezabwereza komanso kuganizira mmene zinthu zinalili kuchokera mbali ya munthuyo](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Mafunso awa ndi othandiza poyang'anitsitsa, osati umboni woti chinthu chimodzi chinachititsa khalidwelo. Musayesere kumuyang'ana, kumugwira mwamphamvu kapena kumupatsa mankhwala nokha.

## Kodi ndingachepetse bwanji mwayi woti vutoli libwerezenso?

Gwiritsani ntchito zomwe mwaona kuti musinthe momwe mungachitire naye nthawi yotsatira. Izi zingathandize, koma sizingatsimikizire kuti khalidwelo silidzachitikanso. Yesani kusintha chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi kuti zikhale zosavuta kuona ngati munthuyo akuoneka kuti ali bwino.

- **Sankhani nthawi yabata.** Ngati ntchito ina nthawi zambiri imavuta pamene munthuyo watopa, ganizirani ngati nthawi ina ingakhale yabwino.
- **Pangani malo kukhala osavuta.** Zimitsani phokoso lakumbuyo, chepetsani chiwerengero cha anthu m'chipindamo ndipo konzani zomwe mukufuna musanayambe.
- **Fotokozani chinthu chimodzi ndipo dikirani.** Yandikirani kuchokera kutsogolo, nenani zomwe mukuchita, pemphani chilolezo musanathandize ndipo mupatseni nthawi kuti ayankhe.
- **Lekani mukazindikira kuti akuvutika.** Kukana, kubwerera m'mbuyo, nkhope yolimba kapena mawu okwera kungakhale chizindikiro choti musiye kaye. Musaone kuti kumaliza ntchito kuli kofunika kuposa kumva bwino kwa munthuyo.
- **Gawanani dongosolo losavuta.** Ngati anthu angapo akusamalira munthuyo, gwirizanani pa zizindikiro zomwe zikutanthauza “siyani kaye”, mawu ofatsa oti mugwiritse ntchito komanso amene mungalankhulane naye ngati machitidwewo asintha.

Kuti mupeze malingaliro okhudza ntchito zinazake, onani malangizo athu okhudza [kusamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/) ndi [kuvala zovala](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/). Malangizo okhudza [njira zolankhulirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) akufotokoza njira zolankhulira ndi kumvetsera popanda kuwonjezera kukakamizidwa.

## Kodi ndingachite chiyani zinthu zikakhazikika?

Mupatseni chilimbikitso ndipo bwererani ku ntchito yodziwika komanso yosavuta ngati munthuyo akufuna zimenezo. Musamudzudzule, musamuimbe mlandu kapena kumulanga. Alzheimer's Society imanena kuti munthuyo angakhalebe wokhumudwa ngakhale khalidwelo litatha, ngakhale sakukumbukira zomwe zinachitika.

Ngati mukufuna kumvetsetsa machitidwe obwerezabwereza, lembani mwachidule m'malo achinsinsi zomwe zinachitika nthawi yomweyo vutoli lisanafike, zomwe munaona, kutalika kwake komanso zomwe zinaoneka kuti zinathandiza. Lembani mfundo zenizeni zokha ndipo gawani ndi gulu losamalira munthuyo ngati kuli koyenera. Pewani kujambula munthuyo kapena kugawana zinthu zachinsinsi popanda chilolezo.

Ngati ntchito ina nthawi zambiri imabweretsa mavuto, sinthani nthawi kapena njira, kapena pemphani munthu wina wodalirika kuti athandize. Mungavomere kukana kwake ndipo muyesenso nthawi ina. Malangizo athu okhudza [zoyenera kuchita munthu akakana](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) akupereka njira zina zosiyira kaye popanda kukakamiza.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufunsira upangiri wa zachipatala liti?

Lankhulani ndi dokotala wa munthuyo za ukali watsopano, wodzidzimutsa, wobwerezabwereza kapena wokulirakulira. [Alzheimer's Association imalangiza kuti munthu ayang'anidwe ndi dokotala pakakhala kusintha kwa khalidwe, makamaka ngati kwachitika mwadzidzidzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Kupweteka, kudwala, tulo, malo okhalamo ndi mankhwala zingafunike kuunikidwanso. Katswiri wa zachipatala angayang'ane momwe zinthu zilili kwa munthuyo.

Funsani thandizo lachangu la zachipatala la m'dera lanu ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi, wavulala kapena akuoneka kuti akudwala kwambiri. Ngati inu kapena wina muli pangozi yomwe ili pafupi, lankhulani ndi ntchito zothandiza mwadzidzidzi za m'dera lanu. Musadikire kuti musonkhanitse zolemba kapena kuti mudziwe chomwe chachititsa poyamba.

Chitetezo chanu nachonso ndi chofunika. Uzani wachibale wodalirika, katswiri wa zachipatala kapena ntchito yothandiza anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala ya m'dera lanu ngati zochitikazi zikupitirira kapena mukumva mantha. Funsani dongosolo lomwe limateteza munthuyo komanso anthu amene ali pafupi naye. Mumayenera kuthandizidwa; kuthana ndi zimenezi nokha si lamulo.
