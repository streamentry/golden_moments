“Kodi tidzapita kwa dokotala nthawi yanji?” Patapita mphindi khumi: “Kodi tidzapita kwa dokotala nthawi yanji?” Kumva funso lomwelo mobwerezabwereza ndi chimodzi mwa zinthu zotopetsa kwambiri posamalira munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala. Zimachitika kawirikawiri, sachita izi dala, ndipo pali njira zabwino zoyankhira zomwe zingapangitse tsiku kukhala losavuta kwa inu nonse.

## Nʼchifukwa chiyani munthu wokhala ndi matenda a kuiwala amafunsanso funso lomwelo mobwerezabwereza?

Kawirikawiri ndi chifukwa cha kukumbukira. Matenda a kuiwala amakhudza kukumbukira zinthu zaposachedwa, choncho nthawi zambiri munthuyo sakumbukira kuti anafunsapo, kapena sakumbukira yankho. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/alzheimers-today-volume-17-number-3/navigating-difficult-behaviors/) imafotokoza kuti kufunsa mafunso mobwerezabwereza nʼkofala komanso koyembekezereka.

Nthawi zambiri funsolo limakhalanso ndi momwe akumvera. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) imafotokoza kuti funso lobwerezabwereza lingasonyeze nkhawa, pempho lothandizidwa, kapena kusowa mtendere ndi chitetezo, ndipo kuti nthawi zambiri munthuyo amafunika kutonthozedwa ndikumva bwino. Funso loti “Tipita nthawi yanji?” lingatanthauze kuti “Ndikuda nkhawa ndi ulendowu.” Kutopa, mantha, phokoso ndi kusintha kwa zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku zingayambitse izi, ndipo funso lobwerezabwereza lingasonyezenso chosowa china, monga kupita ku chimbudzi ([Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/)).

## Kodi ndiyenera kunena chiyani?

Yankhani modekha komanso mwachidule, ngati mukuyankha koyamba.

- **Sungani yankho kukhala lalifupi komanso lofanana.** “Tidzapita kwa dokotala pa naini awiri masana. Ndikhala nanu.” Yankho lofanana nʼlosavuta kulimvetsa.
- **Yankhani momwe akumvera.** “Mukuganizira za ulendo wopita kwa dokotala. Ndi masana ano, ndipo ndidzayendetsa galimoto. Palibe chimene mukuyenera kuchita.”
- **Gwiritsani ntchito mawu ofatsa komanso kukhudza mofatsa,** ngati amakonda kukhudzidwa. Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kukhalabe odekha ndikupewa kutsutsana kapena kukangana.
- **Perekani yankho lomwe akufunalo,** nthawi zonse akafunsa. Alzheimer's Association imati mubwereze yankho kangapo momwe angafunsire.

## Kodi ndiyenera kupewa chiyani?

- **“Munandifunsapo kale zimenezo.”** [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) imalangiza kuti musawadziwitse kuti angofunsa kumene. Sizingawathandize kukumbukira, ndipo zingawapangitse kumva ngati akudzudzulidwa.
- **Mafotokozedwe ataliatali.** Mawu ambiri amapereka zambiri zoti aiwale.
- **Kusisima, kutembenuza maso kapena mawu okwiya.** [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) imalimbikitsa kuleza mtima ndikupewa kusonyeza kukhumudwa. Izi zingakhale zovuta; onani gawo la kupuma pansipa.

## Zida zosavuta zomwe zingayankhe funso mʼmalo mwanu

Munthu akamatha kuwerengabe, yankho lolembedwa lomwe angaliwone nthawi zambiri limathandiza kuposa lolankhulidwa.

| Chida | Momwe mungachigwiritsire ntchito |
| --- | --- |
| Cholembedwa ndi zilembo zazikulu | “Tidzapita kwa dokotala lero pa 2 masana. Mary adzayendetsa galimoto.” Chiikeni pamalo omwe amaayangʼana kwambiri |
| Bolodi yoyera | Dongosolo la lero mʼmizere itatu kapena inayi; fafanizani ndikulembanso mmawa uliwonse |
| Wotchi yayikulu yosonyeza tsiku ndi deti | Imathandiza pa mafunso akuti “Lero ndi tsiku lanji?” ndi “Kodi nthawi yakwana?” |
| Kalendala | Chongani tsiku lililonse likadutsa; zungulirani tsiku la ulendo wotsatira |
| Zithunzi zodziwika bwino | Chithunzi cha munthu amene akubwera chingayankhe funso loti “Ndani akubwera?” |

Malingaliro awa akuchokera ku [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) ndi [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf). Alozeni cholembedwacho mofatsa (“Tiyeni tiwone bolodi limodzi”) mʼmalo monena kuti “Ndinakulemberani.”

## Awuzeni za zochitika nthawi ikayandikira

Kutchula za nthawi yokumana ndi dokotala kapena ulendo masiku angapo pasadakhale kungachititse maola ambiri a mafunso odzaza nkhawa. Family Caregiver Alliance ndi Alzheimer's Los Angeles onse amalimbikitsa kuyankhula za mapulani, makamaka omwe angabweretse nkhawa monga kukaonana ndi dokotala kapena kusamba, nthawi yatsala pangʼono kuti zichitike. Kenako fotokozani zimene zichitike kenako, pangʼonopangʼono.

## Zochita zomwe zingathandize kusintha maganizo

Mukangoyankha ndikumvetsera momwe akumvera, chochita chodekha komanso chosangalatsa chingathandize, makamaka ngati kutopa ndi komwe kukuyambitsa mafunso. Magwero onse ali pamwambawa amalimbikitsa kuwasinthira maganizo ndi china chake chomwe amakonda.

1. **Masewera achidule komanso osavuta limodzi.** [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) amasunga zithunzi zikuonekabe, kotero palibe choti akumbukire.
2. **Ntchito yodziwika bwino.** Kupinda matawulo, kusanja masokosi kapena kukonza tebulo. [Sort together](/games/sort-together/) ndi njira yofananayo pa sikirini.
3. **Lankhulani za zinthu zomwe amakonda.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) amapereka mafunso otseguka opanda yankho lolondola kapena lolakwika.
4. **Nyimbo, kuyenda pangʼono kapena chakudya chopepuka.** National Institute on Aging imatchula izi ngati njira zosavuta zosinthira maganizo.
5. **Kupuma modekha.** Khalani limodzi, yangʼanani pawindo, kapena yesani [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) limodzi.

Ngati abwereranso ku funsolo, liyankheninso, mwachifundo. Palibe vuto.

## Yangʼanani zomwe zimachitika mobwerezabwereza

Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kuyangʼana ngati mafunso amabwera pa nthawi zina za tsiku, akakhala ndi anthu ena kapena mʼmalo ena. Ngati amayamba nthawi zonse alendo asanafike, kapena masana otsiriza, mungakonzekere pasadakhale: cholembedwa chokhudza ulendowo, chochita chodziwika bwino chokonzeka, kapena chipinda chabata. Kalozera wathu wa [zochita zabata masana otsiriza](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) ungathandize ngati madzulo ndi nthawi yovuta kwambiri.

## Dzisamalireni nokha

Kuyankha funso lomwelo kwa nthawi ya makumi awiri kumatopetsa. Ngati mukumva kuti kuleza mtima kwanu kukutha ndipo munthuyo ali otetezeka, lowani mʼchipinda china kwa mphindi zochepa ndikupuma, monga momwe [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf) imalangizira. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) imalimbikitsa osamalira kupempha thandizo ndiganizira zopuma kwakanthawi. Malingaliro ena ali mu [kalozera wathu wodzisamalira kwa osamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).

## Kodi ndiyenera kuimbira foni dokotala liti?

Imbani foni kwa dokotala wawo mwamsanga ngati mafunso, chisokonezo kapena khalidwe lasintha mwadzidzidzi kapena laipirakulatu mʼmaola kapena masiku ochepa. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) imati khalidwe losintha mwadzidzidzi kapena mofulumira, makamaka pambuyo pa matenda opatsirana kapena kusintha kwa mankhwala, liyenera kudziwitsidwa kwa dokotala nthawi yomweyo. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/delirium) imafotokoza kuti matenda opatsirana, mankhwala, kusowa madzi mʼthupi, ululu kapena kudzimbidwa kungayambitse delirium, chisokonezo chadzidzidzi komanso chachikulu chomwe chimafunika chithandizo chofulumira. Imbani 911 ngati wina ali pa ngozi.
