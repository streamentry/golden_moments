Ngati munthu amene ali ndi dementia sakukudziwaninso, nthawi imeneyi ingakhale yopweteka komanso yosokoneza. Mungamadabwe zoti munene kapena ngati muyenera kufotokozanso ubale wanu. Zochitika zimakhala zosiyana. Yambani ndi zomwe zikuoneka kuti zikuthandiza munthuyo kumva otetezeka komanso omasuka, osati kumuyesa zomwe akukumbukira.

[Buku la Alzheimer’s Association lonena za kuiwala ndi kusokonezeka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/memory-loss-confusion) limatchulapo kulephera kuzindikira achibale ngati chimodzi mwa zosintha zimene zingachitike. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) ndi [Alzheimer’s Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/how-dementia-changes-perception) akufotokoza kuti munthu angakuwoneni molakwika ngati munthu wina amene amamudziwa. Uwu ndi upangiri wa zambiri; sungakuuzeni chifukwa chake kusintha kumeneku kukuchitika kwa munthu winawake.

## N’chiyani chingakhale chikuchitika ngati munthuyo sakundidziwa?

Dementia ingakhudze kukumbukira komanso mmene munthu amatanthauzira zomwe akuona. Wina angakuwoneni ngati wachibale wina kapena angakhale wosatsimikiza za ubale wanu. Izi sizikutanthauza ndendende mmene akukumverani. Yang’anani pa chitonthozo chake ndi mmene akuyankhira pa nthawiyo.

Kuzindikira munthu kungakhudzidwenso ndi kuwala kochepa, mavuto a maso kapena a makutu, matenda, zotsatira za mankhwala, malo achilendo kapena kusintha kwa zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku. Izi ndi zinthu zomwe zingachitike, osati njira yodziwira chomwe chayambitsa vutoli. Ngati kusintha kumeneku ndi kwatsopano kapena kukupitiriza kumupweteka munthuyo, uzani dokotala wake zomwe zinachitika ndipo funsani ngati ayenera kuyesedwa.

Ngati chipinda chili ndi mdima kapena muli phokoso, yesani kusintha chinthu chimodzi: yatsani nyali, chepetsani phokoso lakumbuyo kapena pitani kumalo odziwika. Funsani ngati munthuyo akufuna magalasi ake kapena zida zomvera ngati amazigwiritsa ntchito. Njirazi zingathandize, koma sizingafotokoze nthawi zonse chifukwa chake sakukudziwani.

## Ndinganene chiyani pa nthawiyo?

Yandikirani pamalo pomwe munthuyo angakuoneni ndipo lankhulani ndi mawu ofatsa komanso odziwika. Nenani dzina lanu ndi ubale wanu m’njira yosavuta, monga momwe mungamulankhulire tsiku lina: “Moni, ndine Alex, mwana wanu wamkazi.” Kenako imani pang’ono ndipo onani mmene akuyankhira.

- **Ngati akuoneka kuti ali bwino,** pitirizani kukambirana mwachibadwa. Lankhulani za zomwe zikuchitika panopa, kapena funsani ngati angafune kukhala nanu limodzi.
- **Ngati akuoneka kuti sakutsimikiza,** mupatseni chitsimikizo chachidule: “Muli otetezeka. Ndili nanu pano.” Mupatseni nthawi yoyankha.
- **Ngati akuoneka kuti akuopa kapena akukupemphani kuti muchoke,** bwererani m’mbuyo ndipo mupatseni mpata. Musamukakamize kukukumbatirani, musaumirire kuti akudziweni kapena kubwerezabwereza zomwe zikuoneka kuti zikumuipira.
- **Ngati akukutchulani dzina lina,** mungadzidziwitse kamodzi popanda kusintha kukhala kumuwongolera. Ngati akupitiriza kukhala omasuka, pitani ku nkhani ina kapena chochitika chomwe amasangalala nacho.

[Uthenga wa malangizo a kulankhulana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) umalimbikitsa mawu achidule, ofatsa komanso kupewa mafunso oyesa kapena mikangano. Yesetsani kupewa kufunsa kuti, “Mukukumbukira kuti ndine ndani?” Funso limeneli lingamveke ngati mayeso ndipo silingathandize munthuyo kukudziwani.

## Kodi chinthu chodziwika chingathandize?

Nthawi zina chinthu chodziwika chikhoza kuthandiza munthuyo kukukumbukirani, koma sizingagwire ntchito kwa aliyense. Yesani chinthu chimodzi chofatsa pa nthawi imodzi, ndipo siyani ngati chikuwonjezera kukakamizidwa.

- **Nenani dzina lanu mwachibadwa.** Mawu odziwika kapena kudzidziwitsa mwachidule kungakhale kokwanira.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito chithunzi kapena chinthu chomwe mumagawana.** Ngati munthuyo amasangalala ndi zithunzi, mungathe kuyang’ana chimodzi limodzi popanda kumupempha kutchula mayina a anthu. [Uthenga wathu wokhudza kukumbukira zakale](/activities/reminiscence/) uli ndi malingaliro ogwiritsira ntchito zithunzi popanda kumuyesa.
- **Yesani chochitika chodziwika chomwe mungachite limodzi.** Nyimbo yodziwika, chakumwa kapena ntchito inayake ingapangitse nthawiyo kukhala yosavuta. Tsatirani mmene munthuyo akuyankhira; ndi bwino kusintha nkhani kapena kumaliza ulendo wanu.
- **Chepetsani chisokonezo chapafupi.** Onetsetsani kuti chipindacho chili ndi kuwala kokwanira komanso kuti munthuyo ali ndi magalasi ake kapena zida zomvera ngati amazigwiritsa ntchito. Malo opanda phokoso angathandize ngati anthu ambiri akulankhula.

Musapitirize kuwonjezera zinthu zothandizira kukumbukira ngati munthuyo akupitiriza kukhala wosamasuka. Kufotokozera kwa nthawi yaitali kapena zithunzi zambiri kungamulemeretse. Kukhala naye modekha kungakhale kokwanira; ndibwinonso kupuma pang’ono ndikuyesanso nthawi ina.

## Ndingathandize bwanji kuti maulendo akhale osavuta?

Ngati munthuyo amakhala m’nyumba yosamalira anthu kapena amaona anthu ambiri omuthandiza, funsani anthu amene amadziwa zochitika zake za tsiku ndi tsiku za nthawi yomwe nthawi zambiri amakhala omasuka. Mungapite pa nthawi yopanda phokoso, kupita ndi munthu amene amamuzindikira kapena kubweretsa chinthu chimodzi chodziwika. Izi ndi njira zomwe mungaganizire, osati ndondomeko yomwe banja lililonse liyenera kutsatira.

Musanapite kukamuona, ganizirani zomwe zingakhale zosavuta kwa inunso. Mungakonzekere chochitika chimodzi chachifupi, kubweretsa chikwatu cha zithunzi kapena kungokhala naye limodzi. Ngati munthuyo akuoneka kuti sakudziwa kuti ndinu ndani, mutha kuyankhabe pa zosowa zake pa nthawiyo. Musadalire ulendo wanu pa ngati angatchule dzina lanu kapena ayi.

N’zachibadwa kumva chisoni, kukhumudwa kapena kukwiya. Lankhulani ndi munthu amene mumamukhulupirira, mlangizi kapena gulu lothandizira anthu osamalira ngati zimenezi zikukulemerani. [Uthenga wokhudza kudzisamalira kwa munthu wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) uli ndi njira zopempha ena kuti akuthandizeni.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufuna thandizo lachipatala liti?

Ngati kulephera kuzindikira munthu kwakhala chizolowezi chatsopano, kukuchitika kawirikawiri kapena kukubweretsa nkhawa, uzani dokotala wa munthuyo. Lembani nthawi yomwe zimachitika komanso zomwe zinkachitika, monga kusintha kwa thanzi, tulo, kuwala, kumva, mankhwala kapena malo. Musasiye kapena kusintha mankhwala potengera nkhani ya zambiri yokha.

Kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kapena kusintha kwadzidzidzi kwa khalidwe kapena thanzi kumafuna thandizo lachipatala mwachangu. [Malangizo a NHS okhudza kusokonezeka kwadzidzidzi](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) amati mupeze thandizo lachipatala nthawi yomweyo. Gwiritsani ntchito njira za chithandizo chadzidzidzi kapena zachangu zomwe zilipo komwe munthuyo amakhala.

Kusazindikiridwa kungakhale kovuta kwa onse awiri. Gwiritsani ntchito mawu osavuta, perekani chinthu chokumbutsa pokhapokha ngati chikuthandiza, ndipo lolani chitonthozo cha munthuyo chitsogolere zomwe zingachitike pambuyo pake.
