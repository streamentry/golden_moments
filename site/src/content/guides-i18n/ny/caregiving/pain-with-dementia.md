Anthu amene akukhala ndi matenda oyiwala amamva ululu, ngakhale pamene sangathe kuufotokoza momveka bwino. Munthu angagwiritse ntchito mawu osiyana, manja kapena kusintha kwa khalidwe kusonyeza kuti chinachake sichili bwino. Wosamalira angazindikire ndi kugawana zosinthazi, koma sayenera kudziwira yekha chomwe chikuyambitsa vutolo.

[Malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza ululu](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/pain) amalimbikitsa kufunsa mwachindunji ndikupeza kuunika kwa dokotala ngati mukuganiza kuti pali ululu. [Bungwe la Alzheimer's Association limanenanso](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger) kuti kusamasuka kwa thupi kungathandizeko pakusintha kwa khalidwe. Nkhaniyi ikupereka chidziwitso chachidule, osati kuunika kwa ululu kapena ndondomeko ya chithandizo.

## Kodi munthu amene ali ndi matenda oyiwala angamve ululu osanena?

Inde. Ululu umakhalabe weniweni ngakhale munthu atalephera kupeza mawu oufotokozera. Sangamvetse funso lokhudza mbali ina ya thupi, kapena angavutike kuuza wina kuti zinthu zinayamba liti kapena zikupweteka bwanji. Musaganize kuti ngati ali chete ndiye kuti sakumva ululu.

Funsani pa nthawi yabata pogwiritsa ntchito mawu amene amadziwa. “Mukupweteka?” kapena “Apa pakupweteka?” zingakhale zosavuta kumva kuposa kufotokozera kwatali. Anthu ena amazindikira mawu ngati “kukuwawa,” “kusamasuka” kapena “kupweteka.” Apatseni nthawi yoyankha ndipo landirani yankho lawo popanda kuwayesa kapena kuwongolera.

Funsani funso limodzi losavuta, kenako dikirani. Munthuyo angaloze, kugwiritsa ntchito manja kapena mawu odziwika m’malo motchula mbali ya thupi. Pewani mafunso ambiri mofulumira kapena kuwatsogolera kuti avomereze. Ngati anena kuti ayi koma mwaona kusintha, gawanani zimene mwaona ndi katswiri wa zaumoyo m’malo motsutsana nawo pa nkhani yoti ululu ulipo kapena ayi.

## Ndi kusintha kotani kumene kungakhale koyenera kuzindikira?

Yang’anani kusintha pa nkhope, mayendedwe ndi zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku zimene munthuyo amachita nthawi zonse. Angakwinye nkhope, kukhumudwa, kulira, kubuwula, kuteteza mbali ina ya thupi, kuyenda pang’ono, kugona mosiyana kapena kutaya chidwi ndi chakudya. Angakhalenso chete modabwitsa, wokhumudwa kapena wosakhazikika.

Palibe chimodzi mwa zizindikirozi chomwe chimatsimikizira kuti munthuyo akumva ululu. Zitha kukhala ndi zifukwa zambiri. Mwachitsanzo, kukana chakudya kungakhale kokhudzana ndi ululu wa mkamwa, koma kungakhalenso ndi chifukwa china. Gawanani zimene mwaona ndi dokotala kapena namwino, ndipo aloleni kuti aunike chomwe chikuyambitsa vutolo.

## Kodi ndingafotokoze bwanji zimene ndazindikira?

Lembani mfundo zochepa kuti zithandize kufotokoza kusinthako. Lembani zomwe zinachitika, nthawi imene zinayambira, kangati mukuziona komanso zomwe zinkachitika pa nthawiyo. Mwachitsanzo, tchulani ngati munthuyo akuwonetsa ululu akayimirira, akupewa zochitika zimene amazolowera kapena akudya pang’ono kuposa masiku onse. Phatikizanipo mawu kapena manja amene munthuyo wagwiritsa ntchito.

Yerekezerani kusinthako ndi momwe munthuyo amakhala nthawi zonse, osati ndi munthu wina. Munthu amene amakhala chete sangafuule akumva ululu; wina angasonyeze kusamasuka poyenda kapena kuyankhula kwambiri. Pewani kupanga mlingo wa ululu kapena kuganiza kuti khalidwe linalake lili ndi chifukwa chimodzi chokha. Cholinga chake ndi kupereka kwa katswiri wa zaumoyo zomwe mwaona zothandiza.

Ngati munthuyo wavomera, gawanani zomwe mwalemba ndi wachibale, namwino kapena dokotala amene akukhudzidwa ndi chisamaliro chake. Sungani zimenezi mwachinsinsi ndipo perekani zokhazo zomwe zingathandize munthuyo kulandira chisamaliro. [Kalozera wathu wokhudza ulendo wopita kuchipatala](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) ali ndi malangizo ena okhudza kugawana ndi ogwira ntchito kuchipatala momwe munthuyo amafuna kulankhulirana.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuchita chiyani ngati ndikuganiza kuti munthuyo angakhale akumva ululu?

Lankhulani ndi katswiri wa zaumoyo ndipo fotokozani zomwe zasintha. Ululu ungakhale ndi zifukwa zambiri, kuphatikizapo kuvulala kwakale, nyamakazi, mavuto a mano, matenda, kudzimbidwa kapena kukakamizika chifukwa chokhala pa malo amodzi kwa nthawi yayitali. Katswiri wa zachipatala angayang’ane thanzi la munthuyo ndikusankha kuunika kapena chithandizo choyenera.

Musayambe kumwa mankhwala ochepetsa ululu, kusintha mlingo kapena kusiya mankhwala ena potengera tsamba la pa intaneti. Mankhwala ena amatha kusagwirizana ndi mankhwala ena kapena kukhala osayenera kwa munthu amene ali ndi matenda ena. Tsatirani ndondomeko ya chisamaliro ya munthuyo ndipo funsani amene analembera mankhwalawo kapena wogulitsa mankhwala ngati pali chilichonse chosamveka bwino.

Mukudikirira malangizo, mutha kuchepetsa mavuto omwe alipo panthawiyo. Funsani zomwe zingawathandize kumva bwino, aloleni kupuma ndipo musawakakamize kuchita mayendedwe kapena ntchito imene ikuwapweteka. Funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo musanagwiritse ntchito kutentha, kuzizira, kukanda kapena njira ina ya chithandizo ngati simukutsimikiza kuti ndi yotetezeka.

## Kodi kusintha kwa khalidwe kungakhale ndi chifukwa china?

Inde. Ululu ndi chimodzi mwa zifukwa zimene zingachititse kusokonezeka, koma kusintha kungakhalenso kokhudzana ndi kutopa, malo osokoneza, njala, matenda, zotsatira za mankhwala kapena vuto lina la thanzi. Yang’anani zomwe zidachitika kusinthaku kusanachitike komanso ngati zikubwereza, koma musayese kudziwira nokha chomwe chikuyambitsa vutolo.

Ngati munthu wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kwambiri kapena wasiyana kwambiri ndi momwe amakhalira, dziwitsani katswiri wa zaumoyo nthawi yomweyo. [NHS imanena kuti kusokonezeka kwadzidzidzi kumafunika chithandizo cha zachipatala mwachangu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Gwiritsani ntchito chithandizo chadzidzidzi cha m’dera lanu kapena njira ya chithandizo chachangu komwe mukukhala.

Ngati kusinthako kukuchitika pang’onopang’ono kapena kukubwerezabwereza, konzani ulendo wokaonana ndi dokotala ndipo mupite ndi zomwe mwalemba. [Kalozera wokonzekera ulendo wokaonana ndi dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ali ndi njira yosavuta yolembera mafunso ndi kufotokoza zosintha.

## Kodi othandizira angayankhe bwanji mwaulemu?

Uzani munthuyo zomwe mukufuna kuchita musanamuthandize. Pemphani chilolezo musanawakhudze kapena kusuntha chovala chawo. Apatseni chisankho chimodzi chaching’ono, monga kupuma panopa kapena kukhala pamalo ena, ndipo apatseni nthawi yoyankha. Ngati akana, imani kaye ndipo funsani katswiri wa zaumoyo njira ina.

Funsani munthuyo mawu kapena mtundu wa thandizo umene angakonde. Ndi chilolezo chawo, gawanani mfundo zothandiza ndi othandizira ena odalirika kuti munthuyo asamafotokoze zomwezo mobwerezabwereza. Musalankhule za iwo ngati kuti palibe.

Ululu ukhoza kukhala wovuta kuuzindikira kuchokera kunja. Kufunsa mofatsa, kuyang’ana kusintha ndikupeza malangizo kwa katswiri kungathandize kuti munthuyo amvedwe popanda kutenga chizindikiro chilichonse ngati chitsimikizo cha vuto.
