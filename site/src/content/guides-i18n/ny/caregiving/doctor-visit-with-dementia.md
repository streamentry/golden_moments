Kukaonana ndi dokotala kungaphatikizepo zambiri mu nthawi yaifupi. Dongosolo lachidule lingathandize munthuyo ndi womuthandiza kufunsa mafunso ofunikira kwambiri, kumvetsetsa mayankho komanso kuchoka ndi zotsatira zomveka bwino. Munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala ayenera kukhalabe mbali ya zokambirana ndi zisankho momwe angafunire.

[Buku la Alzheimer's Association lokhudza kugwira ntchito ndi dokotala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/working-with-the-doctor) limalimbikitsa kulemba mafunso, kuzindikira kusintha kwenikweni komanso kupempha dokotala kuti afotokoze mwachidule dongosololo. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza kugwira ntchito ndi madokotala](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/working-doctors) amatsindika za chilolezo, kumvetsera munthuyo komanso kugawana uthenga mwachinsinsi pakafunika kutero. Tsambali likupereka malangizo wamba okonzekera, osati upangiri wa zachipatala.

## Kodi ndingakonzekere bwanji ndisanakumane ndi dokotala?

Funsani munthuyo zomwe akufuna kwambiri kuti dokotala adziwe. Lembani pamodzi mndandanda wafupikitsa wa mafunso, ndipo ikani funso lofunika kwambiri patsogolo. Ngati mndandandawo ndi wautali, funsani ku ofesi ya dokotala ngati nthawi yokumana yotalikirapo kapena kukaonananso kungathandize. Munthuyo angakonde kubweretsa uthenga wolembedwa m’malo mokumbukira zonse panthawi yokumana.

Fotokozani kusintha momveka bwino. Lembani zomwe zinachitika, nthawi yomwe zinayamba, kangati zimachitika komanso zomwe zinkachitika panthawiyo. Mwachitsanzo, “Wasiya kupita ku gulu la nyimbo la sabata iliyonse mwezi watha” ndi kothandiza kwambiri kusiyana ndi kunena kuti “Akuipiraipira.” Phatikizanipo maganizo a munthuyo pa kusinthako, ndipo musapereke lingaliro lanu ngati kuti ndi matenda otsimikizika.

Bweretsani mndandanda wamakono wa mankhwala, kuphatikizapo mankhwala a pa chipatala, mankhwala ogula popanda kalata ya dokotala, mavitamini ndi zowonjezera. Munthuyo angafunenso magalasi, zothandizira kumva kapena mayina a madokotala ena. Funsani ku ofesi ya dokotala kuti ndi mafomu kapena zolemba ziti zomwe zikufunika, m’malo mosonkhanitsa zambiri zachinsinsi kuposa zomwe zikufunika pa ulendowo.

## Kodi ndingaphatikizepo bwanji munthuyo panthawi yokumana ndi dokotala?

Funsani ngati munthuyo akufuna kuti mulowe nawo m’chipinda chokumaniramo ndi dokotala. Angafune kuti mumuperekeze koma amakonda kukumana ndi dokotala yekha, kapena angakonde kuti mukhale nawo ngati wolemba mfundo. Funsani momwe akufunira kuti muwathandizire musanayambe kukumana.

Muloleni munthuyo ayankhe kaye mafunso a dokotala. Onjezani zina pokhapokha ngati akufuna kuti mutero, kapena dokotala atakufunsani zina zimene munthuyo sangakumbukire. Muzilankhula ndi munthuyo, osati ndi dokotala yekha. Ngati akufuna nthawi kuti ayankhe, dikirani m’malo modzaza mphindi iliyonse yachete.

Munthuyo angafune kukhala ndi dokotala kwaokha kuti akambirane nkhani ina yaumwini. Lemekezani pempho limenelo. Ngati muli ndi nkhawa yomwe ndi yovuta kuyifotokoza munthuyo alipo, funsani ku chipatala momwe mungatumizire uthenga wolembedwa mwachinsinsi. Fotokozani momveka zomwe mwaona ndipo musapemphe chipatala kubisa uthenga kapena kupanga zisankho popanda kutenga nawo mbali kwa munthuyo.

## Kodi ndi mafunso ati omwe angathandize kumveketsa bwino dongosolo?

Pemphani kufotokozera momveka bwino ngati china chake sichikumveka. Munthuyo ndi womuthandiza angafunse:

- Kodi tiyenera kuyang’anira chiyani, ndipo ndi liti pamene tiyenera kukudziwitsani?
- Kodi pali njira ziti zomwe tingasankhe, ndipo ubwino ndi kuipa kwake n’kotani?
- Kodi chotsatira ndi chiyani, ndipo ndani adzachikonze?
- Kodi tiyenera kubweranso liti?

Awa ndi zitsanzo chabe, osati mndandanda womwe uyenera kukwaniritsidwa pa ulendo uliwonse. Munthuyo akhoza kukhala ndi mafunso ena kapena angakonde kuyang’ana pa nkhani imodzi yokha. Pemphani dokotala kuti afotokoze mwachidule mfundo zazikulu musanachoke.

## Kodi ndiyenera kulemba chiyani pambuyo pokumana ndi dokotala?

Lembani mwachidule zomwe dokotala wafotokoza, zomwe mwagwirizana kuchita komanso nthawi ya zotsatira zotsatila. Lembani amene adzachite chilichonse, monga kuyimbira chipatala kapena kukonza tsiku lina lokumana. Ngati munthuyo wavomera, gawani zambiri ndi wachibale kapena wothandiza amene akuyenera kuzidziwa.

Sungani mfundo za zaumoyo mwachinsinsi komanso pamalo omwe munthuyo angaziwone mosavuta. Pewani kuzitumiza kudzera mu akaunti yogawana kapena uthenga wa gulu ngati munthuyo sanavomereze. Ngati malangizo ena sakumveka bwino mukafika kunyumba, imbani ku chipatala ndipo pemphani kufotokozera momveka. Musayambe, musasiye kapena musasinthe mankhwala potengera zomwe mwalemba nokha popanda kufunsa amene anakulembelani mankhwalawo.

Musanachoke, onani ngati kukaonana kotsatira kapena kuyezetsa kwina kuyenera kukonzedwa komanso amene mungalumikizane naye mukakhala ndi mafunso. Munthuyo angafune kukhala ndi kope la dongosolo lolembedwa kapena kusankha wothandiza wodalirika kuti alisunge. Funsani m’malo mowasankhira.

## Nanga bwanji ngati munthu wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi?

Musadikire tsiku lokumana ndi dokotala la nthawi zonse ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi. [NHS imalangiza kupeza chithandizo cha mankhwala mwachangu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), chifukwa kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kungakhale ndi zifukwa zomwe zimafuna chisamaliro chachangu. Gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zadzidzidzi za m’dera lanu kapena tsatirani njira ya chithandizo chachangu komwe mukukhala.

Kusintha komwe kukuchitika pang’onopang’ono kungakhalenso koyenera kukambirana ndi dokotala, koma ndi kosiyana ndi kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi. Ngati simukudziwa ngati kusinthako n’kwadzidzidzi, funsani ku ntchito za zachipatala zoyenerera kuti akupatseni malangizo m’malo moyesa kuzindikira nokha.

## Kodi ndingamuthandize bwanji munthuyo ngati kukaonana ndi dokotala kumakhala kovuta?

Funsani zomwe zingapangitse ulendowo kukhala wosavuta. Munthuyo angakonde nthawi yokumana yodziwika bwino, kudikira kwaufupi kapena kuperekezedwa ndi munthu wodalirika. Imbani ku chipatala pasadakhale kuti mufunse za zofunika pa mayendedwe, chithandizo cha kumva kapena chilankhulo, komanso ngati mafunso angatumizidwe ulendowo usanachitike.

Ngati munthuyo akuda nkhawa, fotokozani dongosololo mwabata komanso momveka bwino m’njira yomwe imamuyenerera. Bweretsani chinthu chodziwika bwino kapena china choti achite podikira, ngati munthuyo akufuna. Funsani kuti akufuna kukumbutsidwa nthawi yotalika bwanji: anthu ena amafuna nthawi yokonzekera, pamene ena angakonde chikumbutso chaufupi ulendowo utatsala pang’ono. Gwiritsani ntchito mafotokozedwe owona omwe amagwira ntchito kwa iye.

Kukaonana kwabwino kumapangitsa munthuyo kumva kuti wamvedwa, ndipo amamvetsetsa zotsatira zotsatila. Konzani limodzi, tetezani zachinsinsi ndipo pemphani chipatala kufotokoza chilichonse chomwe sichikudziwika bwino.
