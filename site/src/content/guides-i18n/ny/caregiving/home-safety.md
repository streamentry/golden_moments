Kusintha kwa chitetezo cha pakhomo kuyenera kuthetsa vuto limene munthu amene mumamusamalira wakumanapo nalo, osati kusandutsa chipinda chilichonse kukhala mndandanda wa zoletsa. Yambani ndi chipinda chimodzi ndi vuto limodzi: njira yamdima yopita kuchimbudzi, chingwe chodutsa njira yoyendera, kapena kusatsimikiza pa chipangizo cha kukhitchini. Cholinga chake ndi kupangitsa zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku kukhala zosavuta kupitiriza.

Mndandanda wa [Alzheimer's Association home safety checklist](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/home-safety) umafotokoza za zoopsa zomwe zingakhalepo kukhitchini, kubafa, m'chipinda chogona, m'malo osungiramo zinthu komanso kunja. Umanenanso kuti zosintha ziyenera kuthandiza zosowa za munthuyo ndi kudziyimira pawokha. Buku la [Alzheimer's Society's home equipment guide](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/what-equipment-improve-adapt-home-person-dementia) limafotokoza momwe zosintha zing'onozing'ono zingapangitsire zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku kukhala zosavuta komanso zotetezeka. Palibe mndandanda uliwonse womwe umagwirizana ndi munthu aliyense kapena nyumba iliyonse.

## Kodi ndiyambe bwanji kuwunika chitetezo cha pakhomo?

Sankhani chipinda chimodzi chomwe chinachitikapo kale vuto kapena chomwe chikuwoneka chovuta. Ngati munthu amene mumamusamalira akufuna kutenga nawo mbali, yendani limodzi ndipo mufunseni zomwe zikuwoneka zovuta. Wosamalira angaone chingwe chomwe sichili bwino; munthuyo angasonyeze kuti chosinthira magetsi n'chovuta kuchifikira kapena mpando wodziwika bwino wasunthidwa. Maganizo onsewa amathandiza kusankha kusintha komwe kuli koyenera.

Lembani vutolo m'mawu osavuta: “Njira yamkati imakhala yamdima chakudya chamadzulo chitatha,” “Chitseko cha bafa n'chovuta kuchipeza usiku,” kapena “Choyeretsera chimasungidwa pambali pa sinki.” Kenako sankhani chinthu chimodzi chothandiza ndipo onani ngati chikuthandiza. Simuyenera kugula zipangizo pa vuto lililonse. Kuwala kwabwino, kuchotsa zinthu m'njira kapena kusunthira pafupi chinthu chomwe chimagwiritsidwa ntchito pafupipafupi kungakhale kokwanira.

Malangizo a [Alzheimer's Society's home safety tips](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/tips-staying-safe-secure-home-dementia) akuphatikizapo zitsanzo zochokera kwa anthu okhudzidwa ndi matenda a kuiwala, monga kusunga uthenga wothandiza pamalo osavuta kuupeza komanso kugwiritsa ntchito magetsi oyaka okha usiku. Zitenge izi ngati malingaliro oti muwaganizire, osati malangizo oti aliyense ayenera kutsatira.

## Kodi ndiyang'ane chiyani m'chipinda chilichonse?

### Polowera, njira zamkati ndi masitepe

- **Pangitsani njira kukhala yosavuta kuiona.** Onani kuti polowera, masitepe ndi njira yochokera kuchipinda chogona kupita kubafa zili ndi kuwala kokhazikika. Nyali yaing'ono yausiku kapena nyali yoyaka ikazindikira kuyenda ingathandize ngati munthuyo amadzuka usiku.
- **Chotsani zinthu m'njira zoyendera.** Chotsani zingwe zotayirira, zinthu zosokonekera ndi zinthu zomwe zingapangitse munthu kupunthwa. Onani kuti makapeti agona bwino; chotsani kapena mangirirani kapeti pokhapokha ngati ikuyambitsa vuto.
- **Yang'anani kusintha kwa mulingo wa pansi.** Ngati sitepe kapena pakhomo n'zovuta kuzizindikira, funsani katswiri wa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku (occupational therapist) kapena katswiri wa chitetezo cha pakhomo kuti akuuzeni kusintha koti kungapangitse kuoneka bwino popanda kupanga mbali ina yopunthwitsanso.
- **Sungani zinthu zodziwika bwino m'malo oyenera.** Mpando, nyali kapena chinthu chomwe chimagwiritsidwa ntchito nthawi zambiri chingathandize munthu kutsatira zochitika zake za tsiku ndi tsiku. Kusuntha chilichonse kuti malo awoneke aukhondo kungapangitse njira kukhala yosadziwika bwino.

### Kukhitchini

- **Yang'anani kagwiritsidwe ntchito ka zipangizo.** Ngati padakhalapo vuto ndi mbaula kapena chipangizo china, fufuzani njira zachitetezo zopangidwira chipangizo chimenecho. Alzheimer's Association imatchula zinthu monga kuzimitsa kokha ndi zipangizo zachitetezo cha mbaula monga njira zomwe zingatheke. Funsani upangiri kwa katswiri woyenerera musanasinthe zipangizo za gasi kapena magetsi.
- **Sungani mankhwala ndi zinthu zomwe si chakudya kutali ndi chakudya.** Bungweli limalimbikitsa kusunga mankhwala motetezeka m'malo mowasiya pa kauntala. Ngati munthuyo amamwa mankhwala yekha, funsani wogulitsa mankhwala kapena katswiri wa zaumoyo za dongosolo lomwe limateteza chizolowezicho.
- **Sungani zoyeretsera ndi zinthu zoopsa motetezeka.** Zisungeni m'mabotolo kapena m'mapaketi ake oyambirira, kutali ndi chakudya ndipo pamalo osafikira ana kapena munthu akafunika kutetezedwa. Musathire mankhwala mu botolo la chakumwa kapena mtsuko wa chakudya.
- **Sungani zinthu zothandiza za kukhitchini pamalo ofikirika.** Dongosolo lotetezeka liyenera kulola munthuyo kupitiriza ntchito zomwe amasangalala nazo ngati n'kotheka. Mukasintha kabati kapena chipangizo, fotokozani kusinthako ndipo onani ngati kukubweretsa chisokonezo.

Ngati nkhaniyi ikubwera pamene mukugwira ntchito limodzi, tsamba lathu la [household tasks guide](/activities/household-tasks/) lili ndi malingaliro osinthira ntchitoyo kwinaku mukumulola munthuyo kutenga nawo mbali.

### Bafa

- **Yatsani kuwala panjira ndi m'chipindamo.** Onani chosinthira magetsi, kuwala kwa usiku ndi njira yopita kuchimbudzi. Alzheimer's Society imalimbikitsa nyali yausiku kapena nyali yoyaka yokha ngati zingathandize munthu kupeza chipindacho.
- **Yang'anani malo oterera ndi zothandizira.** Ngati pansi kapena bafa limaterera, funsani katswiri woyenerera za chinthu choyenera chosaterera, chogwirira kapena chothandizira china. Malo oyenera amafuna kugwirizana ndi munthuyo ndi bafa lake.
- **Pangitsani chipindacho kukhala chosavuta kuchizindikira ndi kuchigwiritsa ntchito.** Ikani zinthu za tsiku ndi tsiku pomwe munthuyo amayembekezera kuzipeza. Ngati amavutika kupeza chimbudzi, chizindikiro chosavuta kapena kusiya chitseko chotseguka kungathandize, ngati n'koyenera panyumbapo.
- **Musabweretse chotenthetsera chonyamulika m'bafa.** Sungani zipangizo zamagetsi kutali ndi malo amadzi ndipo tsatirani malangizo a wopanga.

### Chipinda chogona ndi njira ya usiku

- **Chotsani zinthu panjira yochokera pabedi kupita kubafa.** Chotsani zinthu pansi ndipo onetsetsani kuti munthuyo angafikire magetsi popanda kudutsa m'chipinda chamdima.
- **Yang'anani zotenthetsera ndi zipangizo zofunditsira.** Ngati bulangeti yamagetsi, padi yotenthetsera kapena chotenthetsera chonyamulika chikugwiritsidwa ntchito, onaninso momwe chaikidwira ndi malangizo ake. Funsani upangiri ngati pali chiopsezo chopsereza kapena ngati munthuyo sangathe kuchigwiritsa ntchito motetezeka ali yekha.
- **Ikani zinthu za tsiku ndi tsiku pafupi.** Sungani zovala, magalasi, foni kapena zinthu zina zofunika m'malo odziwika bwino komanso pa msinkhu umene munthuyo angafikire popanda kukwera.

### Malo ochapiramo, osungiramo zinthu ndi kunja

- **Sungani motetezeka zoyeretsera, mankhwala ndi zida.** Yang'anani makabati, malo ochapiramo, magalaja, nyumba zosungiramo zida, ndi zipinda zapansi. Sungani mankhwala m'mabotolo kapena m'mapaketi ake oyambirira ndipo sungani zida zakuthwa kapena makina motetezeka pakakhala chiopsezo china chake.
- **Yang'anani zipangizo ndi machenjezo.** Onetsetsani kuti machenjezo a utsi ndi a mpweya woopsa wa carbon monoxide alipo ndipo akugwira ntchito. Tsatirani malangizo ake pa kuyesa ndi kusintha mabatire.
- **Sungani mndandanda wa manambala ofunikira pamalo osavuta kuupeza.** Phatikizani nambala ya mwadzidzidzi ya m'dera lanu, anthu ofunikira oti munthuyo alumikizane nawo komanso uthenga uliwonse womwe munthu wodalirika angafune. Musayike zambiri zachinsinsi pamalo pomwe mlendo angazione.
- **Yang'anani njira yakunja.** Yang'anani kuwala kosakwanira, malo osafanana, mapaipi amadzi kapena zida panjira yoyendera. Pangani zosintha chimodzi pa nthawi kuti njira zodziwika bwino zisakhale zosokoneza.

Kuti mumve zambiri pa zochitika za usiku, tsamba la [sleep guide](/brain-health/sleep/) lili ndi mfundo zina pa kusintha kwa tulo ndi zizolowezi zodziwika bwino za madzulo.

## Kodi ndingasunge bwanji zosintha mwaulemu?

Chitetezo ndi kudziyimira pawokha ziyenera kugwirira ntchito limodzi. Ngati kusintha kukulepheretsa munthu kugwiritsa ntchito chipinda kapena kuchita ntchito yodziwika bwino, imani kaye ndipo yang'anani njira ina yochepetsera zoletsa. Mabungwe ena amatchula maloko pa zoopsa zina; chimenechi si chilangizo choti aliyense achitsatire. Musanasinthe chitseko kapena kuletsa njira inayake, funsani upangiri wa chitetezo cha pakhomo ndi moto wa m'dera lanu ndipo ganizirani momwe munthuyo angatulukire pakagwa mwadzidzidzi. Sungani njira yotulukira yotetezeka.

Ngati n'kotheka, fotokozani kusintha musanachipange ndipo patsani munthuyo nthawi yozolowera. Alzheimer's Society imalimbikitsa kuganizira ngati chipangizo chidzakhala chotetezeka kuchigwiritsa ntchito munthu ali yekha, ngati chikufunika kuyikidwa mwapadera, komanso ngati chingapangitse ntchito ina ya pakhomo kukhala yovuta. Funsani munthuyo zomwe waona pambuyo pa kusinthako. Ngati akupewa chipindacho kapena waleka kugwiritsa ntchito chinthu chomwe amachikonda, sinthani dongosolo.

Onaninso nyumba pamene zochitika za tsiku ndi tsiku, kayendedwe kapena masomphenya asintha, kapena pakachitika ngozi yaying'ono yomwe sinachitike kwenikweni. Chipinda chomwe chinali chabwino mwezi watha chikhoza kufunika kuwala kosiyana kapena njira yowonekera bwino tsopano. Mungasunge mndandanda wamfupi wa zomwe zasintha ndi zomwe zikugwirabe ntchito bwino, m'malo moyesa kukonzanso nyumba yonse nthawi imodzi.

## Ndani angathandize pa kusintha kwa nyumba?

Katswiri wa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku (occupational therapist) angaone momwe munthu amayendera m'nyumba ndipo angalimbikitse zipangizo kapena zosintha pa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku. Kutengera ndi vutolo, katswiri wothandiza kuyenda (physiotherapist), namwino wa m'dera, wogulitsa mankhwala kapena katswiri wa nyumba angathandizenso. Alzheimer's Society imanena kuti katswiri wa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku (occupational therapist) angapezeke kudzera kwa dokotala, chipatala kapena ntchito za chisamaliro cha m'dera ku UK; njira zimasiyana m'madera ena. Pakuyika kapena kusintha zipangizo za gasi, magetsi, zomangamanga kapena chitetezo cha moto, gwiritsani ntchito katswiri woyenerera wa m'dera lanu.

Ngati mukukhala m'nyumba yobwereka, funsani mwini nyumba kapena amene akuyang'anira nyumbayo musanasinthe zinthu zokhazikika. Ntchito ya m'dera yothandiza okalamba kapena anthu olumala ingafotokoze zowunikira kapena chithandizo chomwe chilipo. Ngati vuto ndi lofunika kuthandizidwa mwachangu, konzani chiopsezo chimenecho poyamba ndipo funsani thandizo kwa katswiri pa chilichonse chomwe simukudziwa momwe mungachisinthire motetezeka.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuwunika nyumba kangati?

Iwunikeni pamene ntchito yodziwika bwino ikuyamba kukhala yovuta, kayendedwe kapena masomphenya asintha, kapena pakachitika ngozi yaying'ono yomwe sinachitike kwenikweni. Yambani ndi chipinda kapena zochitika zokhudzana ndi kusinthako. Simuyenera kumaliza chilichonse pa mndandanda; sungani zomwe zikugwira ntchito bwino ndipo onaninso kusintha kumodzi kothandiza pa nthawi. Ngati kugwa kwachitika kale, tsamba lathu la [guide to what to do after a fall](/caregiving/responding-after-a-fall-with-dementia/) likufotokoza zoyenera kuchita nthawi yomweyo ndi zotsatira zake.
