Mabanja ambiri amaona chinthu chomwecho: m'mawa zinthu zimakhala bata, koma masana akamapita kumapeto, munthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwala amayamba kusakhazikika, kuda nkhawa, kusokonezeka kapena kukwiya. Akhoza kuyendayenda, kupempha "kupita kwawo" ngakhale ali kale kwawo, kapena kukhumudwa mosavuta. Izi nthawi zambiri zimatchedwa sundowning.

Palibe yankho limodzi lokha, koma kusintha ndondomeko ya tsiku, chipinda komanso mtundu wa zochitika zomwe mumapereka kungathandize kuti madzulo akhale abata. Tsamba ili likusonkhanitsa malingaliro othandiza, osagwiritsa ntchito mankhwala, kuchokera ku National Institute on Aging ndi Alzheimer's Association. Awa si malangizo a zachipatala. Ngati zizindikiro ndi zatsopano, zayamba mwadzidzidzi kapena zikuwonjezereka, lankhulani ndi dokotala wa munthuyo.

## Kodi sundowning ndi chiyani?

Sundowning ndi kusakhazikika, kutentheka, kukwiya kapena kusokonezeka komwe kumayamba kapena kumawonjezereka dzuwa likamalowa, madzulo kapena kumayambiriro kwa usiku. [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) imanena kuti kutopa kwambiri kungawonjezere kusakhazikika kumapeto kwa tsiku.

## Kodi ndi chiyani chomwe chingapangitse kusakhazikika madzulo kuwonjezereka?

Pali zinthu zingapo zomwe zingakhale mbali ya vutoli, nthawi zambiri zimachitika limodzi. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/sleep-issues-sundowning) imatchula zinthu monga:

- kutopa pambuyo pa tsiku lalitali;
- malo atsopano kapena osokoneza;
- "wotchi ya m'thupi" yosagwirizana bwino, zomwe zimapangitsa munthuyo kumva tulo masana koma kukhala maso usiku;
- kuwala kochepa ndi mithunzi, zomwe zingasokoneze;
- kutenga nkhawa kapena kukhumudwa kwa anthu omwe ali pafupi;
- kufunikira tulo lochepa, zomwe zimachitika kawirikawiri kwa anthu achikulire.

NIA imawonjezeranso zifukwa za kutentheka monga kupweteka, kukhumudwa kapena nkhawa, kupuma mokwanira pang'ono, kudzimbidwa, phokoso lalikulu kapena anthu ambiri, komanso mankhwala ena kapena momwe mankhwala amagwirira ntchito limodzi.

## Kodi ndingakonzekere bwanji tsiku kuti madzulo akhale abata?

Sinthirani zinthu zovuta kuti zichitike m'mawa ndipo sungani madzulo kukhala odekha komanso odziwikiratu. Onse awiri NIA ndi Alzheimer's Association amalimbikitsa kutsatira ndondomeko yokhazikika. Chitsanzo cha tsiku, chomwe mungasinthe kuti chigwirizane ndi inuyo:

| Nthawi | Zomwe zingathandize |
| --- | --- |
| M'mawa | Ma appointment, kusamba, maulendo ndi ntchito iliyonse yovuta, pamene anthu ambiri amakhala tcheru kwambiri |
| M'mawa mochedwa | Nthawi panja kapena pafupi ndi zenera ladzuwa; kuyenda kapena kuchita zochitika zina |
| Masana | Chakudya chachikulu chamasana (Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa chakudya chachikulu chamasana ndi chakudya chopepuka madzulo) |
| Masana oyambirira | Kupuma ngati pakufunika, koma pewani kugona kwa nthawi yaitali mochedwa masana |
| Masana mpaka madzulo | Zochitika zodekha komanso zodziwika; chakudya chochepa ndi chakumwa; osamwa zakumwa zokhala ndi caffeine |
| Dzuwa lisanalowe | Yatsani magetsi m'nyumba ndipo tsekani makatani mdima usanagwe |
| Madzulo | Chakudya chopepuka chamadzulo, nyimbo zofewa, zinthu zosavuta, ndondomeko yokhazikika yogona |

Mfundo zingapo zochokera ku magwero:

- **Kuwala kwa dzuwa.** NIA imalimbikitsa kukhala panja kapena pafupi ndi zenera kuti mupeze kuwala kwa dzuwa tsiku lililonse.
- **Kuyenda tsiku lililonse, koma osapitirira.** Yesetsani kuchita zochitika zolimbitsa thupi tsiku lililonse popanda kukonza zochitika zambiri, ikutero NIA.
- **Zakumwa mochedwa masana.** Pewani mowa ndi zakumwa zokhala ndi caffeine, monga khofi kapena cola, mochedwa masana (NIA). Alzheimer's Association imatchulanso chikonga (nicotine).
- **Kugona masana.** Pewani kugona kwa nthawi yaitali ndi kusinza mochedwa masana (NIA). Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kuchepetsa kugona masana ngati kugona usiku ndi vuto.

Kuti mudziwe zambiri zokhudza kukonza tsiku lokhazikika, onani [zochitika za ndondomeko ya tsiku ndi tsiku](/activities/daily-routine/).

## Kodi ndingapangitse bwanji nyumba kukhala yabata madzulo?

Chepetsani phokoso, onjezerani kuwala ndipo sungani zinthu zodziwika pafupi.

- **Kuwala kokwanira komanso kofanana.** Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kuti nyumba ikhale yowala bwino madzulo. Yatsani magetsi mithunzi isanaonekere.
- **Phokoso lochepa.** Zimitsani kapena chepetsani TV ndi wailesi, ndipo chepetsani alendo kapena ntchito zapakhomo zambiri pa nthawiyi. Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kuchepetsa zosokoneza madzulo monga TV, ntchito zapakhomo ndi nyimbo zokweza.
- **Zinthu zodziwika zotonthoza.** NIA imalimbikitsa nyimbo zofewa komanso kusunga zinthu ndi zithunzi zokondedwa pafupi m'nyumba.
- **Kuyang'ana chitonthozo.** Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kuyang'ana ngati pali kupweteka, njala, ludzu, kudzimbidwa, chikhodzodzo chodzaza, kutopa, matenda ndi kuyabwa kwa khungu, komanso kusunga chipinda pa kutentha koyenera.
- **Mmene mukumvera inuyo.** Anthu amatenga nkhawa za ena. NIA imalimbikitsa kuchepetsa kuthamanga ndikuyesa kukhala odekha ngati nkhawa zanu zingawonjezere vutoli.

## Ndi zochitika ziti zodekha zomwe tingachite madzulo?

Sankhani zochitika zomwe ndi zodziwika, zosathamanga komanso zosavuta kusiya. Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa zochitika zofewa monga nyimbo zodekha, zithunzi kapena filimu yokondedwa, komanso kuyenda limodzi kuti muchepetse kusakhazikika. NIA imalimbikitsa chakudya chochepa kapena chakumwa, pulogalamu ya pa TV yokondedwa, nyimbo, kuyenda, kuwerenga, kapena ntchito yapakhomo yosavuta monga kupinda zovala.

Malingaliro oti muyesere:

1. **Pindani zovala limodzi.** NIA imatchula ntchito yapakhomo yosavuta yotere. Matawulo ndi osavuta kugwira, ndipo ntchitoyi ili ndi mapeto omveka bwino.
2. **Imbani nyimbo zokondedwa.** Nyimbo zodziwika za nthawi ya ubwana wawo, pa voliyumu yotsika. Kung'ung'uza nawo ndi kokwanira. Malingaliro ena ali pa [zochitika za nyimbo](/activities/music/).
3. **Yang'anani zithunzi.** Buku la zithunzi, kapena zithunzi zingapo za banja mu [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ndi zithunzi zikusiyidwa zosaoneka, zingayambitse kukambirana modekha.
4. **Yendani pang'ono.** M'munda, mozungulira dera kapena m'khonde, mdima usanafike.
5. **Gawanani chakudya chochepa ndi chakumwa.** Chinachake chosavuta, chofunda komanso chopanda caffeine, patebulo limodzi.
6. **Kufananitsa mofatsa.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) yokhala ndi matailosi anayi ndi zithunzi zosaoneka ndiyosavuta kuyamba komanso yosavuta kusiya.
7. **Kupaka utoto.** [Coloring](/games/coloring/) pa sikirini kapena pa tsamba losindikizidwa, popanda cholinga chomaliza.
8. **Kuyang'ana ndi kukambirana.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) zimapereka chithunzi ndi funso lotseguka. Kungoyang'ana kokha ndi kokwanira.
9. **Kupuma modekha.** [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ndi nthawi yopuma pang'onopang'ono komanso yachete yomwe mumayamba ndi kuyimitsa nokha.
10. **Ntchito za manja.** Kusanja mabatani kapena masokosi, kusalaza bulangeti lofewa, kapena kugwira chinthu chodziwika. Onani [zochitika zolimbikitsa mphamvu za m'thupi](/activities/sensory/).

Pewani masewera atsopano, maulendo odzaza ndi anthu komanso chilichonse chomwe chimafuna kukumbukira kapena kuganizira kwambiri mochedwa masana. Zisungireni m'mawa.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuchita chiyani kusakhazikika kukayamba?

Khalani odekha, limbikitsani, ndipo muwapatse china chosangalatsa mofatsa. NIA imalimbikitsa kuyankhula modekha, kumvetsera zomwe munthuyo akudandaula, kupewa kukangana, kuwatsimikizira kuti ali otetezeka, komanso kuwagwira mofatsa ngati zingathandize. Kenako yesani kuwasinthira maganizo: chakudya chochepa, chakumwa, nyimbo kapena kuyenda.

Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa mawu monga "Muli otetezeka kuno" ndi "Pepani kuti mwakhumudwa," ndipo imalangiza kuwonjezera kuwala, kuchepetsa kuthamanga komanso kuchepetsa zosokoneza. Musawapondereze, musawazungulire kapena kuwaletsa kuyenda, kapena kukangana nawo.

Ngati munthuyo ayamba kuchita zachiwawa, NIA imalangiza kukhala patali motetezeka mpaka khalidwelo litatha. Pakagwa ngozi, imbani foni ku ntchito zadzidzidzi za m'dera lanu ndipo fotokozani kuti munthuyo ali ndi matenda a kuiwala. [Kalozera wathu woyankhira kukwiya ndi khalidwe lachiwawa](/caregiving/when-anger-becomes-aggression-with-dementia/) akufotokoza za chitetezo chapompopompo komanso zoyenera kuchita pambuyo pake.

Makalata athu okhudza [kuyankhulana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ndi [zoyenera kuchita akakana](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) ali ndi zambiri zokhudza mawu otonthoza.

## Kodi ndingazindikire bwanji zomwe zimayambitsa vutoli?

Sungani zolemba zachidule kwa sabata imodzi kapena ziwiri. Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kulemba zomwe zimachitika sundowning isanayambe kuti muzindikire zomwe zimayambitsa. Lembani nthawi yomwe kusakhazikika kunayamba, zomwe zinachitika ola limodzi m'mbuyomo, kugona masana, chakudya ndi zakumwa, alendo, phokoso ndi kuwala. Pitani ndi zolembazo kwa dokotala. NIA imanena kuti kulemba kusintha kwa khalidwe kungathandize osamalira ndi azachipatala kuzindikira mtundu wa vutolo.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufikira liti dokotala za sundowning?

Lankhulani ndi dokotala wa munthuyo ngati kusakhazikika ndi kwatsopano, kwawonjezereka mwadzidzidzi, kapena kukubwera ndi kusintha kwina. NIA imanena kuti kusintha kwadzidzidzi kapena kosinthasintha mwachangu kwa khalidwe, makamaka pambuyo pa matenda kapena kusintha kwa mankhwala posachedwapa, kuyenera kufotokozedwa kwa dokotala nthawi yomweyo.

Pemphani dokotala kuti ayang'ane zinthu zomwe zingapangitse madzulo kukhala ovuta. Alzheimer's Association imatchula mavuto a thupi monga matenda a mkodzo, kusadziletsa, restless legs ndi vuto la kupuma pogona (sleep apnea), ndipo imalimbikitsa kufunsa kuti ndi nthawi ziti za tsiku zomwe zili zabwino pa mankhwala aliwonse. NIA imatchula kupweteka, kudzimbidwa komanso zotsatira za mankhwala kapena momwe mankhwala amagwirira ntchito limodzi monga zifukwa zomwe zingayambitse kutentheka.

Alzheimer's Association imanena kuti njira zopanda mankhwala nthawi zambiri zimakondedwa pa mavuto a kugona okhudzana ndi Alzheimer's, komanso kuti mankhwala a kusakhazikika mochedwa masana ayenera kuganiziridwa pokhapokha njirazo zitalephera, mutayang'ana ubwino ndi kuopsa kwake. Chisankho chimenecho ndi cha dokotala. Musayambe, musasiye kapena musasinthe mankhwala aliwonse, mankhwala othandiza kugona kapena zowonjezera popanda malangizo a dokotala.

## Kodi ndingapirire bwanji madzulo ndekha?

Madzulo akhoza kukhala nthawi yovuta kwambiri pa tsiku la munthu wosamalira. Ngati n'kotheka, pemphani wachibale, bwenzi kapena munthu wolipidwa kuti akuthandizeni madzulo masiku angapo pa sabata, ngakhale kwa ola limodzi. Kalozera wathu wa [kudzisamalira kwa wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ali ndi njira zothandizira kupuma komanso nambala yaulere ya Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline.
