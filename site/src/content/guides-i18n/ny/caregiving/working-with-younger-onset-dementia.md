Kudziwa kuti muli ndi matenda okhudza kukumbukira ndi kuganiza mukugwira ntchito kungabweretse mafunso okhudza ndalama, zachinsinsi, kudziwika ndi chitetezo. Anthu ena amafuna kupitiriza ntchito yawo, ena amagwiritsa ntchito zosintha, ndipo ena amasankha kusiya ntchito. Palibe njira imodzi yoyenera kwa aliyense. Zomwe mukufuna, ntchito yanu, umoyo wanu ndi thandizo zimakhala zofunika.

Buku la [Alzheimer's Association pa matenda a Alzheimer's omwe amayamba ali mwana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset) likufotokoza za ntchito, ndalama za banja ndi thandizo la abwana. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead/work-and-dementia) ndi [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) akufotokoza zosankha zotsala kuntchito, kusintha ntchito kapena kusiya ntchito. Malamulo a ntchito amasiyana malinga ndi malo, choncho bukuli limapereka mafunso oganizira, si upangiri wa malamulo kapena ndalama.

## Kodi kudziwa matenda kumatanthauza kuti ndiyenera kusiya ntchito?

Ayi. Kudziwa matenda kokha sikumasankha ngati mungapitirize kugwira ntchito. [Alzheimer's Society ikunena](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/work-dementia) kuti chisankho chimadalira mmene mukumvera, ntchito yanu ndi zochitika zanu. Anthu ena amapitiriza ntchito yomweyo, ena amasintha ntchito kapena maola, ndipo ena amasiya ntchito.

Ganizirani zomwe zikuyenda bwino ndi zomwe zafika povuta. Mutha kulemba ntchito zomwe zimakudziwikani, nthawi imene mumamva kutopa kapena kusokonezeka, ndi ngati panali zolakwika kapena nkhawa za chitetezo. Zitsanzo zomveka zingakuthandizeni inuyo ndi dokotala wanu kukambirana za ntchito popanda kupanga mfundo yokhudza luso lanu kuchokera pa tsiku limodzi lovuta.

Chitetezo n’chofunika kwambiri. Ngati ntchito yanu ikuphatikizapo kuyendetsa galimoto, kugwiritsa ntchito makina, kusamalira anthu ena kapena ntchito ina yokhudza chitetezo, funsani dokotala wanu momwe mungayezere chiwopsezo. Malamulo a kwanuko, malamulo a ntchito ndi mapangano a ntchito angagwire ntchito. Tsamba ili silinganene ngati ntchito inayake ndi yotetezeka kapena zomwe abwana anu ayenera kuchita.

## Kodi ndingalankhule bwanji ndi dokotala wanu za ntchito?

Fotokozerani ntchito zomwe mumagwira ndi kusintha komwe mwazindikira. Funsani ngati vuto la umoyo lingakhudze udindo wina, zizindikiro zotani zimatanthauza kuti mufunse thandizo, ndi ngati katswiri wina angathe kuyesa ntchito inayake. Mutha kufuna kubwera ndi munthu amene mumamudalira, koma sankhani limodzi momwe angakuthandizireni popanda kulankhula m'malo mwanu.

Buku la [kukayendera dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) lili ndi njira zokonzekera mafunso ndi zolemba. Mutha kufunsa dokotala wanu kuti afotokoze zomwe angayesere ndi zomwe zikufuna kuyesa kwina kuntchito kapena kwa katswiri wa ntchito. Buku lofotokoza matenda okhudza kukumbukira ndi kuganiza silingalowe m'malo mwa kuyesa kwaumoyo ndi chitetezo kwa munthu payekha.

## Kodi kusintha kotani kuntchito kungathandize?

Ngati mukufuna kupitiriza kugwira ntchito, funsani zomwe zingatheke mu ntchito yanu. Dementia Australia akupereka zosankha monga kugwiritsa ntchito ndondomeko, kugwira ntchito imodzi imodzi, kulemba zolemba ndi kupanga kalozera wa masitepe a ntchito zomwe mumachita kawirikawiri. Kutengera ntchito yanu, muthanso kufunsa za malangizo olembedwa, kalendala, kuchepetsa zosokoneza kapena nthawi yopumula yokonzedwa.

Onani izi ngati malingaliro oti mukambirane, si mndandanda womwe abwana onse angathe kapena ayenera kugwiritsa ntchito. Sankhani kusintha kumodzi komwe kumayankha ntchito imene mumavutika nayo. Ngati inuyo ndi abwana anu muyesa, vomerezani momwe mudzawunikire ndi ngati zikuthandiza. Kusintha komwe kumagwira ntchito pa ntchito ina sikungagwire ntchito pa ina.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuuza abwana anga?

Inu musankhe zomwe mungafotokoze zaumwini wanu, pokhapokha ngati malamulo a kwanuko, pangano kapena chofunikira cha chitetezo chikugwira ntchito. Zofunikira zimasiyana ndi dziko, boma ndi ntchito. Musanasankhe, yang’anani pangano lanu, malamulo a kuntchito ndi malangizo a kwanuko. Ngati simukutsimikiza, lankhulani ndi mlangizi woyenerera wa ntchito, woimira bungwe la ogwira ntchito kapena loya amene akudziwa malamulo a komwe mumakhala.

Ngati mwasankha kuuza winawake, ganizirani zomwe mukufuna kuti adziwe ndi thandizo lomwe mukupempha. Mutha kukonda kuyamba kulankhula ndi woyang’anira kapena munthu wa HR amene mumamudalira. Funsani mmene chidziwitso chanu chidzagwiritsidwira ntchito ndi amene adzalandire; musaganize kuti malo onse a ntchito ali ndi ndondomeko yomweyo ya zachinsinsi.

Ntchito zina zimakhala ndi ntchito zokhudza chitetezo. Ngati simukutsimikiza ngati muyime kapena kusintha ntchito imodzi mwa zimenezi, lankhulani ndi dokotala wanu ndi mlangizi woyenerera wa ntchito kwanuko msanga. Musadalire buku lofotokoza matenda okhudza kukumbukira ndi kuganiza kuti musankhe ngati ntchito inayake ndi yotetezeka.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuyang’ana thandizo ndi ndalama zotani?

Musanasinthe maola anu kapena kusiya ntchito, onaninso thandizo lomwe likupezeka kudzera kwa abwana anu. Funsani ku HR kapena omwe amasamalira thandizo za tchuthi, thandizo la kuvulala kapena la ndalama, chithandizo chaumoyo ndi masiku aliwonse omalizira. Pemphani malamulo apano olembedwa ndipo sungani chidziwitso chomwe mwalandira.

Ufulu, misonkho ndi nthawi zimadalira abwana anu, mfundo za inshuwaransi ndi malo. [Alzheimer's Association ikulangiza kuyang’ana thandizo la abwana anu ndi kusunga chitsimikizo cholembedwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/younger-onset). Pa chisankho chanu, lankhulani ndi mlangizi woyenerera wa thandizo kapena ndalama amene angayang’ane zikalata zomwe zikugwira ntchito kwa inu. Pewani kusayina kapena kusiya chithandizo pogwiritsa ntchito nkhani yokha.

Banja lanu lingakhudzidwenso ndi kusintha kwa ndalama kapena ndondomeko. Ngati mukufuna, lankhulani ndi anthu amene mumawadalira za zomwe zili zofunika kwambiri ndi zosankha zomwe mukufuna kufufuza. Buku la [masitepe oyamba mutadziwika matenda](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) likufotokoza mafunso aakulu okhudza thandizo ndi kukonzekera zam’tsogolo.

## Kodi ndichite chiyani ngati ndasankha kuchepetsa maola kapena kusiya ntchito?

Kusiya ntchito kungakhale chisankho choyenera kwa anthu ena. Sikutanthauza kuti mwalephera. Mutha kuganizira za ndalama, chithandizo chaumoyo, ndondomeko ya tsiku ndi anthu amene mumakonda kuwona. Funsani thandizo la kwanuko musanasinthe, ndipo mudzipatse nthawi yoyerekeza zosankha zofunika kwa inu.

Ntchito ndi gawo limodzi chabe la moyo. Ngati muyimitsa kapena kuchepetsa maola, sankhani zinthu zina kapena maubwenzi omwe mumawamva ofunika mukakonzeka. Anthu a m’banja angathandize, koma chisankho chiyenera kukuphatikizani. Buku la [kusamalira amene akusamalira ena](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) lili ndi malingaliro opempha ena kuti agawane thandizo popanda kuika udindo wonse pa munthu mmodzi.
