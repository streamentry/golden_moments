Kuona, kumva, kununkhiza, kulawa kapena kumva chinthu chimene ena sachizindikira kungamveke ngati chenicheni kwa munthuyo. Zingamuchititse mantha, kapena sizingamuvutitse. Yambani ndi momwe munthuyo akumvera komanso ngati pali aliyense amene ali pachiopsezo, osati kuyesera kutsimikizira zomwe zinachitika kapena zomwe sizinachitike.

Zomwe zachitikazo zikhoza kukhala kuona kapena kumva zinthu zongoyerekeza, koma zikhozanso kukhala kulakwitsa kuzizindikira. Mthunzi ukhoza kuoneka ngati munthu; munthu wodziwika akhoza kulakwitsidwa ndi munthu wina. Simuyenera kusankha kuti ndi chiti chimene chikuchitika. Fotokozani zimene munthuyo akunena ndi zimene inu mwazindikira, ndipo funsani dokotala ngati mukuda nkhawa.

## Kodi ndingatani pa nthawiyo?

Khalani odekha, funsani momwe munthuyo akumvera, ndipo mupatseni chitonthozo popanda kutsutsana naye. [Alzheimer's Association imalangiza kuti muone ngati zomwe zikuchitikazo zikuvutitsa kapena zili zowopsa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/hallucinations). Ngati munthuyo ali bwino komanso ali otetezeka, pangakhale posafunikira kusokoneza zomwe zikuchitika.

- **Vomerezani momwe akumvera.** Mungathe kunena kuti, “Ine sindikuona munthu, koma ndikumva kuti izi zikukuchititsani mantha. Ndili nanu pano.” Izi ndi zoona ndipo zimamudziwitsa munthuyo kuti mukumutenga mozama.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito mawu achidule.** Kufotokoza mwatali kapena kumuwongolera mobwerezabwereza kungawonjezere nkhawa. Lolani munthuyo afotokoze zimene akuzindikira ngati akufuna.
- **Perekani mwayi, osakakamiza, kusintha.** Funsani ngati angafune kukhala kwina, kuyatsa kuwala kwina kapena kumvetsera nyimbo yodziwika. Kupita kuchipinda chowala bwino komanso chodziwika kungathandize; siyani ngati munthuyo sakufuna kusamuka.
- **Khalani pafupi ngati akufuna kuti mukhale nawo.** Musamukhudze munthuyo modzidzimutsa. Funsani musanapereke dzanja kapena kumukumbatira.

Ngati zomwe zikuchitika sizikuvulaza ndipo sizikuvutitsa, tsatirani zomwe munthuyo akufuna. Musasandutse nthawiyo kukhala mayeso kapena kumupempha kuti atsimikizire zimene waona.

Anthu ena angazimve kuti zomwe akukumana nazo n’zotonthoza kapena zodziwika. Ngati munthuyo ali odekha komanso otetezeka, pangakhale posafunikira kusintha nkhani kapena kumutengera kwina. Funsani zimene angafune; musasandutse nthawi iliyonse yotere kukhala zokambirana za mankhwala. Ngati ali ndi mantha, yankhani manthawo ngakhale simukuona zomwezo.

## Kodi chinachake m’chipindamo chingasokoneze?

Yang’anani malo omwe alipo limodzi ndi munthuyo ngati akufuna thandizo lotero. [Alzheimer's Society imasiyanitsa kuona kapena kumva zinthu zongoyerekeza ndi kulakwitsa kuzizindikira](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations), pamene chinthu chenicheni kapena kachitidwe kena kaoneka molakwika.

- **Wonjezerani kuwala.** Yatsani nyali ndipo yang’anani mithunzi kapena zithunzi zongowoneka m’magalasi ndi m’mazenera.
- **Yang’anani phokoso lodziwika.** Wailesi yakanema, fani kapena chipangizo china chingamveke ngati mawu kapena mapazi. Chepetsani kapena zimitsani pokhapokha ngati munthuyo sakudandaula nazo.
- **Yang’anani mapangidwe a zinthu.** Kapeti, katani kapena pepala la khoma lokhala ndi mapangidwe ambiri litha kulakwitsidwa ngati kuyenda kapena chinthu china. Funsani ngati munthuyo angafune kuyang’ana kuchokera pamalo ena kapena kusintha momwe akuonera.
- **Yang’anani zida zothandizira kumva kapena kuona.** Ngati munthuyo amagwiritsa ntchito magalasi kapena chida chomvera, funsani ngati angafune thandizo kuti muone ngati chilipo ndipo chikugwira ntchito monga nthawi zonse.

Ngati zomwe zikuchitika zimachitika pamalo omwewo, yang’anani chinthu chimene chingabwerezedwe: chithunzi chongowoneka pagalasi, kapangidwe kolimba pansi, wailesi yakanema ikumveka kuchipinda china kapena kuwala kochepa madzulo. Yang’anani kaye phokoso kapena chinthu chenicheni musanaganize kuti ndi kusintha kwa momwe akuzionera zinthu. Ngati mwayesa kusintha malo, fotokozani ndikusintha ndikufunsa ngati zikumva bwino.

Yesani kusintha kumodzi kochepa pa nthawi imodzi. Musasinthe malo a zinthu za munthuyo kapena kuchotsa chinthu chimene amachikonda popanda kufunsa. Zochitika zodziwika komanso malo odekha, owala bwino zingamveke bwino.

## Kodi ndiyenera kulankhula ndi dokotala liti?

Lankhulani ndi dokotala wa munthuyo za zomwe zayamba kumene, zobwerezabwereza, zomwe zatenga nthawi yayitali kapena zomwe zikuvutitsa. Kusintha kwa maso kapena makutu, matenda, mankhwala ndi zinthu zina zingathandizirepo. Dokotala angathe kuunika zomwe zingakhale zikuchitika komanso ngati pakufunika kuunika kwina. [Kalatayi ya Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/hallucinations-and-delusions) imalimbikitsanso kuyang’ana zinthu zokhudza thanzi la thupi, kumva ndi kuona, mankhwala komanso malo omwe munthu alipo.

Kusintha kodzidzimutsa ndi kosiyana. [NHS imanena kuti chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa chimafunika thandizo la mankhwala mwachangu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), ndipo [Alzheimer's Society imalangiza kuti mupeze thandizo la mankhwala mwachangu pamene kuona kapena kumva zinthu zongoyerekeza kukukhudza njira zoposa imodzi zomverera](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/hallucinations). Lankhulani ndi ntchito za mankhwala zachangu za m’dera lanu ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka modzidzimutsa kapena zomwe akuona kapena kumva zikukhudza njira zoposa imodzi zomverera. Ngati wina ali pangozi yapafupi, gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zadzidzidzi za m’dera lanu.

Musayambe, musasiye kapena musasinthe mankhwala nokha. Pemphani amene analemba mankhwalawo kapena wasayansi wa mankhwala kuti aunikenso zosintha zaposachedwa komanso mankhwala onse amene munthuyo akugwiritsa ntchito, kuphatikizapo mankhwala ogula popanda kalata ya dokotala. [Kalatayi yathu yokonzekera ulendo wa kwa dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ingathandize kukonzekera mafunso.

## Kodi ndi mfundo ziti zingathandize pa ulendo wa kwa dokotala?

Ngati munthuyo wavomera, lembani mwachidule zomwe mwaona kuti mugawane ndi gulu losamalira:

- zimene ananena kuti anaona, anamva kapena anazimva, pogwiritsa ntchito mawu ake ngati n’kotheka;
- nthawi ndi malo omwe zinachitikira, komanso kutalika kwa nthawi yomwe zinatenga;
- ngati munthuyo anaoneka kuti ali ndi mantha, odekha kapena ali pangozi;
- momwe chipindacho chinalili, kuphatikizapo kuwala, mithunzi, phokoso komanso amene anali pafupi;
- matenda aliwonse aposachedwa, kusintha kwa kumva kapena kuona kapena kusintha kwa mankhwala komwe mukudziwa.

Gwiritsani ntchito mawu a munthuyo ndipo fotokozani zimene inu mwazindikira. Mwachitsanzo, lembani kuti “ananena kuti pali munthu pafupi ndi zenera” m’malo motcha chochitikacho ndi dzina linalake. Gawani zomwe mwalemba mutavomerezedwa ndi munthuyo ngati n’kotheka.

Zolemba izi sizimadziwitsa chomwe chayambitsa. Ngati zomwe zikuchitika zayamba modzidzimutsa ndi chisokonezo kapena kusintha kwakukulu kwa mmene munthuyo akumvera, pezani thandizo mwachangu m’malo modikira kusonkhanitsa mfundo.
