Kuvala zovala ndi nkhani yaumwini. Munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwalika angavutike kusankha zovala, kumanga mabatani kapena kutsatira ndondomeko ya masitepe kuposa kale. Angakhalenso ndi chikondi chachikulu pa zovala zozoloŵereka kapena kalembedwe kena. Cholinga chake ndi kuthandiza kuti munthuyo akhale omasuka komanso azitha kusankha, osati kumuchitira zonse kapena kukakamiza kuti avale mwanjira inayake.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer's Association okhudza kuvala zovala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) ndi [malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza kuvala zovala](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/getting-dressed) amalimbikitsa kupereka nthawi yokwanira, kusunga zisankho kukhala zosavuta komanso kuthandiza munthuyo kuchita zimene angathe. Tsambali likupereka malingaliro wamba. Funsani dokotala za mavuto atsopano a kuvala zovala kapena nkhawa zina za thanzi.

## Kodi ndingapereke bwanji zisankho popanda kumuchitira zonse?

Funsani munthuyo zimene angakonde kuvala. Ngati kusankha m’kabati yodzaza zovala kuli kovuta, perekani zisankho ziwiri zozoloŵereka. Angasankhe pakati pa malaya abuluu ndi obiriwira, kapena pakati pa juzi ndi khadigani. Zisankho ziwiri ndi poyambira chabe, si lamulo. Ngati munthuyo akufuna kusankha kuchokera pa zovala zambiri, tsatirani zomwe akufuna.

Kumbukirani kalembedwe kamene munthuyo amazolowera. Mitundu, nsalu ndi zovala zomwe amakonda zingathandize kuti kusankha kukhale kosavuta. Musalowe m’malo mwa kalembedwe kake ndi zomwe inu mukuona kuti ndizabwino. Kuvala zovala zosagwirizana si chifukwa choyambitsira mkangano. Ngati munthuyo ali womasuka ndipo zovalazo zikugwirizana ndi nyengo, lemekezani chisankho chake.

Mukamagula zovala, ganizirani za kumasuka komanso zomwe munthuyo angakwanitse kuchita. Nsalu zofewa, maonekedwe ozoloŵereka ndi zomangira zomwe angatsegule zingathandize. Zomangira zofewa kapena zosavuta zingakhale zosavuta kwa anthu ena, koma kapangidwe katsopano kangakhalenso kosazolowera. Funsani musanasinthe zovala zomwe amakonda kapena kusintha chizolowezi chake.

## Ndi masitepe ang’onoang’ono ati omwe angapangitse kuvala kukhala kosavuta?

Sankhani nthawi yomwe pali mpata wochitira zinthu modekha. Kuthamanga kungapangitse ntchitoyi kukhala yovuta kwambiri. Malo abata, owala bwino komanso omwe zovala zikuoneka mosavuta angathandize. Chotsani zinthu zina pamalo omwe munthuyo amavalirapo, ndipo ikani zovala zosankhidwazo pafupi.

Ikani zovala motsatira ndondomeko yomwe munthuyo akufuna kuzivalira. Perekani chovala chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi ndipo gwiritsani malangizo afupi, monga “Nawu malaya anu.” Malangizo akulu ngati “Valani zovala” angakhale ovuta kuwatsatira. Imani pang’ono pakati pa masitepe kuti munthuyo akhale ndi nthawi yomvetsetsa ndikuyankha.

Muloleni munthuyo kuchita gawo lililonse lomwe angathe komanso akufuna kuchita. Ngati sakudziwa chotsatira, mukumbutseni kapena funsani ngati thandizo lingakhale lothandiza. Musamukokere zovala kapena kusuntha thupi lake popanda chilolezo. Ngati sitepe ina ikumukhumudwitsa, imani pang’ono ndikufunsa zomwe zingapangitse kukhala kosavuta. Nthawi zina kuyesanso pambuyo pake kumakhala kokwanira.

## Kodi ndingateteze bwanji ulemu ndi chinsinsi?

Funsani musanakhale m’chipindamo kapena musanathandize kuvala zovala. Munthuyo angamasuke kuvala yekha, kapena angafune kuti wina akhale pafupi popanda kumuthandiza mwachindunji. Tsatirani zomwe akufunsa. Ngati thandizo likufunika, fotokozani zomwe mukufuna kuchita ndipo onetsetsani kuti akumvabe bwino mukupitiriza.

Muphimbeni munthuyo momwe angafunire akamasintha zovala. Tsekani chitseko kapena makatani, ndipo pewani kukambirana nkhani zachinsinsi pomwe anthu ena angamve. Ngati wachibale kapena wosamalira akuthandiza, funsani ngati munthuyo akufuna kuti ameneyo akhale nawo. Zokonda zimatha kusintha tsiku ndi tsiku, choncho funsani nthawi iliyonse.

Kukumbutsa munthu kusintha zovala kungamuchititse manyazi. Perekani zovala zoyera zozoloŵereka popanda kumudzudzula kapena kumuwongolera pamaso pa anthu ena. Ngati munthuyo akufuna kuvala zovala zomwe amakonda mobwerezabwereza, zovala zofanana kapena zina zofanana nazo zingathandize kuti chisankhocho chikhale chozoloŵereka. Musasandutse kusintha zovala kukhala mayeso a kukumbukira kapena kudziyimira pawokha.

Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena okhudza kuteteza ulemu pa ntchito ina ya chisamaliro chaumwini, onani [malangizo athu osamba](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/).

## Ndi zovala ndi nsapato ziti zomwe zingakhale zabwino?

Kumasuka kumadalira munthu. Zovala zofewa, zosavuta kuvala komanso zozoloŵereka zingakhale zabwino kuposa zovala zatsopano zokhala ndi zomangira zambiri. Sankhani nsapato zomwe munthuyo amaona kuti ndi zabwino komanso zomwe siziterera. [Kabuku ka Alzheimer's Association kokhudza kuvala zovala](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/3e31dbc7-baf0-4f18-9c1b-7a8779af3d17/alzheimers-dementia-dressing-ts.pdf) limalangiza kuonetsetsa kuti kutalika kwa zovala kuli koyenera kuti muchepetse chiopsezo chopunthwa.

Ganizirani za kutentha kwa tsikulo komanso zomwe mwakonzekera kuchita. Munthu angakonde kuvala zovala zosanjikizana, koma onani ngati akutentha kapena kuzizira. Mukamapita panja, tengani chovala chowonjezera chomwe chingavalidwe kapena kuchotsedwa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/dressing-grooming) imalangiza kusankha nsapato zabwino komanso kuganizira za nyengo. Izi ndi mfundo zoyambitsira kukambirana ndi munthuyo, osati lamulo la zovala.

## Bwanji ngati kuvala zovala kwavuta posachedwapa?

Ngati kuvala zovala kwasintha kwambiri, funsani munthuyo zomwe zikumuvuta ndipo lankhulani ndi dokotala wake. Dementia Australia imanena kuti mavuto a maso, zotsatira za mankhwala kapena vuto lina la thanzi zitha kupangitsanso kuvala zovala kukhala kovuta. Dokotala angathandize kufufuza zomwe zingakhale chifukwa chake. Musasiye kapena kusintha mankhwala potengera nkhani wamba yokha.

Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi, funsani thandizo lachipatala mwachangu kudzera mu ntchito za mwadzidzidzi za m’dera lanu. [Malangizo a NHS okhudza kusokonezeka kwadzidzidzi](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) amanena kuti izi zimafuna chisamaliro chachangu. Chenjezoli likukhudza kusokonezeka kwadzidzidzi, osati kusintha pang’onopang’ono kwa chizolowezi chovala zovala.

Onani kuti ndi mbali iti ya chizolowezicho yomwe yasintha: kusankha zovala, kuziona, kugwira chomangira kapena kusunga bwino thupi. Gawanani zomwe mwaona ndi munthuyo komanso dokotala wake, ngati avomera. Vuto latsopano silimafotokoza lokha chomwe chayambitsa. Pewani kuganiza kuti munthuyo ndi wamakani kapena wosasamala.

Ngati mukuthandiza kunyumba kapena kumalo osamalira, funsani munthuyo ngati akufuna kuti zokonda zake zigawidwe ndi wothandiza wina. Chidziwitso chachifupi chokhudza zovala zomwe amakonda, ndondomeko yomwe amafuna komanso malangizo omwe amathandiza zingathandize ena kutsatira chizolowezi chomwecho. Sungani chidziwitsocho mwachinsinsi ndipo chisintheni pamene zokonda za munthuyo zasintha.

Munthuyo amakhalabe ndi ufulu wosankha zovala zake momwe angafunire. Perekani thandizo lomwe wavomera lokha, perekani nthawi yokwanira ndipo lemekezani zisankho zomwe zimamupangitsa kukhala womasuka.
