Zisankho zokhudza ndalama zimakhudza ufulu ndi moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku wa munthu. Ngati ntchito ina yakhala yovuta, yambani ndi ntchitoyo m’malo moganiza kuti wina ayenera kulanda zonse. Funsani munthu amene mumamusamalira kuti akufuna thandizo lotani ndipo musungeni pa ntchitoyi.

Kalozera uyu akupereka chidziwitso chachikulu kwa owerenga ku U.S. Njira za kubanki ndi malamulo amalamulo zimasiyana malinga ndi malo. [Kalozera wa Alzheimer's Society wokhudza kasamalidwe ka ndalama](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) akufotokoza njira zothandizira munthu ndikusunga udindo wake. [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) ikufotokoza kuti thandizo losakhazikika, anthu odalirika olumikizana nawo ndi ufulu walamulo ndi zinthu zosiyana.

## Kodi ndingapereke bwanji thandizo popanda kulanda zonse?

Funsani munthu amene mumamusamalira kuti ndi ntchito iti yokhudza ndalama imene angafune thandizo. Angafune kuona bilu limodzi, kukonza makalata, kukumbukira tsiku lolipirira kapena kuyang’ana pempho losayembekezereka. Munthu angafune thandizo pa ntchito zina ndipo angakonde kuchita zina payekha.

- **Funsani musanachite chilichonse.** Yesani kunena kuti, “Kodi mungafune kuti tione bilu iyi limodzi?” Patsani munthuyo nthawi yosankha.
- **Yang’anani pa ntchito imodzi.** Ngati munthuyo avomereza, onani limodzi kuchuluka kwa ndalama, tsiku lolipirira ndi njira zolipirira. Muloleni kupanga zisankho zomwe akufuna kupanga.
- **Pangani zolemba zosavuta limodzi.** Mndandanda wafupi wa mabilu, masiku olipirira kapena masitepe omwe mwagwirizana ungathandize nonse kuona zomwe zachitika.
- **Funsani banki za njira zomwe zilipo.** Ikhoza kukhala ndi njira zopangitsa malipiro kapena kuyang’ana zochitika kukhala kosavuta. Funsani zomwe njira iliyonse imalola musanasinthe akaunti.
- **Funsaninso pambuyo pake.** Zokonda za munthuyo zitha kusintha. Sungani thandizo kukhala logwirizana ndi zomwe akufuna komanso zomwe akufunikira panopa.

CFPB imafotokoza thandizo losakhazikika monga kuonanso mabilu ndi kukonza bajeti ndi munthu pamene munthuyo akuvomerezabe kuchita malonda. Izi zitha kukhala zosiyana ndi kulola munthu wina kutulutsa ndalama kapena kupanga malipiro. Musagawane mawu achinsinsi, ma PIN kapena ma code achitetezo anthawi imodzi ngati njira yachidule.

## Kodi munthu wodalirika wolumikizana naye amaloledwa kuchita chiyani?

Ku United States, mwayi wokhala ndi munthu wodalirika wolumikizana naye ungalole bungwe lazachuma kulumikizana ndi munthu wina pa nthawi zina, monga pakakhala nkhawa yokhudza chinyengo chomwe chingachitike. CFPB imanena kuti kusankha munthu wodalirika wolumikizana naye sikumapereka ufulu wolowa mu akaunti kapena kusamutsa ndalama. Mwayiwu supezeka mu akaunti iliyonse, choncho funsani banki kapena bungwe lazachuma momwe umagwirira ntchito.

| Mtundu wa thandizo | Tanthauzo lake |
|---|---|
| Kuona mabilu limodzi | Munthu ndi wothandiza amaona mabilu limodzi; munthuyo amasankha ndikuvomereza malipiro. |
| Munthu wodalirika wolumikizana naye | Bungwe lazachuma lingalumikizane ndi munthu wosankhidwayo pa nthawi zina. Dzina lokha silimapereka mwayi wolowa mu akaunti. |
| Ufulu wokhazikika wa akaunti kapena walamulo | Chilolezo cha kubanki, mphamvu ya loya kapena dongosolo lofanana lingalole kuchita malonda. Mphamvu zake ndi maudindo ake zimatengera akaunti ndi malamulo akumaloko. |

Musanasiye mwayi wolowa mu akaunti kapena kusintha umwini, funsani banki ndi katswiri woyenerera wazamalamulo wakumaloko kuti afotokoze njira ndi zoopsa zake. Kudziwika kuti ali ndi matenda sikokha komwe kumakuuzani kuti munthu angathe kusamalira ntchito ziti zachuma kapena zisankho zomwe akufuna kupanga. Musungeni pa zisankho momwe angafunire. Ngati akufuna kuti wina awachitire zinthu, funsani momwe mungakhazikitsire zimenezo mwalamulo m’malo mogwiritsa ntchito njira yolowera ya munthu wina.

## Kodi ndi kusintha kotani komwe ndiyenera kuyang’anitsitsa?

Bilu yolipirira yophonya kapena kugula kosazolowereka kungakhale ndi mafotokozedwe osiyanasiyana. Sizikutanthauza kuti munthuyo sangathe kusamalira ndalama kapena kuti wina wamugwiritsa ntchito molakwika. Funsani zomwe zinachitika ndipo, ngati munthuyo avomereza, onani limodzi chikalata cha akaunti kapena bilu yoyenera.

[CFPB imatchula zizindikiro zomwe zingasonyeze kugwiritsidwa ntchito molakwika kwa ndalama](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), kuphatikizapo ndalama kapena katundu kusowa, kutulutsa ndalama kosazolowereka mwadzidzidzi, mabilu osalipidwa, anthu osadziwika kuwonjezeredwa mu akaunti, kapena munthu wina kuletsa mafoni ndi alendo a munthuyo. Onani machitidwe ndi kusintha kuchokera pa zomwe zimachitika nthawi zonse kwa munthuyu; musatenge chizindikiro chimodzi monga umboni.

Ngati munthuyo akunena kuti ndalama zasowa, tengani nkhaniyo mozama. [Kalozera wathu wokhudza kuyankha pa kukaikira](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) akupereka njira zofufuzira nkhawa popanda kuyipeputsa. Funsani munthuyo mwachinsinsi zomwe anaona komanso zomwe akufuna kuti zichitike kenako. Pewani kuimba mlandu wachibale, wosamalira kapena wantchito wa kubanki musanafufuze zoona zake. Ngati mukuganiza kuti wothandiza wodalirika angakhale akukhudzidwa, funsani katswiri wina kapena ntchito yakumaloko kuti akupatseni malangizo.

## Kodi tingachepetse bwanji chiopsezo cha chinyengo?

Achinyengo angadzinamize kukhala banki, ofesi ya boma, bizinesi, bwenzi kapena wachibale. Anganene kuti pali vuto ladzidzidzi ndikukakamiza kuti mulipire nthawi yomweyo. [Kalozera wa Alzheimer's Association wokhudza chitetezo pa ukadaulo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) amalimbikitsa kuyima kaye, kutsimikizira amene walumikizanayo ndi kupeza thandizo musanagawane zambiri kapena ndalama.

- **Imani kaye uthenga ukaoneka wachangu kwambiri.** Musatumize ndalama kapena kugawana zambiri za akaunti pamene woyimba foni kapena uthenga akukukakamizani.
- **Tsimikizirani pogwiritsa ntchito njira yolumikizirana yomwe mumadalira kale.** Tsitsani foni ndikuyimbira banki pa nambala yomwe ili pa chikalata chaposachedwa cha akaunti kapena pa khadi, kapena gwiritsani ntchito pulogalamu yake yovomerezeka. Musagwiritse ntchito ulalo kapena nambala yotumizidwa ndi woyimbayo.
- **Sungani zambiri zachitetezo mwachinsinsi.** Musatumize mawu achinsinsi, zambiri zonse za akaunti kapena ma code otsimikizira poyankha uthenga wosayembekezereka. [FTC imalangiza kuti mutsitse foni yosayembekezereka yomwe ikufunsa code ndikulumikizana ndi banki pogwiritsa ntchito nambala yomwe mumadalira](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Gwirizanani pa thandizo ndi munthuyo.** Funsani kuti angafune kufunsa ndani akalandira pempho lodabwitsa. Mwayi wa munthu wodalirika wolumikizana naye ku banki ungathandize pa kulumikizana, koma pawokha supeleka ufulu wochita malonda.

## Kodi ndichite chiyani ngati ndalama zitha kukhala zitatumizidwa kwa wachinyengo?

Lumikizanani ndi banki, pulogalamu yolipirira, kampani ya khadi kapena kampani yomwe idagwiritsidwa ntchito kutumiza ndalamazo mwachangu momwe mungathere. Gwiritsani ntchito nambala yomwe ili pa khadi kapena pa chikalata cha akaunti, kapena pulogalamu kapena tsamba lovomerezeka la bungwelo. Funsani ngati angayimitse kapena kubweza malipirowo. [Masitepe obwezeretsa a FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) akufotokoza kuti njira zomwe zilipo zimatengera momwe ndalama zinatumizidwira; palibe chitsimikizo kuti zitha kubwezeredwa.

Sungani mauthenga, malisiti, masiku ndi zidziwitso za akaunti. Ku United States, mutha kunena zachinyengo ku [FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Ngati mukuganiza kuti munthu wina akugwiritsa ntchito molakwika munthu wachikulireyo, [Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) imalimbikitsa kulumikizana ndi Adult Protective Services m’boma lomwe munthuyo amakhala. Ngati munthu ali pachiwopsezo chapafupi, lumikizanani ndi ntchito zadzidzidzi zakumaloko. Kunja kwa United States, gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zakumaloko zoteteza ogula ndi zoteteza anthu.

Lankhulani mopanda kuimba mlandu. Zachinyengo zimapangidwa kuti zisocheretse anthu. [Kalozera wa CFPB wokhudza thandizo pa chinyengo ndi kubera](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) akutchula njira zodziwitsira ku U.S. ndipo amalimbikitsa kugwiritsa ntchito masamba ndi manambala a foni odalirika.

## Kodi ndingapeze kuti thandizo pa chisankho china chake chokhudza ndalama?

Funsani banki ya munthuyo kuti ndi zida ndi zilolezo ziti zomwe zilipo. Pa chikalata chalamulo kapena kusintha kwa umwini wa akaunti, lankhulani ndi katswiri woyenerera yemwe amadziwa malamulo a komwe munthuyo amakhala. Ngati mukuda nkhawa ndi kugwiritsidwa ntchito molakwika komwe kungachitike, gwiritsani ntchito ntchito yakumaloko yoteteza anthu. Tsamba ili silingasankhe ufulu womwe munthu ayenera kukhala nawo kapena ngati malonda enaake ndi achinyengo.
