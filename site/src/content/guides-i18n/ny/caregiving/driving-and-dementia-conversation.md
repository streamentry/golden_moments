Kuyendetsa galimoto kungakhale mbali ya umunthu wa munthu, ufulu wake wodzisankhira komanso zochitika zake za tsiku ndi tsiku. Kukambirana za kusintha kwamtsogolo kungakhale kovuta kwa aliyense. Kuyamba msanga kungapereke mpata wochuluka womvetserana, kupanga zisankho ndi kuyesa njira zina zoyendera. Tsamba ili si kuunika kwa luso loyendetsa galimoto kapena chigamulo choti munthu wina wake ali wotetezeka kuyendetsa galimoto kapena ayi.

[Malangizo a Alzheimer's Association okhudza matenda a kuiwala ndi kuyendetsa galimoto](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving), [malangizo a Dementia Australia okhudza kuyendetsa galimoto](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/driving-and-dementia) ndi [malangizo a NHTSA kwa anthu osamalira](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/older-drivers) amalimbikitsa kukonzekera msanga ndi kuphatikiza munthuyo. Dokotala, katswiri wa maphunziro okonzanso oyendetsa galimoto ndi bungwe loyang'anira ziphaso za oyendetsa m'dera lanu angathandize pa mafunso okhudza munthu payekha.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuyamba liti kukambirana?

Yambani kusanachitike chochitika pa galimoto chomwe chingakakamize kupanga chigamulo mwachangu, ngati n'kotheka. Sankhani nthawi yabata pamene munthuyo sakukonzekera kupita kwina kapena kubwera kuchokera ku ulendo wovuta. Iyi ikhoza kukhala nkhani yoyamba mwa zingapo, osati chilengezo chimodzi chokha.

Funsani tanthauzo la kuyendetsa galimoto kwa munthuyo. Kwa iye, kungakhale njira yochezera anzanu, kukagula zinthu, kuthandiza banja, kugwira ntchito kapena kumva kuti ali ndi ufulu wodzisankhira. Kuvomereza tanthauzo limenelo sikuthetsa funso la chitetezo. Koma kumasonyeza kuti mukumvetsa zimene zingatayike komanso chifukwa chake kukonzekera kuli kofunikira.

Phatikizani munthuyo mu zisankho za mayendedwe amtsogolo pamene akufuna kutenga nawo mbali. Angakonde kuti munthu wina wake azimuyendetsa, njira yodziwika bwino kapena nthawi inayake ya tsiku. Lembani njira zomwe amakonda ndipo yesani musanafike tsiku lovuta, ngati avomera.

## Kodi ndingafotokoze bwanji nkhawa yanga popanda kuyambitsa mkangano?

Kambani za chochitika china chake m'malo mopereka chigamulo chachikulu. Mwachitsanzo, fotokozani za kusokera panjira yodziwika bwino, kuphonya chizindikiro choyimitsa galimoto kapena kukanda galimoto posachedwapa. Nenani zimene munaona ndipo funsani zimene munthuyo amakumbukira kapena momwe anamvera. Pewani kumutchula kuti ndi woyendetsa woipa kapena kuyesa kukumbukira kwake ndi mafunso.

Mvetserani maganizo a munthuyo. Iye angakhale ataonanso chisokonezo, kuwala koopsa, kutopa kapena vuto lina. Kapena sangagwirizane ndi nkhawa yanu. Musaganize kuti kukambirana kamodzi kokha kukutsimikizira kuti akumvetsa kapena sakumvetsa chiopsezocho. Khalani aulemu ndipo bwereraninsotu ku nkhaniyi pambuyo pake ngati wina wakwiya.

Dementia Australia imalimbikitsa kukambirana mwachidule mobwerezabwereza, kuganizira mphamvu za munthuyo komanso njira zabwino zoyendera. Imachenjezanso kuti kubisa makiyi mwachinsinsi kapena kuwononga galimoto dala kungamveke ngati udani panthawi yokonzekera koyambirira. Ngati pali nkhawa yaikulu ya chitetezo cha nthawi yomweyo, chitani zinthu zothandiza kupewa ulendo wotsatira wowopsa ndipo funsani malangizo mwachangu kwa akatswiri oyenerera.

## Kodi ndi uthenga uti womwe ungathandize pokambirana ndi akatswiri?

Lembani zochitika zenizeni ndi nthawi yomwe zinachitika. Zitsanzo zingaphatikizepo kusokonezeka pa mphambano ya misewu, kusokera panjira yopita kumalo odziwika bwino, kusazindikira zizindikiro kapena kuwonongeka kwatsopano kwa galimoto. Phatikizanipo zimene munthuyo ananena za ulendowo ndi kusintha kulikonse kwa thanzi kapena mankhwala komwe akufuna kukambirana.

Sungani zolemba zomwe zili zenizeni komanso zachinsinsi. Musajambule, kutsatira kapena kuyesa munthuyo mwachinsinsi. Ngati munthuyo avomera, wachibale amene anakwerapo naye galimoto angagawane zimene anaona. Kufotokozera momveka bwino kumapereka kwa dokotala kapena katswiri nkhani yeniyeni yokambirana; sikuzindikira chomwe chikuyambitsa vutolo kapena kutsimikizira ngati munthu ali oyenera kuyendetsa galimoto.

Zizindikiro zochenjeza zomwe zili mu [malangizo a Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving) ndi zifukwa zofunira malangizo kwa akatswiri. Sizilowa m'malo mwa kuunika kwa munthu payekha. Funsani dokotala wa munthuyo za kutumizidwa kwa katswiri wa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku kapena katswiri wina woyenerera wa maphunziro okonzanso oyendetsa galimoto. Funsani za malamulo a ziphaso za oyendetsa m'dera lanu ku bungwe loyang'anira ziphaso za oyendetsa m'chigawo kapena m'dera lanu.

## Kodi tingakonze bwanji pulani yoyendera limodzi?

Yambani ndi maulendo omwe munthuyo akufuna kupitiriza: kucheza ndi anzanu, kugula zinthu, kupita ku chipatala, kupemphera kapena kuchita zinthu zodziwika bwino. Pa ulendo uliwonse, kambiranani kuti ndani angayendetse, njira ziti zoyendera zomwe zingakhale zovomerezeka komanso thandizo liti lomwe lingapangitse ulendowo kutheka.

Njira zingaphatikizepo kukwera ndi achibale kapena anzanu, matakisi kapena ntchito zoyendera, mayendedwe a anthu onse, mayendedwe a m'dera kapena kutumizidwa kwa zinthu zina. Ntchito, mtengo ndi kupezeka kwake zimasiyana malinga ndi malo. Onani zimene zilipo m'dera lanu m'malo moganiza kuti njira imodzi igwira ntchito kwa aliyense.

Yesani njira kapena ntchito inayake limodzi musanakhale njira yokhayo. Funsani ngati munthuyo akufuna mnzake woyenda naye kapena akufuna kuyenda pa yekha. Tsamba lathu la [malangizo okonza maulendo](/caregiving/traveling-with-dementia/) lili ndi malingaliro osungira maulendo kukhala odziwika bwino komanso kusiya mpata wosinthira mapulani.

## Bwanji ngati ndikuganiza kuti ulendo wotsatira ungakhale wowopsa?

Tengani nkhawayi mozama ndipo konzani njira ina yoyendera pamene mukufuna malangizo mwachangu. Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi, [NHS imalangiza kufunafuna thandizo lachipatala mwachangu](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/); gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zadzidzidzi za m'dera lanu. Kusintha kwadzidzidzi kwa momwe munthu akuchitira zinthu sikuyenera kutengedwa ngati mkangano wamba wa kuyendetsa galimoto.

Pa nkhawa zina zazikulu, lankhulani ndi dokotala wa munthuyo ndi bungwe loyang'anira ziphaso za oyendetsa m'dera lanu kuti mudziwe njira zomwe zikugwira ntchito komwe amakhala. Musadalire lonjezo la m'banja kapena tsamba ili kuti musankhe zofunikira za malamulo. Ngati n'kotheka, fotokozereni munthuyo za pulani ya nthawi yomweyo ndipo mupitirize kumuphatikiza pa zimene zichitike.

## Kodi ndingamuthandize bwanji munthuyo atasiya kuyendetsa galimoto?

Kusiya kuyendetsa galimoto kungamveke ngati kutaya kwenikweni. Vomerezani zimenezo momveka bwino. Pitirizani kumuitana munthuyo kumalo ndi zochitika zomwe n'zofunikira kwa iye. Perekani zisankho pa mayendedwe, nthawi ndi amene angapite naye m'malo motenga maulendo ngati ntchito yoti inu muziyang'anira.

Gawanani ntchito yoyendetsa ndi achibale kapena anzanu ngati munthuyo avomera. Funsani mabungwe a m'dera za mayendedwe a anthu achikulire kapena anthu olumala. Onani mtengo, ndandanda, malamulo onyamukira komanso ngati mnzake angaloledwe. Zinthu zing'onozing'ono zingasinthe ngati njira inayake ingakhale yothandiza.

Tsamba la [kudzisamalira kwa anthu osamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) limafotokoza za kupempha ena kuti agawane nawo ntchito yosamalira. Munthuyo ayenera kukhala ndi mwayi waukulu wosankha momwe angapitirizire kukhala wolumikizana ndi moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku.

Cholinga chake ndi kuteteza chitetezo pamene tikulemekeza tanthauzo la kuyendetsa galimoto kwa munthuyo. Yambani kukambirana msanga, mvetserani, pezani malangizo kwa akatswiri oyenerera ndipo konzani pulani yoyendera limodzi.
