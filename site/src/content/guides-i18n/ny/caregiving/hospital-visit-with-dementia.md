Ulendo wa kuchipatala ukhoza kusokoneza zizoloŵezi zozoloŵereka ndipo ukhoza kukhala wovuta kumvetsetsa. Munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala angafunike nthawi yochulukirapo, kufotokozedwa momveka bwino komanso thandizo pa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku ali kumeneko. Kukonzekera zomwe mungawauze ogwira ntchito m’chipatala kungathandize munthuyo komanso anthu amene akumuthandiza.

[Buku la Alzheimer's Association lokhudza kugonekedwa m’chipatala ndi kukonzekera kutuluka](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/d5afc74a-9d22-4a15-9c30-6315f6eb5f35/alzheimers-dementia-hospitalization-discharge-planning-ts.pdf) ndi [buku la Dementia Australia lokhudza kupita kuchipatala](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/going-hospital) zimalimbikitsa kukonzekera zambiri zofunika, zinthu zozoloŵereka komanso mafunso otsatira. Malangizo a ogwira ntchito m’chipatala ndi ofunika kwambiri kuposa malangizo awa a mtundu uliwonse.

## Kodi ndingakonzekere bwanji ulendo wokonzekera kuchipatala?

Ngati ulendowo wakonzedwa, funsani munthuyo zomwe akumvetsa zokhudza ulendowo komanso zomwe angafune kudziwa. Muphatikizeni pa zisankho momwe angafunire. Anthu ena amakonda kumva za ulendowo pasadakhale; ena angakhale opanda nkhawa zambiri akalandira kufotokozera kwachidule komanso modekha ulendowo utatsala pang’ono. Tsatirani zomwe munthuyo amakonda ngati n’kotheka.

Funsani chipatala kuti akuuzeni zambiri komanso katundu amene muyenera kubweretsa. Mndandanda wamakono wa mankhwala, zinthu zimene zimamuyipitsira ngati zikudziwika, komanso mayina ndi manambala a akatswiri azaumoyo amene amamuona nthawi zonse zingathandize ogwira ntchito. Bweretsani magalasi a m’maso, zida zothandizira kumva, zovala zozoloŵereka, zinthu zosamalira thupi komanso chinthu chomutonthoza ngati chipatala chikulola. Sungani zikalata za malamulo kapena zokhudza chisamaliro chamtsogolo ndi munthuyo pokhapokha ngati ndizofunika ndipo munthuyo kapena munthu wovomerezeka kupanga zisankho wasankha kuzibweretsa.

Konzani yemwe adzaperekeze munthuyo komanso mmene banja kapena anthu odalirika adzagawanirane nkhani. Ngati munthu mmodzi sangakhale naye nthawi yonse, funsani munthu wina wodalirika kuti alowemo. Munthuyo angafune kukhala ndi munthu wina m’mbali zina za ulendowo ndipo nthawi zina angafune kukhala payekha.

## Kodi ndiyenera kugawana ndi ogwira ntchito m’chipatala zambiri ziti?

Gawani zambiri zomwe zingathandize ogwira ntchito kulankhulana ndi munthuyo komanso kumusamalira. Munthuyo akavomera, lembani uthenga wachidule womwe uli ndi dzina limene amakonda kuitanidwa, mmene amalankhulira, zomwe zimamuthandiza kumvetsetsa, komanso zizoloŵezi kapena zinthu zomutonthoza zomwe ndi zofunika kwa iye. Mungatchulenso mmene amaonetsela ululu kapena kusowa mtendere ngati angavutike kufotokoza ndi mawu.

Sungani uthengawo wachidule komanso wachinsinsi. Gawanani ndi anthu a gulu lachisamaliro amene akufunikira kuudziwa. Funsani ogwira ntchito mmene angakonde kulandirira uthengawu komanso kumene akufuna usungidwe. Musalembe zambiri zachinsinsi pa bolodi loonekera kwa onse kapena kuzigawana ndi alendo amene sakufunika kuzidziwa.

Munthuyo ayenera kukhala nawo m’zokambirana nthawi zonse ngati n’kotheka. Funsani ogwira ntchito kuti azilankhula naye mwachindunji, azigwiritsa ntchito mawu osavuta komanso azimupatsa nthawi yoyankha. Ngati mukufuna kuwonjezera chinachake chimene munthuyo sangakumbukire, fotokozani zomwe mwaona popanda kungoganizira chomwe chachititsa.

## Kodi ndingathandize bwanji pa ulendo wadzidzidzi kuchipatala?

Ngati ulendowo sunakonzekeredwe, fotokozani zomwe zachitika, nthawi imene zidayamba komanso zomwe zasintha pa khalidwe la tsiku ndi tsiku la munthuyo. Ogwira ntchito angafunse funso limodzi mobwerezabwereza. Uthenga wachidule wolembedwa ungakuthandizeni kufotokoza momveka bwino chimodzimodzi kwa anthu osiyanasiyana.

Uzani ogwira ntchito kuti munthuyo akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala komanso thandizo lomwe nthawi zambiri limamuthandiza. Funsani yemwe adzakhale munthu wolumikizirana nawo pa nkhani zatsopano. Ngati munthuyo akudikira, mawu ozoloŵereka, chinthu chabata choti ayang’ane kapena magalasi ake a m’maso kapena zida zothandizira kumva zingathandize, ngati ogwira ntchito anena kuti n’zotetezeka kugwiritsa ntchito.

Tsatirani malangizo a gulu lachipatala. Musanathandize ndi chakudya, chakumwa, mankhwala, kuyenda kapena chisamaliro cha thupi, funsani ogwira ntchito zomwe zili zotetezeka pa nthawiyo. Mayeso kapena chithandizo cha m’chipatala chikhoza kusintha zomwe munthuyo angakwanitse kuchita kwa kanthawi.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuchita chiyani ngati munthuyo akuoneka wosiyana mwadzidzidzi?

Uzani namwino kapena dokotala mofulumira ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka kwambiri mwadzidzidzi kapena akuchita zinthu zosiyana kwambiri ndi mmene amachitira nthawi zonse. Musaganize kuti ndi matenda a kuiwala okha. [Malangizo a NHS okhudza kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) amanena kuti kusokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kumafunika kuyang’anidwa ndi achipatala nthawi yomweyo.

Fotokozani nthawi imene kusinthaku kunayamba komanso zomwe mwaona. Munthu angasonyeze kusowa mtendere kudzera mu kusintha kwa nkhope, mayendedwe, kugona kapena kudya ngakhale sangathe kufotokoza. Funsani gulu lachisamaliro kuti liwunikenso kusinthaku m’malo moyesa kudziwa nokha chomwe chachititsa.

## Kodi ndingamuthandize bwanji munthuyo ali m’chipatala?

Sungani zizoloŵezi zozoloŵereka pamene ogwira ntchito anena kuti n’zotetezeka. Bweretsani chithunzi chozoloŵereka kapena chinthu china chomutonthoza, chepetsani phokoso losafunikira ngati n’kotheka, ndipo fotokozani chinthu chimodzi panthawi imodzi. Lolani munthuyo kuchita zomwe angathe komanso zomwe akufuna kuchita. Funsani ogwira ntchito musanasunthe zipangizo, kusintha chipinda kapena kuthandiza pa chakudya ndi chisamaliro cha thupi.

Munthuyo angafunike thandizo kuti agwiritse ntchito batani loyitanira thandizo, kupeza bafa kapena kumvetsetsa ndondomeko yatsopano. Uzani ogwira ntchito thandizo lomwe nthawi zambiri amafunikira, ndipo funsani mmene mungapemphele thandizo pakati pa maulendo. Ngati munthuyo akufuna kuti wina akhale naye, funsani za malangizo a ulendo wodzaona kapena kukhala naye usiku omwe chipatala chingalole.

Kukhala m’chipatala kungatopetsenso anthu osamalira. Konzani munthu wina wodalirika kuti alowemo, munthuyo akavomera. Funsani munthu wolumikizirana naye m’chipatala kapena wogwira ntchito za chikhalidwe cha anthu za thandizo lomwe mungapezere munthuyo ndi banja pamene kugonekedwako kukupitirira.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuonetsetsa chiyani munthuyo asanatuluke?

Funsani ndondomeko yotulukira yolembedwa. Onetsetsani kuti ikufotokoza masiku okumana ndi dokotala otsatira, ntchito za chisamaliro, kusintha kulikonse kwa mankhwala komanso yemwe mungalumikizane naye ngati vuto litabuka. Ngati malangizo sakumveka bwino, funsani gulu loyang’anira kutuluka kuti akufotokozereni musanatuluke.

Kambiranani za thandizo lomwe lidzafunike kunyumba. Ganizirani yemwe adzathandize pa chakudya, kuyenda, kusamba, masiku okumana ndi dokotala komanso zipangizo zatsopano kapena ntchito zothandizira kunyumba. Fotokozani moona mtima zomwe banja ndi anzanu angakwanitse kuchita. Funsani wokonza za kutuluka m’chipatala za ntchito zina za m’dera lanu ngati ndondomekoyo ikuoneka yovuta kuyitsatira.

Mukabwerera kunyumba, perekani nthawi yopuma ndikubwerera ku zizoloŵezi zozoloŵereka. Gawani ndondomeko yolembedwayo ndi anthu okhawo amene munthuyo wavomera kuti aphatikizidwe. Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kapena akudwala kwambiri, funsani thandizo lachipatala mwachangu m’malo modikira tsiku lokumana ndi dokotala lokhazikika.

Ulendo wamtendere sungatheke nthawi zonse, makamaka pa nthawi yadzidzidzi. Komabe, kukonzekera, kulankhulana momveka bwino komanso ndondomeko yotulukira yolembedwa zingathandize aliyense kudziwa zomwe zidzachitike kenako.
