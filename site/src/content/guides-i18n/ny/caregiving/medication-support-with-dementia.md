Ndondomeko ya mankhwala imatha kusintha pamene zosowa zikusintha. Munthu angafune thandizo pokumbukira nthawi yomwa mankhwala, kuwerenga zomwe zalembedwa pa chizindikiro, kapena kukonzanso kalata ya mankhwala, koma nʼkufunabe kusamalira mbali zina payekha. Cholinga chake ndi kuthandiza kuti mankhwala agwiritsidwe ntchito motetezeka popanda kulanda udindo wochuluka kuposa momwe pakufunikira.

[Buku la Alzheimerʼs Association lokhudza chitetezo pa mankhwala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/medication-safety) limalimbikitsa kugwira ntchito limodzi ndi madokotala komanso azamankhwala, kusunga mndandanda wa mankhwala womwe wasinthidwa posachedwa, komanso kugwirizana pa ndondomeko. Tsambali lili ndi mfundo za chidziwitso chokha. Tsatirani malangizo ochokera kwa dokotala amene analemba mankhwala komanso wazamankhwala pa mankhwala a munthuyo.

## Kodi ndingaphatikizepo bwanji munthuyo pa ndondomeko ya mankhwala?

Funsani kuti ndi thandizo liti lomwe munthuyo akufuna. Angafune chikumbutso, thandizo potsegula chotengera, kapena kuti munthu wa mʼbanja aone mndandanda. Ngati munthuyo angathe kusamalira mankhwala ena pa yekha, sungani kuthekera kumeneku. Njira yatsopano ya chikumbutso iyenera kukambidwa naye ndikusinthidwa ngati ikuoneka ngati ikulowerera kwambiri kapena ikusokoneza.

Gwiritsani ntchito mawu omveka komanso aulemu kwa munthu wamkulu. Ngati munthuyo amamwa mankhwala pa nthawi yake yodziwika, mungalumikize chikumbutsocho ndi ndondomeko ya tsiku ndi tsiku yokhayo ngati zimenezo zikugwirizana ndi malangizo ochokera kwa wazamankhwala. Patsani munthuyo nthawi yoyankha. Musamufunse mafunso ngati mayeso, musamuchititse manyazi chifukwa waiwala, kapena kukangana naye ngati wamwa kale mankhwalawo.

Ngati chikumbutso sichikuthandiza, funsani kuti ndi chiti chimene chikuvuta. Zolembedwa pa chizindikiro zikhoza kukhala zovuta kuwerenga, chotengera chikhoza kukhala chovuta kutsegula, kapena mankhwala akhoza kubweretsa kumva kosasangalatsa. Dokotala kapena wazamankhwala angathandize kupeza njira zina. Musasinthe ndondomeko ya mankhwala pongoganiza.

## Kodi ndi mfundo ziti zomwe ziyenera kukhala pa mndandanda wa mankhwala?

Sungani mndandanda umodzi waposachedwa wokhala ndi dzina la mankhwala aliwonse olembedwa ndi dokotala, mlingo wake ndi nthawi yake, komanso chifukwa chake analembedwera ngati mukuchidziwa. Phatikizani mankhwala ogula popanda kalata ya dokotala, mavitamini, mankhwala azitsamba ndi zowonjezera. Lembani za ziwengo kapena zotsatira zosafunika zomwe zinachitikapo kale komanso mayina a akatswiri azaumoyo amene amasamalira munthuyo.

Pitani ndi mndandandawo ku maulendo a zachipatala ndipo sinthani pamene dokotala wasintha zinazake. Ngati munthuyo amaona madokotala oposa mmodzi, gawani mndandandawo ndi gulu lililonse la chisamaliro pokhapokha ngati munthuyo wavomereza. Sungani mndandandawo pamalo pomwe munthuyo ndi anthu amene wasankha kuti amuthandize angaupeze mosavuta, ndipo uusunge monga chidziwitso chachinsinsi cha zaumoyo.

Funsani dokotala kapena wazamankhwala kuti aone ngati pangakhale kugwirizana kosayenera pakati pa mankhwala pamene mankhwala atsopano alembedwa. Ngati munthuyo wakumana ndi vuto latsopano pambuyo pa kusintha kwa mankhwala, lembani nthawi imene linayambira ndipo lankhulani ndi dokotala amene analemba. Kusintha kwa khalidwe kungakhale ndi zifukwa zambiri, choncho musaganize kuti mankhwala ndiwo akuyambitsa popanda upangiri wa akatswiri.

## Kodi bokosi losungiramo mankhwala kapena chikumbutso chingathandize?

Kalendala, tchati cholembedwa kapena bokosi losungiramo mankhwala zitha kuthandiza anthu ena. Chida choyenera chimadalira mankhwala ndi luso la munthuyo. Funsani wazamankhwala ngati bokosi losungiramo mankhwala ndi loyenera musanasamutse mankhwala kuchokera mʼphukusi lake loyambirira. Mankhwala ena amafunika kusamalidwa kapena kusungidwa mwanjira yapadera.

Gwirizanani kuti ndani adzadzaza kapena kuyangʼana bokosilo komanso momwe mudzapewere mlingo wodumphidwa kapena wobwerezedwa. Ngati anthu oposa mmodzi akuthandiza, gwiritsani ntchito njira imodzi yogawana polemba zomwe zaperekedwa. Ngati munthuyo akufuna kusamalira bokosilo pa yekha, funsani wazamankhwala momwe mungachitire zimenezo motetezeka komanso moyenera.

Ngati mlingo wadumphidwa, onani malangizo olembedwa a mankhwala amenewo kapena imbani foni kwa wazamankhwala musanasankhe zoyenera kuchita. Musaganize kapena kuwirikiza mlingo potengera chidziwitso cha anthu onse.

## Kodi ndichite chiyani ngati munthuyo akukana kapena akupeza vuto pomeza?

Imani kaye ndipo funsani kuti vuto ndi chiyani. Munthuyo angakhale sakukonda kukoma kwake, akumva kudwala, watopa, kapena sakumvetsa cholinga cha mankhwalawo. Fotokozani momveka ndipo patsani nthawi yoyankha. Musakakamize munthuyo kumwa mankhwala kapena kuwabisa mʼchakudya popanda mgwirizano ndi upangiri wa akatswiri.

Musaswe pirisi, kutsegula kapisozi, kapena kusakaniza mankhwala ndi chakudya pokhapokha ngati dokotala amene analemba kapena wazamankhwala watsimikiza kuti nʼkotetezeka. Mankhwala ena sangagwire ntchito momwe anayenera kapena angayambitse vuto akasinthidwa. Ngati kumeza kuli kovuta, funsani ngati pali mankhwala amadzi kapena mtundu wina. Lankhulani ndi dokotala kapena wazamankhwala mʼmalo mosintha mlingo nokha.

## Kodi mankhwala ayenera kusungidwa ndi kusamalidwa bwanji?

Sungani mankhwala motsatira malangizo a pa chizindikiro ndi a wazamankhwala. Ngati munthuyo angamwe mankhwala owonjezera mwangozi, funsani munthuyo ndi gulu la chisamaliro kuti ndi njira iti yosungira kapena yothandizira yomwe ndi yoyenera. Kabati yokhoma ingathandize nthawi zina, koma mwayi wogwiritsa ntchito komanso ufulu wodzisamalira nawonso ndi ofunika. Onaninso ndondomekoyi pamene luso la munthuyo likusintha.

Sungani mankhwala omwe nthawi yawo yatha kapena omwe anasiyidwa pa malo osiyana ndipo funsani wazamankhwala momwe mungawatayire motetezeka. Musasunge mankhwala akale ngati osungira kapena kugawana mankhwala pakati pa anthu. Ngati mankhwala asowa kapena mlingo waonedwa ngati wabwerezedwa, lankhulani ndi wazamankhwala, bungwe loletsa poyizoni, kapena ntchito zadzidzidzi kuti mupeze upangiri mwachangu.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufuna thandizo la zachipatala liti?

Lankhulani ndi dokotala kapena wazamankhwala ngati munthuyo ali ndi zotsatira zosafunika zomwe zikudetsa nkhawa, vuto pomeza, kudumpha mlingo mobwerezabwereza, kapena kusintha kwadzidzidzi pambuyo pa mankhwala atsopano. Funsani malangizo omveka olembedwa. Musasiye kapena kuchepetsa mankhwala nokha.

Ngati mukuganiza kuti munthuyo wamwa mankhwala ochuluka, imbani foni ku bungwe la mʼdera lanu loletsa poyizoni kapena ku ntchito zadzidzidzi nthawi yomweyo. Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi, [NHS imalangiza kuti mupeze thandizo la zachipatala nthawi yomweyo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Gwiritsani ntchito ntchito zadzidzidzi za mʼdera lanu kapena ndondomeko ya chisamaliro chachangu komwe mukukhala.

Pa maulendo a nthawi zonse a zachipatala, [buku lokhudza ulendo wokaonana ndi dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) lingakuthandizeni kukonzekera mafunso ndi mndandanda wa mankhwala. [Buku lokhudza ulendo wopita kuchipatala](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) likufotokoza za kugawana mfundo za mankhwala ndi gulu la chisamaliro.

Ndondomeko yotetezeka kwambiri ndi imene munthuyo amavomereza ndipo gulu la chisamaliro limamvetsetsa. Funsani mafunso, sungani mndandanda kuti ukhale waposachedwa, ndipo pezani upangiri wa akatswiri musanasinthe chilichonse.
