Kusamba ndi chinthu cha chinsinsi. Kwa munthu amene akukhala ndi dementia, kusamba kapena kusamba mu bafa kungamvekenso kuzizira, kuwoneka momasuka kwambiri, kusokoneza kapena kusamva bwinobwino. Yambani pofunsa zomwe zingapangitse ntchitoyi kukhala yosavuta, kenako konzekerani chipinda musanapereke thandizo. Cholinga chake ndi kuthandiza chizoloŵezi cha munthuyo popanda kusandutsa kukana kukhala mkangano.

[Chitsogozo cha Alzheimer's Association pa kusamba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/bathing) chimanena kuti kulandira thandizo pa chinthu chaumwini kungamveke mosavuta. [Malangizo a Dementia Australia pa ukhondo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/hygiene) amalimbikitsa kutsatira zomwe munthuyo amakonda, kugwiritsa ntchito masitepe osavuta ndi kufunsa mtundu wa thandizo amene akufuna. Anthu amasiyana; yesani kusintha kokha komwe kuli koyenera kwa munthu ameneyu.

## Ndiyenera kukonzekera chiyani ndisanapereke thandizo?

Funsani za chizoloŵezi cha munthuyo chachikale. Kodi amakonda kusamba mwachangu kapena mu bafa, m’maŵa kapena madzulo, kuthandizidwa ndi munthu mmodzi, kapena kusamba mbali zina yekha? Tsatirani zomwe amakonda ngati n’zotheka. Ngati akufuna chosankha, perekani zosankha ziwiri zomveka bwino, monga “Kodi mukufuna kusamba mwachangu tsopano kapena mukadya chakudya cha m'maŵa?”

Musanalowe nawo kuchipinda chosambiramo:

- **Sonkhanitsani zinthu pasadakhale.** Ikani mathawulo, zovala zatsopano, sopo, shampu ndi nsalu yofewa m’ndondomeko yomwe zidzagwiritsidwe ntchito. Izi zimalepheretsa kusiya munthuyo yekha pamene mukuyang’ana zinthu.
- **Pangani chipinda kukhala chosangalatsa.** Onani ngati ndi chofunda mokwanira, yatsani kuwala, tsekani chitseko kapena makatani, ndipo sungani pansi kukhala pouma momwe mungathere.
- **Yang’anani madzi.** Yesani kutentha kwa madzi munthuyo asanalowe mu bafa kapena kusamba. Musadalire kuti adziwe ngati madzi ndi otentha kwambiri.
- **Konzekerani zinthu zoteteza ufulu.** Thaulo lalikulu, malaya ogonera kapena chophimba chabafa chingathandize munthuyo kukhala wophimbidwa akusuntha pakati pa masitepe.

[Chitsogozo chathu cha chitetezo cha m’nyumba](/caregiving/home-safety/) chili ndi zambiri za kuwala kwa m’chipinda chosambiramo, malo oterereka komanso kufunsa katswiri woyenerera za mipando yolowera kapena mpando wosambiramo.

## Ndingathandize bwanji popanda kulanda udindo wake?

Funsani chilolezo musanakhudze munthuyo kapena kuthandiza pa malo aumwini. Fotokozani zomwe mukufuna kuchita m’mawu achidule, kenako yimirani kuti mupeze yankho. Mawu ofatsa monga “Nayi nsalu yofewa” ndi omveka bwino kuposa mndandanda wautali wa malangizo.

Lolani munthuyo asambe mbali zomwe akufuna komanso zomwe angathe. Mutha kumuphimbira ndi thaulo ndipo mumangopukuta mbali imene mukusamba yokha. Ngati akufuna, m’patseni nsalu yofewa kapena sopo kuti agwire. Munthuyo angafunike nthawi yochulukirapo kuyamba kapena kumaliza; dikirani osachitapo mofulumira.

Ngati munthuyo akukwiya, akukoka kapena akunena kuti ayi, yimirani. M’patseni malo ndipo yesani kumvetsetsa chomwe chikuvuta. Chipinda chikhoza kukhala chozizira, madzi akhoza kumveka osadziwika, kapena munthuyo akhoza kufuna wina womuthandiza. Munthu amene amamudziwa bwino angamveke bwino ngati munthuyo amusankha. Musagwiritse ntchito modabwitsa, kukakamiza kapena kuwongolera thupi kuti mutsirize kusamba.

## Ndingatani kuti chizoloŵezi chikhale chofanana?

Sungani nthawi ya tsiku komanso ndondomeko ya masitepe ngati zimagwira ntchito bwino. Ikani sopo ndi mathawulo odziwika pamene munthuyo amawayembekezera, ndipo ikani zovala zatsopano pasadakhale musanayambe. Fotokozani sitepe imodzi pa nthawi imodzi; ngati malangizo olankhula sakumveka, sonyezani nsalu yofewa kapena lozani chinthu chotsatira m’malo mobwereza kufotokoza kutali. Ngati kusamba tsitsi kukuvuta, chitani pa tsiku lina kapena lekani kwa tsiku lina. Kusintha kochepa kumeneku kungapangitse ntchitoyi kukhala yosavuta kutsatira popanda kuchotsa udindo wa munthuyo.

## N’chiyani chingachitike ngati kusamba kwathunthu kapena kusamba mwachangu kuli kovuta kwambiri?

Kusamba kwathunthu si njira yokhayo yothandizira munthu kumva kuti ali woyera. Ngati munthuyo akumva bwino, yesani kusamba pampopi, kugwiritsa ntchito nsalu yofewa yotentha kapena kusamba mbali imodzi pa nthawi imodzi. Kusamba tsitsi kungachitike pa tsiku lina ngati ndilo gawo limene sakukonda. Sungani munthuyo wophimbidwa komanso wogwira nawo ntchito, ndipo yimirani ngati njira inayo ikumvetsanso chisoni.

Ngati munthuyo akukana lero, landirani ndipo yesaninso nthawi ina. Kusamba kosangalatsa mawa ndikosavuta kuposa kupanga kuyesa kwa lero kukhala koopsa. [Chitsogozo cha pamene munthu akunena kuti ayi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) chili ndi njira zina zothandizira popanda kukakamiza.

## Ndingatani kuti chipinda chosambiramo chikhale chotetezeka?

Sungani njira yolowera ku bafa kapena kusamba kukhala yomasuka ndipo gwiritsani ntchito zida zoyenera zosowa za munthuyo. Malo osaterereka, mpando wokhazikika kapena mipando yolowera zingathandize, koma zida zimafunika kuyikidwa bwino. Funsani katswiri wa ntchito zokuthandizani kapena katswiri wina woyenerera musanayike mipando yolowera kapena kusintha zinthu zomangidwa. Musabweretse chotenthetsera chonyamulika m’chipinda chosambiramo, ndipo tsatirani malangizo a zida zilizonse zamagetsi.

Ngati munthuyo akugwa kapena wagwa, musayese kumunyamula kapena kumusuntha m’njira imene ingavulaze inu kapena iye. Funsani upangiri wa akatswiri pa zida ndi thandizo lofunikira. Sungani ndondomeko iliyonse ya chitetezo yomwe ilipo mogwirizana ndi ufulu wa munthuyo komanso kusankha kwake.

## Ndiyenera kufunsa liti upangiri wa akatswiri?

Lankhulani ndi katswiri wa zaumoyo ngati kusamba kukuvuta chifukwa cha ululu watsopano, kugwa, nkhawa za khungu kapena kusintha kwa luso la munthuyo losamba mosatekeseka. Dokotala wa mano, dokotala, namwino kapena katswiri wa ntchito zokuthandizani angathandize pa vuto linalake. Musagwiritse ntchito chitsogozo cha pa intaneti chonse kuti mugamule ngati munthu akufunika thandizo lakumanja ndi munthu.

Ngati munthuyo wakhala osasamba kwa nthawi yaitali, musamunene mlandu. Funsani gawo limene likuvuta kwambiri komanso ngati kusintha kochepa kungathandize. Chizoloŵezi chodziwika, nsalu yofewa kapena thandizo la munthu amene amamukhulupirira zingakhale zokwanira; ngati sizili choncho, funsani gulu losamalira kuti likuthandizeni.
