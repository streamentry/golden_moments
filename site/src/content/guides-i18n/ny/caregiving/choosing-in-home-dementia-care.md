Kupeza thandizo la pakhomo kumayamba ndi kuganizira munthuyo, osati mndandanda wa ntchito zokha. Funsani zomwe akufuna kupitiriza kuchita yekha komanso mtundu wa thandizo lomwe angalandire. Wopereka chithandizo akhoza kuthandiza pocheza, pa ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku, ntchito za pakhomo, kapena chisamaliro cha zaumoyo cha akatswiri. Thandizo loyenera limadalira munthuyo, ntchito zomwe zikufunika, ndi chithandizo chomwe chilipo kale.

[Upangiri wa Alzheimer's Association wokhudza chisamaliro cha pakhomo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/in-home-care) ndi [chidule cha Dementia Australia chokhudza chisamaliro cha pakhomo](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/home-care) akufotokoza mitundu yosiyanasiyana ya ntchito. [Mndandanda wa mafunso wa bungweli kwa wopereka chithandizo](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-in-home-care-professional-ts.pdf) umalimbikitsa kufunsa za maphunziro, luso, umboni wa anthu ena, ndondomeko yosinthira, ndi mtengo. Ntchito, ziyeneretso, ndi malamulo a ndalama zimasiyana malinga ndi dera; tsambali silikuvomereza wopereka chithandizo aliyense kapena kufotokoza za kuyenerera.

## Kodi munthuyo akufuna thandizo lotani?

Yambani ndi ntchito zomwe zili zofunika pa tsiku la munthuyo. Akhoza kufuna munthu woti azicheza naye, kuthandizidwa pogula zinthu kapena kukonza chakudya, kapena kuthandizidwa posamba kapena kuvala. Chisamaliro cha zaumoyo cha akatswiri, monga cha anamwino kapena chothandiza kuyenda bwino kwa thupi, ndi ntchito yosiyana ndipo chikhoza kufuna katswiri wovomerezeka kapena kalata yochokera kwa wothandizira zaumoyo.

Lembani thandizo lomwe likufunika, nthawi yomwe lingakhale lothandiza, ndi zomwe munthuyo amakonda. Phatikizani zomwe angachite popanda thandizo komanso nthawi zomwe amafuna kukhala payekha. Izi zingathandize kuti chithandizo chikhale chothandiza munthuyo kukhala wodziyimira pawokha m’malo motenga mbali yonse ya tsiku lake.

Ngati munthuyo sakutsimikiza, yambani ndi chosowa chimodzi chothandiza kapena ulendo waufupi ngati n’kotheka. Funsani mmene adamvera pambuyo pake. Akhoza kufuna kusintha ntchito, nthawi, kapena munthu womuthandiza. Ndondomeko ya chisamaliro ikhoza kusinthidwa pamene zosowa ndi zokonda zikusintha.

## Kodi ndiyenera kufunsa chiyani kwa wopereka chithandizo ndisanamusankhe?

Imbani mafoni kwa opereka chithandizo angapo ndipo fotokozani ntchito zomwe munthuyo akufuna thandizo. Funsani ngati angakwaniritse zosowazo, nthawi yomwe amapezeka, komanso ngati anathandizapo kale munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala. Funsani za maphunziro omwe antchito awo amalandira okhudza matenda a kuiwala komanso momwe amathandizira munthu amene wasokonezeka, akuda nkhawa, kapena watopa.

Mvetsetsani zinthu zomwe zimakhudza moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku: ndani adzabwere, kangati, ulendo uliwonse utenga nthawi yayitali bwanji, chimachitika n’chiyani ngati wogwira ntchito wadwala, komanso ndani mungayimbire foni mukakhala ndi dandaulo. Ngati munthuyo akufuna wothandizira yemweyo nthawi zonse ngati n’kotheka, funsani ngati ntchitoyo ingakwaniritse zimenezo.

Pemphani umboni wa anthu ena komanso funsani momwe bungweli limafufuzira antchito ake. Dziwani ngati mbiri ya antchito, maphunziro, ndi ziyeneretso zimafufuzidwa, komanso ntchito zomwe wogwira ntchito amaloledwa kuchita. Malamulo ndi mayina a ntchito zimasiyana malinga ndi dera, choncho tsimikizani zofunikira za ukatswiri kapena zilolezo m’dera lanu.

## Kodi ndingadziwe bwanji ngati munthuyo ndi wopereka chithandizo akugwirizana bwino?

Ngati munthuyo wavomera, konzaninso msonkhano kunyumba musanayambe ulendo woyamba. Onani ngati wopereka chithandizo akulankhula ndi munthuyo mwachindunji, akumvetsera zomwe amakonda, komanso akumupatsa nthawi yoyankha. Funsani munthuyo mmene msonkhanowo wamvekera, m’njira yomwe angayankhe momasuka.

Alzheimer's Association imalimbikitsa kukhala ndi munthu wina wodalirika pa zokambirana kuti mudzakambirane maganizo anu pambuyo pake. Gwiritsani ntchito msonkhanowo kukambirana mmene ulendo wokhazikika ungakhalire: kufika, kulonjera, ntchito yomwe yakonzedwa, komanso momwe munthuyo angapemphe kuti asiye kapena apume.

Ngati n’kotheka, gwirizanani pa ulendo woyamba waufupi ndipo unikeni ndi munthuyo. Wopereka chithandizo akhoza kuoneka woyenerera pa mapepala koma osakhala woyenera kwa munthuyo. Lemekezani maganizo a munthuyo ndipo kambiranani njira zina ngati inu kapena munthuyo muli ndi nkhawa.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuuza wothandizira uthenga wotani?

Gawani zokhazo zomwe zingathandize wopereka chithandizo kuthandiza munthuyo motetezeka komanso mwaulemu. Munthuyo akavomera, fotokozani momwe amakonda kuitanidwa, zizolowezi zomwe zili zofunika, zomwe zimamuthandiza kulankhulana, komanso zomwe angakonde kuchita yekha.

Ngati malangizo a zaumoyo akukhudza ntchitoyo, funsani dokotala wa munthuyo zomwe wopereka chithandizo ayenera kudziwa. Musapemphe wogwira ntchito wopanda chilolezo cha zaumoyo kuchita ntchito za chipatala zomwe sizili pa maphunziro kapena udindo wake. Sungani uthenga wa munthuyo mwachinsinsi, ndipo onani amene angathe kuona zolemba zilizonse za chisamaliro.

[Upangiri wokonzekera kukaonana ndi dokotala](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ungathandize kukonza mafunso kwa katswiri wa zaumoyo. [Upangiri wa chitetezo chapakhomo](/caregiving/home-safety/) ukufotokoza za kusintha kwa pakhomo komanso nthawi yoti mufunse katswiri woyenerera.

## Kodi ndingayang’anire bwanji ndondomekoyi pakapita nthawi?

Lankhulani ndi munthuyo pambuyo pa maulendo. Funsani zomwe zinamveka bwino, zomwe anasangalala nazo, komanso zomwe angafune kusintha. Ngati sagwiritsa ntchito mawu ambiri, onani ngati akulandira wothandizira, akuoneka womasuka, komanso akufuna kuti ulendowo upitirire. Zimenezi si kuyesa kapena kuyeza; gwiritsani ntchito ngati chitsogozo pa zokambirana.

Gwirizanani ndi wopereka chithandizo pa momwe mungagawirane nkhani za nthawi zonse komanso momwe mungathanirane ndi nkhawa. Ngati ulendo waletsedwa, dziwani amene angabwere m’malo mwake kapena zomwe munthuyo angakonde kuchita. Unikani ntchito pamene moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku wa munthuyo, thanzi lake, kapena zosowa za chithandizo zikusintha.

Mtengo, nthawi, ndi ntchito zomwe zilipo zimasiyana. Pemphani wopereka chithandizo kuti akupatseni mfundo zolembedwa, kuphatikizapo mitengo, kutalika kochepa kwa ulendo, malamulo a kuletsa ulendo, ndi malipiro owonjezera. Dokotala wa munthuyo, mabungwe a chithandizo cha okalamba a m’dera lanu, kapena mabungwe a matenda a kuiwala angakuthandizeni kudziwa komwe mungafufuze, koma yerekezerani opereka chithandizo nokha limodzi ndi munthuyo.

Ngati munthuyo wasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi kapena akudwala kwambiri kuposa kale, musaganize kuti kusinthaku kwachitika chifukwa cha matenda a kuiwala. [NHS imalangiza kuti mupeze thandizo lachipatala mwamsanga munthu akasokonezeka mwadzidzidzi](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Pa nkhawa za chisamaliro chopitilira, lankhulani ndi dokotala kapena katswiri wina woyenerera.

Cholinga chake ndi chithandizo chomwe chikugwirizana ndi moyo wa munthuyo. Fotokozani ntchito yomwe ikufunika, onani luso la wopereka chithandizo ndi ndondomeko yosinthira, phatikizani munthuyo pa chisankho chilichonse, ndipo unikeni mmene ndondomekoyi ikumvekera pakapita nthawi.
