Masewera a ubongo amapezeka paliponse, ndipo ambiri amalonjeza kuti amasunga malingaliro anu kukhala achinyamata. Ngati mumasamalira munthu amene akukhala ndi dementia, kapena mukuda nkhawa ndi kukumbukira kwanu, n’zachibadwa kudzifunsa ngati kusewera masewerawa kungathandize. Nazi zomwe kafukufuku akusonyeza kwenikweni, kuphatikizapo mbali zolimbikitsa komanso zomwe sizili choncho.

Izi ndi mfundo zofotokozera chabe, osati malangizo a zachipatala. Onani [chenjezo lathu la zachipatala](/medical-disclaimer/).

## Kodi masewera a ubongo amateteza ku dementia?

Palibe masewera a ubongo amene atsimikizidwa kuti amateteza ku dementia. National Institute on Aging (NIA) imalangiza anthu kuti azichenjera ndi zonena kuti masewera a pa kompyuta ndi a pa intaneti angathandize kukumbukira, ndipo imanena kuti palibe umboni wokwanira woti pulogalamu iliyonse yamalonda yophunzitsa ubongo ili ndi zotsatira zofanana ndi maphunziro oyesedwa m’mayesero a zachipatala ([NIA pa kuteteza ku Alzheimer’s](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Izi sizikutanthauza kuti masewera alibe phindu. Zikutanthauza kuti phindu lawo lili pa chisangalalo, kucheza limodzi ndi chizolowezi, osati kuteteza aliyense ku dementia.

## Kodi “near transfer” n’chiyani, ndipo n’chifukwa chiyani ili yofunika?

Mukamayeserera ntchito ina, nthawi zambiri mumakhala bwino pa ntchitoyo. Ofufuza amatcha izi zotsatira za maphunziro. Funso lovuta kwambiri ndi lakuti kaya kusinthaku **kumafalikira** ku zinthu zina: ku ntchito zofanana (near transfer) kapena ku moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku, monga kukumbukira nthawi yokumana kapena kusamalira ndalama (far transfer).

Kuwunika kwakukulu kwa 2016 kunayang’ana kafukufuku amene makampani ophunzitsa ubongo anatchula ngati umboni wawo wabwino kwambiri. Kunapeza umboni wochuluka woti anthu amakhala bwino pa ntchito zimene amayesera, umboni wochepa woti amakhala bwino pa ntchito zofanana kwambiri, ndipo umboni wochepa kwambiri woti maphunzirowo amathandiza pa kuganiza kwa tsiku ndi tsiku. Kafukufuku wambiri analinso ndi mavuto pa kapangidwe kake, zomwe zinapangitsa kuti zikhale zovuta kufika pa mfundo zomveka bwino ([Simons ndi anzake, Psychological Science in the Public Interest](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100616661983)).

## Kodi kuwunika kwa kafukufuku kumati chiyani pa maphunziro a ubongo a pa kompyuta?

Ndemanga za Cochrane, zomwe zimasonkhanitsa mayesero abwino kwambiri omwe alipo, zayang’ana maphunziro a pa kompyuta omwe anatenga milungu 12 kapena kuposerapo:

- **Akuluakulu athanzi azaka 65 kapena kuposerapo:** mayesero asanu ndi atatu okhala ndi anthu 1,183. Umboni unali wochepa kapena wochepa kwambiri pa ubwino wake. Pakhoza kukhala phindu lochepa pambuyo pa maphunziro, koma palibe umboni womveka woti limapitirira ([ndemanga ya Cochrane, akuluakulu athanzi](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32104914/)).
- **Anthu omwe ali ndi vuto lochepa la kuganiza:** mayesero asanu ndi atatu okhala ndi anthu 660. Palibe kafukufuku amene anafotokoza ngati anthuwo anadzakhala ndi dementia, ndipo umboni unali wofooka kwambiri kuti ufike pa mfundo zomveka ([ndemanga ya Cochrane, vuto lochepa la kuganiza](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/)).

## Kodi kafukufuku wa ACTIVE anapeza chiyani?

ACTIVE ndi kafukufuku wamkulu ku US womwe unayamba mu 1998 ndi akuluakulu 2,802 azaka 65 kapena kuposerapo omwe ankakhala paokha ndipo analibe mavuto akulu a kukumbukira kapena kuganiza. Anthu anagawidwa mwachisawawa m’magulu atatu a maphunziro ang’onoang’ono (liwiro la kukonza zinthu, kukumbukira, kapena kuganiza) kapena gulu lomwe silinaphunzitsidwe. Maphunziro anali magawo okwana khumi pa masabata asanu kapena asanu ndi limodzi. Ena mwa anthu omwe anamaliza magawo ambiri anapatsidwanso mwachisawawa mwayi wa **magawo owonjezera** pafupifupi chaka chimodzi ndi zitatu pambuyo pake.

Maphunziro a liwiro anaphatikizapo kuzindikira ndi kukonza zinthu zomwe amaona m’kanthawi kochepa kwambiri, ndipo zovutazo zinkasintha pamene munthu akupita patsogolo.

Mu 2026, ofufuza analumikiza anthu 2,021 otenga nawo mbali ndi zolemba zawo za Medicare pa zaka 20 ([kusanthula kwa ACTIVE kwa zaka 20](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12884427/)). Nazi zomwe anapeza:

- **Maphunziro a liwiro ophatikizidwa ndi gawo limodzi kapena angapo owonjezera:** chiwerengero chochepa cha matenda a dementia poyerekeza ndi gulu lomwe silinaphunzitsidwe (hazard ratio ya 0.75, zomwe pafupifupi zikutanthauza kuchepa kwa 25%).
- **Maphunziro a liwiro opanda magawo owonjezera:** palibe kuchepa kwa chiwerengerocho.
- **Maphunziro a kukumbukira kapena kuganiza:** palibe zotsatira zazikulu pa matenda a dementia.
- **Gulu lililonse la maphunziro lonse poyerekeza ndi gulu lomwe silinaphunzitsidwe:** panali chizolowezi chochepa cha kuchepa, koma sichinali chotsimikizika pa ziwerengero.

### Malire a zomwe zapezekazi

- Zikukhudza **mtundu umodzi wokha wa maphunziro a liwiro oyang’aniridwa ndi magawo owonjezera**, osati masewera onse a ubongo.
- Zotsatira zazikulu zinachokera ku **gulu laling’ono** (anthu omwe anamaliza magawo ambiri oyamba ndipo anapatsidwa mwayi wa magawo owonjezera), osati gulu lonse la liwiro.
- Dementia inayezedwa pogwiritsa ntchito **ma code a matenda m’zolemba za Medicare**, osati poyang’ana munthu aliyense payekha. Pafupifupi gawo limodzi mwa magawo anayi a anthu oyamba sanaphatikizidwe chifukwa analembetsa mu Medicare Advantage, yomwe ilibe zolemba zonse.
- Otenga nawo mbali analibe mavuto akulu a kuganiza pamene anayamba. Zotsatirazi sizikunena chilichonse chokhudza masewera a anthu **omwe akukhala kale ndi dementia**.

Izi ndi zotsatira zosangalatsa zomwe zikufunika kafukufuku wina. Sizikutanthauza kuti pulogalamu iliyonse imateteza ku dementia.

## Kodi FTC inanena chiyani za Lumosity?

Mu January 2016, Lumos Labs, kampani yopanga Lumosity, inavomera kulipira $2 miliyoni kuti ithetse milandu ya Federal Trade Commission yokhudza kutsatsa konamiza. FTC inanena kuti kampaniyo inanena, popanda umboni wokwanira wa sayansi, kuti masewera ake angathandize kuchita bwino kuntchito ndi kusukulu komanso angachedwetse kuchepa kwa kuganiza chifukwa cha ukalamba, vuto lochepa la kuganiza, dementia ndi Alzheimer’s. Lamuloli limafuna kuti kampaniyo ikhale ndi umboni wodalirika wa sayansi isananenenso zonena zotere m’tsogolo ([nkhani ya FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program)).

Phunziro kwa mabanja: khalani okayikira pa masewera, pulogalamu kapena ndondomeko iliyonse yomwe imalonjeza kuteteza kukumbukira kapena kuletsa dementia.

## Kodi masewera angathandize munthu yemwe ali kale ndi dementia?

Angathandize kuti tsiku likhale labwino, zomwe ndi zofunika kwambiri. Njira yophunziridwa kwambiri ndi **kulimbikitsa kuganiza**: zochitika zosangalatsa za m’gulu monga kukambirana, kukumbukira zinthu zakale, masewera a mawu, ma puzzles ndi nyimbo, nthawi zambiri m’gulu laling’ono lotsogozedwa ndi mtsogoleri.

Ndemanga ya Cochrane ya 2023 ya mayesero 37 okhala ndi anthu 2,766 omwe ali ndi dementia yochepa kapena yapakatikati inapeza ([ndemanga ya Cochrane pa kulimbikitsa kuganiza](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39804128/)):

- **phindu lochepa la kanthawi kochepa** pa mayeso a kuganiza (umboni wapakatikati);
- **kusintha kothandiza pa kulankhulana ndi kucheza** (umboni wapamwamba, kuchokera ku kafukufuku ochepa);
- **phindu lochepa** pa ubwino wa moyo, momwe munthu akumvera komanso khalidwe.

Phindu limeneli linafufuzidwa makamaka atangomaliza ndondomeko za pafupifupi milungu khumi, ndipo kafukufuku ochepa anayang’ana ngati linapitirira. Kulimbikitsa kuganiza ndi ndondomeko ya gulu yokonzedwa, choncho zotsatirazi sizingagwire ntchito mwachindunji kwa munthu amene akusewera masewera yekha pa tabuleti.

Lipoti la Lancet Commission la 2024 limanenanso kuti ndondomeko za zochitika ndizofunika kwa anthu okhala ndi dementia, kuti asunge chisangalalo ndi cholinga ([Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)).

## Kodi kwenikweni masewera amathandiza pa chiyani?

Akasankhidwa bwino, masewera angapereke:

- **Chisangalalo.** Nthawi yosangalatsa ya theka la ola ndi yofunika pa yokha.
- **Kugwirizana.** Kusewera limodzi pafupi kumapereka china chake chosavuta kugawana pamene kukambirana kuli kovuta. Onani [mmene mungasewerere limodzi](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).
- **Chizolowezi.** Chochitika chodziwika pa nthawi yomweyo tsiku lililonse chingabweretse chitonthozo.
- **Kudzidalira.** Zochitika zogwirizana ndi zomwe munthu angathe kuchita bwino zimamuthandiza kumva kuti akutha, osati kuyesedwa.
- **Kukambirana.** Chithunzi kapena mawu odziwika angayambitse nkhani. Yesani [makadi oyambitsa kukambirana](/games/conversation-cards/) kapena [malizitsani mawu](/games/finish-the-saying/).

Zomwe muyenera kupewa: zowerengera nthawi, maphunziro, mizere ya kupambana ndi chilichonse chomwe chimamveka ngati kuyesa kukumbukira. Ngati munthuyo wakhumudwa, siyani. Kusiya msanga, kapena kungokhala limodzi, ndi zotsatira zabwino. Kalozera wathu wa [kusankha mapulogalamu ndi masewera](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) uli ndi mndandanda wothandiza.

## Kodi Golden Moments ili pati

Masewera a Golden Moments, monga [picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/), [picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) ndi [kusaka mawu a zilembo zazikulu](/games/word-search/), amapangidwa kuti azisangalatsa komanso kuti anthu azikhala limodzi. Alibe wowerengera nthawi ndipo alibe maphunziro. **Si** mankhwala, ndipo **samateteza** kapena kuchepetsa dementia. Tikufuna kukuuzani zimenezi momveka bwino m’malo molonjeza zomwe palibe masewera angakwaniritse.

## Lankhulani ndi dokotala

Ngati mukuda nkhawa ndi kusintha kwa kukumbukira kwanu kapena kwa munthu amene mumamusamalira, onani dokotala m’malo modalira pulogalamu. Dokotala angayang’ane zomwe zingathe kuchiritsidwa ndikukambirana njira zomwe zili ndi umboni wabwino, monga kuyang’anira kuthamanga kwa magazi ndi kuyezetsa kumva. Onani [Kodi dementia ingatetezedwe?](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/) kuti mudziwe zambiri. Ngati pali chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa kapena kusintha kwina kwadzidzidzi, pezani thandizo lachangu la zachipatala.
