Chinthu chowonjezera chiwopsezo ndi china chake chomwe chimalumikizidwa ndi mwayi wokulirapo wokhala ndi vuto linalake la thanzi. Sichifukwa chokhacho nthawi zonse, ndipo kukhala nacho sikutanthauza kuti mudzakhala ndi matenda a kuiwala. Patsamba lino pali zinthu 14 zomwe zimalumikizidwa ndi chiwopsezo cha matenda a kuiwala zomwe Lipoti la Lancet la 2024 limati zingathe kusinthidwa, zomwe kafukufuku akulumikiza nazo, komanso zomwe mungakambirane ndi dokotala.

Kuti mumvetse bwino, kuphatikizapo zomwe mayeso akulu anapeza, yambani ndi
[Kodi matenda a kuiwala angapewedwe? Zomwe kafukufuku akunena](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).
Izi ndi uthenga wamba, osati malangizo a zachipatala; onani
[chidziwitso chathu cha zachipatala](/medical-disclaimer/).

## Kodi zinthu 14 zomwe zimawonjezera chiwopsezo cha matenda a kuiwala ndi ziti?

[Lipoti la Lancet la 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0),
lolembedwa ndi ofufuza 27 a matenda a kuiwala, linayerekezera kuti zinthu 14
zosinthika zomwe zimalumikizidwa ndi chiwopsezo zimapanga pafupifupi 45% ya
milandu ya matenda a kuiwala padziko lonse. Peresenti ya chinthu chilichonse
pansipa ndi kuyerekezera kwa bungweli kwa gawo la milandu padziko lonse lomwe
likulumikizidwa nacho, lozunguliridwa kukhala manambala athunthu. Ziwerengerozi
zimadutsana, chifukwa anthu ambiri amakhala ndi zinthu zingapo zowonjezera
chiwopsezo nthawi imodzi, nʼchifukwa chake zimafika pa 45% osati kuposa pamenepo.

## Kodi ndiyenera kuwerenga bwanji maperesenti awa?

Mosamala. Lipoti la bungweli lomwe limafotokoza mfundo zingapo zoyenera kukumbukira:

- **Zimafotokoza za anthu ambiri, osati munthu mmodzi.** [Chidule cha lipotilo](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) cha University College London chikunena kuti kuchepetsa chiwopsezo kumathandiza pa gulu la anthu ambiri ndipo sikutsimikizira kuti munthu mmodzi apewa matenda a kuiwala.
- **Zimaganiza kuti pali chifukwa chenicheni.** Olemba anaphatikizapo zinthu zokhazo zomwe zinali ndi umboni wokwanira, koma amati maulalo ena angakhale okhudza pangʼono chabe.
- **Ndi ziwerengero za padziko lonse.** Zambiri zimachokera ku mayiko olemera, ndipo gawo lenileni la dziko kapena dera lililonse lingasiyane.
- **Maperesenti angʼonoangʼono angakhalebe ofunika.** Chinthu chomwe chimalumikizidwa ndi 2% ya milandu padziko lonse chimakhudzabe anthu ambiri.

## Kodi ndi zinthu ziti zowonjezera chiwopsezo zomwe zinawonjezedwa mu 2024?

Lipoti la 2024 linawonjezera zinthu ziwiri pa zinthu 12 zomwe zinatchulidwa mu 2020:

- **Cholesterol yambiri ya LDL pakati pa moyo**, yolumikizidwa ndi pafupifupi 7% ya milandu.
- **Mavuto amaso osalandira chithandizo pa ukalamba**, olumikizidwa ndi pafupifupi 2% ya milandu.

## Kodi ndi zinthu ziti zowonjezera chiwopsezo zomwe sizingasinthidwe?

National Institute on Aging imati
[ukalamba ndi chinthu chachikulu chodziwika chomwe chimawonjezera chiwopsezo cha matenda a Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease),
ndipo palibe amene angasinthe. Majini nawonso amathandizira; jini imodzi yodziwika yomwe imakhudza chiwopsezo cha matenda a Alzheimer imatchedwa APOE. NIA imawonjezera kuti asayansi sanamvetsetsebe kwathunthu zomwe zimayambitsa matenda a Alzheimer kwa anthu ambiri. Lipoti la Lancet limanena kuti zinthu zosinthika zomwe zimalumikizidwa ndi chiwopsezo zingachepetsedwe ngakhale munthu atakhala ndi chiwopsezo cha majini chotani.

Chifukwa ukalamba ndi majini zimathandizira kwambiri, anthu ambiri amene alibe chilichonse mwa zinthu 14zi amakhalabe ndi matenda a kuiwala. Izi si kulakwa kwa munthu aliyense.

## Kodi umboni wa mayeso ukunena chiyani za kusintha zinthu izi?

Kulumikiza chinthu ndi matenda a kuiwala si chimodzimodzi ndi kutsimikizira kuti kuchisintha kumachepetsa chiwopsezo cha matenda a kuiwala. Zitsanzo zingapo za momwe umboni wa mayeso ulili:

- **Kuthamanga kwa magazi.** Mu mayeso a SPRINT MIND, cholinga chotsika cha kuthamanga kwa magazi chinachepetsa kwambiri mavuto ochepa a kuganiza. Sichinachepetse kwambiri matenda a kuiwala omwe amaganiziridwa, ngakhale kuti mayesowo akhoza kukhala anali aangʼono kwambiri kusonyeza zimenezo ([SPRINT MIND mu JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Zipangizo zomvera.** Lipoti la Lancet limafotokoza mayeso a zaka zitatu a zipangizo zomvera omwe sanapeze zotsatira zonse pa kuganiza, koma anapeza phindu mʼgulu la anthu omwe anali ndi chiwopsezo chachikulu. Kafukufuku amene amatsatira anthu kwa nthawi yaitali amalumikiza kugwiritsa ntchito zipangizo zomvera ndi chiwopsezo chochepa. [Malangizo athu a kumva](/brain-health/hearing/) ali ndi zambiri.
- **Kuchita masewera olimbitsa thupi.** National Institute on Aging imati palibe umboni wokwanira wa mayeso otsimikizira kuti kuchita masewera olimbitsa thupi kungapewere kapena kuchepetsa mavuto ochepa a kuganiza kapena matenda a Alzheimer, ngakhale kuti kafukufuku wina akusonyeza kuti kungathandize pa kuchepa kwa maganizo chifukwa cha ukalamba
  ([NIA pa kupewa matenda a Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Mayeso ataliatali omwe amayembekezera kuti matenda a kuiwala ayambe ndi ochedwa komanso okwera mtengo, choncho pa zinthu zambiri umboni wabwino kwambiri udzapitirira kuchokera ku kafukufuku amene amatsatira anthu kwa zaka zambiri. Umboni umenewu ndi wothandiza, koma ndi wochepa mphamvu poyerekeza ndi mayeso.

## Nanga bwanji kugona ndi zakudya?

Bungweli linaunikanso za kugona ndi zakudya koma silinazionjezere pa mndandandawu, chifukwa umboni sunali wokwanira kapena wogwirizana mokwanira. Malangizo athu a
[kugona](/brain-health/sleep/) ndi [zakudya](/brain-health/diet/) akufotokoza zomwe zikudziwika.

## Kambiranani ndi dokotala

Kuyezedwa kwa thanzi pachaka ndi nthawi yabwino kukweza nkhani izi. Mungabweretse mndandanda uwu ndikufunsa:

- Kodi manambala anga a kuthamanga kwa magazi, shuga mʼmagazi ndi cholesterol ndi otani, ndipo pali ena omwe akufunika chisamaliro?
- Kodi ndiyenera kuyezetsa kumva ndi maso anga?
- Kodi zomwe ndimachita ndizotetezeka komanso zokwanira kwa ine?
- Kodi mankhwala anga ena angakhudze kukumbukira kapena kuganiza?

Musanachite kusintha pa mankhwala, zakudya, kusala kudya, masewera olimbitsa thupi kapena zowonjezera zakudya, kambiranani ndi dokotala kaye. Ngati inu kapena munthu amene mumamusamalira wadziwika ndi chisokonezo chodzidzimutsa, kufooka mbali imodzi ya thupi, kapena vuto loyankhula, imbani 911.
