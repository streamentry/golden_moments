Kutaya kumva kumachitika kawirikawiri tikamakalamba, ndipo ofufuza amachiona ngati chinthu chomwe chingawonjezere kuopsa kwa matenda oyiwala. Izi sizikutanthauza kuti zida zothandizira kumva ndi njira yotsimikizika yopewera matenda oyiwala; mayeso abwino kwambiri omwe alipo mpaka pano anapeza zotsatira zosiyanasiyana. Tsambali likufotokoza zomwe kafukufuku akuonetsa, momwe mungayezetsere kumva, zomwe zida zothandizira kumva za over-the-counter ku US zili, ndi momwe mungapangire kukambirana kukhala kosavuta.

## Nʼchifukwa chiyani kumva kuli kofunika pa thanzi la ubongo?

Kutaya kumva kumalumikizidwa ndi kuopsa kwakukulu kwa matenda oyiwala. [Lancet Commission ya 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) ikuyerekeza kuti kumachititsa pafupifupi 7% ya matenda oyiwala padziko lonse. Izi zikufanana ndi kuchuluka kwa LDL cholesterol monga gawo lalikulu kwambiri pakati pa zinthu 14 zomwe zingasinthidwe mu chitsanzo cha Commission, malinga ndi [chidule cha UCL cha lipotilo](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors).

Zomwe Commission inapeza:

- **Kuopsa kwakukulu ndi kutaya kumva.** Mu kusanthula kwake kwa maphunziro asanu ndi limodzi abwino kwambiri, anthu omwe anali ndi vuto la kumva anali ndi kuopsa kwakukulu pafupifupi 37% kwa matenda oyiwala kusiyana ndi anthu omwe amamva bwino.
- **Kumvetsera kukavuta kwambiri, kuopsa kumawonjezeka.** Maphunziro omwe anayangʼana kuopsa kwa vutoli anapeza kuti kuopsa kumakwera pamene kumva kukuvuta kwambiri.
- **Zifukwa zomwe zingatheke, koma sizinatsimikizidwe.** Maganizo akuphatikizapo kusungulumwa ndi kudzilekanitsa, kukhumudwa, kuchepa kwa zolimbikitsa ubongo, komanso khama lowonjezera lomwe kumvetsera kumafuna.

Kutaya kumva kumachitika kawirikawiri. [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/hearing-and-hearing-loss/hearing-loss-common-problem-older-adults) imanena kuti pafupifupi gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu la anthu achikulire ali ndi vutoli, ndipo mwayi umakwera ndi ukalamba.

## Kodi mayeso a zida zothandizira kumva a ACHIEVE anapeza chiyani?

ACHIEVE anali mayeso oyamba oyerekeza a zida zothandizira kumva ndi kuchepa kwa kugwira ntchito kwa ubongo. Zotsatira zake nthawi zambiri zimanenedwa molakwika, kotero nazi zambiri zochokera ku [lipoti la mayesowo losindikizidwa](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10529382/).

- **Omwe anatenga nawo mbali:** anthu achikulire 977 azaka 70 mpaka 84 omwe anali ndi vuto la kumva lomwe silinalandire chithandizo ndipo analibe mavuto akulu a kuganiza, mʼmadera anayi ku US.
- **Zomwe zinayerekezedwa:** Hafu ya anthu analandira zida zothandizira kumva komanso uphungu kuchokera kwa katswiri wa kumva. Hafu ina inalandira pulogalamu ya maphunziro a zaumoyo okhudza kukalamba bwino.
- **Zotsatira zazikulu:** Pambuyo pa zaka zitatu, panalibe **kusiyana kulikonse** pa kuchepa kwa kugwira ntchito kwa ubongo pakati pa magulu awiriwa pa anthu onse.
- **Zotsatira za gulu lalingʼono:** Mayesowa anatenga anthu kuchokera mʼmagwero awiri. Anthu 238 anachokera mu kafukufuku wa nthawi yayitali wokhudza thanzi la mtima (ARIC), ndipo monga gulu anali pachiopsezo chachikulu cha kuchepa kwa kugwira ntchito kwa ubongo. Mu gulu ili, kuchepa kunali **kochepa ndi 48%** kwa omwe anagwiritsa ntchito zida zothandizira kumva. Pakati pa anthu 739 odzipereka ochokera mʼdera, panalibe kusintha koonekeratu.

Momwe mungawerenge izi moona mtima:

- Yankho lonse linali loti panalibe kusintha.
- Zotsatira za gulu lalingʼono zinali zokonzekera pasadakhale, koma zinachokera mu kusanthula kwachiwiri kwa gulu lalingʼono. Zikufunika kutsimikiziridwa mu maphunziro ena.
- Otenga nawo mbali ankadziwa pulogalamu yomwe anali nayo, zomwe zingakhudze zotsatira.
- Lancet Commission imaona umboniwu ngati ukusonyeza kuti zida zothandizira kumva zingathandize kwambiri anthu omwe ali ndi vuto la kumva limodzi ndi zinthu zina zomwe zingawonjezere kuopsa kwa matenda oyiwala.

Mfundo yaikulu ndi yakuti zida zothandizira kumva si njira yotsimikizika yopewera matenda oyiwala. Komabe zimathandiza anthu kumva, kutenga nawo mbali pa zokambirana ndi kukhalabe olumikizana, zomwe ndi zifukwa zokwanira kuziganizira.

## Kodi ndingadziwe bwanji ngati kumva kuyenera kuyezetsedwa?

NIA imalangiza kukaonana ndi dokotala ngati inu kapena munthu amene mumamusamalira:

- Amavutika kumvetsetsa anthu akamalankhula pa foni
- Zimakhala zovuta kutsatira zokambirana pamene anthu awiri kapena kuposerapo akulankhula
- Amapempha anthu mobwerezabwereza kuti abwereze zomwe anena
- Amakweza TV kwambiri moti ena amadandaula
- Amavutika kumvetsetsa mawu pamene pali phokoso
- Amaona kuti anthu ena akungʼungʼudza

Yambani ndi dokotala wa chisamaliro choyambirira, yemwe angakutumizeni kwa katswiri wa kumva (audiologist) kapena dokotala wa khutu, mphuno ndi mmero. Nkhutu zotsekeka ndi phula ndi chimodzi mwa zifukwa zomwe zimachititsa kutaya kumva zomwe dokotala angaziyangʼane. **Kutaya kumva mwadzidzidzi ndi vuto lachipatala lofunika chisamaliro mwamsanga**, ndipo NIA imalangiza kukaonana ndi dokotala nthawi yomweyo.

Kutaya kumva kungatengedwe molakwika ngati kusokonezeka. NIA imanena kuti anthu achikulire omwe sakumva bwino nthawi zina amaganiziridwa molakwika kuti asokonezeka, osayankha kapena osafuna kuthandizana. Ngati munthu amene mumamukonda akuoneka kuti akudzilekanitsa kapena sakumva zomwe zikunenedwa, kuyezetsa kumva ndi sitepe yabwino yoyambira.

## Kodi zida zothandizira kumva za over-the-counter ndi chiyani?

Kuyambira pa Okutobala 17, 2022, [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/hearing-aids/otc-hearing-aids-what-you-should-know) yaloledza gulu la zida zothandizira kumva za over-the-counter (OTC). Umu ndi momwe zimasiyanirana ndi zida zolembedwa ndi dokotala:

| Mbali | Zida za OTC | Zida zolembedwa ndi dokotala |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zimapangidwira ndani | Anthu achikulire a zaka 18 kapena kupitirira omwe akuona kuti ali ndi vuto lochepa kapena lapakati la kumva | Vuto lililonse la kumva, msinkhu uliwonse |
| Kumene mungazipeze | Mʼmasitolo kapena pa intaneti, popanda kuyezetsa kapena chikalata cha dokotala | Kudzera mwa katswiri wovomerezeka wa kumva |
| Kuyika ndi kukonza | Mumakonza nokha, nthawi zambiri pogwiritsa ntchito pulogalamu | Katswiri amakonza malinga ndi kumva kwanu |

FDA imanena kuti zida za OTC sizimapangidwira anthu omwe ali ndi vuto lalikulu kapena loopsa kwambiri la kumva. Imalangiza kukaonana ndi dokotala, makamaka dokotala wa khutu, mphuno ndi mmero, musanagule ngati muli ndi:

- Kupweteka kapena kusamasuka kwa khutu
- Madzi, mafinya kapena magazi otuluka mʼkhutu mʼmiyezi isanu ndi umodzi yapitayi
- Kusintha kwadzidzidzi kapena kosinthasintha kwa kumva
- Kumva kochepa mʼkhutu limodzi, kapena kulira kwa khutu limodzi lokha
- Chizungulire
- Phula lochuluka mʼkhutu

Kuzolowera zida zothandizira kumva kumafuna kuyeserera, kotero onani ndondomeko yobwezera musanagule, monga momwe FDA imalangizira. NIA imalangiza kufunsa ngati inshuwalansi yanu ingalipire gawo lina la mtengo. Kwa munthu amene ali ndi matenda oyiwala, katswiri wa kumva angathandize kusankha mtundu wa chida ndi momwe chingayikidwe kuti chikhale chosavuta kuchigwiritsa ntchito tsiku ndi tsiku.

## Kodi ndingalankhule bwanji mosavuta ndi munthu amene ali ndi vuto la kumva?

NIA imalangiza:

1. **Yangʼanani munthuyo ndikukhala maso ndi maso**, pamalo owala bwino, kuti athe kuona milomo ndi maonekedwe anu.
2. **Pezani malo opanda phokoso.** Zimitsani TV kapena chokerani pamalo aphokoso.
3. **Lankhulani mokweza pangʼono, koma musafuwule.** Lankhulani mwachibadwa komanso pa liwiro loyenera.
4. **Sungani pakamwa panu popanda chopinga.** Musadye, musatafune chingamu kapena kuphimba pakamwa pamene mukulankhula.
5. **Fotokozaninso mʼmawu ena mʼmalo mobwereza** pamene china chake sichinamveke.
6. **Munthu mmodzi pa nthawi imodzi.** Mu gulu, onetsetsani kuti munthuyo akuphatikizidwa.
7. **Khalani oleza mtima ndipo funsani momwe mungathandizire.**

## Maso nawonso ndi ofunika

Mu 2024 Lancet Commission inawonjezera kutaya kuona komwe sikunalandire chithandizo ngati chinthu chatsopano chomwe chingawonjezere kuopsa. Chidule cha UCL chikuyerekeza kuti chimachititsa pafupifupi 2% ya milandu ya matenda oyiwala, ndipo Commission imalimbikitsa kupezeka kwa kuyezetsa ndi chithandizo cha mavuto a maso kwa onse. [NIA imalangiza](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/vision-and-vision-loss/aging-and-your-eyes) kuyezetsa maso mozama chaka chilichonse kapena zaka ziwiri zilizonse mutakwanitsa zaka 60, komanso kuyangʼana manambala a magalasi. Ngakhale kusintha kochepa kwa kuona kungawonjezere kuopsa kwa kugwa.

## Lankhulani ndi dokotala

Funsani dokotala za kuyezetsa kumva ndi maso ngati mwaona zizindikiro zilizonse zomwe zatchulidwa pamwambapa. Fufuzani chithandizo mwamsanga pa kutaya kumva mwadzidzidzi, kupweteka kwa khutu, kapena madzi otuluka mʼkhutu. Ngati mumamusamalira munthu amene ali ndi matenda oyiwala, uzani dokotala wake za kusintha kwa kumva kapena kuona, zomwe zingakhudze momwe akumvera, chitetezo chake komanso momwe angatengere nawo mbali.

Tsambali ndi la chidziwitso chachikulu, osati malangizo achipatala. Onani [chenjezo lathu lachipatala](/medical-disclaimer/). Werengani zambiri za [kulumikizana ndi ena ndi thanzi la ubongo](/brain-health/social-connection/).
