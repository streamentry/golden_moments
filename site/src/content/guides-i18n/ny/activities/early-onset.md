Zambiri zokhudza dementia zimaganiza kuti munthuyo ali ndi zaka za m'ma seventies kapena m'ma eighties. Kwa munthu amene wapezeka ndi vutoli ali ndi zaka za m'ma forties kapena m'ma fifties, moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku umasiyana. Angakhale akugwirabe ntchito, ali ndi ngongole ya nyumba, ali ndi ana achinyamata kunyumba kapena akusamalira kholo lake. Zochita ziyenera kuzindikira moyo umenewu wa uchikulire. Bukuli likufotokoza zomwe zimasintha, zomwe mungayesere, ndi komwe okondedwa ndi ana aakulu angapeze chithandizo.

## Kodi dementia yoyambira msanga ndi chiyani?

Dementia yoyambira msanga ndi dementia imene imayamba munthu asanakwanitse zaka 65, nthawi zina imatchedwa younger-onset. [Nkhani ya pa blog ya Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) imafotokoza kuti Alzheimer's imatchedwa younger-onset pamene ikukhudza munthu wa zaka zosakwana 65, ndipo imanena kuti kusamalira munthu wamng'ono choncho “kumabweretsa mavuto apadera.” Zizindikiro zingakhale zofanana ndi za moyo wakutsogolo, koma zomwe munthuyo amayembekezera, maudindo ake ndi moyo wake wocheza ndi anthu si zofanana.

Chifukwa si yofala kwambiri, anthu angachedwe kupeza matenda, ndipo mabanja angaone kuti alibe munthu woti alankhule naye. Kudziwa kuti pali anthu ena omwe akukumana ndi zomwezo nthawi zambiri kumabweretsa mpumulo.

## Musiyeni munthu wamkulu akhale nawo pa chochitacho

Anthu akuluakulu amene ali ndi dementia yoyambira msanga nthawi zambiri safuna kuchitiridwa ngati ana. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) imalangiza kusintha zochita kuti zigwirizane ndi zomwe munthuyo angakwanitsebe kuchita ndi kusangalala nazo. Kwa munthu wa zaka za m'ma fifties, izi zingatanthauze:

- **Pitirizani zomwe amakonda kuchita,** monga ukalipentala, masewera, masewera a makadi, kalabu ya mabuku kapena kwaya, mothandizidwa pang'ono kapena pa nthawi yochepa.
- **Yendani kapena palani njinga ndi mnzake,** panjira yomwe amayidziwa bwino.
- **Phikani chakudya cha banja,** munthuyo akuchita mbali zomwe angakwanitse.
- **Dziperekeni kapena thandizani** pa ntchito yomwe wasankha, monga kusanja zopereka kapena kuthandiza pa chochitika cha kusukulu.
- **Konzani ulendo** wopita kumalo ofunika kwa iye, muli ndi mnzake komanso ndondomeko yosavuta.
- **Khalani ndi nthawi limodzi ndi adzukulu, mphwake kapena ana a abale,** mukusewera masewera kapena kugwira ntchito ina limodzi.

Muloleni munthuyo asankhe yekha. Kufunsa kuti “Kodi mukufuna kuchita chiyani masana ano?” ndi ulemu waukulu kuposa kusankhira iwo.

## Gwiritsani ntchito nyimbo ndi zokumbukira za zaka zawo

Nyimbo ndi zithunzi za zaka za m'ma twenties, thirties ndi forties za munthu zingabweretse malingaliro abwino komanso kucheza kosavuta. Buku lathu la [zochita za nyimbo](/activities/music/) limafotokoza momwe mungapangire mndandanda wa nyimbo, ndipo [zochita zokumbukira](/activities/reminiscence/) zimasonyeza momwe mungagawire zithunzi ndi nkhani popanda kumufunsa mafunso ngati mayeso. Pa masewera a uchikulire, yesani [makadi ochezera](/games/conversation-cards/), okhala ndi mafunso otseguka okhudza ntchito, maulendo, abwenzi ndi banja.

Masewera a zithunzi ziwiri opangidwa kuchokera ku zithunzi za banja angathandizenso, chifukwa zithunzizo zimakhala za anthu ndi malo ofunika kwa munthuyo. [Zithunzi ziwiri](/games/photo-pairs/) zimasunga zithunzizo mu msakatuli pa chipangizo chimenecho.

## Ntchito, kuyendetsa galimoto ndi moyo wa tsiku ndi tsiku

Anthu ambiri amene ali ndi dementia yoyambira msanga akugwirabe ntchito, kuyendetsa galimoto kapena kuyang'anira ndalama. Izi si zochita monga momwe timazidziwa, koma zimakhudza zomwe munthuyo angachite. Lankhulani ndi dokotala wawo za chitetezo pa kuyendetsa galimoto ndi kuntchito, ndipo ganizirani ngati akufuna thandizo pa nkhani za ndalama kapena ma apointi. Cholinga chake ndi kuteteza ulemu wawo kwinaku mukuwalola kutenga nawo mbali momwe angafunire.

Kuti mupeze malangizo okhudza machitidwe a tsiku ndi tsiku komanso kukhala otanganidwa, onani [ndondomeko yofatsa ya tsiku ndi tsiku](/activities/daily-routine/) ndi [ntchito zapakhomo ngati zochita](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Chithandizo kwa okondedwa ndi ana aakulu

Okondedwa ndi ana aakulu nthawi zambiri amamva kukhala okha. Kusamalira munthu kungamveke ngati ntchito yachiwiri kuwonjezera pa ntchito ndi banja. Nkhani ya pa blog ya Alzheimer's Association imanena kuti osamalira m'magulu a younger-onset anaona kuti maguluwo anali othandiza pa nkhani zogwira ntchito monga kukonzekera zamalamulo, komanso kungomva kuti akumveka. Imanenanso kuti Association ili ndi magulu aulere othandizira osamalira, kuphatikizapo magulu a anthu omwe amasamalira munthu amene ali ndi dementia yoyambira msanga, ndipo atatu mwa iwo amakumana pa intaneti Lachiwiri ndi Lachitatu.

Kuti mufunse za magulu, imbani 24/7 Helpline ya Association pa 800.272.3900. Buku lathu la [kudzisamalira kwa osamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) limafotokoza za kupuma, nthawi yopumula komanso zizindikiro za kutopa kwambiri.

## Magawo akadali ofunika

Gawo la dementia la munthuyo limakhudzabe zomwe zingathandize. Kumayambiriro, anthu ambiri angachitebe zochita zawo za tsiku ndi tsiku ndi kusintha pang'ono, ndipo buku lathu la [zochita za gawo loyambirira](/activities/early-stage/) lili ndi malingaliro ena. Pambuyo pake, zochita zimakhala zosavuta komanso zogwiritsa ntchito mphamvu za m'thupi, monga momwe tafotokozera mu [zochita za gawo lotsiriza](/activities/late-stage/). Sinthani chochitacho pamene munthuyo akusintha, ndipo mufunseni zomwe akufuna nthawi iliyonse imene angathe kunena.
