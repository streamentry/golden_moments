Kupaka utoto ndi imodzi mwa njira zosavuta zolimbikitsira luso kwa munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a Alzheimer's kapena mtundu wina wa dementia. Sikufuna kuwerenga, kukumbukira kapena yankho lolondola. Manja amagwira ntchito, mitundu imaoneka, ndipo pamakhala chithunzi choti mungachezepo. Bukuli likufotokoza mmene mungasankhire masamba, zimene mungakonzekere, komanso mmene mungapakire utoto limodzi popanda kupanga kuti zioneke ngati za ana kapena ngati mayeso.

## Chifukwa chake kupaka utoto kumawayenera anthu ambiri okhala ndi dementia

Kupaka utoto kumapatsa manja choti achite ndipo maso amakhala ndi choti ayangʼane. Kungachitike munthu atakhala patebulo kapena pampando pafupi ndi zenera, ndipo nʼkosavuta kuyamba ndi kusiya. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) imafotokoza zaluso ngati njira yosonyezera momwe munthu akumvera ndikusangalala ndi nthawi yomwe ilipo, ndipo imanena kuti sikufuna luso lapadera lojambula.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) imalangiza kusankha zochita zimene zikugwirizana ndi zimene munthuyo angakwanitsebe kuchita, ndikusintha pamene kuthekera kwake kukusintha. Kupaka utoto nʼkosavuta kusintha: chithunzi chachikulu, makrayoni akulu, mitundu yochepa, kapena sikirini yopaka utoto podina.

## Sankhani zojambula za akulu

Akulu nthawi zambiri sakonda zithunzi za ana. Yangʼanani izi:

- **Malo achilengedwe:** munda wokhala ndi maluwa akulu, nyanja yokhala ndi bwato, mbalame pamtengo.
- **Zinthu zodziwika:** ketulo ya tiyi, njinga, chipatso, chipewa.
- **Mapangidwe akulu, osavuta:** mandala ndi mawonekedwe akulu ochepa okha.
- **Zithunzi zaluso kapena malo** amene munthuyo angawadziwe kuchokera kumalo kapena wojambula amene amakonda.

Pewani masamba okhala ndi malo angʼonoangʼono, ochulukana, chifukwa amatha kukhumudwitsa. Masamba osindikizidwa a akulu ndi njira yabwino, ndipo [zochita zaulere zosindikizidwa za anthu okhala ndi dementia](/activities/printable/) zathu zikuphatikizapo masamba okhala ndi zilembo ndi zithunzi zazikulu.

## Zimene mungakonzekere

- **Zida zazikulu, zosavuta kugwira.** Makrayoni akulu, zolembera zazikulu, kapena mapensulo okhala ndi chogwirira chachikulu ndi osavuta kugwiritsa ntchito kusiyana ndi zolembera zazingʼono.
- **Mitundu yochepa.** Mitundu itatu kapena inayi ndi yokwanira. Zosankha zambiri zingasokoneze.
- **Pepala kapena khadi lolimba.** Pepala lopyapyala limangʼambika ndipo limaterera. Mamatirani ngodya zake kapena gwiritsani ntchito bolodi yogwirira.
- **Tebulo lopanda zinthu zambiri.** Chotsani zinthu zosafunikira ndipo ikani tsamba limodzi panthawi imodzi.
- **Chinthu chofafanizira kapena chosakanizira,** monga nsalu kapena siponji, kuti zioneke mofewa.

## Kupaka utoto pa tabuleti

[Masewera opaka utoto](/games/coloring/) pa Golden Moments amagwira ntchito podina mtundu kenako podina gawo la chithunzi. Safuna kugwiritsa ntchito bwino kwambiri manja, choncho angakhale oyenera anthu amene kugwira kwawo kwafooka. Khalani pafupi ndi munthuyo kuti mugawane sikirini, ndipo amuloleni kusankha mitundu. Zimitsani zidziwitso kuti chithunzi chikhale chinthu chachikulu.

## Mmene mungapakire utoto limodzi

1. **Yambani mzere woyamba limodzi.** Gwirani krayoni limodzi naye, kapena ikani dzanja lanu pamwamba pa lake kwa mizere ingapo, kenako masulani.
2. **Lankhulani za mitundu ndi malo.** “Mtundu wabuluu umenewu ndi wokongola. Kodi mwinaonapo madzi amtundu umenewu kuti?” zimayambitsa kucheza popanda kumufunsa mafunso oyesa kukumbukira.
3. **Tamandani zimene wasankha, osati kulondola.** “Mwasankha mitundu yofunda bwino” kumamveka bwino kuposa “Mwakhala mʼkati mwa mizere.”
4. **Aloleni atsogolere.** Ngati akufuna kujambula paliponse pa tsamba lonse, aloleni.
5. **Lekezani pamene iye walekeza.** Ikani makrayoni pansi, ndipo mungamuuze za tiyi kapena kuyenda pangʼono.

## Kusintha pamene dementia ikusintha

Pa gawo loyamba, anthu nthawi zambiri amasangalala ndi masamba atsatanetsatane ndipo amatha kusankha okha mitundu ndi zojambula. Pa gawo lapakati, zithunzi zazikulu ndi zosankha zochepa zimathandiza. Pa magawo omaliza, yesani mawonekedwe amodzi akulu, pepala la mtundu wofewa, kapena kungogwira krayoni pamene inu mukuyimba kanyimbo modekha. Anthu ena amasangalala kuona chithunzi chomalizidwa kuposa kuchipanga.

Kuti mudziwe zambiri za ntchito zaluso pa gawo lililonse, onani [ntchito zamanja ndi zaluso kwa akulu okhala ndi dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) ndi [ntchito zaluso kwa anthu okhala ndi dementia](/activities/art-projects/).

## Nthawi yoti musiye

Lekezani ngati munthuyo wakhumudwa, wakankhira pepala kutali, kapena akuoneka wotopa. Kutha patatha mphindi ziwiri ndi kupambana ngati anasangalala nazo. Mutha kubwereranso pambuyo pake, kapena kuyesa chithunzi china kapena ntchito ina monga [zochita zolimbikitsa mphamvu za kuzindikira](/activities/sensory/).
