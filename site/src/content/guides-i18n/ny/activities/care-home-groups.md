Bukuli ndi la atsogoleri a zochitika, magulu osamalira anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala ndi atsogoleri a mapulogalamu a masana. Limafotokoza njira za zochitika za gulu zomwe zimagwira ntchito, kakonzedwe ka chipinda, kusintha chochitika chimodzi kuti chigwirizane ndi anthu a luso losiyanasiyana, komanso kagwiritsidwe ntchito ka makadi athu osindikizidwa a bingo ndi masewera a pa chinsalu chachikulu. Zonse zomwe zili pano cholinga chake ndi chitonthozo, kulumikizana ndi kusankha. Palibe chilichonse pano chomwe chili mayeso.

## N’chiyani chimapangitsa chochitika cha gulu kukhala chabwino m’malo osamalira anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala?

Chochitika chabwino cha gulu chimakhala chozoloŵereka, chachifupi, chosavuta kulowa nawo pa mlingo uliwonse komanso chopanda kukakamiza. [Dementia Care Practice Recommendations](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1a0020aa-0bfb-47ac-a545-1dd8a3ee1444/alzheimers-dementia-care-practice-recommendations.pdf) ya Alzheimer’s Association imafotokoza kutenga nawo mbali kothandiza monga chinthu chomwe chimathandizira zokonda za munthu komanso kumapereka mwayi wosankha ndi kuchita bwino, ngakhale pamene matenda a kuiwala ali ovuta kwambiri. Chikalata chomwechi chimanenanso kuti mapulogalamu achikhalidwe nthawi zambiri amadalira chochitika chimodzi kapena ziwiri zazikulu patsiku, zomwe anthu okhalamo nthawi zambiri amangoyang’ana osatenga nawo mbali kwenikweni.

Izi zikusonyeza malamulo ochepa othandiza:

- **Chozoloŵereka n’chabwino kuposa chatsopano.** Nyimbo, mawu odziwika, zithunzi ndi zinthu za m’zaka za ubwana wa anthu okhalamo zimakhala zosavuta kulowa nazo.
- **Chachifupi n’chabwino kuposa chachitali.** Konzani nthawi yofanana ndi mmene gulu lanu limatha kumvetsera, ndi malo achilengedwe oyimapo pakati.
- **Kuchita n’kopambana kungoyang’ana.** Perekani munthu aliyense chinthu choti anyamule, adine, asankhe kapena anene.
- **Milingo yambiri, chochitika chimodzi.** Phatikizani njira zosavuta komanso zovuta pang’ono zotengera nawo mbali.
- **Palibe opambana, palibe mapoints.** Masewera a mpikisano amapangitsa anthu ena kumva ngati alephera.

## Kodi gulu liyenera kukhala lalikulu motani?

Gulu laling’ono nthawi zambiri limakhala losavuta. Alzheimer’s Association imanena kuti munthu amene amavutika ndi msonkhano waukulu akhoza kutenga nawo mbali bwino m’gulu laling’ono. Palibe nambala imodzi yokha yolondola. Zimadalira anthu okhalamo, chipinda komanso kuchuluka kwa ogwira ntchito ndi odzipereka amene alipo.

Njira yothandiza yoyesera: kodi munthu aliyense angathe kuona nkhope ya wotsogolera, kumva bwino ndi kufikiridwa ndi wothandizira mkati mwa masekondi ochepa? Ngati sichoncho, gawani gululo, onjezerani othandizira, kapena pitani kuchipinda chaching’ono.

Pa chochitika chachikulu, monga konsati ya tchuthi, khalitsani ogwira ntchito ndi odzipereka pakati pa anthu okhalamo, osati m’mbali mwa makoma.

## Kodi ndingakonze bwanji chipinda ndi mipando?

- **Mozungulira kapena ngati nsapato ya kavalo.** Malangizo a machitidwewa amafotokoza kuti bwalo logawana limagwira bwino m’malo okwanira kuti aliyense akhale mozungulira, popanda phokoso lina lililonse.
- **Wotsogolera akhale pafupi komanso pa mlingo wa maso.** Khalani pansi m’malo moima ngati n’kotheka.
- **Kumva ndi kuona poyamba.** Khalitsani anthu omwe amavutika kumva pafupi ndi wotsogolera, mbali yomwe amamva bwino. Onetsetsani kuti magalasi ndi zida zothandizira kumva zavalidwa. Alzheimer’s Association imanena kuti mavuto a kuona ndi kumva angapangitse kulankhulana kukhala kovuta.
- **Kuwala kuzichokera kumbuyo kwa anthu okhalamo, osati kumbuyo kwanu.** Pewani kukhalitsa aliyense moyang’anizana ndi zenera lowala.
- **Zimitsani TV ndi wailesi.** Alzheimer’s Association imalangiza kuchotsa phokoso lina lililonse pa nthawi ya zochitika za nyimbo.
- **Kukambirana kumodzi pa nthawi imodzi.** Kukambirana kosiyanasiyana pa nthawi imodzi kumakhala kovuta kwa anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala komanso anthu amene amavutika kumva, monga momwe Alzheimer’s Association imafotokozera mu [malangizo ake a pa tchuthi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays).
- **Njira zotulukira zomveka bwino ndi kona labata.** Aliyense amene akufuna kuchoka ayenera kutero mosavuta, ali ndi wogwira ntchito woti amuyende naye.

## Malingaliro a zochitika za gulu

### Bingo ya zithunzi

Bingo ya zithunzi imagwiritsa ntchito zithunzi zomveka bwino m’malo mwa manambala, monga ketulo, galu kapena duwa, kotero palibe amene akufunika kutsatira nambala yayitali kapena kuwerenga zilembo zazing’ono.

**Mmene mungayitsogolere:**

1. Sindikizani khadi limodzi pa munthu aliyense kuchokera patsamba lathu la [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) pogwiritsa ntchito batani la Print. Pepala lolimba limakhala losavuta kunyamula. Lamitinani kuti mugwiritse ntchito mobwerezabwereza.
2. Perekani zolembera zazikulu komanso zosavuta kunyamula, monga ma chip a poker, zivindikiro za mabotolo kapena mabokosi ang’onoang’ono odulidwa.
3. Gwiritsani ntchito wotcha zithunzi wa pa chinsalu pa TV kapena projekita kuti aliyense aone chithunzi pamene mukutchula dzina lake.
4. Imani pang’ono pambuyo pa chithunzi chilichonse ndipo pemphani ndemanga yaifupi: “Kodi pali wina amene angafune kutiuza za galu amene ankadziwa?” kapena “Kodi mukuona chiyani pachithunzichi?”
5. Pitirizani kutcha mpaka khadi lililonse litadzaza. Aliyense amamaliza.

**Kusintha moyenerera:** Gwiritsani ntchito mabokosi ochepa kwa anthu amene amaona khadi lodzaza ngati losokoneza. Wothandizira angaloze chithunzi chofanana kwa munthu amene akuchifuna. Anthu ena angakonde kunyamula khadi la chithunzi cha wotcha ndi kulionetsa kwa gulu.

### Kuimba limodzi

Nyimbo ndi chimodzi mwa zochitika za gulu zomwe anthu ambiri amasangalala nazo. Alzheimer’s Association imanena kuti ngakhale pa magawo omaliza, munthu akhoza kukhala wokhoza [kuomba kamvekedwe kapena kuimba nyimbo ya paubwana](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music).

**Mmene mungayitsogolere:**

- Sankhani nyimbo zomwe anthu okhalamo anakulira nazo, osati zomwe wotsogolera amakonda. Funsani mabanja zomwe munthu aliyense amakonda.
- Gwiritsani ntchito nyimbo zopanda malonda, zomwe bungweli limanena kuti zingasokoneze.
- Sindikizani mawu a nyimbo m’zilembo zazikulu kwa amene amawerenga, koma musawakakamize.
- Perekani zogwedera, ng’oma za m’manja kapena nsalu zoti agwedeze.
- Limbikitsani kuomba m’manja ndi kuyenda pang’ono, ndipo sungani phokoso pa mlingo wabwino.

**Kusintha moyenerera:** Ena adzaimba mawu onse, ena adzang’ung’udza, ena adzagunda phazi, ndipo ena adzangomvetsera. Zonsezi n’zabwino. Onani [kalozera wathu wa zochitika za nyimbo](/activities/music/) kuti mumve zambiri.

### Magulu okambirana za zakale

Gulu lokambirana za zakale limasonkhana mozungulira mutu umodzi, monga masiku a kusukulu, magalimoto oyamba, makhitchini kapena chilimwe, pogwiritsa ntchito zithunzi, zinthu ndi nyimbo. Cholinga chake ndi kukambirana kosangalatsa, osati kukumbukira molondola.

**Mmene mungayitsogolere:**

1. Sankhani mutu umodzi ndipo bweretsani zinthu zochepa zomwe anthu angathe kugwira: chosakanizira cha m’manja, magolovesi a baseball, chipewa, kabukhu ka mbewu.
2. Perekani chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi. Funsani mafunso otseguka: “Ndiuzeni za ichi,” “Mukuona chiyani?” “Ndani ankagwiritsa ntchito chinthu ngati ichi?”
3. Landirani yankho lililonse. Musakonze tsiku kapena mfundo ina iliyonse.
4. Tsogolerani kukambirana ku zokumbukira zabwino. Malangizo a machitidwewa amati opereka chisamaliro ayenera kutsogolera zokambirana za zakale ku zokumbukira zabwino osati zomwe zingabweretse chisoni.

[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) imapereka makadi osindikizidwa a zithunzi okhala ndi mafunso otseguka malinga ndi mutu, omwe amagwira ntchito bwino poyambitsa zokambirana. Malingaliro ena ali mu [kalozera wathu wa kukambirana za zakale](/activities/reminiscence/).

### Malizitsani mawu odziwika

Mawu odziwika amalimbikitsa aliyense kunena mapeto ake limodzi. Onetsani [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) pa chinsalu chachikulu ndipo lolani gulu liyankhe ngati liwu limodzi. Pali funso loyambitsa kukambirana pambuyo pa mawu aliwonse odziwika. Ngati palibe amene wanena mapeto ake, awonetseni ndipo pitirirani. Palibe cholondola kapena cholakwika.

### Kusaka mawu a zilembo zazikulu, limodzi

Onetsani [Word Search](/games/word-search/) ya zilembo zazikulu pa chinsalu ndikusaka mawu ngati gulu, kapena sindikizani makope kwa amene amasangalala kugwira ntchito okha patebulo. Mawu amayenda mopingasa ndi pansi pokha.

### Mayendedwe ofatsa

Kutambasula mutakhala pansi, kudutsirana mpira wofewa kapena baluni, kapena kuvina mutakhala pansi motsatira nyimbo zozoloŵereka. Onetsani kayendedwe kalikonse m’malo mokafotokoza.

### Kusanja ndi kufananitsa patebulo

Magulu ang’onoang’ono a anthu atatu kapena anayi patebulo amatha kusanja zithunzi, nsalu kapena ziwiya limodzi. Pa chinsalu, [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ingaseweredwe ndi zithunzi zikuoneka, munthu mmodzi akudina pamene anthu patebulo akukambirana za chithunzi chilichonse.

## Kodi ndingagwiritse ntchito bwanji masewera anu pa chinsalu chachikulu?

Masewera athu amagwira ntchito mu msakatuli wa pa intaneti, kotero mutha kuwagwiritsa ntchito pa TV kapena projekita popanda kuyika chilichonse.

1. Tsegulani masewerawo pa laputopu kapena tabuleti yolumikizidwa ku chinsalu ndi chingwe kapena pogwiritsa ntchito njira yowonetsera opanda zingwe.
2. Pangani msakatuli kukhala pa chinsalu chonse ndipo sinthani phokoso kuti likhale pa mlingo wabwino.
3. Lolani wotsogolera mmodzi azidina pamene gulu likutchula zomwe lasankha. Kapena perekani tabuleti kwa munthu wokhala ndi matenda a kuiwala amene akufuna kuyesa.
4. Imani nthawi iliyonse pamene gulu lakonzeka. Masewerawa alibe nthawi yowerengera, mapoints kapena kumaliza mokakamiza.

[Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/), [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/), [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ndi [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) nthawi zambiri amagwira bwino ndi magulu. Onani zosankha zonse patsamba lathu la [masewera](/games/).

## Kodi ndingasinthe bwanji chochitika chimodzi kuti chigwirizane ndi anthu a luso losiyanasiyana?

Konzani njira zosachepera zitatu zoloweramo pa chochitika chilichonse.

| Chochitika | Wodziimira payekha kwambiri | Thandizo pang’ono | Thandizo lalikulu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bingo ya zithunzi | Amapeza ndi kuphimba zithunzi zake yekha | Wothandizira amaloza; munthuyo amaphimba | Amanyamula khadi la chithunzi; amamvetsera ndi kuyang’ana |
| Kuimba limodzi | Amaimba, amasankha nyimbo yotsatira | Amang’ung’udza, amaomba m’manja, amagwiritsa ntchito chogwedera | Amamvetsera, amanyamula nsalu, amagunda dzanja |
| Kukambirana za zakale | Amafotokoza nkhani | Amayankha inde kapena ayi kapena amaloza | Amanyamula ndi kumva chinthucho |
| Kusaka mawu | Amagwira ntchito pa khadi lake losindikizidwa | Amapeza mawu pa chinsalu chachikulu ndi wothandizira | Amamvetsera pamene mawu akuwerengedwa |

Kwa anthu amene ali pa magawo omaliza, NHS imanena kuti zochitika nthawi zambiri zimafunika kukhala zosavuta komanso kuyang’ana kwambiri pa zomverera, monga nyimbo, zinthu zogwira ndi kusisita m’manja. Nthawi ya munthu mmodzi ndi mmodzi pa nthawi ya gulu kapena pambuyo pake ingamuyenerere bwino kuposa gululo lenilenilo. Onani [zochitika za magawo omaliza](/activities/late-stage/).

## Kodi atsogoleri ayenera kulankhula bwanji pa nthawi ya gulu?

- Dzidziwitseni nthawi iliyonse, ndipo gwiritsani ntchito dzina limene munthu aliyense amakonda.
- Lankhulani pang’onopang’ono, perekani sitepe imodzi pa nthawi imodzi, ndipo onetsani musanafotokoze.
- Funsani mafunso otseguka (“Ndiuzeni za...”) kapena zosankha zosavuta, osati mafunso oyesa kukumbukira (“Kodi mukukumbukira...?”).
- Musakonze zolakwika. Yamikirani aliyense pa zomwe wanena.

Mutha kuwerenga zambiri mu [malangizo athu olankhulirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/).

## Kutsiriza bwino, ndi kulola anthu kuchoka

Kuima ndi zotsatira zabwino. Yang’anani zizindikiro zosonyeza kuti wina watopa, sakukhazikika kapena wakwiya, ndipo lolani wothandizira apereke kuyenda naye kapena mpando wabata kwina. Munthu amene wagona pang’ono pa nthawi yoimba limodzi akhoza kukhala akungomva bwino.

Tsekani gulu lililonse mofanana, ndi nyimbo yozoloŵereka kapena kuyamika kosavuta, kuti mapeto ake amveke abata komanso odziwika. Pambuyo pake, lembani mwachidule zomwe zayenda bwino ndi zomwe munthu aliyense wasangalala nazo. Zolemba zimenezo zimathandiza kuti nthawi yotsatira ikhale yabwino kwambiri.
