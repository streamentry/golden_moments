Mukafufuza “memory games for seniors”, zambiri zomwe mumapeza zinapangidwira anthu amene akufuna kulimbitsa maganizo: makadi obisika, mawotchi owerengera nthawi, milingo ndi mapointi. Kwa munthu amene akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwalika, zimenezi zimasintha masewera kukhala mayeso a chinthu chimene chakhala chovuta. Masewera omwewo angaseweredwe mʼnjira yosavuta komanso yodekha. Tsamba ili likufotokoza mmene mungachitire, komanso likutchula masewera oyenera pa gawo lililonse.

## Nʼchifukwa chiyani masewera achikale a kukumbukira amakhumudwitsa anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwalika?

Chifukwa amadalira kusunga mfundo zatsopano mʼmaganizo, zomwe nthawi zambiri ndi chinthu choyamba chimene matenda a Alzheimer, omwe ndi chifukwa chodziŵika kwambiri cha matenda a kuiwalika, amakhudza. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) imanena kuti chizindikiro choyambirira chodziŵika kwambiri cha matenda a Alzheimer ndi kuvutika kukumbukira mfundo zomwe zangophunziridwa kumene. Mu Concentration yachikale, mumatembenuza makadi awiri, kukumbukira komwe anali, ndikugwiritsa ntchito zimenezo patapita kanthawi. Izo ndi mfundo zatsopano, zosungidwa ndi kukumbukiridwa.

Mukawonjezerapo wowerengera nthawi, mapointi kapena kulira kwa mayankho olakwika, munthuyo amakumbutsidwa za zimene sangathe kuchita, pamaso pa munthu amene amamukonda. Anthu ambiri amasiya kufuna kusewera modekha.

Kuzindikira chinthu chimene mukuchiona ndi ntchito yosiyana ndipo nthawi zambiri imakhala yosavuta. Kuloza chikho chachiwiri cha tiyi pamene zonse zikuonekera kumakhala kosangalatsa. Choncho chinsinsi chake ndi kusunga kufananitsa koma kuchotsa kuyesedwa kwa kukumbukira.

## Kodi mungapangire bwanji masewera a kukumbukira kukhala osavuta?

Kusintha kumeneku kumagwira ntchito pa masewera a makadi, masewera a pa bolodi ndi masewera a pa sikirini.

1. **Siyani zithunzi zoonekera.** Kufananitsa zithunzi zomwe mukuziona ndikadali masewera enieni, ndipo nthawi zambiri amakhala osangalatsa.
2. **Gwiritsani ntchito zidutswa zochepa.** Yambani ndi awiriawiri awiri kapena atatu. Onjezerani pokhapokha zitaoneka zosavuta kwambiri ndipo munthuyo akufuna zambiri.
3. **Sankhani zithunzi zazikulu, zozoloŵereka komanso za anthu akulu.** Chikho cha tiyi, duwa la mpendadzuwa, bwato la ngalawa. Osati makatuni, komanso osati zithunzi zodzaza zinthu zambiri.
4. **Sewerani ngati gulu.** Muuzeni kuti “Tiyeni tipeze agalu” mʼmalo mwa “ndi nthawi yanu.” Sinthanani kuloza ndi kuthandizana.
5. **Siyani kuwerengera mapointi.** Palibe amene akufunika kupambana. Ngati wina afunsa, kungoti “tachita” kumakwanira.
6. **Musathamange ndi nthawi.** Palibe wowerengera nthawi, ndipo musathamange pakati pa masinthano.
7. **Kambiranani za zithunzi.** “Onani bwato lija. Kodi munayendapo ngalawa?” Nthawi zambiri kukambiranaku ndiye mbali yabwino kwambiri.
8. **Lekani pamene zonse zikuyenda bwino.** Malizani mutapeza awiriawiri, pamene zikadali zosangalatsa.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) imapereka malangizo ofanana: sankhani zochitika mogwirizana ndi zimene munthuyo angathe kuchita, onani zizindikiro za kukhumudwa, ndipo onetsetsani kuti akumva kuti wachita bwino.

## Masewera osavuta a kukumbukira ndi kufananitsa oyenera kuyesa

### Picture pairs (kufananitsa ndi zithunzi zoonekera)

[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ndi masewera ofananitsa okhala ndi zithunzi zazikulu ndi matailosi 4 mpaka 12. Zithunzi zimatha kukhala zoonekera, choncho cholinga chake ndi kupeza zithunzi zofanana, osati kukumbukira komwe zinali. Yambani ndi matailosi anayi. Kwa munthu amene ali mʼgawo loyambirira ndipo amasangalala ndi zovuta pangʼono, mungasinthe kukhala **Hidden pictures**, koma bwererani ku kusonyeza zithunzi pomwepo zikasiya kukhala zosangalatsa.

### Find the same

[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) imasonyeza chithunzi chimodzi pamwamba ndi zisankho ziwiri mpaka zinayi pansi. Dinani chimene chikufanana. Ndi masewera ofananitsa osavuta kwambiri patsamba lino ndipo ndi oyenera anthu amene amaona kuti gulu lonse la zithunzi ndi lochulukirapo, kuphatikizapo ambiri amene ali mʼmagawo omaliza ali ndi munthu wina pambali pawo.

### Photo pairs (masewera ofananitsa zithunzi za banja)

[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) imasintha zithunzi zomwe mwasankha kukhala masewera ofananitsa: galu, dimba, nyumba ya pa nyanja. Nkhope ndi malo odziŵika bwino nthawi zambiri amayambitsa kukambirana kuposa chithunzi chilichonse wamba. Zithunzizo zimakhala mu msakatuli wanu ndipo sizitumizidwa kulikonse.

### Sort together

[Sort together](/games/sort-together/) imakupemphani kuyika chithunzi chilichonse mʼdengu loyenera, monga kukhitchini kapena mʼdimba. Kusanja kumadalira chidziŵitso chokhalitsa cha mmene zinthu zimagwirira ntchito, osati kukumbukira kwakanthawi kochepa.

### Picture puzzle

[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) imadula chithunzi kukhala zidutswa 4 kapena 9. Dinani zidutswa ziwiri kuti musinthane malo ake mpaka chithunzi chikhale chathunthu. Chithunzi chathunthu chimakhala pa sikirini ngati chitsogozo.

### Makadi enieni pa tebulo

Simufunikira sikirini. Tengani awiriawiri ofanana mʼmatumba awiri a makadi kapena mu makadi azithunzi. Awayale pansi ali oonekera mʼmizere iwiri ndipo pezani awiriawiri limodzi. Pambuyo pake, sankhani ndi mtundu kapena mtundu wa makadi. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) imatchula masewera a makadi ndi a pa bolodi pakati pa zochitika za gawo loyambirira.

## Kodi masewera a kukumbukira ndi oyenera gawo liti?

Magawo ndi mayina ongowonetsa. Yangʼanani pa zimene munthuyo akusangalala nazo lero.

| Gawo | Masewera omwe nthawi zambiri amayenera | Mmene mungawakhazikitsire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loyambirira | Picture pairs, Photo pairs, Picture puzzle yokhala ndi zidutswa 9, masewera odziŵika a makadi | Muloleni asankhe. Matailosi angathe kutembenuzidwa ngati akusangalala nazo; siyani kuwerengera mapointi ngati zikumusokoneza |
| Lapakati | Picture pairs yokhala ndi matailosi 4 mpaka 6 oonekera, Find the same, Sort together | Zithunzi zikhale zoonekera nthawi zonse. Khalani pambali pake ndipo sinthanani kuthandizana |
| Lomaliza | Find the same yokhala ndi zisankho 2, kuyangʼana chithunzi chimodzi chachikulu limodzi | Kwa kanthawi kochepa kwambiri. Inu gwirani tabuleti; iwo aloze, kapena angoyangʼana ndi kumvetsera |

## Kodi masewera a kukumbukira amathandiza pa matenda a kuiwalika?

Palibe masewera a kukumbukira amene aonetsedwa kuti amachiza, amachedwetsa kapena kuletsa matenda a kuiwalika. [Kufufuza kwa Cochrane](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/) pa kuphunzitsa ubongo pogwiritsa ntchito kompyuta kwa anthu amene ali ndi vuto lochepa la kuzindikira linapeza kuti umboni ndi wochepa kwambiri kuti unene ngati umalepheretsa matenda a kuiwalika. Tsamba lathu la [masewera a ubongo ndi matenda a kuiwalika](/brain-health/brain-games/) likufotokoza zimene kafukufuku amasonyeza ndi zimene sasonyeza.

Ichi si chifukwa chosiira masewera. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) imalangiza kuika maganizo pa kusangalala, osati pa kupambana. Mphindi khumi zofananitsa zithunzi ndi kukambirana za izo zingakhale gawo labwino kwambiri la tsiku.

## Nanga bwanji masewera a kukumbukira kwa achikulire amene alibe matenda a kuiwalika?

Ngati munthu wachikulire amene alibe mavuto a kukumbukira amasangalala ndi mapazulo a nthawi, mapointi ndi milingo yovuta, palibe cholakwika ndi zimenezo. Kungoti musadalire zimenezo kuti zingamuteteze ku matenda a kuiwalika; palibe masewera amene aonetsedwa kuti amachita zimenezo. Mitundu yosavuta yomwe yafotokozedwa pamwambapa imagwiranso bwino ntchito pamene mibadwo ikusewera limodzi: agogo amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwalika ndi mdzukulu angafanane zithunzi ngati gulu, popanda aliyense kuyesedwa.

## Kodi muyenera kusiya kapena kusintha masewera liti?

Kusisima, kukankhira tabuleti kutali, kunena kuti “sindingathe” kapena kuoneka kuti ali ndi nkhawa ndi zizindikiro zonse zoti mufunika kupangitsa zinthu kukhala zosavuta kapena kusiya. Yesani matailosi ochepa, tembenuzani zithunzi kuti zikhale zoonekera, sinthani kupita ku [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/), kapena ingoyangʼanani zithunzi ndi kucheza. Ngati sakufuna kusewera nʼkomwe, zilibwinonso. Tsamba lathu la [mmene mungasewere limodzi](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) lili ndi malangizo ambiri pa kayendedwe ndi kuthandizana.
