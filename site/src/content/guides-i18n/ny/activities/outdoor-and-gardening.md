Kwa anthu ambiri, dimba ndi malo odziwika bwino kwambiri. Kukumba, kuthirira ndi kuthyola maluwa zimagwiritsa ntchito luso limene anaphunzira kalekale, ndipo kukhala panja kumabweretsa mpweya wabwino, kuwala kwa dzuwa ndi kuyimba kwa mbalame. National Institute on Aging imatchula kusamalira zomera, kubzala maluwa ndi ndiwo zamasamba, komanso [kukambirana za momwe zomera zikukulira](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) pakati pa zochita zimene anthu okhala ndi Alzheimerʼs angasangalale nazo. Tsambali likufotokoza zimene mungayesere, momwe mungapangire dimba kukhala losavuta kugwiritsa ntchito, komanso momwe mungakhalire otetezeka pa kutentha, dzuwa ndi kusokera.

## Kodi ndi zochita ziti zakunja ndi za mʼdimba zimene zimagwira ntchito bwino?

Ntchito zabwino kwambiri ndi zosavuta, zodziwika ndipo zikhoza kuchitika munthu ataimirira kapena atakhala. Sankhani malinga ndi zimene munthuyo ankakonda kale.

### Mʼdimba

1. **Kuthirira.** Chidebe chothirira chopepuka ndi chachingʼono kapena payipi yokhala ndi nsonga yofatsa ya madzi ndi miphika ingapo pafupi. Kuthirira kuli ndi chiyambi ndi mapeto omveka bwino ndipo nʼkovuta kulakwitsa.
2. **Kubzala mbewu mʼmiphika.** Alzheimerʼs Foundation of America imalimbikitsa kubzala mbewu mʼmiphika yaingʼono ndi [kuziyangʼanira zikamakula](https://alzfdn.org/dementia-activity-gardening/). Mbewu zazikulu monga nyemba, mpendadzuwa kapena nandolo nʼzosavuta kugwira.
3. **Kuchotsa maluwa ofota.** Kuthyola maluwa ofota ndi manja ndi ntchito yofatsa komanso yobwerezabwereza. Sankhani zomera zopanda minga.
4. **Kuthyola ndiwo zamasamba kapena zitsamba.** Tomato tatingʼono, nyemba ndi zitsamba nʼzosavuta kuthyola ndipo nʼzabwino kulawa. Bweretsani mʼnyumba kuti mugwiritse ntchito pa chakudya cha masana.
5. **Kununkhiza ndi kukhudza zitsamba.** Rosemary, mint ndi lavender zili ndi fungo lamphamvu komanso lokoma. Pukutani tsamba pakati pa zala zanu ndikulipereka.
6. **Kusesa njira kapena bwalo.** Tsache lopepuka pa malo athyathyathya komanso owuma, ngati munthu angayende bwino.
7. **Kudzaza chodyeramo mbalame.** Kenako khalani limodzi ndikuyangʼana mbalame zikubwera.

### Mʼkati mwa nyumba, ngati simungathe kutuluka

- **Dimba la zitsamba pa windo** la basil, parsley kapena chives.
- **Kubzala pa tebulo lokhala ndi chophimba.** AFA imalimbikitsa kugwiritsa ntchito mathireyi ngati a mu cafeteria kuti dothi likhale pamalo amodzi ndikuyendetsa dothi ndi makapu angʼonoangʼono.
- **Kukonza maluwa** pogwiritsa ntchito maluwa odulidwa ndi masamba kuchokera mʼbwalo kapena mʼsitolo.
- **Maluwa ofinyidwa** kuchokera pa mtolo wa maluwa, omwe pambuyo pake angamatidwe pa makadi. Onani [zaluso ndi ntchito zamanja](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) kuti mudziwe zambiri.
- **Kupanga timatumba ta lavender kapena rose**, lingaliro linanso la AFA lomwe limasunga fungo la dimba mʼnyumba.

### Kutuluka panja

- **Kuyenda pangʼono** mozungulira dera, paki kapena malo ogulitsa zomera za mʼdimba, pangʼonopangʼono komanso pali malo okhalapo.
- **Kukhala panja** pa khonde kapena pa benchi kumvetsera mbalame ndi kumva mphepo. Imeneyi ndi ntchito yokwanira yokha.
- **Kupita ku dimba la anthu onse** nthawi yomwe kulibe anthu ambiri. NHS imanena kuti malo ena ali ndi minda yolimbikitsa mphamvu za thupi yokhala ndi zomera zonunkhira, zinthu zosiyanasiyana zokhudza komanso madzi.
- **Kudyetsa abakha kapena kuyangʼana mbalame** pa dziwe lapafupi, mukhale kutali motetezeka ndi mʼmphepete mwa madzi.

## Kodi ndingapangire bwanji dimba kukhala losavuta kugwiritsa ntchito?

Bweretsani dimba pafupi ndi munthuyo, osati munthuyo kupita ku dimba.

- **Mabedi okwezeka kapena miphika yaitali** pafupifupi msinkhu wa chiuno amatanthauza kuti sipafunika kugwada kapena kuwerama.
- **Miphika pa tebulo** imagwira ntchito kwa anthu amene amagwiritsa ntchito njinga ya olumala kapena amakonda kukhala pansi.
- **Njira zazikulu komanso zathyathyathya** zopanda miyala yotayirira kapena mapaipi ogona panjira zimachepetsa chiopsezo chopunthwa.
- **Mipando yokhala ndi zotsamira manja ndi mthunzi** mʼnjira, kuti kupuma kukhale kosavuta nthawi zonse.
- **Zida zokhala ndi zogwirira zazikulu komanso zofewa** zomwe nʼzosavuta kugwira. Pewani zometera nthambi, mipeni ndi chilichonse chakuthwa.
- **Malo otetezedwa okhala ndi mpanda ndi chipata chotsekeka** omwe munthuyo angayendemo momasuka pamene inu muli pafupi.

## Kodi ndi zomera ziti zimene ndiyenera kuzipewa?

Sankhani zomera zomwe nʼzotetezeka kukhudza ndipo, ngati nʼkotheka, zotetezeka ngakhale munthu atazilawa. AFA imalangiza kugwiritsa ntchito zomera zomwe sizimakwiyitsa khungu kawirikawiri, kuyangʼana ngati pali ziwengo, komanso kuyangʼanitsitsa aliyense amene angaganize kuti dothi ndi chakudya. Malangizo a chitetezo kunyumba a NIA akuphatikizapo kuchotsa kapena kutseka zomera za poizoni.

- Pewani maluwa a minga, cacti, ndi zomera zokhala ndi madzi amene amakwiya khungu.
- Ngati simukutsimikiza za chomera chilichonse, funsani ku malo othandizira pa nkhani za poizoni kapena kwa ogulitsa zomera za mʼdimba musanachibzale.
- Zomera zokondedwa zomwe munthuyo amazidziwa zikhoza kubweretsa malingaliro abwino. AFA imatchula maluwa ndi Christmas cactus monga zitsanzo. Sankhani mitundu yopanda minga.
- Sungani feteleza, mankhwala ophera tizilombo ndi mankhwala opha udzu mʼmalo otsekedwa, ndipo musawagwiritse ntchito mʼmalo amene munthuyo angafikire.

## Kodi ndingakhalire bwanji otetezeka pa kutentha ndi dzuwa?

Konzani nthawi yokhala panja mogwirizana ndi nyengo. NIA imafotokoza kuti anthu achikulire ali pachiopsezo chachikulu cha [matenda obwera chifukwa cha kutentha](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/hot-weather-safety-older-adults), mwa zina chifukwa thupi silisintha bwino pa kusintha kwa kutentha ndipo mankhwala ena amakhudza momwe thupi limagwirira ntchito ndi kutentha.

- **Tulukani mʼmawa kapena madzulo.** Pewani pakati pa tsiku mʼmiyezi yotentha. Yangʼanani kutentha komwe thupi limamva, osati kutentha kwa pa thermometer kokha.
- **Perekani madzi pafupipafupi.** Musadikire kuti munthuyo anene kuti ali ndi ludzu. Pewani mowa ndi zakumwa zokhala ndi caffeine pa nthawi yotentha.
- **Valani moyenera.** Zovala zopepuka, zotayirira, chipewa chokhala ndi mʼmphepete mwake ndi magalasi adzuwa.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito mafuta oteteza khungu ku dzuwa.** NIA imalimbikitsa mafuta oteteza khungu a broad-spectrum SPF 15 kapena kupitirira apo, ndikuwapakapakanso masana.
- **Sankhani malo a mthunzi.** Ikani tebulo lobzalirapo pansi pa mtengo kapena ambulera.
- **Ikhaleni yaifupi.** Kukhala panja kwa kanthawi kochepa kungakhale kokwanira, makamaka masiku otentha.
- **Yangʼanirani zizindikiro zochenjeza.** NIA imatchula kupweteka kwa mutu, kusokonezeka maganizo, chizungulire kapena nseru ngati zizindikiro zomwe zingasonyeze matenda obwera chifukwa cha kutentha. Munthu amene ali ndi dementia sangakuuzeni kuti sakumva bwino, choncho yangʼanirani nkhope yake ndi khalidwe lake. Lowani mʼnyumba, muziziritseni ndi madzi ndi nsalu yozizira, ndipo funani thandizo lachipatala ngati sakusintha mwamsanga. Heat stroke, yokhala ndi zizindikiro monga kukomoka, kusokonezeka maganizo, kapena khungu louma ndi lotuwa, ndi vuto ladzidzidzi lachipatala: imbani 911.

Nyengo yozizira ndiyofunikanso kuiganizira. Mʼnyengo yozizira, khalani panja kwa kanthawi kochepa ndipo onetsetsani kuti manja ndi mapazi zikhale zotentha komanso zowuma.

## Kodi ndingatani ngati munthu amasokera tikakhala panja?

Khalani ndi munthuyo nthawi iliyonse imene ali panja. Alzheimerʼs Association imanena kuti [anthu asanu ndi mmodzi mwa khumi aliwonse okhala ndi dementia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering) adzasokera kamodzi, ndipo ambiri amasokera kangapo. Izi zikhoza kuchitika pa gawo lililonse.

[Malangizo a NIA okhudza kusokera ndi kusowa](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/wandering-and-alzheimers-disease) akuphatikizapo:

- Onetsetsani kuti munthuyo wavala chibangili cha chiphaso kapena chachipatala chokhala ndi dzina lake, adiresi ndi nambala yanu ya foni, kapena lembani pa zovala zake.
- Khalani ndi chithunzi chaposachedwa kapena kanema wa munthuyo.
- Uzani anansi kuti munthuyo akhoza kusokera.
- Tetezani bwalo ndi mpanda ndi chipata chotsekedwa.
- Musasiye munthu amene ali ndi mbiri yosokera ali yekha.

Alzheimerʼs Association imalimbikitsanso kupanga malo a mʼkati ndi akunja omwe angayendewemo motetezeka, ndikukonza zochita pa nthawi ya tsiku pamene kusakhazikika kumachitika kwambiri. Kuyenda mʼdimba lotetezeka komanso lotsekedwa kungakhale koyenera pa kusakhazikika kwa madzulo. Onani malangizo athu okhudza [kusakhazikika kwa madzulo](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Ngati munthuyo wasowa, yangʼanani malo otetezeka apafupi pamene mukufunira thandizo kwa apolisi kapena ntchito zadzidzidzi zakumaloko mwamsanga. Musadikire mphindi zinazake zokhazikika musananene. Nkhani ya mu 2021 ya [U.S. Department of Justice yokhudza kufufuza anthu osowa](https://www.justice.gov/usdoj-media/usao/media/1134031/dl?inline=) imanena kuti mabungwe azamalamulo sayenera kufuna nthawi yokhazikika yodikira ndipo imatchula zizindikiro zofanana ndi za dementia ngati nkhani yachiwopsezo chachikulu. Njira zofotokozera zakumaloko zimasiyana. Fotokozani nthawi ndi malo amene munthuyo anaonedwa komaliza, zomwe wavala ndi chithunzi chaposachedwa. Tsatirani malangizo a oyankha akumaloko; musakasake nokha mʼmalo oopsa.

## Kodi ndingasinthire bwanji ntchito za mʼdimba zinthu zikasintha?

| Ngati izi zakhala zovuta | Yesani izi mʼmalo mwake |
| --- | --- |
| Kubzala bedi lonse | Kubzala mbewu imodzi mu mphika umodzi |
| Kupalira (kusiyanitsa udzu ndi zomera) | Kuthirira, kapena kuchotsa maluwa ofota ooneka bwino |
| Kuyenda mozungulira dimba | Kukhala pa tebulo ndi thireyi ya dothi ndi chomera |
| Kutuluka panja konse | Mphika wa zitsamba pa tebulo la kukhitchini, kapena mpando pafupi ndi windo lotseguka |
| Kuchita ntchito iliyonse | Kukhala panja limodzi, kumvetsera ndi kuyangʼana |

Anthu ena amakonda kukuonani mukugwira ntchito. NIA imanena kuti kulola munthu kungoyangʼana nʼkolondola ngati zimenezo ndi zimene akusangalala nazo.

## Kodi ndi nthawi iti yomwe tiyenera kulowa mʼnyumba?

Lowani mʼnyumba pamene munthuyo akuoneka wotopa, akumva kutentha, kuzizira, kusamasuka kapena wakonzeka kusiya. Mphindi khumi zazifupi komanso zosangalatsa nʼzabwino kuposa masana atali omwe amatha movutikira. Kusiya msanga ndi zotsatira zabwino.

## Bweretsani dimba mʼnyumba pa sikirini

Masiku a mvula, [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) ili ndi dengu la dimba losankhiramo zithunzi, ndipo [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ili ndi mafunso otseguka omwe angayambitse nkhani za mʼdimba. Ngati mwalowa mʼnyumba kuchokera ku kutentha mukumva kuthamanga, [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) imapereka nthawi yodekha komanso yofatsa yomwe mungagawane. Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena okhudza kukhudza, kununkhiza ndi kumva, onani malangizo athu a [zochita zolimbikitsa mphamvu za thupi](/activities/sensory/).
