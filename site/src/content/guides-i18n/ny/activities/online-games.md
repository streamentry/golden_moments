Mabanja ambiri amafunafuna masewera a pa intaneti aulere a anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala chifukwa amafuna china choti achite limodzi chomwe sichifuna sitolo, kulowa mu akaunti kapena khadi yolipirira. Zosankha zabwino kwambiri ndi zosavuta, zozikidwa pa zithunzi, komanso zosavuta kuzisiya. Ziyenera kumveka ngati nthawi yocheza limodzi, osati mayeso. Kalozerayu akufotokoza zomwe muyenera kuyangʼana, momwe mungakonzekerere masewera, komanso zomwe masewerawa angachite ndi zomwe sangachite.

## Kodi nʼchiyani chimapangitsa masewera a pa intaneti kukhala oyenera kwa anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala?

Masewera amakhala oyenera kwa anthu ambiri okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala akamafuna kuzindikira zinthu osati kukumbukira. Kufananitsa zithunzi ziwiri zofanana, kugwira chithunzi chomwe chikufanana ndi mthunzi, kapena kuika chinthu mʼdengu loyenera zonse zimadalira zomwe maso akuona panthawiyo. Masewera omwe amadalira kukumbukira lamulo, mawu, nambala kapena kusuntha komwe kunachitika kale nthawi zambiri amabweretsa kukhumudwa.

Bungwe la [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) limalimbikitsa zochitika zomwe zimamangidwa pa zomwe munthuyo amasangalalabe nazo ndipo angakwanitse kuchita, ndiponso zomwe zimamulola kuchita bwino popanda thandizo. Bungwe la [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) limalangiza kuti muchepetse kuvuta kwa chochitikacho mʼmalo mosiyiratu pamene kuthekera kukusintha.

## Masewera a Golden Moments omwe mungatsegule mu msakatuli

Masewera aliwonse pansipa amatseguka pa tsamba la intaneti popanda kutsitsa. Ambiri amaseweredwa pa tabuleti kapena kompyuta; ena angathenso kusindikizidwa.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Zithunzi zazikulu zofananitsa zomwe zimaoneka, popanda nthawi yowerengera kapena chiwerengero. Ndi abwino poyambira.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Chithunzi chimodzi pamwamba, ndipo chomwecho chili pakati pa zosankha ziwiri mpaka zinayi pansi.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Mthunzi wofewa umaoneka pamwamba; gwirani chithunzi chomwe chimaupanga.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Gwirani chithunzi, kenako dengu lomwe likuyenera, monga la kukhitchini kapena la mʼdimba.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Chithunzi chimadulidwa mʼzidutswa ndipo chithunzi chonse chimakhalabe chooneka ngati chitsogozo.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Miyambi yodziwika bwino yomwe ikusowa mawu omaliza; sankhani yankho kapena onetsani mapeto ake.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Makhadi azithunzi okhala ndi mafunso otseguka oyankhulirana, osati kufunsa mayeso. Angathenso kusindikizidwa.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Zithunzi zofananitsa zopangidwa kuchokera ku zithunzi zomwe mwasankha pa chipangizo chanu. Zithunzizo zimakhala mu msakatuli pa chipangizo chimenecho.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Nthawi yopuma modekha kuti mupume limodzi. Inu mumayamba ndipo inu mumasiya.

Kusaka mawu, kujambula utoto ndi bingo ya zithunzi ziliponso ngati zochitika zosindikizidwa. Onani [zochitika zaulere zosindikizidwa za anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/printable/) kuti mudziwe zambiri.

## Momwe mungakonzekerere nthawi yosewerera

1. **Sankhani nthawi yabata** pamene munthuyo wapumula. Nthawi zambiri masana otsiriza amakhala ovuta kwa anthu ena, choncho onani kaye momwe zimakhalira kwa inu.
2. **Gwiritsani ntchito chophimba chachikulu.** Tabuleti yoyikidwa pa choyimikapo, kapena kompyuta yokhala ndi msakatuli wokulitsidwa, imakhala yosavuta kuiona ndi kuigwira.
3. **Tsekani ma tabu ena ndipo zimitsani zidziwitso.** Zinthu zodumphadumpha mwadzidzidzi zimadabwitsa ndipo zingathe kusokoneza nthawi yabwino.
4. **Khalani pambali pa munthuyo, osati moyangʼanizana naye.** Kuonera limodzi kumamveka ngati kucheza osati mayeso.
5. **Yambani ndi masewera amodzi kwa mphindi zisanu mpaka khumi.** Lolani munthuyo agwire chophimba kapena aloze pamene mukusinthana.
6. **Malizani bwino.** Malizani kuzungulira kumodzi, kenako lankhulani za chithunzi chimodzi kapena muuzeni kuti amwe tiyi.

## Chinsinsi ndi chitetezo pa zipangizo zogwiritsidwa ntchito ndi anthu ambiri

- **Zithunzi zimakhala pa chipangizocho.** Photo pairs imagwiritsa ntchito zithunzi zomwe mwasankha, ndipo zimakhala mu msakatuli pa chipangizo chimenecho. Onani kuti ndani wina angathe kutsegula kompyuta kapena tabuleti.
- **Pewani maulalo akunja ndi zotsatsa.** Gwiritsani ntchito masewerawa molunjika pa tsambali, ndipo musadina zikwangwani kapena mauthenga omwe akupempha zambiri zanu.
- **Sungani zolipira ndi ma akaunti padera.** Musasiye munthuyo yekha pa chophimba chomwe chikupempha mawu achinsinsi kapena nambala ya khadi.
- **Samalani za kuyendayenda.** Sungani tabuleti kutali ngati munthuyo angathe kutuluka nayo panja.

## Zomwe masewerawa angachite ndi zomwe sangachite

Masewera a pa intaneti amapatsa anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala mwayi wosangalala ndi nthawi ina, kuyangʼana zithunzi limodzi, ndi kutenga nawo mbali mu china chake popanda kukakamizidwa. Samachiza matenda a kuiwala, sawachepetsa, kapena kuwapeŵa. Chitsanzo chodziwika bwino ndi [zochita za Federal Trade Commission mu 2016 pa Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) pa zonena za kuphunzitsa ubongo zomwe sizikanatsimikiziridwa. Chenjerani ndi masewera, pulogalamu kapena tsamba lililonse lomwe likulonjeza kukumbukira bwino, kuwerengera ubongo wanu kapena kunena kuti lingathetse kuchepa kwa kuthekera.

Gwiritsani ntchito masewerawa ngati gawo limodzi la tsiku lomwe limaphatikizaponso nyimbo, kuyenda, kucheza ndi kupuma. Kuti muwerenge momwe mungapangire masewera kukhala omasuka osati ovuta, onani [momwe mungasewerere limodzi ndi munthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwala](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ndi [masewera a kukumbukira omwe samayesa](/activities/memory-games/).

## Nthawi yosiya

Siyani pamene munthuyo akuoneka wotopa, wosakhazikika, wokhumudwa kapena wosamasuka. Siyani pamene wasiya kukhala ndi chidwi. Kusiya msanga ndi chinthu chabwino. Mutha kubwereranso tsiku lina, kapena kusintha nʼkuyamba kucheza kapena kuyenda.
