Bingo ndi masewera odziwika komanso ochezera kwa anthu ambiri achikulire, koma manambala akhoza kukhala ovuta kuwatsatira kwa munthu amene akukhala ndi dementia. Bingo ya zithunzi imasunga kayendedwe komweko kofatsa ndikusintha manambala ndi zithunzi zazikulu, zomveka bwino. Imagwira ntchito kunyumba, mu pulogalamu ya masana, kapena m'nyumba yosamalira anthu. Kalozerayu akufotokoza momwe mungasewerere, momwe mungasinthire, komanso momwe mungasungire chidwi pa chisangalalo.

## Momwe bingo ya zithunzi imagwirira ntchito

Wosewera aliyense amakhala ndi khadi lokhala ndi zithunzi zoikidwa m'mizere, monga ketulo, mphaka, duwa, boti la ngalawa kapena njinga. Woyitana amasonyeza kapena kutchula chithunzi chimodzi pa nthawi. Osewera amazindikira chithunzi chofanana pa khadi lawo. Wosewera amene wazindikira mzere wonse, kapena gulu lomwe gululo lagwirizana, akhoza kufuula kuti «Bingo», koma magulu ambiri amasiya gawo la wopambana.

Mutha kugwiritsa ntchito makadi osindikizidwa ochokera pa [ntchito zathu zaulere zosindikizidwa](/activities/printable/), kapena [masewera a bingo ya zithunzi](/games/picture-bingo/), omwe ali ndi woyitana pa chophimba wogwiritsa ntchito ndi magulu. Zonsezi zimagwira bwino ntchito pa tabuleti yokwezedwa kuti gulu lonse liziona.

## Kukonzekera nthawi yosewera

- **Sankhani gulu laling'ono.** Anthu anayi mpaka asanu ndi atatu ndi chiwerengero chabwino. Magulu akulu ndi ovuta kuwatsatira ndipo amatha kumva kuthamanga.
- **Pangani zithunzi kukhala zazikulu.** Gwiritsani ntchito mizere yowoneka bwino ndi chinthu chimodzi chodziwika pa bokosi lililonse. Pewani makadi odzaza kwambiri.
- **Khalitsani anthu bwino.** Ikani wosewera aliyense pamalo pomwe angaone woyitana ndi khadi lake, komanso pafupi ndi wothandiza ngati akufunika.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito zoyikira zosavuta.** Tizipangizo zazikulu, ndalama zachitsulo, kapena kudina ndi chala zimagwira bwino ntchito kuposa mapensulo. Sungani zina zowonjezera.
- **Konzani nthawi yaifupi.** Mphindi makumi atatu nthawi zambiri zimakhala zokwanira. Lekani msanga ngati mphamvu zikuchepa.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) imalangiza kusintha ntchito mogwirizana ndi zomwe munthu angakwanitsebe kuchita, choncho pewani makadi ndi malamulo nthawi iliyonse zomwe zingathandize.

## Momwe mungaitanire zithunzi

1. **Kwezani chithunzi** kapena mulozeni pa chophimba musanalankhule.
2. **Tchulani momveka bwino,** ndi mawu osavuta: «Ketulo.» Bwerezani kamodzi, pang'onopang'ono.
3. **Dikirani.** Apatseni anthu nthawi yoyang'ana ndi kuzindikira. Musaitane chithunzi chotsatira mpaka anthu ambiri atakhala ndi mwayi.
4. **Fotokozani, ngati zingathandize.** «Duwa lofiira» kapena «lomwe tinali nalo pa pikiniki» zitha kuyambitsa kucheza.
5. **Sungani kayendedwe kofatsa.** Woyitana wodekha ndi wofunika kwambiri kuposa wothamanga.

## Kupangitsa kupambana kukhala kosafunikira

Mpikisano ukhoza kukhala vuto. Munthu amene sakupambana akhoza kumva ngati akuyesedwa, ndipo munthu amene ali ndi nkhawa za kupambana akhoza kukhala wokakamizika. Nazi njira zina:

- **Aliyense amapambana chithunzi.** Wosewera aliyense amatchula chithunzi chimodzi chomwe akuchikonda, ndipo gululo limachiyamikira.
- **Gawanani khadi lathunthu.** Funsani osewera kunena china chake chokhudza chithunzi chimodzi pa khadi lawo.
- **Sewerani mpaka zithunzi zithe.** Itanani chithunzi chilichonse pa khadi, kenako yambani lina, popanda kufunafuna wopambana woyamba.
- **Sungani mphatso yaing'ono kapena siyani.** Keke kapena nyimbo zimagwira bwino ntchito kuposa chikho chachikulu.

## Kusintha kwa anthu amene ali mu magawo apambuyo

Kwa anthu amene amaona kuti kupeza chithunzi ndi kovuta, gwiritsani ntchito khadi lokhala ndi mabokosi anayi kapena asanu ndi limodzi okha, kapena perekani khadi limodzi pa nthawi kwa wosewera aliyense. Lolani wothandiza kugwira khadi pamene munthuyo akuloza, kapena sunthani chipangizo choyikira modekha ndi chilolezo chake. Anthu ena amaona kuti n'kosavuta kungomvetsera woyitana ndikusangalala ndi mawu ake.

Yang'anirani zizindikiro za kusapeza bwino, monga kubwerera m'mbuyo kapena kukwinya nkhope, ndipo siyani ngati zikuoneka. Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena a ntchito zodekha, zokhala pansi, onani [ntchito za magawo apambuyo](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo m'nyumba zosamalira anthu ndi mapulogalamu a masana

Ntchito za magulu zimagwira bwino ntchito zikakonzedwa moganizira anthu amene ali m'chipindamo. [Kalozera wathu wa ntchito za magulu m'nyumba zosamalira anthu ndi mapulogalamu a masana](/activities/care-home-groups/) akufotokoza momwe mungakonzekere nthawi yosewera, kukhalitsa anthu, komanso kuthandiza anthu amene akufuna kuchoka. Bingo ya zithunzi imagwirizananso bwino ndi kuyimba limodzi kapena nkhani, komanso ndi [malizitsani mawu](/activities/finish-the-saying/) ngati masewera abata pambuyo pake.

## Zomwe bingo ingachite ndi zomwe singachite

Bingo ya zithunzi ndi yochezera komanso yosangalatsa. Ingabweretse anthu pamodzi, ipereke chifukwa choyang'ana zithunzi ndi kumvetsera, ndipo ipangitse gulu kumva bwino. Sichichiza dementia kapena kuchepetsa momwe imapitira patsogolo. Weruzani kupambana kwake potengera momwe anthu akuonekera komanso momwe akumvera pa nthawiyo, osati amene wapambana.
