Pa gawo lomaliza la matenda a kuiwala, munthu akhoza kulankhula pangʼono kwambiri, amafunikira thandizo pa chisamaliro chonse cha tsiku ndi tsiku, ndipo amakhala nthawi yambiri ali pa bedi kapena mpando. Zingawoneke ngati palibe chinthu chotsala chochita limodzi. Koma chilipo. Bungwe la [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) limanena kuti mbali ina ya umunthu wa munthuyo ingakhalebe, komanso kuti pa gawo ili munthu amadalira kwambiri pa zimene amamva ndi kuona. Nyimbo, kukhudza, mawu odziwika komanso kukhala nanu modekha kungabweretsebe chitonthozo ndi nthawi za kugwirizana.

## Ndi zochita ziti zimathandiza pa matenda a kuiwala a gawo lomaliza?

Zochita zosavuta zogwiritsa ntchito zimene munthu amamva ndi kuona, komanso kukhala naye modekha, nthawi zambiri zimathandiza kwambiri pa gawo lomaliza. Bungwe la Alzheimer's Association limalimbikitsa kusewera nyimbo zomwe amakonda, kuwerenga mbali za mabuku ofunika kwa iwo, kusisita mafuta odzola onunkhira bwino omwe amakonda pakhungu, komanso kukhala panja limodzi tsiku likakhala labwino. Bungwe la [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) limawonjezerapo kufotokoza nkhani, kuonetsa zithunzi, kukhudza mofatsa kapena kusisita, komanso nyimbo, phokoso lofatsa kapena phokoso la chilengedwe, ndipo limanena kuti kungokhala nanu pafupi kungakhale kokhazika mtima pansi.

Bungwe la [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) limanena kuti anthu amene ali pa gawo lomaliza nthawi zambiri amafuna kugwira chinthu pafupi kuti adzimve otetezeka, ndipo limalimbikitsa zochita zosavuta zogwiritsa ntchito zimene munthu amamva ndi kuona, kutambasula mofatsa, kuyendetsa mpango, nyimbo, kuyendera kwa nyama, kukhudza kwaumunthu ndi zaluso za ufulu.

## Zochita kwa munthu amene ali pa bedi kapena mpando

1. **Muzisewerera nyimbo zawo.** Sankhani nyimbo za nthawi imene anali achinyamata kapena achikulire, za chikhulupiriro chawo kapena za banja lawo. Phokoso likhale lotsika ndipo khalani pafupi. Ngakhale tsopano, anthu ena amatha kugunda chala kapena kungʼungʼudza. Onani [zochita za nyimbo](/activities/music/).
2. **Mugwireni dzanja.** Kungogwirana dzanja, ndi kusisita chala chachikulu mofatsa, ndi chinthu chofunika. Fotokozani kaye zimene mukufuna kuchita musanakhudze.
3. **Musisiteni manja mofatsa.** Fuditsani mafuta odzola pangʼono mʼmanja mwanu ndikutsika kuchokera mʼdzanja kupita ku zala. Sankhani mafuta osanunkhira, kapena onunkhira omwe amakonda ngati khungu lawo limawalolera.
4. **Werengani mokweza.** Ndakatulo yomwe amakonda, gawo la buku lomwe amakonda, malemba kapena tsamba la zamasewera. Kayimbidwe ka mawu anu nʼkofunika kuposa mawu enieniwo.
5. **Fotokozani nkhani.** Lankhulani za ulendo wa banja kapena tsiku limene anakwatirana. Simuyenera kuyembekezera mayankho.
6. **Onetsani zithunzi.** Gwirani chithunzi chimodzi chachikulu pa nthawi imodzi pa mlingo wa maso awo ndipo mufotokozereni mofatsa. Pewani kufunsa kuti ndani ali pa chithunzicho.
7. **Perekani chinthu chofewa chogwira.** Nsalu yopindidwa, kansalu ka ubweya wofewa kapena chidole chofewa. Bungwe la NIA limalimbikitsa kupereka nsalu kwa munthuyo kuti agwire pamene akusamutsidwa, kuti asagwire inuyo kapena mipando.
8. **Sewerani phokoso la chilengedwe.** Mvula, mafunde kapena kulira kwa mbalame pa phokoso lotsika kungachepetse phokoso la mʼchipindamo.
9. **Bweretsani kunja mʼkati.** Tsegulani makatani, ikani chodyetsera mbalame panja pa zenera, kapena bweretsani maluwa atsopano kapena masamba ochepa kuti ayangʼane ndi kuwakhudza.
10. **Pitani panja.** Ngati munthuyo angakhale pa mpando wa olumala, kukhala kwa mphindi zochepa mumthunzi pa tsiku lofatsa kungakhale kosangalatsa.
11. **Mupsatseni tsitsi kapena mupatseni nsalu yofunda.** Kusamalira mofatsa ndi pangʼonopangʼono, komanso nsalu yofunda ya mʼmanja ndi nkhope, kungamupangitse kumva kuti akusamalidwa.
12. **Yendani mofatsa ndi nyimbo.** Gwirani mpango wopepuka limodzi, kapena gwedezani manja motsatira nyimbo. Pa kutambasula ndi kuyendetsa mafupa, funsani katswiri wa zolimbitsa thupi kapena namwino kuti akuonetseni njira zotetezeka poyamba.
13. **Pangani zaluso za ufulu.** Pakani utoto wopanda poizoni pa pepala lalikulu ndi siponji kapena dzanja, motsogozedwa ndi lanu. Palibe zotsatira zolondola kapena zolakwika.
14. **Landirani kuyendera kwa nyama.** Chiweto chodekha pa bedi kapena pa miyendo, ngati munthuyo ankakonda nyama ndipo malo amalola.
15. **Pumani mofatsa limodzi.** Khalani pafupi ndikupuma pangʼonopangʼono ndi momveka. [Kupuma modekha](/games/calm-breathing/) kungakuthandizeni nonse awiri kuyenda pa kayimbidwe kamodzi, ndipo inu mumayamba ndi kusiya.
16. **Ingomukhalirani pafupi.** Khalani pafupi, ngʼungʼudzani, lukani kapena werengani modekha. Kungokhala naye pafupi nʼkokwanira.

## Mmene mungachitire chinthu limodzi pa gawo ili

- **Yandikirani kutsogolo.** Tchulani dzina lake ndi dzina lanu, ndipo lankhulani pangʼonopangʼono ndi mofatsa musanamukhudze.
- **Perekani chinthu chimodzi pa nthawi imodzi.** Zimitsani TV musanayambe kusewera nyimbo. Chitani chinthu chimodzi chokhudza kumva kapena kuona pa nthawi imodzi.
- **Khalani naye kwa kanthawi kochepa.** Mphindi zochepa zingakhale zokwanira. Nthawi zingapo zazifupi patsiku zimakhala bwino kuposa nthawi imodzi yayitali.
- **Onetsetsani kuti ali bwino kaye.** Onani kuti munthuyo wakhala bwino. Bungwe la NIA limalimbikitsa bolodi la pamiyendo kuti apumitse manja ndi kuthandiza thupi lakumtunda akakhala atakhala.
- **Yangʼanirani mayankhidwe ake.** Manja omasuka, nkhope yofewa, kuyangʼana maso ndi maso kapena kungʼungʼudza zingasonyeze kuti ali bwino. Bungwe la NIA limanena kuti kubuula, kupotokola nkhope, kuteteza mbali ina ya thupi kapena kusakhazikika mwadzidzidzi kungakhale zizindikiro za kupweteka. Siyani, ndipo dziwitsani gulu losamalira.

## Chitsogozo chachidule motsatira zimene munthu amamva ndi kuona

| Zimene munthu amamva kapena kuona | Malingaliro | Chenjerani |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kumva | Nyimbo zomwe amakonda, mawu anu powerenga, phokoso la chilengedwe | Phokoso likhale lotsika; phokoso limodzi pa nthawi imodzi |
| Kukhudza | Kusisita manja, kugwirana manja, nsalu yofewa, nsalu yofunda | Funsani kapena fotokozani kaye; onani kuti khungu silikupweteka kapena silinangʼambika |
| Kuona | Zithunzi, maluwa, mawonedwe a pa zenera, kanema wa chilengedwe woyenda pangʼonopangʼono | Gwirani zinthu pa mlingo wa maso; kuwala kokwanira, popanda kunyezimira |
| Kununkhiza | Mafuta odzola omwe amakonda, maluwa atsopano, khofi likuphikidwa pafupi | Pewani fungo lamphamvu; onani ngati ali ndi vuto la ziwengo kapena kupuma |
| Kulawa | Kukoma pangʼono kwa chakudya chomwe amakonda | Perekani zakudya ndi mawonekedwe okhawo omwe gulu losamalira lanena kuti nʼkotetezeka |

## Chitetezo pa gawo ili

Kumeza kungakhale kovuta pa gawo lomaliza, ndipo bungwe la NIA limachenjeza kuti izi zingawonjezere chiopsezo cha kutsamwa. Musasiye zinthu zingʼonozingʼono pafupi, ndipo perekani chakudya kapena chakumwa pokhapokha mʼmawonekedwe ndi malo omwe gulu losamalira lalimbikitsa. Yangʼanani khungu mofatsa pamene mukuchita zochita zokhudza kukhudza, chifukwa khungu lingangʼambike kapena kuvulala mosavuta. Kuti mudziwe zambiri za malingaliro otetezeka ogwiritsa ntchito zimene munthu amamva ndi kuona, onani [zochita zogwiritsa ntchito zimene munthu amamva ndi kuona](/activities/sensory/).

## Kudzisamalira inunso

Kusamalira munthu pa gawo lomaliza kumakhala kovuta pa thupi ndi maganizo, kaya kunyumba kapena pamene mukuyendera kunyumba yosamalira anthu. Magulu a chisamaliro cha chitonthozo ndi chithandizo akhoza kuthandiza pa chitonthozo, chithandizo ndi kukonzekera, ndipo bungwe la NIA limawatchula ngati gwero la thandizo. Maulendo afupiafupi komanso odekha ndi ofunika; onani [kuyendera munthu amene ali ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/visiting/). Ngati mumatsogolera magulu, [zochita za magulu mʼnyumba yosamalira anthu](/activities/care-home-groups/) zili ndi malingaliro osinthira zochita. Ndipo chonde werengani [kudzisamalira kwa wosamalira](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/): kupuma ndi mbali ya kusamalira bwino.
