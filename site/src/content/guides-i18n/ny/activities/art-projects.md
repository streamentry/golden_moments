Ntchito zaluso ndi njira yabwino yochezera ndi munthu wokhala ndi matenda a kuiwala. Zimagwiritsa ntchito manja, maso ndi zinthu zomveka ndi thupi, ndipo zimakupatsani nkhani zochezerapo. Tsambali likupereka ntchito zina zokhazikika ndi njira zosavuta, komanso malangizo osankha zida mosamala. Kuti mumve mfundo za zaluso za anthu akuluakulu, monga kupewa zinthu za ana, onani [zaluso ndi ntchito zamanja za anthu akuluakulu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Momwe mungayambire ntchito iliyonse yaluso

1. **Konzani musanayambe.** Ikani chinthu chimodzi kapena ziwiri, chophimba pamwamba ndi nsalu yonyowa.
2. **Onetsani gawo loyamba.** Viikani burashi, ngʼambani chinthu choyamba, kapena finyani dongo pangʼono, ndipo mupempheni kuti achitenso chimodzimodzi.
3. **Gwirani ntchito pafupi ndi pafupi.** Khalani ndi ntchito yanu pafupi kuti ntchitoyo imveke yogawana, osati yoyangʼaniridwa.
4. **Kambani zomwe mukuona.** Fotokozani mitundu, mmene zinthu zimamvekera ndi maonekedwe, osati kuweruza ntchito.
5. **Lekani mphamvu zisanatsike.** Mphindi khumi mpaka makumi awiri nthawi zambiri zimakhala zokwanira.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) imanena kuti zaluso ndi njira yosonyezera mmene munthu akumvera komanso kukhala waluso popanda kufunikira luso lapadera. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) imalangiza kugawa ntchito kukhala magawo angʼonoangʼono osavuta kutsatira.

## Utoto wa madzi wofewa

Gwiritsani ntchito pepala lokhuthala la utoto wa madzi, burashi lalikulu lofewa ndi mitundu ingapo mʼmakapu osaya. Nyowetsani pepala ndi madzi oyera, kenako muloleni kuti agwetse utoto pa pepalalo. Pendetsani pepala pangʼonopangʼono kuti mitundu iyende. Chotsatira chake chimakhala chithunzi chofewa chomwe sichifuna dongosolo lililonse.

- **Gawo loyambirira:** Muloleni kusankha mitundu ndikupenta malo.
- **Gawo lapakati:** Patsani mitundu iwiri kapena itatu ndipo nyowetsani pepalalo mʼmalo mwake.
- **Gawo lotsiriza:** Gwirani pepalalo pamene iye akudinda ndi siponji.

## Kolaji ya zithunzi za mʼmagazini

Sonkhanitsani masamba a magazini okhala ndi zithunzi zowala za maluwa, zakudya, nyama, magalimoto kapena malo oyendera. Ngʼambani zithunzizo mʼzidutswa zazikulu, zomwe zimakhala zosavuta kuposa kudula, ndipo muzimatire pa khadi. Kolaji ndi yabwino kuyambitsa kukambirana, chifukwa chithunzi chilichonse chingabweretse chikumbutso. Imayeneranso munthu amene amakonda kusankha ndi kukonza zinthu.

## Dongo lofewa

Dongo louma palokha kapena dongo lofewa lopangira zinthu limatha kufinyidwa, kukulungidwa ndi kukanikizidwa. Pangani mbale yosavuta kapena chinthu chathyathyathya. Dongo limakhudza manja kwambiri, kotero lingakhale lokhazika mtima pansi kwa anthu amene amakonda kugwira zinthu. Sungani zidutswa kukhala zazikulu, ndipo pewani zida zokhala ndi nsonga zakuthwa.

## Choko pa pepala lakuda

Pepala lakuda limapangitsa mitundu ya choko kuwala kwambiri. Gwiritsani ntchito choko chachikulu ndipo muloleni munthu uja kujambula mizere ikuluikulu kapena kusakaniza ndi chala kapena mpukutu wa pepala. Choko chimatulutsa fumbi, choncho konzani pa thireyi ndipo pukutani manja pambuyo pake. Sankhani choko chofewa cholembedwa kuti chopanda poizoni.

## Kusindikiza masamba

Tengani masamba akuluakulu mʼmunda, pakani utoto wochapika pa mitsempha ya tsamba, ndipo sindikizani tsambalo pa pepala. Chotsani mosamala ndikuyangʼana chithunzi chake. Ntchito imeneyi imagwirizana bwino ndi [ntchito zakunja ndi zolima mʼmunda](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), ndipo ingayambe ndi kuyenda pangʼono kukatola masamba.

## Kukonza nsalu ndi maluwa

Sankhani nsalu za maonekedwe osiyanasiyana potengera mmene zimamvekera, kapena konzani maluwa ochepa atsopano mu vase. Izi ndi ntchito zomveka ndi manja zomwe sizipereka kukakamizidwa kwakukulu. Zimayeneranso anthu ali pa magawo otsiriza. Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena, onani [ntchito zomveka ndi thupi za anthu okhala ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/sensory/).

## Kusankha zida zaluso

Zida ndizofunika kwambiri pa chitetezo ndi chitonthozo kuposa pa ubwino wake. Mukamasankha, ganizirani izi:

- **Zopanda poizoni komanso zochapika:** Yangʼanani zilembo zosonyeza kuti zopanda poizoni ndipo onetsetsani kuti utoto ndi wochapika.
- **Zogwira mosavuta:** Makrayoni akuluakulu, mabulashi okhuthala ndi choko chachikulu zimakhala zosavuta kugwira.
- **Zopanda tizidutswa tatingʼono:** Pewani mikanda, mabatani, zinthu zonyezimira ndi zonyezimira zotayirira zomwe zingamezedwe.
- **Zopanda zida zakuthwa:** Gwiritsani ntchito lumo lotetezeka kapena ngʼambani pepala ndi manja.
- **Ziwengo:** Onani ngati pali latex, kapena ngati pali khungu lomwe limakhudzidwa mosavuta.
- **Kusiyana kowonekera:** Mitundu yowonekera pa pepala loyera kapena lakuda imakhala yosavuta kuona.
- **Mndandanda wafupikitsa:** Zida ziwiri kapena zitatu ndizokwanira. Zosankha zambiri zingasokoneze.
- **Kuyeretsa kosavuta:** Gwiritsani ntchito thireyi, pepala la pulasitiki ndi nsalu yonyowa, kuti chisokonezo chisakhale chifukwa chosiira ntchitoyo.

## Kusintha malinga ndi gawo

Pa gawo loyambirira, anthu ambiri amatha kukonza ndi kumaliza ntchito mothandizidwa pangʼono. Pa gawo lapakati, patsani zosankha zochepa komanso chitsogozo chochulukirapo. Pa gawo lotsiriza, yangʼanani pa njira zomveka ndi manja monga kufinya dongo kapena kudinda utoto ndi siponji, ndipo sangalalani ndi zotsatira zake osati kumaliza kwake.

Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena malinga ndi gawo, onani [gawo loyambirira](/activities/early-stage/), [gawo lapakati](/activities/middle-stage/) ndi [gawo lotsiriza](/activities/late-stage/). Ngati munthuyo akukana kapena kukhumudwa, malangizo athu pa [pamene wina akuti ayi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) akufotokoza momwe mungapumire pangʼono.

## Phatikizani ndi masewera

Mukamaliza kujambula, yesani [kupaka utoto](/games/coloring/) pa tabuleti ngati ntchito yotsatira yodekha, kapena [chithunzi cha puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) ngati ntchito yomaliza. Zonsezi zimapangitsa manja kukhala otanganidwa popanda kufuna luso lapadera.
