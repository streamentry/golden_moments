Masewera omaliza mawu amagwiritsa ntchito mawu odziwika bwino omwe amaima liwu lomaliza lisanafike. Kwa anthu ambiri, imeneyi imakhala njira yosangalatsa komanso yopanda kukakamiza yogawana nthawi limodzi. Masewerawa amagwira ntchito chifukwa mawuwo ndi akale komanso odziwika bwino, ndipo munthuyo safunika kukumbukira chilichonse chokhudza lero. Kalozera uyu akufotokoza momwe mungasewerere, momwe mungasankhire mawu, komanso momwe mungasungire masewerawa kukhala omasuka.

## Kodi masewera omaliza mawu ndi chiyani?

Ndi masewera aafupi amene munthu mmodzi amawerenga gawo loyamba la mawu ndipo winayo amaliza. Zitsanzo ndi monga “Actions speak louder than...” ndi “The early bird catches the...” Munthuyo akhoza kunena liwu lomaliza mokweza, kuloza, kapena kusankha pa mayankho awiri kapena atatu omwe mwapereka. Anthu ena amasangalala kukhala amene akuwerenga gawo loyamba.

Golden Moments imapereka izi ngati [masewera aulere omaliza mawu](/games/finish-the-saying/) okhala ndi zilembo zazikulu. Mungathenso kusewera popanda chophimba, pongonena gawo loyamba ndi kudikira.

## Momwe mungasewerere, sitepe ndi sitepe

1. **Sankhani nthawi yabata** ndipo khalani moyang’anana ndi munthuyo, maso ndi maso.
2. **Werengani gawo loyamba pang’onopang’ono,** ndi mawu ofatsa, kenako imani pang’ono. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) imalangiza kuyankhula pang’onopang’ono ndikugwiritsa ntchito ziganizo zazifupi komanso zosavuta.
3. **Dikirani.** Perekani masekondi angapo. Ngati palibe yankho, perekani mayankho awiri kapena atatu, limodzi mwa iwo likhale lolondola.
4. **Yamikirani yankho lililonse.** “Inde, ndi limenelo” kapena “Sindinaganizepo zimenezo” kumapangitsa zonse kukhala zaubwenzi.
5. **Chezerani za mawuwo.** Funsani zomwe akutanthauza kwa iye, kapena kumene anawamva. Apa ndipamene phindu lenileni limapezeka.
6. **Lekani asanatope.** Mawu ochepa ndi kuseka pang’ono ndi nthawi yokwanira.

## Kupereka zisankho popanda kumufunsa ngati mayeso

Kupereka mayankho osankhidwa kungapangitse masewerawa kukhala osavuta, koma kungamvekenso ngati mayeso ngati zaperekedwa ngati vuto. Sungani kamvekedwe kake kukhala kofewa. Nenani “Kodi mukufuna kumva mapeto ake kapena kusankha limodzi?” m’malo monena “Ndi liti lolondola?” Perekani zisankho mutadikira pang’ono, osati munthuyo asanayesere kuyankha.

Ngati munthuyo wakhumudwa kapena wachita manyazi, siyani masewerawo ndipo pitirirani ndi zina. [Malangizo athu olankhulirana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) akufotokoza momwe mungasinthire popanda kuchititsa phokoso.

## Kusankha mawu oyenera munthuyo

- **Gwiritsani ntchito mawu a munthuyo.** Funsani achibale zomwe kholo kapena mnzawo wa m’banja ankakonda kunena. Mawu ochokera mu ubwana nthawi zambiri amakhala osavuta kuwazindikira.
- **Sankhani mawu aafupi a tsiku ndi tsiku.** Mawu okhudza nyengo, khitchini, dimba, banja ndi nyengo za chaka ndi ofala komanso osavuta kumva.
- **Sungani kukhala kosavuta.** Miyambi yomwe imadalira mawu akale kapena phunziro lina lake imatha kusokoneza. Isungeni kwa anthu omwe amasangalala nayo.
- **Onani chilankhulo.** Miyambi yambiri simasinthika bwino m’zinenero zina. Gwiritsani ntchito mawu omwe munthuyo amawadziwa m’chilankhulo chake, ngakhale mutafunika kupeza ena atsopano.

## Mawu mogwirizana ndi mitu

Chitsanzo chilichonse chili ndi mapeto ake m’makolo kwa inu. Werengani gawo loyamba lokha, kenako dikirani.

- **Nyengo:** “Rain before seven...” (mapeto: “fine by eleven”); “Every cloud has...” (a silver lining).
- **Khitchini ndi chakudya:** “Too many cooks...” (spoil the broth); “Don't count your chickens...” (before they hatch).
- **Kunyumba ndi banja:** “Home is where the heart...” (is); “Blood is thicker than...” (water).
- **Nyengo za chaka ndi dimba:** “April showers bring...” (May flowers); “You reap what you...” (sow).

## Nanga bwanji munthu amene sangayankhe?

Anthu ena sangathe kupeza mawu kumapeto kwa matendawa. Musasiye mawuwo. Awerengeni, mwetulirani, ndipo pemphani yankho pogwiritsa ntchito manja, kugwedeza mutu, kapena kusankha chithunzi. Kugawana kamvekedwe kodziwika bwino kumakhalabe nthawi yogawana. Mungaphatikize masewerawa ndi nyimbo, chakumwa chofunda, kapena kusisita dzanja, monga momwe tafotokozera mu [zochitika za kumapeto kwa matendawa](/activities/late-stage/).

## Njira zina zogwiritsira ntchito mawu

Mawu angathandize kuyambitsa kucheza za zakale za munthuyo. Mungapemphe nkhani pogwiritsa ntchito mafunso ochokera pa [mafunso okumbukira zakale](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), kapena kusewera masewera ofanana monga [makadi ochezera](/games/conversation-cards/). Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena pa tsiku lonse, onani [zochitika za anthu omwe ali ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/ideas/).
