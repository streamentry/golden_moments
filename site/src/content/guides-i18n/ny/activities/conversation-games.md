Mabanja ambiri amafuna masewera oti azisewera limodzi omwe amamveka ngati kucheza, osati kuyesedwa. Mafunso makumi awiri ndi masewera ena ofanana olingalira akhoza kugwira ntchito bwino, chifukwa malamulo ake ndi osavuta ndipo kucheza kungapite kulikonse. Chinsinsi chake ndi kusintha maudindo omwe amakhala wamba: munthuyo amaganizira yankho, ndipo inu mumafunsa. Tsambali likufotokoza momwe mungasewerere masewerawa mofatsa, komanso masewera ati ochezera omwe angakhale oyenera kwa anthu okhala ndi dementia.

## N'chifukwa chiyani kusintha malamulo wamba?

Pa mayeso wamba, munthu m'modzi amafunsa ndipo winayo ayenera kudziwa. Izi zimayika munthu amene akukhala ndi dementia pa malo pomwe angalephere, ndipo kulephera kungamupweteke. Akasankha chinthucho ndipo inu n'kumalingalira, iye amakhala katswiri. Amakhala ndi yankho, amasankha nthawi yoti anene inde kapena ayi, ndipo angasangalale kukuonani mukuyesera.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) imalangiza osamalira kuti azigwiritsa ntchito ziganizo zazifupi komanso zosavuta, funso limodzi pa nthawi imodzi, komanso mawu ofatsa ndi oleza mtima. Mchitidwe umenewu umagwirizana bwino ndi masewera olingalira.

## Momwe mungasewerere mafunso makumi awiri, mofatsa

1. **Sankhani mutu** wochokera m'moyo wa munthuyo, monga khitchini, dimba, nyama kapena nyanja.
2. **Muloleni aganizire chinthu** ndikuchinena modekha, kapena kusonyeza khadi la chithunzi kuti musafunikire kuchimva.
3. **Funsani funso limodzi loyankhidwa ndi inde kapena ayi pa nthawi imodzi.** "Kodi ndi chinthu chomwe mumadya?" "Kodi ndi chachikulu kuposa nsapato?"
4. **Landirani yankho lililonse.** Kuvomereza mutu, kugwedeza mutu, kapena kuseka zonse zimawerengedwa.
5. **Nenani zomwe mukuganiza mokweza** ndipo sangalalani nazo. Mukalingalira molondola, sangalalani limodzi.
6. **Perekani malangizo othandiza** pamene munthuyo akuoneka kuti wavutika: "Ndi chinthu cha m'khitchini."
7. **Musawerenge mafunso** ndipo musawerenge amaneba.

Ngati munthuyo sangathe kutsatira malamulo, sinthani kukhala njira yosavuta: tchulani zinthu zochepa ndipo iye aloze choyenera.

## Zinthu zabwino zosankhira

- Chipatso, ndiwo zamasamba kapena duwa lomwe amakonda
- Nyama ya pafamu, ya ku malo osungira nyama kapena chiweto chomwe anali nacho
- Chida, monga nyundo, foloko ya dimba kapena singano yosokera
- Chinthu cha m'nyumba kapena chogwiritsidwa ntchito pakhomo
- Malo omwe amawadziwa bwino, monga paki kapena tawuni
- Chovala chomwe amakonda kuvala

Pewani mayina a anthu, chilichonse chomwe chingabweretse chisoni, ndi zinthu zomwe zingakhale zoopsa kapena zosavuta kusokoneza.

## Masewera ena ochezera

- **Ichi kapena icho.** Perekani zosankha ziwiri ndikufunsa zomwe amakonda: tiyi kapena khofi, mapiri kapena nyanja, diresi lofiira kapena labuluu. Palibe yankho lolondola.
- **Mungakonde chiyani.** Funsani funso lofatsa komanso losavuta: "Mungakonde kupita ku pikiniki kapena kukadya kunja?"
- **Malizitsani mwambi.** Mawu odziwika bwino omwe mapeto ake asiyidwa. Yesani [masewera athu omaliza mwambi](/activities/finish-the-saying/) kuti mupeze malangizo.
- **Tchulani malo.** Mupempheni afotokoze chipinda, tawuni kapena malo omwe amakonda, ndipo mukuitaneni kuti muwaganizirenso.
- **Makadi a mafunso otseguka.** [Makadi athu ochezera](/games/conversation-cards/) amapereka mafunso otseguka malinga ndi mutu, ndipo angasindikizidwe kuti agwiritsidwe ntchito patebulo.

Kuti mupeze malingaliro ena okhudza kukumbukira ndi nkhani za moyo, onani [mafunso okumbukira zakale](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Kuti mupeze zosangalatsa za mafunso ndi mayankho mochita mofatsa, onani [mafunso osavuta osangalatsa kwa anthu achikulire okhala ndi dementia](/activities/trivia/), omwe amagwiritsa ntchito zosankha ndipo alibe kuwerenga amaneba.

## Bwanji ngati munthuyo wakwiya?

Lekani, mwetulirani ndikunena monga, "Tiyeni tisewere china chake." Kukwiya ndi chizindikiro choti musinthe masewera, osati kukakamiza kwambiri. Kalozera wathu pa [momwe mungasewerere masewera ndi munthu amene akukhala ndi dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) akufotokoza momwe mungasinthire malamulo nthawi yomweyo.

## Zomwe masewera ochezera angachite komanso sangachite

Masewera ochezera ndi osangalatsa komanso amalimbikitsa kucheza. Angathandize anthu kulumikizana ndi kugawana nkhani. Sachiza dementia, saiichepetsa kapena kuipewa. Sungani cholinga kukhala chosavuta: nthawi yofunda limodzi.
