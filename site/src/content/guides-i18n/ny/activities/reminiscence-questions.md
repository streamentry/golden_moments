Kukumbukira pamodzi ndi kukambirana za zakale: nyumba za ubwana, ntchito zoyamba, zakudya zokondedwa, nyimbo, maukwati ndi zikondwerero. Kwa anthu ambiri omwe akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala, kukambirana mwachikondi za moyo wawo kungakhale chimodzi mwa zinthu zosangalatsa kwambiri pa tsiku. Chofunika kwambiri ndi kufunsa m’njira imene imapempha nkhani ndipo siiyesa kukumbukira. Mafunsowa agawidwa m’magulu a mitu, pamodzi ndi malangizo a mmene mungawagwiritsire ntchito.

## Malangizo a mafunso okumbukira

- **Funsani mafunso otseguka.** “Tandiuzani za...” ndi “Zinali bwanji...” zimapempha nkhani. “Kodi mukukumbukira...?” imafuna yankho lolondola.
- **Musamuyese.** Pewani mafunso akuti “Uyu ndani?”, “Zinali chaka chiti?” ndi “Kodi mukukumbukira dzina la...?”
- **Vomerezani chilichonse chimene wabweretsa.** Ngati nkhani yasintha kapena malo asokonekera, sangalalani nayo ndipo mufunseni za momwe amamvera.
- **Gwiritsani ntchito funso limodzi panthawi imodzi.** Dikirani, mvetserani ndipo tsatirani ndi funso lina losavuta.
- **Lolani munthuyo atsogolere.** Ngati akufuna kukamba za china, mutsatireni.

Malangizo a [National Institute on Aging on communicating](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) amalimbikitsa kugwiritsa ntchito ziganizo zazifupi, mawu ofatsa, ndi kuleza mtima pamene munthuyo akufuna kupeza mawu. Malangizowa ndi ofunika kwambiri pano monga m’malo ena.

## Nyumba ndi ubwana

- Tandiuzani za nyumba imene munakuliramo. Khitchini yake inali bwanji?
- Munkakonda kuchita chiyani panja muli mwana?
- Ndi anthu ati amene munkasewera nawo pa msewu wanu?
- Chipinda chanu chogona chinali bwanji? Munkagonamo ndi wina aliyense?

## Ntchito ndi kuchita zinthu

- Ndi ntchito yanji imene munkakonda kwambiri?
- Tandiuzani za tsiku la kuntchito limene linakupangitsani kunyadira.
- Munkakonda kupanga kapena kukonza chiyani ndi manja anu?
- Ndi ndani amene anakuphunzitsani zambiri pa ntchito yanu?

## Chakudya ndi kuphika

- Banja lanu linkadya chiyani pa Lamlungu?
- Tandiuzani za chakudya chimene munthu wina m’banja lanu ankachiphika bwino kwambiri.
- Kodi munali ndi malo amene munkakonda kupita kukatenga chokoma muli mwana?
- Ndi fungo liti limene limakukumbutsani za kunyumba?

Kukambirana za chakudya kumayendera bwino ndi malangizo a [zochitika zogwiritsa ntchito mphamvu za thupi](/activities/sensory/), komanso ndi kulawa pang’ono ngati n’kotetezeka kupereka.

## Nyimbo ndi kuvina

- Munkayimba kapena kuvina nyimbo ziti muli achinyamata?
- Kodi woimba kapena gulu la nyimbo limene munkalikonda kwambiri linali ndani?
- Munkamvetsera nyimbo kuti m’masiku amenewo: kunyumba, kumaphwando ovina, kapena kutchalitchi?

Kuti mupeze mndandanda wa nyimbo ndi malingaliro ena, onani [zochitika za nyimbo kwa anthu omwe akukhala ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/music/).

## Zikondwerero ndi maphwando

- Kodi chikondwerero chimene munkakonda kwambiri muli mwana chinali chiti?
- Tandiuzani za phwando la tsiku lobadwa limene mukulikumbukira.
- Banja lanu linkachita chiyani madzulo a chilimwe?
- Banja lanu linkakondwerera bwanji nyengo za chaka?

Kuti mupeze malingaliro oyenera chikondwerero china chake, onani [zochitika za zikondwerero kwa munthu yemwe ali ndi matenda a kuiwala](/activities/holidays/).

## Banja ndi anzanu

- Tandiuzani za munthu amene ankakuseketsani kwambiri.
- Tsiku laukwati wanu linali bwanji?
- Tandiuzani za ana anu ali aang’ono.
- Anzanu ankakutchani dzina lotani?

Funsani mafunso okhudza banja mofatsa. Ngati munthuyo akulira munthu amene anamwalira, mutsatireni ndipo pewani kumupempha kukumbukira zinthu zimene zingamupweteke.

## Malo ndi chilengedwe

- Tandiuzani za malo amene mumakonda kuwaganizira.
- Nyengo inali bwanji kumene munakulira?
- Kodi munali ndi dimba, kapena mtengo umene munkaukonda?
- Tandiuzani za ulendo umene munasangalala nawo.

## Kugwiritsa ntchito zithunzi ndi zinthu

Zithunzi ndi njira yabwino yoyambira. Gwirani chithunzi pafupi ndi maso ndipo nenani, “Tandiuzani za ichi.” Malangizo a [Alzheimer's Association on activities](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) amasonyeza kugwiritsa ntchito zinthu zodziwika, zithunzi ndi nyimbo kuyambitsa kukambirana. Bokosi la zikumbutso lokhala ndi zinthu zochepa za moyo wa munthuyo lingathandizenso; onani [zochitika zokumbukira pamodzi](/activities/reminiscence/) kuti mudziwe momwe mungakonzekerere limodzi.

## Pamene kukumbukira kukupweteka

Zikumbutso zina zimabweretsa chisoni, mantha kapena mkwiyo. Ngati munthuyo wakhumudwa, musamukakamize. Nenani mawu achikondi monga, “Zikumveka kuti inali nthawi yovuta. Kodi mungakonde tikambe za china chosangalatsa?” Kenaka sinthani nkhani, mupatseni tiyi, kapena yimbani nyimbo imene amakonda.

## Phatikizani ndi masewera kapena kuyenda

Pambuyo pa kukambirana zokumbukira, masewera ofatsa angathandize kuti mukhale omasuka. Yesani [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) kuti mupeze mafunso ena otseguka, kapena [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) kuti mukumbukire mawu odziwika a paubwana wa munthuyo. Kuti mupeze malangizo ambiri okhudza kulankhulana, onani [malangizo olankhulana ndi munthu yemwe ali ndi matenda a kuiwala](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
