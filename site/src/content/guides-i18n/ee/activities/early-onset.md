Nyatakaka geɖe tso ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ ŋu bua ame siwo xɔ ƒe blaadre alo blaenyi ƒe ha mee. Na ame si wodi ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ na le eƒe blaene alo blaatɔ̃ me la, agbe ƒe nɔnɔme to vovo ŋutɔ. Ate ŋu gale dɔ wɔm, ɖe fe na xɔ, kpɔ viwo dzi alo kpɔ dzilawo dzi kokoko. Ele be dɔwɔwɔwo naɖee afia be enye ame tsitsi. Mɔfiame sia fia nu si trɔna, nu siwo woate ŋu atrɔ asi le, kple afi si srɔ̃wo kple vi tsitsiwo ate ŋu akpɔ kpekpeɖeŋu le.

## Nukae nye ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ si dze egɔme do ŋgɔ?

Ðoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ si dze egɔme do ŋgɔ nye ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ si dzea egɔme hafi ame xɔ ƒe 65, wogayɔe hã be younger-onset. [Alzheimer's Association ƒe blog](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) gblɔ be woyɔa Alzheimer's be younger-onset ne eƒo ame si xɔ ƒe siwo me womeɖo 65 o, eye wògblɔ be "kpekpeɖeŋu na ame siawo kple woƒe ƒometɔwo tsoa dz challenges veviwo." Dzesiwo ate ŋu asɔ kple esiwo dzea egɔme le tsitsi me, gake ame la ƒe mɔkpɔkpɔwo, dɔdeasiwo kple hadede to vovo.

Esi menɔa bɔbɔe o ta la, zi geɖe amewo xɔa ɣeyiɣi didi hafi wodina ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ na wo, eye ƒometɔwo ate ŋu ase le wo ɖokui me be yewomele ame aɖeke gbɔ be yewoagblɔ nu na o. Ne wonya be ame bubuwo hã le nɔnɔme ɖeka me la, enaa dzidzɔkpɔkpɔ.

## Nàna ame tsitsi la nanɔ dɔwɔwɔ la me

Ame siwo le ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ si dze egɔme do ŋgɔ me medi be woawɔ wo abe ɖeviwo ene o. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ɖo aɖaŋu be woatrɔ dɔwɔwɔwo ɖe nu siwo ame la ate ŋu awɔ kple esiwo wòlɔ̃ ɖa la nu. Na ame si le eƒe blaatɔ̃ me la, esia ate ŋu anye:

- **Dzra lɔlɔ̃nu si wozãna ɖaa la ɖo**, abe ati dɔwɔwɔ, kamedede, kaƒoƒo, agbalẽ ƒuƒoƒo alo hadzihati ƒuƒoƒo ene, kple kpekpeɖeŋu sue aɖe alo ɣeyiɣi kpui aɖe.
- **Zɔ alo ʋu kple ame si kplii**, le mɔ si wonya nyuie dzi.
- **Wɔ ƒomeɖuɖu aɖe**, eye ame la nawɔ akpa siwo wòate ŋu.
- **Wɔ dɔ aɖe le lɔlɔ̃ me** si wòa tia, abe nuwo dzraɖoɖo alo kpekpeɖeŋu na suku ƒe ɖoɖo aɖe ene.
- **Ɖo mɔzɔzɔ ɖi** yi teƒe aɖe si le vevie na wo, kple ame si kplii kpakple ɖoɖo bɔbɔe.
- **Nɔ kple tɔgbuiyɔviwo, fianyɔviwo alo tɔvi yɔviwo**, nɔ nu fiam wo alo wɔ dɔ aɖe kpli wo.

Nàna ame la ŋutɔ natia. Bia be "Nu kae nèdi be yeawɔ fiɛ gbe sia?" la wɔa dɔ nyuie wu ne ètia nu na wo.

## Zã hadzidzi kple ŋkuɖodzinuwo tso woawo ŋutɔ ƒe ƒeawo me

Hadzidzi kple fotowo tso ame la ƒe ƒe 20, 30 kple 40 me ate ŋu ana wòase le eɖokui me be yele dedie eye ana dzeɖoɖo bɔbɔe nadze. Míaƒe [hadzidzi ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo](/activities/music/) ƒe mɔfiame fia alesi woawɔ hadzidzi ƒe xexlẽdzesi, eye [ŋkuɖodzi ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo](/activities/reminiscence/) fia alesi woama fotowo kple ŋutinya me evɔ womabia biabiawo abe ɖe wole dodokpɔ dzi ene. Na nu fefe si sɔ na ame tsitsiwo wu la, zã [dzeɖoɖo ƒe kaɖiwo](/games/conversation-cards/), siwo me biabia siwo mewɔa du o le tso dɔ, mɔzɔzɔ, xɔlɔ̃wo kple ƒome ŋu.

Fotowo ƒe pair siwo wowɔ tso ƒome ƒe fotowo me hã sɔ nyuie, elabena fotowo nye ame kple teƒe siwo le vevie na ame la tɔ. [Fotowo ƒe pair](/games/photo-pairs/) léa fotowo ɖe mɔ̃ ma dzi le ʋuƒo dzi.

## Dɔ, mɔ dzi yiyi kple gbeŋkeke sia gbe ƒe agbe

Ame geɖe siwo le ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ si dze egɔme do ŋgɔ me gale dɔ wɔm, le mɔ dzi yem alo le ga ŋu dzrom. Menye dɔwɔwɔwo le gɔmedzedze ƒe susu nu o, gake wonana nu siwo ame la ate ŋu awɔ la trɔna. Dze kple woƒe dɔktɛ be nàbia nu tso mɔ dzi yiyi kple dɔ wɔwɔ ƒe dedienɔnɔ ŋu, eye nàbu tame ne woate ŋu ahiã kpekpeɖeŋu le gadzikpɔkpɔ alo ɖoɖowɔɖiwo ŋu. Taɖodzinu lae nye be woakpɔ dzidzeme nɔƒe evɔ woana ame la nanɔ akpa si wòdi me.

Na mɔfiame tso gbeŋkeke sia gbe ƒe ɖoɖowo kple dɔwɔwɔ ŋu la, kpɔ [ɖoɖo bɔbɔe na gbe sia gbe](/activities/daily-routine/) kple [aƒeme dɔwɔwɔwo abe dɔwɔwɔwo ene](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Kpekpeɖeŋu na srɔ̃wo kple vi tsitsiwo

Srɔ̃wo kple vi tsitsiwo zi geɖe sena le wo ɖokui me be yewomele ame aɖeke gbɔ o. Kpɔ̃kpɔ ate ŋu anɔ abe dɔ evelia ene le dɔ kple ƒome dɔdeasiwo dzi. Alzheimer's Association ƒe blog nyati gblɔ be kpɔlawo siwo le younger-onset ƒuƒoƒowo me kpɔe be ƒuƒoƒowo kpe ɖe yewo ŋu le nu veviwo abe se ƒe ɖoɖowo ŋu, eye kpe ɖe eŋu be wonɔa dzidzeme le esi wonya be ame bubuwo se wo gɔme. Egblɔ hã be Association léa kpɔkpɔ ƒuƒoƒo femaxee, siwo dome ƒuƒoƒo na ame siwo le kpɔ̃m na ame si le ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ si dze egɔme do ŋgɔ me hã le, eye wo dometɔ etɔ̃ doa go le Internet dzi le Dzoɖa kple Brãɖa dzi.

Be nàbia tso ƒuƒoƒowo ŋu la, ɖe telefon na Association ƒe 24/7 Kpekpeɖeŋu Dzidzɔgbeƒãƒoƒe le 800.272.3900. Míaƒe [kpɔla ƒe ɖokui dzikpɔkpɔ ƒe mɔfiame](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) bua dzudzɔ, ɣeyiɣi si woɖe na wo ɖokui kple gbɔdzɔgbɔdzɔ ƒe dzesiwo ŋu.

## Ƒe ɖoɖoa gakpɔtɔ le vevie

Ame la ƒe ɖoɖoɣi me gbegblẽ ƒe ɣeyiɣi la gakpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe nu siwo kpena ɖe eŋu dzi. Le gɔmedzedze la, ame geɖe ate ŋu awɔ woƒe dɔwɔwɔ siwo wozãna ɖaa kple tɔtrɔ suewo, eye míaƒe [gɔmedzedze ƒe ɣeyiɣi ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo](/activities/early-stage/) ƒe mɔfiame me aɖaŋu bubuwo le. Le emegbe la, dɔwɔwɔwo zua bɔbɔe wu eye wonɔa nu sesẽwo zazã me wu, abe alesi woɖee fia le [mlɔeba ƒe ɣeyiɣi ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo](/activities/late-stage/) me ene. Trɔ dɔwɔwɔ la abe alesi ame la trɔna ene, eye nàbiae nu si wòdi ne ate ŋu agblɔe.
