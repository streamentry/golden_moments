Oi emu memory eiava oi lalokau tauna ena memory e idau, unai be oi hagarimu diba. Inai pepa be oi durumu dokta dekenai lao negana oi abia hidi bona dahaka oi hegaegae. Ia be tau ta dementia ida eiava lasi, e gwau diba lasi. **Dokta sibona be dementia e diagnose karaia diba**, bona memory hekwakwanai e havaraia gaudia momo be dementia lasi. Online quizzes eiava self-tests oi badinaia lasi.

## Dementia ena early signs be dahaka?

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/10_signs) e gwau early signs 10. Ta ta e hahetoalea lagani ida e vara gauna ida. Inai be ai emai hereva amo:

| Check karaia namona sign | Lagani ida e vara gauna |
| --- | --- |
| Memory loss dina gaudia e hadikaia, heto hari gauna e lalohadai lasi eiava gau tamona e henia lou lou | Nega haida ladana eiava appointment e lalohadai lasi, gabeai e lalohadai lou |
| Plan eiava problem solve karaia e auka, heto biasa recipe e badinaia eiava bill e naria | Ruma bill dekenai nega haida kerere maragina |
| Ruma, gaukara eiava lao gabuna ai biasa gaudia karaia e doko lasi | Nega haida microwave eiava TV show record totona help e goada |
| Dina, season eiava gabuna e lalohadai kerere | Wiki dina e kerere, gabeai e laloa lou |
| Distant, duahiduahi eiava color idau davana e auka matamata | Cataracts dainai mata e idau |
| Hereva hekwakwanai matamata, heto hereva e hadokoa huanai eiava gau ladana e kerere | Nega haida hereva namona e tahua auka |
| Gau e atoa gabuna kerere ai bona e tahua lou dalana e diba lasi | Nega haida gau e haboio, gabeai e tahua lou |
| Judgment e namo lasi, heto moni eiava sibona naria | Nega haida abia hidi kerere maragina |
| Gaukara, hobby eiava hebou gaudia amo e dobi | Nega haida ruma eiava hebou dekenai lao e uramu lasi |
| Mood eiava kara idau, heto e laloa kerere, e kamonai lasi, e gari eiava e hekwakwanai | Ia ena daladia e badinaia goada, routine e idau neganai e badu |

Sign tamona sibona be dementia e gwau lasi. Anina bada be **tau ia ena kara namona amo idau**, unai amo dina gauna e auka.

## Lagani ida e vara gauna bona dementia be dahaka idau?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/memory-loss-and-forgetfulness/memory-problems-forgetfulness-and-aging) e gwau, tau bada momo e kamonai edia lalohadai e metau, eiava nega haida gau e haboio eiava bill e lalohadai lasi. Unai be lagani ida e vara lalohadai maragina. **Dementia be lagani ida e vara gauna namona lasi.** Memory hekwakwanai bada e dina gaudia e auka, heto draiva, phone gaukaralaia eiava ruma dekenai lou lao dalana tahua.

NIA e gwau, dokta ida oi herevala bema tau ta:

- Henanadai tamona e henia lou lou
- Ia e diba gabudia ai e boio
- Recipe eiava hakaua badinaia e auka
- Nega, taunimanima bona gabuna dekenai e laloa kerere momo
- Sibona naria e doko, heto aniani e namo lasi, e diho lasi eiava kara e namo lasi

Tau haida be **mild cognitive impairment (MCI)** e abia. Unai anina, memory eiava laloa hekwakwanai e momo edia lagani taudia haida ida e hahetoalea, to dina gauna e karaia diba. NIA e gwau MCI be Alzheimer's ena early sign diba, to MCI ida taudia iboudiai be e abia lasi.

## Memory hekwakwanai be gau haida amo e vara diba?

Dementia lasi gaudia momo be memory e hadikaia diba, haida be treatment murinai e namo lou. NIA e gwau:

- **Thyroid, kidney eiava liver hekwakwanai**
- **Muramura ena side effects**
- **Depression bona anxiety**
- **Vitamin B12 e maragi** eiava aniani namona e ania lasi
- **Mahuta hekwakwanai**
- **Alcohol eiava muramura kerere gaukaralaia**
- **Infections, blood clots eiava tumors** kwarana lalonai
- **Kwara hekwakwanai**, heto concussion
- **Hekwakwanai bada eiava lalohisihisi**, heto gaukara doko eiava adavana e mase

Kamonai bona mata hekwakwanai danu e laloa kerere heto. Ai emai [kamonai dalana](/brain-health/hearing/) oi itaia. Unai dainai medical check e mai anina bada. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor) e gwau, dokta be nega momo rara check e karaia thyroid hekwakwanai, vitamin e maragi bona infection e itaia totona. [Mahuta namona](/brain-health/sleep/) bona depression treatment danu oi herevalaia namona.

## Confusion e vara haraga be emergency

**Confusion e vara haraga, hour eiava dina haida lalonai, harihari medical help e goada.** Delirium diba. [VA e gwau delirium](https://www.va.gov/geriatrics/pages/delirium_when_should_i_worry_about_delirium.asp) be medical emergency bona e gwaumu, tau ia naria tauna ena health care provider dekenai harihari oi phone eiava 111 dekenai oi phone. Signs haida:

- Laloa maoromaoro ena siahu e daekau bona e diho
- Kamonai bona laloa e atoa auka
- Laloa e heudeheude
- E mahuta bada, eiava e heau bada bona e hekwakwanai

Unai be tau iboudiai dekenai e vara diba, dementia ida e noho taudia danu. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) e gwau, dala haida be infections (urinary tract infections be tau bada bona dementia ida taudia dekenai e vara momo), stroke, diabetes ida taudia dekenai blood sugar e maragi, kwara hekwakwanai bona muramura haida. Help oi naria neganai, tau ia ida oi noho bona hereva kwadogidia, maoromaoro ida oi hagoadaia. Henanadai momo oi henia lasi, bona ia e abia muramura oi torea.

## Dokta dekenai lao totona lau hegaegae be ede?

Appointment namona e matamaia torea namodia amo. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor) e gwaumu oi mailaia:

1. **Idau gaudia ena list**, memory, mood bona kara dekenai, example maorodia ida bona edena negai e matamaia
2. **Guna bona hari gorere hekwakwanaidia**, ruma lalonai memory hekwakwanai e vara bona
3. **Muramura iboudiai**, dokta e henia bona shop amo e hoia
4. **Vitamins eiava supplements iboudiai**
5. **Oi emu henanadai**, heto dahaka test e karaia, edia nega be daika, bona result edena negai e vara

Ruma tauna eiava turana ta, tau ia e diba namonamo tauna, ia ida oi lao, unai danu e durumu. [NIA e hahedinaraia](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-alzheimers-disease-diagnosed) dokta be tau ia bona ruma tauna dekenai e henanadai diba, gorere, muramura, dina gaudia bona kara idau dekenai. Dokta be danu e karaia diba:

- Memory, problem solve, laloa atoa, namba bona hereva test
- Rara, urine bona test haida, dala ma haida e tahua totona
- Depression eiava laloa gorere hekwakwanai haida e itaia
- Brain scans, heto CT eiava MRI
- Specialist ta dekenai e siaia, heto geriatrician, neurologist, geriatric psychiatrist eiava neuropsychologist

NIA e gwau, cognitive assessment be Medicare Annual Wellness Visit ena gauna ta, unai negai oi emu hekwakwanai oi herevalaia diba. Dokta e gaukaralaia test kwadogidia danu, edia sibodia be dementia diagnose totona e hegeregere lasi. Edia be evaluation badana ena matamana, bona diagnose e abia totona lao haida e goada. NIA e gwaumu, memory hekwakwanai ida taudia be hua 6 eiava 12 lalonai dokta dekenai e lou.

## Hereva namona amo herevalaia

Bema oi tau ma ta dekenai oi hekwakwanai, nega manada bona gabuna gado ai oi abia hidi. Oi itaia gaudia maorodia oi herevalaia, ia ena gorere dekenai oi laloa, e hadikaia hereva lasi. Check-up ta oi gwau, unai be e auka lasi. Tau ia oi abia dae abia hidi lalonai bona ia be tau bada, unai heto oi hereva henia. Ai emai [hereva daladia](/caregiving/communication-tips/) be e durumu diba.

## Dokta ida oi herevala

Bema oi itaia memory, laloa, mood eiava dina kara dekenai idau gauna matamata e vara bona e noho, primary care dokta ida appointment oi karaia. Confusion e vara haraga neganai, 111 dekenai eiava tau ia naria tauna ena health care provider dekenai harihari oi phone. Harihari check be e durumu, treatment e karaia diba daladia e davaria diba, bona bema dementia, plan bona help e tahua totona nega e mauri. Oi sibomu danu oi naria; ai emai [caregiver sibona naria dalana](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) oi itaia.

Inai pepa be sivarai, medical advice lasi. Ai emai [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/) oi itaia.
