Inai pagina ai 2024 Lancet Commission ese e gwauraia dementia ena risk 14, haida hahedinaraia diba, stadi ese dahaka e hahedinaraia, bona dahaka oi dokta ida herevala herevana diba.

## Dementia ena risk 14 be dahaka?

[2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0),
dementia stadi taudia 27 ese e torea, e hahedinaraia risk 14 haida hahedinaraia diba
ese tanobada ai dementia karadia 45% dekenai hegeregere. Henunai ai risk ta ta ena
namba be Commission ena hahedinaraia, tanobada ai karadia edana hegeregere, namba maorodia ai.
Namba idia e hebou, badina be taunimanima momo dekenai risk momo e noho, una dainai idia 45% ai e do lao, momo lasi.

## Inai namba edana bamona lau duahia be namo?

Namonamo ai. Commission ena ripoti sibona ese hereva haida e gwauraia, laloatao namo:

- **Idia be taunimanima ibounai totona, tau ta ta lasi.** University College London ena
  [ripoti ena haheitalai](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors)
  ese e gwauraia, risk e hahererea karana be taunimanima ibounai totona e gaukara, to tau ta ta dekenai dementia do ia vara lasi e gwaraniava lasi.
- **Idia be badina bona dika e laloa.** Toia taudia ese hahedinaraia momokani gaudia sibona e abia, to idia e gwauraia hegeregere haida be dala maraginai sibona lasi.
- **Idia be tanobada ai namba.** Sivarai momo be moni bada tanodia amo, bona tano ta ta eiava taunimanima ta ta ena namba maorona be idau.
- **Namba maragidia danu e badina.** Risk ta, karadia 2% dekenai e hegeregere, ia taunimanima momo herea dekenai e gaukara.

## 2024 ai risk ededia e haboua?

2024 ripoti ese risk rua e haboua 2020 ai e gwauraia 12 dekenai:

- **LDL kolesterol bada, mauri bogaragina ai**, karadia 7% dekenai e hegeregere.
- **Mata kerere e naria lasi, mauri dokona ai**, karadia 2% dekenai e hegeregere.

## Risk ededia ita hahedinaraia diba lasi?

National Institute on Aging ese e gwauraia
[mauri lagani be Alzheimer's ena risk badana herea](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease),
bona ta ese hahedinaraia diba lasi. Gene danu e gaukara; Alzheimer's ena risk e hahedinaraia gene ta e dibaia be APOE. NIA ese e gwauraia danu, stadi taudia be taunimanima momo dekenai Alzheimer's ena badina be e diba momokani lasi. Lancet Commission ese e gwauraia, haida hahedinaraia diba risk be e hahererea diba, tau ta ta ena gene risk be dahaka.

Mauri lagani bona gene e badina bada, una dainai taunimanima momo, risk 14 idia dekenai lasi, to dementia e vara. Unai be ta ena kerere lasi.

## Inai risk e hahedinaraia karana dekenai trial ena hahedinaraia be dahaka?

Risk ta dementia ida e hegeregere, unai ese e hahedinaraia karana be dementia ena risk e hahererea e hahedinaraia lasi. Trial ena hahedinaraia ena hegeregere haida:

- **Rara kwatua.** SPRINT MIND trial ai, rara kwatua namba maragina ese lalo-gaukara kerere maragina e hahererea momokani. Dementia e hahedinaraia lasi momokani, to trial be maragi diba, una dainai e hahedinaraia lasi ([SPRINT MIND in JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Hearing aid.** Lancet Commission ese lagani toi hearing aid trial ta e gwauraia, lalo-gaukara dekenai hahedinaraia lasi, to risk bada taudia dekenai durua e vara. Taunimanima e daevaia stadi ese e hahedinaraia, hearing aid gaukaralaia e hegeregere risk maragina ida. Ita eda [hearing guide](/brain-health/hearing/) ai sivarai momo.
- **Tauanina.** National Institute on Aging ese e gwauraia, trial ena hahedinaraia be e hegeregere lasi, exercise ese lalo-gaukara kerere maragina eiava Alzheimer's e taravatua eiava e hamaragina e gwauraia totona, to stadi haida ese e hahedinaraia mauri lagani ida lalo-kerere dekenai e durua diba
  ([NIA on preventing Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Dementia e vara e naria trial daudau be nega daudau bona moni bada e abia, una dainai risk momo totona hahedinaraia namodia be taunimanima e daevaia lagani momo lalonai stadi amo e mai. Unai hahedinaraia be namo, to trial bamona e goada lasi.

## Mahuta bona aniani be ede?

Commission ese mahuta bona aniani e itaia, to idia e haboua lasi, badina be hahedinaraia e goada lasi eiava e hegeregere lasi. Ita eda
[mahuta](/brain-health/sleep/) bona [aniani](/brain-health/diet/) gaidedia ese dahaka e dibaia e hahedinaraia.

## Dokta ida herevalaia

Lagani ta ta ai chek be negana namona inai herevadia e abia dae totona. Oi inai
lisi oi abia mai diba bona oi henanadai:

- Lau egu rara kwatua, rara ai suka, bona kolesterol namba be dahaka, bona ta e naria be namo, a?
- Lau egu kamonai bona mata e chekalaia be namo, a?
- Lau egu tauanina be namo bona e hegeregere, a?
- Lau egu muramura haida ese lalo eiava lalohadai e hahedinaraia diba, a?

Muramura, aniani, aniani lasi noho (fasting), tauanina, eiava supplement oi hahedinaraia lasi guna, dokta ida herevalaia guna. Oi eiava oi naria tauna egu ia dekenai, harihari lalohadai e kerere, kahana ta be e goada lasi, eiava hereva e dika, bema 111 dekenai kolaia.
