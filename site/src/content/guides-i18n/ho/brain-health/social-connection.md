Tau be turadia ida noho ura, bona unai be buruka neganai ia lao lasi. Diba toi be tau buruka lalonai sibona bona se lalonai idia noho neganai, dementia kahirakahira be bada, kudouna gorere bona lalo-hisihisi danu. Diba toi be harihari unai hegwau be dahaka dainai, o edena momo, ia hahedinaraia lasi. Inai pepa ai diba toi ia hahedinaraia gaudia, hebou noho daladia, bona dahaka dainai hebou gaukara be dementia ida tau edia anina bada.

## Sibona bona se lalonai be dementia ida hegwau, a?

Oi, diba toi momo be idia hegwau, to dalana be idia diba momokani lasi. [2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) ia gwauraia sibona be tau buruka lalonai dementia kahirakahira 14 edia ta. [UCL ena ripoti herevadia](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) ia gwau unai be tanobada ai dementia karadia 5% bamona.

Diba toi ia hahedinaraia gaudia:

- **Hebou maragi, kahirakahira bada.** Commission ia gwau diba toi rua be hebou maragi ia maragi neganai dementia kahirakahira ia bada. Ta ia gwau 57% bamona ia bada, ma ta ia gwau 18% bamona.
- **Sibona lalo danu.** Sibona, idia siri lasi henia lalo, be diba toi momo ai dementia kahirakahira bada ida hegwau.
- **Surgeon General ena hereva.** 2023 May ai, US Surgeon General ia [sibona bona se lalonai totona hereva](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) ia gwauraia. Ia gwau sibona bona se lalonai daudau be tau buruka edia dementia kahirakahira 50% bamona ia habadaia diba. Hebou namo lasi be kudouna gorere kahirakahira 29% bada bona stroke kahirakahira 32% bada ida danu hegwau.
- **Edana momo.** [National Academies ripoti](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557972/) ia gwau Amerika ai tau buruka 65 bona ia hanaia, community ai idia noho, 24% bamona be se lalonai idia noho.

Laloatao namo: Lancet Commission ia gwau tau haida be dementia ia matamaia neganai, idia diba lasi neganai, turadia hebou gaukara idia hadokoa. Unai dainai, hegwau haida be dementia ia havaraia tau ia siri noho, to siri noho be kahirakahira ia habadaia lasi. Diba toi momo be itaia diba lasi gaudia.

## Sibona bona se lalonai edia idau be dahaka?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/loneliness-and-social-isolation/loneliness-and-social-isolation-tips-staying-connected) ia hahedinaraia bamona. **Se lalonai** be tau momo lasi ida hebou noho o hebou nega maragi. **Sibona** be sibona noho o idia siri lasi henia lalo-hisihisi. Tau be sibona ia noho to ia moale diba, tau be ruma ai tau momo ida ia noho to sibona ia laloa diba.

NIA ia gwauraia kahirakahira ia habadaia gaudia:

- Kamonai o mata kerere
- Lao dalana ia auka, o transportation lasi
- Sibona noho, o gau bada ta ia ore, hegeregerena adavana ia mase o gaukara ia dokona
- Laloatao o laloa kerere
- Gorere tau ta oi naria
- Moni hekwakwanai o gado idau

Kamonai be gau bada. NIA ia gwau tau be namonamo ia kamonai lasi neganai, hereva ia auka o ia hemarai dainai ia siri noho diba. [Kamonai bona kwalimu namona eda pepa](/brain-health/hearing/) ai kamonai check bona over-the-counter kamonai durua ia hahedinaraia.

## Hebou noho daladia

NIA ia lalo-hadai momo ia henia. Oiemu ai ia namo rua oi abia hidi:

- **Hebou nega oi atoa.** Dina ta ta o wiki ta ta nega oi atoa, phone, text, email o card amo ranu, turamu o vadaeni tau ida.
- **Video call o smart speaker oi gaukaralaia.** Technology be matamata neganai, library bona community center momo ai diba class idia henia. [Phone bona video call eda pepa](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) oi itaia.
- **Gaukara ta oi vareai.** Class, ekalesia, lao group o volunteer gaukara be hebou bona anina ia henia.
- **Guna hobby oi hamatamaia lou** o matamata hobby ta tau ma haida ida oi diba.
- **Turamu ida oi heau.** Turamu o group ida exercise. [Exercise eda pepa](/brain-health/exercise/) ai dalana maragi.
- **Ranu animal ta oi laloa** bema oi naria diba.
- **Gabuna durua oi gaukaralaia.** Senior center, Area Agencies on Aging, bona Eldercare Locator (800-677-1116) be program bona transportation totona idia durua diba.

## Dahaka dainai hebou gaukara be dementia lalonai anina bada?

Dementia ida tau edia, hebou be moale, refurefu, bona idia abia dae totona, kahirakahira ia hahererea lasi totona. Lancet Commission ia gwau gaukara be dementia ida tau edia moale bona anina ia durua. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ia gwau mauri namona ia habadaia, bona gaukara haida be lao siri o badu daladia ia hahererea diba. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ia gwau Alzheimer's ida tau be heau noho bona idia moalelaia gaudia idia karaia be namo.

Namo daladia:

1. **Lada oi itaia hebou.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) be henanadai namo, quiz lasi, hegeregerena "Oi hereva henia lau...". [Laloatao lou eda pepa](/activities/reminiscence/) oi itaia.
2. **Kahana kahana oi gadara.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ai lada be idia itaia noho, timer o score lasi, unai dainai hekwakwanai lasi. [Ede hebou gadara](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) eda pepa ai lalo-hadai.
3. **Ranu lada oi gaukaralaia.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) be oi abia hidi lada amo matching game ia karaia, bona lada be oiemu device lalonai idia noho.
4. **Tau oi haboua.** [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) be ranu hebou totona bona [care home group](/activities/care-home-groups/) totona ia namo.
5. **Ane, ruma gaukara o uma oi karaia hebou.** [Ane gaukara](/activities/music/), [ruma gaukara](/activities/household-tasks/) bona [gaukara lalo-hadai iboudiai](/activities/ideas/) oi itaia.
6. **Vadivadi oi hamaragia.** [Vadivadi eda pepa](/activities/visiting/) ai maragi, refurefu vadivadi daladia.

Maragi bona refurefu oi karaia. Ruma refurefu ai tau ta o rua be namo, tau momo bada lasi. Bema tau ia fatigue o ia ura lasi, dokona be namo. Ti kapu ta ida kahana kahana noho danu be namo.

Dementia ida tau be sibona ia noho neganai, NIA ia gwau tau ta oi abia hidi ia kamonai henia, ia vadivadi o video call nega iboudiai, bona emergency contact ia lao. Gabuna memory cafe danu ia gwauraia, unai be laloatao kerere tau bona edia ranu edia refurefu hebou gabuna.

## Tau naria taudia danu sibona idia laloa diba

NIA ia gwau gorere tau naria be sibona kahirakahira ta. Oiemu turamu, durua group, bona laga-ani totona nega maragi oi naria. [Tau naria sibodia edia nari](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ai dalana.

## Dokta ida oi hereva

Bema oi o oi naria tau be nega momo sibona o se lalonai ia laloa, dokta oi hamaoroa. NIA ia gwau mauri lalonai kerere bada, hegeregerena ranu ta ia mase, dokta oi hamaoroa, dokta ia diba momokani totona. Wiki momo lalonai lalo-hisihisi ia noho neganai, unai be depression diba, bona durua ia noho. Bema tau buruka be ia siri noho matamata turadia o gaukara ia moalelaia guna amo, unai kerere danu dokta oi hamaoroa be namo. [Matamata toana bona edana negai dokta oi itaia](/brain-health/warning-signs/) oi duahia.
