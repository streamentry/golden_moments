Sensory gaukara ese ita eda sense 5 e gaukaralaia: touch, bono, aniani, kamonai, bona itaia. Edia laloatao, step, o haereva maoromaoro be e ura lasi, unai dainai nega ibounai dekenai e gaukara diba. Gabeai neganai e laloa bada. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) ese e gwauraia, gabeai nega ai, tanobada be sense amo e diba, bona oi lalokau henia diba touch, regena, itaia, aniani, bona bono amo. Namo toana maragi dalana haida ese unai gaukara e namo henia, iboudiai dekenai.

## Dementia dekenai, sensory gaukara be dahaka?

Sensory gaukara be heḡereḡere gaukara, nega tamona ai sense tamona dekenai e lao: laplap oi toho, raurau oi bono, o medu regena oi kamonai. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ese e gwauraia, gabeai nega edia gaukara be sense dekenai e lao be namo, bona ane, toho gaukara karadia, bona ima massage e gwauraia. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ese e gwauraia, gabeai nega ai taudia haida ese gau ta e abia noho, e namo henia totona.

## Sensory lalo, sense ta ta

### Touch

- **Ima massage.** Oi emu ima ai lotion maragi oi atoa, bona heḡereḡere oi laitai, wrist amo ima douna dekenai. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) ese e gwauraia, bono lasi lotion ida massage be namo henia totona.
- **Laplap basket.** Velvet, corduroy, fleece, satin, bona wool edia maragi laplap, e toho, e lokua, bona e herevalaia diba.
- **Tau towel maragi.** Keru neganai ima dekenai maragi, paripari towel. Guna oi emu wrist ai oi toho, temperature oi diba totona.
- **Activity apron o lap blanket.** Maragi button, zipper, ribbon, bona texture patch e humia goadagoada.
- **Lokua bona hamatamata.** Nohomaoro washcloth o napkin basket ta, e lokua, e atoa, bona e lokua lou.
- **Malumu gau ta e abia.** Fleece throw, cushion, o malumu animal ta, heḡereḡere oi henia, gado bamona lasi.

### Bono

- **Matamata raurau.** Oi emu ima ai mint, rosemary, basil, o lavender rau oi habodoa, bona ia dekenai oi henia.
- **Aniani rum edia bono.** Coffee e daekau, buredi e torea, cinnamon, o orange e kokia kahana.
- **Auede.** Gaden amo o stoa amo matamata auede haida oi mailaia.
- **Ia diba gau ta.** Ia ese nega ibounai e gaukaralaia soap o lotion, bema ia ena kopi e namo.

### Aniani

- **Ia ura henia aniani.** Ia e ura henia aniani maragina, heḡereḡere applesauce, pudding, o ice cream, ia ania diba heḡereḡere ai.
- **Baking ai durua.** Oi baking neganai, auhu o frosting maragina ia e toho diba.
- **Nega edia aniani.** Laḡani ai watermelon, keru ai cider maragi, namo temperature ai.

### Kamonai

- **Ia ura henia ane.** Ia diba ane, regena maragi ai; [music activities](/activities/music/) oi itaia.
- **Natura regena.** Medu, davara, o manu regena. NIA ese e gwauraia, ane, white noise, bona natura regena ese haida e hamatamata diba.
- **Oi emu regena.** Heḡereḡere duahiduahi, ane maragina, o sivarai oi gwauraia.
- **Heḡereḡere instrument.** Ima drum, shaker, o bell, ane ida e gadara diba.

### Itaia

- **Windows ai itaia.** Windows murinai manu edia aniani gauna, natunaudia e lao, o sino e moru.
- **Fish tank o heḡereḡere video.** Heḡereḡere move, regena bada lasi.
- **Maragi photo.** Nega tamona ai maoromaoro photo tamona, mata dekenai oi abia isi.
- **Color bona pattern.** Kakala laplap maragi, auede, o napkin kala, o [coloring](/games/coloring/) ai heḡereḡere coloring page.
- **Murimuri ai.** NHS ese e gwauraia, sensory gaden ai raurau bona karadia e noho, bono, texture, bona ranu gauna ida. [outdoor and gardening activities](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) oi itaia.

## Sensory box edena bamona oi karaia

1. **Sivarai ta oi abia hidi** tau oi naria ena mauri ida: kone, gaden, aniani rum, gaukara rum, o humia gaukara.
2. **Maragi, namo gau 5 o 6 oi abia,** heḡereḡere maragi kone gauna, au sipuni, malumu scarf, lavender maragina e humia gauna, o au block malumu.
3. **Gau ibounai oi itaia namonamo,** maragi kahana, koi douna, lohi varo, bona makohia diba gauna totona.
4. **Nega tamona ai gau tamona oi abiaisi.** Oi henia, oi gwauraia, bona ia e toho.
5. **Gabeai box oi atoa noho,** unai dainai ia be namo, bona gabeai neganai lou oi itaia namonamo.

## Namo toana guna

Sensory gaukara be maragi, to heḡereḡere kerere haida be mai laloa. Dementia ida lao taudia haida ese aniani lasi karadia e laloa aniani, bona hadikakau e goada diba nega e lao neganai; NIA ese e gwauraia, gabeai nega ai hadikakau heḡereḡere ese maragi kerere e habadaia diba.

| Kerere | Dahaka oi karaia |
| --- | --- |
| Maragi (hadikakau ai) | Hadikia lasi gauna oi gaukaralaia. Box ai button, bead, moni, marble, o maragi gado karadia oi atoa lasi. Naria taudia ese e gwauraia aniani heḡereḡere sibona oi henia. |
| Aniani bamona karadia | NIA ese e gwauraia, lotion, soap, shampoo, bona perfume oi kiuna, badina edia e aniani bamona diba, bona plastic auhu oi abia lao. Sensory karadia gabeai oi atoa. |
| Allergy | Oi hamatamaia lasi neganai, nut, latex, wool, au, o bono allergy totona oi diba. |
| Goada bono | Goada perfume bona essential oil oi rakatania, asthma o ma lure heḡereḡere e noho neganai. Matamata, natura bono be namo. |
| Kopi | Massage lasi neganai, hisihisi, makohia, o maragi kopi oi itaia. Heḡereḡere oi toho, bona ia e hisihisi neganai oi dokona. |
| Temperature | Tau towel bona inuinu maragi, guna oi emu wrist ai oi toho. |
| Koi o dika karadia | Koi gaudia, galasi, o dika craft karadia lasi. |

## Aromatherapy ese e durua?

Reana e durua diba lasi, maoromaoro ai. [Cochrane review of aromatherapy for dementia](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) ese stadi 13, tau 708, iboudiai naria rum ai, e itaia, bona diba maoromaoro e davaria lasi. Stadi karadia be maragi, diba e idauidau, bona dika hereva e gwauraia namonamo lasi. Bema tau oi naria ia ese malumu, ia diba bono ta e moalelaia, heḡereḡere gaden lavender, unai be namo badina oi herevalaia diba. Unai be muramura lasi.

## Sensory gaukara edena bamona oi henia

- **Nohomaoro negana oi abia hidi** bona TV oi hadokoa.
- **Vaira amo oi lao kahana,** ia ena ladana oi gwauraia, bona oi toho lasi neganai oi nanadaia o oi gwauraia: "Lau dekenai lotion e nohova. Oi ura ima massage?"
- **Nega tamona ai gau tamona oi henia,** unai dainai ia e lalohisihisi lasi.
- **Ia edena bamona e kara diba oi itaia.** Vaira e maragi, ima e nohomaoro, o kirikiri be namo toana. Ia e daekau, vaira e dika, o e nohomaoro lasi neganai, oi dokona.
- **Heḡereḡere negana oi abia.** Nohomaoro minute 5 be namo gaukara ta.

Heḡereḡere lure gaukara ta, heḡereḡere [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/), be namo dokona dalana. Bed kahana edia lalo ma haida totona, [late-stage activities](/activities/late-stage/) oi itaia, bona adorahi nohomaoro lasi totona, [late-day restlessness](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) oi itaia.
