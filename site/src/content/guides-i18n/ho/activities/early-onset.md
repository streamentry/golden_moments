Dementia hereva momo idia laloa taunimanima be ia ena lagani 70 eiava 80 lalonai. Taunimanima ta ia ena lagani 40 eiava 50 lalonai dementia ia abia neganai, dina ta ta edia mauri be idau. Ia gaukara noho diba, ruma davana ia hahedinaraia noho diba, natuna mero idia ruma dekenai, eiava ia tamana eiava sinana ia naria noho diba. Gaukara be una mauri oi laloa be namo. Inai hereva ia hahedinaraia dahaka ia idau, dahaka oi tohohoa diba, bona raru taudia bona natudia edia heduru ede idia davaria diba.

## Dementia murina gauna be dahaka?

Dementia murina gauna be dementia ia vara 65 laganina murinai, momo neganai idia gwau murina gauna dementia. [Alzheimer's Association ena blog](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) ia gwau Alzheimer's be murina gauna dementia ia vara neganai, ia taunimanima ta 65 murinai ia abia, bona ia gwau una lagani tauna naria karana "ia mai ena hekwakwanai idauidau." Gorere be murina lagani edia gorere bamona, to taunimanima ena laloa, ena gaukara, bona ena taunimanima ida noho be idau.

Ia vara momo lasi dainai, taunimanima idia dokta idia itaia nega ia daudau diba, bona ruma bese idia lalohadai idia hereva henia tauna be lasi. Ma taudia idia una bamona idia mauri noho idia diba neganai, una be ia heduru bada.

## Mauri tauna ia gaukara lalonai oi noho henia

Dementia murina gauna ida mauri taudia momo idia ura lasi idia be natunatu bamona idia karaia. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ia gwau gaukara oi haidaua taunimanima ia karaia diba bona ia moalelaia gaudia dekenai. Taunimanima ta ia ena lagani 50 lalonai neganai, inai bamona:

- **Ia diba gaukara oi noho henia,** hegeregere au gaukara, tauanina, kad, buka duahiduahi grup, eiava ane grup, heduru maragina eiava nega kwadogina ida.
- **Turana ida oi lao eiava baik oi ka,** dala ia diba namonamo dekenai.
- **Ruma bese edia aniani oi karaia,** bona taunimanima ia karaia diba gauna ia karaia.
- **Ia abia hidi hebou gaukara eiava heduru oi karaia,** hegeregere kohu oi atoa eiava sikuli hebou dekenai oi heduru.
- **Gabu ta ia laloa bada dekenai lao oi plan karaia,** heduru tauna ida bona plan isiaina ida.
- **Tubuna, eiava ruma bese edia natunatu ida nega oi hahedinaraia,** gem ta eiava gaukara ta oi karaia hebou.

Taunimanima ia sibona ia abia hidi. "Hari adorahi oi dahaka karaia ura?" oi nanadaia be ia abia hidi karana amo ia namo herea.

## Ane bona laloatao ena lagani amo oi gaukaralaia

Ane bona laulau taunimanima ena 20, 30, bona 40 lagani gauna amo be lalo namodia bona hereva isiaina idia havaraia diba. Emai [ane gaukara](/activities/music/) hereva ia hahedinaraia playlist oi karaia dalana, bona [laloatao gaukara](/activities/reminiscence/) ia hahedinaraia laulau bona sivarai oi hahedinaraia dalana, nanadai lasi. Mauri tauna edia gem totona, [hereva kad](/games/conversation-cards/) oi tohohoa diba, gaukara, lao, turana, bona ruma bese dekenai hereva daudau ida.

Ruma bese edia laulau amo photo pairs danu ia namo diba, badina laulau be taunimanima ia laloa bada taudia bona gabudia. [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ia laulau ia noho una masin lalonai browser dekenai.

## Gaukara, motuka, bona dina mauri

Dementia murina gauna ida taunimanima momo idia gaukara noho, idia ka motuka, eiava idia moni idia naria noho. Inai be gaukara korikori lasi, to idia taunimanima ia karaia diba gauna idia haidaua. Ia ena dokta ida oi herevalaia motuka bona gaukara namona totona, bona ia sibona ia ura neganai moni eiava apoinmen dekenai heduru oi laloa. Dalana be mataurai oi naria bona taunimanima ia sibona ia ura noho gauna lalonai ia vareai.

Dina gaugau bona gaukara noho totona hereva, [dina gaugau isiaina](/activities/daily-routine/) bona [ruma gaukara bamona gaukara](/activities/household-tasks/) oi itaia.

## Raru taudia bona natudia edia heduru

Raru taudia bona natudia momo idia lalohadai idia sibodia. Naria gaukara be gaukara bona ruma bese gaukara dekenai ia idau. Alzheimer's Association ena blog ia gwau murina gauna grup lalonai naria taudia idia davaria ia heduru, hegeregere lo herevadia plan karana, bona idia laloparara karana. Ia gwau danu Association ia naria taudia edia heduru grup ia henia, davana lasi, murina gauna dementia ida taunimanima idia naria taudia edia grup danu, bona toi idia Tuesday bona Wednesday dekenai virtual bamona idia hebou.

Grup oi nanadaia totona, Association ena 24/7 Helpline 800.272.3900 dekenai oi phone. Emai [naria taudia edia sibodia naria hereva](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ia laga-ani, heduru nega, bona gogoa badadia toana ia herevalaia.

## Dementia ena gauna danu ia anina bada

Taunimanima ena dementia gauna be dahaka ia heduru ia haidaua noho. Murinai, momo taudia idia edia gaukara korikori idia karaia diba, haidaua maragina ida, bona emai [gaukara dementia murina gauna](/activities/early-stage/) hereva ia mai dala momo. Gabeai, gaukara idia isi isi bona idia tauanina dekenai idia lao, [gaukara dementia gabeai gauna](/activities/late-stage/) ia hahedinaraia bamona. Taunimanima ia idau neganai, gaukara oi haidaua, bona ia hereva diba neganai ia dahaka ura oi nanadaia.
