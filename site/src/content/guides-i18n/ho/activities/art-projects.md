Art gaukara na dala namona ta, dementia tauna ta ida negana oi gaukaralaia totona. Imana, mata bona diba ia havaraia, bona hereva dalana ta danu ia henia. Inai pepapana ai gaukara haida badibadina oi itaia, bona art gaukara namodia edia abia hidiho dalana danu. Adault art ena badina herevadia, hegeregere natuna edia gaukara lasi, dekenai [dementia taudia edia arts bona crafts](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) oi itaia.

## Art gaukara ibounai ede heto oi matamaia

1. **Oi matamaia lasi neganai oi hegaegae.** Gaukara ta eiava rua, gima pepapana ta, bona dabua paripari ta oi atoa.
2. **Matamana badina oi hahedinaraia.** Brush oi utua, matamana maragina oi dikaia, eiava clay maragina ta oi gigia, gabeai ia karaia toma totona oi noia.
3. **Oi ruaosi kahana ta ai oi gaukara.** Oiemu gaukara kahana dekenai oi atoa, unai dainai gaukara na hebou karana, naria karana lasi.
4. **Oi itaia gaudia oi herevalaia.** Kala, diba bona foma oi gwauraia, gaukara oi hahemaoro lasi.
5. **Goada ia diho lasi neganai oi dokona.** Miniti 10 bona 20 huanai na negana namona momo neganai.**

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ia gwau art na lalohadai hahedinaraia bona karaia dalana ta, art aonega be tahua lasi. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ia gwaurai gaukara badibadia oi karaia, maragidia bona namodia.

## Watercolor wash

Heavy watercolor pepapana, brush manau badana ta, bona kala haida kapu maragidia lalonai oi gaukaralaia. Pepapana ranu goevana ida oi hapariparia, gabeai kala ia utua dekenai oi noho. Pepapana oi haidaua maragimaragi, kala idia heau totona. Unai amo laulau manau ta ia vara, plan ta be tahua lasi.

- **Matamana stage:** Kala ia abia hidiho bona tano laulau ta ia paint diba.
- **Huanai stage:** Kala rua eiava toi oi henia, bona pepapana hapariparia karana oi karaia.
- **Murinai stage:** Pepapana oi dogoatao, ia sponge ida ia dabua neganai.

## Magasin collage

Magasin pepana oi abia, au hururu, aniani, boroma, motuka eiava lao tano edia laulau maoro ida. Laulau oi dikaia maragidia, scissors amo dikaia lasi, bona kadi dekenai oi kapata. Collage na hereva dalana namona ta, badina laulau ta ta be laloatao dalana ta ia kehoa diba. Abia hidiho bona atoa karana ia ura tauna dekena na namo.

## Manau clay

Air-dry clay eiava modeling clay manau na gigia, birulia bona butua diba. Kapu maragina ta eiava tile palapala ta oi karaia. Clay na imana diba karana bada, unai dainai gau ta dogoatao urana taudia dekenai ia hamoalea diba. Gaukara maragidia oi atoa, bona kamokamo badadia lasi.

## Chalk pastels pepapana korema dekenai

Pepapana korema na pastel kala ia hamaoromaoroa. Chunky pastels badadia oi gaukaralaia, bona tau oi naria tauna ia torea badadia eiava kwarana eiava tissue ida ia hamanea. Pastels na momi, unai dainai tray ta dekenai oi gaukara, gabeai imana oi hururu. Non-toxic gwauhamata pastel manaudia oi abia.

## Au rara toana

Gadena amo au rara badadia oi abia, washable paint rara lain dekenai oi atoa, bona pepapana dekenai oi gigia. Maragimaragi oi abia isi, bona toana oi moalelaia. Inai gaukara na [murimuri bona gadena gaukara](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) ida ia hebou namonamo, bona au rara abia totona lao gabuna amo ia matamaia diba.

## Dabua bona au hururu abia hidiho

Dabua diba haida edia diba hegeregerena oi atoa, eiava au hururu haida vase ta lalonai oi atoa. Idia na diba gaukara, hekwakwanai maragi ida. Murinai stages ai taudia dekenai danu idia namo. Hereva ma haida totona [dementia taudia edia diba gaukara](/activities/sensory/) oi itaia.

## Art gaukara edia abia hidiho

Art gaukara namodia na namo bona naria totona idia bada, mai kunana lasi. Oi abia hidiho neganai, inai oi laloa:

- **Non-toxic bona hururu diba:** Non-toxic gwauhamata oi tahua, bona paint be hururu diba oi diba tohoa.
- **Imana badadia:** Jumbo crayons, brush gaudia, bona chunky pastels na imana totona idia namo.
- **Maragi maragidia lasi:** Bead, button, sequin bona glitter maragidia oi rakatania, badina e tomo diba.
- **Kamokamo lasi:** Safety scissors oi gaukaralaia eiava pepapana imana ida oi dikaia.
- **Allergy:** Latex oi diba, bona kopi diba gado haida danu.
- **Diba namona:** Kala maorodia pepapana kurokuro eiava korema dekenai na itaia namonamo.
- **Gaukara maragidia:** Gaukara rua eiava toi be namo. Abia hidiho momo na ia havaraia diba.
- **Hururu namonamo:** Tray, plastic pepapana bona dabua paripari ta oi gaukaralaia, unai dainai miro na dokona badina lasi.

## Stage hegeregerena haidaua

Matamana stage ai, tau taudia momo be gaukara idia plan bona idia karaia diba, durua maragina ida. Huanai stage ai, abia hidiho maragidia oi henia bona durua ma haida oi karaia. Murinai stage ai, diba gaukara dekenai oi laloa, hegeregere clay gigia eiava sponge ida paint dabua, bona gaukara amo moale oi abia, ia ore vadaeni lasi.

Stage hegeregerena hereva ma haida totona, [matamana stage](/activities/early-stage/), [huanai stage](/activities/middle-stage/) bona [murinai stage gaukara](/activities/late-stage/) oi itaia. Bema tau oi naria tauna ia ura lasi eiava ia badu, [tauna ia gwau lasi neganai](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) eda hereva diba bukana ai siri dalana ia hahedinaraia.

## Game ta ida oi heboua

Paint gaukara murinai, tablet dekenai [coloring](/games/coloring/) oi tohohoa manada totona, eiava [picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) dokona gaukara totona. Ruaosi be imana idia havaraia noho, aonega maragina tahua lasi.
