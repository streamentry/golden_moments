Bema emui lalokau tauna be Alzheimer ida e nohona, reana oi itaia vadaeni: haida ḡau be haraga ai e boio, haida be e noho. Hari daba ena phone hereva ia laloaboio diba, to ane ta ena hereva ibounai ia abia diba, dabua ta e torea ena ḡaukara ḡunana heḡereḡerena, eiava card ia haḡaua, laloa lasi. Namo gaudia be unai e noho diba gaudia dekenai ai badinaia. Inai pepa ese dahaka dainai e hahedinaraia, gabeai hakaua ta ta totona lalohadai e henia, bona inai site ena free gaudia danu e hahedinaraia.

## Alzheimer be dementia amo e idau, gaudia dekenai, a?

Bada lasi. Dementia be ladana bada, bona Alzheimer gorere be una badina ibounai ai ma haida momokani: [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) e gwaumu dementia kahana 60 amo 80 ai e abia. Dementia gaudia totona ai ḡwalaia ḡau ibounai be Alzheimer dekenai danu e ḡaukara, eda [101 ginigini lalohadai](/activities/ideas/) danu.

Namo ba diba ḡau ta be, laulau kerere be ede e hamatamaia. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-alzheimers-disease) e hahedinaraiamu Alzheimer ese laloatao kahana be guna e hadikaia, gabeai gado, laloa bona taunimanima ḡaukara danu e hadikaia. Alzheimer's Association e gwaumu, matamaia toana ma haida be matamata ḡaukara laloatao be auka. Tauna ta ta be idau, bona haida taudia be badina rua eiava haida e abia, to inai dalana ese dahaka gaudia be e namo, una e durua.

## Dahaka gaudia bamona be Alzheimer totona e namo?

Nega daudau ai dibaia ḡaukara gunana dekenai ai badinaia gaudia be, matamata ḡaukara dibaia bona laloatao dekenai ai badinaia gaudia ai e maoro.

- **Nega daudau ai dibaia diba.** Alzheimer's Association e gwaumu, tauna be nega daudau guna piano ai e dibaia ane malakamalaka ia abia diba noho. Koikoi, au hadibaia, card haḡaua, palaua haheḡaeḡae, bona dabua torea danu unai bamona.
- **Gunalaia ane bona familiari musik.** Ma haida taudia be edia mero eiava tauhau negana anedia, dubu ane, bona headava anedia ena hereva ibounai e diba noho. [Musik gaudia](/activities/music/) ba itaia.
- **Anina ida dina ḡaukara.** [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ese maua huria, pata atoa, kaukau hahetohoa, bona dabua torea e gwauraia, taudia ese e moalelaia diba gaudia ai. Durua herea laloana be bada.
- **Dibaia, to laloatao lasi.** Hegereḡere ladana dekenai ba gwauraia be, ladana be laloatao amo ba gwauraia ai e malakamalaka. Una dainai ladana ibounai itaia gaudia be henanadai ai e namo.
- **Sene ḡaukara.** Dogodogo, bona, siahu, aniani mamina, bona regena be hereva e auka muri danu e noho bada. [Sene gaudia](/activities/sensory/) ba itaia.

E auka diba gaudia: taima ida, matamata taravatu dibaia, kara haida laloatao, eiava laloatao e dibaia henanadai, heḡereḡere «Inai be daika, oi laloa?». Unai gaudia ese Alzheimer ena auka kahana e atoa kamonai.

## Matamaia hakauana Alzheimer totona gaudia

Matamaia hakauana dekenai, [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/stages) e gwaumu tauna be motuka ia draiva diba, ḡaukara ia karaia diba, bona taunimanima gaudia ai ia vareai diba, to laloaboio gaudia ia itaia, heḡereḡere familiari hereva eiava ḡau gabuna e laloaboio. Namo dalana be hebou ḡaukara: dahaka ia ura e karaia noho, bona dahaka durua ia ura, ba nanadaia.

1. **Guna hobi ba noho karaia.** Golf, bridge, koikoi, abia ruma, bona dubu hebou, mara kerere ida: ruma maragi bona laga-ani negana.
2. **Familiari card bona board gaudia ba gadaraia.** [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ese trivia, hereva davaria, card gaudia bona board gaudia e gwauraia, matamaia hakauana totona. Bema poin e habaduaia, hebou ai ba gadaraia, eiava poin ba haboio.
3. **Hebou ai nadia ba ḡaukara.** Ia ese e dibaia vadaeni resipe ba karaia, oi ese kara duahia ba duahia.
4. **Mauri sivarai ḡaukara ta ba hamatamaia.** Ladana, resipe bona sivarai be scrapbook ta ai, ia ese e gunalaia. [Laloatao lou gaudia](/activities/reminiscence/) ba itaia.
5. **Lao-lai ba karaia.** Moale aniani gabuna ta, laga-ani negana ai; museum ta ai nega kwadogi; gabu ta ai raka lao.
6. **Ia ese e abi hidi hedavari ba hahedinaraia.** Badadia leta crossword, hereva davaria bona jigsaw. [Dementia totona hedavari](/activities/puzzles/) ba itaia.

Lalohadai ma haida: [matamaia hakauana gaudia](/activities/early-stage/).

## Kamonai hakauana Alzheimer totona gaudia

Alzheimer's Association e gwaumu kamonai hakauana be ma haida nega daudau momokani, bona lagani haida ia lao diba. Hereva e hebou hebou, badu e haraga mai, bona tauna be gaudia hamatamaia eiava karaia noho totona durua e ura bada. Alzheimer's Foundation of America e gwaumu danu, ma haida taudia be gaudia hamatamaia eiava karaia noho totona durua e ura. Oiemu ḡaukara be: ḡau ba haḡoevaia, hahedinaraia to hereva lasi, bona auka kahana ba abia.

1. **Toia, hahetohoa, bona hegereḡere.** Dabua, kaukau, aniani kaia, buton kala heḡereḡerena. Momokani ḡaukara, hebou ai.
2. **Ane abia bona musik ida ba marere.** Ima pata, ba lou, eiava kitchen ai ba danisi. Oi ba hamatamaia ane abia, ia ba daḡe.
3. **Ladana album ba itaia.** Oi itaia ḡau ba hahedinaraia, bona sivarai ba noia: «Inai motuka be namo. Ba sivaraigu.» Ia ese e ḡaukaralaia ladana ba abia dae.
4. **Malakamalaka ladana gaudia ba gadaraia.** [Ladana rua rua](/games/picture-pairs/) be tile hani eiava sikisi ida, ladana itaia noho; eiava [Unai ta ba davaria](/games/find-the-same/) be hidi rua eiava toi ida.
5. **Familiari hereva dokoa ba haḡunalaia.** «An apple a day keeps the...» Nega daudau ai dibaia hereva dokoa be haraga e mai. [Hereva dokoa ba haḡunalaia](/games/finish-the-saying/) ba hahedinaraia, unuseniai dokona ta ba hahedinaraia diba, pata ta mo.
6. **Au ba ranu henia bona ba naria.** Ruma lalonai au eiava gabu daena ai, ranu kaia maragi ta ida.
7. **Familiari ladana amo hegereḡere ginigini ta ba karaia.** [Ladana rua rua, emui ladana amo](/games/photo-pairs/) ese oi abi hidi ladana e ḡaukaralaia, emui masin lalonai, bona e lao lasi.

Lalohadai ma haida: [kamonai hakauana gaudia](/activities/middle-stage/).

## Dokona hakauana Alzheimer totona gaudia

Dokona hakauana dekenai, Alzheimer's Association e gwaumu taudia be edia nohona dekenai haere davana bona hereva ḡaukara haerelaia diba e boio, to haida hereva eiava hereva kahana e gwauraia diba noho. Ginigini ena anina be: heḡeḡae bona hebou, tauna e nohona gabuna dekenai ba lao henia.

1. **Ena moale musik ba abia** laga-ani, TV ida heai lasi.
2. **Ima tauanina maragimaragi ba henia,** ia e moalelaia bonana ida.
3. **Maragimaragi ḡau dogoatao totona ba henia:** siahu labalaba, fleece, eiava nadi maragimaragi.
4. **Daedae ba duahia:** psalm, poema eiava familiari sivarai. Ena regena be anina ai e bada.
5. **Murimuri ai ba noho** keru henunai, eiava windo badinai, manu itaia totona.
6. **Bada ladana ta ba itaia hebou.** Sunflower eiava sisia ta, tablet matana ai, oi itaia ḡau ba gwauraia.

Lalohadai ma haida: [dokona hakauana gaudia](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ginigini ta ta edena ba hamalakamalakaia

NIA ena lalohaḡani be ḡaukara maragi dekenai e lao: tauna ese e karaia diba ḡaukara ba abi hidi, hamatamaia ba durua, badu ba itaia, namo laloana ia abia ba haḡoevaia, bona bema ia ura itaia mo, una ba habadae.

- **Ena namo negana ba abi hidi,** bona nega kwadogi ba karaia.
- **Ḡau ibounai ba haḡoevaia guna,** una dainai haraga ba hamatamaia diba.
- **Ba noia, to ba oda lasi.** «Oi duruaigu diba, a?» be «Ginigini negana» ai e namo.
- **Guna karana oi ba karaia,** gabeai ia ba daḡe.
- **Kara ta bona hidi ta sibona ba henia negana ta ai.**
- **Ena dalana heḡereḡerena ba karaia.** Dabua idauidau be namo.
- **Moale noho neganai ba dokona.** Guna dokona be namo kerere, kerere lasi. Bema ia e gwau lasi, ba itaia [dementia ida noho tauna e gwau lasi neganai](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/).

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) e gwaumu malakamalaka: moale dekenai ba laloa, abia lasi.
