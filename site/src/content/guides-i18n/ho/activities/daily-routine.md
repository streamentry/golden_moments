Dina taura namona ta be dina ia mai badina ia hegeregere: toreisi, aniani, gaukara maragi, mahuta, bona mahuta negana be hegeregere. Hidi bona hoa momo be ia hagugurua, bona oi be haheitalai ta ia henimu neganai oi diba dahaka ba karaia. Ina dina taura be haheitalai ta, taravatu lasi. Una amo ba hamatamaia, bena tauhanai oi naria ena noho dekenai ba haidaua.

## Dahaka dainai dina taura be ia durua?

Dina taura ta ia hegeregere neganai, dala momo karadia be dala matamata bada to dia hagoevaia. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) ia gwau, dina ta ba haheitalaia neganai, dahaka ba karaia o laloa negana be maragi, bona gaukara namodia ba karaia negana be bada. Ia gwau danu, toreisi bona mahuta negana hegeregere be mahuta kerere bona adorahi heau auka ia durua diba. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) ia gwau, tui, dabua abia, bona aniani be dina ta ta ai negana hegeregere ai ba karaia, ruma ia momokani totona. [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) danu ia gwau, dina taura be baine noho hegeregere.

## Dina namona ta ena haheitalai

Nega be haheitalai sibona. Dina ibounai ba haboua o ba haḡaea, tauhanai oi naria ena negana dekenai ba haidaua.

| Nega | Dahaka e vara | Laloa | Dina ia auka neganai |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8:00 a.m. | Toreisi, hururu, dabua abia | Dina ta ta ai dala tamona; dabua ba atoa daladia hegeregerenai | Sibona naria ba hagoevaia; sioti matamata ta be namo |
| 8:45 a.m. | Daba aniani | Kofi bona nius ba duahia, o redio ba kamonai | Bamoa aniani, hereva basio hamoalea |
| 9:30 a.m. | Gaukara badana | Dabua ba lokua, ba nadua, gaden ai ba gaukara, ba raka o ba lao maragi | Kwadogia ba karaia, o ia ba itaia oi o gaukara |
| 10:30 a.m. | Nohomokani negana | Musika, laulau buka, o windo badinai noho | Sia namona ai ba mahuta |
| 11:00 a.m. | Adorahi aniani ba durua | Aniani ba hururu, pata ba atoa | Oi ba nadua; ia be oi ida ba noho |
| 12:00 p.m. | Adorahi aniani bona ba hagoevaia | Pata ba hururu, mereki maragi ba hagoevaia | Gaukara ba rakatania |

Ina haheitalai be Alzheimer's Association ena haheitalai ginigunana bona huanai dala dekenai ia hahedinaraia: sibona naria bona daba aniani amo, gaukara, raka, musika, mahuta, bona vadivadi lao, bena adorahi aniani, herevala lou, bona mahuta totona hegaegae.

## Emu dina taura edena ba karaia?

Tauhanai oi naria ia noho dala momo amo ba hamatamaia, siat lasi. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, ba haheitalaia neganai unai ba laloa:

- **Ena ura, ia ura lasi, ena goada, bona ena ura gaukara.**
- **Guna ia dina ede ia gaukaralaia.** Daba momokani toreisi tauna sisia ida ia raka be hari danu daba momokani ba raka. Adorahi mahuta tauna be 8:00 a.m. ai ia moale lasi.
- **Ena negana namona dina ai.** Tui, lao, bona gaukara badana una ai ba atoa.
- **Nega maorona** aniani, hururu, bona dabua abia totona, gaukara ta basio haraga.
- **Toreisi bona mahuta negana hegeregere.**

Sivarai una ia gwau danu, ruma gaukara, aniani, sibona naria, gaukara namodia, tau anina gaukara, bamoa negana, tomadiho gaukara, bona hoa lalonai, heto turana ta ia mai vadivadi, unai ba atoa.

### Wiki ta ai ba tohohoa, bena ba haidaua

Dina ta ta ai sivarai maragi ba torea. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, ba nanadaia: edena gaukara be namo bada bona dahaka dainai, negana haida ai gaukara be momo o gaukara be maragi, bona hoa lalonai haheitalai be namo o ia haheauka. Namodia ba abia noho, namo lasi ba haidaua.

### Ba hahedinaraia

Bema tauhanai oi naria be ia duahiduahi diba, whiteboard o pepapana badana ai dina haheitalai ba torea, unai be ia hagoadaia diba. Ba hagoevaia: "Daba aniani. Sarah ida raka. Adorahi aniani. Mahuta." Taunimanima haida be dia diba lasi neganai dia namo; Family Caregiver Alliance ia gwau, bema haheitalai e haheauka, haheitalai ba herevala neganai una gaukara e kahilakahila neganai.

## Dina taura edena ba haidaua

### Nega ba rakatania

Nega ibounai basio gunaia gaukara ida. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, tauhanai oi naria be gaukara bona mahuta baine hagoevaia, bona mahuta negana momo ia ura diba. Bema ia borea, ia lao kava, o ia badu, gaukara ba haidaua o ba mahuta.

### Adorahi ba naria namonamo

Taunimanima momo be dina ia diho neganai dia heau auka. NIA ia gwau, dina ta ta murimuri ai o windo badinai dina maraḡina ba abia, dina ta ta ba lau to gaukara momo basio haheitalaia, adorahi kofi bona inu strong basio inua, bona adorahi mahuta daudau basio abia dae. Ruma haida danu dia gwau, dina ia diho lasi neganai kateni ba koua bona lamepa ba hakaia be ia durua. Ba itaia [adorahi heau auka](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) bona [mahuta](/brain-health/sleep/).

### Ura e haidaua neganai, ba haidaua

- **Dala ginigunana:** bamoa ba haheitalaia. Tauhanai oi naria be torea haheitalai ta ia ura diba, sibona baine badinaia.
- **Dala huanai:** oi ba gunalaia momo. Gaukara kwadogi, mahuta momo, bona gaukara momo ia diba karadia; ba itaia [dala huanai gaukara](/activities/middle-stage/).
- **Dala dokona:** dina taura be namo, aniani, bona naria ai ia noho, huanai ai gaukara maragi heto musika o ima hamomokania; ba itaia [dala dokona gaukara](/activities/late-stage/).

### Gaukara ba haidaua, to dina taura dalana ba abia noho

Aniani, mahuta, bona mahuta negana ba atoa hegeregere, bona huanai gaukara ba haidaua. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ia gwau, dina idauidau ai gaukara idauidau ba tohohoa, gaukara ia namo totona. Monday ai ba nadua, Tuesday ai gaden ai ba gaukara, Wednesday ai laulau ba itaia. Emai [gaukara laloa 101](/activities/ideas/) bona [ruma gaukara](/activities/household-tasks/) una negadia ba gunaia diba.

## Dina ia kerere neganai

Dina haida be haheitalai ta do ia badinaia lasi. Gorere, hanuaboi kerere, dokta vadivadi, o guba ia haidaua dainai gaukara ibounai ia kerere diba. Haheitalai ba rakatania, gaukara badadia sibona ba abia noho (aniani, inu, mahuta, namo), bona kerukeru ba haheaua lou. Bema tauhanai oi naria ia badinaia lasi, ba itaia [ia gwau lasi neganai](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Bamoa hoho momokani ta, heto [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/), oi ruaosi ba hagoadaia lou diba.

Emu dina taura danu be namo. Oi sibomu totona negana ba atoa, bona ruma taudia o durua taudia ba noia emu mahuta negana ba abia. Ba itaia [naria tauna sibona naria](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
