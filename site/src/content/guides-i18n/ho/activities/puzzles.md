Jigsaw ta aniani pata ai oi atoa, inai be gaukara hebou hahanaia ta. Oi ruaosi oi helai hebou diba, hereva momo dia lou, laulau ese hereva dalana ia kehoa, bona hapana gabena oi atoa negana ia namo. Dementia ia haidaua negana, puzzle danu do ia haidaua be namo. Puzzle ia auka momokani, dina namona do ia haidaua ia badu. Inai rau ai, edena dala oi jigsaw, hereva puzzle, bona online puzzle hahanaia oi abia hidi bona oi hegaegae, bona idia auka negana dahaka oi karaia, oi dibaia.

## Puzzle be namo dementia ida noho taudia totona?

Tau momo idia moalelaia, gunalaia puzzle idia gaukaralaia taudia. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ese hereva tahua, kad gemu, bona bodo gemu idia gwauhamata, dementia ena matamana negana totona. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ese online gemu bona puzzle tablet ai idia gwauhamata, gaukara ta.

Puzzle be muramura lasi. Puzzle ta ese dementia ia hanamoa eiava ia habadaia lasi, unai be [brain gemu bona dementia](/brain-health/brain-games/) eda rau ai ia herevalaia. Puzzle oi gaukaralaia badina be unai: nega namona bona moale oi abia hebou diba. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ia gwau: moale oi laloa, dia hagugurua.

## Edena dala oi jigsaw puzzle abia hidi dementia ida noho tauna totona?

Tau ia gaukaralaia dalana bona puzzle guna ia lao edenaito, unai oi itaia, dia stadi ladana.

### Hapana ihaboudaina

Namba maorona ta dia. Puzzle ia laloa namonamo tauna be hapana momo ia moalelaia noho diba matamana; ma ta be hapana badadia maragimaragi ia moalelaia. Dala hahanaia: puzzle guna ia auka, hapana iboudaina hahegeregere oi abia hidi. Ia hagugurua haraga bona ia ura ma haida, hapana maragimaragi oi haboua.

### Hapana maragina bona ia toho dalana

- **Hapana badadia** be itaia, abia, bona hurihaia hahanaia.
- **Hapana auka bona goada** be dia hekure bona idia moru negana dia dika haraga.
- **Laulau dia kerea (matte)** be lamepa bona windo diari ia hahanaia.
- **Hapana maragimaragi oi rakatania** bema tau ia gau ta ia atoa uduna ai, unai dementia murinai ia vara diba.

### Laulau

- **Tau badadia edia laulau.** Gadena, kumana ruma, motuka gunadia, boromakau bona manu, davara tauna ta, eiava gemu ia laloa. Dia meme eiava natunatuna edia gau.
- **Kala maoromaoro.** Ruma kakakaka, bouti blu, bona tano gadigadi be hahanaia, guba laulau bada ta do ia auka.
- **Ia diba bona ia laloa.** Tau ia noho gabuna eiava gaukara ia laloa namonamo, unai ese hereva dalana ia kehoa. Print gaukara taudia haida ese famili laulau jigsaw ai idia karaia diba.

## Edena dala oi puzzle gaukaralaia hebou?

Oi hegaegae namonamo, tau ia moale kahana ia gaukaralaia diba.

1. **Gabuna namona oi abia hidi.** Diari namona, helai gauna ima idau ida, bona pata eiava trei ai puzzle ia noho diba.
2. **Boks ena laulau** oi atoa, oi ruaosi oi itaia diba.
3. **Hapana iboudiai oi hurihaia** gaukara ia matamaia lasi neganai.
4. **Isena hapana oi abia hidi**, eiava isena oi karaia guna bona kahana oi rakatania ia totona.
5. **Hapana kala dainai oi atoa** trei eiava pleiti maragimaragi ai.
6. **Hapana ta ta oi henia** bema hapana momo ia auka. "Inai hapana be ruma kahana."
7. **Laulau oi herevalaia,** dia hakaua. "Inai bouti be lau tamagu ena bouti bamona."
8. **Puzzle oi rakatania noho.** Dina haida murinai oi giroa lou. Dia haraga.
9. **Nega namona ai oi dokona,** hapana ta ia lao neganai, moale ia noho neganai.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ia gwau: tau oi durua ia hamatamaia, badu oi itaia, bona ia ura ia itaia sibona bema unai ia moalelaia. Oi hapana oi atoa ia itaia bona gabuna ia hahedinaraia, unai danu be hebou gaukara.

## Online puzzle hahanaia

Tablet ai puzzle be namo dalana haida: hapana dia boio, laulau badadia screen ai, bona pata oi hagoevaia lasi.

Emai free [Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) ese laulau ta ia utua hapana 4 eiava 9 ai. Hapana rua oi patu, idia hurihaia, ela bona laulau ia maoro lou. Laulau maorona be screen ai ia noho, dala ia hahedinaraia; nega tauna dia noho, bona oi dokona diba nega iboudiai. Hapana 4 be namo dementia kahana negana ai, tau ta ia kahana noho negana.

Picture gaukara hahanaia momokani totona, [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) oi tohoa: laulau ta dorina ai ia noho, laulau hegeregerena oi patu. Eiava [Sort together](/games/sort-together/): laulau oi atoa basket ai, kumana ruma eiava gadena bamona.

## Hereva puzzle: hereva tahua bona crossword

Hereva puzzle be namo taudia idia laloa gunalaia, dementia matamana negana ai.

- **Leta badadia ida hereva tahua.** Emai free [Word search](/games/word-search/) ai leta badadia bona hereva maragimaragi, siri bona daudau sibona, dia diagonal eiava murimuri. Screen ai oi gaukaralaia eiava oi printaia.
- **Lista oi duahia.** Tau ta hereva ia duahia, ma ta ia tahua. Leta matamana sibona ia hekulaia be namo.
- **Leta badadia ida crossword** buku ai be namo matamana negana ai. Henanadai oi duahia, pen oi abia hebou, bona haere oi atoa hebou. Crossword hahanaia, hereva ta tauna ida, be namo, dia crossword auka momokani.
- **Hereva oi herevalaia.** "Gadena" ese tomato herevadia ia hamatamaia diba.

Pepa ai gaukara ma haida bona edena dala oi printaia itaia bona abia hahanaia totona, [free printable dementia gaukara](/activities/printable/) oi itaia.

## Nega edana ai puzzle oi hahanaia, eiava oi dokona?

Hekoda, hapana ia lulua lao, "Lau diba lasi" ia gwau lou, eiava ia lalohekwarahi, unai oi itaia. Unai be haidaua dalana ia hahedinaraia, dia goada karaia totona.

- **Oi ia hahanaia.** Hapana maragimaragi, eiava hapana gabudia sibona oi rakatania ia totona.
- **Oi gaukara momo oi karaia** bona kahana hahanaia ai ia durua oi noia.
- **Gaukara ma ta oi abia hidi:** hapana kala dainai oi atoa, online puzzle hapana 4, eiava [coloring](/games/coloring/).
- **Oi dokona.** Oi atoa, ti oi inu hebou. Dina ma ta oi giroa lou diba, eiava dia.

Puzzle ese ia hahedinaraia dahaka be auka, bema unai ia vara, oi rakatania lasi be namo. Nega namona hebou abia dalana momo ia noho; [101 gaukara idea](/activities/ideas/) ai oi itaia diba.
