Trivia be care home bona day program lalonai group gaukara badadia ta, bona ruma bese danu kitchen table ai ia gaukaraia. Bema umui karaia dalana gunana, point ida bona taunimanima ta sibona ese ia boka henia, dementia ida noho taunimanima ta be ia kamonai ia be test henia. Bema namonamo umui karaia, ia be sivarai kamonai dalana namo. Inai henunai 50 helai namonamo, abidadama helai ida bona hereva toana ta ta edia, gabeai edena bamona session ta umui karaia.

## Edena bamona trivia be dementia ida noho taunimanima ta dekenai ia gaukara namonamo?

Ia karaia bema ia be negai dokona amo dibaia gaudia abia bona lalohadai, bona boka ibounai be namo.

1. **Negai dokona gaudia helai.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) ese ia gwauraia, Alzheimer's ena badina matamata be harihari dibaia gaudia lalohadai ia auka. Lagani momo gunadia edia ane, hereva bona dina ta ta gaukara be ia namo.
2. **Abidadama helai hanaihanai henia.** Boka maoromaoroa abia rua eiava toi lalonai be ia namo, sibona amo bokana hamatamaia ia auka.
3. **Helai be gadona bada ida umui duahia bona umui hahedinaraia.** Whiteboard eiava tablet badana umui hahedinaraia, bema taudia ese ia kamonai ia auka.
4. **Boka be namonamo umui gwauraia.** Bema ta ese ia diba lasi, namonamo umui gwauraia bona umui lao. “Oi mai, oi dibaia” umui gwauraia lasi.
5. **Mark lasi.** Taunimanima ta edia lasi, table edia lasi. Bema taudia ese namba ia ura, group ibounai edia namba ta umui atoa.
6. **Taunimanima ta umui boiria lasi.** Taunimanima ibounai amo boka do ia mai. Bema umui rua sibona, umui hebou boka henia: “Lau laloa ia be piano. Oi dahaka oi laloa?”
7. **Hereva toana umui gaukaraia.** Boka be hamatamaia, dokona lasi. Sivarai be anina bada.
8. **Negana kwadogi umui atoa.** Helai 10, sivarai ida, ia namo.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ese taravatu badana ia gwauraia: hekwakwanai umui itaia, bona taunimanima ese ia kamonai ia be ia gaukara namonamo. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ese trivia ia atoa, murinai stage edia gaukara lalonai. Lalonai stage dekenai, abidadama helai rua umui atoa, bona hereva toana dekenai umui badinaia. Gabeai, inai henunai "ina eiava una" helai dekenai umui lao.

## "Ina eiava una" helai, kerere lasi

Inai helai be stage ibounai dekenai ia namo, murinai stage danu, badina kerere ta lasi. Umui helai, umui kamonai, bona emui boka danu umui gwauraia.

- Coffee eiava tea?
- Beach eiava ororo?
- Sisia eiava pusi?
- Laulau eiava kerukeru?
- Pie eiava cake?
- Danisi eiava itaia?
- Daba matana eiava adorahi?
- Ruma vairanai eiava ruma murinai?
- Radio eiava record?
- Pancake eiava nanigo huahua?
- Rose eiava daisy?
- Train lao eiava car lao?

Lada ida hereva hamatamaia totona, emai [Hereva hamatamaia kadi](/games/conversation-cards/) umui tohoa.

## Care home eiava group lalonai trivia session edena bamona umui karaia?

1. **Taudia umui hamatamaia bema idia itaia bona idia kamonai diba.** Hearing aid, glasses, TV do umui hakapua.
2. **Ia hamatamaia bema hereva momo.** “Lau ese negai dokona dekenai helai haida lau henia, lau laloa umui ese lau oi hereaia.”
3. **Helai ta ta be rua umui duahia bona abidadama helai badadia umui hahedinaraia.**
4. **Ruma ibounai amo boka umui abia dae.** Boka namodia umui lou gwauraia, bema ibounai idia kamonai.**
5. **Boka maoromaoroa be ibounai dekenai umui gwauraia,** gabeai hereva toana group dekenai umui henia.
6. **Taudia edia moale umui badinaia.** Bema helai ta ese minute 10 edia sivarai ia hamatamaia, umui lao ia lao.
7. **Dokona ai ane ta eiava "ina eiava una" umui karaia,** bema ibounai ese gaukara namo ta ida idia dokona.

Group gaukara ma haida totona, hegeregere picture bingo bona ane hebou, inai itaia: [care home edia group gaukara](/activities/care-home-groups/).

## Edena neganai dalana umui haidaua?

Bema ta be ia hekwakwanai, ia noho kunea, eiava "Lau diba lasi" ia gwauraia rua, ia hamonamonaia: abidadama helai rua umui atoa, umui hebou boka henia, eiava "ina eiava una" helai dekenai umui lao. Bema group ese helai ta sibona dekenai adorahi ibounai idia hereva, ia be namo. Anina badana be boka maoromaoroa lasi. Ia be negana namo, taunimanima namodia ida.
