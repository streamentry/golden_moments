Art bona mauri gaukara be taudia badadia be dementia ida toadia dalana ta henia idia edia lalo baine gwauraia, hereva maorodia davaria lasi. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, art gaukara ese [namona ia davaria bona gaukara badana ia henia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) bona tau ia ena hereva dalana ia henia, tau ia naria tauna bona dementia ida tau ta toadia. Gau badana be gaukara baine taudia badadia edia, baine maoromaoro, bona lalo hegege lasi. Inai lau ai gaukara haida, edena dala ai baine hegaegae, bona edena dala ai baine haidaua negana ia idau neganai ai herevalaia.

## Dahaka art gaukara be taudia badadia edia, to natudia edia lasi?

Taudia badadia edia art gaukara be art gaukara momokani, taudia badadia edia hereva, bona mataurai ida hereva ia gaukaralaia. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, gaukara baine taudia badadia edia, bona natudia edia dalana bamona ia itaia gaukara baine abia dae.

| Natudia edia dalana ia itaia | Taudia badadia edia dalana ia itaia |
| --- | --- |
| Glitter, foam sticker, googly eye | Watercolor pepa, dabua, au ia maragi |
| Natudia edia cartoon coloring lau | Gaden, quilt, ka, eiava tano lalona edia coloring lau |
| "Namo herea" hereva diba ta ta dekenai | Hereva momokani: "Inai blue be green ida ia namo herea" |
| Gaukara ta, gau ta do ia karaia lasi | Turana ta ena card, teibol ena au, henia gauna |
| Stadilai hereva bamona hadibaia | Boiboi: "Inai ai oi durua lau, a?" |

Tau ia ena ura lalonai. Bema ia needlework ia ura henia, dabua ia dogoatao ia ura henia diba. Bema ia engineer, gaukara badadia ida model kit eiava gaukara ta toana hardware abia hidi ia ura henia diba. Bema ia tisa gunana, card karaia ia ura henia diba.

## Dahaka dainai gaukara dalana ai laloa, to gaukara ia ore lasi?

Badina negana oi ruaosi noho be gau badana. Alzheimer's Association ena [ruma ai gaukara](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/5337a7c3-15c6-4b4d-a3a0-ca00a0839a6b/alzheimers-dementia-activities-at-home-ts.pdf) hadibaia ai ia gwau, gaukara dalana dekenai laloa, to diba lasi. Painting ta be "blue sibona" bema ia noho, ia be painting. Collage ai laulau toi bema ia noho, ia be collage.

Association ia gwau danu, art tau ta ia diba bada bema ia ena gaukara ia idau neganai ia lalo hegege diba, to art tau ta ia diba lasi ia be gaukara matamata dalana ta ia davaria diba baine ura henia. Bema tau ia be art tau diba, ia diba bada gauna idau ta abia hidi, unai bamona ia be gadara dalana, to hahetoho dalana lasi.

## Mauri gaukara haida baine tohoa

Gaukara ta ta ai, edena dala ai baine maoromaoroa bema ia auka.

1. **Watercolor karaia.** Watercolor pepa bada ta dekenai ranu atoa, murinai tau ia brush bada ida color baine haboua. Color sibona ia lao, unai dainai kerere lasi. *Maoromaoroa:* color rua sibona henia, eiava pepa korema dekenai ranu ida karaia, ranu ia itaia dalana.
2. **Magasin collage.** Gaden, aniani karaia, ka, lao, eiava tau ia ena ura gunadia dekenai magasin amo laulau abia hidi, ginigunai. Ia baine abia hidi bona card stock dekenai baine glue. *Maoromaoroa:* laulau hani abia hidi guna henia bona glue stick, murinai oi ruaosi glue.
3. **Au hururu.** Alzheimer's Foundation of America ia gwau [au bona laulaudia abia hidi](https://alzfdn.org/dementia-activity-gardening/) nega ena au hururu maoromaoro karaia. Vase metau bona gini ia noho abia hidi, bona au ramudia ginigunai abia hidi. *Maoromaoroa:* au haida cup maragina ai atoa, ta ta.
4. **Au maragi card.** Bouquet amo au abia hidi, wiki ta buki lalonai atoa baine maragi, murinai card stock ia lou dekenai glue. *Maoromaoroa:* oi glue atoa, ia au ia gogoa.
5. **Taudia badadia edia coloring lau.** Laini maoro bona hereva momokani ida coloring lau abia hidi. Ai emai [coloring lau](/games/coloring/) oi diba oi print eiava screen ai color atoa, color ta oi abia hidi, murinai laulau kahana ta. *Maoromaoroa:* kahana badadia, color haida lasi, marker badadia.
6. **Clay eiava dough malakamalaka.** Air-dry clay eiava modeling dough malakamalaka hururu, gogoa, bona karaia. Clay dekenai lau ta gogoa be tile maoromaoro ta. *Maoromaoroa:* gogoa bona hururu sibona be gaukara ta. Tau ia clay ia ania lasi baine naria.
7. **Card karaia.** Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, gaukara momokani bamona herevalaia: "Ai turana ta toana gorere card ai siaia. Oi durua lau, a?" Card stock abia hidi guna bona au eiava stamp sticker haida be namo.
8. **Scrapbooking.** Laulau gunadia edia kopi, postcard, eiava ticket stub abia hidi, album lau dekenai oi ruaosi atoa. Ta ta dekenai hereva. Ai emai [reminiscence hadibaia](/activities/reminiscence/) itaia, nega gunana dekenai maragimaragi hereva daladia toadia.
9. **Dabua bona auka board.** Corduroy, velvet, burlap, bona silk dabua haida card auka dekenai glue. Karaia be gaukara ta; gabeai dogoatao be gaukara ma ta. Daladia ma haida ai emai [sensory gaukara hadibaia](/activities/sensory/) ai.
10. **Au gaukara maoromaoro.** Bema tau ia au gaukara ia ura henia, au kahana malakamalaka ta sand eiava birdhouse kit ta paint ia karaia be ia diba gauna. Gaukara kakakaka abia dae bona naria.
11. **Musika ida paint.** Ia ura henia ane ta atoa, murinai ia ena lalo hegeregerena daladia eiava color paint. Inai be tau momo idia ura henia gaukara rua idia hebou. [Musika gaukara](/activities/music/) itaia.

## Edena dala ai art nega ta lau hegaegae?

1. **Ginigunai hegaegae.** Tau ia oi boiria lasi neganai, gau ibounai abia hidi, atoa, bona hegaegae. Oi sisisosi davaria neganai nega namona ia lao diba.
2. **Kahana sibona atoa.** Color rua eiava toi, brush ta, pepa ta. Abia hidi momo ese lalo ia haheauka diba.
3. **Teibol naria.** Plastic dabua eiava dabua gunana ta ese miro be lalo hegege lasi.
4. **Kahana ta ai noho.** Oiemu gaukara ta ia ena kahana dekenai karaia. Unai be hebou dalana, to naria dalana lasi.
5. **Ia durua baine hamatamaia.** Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, brush hamatamaia dalana oi tau ia ida oi karaia be gaukara. Ia hamatamaia murinai, tau momo idia lao.
6. **Oi gaukara neganai hereva.** Color bona auka dekenai hereva, eiava art ese sivarai baine havaraia. Henanadai hereva, hegeregere "Inai be dahaka?" baine gwau lasi.
7. **Nega daudau henia.** Gaukara ta be nega tamona ai do ia ore lasi.

## Edena dala ai art lau haidaua dementia ia idau neganai?

| Laulau | Dahaka be namo | Dahaka baine haidaua |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matamana | Klase, coloring lau bada, scrapbook, card karaia, laulau amo paint | Gaukara abia hidi henia; hebou ai karaia |
| Lalonai | Collage abia hidi guna, watercolor, au hururu, kahana badadia ida coloring | Karaia ta ta; abia hidi bona glue oi karaia |
| Gabeai | Brush lahi badadia, clay gogoa, dabua dogoatao, oi paint oi itaia | Auka dekenai laloa; ima maragimaragi durua bema ia ura |

NHS ia gwau, laulau gabeai neganai, gaukara be nega momo idia maoromaoroa bona auka dekenai idia laloa. Tau ma ta ia paint oi itaia, bona color dekenai hereva, be gaukara dalana momokani. Laulau ta ta edia daladia ma haida [laulau ibounai edia gaukara](/activities/ideas/) ai itaia.

## Art gaukara gauna edia naria

- Paint, marker, bona glue stick be dika lasi abia hidi.
- Sisisosi, craft naifi, bona hot glue gun abia dae, davaria lasi gabuna ai atoa. Abia hidi ibounai oi sibomu, ginigunai, karaia.
- Tau ta clay, paint, eiava gau maragidia ia ania lasi baine naria.
- Ranu bona vase toadia, gaudia metau bona gini idia noho abia hidi.

## Edana negai ai hadokoa?

Hegoada ia lao eiava lalo hegege ia matama neganai hadokoa. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau, hegoada ia maragi eiava lalo hegege ia tubu neganai gaukara baine hadokoa eiava baine haidaua. Gaukara namo gabuna ai atoa bona hanamoa gauna ta henia. Kerukeru oi giroa lou diba, eiava lasi. Ruaosi be namo.

## Gaukara ia karaia gauna hahedinaraia, bema tau ia ura

Painting ta fridge dekenai atoa eiava collage ta frame ai atoa be mataurai maragimaragi dalana ta. Ginigunai henanadai. Tau haida idia ura idia edia gaukara baine itaia; tau haida be lasi.

## Ai emai gadara ida gaukara heboua

Dina haida ai paint hegaegae be auka, [Coloring](/games/coloring/) be tablet ai gaukara, miro lasi. [Picture Puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) laulau ibounai ia itaia noho neganai oi kahana haida oi haidaua. [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ese henanadai maragimaragi idia henia, laulau amo sivarai ta baine hamatamaia diba. Idia ibounai ai taim eiava poin lasi. Oi diba danu ai emai [print gaukara](/activities/printable/) amo lau haida oi print.
