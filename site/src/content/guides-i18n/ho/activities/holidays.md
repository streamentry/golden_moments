Holiday negai laloatao namodia do idia giroa mai, bona regerege, taunimanima momo bona dina dinana haidaua danu do idia mailaia. Dementia tauna ida noho dekenai, holiday namodia be momo maragina, metau bona ia karaia diba bona ia moale diba gaudia ai idia haginia. Inai hereva be Thanksgiving, Christmas, Hanukkah, Mataura Matamata bona ruma bese havara dina dekenai idia gaukara diba.

## Holiday hegamegamea edena ai lau hegaua?

Tauna ena dina dinana gaudia amo emu hamatamaia, bona dina emu hegaua unai dekenai. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/6-steps-to-make-thanksgiving-dementia-friendly/) ia gwau moalelaia be daba matamata ai emu karaia, badina tauanina hevarivari bona heau helulu be adorahi dokona bona hanuaboi ai idia tubu momo (nege haida ai sundowning idia gwau). Holiday adorahi o brunch be hanuaboi aniani dokona toana mai hidiho.

- **Maragina namona emu abia.** Haboua maragina, hegamegamea, o vadivadi taudia be maragi maragi ai idia mai, be pata ta badana ai taunimanima momo toana mai hidiho.
- **Gaukara emu henia.** Ruma bese taudia emu noia kaikai idia mailaia, hohoi idia karaia, o tauna ida idia noho neganai oi nadua. Dahaka do ia durua emu gwauraia maoromaoro.
- **Gabuna gabuani emu abia hidiho.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) ia gwau kibi kikikiki be tauanina ai diba, bona loli o kaikai bamona gabuna gabuani be kaikai korikori idia laloa diba.
- **Dahaka emu rakatania emu abia hidiho.** Bema gaudia haida be tauanina heudahi o hemarai, emu rakatania o gaudia hegamegamea emu karaia be namo.

## Holiday gaukara dahaka ai karaia hebou diba?

Gaukara bona moale gaudia emu abia hidiho, harihari tauna ia karaia diba gaudia dekenai. Dina ai ia vareai be gaukara ena dika o namo toana mai bada.

1. **Pata emu atoa hebou.** Plate emu atoa guna, gabeai napkin, gabeai kapu. Napkin gigia be helai ai gaukara namona.
2. **Kaikai aniani emu huria bona emu atoa.** Potato huria o green bean bubu nadua pata ai be namo bona diba, taunimanima momo guna idia nadua taudia dekenai.
3. **Bubua, haboua o atoa.** Cranberry sauce bubua, kaikai maragi haboua o bisket plate ai atoa be ima idia gaukara, stove bona kaia amo daudau.
4. **Harihari emu koua.** Pepa bona teip emu utua gunalaia, bona tauna ia gigia ia hamalumalu. Koua namona be hereva badana lasi.
5. **Gabuna gabuani maragi emu atoa.** Gaudia bubu lasi emu abia hidiho, bona gabu maragi ta, hetohu wreath o diari maragi.
6. **Gaukara maragi emu karaia.** Pepa chain, au rau collage o holiday card be namo. Emai [arts and crafts dalana](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) ai hereva ma haida oi itaia diba.
7. **Ane emu ania bona emu kamonai.** Carol bona holiday ane diba be mai hidiho idia vareai, bema tauna harihari hereva maragi. Emai [music dalana](/activities/music/) ai playlist hegaua hereva oi itaia.
8. **Guna holiday ladana emu itaia.** Guna moalelaia album emu abia mai, bona sivarai emu noia ("Inai pata dekenai dahaka oi laloatao") toana, be daika be daika emu nanadaia lasi. Emai [reminiscence dalana](/activities/reminiscence/) ai edena dalana ia hahedinaraia.
9. **Grup game malumaluna emu gadaraia.** [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) be minit maragi ai emu printaia diba bona taunimanima iboudiai dekenai namo, bona [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) be hereva dalana idia henia.
10. **Hebou emu koloro.** [Koloro pepadia](/games/coloring/) mai hidiho emu printaia diba, natuna bona tubudia idia herevalaia hebou pata ai.
11. **Holiday movie ura gauna emu itaia.** Movie diba be hegamegamea, bona kahana sibona emu itaia be namo.
12. **Kibi itaiamu dala maragi emu lao.** Ruma gabuani dalanai ka motuka metau ai lao be event badana toana mai hidiho.

Bema nadua be tauna ena mauri ai gaukara badana, emai dalana [ruma gaukara be gaukara namodia](/activities/household-tasks/) ai nadua gaukara namo bona hegamegamea daladia oi itaia diba.

## Vadivadi taudia edena ai lau hamaoroa?

Vadivadi taudia emu hamaoroa idia mai lasi neganai, ruma bese nega daudau idia mai lasi taudia. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau emu hahedinaraia, laloa bona kara haidaua be gorere amo idia vara, tauna amo lasi.

- **Hereva maragi haida emu henia:** ta ta idia hereva, laloa ia dokona diba toana idia naria, idia gwauhamata o idia henanadai lasi, bona hereva ia auka bema, tauna ena hereva regena idia kamonai.
- **Dahaka ia durua bona dahaka ia haheudahi emu gwauraia.** Bema regerege bada, haraga haraga, o hereva haida idia haheudahi, emu gwauraia.
- **Vadivadi taudia emu hahedinaraia gunalaia.** Tauna dekenai idia do mai taudia edia ladana emu hahedinaraia bona sivarai maragi emu herevalaia, o dina lasi neganai phone o video hereva maragi emu karaia.

Emai [herevahereva herevadia](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ai hereva ma haida oi itaia.

## Bema ia heudahi bada, dahaka ai karaia?

Vadivadi taudia idia mai lasi neganai dautu rumana emu hegaua gunalaia, regerege amo daudau, gaudia tauna ia diba ida, hetohu blanket ura gauna, sweater o gaukara maragi. Nega ginigunana ai emu henia: "Ai lao gabu hegamegamea ai ai helai nega maragi?"

Pata amo lao, mahuta gunalaia, o dina kahana rakatania be dika lasi, namo. Bema tauna ia lalohedinaraia, hereva metau, ane diba o rumana ma ta ai lao be momo hereva hereva toana mai namo. Emai dalana [adorahi dokona heau helulu](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) ai hanuaboi dekenai hereva ma haida oi itaia.

## Ruma bese daudau idia noho edena ai idia vareai diba?

Video call amo ruma bese idia vareai diba, taunimanima momo idia habada lasi. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau call ai gaudia emu hegaua, hetohu holiday ane emu ania hebou, guna moalelaia ladana emu herevalaia, nadua hegeregere emu nadua hebou o holiday movie emu itaia hebou. Call emu abia maragina, bona ta ta idia hereva emu noia, badina hereva momo hebou be kamonai auka. Emai [phone bona video call dalana](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) ai daladia oi itaia.

Bema tauna be memory care ai ia noho, vadivadi daladia emu nanadaia bona staff emu noia idia durua call ai. Emai dalana [memory care ai vadivadi](/activities/visiting/) ai dahaka emu laohaia bona edena ai namonamo emu lao ia hahedinaraia.

## Harihari dahaka be dementia tauna dekenai namo?

Harihari mai hidiho, namo, harihari ia moale diba be namo momo: kaikai ura gauna, blanket o cardigan malumaluna, ane diba, kalenda leta badadia ida, o ruma bese ladana album. Alzheimer's Association ia gwau gaudia hemarai, elektronik hegegemadai bona game hegegemadai emu rakatania.

Henia be abia hegeregerena, badina be bada. Tauna ia durua bisket ipaketi ai, o harihari oi hoia gauna ia koua, unai amo ia vareai holiday ai. Ruma bese ladana game danu be dalana: [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) be ladana haida emu abia hidiho amo game malumaluna browser ai ia karaia.

## Oiemu danu emu naria

Holiday be naria gaukara ai ia habada. Ta ma ia gabu o turkey ia nadua, moale oi laloa lasi gaudia emu gwau lasi, bona taudia ma tauna ida idia noho neganai emu laga-ani. Bema oi o tauna oi naria ia gorere, driveway amo ima emu gabuani o video call be vadivadi namona. Emai dalana [naria tauna ena naria sibona](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ai durua ma haida oi itaia.
