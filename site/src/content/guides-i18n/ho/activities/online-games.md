## Dahaka dainai online geimu ta be dementia totona e namo?

Geimu ta be dementia ida noho taunimanima totona e namo, bema ia be picture e itaia bona e diba gaukara, to laloa gaukara lasi. Picture tamona rua match, shadow ida picture tamona match, o gau ta basket maorona ai atoa — inai be mata ese harihari e itaia gaudia dekenai e hagerea. Rule, hereva, namba o move gunana laloa geimu be lalohisihisi e havaraia diba.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ese e gwauraia, gaukara be taunimanima ese ia ura bona ia karaia diba gaudia dekenai e hagerea, bona durua lasi ai ia e kwalimu diba. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ese e gwauraia, bema karaia diba e haidaua, gaukara be e hamai namoa, to e atoa lasi.

## Golden Moments geimu, browser ai oi kehoa diba

Inai geimu ta ta be website amo e kehoa, download lasi. Momo be tablet o computer ai e gaukaralaia; haida be print diba.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Picture match rua badadia, picture e itaia noho, timer o score lasi. Geimu ginigunana totona e namo.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Picture ta dorina ai, bona picture tamona be option rua amo hani dekenai.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Shadow maragina ta dorina ai e hedinaraia; picture e havaraia tauna tou.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Picture ta tou, bena basket maorona ai atoa, hegeregere kitchen o garden.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Picture ta e pararaia pieces ai, bona picture korikorina be itaia noho, dalana totona.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Hereva diba haida, hereva dokona e lasi; option ta tou o dokona hahedinaraia.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Picture card haida, herevahereva totona, test lasi. Print diba danu.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Oiemu device ai oi abia hidi photo amo match rua e karaia. Photo be inai device ena browser lalonai e noho.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Hebou slow breathing negana maragina. Oi hamatamaia bona oi stop karaia.

Word search, coloring bona picture bingo be print diba gaukara ai danu e noho. Haida totona, [free printable dementia activities](/activities/printable/) itaia.

## Geimu negana edena oi hamatamaia

1. **Nega maragina abia hidi**, bema oi naria tauna e laga-ani. Adorahi kahanai be haida totona e auka diba, una dainai oiemu negana duahia.
2. **Screen badana gaukaralaia.** Tablet be stand ai, o computer browser e zoom karaia, be itaia bona tou diba mai namo.
3. **Tab ma haida koua bona notification off karaia.** Pop-up be e hagarerea diba bona negana namona e hadikaia diba.
4. **Oi naria tauna badina ai noho, ia vairana ai lasi.** Hebou itaia be ruma taudia bamona, test bamona lasi.
5. **Geimu ta amo hamatamaia, miniti 5 amo 10.** Oi naria tauna ese screen e tou o e gwauraia, bona umui ruaosi e heheni.
6. **Namo ai dokona karaia.** Round ta haorea, bena picture ta dekenai hereva o ti kapu ta herevalaia.

## Shared device ai privacy bona safety

- **Photo be device ai e noho.** Photo pairs be oi abia hidi photo e gaukaralaia, bona idia be inai device ena browser lalonai e noho. Daika be computer o tablet e kehoa diba, duahia.
- **External link bona ad dadaraia.** Geimu be site amo mo e gaukaralaia, bona banner o personal hereva e noia prompt oi tou lasi.
- **Payment bona account be ma ta.** Password o card namba e noia screen ai oi naria tauna sibona e noho lasi.
- **Raka lao negana naria.** Bema oi naria tauna ese tablet e abia lao diba, device be mai namo sekea.

## Inai geimu be dahaka e karaia diba bona dahaka e karaia lasi

Online geimu ese dementia ida noho taunimanima be negana namona e moalelaia, picture hebou e itaia, bona pressure lasi ai gaukara ta e karaia diba. Idia be dementia e hanamoa lasi, e slow lasi, o e taravatua lasi. Gau ta be [Federal Trade Commission ena 2016 action, Lumosity dekenai](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program), brain-training hereva be hahedinaraia lasi dainai. Geimu, app o website ta be laloa e hanamoa, kwarana e score, o decline e haidaua lou diba e gwau bema, naria.

Inai geimu be dina ta ena kahana ta, bona ane, raka, herevahereva bona laga-ani danu ida. Geimu edena calm ai gaukaralaia, to badu lasi, duahia totona [dementia ida noho tauna ida geimu edena gaukaralaia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) bona [test lasi memory geimu](/activities/memory-games/) itaia.

## Edana negai stop

Bema oi naria tauna e laga-ani, e nohobada lasi, e lalohisihisi o e namo lasi, stop. Bema ia ena ura e ore, stop. Nega kwadogina ai stop be namo. Dina ma ta oi giroa lou diba, o herevahereva o raka abia hidi.
