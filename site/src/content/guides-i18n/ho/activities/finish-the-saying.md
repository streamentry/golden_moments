Tok bado gemu ese diba tok bada ta ia gaukaralaia, bona ginigunana kahirakahira ia dokona. Taunimanima haida dekenai inai be moale bona metau metau dalana ta, nega ta oi hebou hebou ai karaia totona. Gemu inai ia gaukara, badina tok badodia be gunana bona diba gauna, bona taunimanima ese hari dina dekenai gaudia ia basine laloatao. Inai henanadai ese baine hahedinaraia edena dala oi baine goada, edena tok bado oi baine abia hidi, bona edena dala metau metau oi baine dogoatao.

## Tok bado gemu be dahaka?

Inai be gemu kwadogi ta. Taunimanima ta ese tok bada ta ena kahana baine duahia, bona ma ta ese ginigunana baine gwauraia. Hegeregere, “Actions speak louder than...” bona “The early bird catches the...” Taunimanima ese ginigunana baine gwauraia, baine poinia, eiava oi henia diba rua eiava toi lalonai ta baine abia hidi. Taunimanima haida ese tok badana kahana idia duahia diba ia moalelaidia.

Golden Moments ese inai [do davana tok bado gemu](/games/finish-the-saying/) bamona ia henia, letra badadia ida. Skrini lasi oi goada diba danu: tok badana kahana sibona oi gwauraia bona oi naria.

## Edena dala oi baine goada, hakapua hakapua

1. **Nega metau ta oi abia hidi** bona taunimanima ida mata hegeregere oi helai.
2. **Tok badana kahana oi duahia metau metau,** hereva namona ida, bona oi lasi. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) ese ia gwauraia, metau metau oi hereva bona hereva kwadogidia oi gaukaralaia.
3. **Oi naria.** Sekon haida oi henia. Diba ta ia lasi neganai, ginigunana rua eiava toi oi henia, ta be maorona.
4. **Diba ibounai oi abia dae moale ida.** “Oi, unai be maorona” eiava “Lau na laloatao lasi unai” ese hereva baine namo.
5. **Tok badana dekenai oi hereva.** Ia dekenai anina be dahaka, eiava edeseni ia kamonaia. Inai lalonai anina bada ia noho.
6. **Ia baine hekwarahi lasi neganai oi dokona.** Tok bado haida bona kiri ta be nega namona ta.

## Henanadai lasi, to diba oi henia dalana

Diba haida oi henia neganai gemu baine haraga diba, to henanadai bamona oi karaia neganai test bamona ia hedinaraia diba. Hereva dalana baine metau. “Ginigunana oi kamonai goada, a, eiava ta oi abia hidi?” oi gwauraia, “Edana be maorona?” lasi. Taunimanima ia diba neganai nega ta oi henia murinai, diba oi hahedinaraia.

Taunimanima ia lalohisihisi eiava ia hemarai neganai, gemu oi rakatania bona gaudia ma ta oi karaia. Emai [hereva henanadai](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ai oi itaia edena dala oi badilaia, hekwakwanai lasi.

## Taunimanima dekenai tok bado namodia oi abia hidi dalana

- **Taunimanima edia tok badodia oi gaukaralaia.** Ruma bese oi nanadaidia, edia tama eiava adavana ese dahaka tok badodia ia gwauraia noho. Maragi negai tok badodia be idia haraga diba.
- **Tok bado kwadogidia, dina ibounai gaudia dekenai oi abia hidi.** Guba, adava, gabu, ruma bese bona nega taudia dekenai tok badodia be diba gauna bona idia haraga.
- **Oi karaia maoromaoro.** Proverb haida ese hereva gunadia eiava sivarai auka ta idia ura neganai, idia haheauka diba. Idia moalelaidia taunimanima dekenai sibona oi gaukaralaia.
- **Gado oi itaia namonamo.** Idiom haida be gado ma ta dekenai idia basine anina maoro. Taunimanima ia diba gado ai tok badodia oi gaukaralaia, bema oi tahua matamata karana.

## Buka taudia hegeregeredia ai tok bado haida

Unai dekenai, ginigunana be ( ) lalonai ia noho, oi dekenai diba. Tok badana kahana sibona oi duahia, bena oi naria.

- **Guba:** “Rain before seven...” (ginigunana: “fine by eleven”); “Every cloud has...” (a silver lining).
- **Adava bona aniani:** “Too many cooks...” (spoil the broth); “Don't count your chickens...” (before they hatch).
- **Ruma bona ruma bese:** “Home is where the heart...” (is); “Blood is thicker than...” (water).
- **Nega taudia bona gabu:** “April showers bring...” (May flowers); “You reap what you...” (sow).

## Taunimanima ia basine diba neganai be dahaka?

Stag gabeai taunimanima haida ese hereva idia basine davaria diba. Tok badana oi basie rakatania. Oi duahia, oi kiri, bona imana, kwarana eiava laulau ta amo diba baine henia oi noia. Diba tok bada ta oi hebou hebou ai kamonai be nega hebou ta. Gemu oi heboua ane, ranu siahu ta, eiava imana amo helalo ta ida, hegeregere [stag gabeai kara](/activities/late-stage/) ai.

## Tok bado oi gaukaralaia dala ma haida

Tok badodia amo taunimanima edia mauri gunana dekenai hereva ia mai haraga. Oi diba henanadai gaukaralaia [laloatao henanadai](/activities/reminiscence-questions/) amo, eiava gemu hegeregere [hereva kad](/games/conversation-cards/) oi goada. Dina lalonai kara ma haida dekenai, [dementia taudia edia kara](/activities/ideas/) oi itaia.
