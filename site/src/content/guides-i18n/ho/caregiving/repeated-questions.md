"Dokta be edana ora ai?" Minuta 10 murinai: "Dokta be edana ora ai?" Henanadai ta be oi kamonai lou lou, unai be dementia tauna oi naria neganai gaukara dika ta. Unai be ia vara gauna, ia ura dainai lasi, bona maragi daladia oi karaia diba, unai be umui ruaosi dinami ba hanamoa.

## Dahaka dainai dementia tauna ia henanadai ta ia henia lou lou?

Dina badina be laloaboio. Dementia be laloaboio kwadogina ia hadikaia, unai dainai taunimanima be ia henanadai vadaeni o haere ia kamonai vadaeni be ia laloaboio lasi. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/alzheimers-today-volume-17-number-3/navigating-difficult-behaviors/) ia gwauraia henanadai momo ba henia lou lou be ia vara gauna bona ia vara diba.

Henanadai lalonai lalohekwarahi ta ia noho diba. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) ia gwauraia henanadai lou lou be lalohekwarahi, durua ura, o namo lasi kamonai ia hahedinaraia diba, bona taunimanima be namo kamonai bona ia manada gauna ura. "Ita lao edana neganai?" anina be "Lau lalohekwarahi lau lao dainai." Boredom, gari, regere bona dinana dalana kerere be unai ia havaraia diba, bona henanadai lou lou be ura ta ia hahedinaraia diba, hegeregerena toilet lao ura ([Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/)).

## Dahaka lau ba gwaurai?

Ia haere maragi bona kwadogi, ginigunana bamona.

- **Haere be kwadogina bona tamona ba hamatamaia.** "Dokta be hora rua ai. Lau be oi ida." Haere tamona be ia abia namo.
- **Lalohekwarahi ba haere.** "Oi dokta oi laloa. Ia be hari adorahi ai, bona lau ba driver. Oi gaukara ta ba karaia lasi."
- **Gado maragina bona mai maragi ba gaukaralaia,** bema ia ura. Alzheimer's Association ia hamaoroa maragi ba noho bona hereva badadia o heai ba gaukaralaia lasi.
- **Ia ura haere ba henia,** edana nega momo ia ura. Alzheimer's Association ia gwauraia ia henanadai negana ibounai, oi ba haere lou.

## Dahaka lau ba karaia lasi?

- **"Oi henanadai vadaeni."** [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) ia hamaoroa ia henanadai vadaeni ba hamaoroa lasi. Unai be ia laloaboio durua lasi, bona ia be ia hadikaia bamona ia kamonai diba.
- **Hereva daudau.** Hereva momo be laloaboio momo ia ura.
- **Lagu dika, mata kerere o gado dika.** [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) ia hamaoroa ba heauka bona badu ba hahedinaraia lasi. Unai be gaukara auka; nega maragina abia herevana be ia noho henuai.

## Gaukara namodia haere ba henia

Taunimanima be ia duahia diba neganai, ia itaia diba ravana ta be hereva amo ia durua namo diba.

| Gaukara | Edana dalana ba gaukaralaia |
| --- | --- |
| Revareva badadia ida ravana | "Hari dokta hora 2 ai. Mary ba driver." Ia itaia gabuna badana ai ba atoa |
| Whiteboard | Hari dina plan laini toi o hani ai; daba ibounai ba hamatamaia lou |
| Ora badana dina bona date ida | "Hari be edana dina?" bona "Negana vadaeni, a?" ia durua |
| Kalenda | Dina ibounai ba gwau; hebou gabena ba hahedinaraia |
| Ia manada ladana | Ia mai tauna ladana be "Daika ia mai?" ia haere diba |

Unai lalo herevadia be [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) bona [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf) amo. Mai maragi ravana ba hahedinaraia ("Ita board ba itaia hebou") "lau torea vadaeni" ba gwaurai lasi.

## Kara ba hamaoroa ia kahilakahila neganai

Hebou o lao ta be dina momo gunalaia ba hamaoroa neganai, unai be hora momo lalohekwarahi henanadai ba vara diba. Family Caregiver Alliance bona Alzheimer's Los Angeles umui ruaosi be plan, hegeregerena dokta itaia o digu, ia vara neganai sibona ba herevalaia. Gabeai dahaka be ia vara ba gwaurai, hatua ta ta.

## Gaukara namodia ba durua

Oi haere vadaeni bona lalohekwarahi oi kamonai vadaeni murinai, gaukara namona ta be durua diba, boredom be henanadai badina neganai. Lalo tauna ibounai be ia ura gauna ta ba hahedinaraia.

1. **Gaukara maragina ta umui karaia hebou.** [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) be ladana ibounai ia hahedinaraia, unai dainai gau ta ba laloaboio lasi.
2. **Ia manada gaukara ta.** Taulo ba koua, kaus ba atoa o pata ba hamatamaia. [Sort together](/games/sort-together/) be unai bamona screen ai.
3. **Ia ura gauna ba herevalaia.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) be haere maoromaoro lasi henanadai ia henia.
4. **Ane, raka o aniani maragina.** National Institute on Aging be unai gaukara maragidia ia gwauraia.
5. **Nega maragina ta.** Umui noho hebou, window amo ba itaia, o [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ba karaia hebou.

Bema ia henanadai ba giroa lou, mai namo ba haere lou. Unai be namo.

## Dalana ba itaia

Alzheimer's Association ia hamaoroa henanadai be edana negana, edana taunimanima o edana gabuna ai ia vara ba itaia. Bema ia mai taudia gunalaia neganai o adorahi ai ia vara, oi ba hegaegae diba: hebou herevana ravana, gaukara namona ta, gabuna maragina ta. Bema adorahi be ia auka bada, emai [adorahi maragi gaukaradia](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) ba itaia.

## Oiemu ba naria

Henanadai ta ba haere lou lou, namba tuenti ai, be ia hahisia. Bema oiemu heauka ia doko bona taunimanima be ia namo, minute ginigunana rumana ta ai ba lao bona ba lagu, [Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf) ia hamaoroa bamona. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) ia hamaoroa naria taudia be durua ba noia bona respite care ba laloa. Lalo hereva ma haida be [naria tauna sibona naria](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ai.

## Edana neganai dokta ba kolia?

Dokta ba kolia haraga bema henanadai, laloa kerere o kara ia kerere haraga, o hora o dina lalonai ia dika bada. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) ia gwauraia kara kerere haraga o haraga ia kerere, hegeregerena gorere o muramura kerere murinai, dokta ba hamaoroa haraga. [Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/delirium) ia gwauraia gorere, muramura, ranu lasi, hisihisi o constipation be delirium ia havaraia diba, unai be laloa kerere haraga ia vara haraga bona durua haraga ia ura. Bema ta be dika bada ai ia noho, 111 ba kolia.
