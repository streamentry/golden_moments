Taunimanima gado momo ia diba taudia be dementia ese edia hereva davana eiava dibaia ia haidaua neganai, edia hereva dalana ia idau diba. Haida be gado gunana ai ia diba ia gaukaralaia lou diba; haida be lasi. Durua namona ginigunana be nanadaia hari dahaka gado ia namo, bena taunimanima murinai oi raka.

[Alzheimer's Society ia gwau gado ura be ia idau diba bona taunimanima ia gaukaralaia gado ia gaukaralaia](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association danu ia gwau gado ginigunana dekenai ia giroa lou be hereva haidaua ta diba](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Inai haidaua be taunimanima ibounai dekenai ia vara lasi, bona taunimanima ena stage ia se hahedinaraia.

## Dahaka gado lau gaukaralaia?

Nanadaia inai hereva totona dahaka gado ia ura ia gaukaralaia. Ena ura be hereva gauna, gabuna, eiava dina negana dekenai ia idau diba. Bema ia gado ia haidaua, ia se hahenamo bamona eiava oi diba gado dekenai ia gaukaralaia lou totona se hadoa.

Bema ia ura gado oi diba, hereva momokani gaukaralaia bona ia haere henia neganai naria. Bema hereva karukaru sibona oi diba, momokani gwaurai bona toana, gau, eiava revareva danu gaukaralaia. [Alzheimer's Society ena gado hakaua hereva](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) ia gwau gado dibaia be dina ta ta ia idau diba, bona hesiku, hisihisi, eiava gorere ese ia hadikaia diba.

## Dina ta ta hereva lalonai dahaka gau ese ia durua diba?

Hereva namo daladia oi laloatao, bona taunimanima ena gado dekenai durua oi haboua:

- **Hereva momokani bona ladana gaukaralaia.** Taunimanima eiava ia abidadamaia tauna nanadaia, dina ta ta gaudia, ruma bese taudia, bona dala gaudia totona edia hereva dahaka.
- **Hahedinaraia bona gwaurai.** Kapu tualaia, ruaosi edia ura ta hahedinaraia, eiava revareva momokani eiava gau ta gaukaralaia. Bema hereva maoromaoro davana ia auka, toana ese ia durua diba.
- **Naria.** Taunimanima dekenai nega henia ia kamonai bona ia haere. Ena hereva ibounai se dokona eiava gado momo dekenai nanadaia tamona se gwaurai lou lou.
- **Lalo tamona sibona abia.** Hereva daudau be gado ibounai lalonai ia auka diba. Hereva kwadogi gwaurai, vadaeni, bena ia ura hereva ma haida ia kamonai, a, oi itaia.
- **Kamonai bona itaia namonamo.** Mata gini taudia, kamonai gaudia, diari namona, bona gabuna nois lasi ese gado kamonai ia hagoevaia diba.

Bema taunimanima ia ura ia duahia, nanadaia sivarai badadia ena gado ai ia noho, a. Medical laloa, abidadama henia, eiava muramura hakaua hereva totona automatic translation se abidadamaia lasi; sivarai badadia naria tauna ida oi hamaoromaoroa.

## Bema taunimanima ia ura gado lau se diba?

Nanadaia ia ura ena turana eiava rara tauna gado rua diba ta ia vareai dina hereva lalonai, a. Taunimanima ia ura ma tauna ia tok hedavaia oi se laloa lasi; ia ura toana, revareva, eiava gado ma ta umui ruaosi diba ia gaukaralaia.

Medical appointment eiava hereva ma ta sivarai maoromaoro ia ura neganai, klinik eiava naria gauna oi kontak henia ginigunai. Taunimanima ia ura gado hamaoroa bona dahaka tok hedava durua ia noho nanadaia. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services ia hahedinaraia](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) rara taudia eiava turadia be medical sivarai badadia maoromaoro tok hedavaia lasi diba; naria gauna ese ena dala bona gabuna ena taravatu ia hahedinaraia diba. Taunimanima sibona ia abia hidi durua tauna ia ida ia lao, to provider ese gado durua ia hegaegae.

Hereva neganai, taunimanima dekenai oi hereva bona tok hedava tauna ia hereva neganai nega henia. Taunimanima ia diba nanadaia bona ia ena hereva amo ia haere diba negana ia noho hamaoromaoroa. Bema oi ia durua, ia ura sibona neganai sivarai ma haida oi haboua.

## Edana negai lau dokta durua tahua be namo?

Hereva auka be nega ai ia haidaua diba, to haidaua haraga be ia idau. [NHS ia gwau taunimanima ta ia laulau haraga neganai harihari medical durua tahua](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Hereva ia ore haraga be dementia sibona ena dala oi se laloa lasi. Bema taunimanima ia laulau haraga, ia gorere bada, eiava ia mahuta auka, gabuna ai emergency medical naria taudia kontak henia.

Haidaua maragimaragi totona, oi itaia gaudia gwauhamata bona taunimanima ena dokta nanadaia. Dahaka gado ia gaukaralaia, dahaka ese ia durua, bona ia hesiku eiava ia gorere, oi torea. Inai be hereva totona sivarai, diagnosis lasi. Emai [dokta dekenai lao hakaua hereva](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ese nanadaia oi hegaegae diba.

## Durua taudia ma haida dekenai dahaka lau hamaoroa diba?

Taunimanima ena abidadama danu, ia ura taudia totona hereva kwadogi ta torea. Ena hereva bona duahia gado eiava dialect, hereva momokani eiava ladana, bona dahaka ese hereva ia hagoevaia ia gwaurai diba. Nanadaia ia ura tau be appointment eiava naria sivarai lalonai ia vareai. Durua totona ia ura lasi sivarai sibona se torea lasi, bona inai sivarai tau be ia itaia diba ia hamaoroa. Gado sivarai kwadogi ese hereva namonamo ia hamatamaia diba; to health hereva auka totona tok hedava tauna ia se abia vadaeni.

## Taunimanima ena ura ede lau durua diba?

Gado be taunimanima ena hebou dalana ta, to ia sibona lasi. Ena dala gaudia, ia ura gaudia, duahia ura, bona ia ura tau ia vareai nanadaia. Bema taunimanima ia abidadama, durua taudia ma haida totona hereva eiava hereva kwadogi namodia torea bona edia gaukaralaia dalana hahedinaraia. Sivarai oi naria namonamo bona taunimanima ia abidadama sivarai sibona oi hahedinaraia.

Taunimanima se hadoa ia hegaegae, ma haida totona ia tok hedava, eiava ia diba gaudia ia hahedinaraia. Hereva haida be gado ta ai ia hagoevaia, ma haida be gado ma ta ai. Taunimanima ena namo, ena sibona sivarai, bona ena ura be gado tamona sibona gaukaralaia totona badina bada.

Emai [hereva namo daladia](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ai hereva maoromaoro, kamonai, bona revareva gaukaralaia ai hereva ia noho. Diagnosis murinai hegaegae totona, [dementia diagnosis murinai ginigunana dala](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) itaia.
