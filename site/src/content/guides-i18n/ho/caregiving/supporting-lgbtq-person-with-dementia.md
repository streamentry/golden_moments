LGBTQ+ tauna ena identiti be ia be daika, unai ena kahana ta sibona. Dahaka ia laloa be mai anina, oi nanadaia, ia abia hidi hereva oi gaukaralaia, bona daika ese iena sivarai idia diba, unai ia sibona ia abi hidi. Tauna ena identiti, partner, family durua, o privacy urana basio laloa lasi ia itaia dalana, guna record, o daika idia vadivadi dekenai.

[Alzheimer's Society's guide to supporting an LGBTQ+ person with dementia](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/supporting-lgbtq-dementia) ese ia gwau, tauna ena ura oi nanadaia bona hebou dekenai basio laloa lasi. [Alzheimer's Association's LGBTQ+ dementia resources](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) ese danu stigma bona namo naria tahua dekenai hekwakwanai ia herevalaia. Taunimanima idia idauidau; unai lalo be herevahereva dalana totona, ta tauna ia dekenai hadibamu lista lasi.

## Edena bamona lau nanadaia ladana, pronoun, bona privacy totona?

Private bona malokihi negana ta oi abia hidi. Oi nanadaia diba, “Edena ladana bona pronoun oi ura ai gaukaralaia?” o “Emu identiti o hebou dekenai dahaka oi ura oi nariamu taudia idia diba?” Tauna ia haerelaia kahana ta, ia gwau lasi, o gabeai ia haerelaia lou diba. Iena naria dekenai ia noia lasi sivarai oi nanadaia lasi be namo.

Oi nanadaia, daika ese dahaka idia diba. Tauna ta ia ura ena partner ia dekenai gaukara taudia idia diba, to ena rami ia ura iena sexual orientation o gender identity iena family ma haida idia diba lasi. Gabu idauidau dekenai tauna ia ura hereva idauidau ia gaukaralaia diba. Oi diba sivarai, unai dainai sibona varavarana, vadivadi taudia, o gaukara taudia dekenai basio herevalaia.

Bema tauna ia ura ena urana idia record-ia, dahaka idia torea bona daika ese idia itaia, oi nanadaia. Bema tauna ia abia hidi ladana be record ma haida ida ia idau, provider oi nanadaia gaukara taudia ese herevahereva ai unai ladana idia gaukaralaia diba, eiava lasi. Tauna ena privacy oi laloa noho; naria ai idia durua taudia iboudiai ese sivarai ibounai idia diba be namo lasi.

Ura idia haidaua diba. Bema tauna ia hidi diba, oi nanadaia lou, moale gabu matamata o naria haida karana oi haheauka neganai. Bema ia ura dahaka ia herevalaia ia mai diba, basio laloa lasi. Guna ia gwauhamata dalana oi badinaia, bona naria abia hidi karana o sivarai herevalaia taravatu dekenai henanadai bema oi ura, gabu ai ia diba tauna ta oi nanadaia durua totona.

## Daika ese naria herevahereva ai idia vareai be namo?

Tauna oi nanadaia, daika ia abidadama henia bona ia ura idia vareai. Iena durua network dekenai adavana, partner, turana namona, varavarana, o “family tauna abidadama” idia noho diba — unai be taunimanima ia durua henia family bamona, ena be idia be varavarana lasi. Haida idia ura family dekenai, haida lasi. Tauna sibona ena hebou ia herevalaia dalana oi kamonai.

Gau namodia haida oi nanadaia: Daika idia vadivadi diba? Plan idia haidaua neganai, gaukara taudia ese daika idia phone henia be namo? Daika ese update idia abia diba? Bema provider ese gwauhamata o pepa ia ura sivarai ia herevalaia totona, dahaka ia ura oi nanadaia bona tauna dekenai dala oi hahedinaraia. Partner o turana abidadama be sibona dainai ia mai abia hidi karana lasi; taravatu be gabu idauidau dekenai idia idau.

Tauna ia ura hebou ladana oi gaukaralaia. Bema gaukara tauna ta ese partner ia gwauraia “turana” o “varavarana”, to tauna ia ura ia diba partner, unai ese tauna ia hahisia diba. Gwauhamata murinai torea sivarai maragina ta ese gaukara taudia ia dibaia edena bamona taunimanima idia introduced-ia bona dahaka sivarai idia herevalaia diba.

## Naria provider dekenai dahaka lau nanadaia diba?

Residential naria, ruma dekenai durua, o dina naria program oi hahegeregerea neganai, tauna oi nanadaia edena henanadai ia ura ia hatoa bona ia ura oi noho ida, eiava lasi. [SAGE guide to affirming services](https://www.sageusa.org/resource/10-tips-for-finding-lgbt-affirming-services/) ese ia gwau, personal sivarai provider dekenai herevalaia be haida dekenai auka diba. Provider oi nanadaia edia gaukara karana harihari idia herevalaia dalana namona ai.

- Edena ladana bona pronoun oi ura, edena bamona umui record-ia? Daika gaukara taudia ese unai sivarai idia itaia diba?
- Edena bamona tauna ia abi hidi daika idia vadivadi bona partner o durua tauna ma haida ida private nega ia abia?
- Rum, dabua, bona personal naria dekenai tauna ena urana umui edena bamona umui diba bona umui matauraia?
- Gaukara taudia ese LGBTQ+ tau badadia bona dementia dekenai edena training idia abia?
- Resident o durua tauna ese privacy o matauraia lasi dekenai hekwakwanai edena bamona ia hatoa diba, bona gabeai dahaka ia vara?

[SAGE and Human Rights Campaign Foundation guide to inclusive long-term care](https://www.sageusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/lei-consumer-guide-finding-lgbtq-inclusive-ltcc.pdf) ese ia gwau, vadivadi, rum, bona gaukara taudia edia policy idia torea gauna oi itaia. Ia be 2021 ai idia halasia; unai oi gaukaralaia henanadai totona, harihari gabu taravatu hereva lasi. Policy bona durua be gabu idauidau dekenai idia idau.

Logo, brochure, o haerelaia namona ta sibona ese dina iboudiai naria edena bamona, unai ia hahedinaraia lasi. Bema tauna ia ura ia vadivadi, oi itaia gaukara taudia edena bamona idia gwauraia bona ena partner o iena tau taudia abidadama dekenai edena bamona idia haerelaia. Bema unai ese tauna ia hagoadaia, gaukara tauna ta ida private ai herevahereva oi noia.

## Edena bamona lau durua henia tauna ibounai totona?

Tauna ena gau namodia, dina karana, gado, culture, abidadama, turana, bona community dekenai oi nanadaia. Iena identiti herevalaia be mai anina diba, o ia ura mauri kahana ma haida dekenai nega ia gaukaralaia diba. Vadivadi ibounai dekenai identiti sibona oi herevalaia lasi, bona LGBTQ+ taunimanima iboudiai ese lalo tamona idia abia, basio laloa.

Haida ese dadaraia lasi o dika idia davaria vadaeni, unai dainai durua gabu dekenai idia mataurai diba. Haida ese unai idia laloa bada lasi. Namonamo ai oi kamonai, tauna oi gwau lasi ia hahedinaraia o ia hahedinaraia karana ia gwauraia. Bema guna sivarai, foto, o stori ta ese ia hahisia, oi nanadaia ia ura ia dokona o hereva ia haidaua.

Bema oi be partner, turana, o varavarana, oi sibona emu durua danu mai anina. Tauna oi nanadaia edena bamona ia ura oi vareai, bona bema oi ura dala hereva, gabu ai dementia o LGBTQ+ tau badadia durua organization oi tahua. [Alzheimer's Association resource page](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) dekenai community bona caregiver durua idia hahedinaraia; dahaka ia noho be emu gabu dekenai ia depend.

Care home oi hahegeregerea neganai lalo ma haida totona, oi itaia [choosing residential dementia care](/caregiving/choosing-residential-dementia-care/). Dementia diba murinai plan karana dekenai durua totona, oi itaia [first steps after a dementia diagnosis](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
