Bingo ni umukino w'ubusabane umenyerewe ku bakuru benshi, ariko imibare ishobora kugora umuntu ufite dementia kuyikurikira. Bingo y'amashusho igumana injyana imwe yoroshye maze igasimbuza imibare amashusho manini kandi asobanutse. Ikora mu rugo, mu kigo cy'ibikorwa cyo ku manywa, cyangwa mu kigo cyita ku bakuru. Uru rupapuro rusobanura uko ikinwa, uko uyihindura, n'uko ukomeza kwibanda ku byishimo.

## Uko bingo y'amashusho ikora

Buri mukinnyi afite ikarita ifite umurongo w'amashusho, nk'ikibindi cy'icyayi, injangwe, roza, ubwato cyangwa igare. Uhamagara yerekana cyangwa avuga ishusho imwe icyarimwe. Abakinnyi bashyira ikimenyetso ku shusho ihuye iri ku ikarita yabo. Umukinnyi wujuje umurongo wose, cyangwa icyo itsinda ryemeranyijeho, ashobora kuvuga "Bingo," ariko amatsinda menshi asimbuka uwatsinze burundu.

Ushobora gukoresha amakarita yo gucapwa ari mu [bikorwa byacu bya ubuntu byo gucapwa](/activities/printable/), cyangwa [umukino wa bingo y'amashusho](/games/picture-bingo/), ufite uhamagara kuri ecran ku matsinda. Byombi bikora neza kuri tablet izamuwe ngo itsinda ryose ribone.

## Gutegura igihe cy'umukino

- **Hitamo itsinda rito.** Abantu bane kugeza ku munani ni ubunini bwiza. Amatsinda manini akurikirwa bigoye kandi ashobora kumera nk'uwihuta.
- **Shyira amashusho manini.** Koresha imirongo itomoye n'ikintu kimwe kimenyerewe muri buri kazu. Irinde amakarita yuzuye.
- **Wicaze abantu neza.** Shyira buri mukinnyi aho abona uhamagara n'ikarita ye, kandi hafi y'umufasha niba ayikeneye.
- **Koresha ibimenyetso byoroshye.** Uduhumbikano duto, ibiceri, cyangwa gukoza urutoki biruta ibikaramu. Bika bike by'inyongera.
- **Teganya igihe kigufi.** Iminota mirongo itatu akenshi irahagije. Hagarara hakiri kare iyo imbaraga zigabanutse.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) igira inama yo guhuza ibikorwa n'ibyo umuntu agishoboye, bityo ujye woroshya amakarita n'amategeko igihe cyose bifasha.

## Uko uhamagara amashusho

1. **Zamura ishusho** cyangwa uyerekane kuri ecran mbere yo kuvuga.
2. **Uyivuge neza,** mu magambo yoroshye: "Ikibindi cy'icyayi." Subiramo rimwe, buhoro.
3. **Tegereza.** Tanga igihe cyo kureba no gushyira ikimenyetso. Ntuhamagare ishusho ikurikiyeho kugeza abenshi bagize uburyo.
4. **Sobanura, niba bifasha.** "Ururabo rutukura" cyangwa "iryo twagize ku munsi wo gusangirira hanze" bishobora gutangiza ikiganiro.
5. **Komeza umuvuduko woroshye.** Uhamagara utuje ni we w'ingenzi kurusha uwihuta.

## Gutuma gutsinda biba amahitamo

Guhatana bishobora guteza ikibazo. Umuntu uri gutsindwa ashobora kumva akorerwa ikizamini, kandi umuntu uhangayikishijwe no gutsinda ashobora gutera impagarara. Dore ubundi buryo:

- **Buri wese atsindira ishusho.** Buri mukinnyi avuga ishusho imwe akunda, maze itsinda rikayishima.
- **Musangire ikarita yuzuye.** Saba abakinnyi kugira icyo bavuga ku shusho imwe iri ku ikarita yabo.
- **Mukine kugeza amashusho ashize.** Hamagara amashusho yose ari ku ikarita, hanyuma utangire indi, nta wiyitirira kuba uwa mbere watsinze.
- **Komeza impano ntoya cyangwa uyisimbuke.** Agakeke cyangwa indirimbo biruta igikombe.

## Guhuza ku bantu bari mu byiciro bya nyuma

Ku bantu bagorwa no kubona ishusho, koresha ikarita ifite uduce dune cyangwa dutandatu gusa, cyangwa uhe buri mukinnyi ikarita imwe icyarimwe. Reka umufasha afate ikarita umuntu yerekana, cyangwa wimure buhoro agashumi ke ubimwemereye. Bamwe bibona byoroshye kumva gusa uhamagara no kwishimira ijwi ry'amagambo.

Reba ibimenyetso by'ubwoba, nko kwikura inyuma cyangwa guhumura, kandi uhagarare iyo bigaragaye. Ku bindi bitekerezo ku bikorwa bituje byo kwicara, reba [ibikorwa byo mu cyiciro cya nyuma](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo mu bigo byita ku bakuru no mu bigo byo ku manywa

Ibikorwa by'itsinda bikora neza iyo bitegurirwa abantu bari mu cyumba. [Inyandiko yacu ku bikorwa by'amatsinda mu bigo byita ku bakuru no mu bigo byo ku manywa](/activities/care-home-groups/) isobanura uko utegura igihe, wicaza abantu, kandi ugenzura abashaka kugenda. Bingo y'amashusho kandi ijyana neza no kuririmbana cyangwa inkuru, na [kurangiza imvugo](/activities/finish-the-saying/) nk'umukino utuje nyuma mu gihe.

## Icyo bingo ishobora gukora n'icyo itashobora

Bingo y'amashusho ni iy'ubusabane kandi inezeza. Ishobora guhuza abantu, igatanga impamvu yo kureba amashusho no gutega amatwi, kandi igatuma itsinda rigira urugwiro. Ntivura dementia kandi ntitinza uko igenda. Gupima intsinzi yayo ukurikije uko abantu basa kandi bumva muri iyo saha, si uwatsinze.
