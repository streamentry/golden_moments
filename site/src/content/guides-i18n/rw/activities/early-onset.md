Amakuru menshi kuri dementia atekereza ko umuntu afite imyaka mirongo irindwi cyangwa mirongo inani. Ku muntu wasuzumwe afite imyaka mirongo ine cyangwa mirongo itanu, ubuzima bwa buri munsi buratandukanye. Ashobora kuba agifite akazi, umwenda w'inzu, ingimbi n'abangavu mu rugo cyangwa umubyeyi wo kwitaho. Ibikorwa bikwiriye kubahiriza ubwo buzima bw'abantu bakuru. Uru rupapuro rusobanura ibihinduka, ibyo wagerageza, n'aho abashakanye n'abana bakuru bashobora kubona ubufasha.

## Dementia itangira kare ni iki?

Dementia itangira kare ni dementia itangira mbere y'imyaka 65. Rimwe na rimwe bayita itangira hakiri kare. [Urubuga rwa Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) rusobanura ko Alzheimer ifatwa nk'itangiye kare iyo ifata umuntu uri munsi y'imyaka 65, kandi rukavuga ko kwita ku muntu ukiri muto bityo "bizana ibibazo byihariye." Ibimenyetso bishobora kuba bimwe n'ibyo mu busaza, ariko ibyo umuntu yiteze, inshingano ze n'ubuzima bwe bwo gusabana ntibimeze kimwe.

Kubera ko itaba kenshi, abantu bashobora gutegereza igihe kirekire ngo basuzumwe, kandi imiryango ishobora kumva nta wo kuganira na we. Kumenya ko abandi bantu basangiye uko bimeze akenshi biruhura.

## Komeza ubukuru bw'umuntu mu gikorwa

Abakuze bafite dementia itangira kare akenshi ntibashaka gufatwa nk'abana. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) igira inama yo guhuza ibikorwa n'ibyo umuntu agishoboye kandi yishimira. Ku muntu ufite imyaka mirongo itanu, bishobora gusobanura:

- **Komeza icyo akunda gukora,** nk'ububaji, siporo, amakarita, itsinda ry'ibitabo cyangwa korari, afashijwe gato cyangwa mu gihe kigufi.
- **Mugende n'amaguru cyangwa ku igare n'umuherekeza,** mu nzira azi neza.
- **Teka ifunguro ry'umuryango,** umuntu akora ibice ashoboye.
- **Fasha cyangwa ukore ku bushake** mu nshingano yihitiyemo, nko gutondekanya impano cyangwa gufasha ku munsi mukuru w'ishuri.
- **Tegura urugendo** ahantu hamufitiye akamaro, hamwe n'umuherekeza na gahunda yoroshye.
- **Mumarane igihe n'abuzukuru, abana b'abavandimwe,** musangira umukino cyangwa umushinga.

Mureke umuntu ahitemo. Kubaza uti "Wakwishimira gukora iki muri iki gicamunsi?" biruta kumuhitiramo.

## Koresha umuziki n'ibyibutso byo mu myaka ye

Umuziki n'amafoto byo mu myaka y'ubusore, imyaka mirongo itatu n'imyaka mirongo ine bishobora kuzana ibyiyumvo byiza n'ikiganiro cyoroshye. Inyandiko yacu ku [bikorwa by'umuziki](/activities/music/) isobanura uko wakora urutonde rw'indirimbo, naho [ibikorwa byo kwibuka](/activities/reminiscence/) bigaragaza uko musangira amafoto n'inkuru nta kubazwa nk'ikizamini. Ku mukino w'abakuze kurushaho, gerageza [amakarita y'ikiganiro](/games/conversation-cards/), afite ibibazo bifunguye ku kazi, ingendo, incuti n'umuryango.

Imikino y'amafoto ahuje ikozwe mu mafoto y'umuryango na yo ishobora gukora neza, kuko amashusho ari ay'abantu n'ahantu bifitiye umuntu akamaro. [Amafoto ahuje](/games/photo-pairs/) abika amafoto muri mushakisha kuri icyo gikoresho.

## Akazi, gutwara imodoka n'ubuzima bwa buri munsi

Abantu benshi bafite dementia itangira kare baracyakora, batwara imodoka cyangwa bayobora amafaranga. Ibi si ibikorwa mu buryo busanzwe, ariko bigena icyo umuntu ashobora gukora. Ganira na muganga we ku mutekano wo gutwara imodoka no ku kazi, kandi utekereze niba ashaka ubufasha ku mafaranga cyangwa ku bibazo by'abaganga. Intego ni ukurinda icyubahiro, mukomeza umuntu kugira uruhare uko abishaka.

Ku nama ku mikorere ya buri munsi no gukomeza gukora, reba [gahunda yoroshye ya buri munsi](/activities/daily-routine/) n'[imirimo yo mu rugo nk'ibikorwa](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Ubufasha ku bashakanye no ku bana bakuru

Abashakanye n'abana bakuru akenshi bumva bonyine. Kwita ku muntu bishobora kumera nk'akazi ka kabiri hejuru y'akazi n'umuryango. Inyandiko ya blog ya Alzheimer's Association ivuga ko abafasha bo mu matsinda y'abafite dementia itangiye kare babonye atabafasha ku bibazo bifatika nko gutegura ibyerekeye amategeko, no kumva gusa ko basobanuriwe. Inavuga ko uyu muryango ufite amatsinda y'ubufasha ku buntu ku bafasha, harimo amatsinda y'abafasha umuntu ufite dementia itangiye kare, kandi atatu muri yo ahurira kuri murandasi ku wa kabiri no ku wa gatatu.

Kubaza ku matsinda, hamagara umurongo w'ubufasha w'uyu muryango ukora amasaha 24 ku munsi, buri munsi, kuri 800.272.3900. [Inyandiko yacu yo kwiyitaho ku bafasha](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ivuga ku kuruhuka, ubufasha bw'agateganyo n'ibimenyetso byo kunanirwa.

## Ibyiciro biracyafite akamaro

Icyiciro cya dementia cy'umuntu kiracyagena icyamufasha. Mu ntangiriro, abantu benshi bashobora gukora ibikorwa byabo bisanzwe bahinduyeho bike, kandi inyandiko yacu y'[ibikorwa byo mu cyiciro cy'itangiriro](/activities/early-stage/) ifite ibindi bitekerezo. Nyuma, ibikorwa biba byoroshye kandi bikoresha imyumvire kurushaho, nk'uko bisobanurwa mu [bikorwa byo mu cyiciro cya nyuma](/activities/late-stage/). Muhindure igikorwa uko umuntu ahinduka, kandi mumubaze icyo ashaka igihe cyose ashobora kukivuga.
