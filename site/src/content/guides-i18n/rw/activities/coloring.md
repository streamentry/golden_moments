Gusiga amabara ni kimwe mu bikorwa byo guhanga byoroshye cyane wakwegera umuntu ufite Alzheimer cyangwa ubundi bwoko bwa dementia. Nta gusoma, nta kwibuka kandi nta gisubizo gikwiriye bikenerwa. Amaboko aragenda, amabara arasohoka, kandi hari ishusho yo kuganiraho. Iyi nyandiko isobanura uko wahitamo impapuro, ibyo gutegura, n'uko musiga amabara hamwe nta kubigira nk'iby'abana cyangwa nk'ikizamini.

## Impamvu gusiga amabara bikwiriye abantu benshi bafite dementia

Gusiga amabara biha amaboko icyo gukora n'amaso icyo akurikira. Bishobora gukorwa wicaye ku meza cyangwa ku ntebe iruhande rw'idirishya, kandi biroroshye kubitangira no kubihagarika. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ivuga ko ubugeni ari uburyo bwo kugaragaza ibyiyumvo no kwishimira ubu, kandi ivuga ko budasaba ubuhanga bw'ubugeni.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) igira inama yo guhitamo ibikorwa bihuye n'ibyo umuntu agishoboye, no kubihindura uko ubushobozi buhinduka. Gusiga amabara biroroshye guhindura: ishusho nini, ibikaramu binini, amabara make, cyangwa ecran yo gukoraho amabara.

## Hitamo ibishushanyo by'abakuru

Abakuze akenshi ntibakunda amashusho y'abana. Shakisha:

- **Amashusho y'ibidukikije:** ubusitani bufite indabo nini, ikiyaga gifite ubwato, inyoni ku ishami.
- **Ibintu bimenyerewe:** ikibindi cy'icyayi, igare, imbuto, ingofero.
- **Imiterere minini yoroshye:** mandala n'imiterere y'ibigabanyo ifite ibice bike binini.
- **Amashusho yacapwe cyangwa ahantu** umuntu ashobora kumenya mu hantu akunda cyangwa ku muhanzi akunda.

Irinde impapuro zifite ahantu hato huzuye, hagoye kuzuza. Impapuro z'abakuru zicapwa ni amahitamo meza, kandi [ibikorwa byacu bya ubuntu byo gucapwa ku bafite dementia](/activities/printable/) birimo impapuro zifite inyuguti nini.

## Ibyo gutegura

- **Ibikoresho binini byoroshye gufata.** Ibikaramu binini, ibyuma binini by'amabara, cyangwa ibikaramu by'amabara bifite aho gufata hanini biroroshye kurusha amakaramu yoroheje.
- **Amabara make.** Amabara atatu cyangwa ane arahagije. Amahitamo menshi ashobora kuvuna umutwe.
- **Urupapuro rukomeye.** Urupapuro rworoshye rurashishimuka kandi rukanyerera. Rekeresha impande cyangwa ukoreshe agakaratasi gafata.
- **Ameza afunguye.** Kuraho akajagari kandi ushyireho urupapuro rumwe icyarimwe.
- **Ikintu cyo gukoza cyangwa kuvanga,** nk'agatambaro cyangwa sponge, ku ngaruka yoroshye.

## Gusiga amabara kuri tablet

[Umukino wo gusiga amabara](/games/coloring/) wa Golden Moments ukora ukoraho ibara hanyuma ugakoraho igice cy'ishusho. Nta buhanga bw'intoki bukenerwa, bityo ushobora gukwira abantu bafite imbaraga z'ukuboko zagabanutse. Icara iruhande rw'umuntu kugira ngo musangire ecran, kandi umureke ahitemo amabara. Zimya amatangazo kugira ngo ishusho ikomeze kuba intego.

## Uko musiga amabara hamwe

1. **Mutangire igikoma cya mbere hamwe.** Fata ikaramu na we, cyangwa ushyire ukuboko kwawe ku rwe ku bikoma bike, hanyuma ureke.
2. **Muganire ku mabara n'ahantu.** "Iryo ni ubururu bwiza. Wabonye he amazi afite iryo bara?" ritumira ikiganiro nta kubaza nk'ikizamini.
3. **Shimira amahitamo, si isuku.** "Wahisemo amabara ashyushye cyane" biryoha kurusha "Wasigiye mu mirongo."
4. **Mureke ayobore.** Niba ashaka kuzenguruka urupapuro rwose, mureke.
5. **Hagarara iyo ahagaze.** Shyira ibikaramu hasi, maze utange agakombe k'icyayi cyangwa urugendo.

## Guhuza uko dementia ihinduka

Mu cyiciro cy'itangiriro, abantu akenshi bishimira impapuro zirambuye kandi bashobora guhitamo amabara n'ibishushanyo byabo. Mu cyiciro cy'hagati, amashusho manini n'amahitamo make arafasha. Mu byiciro bya nyuma, gerageza ishusho imwe nini, urupapuro rufite amabara yoroheje, cyangwa gufata ikaramu gusa mu gihe uvugiriza injyana. Bamwe bishimira kureba ishusho yarangiye kurusha kuyikora.

Ku bindi ku kazi ko guhanga mu byiciro byose, reba [ubugeni n'ubukorikori ku bakuru bafite dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) n'[imishinga y'ubugeni ku bantu bafite dementia](/activities/art-projects/).

## Igihe cyo guhagarara

Hagarara iyo umuntu arakaye, asunika urupapuro, cyangwa asa nunaniwe. Gusoza nyuma y'iminota ibiri ni intsinzi niba yarishimye. Ushobora gusubira nyuma, cyangwa ugerageza ishusho itandukanye cyangwa ikindi gikorwa nka [gukinisha imyumvire](/activities/sensory/).
