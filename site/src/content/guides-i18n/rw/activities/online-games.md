Imiryango myinshi ishakisha imikino ya ubuntu yo kuri murandasi ku bafite dementia kuko ishaka ikintu cyo gukorana kidakeneye iduka, kwinjira cyangwa ikarita y'ubwishyu. Amahitamo meza ni ayoroshye, ashingiye ku mashusho, kandi yoroshye guhagarika. Agomba kumera nk'akanya gasangiwe, aho kuba ikizamini. Uru rupapuro rusobanura icyo ugomba gushaka, uko utegura umukino, n'ibyo iyi mikino ishobora gukora n'ibyo itashobora.

## Ni iki gituma umukino wo kuri murandasi ukwiriye dementia?

Umukino ukwiriye abantu benshi bafite dementia iyo usaba kumenya aho gusaba kwibuka. Guhuza amashusho abiri amwe, gukoza ku shusho ihuye n'igicucu, cyangwa gushyira ikintu mu mbehe iboneye byose bishingira ku byo amaso abona ubu. Imikino ishingira ku kwibuka itegeko, ijambo, umubare cyangwa intambwe yabanje ikunda guteza umujinya.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) irasaba ibikorwa bishingira ku byo umuntu akunda kandi agishoboye, kandi bimureka agatsinda nta bufasha. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) igira inama yo koroshya igikorwa aho kukireka iyo ubushobozi buhindutse.

## Imikino ya Golden Moments ushobora gufungura muri mushakisha

Buri mukino ukurikira ufungurirwa ku rubuga nta gukuramo. Myinshi ikinirwa kuri tablet cyangwa mudasobwa; imwe ishobora no gucapwa.

- **[Amashusho ahuje](/games/picture-pairs/).** Amapaji manini ahuje afite amashusho agaragara, nta gihe kandi nta manota. Mwiza ku mukino wa mbere.
- **[Shaka rimwe](/games/find-the-same/).** Ishusho imwe hejuru, n'iyo imwe mu mahitamo abiri kugeza kuri ane hepfo.
- **[Guhuza igicucu](/games/shadow-match/).** Igicucu cyoroshye kigaragara hejuru; kandaho ishusho igikora.
- **[Gutondekanya hamwe](/games/sort-together/).** Kandaho ishusho, hanyuma ku mbehe iyijyamo, nk'igikoni cyangwa ubusitani.
- **[Puzzle y'ishusho](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Ishusho icitsemo ibice kandi ishusho yose igaragara nk'icyerekezo.
- **[Rangiza imvugo](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Imvugo zimenyerewe zibura ijambo rya nyuma; kandaho ihitamo cyangwa werekane iherezo.
- **[Amakarita y'ikiganiro](/games/conversation-cards/).** Amakarita y'amashusho afite ibibazo bifunguye byo kuganira, si ibyo kubaza nk'ikizamini. Na yo arashobora gucapwa.
- **[Amafoto ahuje](/games/photo-pairs/).** Amapaji ahuje akozwe mu mafoto uhitamo ku gikoresho cyawe. Amafoto aguma muri mushakisha kuri icyo gikoresho.
- **[Guhumeka utuje](/games/calm-breathing/).** Akanya gatuje, gahoro ko guhumeka hamwe. Ukatangiza kandi ukagahagarika.

Gushaka amagambo, gusiga amabara n'ishusho ya bingo na byo biboneka nk'ibikorwa byo gucapwa. Reba [ibikorwa bya ubuntu byo gucapwa ku bafite dementia](/activities/printable/) kugira ngo ubone ibindi.

## Uko utegura igihe cy'umukino

1. **Hitamo igihe gituje** umuntu aruhutse. Nimugoroba hakunda kugorana ku bantu bamwe, bityo banza urebe uko bimeze kuri wowe.
2. **Koresha ecran nini.** Tablet iri ku gihagararo, cyangwa mudasobwa ifite mushakisha yagurishijwe, byoroshye kubona no gukoraho.
3. **Funga izindi tab kandi uzimye amatangazo.** Amatangazo yigaragaza gitunguranye arakangura kandi ashobora gusoza igihe cyiza.
4. **Icara iruhande rw'umuntu, atari imbere ye.** Kureba hamwe bimera nk'ubusabane, bidasa n'ikizamini.
5. **Tangira n'umukino umwe wo mu minota itanu kugeza ku icumi.** Mureke umuntu akoreho cyangwa yerekane mutangana.
6. **Musoze neza.** Mwuzuze icyiciro, maze muganire ku shusho imwe cyangwa utange icyayi.

## Ibanga n'umutekano ku bikoresho bisangiwe

- **Amafoto aguma ku gikoresho.** Amafoto ahuje akoresha amafoto uhitamo, kandi aguma muri mushakisha kuri icyo gikoresho. Reba abandi bashobora gufungura mudasobwa cyangwa tablet.
- **Irinde imiyoboro yo hanze n'amatangazo.** Koresha imikino ukoresheje urubuga ubwarwo, kandi ntukande ku matangazo cyangwa ku byo bagusaba amakuru bwite.
- **Gutandukanya ubwishyu n'amakonti.** Ntusige umuntu wenyine ku rupapuro rusaba ijambo ry'ibanga cyangwa nomero y'ikarita.
- **Witondere kuzerera.** Bika tablet aho itagerwa niba umuntu ashobora kuva mu nzu ayijyanye.

Ku rutonde rwagutse, reba [uko wahitamo porogaramu n'imikino ku bafite dementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) na [gutegura tablet cyangwa telefoni](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Icyo iyi mikino ishobora gukora n'icyo itashobora

Imikino yo kuri murandasi iha abantu bafite dementia amahirwe yo kwishimira akanya, kureba amashusho hamwe, no kugira uruhare mu gikorwa nta gitutu. Ntivura dementia, ntiyiyoroshya kandi ntiyirinda. Urugero rwavuzwe cyane ni [igikorwa cya Federal Trade Commission cyo mu 2016 kuri Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) ku byo yavugaga ku myitozo y'ubwonko bidashobora gushyigikirwa. Witondere umukino, porogaramu cyangwa urubuga rwose rwemeza kunoza kwibuka, rupima ubwonko bwawe cyangwa rwemeza ko rushobora guhindura ukwiyongera kw'indwara.

Koresha iyi mikino nk'igice kimwe cy'umunsi urimo n'umuziki, ingendo, ikiganiro no kuruhuka. Ku bindi bisomwa ku buryo imikino iba iruhutse aho kuba itera impagarara, reba [uko wakina imikino n'umuntu ufite dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) n'[imikino yo kwibuka itagerageza](/activities/memory-games/).

## Igihe cyo guhagarara

Hagarara iyo umuntu asa nunaniwe, utuje, arakaye cyangwa atameze neza. Hagarara iyo atakigira ubushake. Guhagarara hakiri kare ni ibisubizo byiza. Ushobora guhora usubira ikindi gihe, cyangwa ugahindura ukajya mu kiganiro cyangwa mu rugendo.
