Umukino wo kurangiza imvugo ukoresha interuro imenyerewe ihagarara mbere gato y'ijambo ryayo rya nyuma. Ku bantu benshi ni uburyo bwiza, budafite igitutu, bwo gusangira akanya. Umukino ukora kuko amagambo ari aya kera kandi azwi neza, kandi umuntu ntakeneye kwibuka ikintu cyo muri iki gihe. Uru rupapuro rusobanura uko wakina, uko wahitamo imvugo, n'uko wakomeza ibintu biruhukije.

## Umukino wo kurangiza imvugo ni uwuhe?

Ni umukino mugufi aho umuntu umwe asoma igice cya mbere cy'imvugo, undi akuzuza iherezo. Ingero zirimo "Ukuri guca mu ziko ..." no "Ak'imuhana kaza imvura ...". Umuntu ashobora kuvuga iherezo n'ijwi riranguruye, kurimenyesha n'ikimenyetso, cyangwa guhitamo mu mahitamo abiri cyangwa atatu utanze. Bamwe bishimira kuba ari bo basoma igice cya mbere.

Golden Moments itanga uyu mukino nk'[umukino wo kurangiza imvugo wa ubuntu](/games/finish-the-saying/) ufite inyuguti nini. Ushobora kandi kuwukina nta ecran, ukavuga gusa igice cya mbere ugategereza.

## Uko wakina, intambwe ku yindi

1. **Hitamo akanya gatuje** maze wicare ku rwego rw'amaso, urebana n'umuntu.
2. **Soma igice cya mbere buhoro,** mu ijwi rishyushye, maze uhagarare. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) igira inama yo kuvuga buhoro no gukoresha interuro ngufi kandi zoroshye.
3. **Tegereza.** Tanga amasegonda make. Iyo nta kiza, tanga iherezo abiri cyangwa atatu, kimwe muri byo cyiza.
4. **Shimira igisubizo icyo ari cyo cyose.** "Yego, ni ryo" cyangwa "Ntabwo nari mbitekereje" bituma bikomeza kuba byiza.
5. **Muganire ku mvugo.** Mubaze icyo isobanura kuri we, cyangwa aho yayumvise. Aha ni ho agaciro nyako kari.
6. **Mwihagarare mbere y'uko ananirwa.** Imvugo nke n'ubuseke ni igikorwa cyuzuye.

## Gutanga amahitamo utabaza nk'ikizamini

Amahitamo y'iherezo ashobora koroshya umukino, ariko ashobora no kumera nk'ikizamini iyo atanzwe nk'inshingano. Komeza imvugo yoroshye. Vuga uti "Wakwifuza kumva iherezo cyangwa kurihitamo?" aho kuvuga uti "Ni irihe?" Tanga amahitamo nyuma yo guhagarara, atari mbere y'uko umuntu abona uburyo bwo gusubiza.

Iyo umuntu ababaye cyangwa ahindukiye, reka umukino maze ukomeze. [Inama zacu zo kuganira](/caregiving/communication-tips/) zisobanura uko uhindura icyerekezo nta makuru.

## Guhitamo imvugo zikwiriye umuntu

- **Koresha imvugo z'umuntu ubwe.** Baza umuryango icyo umubyeyi we cyangwa uwo bashakanye yahoraga avuga. Imvugo zo mu bwana akenshi ziroroshye.
- **Hitamo imvugo ngufi za buri munsi.** Interuro zerekeye ikirere, igikoni, ubusitani, umuryango n'ibihe by'umwaka ziboneka cyane kandi ziroroshye gukurikira.
- **Koresha ibyoroshye.** Imigani ishingiye ku magambo ya kera cyangwa ku nyigisho ishobora kuzana urujijo. Iyibike abayishimira.
- **Reba ururimi.** Imvugo nyinshi ntizihindurwa. Koresha imvugo umuntu azi mu rurimi rwe, nubwo wakenera gushaka izindi nshya.

## Imvugo ukurikije insanganyamatsiko

Buri rugero rukurikira rwerekana iherezo mu mabara y'umurongo kugira ngo uryimenye. Soma gusa igice cya mbere, hanyuma utegereze.

- **Ikirere:** "Ak'imuhana kaza ..." (iherezo: imvura ihise).
- **Igikoni n'ibiryo:** "Ukuri guca mu ziko ..." (iherezo: ntigushye).
- **Urugo n'umuryango:** "Inyana ni iya ..." (iherezo: mweru).
- **Ibihe by'umwaka n'ubusitani:** baza umuryango imvugo umuntu yakoreshaga mu gihe cyo guhinga cyangwa gusarura, maze uzandike.

## Bite se ku muntu udashobora gusubiza?

Bamwe ntibashobora kubona amagambo na gato mu byiciro bya nyuma. Ntuhagarike imvugo. Yisome, useke, maze utumire igisubizo ukoresheje ikimenyetso, kunyeganyeza umutwe, cyangwa guhitamo ishusho. Gusangira injyana imenyerewe biracyari akanya gasangiwe. Huza umukino n'umuziki, ikinyobwa gishyushye, cyangwa gukanda ukuboko, nk'uko bimeze mu [bikorwa byacu byo mu cyiciro cya nyuma](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ubundi buryo bwo gukoresha imvugo

Imvugo zijyana neza mu kiganiro ku byahise by'umuntu. Ushobora gutumira inkuru ukoresheje ibibazo biva mu [bibazo byo kwibuka](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), cyangwa gukina umukino uhuye nk'[amakarita y'ikiganiro](/games/conversation-cards/). Ku bindi bitekerezo mu munsi wose, reba [ibikorwa ku bantu bafite dementia](/activities/ideas/).
