Imishinga y'ubugeni ni uburyo bwiza bwo kumarana igihe n'umuntu ufite dementia. Ikoresha amaboko, amaso n'imyumvire, kandi iguha icyo muganiraho. Uru rupapuro rutanga imishinga runaka ifite intambwe zoroshye, hamwe n'inama zo guhitamo ibikoresho mu buryo butekanye. Ku mahame y'ubugeni bw'abakuru, nko kwirinda ibikoresho by'abana, reba [ubugeni n'ubukorikori ku bakuru bafite dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Uko watangira umushinga uwo ari wo wose w'ubugeni

1. **Tegura mbere yo gutangira.** Shyira hanze igikoresho kimwe cyangwa bibiri, igitambaro gikingira n'agatambaro gatose.
2. **Erekana intambwe ya mbere.** Koza uruhago mu mazi, tanyura igice cya mbere, cyangwa ukamure agace gato k'ibumba, hanyuma umutumire kubigenza atyo.
3. **Mukorere hamwe iruhande rw'undi.** Gumana umushinga wawe hafi kugira ngo igikorwa kigaragare nk'igisangiwe, aho kuba igenzurwa.
4. **Muganire ku byo mubona.** Vuga amabara, imiterere n'ishusho, aho gucira urubanza igikorwa.
5. **Hagarara mbere y'uko imbaraga zigabanuka.** Iminota icumi kugeza kuri makumyabiri akenshi ni igihe cyiza.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ivuga ko ubugeni ari uburyo bwo kugaragaza ibyiyumvo no guhanga udakeneye ubuhanga bwihariye. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) igira inama yo kugabanya ibikorwa mu ntambwe nto zishoboka.

## Gusiga amabara y'amazi

Koresha impapuro nini zikomeye zo gusigaho amabara y'amazi, uruhago runini rworoshye n'amabara make mu bikombe bidakuri. Tose urupapuro n'amazi meza, hanyuma umureke ashyireho amabara. Nyeganyeza urupapuro buhoro kugira ngo amabara atemba. Ibisubizo ni ishusho yoroshye itarimo ibisobanuro, itagusaba gutegura.

- **Icyiciro cy'itangiriro:** Mureke ahitemo amabara asige ishusho y'ahantu.
- **Icyiciro cy'hagati:** Muhe amabara abiri cyangwa atatu kandi umutose urupapuro.
- **Icyiciro cy'inyuma:** Fata urupapuro we asige akoresheje sponge.

## Gukora ishusho mu mashusho yatanyaguwe

Kusanya impapuro z'ibinyamakuru zifite amafoto meza y'indabo, ibiribwa, inyamaswa, imodoka cyangwa ingendo. Tanyura amashusho mu bice binini, biruta kuyatema, maze uyomeke ku rupapuro rukomeye. Iki gikorwa kiza mu gutangiza ikiganiro, kuko buri shusho ishobora kuzana urwibutso. Gihuye n'umuntu ukunda gutondekanya no guhitamo.

## Ibumba rworoshye

Ibumba ryumira mu kirere cyangwa ibumba rworoshye ry'ubukorikori rishobora gukandwa, kuzingurwa no gukamurwa. Kora igikombe cyoroshye cyangwa urupapuro ruringaniye. Ibumba rikoreshwa cyane mu gukoraho, bityo rishobora gutuza abantu bakunda gufata ibintu mu ntoki. Komeza ibice binini, kandi wirinde ibikoresho bifite ibyuma bityaye.

## Ingwa z'amabara ku mpapuro z'umukara

Impapuro z'umukara zituma amabara y'ingwa yaka. Koresha ingwa nini nini maze umureke ashushanye imirongo mini cyangwa avange akoresheje urutoki cyangwa agatambaro. Ingwa zitera umukungugu, bityo tegurira ku isahani maze woze intoki nyuma. Hitamo ingwa zoroshye zanditse ko zitagira ubumara.

## Gucapa ku mababi

Kusanya amababi manini mu busitani, usige irangi ryoga ku miyoboro yayo, hanyuma ukande ibabi ku rupapuro. Rikuremo witonze maze wishimire icyapa. Uyu mushinga uhuza neza n'[ibikorwa byo hanze n'ubuhinzi bw'ubusitani](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), kandi ushobora gutangira n'urugendo rwo gukusanya amababi.

## Gutunganya imyenda n'indabo

Tondekanya uduce tw'imyenda ifite imiterere itandukanye ukurikije uko ikozweho, cyangwa utunganye indabo nke nshya mu gikombe. Iyi ni imishinga ikoresha imyumvire kandi idafite igitutu. Ikwiriye kandi abantu bari mu byiciro bya nyuma. Ku bindi bitekerezo, reba [ibikorwa bikoresha imyumvire ku bantu bafite dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Guhitamo ibikoresho by'ubugeni

Ibikoresho bifite akamaro cyane ku mutekano no ku buruhukiro kurusha ubwiza. Mu guhitamo, tekereza kuri ibi:

- **Bitagira ubumara kandi byoga:** Shaka ibyanditseho ko bitagira ubumara kandi urebe ko amarangi yoga.
- **Aho gufata hanini:** Ibikaramu binini, impuruza z'amabara zinini n'ingwa nini biroroshye gufata.
- **Nta duce duto:** Irinde amaseke, utubuto, ibyuma bimurika n'ibimurika bishobora kumirwa.
- **Nta bikoresho bityaye:** Koresha imikasi itekanye cyangwa utanyure impapuro n'intoki.
- **Indwara z'ubworoherane (allergies):** Reba latex, n'uruhu rworoha ku bintu bimwe.
- **Itandukaniro ry'amabara:** Amabara yerurutse ku rupapuro rwera cyangwa ku rw'umukara aroroshye kubona.
- **Urutonde rugufi:** Ibikoresho bibiri cyangwa bitatu birahagije. Amahitamo menshi ashobora kuvuna umutwe.
- **Gusukura byoroshye:** Koresha isahani, ikiringiti cya pulasitiki n'agatambaro gatose, kugira ngo akajagari kadahinduka impamvu yo guhagarara.

## Guhuza n'icyiciro

Mu cyiciro cy'itangiriro, abantu benshi bashobora gutegura no kurangiza umushinga bafashijwe gato. Mu cyiciro cy'hagati, tanga amahitamo make n'ubuyobozi bwinshi. Mu cyiciro cya nyuma, wibande ku mikorere ikoresha imyumvire nko gukamura ibumba cyangwa gukoza irangi akoresheje sponge, kandi wishimire uko bimeze aho kwishimira ibisoje.

Ku bindi bitekerezo byihariye ku cyiciro, reba [icyiciro cy'itangiriro](/activities/early-stage/), [icyiciro cy'hagati](/activities/middle-stage/) n'[ibikorwa byo mu cyiciro cya nyuma](/activities/late-stage/). Iyo umuntu yanze cyangwa akarakara, inyandiko yacu ku [gihe umuntu avuze oya](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) isobanura uko wigira inyuma.

## Uhuze n'umukino

Nyuma yo gusiga amabara, gerageza [gusiga amabara](/games/coloring/) kuri tablet kugira ngo mukomeze mu mutuzo, cyangwa [umukino w'ishusho ihuza](/games/picture-puzzle/) nk'igikorwa cyo gusoza. Byombi bikomeza amaboko akora bidasabye ubuhanga bwimbitse.
