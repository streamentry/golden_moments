Imiryango myinshi ishaka umukino wo gukinira hamwe umeze nk'ikiganiro, aho kuba ikizamini. Ibibazo makumyabiri n'indi mikino isa nacyo yo gukeka bishobora gukora neza, kuko amategeko ari yoroshye kandi ikiganiro gishobora kujya hose. Ibanga ni uguhindura uko bisanzwe: umuntu atekereza igisubizo, wowe ukabaza. Uru rupapuro rusobanura uko wakina iyi mikino mu bwitonzi, n'imikino y'ikiganiro ikwiriye abantu bafite dementia.

## Kuki duhindura amategeko asanzwe?

Mu kizamini gisanzwe, umuntu umwe abaza undi agomba kumenya. Ibyo bishyira umuntu ufite dementia mu mwanya aho ashobora gutsindwa, kandi gutsindwa bishobora kumubabaza. Iyo ari we uhitamo ikintu, wowe ugakeka, aba ari we uzi. Afite igisubizo, ni we uhitamo igihe cyo kuvuga yego cyangwa oya, kandi ashobora kwishimira kukureba ugerageza.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) igira inama abafasha gukoresha interuro ngufi kandi zoroshye, ikibazo kimwe icyarimwe, n'ijwi ituje kandi rifite icyihangane. Ibi bihuye neza n'imikino yo gukeka.

## Uko wakina ibibazo makumyabiri mu bwitonzi

1. **Hitamo insanganyamatsiko** iva mu buzima bw'umuntu, nk'igikoni, ubusitani, inyamaswa cyangwa inkombe y'inyanja.
2. **Mureke atekereze ikintu** akivuge bucece, cyangwa werekane ikarita y'ishusho kugira ngo utagomba kukumva.
3. **Baza ikibazo kimwe cya yego cyangwa oya icyarimwe.** "Ese ni ikintu kiribwa?" "Ese kiruta inkweto?"
4. **Emera igisubizo icyo ari cyo cyose.** Kunyeganyeza umutwe, kuwuhakana cyangwa guseka byose birabarwa.
5. **Vuga ibyo ukeka n'ijwi riranguruye** kandi ubyishimire. Iyo ukekesheje neza, mwishimire hamwe.
6. **Tanga icyerekezo** iyo umuntu asa n'uwahagaritswe: "Ni ikintu cyo mu gikoni."
7. **Ntuzigere ubara ibibazo** kandi ntuzigere ukora amanota.

Iyo umuntu adashobora gukurikira amategeko, hinduka ujye ku buryo bworoshye: wowe uvuga ibintu bike, we akerekana icyo ashaka.

## Ibintu byiza byo guhitamo

- Imbuto, imboga cyangwa ururabo akunda
- Inyamaswa yo mu isambu, mu ngoro y'inyamaswa cyangwa iyo yari afite mu rugo
- Igikoresho, nk'inyundo, umuhoro cyangwa urushinge rwo kudoda
- Ikintu cyo mu nzu cyangwa mu rugo
- Ahantu azi neza, nk'ubusitani rusange cyangwa umujyi
- Umwenda yakundaga kwambara

Irinde amazina y'abantu, ikintu cyose cyateza agahinda, n'ibintu bishobora guteza akaga cyangwa bishobora kwitiranywa.

## Indi mikino y'ikiganiro

- **Iki cyangwa kiriya.** Tanga amahitamo abiri maze ubaze ikihe akunda: icyayi cyangwa ikawa, imisozi cyangwa inyanja, ikanzu y'umutuku cyangwa iy'ubururu. Nta gisubizo gikwiriye.
- **Wahitamo iki.** Baza ikibazo cyoroshye kandi cyoroheje: "Wahitamo gufata ifunguro hanze, cyangwa gusangirira mu busitani?"
- **Rangiza imvugo.** Interuro imenyerewe ifite iherezo ridasozwa. Gerageza [imikino yo kurangiza imvugo](/activities/finish-the-saying/) kugira ngo ubone ubuyobozi.
- **Vuga ahantu.** Musabe asobanure icyumba, umujyi cyangwa ahantu akunda, kandi agutumire kubitekereza nawe.
- **Amakarita afite ibibazo bifunguye.** [Amakarita yacu y'ikiganiro](/games/conversation-cards/) atanga ibibazo bifunguye ukurikije insanganyamatsiko, ashobora gucapwa ku meza.

Ku bindi bitekerezo bishingiye ku kwibuka n'inkuru y'ubuzima, reba [ibibazo byo kwibuka](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Ku mikino imeze nk'ibibazo ikozwe mu bwitonzi, reba [ibibazo byoroshye ku bakuru bafite dementia](/activities/trivia/), bikoresha amahitamo kandi nta manota.

## Bite se iyo umuntu arakaye?

Hagarara, useke maze uvuge ikintu nka: "Reka dukine ikindi." Umujinya ni ikimenyetso cyo guhindura umukino, si icyo gukomeza ku gahato. Inyandiko yacu ku [buryo bwo gukina n'umuntu ufite dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) isobanura uko uhindura amategeko ako kanya.

## Icyo imikino ishobora gukora n'icyo itashobora

Imikino y'ikiganiro iranezeza kandi iteza ubusabane. Ishobora gufasha abantu gusabana no gusangira inkuru. Ntivura dementia, ntiyiyoroshya kandi ntiyirinda. Komeza intego yoroshye: akanya keza mugirana.
