Okuzemburuka omuano wokuhungira pamwe ohunga norukapitaveze: ozonganda zouṱiṱi, oviungura ovitenga, ovikurya mbi wa suverere, omizumbi, omakupiro nomayuva omanene. Kovandu ovengi mbe hupa nomutjise wokuzemba, ehungi ewa nohange ohunga nehupo rawo oveni ri yenena okurira orupa rumwe orunyande tjinene meyuva. Otjiṋa otjinanḓengu okutjita kutja omapuriro ye ṋange ehungi nu ye ha rora ozemburuka. Omapuriro nga ya tuwa momikambo, pamwe nomaziriro wokuyetungurisa.

## Omahongononeno womapuriro wokuzemburuka

- **Pura omapuriro omapaturuke.** “Ndji serekarera ohunga na...” na “Kwa ri vi...” maye ṋanga ehungi. “Mo zemburuka...?” ri pura eziriro ndi ri ri mondjila.
- **O aruhe rora.** Tonda “Ouṋe ngwi?”, “Ombura ndji ya ri ndjiṋe?” na “Mo zemburuka ena ra...?”
- **Yakura atjihe tji matji kotoka.** Ehungi tji ma ri runduruka poo omaruveze tji maye runduruka, nyanda na ro nu u pure ohunga nomerimwino.
- **Ungurisa epuriro rimwe poruveze.** Undja, puratena nu u kongorere nepuriro epu.
- **Yandjera omundu a hongorere.** Tji mave vanga okuhungira otjiṋa tjarwe, yenda na wo.

[Omaziriro wa National Institute on Aging ohunga nokuhungira](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) maye tunduuza omambo omasupi, eraka epori, nomuretima indu omundu tji ma paha omambo we. Omahongononeno nga omanandengu mba wina.

## Onganda nouṱiṱi

- Ndji serekarera ohunga nonganda mu wa kurira. Otjizunino tja ri vi?
- Wa suverere okutjita tjike pendje tji wa ri okaṱiṱi?
- Ovaṋe mba mamu nyanda pamwe momuvanda weṋu?
- Ondjuwo yoye yokurara ya ri vi? Wa kara mo nomundu warwe?

## Oviungura nokutjita oviṋa

- Otjiungura tjiṋe tji wa suverere tjinene?
- Ndji serekarera ohunga neyuva rimwe poviungura ndi re ku pa ondjoroka.
- Wa suverere okutunga poo okuvyura tjike nomake woye?
- Ouṋe ngwa ku honga ovingi ohunga noviungura vyoye?

## Ovikurya nokutereka

- Eṱunḓu reṋu ra ri ovikurya viṋe mOsondaha?
- Ndji serekarera ohunga notjikurya tji tja terekerwe nawa tjinene i omundu mweṱunḓu reṋu.
- Wa ri noruveze ru wa suverere okukaranda otjinyandi tji wa ri omutanda?
- Otjivavara tjiṋe tji tji ku zemburukisa onganda?

Omahungi ohunga novikurya maye hitasana nawa nomuhunga [woviṋa mbi tu muna noviṋa mbi tu fula](/activities/sensory/), na wina nokatiṋa kokurora tji tje ri nawa okuyandjwa.

## Omizumbi nomapundo

- Omizumbi viṋe mbi wa imbura poo okupunda ku vyo tji wa ri omutanda?
- Omuyimbi poo okambumba koye ku wa suverere ka ri ouṋe?
- Ove wa zuvire omizumbi pi momayuva ngo: ponganda, pomapundo, pondjuwo yomerikotameneno?

Komambo womizumbi nomaziriro warwe, tara [oviṋa vyomizumbi kovandu mbe hupa nomutjise wokuzemba](/activities/music/).

## Omayuva omanene nomazemburukiro

- Eyuva enene riṋe ndi wa suverere tjinene tji wa ri okaṱiṱi?
- Ndji serekarera ohunga neyuvakuzemburukwa rimwe ndi mo zemburuka.
- Eṱunḓu ra tjitire tjike mongurova yoruveze rwomutenya?
- Eṱunḓu reṋu ra zemburukire vi omaruveze womombura?

Komaziriro nge hitasana neyuva enene rimwe, tara [oviṋa vyomayuva omanene komundu ngwa hupa nomutjise wokuzemba](/activities/holidays/).

## Eṱunḓu nomapanga

- Ndji serekarera ohunga nomundu ngwa ku yora tjinene.
- Eyuva roye romakupiro ra ri vi?
- Ndji serekarera ohunga novanatje voye tji va ri ovaṱiṱi.
- Omapanga woye ye ku isanene vi?

Pura omapuriro weṱunḓu noupore. Omundu tji ma zeri omundu, kongorera ehungi re nu o mu ṋingire okuzemburuka oundjendje mbu mau yenene okumuhihamisa.

## Oruveze nouye

- Ndji serekarera ohunga noruveze ru wa suvera okuripura na ro.
- Ombepo ya ri vi poruveze ku wa kurira?
- Wa ri notjikunino, poo nomuti umwe mbu wa suverere?
- Ndji serekarera ohunga nouyenda mbu wa nyanda.

## Okungurisa oviperendero noviṋa

Oviperendero vi yandja omapaturukiro omawa. Ṱakamisa otjiperendero pomurungu womeho nu u tje, “Ndji serekarera ohunga na hi.” [Omaziriro wa Alzheimer's Association ohunga noviṋa mbya tjitwa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) maye tunduuza okungurisa oviṋa mbya tjiukwa, oviperendero nomizumbi okupaturura ehungi. Okambumba kozemburuka noviṋa ovititi okuza mehupo romundu wina make yenene okuungura nawa; tara [oviṋa vyokuzemburuka](/activities/reminiscence/) okutara kutja mo vi tungu vi.

## Indu ozemburuka tji ze hihamisa

Ozemburuka tjiva maze eta oruhoze, omaumba poo omazenge. Omundu tja pindikisiwa, o mu ṋiṋikiza. Tja otjiṋa tjomahange tjimuna, “Tji matji munika otja oruveze oruzeu. Mo vanga okuhungira otjiṋa otjinyande?” Okuzambo rundurura otjiṋa, mu pe oteya, poo u yandje orwizumbo ndu va suvera.

## Oruveze pamwe notjinyanda poo okukaenda

Kombunda yoruveze rwokuzemburuka, otjinyanda otjipore matji yenene okutjiza omuinyo omuwa. Rora [omakarata wehungi](/games/conversation-cards/) komapaturukiro omengi, poo [manisa omambo](/games/finish-the-saying/) komambo nga tjiukwa okuza kouṱiṱi womundu. Komaziriro wokuhungira momuano omunene, tara [omaziriro wokuhungira nomundu ngwa hupa nomutjise wokuzemba](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
