Ondjiviro oyingi ohunga nondjovazu yomerizemburukiro i kambura kutja omundu u nozombura omirongo hambombari poo hambondatu. Komundu ngwa munikwa ondjovazu ndji mozombura omirongo vine poo vitano, ehupo re eyuva arihe ri panguka. Eye ma yenene okukara notjiungura, ondjuwo ndji ma sutu, omitanda ponganda poo omukurundu ngwa sokurikendera. Oviungura vi sokuyakura omuinyo mbwo omukurundu. Omuhunga mbwi mau kahurura kutja ovikwaye mbi runduruka, ovikwaye mbi mo yenene okurora, nokutja ovakwetu novanatje ovakurundu mave yenene okumuna pi ombatero.

## Ondjovazu yomerizemburukiro pokati komuinyo oyatjike?

Ondjovazu yomerizemburukiro pokati komuinyo ondjovazu ndji i uta komurungu wozombura 65, nu potuingi i isanewa ondjovazu ndji i uta omusemba. [Ekurungu ra Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) ri kahurura kutja Alzheimer’s i varwa otjondjovazu ndji i uta omundu tji e ri kehi yozombura 65, nu ri tja okurikendera omundu omutanda „ku nozongendo zapeke“. Oviraisiro vi yenena okukara otja imbi vyomuinyo omukurundu, posi ozondando, omerizirira nomuinyo wovandu womundu mbwo kavi ri otjingeyo.

Mena rokutja kai munika tjinene, ovandu mave yenene okukara orure ngunda ave hiya munikwa, nu omaṱunḓu mape ya aye munu kutja kaye nomundu wokuhungira kuna ye. Okutjiwa kutja ovandu varwe ve ri mongaro otjingeyo potuingi ku yandja ombepo ombwa.

## Yandjera omukurundu a kare motjiungura

Ovakurundu mbe hupa nondjovazu yomerizemburukiro pokati komuinyo potuingi kave vanga okutjindwa otja ovanatje. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i tunduuza okurundurura oviungura kovikwaye omundu mbya yenena okukayenda komurungu okurora nokunyanda. Komundu ngu nozombura omirongo vitano, ihi matji hee:

- **Kayenda komurungu notjiṋa tja tjiwa okurora,** otja okuungura noviti, omanyando, oukarata, okuresa pamwe poo okwiimba motjimbumba, nombatero okaṱiṱi poo oruveze orusupi.
- **Ryanga poo hingi okahinyame pamwe nomundu,** mondjira ndji ma tjiwa nawa.
- **Tereka eṱunḓu ovikurya,** omundu tji ma ungura imbi mbya yenena.
- **Vatera moruveze poo ungura otjiungura tjokuyandja ombatero** motjiungura eye tja toorora, otja okuzikamisa oviyandjewa poo okuvatera poruveze rwosikore.
- **Zikamisa oruyenda** koruveze ndu ri nombango ku ye, puna omundu wokuvatera nondyero ombe.
- **Kara noruveze novatekuru, ovazamumwe poo ovazandu novakazona vomapanga,** amamu nyanda pamwe otjipiriko poo otjiungura.

Yandjera omundu a toorore. Okupura „Mo vanga okutjita tjike omutenya mbwi?“ ku nondengero pu okutoororera ye.

## Ungurisa omuimbu nomerizemburukiro wozombura zawo

Omuimbu nomafano wozombura zomundu 20, 30 no 40 vi yenena okuraisa omahaka omawa nomahungi omapupu. Omuhunga wetu ohunga [noviungura vyomuimbu](/activities/music/) u kahurura omuano wokutunga oruteto rwomuimbu, nu [oviungura vyomerizemburukiro](/activities/reminiscence/) vi raisa omuano wokuhana omafano nomahungi nokuhina okupura omapuriro omakukutu. Kotjipiriko tjovakurundu, rora [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/), nomapuriro omapaturuke ohunga noviungura, omayenda, omapanga neṱunḓu.

Photo pairs ndja ungurwa komafano weṱunḓu wina mai yenene okuungura nawa, mena rokutja omafano nge ri ovandu noruveze ndu ri nombango komundu. [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) i kara nomafano motjiyandjewa tjomerizemburukiro tjomutjito mbwo pehi ya tjiwa.

## Otjiungura, okuhinga nomuinyo weyuva arihe

Ovandu ovengi mbe hupa nondjovazu yomerizemburukiro pokati komuinyo ngunda mave ungura, mave hingi poo mave tjevere ovimariva. Oviṋa mbi kavi ri oviungura otja tji vi ri potuingi, posi vi ṱuna ku imbi omundu mbya yenena okutjita. Hungira nonganga yawo ohunga nondjeverero yokuhinga noyotjiungura, nu ripura kutja mave vanga ombatero movimariva poo mozombongarero. Ondando oyokutjevera ondengero yawo nokukara nomundu motjiṋa eye tji ma vanga okukara mo.

Kondjiviro ohunga nondyero yeyuva arihe nokukara nomasa, tara [ondyero yeyuva arihe ombouze](/activities/daily-routine/) na [oviungura vyoponganda otjoviungura](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Ombatero kovakwetu novanatje ovakurundu

Ovakwewe novanatje ovakurundu potuingi ve rimuna oveni. Okurikendera ku yenena okumunika otjotjiungura otjitjavari kombanda yoviungura neṱunḓu. Ekurungu ra Alzheimer’s Association ri tja ovarikenderi motuzo twondjovazu ndji ya uta omusemba va muna kutja twa ri nombatero moviṋa vyotjivaro otja ondungekoveta, nokumuna kutja ve zuvaka. Ri tja wina kutja Association i notuzo twovarikenderi otjari, nu mu na otuzo twovandu mbe rikendera omundu ngu nondjovazu ndji ya uta omusemba, nu tutatu twazo tu haka morungovi mOtjivarakwa nomOtjitatu.

Okuningira ondjiviro ohunga notuzo, toona konomora yombatero 24/7 ya Association ndji ri 800.272.3900. Omuhunga wetu ohunga [nomeritjevero womurikenderi](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) u kahurura ohunga nokusuva, oruveze rwokupumuna noviraisiro vyokururuma.

## Ozongaro wina ze nomba

Ongaro yondjovazu yomerizemburukiro yomundu wina i ṱuna ku imbi mbi mavi vatere. Pomautiro, ovandu ovengi ve yenena okutjita oviungura vyawo otja tji ve tjita, nomarundurukiro omaṱiṱi, nu omuhunga wetu ohunga [noviungura vyongaro ombunga](/activities/early-stage/) u nozondunge zarwe. Kombunda, oviungura vi rire omipupu nu vi kambure omazuve, otja tji pa kahururwa mu [oviungura vyongaro onḓikura](/activities/late-stage/). Rundurura otjiungura omundu tja runduruka, nu mu pura kutja ma vanga tjike indu tji ma yenene okutja.
