Bingo omudano ou wa tjiwa na ou wokukara kumwe kovakuru avengi, nungwari ozonamba kaze nomwano okutjiwa komundu ngu kara nondementia. Bingo yomifano i karaera otjikando tjo tjimwe otjinyenye, nu i twaa omifano omizeu posi jozonamba. I ungura mouṋe, motjitjitwa tjezuva, poo mondjuwo yokurera ovakuru. Ombapira ndji mai hee kutja ma dane vi, mai sokisa vi komundu, nu mai isa otjari tji kare komurungu.

## Bingo yomifano mai ungura vi

Omudani auhe u notjibapira tji nomifano mbi kara mongano, otja otjikarija tjotjai, omburu, orose, otjikepe, poo otjibasikira. Omutjivisi ma rweja poo ma hee otjifano otjiimwe komurungu. Ovadani ve tjangere po otjifano tji soka kotjibapira tjawo. Omudani ngu tjangere po omurongo auhe, poo otjimbumburu tji tja soka, ma kambura "Bingo," nungwari ovimbumburu avengi kave vanga omuwini.

Ove wa soku karaesa ozombapira ozokuprinta okuza ku [omidano yokombapira tja yandjwa](/activities/printable/), poo [omudano womifano Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/), ou u nomutjivisi korukuma kovimbumburu. Avye ari vari ma ungura nawa kotablet ndji rwejiwa kutja otjimbumburu atjihe tji mune.

## Okurongera oruweze rwomudano

- **Sora otjimbumburu otjinini.** Ovandu avane ku hambondatu ouṋe uwa. Ovimbumburu ovizeu kavi sokuwa okukwama, nu otji zuu otjimanga.
- **Tjita omifano omizeu.** Karaesa omirongo omizeu, nu twaa otjina otjimwe tji tjiwewa motjikunino otjiimwe. Tjivara ozombapira ndji nomifano ovengi.
- **Pa ovandu otjirongo tjiwa.** Pa omudani auhe otjirongo tji ma munu omutjivisi notjibapira tjaye, nu popezu nomuvatere tji ma hepa umwe.
- **Karaesa ovipara ovisemba.** Ovipara ovizeu, ovimariva, poo okutjaa nomunwe vi ungura nawa komurungu wozopenisele. Karaesa ovimwe rukwao.
- **Rongera oruweze oruṱuhu.** Ominute omirongo vitatu otjiwa rukuru. Tjinda tjimanga tji ovandu ve kukuta.

Ombapira ya [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) mai hee kutja omidano vi sokisirwe ku tji omundu ma soku tjita natjo, komurungu wokutja sokesa ozombapira nomazikamisiro auhe tji tji vatere.

## Okutjivisa omifano

1. **Rweja otjifano komurungu** poo tjaa kotjo korukuma komurungu wokutja u hee.
2. **Hee ena rotjo nawa,** nomambo omasemba: "Otjikarija tjotjai." Hee rukwao rukuru, ombemba.
3. **Rinda.** Pa ovandu oruweze rwokutara nokutjangarera po. Ina tjivisa otjifano tji landa ko komurungu wokutja ovandu avengi va kara noruweze.
4. **Hee omahandjauriro, tji tji vatere.** "Otjitai otjitune" poo "tji twa ri natjo kotjitjitwa tjokuhakaera" tji vatere okurukisa omambo.
5. **Kara omunyenye.** Omutjivisi omunyenye ouwa komurungu womutjivisi ngu tjita tjimanga.

## Okuwina kape nomurungu

Okukondja okuwina otji sokuwa otjipu. Omundu ngu hiya wina ma zuu otja ngu zejwa, nu omundu ngu tira okuwina ma kara nouzeu. Indji ovikwao mbi sokuungurwa:

- **Omundu auhe ma wina otjifano.** Omudani auhe ma kambura otjifano otjiimwe tji ma vanga, nu otjimbumburu tji tara kotjo notjari.
- **Karaesa otjibapira tji yenene kumwe.** Pura ovadani kutja ve hee otjiṋa kotjifano otjiimwe kotjibapira tjawo.
- **Dana ku tjita omifano mbi mana.** Tjivisa omifano aehe kotjibapira, nu uta otjikwao, nu kape nomuwini wotjitenga.
- **Tjita ombereko onini, poo isa okuyandja.** Otjikuki poo otjimburiro tji ungura nawa komurungu wotjipandi otjizeu.

## Okusokisa kovandu momitunda mokuhita

Kovandu mbe hiya sokuwa okumuna otjifano, karaesa otjibapira tji nomikunino vine poo hamboumwe peke, poo pa omudani auhe otjibapira otjiimwe komurungu. Runga omuvatere a kambure otjibapira indu omundu ma tjaa kotjo, poo a imbe otjipara ombemba, indu omundu e mu runga. Ovandu vamwe ve sokuwa nawa tji ve zuva omutjivisi nokuhakera omambo.

Tara koviraisiro vyokukara nai, otja okukakara poo okuteta omurungu, nu tjinda tji mavi raisire. Komahandjauriro womidano vyombemba, tara ku [omidano yomomahitiro](/activities/late-stage/).

## Bingo mozondjuwo zokurera ovakuru notjitjitwa tjezuva

Omidano vyozonganda vi ungura nawa tji mavi rongerwa otja kovandu mbe ri mondjuwo. Ombapira yetu y[omidano vyozonganda mozondjuwo zokurera ovakuru notjitjitwa tjezuva](/activities/care-home-groups/) mai hee okurongera oruweze, okupa ovandu otjirongo, nokutjita novandu mbe vanga okutjinda. Bingo yomifano i ungura nawa pamwe notjimburiro tjokukara kumwe poo otjikoro, na pamwe na [finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) otja omudano omunyenye tji ma landa ko.

## Bingo mai tjita nu kai tjiti tjike

Bingo yomifano i tjita kutja ovandu va kare kumwe nu i yandja otjari. I sokuwa okukongorera ovandu kumwe, okuyandja ondjivisiro yokutara komifano nokuzuva, nokutjita kutja otjimbumburu tji zuu ouwa. Kai pangisa dementia nu kai kamburisa okupita kwayo. Sokisa okuwa kwayo kotja ovandu mave tara nu mave zuu moure wotjikando, nu hiya otja ngu wa wina.
