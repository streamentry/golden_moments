Ehangwa rokumana omambo ri ungurisa embo ndi tjiukwa ndi kurama komurungu wembo esenina. Kovandu ovengi i ri omuano omuwa, mbu hi nokupata, wokutja oruveze pamwe. Ondyero i ungura mena rokutja omambo omakuru nu ye tjiukwa nawa, nu omundu ke hepa okuzemburuka otjiṋa atjihe tji tja tjitwa ndino. Omuhunga mbwi mau kahurura omuano wokunyanda, omuano wokutohora omambo, nomuano wokukara noupore.

## Ehangwa rokumana omambo otjikeṋa?

Oro ondyero osupi mu mu resa orupa orutenga rwembo nu ingwi warwe ma mana embo esenina. Otjihorera: "Ovitjitwa vi hungira kombanda yomambo..." na "Onderera omunene ai kambura..." Omundu ma yenene okutja embo esenina neraka, okuraisa, poo okuritohora pokati komambo yevari poo yetatu ngu mo yandja. Ovandu tjiva ve nyanda okuhungira orupa orutenga.

Golden Moments i yandja otja [ehangwa rokumana omambo erehere](/games/finish-the-saying/) nomambo omanene. Mo yenene okutji nyanda nokuhina otjivavara, mokutja uriri orupa orutenga nokundja.

## Omuano wokunyanda, katjikando na katjikando

1. **Tohora oruveze orupore** nu haama pu ve ri pamwe nomeho, amu tarasana.
2. **Resa orupa orutenga kouṱiṱi,** neraka oruwa, nu kurama. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) i tunduuza okuhungira kouṱiṱi nokungurisa omambo omasupi, omapupu.
3. **Undja.** Yandja ousekunde tjiva. Tji pe hi na tji tja za, yandja omambo yevari poo yetatu nge manisa, rimwe mari ri mondyo.
4. **Yakura ondjiviro aihe.** "Ii, indji ondyo" poo "Hi ri ripurire nao" tji tjita kutja pa kare noupore.
5. **Hungira kembo ndi.** Pura kutja mari hee tjike ku ye, poo kwe ri zuva. Opo pe ri ondjandje onene.
6. **Kurama ngunda ave hi ya urwa.** Omambo omatiti nondjoroka ondjo ondjero ndja yenene.

## Okuyandja ozondyero nokuhina okurora

Omambo nge manisa pokati kozondyero ye yenena okutjita kutja ondyero i rire ombupu, posi yokutja wina mape ya ai munika otja orondu indu tji ya yandjwa otja ombata. Kara neraka orupore. Tja "Mo vanga okuzuva embo esenina poo okuritohora rimwe?" pendje nokutja "Ondi ri pi?" Yandja ozondyero kombunda yokukurarama, hakomurungu wokutja omundu wa pewa oruveze okuzirira.

Omundu tja pindike poo tja ṱohoṋi, esa ondyero nu yaruka ku yarwe. [Omiano vyetu vyokuhungira](/caregiving/communication-tips/) vi kahurura omuano wokurundurura nokuhina okupata.

## Okutohora omambo nge ṱuna komundu

- **Ungurisa omambo womundu omuini.** Pura eṱunḓu kutja ihe poo omurumendu we aa tja tjike aruhe. Omambo okuza kouṱṱiṱi potuingi ye zemburuka tjimanga.
- **Tohora omambo omasupi, eyuva arihe.** Omambo nge notjiṋa nombepo, nozohaṱi, notjikunino, neṱunḓu nozombura zomuinyo ye tjiukwa nawa nu ye pupu okutjiwa.
- **Kara noupupu.** Ovisasanekero mbi zikamena komambo omakuru poo kombazu mavi yenene okuṱuna. Vi ungurisa kovandu mbe vi nyanda.
- **Tara keraka.** Omambo omengi kae runduruka nawa momaraka warwe. Ungurisa omambo omundu nge tjiwa meraka re omuini, nandarire tji mo sokutjivaza omape.

## Omambo motupa

Otjihorera atjihe tji raisa embo esenina mozombandi kove. Resa uriri orupa orutenga, nu undja.

- **Ombepo:** "Ombura komurungu wovirikando hambombari..." (embo esenina: "omuinyo omuwa povirikando murongo na imwe"); "Ekamba arihe ri na..." (orutenda orusilveri).
- **Ozohaṱi novikurya:** "Ovahape ovengi..." (ve yonoza ombazu); "O undjire ozohuwi zoye..." (ngunda inaze pata).
- **Onganda neṱunḓu:** "Onganda opu pe ri omutima..." (u ri); "Ombindu ombindu i komeṱe..." (omeva).
- **Ozombura zomuinyo notjikunino:** "Ozombura za April ze eta..." (ozonḓera za Mei); "Mo kondo tji wa..." (kunu).

## Omundu tji hi nokuyenena okuzirira vi?

Ovandu tjiva kave yenene okumuna omambo komaandero wouyeze wokuzemba. O esa embo. Ri resa, yoroha, nu ṋanga ondjiviro notjiraiso tjomake, nokutenderera otjiuru, poo mokutohora otjiperendero. Okutja omuzumbi mbu tjiukwa pamwe wina oruveze ndu mwa kara pamwe. Waneka ondyero nomizumbi, otjinwino otjiṋamu, poo okunyandisa eke, otja [mozondyero zomaandero wouyeze wokuzemba](/activities/late-stage/).
