Oviungura vyokutjanga noviwonge ovizandu omuano omuwa wokukara noruveze pamwe nomundu ngu ri nombura yokuṱakamisa ombango. Vi ungurisa omake, omeho noviyave, nu vi mu pa otjiṋa tji mu yenena okuhungirira ko. Ombapira ndji i yandja oviungura ovitjiti nozondjira ozisupi, nouhongore wokuṱiṱira oviṋa vyokutjita nawa. Komirari ohunga noviṋa mbi ri kongotwe yoviwonge vyovanene, tjimuna okuyanda oviṋa vyovanatje, tara [oviwonge vyomake noviwonge ovizandu ku ovanene mbe ri nombura yokuṱakamisa ombango](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Omuano wokuta uta otjiungura atjihe tjokutjanga noviwonge

1. **Rirongera ngunda au hiya uta.** Twa po otjiṋa tjimwe poo vivari vyokutjita, otjiṋa tjokuyama, nokatjapa okanyondoroke.
2. **Raisa otjiṋa otjitenga.** Kuta orutjange, taura otjiṋa otjitenga, poo nyateka okaṋa oruuma, nu mu ningire kutja ma tjiti nao wina.
3. **Ungura mene.** Kara notjiungura tjoye popezu, kokutja otjiṋa tji rire otjokutjita pamwe, nungwari kaotjokutarerwa.
4. **Hungira ohunga na imbi mbi mo munu.** Kahurura omivara, omavave nozomuinyo nandarire okuyandja ombanguriro yoviungura.
5. **Kurama ngunda omasa ae hiya yanda.** Ominute 10 nga 20 potuingi oruveze oruwa.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) i tja oviwonge ovizandu omuano wokuraisa omaṋingandu nokutja ovitjiti nandarire tji pe hi nounongo wokutjanga. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i tunduuza okutaura oviṋa movikando ovisupi, mbi yenena okutjitwa.

## Omurari womeva

Ungurisa ombapa ondjaukise yomeva, orutjange orunene oruporoto novivara ovingi mozombakete ozonḓiṱi. Yandeka ombapa nomeva omakohoke, nu okuzambo mu esa a wize ovivara kombanda yamo. Ṱunḓa ombapa ouṱiṱi kutja ovivara vi tuke. Imbi mbi pita po otjiperendero otjiporoto, tji tji hi nokuvanga ondunge.

- **Otjikaro otjipenga:** Mu esa a toorore ovivara nokutjanga otjirongo.
- **Otjikaro tjopokati:** Mu pa ovivara vivari poo vitatu nu u mu yandekere ombapa.
- **Otjikaro otjisenina:** Tjinda ombapa ngunda eye ama tu norutjange rwokomake poo orusponji.

## Oviṱupa vyombapa okuza mozombapira

Wonga ozombapira nḓe noviperendero ovipaturuke vyongara, ovikurya, ovipuka, ovihauto poo omauyenda. Taura oviperendero moviṋa ovinene, ihi otjipupu okukapita okutaura, nu vi namanene kombapa ondjaukise norutjange rwokunamisa. Oviṱupa vyombapa vi ungura nawa otjomautiro wehungi, mena rokutja otjiperendero atjihe matji yenene okuyeta ondjiviro. Tji wapera omundu ngu suvera okutoorora nokuronga.

## Oruuma oruporoto

Oruuma ndu ru kukuta urwini poo oruuma oruporoto rwokutjita ru yenena okupinwa, okukondwa nokunyatwa. Tjita otjiyaha otjisupi poo otjitara otjipapuke. Oruuma ru nemavave omanene, notji matji yenene okuhuhumiṋa imba mbe suvera okuṱuna oviṋa. Kara noviṋa ovinene, nu yanda oviṋa mbi noviṋa ovitwe.

## Ovitjange vyorutenda kombapa ondorozu

Ombapa ondorozu i tjita kutja omivara vyovitjange vyorutenda vi yere. Ungurisa ovitjange vyorutenda ovinene nu esa omundu a tjite omirari ominene poo a vande nomunwe poo nokatjapa. Ovitjange vyorutenda vi norutenda, notji rirongera motjiyaha nu koha omake kombunda. Toorora ovitjange vyorutenda oviporoto mbi tja ovyo kavi nokuhihamisa.

## Okutjanga nomazeva

Wonga omazeva omanene okuza motjikunino, twa orutjange ndu ru kohororwa komitutu vyezeva, nu ṱakamisa ezeva kombapa. Ri isamo nawa nu tara otjiperendero. Otjiungura hi tji ri pokati nawa noviyandjewa vyopendje novyotjikunino, nu matji yenene okuta uta nokukaryanga okukawonga omazeva. Tara wina [oviyandjewa vyopendje novyotjikunino](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Okuronga ozombanda nozongara

Toorora ozombanda nḓe nomavave pekepeke otja komavave, poo ronga ozongara ozopevari motjiyaha. Ivi oviyandjewa vyokuvave nu vi noupyu ouṱiṱi. Vi wapera wina ovandu motjikaro otjisenina. Komiano vyarwe, tara [oviyandjewa vyokuvave ku imba mbe ri nombura yokuṱakamisa ombango](/activities/sensory/).

## Okutoorora oviṋa vyokutjita

Oviṋa vyokutjita ovyo ovinanḓengu tjinene kouharo nokukara nawa okukapita omuano. Tji mo toorora, ripura na ihi:

- **Kavi nokuhihamisa nu vi kohororwa:** Paha omatjangero ngu maye tja kavi nokuhihamisa nu tara kutja orutjange ru kohororwa.
- **Omahoko omanene:** Ovitjange vyovanatje ovinene, orutjange orunene novitjange vyorutenda ovinene vi ṱunwa noupupu.
- **Kape noviṋa ovisupi:** Yanda ouṋa, ozokomba, ozongirita norutenda ndu ru pandjara ndu ru yenena okuninwa.
- **Kape noviṋa ovitwe:** Ungurisa ovisakete vyokuyama poo taura ombapa nomake.
- **Ouzeu wovikurya poo woviyave:** Tara orateksa, nouzeu auhe wombanda mbu tjiukwa.
- **Ombanguro:** Omivara omipaturuke kombapa ombapaura poo kombapa ondorozu vi munika nawa.
- **Oruteto orusupi:** Oviṋa vivari poo vitatu vya yenene. Omahitiro omengi maye yenena okutiringa omundu.
- **Okukoha noupupu:** Ungurisa otjiyaha, ombapa yapurasitika nokatjapa okanyondoroke, kokutja ouzunde au ha rire etjitro rokukurama.

## Okurundurura otja kotjikaro

Motjikaro otjipenga, ovandu ovengi mave yenene okurongerisa nokumanisa otjiungura nombatero okaṱiṱi. Motjikaro tjopokati, yandja omahitiriro omaṱiṱi nombatero onyingi. Motjikaro otjisenina, yandja ombango koviṋa vyokuvave, tjimuna okunyata oruuma poo okutjanga norusponji, nu yorokera imbi mbi pita po nandarire okutja ka manukire.

Komiano vyarwe otja kotjikaro, tara [otjikaro otjipenga](/activities/early-stage/), [otjikaro tjopokati](/activities/middle-stage/) na [oviyandjewa vyotjikaro otjisenina](/activities/late-stage/). Tji omundu ma panda poo ma kara noupyu, omuhunga wetu ohunga [nomundu tji ma tja indee](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) u kahurura omuano wokukara kokure katjiti.

## Waneka notjinyanda

Kombunda yokutjanga, rora [okutjanga omivara](/games/coloring/) motjirwise tjokombanda tjomake otjoruveze orupore, poo [otjiperendero tjokuwonga](/games/picture-puzzle/) otjotjiṋa tjokumanisa. Avyevari vi kara nomake noruveze nandarire tji kavi nokuvanga ounongo ounene.
