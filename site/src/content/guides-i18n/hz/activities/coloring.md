Okutjita omarangi otjiṋa tjimwe pu imbi ovipupu okuṱuna omundu ngu hupa noungundi wokuzemba poo oungundi warwe mbu ṱuna okuzemburuka. Katji hepa okuresa, okuzemburuka poo eziriro ndi ri ri mondjira. Omake ye nyinganyinga, omarangi ye munika, nu pe kara otjiperendero tji mu yenena okuhungirira ko. Omuhunga mbwi mau kahurura kutja omapandje ye toororwa vi, ovikwaye mbi sokutwa pehi, nokutja mamu yenene vi okutjita omarangi pamwe nokuhinokukara otja otjiṋa tjovanatje poo otjiraisiro.

## Omena raye okutjita omarangi tji tjatera ovandu ovengi mba vera oungundi wokuzemba

Okutjita omarangi ku yandja omake otjiṋa okutjita nu omeho otjiṋa okutara. Tji yenena okuningwa amu haama potjitjaro poo motjihavero pomuvero, nu otjipupu okututa nokuyerisa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) i hungira ohunga noungaro wokutjita oviṋa vyomake otjomuhingo wokuraisa omuano omundu mbu ma f eel nokunyanda oruveze ndwi, nu i tja katji hepa ounongo wokuṱuna oviperendero.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i tunduuza okutoorora oviungura mbi hitasana na imbi omundu mbya yenena okuungura nai, nokuvirundurura indu omazikamisiro tji maye runduruka. Okutjita omarangi otjipupu okurundurura: otjiperendero otjinene, oviṋa vyokutjita omarangi ovinene, omarangi omaṱiṱi, poo ozongo yokutjita omarangi mokutapa.

## Toorora omiano vyovakuru

Ovakuru potuingi kave suvere oviperendero vyovanatje. Tara ku imbi:

- **Oviperendero vyomeva:** ekunino nozondura ozinene, otjikunino nomeva nombaze, ozonḓera kotjitayi.
- **Oviṋa mbi tjiukwa:** okatina kokutea, okambasikela, otjihape, ehoro.
- **Oviperendero ovipupu ovinene:** omandala nomirungu ominene vyomiano mbi nomaruveze omanene omaṱiṱi.
- **Oviperendero vyounongo poo oviperendero vyoruveze** omundu ndwa yenena okutjiwa koruveze poo komutjite woviperendero ngwa suverere.

Nakaura omapandje nge nomaruveze omaṱiṱi, omanene nu nga urisiwa, mena rokutja ye pindikisa. Omapandje wovakuru wokutjita omarangi omawa, nu [oviungura vyetu vyokutjita omarangi ovitjandjwa oviyandjewa](/activities/printable/) vi nomapandje nge nozombaze ozinene.

## Ovikwaye mbi sokutwa pehi

- **Oviṋa ovinene mbi kambura nawa.** Oviṋa vyokutjita omarangi ovinene, omapena omanene, poo opena ozombomba ozinene ovipupu okuungurisa pozopena ozondeu.
- **Omarangi omaṱiṱi.** Omarangi yetatu poo yane ya yenene. Omatoororero omengi ye yenena okupindikisa.
- **Ombapira onḓikame poo okarata.** Ombapira ondeu i tauka nu i nyinganyinga. Ṱiza ohinga nooteipa poo ungurisa okatjaro kokutjiza ombapira.
- **Otjitjaro otjikohoke.** Isa po oviṋa vyarwe nu twa epandje rimwe poruveze rumwe.
- **Otjiṋa tjokukwata poo okurunga,** tjimuna ondjatu poo otjiponge, tji tji vanga okutjita omarangi momuano ombore.

## Okutjita omarangi motableta

[Otjiṋangero tjokutjita omarangi](/games/coloring/) mu Golden Moments tji ungura mokutapa erangi nu okuzambo okutapa otjitupa tjotjiperendero. Katji hepa omazikamisiro omanene womake, nu tji yenena okutatera imba mba ungurisa omake nouzeu. Kara pehi pu nomundu kutja mu tare ozongo pamwe, nu mu ese a toorore omarangi. Zepa omaraisa omakwazikwa kutja otjiperendero tji kare otjinanḓengu.

## Omuano wokutjita omarangi pamwe

1. **Uta omutato omutenga pamwe.** Kambura otjiṋa tjokutjita omarangi pu na ye, poo twa eke roye kombanda yeke re oure womitato omititi, okuzambo ri esa.
2. **Hungira ohunga nomarangi noruveze.** "Ndjo omburau ombwa. Ove wa mwine pi omarangi ngo?" i eta ehungiriro nokuhinokupura otjovandu mave raisirwa.
3. **Tanga omatoororero, ka oukohoke.** "Ove wa toorora omarangi omawa nomapyu" ya ri nawa pozombaze.
4. **Mu esa a hongorere.** Tji ma vanga okutjita omarangi kepandje arihe, mu esa.
5. **Yerisa indu tji ma yerisa.** Twa oviṋa vyokutjita omarangi pehi, nu mu ningirire okuti poo okuryanga.

## Okurundurura indu oungundi wokuzemba tji mau runduruka

Morutenga, ovandu potuingi ve nyanda omapandje nge novingi nu ve yenena okutoorora oveni omarangi nomiano. Pokati, oviperendero ovinene nomatoororero omaṱiṱi ye vatera. Kombunda, rora otjiperendero otjinene tjimwe, ombapira yerangi rimwe ndi ri ozongara, poo okukambura uriri otjiṋa tjokutjita omarangi ngunda ove tji mo imbura. Ovandu tjiva ve nyanda okutara kotjiperendero tja pu tji tja manuka okukapita okutjita.

Tji mo vanga okumuna ovingi ohunga noviungura vyomake pomuhingo auhe, tara [oviungura vyomake kovakuru mba vera oungundi wokuzemba](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) na [oviungura vyounongo kovandu mba vera oungundi wokuzemba](/activities/art-projects/).

## Oruveze rwokuyerisa

Yerisa indu omundu tji ma pindikisiwa, tji ma undurire ombapira kokure, poo tji ma munika a urwa. Okuyerisa kombunda yominute vivari ondjandje ndji ya ri nawa tji va nyandere. Mo yenene okukotoka kombunda, poo okurora otjiperendero tjarwe poo otjiungura tjarwe tjimuna [oviṋa mbi ṱuna omuano mbu mo f eel](/activities/sensory/).
