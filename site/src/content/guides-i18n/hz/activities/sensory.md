Oviungura vyomazuviro vi ungurisa omazuviro yetano: okukwata, okunuka, okuroora, okuzuva nokumuna. Kavi hepa ozondjiviro, ozonḓunino poo omaziriro omawa, ku nao mavi sora okuungura motjipaze atjihe. Vi ri nonḓengu tjinene kombunda. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) i tja motjipaze otjisembasemba ouye au munika tjinene nomazuviro, nu ove mo sora okuraisa orusuvero okupitira mokukwata, okuzuva, okumuna, okuroora nokunuka. Omatwiro omaṱiṱi wondjeverero ye tjita oviungura mbi vi rire ovihange ku avehe.

## Oviungura vyomazuviro kondjenda yokuzemba ovipi?

Oviungura vyomazuviro omiano omapupu nge ungurisiwa nezuviro rimwe poruveze rumwe, tjimuna okukwata ombanda, okunuka omiti poo okupuratena kombura. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) i tja oviungura vyokombunda potuingi vi sokuyandja ombango komazuviro, nu i tamuna omizumbi, oviṋa vyokukwata nokupata omake. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) i tja ovandu motjipaze otjisembasemba mape ya ave kara nonḓero onamasa yokukambura otjiṋa popezu okuvepa omahuhumiṋino.

## Omiano vyomazuviro, kezuviro arihe

### Okukwata

- **Okupata omake.** Ṱunisa okamutanda komuti wosikoti momake woye nu pata kouṱiṱi okuza koruwoko nga kominwe. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) i tamuna okupata nomuti wosikoti mbu hi nombepo kokupa omahuhumiṋino.
- **Okambaze kozombanda.** Ozombanda ozonene za velvet, corduroy, fleece, satin nowool okukwata, okuzinga nokuhungirira ko.
- **Otjitambaa otjiṋamu.** Otjitambaa otjiṋamu, otjinyondorokwa tjomake meyuva ekutu. Tara oupyu wotjo koruwoko rwoye rutenga.
- **Otjimbamba tjoviungura poo otjiyapuke tjomakoti.** Ozombandi ozonene, omazipero, omazenga nozombanda zomivare pekepeke mbi hondwa nawa.
- **Okuzinga nokuhinina.** Okambaze kovitambaa poo omatowel omakohoke okuzinga, okututa nokuzinga rukwao.
- **Otjiṋa otjingozu tjokukambura.** Otjiyapuke tja fleece, otjiyandekwa poo otjipuka otjingozu tji tji munika tjiri, tji yandjwa mouṱiṱi, ka otjiraisiro tjovanatje.

### Okunuka

- **Omiti omipe.** Twanga omayeva wa mint, rosemary, basil poo lavender pokati kominwe vyoye nu yandja kombanda.
- **Ozombepo zondjuwo yokurongera.** Okofi ndji mai telekwa, omboroto ndji mai ningirwa, cinnamon poo ehape ndi mari tuwa popezu.
- **Ozongara.** Eta ozongara ozonḓiṱi okuza motjikunino poo motjitoro.
- **Otjiṋa tji tji tjiukwa.** Osopo poo omuti wosikoti mbu va ungurisa aruhe, indu osikoti tji tji yenena okutjita nao.

### Okuroora

- **Omunaningiriro omusuverwa.** Okatjiteṋi kotjiṋa tji va suvera, tjimuna applesauce, pudding poo aisikirimi, momuhingo omuwa ngu ve yenena okuroora nawa.
- **Okuvatera mokuninga ovikurya.** Okuroora ovihape poo okatjiteṋi komuti omutjate tji mamu ningi ovikurya pamwe.
- **Ovikurya vyoruveze rwombura.** Ovihape mombura yomeyuva, otjinwino otjiṋamu motjikukututu, tji yandjwa moupyu omuwa.

### Okuzuva

- **Omizumbi omisuverwa.** Oviimbo mbi vi tjiukwa mombosiro ombupu; tara [oviungura vyomizumbi](/activities/music/).
- **Ombosiro yozondjendje.** Ombura, omakuthikuthi womuronga poo omizumbi vyozonḓera. NIA i tja omizumbi, ombosiro ombupu nozombosiro zondjendje mavi munika amavi huhumiṋa ovandu tjiva.
- **Eraka roye.** Okuresa mokuposa, okuimburura kouṱiṱi, poo okuserekarera ehungi.
- **Ovihumba oviṱiṱi.** Ongoma yomake, otjihumba tjokuzunguza poo ongonga okunyanda pamwe nomizumbi.

### Okumuna

- **Okutara pendje pomahoro.** Otjiraisiro tjozonḓera pendje pomahoro, ovanatje mbe ryanga okuyaruka konganda, poo ombura yomakende ndji mai u.
- **Otjiyanda tjehi poo otjiperendero otjingozu.** Omakurungiro omangozu nokuhina ombosiro onyingi.
- **Ozombirimbi ozonene.** Otjiperendero tjimwe otjipaturuke poruveze rumwe, tja kamburwa poruveze rwomeho.
- **Omivare nomiano.** Okutoorora ozombanda ozombomba, ozongara poo omatowel womivare, poo ehanyo eripupu okuza motjiyandjewa tjetu [tjokutjanga omivare](/games/coloring/).
- **Pendje.** NHS i tunduuza ovikunino vyomazuviro pu pe nomiti mbi nuka nawa, omiano pekepeke nomeva. Tara [oviungura pendje nokututa otjikunino](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Omuano wokutunga okambaze komazuviro

1. **Toorora otjitjitwa** tji tji hitasana nehupo romundu: omukuro wokuvare, otjikunino, ondjuwo yokurongera, otjiunguro tjomapiranganda, okuhondja.
2. **Toorora oviṋa vitano poo hamboumwe ovinene, oviwa,** tjimuna otjitenda otjinene tjokuvare, otjihuro tjomuti, otjitambaa otjingozu, okaputu komayeva wa lavender nga omakukutu nga hondwa, poo ekende romuti ndi hina omiano omizeu.
3. **Tara otjiṋa atjihe** kutja kape noviṋa oviṱiṱi, omiano omaṱwe, ozongodhi nḓa kutuka poo otjiṋa tji tji teya.
4. **Pita notjiṋa tjimwe poruveze rumwe.** Tji yandja, tji kahurura, nu yandjera omundu e tji roro.
5. **Twa okambaze kokure kombunda** kokutja ke kare otjisemba nu oviṋa vi tarwe rukwao komurungu woruveze ndu maru ya.

## Ondjeverero rutenga

Oviungura vyomazuviro ovihange, posi ya omauzeu tjiva ye nonḓengu. Ovandu tjiva mbe hupa nondjenda yokuzemba mave vara oviṋa mbi ha ri ovikurya otjovikurya, nu okuninina ku kara nouzeu oruveze tji maru kayenda; NIA i raere kutja omauzeu wokuninina motjipaze otjisembasemba ye kurisa ombango yokuninwa.

| Ombango | Tji mo sokutjita |
| --- | --- |
| Okuninwa | Ungurisa uriri oviṋa ovinene mbi ha sorwa okuninwa. O ha twamo ozombandi, ouṱiṱi, ovimariva, omakende omaṱiṱi poo ovitjendjere oviṱiṱi mokambaze. Yandja ovikurya uriri momiano otjira tjondjeverero tji tja tja vi ri nawa. |
| Oviṋa mbi munika tjimuna ovikurya | NIA i tunduuza okupata omiti vyosikoti, omisopo, omitjise vyoviṱumbu nozombepo, mena rokutja mavi munika nokunuka tjimuna ovikurya, nokuisapo ovihape mbya ungurwa. Twa oviṋa vyomazuviro kokure kombunda yokuvitjita. |
| Omauzeu womukova | Tara omauzeu womukova ku nondovi, ku na latex, ku na wool, ku nomiti poo kozombepo komurungu wokuta. |
| Ozombepo ozonamasa | Yepa ozombepo ozonamasa nomaze wombepo omanamasa, tjinenenene indu pe nouzeu wokusuva poo omauzeu warwe wokusuva. Ozombepo ozombupu, ozondjendje ze ri nawa tjinene. |
| Osikoti | Tara osikoti kutja kape nomaputiro, omapateko poo osikoti osingozu komurungu wokupata. Ungurisa omasa omangozu nu kurama indu tja munika a hihamwa. |
| Oupyu | Tara ovitambaa oviṋamu noviṋa vyokunwa oviṋamu koruwoko rwoye rutenga. |
| Oviṋa oviṱwe poo ovizeu | O ha ungurisa ovitjendjere oviṱwe, omagirasi poo oviungurisiwa vyomake mbi novizeu. |

## Okuzuva ozombepo ku vatera?

Mape ya a ha ri momuano mbu mau sora okumunika. [Omatarero wa Cochrane wokuzuva ozombepo kondjenda yokuzemba](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) wa tara omakonḓononeno 13 novandu 708, avehe mozonganda zondjeverero, nu kaa muna oumbangi mbu raisa oukahu wombatero. Omakonḓononeno ya ri omaṱiṱi, omaziriro kaa hitasana, nu omauzeu kaa kahururwa nawa. Indu omundu tja suvera ombepo ombupu ndje i tjiwa, tjimuna lavender okuza motjikunino, otjo otjiṋa otjiwa okutjihaṋa. Katji ri omuti.

## Omuano wokuyandja otjiungura tjomazuviro

- **Toorora oruveze rwombepo** nu zepa oratiyo.
- **Ya komurungu,** tja ena re, nu pura poo mu raere komurungu wokukwata: "Mbi nomuti wosikoti. Mo vanga okupatwa komake?"
- **Yandja otjiṋa tjimwe poruveze rumwe** kokutja a ha urisiwa.
- **Tara eziriro.** Omurungu omungozu, omake nga suva poo onyemumwe oviyakisa oviwa. Okuritana, okuṱuna omurungu poo okuhina omahuhumiṋino mavi hee kutja kurama.
- **Tji ṱiza otjisupi.** Ominute vitano vyombepo otjiungura otjiwa.

Okusuva pamwe kouṱiṱi, tjimuna [okusuva nombepo](/games/calm-breathing/), ku sora okurira omuano omungozu wokumana. Komiano vyarwe pombete, tara [oviungura vyotjipaze otjisembasemba](/activities/late-stage/), nu komahindiro wongurova, tara [okuhina omahuhumiṋino kongurova](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
