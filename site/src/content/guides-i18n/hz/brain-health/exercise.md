Okuhupa nokuhonga orutu otjiṋa tjimwe moviṋa mbi vya konḓononwa tjinene momakonḓononeno wouhupi wotjiuru. Omakonḓononeno maye tundu ihi pamwe noundjova outiṱiṱiṱiṱi wouvera wokuzemba, posi yokutja kaye raisire kutja omahongero maye tjaere ouvera wokuzemba. Otjiṋa tji tja ri oukahu kutja okuyenda ku vatera ozongozu, ombalanḓe, omuinyo omuwa nomboṱu mozombura azehe. Omukuma mbwi mau hungire kutja omakonḓononeno maye tja vi, ovakurundu ve sokutjita omahongero ae ngapi, nomiano omipore vyokuyenda pamwe nomundu ngu hupa nouvera wokuzemba.

## Omahongero maye ṱuna oundjova wouvera wokuzemba?

Omakonḓononeno maye raisa kutja ovandu mbe honga orutu mape ya ave ha kara nouvera wokuzemba tjinene, posi yokutja omahongero ye nozongaro. [Oruteto rwa Lancet Commission rwouvera wokuzemba rwa 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) ru tja okuhinokuhonga orutu otjiṋa tjimwe moviṋa 14 mbi mavi yenene okurundururwa. Oviṋa mbi avihe 14 mavi tjiti oukukutu mbu ri pu 45% wouvera wokuzemba mouye auhe motjihorera tjavyo.

Omahongero maye munika nai:

- **Omakonḓononeno wokutarera maye raisa otjiṋa tjimwe.** Omakonḓononeno womakonḓononeno 58, nge hungirwa i Oruteto, ya muna kutja ovandu mbe honga orutu va ri noundjova outiṱiṱiṱiṱi mbu ri pu 20% wouvera wokuzemba. Ombangu onene ya ri pokati kovandu mbaave ha hongo kaparukaze na imba mbaave tjiti nandarire okaṱiṱi.
- **Ombwaneno mape ya ai zu momikuma vivari.** Oruteto ru tja okuhinokuhonga orutu mape ya aru kurisa oundjova. Ru tja wina omarundurukiro omatenga motjiuru wouvera wokuzemba, nge uta ozombura ozengi komurungu wondjiviro, mape ya aye tjiti kutja ovandu ve hongo oviṱiṱi.
- **Omazembo kaye ri oukahu tjinene.** Mezembo rimwe, ovakurundu 945 mbu va ri nozombura 78 pokati va pewa omahongero wokutongwa tuvari mosondaha oure wozombura ndano poo va pewa otjimwe. Komaandero, ouvara wokuripura kaau ri nombangu onene pokati kozombunga. Ovandu ovengi motjimwe va ri amave hongo wina, nu ihi tji tjita kutja ondjiviro i rire ouzeu okulesa.

[Omutjuta wOmautiro wOuhona (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know) mau hungire oukahu. Kape nombwaneno onene pokati komahongero worutu nokutjaera ouvera wa Alzheimer ndja raisirwa, nu kape nomahongero womazembo nga yenene okutja omahongero maye tjaere poo maye kuramisa. Omahongero nandarire nao maye ṱuna oundjova wokuwira, omuinyo mbu wa nyanda, ouvera wosukari, ombindu yokombanda nondjise yombinḓu motjiuru. Ozombara tjiva mu zo ze ri nombwaneno nokunyanguka kwouripura.

Ondjiviro youatjiri oyo kutja mo sokuyenda mena rokutja ku ku vatera okumuna nokutjita nawa ndino. Ouwa auhe wotjiuru mape ya au kara otjiyandjewa, kakwizike.

## Ovakurundu ve hepa omahongero ae ngapi?

[Omirari vya CDC vyovakurundu mbe nozombura 65 kombanda](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/older-adults.html) mavi tunduuza omihoko vitatu vyomahongero mosondaha aihe:

| Omuhoko | Omuano mosondaha | Ovitjitwa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Omahongero wombepo | Ominute 150 pu pe ṱiṱi vyomahongero wopokati, tjimuna ominute 30 momayuva 5 | Okuyenda hakahana, okupunda, omahongero wombepo momeva |
| Okupamisa ozongozu | Omayuva 2 pu pe ṱiṱi, okuungura ozongozu azehe ozonene | Okuyeura ovipamisa ovipupu poo ovikopo vyosupa, omiveto vyokunana |
| Ombalanḓe | Aruhe | Okuyenda otjitende kombunda yotjitende, okusekama okuza motjihavero |

Ihi ovihavero, kaviṋa mbi mo sokutjita rutenga. CDC mai tja tji u hi nokuyenena okuvihazenga, mo sokuhonga pu mo yenene otja kounongo nouhupi woye tji mau yandjere. Okuhonga kaṱiṱi ku ri nawa pu okuhinakutjita kaparukaze.

## Omiano omipore vyokuyenda kovakurundu

- **Mu kayende pamwe.** Okuyenda pamwe nepanga, omuraranganda poo otjimbumba tjovayendi eyuva arihe ku weza ehungiriro nondjeverero.
- **Omahongero motjihavero.** Okuyeura omaoko amu haere, okutjendja ozombaze, okuyenda poruveze nokuyoha motjihavero vi pamisa ozongozu nokuhinoundjova wombalanḓe.
- **Mu punde mozombanguriro.** Mu yandeke ombosiro yoruveze rwomundu rwomitanda nozombura omirongo vivari nu mu nyenganyenga, mu tone poo mu yende. [Omutwaro wetu womahongero wombosiro](/activities/music/) u nozondunge.
- **Ozongozu noviṋa vyoponganda.** Mu yeure ovikopo vyomeva mbi vya ura poo ovikopo vyosupa amu tarere otjiprograma tji mwa suvera.
- **Mu ungure motjikunino poo mu ungure oviungura vyoponganda.** Okutjatja ovikunwa, okukomba, okututa nokupeta ozombanda avihe vi varwa. Tara [omahongero wopendje nomotjikunino](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) novitjitwa [vyoviungura vyoponganda](/activities/household-tasks/).
- **Omahongero momeva.** NIA mai hungire omahongero wombepo momeva otjomuhingo umwe kovandu mbe muna kutja omahongero wokombanda yehi omauzeu kozombura.

## Omahongero pamwe nomundu ngu hupa nouvera wokuzemba

Komundu ngu hupa nouvera wokuzemba, ondando oyo ombwiro, ondjoroka nokukara nouvara wokutjita oviṋa vyeyuva arihe, kakuyenenisa onomora. NIA mai tja omahongero worutu ye vatera okukara nozongozu, ozombura nomutima vyomundu ngu nouvera wa Alzheimer mouhupi ouwa, nu mape ya aye vatere wina omuinyo omuwa nomboṱu. Hungira nonganga yawo ngunda amu hiya uta omuano omupe. Omaziriro okuza [momutwaro wa NIA kovateṱi](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) ye kamburira mo:

1. **Mu kayende pamwe eyuva arihe.** Mu toorore ondjira ndji ri mondjeverero, ndji mwa tjiwa nawa nu ndji hi noviṋa mbi mavi tjaere. Omundu tji ma kayenda erike, ouhande wokomoko mbu u nondjombo yoye yongodi otjiṋa otjiwa.
2. **Mu roro ovivideo vyomahongero vyovakurundu.** Mu kongorere pamwe, amu haere poo amu kurama.
3. **Mu kare noruveze orusupi.** "Omahongero omasupi" omengi mape ya aye ungura nawa pu rimwe orire.
4. **Mu weze ombosiro.** Ombosiro ndji va tjiwa i tjiti kutja okuyenda ku munike otjotjiṋa tjokuyorokera, katjiungura. Mu punde omundu tji me tji vanga.
5. **Mu pe omeva nozombahu ozombwa.** Mu pe omeva poo omeva wovihape moruveze nokombunda, nu mu toorore ozombanda ozombwa nozombahu nḓe ri nawa, nḓa ungurirwa omahongero.

Okuyenda tji tje ri ouzeu, [omutwaro wa NIA wokutjevera omundu nouvera wa Alzheimer](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) mau tunduuza oviṋa mbi mavi tjitwa amu haere. Mbi kamburira mo omahongero motjihavero poo oyoha, okuyumba ozombehe, okuyumba otjimbundu otjipupu poo ombalona, omiveto vyokunana, okuyeura oviṋa ovipupu vyoponganda, nomatemba womahongero womakoti. Oviungura ovipupu tjimuna okututa nokukomba wina vi varwa.

Mu kare nouatjiri kutja okuyenda ku yenena okutjita tjike. Oruteto rwa Lancet kaaru muna omahongero wokutja omahongero maye ṱuna omarundurukiro womuinyo tjimuna ondjindiro. Mu kayende pamwe mena rokutja mape ya atji munika nawa, hakutja mu rundurure eyuva ezeu. Omundu tji ma tja indee, pe ri nawa. Omutwaro wetu ohunga na [indu tji ma tja indee](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) u nomiano omipore vyokurora rukwao kombunda. Okumana rukuru, poo okukara pamwe pendje, wina ondjiviro ombwa.

Kombunda yokuyenda, okusuva okapaze tjimuna [Okusuva Momporiro](/games/calm-breathing/) ku yenena okumuvatera amuyevari okuporimana. Kotjiṋa tji matji tjitwa amu haere nokuyenda okaṱiṱi kwomaoko, [Okutwaa Pamwe](/games/sort-together/) na [Ombingo Yozombisasaneno](/games/picture-bingo/) vi ungura nawa potjiriro.

## Ombalanḓe nondjeverero koviwa

Okuwira ku tjitwa potuingi nu ku ri ounandengu. [Omutjuta wa CDC mau tja](https://www.cdc.gov/falls/data-research/facts-stats/index.html) ovakurundu kombanda yotjimwe movine ve wa ombura aihe, posi yokutja kehi yokapindi ke hungire nonganga yawo. Okuwira rumwe ku tjita kutja omaundjiro wokuwira rukwao ye rise po tuvari. Oviṋa vyoundjova CDC mbi mai hungire vi kamburira mo:

- Okungundipara mozoma nomozombinga
- Ouzeu wokuyenda nowombalanḓe
- Omitjise tjiva, mu mbyo omu na imbi mbi porimanisa notjiva vyomuinyo mbu wa nyanda
- Ouzeu womeho
- Ombindu mozombaze poo ozombahu nḓe he ri nawa
- Oundjova ponganda tjimuna ovitenda, ouyapuke wokombanda nozombandero nḓa teka

Okuyenda mondjeverero tjinene:

- **Mu ungurise otjihavero otjinamasa** tji tji hi novikokavero, nu mu kare notjiriro poo otjiyameke popezu komahongero wokusekama.
- **Mu kohorore poṋa** mu ise ouyapuke, ozongoye novitenda rutenga.
- **Mu sekame noupore**, nu mu kurameke indu omundu tji ma munika otjizunguzungu poo ke nombalanḓe.
- **Mu zare ozombahu nḓe yama**, kazokauzu poo ozondjatate nḓa kutuka.
- **Mu rihonge ombalanḓe popezu nopoṋa pu pa yama.** Okusekama okuza motjihavero nokuyenda otjitende kombunda yotjitende ovitjitwa vyombalanḓe mbi vya hungirwa i CDC.

CDC mai tunduuza kutja omukurundu ngwa wa nu a tono motjiuru ma sokukaryanga konganga tjimanga, tjinene tji ma nu omitjise vyokuhinotjita kutja ombinḓu i pame.

## Hungira nonganga

Hungira nonganga ngunda au hiya uta omuano omupe womahongero, tjinene komundu ngu nouzeu womutima poo womapunga, ombindu yozombura, ouzeu wombalanḓe, poo ouvera wokuzemba. Onganga poo omuvatere womahongero worutu ma yenene okutunduuza omahongero nge ri mondjeverero nokutara kutja omitjise vi ṱuna ombalanḓe. Raera onganga okuwira akuhe, nandarire ku ku hi noruvio. Otjiṋa tji tji hihamisa poo tji tji hi nokumunika nawa indu tji mo hongo, kurama. Tji pe nombindu yomutima tjimanga, ouzeu wokusuvana, poo omapirukira tjimanga mouripura, tuna kongombe yozombanguriro zongendero popezu na ove.

Omukuma mbwi omahunganekero womuinyo, kawo omirari vyonganga. Tara [omazikameno wetu womitjise](/medical-disclaimer/). Okumuna otjiperendero otjinene, lesa [kutja omakonḓononeno maye tja vi ohunga noviṋa vyoundjova wouvera wokuzemba](/brain-health/risk-factors/) na [kutja ouvera wokuzemba mau yenene okutjaerwa](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).
