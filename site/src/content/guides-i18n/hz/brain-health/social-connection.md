Ovandu va ungurirwa okukara pamwe novakwao, nu indi katji runduruka noukurundu. Omapekapeko maye panga okukara porwe nokurihuurira motjiwondo tjokombunda noumba mbwa kurira wokuvazemba ombongo, wina nomitjise vyomutima nokuhepa momuinyo. Omapekapeko nga kaye yenene nai okutja kutja orupa oruṋe rwondjira ndji ru ri ombango poo otjihorera. Ehupo ndi mari kahurura imbi omaṋunḓiro tji maye raisa, omiano vyorutu oku kara motjihuro novakwao, nokutja ongwaye oviungura mbi mu ungura pamwe tji vi nondengu tjinene kovandu mbe kara nokuvazemba ombongo nai.

## Okukara porwe nokurihuurira vi ri pokati nokuvazemba ombongo?

Ii, omapekapeko omengi maye vi panga, nungwari ondjira kaina raisiwa nawa. [Oruteto rwa Lancet ndwa 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) ru tja okurihuurira motjiwondo tjokombunda otjimwe tjoviṋa 14 mbi yenena okurundururwa mbi yandja oumba. [Omahungiriro wa UCL woruteto ndwi](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) ye ru twa nozoperesende 5 zovihorera vyokuvazemba ombongo mouye auhe.

Imbi omaṋunḓiro tji maye raisa:

- **Omakutiro omaṱiṱi, oumba mbwa kurira.** Oruteto ru hungira omakonḓononeno yevari nga panga okukara novandu ovaṱiṱi pokati noumba mbwa kurira wokuvazemba ombongo. Rimwe ra muna oumba mbwa kurira nozoperesende 50 nu 7, nu rukwao nozoperesende 18.
- **Okukara porwe wina.** Okukara porwe, omuano wokurimuna wa haṋewa, wina wa ri pokati noumba mbwa kurira wokuvazemba ombongo momakonḓononeno omengi.
- **Omuhunga wa Ombara Onduna youveruke.** Mu May 2023, Ombara Onduna youveruke moAmerika ya pitisa [omambo womahongero wokukara porwe nokurihuurira](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf). Owo maye tja okukara porwe nokurihuurira oruveze orure ku yenena okukurisa oumba wokuvazemba ombongo nozoperesende 50 movakurundu. Wina ya panga omapanga omayakise novandu noumba mbwa kurira nozoperesende 29 womitjise vyomutima noumba mbwa kurira nozoperesende 32 wostroke.
- **Omu tji tji takavara.** [Oruteto rwomaunguriro omakuru wouye](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557972/) rwa muna kutja ovandu moAmerika mbe nozombura 65 nokombanda mbe kara motjirongo, nozoperesende 24 ve rihuurira.

Omahongero omanandengu: Oruteto rwa Lancet ru tja ovandu mape ya ave ripunguhire koviungura vyovandu moruveze rwokutenga rwokuvazemba ombongo, ngunda katjiya tjivirwa. Ihi matji hee kutja orupa rwondjira ndji ru yenena okurira okuvazemba ombongo tji ku tjita kutja omundu e riyerure, kaku ri okuriyerura porwakuo uriri tji ku kurisa oumba. Ouingi womaṋunḓiro nga wa zikamena komakonḓononeno womatarero uriri.

## Ombanguṋe pokati kokukara porwe nokurihuurira?

[Orutu rwongerki roukurundu moAmerika (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/loneliness-and-social-isolation/loneliness-and-social-isolation-tips-staying-connected) ru kahurura nai. **Okurihuurira** ku heya okukara nomakutiro omaṱiṱi novandu poo novandu ovaṱiṱi okuhungira na vo aruhe. **Okukara porwe** omuano omuzeu wokurimuna u ri erike poo wa haṋewa. Omundu ma yenene okukara erike nenyando, nu omundu ma yenene okukara porwe nandarire mondjuwo ndje ura novandu.

NIA mai tja oviṋa mbi kurisa oumba wyoviṋa aviyevari:

- Okuzuva poo okumuna mbya ngundipara
- Ouzeu wokuryanga, poo okuhinotjiyendewa
- Okukara erike, poo otjiponga otjinene otja onḓiro yomukwepo poo okupata oviungura
- Omarundurukiro mozemburukiro poo mozondunge
- Okutjevera omusuverwa ngu nouzeu wouveruke
- Omerikendero wouini poo ombanguṋe yeraka

Okuzuva otjiṋa otjinene. NIA mai tja ovandu mbe hi nokuzuva nawa mape ya ave riyerure mena rokutja omahungiriro maye rire omauzeu poo omayonḓi. [Omahongero wetu wokuzuva nouveruke wombongo](/brain-health/hearing/) maye hungire omarorero wokuzuva novivatera vyokuzuva mbi randwa noupupu.

## Omiano vyorutu oku kara motjihuro novakwao

NIA mai yandja omiano omingi. Toorora rumwe poo vivari mbi ri nawa kove:

- **Rongera omakutiro aruhe.** Oruveze ndu rwa zikama eyuva arihe poo otjivike atjihe okuhaka motjingo, okutuma ombuze, okuhungira moemail poo okutuma okarata keṱunḓu, komapanga poo kovararanganda.
- **Ungurisa ovidiyo poo ovitjikwa vyondunge.** Indu outjindjikwa tji ri oupe, omara omengi womambo novitjitwa vyotjirongo vi yandja omarorero. Tara [omahongero wetu womahungiriro motjingo na movidiyo](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/).
- **Hita motjiṋa.** Orutu rwokurihonga, otjira tjeongarero, otjimbumba tjokuryanga poo oviungura vyokuvatera vi ku pa omakutiro nomuano wokumuna ondando.
- **Uta rukwao otjiungura otjikuru** poo rihonga otjipe pamwe novakwao.
- **Ryanga pamwe novakwao.** Ungura omaroroke pamwe nepanga poo otjimbumba. [Omahongero wetu womaroroke](/brain-health/exercise/) ye noviyandjewa oviṱiṱi.
- **Ripura otjipuka** indu tji mo yenene okutjitjevera.
- **Ungurisa oviungura vyopopezu.** Ovitjitwa vyovakurundu, omaunguriro wovakurundu wotukondwa na Eldercare Locator (800-677-1116) mave yenene okukuvatera okumuna ovitjitwa noviyendewa.

## Ongwaye oviungura mbi mu ungura pamwe tji vi nondengu movazemba ombongo?

Komundu ngwa kara nokuvazemba ombongo nai, okukara pamwe ku na ondengu yenyando, ombepo omuwa nokurimuna wa yakurwa, kaku ri okuṱuna oumba. Oruteto rwa Lancet ru tja oviungura vi vatera ovandu mbe novazemba ombongo okukara nenyando nondando. [Orutu rwokuvatera ovandu novazemba ombongo rwa Alzheimer (Alzheimer's Association)](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ru tja, pendje yokuyarura omuinyo omuwa, oviungura mavi yenene okuṱuna omikambo otja okuryanga nokuhinondando poo ombamburiro. [NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) mai weza ko kutja ovandu mbe novazemba ombongo wa Alzheimer ve hepa okukara novitjitwa nokutjita oviṋa mbi ve nonyuṋe mu vyo.

Omiano nge ungura nawa:

1. **Tarera oviperendero pamwe.** [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ye yandja omapuriro omapaturuke, nge hi nomakonḓononeno, otja “Ndji serekarerera...”. Tara [omahongero wetu womazemburukiro](/activities/reminiscence/).
2. **Nyanda pamwe.** [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) i kara noviperendero avi munika nu kai noruveze poo oviṋa vyokuvaza, ku nao kape nomahindiriro. [Omahongero wetu wokunyanda pamwe](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ye nomahongero.
3. **Ungurisa oviperendero vyomaṱunḓu.** [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) i tjita oviperendero mbi wa toorora otjinyanda tjokupasana, nu oviperendero vi kara motjitjikwa tjoye.
4. **Eta ovandu pamwe.** [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) i ungura nawa pomawongarero womaṱunḓu na [movitjimbumba vyotjivanda tjondjevero](/activities/care-home-groups/).
5. **Hana omuimbu, oviungura vyonganda poo otjikunino.** Tara [ovitjitwa vyomuimbu](/activities/music/), [oviungura vyonganda](/activities/household-tasks/) nondjira yetu aihe [yomiano vyoviungura](/activities/ideas/).
6. **Tjita omayendo omapupu.** [Omahongero wetu womayendo](/activities/visiting/) maye hungire omayendo omasupi, omaporome nge mu pa avehe ombepo omuwa.

Kara notjiṋa otjiṱiṱi nu porumwe. Ondjuwo ndji poroma nomundu umwe poo vevari potuingi i ungura nawa okukapita otjimbumba otjinene. Indu omundu tja urwa poo tja pandjara onyune, okukurama ku ri nawa. Okukara pamwe nokunwa otee wina otjihorera otjiwa.

Komundu ngu kara nokuvazemba ombongo erike, NIA mai yandja ondunge yokupaha omundu omuṱakame ngu ma yenene okumuyenda poo okumuhaka movidiyo aruhe nokurira omuhaka womehungo. Wina mai hungire okafe yomazemburukiro yopopezu, oruveze rwomapanga oruporome kovandu mbe nozemburukiro nḓa ngundipara nomaṱunḓu wawo.

## Ovatjevere wina ve kara porwe

NIA mai tja okutjevera omusuverwa otjimwe tjoviṋa mbi kurisa oumba wokurihuurira. Tjevera oruveze kovyo omuini rwomapanga woye, ovitjimbumba vyombatero nokusuva. [Omahongero wetu wokuritjevera kwomutjevere](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ye nomiano vyorutu.

## Hungira nonganga

Indu ove poo omundu ngu mo tjevere mu rimuna mu ri porwe poo mwa rihuurira potuingi, raera onganga. NIA mai tunduuza ovandu okuhungira omarundurukiro omanene momuinyo, otja onḓiro meṱunḓu, kokutja onganga i tjiwe ondjiviro aihe. Okurimuna wa nyonoka oure wovivike ku yenena okurira okuhepa momuinyo, ku ku yenena okuverwa. Indu omukurundu a uta okuriyerura komapanga poo koviṋa mbyaa suverere, erundurukiro ndi ri ri nawa okuritjaera konganga. Lesa [oviṋa vyokutenga nokutja oruṋe tji mo sokuyenda konganga](/brain-health/warning-signs/).

Ehupo ndi ondjiviro ya ngamwa, kai ri omuhunga wonganga. Tara [omahongononeno wetu wokutja kai ri omuhunga wonganga](/medical-disclaimer/).
