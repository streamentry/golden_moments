Oudano okupweda ko etumbulo ohau longifa etumbulo la shiivika nawa olo tali kanghama manga inali fika koshitya shalo shaxuuninwa. Kovanhu vahapu, osho ohashi kala ondjila iwa yokuhafela efimbo pamwe nopehe na oudjuu. Oudano ou ohau longo nawa molwaashi eendjovo oda kala da shiivika nale nokuli onale, nomunhu ina pumbwa okudimbuluka sha kombinga yonena. Omukanda ou otau yelifa nghee tamu dulu okudana, nghee tamu hoolola omatumbulo, nanghee tamu shi kaleke sha ngungumana.

## Omaudano okupweda ko omatumbulo oshike?

Oudano omuxupi omo omunhu umwe ta lesha oshitukulwa shotete shetumbulo ndele mukwao ta pweda ko exulifo. Oihopaenenwa oi li ngaashi «Oilonga ohai popi i dule...» na «Okaana keenyofi ohaka kwata...» Omunhu ota dulu okupopya exulifo mokule, oku li ulika, ile okuhoolola mexulifo limwe lomomahoololo avali ile atatu oo tamu mu pe. Ovanhu vamwe ohava hafele okukala ovo tava lesha oshitukulwa shotete.

Golden Moments oi na oudano ou onga [oudano wa yandjwa oshali okupweda ko omatumbulo](/games/finish-the-saying/) noitya inene. Otamu dulu yo oku u dana nopehe na oshivi, tamu tumbula ashike oshitukulwa shotete ndele tamu teelele.

## Nghee tamu dana, okatukulwa kokatukulwa

1. **Hoololeni efimbo la ngungumana** ndele tamu kala omutumba mu li pomesho, mu taalelafana.
2. **Lesheni oshitukulwa shotete kashona nakashona,** newi la nyika ohole, ndele tamu kanghama. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) otai kumaida okupopya kashona nokulongifa omatumbulo maxupi nomayele.
3. **Teeleleni.** Mu peeni oule weedimbuluko. Ngeenge kape na sha tashi uya, mu peeni omahoololo avali ile atatu exulifo, limwe lomuo li li mondjila.
4. **Hafeleni enyumuko keshe.** «Heeno, olo tuu olo» ile «Inandi shi diladila ngaho nale» otashi kaleke oinima ya nyika oukaume.
5. **Kundafaneni kombinga yetumbulo.** Mu puleni kutya otali ti shike kuye, ile okwe li uda peni. Apa opo pe na oshilonga sholela.
6. **Kanghameni manga inava loloka.** Omatumbulo mashona nehafo lixupi otashi wanifa efimbo liwa.

## Okuyandja omahoololo nopehe na omapulo taa yelekwa

Omahoololo okuhoolola exulifo otaa dulu okuningifa oudano u kale upu, ashike otaa dulu yo okuuviwa ngaashi ekonakono ngeenge otaa yandjwa onga eshongo. Kalekeni omhepo ya manguluka. Tumbuleni «Owa hala okuuda exulifo ile okuhoolola limwe?» ponhele yokutya «Oshilipi shi li mondjila?» Yandjeni omahoololo konima eshi mwa teelela, hamanga omunhu inaa mona ompito yokunyamukula.

Ngeenge omunhu okwa handuka ile okwa fya ohoni, efaeni oudano ndele tamu i kushimwe. [Omayele etu okukwatafana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) otaa yelifa nghee tamu dulu okulundulula oshinima nopehe na okupiyaaneka.

## Okuhoolola omatumbulo oo taa wapalele omunhu

- **Longifeni omatumbulo omunhu mwene.** Puleni ovakwaneumbo kutya xe ile omushamane wavo oha ti shike luhapu. Omatumbulo okounona ohaa kala mapu okudimbulukiwa.
- **Hoololeni omatumbulo maxupi omomwenyo wa keshe efiku.** Outumbulilo kombinga yonghalo yomhepo, okombitha, moshikunino, oukwaneumbo nomafimbo omudo ohau shiivika nawa nohapu oku u landula.
- **Kalekeni mayela.** Omatumbulo oo a kwatelela keendjovo donale ile kelongo la kwata moiti otashi dulu okunyemateka. A kalekeni kwaavo have a hafele.
- **Taleni elaka.** Omatumbulo mahapu ihaa tolokwa nawa. Longifeni omatumbulo oo omunhu e a shiiva melaka laye mwene, nonande omu na oku a konga.

## Omatumbulo paitukulwa

Oshihopaenenwa keshe shi li apa otashi ulike exulifo momakefa opo mu shi mone. Lesheni ashike oshitukulwa shotete, ndele tamu teelele.

- **Onghalo yomhepo:** «Odula manga inai toka...» (exulifo: «etango otali piti diva»); «Efuta keshe oli na...» (ongaba yalo).
- **Okombitha noikulya:** «Ovateleki vahapu...» (ohava nyona oshuumbululwa); «Ino vala oudjona manga...» (inakwi dalwa).
- **Eumbo noukwaneumbo:** «Eumbo omo omutima...» (u li); «Ohonde oi dule...» (omeva).
- **Omafimbo omudo noshikunino:** «Odula yaApilili otai eta...» (eengala daMei); «Osho to kunu osho to...» (teya).

## Ongahelipi ngeenge omunhu ita dulu okunyamukula?

Ovanhu vamwe otashi dulika vehe mone eendjovo peenhele dopokuxuuninwa. Inamu efa po etumbulo. Li lesheni, limweneneni, ndele tamu shivi enyumuko neuliko lomake, nokunwefa omutwe, ile mokuhoolola efano. Okukala pamwe nomulandu wa shiivika natango oku li efimbo tamu hafele pamwe. Udafanifeni oudano ou nomusika, oshikunwa sha xwama, ile okuhola eke, ngaashi mu [oilonga yavo ve li peenhele dopokuxuuninwa](/activities/late-stage/).

## Eenghedi dikwao dokulongifa omatumbulo

Omatumbulo otaa twala nawa keenghundafana kombinga yonakudiwa yomunhu. Otamu dulu okushiva omahokololo nomapulo aa e li mu [omapulo okudimbulukifa onakudiwa](/activities/reminiscence-questions/), ile tamu dana oudano wa faafana ngaashi [oukalata veenghundafana](/games/conversation-cards/). Komadiladilo mahapu efiku alishe, taleni [oilonga yovanhu ovo ve na oudimbu komadiladilo](/activities/ideas/).
