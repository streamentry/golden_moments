Okudimbuluka pamwe oshinima shokupopya pamwe kombinga yonakudiwa: omaumbo ounona, oilonga yotete, oikulya oyo omunhu e hole, omaimbilo, eehango nomafudo. Kovanhu vahapu hava kala noupyakadi wokudimbwa, eenghundafana diwa kombinga yonghalamwenyo yavo otadi dulu okukala oshitukulwa shimwe shiwa mefiku. Osho sha fimana okupula monghedi tai shivi ehokololo ndele itai konakona edimbuluko. Omapulo aa okwa tulwa moitopolwa, pamwe nomayele okukulongifa.

## Omilandu domapulo okudimbuluka

- **Pula omapulo a manguluka.** «Lombwele nge kombinga ya...» na «Okwa li kwa tya ngahelipi...» otaa shivi ehokololo. «Oho dimbuluka tuu...?» otali pula enyamukulo li li mondjila.
- **Ino konakona nande.** Henuka «Ou olyelye?», «Osha li modula ilipi?» na «Oho dimbuluka tuu edina la...?»
- **Tambula ko keshe eshi tashi uya.** Ngeenge ehokololo ola lunduluka ile eenhele oda lumbakana, hafeleni pamwe ndele to pula kombinga yomaliudo.
- **Longifa epulo limwe pashita.** Teelela, pwilikina ndele to pula sha shipu sha shikula ko.
- **Efa omunhu oye a wilike.** Ngeenge okwa hala okupopya oshinima shimwe shi lili, mu shikula.

[Omayele eenghundafana aNational Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) otaa ladipike okulongifa eendjovo difupi, ondaka ya ngungumana nelididimiko ofimbo omunhu ta kongo eendjovo daye. Omilandu edi oda fimana apa ngaashi keenhele dikwao.

## Eumbo nounona

- Lombwele nge kombinga yeumbo omo wa kulila. Okombitha okwa li kwa tya ngahelipi?
- Owa li ho hafele okuninga shike pondje eshi wa li omunini?
- Oolyelye mwa li hamu danauka pamwe momukunda weni?
- Onduda yoye yokukofa oya li ya tya ngahelipi? Owa li ho i topola nomunhu umwe?

## Oilonga nokulonga oinima

- Oilonga ilipi wa li ho hafele unene?
- Lombwele nge efiku limwe poilonga olo le ku ningifa u kale u udite etumba.
- Oshike wa li ho hafele okuhanga ile okulonga nomake oye?
- Olyelye e ku longa shihapu kombinga yoilonga yoye?

## Oikulya nokuteleka

- Ou kwaneumbo weni omwa li hamu li shike mOsoondaxa?
- Lombwele nge oshikulya osho umwe moukwaneumbo weni a li ha teleke nawa unene.
- Eshi wa li omunini, opa li onhele imwe wa li ho hafele oku ka lya ko sha shiwa?
- Omadimba elipi haa ku dimbulukifa eumbo?

Eenghundafana kombinga yoikulya otadi wapalele nawa nomayele oo e li moshitukulwa [oilonga yokulongifa omaliudo](/activities/sensory/), ile nokumakela sha shinini ngeenge oshi li nawa oku shi yandja.

## Omusika nokudana

- Omaimbilo elipi mwa li hamu imbi ile hamu danene eshi mwa li ovanyasha?
- Olyelye a li omwiimbi ile ongudu yoye oyo wa li u hole unene?
- Pefimbo opo omwa li hamu udu omusika peni: keumbo, peendano, ile kongeleka?

Omalideulo nomayele omusika, tala [oilonga yomusika yovanhu hava kala noupyakadi wokudimbwa](/activities/music/).

## Omafudo nomafimbo ehafo

- Efudo lilipi wa li u hole unene eshi wa li to kulu?
- Lombwele nge efiku ledalo limwe to dimbuluka.
- Ou kwaneumbo omwa li hamu ningi shike konguloshi mefimbo loudjala?
- Ou kwaneumbo weni omwa li hamu dana ngahelipi omafimbo omudo?

Omayele oo taa wapalele efudo lonhumba, tala [oilonga domafudo domunhu ta kala noupyakadi wokudimbwa](/activities/holidays/).

## Ou kwaneumbo nookaume

- Lombwele nge kombinga yomunhu oo a li he ku yoleke unene.
- Efiku lenyumbo leni ola li la tya ngahelipi?
- Lombwele nge kombinga yovana voye eshi va li vanini.
- Ookaume koye ova li have ku ifana ngahelipi?

Pula omapulo kombinga youkwaneumbo noukeka. Ngeenge omunhu ota nyika oluhodi omolwaumwe a fya, mu shikula ndele ino mu pula a dimbuluke oinima oyo tai dulu oku mu ehameka.

## Eenhele neshito

- Lombwele nge onhele imwe oyo u hole okudiladila kuyo.
- Onghalo yomhepo oya li ya tya ngahelipi konhele oko wa kulila?
- Omwa li mu na oshikunino, ile omuti umwe wa fimana?
- Lombwele nge olweendo limwe wa li wa hafela.

## Okulongifa omafano noinima

Omafano ohaa kala etameko liwa leenghundafana. Kwata efano popepi nomesho ndele to ti: «Lombwele nge kombinga yaashi.» [Omayele oilonga aAlzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) otaa ulike okulongifa oinima odo omunhu e shii, omafano nomusika opo ku tamekwe eenghundafana. Okandjato kedimbuluko noinima inini yomonghalamwenyo yomunhu otaka dulu yo okukwafela; tala [oilonga yokudimbuluka](/activities/reminiscence/) u mone nghee to ka longekida.

## Ngeenge edimbuluko otali ehameke

Omadimbuluko amwe otaa eta oluhodi, outile ile ehandu. Ngeenge omunhu ta kala a nyika oluhodi, ino fininika. Popya noukeka to ti: «Osho otashi monika sha li efimbo lidjuu. Owa hala tu popye kombinga yoshinima shimwe tashi hafifa?» Opo nee lundulula oshikundafanwa, mu pe otee, ile u shike eimbilo olo e hole.

## Okulongifa pamwe nomudano ile okweenda pamwe

Konima yokudimbuluka pamwe, omudano tau ende noukeka otau dulu okukaleka omhepo iwa. Kendabala [oukalata veenghundafana](/games/conversation-cards/) opo u mone omapulo amwe a manguluka, ile [manifa eyeletumbulo](/games/finish-the-saying/) u longife outumbulilo ovo omunhu a shiiva okudja kounona waye. Omayele mahapu eenghundafana, tala [omayele eenghundafana nomunhu ta kala noupyakadi wokudimbwa](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
