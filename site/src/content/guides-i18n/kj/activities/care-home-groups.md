Omukanda ou owa nuninwa ovawiliki voilonga, omapangelo oitufi yofilela nosho yo ovawiliki vomalongekido omutenya. Otau popi omaludi oilonga yongudu oyo hai longo, omalongekido olududa, nghee oshilonga shimwe tashi dulu okulundululwa shi wapalele eenghono di lili, nosho yo nghee tamu dulu okulongifa oukadidiliko vetu va nyanyangidwa va picture bingo nomaudano oko-shiyelekifo shinene. Keshe shimwe shomwaai osha lalakanena ombili, ekwatafano nehololo. Kape na sha shomwaai shi li eyeleko.

## Oshike tashi ningifa oshilonga shongudu shi longe nawa moitufi yofilela?

Oshilonga shongudu shiwa oshi shiivike nawa, oshixupi, oshipu okukufa ombinga ponhele keshe noshihe na efininiko. [Dementia Care Practice Recommendations](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1a0020aa-0bfb-47ac-a545-1dd8a3ee1444/alzheimers-dementia-care-practice-recommendations.pdf) ya Alzheimer's Association oya fatulula ekufombinga li na eityo onga oshinima tashi yambidida osho omunhu e hole noha hoolola, notashi mu pe omhito yokuhoolola nokumona ondjabi, nonande omunhu ota dimbulukwa kanini neenghono. Omukanda oo tuu oo otau ti yo kutya omalongekido a ikililwa ohaa kankamene koilonga imwe ile ivali inene yongudu mefiku, omo ovakalimo luhapu hava kala ashike va tala.

Osho otashi ulike komilandu donhumba tadi longo:

- **Osho shi shiivike nawa oshiwa shi dulife oshipe.** Omaimbilo, omatumbulo, omafano noinima yomido donale dovakalimo oi li puyela okukufa mo ombinga.
- **Oshixupi oshiwa shi dulife oshile.** Longekida shi fike pefimbo olo ongudu yeni hai dulu okutula omadiladilo, u na onhele ya yela yokuxulifa po pokati.
- **Okuninga oshiwa shi dulife okutala.** Pa keshe umwe oshinima a kwate, a tofe, a hoolole ile a popye.
- **Eemhito dihapu, oshilonga shimwe.** Tula mo eendjila dipu nodidjuu dokukufa ombinga.
- **Kape na ovo tava findana, kape na omuvalu woitwa.** Oilonga yomafinana ohai ningifa vamwe va kale ve udite va nyengwa.

## Ongudu oi na okukala inene shi fike peni?

Luhapu ongudu inini oi li puyela. Alzheimer's Association oya ti kutya omunhu oo ha kala a tila oshiningwanima shinene otashi dulika a kwatafane nawa mongudu inini. Kape na ondado imwe i li mondjila. Osha ikolelela kovakalimo, kolududa nokovanailonga ile ovakwafi ve li po.

Eyeleko liwa: omunhu keshe ota dulu ngaa okumona oshipala shomuwiliki, okuuda nawa nokumonika komukwafi moule womafimbo manini? Ngeenge hasho, topola ongudu, wedela ovakwafi, ile inda kolududa linini.

Moshiningwanima shinene, ngaashi okongelata yefimbo loivilo, kaleka ovanailonga novakwafi mokati kovakalimo, hakoixulo yolududa.

## Ohandi longekida ngahelipi olududa nomutumba?

- **Mongonga ile momukweyo wa fa oshimato shonghambe.** Omayele oilonga otaa ti kutya ongonga yokutukulilafana oi li nawa monhele ya kula ya wana opo keshe umwe a kale omutumba mongonga, nopehe na omakako omhepo.
- **Omuwiliki popepi nokomesho yomesho.** Kala omutumba ponhele yokukala wa fikama ngeenge tashi shiiva.
- **Okuuda nokumona osho shotete.** Kaleka ovanhu ava hava udu kanini popepi nomuwiliki, kolulyo lavo liwa. Shilipaleka kutya omakende omesho nomakende okuuda oku li po. Alzheimer's Association oya ti kutya omaupyakadi omesho nokuuda otaa dulu okuninga eenghundafana di kale didjuu.
- **Ouyelele nau kale konima yovakalimo, hakonima yoye.** Henuka okukaleka omunhu a taalela eefitola dinene tadi eta ouyelele muhapu.
- **Dima o-TV noradio.** Alzheimer's Association otai kumaida okudima omakako taa piyaaneke pefimbo loilonga yomusika.
- **Eenghundafana imwe poshikando.** Eenghundafana dihapu poshikando odidjuu kovanhu tava dimbulukwa kanini nokovanhu tava udu kanini, ngaashi Alzheimer's Association ya yelifa momayele ayo [e na sha noivilo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays).
- **Omapito a yela nosho yo onhele ya mwena.** Keshe oo a hala okudja mo oku na okukala ta dulu oku shi ninga noupu, e na omulongi te mu findikile.

## Omadiladilo oilonga yongudu

### Picture bingo

Picture bingo ohai pingena po eengodi nomafano a yela, ngaashi okatemba kotee, ombwa ile orosa, opo paha kale oo ta pumbwa okudimbulukwa ongodi ile, ile okulesha oinima inini.

**Nghee tai danwa:**

1. Nyanyangida okadidiliko kamwe komunhu keshe okudja kepandja letu [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) to longifa okatofa ka Print. Opepa la kola oli li puyela okukwata. Li lamineka opo li longifwe natango.
2. Yandja oinima inene i li puyela okukwata, ngaashi oipapa yo-poker, oifiko yomabotela ile oumbapila va tetwa.
3. Longifa omuifani woko-shiyelekifo ko-TV ile ko-projekita opo keshe umwe a mone efano eshi to tumbula edina lalo.
4. Kanghama kanini konima yefano keshe ndele to shivi epopyo lixupi: “Ope na ngoo oo a hala oku tu lombwela kombwa ei a li e shi?” ile “Omwa mona mo shike apa?”
5. Twikila okwiifana fiyo oukadidiliko aveshe va yada. Keshe umwe ota mane.

**Okulundulula:** Longifa eembokis inini kovanhu ovo tava mono okadidiliko ka yada taku monika kwa fa ku na oinima ihapu. Omukwafi ota dulu okuulika efano la faafana komunhu oo te shi pumbwa. Vamwe otashi dulika va kale va hala okukwata okadidiliko kefano lomuifani nokukeulika kongudu.

### Okwiimba pamwe

Omusika oi li imwe yomoilonga yongudu hai hafelwa unene. Alzheimer's Association oya ti kutya nokuli peenhele dokuxuuninwa, omunhu otashi dulika a dule [okudenga omusika ile okwiimba eimbilo lounona waye](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music).

**Nghee tai ningwa:**

- Hoolola omaimbilo ovakalimo va kula nao, haao omuwiliki e hole. Pula omaumbo kutya keshe umwe okwa li e hole shike.
- Longifa omusika ihe na omashiivifo, oo ehangano tali ti kutya otaa dulu okunyemateka.
- Nyanyangida eendjovo momafutilo manene kwaavo hava lesha, ndele ino shi fininika.
- Yandja oishiimbi shomake, eengoma dinini ile oimaliwa ya fa omalapi va pepe.
- Ladipika okupandula omake nomalinyengo, ndele to kaleka omusika i li pefimbo liwa.

**Okulundulula:** Vamwe otava imbi eendjovo adishe, vamwe otava imbi noupu, vamwe otava denga omhadi, navamwe otava pwilikine ashike. Aishe ei oi na oshilonga. Tala [omukanda woilonga yomusika](/activities/music/) u mone shihapu.

### Eengudu domahokololo

Ongudu yomahokololo ohai ongala kombinga yoshikundafanwa shimwe, ngaashi omafiku ofikola, eehauto dotete, ombishi ile okwenye, tai longifa omafano, oinima nomusika. Elalakano olo eenghundafana diwa, hakudimbulukwa oinima aishe naanaa ngaashi ya li.

**Nghee tai ningwa:**

1. Hoola oshikundafanwa shimwe ndele to eta oinima inini ovanhu tava dulu okukwata: oshikanghifo shomake, eenghaku dokudenga ombapila, ombaki ile okambo komiti.
2. Pitifa oshinima shimwe poshikando. Pula omapulo a manguluka: “Tu lombwela kombinga yaashi,” “Omwa mona mo shike?” “Olyelye a li ha longifa sha fa eshi?”
3. Tambula ko enyamukulo keshe. Ino yukifa nande efiku ile oshili.
4. Endifa eenghundafana di ye komahokololo mawa. Omayele oilonga otaa ti kutya ovafile voshisho ova pumbwa okweendifa omahokololo komadiladilo mawa, hakuoo taa dulu okunyemateka.

[Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) otai yandje oukadidiliko vomafano va nyanyangidwa ve na omapulo a manguluka paitukulwa, ovo hava longo nawa onga eetameko deenghundafana. Omadiladilo mahapu oku li [momukanda womahokololo](/activities/reminiscence/).

### Finish the saying

Omatumbulo a shiivika nawa (“A penny saved is a penny...”) ohaa shivi keshe umwe a tye exulifo pamwe. Ulika [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) koshiyelekifo shinene ndele to efa ongudu i nyamukule newi limwe. Konima yetumbulo keshe oku na epulo leenghundafana. Ngeenge kape na oo ta tongo exulifo, li ulika ndele to twikile. Kape na oshili ile epuko.

### Okukonga omatumbulo momafutilo manene, pamwe

Ulika [Word Search](/games/word-search/) yomafutilo manene koshiyelekifo ndele to kongo omatumbulo onga ongudu, ile nyanyangida oukopi kwaavo va hala okulonga voovene potafule. Omatumbulo ohaa ende ashike kombinga nomokule.

### Omalinyengo mapu

Omalinyengo omutumba, okupitifa okambola kanaana ile okabalona, ile okudana omutumba nomusika i shiivike. Ulika elinyengo keshe ponhele yokulifatulula.

### Okutula melandulafano nokufathaneka potafule

Outafule vanini vovanhu vatatu ile vane otava dulu okutula momalupe omafano, oipambu yoimaliwa ile oikwatelifo yokulya pamwe. Koshiyelekifo, [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) otai dulu okudanwa nomafano a kala a ulikwa, umwe ta tofa ofimbo potafule tapa popiwa kombinga yefano keshe.

## Ohandi longifa ngahelipi omaudano eni koshiyelekifo shinene?

Omaudano etu ohaa longo mo-web browser, onghee oto dulu oku a longifa ko-TV ile ko-projekita nopehe na okutula mo sha.

1. Patulula omaudano ko-laptop ile ko-tablet ya kwatakanifwa koshiyelekifo nongodi ile nokutuma.
2. Ningifa browser i yadife oshiyelekifo ndele to tula omusika pefimbo liwa.
3. Kala u na omuwiliki umwe ta tofa ofimbo ongudu tai ifana omahoololo. Ile yandja o-tablet komunhu oo ta kala a hala olweendo laye.
4. Kanghama nena eshi ongudu ya hala. Omaudano kae na efimbo la tulwa po, omuvalu woitwa ile exulifo tali fininikwa.

[Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/), [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/), [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) na [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) otaa wapalele eengudu unene. Tala omahoololo aeshe [kepandja letu lomuudano](/games/).

## Ohandi lundulula ngahelipi oshilonga shimwe shi wapalele eenghono di lili?

Longekida hanga eendjila nhatu dokukufa ombinga moshilonga keshe.

| Oshilonga | E liholola yoovene | E na ekwafo linini | E na ekwafo lihapu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Picture bingo | Ota kongo nokufunika omafano aye | Omukwafi ta ulike; omunhu ta funike | Ota kwata okadidiliko kefano; ta pwilikine nota tale |
| Okwiimba pamwe | Ota imbi, ta hoolola eimbilo tali shikula | Ota imbi noupu, ta pandula omake, ta longifa oshiimbi shomake | Ota pwilikine, ta kwata oshali yopeke, ta denga omhadi |
| Omahokololo | Ota hokolola omahokololo | Ota nyamukula heeno ile ahawe ile ta ulike | Ota kwata nokukwata oshinima |
| Okukonga omatumbulo | Ota longo okopi yaye | Ota kongo omatumbulo koshiyelekifo shinene nomukwafi | Ota pwilikine eshi omatumbulo taa leshwa |

Kovakalimo ve li peenhele dokuxuuninwa, NHS oya ti kutya oilonga luhapu oi na okukala i li paunafangwa nokuyukifa elitulemo komaliudo, ngaashi omusika, oinima dokukwata nokumassa omake. Okafimbo kamwe nomunhu aeke pefimbo ile konima yongudu otashi dulika shi mu wapalele shi dulife ongudu yovene. Tala [oilonga yovanhu ve li peenhele dokuxuuninwa](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ovawiliki ova pumbwa okupopya ngahelipi pefimbo longudu?

- Liholola keshe efimbo, ndele to longifa edina olo keshe umwe a hala.
- Popya kanini, yandja oshinima shimwe poshikando, ndele to ulike manga ino popya.
- Pula omashivo a manguluka (“Tu lombwela kombinga ya...”) ile omahoololo mayela, hapula omapulo taa yeleke (“Oto dimbuluka ngaa...?”).
- Ino yukifa. Pandula keshe umwe molwaasho a yandja.

Opo to mono shihapu kombinga yaashi, tala [omayele eenghundafana](/caregiving/communication-tips/).

## Okuxulifa nawa, nokweefa ovanhu va dje mo

Okuxulifa po osho oshinima shiwa. Tala omandidiliko oo taa ulike kutya omunhu okwa loloka, ke na ombili ile okwa nyemata, ndele to kala u na omukwafi te mu pe omhito yokweenda ile okukala omutumba monhele ya mwena. Omunhu ta kofele pefimbo lokuimba pamwe otashi dulika ashike e udite nawa.

Xulifa ongudu keshe monghedi ya faafana, neimbilo li shiivike ile nepandulo li li paunafangwa, opo exulifo li kale la ngungumana nola teelelwa. Konima yaasho, shanga osho sha longa nawa naavo va hafela shike. Oinima oyo otai ningi oshikando tashi shikula shi kale shiwa.
