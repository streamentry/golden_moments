Oilonga yoikwauninga oi li onghedi iwa yokukala pamwe nomunhu e na oudu wokudimbwa. Ohai longifa omake, omesho nomaliudo, nohai mu pe sha mu dule okupopya kombinga yasho. Epepa eli otali yandje oilonga ya yela noinhano iipu, pamwe nomayele okukwafa okuhoolola oinima noukeka. Omolwomafinamhango oikwauninga ya koka, ngaashi okuhenuka oinima youhanana, tala [oikwauninga nomake kwaavo ve na oudu wokudimbwa](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Nghee to hovele oshilonga keshe shoikwauninga

1. **Longekida ofimbo inamu hovela.** Tula po oshinima shimwe ile ivali, oshiyaleko shi amene noshitukutuku sha tutika.
2. **Ulika onhanho yotete.** Nyika oninga, taula oshipambu shotete, ile finya okapambu kanini kedu lokuma, ndele to mu shivi a ninge sha faafana.
3. **Longeni ombinga nombinga.** Kaleka oshilonga shoye popepi opo oshilonga shi kale sha fa sheni amushe, ndele hashokupashukila.
4. **Popyeni kombinga yaasho tamu mono.** Hokolola omavala, omaukwatya nomaluvalo ponhele yokutokola oshilonga.
5. **Kangheka ofimbo eenghono inadi shuna pedu.** Ominute omulongo fiyo omilongo mbali luhapu ohadi wana.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ota ti kutya oikwauninga oi li onghedi yokuulika omaliudo nokukala nomadiladilo, nopehe na okudula ouhaku. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ota yandje omayele okutukula oilonga moinhano inini, tai dulu okuwanifwa noupu.

## Okutanga nomavala omeya

Longifa opepa la kola lomavala omeya, oninga inene ya nanga nomavala mashona momakopi a mbwalangadja. Tutike opepa nomeya a yela, ndele to mu efa a tule mo omavala. Endamifa opepa kanini nelungi opo omavala a tondoke. Oshidjemo otashi kala efano la nanga lihe na ondunge ya pumbiwa.

- **Onghalo yopehovelo:** Mu efa a hoolole omavala nokutenga po efano loshitukulwa.
- **Onghalo yopokati:** Mu pa omavala avali ile atatu ndele to mu tutikile opepa.
- **Onghalo yokomesho:** Kwata opepa ofimbo ta tula mo omavala noshipontsho.

## Okukonga nokunambela omafano omomifo

Ongela omapandja omomifo e na omafano a yela eengala, oikulya, oinamwenyo, eehauto ile eendawo. Taula omafano moinhano inene, osho shipu shi dule okushikula noikifo, ndele to a nambeke kombada yokakalata. Okukonga nokunambela omafano oku li onghedi iwa yokuhovela eenghundafana, shaashi efano keshe otali dulu okweetifa edimbuluko. Otashi wapalele omunhu ou e hole okutula oinima momusholondodo nokuhoolola.

## Edu lokuma la nanga

Edu lokuma tali kukuta poluhaela ile edu lokuma la nanga otali dulu okufinwa, okudingilwa nokukwatwa. Ningeni okambiya ka yela ile oshipambu sha mbwalangadja. Edu lokuma otali kumu omake unene, onghee otali dulu okungungumaneka ovo ve hole okukwata oinima. Kaleka oipambu inene, nokuhenuka oilongifo i na omutwe wa twa.

## Oikalki yepastela kombada yopepa la tila

Opepa la tila ohali ningifa omavala oikalki a veme nawa. Longifa oikalki inene ndele to efa omunhu a tule ko omalenga manene ile a vande neke ile noshitukutuku. Oikalki yepastela ohai eta ondwi, onghee longekidila mokatemba nokukosha omake konima. Hoolola oikalki ya nanga ya shangwa kutya kai na oudio.

## Okushita omafoloma

Ongela omafoloma manene moshikunino, vaeka epende tali koshwa komitwe domafoloma, ndele to a fininikile kopepa. A kufa ko nelungi ndele to hafele oshiholekwa. Oshilonga eshi otashi kwatakanifwa nawa noilonga [yopondje noyokulonga moshikunino](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), notashi dulu okuhovela neenda lokwoongela omafoloma.

## Okulongekida oiyata neengala

Tula momusholondodo oipambu yoiyata i na omaukwatya e lili noku lili she likolelela komaukwatyo ayo, ile longekida eengala dishona mokavasa. Eshi oilonga tai kumu omaliudo noihe na oudjuu muhapu. Otai wapalele yo ovo ve li monghalo yokomesho youdu. Omolwomadiladilo mahapu, tala [oilonga tai kumu omaliudo kwaavo ve na oudu wokudimbwa](/activities/sensory/).

## Okuhoolola oinima yoikwauninga

Oinima oyo ya fimana unene omolweameno netulumuko shi dule omolwongushu yayo. Ngeenge to hoolola, diladila kombinga yaashi:

- **Ihe na oudio notai koshwa:** Konga omashangelo taa ulike kutya kai na oudio nokutala ngeenge omavala otaa koshwa.
- **Omakwatelo manene:** Oikalki inene, eeninga da kula noikalki inene oshipu okukwatwa.
- **Ihe na ounghulungu vanini:** Henuka eembuma, eembatu, oinima tai vema noinima tai i mo moshipala oyo tai dulu okup swallowed.
- **Ihe na oilongifo i na omutwe wa twa:** Longifa oikifo yokushikula yeameno ile taula opepa nomake.
- **Omapyakadi omolwomaludi amwe:** Talela po latex nosho yo omaupyakadi amwe olutu.
- **Omalunduluko a yela:** Omavala a kola kombada yopepa litoka ile la tila ohaa monika noupu.
- **Omusholondodo muxupi:** Oinima ivali ile itatu oi li po ya wana. Omahoololo mahapu otaa dulu okungwangwaneka.
- **Okukosha noupu:** Longifa okatemba, oshiyaleko shoplastika noshitukutuku sha tutika, opo onyata iha kale etomheno lokukangheka.

## Okulundulula she likolelela konghalo

Monghalo yopehovelo, ovanhu vahapu otava dulu okulongekida nokumanifa oshilonga nekuwafo linini. Monghalo yopokati, yandja omahoololo mashona nomawiliko mahapu. Monghalo yokomesho, yandja elitulemo koilonga tai kumu omaliudo ngaashi okufinya edu lokuma ile okutula omavala noshipontsho, nokuhafela oshidjemo ponhele yokumanifa oshilonga.

Omolwomadiladilo mahapu e likolelela konghalo, tala [onghalo yopehovelo](/activities/early-stage/), [onghalo yopokati](/activities/middle-stage/) noilonga [yonghalo yokomesho](/activities/late-stage/). Ngeenge omunhu ota anye ile ta kala e udite oudjuu, ewiliko letu kombinga [yangeenge umwe ta ti ahowe](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) otali yelifa nghee to dulu okushuna monima nelungi.

## Weda ko oudano

Konima yokutenga, kendabala [okutanga omavala](/games/coloring/) kotableta ongoshinima sha ngungumana tashi landula, ile [oshilonga shokutungulula efano](/games/picture-puzzle/) ongoshilonga shokumanifa. Aishe ivali ohai kaleka omake e lipyakidila nopehe na okudula ouhaku u li pombada.
