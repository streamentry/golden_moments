Omaumbo mahapu ohaa kongo ehololo lokukala pamwe, hali fa omakundafano, haali fa epulo louladi. Omepulo 20 nomaudano oo haa shikula otashi dulu okuhamba nawa, molwaashi omilandu oipupi nonda yeuluka nomakundafano otashi dulu okuya kese apa. Oshinima sha fimana oshokulundulula omaukwatya: omunhu oo to mu file oshisho oye ta diladile omanyamukulo, ndele ove to mu pula. Epandja eli otali ku kwafele u longe omaudano aa nouwa, nokutya omaudano elipi e li wapala ovanhu ve na oulunde womadiladilo.

## Omolwashike tu na okulundulula omilandu?

Mepulo louladi lopaushitwe, umwe oha pula nomukwao e na okwiitavela. Osho otashi ningifa omunhu oo e na oulunde womadiladilo a kale meenghalo omo ta dulu okupunduka, ndele okupunduka otashi dulu oku mu udifa nai. Ngeenge oye ta hoolola oshinima ndele ove to li konakona, oye omunongo. Oye e na omanyamukulo, oye ta tokola efimbo lokunyamukula naaye ile ahawe, nota dulu nokuhafa eshi to li kongo.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) oya popya kutya ovali oshisho va longife eendjovo dipupi, da yela, omapulo umwe pauxupi, nonda hafa noudiinini. Omakwafo aa a wapala unene komaudano okudiladila.

## Ngaashi to dulu okudana omepulo 20 nouwa

1. **Hoololeni oshilongwa** shi li momwenyo womunhu oo to mu file oshisho, ngaashi okwoongela, oshikunino, oinamwenyo ile okafudepo keku.
2. **Efa a diladile sha** noku shi popya pamwe naye, ile a ulikile kefano, opo inamu pumbwa oku shi uda mohole.
3. **Puleni omapulo taa nyamukulwa naaye ile ahawe umwe pauxupi.** "Oshinima shokulya tuu?" "Oshinene tuu shi dule ekaku?"
4. **Tambuleni omanyamukulo aeshe.** Okupeva omutwe, okutengauka omutwe, ile okukwena nako otai dulu okunyamukula.
5. **Dileneni omamoniko moipafi** nokuhafa pamwe naye. Ngeenge wa mona omanyamukulo, hafeni pamwe.
6. **Yandjeni ekwafo** ngeenge omunhu a fa a nyika oluhodi: "Oshinima shili mokwoongela."
7. **Inamu valula omapulo** ndele inamu valula yo oitungilo.

Ngeenge omunhu ita dulu okuudite ko omilandu, lundululeni okudana mupe: popyeni oinima ihapu ndele mu efa a ulike kwaayo a hala.

## Oinima iwa yokudiladila

- Oiimati, omidi ile oshiimati shomikunino sha hokiwa
- Oshinamwenyo shomomapya, shozoologika ile omhunda oyo a li e na nale
- Oshoongifo, ngaashi ekonde, oshihakululo ile ondelu yokuteta
- Oipala yonhumba ile oinima yomongulu
- Onhele ye shii nawa, ngaashi opaka ile oshilando
- Oikutu oyo a li e hole okudjala

Henukeni omadina ovanhu, oinima tai dulu oku mu udifa oluhodi, nosho yo oinima tai dulu okukala ya nyika oshiponga ile i shii okupukifa.

## Omahokololo makwao

- **Eshi ile shinya.** Yandjeni oinima ivali noku mu pula kutya oshike a hala: omavele ile okofi, omakulu ile efuta, oshikutu shitilyana ile shimbulau. Kape na omanyamukulo a puka.
- **Owa hala tuu.** Puleni epulo li na ouwa nouyelele: "Owa hala tuu okulya pombada ile okwoongala mongulu?"
- **Manifa eendjovo.** Eendjovo da fimana da li da tumbulwa nale, ndele exulilo ola fiwa po. Tala omaudano etu [okuuda eendjovo da xupa po](/activities/finish-the-saying/) omolwewiliko.
- **Tumbula onhele.** Mu pule a hokolole omuwo, oshilando ile omoniko wa hokiwa, noku mu shiva yo a diladile pamwe naave.
- **Okapepa komahokololo.** [Okapepa ketu komahokololo](/games/conversation-cards/) otaka yandje omahokololo e li pavahapu pahoololo, taa dulu yo okunyanyangidwa pefano.

Omolwoitwa imwe i na ko nasha neendululo nomahokololo omwenyo, tala [omahokololo okudimbulukwa](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Omolwokudana kwa yela nouwa, tala [omayeleko a pupa ovanhu ve na oulunde womadiladilo](/activities/trivia/), oo haa longifa ehololo ndele kape na omavalulo.

## Ngeenge omunhu a nyengwa

Xulifa, nyamifa ndele popya ngaha: "Natu dane sha shimwe." Okuhaluka okwoondaha otaku ulike kutya omunhu a hala okulundulula ehololo, ha oku mu fininika. Ewiliko letu li na ko nasha [nokudana pamwe nomunhu e na oulunde womadiladilo](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) otali ku kwafele okulundulula omilandu mefimbo olo.

## Oshike omahokololo taa dulu noshike inaa dulu okuninga?

Omahokololo otaa hafifa nokukwafela okukala pamwe. Otaa dulu oku kwafela ovanhu va kale pamwe nokuhokolola omahokololo avo. Ndele inaa kwafa okuvelula oulunde womadiladilo, oku u shunifa monima ile oku u kelela. Elalakano li li pauxupi olo ashike: okafimbo kouwa pamwe.
