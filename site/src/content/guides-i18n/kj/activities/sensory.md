Oilonga yomaiyuvo ohai longifa omaiyuvo atano: okukwata, okunuka, okutomba, okuuda nokumona. Inai pumbwa okudimbuluka, eenghatu ile omanyamukulo a yuka, onghee otai dulu okulonga keenghatu adishe. Otai kala ya fimana unene peenghatu daxuuninwa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) oya ti pefimbo laxuuninwa ounyuni ohau monika unene okupitila momaiyuvo, notashi dulu okuulika ohole okupitila mokukwata, okuuda, okumona, okutomba nokunuka. Omashangelo manini eameno otaa ningifa oilonga ei i kale tai tulumukifa aveshe.

## Oilonga yomaiyuvo youdu wokudimbwa oshike?

Oilonga yomaiyuvo oyo omamoniko mapu a tungilwa keuviko limwe pefimbo, ngaashi okukwata oipumbiwa, okunuka oimeno ile okupwilikina kodula. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) oya ti oilonga yefimbo laxuuninwa ohai pumbwa okutula omadiladilo komaiyuvo, notai tumbula omusika, oinima yokukwata nokukwata omake nelitulemo. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) oya ti ovanhu ovo ve li pefimbo laxuuninwa otava dulu okukala va hala oku kwatela oshinima popepi opo va mone ehekeleko.

## Omaetepo oilonga yomaiyuvo, euviko neuviiko

### Okukwata

- **Okukwata omake nelitulemo.** Fufya okalwaadi kanini momake oye ndele to shilile nelitulemo okudja pokwooko fiyo komunwe. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) oya tumbula okukwata nomwaadi uhe na edimba opo omunhu a kale e udite nawa.
- **Okandjato koipumbiwa.** Oipambu inene ya velvet, corduroy, fleece, satin nowooli yokukwata, okupeta nokupopya kombinga yayo.
- **Ekumbafa leenyata la pupyala.** Ekumbafa leenyata la pupyala nola tutika komake mefiku loutalala. Tala tete oupyu wako pokwooko kwoye mwene.
- **Oapuloni yoilonga ile ekumbafa lomomaluha.** Eembato dinene, ozipa, oumbandja noipambu i lili yokukwata ya hondelwa ko nawa.
- **Okupeta nokuyelakanifa.** Okandjato keenyata ile oinapekifa ya koshoka yokupeta, okutula mumwe nokupeta natango.
- **Oshinima shilula shokukwata.** Ekumbafa la fleece, okashiyelekifo ile oshinamwenyo shilula tashi monika shoshili, sha yandjwa noupu, hashi ifanwa oshidjalomwa.

### Okunuka

- **Oimeno ipe.** Nyanya omafo a mint, rosemary, basil ile lavender momunwe woye ndele to a yandje a nuke.
- **Omanuko omeumbo lokuteleka.** Okofi tai telekwa, omungome tau fulukwa, cinnamon ile elemu tali xululwa popepi.
- **Eengala.** Eta eengala dishona dipe okudja moshikunino ile kositola.
- **Oshinima osho a shiivila.** Osepo ile omwaadi oo a li ha longifa alushe, ngee oshipa shaye otashi shi lididimikile.

### Okutomba

- **Eshito olo a hokwa.** Okalola kanini koshinima osho a hokwa, ngaashi applesauce, pudding ile aisikilimi, shi li monghalo oyo ta dulu okulyata nawa.
- **Okukwafela mokuteleka.** Okutomba oiimati ile okafuta kanini fimbo tamu teleke pamwe.
- **Oikulya yefimbo lokomudo.** Omandjembele pefimbo loutekulu, oshikunwa sha pupyala shoapula pefimbo lokwenye, sha yandjwa poupyu u li nawa.

### Okuuda

- **Omusika oyo a hokwa.** Eengalo odo a shiivila newi itali udika unene; tala [oilonga yomusika](/activities/music/).
- **Omawi opaushitwe.** Odula, omakufikufi omefuta ile eedila tadi imbi. NIA oya ti omusika, omawi a ngungila nomaumbo opaushitwe otaa monika taa tulumukifa ovanhu vamwe.
- **Ewi loye.** Okulesha mokule, okuimbila pedu ile okuhokolola ehokololo.
- **Oihongomwa ilula.** Ongoma yokeenyala, oshinyanyudi ile ongengwa yokushika pamwe nomusika.

### Okumona

- **Okutala pokafenstela.** Okandjuwo keedila pondje yokafenstela, ounona tava ende ve li mondjila yokuya keumbo, ile omundjele tau loko.
- **Okandjuwo keeshi ile okavidio tai ende kashona.** Okweenda kwa ngungila nopehe na ewi linene.
- **Omafano manene.** Efano limwe la yela pefimbo, la tulwa poule weisho.
- **Ombala nomapangadja.** Okutopola oipambu yoiyata i na ombala, eengala ile oinapekifa i na ombala, ile epandja liwa lokupenda koipambuliko yetu [yokupenda](/games/coloring/).
- **Pondje.** NHS otai yandje ediladilo loshikunino shomaiyuvo shi na oimeno tai nuka, oipumbiwa yokukwata noinima yomeva. Tala [oilonga yokuya pondje nokulonga moshikunino](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Nghee to longekida okandjato komaiyuvo

1. **Hoolola oshipalanyole** shi na ekwatafano nonghalamwenyo yomunhu: omunghulofuta, oshikunino, eumbo lokuteleka, oshilonga shoipilangi, ile okuhondja.
2. **Hoolola oinima itano ile ihamano inene noya amenwa,** ngaashi oshikumbu shinene shomunghulofuta, oshikuti shomiti, osikafu ilula, okandjato kanini ka lavender ya kukuta ka hondelwa sha pwa, ile okapambu komiti ka yeleka.
3. **Tala oshinima keshe** opo u mone ngee oshi na oipambu inini, eemhadi da twa, eengodi da manguluka ile sha tashi dulu okuteya.
4. **Yandja oshinima shimwe pefimbo.** Shi mu pe, shi fatulula, ndele to efa omunhu e shi konakone.
5. **Tula okandjato kokule konima** opo ka kale ke likalekelwa noinima i talike natango manga inaku longifwa vali.

## Eameno lotete

Oilonga yomaiyuvo oi li ngungila, ashike oiponga imwe oi na oshilonga. Vamwe vomovanhu ovo ve na oudu wokudimbwa otava dulu okutala ko oinima ihe fi oikulya onga oikulya, nokufina otaku dulu okukala kudjuu mokweenda kwefimbo; NIA otai londwele kutya omaupyakadi okufina pefimbo laxuuninwa otaa hapupala oshiponga shokufina.

| Oshiponga | Osho u na okuninga |
| --- | --- |
| Okufina | Longifa ashike oinima inene itai dulu okufinwa. Ino tula eembato, oumanya vanini, oimaliwa, eembola dinini ile oidjalomwa inini ya manguluka mokandjato. Yandja oikulya ashike monghalo oyo ongudu yoshipangelo ya ti oi li nawa. |
| Oinima tai monika ngaashi oikulya | NIA otai kumaida u pate omaadi, eesepo, oishampoo noinima tai nuka, osheshi otai dulu okumonika ile okunuka ngaashi oikulya, nokukufa po oiimati yoipupulu. Tula oinima yomaiyuvo kokule konima yokulongifa. |
| Omikifi dokuhanyena oinima | Tala ngee ope na omikifi dokuhanyena eembiya, olateksa, owooli, oimeno ile oinima tai nuka manga ino hovela. |
| Oinima tai nuka ya kola | Henuka oinima tai nuka ya kola nomaadi a kola, unene ngee pe na oshinhwi ile omaupyakadi makwao okufuda. Omunuko muwa wa ngungila ou li nawa. |
| Oshipa | Tala ngee oshipa osha tuwa, sha pambuka ile sha nghundipala manga ino kwata omake nomwaadi. Kwata nelitulemo ndele to kanghama ngee ta monika ta yahama. |
| Oupyuu | Tala oupyu weenyata ile woikunwa pokwooko kwoye mwene tete. |
| Oinima ya twa ile ya nyika oshiponga | Ino longifa oilongifo ya twa, oigalss ile oinima yokulonga ya nyika oshiponga. |

## Aromatherapy ohai kwafele tuu?

Otashi dulika ahawe, meenghedi tadi dulu okuyelekwa. [Omapekapeko a Cochrane kombinga ya aromatherapy noudu wokudimbwa](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) okwa tala omapekapeko 13 novanhu 708, aveshe ve li momaumbo okufilwa oshisho, nokwa mona kutya kape na oumbangi wa kola oo tau ulike ouwa. Omapekapeko okwa li manini, oidjemo kaya li tai tu kumwe, nomaupyakadi opamadiladilo inaa lopotelwa nawa. Ngee omunhu oku hole edimba liwa noli shiivike, ngaashi lavender ya dja moshikunino, osho etomheno liwa oku li topola naye. Hashi ongodi yokuhakula.

## Nghee to yandje oshilonga shomaiyuvo

- **Hoolola efimbo la ngungila** ndele to dima oTV.
- **Ehena komesho yaye,** ifana edina laye, ndele to pula ile to mu lombwele manga ino mu kuma: „Ondi na omwaadi. Owa hala ndi ku kwate omake?“
- **Yandja oshinima shimwe pefimbo** opo shiha kale shihapu unene.
- **Tala enyanyu laye.** Oshipala sha ngungila, omake a tulumukwa ile omumwenyo oo ouyelele muwa. Okukufa omake, okufinyika oshipala ile okuhe na ombili otashi ti kanghama.
- **Shi kaleka shipu.** Ominute nhano da ngungila odo oshikando shiwa.

Okufuda nelitulemo pamwe, ngaashi [okufuda nelitulemo](/games/calm-breathing/), otaku dulu okukala onghedi iwa yokuxulifa po. Opo u mone omaetepo amwe opombete, tala [oilonga yefimbo laxuuninwa](/activities/late-stage/), nongeenge pe na okuhe na ombili konguloshi, tala [okuhe na ombili kwonguloshi](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
