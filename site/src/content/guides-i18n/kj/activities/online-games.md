Omafimbo mahapu omaukwaneumbo ohaa kongo oudano woshali wopaintaneta wovanhu ve na dementia molwaashi va hala sha ve shi ninge pamwe inashi pumbwa ostore, okwiidifa ile okaata yofuto. Omahoololo mawa ohaa kala mayeluka, taa longifa omafano, nohapu xulifwa po noupu. Ohaa kala a fa efimbo loku hafela pamwe, ndele haninga eyeleko. Ewiliko eli otali yelifa osho u na okutala, nghee to longekida oudano, nosho yo osho oudano ou tau dulu naashi itau dulu okuninga.

## Oshike hashi ningifa oudano wopaintaneta u wapalele dementia?

Oudano ohau wapalele ovanhu vahapu tava kala ne dementia ngeenge tau pula okudimbulula ponhele yokudimbulukwa. Okutula kumwe omafano avali a faafana, okukwata efano tali fa omudidimbe, ile okutula oshinima metemba la yuka, aishe ei oya kwatela mo osho omesho taa mono paife. Oudano tau likolelele kokudimbulukwa omhango, oshitya, onomola ile eshi sha ningwa nale luhapu ohau eta oudjuu.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) otai ladipike oilonga oyo tai tungilwa kwaasho omunhu e hole natango nota dulu okuninga, noye mu pitika a pondole nopehe na ekwafo. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) otai ti sha wapala okuningifa oshilonga shipu ponhele yokushi efa filu ngeenge eenghono oda lunduluka.

## Oudano wa Golden Moments to dulu okupatulula mombesa

Oudano keshe apa ohau patuluka ko-website nopehe na sha tashi landwa. Ouhapu ohau danwa kotableta ile kokombiyuta; imwe otai dulu yo okunyanyangidwa.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Omafano manene taa tulwa kumwe e li po, kapa li efimbo ile omuvalu. A wapala koudano wotete.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Efano limwe pombada, nefano la faafana mokati komahoololo avali fiyo onhee pedu.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Omudidimbe wa ngungila tau holoka pombada; kwata efano tali u ningi.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Kwata efano, opo nee etemba omo li na okukala, ngaashi okombitha ile moshikunino.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Efano ola tukulwa moitukulwa nefano alishe otali kala po tali ulike ondjila.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Omayeletumbulo a shiivika oshitya shaxuuninwa sha puka; kwata ehoololo ile ulikila exulilo.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Oukalata vomafano ve na omapulo a manguluka okupopya, ndele hakupula omapulo okukonakona. Otaa dulu yo okunyanyangidwa.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Omafano taa tulwa kumwe a ningwa komafano oo wa hoolola koshilongifo shoye. Omafano ohaa kala mombesa yoshilongifo osho.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Efimbo la ngungila lokufuda pamwe nombili. Oove to li tameke noove to li xulifa.

Okukonga oitya, okutula omaluvala nomafano e li moobingo otaa monika yo koilonga tai nyanyangidwa. Tala [oilonga ya nyanyangidwa yovanhu ve na dementia](/activities/printable/) u mone shihapu.

## Nghee to longekida efimbo lokudana oudano

1. **Hoolola efimbo la ngungila** eshi omunhu a tulumukwa. Konguloshi omafimbo amwe ohaa kala madjuu kuvamwe, onghee tala tete onghedi yeni.
2. **Longifa oskela inene.** Otableta i li koshikwatelifo, ile okombiyuta nombesa ya nenenekwa, oi li noupu okumona nokukwata.
3. **Pata omatab makwao ndele to dima omashiivifo.** Omapop-up taa holoka ombadilila otaa halukifa notaa dulu okuxulifa po efimbo liwa.
4. **Kala omutumba popepi nomunhu, ndele ha u li omhinge naye.** Okutala pamwe ohaku kala kwa fa ekwatafano, ndele haku kala kwa fa eyeleko.
5. **Hovela noudano umwe oule wominute nhano fiyo omulongo.** Efa omunhu a kwate oskela ile a ulike ofimbo tamu pingakanifa.
6. **Xulifa po nawa.** Mana etukulwa limwe, opo nee tamu popi kombinga yefano limwe ile tamu pula mu nwe otee.

## Oiholekwa neameno koilongifo tamu longifa pamwe

- **Omafano ohaa kala koshilongifo.** Photo pairs oha i longifa omafano oo wa hoolola, nohaa kala mombesa yoshilongifo osho. Tala kutya olyelye vali ta dulu okupatulula okombiyuta ile otableta.
- **Henuka omalungula taa twala keenhele dimwe nomashiivifo.** Longifa oudano owa yukilila ko-website, ndele ino kwata omabanna ile omashiivifo taa pula ouyelele woye mwene.
- **Kaleka oofuto nomakaunti kokule.** Ino efa omunhu oye aeke poskela tai pula ondjovo yokupitifa ile onomola yokaata.
- **Lungama ngeenge ta iendaenda.** Kaleka otableta kokule ngeenge omunhu ota dulu okudja mo meumbo nayo.

U mone elandulafano la mbwalangadja, tala [nghee to hoolola eeapp noudano wovanhu ve na dementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) nosho yo [okulongekida otableta ile ongodi](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Osho oudano ou tau dulu naashi itau dulu okuninga

Oudano wopaintaneta ohau pe ovanhu ve na dementia ompito va hafele okafimbo, va tale omafano pamwe, nokukufa ombinga mosha nopehe na engabeko. Itau velula dementia, itau i ende pamwe kanini, ile tau i kelele. Oshihopaenenwa sha shiivika unene osho [eenghatu da katukwa mo 2016 kuFederal Trade Commission omolwomahokololo a Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) kombinga youdano womadiladilo oo itaa dulu okuulikwa. Lungama koudano keshe, koapp ile ko-website tai udaneke edimbuluko liwa, tai pe omadiladilo oye omuvalu ile tai ti otai dulu okushunifa monima eshi sha nghundipala.

Longifa oudano ou u li oshitukulwa shefiku olo li na yo omusika, okweendaenda, eenghundafana nokutulumukwa. Ngeenge wa hala okulesha kombinga yanghee to ningifa oudano u kale nombili ndele hau eta oudjuu, tala [nghee to dana pamwe nomunhu e na dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) nosho yo [oudano wedimbuluko uhe fi eyeleko](/activities/memory-games/).

## Naini u na okuxulifa po

Xulifa po ngeenge omunhu ta monika a loloka, e he na ombili, a nyika oluhodi ile a limbililwa. Xulifa po ngeenge e he na vali ohokwe. Okuxulifa po diva oshi li nawa. Oto dulu okualuka vali efiku limwe, ile to lundulula mu popye ile mu ende.
