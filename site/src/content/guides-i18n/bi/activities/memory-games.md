Sipos yu serj "memory games for seniors" mo bigfala namba long ol samting we i bin mekem blong ol man we oli wantem wan workout blong tingting: ol kad we oli hid, ol klok we oli kaontdaon, ol level mo skoa. Long man we gat dementia, ol fitja ya oli mekem gem i kranki long samting we i jes hard finis long hem. Ol sem gem ya oli ken pleim long wan gentel fasin. Pej ya i soem hao, mo i lisimap ol gem we i gud long ea stej.

## Wae ol klasik gem blong memori i mekem ol man we gat dementia oli fil hevi?

From oli dipen long holem nyufala information long tingting, mo dat i olsem fastfala samting we Alzheimer's, we i sem kaos blong dementia, i stat hitim. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) i talem se fastfala saen blong Alzheimer's i hard blong rimembem nyufala information we man i jes lanem. Long klasik Concentration, yu flip tu kad, yu rimembem ples blong olgeta, mo yu yusum dat wan minit bihaen. Dat i nyufala information, we yu holem mo yu rikolem.

Sipos yu putum klok, skoa o bisa blong rong ansa, man i ken rimembem samting we hem i no save mekem, long fran blong man we i lavem hem. Plante man oli stop kwaet blong wantem pleim.

Rikognaes samting we yu save luk i difren mo i yusually isi moa we blong mekem. Taem tufala kap i stap long ae mo yu pointim seken kap i gud fil. So trik i blong holem matsing mo drop memori test.

## Hao blong mekem wan gem blong memori i gentel?

Ol jenjenj ya i wok long ol kad gem, ol bod gem mo ol gem long skrin.

1. **Leftemap ol pikjas i stap antap.** Matsing ol pikjas we yu luk long ae i stil wan real gem, mo ofen i gat gud fil.
2. **Yusum fewfala pis.** Stat wetem tufala o tri pea. Putum moa sipos i fil se i isi tumas mo oli wantem moa.
3. **Jusum bigfala, fomlar, adalt pikjas.** Wan kap blong tea, wan sanflaoa, wan sel boat. No ol katun, no ol bisi sin.
4. **Pleim olsem tim.** "Yumi faenem ol dog" i gud moa dan "tan blong yu." Sherem tan blong pointim mo help.
5. **Drop skoa.** Nating man i nidim win. Sipos wan man i askem, wan isi toktok "yumi mekem i pinis" i enaf.
6. **Nating ren wetem klok.** No taemaot, mo no ren long ol tan.
7. **Toktok long ol pikjas.** "Luk long dat boat. Yu bin go sel long taem blong yu?" Toktok ofen i samting we i gud moa.
8. **Stop long wan gud nout.** Finisim afta long wan matsing, long taem we i stil fil gud.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i givim sem spirit olsem jeneral advaes: matsim aktiviti long samting we man i save mekem, lukaot long fasin we i mekem hem kros o sori, mo mekem sua hem i fil se hem i winim.

## Ol gentel gem blong memori mo matsing blong trae

### Picture pairs (matsing wetem pikjas we oli soem)

[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) i wan gem blong matsing wetem bigfala pikjas mo 4 i go 12 tile. Ol pikjas oli ken stap luk, so i wan blong faenem tuwin, no blong rimembem ples blong olgeta. Stat wetem fo tile. Blong man long ea stej we i wantem sam moa jalenj, yu ken jenjem i go long **Hidden pictures**, be go bak long soem pikjas bambae i stop fun.

### Find the same

[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/) i soem wan pikjas long saed antap mo tu i go fo jois long botem. Tajem ol one we i matsing. I wan sipel gem long saet ya mo i gud long ol man we oli fil se gris blong ful i bisi tumas, inkludim plante long leta stej we wan man i sidaon klosap.

### Photo pairs (gem blong memori long famili fotou)

[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) i jenjem ol fotou we yu jusum i kam wan matsing gem: dog, gaden, lekhaos. Ol famili fes mo ol ples we oli fomlar ofen i kiaktem toktok moa dan eni stoc pikjas. Ol fotou oli stap long browser blong yu mo oli no saed i go antap.

### Sort together

[Sort together](/games/sort-together/) i askem yu blong putum ea pikjas long basket we i stap raet, olsem kitjin o gaden. Sortem i yusum save we i stap long longfala taem abaot hao wol i wok, no long shotfala memori.

### Picture puzzle

[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) i katem wan pikjas i kam 4 o 9 pis. Tajem tufala pis blong swapem olgeta kasem pikjas i kam fulap. Fulap pikjas i stap long skrin olsem wan gaed.

### Real kad long tebol

Yu no nidim wan skrin. Pulum ol pea blong kad from tu deck blong kad o from wan set blong pikjas kad. Lei olgeta i stap antap long tu rou mo faenem ol pea tugeta. Afta, sortem long kala o long sut. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) i lisimap kad gem mo bod gem long ol aktiviti blong fastfala stej.

## Wanem ol gem blong memori i fit long ea stej?

Ol stej oli ol lus leben. Folem wanem man i enjoy long dei ya.

| Stej | Ol gem we oli ofen fit | Hao blong setem olgeta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fastfala | Picture pairs, Photo pairs, Picture puzzle wetem 9 pis, ol famili kad gem | Letem hem jusum. Ol tile i ken tanem sipos hem i enjoy; drop skoa sipos i wari hem |
| Midol | Picture pairs wetem 4 i go 6 tile we i stap antap, Find the same, Sort together | Ol pikjas i stap luk olwe. Sidaon klosap mo sherem tan blong help |
| Leta | Find the same wetem 2 jois, lukluk long wan bigfala pikjas tugeta | Fasfala tumas. Yu holem tablet; hem i pointim, o jes hem i lukluk mo herem |

## Ol gem blong memori i helpem dementia?

Nating gem blong memori i bin soem se i ken treatem, slowem o stopem dementia. Wan [Cochrane review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/) blong computerized cognitive training long ol man we gat mol kognitiv impairment i faenem ol pruf i no stret blong talem se i ken prevent dementia. Pej blong mifala abaot [brain games and dementia](/brain-health/brain-games/) i eksplein wanem ol stadi i soem mo wanem oli no soem.

Dat i no rijen blong skipem ol gem. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) i advaesem blong fokas long enjoyment, no long acievment. Ten minit blong matsing ol pikjas mo toktok abaot olgeta i ken i wan gud pat blong dei.

## Wanem abaot ol gem blong memori blong ol olfala we no gat dementia?

Sipos wan olfala we no gat ol memori trabol i enjoy ol puzzle wetem taem, skoa mo ol hadfala level, i no gat rong long hem. Be no kaontem long hem blong protectem agensem dementia; nating gem i bin soem se i save. Ol gentel ves we i stap abov oli gud tu taem ol jenereson pleim tugeta: wan grendadi we i gat dementia mo wan yangfala pikinini blong hem ken matsing pikjas olsem tim, mo nating wan i stap long test.

## Wanem taem blong stop o jenjem gem?

Sakem vois, pusum tablet i go wei, talem "mi no save mekem dis" o luk i wari, dat olgeta i saen blong mekem i isi moa o stop. Traem ol les tile, tanem ol pikjas i stap antap, jenjem i go long [Find the same](/games/find-the-same/), o jes lukluk long ol pikjas mo toktok. Sipos hem i no wantem pleim nating, i OK tu. Pej blong mifala abaot [how to play together](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) i gat moa long pes mo help.
