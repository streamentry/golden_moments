Plante famli oli wantem wan gem we oli mekem wetem, we i olsem toktok, no olsem test. Twenty questions mo ol narafala gem blong gesem i save wok gud, from ol rul i to-to mo toktok i save go long eni samting. Samting we i impoten hemi se yumi jenisim ol rol we oli yusum usual: man ya i tinkem ansa, mo yu nomo i askem. Pej ya i eksplein hao blong ranem ol gem ya wetem sotfala fasin, mo wanem ol gem blong toktok we oli sut long ol man we oli gat dementia.

## Wanem nao mekem yumi jenisim ol rul we oli yusum?

Long wan kwis we i yusum usual, wan man i askem mo narafala i mas save long ansa. Olsem ia, man we i gat dementia i stap long wan presha we hem i save fail, mo fail i save mekem hem sed. Taem hem i jusum samting mo yu i gesem, hem i man we i save. Hem i holem ansa, hem i jusum taem blong talem yes o no, mo hem i save enjoyem taem hem i lukim yu i traem.

Ol [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) i advaesem ol man we oli lukaotem sipos oli yusum ol sot mo simpol sentens, wan kwestin i wan taem, mo wan kwaet mo patient toun. Ol fasin ya i sut gud long ol gem blong gesem.

## Hao blong ranem twenty questions, wetem sotfala fasin

1. **Jusum wan tema** long laef blong man ya, olsem kitjen, gaden, ol animol o solwota.
2. **Lusim hem i tinkem wan samting** mo talem i kwaet, o soem wan pikja kad blong yu no nid harem.
3. **Askem wan kwestin i wan taem, we ansa blong hem yes o no.** "Hem i samting we yu save kakae?" "Hem i bigfala moa long wan sus?"
4. **Akseptem eni ansa.** Wan nod, wan sek hed, o wan laf, evri wan i kaontem.
5. **Gesem long voes** mo enjoyem. Sipos yu gesem stret, selebretem wetem.
6. **Givim hint** taem man ya i olsem i stap long wan ples: "Hem i samting we i stap long kitjen."
7. **Nating kaontem ol kwestin** mo nating keepem score.

Sipos man ya i no save folem ol rul, jenisim i go long wan simpol woan: yu nemem sam samting mo hem i pointim we i stret.

## Ol gud samting blong jusum

- Wan fevrit fruit, vejitebol o flaoa
- Wan animol long farm, long zu o wan pet we hem i bin gat
- Wan tul, olsem hama, wan reik o wan nidol blong sew
- Wan furnitja o wan haosples samting
- Wan ples we hem i save gud, olsem wan pak o wan taon
- Wan kaen klos we hem i laekem blong wearem

Lusim ol nem blong ol man, eni samting we i save mekem sed i kam, mo ol samting we i save danesras o we ol man i save konfiusem.

## Ol moa gem blong toktok

- **Ya o Ya.** Givim tu jus blong jusum mo askem hem i laekem wanem: tea o kofi, ol maunten o solwota, wan red drես o wan blu wan. Nogat wan stret ansa.
- **Yu laekem moa.** Askem wan sot, laet kwestin: "Yu laekem moa wan pikniks o wan kaekae long aotsaed?"
- **Finisim ol toktok.** Wan fes toktok we i gat ol las wod i stap op. Traem ol [gem blong finisim toktok](/activities/finish-the-saying/) blong wan gaed.
- **Nemem ples.** Askem hem i tokem ol fevrit rum, taon o lukluk, mo askem hem blong soem yu tu.
- **Open prompt kad.** Ol [conversation kad](/games/conversation-cards/) blong mifala i givim open prompt long ol tema, mo yu save prentem i go long tebol.

Blong moa aedia we i yusum memori mo laef stori, lukim [ol kwestin blong tingting bak](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Blong kwis-stael fan we i stap gud, lukim [isi trivia blong ol sinia we oli gat dementia](/activities/trivia/), we i yusum jus mo nating skoring.

## Wanem nao sapos man ya i kros o i sed?

Stopem, smael mo talem samting olsem, "Yumi plei samting narafala." Kros i sign blong jenisim gem, nating blong pusum harder. Gaed blong mifala long [hao blong plei gem wetem man we i gat dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) i eksplein hao blong jenisim ol rul long taem.

## Wanem nao ol gem i save mekem, mo wanem i no save mekem

Ol gem blong toktok oli fanem mo oli mekem man i save kam tugeta. Oli save helpem man i kanekt mo sherem ol stori. Oli no save tretem dementia, slowem o preventem. Keepem ol aem i sipel: wan warm taem tugeta.
