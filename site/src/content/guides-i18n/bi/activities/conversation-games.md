Plante famli oli wantem wan gem blong pleim tugeta we i olsem toktok, no olsem test. Twenty questions mo ol narafala gem blong gesem oli save wok gud, from ol rul i simpol mo toktok i save go long eni samting. Samting i impoten, yu mas jenisim ol rol: man ya nao i tingting long ansa, mo yu nao i askem. Pej ya i eksplenem hao blong ranem ol gem ya wetem sves, mo wanem ol gem blong toktok we i fitim ol man wetem dementia.

## Wae nao yu jenisim ol usual rul?

Long wan kwiz we oli yusum, wan man i askem mo narafala i mas save ansa. Hemia i putum man we i gat dementia long ples we hem i save fel, mo fel i save mekem hem sore. Sipos hem nao i jusum samting mo yu nao i gesem, hem i expert. Hem i holem ansa, hem i disaed wen hem i talem yes o no, mo hem i save enjoe fo luk yu traem.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) i advaesem ol man we oli save kea long ol olfala blong yusum ol sentens we i sot mo simpol, wan kwestin long wan taem, mo wan toktok we i kwaet mo i gat patiens. Ol fasin ya i fitim ol gem blong gesem.

## Hao blong pleim twenty questions, wetem sves

1. **Jusum wan tema** long laef blong man ya, olsem kitchen, garden, ol animal o sipsaed.
2. **Letem hem i tingting long wan samting** mo talem long lelebet vois, o soem wan kad wetem piksa blong yu no nid harem.
3. **Askem wan kwestin long wan taem, we ansa i yes o no.** "Hemi wan samting we yu save kakae?" "Hemi bigfala moa long wan sus?"
4. **Akseptem eni ansa.** Wan nod, wan fes we i go saed, o wan lap, evri samting oli kaontem.
5. **Givim ol gess long vois** mo enjoe long olgeta. Sipos yu gesem stret, selebretem tugeta.
6. **Givim ol hint** taem man ya i luk i stakem: "Hemi wan samting long kitchen."
7. **Nating kaontem ol kwestin** mo nating kipim score.

Sipos man ya i no save folem ol rul, jenisim i go long wan vesen we i isi moa: yu nemem sam samting mo hem i pointim we i stret.

## Ol gud samting blong jusum

- Wan kaikai frut, vegetabol o flower we hem i laekem
- Wan animal long fam, long zoo o wan pet we hem i bin gat
- Wan tul, olsem hamma, rake o nidel blong sew
- Wan furnitja o wan samting blong haos
- Wan ples we hem i save gud, olsem wan gaden o wan taon
- Wan kaen klos we hem i laekem blong wearem

Yu mas no yusum nem blong ol man, nating samting we i save mekem sore, mo ol samting we i save kasem man o we i isi blong konfius.

## Ol moa gem blong toktok

- **This or that.** Givim tufala jvis mo askem wanem nao hem i prefer: ti o kofi, ol maon o ol solwota, wan red dres o wan blu wan. I no gat wan stret ansa.
- **Would you rather.** Askem wan gud, laet kwestin: "Yu bin laekem wan piknik o wan dinner long wan resto?"
- **Finish the saying.** Wan famili fraes we i gat ending we i no finis. Traem [ol gem blong finish-the-saying](/activities/finish-the-saying/) blong gaed.
- **Name the place.** Askem hem blong discraebem wan ples, wan taon o wan vyu we hem i laekem, mo askem hem blong letem yu tu i imajinem.
- **Open prompt cards.** [Ol kad blong toktok](/games/conversation-cards/) blong mifala i givim ol opem prompt long ol tema, we yu save printem blong putum long tebol.

Blong moa aedia we i yusum memori mo laef stori, luk [ol kwestin blong remembre](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Blong kwiz we i fanny we i gat laef, luk [isi trivia blong ol olfala wetem dementia](/activities/trivia/), we i yusum jus mo i no kaontem score.

## Wat sipos man ya i kros o i fraestret?

Stopem, smail mo talem wan samting olsem, "Mifala i pleim wan narafala samting." Fraestret hemi wan saen blong jenisim gem, i no blong pusum moa. Gaed blong mifala long [hao blong pleim gem wetem wan man we i gat dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) i eksplenem hao blong jenisim ol rul we yu stap pleim.

## Wat ol gem oli save mekem, mo wat oli no save mekem

Ol gem blong toktok oli fani mo oli mekem man i save wok wetem ol narafala. Oli save helpem ol man blong konektem mo sherem ol stori. Oli no tritim dementia, oli no slowem o stopem. Mekem gol i simpol: wan warm taem tugeta.
