Sometimes the best activity is one sheet of paper and a pen at the kitchen table. Printables need no charging, no passwords and no screen, and they are easy to take to a visit or a group. This page describes the free printable activities on our site, how to print them so they are easy to see and hold, and some simple homemade printables that families and activity staff find useful.

## Wanem fri printable activities yu gat?

Mifala i gat fo ol fri printable. Opnem eni long ol game pej ia mo yusum button **Prin** blong kasem wan versen we i gud blong printem.

| Printable | Wanem i hem | Gud long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wod Serch](/games/word-search/) | Wan wod serch long bigfala print, wetem ol wod nomo long saed mo long dong | Kwaet taem wetem pen; man o woman we i laekem ol puzzle |
| [Kalarem](/games/coloring/) | Ol pej blong kalarem blong printem, o blong kalarem long skrin | Relaks mo kriet taem; eni stej wetem help |
| [Kad blong Toktok](/games/conversation-cards/) | Kad wetem piksa mo open prompt long ol tema, no kwestin blong test | Vizit, kol, famili kaikai, grup blong toktok long taem blong bifoa |
| [Bingo wetem Piksa](/games/picture-bingo/) | Kad blong bingo wetem piksa instead blong namba, plus wan kolas long skrin | Haos blong olfala, dei program, famili gedering |

### Wod serch long bigfala print

[Wod Serch](/games/word-search/) i gat bigfala leta mo ol wod i stap long saed mo long dong nomo, no long kornar o go backwad. Wokem long hem tugeta: wan man i ridim wan wod long list, narafala i lukim long wod ia. Sipos i had blong faendem ol wod, traehem sokemaot long fes leta blong wan-wan wod tugeta, o jes ridim list blong ol wod long vois mo tokbaot long wan-wan wod.

### Pej blong kalarem

Ol [pej blong Kalarem](/games/coloring/) yu save printem blong ol marka o kala pensol, o yu save kalarem long tablet long taepem wan kala, long vet samting long piksa. Long ol printed pej, givim thik marka mo sam kala long wan taem nomo. Lukim [gaed blong arts and crafts](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) blong ol hop blong kipim kalarem i olfala mo i relaks.

### Kad blong toktok

[Kad blong Toktok](/games/conversation-cards/) i putum wan klia piksa wetem wan open prompt, olsem "Tokbaot long..." o "Wanem yu lukim?" Oli raetem olsem open invitesen, no kwestin blong test. Printem wan set, katemaot, mo hipim sam long wan bag blong vizit, o long sajsaed blong telefon blong kol. Oli gud tu long [grup blong toktok long taem blong bifoa](/activities/reminiscence/).

### Kad blong bingo wetem piksa

Kad blong [Bingo wetem Piksa](/games/picture-bingo/) oli yusum piksa instead blong namba. Printem wan kad blong wanwan plea mo yusum kolas long skrin long laptop, TV o projector. Mifala i sajestem blong kolem go kasem ol kad oli fulap, blong no eniwan i stap aot. Lukim [ol grup aktiviti blong care home](/activities/care-home-groups/) blong save hao blong ranem wan sesen.

## Hao blong printem ol aktiviti blong oli isi blong yusum?

Ol smol jenj long hao blong printem i mekem bigfala difrens.

### Mekem i isi blong luk

- **Bigfala, klia leta.** Yusum bigest saes we i stil fit long pej. Bambae yu no yusum ol fansi o skript fon.
- **Strong kontrast.** Dak ink long waet o krimi pepa. No yusum pale gre o kala bak.
- **Matte pepa** i redusum glea long ol bright lam.
- **Jeksem glas mo laet.** Alzheimer's Association i talem se ol problem long ae mo han i save mekem kominikesen i hadwan, mo i sajestem blong jekem ol ya oltaem. Wan bright, evin lam i helpem tu.

### Mekem i isi blong holem

- **Hevi pepa o kad stock** i stap flat, i isi blong pikimaot, mo i stanap gud long plante taem blong yusum.
- **Wan clipboard** o non-slip placemat i stopem pej blong no slaed.
- **Thik marka o fat pensol** i isi blong holem i winim tin pen.

### Mekem i save yusum bakegen

- **Laminetem ol pej** o putum long ol klia sit protector. Yusum dry-erase mako long wod serch mo bingo kad, afta wasem i klin.
- **Jusum matte pautj** sipos glea i wan problem, mo **raontem ol kona** blong ol laminetem kad wetem sisa blong i isi blong holem.
- **Kipim wan fóda** blong ol fevrit blong yu no mas printem sem pej evri wik.

### Kipim i simpol

- **Wan wok long wan pej.** Wan wod serch o wan piksa nomo, no sevral aktiviti i kroud tugeta.
- **Plante waet spes.** Wan bisi pej i save mekem man i fil i hevi.
- **No gat ol instraksen blong ridim, sipos i posibol.** Soem long man wot blong mekem, no talem long hem.
- **Katemaot ol sit.** Sipos wan fulpej i tumas, katemaot long hap o foldem blong jus sam i saed.

## Narafala yusful printable we yu save mekem long haos

Ol printable we i yusful i no olgeta i gem. Ol ia i isi blong mekem long wod prosesa mo printa.

### Pej blong stori blong laef

Wan pej blong "abaot mi" wetem wan foto, nem we man o woman i laekem blong oli singautem hem, ples we hem i bon long hem, wok blong hem, famili, kaikai we hem i laekem, singsing mo samting we i kamfotem o i mekem hem i sot. Dementia Australia i talem se wan [memory buk](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/visiting-someone-dementia) wetem ol detel abaot laef, intres mo ol important man o woman i help ol vizitor mo ol man we i lukaftarem, mo i helpem toktok.

Hem i spesol yusful long ol hospitol mo ol niu care setting. NIA i sajestem se yu givim long ol wok blong hospitol wan [pej blong personal infomesen](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/taking-person-alzheimers-disease-hospital) we i gat routin blong man o woman, nem we hem i laekem, samting we hem i laekem mo i no laekem, samting we i save mekem hem i sot, mo sain blong pein we hem no save talem.

### Skedul blong dei

Wan bigfala, simpol skedul blong dei wetem taem, sam wod, mo wan smol piksa blong wanwan pat: brekfast, wokabaot, lanj, rest, singsing, dinna. Alzheimer's Association i sajestem long [daily care plan](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) blong planem aroun ol bes taem blong dei blong man o woman, wetem taem blong wekap mo slip we i stap sem, mo balans blong wok mo rest. Putum long ae lefel, maybe long fridge, mo mekem i flexibol. Lukim [gaed blong routin blong dei](/activities/daily-routine/) blong moa.

### Piksa leba blong ol rum mo ol drower

NIA i sajestem blong yusum ol braet kala sain o simpol piksa blong [leba long bathrum, bedrum mo kitjen](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips). Sem aedia i wok tu long ol drower mo ol kabod: wan piksa blong sok long sok drower, kap long kap kabod. Printem, laminetem, mo tapem.

### Set blong sotem

Printem piksa long tu simpol kategori, olsem frut mo flaoa o tul mo kitjen samting, long kad stock. Katemaot mo sotem tugeta long tu basket. Long skrin, ting ia i [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/).

### Pej blong singsing wod

Printem ol wod blong sam fevrit singsing long veri bigfala taep blong singsing tugeta. Evin man o woman we i no ridim bigwan nao save laekem blong holem pej.

### Pej blong famili foto

Wan pej wetem foto blong famili fo i go kasem sikis, long wanwan foto wetem fasnem nem andanit. Hem save stap long tebol blong eniwan blong pikimaot mo tokbaot. Ol leba i helpem ol vizitor mo ol wok blong joinem long toktok, mo no gat wan we i mas askem "Hu nao hem?"

### Kad blong resipi

Printem wan fevrit famili resipi long bigfala taep. Yusum hem blong kuk tugeta, o jes blong tokbaot ol kaikai long taem blong bifoa.

## Hao blong ofem wan printable taem hem no fil olsem homwok?

Presentem hem olsem samting blong sheraot, no wan test.

- **Sidaon mo stat hem yu seleva.** "Mi wokem diswan wod serch. Yu laekem blong helpem mi faendem 'garden'?"
- **Wokem tugeta.** Tekem tan, o wan i faendem mo narafala i sekem.
- **No save markem stret o rong.** Gaed blong Alzheimer's Association long [aktiviti long haos](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/5337a7c3-15c6-4b4d-a3a0-ca00a0839a6b/alzheimers-dementia-activities-at-home-ts.pdf) i advaesem blong fokas long prosesi, no long rizolt, mo long fal gud, no long kasem wan samting.
- **Lusum ol pej.** Sem gaed ia i sajestem blong putum ol scrapbuk, foto albam o ol olfala magasin long ples we i isi blong kasem. Ol printed kad mo foto pej oli save stap long wan saed tebol long sem wei.
- **Stopem wen yu laekem.** Hap pej blong kalarem i fin nomo blong wan aftanun. Mo gud tu blong putum pej i go wan saet mo drinkem wan kap tea.
