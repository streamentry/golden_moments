Ol projek art i wan gud fasin blong spendem taem wetem wan man o woman we i gat dementia. Oli wakem han, ae mo ol sens blong hem, mo oli givim yu samting blong toktok long hem. Pej ya i givim spesifik projek wetem ol simpol stap, mo gaed blong jusum ol art samting we i sef. Blong ol prinsipel blong adalt art, olsem blong no yusum ol samting blong pikinini, luk [ol art mo kraf blong ol adalt we oli gat dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Hao blong stat eni projek art

1. **Redi evrisamting bifo yu stat.** Putum aot wan o tu samting, wan sit blong kavremap, mo wan wara klos.
2. **Soem fes stap.** Dipim bras, rip fes pis, o skwiss wan smol pis kle, mo askem hem blong mekem sem samting.
3. **Wok saed-bae-saed.** Karem projek blong yu i stap klosap, blong aktiviti i fil olsem wan samting we yumi duim tugeta, no olsem yu stap lukaotem hem.
4. **Toktok long wanem we yu luk.** Talemaot ol kala, teksja mo ol sep, no jajem wok ya.
5. **Stopem bifo enesi i les.** Ten to twenti minit i oftentaem wan gud lelen.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) i talem se art i wan fasin blong soemaot fil mo kriet, withaot yu nidim wan artist skil. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i advaesem blong brekem aktiviti i go long ol smol stap we i ken mekem.

## Wasa kala

Yusum hevi wota kala pepa, wan bigfala soft bras, mo sam kala long ol smol kap we i sel. Wetem klin wota, wasem pepa, thenem lukim man i drop kala i go long hem. Tiltem pepa gentol blong lusim ol kala i ran. Risalt i wan soft, abstrak pikja we i no nidim wan plan.

- **Ea stej:** Lusim hem jusum ol kala mo pentem wan lanskep.
- **Midol stej:** Givim tu o tri kala mo yu wasem pepa blong hem.
- **Leta stej:** Holem pepa mo hem i dab wetem wan sponj.

## Kolaj blong magasin

Kasem ol magasin pej we i gat brait pikja blong ful, kakae, animol, ka o trevel. Rip ol pikja i go long bigfala pis, we i isi moa winim kat, mo glu i go long wan pis kadbod. Kolaj i gud blong stat toktok, from wan pikja i ken kasem wan memori. I fitim man we i laekem blong sotem mo jusum samting.

## Soft kle

Air-dry kle o soft modeling kle i ken pinisim, rolem, mo skwiss. Mekem wan simpol bolo o wan flat tael. Kle i gat strong tatail fil, mo i ken mekem man i kwaet taem hem i holem samting. Kipim ol pis i bigfala, mo no yusum ol tul we i gat sap tip.

## Sok pastel long dak pepa

Dak pepa i mekem ol pastel kala i laet. Yusum ol bigfala pastel we i tik mo lusim man i mekem bros stroek o mikis wetem fingga o wan tisiu. Pastel i gat dast, so setemap long wan tre mo wasem han afta. Jusum soft pastel we i gat leibel se i no gat pwaesen.

## Prin lif

Kasem ol bigfala lif long gaden, brasem wasebol pent long ol vein, mo presem lif i go long pepa. Pilim i aot long fasin i kea, mo luk gud long prin. Projek ya i konekt gud wetem [aktiviti long aotsaed mo gaden](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), mo i ken stat wetem wan wokabaot blong kasem ol lif.

## Kapet mo ful arenjmen

Sotem ol kapet pis we i gat teksja long fil, o arenjem sam fres ful long wan vaes. Ol projek ya i sensori mo i gat veri smol presa. Oli fitim tu ol man long leta stej. Blong moa aedia, luk [sensori aktiviti blong ol man wetem dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Jusum ol art samting

Samting i impoetant moa blong sef mo komfot, i no blong kwaliti. Taem yu jusum, lukaotem:

- **No gat pwaesen mo i ken wasem:** Lukim ol leibel we i talem se i no gat pwaesen, mo jekem se pent i ken wasem.
- **Bigfala hanol:** Bigfala kreyon, tik bras mo tik pastel i isi blong holem.
- **No gat smol pis:** Hemi nogud blong yusum ol bid, batan, sekwin, mo lous glita we i ken kakae.
- **No gat sap tul:** Yusum sef sisa o rip pepa wetem han.
- **Alajik:** Jekem latex, mo eni skin problem we man i save gat.
- **Kontrast:** Bold kala long plen waet pepa, o long dak pepa, i isi blong luk.
- **Smol lis:** Tu o tri samting i stret. Plante jus i ken mekem man i konfiusem.
- **Isi klinap:** Yusum wan tre, wan plastik sit mo wan wasem klos, blong mes i no stopem projek.

## Adapt long stej

Long ea stej, plante man oli ken planem mo finisim wan projek wetem sam help. Long midol stej, ofa smol jus mo moa gaed. Long leta stej, lukaotem sensori proses olsem skwiss kle o dab pent wetem sponj, mo injoe risalt, no finis lukluk.

Blong moa aidia long stej, luk [ol aktiviti long ea stej](/activities/early-stage/), [midol stej](/activities/middle-stage/) mo [leta stej](/activities/late-stage/). Sapos man o woman i no wantem o i kros, gaed blong yumi long [wen someone i talem no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) i soem hao blong stepbak.

## Pea wetem wan geim

Afta long wan sesen blong pentem, traem [coloring](/games/coloring/) long wan tablet blong wan kwaet folo-ap, o wan [picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) blong wan finis aktiviti. Tufala i kipim han i bisi withaot yu askem wan fain skil.
