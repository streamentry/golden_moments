Ol projek aat i wan gudfala fasin blong spendem taem wetem man o woman we i gat dementia. Hem i yusum han, ae mo ol sens blong olgeta, mo i givim yu wan samting blong toktok long hem. Pej ya i givim ol spesifik projek wetem ol simpol step, mo advaes long hao blong jusum ol samting aat we i sef blong yusum. Blong ol prinsip long adalt aat, olsem blong no yusum ol samting we oli fit ol pikinini, luk [arts and crafts for adults with dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Hao blong statem eni projek aat

1. **Redi bifo yu statem.** Putum wan o tu samting, wan protektiv sit mo wan wet klaot.
2. **Soem fastem stap.** Dipim brush, tearim fastem pis, o skwisim wan smol lum klei, mo askem olgeta blong duim sem samting.
3. **Wok long saed.** Kipim projek blong yu klosap, so aktiviti i fil olsem wan wok tugeta, no wan wok we yu stap lukaotem.
4. **Toktok long wanem yu luk.** Talem ol kala, teksja mo ol sep, no jajem wok ya.
5. **Stopem bifo enejis i ded.** Ten long twenty minit i ofen i gud lenkt.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) i talem se aat i wan rod blong soem fil mo kriet, nogat nid blong gat skil blong aat. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i advaesem blong brekem ol aktiviti long ol smol step we oli isi blong mekem.

## Wota-kala wash

Yusum hevi pepa blong wota-kala, wan bigfala sof brush mo sam kala long ol smol kap we i no hae. Wetem klin wota, wetem pepa, then letem olgeta drop kala long hem. Tiltim pepa isi blong letem ol kala i ran. Oli kasem wan sof piksa we i no abstrak mo i no nid wan plan.

- **Erli stej:** Letem olgeta jusum ol kala mo pentem wan lanskep.
- **Midol stej:** Ofrim tru o tri kala, mo yu mekem wetting blong olgeta.
- **Laswan stej:** Holem pepa taem olgeta i daebem wetem wan sponj.

## Collage blong ol piksa long magasin

Kasem ol pej long magasin we i gat bright piksa blong ol flaoa, kakae, animol, kar o travel. Tearim ol piksa long bigfala pis, we i isi moa from katem, mo gluim olgeta long wan sit blong kad. Collage i wok gud olsem wan stat blong toktok, from wan piksa i save lidim long wan memori. Hem i sut long man o woman we i laekem blong sotem mo jusum.

## Sof klei

Air-dry klei o sof modelling klei i save pinsim, rolem mo skwisim. Mekem wan simpol bolo o wan flat tael. Klei i gat strong fil blong tas, so hem i save mekem man o woman we i laekem blong holem ol samting i fil kwaet. Kipim ol pis i bigfala, mo avoidim ol tul we i gat sharp pointi.

## Cok pastel long blakfala pepa

Blakfala pepa i mekem ol pastel kala i laet. Yusum bigfala tikfala pastel mo letem man o woman mekem ol broad stroke o blendem wetem fingga o wan tissue. Pastel i gat dast, so setemap long wan tre mo klinim han afta. Jusum ol soft pastel we oli label se non-toxic.

## Printing wetem lif

Kasem ol bigfala lif long gaden, brushim washable pent long ol vens, mo pressem lif long pepa. Pilim i aot isi mo lukluk long print. Projek ya i konekt gud wetem [ol aktiviti aotsaed mo gaden](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), mo hem i save statem wetem wan walk blong kasem ol lif.

## Ol kapa mo flaoa blong arenjem

Sotem ol textured fabric swatches long wei oli fil, o arenjem sam fresh flaoa long wan vaes. Ol projek ya i sensory mo oli gat very smol presa. Oli sut tu long ol man mo woman long laswan stej. Blong moa ol aidia, luk [sensory activities for people with dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Jusum ol samting blong aat

Ol samting blong yusum i impoten moa blong sef mo kamfot, no blong kwaliti. Taem yu jusum, lukaotem:

- **Non-toxic mo washable:** Lukaotem ol lebel we i talem non-toxic mo jekim se ol pent i washable.
- **Bigfala handel:** Jumbo kreon, tik brush mo tik pastel i isi blong holem.
- **No smol pis:** Avoidim ol bid, batan, sikwin mo lus glitter we oli save kakae.
- **No sharp tul:** Yusum sef sisa o tearim pepa wetem han.
- **Alajik:** Jekim blong latex mo eni skin sensitiviti we yu save long hem.
- **Kontrast:** Bold kala long plain waet o long blakfala pepa i isi blong luk.
- **Shot lis:** Tu o tri samting i stret. Tumas jus i save konfusem.
- **Isi blong klinim:** Yusum wan tre, wan plastik sit mo wan wet klaot, so mes i no save stopem yu.

## Jenisim long stej

Long erli stej, plande man mo woman oli save plan mo finisim wan projek wetem smol helpim. Long midol stej, ofrim lesfala jus mo moa gaed. Long laswan stej, fokes long sensory proses olsem skwisim klei o daebem pent wetem sponj, mo enjoyim rijal samting, no finis wok ya.

Blong moa ol aidia long stej, luk [ol aktiviti long erli stej](/activities/early-stage/), [ol aktiviti long midol stej](/activities/middle-stage/) mo [ol aktiviti long laswan stej](/activities/late-stage/). Sapos man o woman i no wantem o i kros, gaed blong yumi long [taem man o woman i talem no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) i eksplenem hao blong stap bak.

## Pea wetem wan gem

Afta wan seson blong pentem, traem [kalaring](/games/coloring/) long tablet blong wan kwaet folo-ap, o wan [pikja pasol](/games/picture-puzzle/) blong wan finis aktiviti. Tufala i kipim han i stap busy wetem no askem wan fain skil.
