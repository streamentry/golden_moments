Kala i wan long ol isi kriesen aktiviti we yu save ofa long man o woman we oli gat Alzheimer's o narafala kaen dementia. I no nidim ridim, i no nidim memori, mo i no gat stret ansa. Han i save muv, ol kala i save kamaot, mo i gat wan pija we yumi save toktok long hem. Dokumen ya i talem hao blo jusum ol pej, wanem blo setem, mo hao blo kala tugeta we i no mekem i luk olsem pikinini o olsem wan test.

## From wanem kala i gud long plante man mo woman we oli gat dementia

Kala i givim ol han wan samting blo mekem mo ol ae wan samting blo lukluk. Yu save mekem long wan tebol o long wan jea klosap long wan windo, mo yu save kasem mo leftemap i isi. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) i talem se art i wan rod blo soem ol fil mo blo enjoyem naoia, mo i talem se i no nidim skil blo art.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) i sajestem blo jusum ol aktiviti we i fitim wanem man ya i save stil mekem, mo blo jenisem taem ol abiliti i jenis. Kala i isi blo jenisem: wan bigfala pija, tik kreyon, fewfala kala, o wan skrin we yu tajem blo kala.

## Jusum ol adult dizaen

Ol adult oli no laekem ol pija blo pikinini. Lukem ol samting ya:

- **Ol nature sin:** wan gaden wetem ol bigfala flaoa, wan lek wetem wan bot, ol pijin long wan brans.
- **Ol samting we i fala:** wan tipot, wan bisik, wan pis fruit, wan hat.
- **Ol bigfala simpol pattern:** ol mandala mo ol bold geometric fom we i gat fewfala bigfala sekson.
- **Ol art print o ol lanskep** we man ya i save rekognaes from wan fevret ples o wan fevret pentor.

Ting long ol pej we i gat smol, krauded ples, we i mekem frustresen blo filimap. Ol adult pej blo printem i gud wan jojo, mo ol [fri aktiviti blo dementia we yu save printem](/activities/printable/) i gat ol pej we i gat bigfala raet.

## Wanem blo setem

- **Tik mo isi blo holem ol tul.** Ol jambo kreyon, ol chunky mak, o ol kala pensol we i gat waed grip, oli isi moa long ol tin pen.
- **Wan smol palet.** Tri o foa kala i stap gud. Plante jes i save mekem man i konfiusem.
- **Strong pepa o kad.** Tin pepa i save brok mo slaed. Tajem ol kona blo hem o yusum wan klipbod.
- **Wan klia tebol.** Rimuvum ol samting we i stap aroun mo setem wan pej long wan taem.
- **Samting blo dip o blendim,** olsem wan klos o wan spans, blo mekem wan sofwan efek.

## Kala long tablet

[Kala gem](/games/coloring/) long Golden Moments i wok long we yu tajem wan kala mo tajem wan pat blo pija. I no nidim strong han blo kasem, so i save fitim ol man we ol han blo olgeta i wik finis. Sitdaon klosap long man ya blo yu save sherem skrin, mo letem hem jusum ol kala. Ofum ol notifikesen blo pija i stap fokes.

## Mekem kala tugeta

1. **Statem fastfala strok tugeta.** Holem kreyon wetem hem, o putum han blo yu antap long han blo hem long sam strok, bambae yu lusum.
2. **Toktok long ol kala mo ol ples.** "Dis wan i gud blu. Yu bin lukim wota we i kala olsem ya wea?" i kolem toktok nomo, i no kwestin.
3. **Givim gud toktok long ol jusum blo hem, no long hao i klin.** "Yu jusum ol warm kala we i gud tumas" i gud moa blong harem dan "Yu stap insaed long ol lain."
4. **Letem hem i hed.** Sipos hem i wantem blakem ful pej, letem hem.
5. **Stopem taem hem i stopem.** Putumdaon ol kreyon, mo sajesta wan kap blo ti o wan wakabaot.

## Jenisem taem dementia i jenis

Long fasfes stej, ol man oli enjoyem ol detaelt pej mo oli save jusum ol kala mo ol dizaen blo olgeta. Long midol stej, ol bigfala pija mo fewfala jes i help. Long leitfala stej, traem wan bigfala fom, wan sit blo soft kala, o jes holem wan kreyon taem yu singsing wan smol soen. Sam man i enjoyem lukim wan finis pija moa dan mekem wan.

Blo moa long ol kriesen wok long eni stej, lukim [arts and crafts for adults with dementia](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) mo [art projects for people with dementia](/activities/art-projects/).

## Taem blo stop

Stopem sipos man ya i kros, i pusum pepa i go, o i luk i taed. Finisim long tu minit i wan sakses sipos hem i enjoyem. Yu save kambak bihaen, o traem wan narafala pija o wan narafala aktiviti olsem [ol gem blo fil long banis](/activities/sensory/).
