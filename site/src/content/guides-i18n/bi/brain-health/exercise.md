Muv i wan long ol habit we ol man i stadi moa long hem long risej blong brain. Ol stadi i konektem hem wetem lo risk blong dementia, be oli no pruvum se wok blong bodi i stopem dementia. Wanem we i klia, muv i help long strong, balance, fasin mo slip long eni ej. Pej ya i talem wanem risej i talem, hamas muv we ol eldaly oli nidim, mo ol naesfala wei blong muv wetem man o woman we oli lukaftarem wan pipol we oli gat dementia.

## Ez wok blong bodi i low risk blong dementia?

Risej i soemaot se ol pipol we oli muv oli less laekli blong devlopem dementia, be ol evidens oli gat ol limit. Long [2024 Lancet Commission long dementia](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0), i lisimaot no muv i wan long 14 risk factor we ol man i save jenisim. Tugeta, ol 14 factor ya oli kasem abaot 45% blong dementia long wol, long model blong hem.

Ol evidens oli olsem ya:

- **Ol stadi blong obsevesen i soemaot wan fasin.** Wan review blong 58 stadi, we Commission i diskasim, i faenem se ol pipol we oli muv oli gat abaot 20% lo risk blong dementia. Bigfala difren i stap bitwin ol pipol we oli no muv hemgud nating mo ol we oli muv sam samting.
- **Link ya i ken go tu stret.** Commission i talem se no muv i ken rendem risk. I talem tu se ol jenis long brain we oli stat plande yia bifo dagnosis, oli ken mekem man i muv les.
- **Trial oli no klia olsem.** Long wan trial, 945 eldaly we avresem edj blong olgeta i 78, oli sanem olgeta blong wok blong bodi wetem wan dokta blong lukaotem twis long wik long faev yia, o oli stap long wan grup blong kompere. Long las, tingting skil i no difren klia namel long ol grup. Plande man long grup blong kompere tu oli muv, we i mekem risej ya i harem blong ridim.

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know) i talem klia. Oli no bin soemaot klia link namel muv blong bodi mo prevensen blong Alzheimer, mo i no gat enaf evidens long trial blong talem se wok blong bodi i prevent o slowem hem. Wok blong bodi i stil redyusim risk blong fol, depresen, diabetes, hae blad mo stroke. Sam long ol kondisen ya oli stret konektem wetem tingting we i wikdaon.

Tru fasin blong muv hem from hem i help yu fil gud mo wok gud tedei. Eni gud samting long brain i wan posibol bonus, no wan promis.

## Hamas wok blong bodi ol eldaly oli nidim?

[Gaed blong CDC long ol adalt 65 mo olsem](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/older-adults.html) i sujestem tri kaen muv long wan wik:

| Kaen | Wik hamas | Ol example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aerobic | Namel 150 minit long midol muv, olsem 30 minit long 5 dei | Kuik wokabaot, dens, wota aerobic |
| Strong blong muskol | Namel 2 dei, we i wokem ol bigfala muskol blong bodi | Lift lait wet o tin soup, stretch band |
| Balance | Oltaem | Wokabaot heel to toe, stanap from wan jea |

Ol ya oli goal, no ol kondisen blong stat. CDC i talem se sipos yu no kasem ol ya, yu mas muv olsem wanem yu kapablem mo helt blong yu i alaoem. Sam muv i gud moa dan nating.

## Gud wei blong muv long ol eldaly

- **Wokabaot wetem kompani.** Wan dei wokabaot wetem fren, neiba o wan grup blong wokabaot i ademap tok mo saf fasin.
- **Chea exercise.** Hanem raes sitdaon, ankel sekol, marsj long ples mo chea yoga i strongem bodi wetem lo risk blong lus balance.
- **Dens long kitchen.** Putum music we man ya i save long taem blong hem i yang, mo swae, tap o step. [Gaed blong music activity](/activities/music/) i gat ol aedia.
- **Strong wetem ol samting long haos.** Lift botol wota we i fulap o tin soup taem yu lukluk wan sho we man ya i save.
- **Gaden o duim ol wok long haos.** Waterem ol plant, rek, swip mo foldem ol klos, olgeta i kaontem. Lukluk [ol activity long aotsaed mo gaden](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) mo [ol wok long haos](/activities/household-tasks/).
- **Wok long wota.** NIA i lisimaot ol klas aerobic long wota olsem wan chans blong ol man we ol joint blong olgeta i hard long muv long graon.

## Hao blong muv wetem wan man we i gat dementia

Long wan man we i gat dementia, aim i blong kamfot, hapi mo keepem ol abiliti blong ol dei, no blong kasem wan namba. NIA i talem se muv i helpem blong keepem ol muskol, joint mo heart blong wan man we i gat Alzheimer i stap gud, mo i ken tu sanem gud fil long mood mo slip. Bifo yu stat nyufala program, askem dokta blong hem. Ol tips long [gaed blong NIA long ol caregiver](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) i incud:

1. **Wokabaot tugeta long dei.** Jusim wan saf, familia rod we i klia long ol obstakol. Sipos man ya i wokabaot wanem, wan ID bracelet we i gat nomba blong fon blong yu i gud blong duim.
2. **Traem ol video exercise blong ol eldaly.** Folem tugeta, sitdaon o stanap.
3. **Kipim ol sesen i sot.** Plande smol "mini-workout" i ken gud moa dan wan longfala.
4. **Putum music.** Ol song we i familia mekem muv i fil olsem wan activity, no wan wok. Dens sipos man ya i laekem.
5. **Givim wota mo ol sup we oli kamfot.** Givim wota o jus long taem mo afta, mo jusim ol klos we oli kamfot mo ol sus we oli fit, we oli mekem blong exercise.

Sipos wokabaot i hard, [gaed blong NIA long kea blong man we i gat Alzheimer](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) i sujestem ol wei blong sitdaon. Ol ya i incud chea exercise o yoga, bean bag toss, paoem wan soft bol o balun, stretch band, lift ol lait samting long haos, mo pedal exercise machine. Ol wok smol olsem swip mo dast tu i kaontem.

Yu mas oning long wat muv i ken duim. Lancet Commission i no faenem evidens se exercise i redyusim ol jenis long bihevia olsem agitesen. Muv tugeta from i ken mekem yu fil gud, no olsem wan wei blong fiksim wan hard afta. Sipos man ya i talem no, i oraet. Gaed blong yumi long [taem oli talem no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) i gat ol wei blong traem gen leta. Stopem erli, o jus sitdaon aotsaed tugeta, i wan gud rizolt tu.

Afta muv, wan kuaet pos olsem [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) i ken helpem yu mo hem blong settol. Long wan seated activity we i gat gentel muv blong han, [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) mo [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) i gud long wan tebol.

## Balance mo seif long fol

Fol i kamen mo i serius. [Ol risej blong CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/falls/data-research/facts-stats/index.html) i talem se moa dan wan long fo ol eldaly i fol long wan yia, be lesthan hafala i talem long dokta blong olgeta. Wan taem fol i dablem chance blong fol gen. Ol risk factor we CDC i lisimaot i incud:

- Wik leg mo hip
- Hard blong wokabaot mo balance
- Sam medisin, incud sedative mo sam antidepressant
- Problem long ae
- Pen long fut o ol sus we oli no gud
- Ol denja long haos olsem kluta, rag we i no fiks gud, mo ol step we oli brok

Blong muv i moa saf:

- **Yusum wan strong jea** we i no gat wil, mo kipim wan kaontta o rel we i klos long hand blong stanap long taem blong exercise.
- **Klinim ples** blong rag, kebol mo kluta fastaem.
- **Go slo taem yu stanap**, mo stopem sipos man ya i fil dizi o i no stedi.
- **Wearem sus we i sapotem fut**, no sok o ol slipa we i lus.
- **Praktisim balance klosap long samting we i sapotem.** Stanap from jea mo wokabaot heel to toe, CDC i givim olgeta olsem ol example blong balance activity.

CDC i talem se wan eldaly we i fol mo hitim hed blong hem i mas go long dokta kwiktaem, namo sipos hem i yusum blood thinner.

## Toktok wetem dokta

Askem dokta bifo yu stat wan nyufala program blong exercise, nemoa sipos man ya i gat problem long hat o long lung, pen long joint, problem blong balance, o dementia. Dokta o physical therapist i ken sujestem ol saf activity mo jekem sipos medisin i affektem balance. Talem long dokta long eni fol, nomo sipos i no gat injuri. Sipos eni samting i pen o i no fil stret taem yu muv, stopem. Long sudden pen long jest, hevi blong win, o sudden konfiuzen, kolem emergency namba blong ples ya.

Pej ya i jis gener infomesen, no medikol advaes. Lukluk long [medical disclaimer blong yumi](/medical-disclaimer/).
