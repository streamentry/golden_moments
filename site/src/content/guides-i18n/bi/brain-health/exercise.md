Aktiviti blong bodi i wan long ol habit we ol pipol i stadi moa long saentis blong brein. Ol stadi i link long hem wetem lo risk blong dementia, be oli no bin pruvum se wok blong bodi i stopem dementia. Wanem we i klia, hem se muv i halpem strong, balans, fil gud mo slip long eni ej. Pej ia i talem wanem ol risej i talem, hao moas aktiviti ol olfala oli nidim, mo ol sofman wei blong muv wetem wan man we i stap wetem dementia.

## Wok blong bodi i save lowasem risk blong dementia?

Ol risej i sanem se ol pipol we oli muv plante oli less lelkely blong gat dementia, be ol evidens oli gat ol limit. [Lancet Commission long 2024 blong dementia](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) i lisimbetem fiziks no-wok olsem wan long 14 risk factor we pipol i save jenjem. Long ol namba ia long model blong hem, ol 14 factor ia i kasem abaot 45% blong dementia long wol.

Hemia nao evidens i lukem olsem:

- **Ol stadi we oli lukluk nomo i soem wan save.** Wan risiu blong 58 stadi, we Commission i talem, i faendem se ol pipol we oli muv oli gat abaot 20% lo risk blong dementia. Biggest defren i kam bitwin ol pipol we oli no muv hardhad nomo mo ol we oli muv sam samting.
- **Link ia i save go tu wanem.** Commission i talem se no muv i save raes risk. I talem tu se ol earl jenis long brein blong dementia, we i stat planti yia bifo ol i faendemaot, i save mekem pipol less muv.
- **Trial oli no klia.** Long wan trial, 945 olfala we oli gat avrej ej 78 oli bin putum long faev yia blong wok blong bodi tu taem long wik we wan man i lukaftarem, o long wan grup blong lukluk nomo. Long en, save blong tingting i no defren klia bitwin ol grup. Plante man long grup blong lukluk nomo tu oli muv, so rizol ia i hard blong ridim.

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know) i talem klia. Oli no bin soem klia link bitwin aktiviti blong bodi mo stopem Alzheimer's, mo i no gat enaf evidens long trial blong talem se wok blong bodi i stopem o slowem ia. Wok blong bodi i stap lowasem risk blong foldaon, sedness, daebetis, hae blad mo stroq. Sam long ol kondisen ia i stap link tu wetem slo blong tingting.

Wanem we i stret blong talem, hemia se yu muv from hem i halpem yu fil gud mo wok gud long distrik. Eni gud samting long brein i wan posibol bonas, no wan promes.

## Hao moas aktiviti ol olfala oli nidim?

[Gaed blong CDC long ol pipol 65 yia i go antap](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/older-adults.html) i sanem tri kaen aktiviti long wan wik:

| Kaen | Wik amaunt | Ol samp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aerobic | Sot long 150 minit blong midol aktiviti, tosem 30 minit long 5 de | Kwik wokabaot, dans, wota aerobic |
| Strong blong muskol | Sot long 2 de, wok long ol bigfala muskol | Lift ol lait wet o sup kan, stretsing bens |
| Balans | Oltaem | Wokabaot wetem hil long tos, stanap long sia |

Ol ia i ol goal, no ol fasin blong stat. CDC i talem se sipos yu no save mekem ol ia, yu mas muv olsem olsem wat yu save mekem long bodi mo helt blong yu. Sam aktiviti i gud moa lo nating.

## Gud ways blong muv long ol olfala

- **Wokabaot wetem kampani.** Wan dei wokabaot wetem wan fren, neba o wokabaot grup i ademap tok mo sef.
- **Wok long jea.** Sakem han i go antap long sia, ankol sekel, mas long ples mo joga long jea i strongem bodi wetem no risk blong ol.
- **Dans long kicen.** Putum musik we man ia i save long taem blong hem i yangfala, mo swing, tap o step. [Gaed blong musik](/activities/music/) i gat ol aedia.
- **Strong wetem ol samting long haos.** Lift ol botol wota we i fulap o sup kan taem yu lukluk wan fasin program we hem i like.
- **Gaden o wok long haos.** Waterem ol plant, rek, swip mo foldem ol klos, ol ia evri wan i kaontem. Lukluk [aktiviti long gaden mo aotsaed](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/) mo [wok long haos](/activities/household-tasks/).
- **Wota wok.** NIA i lisimbetem ol klas blong wota aerobic olsem wan jans blong ol pipol we long jea i hard long jont blong olgeta.

## Hao blong wok wetem wan man we i stap wetem dementia

Long wan man we i stap wetem dementia, presten i komfot, fulhapi mo kipim ol save blong ol dei, no blong kasem wan namba. NIA i talem se wok blong bodi i halpem blong kipim muskol, jont mo heat blong wan man wetem Alzheimer's i stap gud, mo i save impruvum fil mo slip. Askem doctor blong hem bifo yu stat niu program. Ol tips long [gaed blong NIA long lukaftarem man](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) i inkludim:

1. **Wokabaot tugeta long ven ded.** Jusum wan sef, faeling rod we i klia long ol samting. Sipos man ia wokabaot wan nomo, wan bres ID wetem nomba blong fon blong yu i gud.
2. **Traem ol vidio blong wok blong olfala.** Folem tugeta, sidaon o stanap.
3. **Kipim ol seson i smol.** Plante smol "mini-workaot" i save wok gud moa winim wan longfala.
4. **Putum musik.** Ol song we oli save mekem muv i fil olsem aktiviti, no wan wok. Dans sipos man ia i laekem.
5. **Givim wota mo ol komfot sus.** Givim wota o juice long taem mo afta, mo jusum ol komfot klos mo ol gud fit sus we oli mekem blong wok.

Sipos wokabaot i hard, [gaed blong NIA long lukaftarem man wetem Alzheimer's](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf) i sajestem ol wei long sidaon. Ol ia i inkludim wok long jea o yoga, bin bag toss, tosem sof bol o bolun, stretsing bens, lift ol lait samting long haos, mo pedal exercise. Ol simpel wok olsem swip mo dasta tu i kaontem.

Yu mas stret long wanem muv i save mekem. Lancet Commission i no faendem eni pruf se wok blong bodi i redyusim jenis long bihevia olsem agitesen. Muv tugeta from i save mekem yufala fil gud, no olsem wei blong fiksim wan had afternun. Sipos man ia i talem no, i oraet. Gaed blong yu long [taem olgeta i talem no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) i gat ol gud wei blong traem gen bihaen. Stopem sia, o jas sidaon aotsaed tugeta, hemia i gud rizol tu.

Afta muv, wan kwaet pos olsem [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) i save halpem yufala boat blong setel. Blong wan sidaon aktiviti we i gat soft han muv, [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) mo [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) i gud long wan tebol.

## Balans mo sef long fol

Fol i komon mo i serious. [Ol CDC ripot](https://www.cdc.gov/falls/data-research/facts-stats/index.html) i talem se moa long wan long foa olfala i foldaon evri yia, be lesthan haf i talemaot long doctor blong olgeta. Foldaon wanfala i dablem jans blong foldaon gen. Ol risk factor we CDC i lisimbetem i inkludim:

- Wik long leg mo hip
- Hard long wokabaot mo balans
- Sam medisin, inkludim sedativ mo sam antidepresen
- Problem long Ae
- Pen long fut o ol sus we oli no gud
- Ol denja long haos olsem ol samting we oli kasem wei, ol rag we oli slip, mo ol steps we oli bruk

Blong muv i sef moa:

- **Yusum wan strong jea** we i no gat wil, mo kipim wan kaontas o rel klosap long hem blong stanap long ol wok.
- **Klinim ples** blong ol rag, ol waea mo ol samting we oli kasem wei, fastaem.
- **Go slow taem yu stanap**, mo stopem sipos man ia i fil dizi o i no stret.
- **Wearem sus we i sapot**, no sok o ol sip we oli loes.
- **Praktisim balans klosap long sapot.** Stanap long jea mo wokabaot wetem hil long tos, ol ia i ekzampol blong CDC long balans wok.

CDC i advaesem se wan olfala we i foldaon mo hitim hed blong hem i mas go lukim doctor kwik, espesol sipos hem i yusum blood thinner.

## Toktok wetem doctor

Askem doctor bifo yu stat niu wok blong bodi, espesol sipos man ia i gat problem long at o lang, pen long jont, problem long balans, o dementia. Wan doctor o fisio-terapist i save talem ol sef wok mo jekem sipos ol medisin i efektem balans. Talemaot long doctor long eni fol, evin sipos i no gat injuri. Sipos wan samting i pen o i no fil gud taem yu wok, stopem. Long sudden pen long jest, had blong wintem, o sudden konfiuzen, kolem namba blong emeijensi long ples blong yu.

Pej ia i jeneral save nomo, i no medikol advaes. Lukluk [medikol disklema blong mifala](/medical-disclaimer/).
