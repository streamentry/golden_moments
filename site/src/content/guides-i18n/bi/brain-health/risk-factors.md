Wan risk factor i wan samting we i garem konekson wetem wan hae janis blong kasem wan sik. Hemia i no oltaem wan kas, mo stap wan risk factor i no mining se yu bambae kasem dementia. ## Wanem ol 14 risk factor blong dementia?

Pesen blong each factor long tebol ya i estimate blong Commission long hao mak blong ol kes long wol we i garem konekson wetem factor ya, we oli raontem i kam long ol namba hol. Ol figiur i overlap, from plante man i garem plante risk long sem taem, mo hemia nao oli adem 45% nomo, no moa.

## Hao nao mi mas readem ol pesen ya?

Kea gud. Ripot blong Commission i talem plante samting we i gud blong tingting long hem:

- **Oli diskraebem ol populeson, no ol man.** [Samri blong ripot ya long University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) i talem se mekem risk i lelebet long wan populeson i no garantii se eni wan man bambae i no kasem dementia.
- **Oli asumem kos mo ples.** Ol man we i raetem i inkludim nomo ol factor we i garem klia proof, be oli talem se sam konekson i save partly kos nomo.
- **Oli ol figiur blong wol.** Plante data i kam long ol kantri we i gat hae inkam, mo tru pesen long wan kantri o komuniti bambae i difrent.
- **Ol smol pesen i save important.** Wan factor we i konektem 2% blong ol kes long wol i stap afektem wan bigfala namba blong ol man.

## Wanem ol risk factor we oli ademap long 2024?

Ripot blong 2024 i ademap tu factor long ol 12 we oli nemem long 2020:

- **Hae LDL cholesterol long middle life**, we i konektem long abaot 7% blong ol kes.
- **Vision loss we no bin tritim long later life**, we i konektem long abaot 2% blong ol kes.

## Wanem ol risk factor we yu no save jenisim?

National Institute on Aging i talem se [eij i wan bigfala risk factor blong Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease), mo no eni man i save jenisim hem. Ol jin tu i garem rol; wan gud nao jin we i afektem risk blong Alzheimer's i nem APOE. NIA i adem se ol saentis oli no save yet long fulfala wei wanem i kosem Alzheimer's long ol man bigfala namba. Lancet Commission i talem se ol risk we oli save jenisim oli save lolowem, no matter wanem jenetik risk blong wan man.

From eij mo jin oli garem bigfala rol, plante man we oli no garem eni long 14 risk factor oli kasem dementia. Hemia i no fol blong eni man.

## Wanem trial evidence i talem abaot jenisim ol risk ya?

Konektem wan risk factor wetem dementia i no sem wetem prufem se jenisim hem i lolowem risk blong dementia. Sam eksampol blong wanem trial evidence i stap:

- **Blood pressure.** Long SPRINT MIND trial, wan lelebet goal blong blood pressure i bigfala lolowem mild cognitive impairment. I no bigfala lolowem probable dementia, be trial ya i save tumas smol blong soemaot hemia ([SPRINT MIND long JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Hearing aids.** Lancet Commission i tokbaot wan trial blong hearing aid we i go long tri yia, we i no faendem eni efet long tingting olgeta, be i gat benefit long wan grup blong ol man we i garem hae risk. Ol stadi we i folem ol man long taem i konektem yusum hearing aid wetem lelebet risk. [Hearing guide blong mifala](/brain-health/hearing/) i garem moa.
- **Physical activity.** National Institute on Aging i talem se i no gat enaf klinik trial evidence blong talem se eksasaes i save stopem o slowem mild cognitive impairment o Alzheimer's, be sam stadi i talem se i save helpem wetem ol jenis we i kamaot from eij ([NIA long preventing Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Long trial we i wet long dementia blong kamap i slow mo i kostem tumas, so long plante factor, bes evidence bambae i kam long ol stadi we i folem ol man long plante yia. Evidence ya i yusful, be i wik moa dan wan trial.

## Wanem abaot sleep mo diet?

Commission i stadi long sleep mo diet, be i no adem ol ya long lis, from evidence i no strong o i no stret. Ol gaed blong mifala long [sleep](/brain-health/sleep/) mo [diet](/brain-health/diet/) i eksplenem wanem i save.

## Toktok wetem dokta

Yearly checkup i gud taem blong kamapim ol topik ya. Yu save karem lis ya mo askem:

- Wanem ol namba blong blood pressure, sugar blong blad mo cholesterol blong mi, mo eni wan i mas tesem?
- Mi mas tekem hearing mo ae blong mi tesem?
- Aktiviti we mi duim i sef mo i gud enaf blong mi?
- Eni medisin blong mi i save afektem memory o tingting?

Befo yu jenisim medisin, kaikai, fasting, eksasaes o sapleman, toktok wetem dokta fes. Sapos yu o man we yu lukaftarem i garem sudden konfiuson, wiknes long wan saed blong bodi, o trabol blong toktok, koolem 112.
