Risk factor i wan samting we i gat konekson wetem hae jans blong kasem wan sik. Hem i no kosem sik long evri kes, mo gat wan risk factor i no minim se yu bambae kasem dementia. Pej ya i listimap 14 risk factor blong dementia we Lancet Commission long 2024 i talem se yumi save jenisim, wanem ol risej oli soem konekson wetem, mo wanem yu save toktok long dokta.

## Wanem ol 14 risk factor blong dementia?

[Lancet Commission long 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0),
we 27 risej man long dementia oli raetem, i estimetem se 14 risk factor we oli
save jenisim, oli kasem abaot 45% long ol kes blong dementia long wol. Pesenteds
blong ong factor i stap long lisen, i minim share blong ol kes long wol we oli
gat konekson wetem factor ya, we oli raontem i kam long ol hol namba. Ol namba
oli overlap, from plante man oli gat plante risk long saem taem, nao oli no
kasem 45% nomo.

## Hao nao yu save ridim ol presentej ya?

Wetem gud fasin. Ripot blong Commission i talem ol samting ya we i gud yu tingting long hem:

- **Oli diskraebem ol pipol, no wan wan man.** Yunivesiti blong London [samri blong ripot](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors)
  i talem se redyus risk i wok long wan grup blong ol pipol, be i no gaarantem se
  wan man bambae i no kasem dementia.
- **Oli tingting se wanem i kosem samting i kasem.** Ol autoa oli inklud nomo ol
  factor we oli gat strong pruf, be oli talem se sam konekson oli save kosem samting nomo i wan pat.
- **Olgeta oli namba blong wol.** Plante data i kam long ol kantri we i gat hae inkam,
  mo sea blong wan kantri o wan komuniti i save i defren.
- **Ol smol presentej oli save impoten tumas.** Wan factor we i gat konekson wetem 2%
  long ol kes long wol, i stap affektem plante man tumas.

## Wanem ol risk factor oli ad long 2024?

Ripot blong 2024 i ad tufala factor long 12 we oli talem long 2020:

- **Hae LDL kolesterol long midol laef**, i gat konekson wetem abaot 7% long ol kes.
- **Lus blong laet we nating i kea long hem long las laef**, i gat konekson wetem abaot 2% long ol kes.

## Wanem risk factor we yumi no save jenisim?

Nasonol Institiut blong Olfala Man i talem se [ej i bigfala risk factor we yumi save long Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease),
mo nogat man i save jenisim. Jin tu i gat paot; wan gud save jin we i stap influenses risk blong Alzheimer hemi i nem APOE. NIA i ademap se ol saentist oli no save yet long wanem i kosem Alzheimer long ol man plante. Lancet Commission i talem se ol risk we yumi save jenisim oli save redyus, no matter wanem jenetik risk wan man i gat.

From ej mo jin oli gat bigfala paot, plante man we oli no gat eni long 14 risk factor ya oli kasem dementia. Hemia i no fol blong eniwan.

## Wanem trael evidens i talem long jenisim ol risk ya?

Konekton wan risk factor wetem dementia i no sem wetem pruvum se jenisim risk ya i redyus risk blong dementia. Hemia nomo sam example blong wanem trael evidens i stap long hem:

- **Presa.** Long trael SPRINT MIND, wan lelebet presa goal i redyusim mail cognitive impairment long strong wei. Be i no redyusim probable dementia long strong wei, maet from trael i smol tumas blong soem hemia ([SPRINT MIND long JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Masin blong herem.** Lancet Commission i talem wan trael blong masin blong herem we i go 3 ye, we i no soem eni nambawan jenis long tingting, be i gat benefit long wan grup blong ol man we oli gat hae risk. Ol stadi we oli folem ol man long taem oli soem se yusum masin blong herem i gat konekson wetem loe risk. Gaed blong yumi long [herem](/brain-health/hearing/) i gat moa.
- **Fizikol muvmen.** Nasonol Institiut blong Olfala Man i talem se i no gat enaf klinikol trael evidens blong talem se wok i save stopem o slouem mail cognitive impairment o Alzheimer, be sam stadi i soem se i save helpem long ol jenis we oli kasem ol olfala man.
  ([NIA long stopem Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Ol longfala trael we oli wetem dementia blong kamap, oli slow mo oli kosum plante, so long plante factor, we bambae i kam moa long ol stadi we oli folem ol man long ol ye. Hemia i yusful, be i wik moa long trael.

## Wanem long saed blong slip mo kaikai?

Commission i lukluk long slip mo kaikai be oli no putem olgeta long lis, from evidens i no strong no i no stedi enaf. Gaed blong yumi long [slip](/brain-health/sleep/) mo [kaikai](/brain-health/diet/) i eksplenem wanem i save.

## Toktok wetem dokta

Yiamel jek i gud taem blong raesem ol samting ya. Yu save kasem list ya mo askem:

- Wanem nambas blong presa, sugar blong blad mo kolesterol blong mi, mo eni long olgeta i nid blong tenkem?
- Mi mas tesem herem mo ae blong mi?
- Wok we mi stap mekem i sef mo i enaf long mi?
- Eni long ol medisin blong mi i save affektem memori o tingting?

Bifo yu jenisim ol medisin, kaikai, fasting, wok o ol supliment, toktok long dokta fastaem. Sapos yu o man we yu stap kea long hem i gat sudden konfiuson, wik long wan saed blong bodi, o i hard blong toktok, kolem ol emergency namba blong ples yu stap long hem.
