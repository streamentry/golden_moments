Ol app stoa i fulap long ol gem we oli stap talem se oli blong ol olfala, memori, o dementia. Sam oli gud tumas blong mekem. Ol narafala oli gat ol medikol promes, taem we i mekem pleiem i kam wan kwiz, o ads mo kolektem data. Pej ya i givim yu wan rod blong jajem eni app long yu wan. Mifala i no rankem o ridim ol narafala product.

## Wanem mi mas disaedem bifo mi jusum wan app blong dementia?

Disaedem wanem yu wantem app ya i mekem. "Wan gem blong Mama" i save minim ol difren samting, mo gud jus blong wan goal i offen i no gud blong narafala.

| Goal | Wanem i lúk olsem | Wanem blong lukaotem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gentel stimuleisen | Matjing, sotem, ol isi puzel, ol gem blong ol toktok | Difikalti we yu save jenisim, ol piksa we i stap luk, no taem presa |
| Tingting long olfala taem | Ol olfala fotu, ol tokinis we oli save, ol song long taem blong yangfala | Ol fotu blong yu wan, ol kwesten we i opin, no ol kwiz kwestin |
| Kalmcalm | Ol slo luk, ol kwaet saon, win blong blif, nesja | Isi blong stat mo stopem, i no gat eni flasing, i no gat sapraes |
| Fren fasin | Pleiem o toktok wetem, long fes o long video | Dizaen blong tu man, ol prompt blong toktok |

Ol stadi long "cognitive stimulation" blong ol man wetem dementia, oli mostli involvem ol grup seson we ol trening wokman i lidim, wetem diskasen, ol gem blong toktok, puzel, song mo ol wok blong kriesen. Wan [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005562_can-cognitive-stimulation-benefit-people-dementia) i faenem smol benefit long avrej, we i difren long ol stadi. Kaen program ya we i gat fren mo plante samting i no sem olsem wan app we man i pleiem wanem. So tretmen eni app olsem wan shared aktiviti long ol narafala.

## Hao mi save se app i fitim ol abiliti blong man?

Lukaotem hao oli rispondem, i no ting bitim wan daiagnosis o stej label. Tu man we oli "long sem stej" i save difren tumas long ae luk, yer, hand kontrol, toktok mo kamfot long ol skrin. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) i talem se yu mas tingting long ol intres blong bifo mo ol abiliti blong tede, mo fokes long fan, i no long atjiv.

Ol saen blong wan gud fit:

- oli stat o joen i nating yu no mas pusum olgeta;
- oli smael, komentem o talem wan stori;
- ol rong i no seem i bada long olgeta;
- oli hapi blong stopem taem i kam taem blong stopem.

Ol saen blong wan nogud fit:

- oli talem olsem "mi no save" o "disfala i silli" olgro;
- oli no save wanem blong tapem;
- oli kros, oli sem, o oli pusum divaes i go wan saed;
- i wok nomo sapos yu wan i mekem olgeta samting.

Ol hevi filing i wan saen, i no samting blong pusum. Lukim [wanem blong mekem taem oli talem no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Blong ol aedia long stej we i pas ol app, lukim ol [aktiviti long ol stej blong fastaem](/activities/early-stage/) mo [ol stej blong let](/activities/late-stage/).

## Wanem design fitja mi sud lukaot long hem o lusum?

Lukaotem kalm, isi, adalt design, mo lusum eni samting we i putum presa.

| Lukaotem | Stap wari long |
| --- | --- |
| Bigfala piksa mo raetin, hae kontrast | Smol aekon, skrin we i fulap |
| Wan klia akson long wan skrin | Plante batan, menu, o ol stap-stap instraksen |
| No taem, o wan taem we yu save tanem ofu | Kaontdaon, bonus blong spiit, "bitim taem blong yu" |
| No skoa, o gud fidbak we i nating | Pointis, laef, streak, ranking, "brain age" |
| Ol rong i handel isi | Baza, red X mak, "Rong" mesej |
| Adalt mo ol piksa blong riel wol | Kaartun o ol tingting blong pikinini |
| I wok ofleen, no akaont i nidim blong stat | Yu mas saenap, login o daonlodem kontent bifo |
| Isi blong pausim mo stopem | Pop-up we i askem yu blong kontinu pleiem o rette app |
| No ads, klia wan-taem o sabskripsen praes | Ads, loot boks, plante taem blong peim |
| Isi blong wan narafala man i joen | Wan man nomo, dizaen blong kompetisen |

Ads mo ol prompt blong peim i isi blong tapem bifo yu mekem tingting.

## Hao mi jekem privasi blong app?

Jekem wanem app i kolektem, hu i sherem wetem, mo wheta yu save dilitim. Bifo yu instolem:

1. **Ridim stoa privasi samari.** Google Play i gat [Data safety section](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) mo App Store i gat [privacy information section](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102399), we oli lisim ol kaen data we app i save kolektem mo sherem. Tufala kompani oli talem se ol developa oli givim ol infomesen ya long olgeta wan, so i wan stat blong lukaotem, i no wan ful odit.
2. **Askem wheta i nidim akaont.** Sapos wan app i soem nomo ol piksa o ol simpol gem, i gat sam risen blong hem i save nem blong yu, email o bateday.
3. **Jekem ol pamisen.** Eni matjing gem i nidim lokesen, kontaks, maekrofon o ful fotolaebri blong yu? Ofem eni samting we hem i no nidim.
4. **Lukaotem ads mo analitiks.** Ol tul blong advertaesing mo trakin i save sendem infomesen long hao app i yusum long ol narafala kompani.
5. **Faenem wea ol fotu i go.** Sapos yu putem famili fotu, oli stap long divaes o oli apload i go long sava? Yu save rimuvum?
6. **Jekem dilit.** Lukaotem long privasi polisi o data samari hao blong dilitim data mo akaont. Wan app we i talem se data i no save dilitim i mas wan moa wari, espesol sapos hem i holem fotu o helt infomesen.
7. **Ridim privasi polisi** blong klia ansa long wanem i kolektem, wae, hao long, mo hu oli sherem wetem.

### Wanem HIPAA i prodektem infomesen long ol app blong dementia?

Olgeta i no, wan samting we plante man i no ting long hem. Yunaet Stet Dipatmen blong Helt mo Hiuman Sevis i [eksplenem](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) se HIPAA i aplaem long helt plan, moa helt kea providá, helt kea klirinhaos mo ol bisnis pateni blong olgeta. Long plante kes hem i no prodektem data we yu putem long app blong yu wan, sakem sapos wan kavered entiti o bisnis pateni blong hem i providim app ya. Wan "app blong dementia" we yu kasem long app stoa, hem ofen i aotsaed long HIPAA, sapos hem i talem long helt.

### Wanem i prodektem helt data long ol app we HIPAA i no kavremap?

Health Breach Notification Rule blong Federal Trade Commission i kavremap plante long olgeta. Long 2024 FTC i [finalaesem ol jenis](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule) we i klia se rul ya i aplaem long ol helt app mo ol narafala teknolosi we HIPAA i no kavremap. "Breach" i includem ol anotorais disklosa, i no jus haking, mo ol kompani i mas talem ol man we i stap long risk mo FTC. Ya i wan seftinet afta samting i rong, i no wan promes se app i kolektem wanem i nidim nomo.

## Hao mi sud ridim klem long ol risej mo evidens?

Ridim ol evidens klem slo, mo i wan moa skeptikol long ol bigfala promes. Ol kwesten blong askem:

- **Wanem i bin stadi gud?** Wan stadi blong wan tren program i no soem se evri gem long wan app i wokem sem wei.
- **Hu i bin stadi?** Risalt long ol fitfala olfala i save no aplaem long wan man we i gat dementia.
- **Wanem i bin mesurem?** Fo gud long gem ya wetem hem wan difren long jenis long laef blong ded, tokinis, filing o independens.
- **Hao strong i stadi ya?** Wan randomaesd kontrol trael (RCT) i kompea wan grup we i yusum app wetem wan grup we i no yusum. Hem i wan gud dizaen, bat wan smol o soti RCT i no pruf. Lukaotem plante stadi, long independen risej, we oli stap wetem wan narafala.
- **Hem i klem se i jenisim sik ya?** Wan stadi we i soem smol gud jenis long wan tes i no evidens se app i slowem, prevent o tritem dementia.

Pej blong [Cognitive Health and Older Adults](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults) blong Nasonal Institiut long Ejing i talemaot wan bigfala trael blong strakjed kognitiv trening we NIH i fanem, afta i wonem yu blong lukaot long ol klem se sam kompiuta mo onlaen gem i save impruvum memori mo tingting. I talem se i no yet gat enaf evidens se ol app blong tren tingting we i kam long makét i gat sem impak olsem trening we oli testem long trael ya. Yu save ridim moa long gaed blong mifala long [gem blong tingting mo evidens](/brain-health/brain-games/) mo [wanem ol risej i talem long prevensen blong dementia](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).

### Wanem wan pablik eksampol blong wan misliding klem?

Long Janueri 2016, FTC i [anaosem wan setelmen](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) wetem ol mekem blong Lumosity, wan "brain training" program. FTC i talem se kompani i klem, bitim nogat gud evidens, se ol gem blong hem i save slowem disklain blong tingting we i kamap wetem ej, mo protektem agensem mail kognitiv impeirmen, dementia mo Alzheimer's disis. Kompani i agri blong peim $2 million mo i mas gat gud mo relaebol saenstifik evidens bifo i mekem sem kaen klem long fiuja. Kes ya i wan gud rimaenda se langwis blong makét i no evidens.

### Wanem wot i sign blong denja?

Stap wari sapos wan app o ad i talem se i:

- "prevent," "reverse," "stop" o "kiurum" dementia o Alzheimer's;
- "klinikli pruvem" we i no nemem stadi, hu i stap long hem, mo wanem i bin mesurem;
- givim wan "brain age" o memori skoa we i tingting se i gat daiagnosis;
- bae i "sharpen," "boost" o "trenem" brein;
- yusum ol tesemoni olsem pruf.

Sapos yu no sua long wan klem, askem doctor blong man ya.

## Wea Golden Moments i stap?

Golden Moments i ofarem ol simpol sheredgem aktiviti, i no wan memori tes, terapi o medikol advaes. Ol gem blong mifala, olsem [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/), [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/), [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) mo [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/), oli mekem blong fan mo toktok. I no daiagnosem, tritem, prevent o mesurem dementia, mo mifala i no mekem eni klem long memori o tingting.

Mifala i mekem tu wan fri app, [Golden Moments for iPhone, iPad and Android](/app/). Hem i gat gentel piksa matjing wetem bigfala tail, gem wetem famili fotu we yu jusum mo piksa pak we i wok ofleen, i no gat akaont, ads, taem o skoa. Sem jekistlis i stap aplae: App Store privasi leb i talem "Data Not Collected" (i no kolektem data), mo [app privacy policy](/app-privacy/) i eksplenem wea ol kopi blong fotu oli stap.

Mifala i laekem yu blong jajem mifala moa, i no blong trastem mifala nomo. Yusum jeklis long pej ya, ridim [pej blong privasi](/privacy/) mo [medikol disklem](/medical-disclaimer/) blong mifala, mo stopem eni samting, i kamap long mifala tu, we i no sut long man we yu lukaotem.

## Wanem i wan gud wei blong traem wan niufala app?

Traem long gud wan kopol wik, mo lukaot wanem i kamaot.

1. **Setem fastaem** long [gaed blong setap blong tablet mo fon](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/), mo stat long isi levol.
2. **Kipim seson i sot,** lukaot ol smael, toktok mo kalm, mo finisim long wan gud taem.
3. **Lusum sapos i kosem frastreisen,** no matta hem i gat hae rating.
