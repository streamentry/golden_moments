Olgeta we oli toktok moa long wan lanwis maet oli toktok long defren fasin taem dementia i jenis fasin blong faenem ol tok o blong andastanem samting. Sam man o woman oli stat yusum wan lanwis we oli lanem fastaem; sam narawan oli no mekem olsem. Nambawan fas step we i save help hem i blong askem wanem lanwis i isi blong hem naoia, mo afta yu folem wanem hem i wantem.

[Alzheimer's Society i talem se lanwis we man o woman i wantem i save jenis mo i givim tingting blong yusum lanwis we hem i yusum oltaem](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association tu i talem se fasin blong gobak long fes lanwis i save hapen olsem wan jenis long fasin blong toktok](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Ol jenis ya oli no hapen long evriwan mo oli no soemaot wanem step man o woman ya i stap long hem.

## ?Wanem lanwis mi mas yusum?

Askem man o woman ya wanem lanwis hem i wantem yusum long storian ya. Samting we hem i wantem i save jenis folem topik, ples, o taem blong dei. Sipos hem i jenis lanwis, no mekem olsem se yu stap testem hem o fosem hem blong gohed long lanwis we yu nomo yu andastanem.

Sipos yu save lanwis we hem i laekem, yusum ol toktok we hem i save gud mo givim taem blong hem i ansa. Sipos yu save sam tok nomo, talem stret mo yusum tu ol saen blong han, ol samting, o ol foto. [Gaid blong Alzheimer’s Society long saed blong lanwis](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) i talem se save blong lanwis i save jenis long wanwan dei mo taet, soa, o sik i save afektem hem.

## ?Wanem i save help long wan nomol storian?

Holem ol nomol tingting blong toktok gud, mo ademap sapot we i stret long lanwis blong man o woman ya:

- **Yusum ol toktok mo ol nem we oli save gud.** Askem man o woman ya o wan man we hem i trastem wanem tok oli yusum blong ol samting long evridei, ol famle, mo ol rutin.
- **Soemaot mo talem tu.** Poenem wan kap, soemaot tu joes, o yusum wan foto o samting we hem i save gud. Wan saen blong han i save help taem stret tok i had blong faenem.
- **Wet.** Givim taem long man o woman ya blong lisin mo ansa. No finisim evri sentens blong hem o ripitim kwestin long plante lanwis wantaem.
- **Yusum wan tingting long wan taem.** Wan longfala eksplenesen i save had long eni lanwis. Yusum wan sot sentens, spel smol, mo afta luk sipos hem i wantem moa.
- **Jekem sora mo lukluk.** Glas, hearing aid, gud laet, mo wan kwaet ples i save mekem i isi moa blong folem lanwis.

Sipos man o woman ya i laekem blong rid, askem sipos ol impoten infomesen we oli raetem i stap long lanwis blong hem. No dipen long otomatik translesen blong wan disisen long helt, konsen, o instraksen blong meresin; jekem ol impoten infomesen wetem man o woman we i stap givim kea.

## ?Wanem sipos mi no save lanwis we man o woman ya i laekem?

Askem sipos hem i wantem wan fren o famle we hem i trastem mo we i save tu lanwis blong joen long wan nomol storian. No ting se hem i wantem narawan i toktok long bihaf blong hem; maet hem i laekem moa ol saen blong han, ol foto, o wan narafala lanwis we yutufala i save.

Blong wan apoinmen long dokta o wan narafala storian we i nidim stret infomesen, kolem klinik o sevis blong kea bifo. Talemaot wanem lanwis man o woman ya i laekem mo askem wanem help blong lanwis i stap. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services i eksplenem](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) se famle mo fren maet oli no save transletem ol impoten infomesen blong helt long stret fasin; sevis ya i save eksplenem ol opsen mo ol rul long ples blong yu. Man o woman ya i save jusum sipos wan sapota i go wetem hem, mo sevis ya i save mekem plan blong eni help blong lanwis we i nidim.

Long taem blong visit, toktok stret long man o woman ya mo givim taem blong man we i stap transletem i toktok. Jekem se man o woman ya i andastanem kwestin mo i gat janis blong ansa long ol toktok blong hem nomo. Sipos yu stap sapotem hem, ademap moa infomesen nomo taem hem i wantem.

## ?Wetaem mi mas lukaot help blong dokta?

Ol hadtaem long lanwis oli save jenis sloslo ova taem, be wan jenis we i hapen kwiktaem i defren. [NHS i givim advaes blong kasem help blong dokta kwiktaem taem wan man o woman i kam konfius wantaem nomo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). No ting se wan man o woman i lusum fasin blong toktok kwiktaem from dementia nomo. Sipos hem i kam konfius kwiktaem, i sik tumas, o i had blong wekemap hem, kontaktem ol sevis blong helt long ples blong yu kwiktaem.

Blong ol jenis we oli stap kam sloslo, raetemdaon wanem yu luk mo askem dokta blong man o woman ya blong advaes. Raetemdaon wanem lanwis hem i stap yusum, wanem i bin helpem hem blong toktok, mo sipos hem i bin taet o sik. Hemia i wan diskripsen blong wan storian, i no wan daenosis. [Gaid blong mifala long saed blong visit long dokta](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) i save helpem yu blong rere ol kwestin.

## ?Wanem mi save talem long ol narafala we oli stap help?

Wetem agriimen blong man o woman ya, yu save kipim wan sot not blong ol man we hem i wantem oli involv. Not ya i save talem wanem lanwis o daelek hem i laekem blong toktok mo rid, ol toktok o ol nem we hem i save gud, mo wanem i helpem hem blong folem wan storian. Askem sipos hem i wantem wan spesel man i joen long ol apoinmen o ol nius blong kea. No raetem ol praevet infomesen we i no nidim blong sapot, mo talemaot long hem se hu i save luk not ya. Wan not long saed blong lanwis i save helpem ol man blong statem wan storian long wan fasin we i moa isi; be hem i no tekem ples blong wan man blong transletem long wan had storian blong helt.

## ?Olsem wanem mi save sapotem ol joes blong man o woman ya?

Lanwis i wan pat blong fasin we man o woman i konek wetem narawan, be hem i no evri samting long hem. Askem abaot ol rutin blong hem, ol samting we hem i laekem, wanem hem i laekem blong rid, mo ol man we hem i wantem oli involv. Sipos man o woman ya i agri, raetemdaon ol tok o ol fraes we i save helpem ol narafala sapota mo eksplenem olsem wanem blong yusum. Kipim ol not ya i praevet mo serem nomo wanem hem i agri blong serem.

No fosem man o woman ya blong praktis, transletem blong narawan, o pruvum wanem hem i andastanem. Sam storian maet oli isi moa long wan lanwis, mo sam narawan long narafala lanwis. Filing we i isi, praevasi, mo joes blong man o woman ya i moa impoten bitim fasin blong folem wan lanwis nomo.

[Ol tingting blong toktok gud](/caregiving/communication-tips/) blong mifala i tokbaot fasin blong toktok long simpol wei, lisin, mo yusum ol foto. Blong help blong mekem plan afta long daenosis blong dementia, luk [ol fas step afta long wan daenosis blong dementia](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
