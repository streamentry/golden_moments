Wan tablet i save i kamaot i gud fasin blong sherem ol pikja, miusik mo ol simpol gem. Be hemi save mekem man i konfiusum: wan tap we i no minim i opanem narafala aplikesen, wan notifikesen i kamaot, ol leta oli smol tumas, o skrin i blakaot taem yu stap pleiy. Wan liklik setap long yu bae i stretem ol samting ya.

Ol stap ya i yusum ol setting we i stap ready long iPhone, iPad mo Android. Ol nem blong menu i save jenis long ol software versen mo long ol kampani blong divaes, so sipos wan samting i luk narakaen, folem ol link blong Apple o Google blong divaes blong yu. Yu mas mekem setap yusef, bifo yu sidaon wetem hem.

## Sapos wan man we i gat dementia i mas yusum tablet o fon?

Hemi depen long man ya. Plante man oli laekem tablet taem wan i sidaon klosap long olgeta, nao sipos oli no save yusum hem olsem olgeta. Stat blong yusum hem wetem, wetem wan aplikesen nomo i stap oppen, long sam minit. Sipos hemi mekem olgeta i glad mo oli toktok, kipim go. Sipos hemi mekem olgeta i konfiusum o i kros, wan album blong ol pikja, ol pepa we oli pirintum o miusik, bae i moa gud long olgeta. [Ol aktiviti blong pirintum](/activities/printable/) blong mifala i wan narafala jues.

Wan tablet i usually moa isi dan wan fon: skrin mo ol pikja i bigfala moa, mo hemi no wan narafala samting we man ya i depen long hem blong kolem long emergensi.

## Hao mo lokem wan tablet o fon long wan aplikesen?

Yusum Guided Access long iPhone mo iPad, o app pinning long Android. Tufala i kipim skrin long wan aplikesen nomo go kasem taem yu putum wan kod, so wan tap o swaep we i no minim i no inap opanem narafala samting.

### iPad mo iPhone: Guided Access

Long [ol instraksen blong Apple long Guided Access](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111795):

1. Opanem **Settings**, tap **Accessibility**, afta tap **Guided Access** mo turnon hem.
2. Anda long **Passcode Settings**, setem wan Guided Access passcode. Yu save tu allowem Face ID o Touch ID.
3. Sipos yu wantem: anda long **Display Auto-Lock**, jusumem hao kwik skrin i lokem taem wan sesen, blong i no blakaot taem yu stap pleiy.
4. Opanem aplikesen we yu wantem yusum.
5. Triple-click long saet button (iPhone), top button (iPad), o Home button long ol olfala divaes.
6. Sipos yu wantem: sekol ol eria long skrin we yu wantem stopem long tos. Tap **Options** (o **Session Settings**) blong turnoff ol volume button o stopem skrin blong i no turn raon.
7. Tap **Start**.

Blong finisim sesen, triple-click sem button gen, putum passcode, mo tap **End**. Apple i talem se long sam ol olfala software versen, yu mas double-click nomo.

**Impoten:** Apple i talem se Crash Detection mo Emergency Services i no wok long taem wan sesen blong Guided Access i stap. No lusum Guided Access i stap ron long fon we man ya i save nidim blong kolem long emergensi.

### Android: app pinning

Long [help blong Google long pinning skrin](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9455138?hl=en):

1. Opanem **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Security** (o **Security & location**), afta **Advanced**, afta **App pinning**. Sipos yu no faenem hem, lukluk long ol help pej blong kampani blong divaes.
3. Turnon **App pinning**. Yu bae nidim PIN, pattern o password blong unpin.
4. Opanem aplikesen we yu wantem. Swaep i go antap i go long middle blong skrin mo holem, blong opanem Overview (o tap Overview sipos yu yusum 3-button navigation).
5. Long top blong preview blong aplikesen, tap aikon blong aplikesen, afta tap **Pin**.

Blong unpin wetem gesture navigation, swaep i go antap mo holem. Wetem 3-button navigation, touch mo holem Back mo Overview. Wetem 2-button navigation, touch mo holem Back mo Home. Afta putum PIN, pattern o password blong yu.

## I gat wan simpol mod blong ful iPhone o iPad?

Yes. Apple Assistive Access i jenisim usual hom skrin blong wan simpol hom skrin. [Apple i deskraebem hem](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-iphone/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ios) olsem wan samting we i disaenem blong mekem iPhone i isi moa blong ol man we oli gat ol difficulty long tingting, blong olgeta i save yusum hem olsem olgeta, wetem ol bigfala samting long skrin mo ol fica we i fokus moa. Apple i givim [sem fica long iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-ipad/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ipados).

- **Hu i setem.** Apple i talem se wan man we i trustem, olsem famle memba o man we i kea long hem, we i savegud long man ya, i mas helpem jusumem ol aplikesen mo ol fica.
- **Hao blong stat.** Go long **Settings > Accessibility > Assistive Access**, tap **Set Up Assistive Access**, afta tap **Continue**.
- **Aplikesen.** Yu jusum ol aplikesen we bae oli kamaot. Sam Apple aplikesen i gat ol simpol vesen, mo ol narafala aplikesen i save ademap long ol usual dizain blong olgeta.
- **Layout.** Jusum **Rows** (wan list we i isi blong ridim) o **Grid** (ol bigfala samting we i sekem ol pikja mo aikon).
- **Passcode.** Yu setem wan separeted Assistive Access passcode blong entahem, eksitim o jenisim setting. Apple i rekomendem blong setem wan recovery Apple Account long taem blong setap, bikos sipos i no gat hem, wan passcode we yu fogetem i minim se yu mas resetem divaes.

Assistive Access i save fitim wan man we i yusum divaes blong hem evri dei. Guided Access o app pinning i kuik moa blong wan sesen blong pleiy wetem.

## Hao mo mekem leta mo ol pikja i bigfala?

Mekem leta mo skrin i bigfala moa, afta lukluk se ol button i stil fitim long skrin.

**iPhone mo iPad.** Folem [ol instraksen blong Apple long fonsize](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102453), go long **Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size** mo drag long slaed. Blong leta i bigfala moa, go long **Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text**. Apple i talem se sipos leta i bigfala tumas, sam button i moa hadwok blong tap, so jusumem sipos i nid.

**Android.** Folem [help blong Google long setting blong leta mo skrin](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/11183305?hl=en), opanem **Settings** mo serjim **Font size**, afta muvumem slaed. Serjim **Display size** blong mekem evri samting long skrin i bigfala. Yu save tu turnon **Bold text** anda long **Accessibility > Display size and text**. Sam aplikesen oli no folem ol setting ya.

Long ol gem olsem [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) mo [Find the Same](/games/find-the-same/), yu save tu jusumem less tael o less jusum, we i mekem ol samting i simpol moa long skrin.

## Hao mo stopem ol notifikesen mo ol pop-up?

Turnoff ol notifikesen blong ol aplikesen we yu no nidim, mo yusum Do Not Disturb taem ol aktiviti i stap.

**iPhone mo iPad.** Go long **Settings > Notifications**, tap wan aplikesen anda long **Notification Style**, mo turnoff **Allow Notifications** ([gaed blong Apple long notifikesen setting](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios)). Blong silentem ol kol, alet mo notifikesen long wan taem, opanem Control Center mo turnon **Do Not Disturb** long **Focus** button ([help blong Apple long Do Not Disturb](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105112)). Yu save setem taem long **Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb > Set a Schedule**.

**Android.** Swaep i go daon from top blong skrin mo tap **Do Not Disturb**, o go long **Settings > Modes > Do Not Disturb** ([help blong Google long Do Not Disturb](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9069335?hl=en)). Anda long **Notification filters** yu save jusumem ol pipol mo ol aplikesen we oli save kasem yu. Setting oli defren long kampani blong divaes.

## Hao mo mekem wan simpol hom skrin?

Kipim sam aplikesen we oli save long wan skrin nomo, mo putum ol narafala we oli no luk long ae. I no gat wan setting nomo blong hemia; hemi wan samting blong rimvum ol samting we i wantem spes.

- **Jusumem tri go sikis aplikesen.** Olsem, ol pikja, miusik, wan video kol aplikesen mo wan gem.
- **Putum olgeta long fes skrin,** long sem ples evri taem, mo muvumem evrisamting narafala i go long seken skrin o wan folda.
- **Yusum wan simpol wallpaper we i gat strong kala,** olsem wan kala nomo o wan famle pikja, blong ol aikon i stanap aot.
- **Dilitim ol aplikesen we yu no nidim.** Less aplikesen i minim less jans blong konfiusion mo less data we i kolektem.
- **Tingting long Assistive Access** long ol Apple divaes sipos yu wantem divaes i mekem hemia blong yu.

## Hao long volum mo saon?

Setem volum we i isi blong man ya bifo yu givim divaes long hem. Wetem Guided Access, yu save turnoff ol volume button blong i no jenis taem wan aktiviti i stap. Lukluk se ol hearing aid i stap long ia mo i wok.

## Wea nao tablet i mas stap?

Givim hem wan stand mo wan ples blong jagem we i no bae i jenis.

- **Wan stand o case** we i holem tablet i stanap long tebol, so nobody i nid holem hem, mo pointim hem i isi moa.
- **Wan ples blong jagem** we i no bae i muv, klosap long wan charging stand long tebol long wan rum we yu yusum evri dei. Tablet i alwe fulap mo i stap long sem ples evri taem.
- **Redyusum glea.** Putum skrin i no kasem bright windo lait.

## Hao mo taem long skrin i stret?

I no gat wan stret namba blong ol minit. Traetem blong ol sot we i sot mo oli sherem, olsem wan pat blong wan fulap dei we i gat movmen, miusik, wok long haos, toktok mo taem long aotsaed. Bikos taem we i bisi long aftenun i save mekem man i no kwaet long leet dei, konsiderem blong kipim ol aktiviti long skrin long fes dei. Lukim [ol kwaet aktiviti long leet aftenun](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Yusum tablet wetem, i no olsem wan wari blong lukaftarem man, i mekem hemia i gat sosol fasin. Gaed blong mifala long [pleiy gem wetem](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) i eksplenem hao.

## Hao mo protectem prvasi blong hem long divaes?

Limitim wanem ol aplikesen i save kolektem, mo no tingting se loa blong prvasi long helt i kavremap hem. Department of Health and Human Services blong Amerika (HHS) [i eksplenem](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) se ol HIPAA Rules, long ol nomol kes, i no protectem helt informesen we i stap long o we oli yusum long ol personal fon mo tablet. Long plante kes, HIPAA i no protectem data we oli putum long ol aplikesen blong personal yus, sipos aplikesen ya i no kamaot long wan kavred helt kea provaeda, helt plan o wan long ol bisnis partner blong olgeta.

HHS i sajestem:

- vos blong yusum ol aplikesen we oli no nid, espesol ol "free" wan;
- no givim ol aplikesen akses long ples we oli stap, sipos oli no real nid, olsem ol map.

Tu, tingting long ol samting ya:

- **Yusum passcode** long divaes, mo kipim wan nout blong hem long wan sef ples.
- **Lukluk ol permission** blong foto, kamera, maekrofon, kontak mo ples, mo turnoff ol we wan aplikesen i no nid.
- **Lukim wanem wan aplikesen i kolektem** bifo yu install. Gaed blong mifala long [jusumem ol dementia aplikesen mo gem](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) i gat wan lis blong prvasi.
- **Kea gud long ol famle pikja.** Sherem nomo ol pikja we man ya bae i gat kamfot long hem, mo save ples we aplikesen i sevem olgeta. Long [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), ol pikja we yu jusum i stap long browser blong yu.
- **Turnoff ol in-app purchase** o rimvum ol pement detail long ol akaont we man ya i yusum.
