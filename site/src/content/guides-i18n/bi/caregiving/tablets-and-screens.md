Wan tablet i save i kam wan gud fasin blong sherem ol piksa, miusik mo ol simpol gem. Be i save tu i mekem konfiusen: wan tap we i nating i opem narafala app, wan notifikesen i kam antap, ol leta i smol tumas, o skrin i blak long midol blong wan gem. Wan smol setemap from yu i stopem bigwan long ol samting ya.

Ol stap ya i yusum ol setting we i stap finis long iPhone, iPad mo Android. Ol nem blong menu i save jenis long ol vesen blong software mo long ol mekea blong divaes, so sipos wan samting i luk narafala, folom Apple o Google pej we i link long divaes blong yu. Yu mas mekem setemap ya yu wan, befo yu sidaon wetem hem.

## Eniwe wan man we i gat dimensia i save yusum wan tablet o fon?

I depend long man ya. Plante man i laekem yusum wan tablet taem wan man i stap klosap long hem, even se hem i no save yusum hem wan nomo. Stat long yusum long gether, wetem wan app nomo i open, long sam minit. Sipos i givim hapi mo toktok, kipim i go. Sipos i mekem konfiusen o krosman, wan album blong ol piksa, ol pej we oli printim o miusik i save moa gud long hem. Ol [aktiviti we yu save printim](/activities/printable/) i wan narafala jeis.

Wan tablet i isi moa dan wan fon: skrin mo ol piksa i bigwan, mo i no mekem man ya i gat fon blong hem blong kolem long emergency.

## Hao nao mi lokem wan tablet o fon long wan app?

Yusum Guided Access long iPhone mo iPad, o app pinning long Android. Tufala i kipim skrin long wan app kasem yu putumap wan kod, so wan tap o wan swaep we i nating i save opem narafala samting.

### iPad mo iPhone: Guided Access

Long [Apple blong Guided Access instraksen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111795):

1. Opem **Settings**, tapem **Accessibility**, mo tapem **Guided Access** mo onem.
2. Long **Passcode Settings**, setemap wan Guided Access passcode. Yu save tu alaod Face ID o Touch ID.
3. Sipos yu wantem: long **Display Auto-Lock**, jusem hao kwik skrin i lok long taem blong sesen, blong i no blak long midol blong gem.
4. Opem app we yu wantem yusum.
5. Triple-click side button (iPhone), top button (iPad), o Home button long ol olfala divaes.
6. Sipos yu wantem: sekelem ol hap blong skrin we yu wantem i no respond long tos. Tapem **Options** (o **Session Settings**) blong ofem ol volum button o stopem skrin blong i no turnem.
7. Tapem **Start**.

Blong finisim sesen, triple-click sem button gen, putumap passcode, mo tapem **End**. Apple i talem se long sam olfala software, yu mas double-click insted.

**Important:** Apple i talem se Crash Detection mo Emergency Services i no wok long taem blong sesen Guided Access. No lusum Guided Access i ron long wan fon we man ya i save nidim blong kolem long emergency.

### Android: app pinning

Long [Google Android help blong pinning skrin](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9455138?hl=en):

1. Opem **Settings** app.
2. Tapem **Security** (o **Security & location**), then **Advanced**, then **App pinning**. Sipos yu no faenem, lukem help pej blong mekea blong divaes.
3. Onem **App pinning**. Yu bambae nidim PIN, pattern o password blong unpin.
4. Opem app we yu wantem. Swaepem i go antap long midol blong skrin mo holem blong opem Overview (o tapem Overview long 3-button navigation).
5. Long top blong preview blong app, tapem aekon blong app, then tapem **Pin**.

Blong unpin long gesture navigation, swaepem i go antap mo holem. Long 3-button navigation, holem Back mo Overview. Long 2-button navigation, holem Back mo Home. Then putumap PIN, pattern o password.

## Eniwe i gat wan moa simpol mod blong whole iPhone o iPad?

Yes. Apple blong Assistive Access i jenisem usual home screen wetem wan moa simpol wan. [Apple i talem](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-iphone/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ios) se i dizaenem blong mekem iPhone i isi moa blong ol man we i gat hadtaem long tingting blong yusum long olgeta, wetem ol samting we i bigwan long skrin mo ol fisa we i stap long wan samting nomo. Apple i givim [sem fisa ya long iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-ipad/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ipados).

- **Hu i setemap.** Apple i sujestem se wan trastem sapota, olsem wan famle memba o wan man we i kea long hem, i save helpem blong jusem ol app mo fisa.
- **Stat wea.** Go long **Settings > Accessibility > Assistive Access**, tapem **Set Up Assistive Access**, then **Continue**.
- **Ol app.** Yu jusem wanem app i soem. Sam Apple app i gat ol simpol vesen spesel, mo moa app we i narafala i save go insaed long ol usual dizaen blong olgeta.
- **LEyaut.** Jusem **Rows** (wan lis we i isi blong ridim) o **Grid** (ol bigwan samting we i sharem ol piksa mo ol aekon).
- **Passcode.** Yu setemap wan sipresen Assistive Access passcode blong entarem, aotem o jenisem ol setting. Apple i sujestem blong setemap wan recovery Apple Account long taem blong setup, from sipos yu fogetem passcode, yu bambae nidim resetem divaes.

Assistive Access i save fitim wan man we i yusum divaes blong hem evri dei. Guided Access o app pinning i kuik moa long wan sesen blong gem we oli sherem.

## Hao nao mi mekem leta mo ol piksa i bigwan?

Mekem text mo display saes i bigwan, then jekem se ol bataen i stil fitim long skrin.

**iPhone mo iPad.** Folom [Apple blong font size instraksen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102453): go long **Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size** mo draggem slida. Blong moa bigwan leta, go long **Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text**. Apple i talem se bigwan tumas leta i save mekem sam bataen i harem blong tapem, so jastem sipos i nidim.

**Android.** Folom [Google help blong text mo display setting](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/11183305?hl=en): opem **Settings** mo serjem **Font size**, then muvem slida. Serjem **Display size** blong mekem evri samting long skrin i bigwan. Yu save tu onem **Bold text** long **Accessibility > Display size and text**. Sam app i no folom ol setting ya.

Long ol gem olsem [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) mo [Find the Same](/games/find-the-same/), yu save tu jusem moa smol tiles o ol jois, we i mekem samting i isisi long skrin.

## Hao nao mi stopem ol notifikesen mo pop-up?

Ofem notifikesen blong ol app we yu no nidim, mo yusum Do Not Disturb taem blong ol aktiviti.

**iPhone mo iPad.** Go long **Settings > Notifications**, tapem wan app anda long **Notification Style**, mo ofem **Allow Notifications** ([Apple gaed blong notifikesen setting](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios)). Blong silensem ol kol, alet mo notifikesen long sam taem, opem Control Center mo onem **Do Not Disturb** from **Focus** button ([Apple help blong Do Not Disturb](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105112)). Yu save setemap long **Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb > Set a Schedule**.

**Android.** Swaepem i go daon from top blong skrin mo tapem **Do Not Disturb**, o go long **Settings > Modes > Do Not Disturb** ([Google help blong Do Not Disturb](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9069335?hl=en)). Long **Notification filters** yu save jusem ol man mo ol app we save stap i kam. Setting i jenis long ol mekea blong divaes.

## Hao nao mi mekem wan simpol haoskrin?

Kipim sam app we oli save long wan skrin nomo, mo muvem ol narafala i go long ples we i no lukluk. I no gat wan setting blong ea; i wok long rimuvum ol samting we i mekem krosman.

- **Jusem tri i go sikis app.** Olsem: ol piksa, miusik, wan app blong video kol mo wan gem.
- **Putumap long fastfes skrin,** long sem ples evri taem, mo muvem ol narafala i go long wan seken skrin o wan folder.
- **Yusum wan plen, strong kala wallpaper,** olsem wan kala nomo o wan piksa we man ya i save, blong ol aekon i stanap aot.
- **Mekem wan app we yu no nidim i go.** Moa app i minim moa cans blong konfiusen mo less data we i kolektem.
- **Traem Assistive Access** long ol Apple divaes sipos yu wantem divaes i mekem dat blong yu.

## Wanem long volum mo saond?

Setemap wan volum we i kamfotebol befo yu givim divaes. Wetem Guided Access, yu save ofem ol volum button blong i no jenis long midol blong aktiviti. Jekem se ol hearing aid i stap insaed mo i wok gud.

## Ples wea tablet i stap?

Givim hem wan stand mo wan ples blong jas we i stap oltaem.

- **Wan stand o kes** we i holem tablet i stanap long tebol, blong nating man i nidim holem hem, mo blong pointem i isi.
- **Wan ples blong jas** we i no save jenis, isi nomo long wan stand blong jas long tebol long wan rum we yu yusum evri dei. Tablet i stap jas oltaem mo i stap long sem ples oltaem.
- **Redyusum glea.** Putumap skrin i no klosap long strong windo.

## Hao nao skrin taem i oraet?

I no gat wan namba minit we i stret. Yu mas traem blong mekem ol sesen we oli sot mo oli wetem narafala samting long wan fulfala dei, we i inkludim wakabaot, miusik, wok long haos, toktok mo taem long aotsaed. From ol bisi aftenun mo nait save mekem restlesnes i kam moa long ol leta eve, yu save kipim ol skrin aktiviti long ol fastfala taem long dei. Lukem [kolem aktiviti long leta aftenun](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Yusum tablet wetem narafala, i no olsem wan babysitta, i mekem i gat sosaet. Gaed blong yu long [pleim ol gem togeta](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) i talem hao.

## Hao nao mi protectem privesi blong hem long divaes?

Limitim wanem ol app i save kolektem, mo no tingting se lo blong helt privesi i kavremap ol samting ya. Ministri blong Helt mo Ol Sevis blong Yunaited Stet (HHS) [i eksplenem](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) se HIPAA Rules bigwan taem i no protectem helt informeson we i stap long o i yusum long personal fon mo tablet. Long ol kes, HIPAA i no protectem tu data we oli putumap long app blong yusum long personal samting, sipos app ya i no kamaot from wan helt provaeda we i kavrem, wan helt plan, o wan long ol bisnis pateni blong olgeta.

HHS i sujestem:

- blong no yusum ol app we i no nidim, speseli ol "free" app;
- blong no givim app ya akses long lokesen sipos i no trufala nidim, olsem ol map.

Tu, lukem ol samting ya:

- **Yusum passcode** long divaes, mo kipim wan nout blong passcode long wan sef ples.
- **Lukem ol permission** blong ol piksa, kamera, maekrofon, kontak mo lokesen, mo ofem enikam we app i no nidim.
- **Jekem wanem app i kolektem** befo yu install. Gaed blong yu long [jusum ol app mo gem blong dimensia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) i gat wan jeklois blong privesi.
- **Kea long famle piksa.** Sherem nomo ol piksa we man ya i stap fitim wetem, mo save wea app i stoem olgeta. Long [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), ol piksa we yu jusem i stap long bra-sa blong yu.
- **Ofem ol in-app pesmen** o rimuvum ol pemen detel long ol akaon we man ya i yusum.
