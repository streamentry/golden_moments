Taem yu tingbaot kea long haos, maet yu harem se yu glad lelebet, yu sore, yu no sua, o yu harem sam filing wantaem. Desisen ya i blong faenem sapot we i stret long ol nid mo ol samting we man ya i laekem. Hem i no wan tes blong soemaot hamas famle i kea, mo hem i no wan step we evriwan i mas kasem long sem taem.

[Alzheimer's Association guide to long-term care](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-term-care), [residential-care checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) blong hem, mo [Dementia Australia's residential-care guidance](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/residential-care) oli talem se i gud blong lukluk long ol nid blong man ya, staf, ol aktiviti, ples mo praes. Pej ya i no rekomendem wan haos, mo i no tekem ples blong advaes blong man blong wok long saed blong helt o mane long eria blong yu.

## ?Olsem wanem mi save luk se i gud blong tingbaot kea long haos?

Lukluk long wanem kaen sapot we man ya i nidim naoia mo sipos sapot ya i save gohed long haos. Tingbaot sefti blong man ya, ol nid blong kea evridei, laef wetem ol narafala man, mo ol samting we hem i laekem. Tingbaot tu sipos wok blong lukaotem hem i stap afektem helt blong man ya o helt blong ol man we oli stap sapotem hem.

I no gat wan stage o wan lis nomo we i save talem wetaem wan man i mas muv. Wan man maet i wantem stap long wan komyuniti ples, o maet hem i wantem stap long haos wetem moa help. Toktok wetem man ya mo, sipos i help, wetem dokta blong hem, sosol woka o man we i stap koordinetem kea. Askem wanem kaen sapot we maet i stret bifo yu mekem desisen blong muv.

Mekem man ya i joen long ol desisen long wei we hem i wantem. Askem wanem samting i impoten long hem: ples, kakae, lanwis, kalja, relijin, ol aktiviti we hem i save finis, o visit blong famle mo ol fren. Ol samting we hem i laekem oli save helpem yu blong jusum ol haos, nating se sam nid blong kea oli stap jenis.

## ?Wanem mi mas faenemaot bifo mi visitim ol haos?

Fastaem, mekem wan sot lis blong sapot we man ya i nidim. Hemia maet i minim help wetem ol wok evridei, kea blong nes, taem wetem ol narafala man, wan lanwis we hem i save finis, o wan ples blong spenem taem aotsaed. Askem wan man blong wok long saed blong helt o sosol kea sipos yu no sua long wanem ol sevis we maet oli nidim.

Ringim ol haos bifo yu visit. Askem sipos oli save sapotem ol nid blong man ya, sipos maet i gat wan rum i stap, wanem nao ol nekis step, mo wanem infomesen we oli nidim long yu. Askemb long infomesen long raeting long saed blong ol sevis mo fi. Ol rul blong hu i save kam insaed, fasin blong pem, mo laesens oli defren long evri ples, so jekem ol lokol infomesen i moagud bitim yu dipen nomo long wan atikol.

Askem sipos staf oli gat trening blong kea blong dementia, olsem wanem oli mekem ol plan blong kea, mo olsem wanem famle i save serem nius o infomesen. Faenemaot olsem wanem ol rutin evridei oli wok, wanem ol aktiviti oli gat, mo olsem wanem oli sapotem ol resident blong mekem ol joes. Wan memory care unit hem i wan opsen, be i no evri man we i nidim; skelem stret sapot we evri ples i givim.

## ?Wanem mi mas lukluk long hem taem mi visit?

Sipos i posibol, visitim moa bitim wan haos. Mekem wan taem blong fas visit, afta askem sipos yu save kam bakegen long wan defren taem o long taem blong kakae. Wan seken visit i save soemaot moa long rutin blong evridei mo i save givim janis blong yu mitim ol narafala staf.

Lukluk long fasin we staf oli toktok wetem ol resident. ?Oli toktok wetem respek, oli givim taem blong man i ansa, mo oli mekem man i joen long ol smol joes evridei? ?Ol resident oli luk olsem se oli komfotabol mo oli gat samting blong mekem we i stret long ol samting we oli laekem mo wanem we oli save mekem? Askem sipos yu save luk ol taemtebol blong aktiviti, ol ples blong kakae, ol ples aotsaed mo ol praevet ples.

Lukaotem ol smol samting we oli impoten long man ya: ol saen we oli isi blong andastanem, rod we i klia blong go long toelet, ol samting we hem i save finis, ol kwaet ples, sapot long lanwis mo kalja, mo janis blong luk ol man we hem i lavem. Wan lobby we i luk naes nomo i no soemaot olsem wanem kea evridei i stap wok.

Karem wan fren o famle we yu trastem sipos hemia i save helpem yu blong tingbaot ol kwestin mo skelem tingting blong yu afta. Yu save yusum [Alzheimer's Association checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) blong raetemdaon wanem we yu luk. Yusum ol not blong yu olsem samting blong helpem tokbaot, i no olsem wan sko we i jusum haos blong yu.

## ?Wanem ol kwestin we oli help blong skelem kea?

Askem olsem wanem fasin blong wok blong staf i jenis long ol defren taem blong dei, olsem wanem staf oli lanem ol rutin blong man ya, mo wanem i hapen taem ol nid blong hem oli jenis. Askem hu i stap jekem ol plan blong kea mo olsem wanem famle i save talem wan wari. Sipos man ya i nidim help wetem kakae, kea blong bodi, o fasin blong toktok, askem olsem wanem sapot ya i luk long evridei laef.

Askem long saed blong visit blong famle, ol taem blong go aotsaed, ol apoinmen blong helt, ol aktiviti, mo sipos man ya i save karem ol samting we hem i save finis i kam wetem hem. Sipos wan haos i gat wan program we i spesel blong dementia, askem wanem trening, ol aktiviti mo sapot evridei i stap insaed. Askem blong oli givim sam eksampol long fasin we ol resident oli joen long ol joes, i no blong yu dipen nomo long nem blong program.

Askemb long wan klia lis blong ol fi, wanem i stap insaed, mo olsem wanem praes maet i jenis. Ridim ol agrimen gud mo kasem advaes long ples blong yu sipos ol toktok long saed blong mane o loa i no klia. Yu no mas ting se wan sevis i kavremap o i stap jes from infomesen we i kam long narafala state o kantri.

Sipos aedentiti, ol rilesensip o praevasi blong man ya i nidim moa tingting, lukluk long [gaed blong yumi blong sapotem wan man we i LGBTQ+ mo i gat dementia](/caregiving/supporting-lgbtq-person-with-dementia/). Hem i gat ol kwestin long saed blong nem we man ya i wantem, ol visita, kea blong bodi mo ol polisi blong ol man we oli givim kea.

## ?Olsem wanem mi save help wetem muv ya?

Mekem man ya i joen long ol joes long wei we hem i wantem. Wan visit long haos ya, wan kakae long ples ya, o wan sot taem blong spel long ples ya maet i helpem sam man blong save gudfala long ples ya, sipos hemia i stap mo sipos hem i stret long hem. Man ya maet i wantem jusum wan samting blong rum, klos o wan rutin blong karem i go wetem hem.

Serem ol yusful infomesen wetem staf, wetem agriimen blong man ya. Hemia maet i minim nem we hem i laekem, fasin blong toktok blong hem, ol rutin evridei, ol kakae we hem i laekem, ol aktiviti we hem i enjoyem, mo wanem i helpem hem blong harem se hem i setel. Kipim infomesen ya i sot mo i stap long saed blong kea. Man ya i mas stap pat blong ol tokbaot we oli mekem long saed blong hem.

Karem ol samting we hem i save finis sipos haos i letem. Askem staf olsem wanem rum blong man ya i save harem olsem wan ples we hem i save, be we i no mekem i had blong muv raon. Wan foto, wan favret blanket, o wan klok we hem i save finis maet i impoten moa bitim traem blong mekem ful haos i kam semmak.

## ?Olsem wanem mi save gohed blong joen afta long muv ya?

Wok wetem staf blong lanem olsem wanem oli serem nius mo hu blong kontaktem. Askem man ya olsem wanem hem i harem long ol visit mo ol aktiviti. Sam man maet oli wantem plante sot visit; sam narafala maet oli wantem taem blong setel. Lukluk wanem i luk olsem se i komfotabol long hem mo jenis tugeta.

Ol nid blong kea mo ol samting we man ya i laekem oli save jenis. Jekem plan blong kea bakegen wetem man ya mo staf, askem ol kwestin taem wan samting i no klia, mo serem niu infomesen we maet i help. Sipos man ya i kam konfius wantaem o i kam sik moa tumas wantaem, talem kwiktaem long tim blong kea; folem [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/), konfiusen we i kam wantaem i nidim help blong helt kwiktaem.

Blong jusum wan haos hem i wan bigfala desisen, mo fas joes maet i no stap olsem beswan joes oltaem. Karem infomesen, mekem man ya i joen, askem long saed blong kea evridei, mo kipim toktok i gohed afta we hem i muv.
