Ekamon ituroro n ana bai ekoroborob e dok me e kanimwin, te ew, te itabwi o e betu ngkekei. Te ekamon bon ekamon n eko kakariri ae e riai ma aia kanikokan ma aia tain te ema ae ko kakariri ion. E bwija ae e ataein eadae ae ko kakariri ion o ae e riai te korio n aian mweimwei n itutu oki.

Te [itin roan n Alzheimer Association n kakariri ae e ejaki](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-term-care), [etan ekamakin ituroro n ana bai ekoroborob](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) ma [itan roan n Dementia Australia n ituroro n ana bai ekoroborob](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/residential-care) a tatanin ae e riai ekamakin aia kanikokan te ema, aia aiyuw, kakariri, taian bai ao mwane. Te bukin ae e moanga e bwija n itatanin ituroro n ana bai ekoroborob o e na itei ekamakin n roan o ma mwane n renan.

## Eadae ae e riai au ataein ae e na irei ekamon ituroro n ana bai ekoroborob?

Ekamakin eadae kakariri ae e riai te ema ngkekei ao in rae e dok e na itangaki ngkekei n ana bai ekoroborob. Ekaeiyow roan te ema, aia kanikokan n ibon, aia korio ma aia tain. Ekaeiyow naba in rae te ekamakin n kakariri e ro n itei roan te ema o roan aia ae a kakariri ion.

E bwija etan itutu o etan ekamakin ae e na ataein eae a riai te ema n ekamon. Te ema e dok e tain te itutu n oki, o e dok e tain n anga ngkekei n ana bai ma kakariri ae e itei. Ekawangaga ma te ema ao, in rae e riai, ma te dotor, te eiyuw n roan o te ema ae e tatanin kakariri. Eberuwa eadae kakariri ae e dok e riai imwin ekamakin ngkekei.

Iow te ema n ekamon ma e tain in rae e eow. Eberuwa eadae ae e imwiin ion: te tabwen, engain, edorer, eiyuw, itutu ae e moanga ion, o erian korio ma roan. Aia tain e dok iow n ataein te bwi e na ekamon, in rae aia kanikokan a itei.

## Eadae ae e riai au ataein imwin ekamon n eteran ituroro?

Ekarorin ma etan bwi n kakariri ae e riai te ema. E dok imwiin kakariri n ibon, kakariri n dotor, kakariri ma roan, edorer ae e moanga ion o tabwen ae e na anga n ian dorer. In rae ko itabwi, eberuwa ma te ema o te eiyuw n roan eadae roan ae e dok e riai.

Eberuwa ma ituroro oki imwin ekamon n eteran. Eberuwa in rae a dok a ro n iow aia kanikokan te ema, in rae e eok bwean n ituroro, eadae ae e na rin, ao eadae roan ae a riai ngkoe. Eberuwa n etan rinan ae e koreiyaki n roan ma mwane. Aia ituroro, mwane, ma etan roan n roan a itei ian pin, ian rae ko riai n ekamakin roan n renan ae e riai ngkoe, e bwija n ewa ngkekei.

Eberuwa in rae aia aiyuw a mwaun ekamakin bwebwenati n dementia, eadae ae a tatanin aia ekamon n kakariri, ao eadae ae a ekawangaga ma korio. Ekaeiyow eadae ae e ririen kakariri n aian bong, eadae kakariri ae e itangaki, ao eadae ae a iow te korio n ekamon. Te bai ekoroborob ae e aten mo dementia bon etan bwi, ma e bwija ngkekei ro ae a riai ion; ekamon eadae kakariri ae e itangaki n ituroro oki.

## Eadae ae e riai au kaeiyow n te eteran?

Eteran n ituroro ae e eow in rae e dok. Ekarorin eteran imwin ekamakin, imwin aio eberuwa in rae ko dok ko eteran n itutu oki o n te engain. Te eteran n karua e dok ataein aio ae e ririen n aian bong ma e dok iow ko n eter aia aiyuw oki ae a riai.

Ekaeiyow eadae aia aiyuw a kekei ma te korio. A a kekei ma itototo, a angan aoa n ekararau ao a iow ro n ekamon n kakariri ae e itutu? A eok te korio ao a kanimwin kakariri ae e riai ma aia taran ao aia ro? Eberuwa in rae ko dok n eter etan kakariri, taian engain, tabwen ian dorer ao taian bai ae e ribu.

Ekaeiyow taian roan ae e imwiin te ema ae: etan ataein ae e eok, iran n nako n bwabwane ae e eok, taian bai ae e moanga ion, taian tabwen ae e eim, ekamakin edorer ao eiyuw, ao irian n eteran korio ma aia ae a imwiin ion. Te roan ae e eok n lobby bon e bwija n ataein eadae ae e ririen n aian bong.

Emon te rimoa o te korio ae e imwiin ion ma ngkoe in rae e na iow ngkoe n mimi ae eberuwa ao n ekamakin aia ekamon imwin aio. Ko dok e use [etan ekamakin n Alzheimer Association](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) n koreyaki eadae ae ko kaeiyow. Ewa n rae ko mimi aio bon ekamakin n ewangaga, e bwija bon etan ekamakin ae e na ataein te bai ekoroborob ngkoe.

## Eadae beruwa ae e na iow n ekamakin ituroro?

Eberuwa eadae ae a ririen aia aiyuw n pin ae pin, eadae ae a ataein aia itutu te ema ao eadae ae e na rin in rae aia kanikokan a itei. Eberuwa ai e na tatanin aia ekamon n kakariri ao eadae ae e na ekawangaga ma korio. In rae te ema e riai kakariri n engain, kakariri n ibon o ekawangaga, eberuwa eadae ae e na ririen aio n renan.

Eberuwa ma erian korio, nako ian dorer, nako ma dotor, kakariri oki ao roan te ema n emon bai ae e moanga ion. In rae te bai ekoroborob e anana te itutu ae e aten mo n dementia, eberuwa eadae ekamakin bwebwenati, kakariri ao kakariri ae e itangaki n aian bong. Eberuwa n taran eadae ae a iow te korio n ekamon, e bwija n ewa ma aran te itutu ngkekei.

Eberuwa n etan mwane ae e koreiyaki, eadae ae e imwiin ion ao eadae ae e na itei mwane n imwin. Ewa n rae ko rinan ae e koreiyaki ao eberuwa ma te ema ae e moan mwaane n roan o inan anan n renan in rae roan a itabwi. E bwija n tataro ae e anana te roan e itangaki o e mwaun ngkekei ian etan itebwina o n te aba oki.

In rae roan te ema, aia iten, o roan ae e ribu a riai ekamakin ae e eow, nener te [itin roan n iow te ema ae LGBTQ+ ae e mwaun dementia](/caregiving/supporting-lgbtq-person-with-dementia/). Te bukin ae e moanga aio e anana beruwa n aran ae a tain, korio ae a tain, kakariri n ibon ao etan roan n aia ae a itangaki.

## Eadae ae e riai au iow te ema n te ekamon?

Iow te ema n ekamon ma e tain in rae e eow. Te eteran n ana bai ekoroborob, te engain iai o te eria ekamon ae e ijaki e dok iow te ema n ataein te tabwen aio, in rae e mwaun ao e kanimwin ion. Te ema e dok e tain n ataein etan bai ae e na emon n ana ru, taraan o itutu ae e na emon ma ion.

Iow roan ae e riai ma aia aiyuw, ma kanimwin te ema. Aio e dok imwiin aran ae e tain te ema, eadae ae e kekei ma ion, aia itutu n aian bong, engain ae e tain, kakariri ae e kanimwin ion ao eadae ae e na iow ion n roan ae e eim. Ewa n rae ko kakawaki te roan aio ao n anain aio ma te kakariri. Te ema e riai n anga n ekawangaga n roan ae a kekei ia ion.

Emon bai ae e moanga ion in rae te bai ekoroborob e kanimwin aio. Eberuwa ma aia aiyuw eadae ae e na eim ana ru te ema e bwija n eaboi te nako nako. Te taeta, te bwebwe ae e moanga ion o te tainin aika e moanga ion e dok e imwiin aio ae e tain te rekereke n ana bai ngkekei.

## Eadae ae e riai au anga n eria imwin te ekamon?

Ewoi n ekawangaga ma aia aiyuw n ataein eadae ae a na ribin roan ao ai e na ekawangaga ma ia. Eberuwa ma te ema eadae ae e ro in ion ma erian ao kakariri. Te ema oki e dok e tain eria ae e kukune n pin; oki e dok e tain aoa ae e eow n roan ae e na eim. Ekaeiyow eadae ae e kanimwin ion ao ekamon n tiantiaki.

Aia kanikokan ma aia tain te ema a dok a itei. Eteran aia ekamon n kakariri ma te ema ao aia aiyuw, eberuwa in rae e itabwi, ao iow roan ae e dok iow n kakariri. In rae te ema e itei n reke n reke o e itei ae e eberabera aio, ribin te korio n kakariri n te tai ae e kukune; te reke n reke ae e kukune e riai n aio ae e kukune n roan n dotor, in ae e ataeinaki n te [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/).

Te ekamon n ituroro bon etan ekamakin ae e imwiin, ao te ekamon imwin aio e dok e bwija ae e riai ngkekei n imwin. Ekorekoreyaki roan, iow te ema, eberuwa ma kakariri ae e ririen ao anga n ekawangaga ma aio imwin te ekamon.
