Kubantu abaningi, ingadi iyenye yezindawo abazijwayele kakhulu. Ukumba, ukunisela nokuqoqa izimbali kusebenzisa amakhono afundwa kudala, futhi ukuba ngaphandle kuletha umoya omusha, ukukhanya kwelanga nengoma yezinyoni. I-National Institute on Aging ibala ukunakekela izitshalo, ukutshala izimbali nemifino, nokukhuluma [ngokuthi izitshalo zikhula kanjani](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) phakathi kwemisebenzi abantu abane-Alzheimer's abangase bayijabulele. Leli khasi lichaza ukuthi yini ongayizama, indlela yokwenza ingadi ibe lula ukuyisebenzisa, nokuthi niphephe kanjani ekushiseni, elangeni nasekuzuleni.

## Yimiphi imisebenzi yangaphandle neyokulima ingadi esebenza kahle?

Imisebenzi emihle ilula, iyajwayeleka futhi ingenziwa umuntu emile noma ehleli. Khethani ngokuthi umuntu wayejabulela ini ngaphambili.

### Engadini

1. **Ukunisela.** Ikani elincane, elilula lokunisela noma ipayipi lokunisela elinomsele othambile nezimbiza ezimbalwa ezifinyelelekayo. Ukunisela kunokuqala nokuphela okucacile futhi kunzima ukukwenza kabi.
2. **Ukutshala imbewu ezimbizeni.** I-Alzheimer's Foundation of America iphakamisa ukutshala imbewu ezimbizeni ezincane nokuzibuka [zikhula](https://alzfdn.org/dementia-activity-gardening/). Imbewu enkulu efana nobhontshisi, izimbali zelanga noma amapizi ilula ukuyibamba.
3. **Ukususa izimbali ezibunile.** Ukukhetha izimbali ezibunile ngesandla kungumsebenzi othambile ophindaphindwayo. Khethani izitshalo ezingenameva.
4. **Ukuqoqa imifino noma amakhambi.** Utamatisi abancane, ubhontshisi namakhambi kulula ukukuqoqa futhi kumnandi ukukunambitha. Yingenisani endlini ukuze isetshenziswe ekudleni kwasemini.
5. **Ukuhogela nokuthinta amakhambi.** I-rosemary, i-mint ne-lavender kunamaphunga aqinile, amnandi. Hlikihlani iqabunga phakathi kweminwe bese nilidlulisa.
6. **Ukukhukhula indlela noma indawo engaphandle.** Umshanelo olula endaweni ecaba, eyomile, uma ukulinganisela kuqinile.
7. **Ukugcwalisa isitsha sokudla kwezinyoni.** Bese nihlala ndawonye nibuka ukuthi ngubani ovakashile.

### Ngaphakathi endlini, uma ukuphuma kungenakwenzeka

- **Ingadi encane yamakhambi efasiteleni** ye-basil, i-parsley noma i-chives.
- **Ukutshala etafuleni elimboziwe.** I-AFA iphakamisa ukusebenzisa amathreyi okudlela ukuze inhlabathi ihlale endaweni eyodwa, nokuhambisa inhlabathi ngezinkomishi ezincane.
- **Ukuhlela izimbali** ngezimbali nemifino eluhlaza enqunywe engadini noma esitolo sokudla.
- **Izimbali ezicindezelwe** ezivela esixheni sezimbali, kamuva ezinamathiselwe emakhadini. Bhekani [ubuciko nezandla](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) ukuze nithole okuningi.
- **Ukwenza izikhwama ezincane ezinephunga ze-lavender noma zamarozi**, omunye umbono we-AFA ogcina iphunga lengadi endlini.

### Ukuphuma ekhaya

- **Uhambo olufushane** ezungeze isitaladi, epaki noma esikhungweni sezitshalo, ngesivinini esihamba kancane, kukhona indawo yokuhlala.
- **Ukuhlala ngaphandle** ephoshini noma ebhentshini ukulalela izinyoni nokuzwa umoya. Lokhu kuwumsebenzi ophelele ngokwawo.
- **Ukuvakashela ingadi yomphakathi** ngesikhathi esithule sosuku. I-NHS ithi ezinye izindawo zinezingadi zezinzwa ezinezitshalo ezinephunga, izinto ezithintekayo nemithombo yamanzi.
- **Ukondla amadada noma ukubuka izinyoni** echibini lendawo, nigcina ibanga eliphephile emphethweni wamanzi.

## Ngiyenza kanjani ingadi ilule ukuyisebenzisa?

Letha ingadi emuntwini, hhayi umuntu engadini.

- **Amabhedi aphakeme noma izimbiza ezide** ezingeni eliseduze nesinqe kusho ukuthi akukho kuguqa noma ukukhothama.
- **Izitsha zezitshalo etafuleni** zisebenza kubantu abasebenzisa isihlalo esinamasondo noma abathanda ukuhlala.
- **Izindlela ezibanzi, ezilinganayo** ezingenamatshe antengantengayo noma amapayipi alele kuzo zinciphisa ingozi yokukhubeka.
- **Izihlalo ezinezingalo nethunzi** endleleni, ukuze ukuphumula kube khona njalo.
- **Amathuluzi anezibambo ezijiyile, ezithambile** ezilula ukuzibamba. Yekani izikelo zokunquma, imimese nanoma yini ebukhali.
- **Indawo ephiwe uthango onomnyango onesikhiya** umuntu angayihlola ekhululekile ngenkathi nihlala eduze.

## Yimiphi izitshalo okufanele ngiyigweme?

Khethani izitshalo ezilondekile ukuzithinta futhi, kungaba kuhle, ezilondekile uma zinambithwa. I-AFA iluleka ukusebenzisa izitshalo ezingavamile ukucasula isikhumba, ukuhlola ama-allergy, nokubuka ngokucophelela umuntu ongase athathe inhlabathi njengokudla. Iseluleko se-NIA sokuphepha ekhaya sihlanganisa ukususa noma ukukhiya izitshalo ezinobuthi.

- Gwemani amarozi anameva, ama-cactus, nezitshalo ezinomsoco ocasula isikhumba.
- Hlolani noma yisiphi isitshalo eningasiqiniseki ngaso nesikhungo sokulawula ubuthi noma esitolo sezitshalo sendawo ngaphambi kokutshala.
- Izinto ezijwayelekile ezithandekayo zingabuyisa imizwa ejabulisayo. I-AFA ikhuluma ngamarozi ne-Christmas cactus njengezibonelo. Khethani izinhlobo ezingenameva.
- Gcinani umanyolo, izibulala-zinambuzane nezibulala-khula zikhiyiwe, futhi ningazisebenzisi ezindaweni umuntu angazifinyelela.

## Ngiphepha kanjani ekushiseni nasemisebeni yelanga?

Hlelani isikhathi sangaphandle ngokuya ngesimo sezulu. I-NIA ichaza ukuthi abantu asebekhulile basengozini enkulu [yokugula ngenxa yokushisa](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/hot-weather-safety-older-adults), ngokwengxenye ngoba umzimba ushintsha kancane ezinguqukweni zokushisa nokuthi ezinye imithi ithinta indlela umzimba ophatha ngayo ukushisa.

- **Phumani ekuseni noma kusihlwa.** Gwemani maphakathi nosuku ezinyangeni ezifudumele. Hlolani inkomba yokushisa, hhayi nje izinga lokushisa.
- **Nikezani amanzi njalo.** Ningalindi ukuthi umuntu athi uyomile. Gwemani utshwala neziphuzo ezinekhafini ekushiseni.
- **Gqokani ngokufanele.** Izingubo ezinemibala ekhanyayo, ezikhululekile, isigqoko esibanzi nezibuko zelanga.
- **Sebenzisani ukhilimu wokuvikela elangeni.** I-NIA incoma ukhilimu ovikela ezinhlotsheni ezibanzi zemisebe, one-SPF engu-15 noma ngaphezulu, ophinde usetshenziswe osukwini.
- **Khethani ithunzi.** Beka itafula lokutshala ngaphansi komuthi noma isambulela.
- **Yenzani kube kufushane.** Isikhathi esifushane ngaphandle singase sanele, ikakhulukazi ezinsukwini ezifudumele.
- **Qapheleni izimpawu zokuxwayisa.** I-NIA ibala ikhanda elibuhlungu, ukudideka, isiyezi noma ukuhlanza njengezimpawu ezingaba khona zokugula ngenxa yokushisa. Umuntu one-dementia angase angakutsheli ukuthi uzizwa engaphilile, ngakho bhekani ubuso nendlela aziphatha ngayo. Ngenani endlini, nipholise ngamanzi nendwangu epholile, futhi nifune usizo lwezokwelapha uma engathuthukisi ngokushesha. I-heat stroke, enezimpawu ezifana nokuquleka, ukudideka, noma isikhumba esomile esibomvu, iyisimo esiphuthumayo sezempilo: shayelani inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo yendawo yakini.

Isimo sezulu esibandayo naso siyabalulekile. Ebusika, yenzani isikhathi sangaphandle sibe sifushane futhi niqinisekise ukuthi izandla nezinyawo ziqhubeka zifudumele futhi zomile.

## Ngibhekana kanjani nokuzula uma siphuma?

Hlalani nomuntu noma nini lapho ekhona ngaphandle. I-Alzheimer's Association ithi [abangu-6 kwabangu-10 abaphila ne-dementia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering) bazozula okungenani kanye, futhi abaningi bakwenza kaningi. Kungenzeka kunoma yisiphi isigaba.

[Izeluleko ze-NIA zokuzula nokulahleka](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/wandering-and-alzheimers-disease) zihlanganisa:

- Qinisekisani ukuthi umuntu ugqoke isongo le-ID noma lezempilo elinegama lakhe, ikheli nenombolo yenu yocingo, noma ulebule izingubo zakhe.
- Gcinani isithombe noma ividiyo yakamuva yomuntu.
- Tshelani omakhelwane ukuthi umuntu angase azule.
- Vikelani ingadi ngothango nomnyango okhiyiwe.
- Ningashiyi umuntu onomlando wokuzula engenamuntu omnakile.

I-Alzheimer's Association iphinde iphakamise ukwenza izindawo zangaphakathi nezangaphandle ezingahlolwa ngokuphepha, nokuhlela imisebenzi ngesikhathi sosuku lapho ukungaphumuli kungenzeka kakhulu. Uhambo engadini evikelekile, ezungezwe uthango, lungaba lufanelekile ekungaphumuli kokuhlwa. Bhekani umhlahlandlela wethu we-[ukungaphumuli ntambama](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Uma umuntu ephela, i-Alzheimer's Association iluleka ukusesha endaweni eseduze ngokushesha futhi nishayele inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo yendawo yakini uma engatholakali emizuzwini engu-15. Tshelani amaphoyisa ukuthi umuntu une-dementia.

## Ngivumelanisa kanjani ukulima uma izinto zishintsha?

| Uma lokhu sekunzima | Zamani lokhu esikhundleni salokho |
| --- | --- |
| Ukutshala ibhedi lonke | Ukutshala imbewu eyodwa embizeni eyodwa |
| Ukukhipha ukhula (ukwehlukanisa ukhula nezitshalo) | Ukunisela, noma ukususa izimbali ezibonakala ngokucacile zibunile |
| Ukuhamba ungena engadini | Ukuhlala etafuleni elinethreyi lenhlabathi nesitshalo |
| Ukuphuma nhlobo | Imbiza yamakhambi etafuleni lasekhishini, noma isihlalo eduze kwefasitela elivuliwe |
| Ukwenza noma yimuphi umsebenzi | Ukuhlala ngaphandle ndawonye, nilalele futhi nibuke |

Abanye abantu bathanda ukukubuka usebenza. I-NIA ithi ukuvumela umuntu abuke nje kulungile uma yilokho akujabulelayo.

## Kufanele singene endlini nini?

Ngenani uma umuntu ebonakala edinwe, eshisa, ebanda, engakhululekile noma esekulungele ukuyeka. Imizuzu engu-10 emifushane, ejabulisayo ingcono kunentambama ende ephetha ngosizi. Ukuyeka kusenesikhathi kuwumphumela omuhle.

## Letha ingadi endlini esikrinini

Ezinsukwini zemvula, [Hlungani ndawonye](/games/sort-together/) inebhasikidi lengadi lokuhlunga izithombe, futhi [Amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) ihlanganisa imibuzo evulekile engaholela ezindabeni zengadi. Uma ungena endlini uphuma ekushiseni uzizwa ushesha, [Ukuphefumula okuthulile](/games/calm-breathing/) inikeza ukuma okuhamba kancane, okuthulile enganabelana ngakho. Ukuthola imibono eminingi esekelwe ekuthinteni, ephunga nomsindo, bhekani [umhlahlandlela wethu wemisebenzi yezinzwa](/activities/sensory/).
