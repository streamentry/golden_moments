Kubantu abaningi, ingadi ingenye yezindawo abazijwayele kakhulu. Ukumba, ukunisela nokukha izimbali kusebenzisa amakhono afundwa kudala, futhi ukuba ngaphandle kuletha umoya omusha, ukukhanya kwelanga nokucula kwezinyoni. I-National Institute on Aging ibala ukunakekela izitshalo, ukutshala izimbali nemifino, kanye [nokukhuluma ngokuthi izitshalo zikhula kanjani](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) phakathi kwemisebenzi abantu abane-Alzheimer’s abangayijabulela. Leli khasi lichaza ongakuzama, indlela yokwenza ingadi ibe lula ukuyisebenzisa, nokuthi ungahlala kanjani uphephile ekushiseni, elangeni nasekuzulazuleni.

## Yimiphi imisebenzi yangaphandle neyokulima esebenza kahle?

Imisebenzi emihle ilula, ijwayelekile futhi ingenziwa umi noma uhlezi. Khetha ngokwalokho umuntu ayekuthanda ukwenza.

### Engadini

1. **Ukunisela.** Ibhodwe lokunisela elincane nelilula noma umbhobho onesifutho esithambile namabhodwe ambalwa afinyelelekayo. Ukunisela kunesiqalo nesiphetho esicacile futhi kunzima ukukwenza ngendlela engalungile.
2. **Ukutshala imbewu emabhodweni.** I-Alzheimer’s Foundation of America iphakamisa ukutshala imbewu emabhodweni amancane bese [niyibukela ikhula](https://alzfdn.org/dementia-activity-gardening/). Imbewu enkulu njengobhontshisi, ama-sunflower noma uphizi kulula ukuyibamba.
3. **Ukususa izimbali ezibunile.** Ukuncinza izimbali ezibunile ngesandla kuwumsebenzi omnene nophindaphindayo. Khetha izitshalo ezingenameva.
4. **Ukukha imifino noma amakhambi.** Utamatisi omncane, ubhontshisi namakhambi kulula ukukukha futhi kumnandi ukukuzwa. Kungeni endlini nikusebenzise esidlweni sasemini.
5. **Ukuhogela nokuthinta amakhambi.** I-rosemary, i-mint ne-lavender zinamakha aqinile namnandi. Hlikihla iqabunga phakathi kweminwe yakho bese ulinikeza.
6. **Ukushanela indlela noma uvulandi.** Umshanelo olula endaweni eyisicaba neyomile, uma ukuma kuzinzile.
7. **Ukugcwalisa isiphakeli sezinyoni.** Bese nihlala ndawonye nibuke ukuthi ubani ozovakasha.

### Ngaphakathi endlini, uma ukuphuma kungenzeki

- **Ingadi yamakhambi efasiteleni** ye-basil, i-parsley noma u-anyanisi oluhlaza.
- **Ukutshala emabhodweni etafuleni elimboziwe.** I-AFA iphakamisa ukusebenzisa amathreyi afana nawasekhefi ukugcina inhlabathi isendaweni eyodwa nokuhambisa inhlabathi ngezinkomishi ezincane.
- **Ukuhlela izimbali** ngezimbali ezisikiwe namahlamvu avela egcekeni noma esitolo sokudla.
- **Izimbali ezicindezelwe** ezivela esixukwini sezimbali, kamuva ezinanyathiselwa emakhadini. Bheka [ubuciko nemisebenzi yezandla](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) ukuze uthole okwengeziwe.
- **Ukwenza amasaka amancane e-lavender noma e-rose**, okunye umqondo we-AFA ogcina iphunga lengadi lingaphakathi endlini.

### Ukuphuma nokuhambahamba

- **Ukuhamba kancane** uzungeze ibhulokhi, epaki noma endaweni ethengisa izitshalo, ngejubane eliphansi nendawo yokuhlala.
- **Ukuhlala ngaphandle** kuvulandi noma ebhentshini ulalele izinyoni uzwe nomoya. Lokhu kuwumsebenzi ophelele ngokwawo.
- **Ukuvakashela ingadi yomphakathi** ngesikhathi sosuku esithule. I-NHS iphawula ukuthi ezinye izindawo zinezingadi zezinzwa ezinezitshalo ezinephunga, ukwakheka okuhlukile nezici zamanzi.
- **Ukupha amadada ukudla noma ukubuka izinyoni** echibini lendawo, uqhele ngokuphepha onqenqemeni lwamanzi.

## Ngingayenza kanjani ingadi ibe lula ukuyisebenzisa?

Letha ingadi kumuntu, hhayi umuntu engadini.

- **Imibhede ephakeme noma izitsha ezinde zokutshala** ezicishe zilingane nokhalo zisho ukuthi akudingeki ukuguqa noma ukugoba.
- **Amabhodwe etafuleni** asebenzela abantu abasebenzisa isihlalo sabakhubazekile noma abakhetha ukuhlala.
- **Izindlela ezibanzi nezilingene** ezingenawo amatshe axegayo noma amapayipi alele kuzo zehlisa ingozi yokukhubeka.
- **Izihlalo ezinezingalo nomthunzi** endleleni, ukuze ukuphumula kuhlale kuyindlela ekhona.
- **Amathuluzi anezibambo eziwugqinsi nezithambile** okulula ukuwabamba. Yeka izikele zokuthena, imimese nanoma yini ebukhali.
- **Indawo ebiyelwe enesango elikhiyiwe** umuntu angayihlola ngokukhululeka ngenkathi uhlala eduze.

## Yiziphi izitshalo okufanele ngizigweme?

Khetha izitshalo eziphephile ukuzithinta futhi, uma kungenzeka, eziphephile uma zinambithwa. I-AFA yeluleka ngokusebenzisa izitshalo ezingavami ukucasula isikhumba, ukuhlola ukungezwani komzimba nezinto ezithile, nokuqapha eduze noma ubani ongaphambanisa inhlabathi nokudla. Iseluleko se-NIA sokuphepha ekhaya sihlanganisa ukususa noma ukukhiya izitshalo ezinobuthi.

- Gwema ama-rose anameva, ama-cactus, nezitshalo ezinobisi olucasula isikhumba.
- Hlola noma yisiphi isitshalo ongaqiniseki ngaso nesikhungo sokulawula ubuthi noma indawo yendawo ethengisa izitshalo ngaphambi kokusitshala.
- Izintandokazi ezijwayelekile zingabuyisa imizwa emihle. I-AFA ibala ama-rose ne-Christmas cactus njengezibonelo. Khetha izinhlobo ezingenameva.
- Gcina umanyolo, izibulala-zinambuzane nezibulala-khula kukhiyiwe, futhi ungakusebenzisi ezindaweni umuntu angazifinyelela.

## Ngingahlala kanjani ngiphephile ekushiseni naselangeni?

Hlela isikhathi sangaphandle ngokwesimo sezulu. I-NIA ichaza ukuthi abantu abadala basengozini enkulu [yokugula okuhlobene nokushisa](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/hot-weather-safety-older-adults), ngokwengxenye ngoba umzimba awuzivumelanisi kahle nezinguquko zokushisa futhi eminye imithi ithinta indlela umzimba obhekana ngayo nokushisa.

- **Phumani ekuseni kakhulu noma ntambama.** Gwemani phakathi nosuku ezinyangeni ezifudumele. Hlola inkomba yokushisa, hhayi izinga lokushisa kuphela.
- **Niphane amanzi njalo.** Ningalindi ukuthi umuntu athi womile. Gwemani utshwala neziphuzo ezine-caffeine lapho kushisa.
- **Gqokani ngendlela efanele.** Izingubo ezinemibala ekhanyayo nezikhululekile, isigqoko esinomphetho obanzi nezibuko zelanga.
- **Sebenzisani isivikelo selanga.** I-NIA incoma i-SPF 15 noma ngaphezulu evikela izinhlobo eziningi zemisebe, ephindwayo phakathi nosuku.
- **Khethani umthunzi.** Bekani itafula lokutshala ngaphansi kwesihlahla noma isambulela.
- **Kwenzeni kube kufushane.** Isikhathi esifushane ngaphandle singanele, ikakhulukazi ngezinsuku ezifudumele.
- **Qaphelani izimpawu eziyisixwayiso.** I-NIA ibala ikhanda elibuhlungu, ukudideka, isiyezi noma isicanucanu njengezimpawu ezingaba khona zokugula okubangelwa ukushisa. Umuntu ophila nesifo sokuwohloka kwengqondo angase angakutsheli ukuthi akazizwa kahle, ngakho qaphela ubuso bakhe nokuziphatha kwakhe. Ngenani endlini, niphole ngamanzi nendwangu epholile, bese nifuna usizo lwezempilo uma engabi ngcono ngokushesha. I-heat stroke, enezimpawu ezifana nokuquleka, ukudideka, noma isikhumba esomile nesibomvu, iyisimo esiphuthumayo sezempilo: shayela u-911.

Isimo sezulu esibandayo naso sibalulekile. Ebusika, gcina isikhathi sangaphandle sifushane futhi uqiniseke ukuthi izandla nezinyawo kuhlala kufudumele futhi komile.

## Ngingabhekana kanjani nokuzulazula lapho siphumela ngaphandle?

Hlala nomuntu noma nini lapho engaphandle. I-Alzheimer’s Association ithi [abantu abayisithupha kwabayishumi abaphila nesifo sokuwohloka kwengqondo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering) bazozulazula okungenani kanye, futhi abaningi bakwenza kaningi. Lokhu kungenzeka kunoma yisiphi isigaba.

[Amathiphu e-NIA ngokuphathelene nokuzulazula nokulahleka](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/wandering-and-alzheimers-disease) ahlanganisa:

- Qiniseka ukuthi umuntu ugqoka umazisi noma isongo lezempilo elinegama lakhe, ikheli nenombolo yakho yocingo, noma ubhale amagama ezingutsheni zakhe.
- Gcina isithombe sakamuva noma ividiyo yomuntu.
- Yazisa omakhelwane ukuthi umuntu angase azulazule.
- Vikela igceke ngocingo nesango elikhiyiwe.
- Ungashiyi umuntu onomlando wokuzulazula engabhekwe.

I-Alzheimer’s Association iphinde iphakamise ukudala izindawo zangaphakathi nezangaphandle ezingahlolwa ngokuphepha, nokuhlela imisebenzi ngesikhathi sosuku lapho ukungahlaliseki kuvame kakhulu. Ukuhamba engadini ephephile nebiyelwe kungaba yisinqumo esihle [ekungahlalisekeni kwantambama](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Uma umuntu enyamalala, hlola izindawo eziseduze eziphephile ngenkathi uxhumana namaphoyisa endawo noma izinsiza eziphuthumayo ngokushesha. Ungawalindi inani elithile lemizuzu ngaphambi kokubika. [Isihloko sephephabhuku le-U.S. Department of Justice sango-2021 mayelana nophenyo lwabantu abalahlekile](https://www.justice.gov/usdoj-media/usao/media/1134031/dl?inline=) sithi izinhlaka zomthetho akufanele zidinge isikhathi sokulinda esinqunyiwe futhi sibala izimpawu ezinjengezesifo sokuwohloka kwengqondo njengento ekhathaza kakhulu. Izinqubo zokubika endaweni ziyahlukahluka. Yabelana ngokuthi umuntu ugcine ukubonwa nini futhi kuphi, ugqokeni nesithombe sakamuva. Landela imiyalelo yabaphendulayo bendawo; ungaseshi wedwa ezindaweni ezingaphephile.

## Ngingakushintsha kanjani ukulima njengoba izinto zishintsha?

| Uma lokhu sekunzima | Zama lokhu esikhundleni salokho |
| --- | --- |
| Ukutshala yonke imibhede | Ukutshala imbewu eyodwa ebhodweni elilodwa |
| Ukukhipha ukhula (ukuhlukanisa ukhula nezitshalo) | Ukunisela, noma ukususa izimbali ezibunile ezibonakala kalula |
| Ukuhamba uzungeze ingadi | Ukuhlala etafuleni nethreyi lenhlabathi nesitshalo |
| Ukuphuma ngaphandle nhlobo | Ibhodwe lamakhambi etafuleni lasekhishini, noma isihlalo eduze kwefasitela elivulekile |
| Ukwenza noma yimuphi umsebenzi | Ukuhlala ngaphandle ndawonye, nilalele nibuke |

Abanye abantu bakhetha ukukubuka usebenza. I-NIA iphawula ukuthi ukuyeka umuntu abuke nje kulungile uma kuyilokho akujabulelayo.

## Kufanele singene nini endlini?

Ngenani endlini lapho umuntu ebukeka ekhathele, eshiswe, egodola, engakhululekile noma eselungele ukuqeda. Imizuzu eyishumi emifushane nemnandi ingcono kunentambama ende ephela ngokukhathazeka. Ukuqeda kusenesikhathi kuwumphumela omuhle.

## Letha ingadi ngaphakathi esikrinini

Ngezinsuku zemvula, [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) inebhasikidi lengadi lokuhlela izithombe, futhi [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) inemibuzo evulekile engaholela ezindabeni zengadi. Uma ningena nivela ekushiseni nizizwa niphuthuma, [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) inikeza ikhefu elihamba kancane nelithule eningabelana ngalo. Ukuze uthole eminye imibono esekelwe ekuthinteni, ekuhogeleni nasekulaleleni, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wemisebenzi yezinzwa](/activities/sensory/).
