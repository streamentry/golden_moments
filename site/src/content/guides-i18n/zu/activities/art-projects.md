Imisebenzi yobuciko iwumkhuba omuhle wokuchitha isikhathi nomuntu one-dementia. Ibamba izandla, amehlo nemizwa, futhi inikeza into engakhulunywa ngayo. Leli khasi linikeza imisebenzi ethile enezinyathelo ezilula, kanye nesiqondiso sokukhetha izinto zokusebenza ngokuphephile. Ngemigomo yobuciko bomuntu omdala, njengokugwema izinto ezifanele izingane, bheka [ubuciko nemisebenzi yezandla yabantu abadala](/activities/arts-and-crafts/).

## Ukuqala noma yimuphi umsebenzi wobuciko

1. **Lungisa ngaphambi kokuqala.** Beka izinto ezimbili noma ezintathu, ishidi lokuvikela nelaphu elinamanzi.
2. **Bonisa isinyathelo sokuqala.** Cwilisa ibhulashi, dabula iphepha lokuqala, noma cindezela ubumba obuncane, bese umemela umuntu ukuthi enze okufanayo.
3. **Sebenzani ecaleni komunye.** Gcina umsebenzi wakho eduze ukuze umsebenzi uzwakale njengowabelwane, hhayi njengokuqapha.
4. **Khuluma ngalokho okubonayo.** Chaza imibala, ukuthungwa nezimo, esikhundleni sokwahlulela umsebenzi.
5. **Yeka ngaphambi kokuthi amandla ehle.** Imizuzu eyishumi kuya kwengamashumi amabili ivame ukuba yisikhathi esihle.

I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ichaza ukuthi ubuciko iyindlela yokuveza imizwa nokudala ngaphandle kokudinga ikhono lobuciko. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) yeluleka ukuhlukanisa imisebenzi ibe yizinyathelo ezincane, ezilawulekayo.

## Ukufafaza ngamanzi ngombala

Sebenzisa iphepha elinzima lewatercolor, ibhulashi elikhulu nelithambile, nemibala embalwa ezindishini ezingajulile. Fafaza iphepha ngamanzi ahlanzekile, bese uvumela umuntu ahlise imibala phezu kwalo. Ngokuthambekisa iphepha kancane, imibala izogeleza. Umphumela uyisithombe esithambile, esingafanekisi into ethile, esidinga uhlelo lutho.

- **Esigabeni sokuqala:** Vumela umuntu akhethe imibala bese epeyinta indawo yasemaphandleni.
- **Esigabeni esiphakathi:** Nikeza imibala emibili noma emithathu, bese wena wenza ukufafaza.
- **Esigabeni sokugcina:** Bamba iphepha kanti umuntu ahlikihle ngesipontshi.

## I-collage yamaphepha ezincwadi

Qoqa amaphepha amamagazini anezithombe ezikhanyayo zezimbali, ukudla, izilwane, izimoto noma ukuvakasha. Dabula izithombe zibe yizingcezu ezinkulu, okulula kunokusika, bese uzinamathisela esiqeshini sekhadibhodi. I-collage isebenza kahle njengesiqalo sengxoxo, ngoba isithombe ngasinye singaholela enkumbulweni. Ilingene kahle umuntu othanda ukuhlunga nokukhetha.

## Ubumba obuthambile

Ubumba obuoma emoyeni noma ubumba obuthambile bungacindezelwa, bugoqwe bese bucindezelwa. Yenza isitsha esilula noma ithayela elilingile. Ubumba buthinta kakhulu, ngakho bungaduduza abantu abathanda ukuthinta izinto. Gcina izingcezu zikhulu, bese ugwema amathuluzi anamaphuzu abukhali.

## Ama-pastel e-chalk esiphepheni esimnyama

Iphepha elimnyama lenza imibala ye-pastel ikhanye. Sebenzisa ama-pastel amakhulu nezinkulu, bese umuntu eyenza imigqa emikhulu noma ahlanganise imibala ngomunwe noma ngethishu. Ama-pastel anothuli, ngakho lungisa ethreyini bese usula izandla ngemva kwalokho. Khetha ama-pastel athambile amaphakheji athi awanabuthi.

## Ukuphrinta ngamaqabunga

Qoqa amaqabunga amakhulu egadini, uphinde upeyinte imithambo ngepeyinti ehlanzekayo, bese ucindezela leli qabunga esiphepheni. Liphakamise ngokucophelela bese ubheka ukuphrinta. Lo msebenzi uhambisana kahle nemisebenzi [yangaphandle nokulima](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/), futhi ungaqala ngokuhamba ukuqoqa amaqabunga.

## Ukuhlunga izindwangu nokuhlobisa izimbali

Hlunga izingcezu zezindwangu ezinokuthungwa okuhlukile ngokuthinta, noma uhlele izimbali ezimbalwa ezintsha engilazini. Lo msebenzi wezimizwa unobunzima obuncane kakhulu. Ulungele nabantu esigabeni sokugcina. Ukuze uthole imibono eengeziwe, bheka [imisebenzi yezimizwa yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/sensory/).

## Ukukhetha izinto zobuciko

Izinto zibaluleke kakhulu ngokuphepha nokududuza kunokuba zibe zinhle kakhulu. Lapho ukhetha, cabangela:

- **Akunabuthi futhi kuhlanzeka:** Funa amalebula athi akunabuthi bese uhlola ukuthi amapeyinti ayahlanzeka.
- **Izibambo ezinkulu:** Ama-crayon amakhulu, amabhulashi ajiyile nama-pastel amakhulu alula ukubamba.
- **Azinazo izingxenyana ezincane:** Gwema amapheli, izinkinobho, izinhlamvu ezikhazimulayo nomcwebe ongase udliwe.
- **Azinawo amathuluzi abukhali:** Sebenzisa izikelo zokuphepha noma dabula iphepha ngesandla.
- **Ukungezwani:** Hlola ukuthi kukhona i-latex, nanoma yiziphi izinto ezikhathaza isikhumba.
- **Ukuqhathanisa:** Imibala eqinile ephepheni elimhlophe noma elimnyama ibonakala kalula.
- **Uhlu olufushane:** Izinto ezimbili noma ezintathu zanele. Izinketho eziningi kakhulu zingadida umuntu.
- **Ukuhlanza kalula:** Sebenzisa ithreyi, ishidi lepulasitiki nelaphu elinamanzi, ukuze ukungcola kungabi yisizathu sokuyeka.

## Ukulungisa ngokwesigaba

Esigabeni sokuqala, abantu abaningi bangahlela bagcwalise umsebenzi ngosizo oluncane. Esigabeni esiphakathi, nikeza izinketho ezimbalwa nesiqondiso esengeziwe. Esigabeni sokugcina, gxila ezinqubweni zezimizwa ezifana nokucindezela ubumba noma ukudabula ipeyinti ngesipontshi, bese ujabulela umphumela hhayi ukuqedwa kwawo.

Ukuze uthole imibono ethile yesigaba, bheka [isigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/), [isigaba esiphakathi](/activities/middle-stage/) nemisebenzi [yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/). Uma umuntu enqaba noma ekhathazeka, umhlahlandlela wethu othi [lapho umuntu esho ukuthi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) uchaza ukuthi ungasuka kanjani.

## Hlanganisa nomdlalo

Ngemva kwesikhathi sokupeyinta, zama [ukudweba imibala](/games/coloring/) ethabulethini ukuze uqhubeke ngokuthula, noma [impicha yezingcezu](/games/picture-puzzle/) njengomsebenzi wokuphetha. Zombili zigcina izandla zikhatheke ngaphandle kokudinga ikhono elincane kakhulu.
