Abantu abaningi bafuna umdlalo abadlalayo ndawonye odlala njengengxoxo, hhayi njengovivinyo. Imibuzo engamashumi amabili nemidlalo efanayo yokukhomba ingasebenza kahle, ngoba imithetho ilula futhi ingxoxo ingaya noma yikuphi. Ukuqhubeka kwalokhu ukuguqula izindima ezivamile: umuntu uyacabanga impendulo, wena ubuza. Leli khasi lichaza ukudlala lezi zidlalo ngobumnene, nokuthi yiziphi imidlalo yengxoxo elingene abantu abane-dementia.

## Kungani kufanele kuguqulwe imithetho evamile?

Kuvivinyo olujwayelekile, omunye uyabuza bese omunye kufanele awazi. Lokho kubeka umuntu one-dementia esimeni lapho angahluleka khona, futhi ukuhluleka kungaba nomphumela ophazamisayo. Lapho ekhetha into futhi wena ukhomba, yena uyisazi. Ubamba impendulo, unquma lapho esho khona ukuthi yebo noma cha, futhi angajabulela ukubona ukuzama kwakho.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) yeluleka abanakekeli ukuthi basebenzise amasentensi amafushane nalula, umbuzo oyedwa ngesikhathi, nezwi elithulile nelinomonde. Lezo zinkambiso zilingana kahle nemidlalo yokukhomba.

## Ukudlala imibuzo engamashumi amabili ngobumnene

1. **Khetha isihloko** esisuka empilweni yomuntu, njengekhishi, igadi, izilwane noma uhlangothi lolwandle.
2. **Vumela acabange ngento** bese eyishoyo ngokuthula, noma akhombise ikhadi lesithombe ukuze ungadingi ukuyizwa.
3. **Buza umbuzo oyedwa wokuthi yebo noma cha ngesikhathi.** “Ingabe kuyinto oyidlayo?” “Ingabe ikhulu kunesicathulo?”
4. **Amukele noma yimuphi umphendulo.** Ukuqhuqha ikhanda, ukuphendula, noma ukuhleka konke kubalwa.
5. **Yenza izimbono ngokujabula** bese ziyajabulisa. Uma ulibambile kahle, nithokoze ndawonye.
6. **Nikeza izimpawu** uma umuntu eshaywa inkathazo: “Kuyinto esekhishini.”
7. **Ungabali imibuzo** futhi ungagcini ngokubala amaphuzu.

Uma umuntu engalandeli imithetho, shintshela ohlobeni olulula: nikeza izinto ezimbalwa bese ekhomba okulungile.

## Izinto ezinhle zokukhetha

- Isithelo, imifino noma imbali ayithandayo
- Isilwane esivela epulazini, esizooweni noma isilwane asasinaso ngaphambilini
- Ithuluzi, njengohamba, irekisi noma inaliti yokuthunga
- Impahla yasendlini noma into yasendlini
- Indawo ayaziyo kahle, njengepaki noma idolobha
- Into yokugqoka ayeyithanda ukuyigqoka

Gwema amagama abantu, nanoma yini engaletha usizi, nezinto ezingaba yingozi noma ezidukisa kalula.

## Ezinye imidlalo yengxoxo

- **Lokhu noma lokho.** Nikeza izinketho ezimbili ubuze ukuthi yiyiphi ayikhethayo: itiye noma ikhofi, izintaba noma ulwandle, ingubo ebomvu noma eluhlaza. Akukho mpendulo eyiyo.
- **Wawuzokhetha ini.** Buza umbuzo omnene nokhanyayo: “Wawuzokhetha ipikiniki noma ukudla ngaphandle?”
- **Phetha isisho.** Umusho ojwayelekile unesiphetho esivulekile. Zama [imidlalo yokuphetha izisho](/activities/finish-the-saying/) ukuze uthole isiqondiso.
- **Chaza indawo.** Cela umuntu achaze ikamelo, idolobha noma ukubuka ayikhethayo, bese umemela nawe ukuthi ulibone.
- **Amakhadi okubuza avulekile.** [Amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) ethu anikeza imibuzo evulekile ngezihloko, anokuphrintwa etafuleni.

Ukuze uthole imibono eminingi esebenzisa inkumbulo nempilo yomuntu, bheka [imibuzo yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence-questions/). Ukuze uthole injabulo yovivinyo ngobumnene, bheka [imibuzo elula yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/trivia/), esebenzisa izinketho futhi ingaboni amaphuzu.

## Uma umuntu ethukuthala?

Yima, unwebe bese uthi into efana nokuthi, “Masidlale okunye.” Ukuthukuthela kuwumqondiso wokushintsha umdlalo, hhayi ukucindezela kakhulu. Umhlahlandlela wethu othi [indlela yokudlala imidlalo nomuntu one-dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) uchaza ukushintsha imithetho ngesikhathi.

## Imidlalo ingakwenzani nangeyini okungenakwenzeka

Imidlalo yengxoxo injabulisa futhi ihlanganisa abantu. Ingasiza abantu bahlanganye futhi babelane ngezindaba. Ayelaphi i-dementia, ayinciphisi futhi ayivimbi. Gcina injongo ilula: umzuzu ofudumele ndawonye.
