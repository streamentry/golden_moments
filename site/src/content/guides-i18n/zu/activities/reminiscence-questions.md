Ukukhumbula kusho ukukhuluma ndawonye ngesikhathi esedlule: amakhaya obuntwana, imisebenzi yokuqala, ukudla okuthandwayo, izingoma, imishado namaholide. Kubantu abaningi abane-dementia, ingxoxo efudumele ngempilo yabo siqu iyenye yezingxenye ezijabulisa kakhulu losuku. Okubalulekile ukubuza ngendlela ememela indaba futhi ingavivinyi inkumbulo. Le mibuzo ihlelwe ngezihloko, nezeluleko zokuyisebenzisa.

## Imithetho yemibuzo yokukhumbula

- **Buza imibuzo evulekile.** “Ngitshele ngokuthi…” kanye “Kwakunjani…” kumemela indaba. “Uyakhumbula…?” kubuza impendulo eyiyo.
- **Ungahloli.** Gwema “Lo ngubani?”, “Kwakunyaka muni lapho?” kanye “Uyakhumbula igama lokuthi…?”
- **Amukela noma yini eyeza.** Uma indaba ishintsha noma izindawo zixubana, yijabulele bese ubuza ngemizwa.
- **Sebenzisa umbuzo owodwa ngesikhathi.** Linda, lalela, bese ulandelela ngokuthile okulula.
- **Vumela umuntu ahole.** Uma efuna ukukhuluma ngokunye, mlandele.

Imihlahlandlela ye-[National Institute on Aging yokuxhumana](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/dos-and-donts-communicating-person-who-has) yeluleka amasentensi amafushane, izwi elithulile, nomonde lapho umuntu efuna amagama akhe. Lezi zimiso zibaluleke lapha njengakwezinye izindawo.

## Ikhaya nobuntwana

- Ngitshele ngendlu owakhulela kuyo. Ikhishi lalinjani?
- Wawuthanda ukwenzani ngaphandle lapho usemncane?
- Ubani ayebadlalisa nabo emgwaqeni wakini?
- Ikamelo lakho lalinjani? Ubelabelana nalo nomuntu?

## Umsebenzi nokwenza izinto

- Yimuphi umsebenzi owawujabulela kakhulu?
- Ngitshele ngalolo suku emsebenzini owakwenza wazizwa uziqhenya.
- Wawuthanda ukwenza noma ukulungisa ini ngezandla zakho?
- Ubani wakufundisa okuningi ngomsebenzi wakho?

## Ukudla nokupheka

- Umndeni wakho wawudla ini ngeSonto?
- Ngitshele ngesidlo ethile umuntu omndenini wakho ayesipheka kahle kakhulu.
- Wawunendawo oyithandayo yokuthola into emnandi lapho usemncane?
- Yiliphi iphunga elikukhumbuza ikhaya?

Ingxoxo ngokudla ihambisana kahle nomhlahlandlela wemisebenzi [yezimizwa](/activities/sensory/), nokunambitha okuncane uma kuphephile ukukunikeza.

## Umculo nokudansa

- Yiziphi izingoma owawuzihlabelela noma udansa kuzo lapho usemncane?
- Wawuthanda muni umculi noma iqembu?
- Wawuzwa kuphi umculo ngalezo zinsuku: ekhaya, emadansini, esontweni?

Ukuze uthole izinhlelo zemculo nemibono, bheka [imisebenzi yomculo yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/music/).

## Amaholide nemicimbi

- Wayiyiphi iholide oyithandayo ukukhula?
- Ngitshele ngomcimbi wokuzalwa owawukhumbula.
- Umndeni wawenzani ngokuhlwa kwasehlobo?
- Umndeni wakho wawubhiyoza kanjani izikhathi zonyaka?

Ukuze uthole imibono efanele iholide ethile, bheka [imisebenzi yamaholide yomuntu one-dementia](/activities/holidays/).

## Umndeni nabangane

- Ngitshele ngomuntu owayekuhlekisa kakhulu.
- Wawunjani usuku lomshado wakho?
- Ngitshele ngezingane zakho lapho zisencane.
- Abangane bakho bakubiza ngani?

Buza imibuzo yomndeni ngobumnene. Uma umuntu elilela umuntu, mlandele futhi gwema ukumcela ukuthi akhumbule imininingwane ayingathanda ukuyikhumbula.

## Izindawo nemvelo

- Ngitshele ngendawo oyithanda ukucabanga ngayo.
- Yayinjani isimo sezulu lapho ukhulela khona?
- Wawunegadi noma isihlahla osithandayo?
- Ngitshele ngohambo owajabulela.

## Ukusebenzisa izithombe nezinto

Izithombe zindawo yokuqala yemvelo. Bamba isithombe ngamehlo bese uthi, “Ngitshele ngalokhu.” I-[Alzheimer's Association ngemihlahlandlela yayo yemisebenzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) iphakamisa ukusebenzisa izinto ezijwayelekile, izithombe nomculo ukuqala ingxoxo. Ibhokisi lenkumbulo elinezinto ezimbalwa ezivela empilweni yomuntu nalo lingasebenza kahle; bheka [imisebenzi yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) ukuze uthole ukuthi ungalakha kanjani eyodwa.

## Uma inkumbulo ilimaza

Ezinye izinkumbulo zivusa usizi, ukwesaba noma ulaka. Uma umuntu ethukuthele, ungamphoqi. Thi okuthile okufudumele okufana nalokhu: “Lokho kubonakala sengathi kwakuyisikhathi esinzima. Ungathanda ukukhuluma ngokuthile okujabulisayo?” Bese ushintsha isihloko, unikeze itiye, noma ubeke iculo ayilithandayo.

## Hlanganisa nomdlalo noma nokuhamba

Ngemva kweseshini yokukhumbula, umdlalo oqabulayo ungagcina umoya ukhanyisa. Zama [amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) ngemibuzo evulekile eengeziwe, noma [phetha umusho](/games/finish-the-saying/) ngamagama ajwayelekile asuka ebuntwaneni bomuntu. Ukuze uthole izeluleko zokuxhumana ngokubanzi, bheka [izeluleko zokuxhumana nomuntu one-dementia](/caregiving/communication-tips/).
