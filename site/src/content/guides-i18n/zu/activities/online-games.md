Abantu abaningi basesha imidlalo yamahhala ku-inthanethi ye-dementia ngoba bafuna into ayo ndawonye engadingi isitolo, ukungena ngemvume noma ikhadi lesikweletu. Izinketho ezingcono kakhulu ziyilula, zisekelwe ezithombeni, futhi zilula ukuyeka. Kufanele zizwakale njengesikhathi esabelwanwe, hhayi njengovivinyo. Leli khasi lichaza okufanele ukubheke, indlela yokulungisa umdlalo, nokuthi lezi zidlalo zingenzani nangeyini okungenakwenzeka.

## Iyini okwenza umdlalo ku-inthanethi ufaneleke ku-dementia?

Umdlalo ufanele abantu abaningi abaphila ne-dementia uma ubuza ukubona kunokukhumbula. Ukufanisa izithombe ezimbili ezifanayo, ukuthepha isithombe esifana nesithunzi, noma ukuphonsa into esitsheni esifanele kuncike ekutheni amehlo angabona khona manje. Imidlalo ethembele ekukhumbuleni umthetho, igama, inani noma inyathelo elidlule ivame ukudala ukukhathala.

I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) incoma imisebenzi eyakha kulokho umuntu asakujabulela futhi angakwenza, futhi evumela ukuthi aphumelele ngaphandle kosizo. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) iphakamisa ukwenza umsebenzi ulula kunokuwuyeka lapho amakhono eshintsha.

## Imidlalo ye-Golden Moments ongayivula ku-browser

Umdlalo ngamunye ongaphansi uvulwa kusayithi ngaphandle kokulanda. Iningi lidlalwa ethabulethini noma ekhompyutheni; ezinye zingaphinde zifakwe ephepheni.

- **[Izithombe ezifanayo](/games/picture-pairs/).** Amapheya amakhulu ezithombe ezifanayo ezisabonakalayo, ngaphandle kwesikhathi noma amaphuzu. Kuhle komdlalo wokuqala.
- **[Thola okufanayo](/games/find-the-same/).** Isithombe esisodwa phezulu, bese kukhona esifanayo phakathi kwezinketho ezimbili kuya kwezine ngaphansi.
- **[Ukufanisa isithunzi](/games/shadow-match/).** Isithunzi esithambile sivela phezulu; thepha isithombe esikwenzayo.
- **[Hlunga ndawonye](/games/sort-together/).** Thepha isithombe, bese uthepha isitsha esifanele kuso, njengekhishi noma igadi.
- **[Ipuzzle yesithombe](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Isithombe sinqunywa zibe yizingcezu, futhi isithombe sonke sihlala sibonakala njengesiqondiso.
- **[Phetha imisho](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Imisho ejwayelekile ingekho igama lokugcina; thepha ukukhetha noma bonisa isiphetho.
- **[Amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/).** Amakhadi ezithombe anemibuzo evulekile yokukhuluma, hhayi ukuhlola. Anokuphrintwa futhi.
- **[Izithombe zakho ezifanayo](/games/photo-pairs/).** Amapheya afanayo akhiwe ngezithombe ozikhethela zona kwidivayisi yakho. Izithombe zihlala ku-browser kuleyo divayisi.
- **[Ukuphefumula ngokuthula](/games/calm-breathing/).** Ukuma kancane nokuthula ukuphefumula ndawonye. Wena uyiqalisa uyiyeke.

Imidlalo yokusesha amagama, ukudweba imibala nebingo yezithombe nayo itholakala njengemisebenzi yokuphrinta. Bheka [imisebenzi yamahhala ye-dementia yokuphrinta](/activities/printable/) ukuze uthole okwengeziwe.

## Ukulungisa iseshini yomdlalo

1. **Khetha isikhathi esithulile** lapho umuntu ephumule. Ntambama kakhulu kungaba nzima kwabanye, ngakho hlola okwakho kuqala.
2. **Sebenzisa iskrini elikhulu.** Ithebhulethi esitendini, noma ikhompyutha ene-browser esikali sokukhulisa, kulula ukuyibona nokuyithinta.
3. **Vala amathebhu amanye bese uvala izaziso.** Izikhangiso ezivelayo ngokuzumayo zidumaza futhi zingaqeda iseshini enhle.
4. **Hlala eduze komuntu, hhayi ngaphambi kwakhe.** Ukubuka ndawonye kuzwakala njengobungane kunokuba yivivinyo.
5. **Qala ngomdlalo owodwa imizuzu emihlanu kuya kweshumi.** Vumela umuntu athinte iskrini noma akhombe ngenkathi nishintshana.
6. **Phetha ngenkathi kukhona okuhle.** Qeda umjikelezo, bese uxoxa ngesithombe esisodwa noma unikeze itiye elincane.

## Ukuphepha nobumfihlo ezidivayisini ezabelwanwa

- **Izithombe zihlala kwidivayisi.** Imidlalo yezithombe isebenzisa izithombe ozikhethelayo, futhi zihlala ku-browser kuleyo divayisi. Hlola ukuthi ngubani omunye ongavula ikhompyutha noma ithebhulethi.
- **Gwema izixhumanisi nezikhangiso zangaphandle.** Sebenzisa imidlalo ngqo kusayithi, bese ungachofozi amabhena noma izaziso ezicela imininingwane yakho siqu.
- **Gcina izinkokhelo namaakhawunti kwehlukile.** Ungamshiyi umuntu yedwa esikrinini esicela iphasiwedi noma inombolo yekhadi.
- **Qaphela ukuzulazula.** Gcina ithebhulethi kude uma umuntu angase aphume nayo endlini.

Ukuze uthole uhlu olubanzi lokuhlola, bheka [indlela yokukhetha izinhlelo nemidlalo ye-dementia](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) kanye [nokulungisa ithebhulethi noma ifoni](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Lokho lezi zidlalo ezingakwenza nalokho ezingakwenzi

Imidlalo ku-inthanethi inika abantu abane-dementia ithuba lokujabulela isikhathi, ukubuka izithombe ndawonye, nokubamba iqhaza esintweni ngaphandle koxinezelo. Ayelaphi i-dementia, ayinciphisi noma ayivimbi. Isibonelo esibikwa kakhulu i-[Federal Trade Commission ka-2016 yenza ngokumelene ne-Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) ngezimangalo zokuqeqesha ingqondo ezingakwazanga ukuqinisekiswa. Qaphela noma yimuphi umdlalo, uhlelo noma iwebhusayithi etshela inkumbulo engcono, ehlola ingqondo yakho noma ethi ingabuyisela ukwehla.

Sebenzisa lezi zidlalo njengengxenye yosuku oluphinde lufake umculo, ukuhamba, ingxoxo nokuphumula. Ukuze ufunde ngokwenza imidlalo ibe nokuthula kunokuba nengcindezi, bheka [indlela yokudlala imidlalo nomuntu one-dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) kanye [nemidlalo yenkumbulo engahloli](/activities/memory-games/).

## Lapho okumele uyeke khona

Yeka uma umuntu ebonakala ekhathele, engazinzile, ethukuthele noma ekhathazekile. Yeka uma eyeka ukuthanda. Ukumisa kusenesikhathi kuhle. Ungabuyela ngolunye usuku, noma ushintshele engxoxweni noma ohambweni.
