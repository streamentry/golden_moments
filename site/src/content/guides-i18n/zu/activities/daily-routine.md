Uhlelo olunomusa lunikeza usuku isimo esijwayelekile: ukuvuka, ukudla, umsebenzi omncane, ukuphumula, nokulala emahoreni acishe afane. Lunciphisa izinqumo nokumangala, futhi lunikeza uhlelo emizuzwini lapho ngabe nizibuza khona ukuthi yini elandelayo. Uhlelo olungezansi luyisibonelo, hhayi umthetho. Lusebenziseni njengendawo yokuqala, bese nilubumba ngokuya ngomuntu eniyamnakekelayo.

## Kungani uhlelo lwansuku zonke lusiza?

Uhlelo olunzinzile luyasiza ngoba izindlela ezijwayelekile ziyalandeleka kalula kunezintsha. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) ithi usuku olusahlelwe lusho isikhathi esincane sokuthatha izinqumo ngokuthi yini okufanele yenziwe nesikhathi esiningi semisebenzi ejabulisayo, futhi ithi amahora avamile okuvuka nokulala angasiza ngezinkinga zokulala nokungaphumuli kwasebusuku. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) iphakamisa ukugeza, ukugqoka nokudla emahoreni afanayo nsuku zonke njengengxenye yekhaya elithulile, futhi i-[Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) nayo incoma ukugcina izinhlelo ezifanayo.

## Usuku oluyisibonelo olunomusa

Amahora ayizibonelo kuphela. Shintshani usuku lonke lube ngaphambili noma emuva ukuze lufane nesigqi somuntu uqobo lwakhe.

| Isikhathi | Okwenzekayo | Imibono | Ezinsukwini ezinzima |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8:00 ekuseni | Ukuvuka, ukuzigeza, ukugqoka | Ngohlelo olufanayo nsuku zonke; beka izingubo ngokulandelana zigqokwa ngakho | Gcinani ukunakekelwa kulula; ihembe elihlanzekile lanele |
| 8:45 ekuseni | Isidlo sasekuseni | Ikhofi nephephandaba efundwa ngokuzwakalayo, noma umsakazo | Yidlani ndawonye ngokuthula, kungekho cindezi yokukhuluma |
| 9:30 ekuseni | Umsebenzi omkhulu | Ukugoqa izingubo ezigeziwe, ukubhaka, ukulima insimu, uhambo olufushane noma ukuhamba | Inguqulo emfushane, noma mvumeleni abuke nenza |
| 10:30 ekuseni | Isikhathi esithulile | Umculo, i-albhamu yezithombe, noma ukuhlala eceleni kwefasitela | Phumulani esihlalweni asithandayo |
| 11:00 ekuseni | Usizo ekulungiseleleni ukudla kwasemini | Ukuwasha imifino, ukumisa itafula | Nina niyapheka, yena ahlale nani |
| 12:00 emini | Ukudla kwasemini nokuhlanza | Ukusula itafula, ukusula izitsha ezimbalwa | Yeqani imisebenzi yokuhlanza |
| 1:00 ntambama | Ukuphumula okufushane | Imilenze iphezulu, umculo othambile | Kuyafana |
| 2:00 ntambama | Ngaphandle noma ukunyakaza | Ukuhamba, isikhathi evulandeni, ukunisela izitshalo, ukuphonsa ibhaluni | Hlalani eceleni kwefasitela elinelanga |
| 3:00 ntambama | Ukuhlangana nabantu noma ukwenza izinto | Ukuvakasha, ucingo, [amapheya ezithombe](/games/picture-pairs/), ukufaka imibala, amakhadi | Into yokuphuza nokudla okuncane ndawonye |
| 4:30 ntambama | Isikhathi sokuzola | Amakhethini ayavalwa, amalambu ayakhanyiswa, umsebenzi othulile onjengokugoqa amalaphu okugeza | Umculo owaziwayo, umsindo ophansi |

Lesi sibonelo silandela isimo sohlelo oluyisibonelo lwe-Alzheimer's Association uqobo lwayo lwesigaba sokuqala nesiphakathi, oluhamba lusuka ekunakekelweni komzimba nasesidlweni sasekuseni lubheke emisebenzini yasendlini, ukuhamba, umculo, ukuphumula nokuvakasha, kuze kube sesidlweni sakusihlwa, ukukhumbula izinto zakudala nokuzilungiselela ukulala.

## Nakha kanjani uhlelo lwenu?

Qalani ngendlela umuntu ahlale ephila ngayo, hhayi ngetshathi. I-Alzheimer's Association iphakamisa ukucabanga ngalezi zinto ngaphambi kokuhlela:

- **Lokho akuthandayo nakungathandi, amandla akhe nezinto ezimthakazelisayo.**
- **Ukuthi wayechitha kanjani usuku lwakhe.** Umuntu ovuka ekuseni owayehlala ehamba nenja kufanele aqhubeke ehamba ekuseni. Umuntu othanda ubusuku angase angalithandi ukuqala ngo-8 ekuseni.
- **Isikhathi sakhe sosuku esingcono kakhulu.** Yibekeni khona ukugeza, uhambo nomsebenzi omkhulu.
- **Isikhathi esanele** sokudla, ukuzigeza nokugqoka, ukuze kungabikho lutho olwenziwa ngokushesha.
- **Amahora avamile okuvuka nokulala.**

Umthombo ofanayo uphakamisa ukufaka inhlanganisela yemisebenzi yasendlini, ukudla, ukunakekelwa komzimba, imisebenzi yokudala, ukunyakaza komzimba, isikhathi nabanye abantu, imisebenzi yenkolo nezikhathi ezingahleliwe, njengomngane omane afike.

### Zamani isonto elilodwa, bese nishintsha

Gcinani inothi elilula nsuku zonke. I-Alzheimer's Association iphakamisa ukubuza ukuthi yimiphi imisebenzi esebenze kangcono nokuthi kungani, ingabe kwakukhona izikhathi lapho kwakwenziwa izinto eziningi noma kunento encane yokwenza, nokuthi izinhlelo ezingahleliwe bezijabulisa yini noma bezibangela ukukhathazeka. Gcinani lokho okusebenzayo bese nishintsha lokho okungasebenzi.

### Yenzani lubonakale

Uma umuntu esakwazi ukufunda, ibhodi elimhlophe noma ikhadi elinokuphrinta okukhulu elinohlelo losuku lungaqinisekisa. Lifushaneni: “Isidlo sasekuseni. Uhambo noSarah. Isidlo sasemini. Ukuphumula.” Abanye abantu bahamba kangcono bengazi kusenesikhathi eside; i-Family Caregiver Alliance iphakamisa ukungakhulumi ngezinhlelo kuze kube seduze nokuba zenzeke uma kubangela ukukhathazeka.

## Indlela yokuvumelanisa uhlelo

### Shiyani indawo yokuphefumula

Ningagcwalisi umzuzu wonke. I-Alzheimer's Association igcizelela ukuthi umuntu udinga ukulinganisa umsebenzi nokuphumula futhi angase adinge ukuphumula kaningi. Uma ebonakala ediniwe, ephazamiseka noma ecasuka, shintshani imisebenzi noma niphumule.

### Vikelani intambama ephuzile

Abantu abaningi baya ngokungaphumuli kakhulu njengoba ukukhanya kwemini kuncipha. I-NIA iphakamisa isikhathi ngaphandle noma eceleni kwefasitela ukuze kutholakale ilanga nsuku zonke, ukunyakaza nsuku zonke ngaphandle kokuhlela okuningi, ukugwema ikhafini notshwala ngasekupheleni kosuku, nokunikeza ugqozi lokungalali isikhathi eside ntambama. Eminye imindeni ibona ukuthi kuyasiza ukuvala amakhethini nokukhanyisa amalambu ngaphambi kokuhlwa. Bheka [ukungaphumuli ekupheleni kosuku](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) kanye nesihloko esithi [ukulala](/brain-health/sleep/).

### Shintshani njengoba izidingo zishintsha

- **Isigaba sokuqala:** hlelani uhlelo ndawonye. Umuntu angase afune uhlelo olubhaliwe analuphatha yedwa.
- **Isigaba esiphakathi:** nina niholela kakhulu. Imisebenzi emifushane, ukuphumula okwengeziwe nemisebenzi eminingi ejwayelekile; bheka [imisebenzi yesigaba esiphakathi](/activities/middle-stage/).
- **Isigaba sokugcina:** uhlelo lugxile enduduzweni, ekudleni nasekunakekelweni, kunezikhathi ezimfushane zemizwa phakathi njengomculo noma ukuphulula izandla; bheka [imisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/).

### Shintshani imisebenzi, hhayi uhlaka

Gcinani ukudla, ukuphumula nokulala kuzinzile, bese nishintshanisa imisebenzi phakathi. I-[NIA](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) iphakamisa ukuzama imisebenzi ehlukene ezinsukwini ezihlukene ukugcina izinto zithakazelisa. Ningabhaka ngoMsombuluko, nilime insimu ngoLwesibili, nibuke izithombe ngoLwesithathu. Imibono yethu eyi-[101 yemisebenzi](/activities/ideas/) kanye [nemisebenzi yasendlini](/activities/household-tasks/) ingagcwalisa izikhala.

## Uma usuku lungahambi kahle

Ezinye izinsuku aziyikulandela uhlelo olunjalo. Ukugula, ubusuku obubi, ukuvakasha kukadokotela noma ushintsho lwesimo sezulu kungaphazamisa konke. Yekani uhlelo, gcinani izidingo eziyisisekelo (ukudla, ukuphuza, ukuphumula, induduzo) futhi nizame futhi kusasa. Uma umuntu enqaba okuthile, bheka [uma esho ukuthi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Ukuma kancane niphefumula kahle ndawonye, njengomdlalo othi [Ukuphefumula okuthulile](/games/calm-breathing/), kungasiza nobabili nibuye nizole.

Uhlelo lwenu uqobo luyabalulekile futhi. Faka izikhathi zokuphumula zenu, futhi nicele umndeni noma isevisi yokuphumuza abanakekeli ukuthi ithathe indawo ngezikhathi ezijwayelekile zokuphumula. Bheka [ukuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
