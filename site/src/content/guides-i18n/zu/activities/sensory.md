Imisebenzi yezinzwa isebenzisa imizwa emihlanu: ukuthinta, ukunuka, ukuzwa ukunambitha, ukuzwa izwi nokubona. Ayidingi inkumbulo, izinyathelo noma izimpendulo ezifanele, ngakho ingasebenza kunoma yisiphi isigaba. Ibaluleke kakhulu kamuva. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) iphawula ukuthi esigabeni sakamuva umhlaba uzwakalwa kakhulu ngezinzwa, nokuthi ungabonisa ukunakekela ngokuthinta, izwi, ukubona, ukunambitha nokunuka. Ukuhlola okumbalwa kokuphepha kwenza le misebenzi ibe nokuphumula kubo bonke.

## Yini imisebenzi yezinzwa yokuphila nokuwohloka kwengqondo?

Imisebenzi yezinzwa yinto elula eyakhiwe ngezwa elilodwa ngesikhathi, njengokuzwa indwangu, ukunuka amakhambi noma ukulalela imvula. I-[NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ithi imisebenzi yesigaba sakamuva ivame ukudinga ukugxila kwezinzwa, futhi ikhomba umculo, izinto zokuthinta nokuphatha, nokudambisa isandla. I-[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) iphawula ukuthi abantu abasesigabeni sakamuva bangase babe nesidingo esiqinile sokubamba into eduze ukuze bazizwe bekhululekile.

## Imibono yezinzwa, umzwa ngomuzwa

### Ukuthinta

- **Ukudambisa isandla.** Fudumeza ifutha elincane lokugcoba ezandleni zakho bese usula kancane kusukela esihlakaleni kuye emunweni. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) ikhomba ukusula ngefutha elingenaphunga ukuze kube nokududuza.
- **Inqwaba yezindwangu.** Izikwele ezinkulu ze-velvet, i-corduroy, i-fleece, i-satin newuol ukuze kuthintwe, kugoqwe futhi kuxoxwe ngakho.
- **Ithawula elifudumele.** Ithawula elifudumele, eliswakeme lezandla ngosuku olubandayo. Hlola izinga lokushisa esihlakaleni sakho kuqala.
- **I-apron yokusebenza noma ingubo yamathanga.** Okunye ukuqoshama okukhulu, oziphu, amaribhoni nezingxenyana zendawo ezinemibala ezithungwa ngokuqinile.
- **Ukugoqa nokusula.** Inqwaba yamathawula okugeza ahlanzekile noma ama-napkin okugoqa, ukubeka phezulu nokugoqa futhi.
- **Into ethambile yokubamba.** Ingubo ye-fleece, umcamelo noma isilwane esithambile esibonakala singokoqobo, esinikezwa nje, hhayi njengethoyizi.

### Ukunuka

- **Amakhambi amasha.** Hlikihla amaqabunga e-mint, i-rosemary, i-basil noma i-lavender phakathi kweminwe yakho bese uwadlulisela.
- **Amanukhu ekhishini.** Ikhofi elibilayo, isinkwa esigazayo, i-sinamoni noma iwolintshi eligayiwe eduze.
- **Izimbali.** Letha izimbali ezimbalwa ezintsha ezivela engadini noma esitolo.
- **Umkhiqizo ojwayelekile.** Insipho noma ifutha abalihlala belisebenzisa, uma isikhumba sabo siyakuthwala.

### Ukuzwa ukunambitha

- **I-flavour abayithandayo.** Isipuni esincane sento abayithandayo, njenge-apula sauce, i-pudding noma i-ayisikhilimu, ngendlela engaphephile ukuyisebenzisa.
- **Usizo ekubhakeni.** Ukunambitha izithelo noma ifrosting encane ngesikhathi nibhaka ndawonye.
- **Amaswidi esizini.** Ikhabe ehlobo, i-cider efudumele ekwindla, esinikezwe ngezinga lokushisa eliphephile.

### Ukuzwa izwi

- **Umculo owuthandayo.** Izingoma ezijwayelekile ngevolumu ethambile; bheka [imisebenzi yomculo](/activities/music/).
- **Imisindo yemvelo.** Imvula, amagagasi noma izwi lezinyoni. I-NIA iphawula ukuthi umculo, umsindo omhlophe nemisindo yemvelo kubonakala kudambisa abanye abantu.
- **Izwi lakho.** Ukufunda kakhulu, ukuhuba, noma ukuxoxa indaba.
- **Izinsimbi ezilula.** Isigubhu sesandla, isivunguvungu noma insimbi yokudlala kanye nomculo.

### Ukubona

- **Ukubuka efasiteleni.** Isiphakeli sezinyoni ngaphandle kwefasitela, izingane ezibuyela ekhaya, noma iqhwa eliwela.
- **Ithangi lezinhlanzi noma ividiyo ehamba kancane.** Ukuhamba okuthambile ngaphandle komsindo ophakeme.
- **Izithombe ezinkulu.** Isithombe esisodwa esicacile ngesikhathi, sibanjwe ezingeni lamehlo.
- **Umbala nendebe.** Ukuhlunga iziqeshana zendwangu ezikhanyayo, izimbali noma ama-napkin anombala, noma ikhasi lokupenda elilula elisuka kusethi yethu ye-[coloring](/games/coloring/).
- **Ngaphandle.** I-NHS iphakamisa izingadi zezinzwa ezinamakhambi anamakha, izindawo zokuthinta nezingxenye zamanzi. Bheka [imisebenzi yangaphandle nokulima](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Ungenza kanjani ibhokisi lezinzwa

1. **Khetha itimu** elihlobene nempilo yomuntu: ulwandle, ingadi, ikhishi, indawo yokusebenza, ukuthunga.
2. **Khetha izinto ezinkulu neziphephile ezinhlanu noma eziyisithupha,** njengengqimba enkulu yasolwandle, isipuni sokhuni, isikhafu esithambile, isikhwama se-lavender esomileyo esithungwe sivaliwe, noma ibhulokhi yokhuni ebushelelezi.
3. **Hlola yonke into** ukuze ubone izingxenye ezincane, amakhona abukhali, imicu exegayo nanoma yini egqwala kalula.
4. **Khipha into eyodwa ngesikhathi.** Yidlulisele, ichaze, bese uyivumela umuntu ayihlole.
5. **Beka ibhokisi emuva ngemva kokusebenzisa** ukuze lihlale likhethekile futhi izinto zihlolwe futhi ngaphambi kwesikhathi esilandelayo.

## Ukuphepha kuqala

Imisebenzi yezinzwa ithambile, kodwa ezinye izingozi zibalulekile. Abanye abantu abaphila nokuwohloka kwengqondo badida izinto ezingezona ukudla nokudla, futhi ukugwinya kungase kube nzima ngokuhamba kwesikhathi; i-NIA ixwayisa ngokuthi izinkinga zokugwinya zesigaba sakamuva zandisa ingozi yokuminza.

| Ingozi | Okufanele ukwenze |
| --- | --- |
| Ukuminza | Sebenzisa izinto ezinkulu kakhulu ukuthi zingaginywa. Akukho ukuqoshama, amabhidi, izinhlamvu zemali noma amathoyizi amancane akhululekile ebhokisini. Nikeza ukudla kuphela ezindleleni zokuthungwa ithimba lokunakekela elithi kuphephile. |
| Izinto ezibukeka njengokudla | I-NIA iyeluleka ukuthi kuvalwe amafutha okugcoba, insipho, ama-shampoo nephunga, okungabukeka noma kunuke njengokudla, nokususwa izithelo zokwenziwa. Beka imikhiqizo yezinzwa kude ngemva kokusebenzisa. |
| Ukungezwani komzimba | Hlola ukungezwani komzimba namantongomane, i-latex, uwolol, izitshalo noma amaphunga ngaphambi kokuthi uqale. |
| Amaphunga anamandla | Gwema amaphunga anamandla kanye namafutha anamakha anamandla, ikakhulukazi uma kune-asthma noma ezinye izinkinga zokuphefumula. Amaphunga emvelo athambile aphephe kakhulu. |
| Isikhumba | Hlola isikhumba esibuhlungu, esiphukile noma esibuthakathaka ngaphambi kokudambisa. Sebenzisa ingcindezi ethambile bese uyayeka uma bekhalaza. |
| Izinga lokushisa | Hlola amathawula afudumele neziphuzo esihlakaleni sakho kuqala. |
| Izinto ezibukhali noma ezinobuthi | Akukho mathuluzi abukhali, ingilazi noma izinto zobuciko ezinobuthi. |

## Ingabe iziphuzo ezinamakha ziyasiza?

Mhlawumbe cha ngendlela engalungiswa. Ukubuyekezwa kwe-[Cochrane kweziphuzo ezinamakha zokuphila nokuwohloka kwengqondo](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) kubuke izifundo eziyi-13 ezinamuntu abangama-708, konke ezindlini zokunakekela, futhi kwathola ukuthi abukho ubufakazi obuqand’ ikhanda bokuthi kuyasiza. Izifundo zazincane, imiphumela ayizange ivumelane, futhi imiphumela emibi ayizange ibikwe kahle. Uma umuntu ethanda iphunga elithambile, elijwayelekile, njenge-lavender evela engadini, leso sizathu esihle sokwabelana. Akusona usizo lokwelapha.

## Unganikela kanjani ngomsebenzi wezinzwa

- **Khetha isikhathi esithule** bese uvala i-TV.
- **Sondela ukusuka phambili,** usho igama lakhe, bese uyacela noma umtshele ngaphambi kokuthinta: “Nginefutha lokugcoba. Ungathanda ukususwa kwesandla?”
- **Nikeza into eyodwa ngesikhathi** ukuze ingakudingi kakhulu.
- **Bheka impendulo.** Ubuso obuthambile, izandla eziphumule noma ukumamatheka izimpawu ezinhle. Ukuhlehla, ukubabaza noma ukungakhululeki kusho ukuthi uyayeka.
- **Igcine imfushane.** Iminzuzu emihlanu ethule yisikhathi esihle sokuhlangana.

Ukuphumula kokuphefumula ndawonye, njenge-[calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/), kungaba indlela ethambile yokuphetha. Ngemibono eminingi yaseceleni kwecansi, bheka [imisebenzi yesigaba sakamuva](/activities/late-stage/), futhi ngokungakhululeki kwakusihlwa, bheka [ukungakhululeki kwasekuseni](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
