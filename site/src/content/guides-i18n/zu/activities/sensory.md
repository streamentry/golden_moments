Imisebenzi yezinzwa isebenzisa izinzwa ezinhlanu: ukuthinta, iphunga, ukunambitha, umsindo nokubona. Ayidingi ukukhumbula, ayidingi izinyathelo futhi ayidingi mpendulo ezifanele, ngakho ingasebenza kunoma yisiphi isigaba. Ibaluleke kakhulu kamuva. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) ithi esigabeni sokugcina umhlaba uzwakala kakhulu ngezinzwa, nokuthi ningabonisa ukunakekela ngokuthinta, umsindo, ukubona, ukunambitha nephunga. Ukuhlola okumbalwa kokuphepha kwenza le misebenzi iphumuzise wonke umuntu.

## Yini imisebenzi yezinzwa ye-dementia?

Imisebenzi yezinzwa yilokho okulula okuhlangenwayo okugxile enzweni eyodwa ngesikhathi, njengokuthinta indwangu, ukuhogela amakhambi noma ukulalela imvula. I-[NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ithi imisebenzi yesigaba sangemuva ivame ukudinga ukugxila ezinzweni, futhi ikhuluma ngomculo, izinto zokuthinta nokubamba, nokuhlikihla izandla. I-[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ithi abantu abasesigabeni sokugcina bangase babe nesidingo esiqinile sokubamba okuthile eduze ukuze baduduzeke.

## Imibono yezinzwa, inzwa ngenzwa

### Ukuthinta

- **Ukuhlikihla izandla.** Fudumezani ukhilimu omncane ezandleni zenu bese nihlikihla kancane kusuka esihlakaleni kuya eminweni. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) ikhuluma ngokuhlikihla ngokhilimu ongenaphunga ukuze kududuze.
- **Ibhasikidi lezindwangu.** Izikwele ezinkulu ze-velvet, i-corduroy, i-fleece, i-satin nouboya ukuze zithintwe, zisongwe futhi kukhulunywe ngazo.
- **Indwangu efudumele.** Indwangu emanzi, efudumele yezandla ngosuku olubandayo. Hlolani izinga lokushisa engalweni yenu kuqala.
- **I-apron yemisebenzi noma ingubo yamadolo.** Izinkinobho ezinkulu, ama-zipper, amaribhoni nezingcezu zendwangu ezithintekayo ezithungwe ngokuqinile.
- **Ukusonga nokubeka phezu komunye.** Ibhasikidi lezindwangu ezihlanzekile zokugeza noma amanapkin ukuze zisongwe, zibekwe phezu komunye futhi zisongwe kabusha.
- **Okuthile okuthambile okubambwayo.** Ingubo ye-fleece, umcamelo noma isilwane esithambile esibukeka sengathi siphila, sinikezwa ngokulula, hhayi njengethoyizi.

### Iphunga

- **Amakhambi amasha.** Hlikihlani amaqabunga e-mint, i-rosemary, i-basil noma i-lavender phakathi kweminwe bese niwadlulisa.
- **Amaphunga asekhishini.** Ikhofi elibilayo, isinkwa esithosiwe, isinamoni noma iwolintshi elisuswa igobolondo eduze.
- **Izimbali.** Letha izimbali ezimbalwa ezintsha engadini noma esitolo sokudla.
- **Umkhiqizo owujwayele.** Isepha noma ukhilimu ayehlala ewusebenzisa, uma isikhumba sakhe siwumukela.

### Ukunambitha

- **Ukunambitheka akuthandayo.** Isipuni esincane senye into ayithandayo, njenge-applesauce, i-pudding noma i-ayisikhilimu, ngendlela angayiphatha ngokuphepha.
- **Usizo lokubhaka.** Ukunambitha isithelo noma i-icing encane yekhekhe ngenkathi nibhaka ndawonye.
- **Okumnandi kwesizini.** Ikhabe ehlobo nesiphuzo esifudumele ebusika, kuphakelwa ekushiseni okuphephile.

### Umsindo

- **Umculo oyintandokazi.** Izingoma ezijwayelekile ngomsindo othambile; bhekani [imisebenzi yomculo](/activities/music/).
- **Imisindo yemvelo.** Imvula, amagagasi noma ingoma yezinyoni. I-NIA ithi umculo, i-white noise nemisindo yemvelo kubonakala kuphumuzisa abanye abantu.
- **Izwi lenu.** Ukufunda ngokuzwakalayo, ukuvunguza, noma ukuxoxa indaba.
- **Izinsimbi ezilula.** Ingoma yesandla, i-shaker noma insimbi encane ukuze kudlalwe ndawonye nomculo.

### Ukubona

- **Ukubuka efasiteleni.** Isitsha sokudla kwezinyoni ngaphandle kwefasitela, izingane ezibuyela ekhaya, noma iqhwa liwa.
- **Ithangi lezinhlanzi noma ividiyo ehamba kancane.** Ukunyakaza okuthambile ngaphandle komsindo ophakeme.
- **Izithombe ezinkulu.** Isithombe esisodwa esicacile ngesikhathi, sibanjwe ezingeni lamehlo.
- **Umbala nephethini.** Ukuhlunga izingcezu zendwangu ezikhanyayo, izimbali noma amanapkin anemibala, noma ikhasi elilula lokufaka imibala evela eqoqweni lethu [lokufaka imibala](/games/coloring/).
- **Ngaphandle.** I-NHS iphakamisa izingadi zezinzwa ezinezitshalo ezinephunga, izinto ezithintekayo nemithombo yamanzi. Bhekani [imisebenzi yangaphandle neyokulima ingadi](/activities/outdoor-and-gardening/).

## Indlela yokwenza ibhokisi lezinzwa

1. **Khethani isihloko** esihlobene nempilo yomuntu: ibhishi, ingadi, ikhishi, indawo yokusebenzela, ukuthunga.
2. **Khethani izinto ezinkulu, eziphephile ezinhlanu noma eziyisithupha,** njengegobolondo elikhulu lolwandle, isipuni sokhuni, isikhafu esithambile, isikhwama esincane se-lavender eyomisiwe esithungwe sivaleke, noma ibhulokhi lokhuni elibushelelezi.
3. **Hlolani into ngayinye** ukuthi ayinazo izingxenye ezincane, imiphetho ebukhali, izintambo ezikhululekile noma okuthile okuphukayo.
4. **Khiphani into eyodwa ngesikhathi.** Yinikezeni, niyichaze, bese nivumela umuntu ukuthi ayihlole.
5. **Bekani ibhokisi eceleni ngemva kwalokho** ukuze lihlale likhethekile futhi izinto zihlolwe kabusha ngaphambi kwesikhathi esilandelayo.

## Ukuphepha kuza kuqala

Imisebenzi yezinzwa ithambile, kodwa izingozi ezimbalwa ziyabalulekile. Abanye abantu abaphila ne-dementia bathatha izinto ezingezona ukudla njengokudla, futhi ukugwinya kungaba nzima ngokuhamba kwesikhathi; i-NIA ixwayisa ukuthi izinkinga zokugwinya esigabeni sokugcina ziphakamisa ingozi yokuvaleka kwendlela yokuphefumula (choking).

| Ingozi | Yini okufanele ukwenze |
| --- | --- |
| Ukuvaleka kwendlela yokuphefumula (choking) | Sebenzisani kuphela izinto ezinkulu kakhulu ukuba zingagwinywa. Akukho zinkinobho, ubuhlalu, izinhlamvu zemali, ama-marble noma amathoyizi amancane akhululekile ebhokisini. Nikezani ukudla kuphela ngendlela ethi ithimba lokunakekela iyaphephile. |
| Izinto ezibukeka njengokudla | I-NIA iluleka ukukhiya ukhilimu, amasepha, ushampu namaphafyumu, angabukeka noma abe nephunga elifana nokudla, futhi nisuse izithelo zokwenziwa. Beka imikhiqizo yezinzwa eceleni ngemva kokuyisebenzisa. |
| Ama-allergy | Hlolani ukuthi akukho ama-allergy kumantongomane, i-latex, uboya, izitshalo noma amaphunga ngaphambi kokuqala. |
| Amaphunga aqinile | Gwemani amaphafyumu aqinile namafutha abalulekile, ikakhulukazi ngesifuba somoya noma ezinye izinkinga zokuphefumula. Amaphunga athambile, emvelo aphephile kakhulu. |
| Isikhumba | Hlolani isikhumba esibuhlungu, esiphukile noma esibuthakathaka ngaphambi kokuhlikihla. Sebenzisani ingcindezi ethambile futhi niyeke uma ebonisa ubuhlungu ebusweni. |
| Izinga lokushisa | Hlolani izindwangu neziphuzo ezifudumele engalweni yenu kuqala. |
| Izinto ezibukhali noma ezinobuthi | Akunamathuluzi abukhali, ingilazi noma izinto zobuciko ezinobuthi. |

## Ingabe i-aromatherapy iyasiza?

Cishe cha, ngendlela engalinganiswa. [Ukubuyekezwa kwe-Cochrane kwe-aromatherapy ye-dementia](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) kwabheka ucwaningo olu-13 olunabantu abangu-708, bonke emakhaya okunakekela, futhi akutholanga siqinisekiso esinamandla sosizo. Ucwaningo lwalungaba lincane, imiphumela ayivumelani, futhi imiphumela emibi yabikwa kabi. Uma umuntu ejabulela iphunga elithambile, elijwayelekile, njenge-lavender evela engadini, lokho kuyisizathu esihle sokwabelana ngalo. Akuyona indlela yokwelapha.

## Indlela yokunikeza umsebenzi wezinzwa

- **Khethani isikhathi esithulile** futhi nivale i-TV.
- **Sondelani ngaphambili,** nibize igama lakhe, futhi nibuze noma nitshele ngaphambi kokuthinta: “Nginokhilimu. Ningathanda ukuhlikihlwa izandla?”
- **Nikezani into eyodwa ngesikhathi** ukuze ingamthwesi umthwalo omkhulu.
- **Qapheleni impendulo.** Ubuso obuthambile, izandla ezikhululekile noma ukumamatheka kuyizimpawu ezinhle. Ukuhoxa, ukufinyela ubuso noma ukungaphumuli kusho ukuthi niyeke.
- **Yenzani kube kufushane.** Imizuzu engu-5 ethulile iyisikhathi esihle.

Ukuthola imibono eminingi yasembhedeni, bhekani [imisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/), futhi ngokungaphumuli kokuhlwa, bhekani [ukungaphumuli ntambama](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
