I-bingo ngumdlalo ojwayelekile futhi ohlanganisa abantu abadala abaningi, kodwa izinombolo zingaba nzima ukuzilandela umuntu ophila ne-dementia. Ibingo yezithombe igcina injwayelo efanayo enobumnene, bese ishintsha izinombolo ibe yizithombe ezinkulu nezicacile. Isebenza ekhaya, esikhathini sosuku emkhakheni wokunakekela, noma endlini yokunakekela. Leli khasi lichaza indlela yokudlala, ukuyilungisa, nokugcina ukugxila ekujabuleni.

## Indlela ibingo yezithombe esebenza ngayo

Umdlali ngamunye unekhadi elinegridi yezithombe, njengetiye, ikati, uwoyi, isikebhe noma ibhayisikili. Umbizi ubonisa noma asho isithombe esisodwa ngesikhathi. Abadlali bamaka isithombe esifanayo ekhadini labo. Umdlali ophelele umugqa ogcwele, noma isethi eqenjini esiyivumelanile, angakhala ngokuthi “Bingo,” kodwa amaqembu amaningi adlula ukunqoba ngokuphelele.

Ungasebenzisa amakhadi aphrintiwe ku-[imisebenzi yamahhala yokuphrinta](/activities/printable/), noma [umdlalo webingo yezithombe](/games/picture-bingo/), onombizi osesikrinini wamaqembu. Zombili zisebenza kahle uma kukhishwa ithebhulethi ukuze iqembu lonke libone.

## Ukulungisa iseshini

- **Khetha iqembu elincane.** Abantu abangaba bane kuya kubayisishiyagalombili yisilinganiso esihle. Amaqembu amakhulu anzima ukuwalandela futhi angazizwa esheshisiwe.
- **Yenza izithombe zibe zikhulu.** Sebenzisa imigqa eqinile nento eyodwa ejwayelekile kwisikwele ngasinye. Gwema amakhadi anesiphithiphithi.
- **Hlala abantu ngendlela efanele.** Beka ngamunye lapho embona khona umbizi nekhadi lakhe, eduze komsizi uma adinga omunye.
- **Sebenzisa izimaki ezilula.** Ama-chip amakhulu, izinhlamvu zemali noma ukuthepha ngomunwe kusebenza kangcono kunezikhwama. Gcina ezinye ezimbalwa ezengeziwe.
- **Hlela iseshini emfushane.** Imizuzu engamashumi amathathu ivame ukwanele. Yeka kusenesikhathi uma amandla ehla.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) yeluleka ukulungisa imisebenzi ngokulingana nalokho umuntu asengakwenza, ngakho yenza amakhadi nemithetho ibe lula uma lokho kusiza.

## Indlela yokubiza izithombe

1. **Phakamisa isithombe** noma khomba kusikrini ngaphambi kokukhuluma.
2. **Sho igama ngokucacile,** ngamagama alula: “Itiye.” Phinda kanye, kancane.
3. **Linda.** Nikeza abantu isikhathi sokubheka nokumaka. Ungabizi isithombe esilandelayo kuze kube yilapho abaningi bethole ithuba.
4. **Chaza, uma kusiza.** “Imbali ebomvu” noma “leyo esasinayo ngesikhathi sepikiniki” ingavusa ingxoxo.
5. **Gcina isivinini sincane.** Umbizi ophola kancane ubalulekile kunowaseshisayo.

## Ukwenza ukuwina kube yinto ekhethwayo

Ukuncintisana kungaba inkinga. Umuntu ophelelwa angazizwa evivinywa, nomuntu okhathazekile ngokuwina angaba nengcindezi. Lezi zinye izindlela:

- **Wonke umuntu uthola isithombe.** Umdlali ngamunye ubiza isithombe akithandayo, bese iqembu liyasibheka.
- **Yabelana ngekhadi eligcwele.** Cela abadlali bakhulume ngesithombe esisodwa ekhadini labo.
- **Dlala kuze kuphele izithombe.** Biza zonke izithombe ekhadini, bese uqala elinye, ngaphandle kokuthi umuntu wokuqala abe ngumnqobi.
- **Gcina umklomelo mncane noma ungawusebenzisi.** Ikhukhi noma iculo kusebenza kangcono kunomklomelo.

## Ukulungisa kubantu basezigabeni zokugcina

Kubantu abathola kunzima ukuthola isithombe, sebenzisa ikhadi elinezikwele ezine noma eziyisithupha kuphela, noma nikeza umdlali ngamunye ikhadi elilodwa ngesikhathi. Vumela umsizi abambe ikhadi ngenkathi umuntu ekhomba, noma unyakazise i-chip ngemvume yakhe. Abanye bathola kulula ukulalela umbizi bajabulele umsindo wamagama.

Qaphela izimpawu zokungakhululeki, njengokuhudula noma ukuqabula ibhulo, bese umisa uma zivela. Ukuze uthole imibono eminingi yemisebenzi ehlala phansi enokuthula, bheka [imisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/).

## Ibingo ezindlini zokunakekela nasezinhlelweni zosuku

Imisebenzi yeqembu isebenza kangcono lapho ihlelelwe abantu abasegumbini. Umhlahlandlela wethu [wemisebenzi yeqembu yezindlu zokunakekela nezinhlelo zosuku](/activities/care-home-groups/) uchaza ukuhlela iseshini, ukuhlala kwabantu, nokubhekana nabantu abafuna ukuhamba. Ibingo yezithombe iphinde ihambisane kahle nesikhathi sokuhlabelela noma indaba, kanye [nokuphetha imisho](/activities/finish-the-saying/) njengomdlalo othule kakhulu ekupheleni kweseshini.

## Yini ibingo engayenza nengayenzi

Ibingo yezithombe iyisenzo sokuxhumana futhi ijabulisa. Ingaletha abantu ndawonye, inike isizathu sokubheka izithombe nokulalela, futhi yenze iqembu lizizwe lifudumele. Ayelaphi i-dementia noma inciphise indlela eqhubeka ngayo. Lalela ukuthi abantu bebukeka futhi bezizwa kanjani phakathi nehora, hhayi ukuthi ubani owinayo.
