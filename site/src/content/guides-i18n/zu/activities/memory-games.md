Uma nisesha “imidlalo yenkumbulo yabadala”, okuningi enikuthola kwenzelwe abantu abafuna ukuzivivinya ngokwengqondo: amakhadi afihliwe, amawashi okubala emuva, amazinga namaphuzu. Kumuntu ophila ne-dementia, lezo zici zishintsha umdlalo ube ukuhlolwa kwaleyo nto esekuyinto enzima. Imidlalo efanayo ingadlalwa ngendlela ethambile. Leli khasi libonisa ukuthi kanjani, futhi libala imidlalo esebenza kahle esigabeni ngasinye.

## Kungani imidlalo yenkumbulo yakudala ikhathaza abantu abane-dementia?

Ngoba incike ekugcineni ulwazi olusha ekhanda, okuvame ukuba yinto yokuqala i-Alzheimer's, imbangela evame kakhulu ye-dementia, ethinta yona. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) ithi uphawu lokuqala oluvame kakhulu lwe-Alzheimer's ubunzima bokukhumbula ulwazi olusanda kufundwa. Ku-Concentration yakudala, niphendula amakhadi amabili, nikhumbule lapho ayekhona, bese niyisebenzisa ngemva komzuzu. Lolo ulwazi olusha, olugcinwayo bese lukhunjulwa.

Uma nengeza isibali sikhathi, amaphuzu noma i-buzzer yezimpendulo ezingafanele, umuntu ukhunjuzwa lokho angasakwazi ukukwenza, phambi komuntu amthandayo. Abantu abaningi bayeka kancane kancane ukufuna ukudlala.

Ukubona into ebonakalayo kuyinto ehlukile futhi ngokuvamile elula. Ukukhomba inkomishi yetiye yesibili uma zombili zibonakala kuyajabulisa. Ngakho iqhinga ukugcina ukufanisa, kushiywe ukuhlolwa kwenkumbulo.

## Wenza kanjani umdlalo wenkumbulo ube mnene?

Lezi zinguquko zisebenza emidlalweni yamakhadi, emidlalweni yebhodi neyesikrini.

1. **Shiyani izithombe zibonakala.** Ukufanisa izithombe obona zona kusengumdlalo wangempela, futhi kuvame ukwanelisa.
2. **Sebenzisani izingcezu ezimbalwa.** Qalani ngamapheya amabili noma amathathu. Engezani eminye kuphela uma kuzwakala kulula kakhulu futhi efuna okunye.
3. **Khethani izithombe ezinkulu, ezijwayelekile, zabadala.** Inkomishi yetiye, imbali yelanga, isikebhe. Hhayi amakhathuni, noma izindawo eziminyene ngezinto.
4. **Dlalani njengeqembu.** “Ake sithole izinja” kunokuthi “Kuyithuba lakho.” Shintshanani ngokukhomba nokusiza.
5. **Shiyani amaphuzu.** Akekho odinga ukunqoba. Uma othile ebuza, “Siyenzile” kwanele.
6. **Ningaqhudelani nesikhathi.** Akukho zibali sikhathi, futhi ningashesheli ngenkathi nishintshana.
7. **Khulumani ngezithombe.** “Bhekani lesi sikebhe. Nake nahamba ngesikebhe yini?” Ingxoxo ivame ukuba yingxenye engcono kakhulu.
8. **Misani ngesimo esihle.** Qedani ngemva kokufanisa, kusamnandi.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) inikeza umoya ofanayo njengeseluleko esijwayelekile: fanelanisa umsebenzi nalokho umuntu akwazi ukukwenza, qaphelani ukukhathazeka, futhi qinisekisani ukuthi uzizwa enempumelelo.

## Imidlalo emnene yenkumbulo nokufanisa ongayizama

### Amapheya ezithombe (ukufanisa izithombe zibonakala)

[Amapheya ezithombe](/games/picture-pairs/) ngumdlalo wokufanisa onezithombe ezinkulu namakhadi angu-4 kuya kwayi-12. Izithombe zingahlala zibonakala, ngakho mayelana nokuthola amawele, hhayi ukukhumbula lapho ayekhona. Qalani ngamakhadi amane. Umuntu osesigabeni sokuqala othanda inselele encane ungashintshela ku-**Izithombe ezifihliwe**, kodwa buyelani ekubonisweni kwezithombe masinyane lapho umdlalo ungasamnandi.

### Thola okufanayo

Umdlalo othi [Thola okufanayo](/games/find-the-same/) ubonisa isithombe esisodwa phezulu nokukhetha okubili kuya kwamane ngezansi. Thintani esifanayo. Ungumdlalo olula kakhulu wokufanisa kule webhusayithi futhi ufanele abantu abathola ukuthi igridi ephelele iminyene kakhulu, kuhlanganise nabaningi esigabeni sokugcina nomuntu eceleni kwabo.

### Amapheya ezithombe zomndeni (umdlalo wenkumbulo wezithombe zomndeni)

Umdlalo othi [Amapheya ezithombe zomndeni](/games/photo-pairs/) uguqula izithombe ozikhethayo zibe umdlalo wokufanisa: inja, insimu, indlu eceleni kechibi. Ubuso nezindawo ezijwayelekile kuvame ukuvusa ingxoxo ngaphezu kwanoma yisiphi isithombe esivela kwabanye abantu. Izithombe zihlala ku-browser yakho futhi azilayishelwa kwenye indawo neze.

### Hlungani ndawonye

Umdlalo othi [Hlungani ndawonye](/games/sort-together/) ucela ukuba nibeke isithombe ngasinye ebhasikidini lapho sifanele khona, njengekhishi noma insimu. Ukuhlunga kusebenzisa ulwazi olugcinwe isikhathi eside lokuthi umhlaba usebenza kanjani, hhayi inkumbulo yesikhashana.

### Iphazili yesithombe

Umdlalo othi [Iphazili yesithombe](/games/picture-puzzle/) uhlukanisa isithombe sibe izicucu ezi-4 noma ezi-9. Thintani izicucu ezimbili ukuzishintshanisa kuze kube yilapho isithombe siphelele. Isithombe esiphelele sihlala esikrinini njengomhlahlandlela.

### Amakhadi angempela etafuleni

Aniludingi isikrini. Khethani amapheya afanayo emapakeni amabili amakhadi noma esethini yamakhadi ezithombe. Wabekeni abonakala ngezinhla ezimbili bese nithola amapheya ndawonye. Kamuva, hlunganisani ngombala noma ngohlobo lwekhadi esikhundleni salokho. I-[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ibala imidlalo yamakhadi neyebhodi phakathi kwemisebenzi yesigaba sokuqala.

## Yimiphi imidlalo yenkumbulo efanele isigaba ngasinye?

Izigaba zingamagama nje angacacile. Landelani lokho umuntu akujabulelayo namuhla.

| Isigaba | Imidlalo evame ukufanela | Indlela yokuyilungisa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sokuqala | Amapheya ezithombe, Amapheya ezithombe zomndeni, Iphazili yesithombe ngezicucu eziyi-9, imidlalo yamakhadi ejwayelekile | Mvumeleni akhethe. Amakhadi angajikwa uma ethanda; shiyani amaphuzu uma emkhathaza |
| Esiphakathi | Amapheya ezithombe ngamakhadi angu-4 kuya kwayi-6 abonakala, Thola okufanayo, Hlungani ndawonye | Izithombe zihlale zibonakala. Hlalani eceleni kwakhe nishintshane ngokusiza |
| Sokugcina | Thola okufanayo ngokukhetha okungu-2, ukubuka isithombe esisodwa esikhulu ndawonye | Kufushane kakhulu. Nina nibamba ithebhulethi; yena ukhomba, noma abuke futhi alalele |

## Ingabe imidlalo yenkumbulo isiza i-dementia?

Akukho mdlalo wenkumbulo oboniswe ukuthi welapha, ulibazisa noma umisa i-dementia. [Ukubuyekezwa kwe-Cochrane](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/) kokuqeqeshwa kokuqonda ngekhompyutha kubantu abanokuphazamiseka okuncane kokuqonda kwathola ubufakazi buncane kakhulu ukuthi busho ukuthi kuyayivimbela i-dementia yini. Umhlahlandlela wethu [wemidlalo yobuchopho ne-dementia](/brain-health/brain-games/) uchaza lokho izifundo ezikubonisayo nezingakubonisi.

Lokho akusona isizathu sokweqa imidlalo. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) incoma ukugxila ekujabuleni, hhayi empumelelweni. Imizuzu eyishumi yokufanisa izithombe nokukhuluma ngazo ingaba yingxenye emnandi ngempela yosuku.

## Kuthiwani ngemidlalo yenkumbulo yabadala abangaphili ne-dementia?

Uma umuntu omdala onganankinga yenkumbulo ethanda amaphazili anesikhathi, amaphuzu namazinga alukhuni, akukho okubi kulokho. Kodwa ningayethembi ukuthi izovikela i-dementia; akukho mdlalo oboniswe ukuthi wenza kanjalo. Izinguqulo ezithambile ezingenhla zisebenza kahle nalapho izizukulwane zidlala ndawonye: ugogo one-dementia nomzukulu omncane bangafanisa izithombe njengeqembu, kungekho nomunye ohlolwayo.

## Kufanele nimise noma nishintshe nini umdlalo?

Ukuphefumula kakhulu ngokudinwa, ukusunduza ithebhulethi, ukuthi “Angikwazi lokhu” noma ukubukeka ekhathazekile zonke izimpawu zokuthi kufanele kwenziwe kube lula noma kumiswe. Zamani amakhadi ambalwa, shiyani izithombe zibonakala, shintshelani ku-[Thola okufanayo](/games/find-the-same/), noma nibuke nje izithombe nixoxe. Uma engafuni ukudlala nhlobo, lokho kulungile futhi. Umhlahlandlela wethu [wokudlala ndawonye](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) unolwazi oluningi ngesivinini nokusiza.
