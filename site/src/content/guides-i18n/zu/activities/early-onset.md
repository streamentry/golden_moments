Izincwadi eziningi mayelana ne-dementia zicabanga ukuthi umuntu useneminyaka yamashumi ayisikhombisa noma yamashumi ayisishiyagalombili. Ngomuntu otholwe eneminyaka yamashumi amane noma amashumi amahlanu, isimo sansuku zonke sihluke. Usengase asasebenza, abe nezikweletu, abe nezingane ezisemasontweni noma abe nomzali okufanele amnakekele. Imisebenzi kufanele iwuvume lowo mpilo wabantu abadala. Leli khasi lichaza okushintshayo, okufanele uzame, nokuthi abalingani nabantwana abadala bangathola kuphi ukwesekwa.

## Iyini i-dementia eqala esemncane?

I-dementia eqala esemncane iyi-dementia eqala ngaphambi kweminyaka engu-65, enye yayo ibizwa ngokuthi equla esemncane. I-[blog ye-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) ichaza i-Alzheimer's njengeqala esemncane uma ithinta umuntu ongaphansi kweminyaka engu-65, futhi iphawula ukuthi ukunakekela umuntu osemncane kangaka “kunezinselele ezikhethekile.” Izimpawu zingafana nezalapho sekuphele iminyaka, kodwa izinhlelo zomuntu, izibopho zakhe nempilo yakhe yokuhlangana azifani.

Ngoba ayivamile kakhulu, abantu bangase balinde isikhathi eside ukuze bathole isifo, nemindeni ingazizwa ingenaye omkhuluma naye. Ukwazi ukuthi abanye abantu nabo bahlangabezana nalokhu kuvame ukududuza.

## Gcina umuntu omdala emsebenzini

Abantu abadala abane-dementia eqala esemncane bavame ukungafuni ukuphathwa njengezingane. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) yeluleka ukulungisa imisebenzi ngokulingana nalokho umuntu asengakwenza futhi ayakujabulela. Kumuntu oneminyaka yamashumi amahlanu, lokhu kungase kube:

- **Gcina isithakazelo esijwayelekile,** njengokwenza izinto ngezinkuni, umdlalo, amakhadi, iklabhu yokufunda noma ikwayala, ngosizo oluncane noma isikhathi esifushane.
- **Hamba noma ushayikili nomngane,** endleleni ayaziyo kahle.
- **Pheka ukudla komndeni,** umuntu enze izingxenye angazilawula.
- **Zithandele noma usize** endimeni ayikhethayo, njengokuhlunga izipho noma ukusiza emcimbini esikoleni.
- **Hlela uhambo** oya endaweni ebalulekile kuye, nomngane nohlelo olulula.
- **Chitha isikhathi nezingane zezingane zakhe, nezinhlobo zakhe,** nabelane ngomdlalo noma ngomsebenzi.

Mvumele umuntu akhethe. Ukubuza ukuthi “Ufuna ukwenza ini ngalolu hlobo emini?” kuhlonipha kunokumkhethela.

## Sebenzisa umculo nezinkumbulo zeminyaka yakhe

Umculo nezithombe kusukela eminyakeni yakhe yamashumi amabili, amashumi amathathu nawamashumi amane kungaletha imizwa emnandi nengxoxo elula. Umhlahlandlela wethu wemisebenzi [yomculo](/activities/music/) uchaza ukwakha uhlu lwemculo, futhi [imisebenzi yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) ikhombisa ukwabelana ngezithombe nezindaba ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo. Ukuze uthole umdlalo wabantu abadala, zama [amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/), anemibuzo evulekile ngomsebenzi, ukuhamba, abangane nomndeni.

Izithombe ezipheyiwe ezenziwe ngezithombe zomndeni nazo zingasebenza kahle, ngoba izithombe zabantu nezindawo ezibalulekile kumuntu. [Izithombe ezipheyiwe](/games/photo-pairs/) zigcina izithombe ku-browser kuleyo divayisi.

## Umsebenzi, ukushayela nempilo yansuku zonke

Abantu abaningi abane-dementia eqala esemncane basasebenza, bashayela noma baphatha imali. Lezi azibona imisebenzi ngendlela ejwayelekile, kodwa ziveza okungenziwa ngumuntu. Khuluma nodokotela wabo ngokushayela nokuphepha emsebenzini, futhi cabanga ukuthi bafuna usizo yini ngezimali noma ngezinhlelo. Injongo ukuvikela isithunzi sabo bese umuntu ehlala ehileleke njengoba efuna.

Ukuze uthole isiqondiso semikhuba yansuku zonke nokuhlala usebenza, bheka [uhlelo olulula lwansuku](/activities/daily-routine/) kanye [nemisebenzi yasekhaya njengemisebenzi](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Ukwesekwa kubalingani nabantwana abadala

Abalingani nabantwana abadala bavame ukuzwa bebodwa. Ukunakekela kungazizwa njengomsebenzi wesibili ngaphezu komsebenzi nomndeni. Umbhalo webhulogi ye-Alzheimer's Association ubika ukuthi abanakekeli emaqenjini equla esemncane bawuthola usizo ezindabeni ezisebenzayo ezinjengokuhlela okomthetho, nokuzizwa beqondwa. Iphinde iphawule ukuthi i-Association inamaqembu okusekela abanakekeli mahhala, kuhlanganise namaqembu abantu abanakekela umuntu one-dementia eqala esemncane, namanye amathathu ahlangana ngokwamanethiwekhi ngoLwesibili nangoLwesithathu.

Ukuze ubuze ngamaqembu, shayela umugqa wosizo ongu-24/7 we-Association ku-800.272.3900. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokunakekela kwakho](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) uchaza ukuphumula, ukuphumula kwesikhashana nezimpawu zokukhathala.

## Izigaba zisabalulekile

Isigaba sesifo salowo muntu sisho ukuthi yini esiza. Ekuqaleni, abantu abaningi bangenza imisebenzi yabo ejwayelekile ngezinguquko ezithile, futhi [imisebenzi yesigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/) inezinye izimbono. Ekamuva, imisebenzi iba lula futhi ibe nemizwa eminingi, njengoba kuchazwe ku-[imisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/). Shintsha umsebenzi njengoba umuntu eshintsha, futhi mbuze ukuthi ufuna ukwenzani nalapho esazi ukusho.
