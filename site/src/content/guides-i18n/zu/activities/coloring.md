Ukudweba imibala yenye yezinto ezilula kakhulu ongazinikeza umuntu ophila ne-Alzheimer's noma nenye indlela ye-dementia. Akudingi ukufunda, inkumbulo noma impendulo eyiyo. Izandla ziyanyakaza, imibala iyavela, futhi kukhona isithombe okungakhulunywa ngaso. Leli khasi lichaza ukukhetha amaphepha, ukulungisa izinto, nokudweba ndawonye ngendlela engenza kungabonakali njengokuba yingane noma njengovivinyo.

## Kungani ukudweba imibala kufanela abantu abaningi abane-dementia

Ukudweba imibala kunika izandla into okufanele ziyenze namehlo into okufanele awalandele. Ingenziwa uhlezi etafuleni noma esitulweni eduze nefasitele, futhi kulula ukukuthatha nokukubeka phansi. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ichaza ubuciko njengendlela yokuveza imizwa nokujabulela isikhathi samanje, futhi iphawula ukuthi aludingi ikhono lobuciko.

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) yeluleka ukukhetha imisebenzi evumelana nalokho umuntu asengakwenza, bese uyashintsha njengoba amakhono eshintsha. Ukudweba imibala kulula ukushintsha: isithombe esikhulu, amakhilayoni ajiyile, imibala embalwa, noma iskrini lokuthepha.

## Khetha imiklamo yabantu abadala

Abantu abadala bavame ukungathandi izithombe zezingane. Funa:

- **Izindawo zemvelo:** igadi enezimbali ezinkulu, ichibi nesikebhe, izinyoni esihlahleni.
- **Izinto ezijwayelekile:** ithawula lokwenza itiye, ibhayisikili, isithelo, isigqoko.
- **Iziklamo ezinkulu nelula:** imandala nezimo zejometri ezinemibala eqinile nezingxenye ezimbalwa ezinkulu.
- **Imidwebo yobuciko noma izindawo** umuntu angazibona esuka endaweni ayithandayo noma komdwebi awuthandayo.

Gwema amaphepha anezindawo ezincane nezinamathelene, ezikhathaza ukuzigcwalisa. Amaphepha abantu abadala okuphrinta angenye yezinketho ezinhle, futhi [imisebenzi ye-dementia yamahhala okuphrinta](/activities/printable/) yethu ihlanganisa namaphepha anombhalo omkhulu.

## Yini okufanele uyibeke phansi

- **Amathuluzi amakhulu, alula ukubamba.** Amakhilayoni amakhulu kakhulu, ama-marker ajiyile noma izinsimbi zombala ezinesibambo esibanzi zilula kunezinsimbi ezincane.
- **Uhlu olufushane lwemibala.** Imibala emithathu noma emine iyanele. Izinketho eziningi kakhulu zingadida.
- **Iphepha noma ikhadibhodi eqinile.** Iphepha elincane liyadabuka futhi lishelele. Bopha amakona ngetheyiphu noma sebenzisa ibhodi lokubhala.
- **Itafula elicacile.** Susa izinto ezingadingekile bese ubeka iphepha elilodwa ngesikhathi.
- **Okokucwilisa noma okokuhlanganisa imibala,** njengelaphu noma isipontshi, ukuze kube nomphumela othambile.

## Ukudweba imibala ethebhulethini

[Umdlalo wokudweba imibala](/games/coloring/) ku-Golden Moments usebenza ngokuthepha umbala bese uthepha ingxenye yesithombe. Awudingi ukulawula izandla ngokucophelela, ngakho ilungele nabantu abangasabambi kahle izinto. Hlala eduze komuntu ukuze nikwazi ukwabelana ngeskrini, futhi uvumele yena akhethe imibala. Vala izaziso ukuze isithombe sihlale siyinto egxilwe kuyo.

## Ukudweba imibala ndawonye

1. **Qala umugqa wokuqala ndawonye.** Bamba ikhilayoni nabo, noma ubeke isandla sakho phezu kwesabo imigqa embalwa, bese uyayikhipha.
2. **Khulumani ngemibala nangezindawo.** “Lolo luhlaza olumnandi kakhulu. Wakhe wayibona kuphi amanzi anombala onjalo?” Lokhu kuvula ingxoxo ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo.
3. **Bonga izinqumo, hhayi ukucwebezela.** “Ukhethe imibala efudumele kakhulu” ilungile kunokuthi “Ugcine emigqeni.”
4. **Vumela bakhokhele.** Uma befuna ukuchoboza ikhasi lonke, bavumele.
5. **Yeka uma eyeka.** Beka amakhilayoni phansi, bese uphakamisa indebe yetiye noma ukuhamba.

## Ukulungisa njengoba i-dementia ishintsha

Esigabeni sokuqala, abantu bavame ukujabulela amaphepha anemininingwane futhi bangakhetha imibala nemiklamo yabo. Esigabeni esiphakathi, izithombe ezinkulu nezinketho ezimbalwa ziyasiza. Ezigabeni zokugcina, zama isimo esisodwa esikhulu, ishidi elinemibala ethambile, noma ukubamba nje ikhilayoni ngenkathi uhlabelela ingoma. Abanye abantu bajabulela ukubheka isithombe esiphelele kunokusenza.

Ukuze uthole ngomsebenzi wobuciko ezigabeni ngazinye, bheka [ubuciko nemisebenzi yezandla yabantu abadala](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) kanye [nemisebenzi yobuciko yabantu abane-dementia](/activities/art-projects/).

## Lapho okumele ukuyeka khona

Yeka uma umuntu ethukuthele, esesula iphepha, noma ebonakala ekhathele. Ukuphela ngemizuzu emibili kuyimpumelelo uma ayijabulele. Ungabuya ngesikhathi esizayo, noma uzame isithombe esihlukile noma omunye umsebenzi onjenge [somdlalo wezimizwa](/activities/sensory/).
