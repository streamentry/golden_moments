Esigabeni sokuqala se-dementia, abantu abaningi basaphila kakhulu njengakuqala. Bangase basebenze, bazinikele ngokuzithandela, bahlangane nabangani futhi baphathe okuningi kosuku lwabo. Imisebenzi ivame ukungadingi ukushintshwa; idinga izilungiso ezimbalwa ukuze ihlale ijabulisa. Indima yenu ifana neyomlingani kunaleyo yomnakekeli: umuntu osekelayo, ohlela nabo futhi angenelayo kuphela uma ecelwa.

## Yiziphi izinto ezinhle esigabeni sokuqala se-dementia?

Imisebenzi engcono kakhulu esigabeni sokuqala ngokuvamile yilezo umuntu asevele ezithanda, ezenziwe zalula kancane. I-[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) ibala imibono efana nemidlalo yamakhadi neyebhodi, ukufuna amagama, ukuhamba, ukudansa, ukulima insimu, ukuhlela izimbali, ukulandisa izindaba, ukudweba, umculo, ukubhala ijenali, ukubhala izincwadi, i-scrapbook, ukupheka nemikhuba yezinsuku zemikhosi.

Okuvame ukushintsha yindawo, hhayi umsebenzi. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ithi abantu abasesigabeni sokuqala bangase baqale ukuyeka izinto ababezijabulela, futhi inikeza isibonelo somuntu ozizwa ehlulekile emcimbini omkhulu owenza kangcono eqenjini elincane. Khulumani ngokuvulekile ngalokho okuzwakala kunzima kakhulu, nilungise ndawonye.

## Buzani kuqala, futhi nivumelane ngendlela yokusiza

[Isiqondiso se-Alzheimer's Association sesigaba sokuqala](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage) siphakamisa imikhuba embalwa esebenza nasemisebenzini:

- **Buzani ngqo.** “Yini engenza iklabhu yezincwadi ibe lula kuwe?” ithola impendulo engcono kunokuqagela.
- **Cabangani ukuthi bayakwazi.** Uma kungekho ingozi, vumelani umuntu azame, futhi nikhuthaze kunokuthatha izintambo.
- **Vumelanani ngophawu.** Khethani igama noma ukuvuma ngekhanda okusho ukuthi “kulungile ukungenela,” isibonelo lapho igama lingeyi ngesikhathi somdlalo wamakhadi.
- **Buzani njalo.** Buzani ukuthi inani losizo lizwakala lilungile, lilikhulu noma lincane.

## Imibono yemisebenzi yesigaba sokuqala

### Gcinani izindima nezintandokazi

- **Gcinani isibopho esijwayelekile.** Ukuzinikela ngokuzithandela, ikwaya noma iklabhu kanye ngesonto kunika iviki isimo. Uma kuba nzima, indima emfushane noma ethule ingase ifanele kangcono.
- **Hlelani ukudla ndawonye.** Uma ukuthenga ukudla sekube nokukhungatheka, i-Alzheimer's Association iphakamisa ukucela umuntu ukuthi asize ahlele imenyu yeviki futhi abhale uhlu esikhundleni salokho.
- **Phekani futhi bhakani.** Landelani iresiphi eyintandokazi ndawonye. Bekani izithako ngokulandelana ngaphambi kokuqala, ukuze izinyathelo zibonakale.
- **Limani insimu.** Hlelani indawo yokutshala, khethani imbewu noma izitshalo futhi nisebenze ndawonye. Uhlu olubhaliwe lwemisebenzi yensimu yeviki luyasiza.
- **Qhubekani nomsebenzi wezandla.** Ukusebenza ngokhuni, ukuthunga, ukuluka noma ukwakha amamodeli kungaqhubeka ngamaphethini alula nokukhanya okuhle.

### Imidlalo yenjabulo

- **Dlalani amakhadi nemidlalo yebhodi.** Imidlalo ejwayelekile ilula kakhulu. Dlalani ngamaqembu ukuze kungabikho umuntu ophoqelekile, futhi gcinani amaphuzu ngokungaqinile noma ningawagcini nhlobo.
- **Zamani ukufuna amagama ngombhalo omkhulu.** [Ukusesha amagama](/games/word-search/) kwethu kungadlalwa esikrinini noma kuphrintwe ngomzuzu othulile.
- **Yenzani amaphazili.** Khethani inani lezingcezu eliphumuzayo, hhayi elinzima. [Iphazili yesithombe](/games/picture-puzzle/) inguhlobo olufushane lwesikrini, futhi umhlahlandlela wethu wokukhetha [amaphazili e-dementia](/activities/puzzles/) ukusiza ukukhetha elilodwa.
- **Joyinani imibuzo yolwazi ngenjabulo.** Abanye abantu bathanda ubusuku bemibuzo yolwazi beqembu. Uma kuqala ukuzwakala njengesivivinyo, khethani okunye, noma nizame [imibuzo yethu elula yolwazi](/activities/trivia/), eza nezimpendulo zokukhetha futhi ingenawo amaphuzu.

Imidlalo lapha ingeyenjabulo. Akukho mdlalo oboniswe ukuthi uyelapha noma wehlisa i-dementia; funda kabanzi ku-[midlalo yobuchopho: okushiwo ucwaningo](/brain-health/brain-games/).

### Bhalani, nicabange futhi nirekhode

- **Gcinani ijenali.** I-Alzheimer's Association iphakamisa ukubhala ijenali njengendlela eyodwa yokudlula emizweni ngemva kokutholwa kwesifo. Imigqa embalwa ngosuku iyanela.
- **Holelani incwadi yendaba yempilo.** Isigaba sokuqala siyisikhathi esihle sokuba umuntu akhethe izithombe zakhe futhi abhale noma akhulume izindaba ezingemuva kwazo. Bhekani [imisebenzi yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/).
- **Bhalani izincwadi.** Incwadi eya kumzukulu, kumngani omdala noma kumuntu owayesebenza naye ingaba nenjongo kulabo bobabili.
- **Xoxani ngezithombe.** [Amapheya ezithombe zomndeni](/games/photo-pairs/) zenza izithombe zomndeni zibe umdlalo othambile wokufanisa, futhi [amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) anikeza izindawo ezilula zokuqala ingxoxo.

### Phumani futhi nihambe

I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) iphakamisa ukuqhubeka nohambo olujwayelekile inqobo nje uma nobabili nizizwa nilungile. Lulungiseleleni isikhathi esingcono sosuku somuntu futhi ningaluyeki lube lude kakhulu.

- **Uhambo olufushane lwamasiko.** Imnyuziyamu, ifilimu yasemini noma umbukiso wobuciko, ngokufanelekile ngesikhathi esithule.
- **Ukubhukuda.** I-NIA iphakamisa echibini ngesikhathi sosuku esingenabantu abaningi.
- **Amaqembu okuhamba namakilasi okudansa.** I-[NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ikhuluma ngokudansa, i-tai chi, i-yoga, ukubhukuda namaqembu okuhamba, nezikhathi ezifanele abaphila ne-dementia lapho zitholakala.
- **Amaqembu okucula nama-memory café.** Amaqembu aphumuzayo lapho abantu abaphila ne-dementia nabalingani babo bengahlangana nabanye abasesimeni esifanayo.

## Izinguquko ezincane ezigcina imisebenzi ijabulisa

| Uma lokhu kuya ngokuba nzima | Zamani lokhu |
| --- | --- |
| Izindawo ezinomsindo noma ezinabantu abaningi | Hambani ngezikhathi ezithulile, khethani amaqembu amancane, hlalani onqenqemeni lwegumbi |
| Ukukhumbula amagama amasha | Wethulani abantu ngegama njalo; celani amaqembu asebenzise amathegi amagama |
| Ukulandela amapulani anezinyathelo eziningi | Bhala izinyathelo phansi, noma nilungise yonke into ngokulandelana kuqala |
| Ukukhathala | Uhambo olufushane, ukuphumula ngemva kwalo, akukho okunye okuhleliwe ngalolo suku |
| Ukulandelela umdlalo | Dlalani ngamaqembu, nenze imithetho ibe lula, noma ningagcini amaphuzu |
| Ukuthola amagama | Nikeze isikhathi; nikeze igama kuphela uma uphawu enivumelene ngalo lusho kanjalo |

## Uma kufanele nehlise

Yekani noma nishintshe umsebenzi uma ulethe ingcindezi ngaphezu kwenjabulo. Ukuphoxeka okuphindaphindwayo, ukugwema isintandokazi noma ukuthi “angisakwazi nje” kufanele kuxoxwe ngakho kahle, hhayi ukuphoqwa. Ngokuvamile impendulo iwuhlobo oluncane lento efanayo. Lapho umuntu edinga usizo oluthe xaxa ekuqaleni nasekulandeleni izinyathelo, imibono [imisebenzi yesigaba esimaphakathi](/activities/middle-stage/) ingase ifanele kangcono. Ukuthola okwengeziwe ngengxoxo esekelayo, bheka [amathiphu okuxhumana](/caregiving/communication-tips/), futhi ukuthola eminye imibono eminingi, bheka [imisebenzi eyi-101](/activities/ideas/).

Abalingani badinga usizo nabo. Isigaba sokuqala siyisikhathi esihle sokwakha inethiwekhi yenu yosizo; bheka [ukuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).
