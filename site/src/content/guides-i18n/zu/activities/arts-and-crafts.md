Ubuciko nemisebenzi yezandla kunikeza abantu abaphila ne-dementia indlela yokuziveza engancikile ekutholeni amagama alungile. I-Alzheimer's Association ithi imisebenzi yobuciko ingaletha [umuzwa wokufeza into nenhloso](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) kanye nethuba lokuziveza, kumnakekeli nakumuntu ophila ne-dementia. Okubalulekile ukugcina izinto zifanele abadala, zilula futhi zingenangcindezi. Leli khasi linikeza imisebenzi ethile, indlela yokuyilungiselela nendlela yokuyilungisa lapho izinto zishintsha.

## Yini eyenza umsebenzi wobuciko ufanele abadala hhayi izingane?

Umsebenzi wobuciko ofanele abadala usebenzisa izinto zangempela, izindikimba zabadala nezwi elinenhlonipho. I-Alzheimer's Association incoma ukugcina imisebenzi ezingeni labadala nokugwema noma yini engabonakala ihlazisa noma ifana neyezingane.

| Kuzwakala njengeyezingane | Kuzwakala njengeyabadala |
| --- | --- |
| I-glitter, ama-sticker e-foam, amehlo adlalayo | Iphepha lopende wamanzi, indwangu, izimbali ezicindezelwe |
| Amakhasi okufaka umbala amakhathuni | Amakhasi okufaka umbala ezingadi, amakwilithi, izimoto noma izindawo ezinhle |
| Ukudumisa nge-“Wenze kahle” kuyo yonke imvimbo | Ingxoxo yangempela: “Lowo mbala okwesibhakabhaka ubukeka kahle eduze kokuluhlaza” |
| Umsebenzi ongenamsebenzi | Ikhadi lomngani, into yokuhlobisa ithebula, isipho |
| Iziqondiso ezifundwa njengesifundo | Isimemo: “Ungangisiza ngalokhu?” |

Cabangani ngokuthanda komuntu uqobo. Umuntu owayethanda ukuthungela kungenzeka akujabulele ukuphatha indwangu. Unjiniyela osethathe umhlalaphansi kungenzeka athande ukwenza imodeli enezingcezu ezinkulu noma ukuhlela izinto zensimbi. Uthisha wangaphambili kungenzeka athande ukwenza amakhadi.

## Kungani kufanele sigxile ekwenzeni hhayi emkhiqizweni?

Ngoba isikhathi enikuchitha ndawonye yiyona nhloso. Umhlahlandlela we-Alzheimer's Association ngemisebenzi [ekhaya](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/5337a7c3-15c6-4b4d-a3a0-ca00a0839a6b/alzheimers-dementia-activities-at-home-ts.pdf) uthi nigxile ekwenzeni hhayi emphumeleni. Umdwebo onombala “okwesibhakabhaka kuphela” usengumdwebo. I-collage enezithombe ezintathu isengi-collage.

I-Association iphinde ithi umdwebi onekhono kungenzeka aphoxeke ngenxa yezinguquko emsebenzini wakhe, kuyilapho owenza ngokuzithokozisa kungenzeka ajabulele ithuba elisha lokuziveza. Uma umuntu ayeyisazi sobuciko, zamani indlela ehlukile kuleyo ayeyiqonda kahle, ukuze kuzwakale njengomdlalo hhayi njengokuqhathanisa.

## Imibono yobuciko nemisebenzi yezandla ongayizama

Umbono ngamunye uhlanganisa indlela yokuwenza ube lula uma ube nzima.

1. **Ukuchoboza upende wamanzi.** Cwilisani iphepha elinzima lopende wamanzi bese nivumela umuntu athonse umbala kulo ngebrashi enkulu. Imibala ibhebhetheka yodwa, ngakho akukho sivuno esiyiphutha. *Kulula kakhulu:* nikeze imibala emibili kuphela, noma nidwebe ngamanzi ephepheni elimnyama elikhombisa imivimbo ecwilile.
2. **I-collage yezithombe zakumagazini.** Sikani izithombe kusengaphambili emagazini ngezingadi, ukupheka, izimoto, ukuvakasha noma izinto ezazithandwa ngumuntu. Mvumeleni akhethe anamathisele izithombe ekhadini eliqinile. *Kulula kakhulu:* nikeze izithombe ezine ezisikiwe kakade neglu yenduku, nenze ukunamathisela ndawonye.
3. **Ukuhlela izimbali.** I-Alzheimer's Foundation of America incoma [ukuhlela izimbali ezisikiwe namahlamvu](https://alzfdn.org/dementia-activity-gardening/) kube ukuhlobisa okulula kwesizini. Sebenzisani imbiza esindayo, ezinzile namahlumela asevele anqunyiwe. *Kulula kakhulu:* bekani amahlumela ambalwa enkomishini encane, linye ngesikhathi.
4. **Amakhadi ezimbali ezicindezelwe.** Cindezelani izimbali zesihlanganiso phakathi kwamakhasi ebhuku isonto, bese ziyanamathiselwa ekhadini eligoqiwe. *Kulula kakhulu:* nina nifaka iglu, yena acindezele ihlamvu.
5. **Amakhasi okufaka imibala abadala.** Khethani amakhasi anemigqa ecacile nezindikimba zangempela. [Amakhasi ethu okufaka imibala](/games/coloring/) aphrintekayo angafakwa nasesikrinini ngokuthinta umbala, bese ingxenye yesithombe. *Kulula kakhulu:* izindawo ezinkulu, imibala embalwa, amapeni aminyene.
6. **Ubumba noma inhlama ethambile.** Gingqani, cindezelani nibumbe ubumba oluma ngokuma emoyeni noma inhlama yokubumba ethambile. Ukucindezela ihlamvu ebumbeni kwenza ithayili elilula elinomkhondo. *Kulula kakhulu:* ukucindezela nokugingqa kuphela kuyisenzo esiphelele. Qaphelani ukuthi umuntu angafaki ubumba emlonyeni.
7. **Ukwenza amakhadi.** I-Alzheimer's Association incoma ukukubeka njengomsebenzi wangempela: “Sithumela ikhadi lokululama kumngani. Ungasisiza?” Ikhadi elisikiwe kakade nama-sticker ambalwa ezimbali noma izitembu kusebenza kahle.
8. **I-scrapbook.** Bekani ndawonye amakhophi ezithombe ezindala, amakhadi aposi noma amathikithi ezikhumbuzo emakhasini e-albhamu. Xoxani ngalunye njengoba nihamba. Bhekani [umhlahlandlela wethu wokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) ngezindlela ezithambile zokuxoxa ngesikhathi esidlule.
9. **Amabhodi endwangu nokuthinta.** Namathiselani izingcezu zekhodurowa, zevelvethi, zebhegi nezesilika ekhadini eliqinile. Ukulenza kuyisenzo; ukulithinta kamuva kuwenye. Eminye imibono isemhlahlandlela wethu wezinzwa: [imisebenzi yezinzwa](/activities/sensory/).
10. **Ukuphothula umsebenzi wokhuni olula.** Kumuntu owayethanda ukusebenza ngokhuni, ukugudluza ibhulokhi lokhuni elibushelelezi noma ukupenda indlu yezinyoni kungazwakala kujwayelekile. Susani amathuluzi abukhali futhi nibabheke.
11. **Ukudweba ngomculo.** Dlalani ingoma ethandwayo bese nidweba amafomu noma imibala efana nomoya wayo. Lokhu kuhlanganisa imisebenzi emibili abantu abaningi abayijabulelayo. Bhekani [imisebenzi yomculo](/activities/music/).

## Ngilungisa kanjani isikhathi sobuciko?

1. **Lungiselelani kuqala.** Sikani, nqumani futhi nilungise yonke into ngaphambi kokumema umuntu. Ukulinda ngenkathi nifuna izikelo kungase kulahlekise isikhathi esihle.
2. **Gcinani kuncane.** Imibala emibili noma emithathu, ibrashi eyodwa, iphepha elilodwa. Ukukhetha okuningi kungase kube nzima kakhulu.
3. **Vikelani ithebula.** Indwangu yepulasitiki noma ishidi elidala lisho ukuthi ukungcola akuyona into ekhathazayo.
4. **Hlalani eceleni komunye nomunye.** Sebenzani umsebenzi wenu eceleni kowabo. Kuzwakala njengokwabiwa, hhayi njengokubhekwa.
5. **Basizeni baqale.** I-Alzheimer's Association ithi kungase kudingeke niqale umnyakazo webrashi nomuntu. Uma seqalile, abantu abaningi bayaqhubeka.
6. **Xoxani nisebenza.** Phawulani ngemibala nokuthinta, noma nivumele ubuciko bulethe izindaba. Gwemani imibuzo efana nokuhlolwa efana nokuthi “Kuyini lokhu?”
7. **Nikeze isikhathi esiningi.** Umsebenzi akudingeki uqedwe ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Ngilungisa kanjani ubuciko lapho i-dementia ishintsha?

| Isigaba | Okuvame ukusebenza | Okufanele kulungiswe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sokuqala | Amaklasi, ukufaka umbala okunemininingwane, ama-scrapbook, ukwenza amakhadi, ukudweba ngesithombe | Nikeze ukukhetha imisebenzi; gcina kunokuxhumana |
| Maphakathi | I-collage esikiwe kakade, ukuchoboza upende wamanzi, ukuhlela izimbali, ukufaka umbala ezindaweni ezinkulu | Isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi; nina nenza ukusika nokunamathisela |
| Sokuphela | Imivimbo ebanzi yebrashi, ukucindezela ubumba, ukuthinta indwangu, ukukubukela nidweba | Gxila ezinzweni; qondisa isandla kahle uma kwamukelekile |

I-NHS iphakamisa ukuthi ezigabeni zakamuva, imisebenzi ivame ukwenziwa ilula futhi igxile ezinzweni. Ukubukela omunye nidweba, nokuxoxa ngemibala, kuyindlela yangempela yokubamba iqhaza. Ukuthola eminye imibono ngesigaba ngasinye, bheka [imisebenzi yawo wonke amazinga](/activities/ideas/).

## Ukuphepha ngezinto zobuciko

- Khethani upende, amapeni nezinduku zeglu ezingenabuthi.
- Gcinani izikelo, amanayifi okusebenza ngezandla nezibhamu zeglu eshisayo ngale kwezandla zabantu. Yenzani konke ukusika nina, kusengaphambili.
- Qaphelani noma ubani ofaka ubumba, upende noma izinto ezincane emlonyeni.
- Sebenzisani izitsha ezizinzile, ezisindayo zamanzi nezimbiza zezimbali.

## Kufanele siyeke nini?

Yekani uma ukunaka kuncipha noma ukuphoxeka kuvela. I-Alzheimer's Association incoma ukuqeda noma ukushintsha umsebenzi uma isikhathi sokunaka sincipha noma ukuphoxeka kukhula. Bekani umsebenzi endaweni ephephile bese nikhetha okuthile okuphumulayo. Ningabuyela kuwo kusasa, noma ningabuyeli neze. Zombili zilungile.

## Bonisa umsebenzi, uma umuntu efuna

Ukunamathisela umdwebo ku-friji noma ukufaka i-collage esithombeni kungaba indlela ethule yokukhombisa inhlonipho. Buzani kuqala. Abanye abantu bathanda ukubona umsebenzi wabo odongeni; abanye bangathanda ukungawubona.

[Amakhadi Engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) anikeza imibuzo evulekile engaholela esithombeni kuya endabeni. Zonke azinawo isibali sikhathi noma amaphuzu.
