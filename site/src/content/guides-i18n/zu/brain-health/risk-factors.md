Isici sobungozi yinto ehlotshaniswa nethuba eliphakeme lokuba nesimo esithile sezempilo. Akuyona imbangela ezimeni zonke, futhi ukuba nesinye akusho ukuthi nizoba ne-dementia. Leli khasi libala izici ezi-14 zobungozi be-dementia ezishiwo yi-Lancet Commission ka-2024 ukuthi zingase zishintshwe, lokho ucwaningo oluzihlobanisa nakho, nalokho enikungaxoxa ngakho nodokotela.

Ukuze nibone isithombe esikhulu, kuhlanganise nalokho izilingo ezinkulu ezakuthola, qalani ngokuthi [I-dementia ingavinjwa? Lokho okushiwo ucwaningo](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/). Lolu ulwazi olujwayelekile, hhayi iseluleko sezokwelapha; bheka [isaziso sethu sezokwelapha](/medical-disclaimer/).

## Yiziphi izici ezi-14 zobungozi be-dementia?

I-[Lancet Commission ka-2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0), eyabhalwa abacwaningi be-dementia abangu-27, yalinganisela ukuthi izici ezi-14 zobungozi ezingase zishintshwe ndawonye zibangela amacala e-dementia acishe abe ngama-45% emhlabeni wonke. Iphesenti lesici ngasinye ngezansi yisilinganiso se-Commission sengxenye yamacala emhlabeni wonke ehlotshaniswa naso, ligoqelwe ezinombolweni ezigcwele. Izibalo ziyahlangana, ngoba abantu abaningi banezici eziningi zobungozi ngesikhathi esisodwa, yingakho ziyisamba ngama-45% kunokuba zibe ngaphezulu.

## Kufanele ngiwafunde kanjani lawa maphesenti?

Ngokucophelela. Umbiko we-Commission uqobo unezinto eziningana okufanele zigcinwe engqondweni:

- **Zichaza abantu bonke, hhayi abantu ngabanye.** [Isifinyezo sombiko](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) se-University College London sithi ukwehlisa ubungozi kusebenza kubantu bonke futhi akuqinisekisi ukuthi noma yimuphi umuntu oyedwa uzigweme i-dementia.
- **Zicabanga ukuthi kunembangela nomphumela.** Ababhali bafaka kuphela izici ezinobufakazi obuqinisekisayo, kodwa bathi ezinye izixhumanisi zingase zibe yimbangela ngokwengxenye kuphela.
- **Yizibalo zomhlaba wonke.** Idatha eningi ivela emazweni anemali engenayo ephakeme, futhi ingxenye yangempela yanoma yiliphi izwe noma umphakathi izohluka.
- **Amaphesenti amancane angabaluleka.** Isici esihlotshaniswa nama-2% amacala emhlabeni wonke sisathinta inani elikhulu kakhulu labantu.

## Yiziphi izici ezingeziwe ngo-2024?

Umbiko ka-2024 wengeze izici ezimbili kweziyi-12 ezabizwa ngo-2020:

- **I-cholesterol ye-LDL ephezulu maphakathi nempilo**, ehlotshaniswa nama-7% amacala, ngokulinganiselwa.
- **Ukulahlekelwa yikhono lokubona okungelashiwe ebudaleni**, okuhlotshaniswa nama-2% amacala, ngokulinganiselwa.

## Yiziphi izici zobungozi okungenakushintshwa?

I-National Institute on Aging ithi [iminyaka iyisici esikhulu kakhulu esaziwayo sobungozi be-Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease), futhi akekho ongayishintsha. Amajini nawo anengxenye; ijini elilodwa elaziwayo elithonya ubungozi be-Alzheimer's libizwa nge-APOE. I-NIA yengeza ukuthi ososayensi abakaqondi ngokuphelele ukuthi yini ebangela i-Alzheimer's kubantu abaningi. I-Lancet Commission ithi izici zobungozi ezingase zishintshwe zingehliswa noma ngabe ubungozi bomuntu bamajini bungakanani.

Ngenxa yokuthi iminyaka namajini kunengxenye enkulu kangaka, abantu abaningi abangenazo izici ezi-14 zobungozi basazoba ne-dementia. Lokho akulona iphutha lomuntu.

## Uthini ubufakazi bezilingo mayelana nokushintsha lobu bungozi?

Ukuhlobanisa isici sobungozi ne-dementia akufani nokufakazela ukuthi ukusishintsha kwehlisa ubungozi be-dementia. Izibonelo ezimbalwa zokuthi ubufakazi bezilingo bumi kuphi:

- **Umfutho wegazi.** Ocwaningweni lwe-SPRINT MIND, inhloso ephansi yomfutho wegazi yehlise ngokubalulekile ukuphazamiseka okuncane kokucabanga (mild cognitive impairment). Ayizange yehlise ngokubalulekile i-dementia ecatshangwa ukuthi ikhona (probable dementia), nakuba ucwaningo lungase lube luncane kakhulu ukuba lukhombise lokho ([SPRINT MIND ku-JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Izinsiza-kuzwa.** I-Lancet Commission ichaza ucwaningo lweminyaka emithathu lwezinsiza-kuzwa olwathola ukuthi akukho siphumela esiphelele ekucabangeni, kodwa kunenzuzo eqenjini elincane labantu abasengozini ephakeme. Izifundo ezilandelela abantu isikhathi eside zihlobanisa ukusebenzisa izinsiza-kuzwa nobungozi obuphansi. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuzwa](/brain-health/hearing/) unolwazi oluningi.
- **Umsebenzi womzimba.** I-National Institute on Aging ithi akukho bufakazi obuphelele bezilingo zomtholampilo ukuphetha ukuthi ukuzivocavoca kungavimbela noma kulibazise ukuphazamiseka okuncane kokucabanga noma i-Alzheimer's, nakuba ezinye izifundo ziphakamisa ukuthi kungase kusize ekwehleni okuhlobene neminyaka ([I-NIA mayelana nokuvimbela i-Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Izilingo ezinde ezilinda ukuthi i-dementia ivele ziyabambezeleka futhi ziyabiza, ngakho ezicini eziningi ubufakazi obungcono buzoqhubeka buvela ezifundweni ezilandelela abantu iminyaka eminingi. Lobo bufakazi buwusizo, kodwa bubuthaka kunobufakazi besilingo.

## Kuthiwani ngokulala nokudla?

I-Commission yabuyekeza ukulala nokudla kodwa ayizange ibengeze ohlwini, ngoba ubufakazi bebungaqinile noma bungavumelani ngokwanele. Imihlahlandlela yethu ethi [Ukulala](/brain-health/sleep/) neyethi [Ukudla](/brain-health/diet/) ichaza lokho okwaziwayo.

## Khulumani nodokotela

Ukuhlolwa konyaka kuyisikhathi esihle sokuphakamisa lezi zihloko. Ningalethe lolu hlu futhi nibuze:

- Zingakanani izinombolo zami zomfutho wegazi, ushukela egazini ne-cholesterol, futhi ingabe ezinye zidinga ukunakwa?
- Kufanele ngihlolise ukuzwa nokubona kwami?
- Ingabe umsebenzi engiwenzayo uphephile futhi wanele kimi?
- Ingabe noma iyiphi imithi yami ingathinta inkumbulo noma ukucabanga?

Ngaphambi kokushintsha imithi, ukudla, ukuzila ukudla, ukuzivocavoca noma izithasiselo, khulumani nodokotela kuqala. Uma nina noma umuntu eniyamnakekelayo eba nokudideka okuzumayo, ubuthakathaka ohlangothini olulodwa, noma enobunzima bokukhuluma, shayelani inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo yendawo yakini.
