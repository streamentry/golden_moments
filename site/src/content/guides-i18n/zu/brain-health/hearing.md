Ukulahlekelwa ukuzwa kuvamile ekuhlaleni kwemva, futhi abacwaningi bakubheka njengento ebalulekile ebeka engozini ye-dementia. Lokho akwenzi izinsiza zokuzwa zibe indlela efakazelwe yokuvimbela i-dementia; isivivinyo esingcono kakhulu kuze kube manje sinemiphumela exubile. Leli khasi lichaza ukuthi ucwaningo lubonisani, indlela yokuhlolisa ukuzwa, ukuthi yini izinsiza zokuzwa ezithengwa esitolo e-US, nokuthi ningayenza kanjani ingxoxo ibe lula.

## Kungani ukuzwa kubalulekile empilweni yobuchopho?

Ukulahlekelwa ukuzwa kuhlobene nobungozi obuphezulu be-dementia. [I-Lancet Commission yango-2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) ilinganisa ukuthi iyingxenye engaba ngu-7% ye-dementia emhlabeni wonke. Lokho kuyenza ilingane ne-LDL cholesterol ephezulu ekubeni nesabelo esikhulu kunazo zonke phakathi kwezinto ezingu-14 ezibeka engozini ezingashintshwa kumodeli ye-Commission, ngokwe-[ngqikithi ye-UCL yalo mbiko](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors).

Okutholwe i-Commission:

- **Ubungozi obuphezulu uma kunokulahlekelwa ukuzwa.** Ekuhlaziyweni kwayo okuhlanganisiwe kocwaningo oluyisithupha olusezingeni eliphezulu, abantu abalahlekelwe ukuzwa babenobungozi obungaba ngu-37% obuphezulu be-dementia kunabantu abanokuzwa okujwayelekile.
- **Ukulahlekelwa okukhulu, ubungozi obukhulu.** Ucwaningo olubheke ubukhulu bokulahlekelwa lwathola ukuthi ubungozi bukhuphuka njengoba ukuzwa kubi kakhulu.
- **Izizathu ezingaba khona, kungakabi kufakazelwe.** Imibono ihlanganisa ukuzizwa uyedwa nokuhoxa, ukudabuka, ukuvuselelwa kancane kobuchopho, nomzamo owengeziwe wengqondo odingwa ukulalela.

Ukulahlekelwa ukuzwa kuvamile. [I-National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/hearing-and-hearing-loss/hearing-loss-common-problem-older-adults) ithi cishe ingxenye eyodwa kwezintathu yabantu asebekhulile inakho, futhi amathuba akhuphuka ngeminyaka.

## Isivivinyo sezinsiza zokuzwa se-ACHIEVE sathola ini?

I-ACHIEVE kwaba yisivivinyo sokuqala esikhethwe ngenhlanhla (randomized) sezinsiza zokuzwa nokwehla kwamakhono okucabanga. Imiphumela yaso ivame ukubikwa kabi, ngakho nansi imininingwane evela [embikweni wesivivinyo osushicilelwe](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10529382/).

- **Ababambe iqhaza:** abantu abadala abangu-977 abaneminyaka engu-70 kuya ku-84 abanokulahlekelwa ukuzwa okungelashiwe futhi abangenazo izinkinga ezinkulu zokucabanga, ezindaweni ezine zomphakathi e-US.
- **Okwaqhathaniswa:** Ingxenye eyodwa yathola izinsiza zokuzwa kanye nezikhathi zokululekwa ne-audiologist. Enye ingxenye yathola uhlelo lwemfundo yezempilo ngokuguga okunempilo.
- **Umphumela oyinhloko:** Ngemva kweminyaka emithathu, **kwakungekho mehluko** ekwehleni kwamakhono okucabanga phakathi kwamaqembu womabili ngokwesilinganiso.
- **Umphumela weqembu elincane:** Ababambe iqhaza bavela ezindaweni ezimbili. Abantu abangu-238 bavela ocwaningweni lwempilo yenhliziyo lwesikhathi eside (ARIC), futhi njengeqembu babesengozini enkulu yokwehla kwamakhono okucabanga. Kuleli qembu, ukwehla kwaba **ngu-48% ngokuhamba kancane** ngezinsiza zokuzwa. Phakathi kwabazinikele bomphakathi abangu-739 abaphilile, kwakungekho mphumela ocacile.

Indlela yokufunda lokhu ngokwethembeka:

- Impendulo ngokwesilinganiso kwaba ukuthi akukho mphumela.
- Okutholwe eqenjini elincane bekuhlelwe kusengaphambili, kodwa kwavela ekuhlaziyweni kwesibili kweqembu elincane. Kudinga ukuqinisekiswa kwezinye izifundo.
- Ababambe iqhaza babazi ukuthi bahlelweni luphi, okungathinta imiphumela.
- I-Lancet Commission ibuchaza ubufakazi ngokuthi izinsiza zokuzwa zibonakala zisiza kakhulu abantu abanokulahlekelwa ukuzwa kanye nezinye izinto ezibeka engozini ye-dementia.

Isiphetho siwukuthi izinsiza zokuzwa azikhona indlela efakazelwe yokuvimbela i-dementia. Zisiza abantu ukuzwa, ukubamba iqhaza ezingxoxweni nokuhlala behlangene, okuyisizathu esanele sokuzicabangela.

## Ngingazi kanjani ukuthi ukuzwa kufanele kuhlolwe?

I-NIA iphakamisa ukubona udokotela uma wena noma umuntu omnakekelayo:

- Kunzima ukuqonda abantu ocingweni
- Kunzima ukulandela izingxoxo uma abantu ababili noma ngaphezulu bekhuluma
- Kuvame ukucelwa ukuthi abantu baphinde kaningi
- I-TV ikhuphulwa ngokuphakeme kangangokuthi abanye bayakhononda
- Kunzima ukuqonda inkulumo uma kunomsindo ngemuva
- Kubonakala sengathi abanye abantu bakhuluma ngokungacacile

Qalani nodokotela wokunakekela kokuqala, ongase anithumele ku-audiologist (uchwepheshe wokuzwa) noma kudokotela wezindlebe, impumulo nomphimbo. Ukuqongelela kwe-earwax (ukungcola kwendlebe) kungenye imbangela evamile yokulahlekelwa ukuzwa udokotela angayihlola. **Ukulahlekelwa ukuzwa ngokuzumayo kuyisimo esiphuthumayo sezokwelapha**, futhi i-NIA iluleka ukubona udokotela ngokushesha.

Ukulahlekelwa ukuzwa kungadidaniswa nokudideka. I-NIA ithi abantu asebekhulile abangezwa kahle ngezinye izikhathi bacatshangwa ngephutha ukuthi badidekile, abaphenduli noma abalaleli. Uma umuntu omthandayo ebonakala ethi cwaka noma engakuzwa okushiwo, ukuhlolwa kokuzwa kuyisinyathelo esihle sokuqala.

## Yini izinsiza zokuzwa ezithengwa esitolo?

Kusukela ngo-17 Okthoba 2022, i-[FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/hearing-aids/otc-hearing-aids-what-you-should-know) e-United States ivumele uhlobo lwezinsiza zokuzwa ezithengwa esitolo ngaphandle kwesiqondiso (OTC). Nansi indlela eziqhathaniswa ngayo nezinsiza ezinesiqondiso:

| Isici | Izinsiza ze-OTC | Izinsiza ezinesiqondiso |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ngubani ozifanele | Abantu abadala abaneminyaka engu-18 nangaphezulu abazibona benokulahlekelwa ukuzwa okuncane kuya kokumaphakathi | Noma yiliphi izinga lokulahlekelwa ukuzwa, noma yiliphi iminyaka |
| Zitholakala kuphi | Ezitolo noma ku-inthanethi, akukho kuhlolwa noma isiqondiso esidingekayo | Ngochwepheshe bokuzwa abanelayisense |
| Ukuzilungisa | Nina nilungisa izilungiselelo, kaningi nge-app | Uchwepheshe uzihlela ngokuzwa kwenu |

I-FDA ithi izinsiza ze-OTC azenzelwe ukulahlekelwa ukuzwa okukhulu noma okujulile. Iluleka ukubona udokotela, kungaba kuhle udokotela wezindlebe, impumulo nomphimbo, ngaphambi kokuthenga uma kukhona lokhu:

- Ubuhlungu noma ukungakhululeki endlebeni
- Uketshezi, i-pus noma igazi eliphuma endlebeni ezinyangeni eziyisithupha ezedlule
- Ushintsho oluzumayo noma olungazinzile ekuzweni
- Ukuzwa kubi kakhulu endlebeni eyodwa, noma umsindo okhalayo endlebeni eyodwa kuphela
- Isiyezi noma i-vertigo
- I-earwax eningi

Ukujwayela izinsiza zokuzwa kudinga ukuzijwayeza, ngakho hlolani inqubomgomo yokubuyisa ngaphambi kokuthenga, njengoba i-FDA iphakamisa. I-NIA iphakamisa ukubuza ukuthi umshwalense wenu umboza ingxenye yezindleko yini. Kumuntu ophila ne-dementia, i-audiologist ingasiza ukunquma ukuthi yiluphi uhlobo lwensiza nokulungiswa okuzoba lula kakhulu ukukuphatha usuku nosuku.

## Ngingakhuluma kanjani kalula nomuntu onokulahlekelwa ukuzwa?

I-NIA iphakamisa:

1. **Bhekanani nomuntu futhi nigcine ukubhekana kwamehlo**, ekukhanyeni okuhle, ukuze akwazi ukubona izindebe zenu nokubukeka kobuso.
2. **Tholani indawo ethulile.** Vala i-TV noma nisuke emsindweni.
3. **Khulumani ngomsindo ophakeme kancane, kodwa ningememeli.** Khulumani ngokwemvelo futhi ngesivinini esifanele.
4. **Gcinani umlomo ucacile.** Ningadli, ningahlafuni i-chewing gum noma nimboze umlomo ngenkathi nikhuluma.
5. **Shintshani amazwi kunokuphinda** uma okuthile kungezwakalanga.
6. **Umuntu oyedwa ngesikhathi.** Eqenjini, qinisekisani ukuthi umuntu uhlanganyele.
7. **Yibani nesineke futhi nibuze ukuthi ningasiza kanjani.**

## Ukubona nakho kubalulekile

Ngo-2024 i-Lancet Commission yengeza ukulahlekelwa ukubona okungelashiwe njengento entsha ebeka engozini. Ingqikithi ye-UCL iyibeka cishe ku-2% yamacala e-dementia, futhi i-Commission incoma ukwenza ukuhlolwa nokwelashwa kwezinkinga zokubona kutholakale kubo bonke. [I-NIA iluleka](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/vision-and-vision-loss/aging-and-your-eyes) ukuhlolwa kwamehlo (dilated eye exam) minyaka yonke noma emibili ngemva kweminyaka engu-60, nokuhlola nokuthi izibuko zamehlo ziyafaneleka. Ngisho nezinguquko ezincane ekubonweni zingakhuphula ubungozi bokuwa.

## Khulumani nodokotela

Buzani udokotela ngokuhlolwa kokuzwa nokubona uma nibona noma yiziphi izimpawu ezingenhla. Funani ukunakekelwa ngokushesha ngokulahlekelwa ukuzwa ngokuzumayo, ubuhlungu bendlebe, noma uketshezi oluphuma endlebeni. Uma nimnakekela umuntu ophila ne-dementia, tshelani udokotela wakhe ngezinguquko ekuzweni noma ekubonweni, ezingathinta isimo sengqondo, ukuphepha nokuthi umuntu angahlanganyela kahle kangakanani.
