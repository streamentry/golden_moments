Ukuhlala yedwa akusho ngokwaso ukuthi umuntu udinga usizo oluningi kangakanani. I-Alzheimer's Association ibonisa ukuthi abantu abaningi abanesifo se-Alzheimer baqhubeka behlala bodwa, ikakhulu ekuqaleni, nezinguquko ezilula, izixwayiso zokuphepha nosizo lwabanye. Umbuzo osebenzayo ucacile kakhulu: umuntu ufuna ukuqhubeka enza yini, futhi yini ezenza lawo msebenzi ube lula?

Qala leyo ngxoxo lapho kunesikhathi futhi kungekho nkinga esheshayo okufanele ixazululwe. Buza ukuthi yini ekhululeka kuye, yiziphi izimo ezibalulekile nokuthi yiluphi usizo angalamukela. Uhlelo olwenziwe ndawonye nalo luzofaniswa nempilo yakhe kunohlu lwemisebenzi olungelwe ngokukhathazeka kwabanye.

## Yini engenza impilo yansuku zonke ibe lula?

Sebenzisa izinsiza ezimbalwa ezijwayelekile ezihambisana nemikhuba yomuntu. I-[Alzheimer's Association incoma izinsiza ezisebenzayo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) njengamakhalenda, izinhlu nezikhumbuzo zezidingo zansuku zonke.

- **Khetha indawo eyodwa yezinto ezibalulekile.** Gcina okhiye, izibuko, isikhwama nefoni endaweni evunyelwene ngayo. Ithreyi eseduze komnyango noma ishalofu elibhalwe amagama lingenza umkhuba ube lula ukuwulandela.
- **Sebenzisa ikhalenda eyodwa noma uhlelo lokukhumbuza.** Khetha ikhalenda yodonga, ibhodi yokubhala, idayari yephepha noma ikhalenda yefoni umuntu ayijwayele ukuyisebenzisa. Gcina izinsuku zokuvakashela nemisebenzi ejwayelekile endaweni eyodwa esikhundleni sokuyihlakaza emasicini nasemanothini amaningi.
- **Yenza imikhuba ibonakale.** Uma umsebenzi ukhohlakala njalo, bhala inothi elifushane noma ubeke into edingekayo kulowo msebenzi endaweni ejwayelekile. Gcina izinyathelo zifushane futhi nizibuyekeze ndawonye uma zingasasizi.
- **Yenza imisebenzi ejwayelekile ibikezeleke.** Ukuthengiselwa okujwayelekile, ukuvakashelwa kwasekhaya noma ukuhleliwe kokudla kungancipisa izinqumo umuntu adinga ukuzithathela ngokwakhe.

Umhlahlandlela wethu wemikhuba [yansuku zonke](/activities/daily-routine/) unezindlela ezilula zokugcina usuku lujwayeleka ngaphandle kokulenza lube lukhuni. Injongo ukusekela izinqumo zomuntu, hhayi ukugcwalisa wonke ihora.

## Umndeni nabangane bangasiza kanjani ngosizo oluwusizo?

Vumelanani ngenani elincane labaxhumana nabo njalo. Ukuvakashela okufushane noma ucingo ngesikhathi esaziwayo kungaba wusizo kakhulu kunabantu abaningi abahlola ngezikhathi ezingalindelekile. Buza umuntu ukuthi ufuna ubani abandakanyeke nokuthi ngabe ungaqhuba ini umuntu ngamunye.

Yenza izinhlinzeko ezicacile. “Ingabe kungasiza uma ngikuletha ugrosa ngoLwesine?” kulula ukuyiphendula kunokuthi “Ngitshele uma udinga noma yini.” Umuntu oyisisekelo angasiza ngokuthenga, ukuthutha, ukudla, izimali noma ikhalenda lokuvakashela udokotela. I-[Alzheimer's Association incoma ukuhlela usizo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/i-have-alz/know-what-to-expect/if_you_live_alone) ngemisebenzi yasekhaya, ukudla, izokuthutha nezikhumbuzo zemithi uma leyo midingo ivela.

Uma abantu abaningi besiza, vumelanani ukuthi ubani ozophatha umsebenzi ngamunye nokuthi bazidlulisela kanjani izibuyekezo ezibalulekile. Gcina ulwazi lulinganiselwe kubantu umuntu abakhethe, ngaphandle uma ukukhathazeka okuphuthumayo mayelana nokuphepha kudinga usizo oluphuthumayo. Uma uhlala kude, bona [umhlahlandlela wokunakekela ukusuka kude](/caregiving/long-distance-caregiving-with-dementia/) ngezindlela zokuxhumana nabantu umuntu abakhethe. [Umhlahlandlela wokuxhumana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) unemininingwane eyengeziwe ngokuxoxa ngosizo ngaphandle kokuyenza isivivinyo.

## Umuntu angaqhubeka kanjani exhumene nabanye ehlala yedwa?

Khetha imisebenzi nokuxhumana umuntu ajabulela kuyo. Kungaba ucingo lwesonto, ukuvakashelwa ngumakhelwane, ukudla komphakathi, iqembu ajwayele ukulihlanganyela noma ukuhamba nomuntu amethembayo. Buza umuntu ukuthi yiziphi izinketho ezizwakala zimamukelekile kuye; ungacabangi ukuthi abavakashi noma amaqembu amaningi ahlala engcono.

Isikhathi esijwayelekile sokuxhumana nabanye singenza isonto libe lula ukulibona futhi libe nento umuntu alindele ngayo. Uma ukuhamba uya emcimbweni kuqala ukuba nzima, cabanga ngocingo, ukuthuthwa umuntu othembekile noma umcimbi omncane oseduze kwekhaya. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuxhumana nabanye](/brain-health/social-connection/) unezindlela ezingacindezeli zokuxhumana.

## Sizobona kanjani ukuthi uhlelo kufanele lushintshe?

Akukho uhlu olulodwa oluchaza ukuthi umuntu angahlala yedwa yini. Esikhundleni salokho, qaphela izinguquko ezithile empilweni yansuku zonke: umsebenzi umuntu ayewenza kahle manje ubangela inkinga ephindaphindayo, izidlo zikhishwa, izinsuku zokuvakashela ziyalahleka, noma uthi umsebenzi othile usuba nzima.

Buza umuntu ukuthi ukubonile yini. Nikeza ushintsho olulodwa oluwusizo bese uhlola ukuthi luyasiza yini. Uma kukhona izinkinga eziphindaphindayo mayelana nokudla, imithi, izimali, ukunyakaza noma ukuphepha ekhaya, buza umuntu ukuthi angakhuluma yini nodokotela wakhe noma nochwepheshe wezenhlalakahle. Ukuhlolwa okusemthethweni kungasiza ukuthola izinketho zosizo; akudingi ukuthi umuntu avume zonke izincomo.

Uma umuntu engaqinisekile ngosizo, qala kancane. Angase akhethe ukulethelwa ukudla kwesonto noma usizo lwemibhalo kunomuntu ahlala naye ekhaya, noma usizo lokunakekelwa komuntu siqu. Mhlanganyele ekukhetheni umuntu ozosiza nasekuhleleni izikhathi zokuvakashela. [Umhlahlandlela wokuphepha ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) uchaza izinguquko egumbini ngalinye uma ukukhathazeka kuhlobene nekhaya ngokwalo.

## Yini uma umuntu engafuni usizo?

Lalela isizathu ngaphambi kokunikeza enye inhlelo. Angase akhathazeke ngobumfihlo, izindleko, ukulahlekelwa ukulawula noma ukuba nomuntu ongamaziwa ekhaya. Buza ukuthi yiluphi usizo olwamukelekayo kuye, bese ubona ukuthi ukuzama okuncane kuyasebenza yini. Abanye bakhetha isikhumbuzo noma umsebenzi ahlanganyela ngawo; abanye bafuna usizo lomuntu oyedwa othembekile kunesevisi entsha.

Gwema ukuguqula ukuvakashelana zibe izivivinyo. Ucingo lungaba yingxoxo, hhayi uhlu lemibuzo mayelana nokuthi umuntu udle yini, uphuzile umuthi yini noma ukhumbule isikhathi sokuvakashela udokotela. Uma kukhona ingozi esheshayo, xhumana namasevisi ezimo eziphuthumayo asendaweni. Ngezinkinga eziqhubekayo, gcina umuntu ehileleke futhi ufune iseluleko kwithimba lakhe lokunakekela noma kwisevisi yosizo yasendaweni.

Ukuhlala yedwa kungaqhubeka kusebenza ngesixubo esishintshayo semikhuba, ukuxhumana nosizo lwezinto zansuku zonke. Buyekeza uhlelo nomuntu, gcina okusizayo bese uguqula izingxenye ezingasafaneleki.
