Ukuxilongwa kwe-dementia kungaletha imibuzo ngempilo, impilo yansuku zonke, usizo nesikhathi esizayo. Abantu baphendula ngezindlela ezahlukene: abanye bafuna isikhathi sokwamukela le ndaba, kanti abanye bafuna ukuqala ukuhlela. Akukho ukuphendula okukodwa okulungile noma isikhathi esisodwa esimisiwe. Qalani ngalokho okubaluleke komuntu manje, bese nibuyela emibuzweni yokunye lapho esekulungele.

[Isiqondisi se-Alzheimer's Association ngempilo emva kokuxilongwa](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) sikhomba abantu ekunakekelweni okulandelayo,osizweni lendawo nasekuhleleni isikhathi esizayo. [Iseluleko se-Dementia Australia ngokuhlangabezana nokuxilongwa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) sigcizelela ukuthi abantu basabela ngezindlela ezahlukene futhi bangafuna usizo uma befuna. Leli khasi liyisiqalo, hhayi uhlelo lomuntu lwezempilo, lwezomthetho noma lwezezimali.

## Yisiphi isinyathelo sokuqala esiwusizo?

Khetha umbuzo owenza leli sonto libe lula. Ungaba ukuthola ukuthi ngubani ozohlela ukunakekelwa okulandelayo komuntu, ukubuza lapho ingatholakala khona ulwazi oluthembekile ngokuxilongwa kwakhe, noma ukukhomba omethembayo ongakhuluma naye. Umuntu angase afune ukuthatha umhola, acele omunye umuntu ukuthi amsize noma alinde ngaphambi kokuthatha isinqumo.

Ekuvakashelweni okulandelayo, bhalani imibuzo embalwa ndawonye uma umuntu efisa lokho. Ungabuza ukuthi ukuxilongwa kusho ukuthini, yini engakaqinisekiswa, nini kufanele kulandelwe, kanye nokuthi kufanele uxhumane ngubani uma kuvela enye inkinga. Cela udokotela achaze noma yiliphi igama noma iseluleko esingacacile. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuvakashela udokotela](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) unohlu olufushane lokulungiselela.

Umuntu akudingeki afunde konke mayelana ne-dementia ngesikhathi esisodwa. Khetha ulwazi oluthembekile ngokuxilongwa akutholile, bese ubhala imibuzo evela kamuva. Qaphelani izithembiso, izinkulumo zokuthi kukhona ukwelapha noma iseluleko esithi wonke umuntu kufanele alandele ukwelashwa okufanayo noma uhlelo olufanayo.

## Ningatholaphi usizo?

Buza umuntu ukuthi yisiphi isidingo sosizo esingamsiza nokuthi ufisa ukuba kubandakanyeke bani. Umngani noma ilungu lomndeni elethembekile lingaba yingxoxo enhle yokuqala. Abanye abantu bakhetha iqembu labasekeli, umeluleki, inhlangano yendawo ye-dementia noma ingxoxo nethimba lezokunakekela. Inketho elungile incike komuntu nalokho okutholakala eduze.

Usizo lwabantu abaxiliwe kanye nomndeni nabangane. Umnakekeli angase adinge ukuphumula noma usizo emisebenzini yansuku zonke; umuntu angase afune ulwazi noma ithuba lokuhlangana nabanye abaphila ne-dementia. Lezi zidingo zingahluka. Buza ngaphambi kokuhlela iqembu, ukufaka isicelo sesikhathi, noma ukwabelana ngemininingwane yomuntu siqu.

[Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) unemibono yokucela abanye ukuthi babelane ngosizo. Uma umuntu efisa usizo oluningi emini, imihlahlandlela ye[mihlangano yansuku yabantu abadala](/caregiving/choosing-adult-day-program-dementia/) nye[kunakekelo ekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) ichaza imibuzo okufanele uyibuze ngezinsizakalo zendawo.

## Umuntu angaqhubeka kanjani ebamba iqhaza ezinqumweni?

Ukuxilongwa okwodwa akutshongo ukuthi umuntu angathatha yiziphi izinqumo noma ufisa ukubandakanyeka kanjani. Buzani umuntu ngqo. Mnikeni isikhathi, chazela isinqumo ngolimi olulula bese nibuza ukuthi yisiphi isisekelo esingenza ingxoxo ibe lula. Ezinye izinqumo kungase kube lula ukuzixoxa endaweni ethule noma ngezingxoxo ezimbalwa.

Uma umuntu efuna ukuhlela ukunakekelwa kwezempilo yakusasa, izimali noma indawo ahlala kuyo, qala ngalokho okubalulekile kuye nangabantu amethemba. [Umhlahlandlela ka-Dementia Australia wokuhlela](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) uncoma ukuxoxa ngezifiso nabantu abathembekile nochwepheshe abafanele. Imibhalo yezomthetho nemithetho yokuthatha izinqumo ihluka endaweni ngayinye, ngakho buza ochwepheshe basendaweni ngaphambi kokuthembela kuhlahlandlela jikelele. Ngosizo lwezikweletu zansuku zonke nokukhathazeka ngobuqili, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wokuphatha imali nge-dementia](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/).

### Yimuphi umbuzo engawubuza engxoxweni yokuqala ngokunakekelwa esikhathini esizayo?

Buza ukuthi umuntu ufuna ukukhuluma manje yini, bese uveza isihloko esisodwa ngesikhathi. [I-Alzheimer's Association incoma izingxoxo ezimbalwa ezincane, ulimi olulula nokulalela izifiso, izinkolelo nezinqubomgomo zomuntu](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [I-Alzheimer's Society iphawula ukuthi abantu bahluka ngokuzimisela kwabo ukucabanga kusengaphambili](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Kulungile ukuyeka futhi ubuyele kwelinye ilanga.

Uma umuntu efuna izikhuthazo, zama enye yalezi:

- "Yiziphi izingxenye zosuku lwakho ozifisa kakhulu ukuzigcina?"
- "Ufisa ukuthi ubani abandakanyeke lapho sikhuluma ngokunakekelwa esikhathini esizayo?"
- "Yini esenza uzizwe uphephile futhi uhlonishwa uma udinga usizo?"
- "Kukhona okufisa ithimba lakho lokunakekela ukuthi likwazi ngezinto ezibalulekile kuwe?"
- "Ufisa ngibhale phansi okushilo nokuthi ubani okufanele akubone?"

Lalela ngaphandle kokucela isinqumo ngokushesha. [Ukuhlola kwe-National Institute on Aging kubonisa ukuthi izingxoxo ezijulile ngamanani nezifiso zibaluleke kakhulu ekuhleleni ukunakekelwa kusengaphambili](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Uma umuntu evumelana, bhala amagama akhe, usuku nabantu ababekhona. Cela imvume ngaphambi kokwabelana ngalesi sibhalo. Lezi zibhalo zingasiza kwezinye izingxoxo, kodwa azisiyo imibhalo yomthetho noma imiyalo yezokwelapha.

Ungabi nezinqumo ngokusekelwe kuphela ekuxilongweni noma engxoxweni eyodwa. Izifiso zomuntu zingashintsha, futhi angacela ukumisa noma ukubuyela kwesihloko ngesikhathi esizayo. Mbandakanyeni umuntu ezinqumweni ezimthinta njengoba efisa.

## Ubani okufanele azi ngokuxilongwa?

Umuntu uyanquma ukuthi uzotshela abanye yini, nini nangayiphi indlela, ngaphandle uma kudingeka iseluleko sochwepheshe ngenxa yokuphepha noma yomthetho. Buza ukuthi ufuna ukutshela bani nokuthi ufisa ukuthi laba bantu bazi ini. Angakhetha umuntu omethembayo kuqala, noma angafuni ukwabelana ngolwazi kubantu abaningi.

Uma umuntu efuna usizo ngengxoxo, buza ukuthi ufisa usho ini nokuthi ufisa ukuba yini lapho. Yabelana kuphela ngolwazi avumelene ngalo. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuxhumana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) uchaza izingxoxo ezihlonipha umndeni nabanye abantu.

## Ingabe impilo yansuku zonke ingaqhubeka kanjani ngenkathi nizijwayeza?

Gcinani izinhlelo nemisebenzi ebalulekile komuntu lapho isazizwa ikhululekile khona. Ukuxilongwa akusho ukuthi zonke izithakazelo, ukuphuma noma indima ekhaya kumele kuyeke. Uma into ithatha isikhathi eside, buza umuntu ukuthi ufisa ukushintsha ini. Uchungechunge olufushane lwendwendwe, umculo ojwayelekile, usizo kwesinye isinyathelo noma isikhathi sosuku esihlukile kungase kumlungele kangcono.

Umuntu angase futhi adinge isikhathi sokucubungula iroyi lalolu daba ngaphandle kokuxoxa ngezinhlelo. Qhubekani nokuhlola kahle ningabenzi zonke izingxoxo zibe yimihlangano yokunakekela. Kuyinto enhle ukuphumula ekuhleleni nichithe isikhathi ndawonye ngendlela ejwayelekile.

## Kufanele nibuze ochwepheshe nini?

Buzani udokotela womuntu ngemibuzo ethile ngokuxilongwa kwakhe, impilo yakhe, imithi yakhe noma izimpawu zakhe. Ngemibuzo yezomthetho nezezimali, khulumani nochwepheshe abafanele abaqonda imithetho yendawo umuntu ahlala kuyo. Iwebhusayithi ejwayelekile ayikwazi ukukutshela ukuthi yiliphi ukwelapha, ukuthi yiliphi ledokhumenti noma yiliphi isevisi elifanele umuntu othile.

Ukudideka okuvelayo noma ukushintsha okungazelelwe empilweni kudinga usizo lwezokwelapha ngokushesha. [Iseluleko se-NHS ngokudideka okuvelayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sithi kufanele uthole usizo lwezokwelapha masinyane. Sebenzisa uhlelo lwezimo eziphuthumayo noma lokunakekelwa okuphuthumayo endaweni ahlala kuyo umuntu; ungacabangi ukuthi ushintsho olungazelelwe kuyingxenye evamile yesimo sakhe.

Uma ukuhlela sekukhathaza kakhulu, khetha umuntu omethembayo noma isevisi eyodwa ukuze isize ihlukanise imibuzo ibe izihloko ezidingekayo manje nezezikhathi ezizayo. Umuntu angasetha isivinini sakhe futhi anqume ukuthi ufuna usizo ngani. Ukuxilongwa kubalulekile, kodwa akuchazi izithakazelo zomuntu, ubudlelwano bakhe noma impilo yakhe yansuku zonke.
