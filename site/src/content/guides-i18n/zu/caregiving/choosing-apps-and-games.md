Izitolo ze-app zigcwele imidlalo ebhalwe ukuthi ingeyabadala, yenkumbulo noma ye-dementia. Eminye yenziwe ngokucophelela. Eminye yengeza izethembiso ezizwakala njengezezokwelapha, izibali sikhathi eziguqula umdlalo ube ukuhlolwa, noma izikhangiso nokuqoqwa kwedatha. Leli khasi linikeza indlela yokuthi nahlulele noma iyiphi i-app nina ngokwenu. Asizilinganisi noma sizibuyekeze ezinye izimikhiqizo.

## Yini okufanele ngiyinqume ngaphambi kokukhetha i-app ye-dementia?

Nqumani ukuthi nifuna i-app ikwenze ini. “Umdlalo kaMama” ungasho izinto ezihluke kakhulu, futhi ukukhetha okungcono kunhloso eyodwa kuvame ukuba ukukhetha okungalungile kwenye.

| Inhloso | Ibukeka kanjani | Okufanele nikufune |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ukuvusa ingqondo kancane | Ukufanisa, ukuhlunga, amaphazili alula, imidlalo yamagama | Ubunzima obulungiseka, izithombe ezihlala zibonakala, kungabi khona ingcindezi yesikhathi |
| Ukukhumbula izinto zakudala | Izithombe zakudala, izisho ezijwayelekile, umculo wobusha bakhe | Izithombe zenu, izikhuthazo ezivulekile, kungabi khona imibuzo yokuhlola |
| Ukuzola | Izithombe ezihamba kancane, imisindo ethulile, ukuphefumula, imvelo | Kulula ukuqala nokumisa, akukho okucwayizayo, akukho okumangazayo |
| Ukuxhumana | Ukudlala noma ukukhuluma ndawonye, ngokwenu noma ngevidiyo | Kwenzelwe abantu ababili, izikhuthazo zengxoxo |

Ucwaningo lwe-“cognitive stimulation” ehleliwe yabantu abane-dementia ikakhulukazi lubandakanya izikhathi zeqembu ezihlelwa abasebenzi abaqeqeshiwe, ezinengxoxo, imidlalo yamagama, amaphazili, umculo nemisebenzi yokudala. [Ukubuyekezwa kwe-Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005562_can-cognitive-stimulation-benefit-people-dementia) kwathola izinzuzo ezincane ngokwesilinganiso, ezihluka phakathi kwezifundo. Uhlelo olunjalo lokuxhumana nabantu, olunhlobonhlobo aluyona into efanayo ne-app ewodwa, ngakho thathani noma iyiphi i-app njengomsebenzi owodwa wokwabelana phakathi kwemisebenzi eminingi.

## Ngazi kanjani ukuthi i-app ihambisana namandla omuntu?

Bukani ukuthi aphendula kanjani, kunokuthembela ekuxilongweni noma egameni lesigaba. Abantu ababili “esigabeni esifanayo” bangahluka kakhulu ngokubona, ukuzwa, ukulawula izandla, ulimi nokukhululeka ngezikrini. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) iphakamisa ukukhumbula izintshisekelo zakudala namandla amanje futhi nigxile ekujabuleni kunasempumelelweni.

Izimpawu zokuhambisana okuhle:

- uqala noma ahlanganyele ngaphandle kokunxenxwa kakhulu;
- uyamamatheka, aphawule noma axoxe indaba;
- amaphutha abonakala engamkhathazi;
- ujabulela ukumisa uma sekuyisikhathi.

Izimpawu zokuhambisana okubi:

- ukuphindaphinda ukuthi “Angikwazi” noma “lokhu kuyiphutha”;
- ukudideka ngokuthi kufanele kuthintwe ini;
- ukukhathazeka, ihlazo noma ukusunduza idivayisi;
- isebenza kuphela uma nina nenza cishe yonke into.

Usizi luyisikhombisi, hhayi into okufanele idluliselwe ngenkani. Bheka [okufanele nikwenze uma esho ukuthi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/). Ukuze nithole imibono ngokwezigaba ngaphandle kwama-app, bheka imisebenzi [yesigaba sokuqala](/activities/early-stage/) [neyesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/).

## Yiziphi izici zomklamo okufanele nizifune noma nizigweme?

Funani umklamo othulile, olula, wabadala, futhi nigweme noma yini ekhulisa ingcindezi.

| Funani | Qaphelani |
| --- | --- |
| Izithombe nombhalo omkhulu, umehluko omkhulu wemibala | Izithonjana ezincane, izikrini ezixinene |
| Isenzo esisodwa esicacile ngesikrini ngasinye | Izinkinobho eziningi, amamenyu, noma imiyalo yezinyathelo eziningi |
| Akukho sibali sikhathi, noma isibali ongasivala | Izibali zokubala emuva, imivuzo yesivinini, “dlula isikhathi sakho” |
| Akukho maphuzu, noma impendulo ethambile | Amaphuzu, izimpilo, ukulandelana kokunqoba, izikhundla, “iminyaka yobuchopho” |
| Amaphutha aphathwa ngokuthula | Ama-buzzer, izimpawu ezibomvu ze-X, imiyalezo ethi “Kungalungile” |
| Izithombe zabadala, zomhlaba wangempela | Izindikimba zamakhathuni noma ezingane |
| Isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ayidingi akhawunti ukuqala | Kudingeka ubhalise, ungene noma ulande okuqukethwe kuqala |
| Kulula ukumisa nokuyeka | Ama-pop-up acela ukuqhubeka nokudlala noma ukukala i-app |
| Akukho zikhangiso, intengo ecacile kanye noma yokubhalisa | Izikhangiso, ama-loot box, izikhumbuzo ezivamile zokuthenga |
| Kulula ukuthi umuntu wesibili ahlanganyele | Umdlali oyedwa kuphela, umklamo wokuncintisana |

Izikhangiso nezikhumbuzo zokuthenga kulula ukuzithinta ngephutha.

## Ngihlola kanjani ubumfihlo be-app?

Hlolani ukuthi i-app iqoqani, yabelana nobani, nokuthi ningayisusa yini. Ngaphambi kokuyifaka:

1. **Funda isifinyezo sobumfihlo sesitolo.** [Isigaba se-Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) se-Google Play kanye [nesigaba solwazi lobumfihlo](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102399) se-App Store zibala izinhlobo zedatha i-app engase iziqoqe futhi yabelane ngazo. Zombili izinkampani zithi abathuthukisi bakha lolu lwazi bona ngokwabo, ngakho yindawo yokuqala, hhayi ukuhlolwa.
2. **Buzani ukuthi idinga yini i-akhawunti.** Uma i-app ibonisa izithombe noma imidlalo elula kuphela, kungase kungabi khona isizathu sokuthi izazi igama lenu, i-imeyili noma usuku lokuzalwa.
3. **Hlolani izimvume.** Ingabe umdlalo wokufanisa udinga ngempela indawo, uhlu loxhumana nabo, imakrofoni noma yonke ingqoqo yezithombe zenu? Valani noma yini i-app engayidingi.
4. **Funani izikhangiso nokuhlaziya.** Izikhangiso namathuluzi okulandela angathumela idatha ngokusetshenziswa kwe-app ezinkampanini ezinye.
5. **Thola ukuthi izithombe ziyaphi.** Uma nengeza izithombe zomndeni, ziyahlala kudivayisi noma ziyalayishelwa kuseva? Ningazisusa yini?
6. **Hlolani ukususwa.** Bhekani inqubomgomo yobumfihlo noma isifinyezo sedatha ukuze nazi ukuthi nisusa kanjani idatha ne-akhawunti. I-app ethi idatha ayikwazi ukususwa idinga ukucophelela okwengeziwe, ikakhulukazi uma ibamba izithombe noma imininingwane yezempilo.
7. **Funda inqubomgomo yobumfihlo** ukuze nithole izimpendulo ezicacile ngokuthi kuqoqwani, kungani, isikhathi esingakanani, nokuthi kwabelwana nobani.

### Ingabe i-HIPAA ivikela ulwazi lwama-app e-dementia?

Ngokuvamile cha, okumangaza abantu abaningi. I-U.S. Department of Health and Human Services [ichaza](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) ukuthi i-HIPAA isebenza ezinhlelweni zezempilo, kubahlinzeki abaningi bokunakekelwa kwezempilo, kuma-health care clearinghouse nakubalingani babo bebhizinisi. Ezimeni eziningi ayivikeli idatha oyifaka ku-app yokusetshenziswa komuntu siqu, ngaphandle kokuthi i-app inikezwe yinhlangano ehlanganisiwe noma umlingani wayo webhizinisi. “I-app ye-dementia” esitolo se-app ivame ukuba ngaphandle kwe-HIPAA, noma ikhuluma ngezempilo. (I-HIPAA ngumthetho wase-United States; ezweni lakini kungase kube khona imithetho ehlukile yokuvikela idatha.)

### Yini evikela idatha yezempilo kuma-app angahlanganiswa yi-HIPAA?

Umthetho we-Federal Trade Commission we-Health Breach Notification Rule uhlanganisa amaningi awo. Ngo-2024 i-FTC [yaqeda ngokusemthethweni izinguquko](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule) ezicacisa ukuthi umthetho usebenza kuma-app ezempilo nobuchwepheshe obufanayo obungahlanganiswa yi-HIPAA. “Ukwephulwa” kuhlanganisa ukudalulwa okungagunyaziwe, hhayi nje ukugqekezwa, futhi izinkampani kumele zazise abantu abathintekile ne-FTC. Lokho kuyinetha yokuvikela ngemva kokuba okuthile kungahambi kahle, hhayi isethembiso sokuthi i-app iqoqa kuphela okudingayo.

## Ngifunda kanjani izimangalo ngocwaningo nobufakazi?

Funda izimangalo zobufakazi kancane, futhi nibe nokungabaza okukhulu ngezethembiso ezinkulu kakhulu. Imibuzo okufanele nibuze:

- **Yini ngempela eyacwaningwa?** Ucwaningo lohlelo olulodwa lokuqeqeshwa alukhombisi ukuthi wonke umdlalo ku-app usebenza ngendlela efanayo.
- **Ubani owacwaningwa?** Imiphumela kubantu abadala abaphilile ingase ingasebenzi komuntu ophila ne-dementia.
- **Yini eyalinganiswa?** Ukuthuthuka emdlalweni ngokwawo kuhlukile ezinguqukweni empilweni yansuku zonke, engxoxweni, esimweni sengqondo noma ekuzimelaneni.
- **Ucwaningo lunamandla kangakanani?** Isilingo esilawulwayo esinokukhethwa okungahleliwe (RCT) siqhathanisa iqembu elisebenzisa i-app neqembu elingayisebenzisi. Uhlaka oluhle, kodwa i-RCT eyodwa encane noma emfushane akuyona ubufakazi. Funani izifundo eziningana, ezenziwa abacwaningi abazimele, ezivumelana.
- **Ingabe ithi iguqula isifo?** Ucwaningo olubonisa ukuthuthuka okuncane ekuhlolweni akuyona ubufakazi bokuthi i-app ilibazisa, ivimbela noma yelapha i-dementia.

Ikhasi le-National Institute on Aging elithi [impilo yokuqonda nabantu abadala](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults) lichaza isilingo esikhulu esixhaswe yi-NIH sokuqeqeshwa kokuqonda okuhlelekile, bese lixwayisa abafundi ukuthi baqaphele izimangalo zokuthi eminye imidlalo yekhompyutha neye-inthanethi ingathuthukisa inkumbulo nokucabanga. Lithi akukabikho ubufakazi obwanele bokuthi ama-app e-brain-training atholakala emalini anomthelela ofanayo nokuqeqeshwa okuhlolwe kuleso silingo. Ungafunda kabanzi emihlahlandlela yethu ethi [Imidlalo yobuchopho nobufakazi](/brain-health/brain-games/) neyethi [Lokho okushiwo ucwaningo ngokuvimbela i-dementia](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/).

### Yisiphi isibonelo esaziwayo sesimangalo esiyiphutha?

NgoJanuwari 2016, i-FTC [yamemezela isivumelwano](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) nabenzi be-Lumosity, uhlelo lwe-“brain training”. I-FTC yasola ukuthi inkampani yathi, ngaphandle kobufakazi obanele, imidlalo yayo ingalibazisa ukwehla kokuqonda okuhlobene neminyaka futhi ivikele ekuphazamisekeni okuncane kokuqonda, i-dementia nesifo i-Alzheimer's. Inkampani yavuma ukukhokha $2 million futhi kumele ibe nobufakazi besayensi obunekhono nobunokwethenjelwa ngaphambi kokwenza izimangalo ezinjalo esikhathini esizayo. Icala liyisikhumbuzo esiwusizo sokuthi ulimi lokukhangisa alukhona ubufakazi.

### Yimaphi amagama angamafulegi abomvu?

Qaphelani uma i-app noma isikhangiso sithi:

- “ivimbela,” “iguqula,” “imisa” noma “yelapha” i-dementia noma i-Alzheimer's;
- “ifakazelwe ngokomtholampilo” ngaphandle kokuqamba ucwaningo, ukuthi bekungobani kulo, nokuthi yini eyalinganiswa;
- inikeza “iminyaka yobuchopho” noma amaphuzu enkumbulo ashiwo njengokuxilongwa;
- izokwenza ubuchopho “bubukhali,” “bukhule” noma “buqeqeshwe”;
- isebenzisa ubufakazi bamakhasimende (testimonials) njengobufakazi.

Uma ningaqiniseki ngesimangalo, buzani udokotela womuntu.

## I-Golden Moments ihlala kuphi?

I-Golden Moments inikeza imisebenzi elula yokwabelana, hhayi ukuhlolwa kwenkumbulo, ukwelashwa noma iseluleko sezokwelapha. Imidlalo yethu, okuyi-[Amapheya ezithombe](/games/picture-pairs/), [Amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/), [Amapheya ezithombe zomndeni](/games/photo-pairs/), [I-bingo yezithombe](/games/picture-bingo/) kanye nomdlalo othi [Ukuphefumula okuthulile](/games/calm-breathing/), yenzelwe ukujabula nengxoxo. Ayihloli, ayelapheli, ayivimbi futhi ayilinganisi i-dementia, futhi asenzi izimangalo ngenkumbulo noma ukucabanga.

Siphinde senze i-app yamahhala, [i-Golden Moments ye-iPhone, i-iPad ne-Android](/app/). Inokufanisa izithombe okuthambile ngamakhadi amakhulu, imidlalo enezithombe zomndeni enizikhethayo namaphakheji ezithombe asebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti, izikhangiso, isibali sikhathi noma amaphuzu. Uhlu lokuhlola olufanayo luyasebenza: ilebula yobumfihlo ye-App Store yayo ifundeka ngokuthi “Data Not Collected”, futhi [inqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-app](/app-privacy/) ichaza lapho amakhophi ezithombe agcinwa khona.

Sizokujabulela ukuba nisihlole kunokusethemba. Sebenzisani uhlu lokuhlola kuleli khasi, funda [ikhasi lethu lobumfihlo](/privacy/) kanye [nesaziso sethu sezokwelapha](/medical-disclaimer/), futhi niyeke ukusebenzisa noma yini, kuhlanganise neyethu, engamfanele umuntu eniyamnakekelayo.

## Iyiphi indlela enengqondo yokuzama i-app entsha?

Zamani ndawonye emasontweni ambalwa nibuke okwenzekayo.

1. **Yilungiseni kuqala,** nisebenzisa [umhlahlandlela wethu wokulungisa ithebhulethi nocingo](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/), futhi niqale ezingeni elilula kakhulu.
2. **Gcinani izikhathi zifushane,** niphawule ukumamatheka, ingxoxo nokuzola, futhi nigcine ngomzuzu omuhle.
3. **Yiyekeni uma ibangela ukukhathazeka,** noma ngabe ihlolwe kahle kangakanani.
