Ulungiselela konke, unikeza umdlalo noma uhambo olufushane, bese uzwa u-“Cha.” Noma umsebenzi uqala kahle bese uphela ngethebhulethi esunduzwayo kanye nezwi elinzima. Kuyadumaza, futhi kuvame kakhulu. Ukwenqaba nokukhungatheka kuvame ukuba umyalezo, hhayi inkani. Ukuqonda ukuthi lowo myalezo uthini kuvame ukukukhombisa ongakuzama ngokulandelayo.

## Kungani umuntu ophila nesifo sokuwohloka komqondo enqaba ukwenza imisebenzi?

Ngokuvamile kuba nesizathu, ngisho noma umuntu engakwazi ukusisho ngamazwi. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) iyaphawula ukuthi ukumelana kungase kusho ukuthi umuntu akakwazi ukwenza lo msebenzi noma uyesaba ukuwuzama. Izizathu ezivamile zihlanganisa:

- **Kuzwakala kunzima kakhulu.** Umsebenzi manje udinga amakhono isifo sokuwohloka komqondo esiwenzé aba nzima, futhi ukwehluleka phambi komunye umuntu kuyahlambalaza.
- **Kuzwakala kufana nokwezingane.** Abantu abadala bayaqaphela izithombe ezifana nezamahlaya, amazwi aculayo nezenzo ezibukeka sengathi zenzelwe izingane.
- **Ukukhathala.** Abantu abaningi baba namandla amancane kamuva ngosuku. Bheka [izenzo ezithulile ntambama](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
- **Ukungakhululeki emzimbeni.** I-[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) ibala ubuhlungu, ukungalali kahle, inkinga yokubona noma yokuzwa, ukuqunjelwa, indlala, ukoma nemiphumela engemihle yemithi emisha njengezimbangela ezingaba khona zokucindezeleka.
- **Kuneningi okwenzekayo.** Umabonakude onomsindo, izingxoxo eziningi noma igumbi eligcwele kungakhungathekisa.
- **Ukuzizwa ephoqwa.** I-NIA ibala ukuphoqwa ukuba enze into isifo i-Alzheimer esiyenzé yaba nzima kakhulu, noma ukukhumbula izehlakalo noma abantu, njengembangela yokungahlaliseki.
- **Umoya ophansi.** Ukulahlekelwa intshisekelo ezintweni kungase kube uphawu lokudangala, okufanele kukhulunywe ngakho nodokotela.
- **Umane nje akafuni.** Abantu abadala bavunyelwe ukuthi bathi cha.

## Yini okufanele ngiyizame uma ethi cha?

Yamukela u-cha ngokuthula, bese ushintsha okuthile: isikhathi, umsebenzi, ubulukhuni noma indlela ocela ngayo.

1. **Kuyeke okwamanje.** “Kulungile. Mhlawumbe kamuva.” Bese udlulela kokunye ngaphandle kokububula.
2. **Zama futhi kamuva.** I-Alzheimer's Association iphakamisa ukuzama futhi kamuva, noma ngesikhathi esihlukile sosuku.
3. **Yenza kube kuncane.** Ukufanisa okukodwa esikhundleni somdlalo wonke. Ithawula elilodwa esikhundleni sebhasikidi lezingubo. Izithombe ezimbalwa, izinketho ezimbalwa, izithombe zishiywe zibonakala.
4. **Nikeza izinketho ezimbili.** “Ungathanda ukubuka izithombe zezinyoni noma ukulalela umculo?” Ukukhetha kubuyisela umuzwa wokuzilawula.
5. **Cela usizo esikhundleni sokumhlola.** “Ungangisiza ukuhlunga lokhu?” noma “Ungangitshela ukuthi yimuphi umbala obukeka umuhle?” Ukuzizwa edingeka kuhluke kakhulu ekuzizweni ehlolwa.
6. **Qala wena ngokwakho.** Qala umsebenzi eceleni kwakhe ngaphandle kokubuza. Abantu abaningi bayajoyina uma okuthile sekuvele kwenzeka, futhi uma bengajoyini, nisuke nisachithe isikhathi ndawonye.
7. **Shintshela kokuthile okunyakazisa umzimba noma okududuza.** Uhambo olufushane, umculo, ukudla okulula, ukugoqa izingubo, noma ukuphumula ndawonye ngokwenza [ukuphefumula okuthulile](/games/calm-breathing/). I-NIA iphakamisa ukuqondisa ukunaka emculweni, encwadini noma ohambweni.
8. **Mvumele akwenze ngendlela yakhe.** Uma efuna ukudlala ngendlela ehlukile kunemithetho, vumelana nakho. I-Alzheimer's Association yeluleka ukuba uvumelane nezimo.

## Yini okufanele ngiyisho uma ekhungathekile?

Yehlisa ijubane, wehlise izwi lakho futhi umduduze. [Isiqondiso se-Alzheimer's Association mayelana nokungahlaliseki](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation) siphakamisa ukuhlehla bese ucela imvume, usebenzise amazwi athulile nanethemba, futhi unikeze ukukhetha okuqondisiwe phakathi kwezinketho ezimbili. Imisho ehambisana nalokho:

- “Ngiyaxolisa, lo uyacasula. Asiwuyeke.”
- “Wenza kahle. Ngikhona lapha.”
- “Unaso isikhathi sokungisiza ngokunye?”
- “Singaphuza itiye esikhundleni salokho?”

Bese uqeda umsebenzi ngaphandle kokuwenza udaba olukhulu. [Amathiphu ethu okuxhumana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) anokunye ongakusho.

## Yini okufanele ngiyigweme?

Ezinye izindlela zemvelo zokuphendula zivame ukwenza izinto zibe zimbi nakakhulu.

- **Ukuphikisana noma ukuphoqa.** “Ake uqhubeke, okukodwa nje” noma “Uyawuthanda lo msebenzi” akuvamile ukusiza.
- **Ukubuza imibuzo yokuhlola noma ukulungisa amaphutha.** “Uyakwazi ukuthi lokhu kuyini” noma “Cha, lokho akulungile” kwengeza ingcindezi.
- **Ukukuthatha njengento eqondiswe kuwe.** I-NIA ikhumbuza abanakekeli ukuthi yisifo, hhayi umuntu, esidala lezi zinguquko.
- **Ukumcindezela noma ukumphuthuma.** I-Alzheimer's Association yeluleka ukuthi ungaphakamisi izwi, ungabonisi ukwesaba, ungamvaleli ekhoneni, ungamsondi kakhulu, ungamvimbi, ungamgxeki, ungamzibi noma uphikisana naye, futhi ugweme ukunyakaza okungazelelwe angakuboni.
- **Ukusebenzisa ukuzizwa enecala.** “Ngikulungisele konke lokhu” kwenza nobabili nizizwe kabi nakakhulu.
- **Ukumbiza ngamagama.** “Unenkani” noma “unzima” kufihla isizathu sangempela futhi kwenze kube nzima ukuxazulula inkinga.

## Ngingathola kanjani ukuthi yini ebangela ukwenqaba okuphindaphindayo?

Gcina inothi elilula isonto elilodwa noma amabili. I-NIA ithi ukulandelela izinguquko zokuziphatha kungasiza abanakekeli nabasebenzi bezempilo babone iphethini futhi bathole imbangela.

Bhala phansi:

- isikhathi sosuku;
- ukuthi yini oyinikezile nokuthi ucele kanjani;
- obekwenzeka eduze kwenu (umsindo, izivakashi, umabonakude);
- ukuthi ulale kanjani, udle futhi waphuza kanjani, nokuthi ugcine nini ukuya endlini yangasese;
- noma yimiphi imithi emisha noma ukugula kwakamuva.

Amaphethini avame ukuvela ngokushesha: ukwenqaba okwenzeka kuphela ngemva kuka-16:00, kuphela ngomdlalo othile, noma kuphela uma umabonakude uvuliwe. [Umhlahlandlela we-Family Caregiver Alliance mayelana nokuziphatha okuhambisana nesifo sokuwohloka komqondo](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) ukusho kalula: asikwazi ukushintsha umuntu, kodwa singashintsha indlela yethu yokuziphatha nendawo ezungezile.

## Kufanele ngikhulume nodokotela nini mayelana nokwenqaba noma ukukhungatheka?

Khuluma nodokotela uma ukuziphatha kushintsha kungazelelwe, ngokushesha, noma kanye nezimpawu zokugula. I-NIA ithi izinguquko zokuziphatha ezivele kungazelelwe noma ezishintsha ngokushesha, ikakhulukazi uma umuntu eke waba nokutheleleka noma izinguquko zakamuva emithini, kufanele zibikwe kudokotela ngokushesha.

Xhumana nodokotela wakhe ngokushesha uma ubona:

- ukudideka okuvele kungazelelwe noma ushintsho olukhulu emahoreni noma ezinsukwini;
- izimpawu zobuhlungu, njengokunyakazisa ubuso ngenxa yobuhlungu, ukuvikela ingxenye ethile yomzimba, noma ubunzima obusha bokunyakaza;
- umkhuhlane noma ezinye izimpawu zokutheleleka;
- imithi emisha noma eshintshiwe elandelwa ushintsho ekuziphatheni;
- ukulahlekelwa intshisekelo cishe ezintweni zonke, ukuba nezinyembezi noma ukuhoxa okuhlala isikhathi esingaphezu kwamasonto ambalwa;
- ukwenqaba ukudla noma ukuphuza.

I-Alzheimer's Association incoma ukuhlolwa kwezempilo okuphelele kunoma ubani onezimpawu zokuziphatha, ikakhulukazi uma zivela kungazelelwe. I-Family Caregiver Alliance nayo yeluleka ukuthi kuqala uxhumane nodokotela, ngoba izinkinga zokuziphatha zingase zibe nembangela yezempilo. Ungaqali, ungayeki noma ungashintshi noma yimuphi umuthi ngaphandle kokukhuluma nodokotela noma usokhemisi.

Uma kukhona osengozini noma kunezimpawu zesimo esiphuthumayo sezempilo, shayela u-911. I-NIA iphakamisa ukuthi utshele ophendulayo ocingweni ukuthi umuntu uphila nesifo sokuwohloka komqondo. Ukuze uthole izinyathelo zokuphepha ezisheshayo lapho intukuthelo iba udlame, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu mayelana nentukuthelo nodlame esifweni sokuwohloka komqondo](/caregiving/when-anger-becomes-aggression-with-dementia/).

## Kulungile yini uma singenzi lutho nhlobo?

Yebo. Ngezinye izinsuku umsebenzi omuhle kakhulu ukuthi kungabi bikho msebenzi: ukuhlala ndawonye, ukubambana izandla, ukulalela ingoma ethandwayo, noma ukubuka izinyoni ngefasitela. Lokho akukhona ukwehluleka. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokwenza izinto ndawonye](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) uyachaza ukuthi kungani ukuyeka kusenesikhathi, noma ungaze uqale nhlobo, kungaba yisinqumo esifanele.
