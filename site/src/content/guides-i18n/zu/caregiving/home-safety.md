Inguquko yokuphepha ekhaya kufanele ixazulule inkinga umuntu asheshe wahlangabezana nayo, hhayi ukuguqula wonke amagumbi abe uhlu lwemikhawulo. Qala ngegumbi elilodwa nenkinga eyodwa: indlela emnyama eya ebhavini, ucingo oluwela endleleni noma ukungaqiniseki ngesisetshenziswa esithile sasekhishini. Injongo ukwenza imikhuba yansuku zonke ibe lula ukuyiqhubeka.

I-[Alzheimer's Association home safety checklist](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/home-safety) ihlanganisa izingozi ezikhishini, ezigcotsheni, ezigumbini zokulala, endaweni yokugcina izinto nasezindaweni zangaphandle. Iphinde ithi izinguquko kufanele zisekele izidingo nokuzimela komuntu. I-[Alzheimer's Society's home equipment guide](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/what-equipment-improve-adapt-home-person-dementia) ichaza ukuthi izinguquko ezincane zingenza imikhuba yansuku zonke ibe lula futhi iphephe. Alikho uhlu olufanelana nowonke umuntu noma nawo wonke amakhaya.

## Ngiqala kanjani ukubuyekeza ukuphepha ekhaya?

Khetha igumbi elilodwa lapho okuthile sekuhambe kabi noma sekuzwakala kunzima. Uma umuntu efuna ukubamba iqhaza, hambani ndawonye nibheke ukuthi yini ezwakala inzima kuye. Umnakekeli angase abone ucingo oluwela phansi; umuntu angase aphawule ukuthi isiwushintsho sokukhanya sinzima ukusifinyelela noma ukuthi isihlalo ajwayele ukusihlala sesishintshile. Zombili izimbono ziyasiza ekukhetheni inguquko eqondile.

Bhala inkinga ngamazwi alula: “Indlela yasengadini imnyama ngemva kokudla,” “Umnyango wangasese ukhula kabuhlungu ebusuku,” noma “Isiphephisi sibekwe eduze kwesinki.” Bese ukhetha isinyathelo sokwenza esisodwa bese ubona ukuthi siyasiza yini. Awudingi ukuthenga izinsiza ngenxa yazo zonke izinkinga. Ukukhanya okungcono, indlela ehlanzekile noma ukuhambisa into esetshenziswa njalo ukuyifinyelela kalula kungase kwanele.

I-[Alzheimer's Society's home safety tips](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/tips-staying-safe-secure-home-dementia) ifaka izibonelo ezabelwana ngabantu abathintekile yi-dementia, njengokugcina ulwazi oluwusizo lulula ukulutholo nokusebenzisa izibani ezizwelayo ebusuku. Zithathe njengemibono okufanele uyicabangele, hhayi imiyalo okufanele wonke umuntu ayilandele.

## Yini okufanele ngiyihlole egumbini ngalinye?

### Izindawo zokungena, izindlela nezitebhisi

- **Yenza indlela ibonakale kalula.** Hlola ukuthi izindawo zokungena, izitebhisi nendlela eya egumbini lokulala iya ebhavini inokukhanya okungaguquki. Isibani sasebusuku noma esizwela ukunyakaza kungasiza uma umuntu evame ukuvuka ebusuku.
- **Vula izindlela zokuhamba.** Susa amakhebuli avulekile, impahla edidekile nezinto ezingasebenza njengezingozi zokukhubeka. Hlola ukuthi amakhaphethi alele phansi; wasuse noma uwabophe kuphela uma ebangela inkinga.
- **Bheka izinguquko zezinga lomhlaba.** Uma isinyathelo noma ukusongela kuba nzima ukukubona, buza ochwepheshe bemisebenzi noma ochwepheshe bokuphepha ekhaya ukuthi ushintsho luphi lungathuthukisa ukubonakala ngaphandle kokudala omunye umphetho okwenze ukhubeke.
- **Gcina izinto ezijwayelekile lapho zisho khona.** Isihlalo, isibani noma into evame ukusetshenziswa ingasiza umuntu alandele umkhuba. Ukuhambisa wonke into ukuze indawo ibukeke ihlanzekile kungenza indlela ingajwayeleki.

### Ikhishi

- **Hlola ukusetshenziswa kwezisetshenziswa.** Uma kube nenkinga nesitofu noma esinye isisetshenziswa, bheka izinketho zokuphepha ezenzelwe leso sisetshenziswa esithile. I-Alzheimer's Association ibhala izinto ezifana nezici zokuvala ngokuzenzakalelayo kanye nezidivayisi zokuphepha esitofini njengezinketho. Funa iseluleko esifanele ngaphambi kokushintsha izisetshenziswa zegesi noma zikagesi.
- **Gcina imithi nezinto ezingezona ukudla zikude nokudla.** I-Association incoma ukugcina imithi ngokuphepha kunokuyishiya ekhawunta. Uma umuntu eyiphatha yena imithi yakhe, buza isazi sezemithi noma ochwepheshe bezempilo ngohlelo olugcina leyo mikhuba ivikelekile.
- **Gcina izinto zokuhlanza nemikhiqizo eyingozi ngokuphepha.** Zigcine ezitsheni zazo zokuqala, kude nokudla futhi kude kwesandla lapho kudingeka. Ungathululeli amakhemikhali ebhodleleni lokuphuza noma embizeni yokudla.
- **Gcina izinto zekhishi ezisetshenzisiwayo zifinyeleleka.** Ukuhleleka okuphephile kufanele kuvumele umuntu aqhubeke nemisebenzi ayijabulela uma kungenzeka. Uma ushintsha ikhabethe noma isisetshenziswa, chaza ushintsho bese uhlola ukuthi lunomphumela wokudideka yini.

Uma inkinga ivela ngesikhathi somsebenzi ohlanganyelwe, [umhlahlandlela wemisebenzi yasekhaya](/activities/household-tasks/) unemibono yokuguqula umsebenzi ngenkathi umuntu esahlanganyela.

### Ibhavu

- **Khanyisa indlela negumbi.** Hlola iswishi, ukukhanya kwasebusuku nendlela eya endlini yangasese. I-Alzheimer's Society incoma isibani sasebusuku noma esizwela ukunyakaza uma kusiza umuntu athole igumbi.
- **Hlola izindawo eziwuxinzelela nezisekelo.** Uma umgqomo noma indawo yokugeza kunzima, buza ochwepheshe abavunyelwe ngendwangu engaxhaphazi, insimbi yokubamba noma esinye isekelo esifanele. Ukubekwa okufanele kuncike emuntwini nasebhavini.
- **Gcina igumbi libonakale kalula futhi lisetshenziseke.** Beka izinto zansuku zonke lapho umuntu azilindele khona. Uma enza kanzima ukuthola indlu yangasese, uphawu oluyisisekelo noma ukugcina isivalo sivulekile kungasiza, uma kulingana nendlu.
- **Ungaletheli isitofu sokushisa esiphathekayo ebhavini.** Gcina izisetshenziswa zikagesi kude nezindawo ezimanzi futhi ulandele imiyalo yomkhiqizi.

### Igumbi lokulala nendlela yasebusuku

- **Vula indlela ukusuka embhedeni iye ebhavini.** Susa izinto esitaweni bese uqinisekisa ukuthi umuntu angafinyelela isibani ngaphandle kokudabula igumbi elimnyama.
- **Hlola izinto zokushisa.** Uma kusetshenziswa ingubo kagesi, iphedi yokushisa noma isitofu sokushisa esiphathekayo, bheka indawo yaso nemiyalo yaso. Cela iseluleko uma kukhona ingozi yokushiswa noma uma umuntu engasisebenzisi ngokuphepha yedwa.
- **Yenza izinto zansuku zonke zifinyeleleka.** Gcina izingubo, izibuko, ucingo noma ezinye izinto ezidingekayo endaweni ejwayelekile nasezingeni umuntu angafinyelela ngaphandle kokukhwela.

### Ukugcina impahla, izindawo zokugcina nezindawo zangaphandle

- **Vikela izinto zokuhlanza, imithi nezinsiza.** Hlola amakhabethi, izindawo zokuwasha, amagalaji, izindlu zangaphandle nezindawo ezingaphansi komhlaba. Gcina amakhemikhali ezitsheni zawo zokuqala futhi gcina amathuluzi abukhali noma imishini ivikelekile lapho kukhona ingozi ethile.
- **Hlola izisetshenziswa nezinsiza zokukhala.** Qinisekisa ukuthi izinzwa zomsi nezinzwa zikacarbon monoxide ziyatholakala futhi zisebenza. Landela imiyalo yazo yokuhlola nokushintsha amabhethri.
- **Gcina uhlu lwezoxhumana ezisenjalo lutholakala kalula.** Faka inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo endaweni yakho, oxhumana nabo abayinhloko nanoma yiluphi ulwazi usizo olungadingwa umsizi wakho ethembekile. Ungabeki imininingwane yangasese lapho isihambi singayibona khona.
- **Hlola indlela ephandle.** Bheka ukukhanya okungafanele, izindawo ezingalingani, amapayipi noma amathuluzi endleleni. Yenza izinguquko ngokulandelana ukuze izindlela ezijwayelekile zihlale zibonakala.

Ngemikhuba yasebusuku, [umhlahlandlela wokulala](/brain-health/sleep/) unemininingwane eyengeziwe ngoshintsho lokulala nemikhuba yasebusuku ejwayelekile.

## Ngingalenza kanjani ukuthi izinguquko zihloniphe umuntu?

Ukuphepha nokuzimela kufanele kusebenze ndawonye. Uma ushintsho luvimbela umuntu ukusebenzisa igumbi noma ukwenza umsebenzi ojwayelekile, misa ubheke enye inketho engakhawulelwe kakhulu. Ezinye izinhlangano zidweba ukukhiya ngezingozi ezithile; lokho akuyona imiyalo ephelele. Ngaphambi kokushintsha umnyango noma ukukhawulela indlela, funa iseluleko sendawo mayelana nokuphepha ekhaya nasemlilweni futhi cabanga ngokuthi umuntu uzophuma kanjani uma kunesimo esiphuthumayo. Gcina indlela ephephile yokuphuma itholakala.

Uma kungenzeka, chaza ushintsho ngaphambi kokulwenza bese unikeza umuntu isikhathi sokujwayela. I-Alzheimer's Society icebisa ukucabanga ukuthi ingabe izinsiza zizophepha yini ukusetshenziswa yedwa, ukuthi zidinga ukufakwa yini, nokuthi zingenza omunye umsebenzi wasekhaya ube nzima yini. Buza umuntu ukuthi ubona yini ngemva koshintsho. Uma egwema igumbi noma eyeka ukusebenzisa into ayiyazisayo, lungisa uhlelo.

Buyela ekhaya lapho imikhuba, ukunyakaza noma ukubona kushintsha, noma ngemva kwesehlo esiseduze nokulimaza. Igumbi elisebenze kahle inyanga edlule lingadinga ukukhanya okuhlukile noma indlela ecacile manje. Ungagcina uhlu olufushane lwalokho okushintshile nalokho okusasebenza, esikhundleni sokuzama ukuhlela kabusha ikhaya lonke ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Ngubani ongasiza ngezinguquko zekhaya?

Ochwepheshe bemisebenzi (occupational therapist) bangahlola indlela umuntu ahamba ngayo ekhaya futhi baphakamise izinsiza noma izinguquko zemisebenzi yansuku zonke. Kuya ngenkinga, nochwepheshe wezokwelapha ngokunyakaza, unesi wasendaweni, isazi semithi noma ochwepheshe bezindlu nabo bangasiza. I-Alzheimer's Society ithi umsebenzi wochwepheshe bemisebenzi ungatholakala ngodokotela wasekhaya, esibhedlela noma ngesevisi yenhlalakahle yasendaweni e-UK; izindlela ziyahluka ngaphandle. Ukufakwa noma inguquko yegesi, ugesi, isakhiwo noma izinsiza zokuphepha komlilo kufanele kwenziwe ochwepheshe abafanelekile basendaweni.

Uma uqashisa, buza umnikazi wendlu noma umhlinzeki wezindlu ngaphambi kokwenza izinguquko eziqhubekayo. Isevisi yasendaweni yabadala noma yokukhubazeka ingachaza ukuthi yiziphi izivivinyo noma usizo olutholakalayo. Uma inkinga iphuthumayo, ukuhlola ingozi ethile kuqala bese ucela usizo lochwepheshe ngakho konke okungaqinisekile ngendlela yokukwenza ngokuphepha.

## Ngiyibuyekeza kangaki ikhaya?

Ibuyekeze lapho umsebenzi ojwayelekile uba nzima, ukunyakaza noma ukubona kushintsha, noma kube nesehlo esiseduze nokulimaza. Qala ngegumbi noma umkhuba oxhunyelwe kuloshintsho. Akudingeki uqedele wonke umkhakha olwini lokuhlola; gcina okusebenza futhi ubuyekeze inguquko eyodwa esebenzayo ngesikhathi. Uma kube nokuwa esekwenzeke, [umhlahlandlela wokuthi yenzeni ngemva kokuwa](/caregiving/responding-after-a-fall-with-dementia/) unezinyathelo ezisheshayo nokulandelela.
