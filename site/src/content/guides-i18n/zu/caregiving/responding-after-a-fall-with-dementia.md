Ukuwa kungaba nokwesabisa komuntu nakubantu abaseduze. Hlala uzolile futhi unake ukuphepha kwangoshaya. Ungacabangi ukuthi umuntu akalimali ngoba engakwazi ukuchaza ukuthi kwenzekeni noma ukuthi kubuhlungu kuphi.

## Yini okufanele ngiyenze ngemva nje kokuwa?

Bheka ukuthi umuntu uphapheme yini, ubonakala elimele noma engaphilile, futhi angasabela njengoba ejwayelekile. Ungamphakamisi ngokushesha noma uzame ukumphakamisa wedwa. [I-NHS ithi usizo oluphuthumayo luyadingeka uma umuntu engase alimale ikhanda, umhlane, entanyeni noma esinqeni, enokunye ukulimala okukhulu, noma engakwazi ukuvuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/).

- **Shayela izinsiza zezimo eziphuthumayo** uma kungenzeka kukhona enye yalezi zimpawu ezinkulu. Landela imiyalelo yomsakazi wekholi.
- **Gcina umuntu ekhululekile futhi efudumele** kuyilaphoulindele usizo. Ungamnyakazisi uma ukhathazekile ngokulimala okukhulu noma ungaqiniseki ukuthi kuphephile.
- **Hlala naye uma ungakwazi.** Khuluma ngamagama amafushane nokuzolile. Ungambuzi ngalokho okwenzekile noma uphikise ukuthi amele.
- **Uma umuntu enobuhlungu, ulimele noma akahle kodwa engekho ingozi esheshayo,** xhumana nosizo lwezokwelapha lwasendaweni noma nochwepheshe wakhe wezempilo ukuthola iseluleko.

Ungazami ukuthwala noma ukumphakamisa umuntu wedwa. Uma kukhona uhlelo lokunakekelwa kokuwa, lulandele kuphela uma lulingana nokwenzekayo manje; thola usizo oluphuthumayo uma umuntu engase alimale kabi.

## Yini uma umuntu esebonakala enganyuka?

Ungaphuthumi umuntu. Buza ukuthi uzizwa kanjani nokuthi uyafuna yini usizo. Uma ebonakala engalimele futhi engavuka ngokusebenzisa okuxhasayo kwakhe ajwayelekile ngaphandle kokuphakanyiswa, mnike isikhathi bese uhlala eduze. Misa uma ezwa ubuhlungu, edidekile noma engakwazi ukukwenza. Uma ungaqiniseki, buza ochwepheshe wezempilo esikhundleni sokuzama ukumhambisa ngendlela ongakaboniswa yona.

[I-NHS ikweluleka ukuthatha isikhathi uphumule ngemva kokuvuka](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Imiyalelo yayo yokuzisiza ingeyomuntu ozizwa enamandla okuvuka; ayisiyo isizathu sokudonsa noma ukuthwala umuntu owele. Izidingo zokunyakaza, ukulinganisela nempilo zomuntu ziyahluka.

## Ngifanele ngixhumane nodokotela noma ngingabona ukulimala?

Yebo. [I-Alzheimer's Society incoma ukukhuluma nodokotela womuntu wasemtholampilo ngemva kokuwa, ngisho noma ungacabangi ukuthi ulimele](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/blog/why-person-dementia-more-likely-have-fall-and-what-can-you-do-help). Uma kukhona ukushaywa ikhanda, ubuhlungu, ukudideka okusha, ukubuthakathaka, ukuzungeza noma ushintsho ezindleleni zomuntu ejwayelekile, khuluma ngakho lapho ushayela. Ungasebenzisi uhlu lwewebhusayithi ukunquma ukuthi ukuhlolwa kwezokwelapha akudingeki.

Uma kukhona ukulimala ekhanda, landela iseluleko sochwepheshe noma usizo lwezokwelapha oluphuthumayo lwasendaweni. [Iseluleko sekhanda se-NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) sichaza izimpawu ezidinga ukunakwa ngokushesha. Uma umuntu edideka ngokuzumayo, elala kakhulu, ehlanza noma ebonakala egula kakhulu, funa usizo oluphuthumayo esikhundleni sokulinda ukuvakasha okujwayelekile.

## Yiluphi ulwazi olungasiza ochwepheshe wezempilo?

Ngemva kokunakekelwa kwezidingo ezisheshayo, bhala izinto ezimbalwa ezibonile ukuze wabelane nazo ngemvume yomuntu:

- lapho nokuthi kwenzekani ukuwa, uma kwaziwa;
- ayenzayo ngaphambi kokuwa;
- uma ukuwa kwabonwa noma uma umuntu mhlawumbe wawela ikhanda;
- ubuhlungu, ukulimala noma ushintsho obubonile;
- noma yiziphi izinguquko ezisanda ezempilweni, ekunyakazeni, ohlelweni noma emithini ozaziyo.

Chaza lokho obukubonile esikhundleni sokuqagela ukuthi umuntu uwele ngani. Inothi lingasekela ingxoxo; alichazi ukuthi kubangele ukuthi. Uma ukulimala okukhulu noma ushintsho olungazelelwe kungenzeka, thola usizo kuqala esikhundleni sokugcwalisa irekhodi.

## Yini okufanele ngibuze ngesikhathi somuthi wokulandelela?

Udokotela angakusiza anqume ukuthi umuntu udinga ukuhlolwa yini nokuthi kufanele ubheke yini ngemva kwalokho. [I-NHS ithi ukuhlolwa kokuwa kungabheka amandla, ukubona, ukuzwa, imithi nokulungiswa kwasekhaya](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/). Ungabuza:

- Umuntu udinga ukuhlolwa emva kokuwa lokhu, ngisho noma ukulimala kungabonakali?
- Yiziphi izinguquko noma izimpawu okufanele zikhuthaze usizo oluphuthumayo?
- Kufanele ibhalansi, ukubona, ukuzwa, ubuhlungu noma imithi kubhekwe yini?
- Ingabe iseveni lokuwa noma ochwepheshe bezokusebenza bangasiza ukuhlola ukunyakaza noma izinto zasekhaya?
- Ngubani umuntu okufanele axhumane naye uma kwenzeka elinye ukuwa?

Tshela udokotela ngalokho okwaziyo nalokho ongakwazi. Uma ukuwa kungabonwanga, tshela njalo esikhundleni sokuqagela ukuthi kwenzeke kanjani. Ungayeki noma ushintshe imithi, uthenge into yokusiza ukuhamba noma ufake izinto ngokusekelwe kuwebhusayithi kuphela. Usizo olufanele luncike ezidingweni zomuntu, ekunyakazeni kwakhe, ekhaya nasezifisweni zakhe.

## Singacabanga kanjani ngokuwa okunye?

Ngemva kokunakekelwa kwezidingo zezokwelapha ezisheshayo, cabanga ngokuthi kwakwenzekani endaweni nasemkhubeni. Indlela enempahla eningi, ukushintsha kokukhanya noma umzila ongajwayelekile kungaba nomthelela, kodwa ungacabangi ukuthi into eyodwa yayiyimbangela. Buza umuntu ukuthi ubonelani nokuthi yini engamenza akwazi ukusebenzisa lendawo kalula. Uma umuntu engakhumbuli noma engachazi ukuwa, yabelana ngalokho obubonile ngaphandle kokumcindezela ukuthi enze imininingwane. Qopha isikhathi, indawo, izicathulo, ukukhanya, umsebenzi nanoma yisiphi ushintsho kokunyakaza noma ukuziphatha okujwayelekile. Irekhodi elifushane lingasiza udokotela ukubona amaphethini; alikhombisi imbangela.

Umhlahlandlela wethu [wokuphepha ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) uhlola igumbi ngegumbi. Uma ukuwa kwenzeke ngesikhathi sokugeza, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wokugeza](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). Udokotela, ochwepheshe bezokusebenza noma insiza yokuwa yasendaweni bangahlola izinkinga ezithile futhi bancome izinguquko ezifanele. Ungafaki izinto noma ushintshe umuthi onqunyiwe ngokusekelwe kulolu khasi kuphela.

Ukuwa akukhombisi ngokwawo ukuthi umuntu angakwenza noma akakwenzi yini. Mgcine ebambe iqhaza ezinqumweni zosizo, futhi landela iseluleko sethimba lakhe lasendaweni lokunakekela.
