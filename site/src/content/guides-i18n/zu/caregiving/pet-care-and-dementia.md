Isilwane singaba yingxenye yesimo sansuku zonke somuntu nasemndenini. Ukondla, ukuhamba nesilwane noma ukuhlala nso nakho kungaba umsebenzi umuntu afuna ukuqhubeka nawo. I-dementia ingenza ezinye izinto zibe nzima, kodwa lokho akusho ukuthi omunye umuntu kufanele athathe kancane kancane.

[Iseluleko se-Alzheimer's Society ngokunakekela izilwane](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/publications-and-factsheets/dementia-together/how-plan-your-pet-care-after-diagnosed-dementia) siphakamisa ukuhlela ukunakekela izilwane kusengaphambili nokusebenzisa izinhlelo nezikhumbuzo. [Iseluleko se-Dementia Australia ngemisebenzi](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/activities-people-dementia) sifaka izilwane phakathi kwemisebenzi engaba nencazelo. Injongo lapha ukusekela okusebenzayo komuntu nesilwane, hhayi ukuthi izilwane zelapha i-dementia.

## Singenza kanjani umkhuba wokunakekela izilwane ube lula?

Qala ngokubuza umuntu ukuthi yimaphi amasiko ajabulela futhi afuna ukuwagcina. Omunye angathanda ukondla ikati kodwa angathandi ukuhlanza indlu yekati; omunye angathanda ukuhamba nenja nelungu lomndeni. Gcina uhlelo lucacile kumuntu, kwisilwane nakho okutholakalayo kusiza.

- **Xhumanisa ukunakekela nomkhuba osuvele ukhona.** Beka ukudla ngemva kokudla kwasekuseni, bheka isitsha samanzi lapho wenza itiye, noma uhambe nenja ngemva kohlelo lomsakazo ojwayelekile. Isignali esivele ibekwe ngosuku ingaba lula ukuyilandela kunohlelo olusha.
- **Gcina izinto zokusebenza endaweni eyodwa ejwayelekile.** Gcina ukudla kwesilwane, ilashi noma ibhulashi ndawonye lapho umuntu elindele khona, kanti izinto ezisindayo namakhemikhali zibekwe ngokuphephile. Ilebula elilula noma inothi lingasiza uma umuntu efuna lokho.
- **Yenza isikhumbuzo esisodwa sibonakale.** Inothi elifushane eduze kwezinto zesilwane noma isikhathi ekhalendeni kungase kwanele. Gwema ukugcwalisa ikhaya ngezikhumbuzo ezenza umuntu azizwe ebhekwa noma elungiswa.
- **Nika isikhathi somsebenzi.** Ukuhamba noma ukondla kungathatha isikhathi eside kunangaphambili. Ungamshesisi umuntu uma eyijabulela lesi sikhathi ndawonye.

Uma abantu abaningi bayabelana ngokunakekela isilwane, sebenzisa uhlu olufushane olubonisa ukuthi ubani ophethe umsebenzi ngalolo suku. Gcina lolu hlu eduze kwezinto zokunakekela noma usebenzise isikhumbuzo umuntu asivele ayithanda. Gwema ukudala ama-alamu namanothi amaningi uma isignali eyodwa elula yanele.

Umhlahlandlela we-[wemikhuba yansuku zonke](/activities/daily-routine/) unemibono eminingi yokuxhumanisa umsebenzi nengxenye ejwayelekile yosuku.

## Yisiphi isisekelo abangane nomndeni abangasinikeza?

Nikeza umsebenzi othile esikhundleni sesithembiso esijwayelekile. Umngane angathenga ukudla kwesilwane nyanga zonke, omakhelwane angajoyina ukuhamba, noma ilungu lomndeni lingaya kokuhlolwa kwezilwane. Uma umuntu efuna ukuqhubeka nokondla noma ukugcoba isilwane, hlela usizo ngokuzungeze leyo ndima hhayi ukuyisusa.

Vumelana ukuthi ubani oyosiza uma umnakekeli ejwayelekile engekho noma umuntu egula. Tshela lowo msizi ukuthi izinto zitholakala kuphi, ngabe umuntu ukhetha luhlobo luni lwemikhuba nokuthi ubani okufanele axhumane naye uma isilwane sibonakala sigulile. Hlola ukuthi lowo msizi uyazizwa ekhululekile nesilwane nokuthi isilwane sizizwa sikhululekile naye.

Uma umuntu enosizo lomhlinzeki wokunakekela, buza ukuthi ukunakekelwa kwesilwane kungafakwa kanjani ezivakashweni. Imvume nemithetho yasekhaya iyabaluleka: ungacabangi ukuthi umnakekeli oholwayo angahamba, andise noma aphathe isilwane njengengxenye yomsebenzi wakhe. Vumelana ngomsebenzi nomuntu, nomhlinzeki nelungu lomndeni elinesibopho sesilwane.

## Singazi kanjani ukuthi kudingeka usizo oluningi?

Qaphela izehlakalo ezithile esikhundleni sokwahlulela umuntu ngesigaba noma ngesikolo. Isitsha esishiywe silanda njalo, ukuhamba okuphuthelwe okwenza isilwane sikhathazeke, noma umuntu obonakala ekhathele ngemva kokunakekela isilwane kungasho ukuthi umsebenzi munye udinga usizo oluningi. Ushintsho ekuziphatheni, ekudleni noma ekubukeni kwesilwane nalo luyisizathu sokuxhumana nodokotela wezilwane.

Khuluma nomuntu ngalokho omunakile. Angase ajabule ngesikhumbuzo, ngokuhamba ndawonye noma ngomsizi womsebenzi oyedwa kuyilapho egcina yonke enye ingxenye yohlelo. Uma inkinga yezempilo, yokunyakaza noma yokuphepha ithinta ukukwazi komuntu ukunakekela isilwane, cela ithimba lakhe lokunakekela noma insiza yasendaweni ukuthi zisize ukuhlola izinketho.

Ungalindi kuze kube sesimweni esiphuthumayo ukucabanga ngosizo lwesipele. Umhlahlandlela we-[wokuhlala wedwa ne-dementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/) uchaza indlela yokuvumelana ngokuhlolwa nosizo oluthile ngokugcina izinqumo zomuntu phakathi nendawo.

## Singahlela kanjani izinguquko zesikhathi esizayo?

Buza umuntu ukuthi yimuphi umuntu amethemba ukuthi anakekele isilwane uma eba esibhedlela, ekude nekhaya noma engasakwazi ukwenza umsebenzi othile. Bhala umkhuba wesilwane ojwayelekile, ukudla, izingcingo zodokotela wezilwane nabantu abavumelene ukusiza. Gcina ulwazi lapho umuntu nabasizi abathembekile bangalitholela khona, ngemvume yomuntu.

Uhlelo lungashintsha ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Buyekeza lapho umkhuba womuntu, ukunyakaza noma isekelo sakhe sishintsha, noma uma izidingo zesilwane zishintsha. Uma kudingeka ukushintsha indawo, bandakanya umuntu ekuthatheni isinqumo sokuthi isilwane sizohlala kuphi nokuthi angaxhumana kanjani naso uma kungenzeka. Ukuhlela kusenesikhathi kunika wonke umuntu isikhathi esengeziwe sokucabangela izinketho ezivikela izifiso zomuntu nokunakekelwa kwesilwane.

## Yini uma ukuvakashelana nesilwane noma umsebenzi kungakhululeki?

Vumela umuntu nesilwane bahlele isivinini. Nikeza isikhathi ndawonye kuphela uma bobabili bebonakala bekhululekile. Isikhathi esifushane, ukuhlala eduze noma ukubuka isithombe sesilwane kungase kwanele. Misa uma umuntu eba nokungakhululeki, isilwane sizama ukuhamba noma sibonakala sinengcindezi. Imibono eyengeziwe yezindlela ezijwayelekile zokuchitha isikhathi ndawonye itholakala kuhlu [lwemibono yemisebenzi](/activities/ideas/) lwalesi sayithi.
