Uhlelo lwansuku lungaletha ushintsho lwendawo nosizo lwemisebenzi yansuku zonke. Lungase futhi lunike umnakekeli womndeni isikhathi sokusebenza, ukwenza imisebenzi nokuphumula. Izinhlelo ziyahlukahluka, futhi isikhungo esifanelana nomuntu othile kungenzeka singafanelani nomunye. Izifiso nokuzwela komuntu kufanele kuqondise isinqumo.

[Ukubuka konke kwe-Alzheimer's Association ngezikhungo zansuku](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/adult-day-centers) kanye [nohlu lokuhlola lokukhetha isikhungo](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-an-adult-day-center-ts.pdf) kuncoma ukuhlola izinsizakalo, abasebenzi, imisebenzi, izindleko nokuthuthwa ngaphambi kokubhalisa. Leli khasi lisiza ekulungiseleleni imibuzo; alilinganisi noma linconome isikhungo esithile.

## Ngingaqala kanjani ukucabanga ngokuzama uhlelo lwansuku?

Qalani ngokubuza umuntu ukuthi yini angayijabulela nokuthi yisiphi usizo angalamukela. Angafuna umsebenzi wezenhlalo, ukudla nabanye abantu, ukuba nomuntu ngenkathi umnakekeli esebenza, noma okunye. Uhlelo luyinketho, hhayi isidingo.

Cabangani ngezindawo umuntu afuna ukuya kuzo, izinkambo zakhe zansuku zonke nosizo adinga emini. Hlolani ukuthi amahora esikhungo, ukuthuthwa nemisebenzi kuyahambelana yini nalezo zidingo. Uma umuntu engathandi amaqembu noma edinwa endaweni enabantu abaningi, buzani ukuthi kukhona yini indawo ethule noma ukuvakashela okufushane.

Uhlelo lungase futhi lusize umnakekeli ukuthi aphumule noma aphathe ezinye izibopho. Lokho kubalulekile, kodwa nesifiso somuntu siyabaluleka. Xoxani kokubili ngokukhululeka bese nifuna ukuhlelwa okwamukelekile kubo bonke abahlanganyela.

## Yiziphi izinsizakalo okufanele ngibuze ngazo?

Izinsizakalo ziyahlukahluka. Ezinye izikhungo zigxile ekuhlanganeni nasemisebenzini; ezinye zingase futhi zinikeze ukudla, ukuthuthwa, ukunakekelwa komuntu siqu noma izinsizakalo ezihlobene nezempilo. Buzani ukuthi yiziphi izinsizakalo ezitholakala ezinsukwini umuntu azoya ngazo nokuthi abasebenzi bangasekela yini izidingo azinazo manje.

Thola mayelana nemisebenzi, hhayi nje isihloko sesikhathi. Buzani ukuthi abahlanganyeli benzani ngempela, ukuthi bakwazi yini ukukhetha phakathi kwezinketho, nokuthi uhlelo lufaka yini izithakazelo ezijwayelekile ezifana nomculo, ubuciko, ukulima izimbali noma ingxoxo. Umuntu akudingeki ahlanganyele kuzo zonke izinto. Buzani ukuthi kukhona yini indawo ethule noma isikhathi sokuphumula.

Uma umuntu edinga usizo ngemithi, ukunyakaza, ukudla noma ukunakekelwa komuntu siqu, buzani ukuthi ubani onikeza lowo sizo nokuthi bayaqeqeshwa yini. Ningacabangi ukuthi uhlelo lwansuku lunikeza ukunakekelwa kwezokwelapha noma lungaphatha zonke izidingo. Qinisekisani noma yiziphi iziqondiso zezempilo ngeqembu lokunakekela komuntu.

## Ngingabheka ini ngesikhathi sokuvakashela?

Hlelani ukuvakashela, futhi uma umuntu efisa, mthathani nani. Qaphelani ukuthi abasebenzi bakhuluma ngenhlonipho yini, banikeze abahlanganyeli isikhathi sokuphendula futhi bamemeze abantu ukuthi bahlanganyele ngaphandle kocindezelo. Funani imisebenzi ehambelana nezithakazelo zomuntu namakhono akhe, hhayi nje ikhalenda elinabantu abaningi.

Buzani ukuthi isikhungo sazi kanjani izintandokazi zomuntu ngamunye nokuthi siyiphendula kanjani uma othile edinwa, ekhathazeka noma efuna ukuhamba. Thola ukuthi isikhungo senzani esimweni esiphuthumayo, ukuthi imindeni izwa kanjani izindaba nokuthi kufanele nixhumane ngubani uma kukhona ukukhathazeka.

Uma kungenzeka, vakashelani phakathi nomsebenzi noma nokudla. Qaphelani ukuthi isikhala sikhululekile yini komuntu, ukuthi kulula yini ukuzulazula nokuthi abasebenzi bayatholakala yini. Isibonelo sokuhamba nesihloko singasiza ekulungiseleleni imibuzo, kodwa ukubona usuku olujwayelekile kungase kukutshele okuningi.

## Yiziphi izinto ezithize ezifanele ngizihlole?

Buzani ngamahora, izinsuku, izindleko, ubuncane bokufika okudingekayo, izinqubomgomo zokukhansela nanoma yiziphi izindleko ezengeziwe. Buzani ukuthi yimaphi amadlelo owengeziwe nokuthi ukudla okukhethekile kungasekelwa yini. Izindleko nezinsizakalo ziyahluka endaweni, ngakho celani isikhungo sichaze imigomo yaso ebhalwe phansi.

Thola ukuthi ukuthuthwa kuyatholakala yini, kwenza malini, nokuthi kuyahambelana yini nomuntu. Buzani ngezikhathi zokuthathwa, usizo lokukhwela nokwehla, nokuthi kwenzekani uma umshayeli ebambezeleka. Uma ilungu lomndeni lizonihambisa, faka lesi sikhathi nezindleko zaso ohlelweni lwakho.

Buzani ukuthi isikhungo singanikeza yini usizo umuntu alidingayo uma impilo yakhe noma amakhono akhe ansuku zonke eshintsha. Uma isikhungo singakwazi ukuhlangabezana nalezo zidingo, buzani ukuthi siphakamisa yiphi indlela noma ukhetho olunye. [Umhlahlandlela wokunakekelwa ekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) uchaza olunye uhlobo lwesekelo oluzofaneleka emindenini eminingi.

## Umuntu angazama kanjani uhlelo ngaphandle koxinzeko?

Buzani ukuthi isikhungo sinikeza yini ukuvakashela okufushane, ukudla noma umsebenzi wesikhashana. Mvumeleni umuntu akhethe ukuthi yini azame nokuthi uzohlala isikhathi esingakanani uma kungenzeka. Ukuvakashela kokuqala kungase kuzwakale kungaziwa. Buzani umuntu ngemva kwalokho ukuthi yini ayithandayo, engayithandi noma angayishintsha.

Uma umuntu eyazisa, thola ukuthi kungani. Ukuthuthwa, umsindo, abantu abangabaziwa noma umsebenzi othile kungase kube yinkinga. Ningabuza isikhungo ukuthi ushintsho oluncane lungenzeka yini noma nivakashele ngolunye usuku. Ningasebenzisi umzwelo wecala noma ukuphikisana ukuze mphoqe ukuthi aye.

Ngemva kwezindlela ezimbalwa, xoxani nomuntu nabasebenzi besikhungo ngokuthi ukuhlelwa kusebenza kanjani. Uhlelo oluhle lungase ludinge ukulungiswa kwesikhathi, imisebenzi noma usizo. Uma umuntu eseqhubeka ekhathazeka noma isevisi ingafanelani, kuyinto efanele ukucabanga kabusha.

## Ngingahlanganisa kanjani nesikhungo?

Ngemvume yomuntu, yabelanani ngolwazi olusiza abasebenzi ukumsekela: igama afisa ukubizwa ngalo, indlela yokukhuluma, izinkambo, izithakazelo nosizo akwamukelayo. Buzani ukuthi isikhungo silondoloza kanjani lolu lwazi lube yimfihlo nokuthi izibuyekezo zabelwana kanjani nomndeni.

Buzani ukuthi ubani okufanele nixhumane naye uma umuntu egula, engekho noma edinga ukushintsha usizo. Gcinani uhlelo lokusimama lwezinsuku lapho uhlelo lulaliwe noma ukuthuthwa kungatholakali. Uma abantu abaningi besiza, vumelanani ukuthi ubani uzophatha ukuthuthwa nokubuyekeza ngakunye.

Uhlelo lwansuku lungaba ingxenye yohlelo olubanzi lwosizo. [Umhlahlandlela wokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ufaka izindlela zokuhlela izikhathi zokuphumula, futhi [imisebenzi yamaqembu ezikhungweni zokunakekela](/activities/care-home-groups/) inikeza imibono yemisebenzi yeqembu elula.

Uhlelo oluhle yilolo umuntu alufisa ukuzama futhi olungakwazi ukusekela izidingo zakhe. Vakashelani, buzani imibuzo eqondile futhi nenze izinguquko ngokusekelwe ekutheni umuntu uzizwa kanjani.
