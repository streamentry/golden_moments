Izinqumo zezimali zithinta ukuzimela komuntu nempilo yansuku zonke. Uma umsebenzi uba nzima, qala ngalowo msebenzi esikhundleni sokucabanga ukuthi omunye kufanele athathe konke. Buza umuntu ukuthi ufuna usizo luni bese umgcina ebandakanyekile.

Leli khasi linikeza ulwazi jikelele kubafundi baseMelika. Izinketho zamabhange nemithetho yomthetho ziyahluka endaweni. I-[Alzheimer's Society guide to managing finances](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/managing-a-persons-finances) ichaza izindlela zokusekela umuntu ngenkathi igcina indima yakhe. I-[Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-would-like-to-be-able-to-have-my-friend-or-family-member-help-with-my-bill-paying-and-banking-what-are-my-options-en-1145/) ichaza ukuthi usizo olungokwesiko, abathembekile nogunya lomthetho kuyizinto ezihlukile.

## Ngingasiza kanjani ngaphandle kokuthatha phezu?

Buza umuntu ukuthi ufuna usizo ngiphi umsebenzi wezimali. Angase afune ukubheka isikweletu ndawonye, ahlele izincwadi, akhumbule usuku lokukhokha noma ahlole isicelo esingalindelekile. Umuntu angafuna usizo ngezinye izinto abe nokuthanda ukuphatha ezinye.

- **Buza ngaphambi kokwenza.** Zama, “Ingabe ungathanda ukubheka lesi sikweletu ndawonye?” Nikeza umuntu isikhathi sokuthatha isinqumo.
- **Gxila emsebenzini oyedwa.** Uma umuntu evumelana, bhekani ndawonye inani, usuku lokukhokha nezindlela zokukhokha. Vumela umuntu enze izinqumo azifisayo.
- **Yenza irekhodi elilula ndawonye.** Uhlu olufushane lwamabhili, izinsuku zokukhokha noma izinyathelo ezivunyelwene ngazo kungasiza nobabili nibone okuphathiwe.
- **Buza ibhange ngezinketho zalo.** Lingase linikeze izindlela zokwenza izinkokhelo noma zokuhlola umsebenzi kube lula. Buza ukuthi yiyiphi inketho ngayinye ivumela ngaphambi kokushintsha i-akhawunti.
- **Hlola futhi kamuva.** Izintandokazi zomuntu zingashintsha. Gcina usizo lukhawulelwe kokufunayo nokudingayo manje.

I-CFPB ichaza usizo olungokwesiko njengokubuyekeza amabhili nokuhlela isabelomali nomuntu ngenkathi lowo muntu esagunyaza izimali. Lokho kungahluka ekuvumeleni omunye umuntu ukuthi athathe imali noma enze izinkokhelo. Ungabelani amaphasiwedi, ama-PIN noma amakhodi okuphepha angasetshenziswa kanye kanye njengendlela emfushane.

## Yini ukuthi umuntu othembekile akuvumele ukuyenza?

Ezweni lase-United States, isici somuntu othembekile singavumela isikhungo sezezimali ukuthi sixhumane nalowo muntu ezimeni ezithile, njengokukhathazeka ngokungaba khona kwesikhwebu. I-CFPB ithi ukubiza umuntu othembekile akumnikezi ukufinyelela ku-akhawunti noma imvume yokuthutha imali. Isici kungenzeka singatholakali kuzo zonke ama-akhawunti, ngakho buza ibhange noma inkampani yezezimali ukuthi lisebenza kanjani.

| Uhlobo losizo | Okusho kona |
|---|---|
| Ukubuyekeza amabhili ndawonye | Umuntu nomsizi babheka amabhili ndawonye; umuntu uyanquma futhi agunyaze izinkokhelo. |
| Umuntu othembekile | Isikhungo sezezimali singaxhumana nalowo muntu abizwe ngaye ezimeni ezithile. Igama lodwa alinikezi ukufinyelela ku-akhawunti. |
| Igunya le-akhawunti noma lomthetho | Imvume yebhange, i-power of attorney noma olunye uhlelo olufanayo lungavumela izinkokhelo. Amandla nemisebenzi yalo kuncike ku-akhawunti nasemthethweni wendawo. |

Ngaphambi kokushintsha ukufinyelela ku-akhawunti noma ukuba umnikazi wayo, buza ibhange nochwepheshe wezomthetho wasendaweni ukuthi achaze izinketho nezingozi. Isimo sezempilo sodwa asikutshelile ukuthi umuntu angayiphatha yiziphi izimo zezimali noma yiziphi izinqumo azifuna ukuzenza. Mgcine eyingxenye yezinqumo njengoba efisa. Uma efuna umuntu emele yena, buza ukuthi ungasetha kanjani lokho ngokomthetho esikhundleni sokusebenzisa ukungena kwomunye umuntu.

## Yiziphi izinguquko okufanele ngizihlole?

Isikweletu esiphuthelwe noma ukuthenga okungajwayelekile kungaba nezincazelo ezahlukene. Akukhombisi ukuthi umuntu akakwazi ukuphatha imali noma ukuthi ukhohliswe. Buza ukuthi kwenzekeni futhi, uma umuntu evumelana, bhekani isitatimende noma isikweletu esihlobene ndawonye.

I-[CFPB ihlu izimpawu ezingaba khona zokuxhashazwa ngezimali](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-can-i-tell-if-a-friend-neighbor-or-family-member-is-a-victim-of-financial-exploitation-en-1933/), kufaka phakathi imali noma impahla enyamalala, ukuhoshwa okungajwayelekile kwemali, izikweletu ezingakhokhiwe, abantu abangaziwa abengezwa kuma-akhawunti, noma umuntu olinganisela izingcingo nezindwendwe zomuntu. Qaphela izinkombandlela nezinguquko ezihlukile kulokho okuvamile kulowo muntu; ungathathi isimpawu esisodwa njengobufakazi.

Uma umuntu ethi imali ilahlekile, thatha ukukhathazeka ngokungathi sína. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuphendula ukusolwa](/caregiving/when-someone-with-dementia-is-suspicious/) unikeza izindlela zokuhlola ukukhathazeka ngaphandle kokukudela. Buza umuntu ngasese ukuthi wakubona yini nokuthi ufuna kwenzeke yini okulandelayo. Gwema ukusola umndeni, umnakekeli noma osebenza ebhange ngaphambi kokuhlola izimo. Uma usola ukuthi umsizi othembekile angaba nesandla kulokho, cela iseluleko kochwepheshe ohlukile noma kwisevisi yasendaweni.

## Singanciphisa kanjani ingozi yokukhwabanisa?

Abakhwabanisi bangazenza ngabasebenzi ebhange, ohulumeni, ebhizinisini, abangane noma izihlobo. Bangase bathi kukhona isimo esiphuthumayo futhi bacindezele ukukhokha ngokushesha. I-[Alzheimer's Association technology-safety guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/technology-safety-older-adults) incoma ukumisa, ukuhlola uxhumana naye nokuthola usizo ngaphambi kokwabelana ngolwazi noma imali.

- **Misa uma umyalezo uzwakala ukuthi uphuthumayo.** Ungathumeli imali noma wabelane ngemininingwane ye-akhawunti ngenkathi umbizi noma umyalezo ukucindezela.
- **Hlola usebenzisa oxhumana naye obathembayo.** Vala bese ubiza ibhange ngenombolo esesitatimendeni esisanda noma ekhadini, noma sebenzisa i-app yalo esemthethweni. Ungasebenzisi isixhumanisi noma inombolo ethunyelwe umbizi.
- **Gcina izinkomba zokuphepha zisezimfihlweni.** Ungathumeli amaphasiwedi, imininingwane ephelele ye-akhawunti noma amakhodi okuqinisekisa ekuphenduleni umyalezo ongalindelekile. I-[FTC incoma ukuvala ucingo olungalindelekile olubuza ikhodi nokuxhumana nebhange ngenombolo oyithembayo](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it).
- **Vumelanani ngosizo nomuntu.** Buza ukuthi ufuna ukuhlola isicelo esimangazayo nobani. Isici somuntu othembekile ebhange singasiza ekuxhumaneni, kodwa asigunyazi ngokwaso izinkokhelo.

## Yini okufanele ngiyenze uma imali ingase yathunyelwa kumgilingqi?

Xhumana nebhange, inkampani yekhadi, umhlinzeki wesikweletu noma inkampani esetshenziswe ukuthumela imali ngokushesha. Sebenzisa inombolo esekhadini noma esitatimendeni, noma i-app noma iwebhusayithi esemthethweni yenhlangano. Buza ukuthi ingayima yini noma ibuyisele inkokhelo. I-[FTC's recovery steps](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) ichaza ukuthi izinketho zincike endleleni imali ethunyelwe ngayo; akukho siqinisekiso sokuthi ingabuyiswa.

Gcina imilayezo, amarisidi, izinsuku nezaziso ze-akhawunti. Ku-United States, ungabika ukukhwabanisa ku-[FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed). Uma usola ukuthi umuntu uxhashazela umuntu omdala, i-[Administration for Community Living](https://acl.gov/programs/protection-and-advocacy-systems/what-if-i-suspect-abuse-neglect-or-exploitation) ithi xhumana ne-Adult Protective Services esifundazweni somuntu. Uma kukhona ingozi esheshayo kumuntu, xhumana namasevisi ezimo eziphuthumayo asendaweni. Ngaphandle kwe-United States, sebenzisa amasevisi asendaweni okuvikela abathengi nokuphepha.

Khuluma ngaphandle kokusola. Ukukhwabanisa kuklelwe ukudukisa abantu. I-[CFPB's guide to scam and fraud help](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/who-do-i-contact-for-help-with-a-scam-or-fraud-en-1777/) ihlu izindlela zokubika e-U.S. futhi icebisa ukusebenzisa amawebhusayithi nezinombolo zocingo ezithembekile.

## Ngingathola kuphi usizo ngesinqumo esithile sezezimali?

Buza ibhange lomuntu ukuthi yiziphi izinsiza nezimvume ezitholakalayo. Ngedokhumenti esemthethweni noma ushintsho lobunikazi be-akhawunti, khuluma nochwepheshe owazi imithetho yendawo umuntu ahlala kuyo. Uma ukhathazekile ngokungaxhashazwa okungenzeka, sebenzisa isevisi yasendaweni yokuvikela. Leli khasi alikwazi ukunquma ukuthi umuntu kufanele abe nagunya lani noma ukuthi kuthile okuthile kwezimali kungukukhwabanisa.
