Ukuhlala kude kungenza kube nzima ukwazi ukuthi yisiphi usizo okudingekayo, kodwa ibanga aliqedi zonke izindlela zokubamba iqhaza. Ungase usize ukuhlela amasevisi, wenze umsebenzi umuntu avumelana nawo, noma usekele umuntu ohlinzeka ngokunakekelwa kwansuku zonke. Qala ngokubuza umuntu ophila ne-dementia ukuthi ufuna ini nokuthi ufuna ubani abandakanyeke.

I-[Alzheimer's Association's long-distance caregiving guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) ichaza izindima ezifana nokuhlela izikhathi zokwelashwa, ukucwaninga amasevisi asendaweni, ukuxhumana nabasizi basendaweni nokunika umnakekeli wansuku zonke ikhefu. I-[Family Caregiver Alliance also notes](https://www.caregiver.org/faq/how-can-i-help-with-caregiving-from-far-away/) ukuthi ukuthola amasevisi nokuhlela ukuvakashela kungaba nzima ukusuka kude. Leli khasi linikeza imibono yokuhlela ejwayelekile; amasevisi nemithetho yasendaweni iyahluka.

## Ngingenzani ngaphandle kwebanga?

Khetha imisebenzi ehambisana nalokho umuntu akufunayo nalokho ongakwenza ngokuthembeka. Usizo lungabandakanya:

- **Hlela izikhathi zokwelashwa.** Buza ukuthi umuntu ufuna yini usizo lokuxhumana nomtholampilo, ukugcina ikhalenda noma ukuhlela ukuthuthwa.
- **Cwaninga amasevisi asendaweni.** Bheka ukulethelwa kokudla, ukuthuthwa, usizo lwasekhaya, izinhlelo zansuku zabadala noma amaqembu abanakekeli endaweni umuntu ahlala kuyo. Hlola imininingwane nomhlinzeki; ukutholakala kuyahluka ngesifunda.
- **Phatha imibhalo noma ama-oda avunyelwene ngawo.** Ungasiza ukuhlela amafomu noma ukuhlela izinto ezidingekayo uma umuntu ekucela. Amarekhodi wezimali, wezinshiyo nezempilo adinga ukunakekelwa okwengeziwe; sebenzisa [umhlahlandlela wosizo lwezimali nokuphepha ekukhwabanisweni](/caregiving/managing-money-with-dementia/) uhlole izimvume noma izidingo zokugunyazwa.
- **Yenza isikhathi somnakekeli wasendaweni.** Ngesikhathi sokuvakashela, buza ukuthi yisiphi umsebenzi noma ikhefu elingasiza. Ungahlela ukudla, uhlanganise isikhathi sochwepheshe ohleliwe noma uchithe isikhathi nomuntu ngenkathi umsizi wasendaweni ephumula.
- **Hlala uxhumene ngendlela umuntu ayijabulela.** Buza ukuthi ufuna ucingo, ividiyo, incwadi noma ukuvakashela. Ucingo lungaba yingxoxo, hhayi isivivinyo mayelana nokudla, imithi noma inkumbulo.

I-[Alzheimer's Association inikeza ukuthi abantu abasekela kude bangase bangakwazi ukulinganisa izidingo ngokuqondile](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage). Uma kungenzeka, xhumana nabantu abambona umuntu nsuku zonke bese uhlela ukuvakashela endaweni yomuntu ngokwakhe. Lowo mbono ungaba nolwazi oluwusizo, kodwa awuyona indawo yokubuza umuntu noma yokuthola ukuhlolwa okuvela kochwepheshe.

## Ngixhumana kanjani nabasizi basendaweni?

Qala ngokubuza umuntu ukuthi ufuna ubani abandakanyeke nokuthi ufuna ukwabelana ngazo yiziphi izibuyekezo. Angase afune ukuthi ihlobo elilodwa lihlele izikhathi zokwelashwa, umakhelwane aqaphele inkinga ethile, noma ngabe akafuni zibuyekezo ezijwayelekile. Ungangeni engxoxweni yokunakekela ngoba nje lowo muntu uhlala eduze.

Uma umuntu evumelana, sungula uhlelo lokuxhumana olulula nabantu abakhethile:

- **Khetha oxhumana naye ojwayelekile.** Vumelani ukuthi ubani ozothumela izibuyekezo ezijwayelekile nokuthi zizofika kangaki.
- **Qoka ohlala engemuva.** Vumelani ukuthi ubani angangena uma umsizi ojwayelekile engatholakali.
- **Hlukanisa izibuyekezo ezijwayelekile nezinkinga eziphuthumayo.** Vumelanani ukuthi umsizi wasendaweni kufanele akuthole kanjani uma ukuphepha noma impilo yomuntu kushintsha ngokuzumayo.
- **Gcina imisebenzi icacile.** Bhala ukuthi ubani ozophatha izikhathi zokwelashwa, ukuthuthwa, amakholi wezinsiza noma usizo lwasekhaya. Buza ngaphambi kokushintsha uhlelo.

I-[Alzheimer's Association incoma ukuxhumana njalo nomndeni, nabakhelwane nabahlinzeki bokunakekela](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Ungaphinde ubuze umsizi wasendaweni ukuthi udinga yini. Isicelo esifushane nesicacile kungase kusetshenziseke kalula kunesithi “Ngitshele uma udinga noma yini.”

## Ngingathola kanjani ulwazi kudokotela noma kumhlinzeki wesevisi?

Buza udokotela womuntu noma umhlinzeki wesevisi ukuthi yimaphi amagunya noma amadokhumenti adingekayo ngaphambi kokwabelana ngezibuyekezo. I-[Alzheimer's Association inikeza ukuthi umhlinzeki angadinga ukukhululwa ngaphambi kokukhuluma ngokunakekelwa nesihlobo esikude](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Amafomu nemithetho ayahluka, ngakho buza umhlinzeki esikhundleni sokucabanga ukuthi ubudlelwano bomndeni bukunika ukufinyelela.

Hlonipha izifiso zomuntu mayelana nokuthi ubani othola ulwazi. Yabelana kuphela ngalokho okudingekayo emsebenzini ovunyelwene ngawo, futhi gcina imininingwane yokuxhumana namanothi kuyimfihlo. Uma kukhona idokhumenti esemthethweni noma igunya elisemthethweni, buza ichwepheshe elifanele endaweni umuntu ahlala kuyo ukuthi lihlanganisa ini. Leli khasi alinquma igunya lalowo muntu ngokomthetho.

## Ngingenza kanjani ukuvakashela kube wusizo kithina sobabili?

Buza umuntu ukuthi ufuna ukuchitha kanjani isikhathi. Shiya indawo yokudla, ukuhamba, umculo noma ingxoxo ethulile, hhayi izikhathi zokwelashwa nemisebenzi kuphela.

Uma umuntu efuna usizo lokuhlela, sebenzisa ingxenye yokuvakashela ukuhlangana nabasizi basendaweni, ukukhuluma nochwepheshe wezokwelapha noma ukuhlola ukuthi ukuhlelelwa kusebenza yini. Buza ngaphambi kokubheka izindawo zomuntu siqu noma amarekhodi. Xoxa ngezinkinga ngokuthula ugxile kushintsho oluthile, hhayi ukwahlulela ukuthi umuntu uyiphethe kanjani.

I-[Alzheimer's Association incoma ukusebenzisa ukuvakashela ukuxhumana kabusha nokuhlanganisa nabanakekeli basendaweni](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving). Uma ubona inkinga, xoxa ngayo nomuntu nabantu abamthembayo. Ukuvakashela okukodwa akuwukuqinisekisa isimo esiphelele sempilo noma amakhono komuntu.

## Yini uma izidingo zomuntu noma usizo kushintsha?

Buyekeza uhlelo lapho umuntu ecela usizo oluhlukile, umkhuba uba nzima noma umsizi wasendaweni engaqhubeka. Buza ukuthi umuntu ufuna ukuqhubeka enza yini nomsebenzi odinga ukunakwa manje. Udokotela, isisebenzi sezenhlalakahle noma isevisi yasendaweni yabadala kungasiza ukuthola izinketho. Ukuthola usizo lwasendaweni, bona imihlahlandlela yethu [yosizo ekhaya](/caregiving/choosing-in-home-dementia-care/) kanye [nokuhlala wedwa ne-dementia](/caregiving/living-alone-with-dementia/).

Qinisekisa ukuthi kukhona umuntu wasendaweni ongasabela esimweni esiphuthumayo. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuhlela izimo eziphuthumayo](/caregiving/emergency-planning-dementia/) ungasiza umuntu nabasizi abakhethile ukubhala oxhumana nabo kanye nohlelo lokusekela. Uma kukhona izingxabano mayelana nokuthi ubani okufanele enze yini, bona [izingxoxo zomndeni mayelana nezindima zokunakekela](/caregiving/family-disagreements-about-dementia-care/).

Ukuhlala kude kungakhawulela usizo lwezandla, kodwa akukhethi ukuthi umuntu uyakhathalela kangakanani noma angakwazi ukunikela ngani. Gcina uhlelo lusekelwe ezifisweni zomuntu, ezimisebenzini eyenziwayo nosizo olutholakala eduze.
