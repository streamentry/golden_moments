Abantu abanesifo se-dementia bayakuzwa ubuhlungu, nakuba bengakwazi ukukuchaza kahle. Umuntu angasebenzisa amagama ahlukile, izinkomba noma ukushintsha ukuziphatha ukuze akhombise ukuthi kukhona okungalungile. Umnakekeli angakubona nokukwabelana nalokho, kodwa akufanele akuxilongele yedwa imbangela.

[Umhlahlandlela wobuhlungu we-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/pain) uncoma ukubuza ngqo nokufuna ukuhlolwa udokotela uma kusolwa ubuhlungu. [I-Alzheimer's Association iphinde iphawule](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger) ukuthi ukungakhululeki komzimba kungaba nomthelela ekushintsheni kokuziphatha. Leli khasi linolwazi jikelele, hhayi ukuhlolwa kobuhlungu noma uhlelo lokwelashwa.

## Ingabe umuntu onesifo se-dementia angaba nobuhlungu angakushongo?

Yebo. Ubuhlungu buyisho nakuba umuntu engakwazi ukuthola amazwi alo. Angase angaqondi umbuzo mayelana nenxenye ethile yomzimba, noma angase abe nobunzima ekutshelweni ukuthi lapho nini noma kanjani kubuhlungu. Ungathathi ukuthula njengokuthi umuntu uyaphumula.

Buza ngesikhathi esithulile usebenzisa amagama amaziwa umuntu. “Ukhathazekile?” noma “Lokhu kubuhlungu yini?” kungaba lula kunencazelo ende. Abanye abantu bazi amagama afana nokuthi “kuyabuhlungu”, “akukhululekile” noma “kukhathaza”. Nika isikhathi sokuphendula futhi wamukele impendulo yakhe ngaphandle kokuhlola noma ukumlungisa.

Buza umbuzo oyedwa bese umisa. Umuntu angase akhombe, enze izinkomba noma asebenzise umshwana ojwayelekile esikhundleni sokubiza ingxenye yomzimba. Gwema imibuzo esheshayo noma ukumqondisa ekutheni yebo. Uma ethi cha kodwa wena usuke wabona ushintsho, yabelana ngalo nochwepheshe wezempilo esikhundleni sokuxabana ngokuthi ubuhlungu bukhona yini.

## Yiziphi izinguquko okufanele zinakwe?

Qaphela izinguquko ezivela ebusweni, ekunyakazeni nasendleleni ejwayelekile yomuntu. Angase adonse ubuso, akhale, agone, anciphise ukunyakaza, alale ngendlela ehlukile noma aphelelwe yisithakazelo ekudleni. Angase abe nokuthula okungavamile, ukudangala noma ukungazinzi.

Akukho lota kwalezi zimpawu okufakazela ukuthi umuntu unobuhlungu. Zingaba nezizathu eziningi. Ukwenqaba ukudla kungahlobana nobuhlungu emlonyeni, kodwa kungaba nenye incazelo. Yabelana nokubonile nodokotela noma unesi, bese bevumela bahlole imbangela.

## Ngingachaza kanjani engikubonile?

Bhala amaqiniso ambalwa ukuze ukuchaze kahle lolo shintsho. Qopha okwenzekile, lapho kuqale khona, ukuthi kuvela kangakanani nokuthi yini eyayenzeka ngaleso sikhathi. Isibonelo, khomba uma umuntu eqhaqhazela lapho emi, egwema umsebenzi ojwayelekile noma edla kancane kunavamile. Faka amagama omuntu noma izinkomba zakhe.

Qhathanisa lolo shintsho nendlela ejwayelekile yomuntu, hhayi nomunye umuntu. Umuntu othulile angase angamemezeli uma elimele; omunye angakhombisa ukungakhululeki ngokunyakaza noma ngokukhuluma kakhulu. Gwema ukwenza isilinganiso sobuhlungu noma ukunquma ukuthi ukuziphatha kunembangela eyodwa. Injongo ukunika ochwepheshe wezempilo izinkomba ezisizayo.

Uma umuntu evumile, yabelana ngamanothi nomndeni, unesi noma udokotela abayingxenye yokunakekelwa kwakhe. Gcina ulwazi luyimfihlo bese wabelana kuphela ngalokho okusiza umuntu athole ukunakekelwa. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuvakashela esibhedlela](/caregiving/hospital-visit-with-dementia/) uchaza kabanzi ngokwabelana ngokuthandayo kwesikhulumi nabasebenzi besibhedlela.

## Yenzenjani uma ngicabanga ukuthi umuntu unobuhlungu?

Xhumana nochwepheshe wezempilo bese uchaza ukuthi kushintshe kanjani. Ubuhlungu bungaba nezizathu eziningi, kuhlanganise nokulimala kwakudala, ukuqhuma kwamalunga, izinkinga zamazinyo, ukungeneka kwegciwane, ukuqunjelwa noma ingcindezi yokuhlala endaweni eyodwa isikhathi eside. Udokotela angahlola impilo yomuntu anqume ukuhlolwa noma ukwelashwa okufanele.

Ungaqali umuthi wobuhlungu, ushintshe isilinganiso noma ume umuthi omunye ngokusekelwe ewebhusayithi. Ezinye izidakamizwa zingahlanganyela noma zibe yingozi kubantu abanezimo ezithile zempilo. Landela uhlelo lokunakekelwa komuntu bese ubuza umbhali wemithi noma ikhemisi uma kukhona okungacacile.

Ungaphinde unciphise ingcindezi esheshayo kuyilapho ufuna iseluleko. Buza ukuthi yini engamlethela induduzo, vumela umuntu aphumule futhi ungaphoqi ukunyakaza noma umsebenzi obuhlungikayo. Hlola nochwepheshe wezempilo ngaphambi kokusebenzisa ukushisa, ukupholisa, ukuhlikihla noma enye indlela yokwelapha uma ungaqiniseki ukuthi iphephile.

## Ingabe ushintsho lokuziphatha lungabangelwa okunye?

Yebo. Ubuhlungu buyinto eyodwa engaba yimbangela yokukhathazeka, kodwa noshintsho lungahlobana nokukhathala, indawo edidayo, indlala, ukungeneka kwegciwane, imiphumela yemithi noma enye inkinga yezempilo. Bheka okwenzeke ngaphambi koshintsho nokuthi luphindeka yini, kodwa ungazami ukuthola imbangela yezempilo wedwa.

Uma umuntu edidekile ngokuzumayo noma eshintshe kakhulu ekwejwayeleke kuye, tshela ochwepheshe wezempilo masinyane. [I-NHS ithi ukudideka okuzumayo kudinga usizo lwezokwelapha oluphuthumayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Sebenzisa izinsiza zezimo eziphuthumayo zendawo noma inqubo yokunakekelwa okuphuthumayo lapho ohlala khona.

Uma ushintsho luba kancane kancane noma luphindeka, hlela ukuvakasha kodokotela bese uletha amanothi akho. [Umhlahlandlela wokulungiselela ukuvakasha kodokotela](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) unendlela elula yokubhala imibuzo nokuchaza izinguquko.

## Abasizi bangaphendula kanjani ngendlela ehlonipha?

Tshela umuntu ukuthi uzokwenzani ngaphambi kokumsiza. Cela imvume ngaphambi kokumthinta noma ukususa into yezingubo. Nikeza ukukhetha okukodwa okuncane, njengokuphumula manje noma ukuhlala endaweni ehlukile, bese unikeza isikhathi sokuphendula. Uma enqaba, misa bese ucela ochwepheshe wezempilo enye indlela.

Buza umuntu ukuthi uthanda amagama noma uhlobo losizo luni. Ngemvume yakhe, yabelana ngolwazi oluwusizo nabanye abasizi abathembekile ukuze umuntu angadingi ukuchaza into efanayo njalo. Ungakhulumi ngabantu ngokuthi abakho lapho.

Ubuhlungu bungaba nzima ukubona ngaphandle. Ukubuza ngobumnene, ukubheka izinguquko nokuthola iseluleko sochwepheshe kungasiza umuntu azwakale ngaphandle kokuthatha isimpawu njengokuxilongwa.
