"Udokotela uzobonwa ngasiphi isikhathi?" Emizuzwini eyishumi kamuva: "Udokotela uzobonwa ngasiphi isikhathi?" Ukuzwa umbuzo ofanayo uphindaphindwa kungenye yezinto ezikhathaza kakhulu ekunakekeleni umuntu ophila nesifo sokukhohlwa. Kuvame kakhulu, akwenziwa ngamabomu, futhi kunezindlela ezizolile zokuphendula ezenza usuku lube lula kini nobabili.

## Kungani umuntu ophila nesifo sokukhohlwa ebuza umbuzo ofanayo kaningi?

Ikakhulukazi kungenxa yenkumbulo. Isifo sokukhohlwa sithinta inkumbulo yesikhashana, ngakho umuntu ngokuvamile akakhumbuli ukuthi usebuzile, noma akayikhumbuli impendulo. I-[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/alzheimers-today-volume-17-number-3/navigating-difficult-behaviors/) ichaza ukuthi ukubuza imibuzo kaningi kujwayelekile futhi kulindelekile.

Umbuzo uvame ukuthwala nomuzwa. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) ichaza ukuthi umbuzo ophindaphindwayo ungaveza ukukhathazeka, isicelo sosizo, noma ukungakhululeki nokungazizwa uphephile, nokuthi umuntu uvame ukufuna induduzo nokujwayelekile. "Sizohamba nini?" kungase kusho ngempela ukuthi "Ngikhathazekile ngokuhamba." Isithukuthezi, ukwesaba, umsindo kanye nezinguquko ohlelweni lwansuku zonke konke kungakubangela, futhi umbuzo ophindaphindwayo ungakhomba nesidingo, njengokuya endlini yangasese ([Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/)).

## Kufanele ngithini?

Phendula ngokuzola nangokufushane, sengathi okokuqala.

- **Gcina impendulo imfushane futhi ifane.** "Udokotela uzobonwa ngehora lesibili ntambama. Ngizoba nawe." Impendulo engaguquki kulula ukuyiqonda.
- **Phendula umuzwa.** "Uyazibuza ngodokotela. Kusihlwa namuhla, futhi ngizoshayela. Akukho okudingeka ukwenze."
- **Sebenzisa izwi elifudumele nokuthinta ngobumnene,** uma ethanda ukuthintwa. I-Alzheimer's Association yeluleka ukuthi uhlale uzolile futhi ungasebenzisi ingqondo ephoqayo noma ukuphikisana.
- **Nikeza impendulo ayifunayo,** kaningi ngendlela edingekayo. I-Alzheimer's Association ithi yiphinde kaningi ngendlela abuza ngayo.

## Yini okufanele ngiyigweme?

- **"Usungibuzile lokho."** I-[Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) yeluleka ngokumelene nokuveza ukuthi usanda kubuza. Lokho ngeke kumsize akhumbule, futhi kungazwakala njengokuthethwa.
- **Izincazelo ezinde.** Amagama amaningi asho okuningi okungakhohlakala.
- **Ukububula, ukugoqa amehlo noma izwi elibukhali.** I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) yeluleka ukubekezela nokungakhombisi ukukhungatheka. Lokho kunzima; bheka isigaba samakhefu ngezansi.

## Amathuluzi alula aphendula umbuzo esikhundleni sakho

Ngesikhathi umuntu esakwazi ukufunda, impendulo ebhaliwe ayibonayo ivame ukusiza kakhulu kuneyashiwo ngomlomo.

| Ithuluzi | Indlela yokulisebenzisa |
| --- | --- |
| Inothi elinezinhlamvu ezinkulu | "Udokotela namuhla ngo-2 pm. UMary uzoshayela." Libeke lapho avame ukubheka khona |
| Ibhodi elimhlophe | Uhlelo lwanamuhla ngemigqa emithathu noma emine; yesule bese ubhala kabusha njalo ekuseni |
| Iwashi elikhulu elinosuku nedethi | Lisiza ngemibuzo ethi "Namuhla usuku luni?" nethi "Sekuyisikhathi yini?" |
| Ikhalenda | Dweba umugqa osukwini ngalunye; kokeleza ukuvakasha okulandelayo |
| Izithombe ezijwayelekile | Isithombe somuntu ozayo singaphendula umbuzo othi "Ubani ozovakasha?" |

Le mibono ivela ku-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/repetition) naku-[Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf). Khomba inothi ngobumnene ("Asibheke ibhodi ndawonye") esikhundleni sokuthi "Ngikubhalele phansi."

## Mtshele ngezehlakalo uma sekusondele isikhathi

Ukukhuluma nge-aphoyintimenti noma ngohambo ezinsukwini ezingaphambili kungaphenduka amahora emibuzo egcwele ukukhathazeka. I-Family Caregiver Alliance kanye ne-Alzheimer's Los Angeles bobabili baphakamisa ukuthi ukhulume ngezinhlelo, ikakhulukazi ezicindezelayo njengokuvakashela udokotela noma ukugeza, kuphela uma sekusondele isikhathi sazo. Bese utshela ukuthi yini ezolandela, isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi.

## Imisebenzi engasiza ukushintsha ukunaka

Uma usuphendulile futhi wavuma umuzwa, umsebenzi ozolile nojabulisayo ungasiza, ikakhulukazi uma isithukuthezi siyimbangela yemibuzo. Yonke imithombo engenhla iphakamisa ukuqondisa ukunaka entweni umuntu ayithandayo.

1. **Umdlalo omfushane nolula ndawonye.** I-[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) igcina izithombe zibonakala, ngakho akukho okudingeka kukhunjulwe.
2. **Umsebenzi ojwayelekile.** Ukugoqa amathawula, ukuhlunga amasokisi noma ukusetha itafula. I-[Sort together](/games/sort-together/) iwuhlobo lwalokhu esikrinini.
3. **Khuluma ngento ayithandayo.** I-[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) inikeza iziqalo zengxoxo ezivulekile ngaphandle kwezimpendulo ezifanele noma ezingalungile.
4. **Umculo, ukuhamba kancane noma isiphuzo esilula.** I-National Institute on Aging ikubala lokhu njengezindlela ezilula zokuqondisa ukunaka kwenye indawo.
5. **Ukuthula isikhashana.** Hlalani ndawonye, nibheke ngaphandle kwefasitela, noma nizame umzuzu wokuphefumula ngokuzola ndawonye nge-[calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).

Uma ebuya embuzweni, mphendule futhi, ngomusa. Kulungile lokho.

## Bheka amaphethini

I-Alzheimer's Association iphakamisa ukuqaphela ukuthi imibuzo ivela ngezikhathi ezithile zosuku, eduze kwabantu abathile noma ezindaweni ezithile yini. Uma iqala njalo ngaphambi kokuba kufike izivakashi, noma ntambama sekwephuzile, ungahlela kusenesikhathi: inothi mayelana nokuvakasha, umsebenzi ojwayelekile osulungile, igumbi elinokuthula okwengeziwe. Umhlahlandlela wethu we-[imisebenzi ezolile ntambama sekwephuzile](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) ungasiza uma kusihlwa kunzima kakhulu.

## Zinakekele nawe

Ukuphendula umbuzo ofanayo okwesikhathi samashumi amabili kuyakhathaza. Uma uzizwa uphelelwa ukubekezela futhi umuntu ephephile, ngena kwelinye igumbi imizuzu embalwa bese uphefumula, njengoba kuphakamisa i-[Alzheimer's Los Angeles](https://eastonad.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/Caregiver-Tip-Sheets_Repeating-AlzLA-2018-2021.pdf). I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) ikhuthaza abanakekeli ukuthi bacele usizo futhi bacabangele ukunakekelwa kwesikhashana kokuphumula. Eminye imibono iku-[mhlahlandlela wethu wokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/).

## Kufanele ngishayele udokotela nini?

Shayela udokotela wakhe ngokushesha uma imibuzo, ukudideka noma indlela yokuziphatha kushintsha ngokungazelelwe noma kuba kubi kakhulu emahoreni noma ezinsukwini. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) ithi ukuziphatha okushintsha ngokungazelelwe noma ngokushesha, ikakhulukazi ngemva kokutheleleka noma ukushintsha komuthi, kufanele kubikwe kudokotela ngokushesha. I-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/mood-and-behaviour-changes/delirium) ichaza ukuthi ukutheleleka, imithi, ukungabi namanzi okwanele emzimbeni, ubuhlungu noma ukuqunjelwa kungabangela i-delirium, ukudideka okungazelelwe nokukhulu okudinga ukwelashwa okusheshayo. Shayela u-911 uma kukhona osengozini.
