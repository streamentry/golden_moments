Izinkinga zokuya endlini yangasese zingakhathaza umuntu nabantu abamsizayo. Bhekana nesigameko njengodaba lokunakekelwa, hhayi njengokwehluleka. Buza ukuthi yisiphi isekelo esizwakala sikhululekile, gcina umuntu ehileleke futhi uvikele ubumfihlo bakhe. Umuntu angase enze ezinye izinyathelo ngokwakhe noma angafuna umsizi ajwayelene naye aseduze.

Umhlahlandlela we-[Alzheimer's Association ngokungazibambi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence), [iseluleko se-Dementia Australia ngokulawula umzimba](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/home-life/continence) kanye [nomhlahlandlela we-NHS wokunakekela umuntu onesifo se-dementia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/looking-after-someone/) incoma isithunzi, ukusekelwa okulula neseluleko sochwepheshe lapho izinkinga ziqala. Leli khasi linolwazi jikelele, hhayi ukuxilongwa noma uhlelo lomuntu siqu lokunakekelwa.

## Ingabe inkinga entsha yendlu yangasese ihlala ibangelwa yi-dementia?

Cha. Inkinga entsha noma ekhulayo ingaba nezimbangela eziningi. I-dementia ingenza kube nzima ukuqaphela isidingo sokuya, ukuthola indlu yangasese noma ukulawula izingubo. Ezinye izimbangela zingaba ukutheleleka, ukuqunjelwa, imiphumela yemithi noma ubunzima bokunyakaza. Umnakekeli akakwazi ukuthola imbangela ngesigameko esisodwa.

Xhumana nodokotela noma omunye ochwepheshe wezempilo uma ukulawula komchamo nokuhamba kwamathumbu kushintsha. Uma kusiza, qaphela ukuthi ushintsho luqale nini, ubone ini, nokuthi umuntu ukhulume yini ngobuhlungu noma ukungakhululeki. Lokhu kungasiza ochwepheshe ukubuza imibuzo egxile. Ungaphinde noma ushintshe imithi ngokusekelwe kuhlahla oluvamile.

Uma umuntu edideka ngokuzumayo, xhumana nosizo lwezokwelapha lwendawo ngokushesha. [Iseluleko se-NHS ngokudideka okuzumayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sichaza ukuthi ukudideka okuzumayo kudinga ukunakwa ngokushesha. Ushintsho oluncane ezindleleni zokuya endlini yangasese luyahluka, kodwa lusafanele ingxoxo yochwepheshe.

## Ngingenza kanjani indlu yangasese ibe lula ukuyithola nokuyisebenzisa?

Yenza indlela ibonakale ilula futhi icace. Ukukhanya kwasebusuku, indawo yokuhamba eyanele nezinto zasekhaya ezibekwe endaweni ejwayelekile kungasiza. Igama elilula noma isithombe emnyango singaba wusizo uma umuntu elibona. Buza ngaphambi kokushiya umnyango wendlu yangasese uvulekile, ngoba umuntu angase akhethe ubumfihlo.

Izingubo ezilula ukuzikhipha zingancisha izinyathelo ezengeziwe. Gcina izingubo ezijwayelekile zingafinyeleleki, bese ugwema ukushintsha isibambi noma indawo yokugcina uma kwenza uhlelo lube lukhulu. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuphepha ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) ubhekela ukukhanya nendlela ecacile eya endlini yangasese. Buza ochwepheshe bezokusebenza noma omunye ochwepheshe oqeqeshiwe ngaphambi kokushintsha izinto zendlu yangasese noma ukufaka izibambezelo.

Igumbi kufanele libe lubumfihlo futhi lijwayelekile. Nciphisa okungaphazamisa okungase kuphindwe njengendlu yangasese noma enye into. Gcina iswishi yokukhanya itholakala kalula. Shintsha into eyodwa ngesikhathi, bese uyigcina uma umuntu enokukhululeka nayo.

## Ngingakhuluma ngani uma umuntu kungenzeka adinge ukuya endlini yangasese?

Sebenzisa amagama ajwayelekile abantu abadala nethoni elulekile. Isimemo esifushane esithi “Ufuna ukusebenzisa indlu yangasese?” sinika umuntu ithuba lokuphendula. Gwema ukukhuluma njengengane, ukuhlekisa, izikhumbuzo esidlangalaleni noma ukubuza phambi kwabanye abantu uma lokho kungamhlazisa.

Abanye abantu bakhombisa isidingo ngezenzo esikhundleni samagama. Bangase babe nokungakhululeki, bahambe phambili nangapha, bahuquzele izingubo noma bahambele egumbini elijwayelekile. Lezi zimpawu azisho ngokuqinisekile ukuthi umuntu udinga ukuya endlini yangasese, ngakho nikeza usizo ngobumnene futhi unake impendulo yakhe.

Uma isikhathi sokuya endlini yangasese esijwayelekile sisebenza komuntu, nikeza kube yingxenye yosuku. Uhlelo ungumemo, hhayi umyalelo. Uma enqaba, ungamphikisi noma umphoqe ukuthi ahlale phansi. Linda, nikeza isikhathi esinye uma efuna, bese ulandela izindlela zomuntu ejwayelekile esikhundleni sokumnikeza uhlelo oqinile.

## Ngingasabela kanjani ngemva kwesigameko?

Gcina izwi lakho lingacwasi futhi ulondoloze isithunzi somuntu. Gwema ukusola, ukunengeka noma ukumlungisa phambi kwabanye. Ukuthi “Ngitshele ukuthi yini engasiza” kuvumela umuntu akuqondise. Nikeza izingubo ezihlanzekile noma indawo eyimfihlo yokushintsha, kodwa buza ngaphambi kokusiza.

Nika umuntu isikhathi sokwenza izinyathelo azikwazi. Angase afune ukushintsha izingubo ngokwakhe, abe nomuntu ajwayelene naye eduze noma ashiywe wedwa isikhashana. Uma edinga usizo lwesandla, chaza ngezinyathelo ezimfushane bese uqinisekisa ukuthi uyazizwa ekhululekile. Ungambambi noma ume ukumshesha ngomsebenzi ongowakhe.

Ungalungisa izingubo ezihlanzekile noma ibhegi elingenakubonakala uma umuntu efuna lokho uma ephuma. Buza ngaphambi kokugcina imikhiqizo yokungazibambi endaweni yokwabelana ngayo noma ukutshela abanye abantu ngesigameko. Uma imikhiqizo noma izicubu zingasiza, buza ochwepheshe wezempilo noma umeluleli wokungazibambi ukuthi yiziphi izinketho ezifanele izidingo nezifiso zomuntu.

## Ngabe kufanele ngivimbele uketshezi noma ngikhethe imikhiqizo yokungazibambi?

Ungancishi uketshezi lomuntu ukuze uvimbele izingozi ngaphandle kokuthi ochwepheshe wezempilo bakunikeze iseluleko esithile. I-Alzheimer's Association ixwayisa ukuthi ukuvimbela uketshezi kungaholela ekomisweni nezinye izinkinga. Abantu abanesifo se-dementia nabo bangadinga izikhumbuzo noma usizo lokuphuza ngokwanele. Landela uhlelo lokunakekelwa komuntu ngamunye uma kukhona imikhawulo yoketshezi.

Amapadi, izingubo ezivikelayo noma izindwangu zokulala ezingangeni amanzi kuyizinqumo zomuntu siqu. Zingasiza abanye abantu, kodwa akukho umkhiqizo owodwa ofanele wonke umuntu. Khuluma nodokotela noma nomeluleli wokungazibambi ngezinketho, induduzo, ukulingana, izinkinga zesikhumba nokuthi umuntu ufuna ukuzisebenzisa kanjani. Ungafaki umkhiqizo ocaleni komuntu anqaba; buza ukuthi yini ayamukelayo.

## Nginganqoba nini usizo lwezokwelapha?

Buza udokotela mayelana nezinkinga ezintsha, ezikhulayo noma ezibuhlungu zendlu yangasese. Ukutheleleka, ukuqunjelwa, umphumela wemithi noma enye inkinga yezempilo ingadinga ukunakwa. Thatha inkulumo emfushane echaza ukuthi yini eshintshile nokuthi kwenzeke nini. Umeluleli wokungazibambi angasiza nangezinketho ezithile endaweni ethile; buza udokotela ukuthi ungathola kanjani usizo lwasendaweni.

Funa usizo oluphuthumayo lwezokwelapha uma umuntu eba nokudideka okuzumayo. Ezinye izinkinga zokungazibambi, xhumana nochwepheshe wezempilo ukuze uthole iseluleko esikhundleni sokuchaza imbangela ngokwakho. Ungancishi uketshezi, ushintshe imithi noma uqale ukwelashwa ngokusekelwe esihlokweni esisewebhusayithi.

## Ngabelana kanjani ngomsebenzi ngendlela ehlonipha?

Uma abantu abaningi besiza, buza umuntu ukuthi ufuna kwabelwane ngani. Inothi elingasese elinamagama akhe ngokuthandayo, indlela ajwayelekile nohlobo losizo lingagwema ukuchaza into efanayo njalo. Yabelana kuphela ngalokho umsizi olandelayo akudingayo ukwazi, bese ubuyekeza inothi uma izifiso zomuntu zishintsha.

Ukusekela indlu yangasese kungakhathaza nabanakekeli. Cela umuntu othembekile noma insiza yokunakekela ukuthi ithathe ithuba uma umuntu evumile. [Umhlahlandlela wokuzinakekela womnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ufaka izindlela zokufuna usizo nokuphumula. Ukunakekelwa okuhloniphayo kusho ukuvikela kokubili ubumfihlo bomuntu nekhono lomnakekeli lokuqhubeka.

Injongo ukwenza usizo lutholakale ngaphandle koxinzelelo. Phendula ngokuzola, vikela ubumfihlo, gcina izinqumo zomuntu bese ubuza ochwepheshe mayelana nezinkinga ezintsha.
