Ukuthola usizo lwasekhaya kuqala ngomuntu, hhayi ngohlu lwezinsizakalo. Buzani ukuthi ufuna ukuqhubeka ukwenza yini ngokwakhe nokuthi yiluphi usizo angalamukela. Umhlinzeki angase asekele ukuba nomuntu, imisebenzi yansuku zonke, imisebenzi yasekhaya noma ukunakekelwa kwezempilo okuqeqeshiwe. Ukuhlanganiswa okufanele kuncike emuntwini, emisebenzini nosizo olukhona ngaleso sikhathi.

[Umhlahlandlela we-Alzheimer's Association wokunakekelwa ekhaya](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/in-home-care) kanye [nesifinyezo se-Dementia Australia sokunakekelwa ekhaya](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/home-care) achaza izinhlobo ezahlukene zezinsizakalo. [Uhlu lwabahlinzeki lwe-Association](https://www.alz.org/media/Documents/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-in-home-care-professional-ts.pdf) luncoma ukubuza ngokuqeqeshwa, ulwazi, izincomo, ukusekelwa nezindleko. Izinsizakalo, izitifiketi nezindlela zezimali ziyahluka endaweni; leli khasi aliphakamisi mhlinzeki noma lichaze ukufaneleka.

## Umuntu ufuna usizo luhlobo luni?

Qalani ngemisebenzi ebalulekile ensukwini yomuntu. Angafuna umuntu azokhuluma naye, asize ekuthengeni noma ekulungiseleleni ukudla, noma asekelwe ekugezeni noma ekugqokeni. Ukunakekelwa kwezempilo okuqeqeshiwe, njengokunesi noma ukwelashwa komzimba, kuyinsizakalo ehlukile futhi kungase kudinge ochwepheshe abagunyaziwe noma isiqondiso sohlinzeko lwezempilo.

Bhalani usizo oludingekayo, lapho ludingeka khona nalokho umuntu akukhethayo. Faka nalokho angakwenza ngaphandle koseko nalapho efuna ukuba yedwa khona. Lokhu kungagcina usizo lugxile ekusekeleni ukuzimela kunokuthatha usuku lonke lwakhe.

Uma umuntu engaqiniseki, qalani ngesidingo esisodwa esisebenzayo noma ukuvakashela okufushane lapho leyo nketho itholakala khona. Buzani ukuthi ezizwe kanjani ngemva kwalokho. Angafuna ukushintsha umsebenzi, isikhathi noma umuntu omsizayo. Uhlelo lokunakekela lungalungiswa njengoba izidingo nezifiso zishintsha.

## Ngibuze ini umhlinzeki ngaphambi kokuqasha?

Shayani ucingo kubahlinzeki abaningi bese nichaza imisebenzi umuntu afuna ukusizwa ngayo. Buzani ukuthi bangakwazi yini ukuhlangabezana nalezo zidingo, bayatholakala nini nokuthi basha ukunakekela umuntu onedementia yini. Buzani ukuthi abasebenzi babo baqeqeshwa kanjani ngokuthi kwenzeka kanjani kuye, nokuthi bamsekela kanjani umuntu oxakile, ekhathazekile noma edinwe.

Chazani imininingwane ethinta impilo yansuku zonke: ubani ozofika, kangakanani, isikhathi sokuhlala ngasinye, kwenzekani uma umsebenzi egula, nokuthi ubani okufanele ushaywe ucingo uma kukhona ukukhathazeka. Uma umuntu efisa umsizi ofanayo uma kungenzeka, buzani ukuthi isevisi ingakunikeza yini lokho okuqhubekayo.

Celani izincomo nokuthi insiza yesevisi ihlola kanjani abasebenzi bayo. Thola ukuthi ukuhlolwa kwangemuva, ukuqeqeshwa nezitifiketi kuyahlolwa yini, nokuthi imisebenzi umsebenzi ovunyelwe ukuyenza yiyiphi. Imithetho nezihloko zihluka endaweni, ngakho qinisekisani noma yiziphi izidingo zobungcweti nezelayisense endaweni.

## Ngingahlola kanjani ukuthi umuntu nomhlinzeki bahambelana?

Uma umuntu evumelana, hlelani ukuhlangana ekhaya ngaphambi kokuvakashela kokuqala. Qaphelani ukuthi umhlinzeki ukhuluma nomuntu ngqo yini, ulalela izintandokazi zakhe futhi umnika isikhathi sokuphendula. Buzani umuntu ukuthi ukuhlangana kumzwe kanjani, ngendlela angakwazi ukuphendula ngayo ngokuzwakalayo.

I-Alzheimer's Association incoma ukuba nomunye umuntu omethembekile obekhona ngesikhathi sokuxoxisana, ukuze nixoxe ngemibono yenu ngemva kwalokho. Sebenzisani ukuhlangana ukuxoxa ngokuvakashela okujwayelekile: ukufika, ukubingelelwa, umsebenzi ohleliwe nokuthi umuntu angacela kanjani ukuyeka noma ukuphumula.

Uma kungenzeka, vumelanani ngokuvakashela okufushane kokuqala bese nikubuyekeza kanye nomuntu. Umhlinzeki angabonakala elungele ephepheni bese engafaneleki umuntu ngokwakhe. Hlonipha impendulo yomuntu bese nixoxa ngezinye izindlela uma kukhona ukukhathazeka kwenu nakwakhe.

## Yiluphi ulwazi okufanele ngabelane ngalo nomsizi?

Yabelanani kuphela ngemininingwane esiza umhlinzeki ukuthi asekele umuntu ngokuphepha nangenhlonipho. Ngemvume yomuntu, chazani ukuthi ufisa ukubizwa kanjani, yimaphi amasu ebalulekile, yini esiza ukuxhumana nokuthi ufisa ukwenza yena ngokwakhe yini.

Uma izincomo zezempilo zithinta umsebenzi, buzani umuntu ongumelaphi wakhe ukuthi umhlinzeki kumele akwazi ini. Ningabuzi umsebenzi ongaqeqeshiwe ukuthi enze imisebenzi yezokwelapha engaphandle kwasemsebenzini wakhe noma kwenqubo yakhe. Gcinani ulwazi lomuntu luyimfihlo, bese nihlola ukuthi ubani onokufinyelela kunoma yiziphi izinothi ezibhaliwe noma amarekhodi okunakekelwa.

[Umhlahlandlela wokulungiselela ukuvakashela udokotela](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ungasiza ukuhlela imibuzo yochwepheshe wezempilo. [Umhlahlandlela wokuphepha ekhaya](/caregiving/home-safety/) umboza izinguquko zasekhaya nokuthi kufanele ubuze ochwepheshe nini.

## Ngingawubheka kanjani umhlahlandlela ngokuhamba kwesikhathi?

Hlolani nomuntu ngemva kokuvakashela. Buzani ukuthi yini ezwakale ikhululekile, yini ayijabulele nokuthi yini angayishintsha. Uma engakhulumi kakhulu, qaphelani ukuthi uyamamukela yini umsizi, uzizwa ekhululekile yini nokuthi ufisa ukuthi ukuvakashela kuqhubeke yini. Lezi zibonakaliso azisiyo amaphuzu; zisetshenziselwe ukuqondisa ingxoxo.

Vumelanani nomhlinzeki ukuthi nizobelana kanjani ngezibuyekezo ezijwayelekile nokuthi nizobhekana kanjani nokukhathazeka. Uma ukuvakashela kukhanselwa, yazini ukuthi ubani ongahlinza uhlelo lokusekela noma ukuthi umuntu ufisa ukwenza yini esikhundleni salokho. Buyekezani imisebenzi lapho uhlelo lwansuku lomuntu, impilo yakhe noma izidingo zakhe zosizo zishintsha.

Izindleko, izikhathi nezinsizakalo ezitholakalayo ziyahluka. Celani umhlinzeki imigomo ebhalwe phansi, okubandakanya amazinga, ubude obuncane bokuvakashela, imithetho yokukhansela nezindleko ezengeziwe. Udokotela womuntu, izinsizakalo zasendaweni zokuguga noma izinhlangano zedementia zingase zincome izindawo zokubheka, kodwa qhathanisani abahlinzeki ngokwenu nomuntu.

Uma umuntu edideka ngokuzumayo noma ephila kabi kakhulu, ungacabangi ukuthi lolo shintsho luvela ku-dementia. [Iseluleko se-NHS sithi usizo lwezokwelapha oluphuthumayo luyadingeka ngokudideka okuvelayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Ngokukhathazeka okuqhubekayo mayelana nokunakekelwa, khulumani nodokotela noma nochwepheshe ofanele.

Inhloso ukusekela okufanelana nempilo yomuntu. Chazani umsebenzi, hlola ukuthi umhlinzeki unolwazi yini nohlelo lokusekela, bandakanya umuntu kunqumo ngalunye bese nibuyekeza ukuthi ulungiselelo luzizwa kanjani ngokuhamba kwesikhathi.
