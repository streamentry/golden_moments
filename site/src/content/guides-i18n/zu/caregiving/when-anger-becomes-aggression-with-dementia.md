Ukuthukuthela kungaguqukela ekuklolodeleni, ekusunduzeni noma ekushayeni, futhi lokho kungesabisa bonke abaseduze. Ukuziphatha okunjalo kungenzeki ngenhloso. Akusho ukuthi umuntu uhlose ukulimaza omunye. Qala ngokwenza isimo siphephe kakhudlwana, bese ubheka ukuthi umuntu ngabe edinga yini.

## Yini okufanele ngiyenze uma ukuthukuthela kuba ulaka?

Misa umsebenzi, shiya indawo bese usho umusho omfushane nokuthula. [I-Alzheimer's Association inikeza abanakekeli iseluleko sokugxila emizweni, ukukhuluma kancane nokunciphisa ukuphazamiseka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Uma usengozini esheshayo noma umuntu elimele, hamba uye endaweni ephephile bese uxhumana nezinsiza zezimo eziphuthumayo zendawo.

- **Misa lokho okwenzekayo uma ungakwazi.** Uma ukugezisa, ukugqoka, ukuvakashela noma omunye umsebenzi kuphazamisa umuntu, yima. Lokho kungalinda uma kungekho sidingo esiphuthumayo sokuphepha noma sezempilo.
- **Nika isikhala.** Shiya indawo bese ugcina indlela esobala yokuthi ngabe ngabe ninokuphuma. Ungamvimbi, ungasondeli kakhulu noma umthinte ngokungazelelwe.
- **Gcina izwi lakho liphansi namazwi akho emfushane.** Ungathi, “Ngiyabona ukuthi uyakhathazeka. Ngizokunika isikhala.” Ungaphikisani, ungasongi noma ufune incazelo.
- **Hambisa abanye abantu.** Ingane, isivakashi noma umsizi angaya enye indlu ngenkathi umuntu ezinza. Umuntu oyedwa ekhuluma ngokuthula ungase ube lula kunabantu abaningi abazama ukusiza ngesikhathi esifanayo.
- **Shiya uma kukhona udlame lwenyama.** Thola indawo ephephile bese ucela omunye umuntu usizo. Ungazami ukubamba umuntu.

I-[Alzheimer's Society incoma ukuthi umuntu ahoxe, agweme ukubamba noma ukuthinta ngokungazelelwe, bese ahamba ayofuna usizo uma kunodlame lwenyama](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). I-[Alzheimer's Association ikwazise futhi ukucela imvume, ukunciphisa ukuphazamiseka nokungamkhandli noma ukumbamba umuntu](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation). Sebenzisa izinsiza zezimo eziphuthumayo zendawo yakho uma kukhona ingozi esheshayo; ungalindi ukuthi isehlakalo sidlule uma umuntu esengase alimale.

## Yini engase ibangele ukuziphatha?

Kungase kube nezinto ezedlula eyodwa, futhi ilungu lomndeni alikwazi ukuchaza imbangela. I-Alzheimer's Association ibala ukungakhululeki emzimbeni, ingcindezi yendawo nezinkinga zokuxhumana njengezinto ezingase zibe nomthelela.

Cabanga ngalokho okwenzeke kungakapheli isikhathi esifushane ngaphambi koshintsho:

- Umuntu ubekhathele, elambile, emele amanzi, eshisekile noma esebanda kakhulu, noma engakhululekile?
- Umsebenzi ubunzima, ushesha noma ungajwayelekile? Ngabe omunye umuntu uqhubeke ngemva kokuthi umuntu ukhombise ukukhathazeka?
- Kube nemisindo emikhulu, abantu abaningi bekhuluma, izinto ezingahlelekile noma indawo engajwayelekile?
- Umuntu ubekuthola kunzima ukubona noma ukuzwa okwenzekayo?
- Ingabe umuthi, umkhuba, umnakekeli noma isimo sezempilo sishintshe kwakamuva?

I-[Alzheimer's Society incoma ukubheka amaphethini nokucabanga ukuthi isimo sizwakala kanjani ohlangothini lomuntu](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/preventing-aggression). Lemibuzo iyizikhumbuzi zokubheka, hhayi ubufakazi bokuthi into eyodwa ibangele ukuziphatha. Ungazami ukuhlola, ukubamba noma ukunika umuntu umuthi ngokwakho.

## Ngingakunciphisa kanjani ukuphinda kwesehlakalo?

Sebenzisa lokho owubonile ukulungisa ukuxhumana okulandelayo. Lokhu kungasiza, kodwa akukwazi ukuqinisekisa ukuthi ukuziphatha kwesi-sine ngeke kuphinde. Zama ukushintsha okukodwa ngesikhathi ukuze kube lula ukubona ukuthi umuntu uzizwa ekhululekile yini.

- **Khetha isikhathi esithulile.** Uma umsebenzi ujwayelekile ukuhamba kabi lapho umuntu ekhathele, buza ukuthi isikhathi esihlukile asiphumelela kangcono yini.
- **Yenza isimo sibe lula.** Vala umsindo ongemuva, nciphisa inani labantu egumbini bese uqoqa lokho okudingayo ngaphambi kokuqala.
- **Chaza isinyathelo esisodwa bese ulinda.** Sondela uvela phambili, tshela ukuthi uyenzani, cela imvume ngaphambi kokusiza bese unikeza isikhathi somuntu sokuphendula.
- **Ima uma kuba khona izimpawu zokukhathazeka.** Ukwenqaba, ukuhoxa, ubuso obuqinile noma izwi eliphakeme kungaba isignali yokuthi kufanele uyeke. Ungabheka ukuqeda umsebenzi njengokubaluleke kunenduduzo.
- **Yabelana ngohlelo oluyisimo.** Uma abantu abaningi benakekela umuntu, vumelanani ngezimpawu ezisho ukuthi “ima,” igama elithulile okufanele lisetshenziswe nokuthi ubani ozobizwa uma iphethini ishintsha.

Ngemisebenzi ethile, bheka imibono yethu yemihlahlandlela yokugeza no[kugqoka](/caregiving/getting-dressed-with-dementia/) nokugeza [ku-dementia](/caregiving/bathing-with-dementia/). [Umhlahlandlela wemibono yokuxhumana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) umboza indlela yokukhuluma nokulalela ngaphandle kokwengeza uxinezeleo.

## Yini ngingayenza ngemva kokuthi isimo sizole?

Nikeza induduzo bese ubuyela emsebenzini ojwayelekile, ongaphezu kwamandla, uma umuntu efuna lokho. Ungakhalimi, ungabeki icala noma ujezise. I-Alzheimer's Society ithi umuntu angase asazizwa ekhathazekile ngemva kokuthi ukuziphatha sekudlulile, nakuba engakhumbuli okwenzeke.

Uma ufuna ukuqonda iphethini, bhala inothi elifushane elisolwini lakho lokuthi kwenzekeni ngaphambili, okubonile, ukuthi kwaqhubeka isikhathi esingakanani nokuthi yini esiyisize. Gcina inothi lingokwenyani bese ulabelana nalo nethimba lokunakekela komuntu uma kufanele. Gwema ukuqopha umuntu noma ukwabelana ngemininingwane yakhe ngaphandle kwemvume.

Uma umsebenzi ngezinye izikhathi uholela ekukhathazekeni, shintsha isikhathi noma indlela, noma cela omunye umuntu othembekile ukuthi asize. Ungamukela ukwenqaba bese uzama kamuva. [Umhlahlandlela wokuthi yini ongayenza uma umuntu esho ukuthi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) unezindlela eziningi zokuma ngaphandle kokucindezela.

## Kufanele ngifune iseluleko sezokwelapha nini?

Xhumana nodokotela womuntu ngolaka olusha, olungazelelwe, olwenzeka kaningi noma olwandayo. I-[Alzheimer's Association ikhuthaza ukuhlolwa okwenziwa yisazi lezokwelapha uma ukuziphatha kushintsha, ikakhulukazi uma kuvela ngokungazelelwe](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/aggression-and-anger). Ukuhlupheka, ukugula, ukulala, indawo ahlala kuyo nemithi kungase kudinge ukubuyekezwa. Usazi wezokwelapha angahlola isimo somuntu.

Funa usizo lwezokwelapha oluphuthumayo uma umuntu edideka ngokungazelelwe, elimele noma ebonakala egule kabi kakhulu. Uma wena noma omunye umuntu useyingozini esheshayo, xhumana nezinsiza zezimo eziphuthumayo zendawo. Ungalindi ukuqoqa amanothi noma ukuzama ukuthola imbangela kuqala.

Ukuphepha kwakho kubalulekile nawe. Tshela ilungu lomndeni elithembekile, usazi wezokwelapha noma isevisi yezokusekela i-dementia yasendaweni uma izehlakalo ziqhubeka noma uzizwa uyesaba. Cela uhlelo oluvikela umuntu nabantu abamzungezile. Uyafanelwa usizo; ukubhekana nalokhu wedwa akuyona imfuneko.
