Uhambo lungaqhubeka lube yinto ongayikhetha uma umuntu efuna ukuhamba nohlelo lufanele izidingo zakhe. Ukuvakashela umndeni okufushane kungase kube lula kunohambo olugcwele izinto eziningi; uhambo lwesikhathi esifushane lungase lube ngcono kunokuhlala ubusuku. Buza ukuthi umuntu ufuna ukuthini kuhambo, bese wakha uhlelo olunesikhathi sokuphumula noma sokulushintsha.

[Umhlahlandlela wohambo we-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/traveling) kanye [neseluleko sohambo sakwa-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/travelling-dementia) bobabili bagcizelela ukuhlela ngokuzwela kwomuntu nezinkambo zakhe. Lesi sihloko sinikeza imibono yokulungiselela ejwayelekile, hhayi isinqumo sokuthi uhambo luphephile yini kumuntu othile.

## Sizokwenza kanjani isinqumo sokuthi uhambo luyafaneleka yini kithi?

Xoxa ngohambo nomuntu, usebenzisa amagama ajwayelekile nencazelo elula yokuthi niyaphi nokuthi ubani oyoba khona. Uma engafuni ukuhamba, yamukela lokho. Ungahlela isidlo, ikholi noma umsebenzi ojwayelekile endlini.

Cabanga ngalokho umuntu akwenza ngokuzwakalayo njengamanje. Qaphela ukuthi usabela kanjani ezinguqukweni zezinkambo, emsindweni, ezindaweni ezigcwele abantu, emabangeni okuhamba, ekulindeni nasekamelweni engajwayelekile. Uma izingxenye eziningi zohambo zingase zibe nzima, zenze zibe lula noma khetha enye indlela yokuchitha isikhathi ndawonye. U-Dementia Australia uncoma ukubuza udokotela uma ungaqiniseki ukuthi uhambo luyafaneleka yini impilo noma izidingo zokusekelwa zomuntu.

Uma umuntu enenkinga entsha yempilo, edinga usizo ngemithi, noma izidingo zokunyakaza ezingashintsha ohambweni, buza umsebenzi wezempilo ofanele ngaphambi kokubhuka. Ungami, ushintshe noma uhlelele kabusha imithi enikezwe ngudokotela ngaphandle kweseluleko sikadokotela onikeze umuthi noma isazi sekhemisi.

## Yiluphi uhlobo lohambo okufanele siluhlele?

- **Khetha indawo ejwayelekile uma kungenzeka.** Indawo umuntu ayaziyo, noma ukuvakashela abantu abajwayelekile, kungase kube nezinguquko ezimbalwa kunendlela entsha nohlelo oluyinkathazo lokuvakashela.
- **Gcina indlela ilula.** Khetha izinguquko ezimbalwa futhi ugweme ukuxhumana okunzima. Hlola lapho kukhona khona izindawo zangasese, izihlalo nezindawo ezithulile ngaphambi kokuqala.
- **Hamba ngesikhathi esihle sosuku.** Hlela ngamandla ajwayelekile omuntu nokuhamba kwezinsuku zakhe. Vumela isikhathi sokudla, imithi, ukuphumula nokulungiselela.
- **Gcina uhlelo lulula.** Umsebenzi oyinhloko owodwa ungase wanele. Ungabhuki yonke ihora; shiya isikhala sokulibaziseka noma sokubuya kusenesikhathi.
- **Buza ngosizo ngaphambi kokuhamba.** Izindiza, izitimela, amahhotela nezindawo zomcimbi zinezinsizakalo nemithetho ehlukile. Xhumana nabo kusenesikhathi ukuze ubuze ngosizo lokunyakaza, izihlalo, izindlu zangasese ezifinyeleleka kalula nosizo lokuthola indawo efanele.

Ihambo elithi [umhlahlandlela wezikhathi zeholide ezithulile](/activities/holidays/) linemibono yokuvakashela nemihlangano yomndeni. Uma uhlangana nezihlobo, [umhlahlandlela wokuvakashela](/activities/visiting/) uhlanganisa ingxoxo, ukuvakashela okufushane nokuhamba ngaphandle kokucindezeleka.

## Yini okufanele siyipakishe?

Gcina ibhegi elincane lezinto ezidingekayo ezihlala nomuntu wokuthembela kunokuba zibe ngaphakathi kwelageji elishiywe.

- **Imithi nolwazi lwezempilo.** Thwala imithi ejwayelekile yomuntu ephaketheni layo lokuqala, ezanele uhlelo lohambo kanye nokubambezeleka okunengqondo, nohlu lwemithi olusha. Hlola imithetho yamanje yokuthutha nendawo oyoya kuyo ngaphambi kokuthutha imithi enomyalelo kadokotela. Gcina izilinganiso nesikhathi njengoba kunikezwe.
- **Amadokhumenti okuqinisekisa nokuxhumana.** Thwala amadokhumenti okuqinisekisa ubunikazi womuntu, ikheli lendawo yokuhlala, imininingwane yokuxhumana esimweni esiphuthumayo nohlelo lohambo olubhaliwe. Uma kusiza futhi kuvunyelwene, faka ikhadi elinemininingwane yokuxhumana yomnakekeli.
- **Izinto zokududuza.** Thwala izibuko, izinsizakalo zokuzwa nezibhethri noma isishaji sazo, okudlwana okujwayelekile, amanzi, amathishu, isendlalelo esilula nezingubo ezengeziwe. Into ejwayelekile noma isithombe kungenza igumbi elingajwayelekile libe lula ukuthi umuntu azinze kulo.
- **Amakhophi wemininingwane ebalulekile.** Gcina ikhophi ephepheni noma ngedijithali yamathikithi, ukubhuka, ukuqinisekiswa nolwazi oluhlobene nempilo. Yabelana ngohlelo lohambo nomuntu othembekile ongahambi nawe.

Ukuthi kufanele uthwale ulwazi oluningi kangakanani kuncike ohambweni. Gcina imininingwane yomuntu iphephile futhi uyabelane kuphela nalabo abayidinga ukuze bakusize.

## Singenza kanjani usuku lohambo lube lula?

Tshela umuntu okuzolandela, isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi. Nikeza isikhathi esengeziwe sokulungiselela, ukubhalisa, ukusebenzisa indlu yangasese nokuhamba phakathi kwezindawo. Uma izinhlelo zishintsha, chaza isinyathelo esilandelayo ngokulula kunokuphinda uhlelo olude.

Hlalani ndawonye ezindaweni ezigcwele abantu noma ezingajwayelekile. Uma umuntu ekhathala noma edideka, yima endaweni ethule, nikeza amanzi noma umsebenzi ojwayelekile, bese nibe nesinqumo ngokuqhubeka. Ungamshiyi umuntu wodwa endaweni engajwayelekile lapho angadideka noma ahlupheke khona.

Uma ugibela indiza noma usebenzisa isiteshi esikhulu, buza umhlinzeki ukuthi yiluphi usizo olutholakalayo nokuthi lucelwa kanjani. Izinqubo zingashintsha, ngakho qinisekisa imininingwane ngqo nomthwalizindiza, isiteshi noma indawo ngaphambi kohambo. Ungathembeli kuhlu lwakudala lokuphepha nokufinyeleleka kwamanje.

## Uhlelo lokusiza olusizayo lungaba yini?

Nquma kusengaphambili ukuthi uzokwenzani uma umuntu efuna ukushiya, uhambo lubambezeleka noma kukhona oyedwa odinga ukuphumula. Khetha umuntu oyedwa oxhumana naye ongasiza ngocingo. Gcina ikheli lendawo yokuhlala nendlela yokubuyela ilula ukuyithola, futhi wazi lapho ungahlala khona ngokuthula noma usebenzise indlu yangasese.

Uma udinga ukufinyeza uhambo, yenza kanjalo. Ukuphuthelwa yisiza noma ukubuya ngokushesha akusiyo ukwehluleka. Uma ukuhamba kusiba nzima ekhaya, khetha umsebenzi ophephile lapho ufika khona. [Umhlahlandlela wokuxhumana](/caregiving/communication-tips/) unemibono yokukhuluma ngokuzola nokunikeza umuntu ulwazi olulodwa ngesikhathi.

## Kufanele sitshele ihhotela noma umhlinzeki wohambo?

Uma umuntu evumelana, tshela abasebenzi okudingeka bakwazi kuphela ukuze banikeze usizo oluwusizo. Isibonelo, ungacela ihhotela ukuthi liqaphele isidingo sokunyakaza noma lilethele igumbi elifinyeleleka kalula. Uma umuntu engafuni ukuthi ukuxilongwa kwakhe kutshelwe abanye, xoxa ngenye indlela yokucela usizo evikela ubumfihlo bakhe.

Uma uhambo lude, gcina izidlo zomuntu ejwayelekile, ukulala nesikhathi semithi kusondele kokujwayelekile ngokusho kwesikhathi sohlelo. Hlela usuku lokuqala olungaphuthumi futhi uvumele isikhathi sokuphumula ngemuva kokubuya. Izinqumo ezincane ezijwayelekile zingenza uhambo lube lula ukujwayela.
