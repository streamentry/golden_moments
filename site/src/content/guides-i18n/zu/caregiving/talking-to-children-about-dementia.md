Lapho i-dementia iguqula umuntu omthandayo ingane, ingase iqaphele ngaphambi kokuthi umuntu omdala aqale ingxoxo. Ingane ingase izizwe ikhathazekile, icasulekile, inolaka, inemizwa yokuba necala noma ingazi ukuthi kufanele yenzenjani. Yonke ingane nomndeni kuhlukile. Ulwazi olucacile nokusekelwa okuzinzile kungasiza ingane iqonde lokho okushintshayo ngaphandle kokuyicela ukuthi ithwale izibopho zabadala.

Umhlahlandlela we-[Alzheimer's Association wokukhuluma nezingane](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) uncoma ukuphendula imibuzo ngokuqotho ngamagama ezingane azi kahle. [Iseluleko somndeni se-Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) siphinde sigcizelele izingxoxo eziqotho, ukuqinisekisa nokuvikela isikhala somntwana sokuba yingane. Sebenzisa lezi zimpendulo njengendawo yokuqala, hhayi umbhalo oqondile wawo wonke umndeni.

## Ngiqala kanjani ingxoxo mayelana ne-dementia?

Qala ngoshintsho ingane elibonile noma umbuzo ebuze. Ungathi: “Uyaqaphela ukuthi uGogo uphinda imibuzo. Unesifo esibizwa ngokuthi i-dementia esithinta inkumbulo nokucabanga kwakhe.” Sebenzisa isifo esiqondile somuntu kuphela uma sesaziwa. Uma umndeni usalindele ukuhlolwa, chaza ushintsho ngaphandle kokulibiza ngegama.

Khetha isikhathi esithulile lapho ungahlala khona ingxoxo. Sebenzisa imisho emifushane bese uyima ukuze ingane iphendule. Akudingeki uchaze yonke imininingwane ngesikhathi esisodwa. Buza ukuthi isiqonda kanjani kakade nokuthi ifuna ukwazi ntoni okulandelayo. Ingane encane ingazuza esithombeni esilula noma esibonelo; ingane endala ingase ifune imininingwane eminingi noma isikhathi sokucabanga yodwa.

Gcina umuntu one-dementia ehileleke uma efuna futhi kwenzeka ukuthi angabamba iqhaza. Xoxa nengane ngokuzikhethela komuntu ngaphambi kokwabelana ngemininingwane yezempilo yakhe nabanye abantu. Ingane ingafuna umzali noma umuntu amethembayo aseduze uma ivakashela, noma ingakhetha ukungakhulumi ngesifo nabangane noma esikoleni okwamanje.

## Yini okufanele ngiyitshele ingane mayelana ne-dementia?

Chaza izingxenye ezibalulekile empilweni yansuku zonke yengane. I-dementia isifo esingashintsha indlela umuntu akhumbula ngayo, acabanga ngayo noma axhumane ngayo. Ayithelwa, futhi ingane ayiyibangelanga. [I-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/kids/talking-kids-about-dementia) iphawula ukuthi izingane zingakhathazeka ngokuthi zingathola i-dementia noma zizibeke icala lapho abadala bekhathazekile. Phendula lezo zinkinga ngqo.

Qotho ngalokho okwaziyo nalokho ongakwazi. Ungachaza lokho umndeni okubonile, ukusekelwa umuntu akutholayo nezinhlelo ezingathinta umkhuba wengane. Gwema ukuthembisa ukuthi umuntu uzophila kangcono, ukuthi akukho okuzoshintsha noma ukuthi wazi ukuthi ushintsho luzofika nini. Uma ungazi impendulo, tshela njalo bese uthembisa ukuthi nizobe nifuna ulwazi oluthembekile ndawonye.

Izingane zingadinga ukuzwa incazelo efanayo ngaphezu kowodwa. Lokhu kujwayelekile. Nika indawo yokuthi ingane ibuze futhi kamuva, bese uhlola ukuthi iqondile yini ngaphandle kokuguqula ingxoxo ibe yivivinyo. Uma ingane isebenzisa amagama ahlukile ngesifo, lalela kuqala bese unikeza ngobumnene amagama acacile angawasebenzisa.

## Ngiphendula kanjani imibuzo enzima?

Phendula ukukhathazeka okusengaphansi kombuzo, bese unikeza impendulo eqotho necacile engenayo. Gcina kufushane bese ungafaki imininingwane ongayaziyo.

- **“Ngingayithola yini?”** Thi cha: i-dementia ayithelwa. Ingane ingaqhubeka ichitha isikhathi nomuntu.
- **“Ngenze yenzeke yini lokhu?”** Yiqinisekise ukuthi isifo asibangelwanga yilokho ebeyisho noma ebeyenzile.
- **“Kungani uMkhulu emabiza ngegama elehlukile?”** Chaza ukuthi i-dementia ingashintsha indlela umuntu akhumbula ngayo amagama. Akuyona impazamo yengane, futhi angase angakwenzi ukuze acasule.
- **“Nami ngizoba nesifo?”** Ungaqagela ngesikhathi esizayo somuntu owodwa. Ingozi yomndeni incike ohlobweni nasemlandweni. Uma kukhona ukukhathazeka okuthile ngohlobo lwefa, buza udokotela womuntu ukuthi ingabe ingoti yezofuzo ingasiza yini.
- **“Uzophila kangcono yini?” noma “Uzofa yini?”** Ungathembisi umphumela noma isikhathi. Tshela ingane ukuthi ithimba lokunakekela lithini, uma umndeni ufuna ukwabelana ngalokho, bese uchaza ukuthi nizokhuluma nini futhi.
- **“Usangithanda?”** Ungamenzi ingane ibe nesibopho sokufakazela impendulo. Ungasho ukuthi ubudlelwano buyabaluleka bese ufuna indlela elula yokuchitha isikhathi ndawonye manje.

Kulungile ukuma uma umbuzo usukhulu kakhulu. Tshela ingane ukuthi uzobuyela kulo nini, bese wenza lokho. “Angikazi okwamanje” onokuthula kuyasiza kunokuqagela okuqinile.

## Ngingasiza kanjani ngingenzi ingane ibe umnakekeli?

Nikeza ukuxhumana ngokukhetha. Uma ingane nomuntu befuna, bangalalela iculo elijwayelekile, bheke izithombe, bazobe, bafunde noma banikelane ngomsebenzi omncane wasekhaya. Umhlahlandlela wethu [wokukhumbula izinto zangaphambili](/activities/reminiscence/) kanye [nomhlahlandlela womculo](/activities/music/) unemibono. Landela impendulo yomuntu nengane; kulungile ukuyeka, ukushintsha umsebenzi noma ukuvakasha esikhathini esinye.

Gcina isibopho sokunakekela nokuphepha ngabantu abadala. Ingane ingakhetha ukubeka isinambiso noma ukuletha incwadi, kodwa akufanele ibe nesibopho sokuqapha, imithi, ukunakekelwa komzimba, izikhathi zokuvakashela noma izimo eziphuthumayo. Ungaphakamisi ukuthi ukuvakasha, ukusiza noma ukuhlala eduze kuyisibopho sengane. Umhlahlandlela we-[Dementia Australia](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/dementia-and-family-relationships) uyaxwayisa imindeni ukuthi ihloniphe imingcele yentsha nesidingo sayo sokuhlala njengezingane.

Vikela isikhathi esingesengane. Gcina isikole, abangane, izithakazelo nemikhuba yomndeni izinzile ngangokunokwenzeka. Uma ukunakekela kushintsha lezo zinhlelo, chaza ngani bese ufuna omunye umuntu omdala ongasiza ukugcina umsebenzi noma uhambo lengane. [Umhlahlandlela wokuzinakekela womnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) uchaza izindlela zokucela abanye abantu abadala ukuthi babelane ngosizo.

Ngaphambi kokutshela uthisha, umqeqeshi noma omunye umuntu omdala, faka ingane ekuthatheni isinqumo ngendlela efanele. Ingase ifune omunye umuntu othembekile ukuthi azi, noma ingase ikhethe ukulinda. Uma wabelana ngolwazi, vumelanani ukuthi yini okufanele iphathwe njengemfihlo nokuthi yini okufanele ihlale ingaziwa.

## Ngabe kufanele ngifune usizo oluthe xaxa nini?

Buza ukuthi ingane ikwenza kanjani phakathi nesikhathi, hhayi kuphela ngemva kokuxilongwa. Ezinye izingane zikhuluma ngokukhululeka; ezinye zibonisa ukukhathazeka ngezinguquko ekulaleni, esikoleni, ebudlelwaneni noma ekuziphatheni. Lezi zinguquko zingaba nezizathu eziningi futhi azifakazeli ukuthi ingane idinga ukuxilongwa. Lalela uphinde ubuze ukuthi yiluphi usizo olungasiza.

Uma ingane ifuna omunye umuntu ongaphandle komndeni okhuluma naye, cabanga ngothisha wakho ahlinzeka ngolwazi, umeluleli wesikole, udokotela wezingane noma ochwepheshe wezempilo yengqondo yezingane. Buza ngaphambi kokuxhumana nabasebenzi besikole mayelana nokuxilongwa lapho ukwazi; ingane ingafuna ukuba yingxenye yalengxoxo. Imindeni ethintwe i-dementia equala kakade ingadinga nosizo lomsebenzi, isikole nokushintsha kwendima emndenini.

Uma ungaqiniseki ukuthi uchaza kanjani ushintsho oluthile, buza ithimba lokunakekela lomuntu noma inhlangano ye-dementia ukuze uthole iseluleko. Ingane ayidingi izimpendulo ezipheleleyo kuwe. Idinga ulwazi oluqotho, ithuba lokubuza futhi kabusha, nabantu abadala abasaqhubeka beza kuyo.
