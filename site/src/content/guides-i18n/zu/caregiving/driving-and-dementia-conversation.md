Ukushayela kungaba yingxenye yobuwena bomuntu, ukuzimela kwakhe nomkhuba wakhe wansuku zonke. Ukukhuluma ngezinguquko ezizayo kungaba nzima kubo bonke. Ukuqala ngesikhathi kungase kunikeze indawo engaphezulu yokulalela, ukwenza izinqumo nokuzama ezinye izindlela zokuzulazula. Leli khasi aliyona ukuhlolwa kokushayela noma isinqumo sokuthi umuntu othile uphephile yini ukushayela.

[Umhlahlandlela we-Alzheimer's Association mayelana nedementia nokushayela](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving), [iseluleko se-Dementia Australia ngokushayela](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/driving-and-dementia) kanye [nomhlahlandlela we-NHTSA wabanakekeli](https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety/older-drivers) kuncoma ukuhlela kusengaphambili nokubandakanya umuntu. Udokotela, ochwepheshe bokuvuselela ukushayela kanye nenhlangano enikeza izincwadi zokushayela yendawo bangasiza ngemibuzo ethile.

## Ngikhulume ngalokhu nini?

Qala ngaphambi kokuthi isigameko sishayele isinqumo esiphuthumayo, uma kungenzeka. Khetha isikhathi esizolile lapho umuntu engalungiseleli ukuya kwenye indawo noma ebuya ohambweni olucindezelayo. Lokhu kungase kube ingxoxo yokuqala yezingxoxo eziningi, hhayi isimemezelo esisodwa.

Buza ukuthi ukushayela kusho ini kumuntu. Kungaba yindlela yokuvakashela abangane, ukuthenga ukudla, ukusiza umndeni, ukwenza umsebenzi noma ukuzizwa ezimele. Ukwamukela lokho okushayela kusho kuye akusixazululi umbuzo wokuphepha. Kukhombisa ukuthi uyaqonda okungase kulahlekele kuye nokuthi ukuhlela kubalulekile ngani.

Bandakanya umuntu ezinqumweni zokuhamba kwesikhathi esizayo uma efuna ukubamba iqhaza. Angase akhethe umuntu othile azoshayela, indlela ejwayelekile noma isikhathi sosuku esithile. Bhala phansi izinketho azithandayo bese uzizama ngaphambi kosuku olunengcindezi, uma evumelana.

## Ngingakhulumisana kanjani ngokukhathazeka ngaphandle kokuguqula kube ukuphikisana?

Khulumani ngesigameko esithile kunokwenza isinqumo esibanzi. Isibonelo, chazani ukuthi umuntu walahlekelwa endleleni ejwayelekile, wadlula isibonakaliso sokuma esikhathini esingelungile, noma kukhona ukushaya okusha esimweni. Tshelani ukuthi nibonani bese nibuza ukuthi umuntu ukhumbula ini noma wazizwa kanjani. Gwema ukumbiza ngokuthi uyisishayeli esibi noma ukuhlola inkumbulo yakhe ngemibuzo.

Lalelani umbono womuntu. Angase abone ukudideka, ukukhanya okungakhululekile, ukukhathala noma enye inkinga. Noma angase avumelane nalokho enikukhathazayo. Ungathathi ingxoxo eyodwa njengobufakazi bokuthi uyaqonda noma akaqondi ingozi. Gcinani ingxoxo ihlonipha futhi ubuyele kuyo ngesikhathi esizayo uma noma yimuphi uzwa ukukhathala.

I-Dementia Australia incoma izingxoxo ezimfishane nezipheténdwayo, bese kugxilwa emandleni komuntu nezindlela ezinhle zokuhamba. Iphinde ixwayise ukuthi ukufihla okhiye ngokufihlakele noma ukuvimbela imoto kungabonakala kuyinkohlakalo ngesikhathi sokuhlela kuqala. Uma kukhona ingozi enkulu ephuthumayo, thathani izinyathelo zokugwema uhambo olulandelayo oluyingozi bese nifuna iseluleko esiphuthumayo sochwepheshe.

## Yiluphi ulwazi olungasiza ingxoxo yochwepheshe?

Bhalani izenzakalo ezithile nalapho zenzeka khona. Izibonelo zingafaka ukudideka emgwaqweni ohlangana, ukulahlekelwa indlela eya endaweni ejwayelekile, ukungabonwa kwezimpawu noma umonakalo omusha emotweni. Faka lokho umuntu akushilo ngohambo nanoma yiziguquko zezempilo noma zemithi afisa ukukhuluma ngazo.

Gcinani amanothi equkethe amaqiniso futhi abe yimfihlo. Ningabhekisisi, nilandele noma nihlole umuntu ngasese. Uma umuntu evumelana, ilungu lomndeni elikhe lahlala emotweni lingabelana ngalokho alubonile. Incazelo ecacile inika udokotela noma ochwepheshe okuthile okuqondile ukuze axoxe ngakho; ayichazi imbangela noma inqume ukuthi umuntu uyakwazi yini ukushayela.

Izimpawu zokuxwayisa ku-[umhlahlandlela we-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/dementia-driving) ziyizizathu zokufuna iseluleko sochwepheshe. Azithathi indawo yokuhlolwa komuntu ngamunye. Buzani udokotela womuntu ngokuthunyelwa kumelaphi wezemisebenzi noma kochwepheshe abanye abaqeqeshelwe ukuvuselela ukushayela. Hlolani imithetho yezincwadi zokushayela yendawo nenhlangano ebusa lezo zincwadi.

## Ningahlela kanjani uhambo ndawonye?

Qalani ngohambo umuntu afuna ukuqhubeka nalo: ukuvakashela abangane, ukuthenga, izinsiza zesikhathi, ukukhonza noma imisebenzi ejwayelekile. Ezohambweni ngalunye, xoxani ukuthi ubani angashayela, yiziphi izinketho zokuhamba ezamukelekayo nalokho okungasisiza ukuthi uhambo lusebenze.

Izinketho zingafaka ukuhanjiswa ngabangane noma emndenini, amatekisi noma izinsizakalo zokuhamba ezifana ne-ride-share, izokuthutha zomphakathi, ukuthuthwa komphakathi noma ukuthunyelwa kwezinye izinto. Izinsizakalo, izindleko nokufinyeleleka kuyahluka endaweni. Hlolani okutholakala endaweni ngokuthi kungabe kuzosebenza kubo bonke ngokuzenzakalelayo.

Zamani indlela noma isevisi ndawonye ngaphambi kokuthi ibe yinketho ethile. Buzani ukuthi umuntu ufuna umuntu omhambisanayo yini noma ufisa ukuhamba yedwa. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuhlela uhambo](/caregiving/traveling-with-dementia/) unemibono yokugcina ukuhamba kwaziwa nokushiya indawo yokushintsha izinhlelo.

## Kwenzenjani uma ngicabanga ukuthi uhambo olulandelayo lungaba yingozi?

Thathani ukukhathazeka kwenu ngokungathi sina bese nihlela enye indlela yokuhamba ngenkathi nifuna iseluleko esiphuthumayo. Uma umuntu eqala ukudideka ngokuzumayo, i-[NHS incoma usizo lwezokwelapha ngokushesha](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/); sebenzisani amasevisi ezimo eziphuthumayo. Ushintsho oluzumayo ekuziphatheni komuntu akufanele lubhekwe njengempikiswano ejwayelekile ngokushayela.

Ngezinye izinkinga ezinkulu, xhumanani nodokotela womuntu nenhlangano enikeza izincwadi zokushayela ukuze nithole izinyathelo ezisebenza endaweni ahlala kuyo. Ningathembeli esithembisweni somndeni noma kulelikhasi ukuze linquma izidingo zomthetho. Uma kungenzeka, chazani umuntu uhlelo lwesikhashana bese nimbandakanya kuyo yonke into elandelayo.

## Ngingamsekela kanjani umuntu uma ukushayela kuphela?

Ukuyeka ukushayela kungazwakala njengokulahlekelwa okukhulu. Mamukelani lokho ngokucacile. Qhubekani nokumemela umuntu ezindaweni nasemisebenzini ebalulekile kuye. Nikezani ukukhetha mayelana nokuhanjiswa, isikhathi nabahambisana nabo, kunokuthatha ukuhamba njengomsebenzi okufanele uwuphathe ngaye.

Yabelanani ngemithwalo yokushayela nabazalwane noma abangane uma umuntu evumelana. Buzani izinhlangano zomphakathi ngokuthuthwa kwabantu abadala noma abakhubazekile. Hlolani izindleko, isikhathi, imithetho yokuthatha nokuthi umhambisanayo uvunyelwe yini. Imininingwane emincane ingase inqume ukuthi inketho ibonakala iwusizo yini.

[Umhlahlandlela wokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) uchaza ukucela abanye ukuthi babelane ngosizo. Umuntu kufanele abe nesandla esikhulu kangangoba kungenzeka ekuthi abe nendlela yokuhlala exhumene nokuphila kwansuku zonke.

Inhloso ukuvikela ukuphepha ngenkathi kuhlonishwa okushayela kusho kumuntu. Qalani ingxoxo kusenesikhathi, lalelani, thola iseluleko sochwepheshe bese nakha uhlelo lokuhamba ndawonye.
