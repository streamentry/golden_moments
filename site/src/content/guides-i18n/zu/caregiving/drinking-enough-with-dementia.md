Umuntu ophila nedementia angase akhohlwe ukuphuza noma angazwa ukoma. Iziphuzo ezingabonakali kahle noma ezinzima ukubamba zingase zingabonwa kalula. Izinguquko ezincane emkhubeni zingenza iziphuzo zamukeleke kalula, ngenkathi umuntu eqhubeka elawula.

[Umhlahlandlela we-Alzheimer's Association ngokudla nokudla nokuphuza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating) uncoma ukunikeza izinkomishi ezincane zoketshezi ngosuku lonke nokudla okunamanzi kukho. [Iseluleko se-Dementia Australia ngokudla](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-healthy/nutrition-and-dementia) sincoma nokuthi kusetshenziswe iziphuzo ezijwayelekile nokufinyeleleka kalula. Leli khasi linikeza imibono jikelele, hhayi inani loketshezi losuku lomuntu othile.

## Ngingenza kanjani iziphuzo zibonakale futhi zifinyeleleke kalula?

Beka iziphuzo umuntu azithandayo lapho angazibona khona futhi azifinyelela khona. Sebenzisa inkomishi noma ibhodlela ajwayele kulo elizwakala lilula ukulibamba. Uma umuntu ethanda izinga lokushisa, ukunambitheka noma inkomishi ethile, gcinani lokho ekhumbulweni. Ukukhetha okukodwa okuncane kungase kube lula kunokumcela ukuthi akhethe phakathi kweziphuzo eziningi.

Nikezani iziphuzo njengengxenye yemikhuba azoyaziyo, njengokudla, ukuphumula noma ukuvakashela ngaphandle. Inkomishi encane ingazwakala ibuthakathaka kunengilazi enkulu. Nikezani esinye isiphuzo ngokuhamba kwesikhathi ngaphandle kokukuguqula kube yisivivinyo noma isikhumbuzo sokuthi umuntu ukhohlwe.

Uma umuntu engathela noma athwale isiphuzo ngokwakhe, mvumeleni. Uma efuna usizo, buzani ngaphambi kokuthela noma kokubeka inkomishi esandleni sakhe. Isitsha esisha, ipayipi noma indawo engajwayelekile kungazwakala kungaziwa, ngakho shintshani into eyodwa ngesikhathi futhi nigcine lokho okusebenza komuntu.

Ngohambo, letha iziphuzo umuntu azithandayo bese nizinikeza lapho aphumula khona noma ngesikhathi sokudla. Uma abantu abaningi besiza, yabelanani ngeziphuzo nenkomishi umuntu ayikhethayo ngemvume yakhe. Gcinani isiphuzo sifinyeleleka kalula, kodwa buzani ngaphambi kokusihambisa noma sokumnika.

## Yiziphi iziphuzo noma ukudla okungangiphakela?

Qalani ngeziphuzo umuntu azijabulelayo futhi azikwazi ngokuphephile. Amanzi ayinketho; ubisi, isobho, ama-smoothie noma eminye imikhiqizo yokuphuza ejwayelekile nayo ingahambisana nokukhetha komuntu nohlelo lwakhe lokunakekelwa. Ezinye izithelo nezinye izinto zokudla nazo zinamanzi. [I-Alzheimer's Association ibala izithelo, isobho, ushekisi nama-smoothie phakathi kwezinketho ezinamanzi amaningi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating).

Ningaphikisi iziphuzo eziyodwa uma umuntu eziqeda. Nikezani enye inketho ejwayelekile noma nizame kamuva. Buzani udokotela womuntu noma udayitishani ngaphambi kokushintsha ukudla kwakhe noma uhlelo lwakhe lweziphuzo, ikakhulu uma kukhona imiyalo yezokwelapha mayelana noketshezi.

## Ngingayinciphisa yini iziphuzo ukuvimbela izikhathi zokuya endlini yangasese?

Ningakunciphisi uketshezi ukuvimbela izingozi ngaphandle uma ochwepheshe bezempilo benze uhlelo oluthile lomuntu. [I-Alzheimer's Association iyaxwayisa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/incontinence) ukuthi ukukhawulela uketshezi kungaholela ekomiseni umzimba nezinye izinkinga. Umuntu odinga usizo lokuthola nokusebenzisa indlu yangasese angase azuze kusizo oluhlukile. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokulawula ukuchama](/caregiving/toileting-and-incontinence-with-dementia/) uchaza imikhuba yendlu yangasese nobumfihlo.

Sabalalisani ukunikezwa kweziphuzo ngosuku kunokucela umuntu ukuthi aphuze kakhulu ngesikhathi esisodwa. Landelani noma yimiyalo yezokwelapha ekhona futhi buzani udokotela ukuthi yiyiphi inani elifanele umuntu. Ningathembeli enanini elijwayelekile le-inthanethi; izidingo zoketshezi ziyahluka, futhi abanye abantu banohlelo lwezokwelapha lukaketshezi.

## Yiziphi izimpawu okufanele ngiziphawule?

Qaphelani okwehlukile emkhubeni ojwayelekile womuntu. Angase aphuze kancane kakhulu kunangokujwayelekile, angashiyi isiphuzo ayesithanda noma adinge usizo oluningi kunangaphambili. Umlomo owomile, ukunyathaza, ukudinwa okungajwayelekile noma ushintsho ekukhipheni umchamo nakho kungaba izizathu zokubuza ochwepheshe bezempilo. Lezi zimpawu zingase zibe nezinye izimbangela, ngakho ningazihlolisisi ngokwenu ukuthi umuntu uyomisa umzimba.

Bhalani phansi okwakubonakala nesikhathi. Faka ukuthi umuntu wayegula yini, esendaweni eshisayo noma enobunzima ekubambeni noma ekuphuzeni. [I-Alzheimer's Association iphawula ukuthi izimpawu zokomisa umzimba zingase zibe nzima ukuzibona kumuntu onedementia](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/safety/in-a-disaster). Uma kukhona ushintsho olungazelelwe noma unokukhathazeka, xhumanani nodokotela noma nochwepheshe wezempilo.

## Kwenzenjani uma ukuphuza sekuba nzima?

Uma umuntu ehefuzela, echoboza, ebamba ukudla noma uketshezi emlonyeni, noma enobunzima obusha bokumunya, yimani nicele iseluleko kochwepheshe abaqeqeshiwe. Ningajiyi iziphuzo, noma nishintshe ukuthungwa kokudla noma nizame amasu okumunya ngaphandle uma ochwepheshe belincome lolo hlelo lomuntu. [Umhlahlandlela wethu wokudla](/caregiving/mealtimes-with-dementia/) uchaza okufanele wenziwe uma kukhona izinkinga ezintsha zokutshiza noma zokumunya.

Uma umuntu enomlomo owomile, amazinyo abuhlungu noma izinsimbi zamazinyo ezingakuzwakali kahle, mbuzeni ukuthi yini ayizwayo bese nixhumana nodokotela wamazinyo noma udokotela. Ubuhlungu emlonyeni bungenza ukuphuza kube nzima. Bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wokunakekela komlomo](/caregiving/oral-care-with-dementia/) ngezindlela ezithambile zokusekela ukunakekelwa komlomo.

Ukudideka okuzumayo kudinga usizo lwezokwelapha ngokushesha. [Iseluleko se-NHS ngokudideka okuzumayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) sincoma abantu ukuthi bangazihlolisisi ngokwabo. Sebenzisani amasevisi ezimo eziphuthumayo endaweni enihlala kuyo noma landelani inqubo yokunakekelwa okuphuthumayo.

## Ngingazinikeza kanjani iziphuzo ngaphandle kocindezelo?

Nikezani ukukhetha bese nipha umuntu isikhathi sokunquma. Angafuna ukuphuza kamuva noma akhethe inkomishi ehlukile. Gwemani ukulungisa, ukuxoxisana noma ukuphoqa isiphuzo. Uma eyala kaningi, hlolani ukuthi isiphuzo siyafinyeleleka yini, ukuthi uyasithanda yini ukunambitheka nezinga lokushisa, nokuthi umuntu uzizwa ekhululekile yini esimweni esizungezile.

Buzani umuntu ukuthi yini esiza, futhi yabelanani ngezintandokazi ezisizayo nabanye abasizi kuphela uma evumelana. Inkomishi ejwayelekile, indawo akhethe noma isikhathi esithulile kungase kwenze ukuphuza kube lula. Uma ukukhathazeka kuqhubeka, buzani udokotela noma udayitishani ngeseluleko esithile somuntu.

Inhloso ukwenza iziphuzo zitholakale ngendlela umuntu ayamukelayo. Nikezani ngobumnene, hlonipha inqumo lakhe nithole iseluleko sochwepheshe uma izidingo zishintsha.
