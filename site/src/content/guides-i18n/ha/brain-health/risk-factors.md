Abin haɗari wani abu ne da yake da alaƙa da ƙarin yiwuwar kamuwa da wani yanayi. Ba shi ne sanadi a kowane hali ba, kuma samun ɗaya ba yana nufin za ku kamu da mantuwa ba. Wannan shafi yana lissafa abubuwan haɗarin mantuwa 14 da Lancet Commission ta 2024 ta ce za a iya canza su, abin da bincike ya haɗa su da shi, da abin da za ku iya tattauna da likita.

Don babban hoto, har da abin da manyan gwaje-gwaje suka gano, ku fara da [Za a iya hana mantuwa? Abin da bincike yake nunawa](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/). Wannan bayani ne na gabaɗaya, ba shawarar likita ba; ku duba [sanarwarmu ta likita](/medical-disclaimer/).

## Mene ne abubuwan haɗarin mantuwa 14?

[Lancet Commission ta 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0), da masu binciken mantuwa 27 suka rubuta, ta ƙiyasta cewa abubuwan haɗari 14 da za a iya canzawa a haɗe suna da alaƙa da kusan 45% na masu mantuwa a duniya. Kaso na kowane abu a ƙasa shi ne ƙiyasin Hukumar na rabon lamura a duniya da yake da alaƙa da shi, an kewaye zuwa lambobi cikakku. Lambobin suna haɗuwa da juna, domin mutane da yawa suna da haɗari da yawa a lokaci guda, shi ya sa suka haɗu zuwa 45% ba fiye da haka ba.

## Yaya zan fahimci waɗannan kaso?

Da hankali. Rahoton Hukumar da kansa ya ba da wasu abubuwa da suka cancanci a tuna:

- **Suna bayyana al’umma, ba mutane ba.** [Taƙaicen rahoton daga University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2024/jul/nearly-half-dementia-cases-could-be-prevented-or-delayed-tackling-14-risk-factors) ya ce rage haɗari yana aiki a cikin al’umma kuma ba ya tabbatar da cewa wani mutum ɗaya zai guje wa mantuwa.
- **Suna ɗauka sanadi da sakamako.** Marubutan sun haɗa abubuwa masu shaida mai gamsarwa kawai, amma sun ce wasu alaƙoƙi na iya zama na dalili ne kaɗan.
- **Lambobin duniya ne.** Mafi yawan bayanai suna zuwa daga ƙasashe masu arziki, kuma ainihin rabo ga kowace ƙasa ko al’umma zai bambanta.
- **Ƙananan kaso na iya zama masu muhimmanci.** Abin da yake da alaƙa da 2% na lamura a duniya har yanzu yana shafar mutane masu yawa.

## Waɗanne abubuwan haɗari aka ƙara a 2024?

Rahoton 2024 ya ƙara abubuwa biyu zuwa 12 da aka ambata a 2020:

- **Yawan LDL cholesterol a tsakiyar rayuwa**, mai alaƙa da kusan 7% na lamura.
- **Rashin gani da ba a magance ba a tsufa**, mai alaƙa da kusan 2% na lamura.

## Waɗanne abubuwan haɗari ba za a iya canzawa ba?

National Institute on Aging ta ce [shekaru su ne mafi girman abin haɗari da aka sani ga Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-causes-and-risk-factors/what-causes-alzheimers-disease), kuma babu wanda zai iya canza su. Kwayoyin halitta ma suna da rawa; wata sananniyar kwayar halitta da take shafar haɗarin Alzheimer ana kiranta APOE. NIA ta ƙara da cewa masana kimiyya ba su gama fahimtar abin da yake haddasa Alzheimer a yawancin mutane ba tukuna. Lancet Commission ta lura cewa za a iya rage haɗarin da za a iya canzawa ko wane irin haɗarin kwayoyin halitta mutum yake da shi.

Saboda shekaru da kwayoyin halitta suna da babban rawa, mutane da yawa da ba su da ko ɗaya cikin abubuwan haɗari 14 har yanzu suna kamuwa da mantuwa. Ba laifin kowa ba ne.

## Me shaidar gwaje-gwaje take cewa game da canza waɗannan haɗari?

Haɗa abin haɗari da mantuwa ba daidai yake da tabbatar da cewa canza shi yana rage haɗarin mantuwa ba. Wasu misalai na inda shaidar gwaje-gwaje take:

- **Hawan jini.** A gwajin SPRINT MIND, manufar hawan jini mai ƙasa ta rage ƙaramar matsalar tunani da ƙwaƙwalwa sosai. Ba ta rage mantuwa mai yiwuwa sosai ba, ko da yake gwajin yana iya zama ƙarami da ba zai iya nuna hakan ba ([SPRINT MIND a JAMA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688979/)).
- **Na’urar ji.** Lancet Commission ta bayyana gwajin na’urar ji na shekaru uku da bai sami tasiri gabaɗaya a kan tunani ba, amma ya sami fa’ida a ƙaramin rukunin mutane masu haɗari mafi girma. Nazarin da suke bin mutane a tsawon lokaci suna haɗa amfani da na’urar ji da ƙarancin haɗari. [Jagorarmu ta ji](/brain-health/hearing/) tana da ƙari.
- **Motsa jiki.** National Institute on Aging ta ce babu isasshiyar shaidar gwaje-gwajen asibiti don a kammala cewa motsa jiki zai iya hana ko jinkirta ƙaramar matsalar tunani da ƙwaƙwalwa ko Alzheimer, ko da yake wasu nazari suna nuna cewa yana iya taimakawa da raguwar da ke zuwa da shekaru ([NIA kan hana Alzheimer](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Dogayen gwaje-gwajen da suke jira mantuwa ta bayyana suna da jinkiri kuma suna da tsada, don haka ga abubuwa da yawa mafi kyawun shaida za ta ci gaba da fitowa daga nazarin da suke bin mutane tsawon shekaru. Wannan shaida tana da amfani, amma ta fi gwaji rauni.

## Me game da barci da abinci?

Hukumar ta duba barci da abinci amma ba ta ƙara su cikin jerin ba, domin shaidar ba ta da ƙarfi ko daidaito sosai. Jagororinmu na [barci](/brain-health/sleep/) da [abinci](/brain-health/diet/) suna bayanin abin da aka sani.

## Ku yi magana da likita

Duba lafiya na shekara-shekara lokaci ne mai kyau na tada waɗannan batutuwa. Kuna iya kawo wannan jeri ku tambaya:

- Menene lambobin hawan jinina, sukarin jini da cholesterol, kuma ko wasu suna buƙatar kulawa?
- Ya kamata a duba jina da idanuna?
- Motsin da nake yi lafiyayye ne kuma ya isa gare ni?
- Ko wasu magunguna nawa za su iya shafar ƙwaƙwalwa ko tunani?

Kafin canza magunguna, abinci, azumi, motsa jiki ko ƙari, ku tattauna da likita tukuna. Idan ku ko wanda kuke kulawa da shi kuna da ruɗewa kwatsam, rauni a gefe ɗaya na jiki, ko wahalar magana, ku kira lambar gaggawa ta yankinku.
