Kyakkyawan wasa da mai rayuwa da mantuwa ba ya kama da gasa. Yana kama da mutane biyu suna zaune kusa, suna nuna hotuna, suna dariya da wani karin magana, kuma suna magana kan duk abin da ya taso. Wasan dalili ne kawai na kasancewa tare.

Wannan jagora ta ƙunshi yadda ake shiryawa, yadda ake tafiyar da wasa, abin da za a ce idan wani abu ya “lalace”, yadda ake sauƙaƙa kowane wasa ko ƙara masa wahala kaɗan, yadda ake mai da shi hira, da yadda za a san lokacin da za a tsaya. Tana aiki ga [wasanninmu](/games/) da katuna, wasannin tunani da wasannin allo da kuke da su.

## Mene ne hanya mafi kyau ta yin wasa da mai mantuwa?

Ku mai da hankali kan jin daɗi, ba nasara ba. Wannan shi ne ainihin shawarar [jagorar Alzheimer’s Association kan ayyuka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities): ku gina a kan ƙwarewar da mutumin yake da ita har yanzu, ku taimaka masa ya fara, ku kammala sassa masu wuya da kanku, kuma kada ku soki ko ku gyara ayyukan da ba su da lahani.

A aikace hakan yana nufin:

- **Ku zauna kusa da shi, ba a ƙetare ba.** Gefe da gefe, ku biyu kuna iya ganin allo ko tebur, kuma taimako yana jin kamar aiki tare ba sa ido ba.
- **Ku ba da wasa guda a lokaci guda.** Jerin zaɓi na iya jin kamar jarrabawa kafin komai ya fara.
- **Ku bi shi.** Idan yana son ya taɓa hotuna bisa tsarinsa, ya yi magana kan hoto ɗaya na minti goma, ko ya tsaya bayan juyi biyu, wannan shi ne wasan yau.
- **Ku ƙirga lokuta masu kyau, ba zagayen da aka kammala ba.** Murmushi, labari ko rabin sa’a mai natsuwa shi ne sakamakon da kuke nema.

## Ta yaya zan shirya kafin mu fara?

Mintuna kaɗan na shiri suna hana yawancin takaicin da mutane suke zargin wasan da shi.

1. **Ku zaɓi mafi kyawun lokacin yininsa.** [Jagorar National Institute on Aging (NIA) kan daidaita ayyuka](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) tana ba da shawarar tsara fita da ayyuka a lokacin yini da mutumin yake a mafi kyawun yanayi. Ga mutane da yawa hakan yana kusa da tsakiyar safiya. Ƙarshen yamma sau da yawa ya fi wuya; ku duba [ayyuka masu natsuwa na ƙarshen yamma](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).
2. **Ku sa ɗakin ya yi shiru.** Ku kashe talabijin da rediyo ku rufe ƙofa idan za ku iya. [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) tana ba da shawarar rage hayaniya da abubuwan da ke karkatar da hankali kafin a yi magana.
3. **Ku fara duba jin daɗi.** Abin sha a kusa, zuwa bayan gida tukuna, kujera mai daɗi, da tabarau da na’urar ji a kunne.
4. **Ku haskaka wurin sosai.** Haske mai ƙarfi kuma daidai ba tare da walƙiya a kan allo ba yana sa hotuna su fi sauƙin gani.
5. **Ku shirya wasan kafin ku zauna.** Ku buɗe shi, ku zaɓi saitunan ku kuma saita ƙarar da kanku. Sannan ku juya allon zuwa gare shi. Don shawarwarin tablet, ku duba [saita tablet ko waya](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).
6. **Ku tallafa na’urar.** Tsayuwa ko murfi da yake riƙe tablet a tsaye yana nufin babu wanda sai ya riƙe ta, kuma ya fi sauƙin nuna wa.

## Ta yaya zan gayyace shi ba tare da matsi ba?

Ku yi gayyatar a sauƙaƙe kuma mai sauƙin ƙi. Wasu kalmomin da yawanci suke aiki:

- “Na sami wasu hotunan lambu. Kuna son ku kalle su tare da ni?”
- “Za ku iya taimaka mini da wannan wasan? Na makale.”
- “Kuna so furanni ko tsuntsaye yau?” (zaɓi biyu ya fi buɗaɗɗiyar tambaya sauƙi)
- “Ina yin kati ga ’yar’uwarku. Wane launi ya kamata wannan ya zama?”

Alzheimer’s Association tana ba da shawarar a bayyana manufar aiki, kamar yin kati ga aboki, domin dalili yana ba shi ma’ana. Neman taimako ma yana aiki domin yana bar mutumin ya ji yana da amfani.

Idan ya ce a’a, ku karɓa. Ku sake gwadawa daga baya ko ku ba da wani abu dabam. Jagorarmu kan [abin da za a yi idan ya ce a’a](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) ta yi bayani sosai.

## Har yaushe wasa ya kamata ya ɗauka, kuma da wane gudu?

Ku bar mutumin ya saita jinkirin, kuma ku shirya zama gajere. Babu tsawon da shaida ta ce ya fi dacewa ga wasa na tare. Mintuna biyu zuwa biyar masu natsuwa na iya zama cikakken aiki.

- **Ku jira fiye da yadda yake jin na halitta.** NIA tana ba da shawarar a ba mutum ƙarin lokaci don ya amsa kuma a yi ƙoƙarin kada a katse shi. Ku ƙirga goma a zuciyarku kafin ku taimaka.
- **Mataki ɗaya a lokaci guda.** Ku ba da umarni guda ɗaya gajere (“Ku taɓa hoto”), sannan ku jira. Ku nuna maimakon bayani: ku nuna inda za a taɓa.
- **Ku dakata da yardar rai.** Idan ya tsaya yana magana kan hoto, wasan zai iya jira. Babu buƙatar gaggawa.
- **Ku bar shi ya kalla.** NIA ta lura cewa wasu mutane sun fi son kallo, kuma zaɓi ne mai inganci.

## Ya kamata in gyara amsoshi marasa daidai?

A’a. Gyara wani a hankali akai-akai yana gaya masa cewa yana kasawa, ko da kuna nufin alheri. Alzheimer’s Association tana ba da shawarar kada a soki ko a gyara ayyukan da ba su da lahani. A wasa, kusan kowane kuskure ba shi da lahani.

| Maimakon | Ku gwada |
| --- | --- |
| “A’a, wannan ba daidai ba ne.” | “Mu gwada ta wannan hanyar.” (misali da NIA ta bayar) |
| “Wannan ba ya yi daidai ba. Ku sake dubawa.” | Ku yi shiru ku ɗauki juyinku na gaba, ko “Ah, duka biyun ja ne.” |
| “Kuna tuna wane ne wannan?” | “Ku ba ni labarin wannan hoton.” |
| “Kun riga kun yi wannan.” | “Wannan mai kyau ne. Mu sake yi.” |
| “Ku yi ƙoƙari, kun san wannan.” | “Kuna so in nuna ƙarshen?” |

Idan kuskure ya bayyana yana damunsa, ku taimaka a hankali. Ku nuna hoton da ya dace, ku ɗauki juyi da kanku, ko ku sauƙaƙa wasan a zagaye na gaba. Idan karin magana ta sami ƙarshe mai ban dariya mara daidai, ku yi dariya tare. Ƙarshensa na iya fi nasu kyau.

| [Rarrabe tare](/games/sort-together/) | Ku riƙe hoton, ya nuna kwandon | Ya taɓa hoto da kwando da kansa |
| [Wasan haɗa guntun hoto](/games/picture-puzzle/) | Guntaye 4, ku yi magana kan cikakken hoton tukuna | Guntaye 9 |

Alamun wasa ya yi wuya da yawa: dogon dakata da yake kama da damuwa maimakon tunani, maimaita “ba zan iya ba”, tura na’urar, ko kuskure da suke ɓata masa rai. Alamun yana iya yin sauƙi da yawa: gundura, ko ya ce na yara ne. Idan hakan ya faru, ku girmama shi ku koma wani abu da yake jin kamar na manya, kamar neman kalmomi, wasan haɗa hoton gaske, ko magana kan hoton tsohon aikinsa.

## Ta yaya zan mai da wasa hira?

Ku yi amfani da kowane hoto a matsayin gayyatar magana, kuma ku ɗauki duk abin da ya faɗa a matsayin amsa mai kyau. Ga iyalai da yawa, wannan shi ne mafi kyawun ɓangaren wasan.

- **Ku yi tambayoyi buɗaɗɗu marasa amsa ta kuskure.** “Me kuke lura da shi?” “Wane ne kuke so?” “Ku ba ni labarin kicin irin wannan.”
- **Ku fara raba.** “Kakata tana da kare irin wannan.” Labarinku yana sauƙaƙa masa ya raba nasa.
- **Ku bi karkacewar.** Idan hoton mota ya kai ga labarin tafiya, labarin shi ne aikin. Ku bar wasan a tsaye.
- **Kada ku tabbatar da gaskiya.** A tuna da dā, hira mai daɗi ta fi daidaiton bayanai muhimmanci. Idan labarin ya bambanta da na baya, ku ji daɗin sabon sigar.
- **Ku yi amfani da tambayoyin da aka haɗa.** [Kammala karin magana](/games/finish-the-saying/) yana bin kowane karin magana da tambayar hira, kuma [Katunan hira](/games/conversation-cards/) an tsara su daidai don wannan. [Haɗa hotunan kanku](/games/photo-pairs/) yana amfani da hotunan iyalinku, waɗanda sau da yawa suke tayar da labarai mafi wadata.

Ƙarin shawarwari suna cikin jagororinmu na [ayyukan tuna da dā](/activities/reminiscence/) da [shawarwarin sadarwa](/caregiving/communication-tips/).

## Yaushe ya kamata mu tsaya?

Ku tsaya a alamun farko na gajiya ko ɓacin rai, kuma ya fi kyau kaɗan kafin hakan. Alzheimer’s Association tana ba da shawarar tsayawa ko canza aiki idan hankali ya dushe ko takaici ya ƙaru.

Ku lura da:

- hamma, kwanciya ko idanu suna karkacewa daga allo;
- ƙaiƙayi, tashi tsaye, ko neman a tafi;
- nishi, gajerun amsoshi, ko “ban iya wannan ba”;
- taɓawa bazuwar ko tura na’urar;
- hawaye, fushi, ko sauyin yanayi kwatsam.

Yadda za a tsaya da kyau:

- **Ku ƙare da wani abu mai sauƙi.** Haɗawa ta ƙarshe da zai iya yi, ko hoto na ƙarshe da za a ji daɗin kallo.
- **Ku mai da shi game da ku.** “Zan so shan shayi. Mu tsaya a nan?” yana guje wa kowane nuni cewa ya yi mummunan wasa.
- **Ku koma wani abu na motsi ko natsuwa.** Ɗan tafiya, abun ciye-ciye, naɗe tufafi ko kiɗa.
- **Ku faɗi wani abu mai ɗumi.** “Na ji daɗin yin wannan tare da ku.” Ba yabon “samun daidai” ba, sai jin daɗin lokacin tare.

Ƙarewa da wuri, canza shiri, ko barin wasan kawai a zauna tare duk sakamako ne masu kyau.

## Za mu iya yin wasa a rukuni ko ta kiran bidiyo?

Eh, da ɗan ƙarin shiri. A rukuni, ku riƙe shi zuwa mutane kaɗan, ku zaunar da masu taimako kusa da duk wanda yake buƙata, kuma ku zaɓi wasa da yake da bayyanannen abin mai da hankali tare. [Bingo na hotuna](/games/picture-bingo/) yana da katunan da za a buga da mai kira a kan allo don rukuni. Ku duba [ayyuka ga rukunin gidajen kulawa](/activities/care-home-groups/) don ƙari.

A kiran bidiyo, mutumin da yake kan kiran na iya bayyana hoto yayin da wani a ɗakin yake nunawa da taɓawa. Ku riƙe kira gajere da sananne. Jagorarmu ta [kiran waya da bidiyo](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) tana da ƙarin shawarwari.

## Idan wasa bai yi aiki ba fa?

Wasu ranaku babu abin da yake kama, kuma hakan ba nuni ba ne a kanku ko a kansa. Ku ajiye wasan ku gwada wani lokacin yini dabam, wasa dabam, ko babu wasa kwata-kwata. Ayyuka masu kyau da yawa ba su da alaƙa da allo: kiɗa, naɗe tawul, duba kundin hotuna, ko ɗan tafiya. Ku duba [shawarwarin ayyukanmu](/activities/ideas/) don ƙari.
