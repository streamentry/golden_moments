Tablet na iya zama kyakkyawar hanya ta raba hotuna, kiɗa da wasanni masu sauƙi. Kuma yana iya zama mai ruɗani: taɓawa ba da gangan ba tana buɗe wata manhaja, sanarwa tana fitowa, rubutun ƙanana ne, ko allon yana yin duhu a tsakiyar wasa. Ɗan shiri daga gare ku yana gyara yawancin wannan.

Waɗannan matakai suna amfani da saitunan da suka zo a iPhone, iPad da Android. Sunayen menu na iya canzawa tsakanin nau’ikan manhajar na’ura da kamfanonin da suka ƙera su, don haka idan wani abu ya bambanta, ku bi shafin Apple ko Google da aka haɗa don na’urarku. Ku yi shirin da kanku, kafin ku zauna tare.

## Ko mai mantuwa ya yi amfani da tablet ko waya?

Ya dogara da mutumin. Mutane da yawa suna jin daɗin amfani da tablet tare da wani kusa da su, ko da ba za su iya shi su kaɗai ba. Ku fara da amfani da shi tare, da manhaja ɗaya a buɗe, na ƴan mintuna. Idan yana kawo jin daɗi da hira, ku ci gaba. Idan yana kawo ruɗani ko takaici, kundin hotuna, shafukan da aka buga ko kiɗa na iya fi dacewa da shi. [Ayyukanmu na bugawa](/activities/printable/) zaɓi ne ɗaya.

Tablet yawanci ya fi waya sauƙi: allon da hotunan sun fi girma, kuma ba shi ne kuma hanyar kiran gaggawa ta mutumin ba.

## Yaya zan kulle tablet ko waya a manhaja ɗaya?

Ku yi amfani da Guided Access a iPhone da iPad, ko app pinning a Android. Dukansu suna riƙe allon a manhaja ɗaya har sai kun shigar da lamba, don taɓawa ko gogewa ba da gangan ba ta kasa buɗe wani abu dabam.

### iPad da iPhone: Guided Access

Daga [umarnin Guided Access na Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111795):

1. Ku buɗe **Settings**, ku taɓa **Accessibility**, sannan ku taɓa **Guided Access** ku kunna shi.
2. A ƙarƙashin **Passcode Settings**, ku saita lambar sirri ta Guided Access. Za ku iya kuma ba da izinin Face ID ko Touch ID.
3. Zaɓi: a ƙarƙashin **Display Auto-Lock**, ku zaɓi da sauri nawa allon zai kulle yayin zama, don kada ya yi duhu a tsakiyar wasa.
4. Ku buɗe manhajar da kuke so ku yi amfani da ita.
5. Ku danna maɓallin gefe (iPhone), maɓallin sama (iPad), ko maɓallin Home a tsofaffin na’urori sau uku.
6. Zaɓi: ku zagaye duk wani ɓangare na allon da kuke so ya daina amsa taɓawa. Ku taɓa **Options** (ko **Session Settings**) don kashe maɓallan ƙarar sauti ko hana allon juyawa.
7. Ku taɓa **Start**.

Don kawo ƙarshen zaman, ku danna maɓallin ɗaya sau uku kuma, ku shigar da lambar sirri, ku taɓa **End**. Apple ta lura cewa a wasu tsofaffin nau’ikan manhajar na’ura ana danna sau biyu maimakon haka.

**Muhimmi:** Apple ta ce Crash Detection da Emergency Services ba sa aiki yayin zaman Guided Access. Kada ku bar Guided Access yana gudana a wayar da mutumin zai iya buƙata don kiran gaggawa.

### Android: app pinning

Daga [taimakon Android na Google kan kulle allo](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9455138?hl=en):

1. Ku buɗe manhajar **Settings**.
2. Ku taɓa **Security** (ko **Security & location**), sannan **Advanced**, sannan **App pinning**. Idan ba za ku same shi ba, ku duba shafukan taimako na kamfanin da ya ƙera na’urarku.
3. Ku kunna **App pinning**. Za a buƙaci PIN, tsari ko kalmar sirri ɗinku don cirewa.
4. Ku buɗe manhajar da kuke so. Ku goge sama zuwa tsakiyar allon ku riƙe don buɗe Overview (ko ku taɓa Overview idan kuna amfani da maɓallai 3).
5. A saman samfotin manhajar, ku taɓa gunkin manhajar, sannan ku taɓa **Pin**.

Don cirewa da kewayawa ta goge, ku goge sama ku riƙe. Da kewayawa ta maɓallai 3, ku taɓa ku riƙe Back da Overview. Da kewayawa ta maɓallai 2, ku taɓa ku riƙe Back da Home. Sannan ku shigar da PIN, tsari ko kalmar sirri ɗinku.

## Akwai tsari mafi sauƙi na iPhone ko iPad gaba ɗaya?

Eh. Assistive Access na Apple yana maye gurbin allon farko na yau da kullum da wanda ya fi sauƙi. [Apple ta bayyana shi](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-iphone/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ios) a matsayin wanda aka tsara don sauƙaƙa wa mutanen da suke da nakasar fahimta amfani da iPhone da kansu, da manyan abubuwa a allo da ƙarin siffofi masu mai da hankali. Apple tana ba da [siffa ɗaya a iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-ipad/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ipados).

- **Wane ne yake saita shi.** Apple ta ba da shawarar amintaccen mai goyon baya, kamar ɗan iyali ko mai kulawa da ya san mutumin, ya taimaka wajen zaɓar manhajoji da siffofi.
- **Inda za a fara.** Ku je **Settings > Accessibility > Assistive Access**, ku taɓa **Set Up Assistive Access**, sannan **Continue**.
- **Manhajoji.** Kuna zaɓar manhajojin da za su bayyana. Wasu manhajojin Apple suna da nau’ikan da aka sauƙaƙa na musamman, kuma yawancin sauran manhajoji ana iya ƙara su a ƙirarsu ta yau da kullum.
- **Tsari.** Ku zaɓi **Rows** (jerin da yake da sauƙin karantawa) ko **Grid** (manyan abubuwa da suke jaddada hotuna da gumaka).
- **Lambar sirri.** Kuna saita lambar sirri ta Assistive Access daban don shiga, fita ko canza saituna. Apple ta ba da shawarar saita Apple Account na farfaɗowa yayin saitawa, domin ba tare da shi ba, manta lambar sirri na nufin sake saita na’urar.

Assistive Access na iya dacewa da wanda yake amfani da na’urarsa kowace rana. Guided Access ko app pinning ya fi sauri don zaman wasa na tare.

## Yaya zan ƙara girman rubutu da hotuna?

Ku ƙara girman rubutu da allo, sannan ku duba cewa maɓallai har yanzu suna shiga allon.

**iPhone da iPad.** Bisa ga [umarnin girman rubutu na Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102453), ku je **Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size** ku ja mai zamiya. Don rubutu mafi girma, ku je **Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text**. Apple ta lura cewa rubutu mai girma sosai na iya sa wasu maɓallai su yi wuyar taɓawa, don haka ku daidaita idan ya zama dole.

**Android.** Bisa ga [taimakon saitunan rubutu da allo na Google](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/11183305?hl=en), ku buɗe **Settings** ku nemi **Font size**, sannan ku motsa mai zamiya. Ku nemi **Display size** don ƙara girman duk abin da yake a allo. Za ku iya kuma kunna **Bold text** a ƙarƙashin **Accessibility > Display size and text**. Wasu manhajoji ba sa bin waɗannan saitunan.

A wasanni kamar [Haɗa hotuna biyu-biyu](/games/picture-pairs/) da [Nemo iri ɗaya](/games/find-the-same/), za ku iya kuma zaɓar ƙarancin tayal ko zaɓuɓɓuka, wanda yake sa abubuwa su fi sauƙi a allo.

## Yaya zan dakatar da sanarwa da tagogin da ke fitowa?

Ku kashe sanarwa ga manhajojin da ba ku buƙata, kuma ku yi amfani da Do Not Disturb yayin ayyuka.

**iPhone da iPad.** Ku je **Settings > Notifications**, ku taɓa manhaja a ƙarƙashin **Notification Style**, ku kashe **Allow Notifications** ([jagorar saitunan sanarwa na Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios)). Don rufe kira, faɗakarwa da sanarwa na ɗan lokaci, ku buɗe Control Center ku kunna **Do Not Disturb** daga maɓallin **Focus** ([taimakon Do Not Disturb na Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105112)). Kuna iya tsara shi a ƙarƙashin **Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb > Set a Schedule**.

**Android.** Ku goge ƙasa daga saman allon ku taɓa **Do Not Disturb**, ko ku je **Settings > Modes > Do Not Disturb** ([taimakon Do Not Disturb na Google](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9069335?hl=en)). A ƙarƙashin **Notification filters** za ku iya zaɓar waɗanne mutane da manhajoji za su iya shiga. Saituna suna bambanta da kamfanin da ya ƙera na’urar.

## Yaya zan yi allon farko mai sauƙi?

Ku bar manhajoji kaɗan da aka saba da su a allo ɗaya ku matsar da sauran daga gani. Babu saiti ɗaya na wannan; batun cire cunkoso ne.

- **Ku zaɓi manhajoji uku zuwa shida.** Misali, hotuna, kiɗa, manhajar kiran bidiyo da wasa ɗaya.
- **Ku sa su a allon farko,** a wurare iri ɗaya kowane lokaci, ku matsar da sauran zuwa allo na biyu ko babban fayil.
- **Ku yi amfani da bangon allo mai sauƙi da bambancin launi mai ƙarfi,** kamar launi ɗaya ko hoto ɗaya da aka sani, don gumaka su fito fili.
- **Ku share manhajojin da ba ku buƙata.** Ƙarancin manhajoji yana nufin ƙarancin damar ruɗani da ƙarancin bayanan da ake tattarawa.
- **Ku yi la’akari da Assistive Access** a na’urorin Apple idan kuna so na’urar ta yi muku wannan.

## Me game da ƙarar sauti?

Ku saita ƙarar da ta dace kafin ku miƙa na’urar. Da Guided Access, za ku iya kashe maɓallan ƙarar sauti don kada ta canza a tsakiyar aiki. Ku duba cewa na’urorin taimakon ji suna kunne kuma suna aiki.

## A ina tablet ɗin zai zauna?

Ku ba shi mariƙi da wurin caji na dindindin.

- **Mariƙi ko murfi** da yake riƙe tablet a tsaye a kan tebur yana nufin babu wanda sai ya riƙe shi, kuma nuni yana da sauƙi.
- **Wurin caji ɗaya** da ba ya motsawa, mafi kyau mariƙin caji a kan tebur a ɗakin da ake amfani da shi kowace rana. Tablet ɗin koyaushe yana da caji kuma koyaushe a wuri ɗaya.
- **Ku rage haske mai bugawa.** Ku sa allon nesa da taga mai haske.

## Yaya tsawon lokacin allo ya dace?

Babu adadin mintuna da ya dace. Ku yi nufin gajerun zama na tare a matsayin ɓangare ɗaya na cikakkiyar rana da ta ƙunshi motsi, kiɗa, ayyukan gida, hira da lokaci a waje. Saboda maraice mai cunkoso na iya ƙara rashin natsuwa na ƙarshen rana, ku yi la’akari da ajiye ayyukan allo a farkon rana. Ku duba [ayyuka masu natsuwa na la’asar](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Amfani da tablet tare, ba a matsayin mai renon yaro ba, yana sa shi zama na zamantakewa. Jagorarmu kan [yin wasanni tare](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) tana bayani yadda.

## Yaya zan kare sirrinsa a na’urar?

Ku taƙaita abin da manhajoji za su iya tattarawa, kuma kada ku ɗauka cewa dokar sirrin lafiya ta shafe ta. Ma’aikatar Lafiya da Ayyukan Jama’a ta Amurka (HHS) [ta bayyana](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) cewa ƙa’idojin HIPAA, dokar sirrin lafiya ta Amurka, gabaɗaya ba sa kare bayanan lafiya da aka adana ko aka samu ta wayoyi da tablet na kai. A mafi yawan lokuta, HIPAA kuma ba ta kare bayanan da aka shigar a manhajoji don amfanin kai sai dai idan manhajar ta zo daga mai ba da kulawar lafiya, shirin inshorar lafiya ko ɗaya daga abokan kasuwancinsu da doka ta shafa.

HHS ta ba da shawara:

- a guji manhajoji marasa amfani ko na bazata, musamman na “kyauta”;
- kada a ba manhajoji damar samun wurin da kuke, sai dai idan da gaske suna buƙata, kamar taswira.

Ku yi la’akari kuma:

- **Ku yi amfani da lambar sirri** a na’urar, kuma ku rubuta ta a wuri mai aminci.
- **Ku duba izini** don hotuna, kyamara, makirufo, lambobin sadarwa da wuri, ku kashe duk wanda manhaja ba ta buƙata.
- **Ku duba abin da manhaja take tattarawa** kafin ku shigar da ita. Jagorarmu kan [zaɓar manhajoji da wasanni na mantuwa](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) tana da jerin dubawa na sirri.
- **Ku yi hankali da hotunan iyali.** Ku raba hotunan da mutumin zai ji daɗinsu kawai, kuma ku san inda manhaja take adana su. A [Haɗa hotunan kanku](/games/photo-pairs/), hotunan da kuka zaɓa suna zama a burauzarku.
- **Ku kashe sayayya a cikin manhaja** ko ku cire bayanan biyan kuɗi daga asusun da mutumin yake amfani da su.
