Wani lokaci mafi kyawun aiki shi ne takarda da alkalami a teburin kicin. Abubuwan bugawa ba sa buƙatar caji, kalmar sirri ko allo, kuma suna da sauƙin kawowa ziyara ko ƙungiya. Wannan shafi yana bayani a kan ayyukan bugawa na kyauta da ke shafinmu, yadda za a buga su don su zama masu sauƙin gani da riƙewa, da wasu abubuwan bugawa masu sauƙi da za a iya yi a gida da iyalai da ma’aikatan ayyuka suke ganin suna da amfani.

## Waɗanne ayyukan bugawa na kyauta kuke bayarwa?

Muna ba da abubuwan bugawa guda huɗu na kyauta. Ku buɗe kowane ɗaya daga waɗannan shafukan wasa ku yi amfani da maɓallin **Print** don samun sigar da ta dace da firinta.

| Abin bugawa | Menene shi | Ya dace da |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Neman kalmomi](/games/word-search/) | Neman kalmomi da manyan haruffa da kalmomi a kwance da a tsaye kawai | Lokacin natsuwa da alkalami; wanda ya ji daɗin wasannin haɗawa |
| [Canza launi](/games/coloring/) | Shafukan canza launi don bugawa, ko don canza launi a allo | Lokaci mai annashuwa da ƙirƙira; kowane mataki da taimako |
| [Katunan hira](/games/conversation-cards/) | Katunan hotuna masu buɗaɗɗun gayyatar hira marasa jarrabawa bisa jigo | Ziyara, kira, cin abinci na iyali, ƙungiyoyin tuna da dā |
| [Bingo na hotuna](/games/picture-bingo/) | Katunan bingo da hotuna maimakon lambobi, da mai kira a allo | Gidajen kulawa, shirye-shiryen rana, taruka na iyali |

### Neman kalmomi da manyan haruffa

[Neman kalmomi](/games/word-search/) yana amfani da manyan haruffa kuma yana barin kalmomi a kwance da a tsaye kawai, babu a kusurwa ko a baya. Ku yi aiki tare: mutum ɗaya yana karanta kalma daga jerin, ɗayan yana nemanta. Idan samun kalmomi ya yi wuya, ku gwada kewaya harafin farko na kowace kalma tare, ko kawai karanta jerin kalmomin da ƙarfi ku yi magana game da kowace kalma.

### Shafukan canza launi

Ana iya buga shafukan [Canza launi](/games/coloring/) don alkalumin launi ko fensir masu launi, ko a canza launi a tablet ta taɓa launi, sannan wani sashe na hoton. Ga shafukan da aka buga, ku ba da alkalumin launi masu kauri da launuka kaɗan a lokaci guda. Ku duba [jagorarmu ta fasaha da sana’o’i](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) don hanyoyin riƙe canza launi a matsayin na manya kuma mai annashuwa.

### Katunan hira

[Katunan hira](/games/conversation-cards/) suna haɗa hoto bayyananne da buɗaɗɗiyar gayyata, kamar “Ku ba ni labarin...” ko “Me kuka lura?” An rubuta su a matsayin buɗaɗɗun gayyata, ba tambayoyin jarrabawa ba. Ku buga saiti, ku yanke su, kuma ku riƙe kaɗan a jaka don ziyara ko kusa da waya don kira. Suna aiki da kyau a ƙungiyoyin [tuna da dā](/activities/reminiscence/) ma.

### Katunan bingo na hotuna

Katunan [Bingo na hotuna](/games/picture-bingo/) suna amfani da hotuna maimakon lambobi. Ku buga kati ɗaya ga kowane ɗan wasa ku yi amfani da mai kira a allo a laptop, talabijin ko na’urar nuna hoto. Muna ba da shawarar kira har sai katin kowa ya cika, don kada a bar kowa a baya. Ku duba [ayyukan ƙungiya na gidajen kulawa](/activities/care-home-groups/) don yadda ake gudanar da zama.

## Yaya zan buga ayyuka don su zama masu sauƙin amfani?

Ƙananan canje-canje a yadda ake bugawa suna kawo babban bambanci.

### Ku sauƙaƙa gani

- **Manyan haruffa masu sauƙi.** Ku yi amfani da mafi girman saitin bugawa da har yanzu zai shiga shafin. Ku guji haruffa masu ado ko na rubutun hannu.
- **Bambancin launi mai ƙarfi.** Tawada mai duhu a kan takarda fara ko mai launin madara. Ku bar launin toka mai haske ko bangon launi.
- **Takarda marar sheƙi** tana rage haske mai ƙarfi a ƙarƙashin fitilu masu haske.
- **Ku duba tabarau da haske.** Alzheimer’s Association ta lura cewa matsalolin gani da ji na iya sa sadarwa ta fi wuya, kuma tana ba da shawarar a riƙa duba su akai-akai. Fitila mai haske kuma daidai ma tana taimakawa.

### Ku sauƙaƙa riƙewa

- **Takarda mai kauri ko kati** tana tsayawa a kwance, ta fi sauƙin ɗauka, kuma tana jure amfani akai-akai.
- **Allon riƙe takarda** ko tabarma marar zamewa yana hana shafin zamewa.
- **Alkalumin launi masu kauri ko fensir masu kauri** sun fi sauƙin riƙewa fiye da alkalumi sirara.

### Ku sa su sake amfani

- **Ku rufe shafuka da leda mai tauri** ko ku zura su cikin jakunkunan leda masu haske. Ku yi amfani da alkalumin da ake gogewa a kan neman kalmomi da katunan bingo, sannan ku goge.
- **Ku zaɓi jakunkuna marasa sheƙi** idan haske mai ƙarfi matsala ne, kuma **ku zagaye kusurwoyi** na katunan da aka rufe da almakashi don su zama masu daɗin riƙewa.
- **Ku ajiye fayil** na waɗanda ake so don kada ku riƙa buga shafi ɗaya kowane mako.

### Ku riƙe shi mai sauƙi

- **Aiki guda a kowane shafi.** Neman kalmomi guda ɗaya ko hoto guda ɗaya, ba ayyuka da yawa a cunkushe ba.
- **Isasshen farin sarari.** Shafi mai cunkoso na iya jin kamar ya wuce kima.
- **Babu umarni da za a karanta, idan zai yiwu.** Ku nuna wa mutumin abin da zai yi maimakon.
- **Ku yanke shafuka.** Idan cikakken shafi ya yi yawa, ku yanke shi rabi ko ku naɗe shi don sashe kawai ya bayyana.

## Sauran abubuwan bugawa masu amfani da za ku iya yi a gida

Ba kowane abin bugawa mai taimako ba ne wasa. Waɗannan suna da sauƙin yi da na’urar rubutu da firinta.

### Shafin tarihin rayuwa

Shafi ɗaya na “game da ni” mai hoto, sunan da mutumin yake son a kira shi, inda ya girma, aikinsa, iyalinsa, abincin da yake so, kiɗa da abubuwan da suke kwantar masa da hankali ko ɓata masa rai. Dementia Australia tana ba da shawarar [littafin tunawa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/visiting-someone-dementia) mai bayanai game da rayuwar mutumin, abin da yake so da mutane masu muhimmanci, don taimaka wa baƙi da masu kulawa da kuma fara hira.

Yana da amfani musamman a asibitoci da sabbin wuraren kulawa. NIA tana ba da shawarar a ba ma’aikatan asibiti [takardar bayanan mutum](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/taking-person-alzheimers-disease-hospital) da ke bayyana tsarin yau da kullum na mutumin, sunan da yake so, abin da yake so da wanda ba ya so, abubuwan da za su iya tayar masa da hankali, da alamun ciwo marasa magana.

### Jadawalin yini

Babban jadawali mai sauƙi na yini mai lokuta, kalmomi kaɗan da ƙaramin hoto ga kowane sashe: karin kumallo, yawo, abincin rana, hutu, kiɗa, abincin dare. [Tsarin kulawa na yau da kullum](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/daily-care-plan) na Alzheimer’s Association yana ba da shawarar shiri bisa mafi kyawun lokutan mutumin a rana, da lokutan tashi da kwanciya na kullum da daidaiton aiki da hutu. Ku liƙa shi a matakin ido, wataƙila a kan firiji, kuma ku riƙe shi mai sassauƙa. Ku duba [jagorarmu ta tsarin yini](/activities/daily-routine/) don ƙari.

### Lakabin hotuna na ɗakuna da aljihun tebura

NIA tana ba da shawarar amfani da alamu masu launi mai haske ko hotuna masu sauƙi don [lakabta bayan gida, ɗakin kwana da kicin](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips). Wannan ra’ayi yana aiki ga aljihunan tebura da kabad ma: hoton safa a aljihun safa, kofuna a kabad ɗin kofuna. Ku buga, ku rufe da leda mai tauri kuma ku liƙa.

### Saitin rarrabewa

Ku buga hotuna daga rukunoni biyu masu sauƙi, kamar ’ya’yan itatuwa da furanni ko kayan aiki da kayan kicin, a kan kati. Ku yanke su ku rarraba su cikin kwanduna biyu tare. Sigar allon wannan ita ce [Rarrabe tare](/games/sort-together/).

### Takardun kalmomin waƙa

Ku buga kalmomin wasu waƙoƙin da yake so da manyan haruffa don waƙar tare. Ko mutanen da ba sa karatu sosai na iya jin daɗin riƙe takardar.

### Shafin hotunan iyali

Shafi ɗaya mai manyan hotunan iyali huɗu zuwa shida, kowanne da sunan farko a ƙasa. Yana iya zama a kan tebur don kowa ya ɗauka ya yi magana a kai. Lakabi suna taimaka wa baƙi da ma’aikata su shiga hira, kuma babu wanda zai tambaya “Wane ne wannan?”

### Katin girke-girke

Ku buga girke-girken iyali da yake so da manyan haruffa. Ku yi amfani da shi don dafa abinci tare, ko kawai don yin magana game da abincin da ya wuce.

## Yaya zan ba da abin bugawa ba tare da ya zama kamar aikin gida ba?

Ku gabatar da shi a matsayin abin da za a raba, ba jarrabawa ba.

- **Ku zauna ku fara da kanku.** “Ina yin wannan neman kalmomi. Kuna so ku taimaka mini in nemo ‘lambu’?”
- **Ku yi aiki tare.** Ku yi juyi-juyi, ko mutum ɗaya yana nemo ɗayan yana kewaya.
- **Kada ku taɓa ba da alamar daidai ko kuskure.** Jagorar Alzheimer’s Association ta [ayyuka a gida](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/5337a7c3-15c6-4b4d-a3a0-ca00a0839a6b/alzheimers-dementia-activities-at-home-ts.pdf) tana ba da shawarar mai da hankali ga tsari, ba sakamako ba, da jin daɗi maimakon nasara.
- **Ku bar shafuka a waje.** Jagorar ɗaya ta ba da shawarar sanya littattafan tarawa, kundin hotuna ko tsoffin mujallu a wurare masu sauƙin kaiwa. Katunan da aka buga da shafukan hotuna na iya zama a kan ƙaramin tebur ta haka.
- **Ku tsaya a duk lokacin da kuke so.** Rabin shafin canza launi la’asar ce mai kyau. Haka ma ajiye shafin da shan shayi.
