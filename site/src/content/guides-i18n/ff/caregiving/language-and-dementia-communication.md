Neɗɗoɓe huutorɓe ɗemɗe keewɗe ina mbaawi haala e no feewri waylude so hakkillo ina wayla faamde kelme walla faamde. Won neɗɗoɓe fuɗɗotoo huutoraade ɗemngal o jangino adan, wonɓe ɗoo mbaawaa ɗuum. Ko adii fof ko ƴamde hol ɗemngal na weltani neɗɗo oo jooni, caggal ɗuum rewindo e ko o cuɓii.

[Alzheimer's Society wi'i](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia) cuɓogol ɗemɗe ina waawi seeroraade, kadi ina wasiya huutoraade ɗemngal neɗɗo oo huutortoowo sahaa kala. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications) kadi ina woyna wonde artugol e ɗemngal neɗɗo makko adanal ngal ina waawi jeyeede e wayle haala. Wayle ɗee mbaawaa waɗde e kala neɗɗo, kadi ɗee hollitataa tolno hakkillo neɗɗo oo.

## Hol ɗemngal miɗo foti huutoraade?

ƴam neɗɗo oo hol ɗemngal o yiɗi huutoraade e yeewtere ndee. Cuɓogol makko ina waawi waylude e toɓɓere, nokku walla sahaa. So o wayli e ɗemɗe goɗɗe, hoto ƴeewndo mo walla waɗ mo huutoraade ɗemngal a faamta tan.

So aɗa andi ɗemngal o cuɓii ngal, huutoro kelme goongɗe kadi hokku mo jam nafoore ngam jaabde. So aɗa andi tan seeɗa, tinno ɗuum e anndude, huutoro kadi jawle, geɗe walla nate. [Alhamdu ngal ɗemɗe to Alzheimer's Society](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) ina wi'i baawɗe haala ina mbaawi waylude ñalnde fof, kadi ina waawi wonde e ɗum haɗaade e yummugol, mettu walla colde.

## Ko hol ko wallata e yeewtere renndo?

Toppito famminorde haala kuuɓal, kadi ɓeydu wallitorde nanndunde e ɗemngal neɗɗo oo:

- **Huutoro kelme e inɗe goongɗe.** ƴam neɗɗo oo walla neɗɗo koolaaɗo mo o hoolaa hol kelme o huutorto e geɗe renndo, leñol walla kuutorɗe ñande fof.
- **Hollu kadi wi'.** Feeñu e kopporol, hollu cuɓe ɗiɗi, walla huutoro natal goongɗel walla geɗel goongɗel. Jawal ina waawi wallude so kelme nde oɗon yiyla waɗii saɗtude.
- **Foo.** Hokku neɗɗo oo jam nafoore ngam heɗde e jaabde. Hoto timminde konngol kala walla ƴeewde ƴamol e ɗemɗe keewɗe e sahaa gooto.
- **Toppito miijoo gooto.** Tinndinoore juutunde ina waawi saɗtude e kala ɗemngal. Huutoro konngol gurtol, foo, caggal ɗuum ƴeew so o yiɗi ɓeydude.
- **Ƴeew nande e gite.** Kuccitɗe, heɗorde, lewru moƴƴo e nokku deƴƴo ina mbaawi wallude faamde haala.

So neɗɗo oo ina yiɗi tarde, ƴam so humpito teeŋtungo woodi e ɗemngal makko. Hoto hoolaade e ciynugol otomatik ngal ngam baɗal saɗtingol, yamiroore walla yamiroore lekki; teeŋtin humpito teeŋtungo e jibinɗo kaɓirɗe laawol hakkude ndeenka.

## So mi andaa ɗemngal neɗɗo oo cuɓii ngal?

ƴam mo so o yiɗi neɗɗo goɗɗo anduɗo ɗemɗe ɗeeɗiɗe walla neɗɗo koolaaɗo arde e yeewtere renndo. Hoto sikka ko o yiɗi neɗɗo goo fammindemo; wonaa ko o yiɗi kadi huutoraade jawle, nate walla ɗemngal goɗɗangal ko ɓeɗɗo faama.

Ngam ndeenka laawol saɗtingol walla yeewtere haajunde humpito goofɗo, noddu opitaal ngal walla carwol wallitorde hade ndeenka. Tinno ngal hol ɗemngal neɗɗo oo cuɓii, kadi ƴam ko wallitorde faamorde ɗemɗe woodani. [Sariyah Dowlawulo saɗtingol e laawol neɗɗo (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services)](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) ina wi'i neɗɗoɓe gorɓe walla gollanooɓe maa ɓe waawaa fammindinde humpito laawol saɗtingol no haanirta; carwol ngal ina waawi fammindinde cuɓe mum en e laawɗe nokkuure. Neɗɗo oo ina waawi cuɓaade so o yiɗi neɗɗo wallitowo yahrude e makko, kono jibinɗo kaɓirɗe laawol hakkude ndeenka ina foti fodde wallitorde faamorde ɗemɗe so ina haani.

E nder ndeenka, haala e neɗɗo oo e yeeso, kadi hokku jam nafoore ngam fammindo oo haala. Teeŋtin so neɗɗo oo faamii ƴamol ngal kadi so o heɓii sahaa jaabde e kelme mum. So aɗa wallita mo, ɓeydu humpito tan so o ƴamii maa.

## Sahaa hommbo miɗo foti ƴamde wallitorde laawol saɗtingol?

Baawɗe haala ina mbaawi waylude e sahaa, kono waylugol ɗoon e ɗoon ina seeroraade. [NHS ina wasiya ƴamde wallitorde laawol saɗtingol ko yaawi so neɗɗo huccitii ɗoon e ɗoon](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Hoto sikka waylugol haala ɗoon e ɗoon ko hakkillo waylaynoo tan. So neɗɗo oo huccitii ɗoon e ɗoon, ina waawi colde no feewi walla waawaa finnde, noddu carwol wallitorde laawol saɗtingol nokkuure.

Ngam wayle laaɓtɗe, winndu ko aɗa yiyde kadi ƴam doktoor neɗɗo oo ngam famminde. Winndu hol ɗemngal o huutortonoo, ko wallitii mo haala, kadi so o yummunoo walla ina nawni. Ɗuum ko tinndinoore ngam yeewtere, wonaa sikkitinde. [Daakirɗe amen ngam ndeenka doktoor](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) ina mbaawi wallude ma heɓtinde ƴamol.

## Hol ko mi waawi winde ngam wallitorde goɗɗe?

So neɗɗo oo yamirii, winndu tinndinoore famɗunde ngam neɗɗoɓe o yiɗi mbaɗe wallude mo. Ina waawi winde ɗemngal walla diiwal o cuɓi ngam haala e tarde, kelme goongɗe walla inɗe, kadi ko wallata mo faamde yeewtere. ƴam so o yiɗi neɗɗo heɓɗo arde e ndeenka walla e humpito hakkude wallitorde. Hoto winndude humpito cuurɗo haa waɗɗa ɗuum ngam wallitorde, kadi tinno neɗɗo oo hol mo waawi yiyde tinndinoore ndee. Tinndinoore ɗemngal ina walla neɗɗoɓe fuɗɗaade yeewtere no weltiri, kono ɗuum fotataa e wallitorde faamorde ɗemɗe e yeewtere teeŋtunde hakkude e laawol saɗtingol.

## Hol no mi waawi wallude cuɓogol neɗɗo oo?

ɗemngal ko jeyal e no neɗɗo oo jokkondirirta, kono wonaa ko fof e neɗɗo oo. ƴam mo hakkude e kuutorɗe makko, belɗe makko, ko o yiɗi tarde, kadi neɗɗoɓe o yiɗi mbaɗe wallude mo. So neɗɗo oo yamirii, winndu kelme walla konngol wallitiiɗo ngam wallitorde goɗɗe kadi fammindin ɓe no kuutortee. Toppito tinndinoore e suturo kadi renndin tan ko neɗɗo oo yamirii renndinde.

Hoto muɗɗude neɗɗo oo janngude, fammindinde ngam goɗɗe walla ƴeewde so o faamii. Won yeewte waawi weeɓude e ɗemngal gootal, wonɗe goɗɗe e ɗemngal goɗɗangal. Weltare, suturo e cuɓogol neɗɗo oo ina ɓuri teeŋtude e muynude e ɗemngal gootal.

[Daakirɗe amen hakkude e haala](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ina fammindina haala newɗo, heɗde e huutoraade nate. Ngam wallitorde heɓtinde caggal sikkitinde hakkillo, yiy [addeyyaaji adii sikkitinde hakkillo](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).
