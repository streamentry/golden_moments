So aɗa toppitoo neɗɗo wuuroowo e majjere hakkille, ina gasa tawa aɗa taroo ɗoo laawol kikiiɗe, loƴƴuɗo, e doggol geɗe ɗe a waawaani waɗde. Ɗuum ko huunde woodnde. Toppitaade hoore maa wonaa huunde moƴƴere walla njoddiigu caggal nde gasɗaa fof. Ko ɗuum wallata maa jokkude.

Hello ngoo ina haalude e maale reentinooje tampere, no keɓirtaa fowtaare goonga, to tawataa wallitorde, e limo wallitorde Alla meho e Amerik ɗe a waawi noddude hannde.

## Hol ko woni maale tampere toppitorde?

Tampere ina waawi naatde satii-satii, ɗuum noon ina moƴƴi anndude maale maa. [National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/caregiving/taking-care-yourself-tips-caregivers) ina limta maale tampere toppitorde, ina jeyaa heen:

- anndude loƴƴere, faddeede walla hakkil anaade;
- seytude walla munnude yaawno;
- anndude keddiiɗo walla taƴaaɗo e woɗɓe;
- caɗeele ɗaanagol, walla heɓaade ɗaanagol yonaani;
- anndude suno walla ŋakkude tammunde, walla ŋakkude weltaare e geɗe ɗe a yiɗnoo;
- nawnaare hoore, muuseeji walla caɗeele cellal ɓalli keewɗe;
- alaa waktu ngam dillere walla defirde ñaamdu moƴƴuru hoore maa;
- ŋakkude lootde walla toppitaare hoore maa;
- huutoraade mandilaaji walla lekki gaadoraaɗi no moƴƴaani, ina jeyaa heen lekki doktora.

[Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/caregiver-health/caregiver-stress) ina ɓeyda maale hono yeddude nyawu nguu, seytude e neɗɗo oo («omo sala tan»), hakkil anaade fii janngo, caɗeele toɓɓude hakkille, e caɗeele cellal ɓalli. Ɗe ina wasiyaa yo a haaldu e doktora maa so aɗa jogii maale hono ɗee sahaaji keewɗi.

Waasi NIA wonaa yo a fad haa a faddaa haa timmi. Anndu maale maa law, tee waɗ huunde heen.

## Hol ko mi waawi waɗde hannde, e hojomaaji 5?

Fowtaare pamarnde ina nafaa. Hay gooto e ɗee ina ñawnda tampere fof, kono ina mbaawi wallitde maa e kikiiɗe satteende.

- **Yaltu yaasin.** Jooɗo e yeeso suudu walla yah haa kaaytuuji, tawa neɗɗo oo ina hisi tee ina deeƴi.
- **Fofa satii-satii.** Fofon seeɗa satii-satii hade maa jaabaade naamnal sattuɗngal. Aɗa waawi kadi renndinde e [Poftere Deeƴre](/games/calm-breathing/) e neɗɗo mo toppitotoɗaa.
- **Yaru ndiyam tee ñaam huunde.** Ina weeɓi yejjitde ñaamdu maa.
- **Neldan giɗɗo gooto ɓatakuru.** Wonaa ngam tororde huunde, kono ngam jokkondirde.
- **Wiy hoore maa konngol moƴƴol.** [Dewtere NIA fii toppitaade hoore maa](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/alzheimers-caregiving-caring-yourself) ina wasiyaa konnguɗi hono «Miɗo waɗa ko mi waawi fof», «Mi timmaani, ɗuum ina moƴƴi», e «Mi weltorto e saa’iiji ɗi min mbaawi wonde e ndeeƴre.»

## Hol ko woni fowtaare toppitaare, tee hol no mi heɓirta nde?

Fowtaare toppitaare ko toppitaare waktu seeɗa wonande neɗɗo mo toppitotoɗaa, ngam a heɓa fowtaare. [Hello NIA fii fowtaare toppitaare](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/caregiving/what-respite-care) ina faamnina wonde nde ina waawi juutde gila waktuuji seeɗa haa e yontere ɗiɗi, tee ina waawi waɗde ka suudu, e nokku toppitaare cellal walla e nokku ñalnde mawɓe.

[Alzheimer’s Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/respite-care) ina sifaa fannuji keewɗi:

| Fannu | Hol ko ɗum woni |
| --- | --- |
| Ballal ka suudu | Giɗɗo, musiɗɗo, wallitoowo yiɗɗo walla balloowo yoɓaaɗo ina ara ka suudu |
| Nokku ñalnde mawɓe | Golle teeltaaɗe hono jimɗi e nate e kawtal kisngal; jolngo e ñaamdu ina keewi heɓeede |
| Dakkere jamma walla dakkere juutnde seeɗa | Nokku toppitaare juutnde ina toppitoo neɗɗo oo e jamma gooto, balɗe seeɗa walla yontere seeɗa |

**Hol mo yoɓata?** Hono no NIA wiyiri, fowtaare immorde e musidɓe, yibbe walla wallitooɓe yiɗɓe ina waawi wonde Alla meho. Carwe karallaagal ina yoɓee e waktu walla ñalnde. Wonande neɗɗo heɓoowo toppitaare hospice, Medicare ina yoɓa ko ɓuri heewde e njoɓdi balɗe 5 jokkuɗe e fowtaare e hopitaal walla nokku toppitaare karallaagal. Medicaid ina waawi wallitde. Ko ɓuri heewde e kisal cellal keeriiɗo ina yoɓa fowtaare, kono won e eɓɓooje kisal toppitaare juutnde ina yoɓa.

**Wasiya:** Alzheimer’s Association ina wasiyaa yo a ƴeewndo rokkirɓe fowtaare e haalaare nde wonaa emergency, ngam ɓe keɓoo so a heɓii haajuuji yaawɗi.

## To tawmi wallitorde sara am?

Carwe keewɗe Alla meho e Amerik ina mbaawi hollude maa wallitorde nokku maa.

- **Alzheimer’s Association 24/7 Helpline: 1-800-272-3900.** [Helpline](https://www.alz.org/help-support/resources/helpline) ko Alla meho tee cuuɗiinde, ina heɓee sahaa kala ñalnde fof e hitaande ndee, e wallitorde e ɗemɗe ɓurɗe 200 rewrude e gollotooɓe ɗemɗe ɗiɗi walla firtooɓe. Noddu 711 ngam heɓde carwol firtorde. Aɗa waawi haalde fii waylooji jikku, tampere toppitorde, cuɓe fowtaare, eɓɓooje nokku maa e ko woɗi, tee tor haalirde juutnde e balloowo toppitaare. Gollotooɓe ndokkataa wasiyaaji doktora.
- **Eldercare Locator: 1-800-677-1116.** [Eldercare Locator](https://eldercare.acl.gov/), ko carwol renndo ngol Administration for Community Living, ina jokkondir maa e carwe mawɓe e ɓesngu mabɓe. Aɗa waawi noddude, neldude ɓatakuru, yeewtidde e geese walla neldude iimeel e gollotooɓe ekkitaaɓe, walla yiilaade tawa ko nokku maa. Ina waawi hollude maa Area Agency on Aging nokku maa.
- **ARCH National Respite Locator Service.** [ARCH respite locator](https://archrespite.org/caregiver-resources/respitelocator/) ina wallita maa yiytude rokkirɓe fowtaare e leydi kala, tee ina wasiyaa yo a jokkondir State Respite Coalition walla Lifespan Respite program maa tawo so leydi maa ina jogii ɗum. ARCH ina teskitina wonde ɓe ƴeewtataa rokkirɓe, ɗuum noon ƴeewto humpito e daartol mabɓe hoore maa.

## Hol no mi heɓirta dental wallitorde?

Naamno Helpline walla yiilo e geese ngam kawte sara maa. Alzheimer’s Association [ina yuɓɓina kawte wallitorde](https://www.alz.org/help-support/community/support-groups) wonande toppitooɓe e yimɓe wuurooɓe e majjere hakkille, no wejjere e no geese, tawa ko ardotooɓe ekkitaaɓe. Nokkuuji keewɗi kadi ina ndokka kawte keertiiɗe, yeru wonande toppitooɓe mawɓe walla wonande yimɓe wuurooɓe e Alzheimer fuɗɗoode law walla fuɗɗorde. Kadi ina yuɓɓina ALZConnected, ko renndo geese Alla meho to toppitooɓe ndenndinta wasiyaaji.

Kawte wallitorde ina mbaawi wonde nokku to a waawi wowlude tawa a ñaawetaake, heɓde wasiyaaji golli immoriiɗi e yimɓe ƴeewndunooɓe ɗum, tee anndude wonaa tan ko aan. So kawtal gadanal ngal yahdaani e maa, eto goɗngal.

## Hol no mi torirta musidɓe e yibbe wallitorde?

Laɓɓin tornde ndee. Yimɓe heewɓe ina njiɗi wallitde kono ɓe anndaa hol ko ɓe ndokkata. NIA ina wasiyaa:

- **Mooftu doggol** golle ɗe woɗɓe mbaawi ƴettude, tee accu ɓe cuɓoo.
- **Fuɗɗo seeɗa** so toragol ina satti.
- **Yahdin golle e neɗɗo oo.** Musiɗɗo yiɗuɗo defde ina addana ñaamdu e aljuma. Kono neɗɗo moƴƴo e kaayitaaji ina wallita e golle binnditagol.
- **Neldu ɓatakuru walla iimeel** so toragol yeeso e yeeso ina mettina maa.
- **Eltu jaabawol** so neɗɗo rokkitii: «A jaaraama e naamnde maa. Ko ɗum nii miɗo yiɗi a waɗa.»
- **Higgo won e salaare** tee eto tawo a jaɓtataa ɗum no mettuɗum.

Miijooji goɗɗi: tor neɗɗo yo jooɗo e neɗɗo oo kikiiɗe wootere e yontere kala, walla yo yahdu e makko njillu pamaru, walla yo ar fijo ɓe pija fii. Amen [dewwere fii fijirde e dental](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ina weeɓi lollinde yilloowo.

## Hol so mi heɓii caɗeele?

Waɗ eɓɓoore lomtorde weeɓnde jooni. NIA ina wasiyaa yo a nanondir e musidɓe walla yibbe ɓadiiɓe hol mo naatata, tee moofta deftere e limo emergency, lekki, wasiyaaji jikku golluɗi, e golle ɗe neɗɗo oo yiɗi. Alzheimer’s Association kadi ina wasiyaa yo jokkondirɓe cellal neɗɗo oo e doggol lekki kesol ngon no weeɓiri yiyteede.

## Mbar sahaa nde mi haani heɓde ballal karallaagal walla yaawngal?

Haaldu e doktora maa so maale tampere ina keewi, so aɗa anndi suno, walla so ina sattu maa faddaade. Halan doktora maa wonde ko a toppitordo. NIA ina teskitina wonde ballooɓe hakkille e gollotooɓe renndo ina mbaawi wallitde e miijooji sattuɗi e teeltagol, tee wonde Medicare, Medicaid e won e kisal keeriiɗo ina mbaawi yoɓde seeɗa e njoɓdi cellal hakkille.

So aɗa anndi ko a e haalaare sattuɗe walla aɗa jogii miijooji bonnude hoore maa, noddu walla neldu 988 ngam yettaade [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/), Alla meho tee ina heɓee 24/7. So hay gooto ina e kulol yaawngol, noddu limo emergency nokku maa.

## Mbar golle renndaaɗe ina mbaawi kadi rokkude mi fowtaare?

Sahaaji ina mbaawi. Golle deeƴɗe ɗe onon ɗiɗo njeɗata ina mbaawi wonde geɗel ɓurngel deeƴde e ñalnde ndee wonande maa kadi e neɗɗo mo toppitotoɗaa: heɗaade jimɗi ɓooyɗi, ndaarde nate, walla fijo hono [Karte Yeewtere](/games/conversation-cards/) ɗe naamnataaki jaabawuuji moƴƴuɗi. So neɗɗo oo ina weltii wondude e yimɓe, fijirde kawtal e [Bingo Nate](/games/picture-bingo/) e nokku mawɓe walla eɓɓoore ñalnde ina waawi rokkude maa waktu fowtaare gooto. Yiy [golle wonande kawte nokku toppitaare](/activities/care-home-groups/) ngam miijooji.
