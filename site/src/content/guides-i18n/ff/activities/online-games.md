Ɓesnguuji heewɗi ena njiyloo fijirde teeŋtuɗe e enternet ngam majjere hakkil sabu ɓe ena njiɗi huunde nde mbaɗata e nder ɓesngu, nde soklaani jeere, naatirde walla karte banki. Suɓre ɓurnde moƴƴude ko fijirde weeɓɗe, tuugnoraaɗe e nate, weeɓɗe dartinde. Ɗe poti anndeede ko sahaa lollirangal, wonaa ƴeewndol. Ndee yamre ena sifta ko ñawetee, hol no fijirde teelte, e ko fijirde ɗee mbaawi e ko ɗe mbaawaa waɗde.

## Hol ko waɗata fijirde e enternet ena fota e majjere hakkil?

Fijirde ena fota e yimɓe heewɓe wondude e majjere hakkil so nde naamnii ko heftinde, wonaa teskude. Jahdingol nate ɗiɗi fotnduɗe, memminde natal jahdoowal e dowdi, walla bugude huunde e paate moƴƴo fof ko tuugii ko gite ɗee njiya jooni. Fijirde tuugiiɗe e teskude doosgal, helmere, limoore walla yahre yawtunde ena keewi addude mettagol.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ena wasiyoo eɓɓooje tuugiiɗe e ko neɗɗo oo haa jooni weli e ko o waawi waɗde, tee ɗe acca mo heɓa moƴƴere tawa ballal alaa. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ena wasiyoo weeɓnude eɓɓoore nde tawa mbaawkaaji ɗii mbayliima, wonaa accude nde.

## Fijirde Golden Moments ɗe mbaawɗaa udditde e wanngorde

Kala fijirde les ɗoo uddittoo ko e lowre ndee, alaa aawtaade. Ɓurɗe ɗee ena pijoo e alluwel walla ordinateer; won e majje ena mbaawi winnditeede kadi.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Jahdinaale nate yaajɗe e nate ɗee peeñɗe, alaa waktu walla keɓal. Ena moƴƴi ngam fijirde adannde.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Natal gootal dow, tee natal gootal nanndungal e hakkunde cuɓe ɗiɗi haa nayi les.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Dowdi deeƴndi ena feeña dow; memmu natal waɗoowo nde.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Memmu natal, caggal ɗuum paate mo jeyi e mum, ko wayi no defirde walla ngesa.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Natal taƴaama geɗe tee natal timmungal ngal ena heddii e yiyeede ngam ballal.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Konguɗi anndaaɗi e helmere sakkitiinde ŋakkiinde; memmu cuɓre walla hollu timmoode nde.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Karte nate e naamne udditiiɗe ngam yewtere, wonaa ƴeewndol. Ena mbaawi winnditeede kadi.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Jahdinaale nate waɗiraaɗe e nate ɗe cuɓɗaa e kaɓirgol maa. Nate ɗee ena keddii e wanngorde e kaɓirgol ngal.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Foofaango deeƴngo, deeƴngol ngam foofde e nder ɓesngu. Ko a fuɗɗata tee ko a dartinta.

Yiylorde kelme, diinngol e bingo nate ena keɓoo kadi ko eɓɓooje baawɗe winnditeede. Ƴeew [ebbbooje majjere hakkil baawɗe winnditeede teeŋti](/activities/printable/) ngam ɓeydaade.

## Hol no fijirde teelte

1. **Suɓo sahaa deeƴngol** nde mo toppitoɗaa fowtike. Kikiiɗe ena keewi saattinde won e yimɓe, nden ƴeewto daartol maa tawo.
2. **Huutoro yaynirde ɓurnde yaajeende.** Alluwel e dow darorde, walla ordinateer e wanngorde mawninaande, ena weeɓi yiyeede e memmeede.
3. **Uddu tabbe goɗɗe tee ñif mbaylaaje.** Pop-up puɗɗuɗo ena haala tee ena waawi dartinde sahaa moƴƴo.
4. **Jooɗo sara mo toppitoɗaa, wonaa yeeso makko.** Yiyde e nder ɓesngu ena wayi ko wonde e wondiinde, wonaa ƴeewndol.
5. **Fuɗɗo e fijirde wootere ngam hojomaaji jowi haa sappo.** Accu mo toppitoɗaa o memmo yaynirde nde walla o fiilo tawa on ndonndondirii e ndeer.
6. **Gasnir e huunde moƴƴere.** Timmin yahre, caggal ɗuum yeewtu natal gootal walla wasiyo njaram teewu.

## Suturo e kisal e kaɓirɗe lollirɗe

- **Nate ena keddii e kaɓirgol ngal.** Photo pairs huutortoo ko nate ɗe cuɓɗaa, tee ɗe ena keddii e wanngorde e kaɓirgol ngal. Ƴeewto hol mo waawi udditde ordinateer walla alluwel ngal.
- **Reeno jokke yaajɗe e ɓennuɗe.** Huutoro fijirde ɗee to lowre ndee tan, tee hoto dobo e ɓennuɗe walla mbaylaaje naamnooje humpito maa keeriiɗo.
- **Seedtin yoɓirde e konte goɗɗum.** Hoto accu mo toppitoɗaa tan e yaynirde naamnoonde finnde naatirde walla limoore karte.
- **Reento yahdu yeeso.** Mooftu alluwel ngal to woɗɓe mbaawaa yettaade ɗum so neɗɗo oo ena waawi yaltude suudu e ngal.

Ngam doggol ɓurngol yaajde, ƴeew [hol no suɓortoo jaaɓirɗe e fijirde majjere hakkil](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) e [teeltugol alluwel walla cinndel](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Ko fijirde ɗee mbaawi e ko ɗe mbaawaa waɗde

Fijirde e enternet ena ndokka yimɓe wondude e majjere hakkil fartaŋŋe weltaade sahaa, ndaara nate e nder ɓesngu, tee tawtoraade e huunde tawa tiiɗnaaki. Ɗe njaɓataa majjere hakkil, ɗe nleeltinaa nde, walla ɗe kaɗataa nde. Yeru anndaandu ko [ebbboore Federal Trade Commission e hitaande 2016 e dow Lumosity](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) sabu yiyanɗe eltugol hakkil ɗe mbaawaano holliteede. Reento kala fijirde, jaaɓngal walla lowre foddude teskagol ɓurngol moƴƴude, ɓetoo hakkil maa walla wi'a ena waawi artirde ustaare.

Huutoro fijirde ɗee ko e geɓal ñalngu jogiingal kadi jimol, yaawugol, yewtere e foofaango. Ngam janngude hol no fijirde waɗirtee deeƴɗe, wonaa tiiɗɗe, ƴeew [hol no fijirtee e neɗɗo wondudo e majjere hakkil](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) e [fijirde teskagol nde ƴeewataa](/activities/memory-games/).
