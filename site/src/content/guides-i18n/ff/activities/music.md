Diɗɗe ko goɗɗum e huunde ɓurnde weeɓde nde a lollirtaa e neɗɗo wuurɗo e dementia. Ɗe naamnaa hay huunde: alaa jaabawuuji moƴƴi, alaa laawol ngal rewee. Yimre anndaande ena waawi addude mooso, koyngal fiyowal walla konngi seeɗa yimaaɗi, hay e ñalnde nde yewtere saɗi. Ko coklu-ɗaa tan ko diɗɗe moƴƴuɗe e oo neɗɗo keeriiɗo e sahaa deeƴɗo ngam fijirde ɗe.

## Hol kuugal diɗɗe moƴƴata e yimɓe wuurɓe e dementia?

Heɗaade, yimude, fiyde juɗe, diwnude e haalaade baɗte diɗɗe fof ina ngolla no feewi. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ina wasiyaa fijirde diɗɗe, haalde baɗte diɗɗe ɗen e diɗɗo oo, yimude walla amde e yimre anndaande, e yahde e batuuji diɗɗe walla joljolleeji e renndo. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ina ɓeyda heen wonde dillere wa ko fiyde juɗe walla amde ena waawi ɓeydu weltaare, kadi diɗɗe ena mbaawi wonde laawol jokkondirde caggal nde konngi caɗti.

## No mahirtaa doggol diɗɗe keeriiɗe e neɗɗo?

Huunde ɓurnde nafa nde a mbaawi mahde ko doggol diɗɗe jogiiɗe maana e oo neɗɗo. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ina teska kadi diɗɗe jogiiɗe maana keeriiɗo. Ndaa laawol weeɓngol ngam mahde ngol.

### Fuɗɗo e nguurndam mum

- **Duuɓi sappo e jeetati e sappo e jeetati e ɗiɗi mum.** Miijo ko wonnoo e raajo, e amdi e e filmuuji tuma o woni suka.
- **Sahaaiji mawɗi.** Diɗɗe tuma jombaaji mum, amre adannde, doggol diɗɗe njillu ɓesngu ngo o yiɗnoo.
- **Diina e aada.** Yimre diina, gospel, diɗɗe dewal, walla yimre e ɗemngal mum adan ngal.
- **Golle e golle renndo.** Diɗɗe konu, diɗɗe golle, walla diɗɗe jokkondirɗe e sahaa o wonnoo e konu.
- **Ko ɓesngu siiftori.** Naamno musidɓe mum, ɓiɓɓe mum e yiɓɓe ɓooyɓe ko ɓe mbaynoo walla njiymoo sahaa kala.

### Miijo e fannuuji diɗɗe, wonaa yimre tan

So a anndaa to fuɗɗoto-ɗaa, eto hojomaaji seeɗa e kala e ɗii, kadi ƴeew jaabawol: big band e swing, doo-wop, Motown e soul, rock and roll gadano, country e western, bluegrass, gospel e yimre diina, yimre Broadway, jazz, diɗɗe Latin wa boleros walla mariachi, polka, folk e classical. Mooftu ko addata mooso walla koyngal fiyowal; woppu ko addata mettu.

### Waɗ ɗum weeɓde fijirde

- **Alaa ɓannginooje.** Alzheimer's Association ina reentinde wonde ɓannginooje ena mbaawi addude jiiɓru. Huutoro diɗɗe aawtaaɗe, CD, taabal diɗɗe walla sarwiis aldaaɗo ɓannginooje.
- **Kaɓirgol gootol weeɓngol.** Kolondooɗo jogiiɗo butoŋ mawɗo gooto, walla doggol diɗɗe danndangel hebulaangel, mbele kala neɗɗo ena waawi fuɗɗinde ɗum.
- **Doggol ɗiɗi juutɗe.** Doggol dewlunngol ngam dewnude hakkil e doggol weltiingol ngam ɓeydu weltaare.
- **Winndu heen.** Winndu innde neɗɗo oo e "diɗɗe mum yiɗaaɗe" e kaɓirgol ngol mbele ɓesngu yilloowo e gollanooɓe toppitiiɓe ena mbaawi kadi huutoraade ɗum.

## Kuugal diɗɗe 15 ngam etaade

1. **Heɗee koɗon.** Jooɗee sera e sera tawa alaa huunde woɗnde wonnde. Heɗaade tan ko kuugal timmungal.
2. **Yimee e makko.** Fuɗɗo yimde hoore maa. Duumude walla dillinde tonndu e konngi ina jogii nafoore.
3. **Fiyo juɗe walla tappu doole.** Tappu coppi maa, tappu taabal oo, walla huutoro dummbirgel juŋngo walla yerɓindirde.
4. **Ama seeɗa seeɗa.** Jogee juɗe kadi diwnodon e defirde maafe, walla amee e jooɗagol, dillindiron juuɗe koɗon.
5. **Amdude e wutte weelaaɗe.** Yerɓin wutte weelaaɗe no diɗɗe ɗen njahiri, e daragol walla jooɗagol.
6. **Haal baɗte diɗɗe ɗen.** Noddin, hoto ƴeewndo: "Ɗum ina nanndi nokku amdu. Haalan kam baɗte amdi ɗin." Hoto naamno hay sahaa "A siiftorii ndee yimre?"
7. **Fijir doggol timmungol.** Fijir taabal diɗɗe ngo o yiɗnoo, gila fuɗɗoode haa timma, e kafe.
8. **Ndaaree ɓaŋŋe taabal diɗɗe.** Ɓaŋŋe taabal diɗɗe ɓooyɗe ina moƴƴi ngam haalde baɗte conci, otooji e geece.
9. **Ndaaree film diɗɗe.** Film diɗɗe walla joljolre diɗɗe e sahaa mum ena waawi wonde kikiiɗe moƴƴere. Dartin ngam fowtitaade.
10. **Fijir kaɓirgel diɗɗe mo o anndi.** Neɗɗo fijinooɗo piano walla gitaar ena waawi weltaade fijirde weeɓnde. Caggal ɗuum, suɓaade daande seeɗa koɗon ina yonii.
11. **Diɗɗe tuma golle.** Fijir yimre mum yiɗaaɗe tuma taggugol conci walla teeltagol taabal.
12. **Yimre weetnde.** Fuɗɗin kala ñalawma e yimre wootere welnde, no huunde e ɓernde.
13. **Yim yimre diina walla diɗɗe dewal.** Fijir diɗɗe diina mum kadi yimee koɗon.
14. **Naat e fedde yimɓe.** NHS ina sifora pelle yimɓe ko huunde moƴƴere ngam weeyo e cellal hakkil. Naamno nokku mon baɗte fedde yimɓe moƴƴinanaande yimɓe wuurɓe e dementia.
15. **Yah e diɗɗe yeeso.** Joljolre diɗɗe juutnde seeɗa e renndo walla fedde diɗɗe e yaasin. Jooɗo ɓadii yaltirde kadi yaltu tuma nde njiɗ-ɗaa.

Ngam kuugal konngi welngal e konguɗi anndaaɗi, eto [timmin kongol ngol](/games/finish-the-saying/), ɗo a mbaawi sahaa kala hollude timmitol ngol.

## No toɗɗirtaa diɗɗe e sahaa oo

Alzheimer's Association ina wasiyaa huutoraade diɗɗe ngam sosde weeyo mo njiɗ-ɗaa: diɗɗe dewlinaaɗe ngam weeyo dewo, e yimre welnde e sukaaku neɗɗo ngam ɓeydu weltaare.

| Sahaa | Eto | Wasiya |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ummital | Yimre anndaande, welnde | Yimre wootere gootonde kala weetndo ina waawi wonde maandinol dewngol |
| Golle suudu | Diɗɗe weltiiɗe yiɗaaɗe | Diɗɗe ena mbaawi waɗde golle nanndude e wondiinde |
| Ñaametee | Diɗɗe kaɓirɗe dewlinaaɗe | Joga ɗe les mbele ɗe njokkaani e ñaamde |
| Kikiiɗe | Diɗɗe dewlinaaɗe, anndaaɗe e annoore moƴƴere | Yillo [mettu hakkil kikiiɗe](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) |
| Waalaade | Diɗɗe lewɗe, dewlinaaɗe | Ustin hoolaare e annoore koɗon |
| Tolno sakkitiingo | Yimre mum ɓurnde yiɗeede, seeɗa seeɗa | Jooɗo ɓadii mum kadi jog juŋngo mum |

## Ballal ngam humpito moƴƴo

- **Ñif huunde kala hoolnde.** Alzheimer's Association ina wasiyaa ommude dammugal e baafe kadi ñifde televisiyoŋ oo mbele diɗɗe tan wonu hoolaare.
- **Joga hoolaare ndewnde.** Diɗɗe ina poti welde, wonaa hoolde. So neɗɗo oo ina huutoroo nanirɗe noppi, ƴeew ɗe ina e nder kadi ina ngolla no feewi; yillo [nannde](/brain-health/hearing/).
- **Ƴeew jokkondire muusuɗe.** Yimre ena waawi addude suno, hare walla majjere. So yimre mettini mo, ɓuttu mo kadi waylu nde.
- **Accu mo ardii.** So ina waawi, accu neɗɗo oo suɓoo diɗɗe ɗen, kadi jaɓ "alaa".
- **Dartin tuma kala.** Hojomaaji jowi e diɗɗe ko kuugal moƴƴal. Ñifde ɗe kadi jooɗaade deeƴa ko kadi moƴƴi.

## Hol ko ƴeewndeeji mbiyi baɗte diɗɗe e dementia?

Seedeeji ɗin ko famɗuɗi. [Ƴeewtere Cochrane 2025](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) ƴeewii jaŋdeeji 30 baɗte batuuji diɗɗe teeltaaɗi, ko heewi e pelle e cuuɗi toppitagol. Ndee yi’i wonde ɗee batuuji ena mbaawi ustude seeɗa maale muusuuseede so yerondiraa e toppitagol ɓoowal, kono nde yi’aani battane laaɓtuɗe e waylude, cellal hakkil walla miijaade e sifaade, kadi alaa battane nduutiiɗe caggal batuuji ɗin.

Min njokkondirta e diɗɗe sabu ko laawol welngol, mo ɓurɗo hoyde ngam wuurdude sahaa koɗon, wonaa safara. Ngam nokkuuji pelle, yillo [kuugal pelle e suudu toppitagol](/activities/care-home-groups/); ngam huutoraade diɗɗe e nate e daartol, yillo [kuugal siiftorde](/activities/reminiscence/).
