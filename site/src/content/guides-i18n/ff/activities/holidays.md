Julirde ena waawi artirde ciftorɗe welɗe, tee ena waawi kadi addude hoolo, jamaa e bayle e ñalnde woowaande. Wonannde mo wonnduɗo e majjere hakkil, julirde ɓurnde moƴƴude ko heewi ko nde famɗunde, leelnde, tee mahiraande e huunde nde o waawi haa jooni weltoraade. Ɗee miijooji ena njogoo e Thanksgiving, Christmas, Hanukkah, Hitaande Hesere e mawlanaaji ɓesngu fof.

## Hol no mi feewnirta julirde ɓurnde dewtude?

Fuɗɗo e jokkondiral ñalnde mo toppitɗaa, tee feewniraa ñalnde ngal e dow mum. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/6-steps-to-make-thanksgiving-dementia-friendly/) ena wasiyaa waɗde julirde ndee e lawol subaka, sabu jiiɗgol e weltaare ɓe ngalaa fawaade no keewi ɓeydaade e kikiiɗe e jamma (woni ko wiyetee sundowning e goɗɗungol). Ñaamdu julirde subaka walla brunch ena waawi newaade e ñaamdu kikiiɗe.

- **Woppu nde fotndi.** Kawtal famɗungal, ɓurngal newaade, walla hoɓɓe ara e pelle tokoose, no keewi ena newi e taabal heewɗo yimɓe.
- **Senndu golle.** Ƴamu musidɓe yoo ɓe ngaddu ñaametee, ɓe njaha soodde walla ɓe njogondira e mo toppitɗaa tuma nde defirɗaa. Haalan no laaɓiri ko maa wallu.
- **Woppu cuɗe ɗee newii.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) ena siftina wonde annoore jalbooje ena mbaawi jiiɗnude, tee sukkara walla ɓiɓɓe leɗɗe waɗiraaɗe huutoraaɗe no cuɗagol ena mbaawi nanndeede e ñaametee.
- **Suɓo ko woppetee.** So aadaaji ena ngoni mettiniiɗi walla ena mbaawi bonnude kisal, alaa caɗeele woppude ɗum walla etaade huunde ɓurnde dewtude.

## Hol golle julirde ɗe min mbaawi waɗde e renndo?

Suɓo golle e huunde ɗe jaaɓii e ko mo toppitɗaa waawi hannde. Wonnde e nder ñalnde ngal ɓuri himmude e no ɗum yahrata.

1. **Feewnee taabal ngal e renndo.** Waɗu leppe taho, caggal ɗum leppi taabal, caggal ɗum koppe. Taggude leppi taabal ko golle moƴƴe ɗe waawaa waɗeede tuma nde jooɗɗaa.
2. **Lootaa tee senndaa leggal.** Lootaade pomme de terre walla taƴde pooruuji e taabal defirdu ko huunde kisnde, anndaande wonannde yimɓe heewɓe ɓe defiraynoo.
3. **Yilɓu, jilɓu walla feewnu.** Yilɓude soos cranberry, jilɓude deefre newiinde walla waɗde kukiiji e leppel ena jogoo juuɗe e golle, woɗɗii e maayo e laɓɓe.
4. **Ɓoɗɗu dokkal.** Taƴu kaayitaaji e teepu ado, tee woppu mo toppitɗaa yoo taggu, yoo moƴƴin. Ɓoɗɗugol laaɓtungol wonaa faandaare.
5. **Feƴƴu cuɗe seeɗa.** Suɓo cuɗe ɗe mbaawaa fercude tee nokku gooto famɗungo, ko wayi no wreath walla lekki julirde famɗuki.
6. **Waɗu golle juuɗe newiiɗe.** Calol kaayitaaji, kolaj haako walla kartal julirde wondude e tampooji ena ngolloo no moƴƴi. [Janngu amen e golle juuɗe](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) ena jogii miijooji goɗɗi.
7. **Yimee tee heɗo.** Gaaci julirde ɗi o anndii ena newi naatde heen, hay so mo toppitɗaa haalata seeɗa jooni. Yiy [janngu amen e gaaci](/activities/music/) ngam mahde doggol gaaci.
8. **Ndaaree nate julirde kadiiɗe.** Yaltin albomaaji julirde ɓennuɗe tee noddu taali («Haalanam e ngal taabal»), hara a naamnaaka hol mo woni e nder natal ngal. [Janngu amen e ciftagol](/activities/reminiscence/) ena ƴeewta hol no ɗum waɗirtee.
9. **Fijee fijo ɓesngu newiɗo.** [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) ena winndetee e hojomaaji seeɗa tee ena moƴƴi wonannde duuɓi fof, tee [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ena hokku mo woni kala huunde nde kaalta.
10. **Diŋnoree e renndo.** [Hello diŋngorɗe](/games/coloring/) newiiɗe ena mbaawi winndeede ngam sukaaɓe e mawɓe renndinde e taabal ngal.
11. **Ndaaree fiilde julirde nde o yiɗi.** Fiilde anndaande ena fowtina, tee alaa caɗeele ndaarde tan seeɗa heen.
12. **Njaha njillu juutu ngam yiyde annoore.** Njillu dewtundu e sare cuɗaaɗe ena waawi newaade e kawtal heewngal yimɓe.

So defirgol no joginoo nokku mawngu e nguurndam mo toppitɗaa, [janngu amen e golle galle no golle](/activities/household-tasks/) ena jogii laawol goɗɗo ngam waɗde golle defirdu kisɗe, welɗe.

## Hol no mi heblirta hoɓɓe?

Haalan hoɓɓe ko ɓe keɓoyta ado ɓe araade, haa teeŋti e musidɓe ɓe ngarii ko juuti. Alzheimer's Association ena wasiyaa ƴeewtude wonde bayle e ciftagol e jikku ngoni sabu ñawu nguu, wonaa mo toppitɗaa.

- **Lollin ƴoƴre newiinde seeɗa:** kaalee gooto gooto, ndokkee waktu ngam timminde miijo, tawo feewnitde walla ƴeewndo naamne, tee jaabaa e nanondiral mo toppitɗaa so konnguɗi ɗii ena metti faamde.
- **Haalan ko wallata e ko mettinata.** So daande toownde, yaawnugol walla huunde haalaande ena mettinude mo, haalan ɗum.
- **Anndin hoɓɓe ado waktu.** Hollu mo toppitɗaa nate ɓen warɓe tee lollin taali gootel walla ɗiɗi, walla feewniraa noddaango telefoŋ walla wideyoo juutngo ado ñalnde ngal.

[Janngu amen e ƴoƴre haalirde](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ena ƴeewta ɗum no ɓuri laaɓde.

## Hol ko waɗetee so ɗum ɓeydii ko metti?

Feewnu suudu dewtundu ado hoɓɓe araade, woɗɗituɗo e hoolo ngoo, wondude e huunde fowtinooje ɗe o anndii, ko wayi no dolokke walla jaket mo o yiɗi, walla golle newiɗe. Hokku ɗum e lawol, tee so sahaa fof: «Njooɗen seeɗa nokku ɓurngu dewtude?»

Yaltude taabal ngal, daanaade lawol, walla woppude feccere ñalnde ngal ko battane moƴƴo, wonaa ŋakkere. So mo toppitɗaa mettinii, daande dewtunde, yimre nde o anndii walla yahde suudu woɗndu ena keewi wallude ɓurii ƴeewtaade. [Janngu amen e weltaare ɓe ngalaa fawaade e kikiiɗe](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) ena jogii miijooji goɗɗi wonannde jammaaji.

## Hol no ɓesngu woɗɗuɓe mbaawi jeyeede heen?

Noddaango wideyoo ena addana musidɓe naatde e nder julirde ndee, tawa ɓe ɓeydaani jamaa. Alzheimer's Association ena wasiyaa rokkude noddaango ngoo feere, yeru yimde gaaci julirde, lollinde nate kawte ɓennuɗe, defirde hono gootiri ñaametee e renndo walla ndaarde fiilde julirde e waktu gooto. Woppu noddaango ngoo juuta, tee ƴamu yimɓe yoo ɓe kaala gooto gooto, sabu kaalaango renndo ena metti rewindaade. [Janngu amen e noddaango telefoŋ e wideyoo](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) ena jogii miijooji e laawol laawol.

So mo toppitɗaa no woni e suudu toppitaagu majjere hakkil, ƴeewto laawol yillagol tee ƴamu gollooɓe yoo ɓe mballu feewnude noddaango. [Janngu amen e yillaade mo woni e toppitaagu majjere hakkil](/activities/visiting/) ena haalana ko nawrataa e hol no yahrirtee no moƴƴi.

## Hol dokke jaaɓɗe wonannde mo wonnduɗo e majjere hakkil?

Dokke newiiɗe, kisɗe ɗe o waawi weltoraade jooni no keewi ɓuri moƴƴude: ñaametee mo o yiɗi, dolokke walla kardigan no weli, gaaci ɗi o anndii, haalaande hitaande e binndi mawɗi, walla albom nate ɓesngu. Alzheimer's Association ena wasiyaa woppude kuutorɗe baawɗe bonnude, kaɓirɗe elektirik caɗtuɗe e fijo njeeɓu caɗtuɗe.

Rokkude ena waawi himmude hono heɓirde. Waɗde kukiiji ɗi mo toppitɗaa walli defde e pacce, walla woppude mo yoo ɓoɗɗu dokkal ngal coodɗaa, ena addana mo jeyeede e julirde ndee. Fijo nate ɓesngu ko miijo goɗɗo: [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ena wayla nate seeɗa cuɓaaɗe e fijo nanndingol newiɗo e wanngorde.

## Toppito kadi hoore maa

Julirde ena ɓeyda golle e nder toppitagol. Woppu goɗɗo yoo jaɓɓo walla defu, wiy «alaa» e kawte baɗte satterde, tee ƴettu fowtaare tuma nde woɗɓe njogondiri e mo toppitɗaa. So an walla mo toppitɗaa sellaani, juŋngo daande iwde e laawol walla noddaango wideyoo ko yillaango moƴƴo haa laaɓi. [Janngu amen e toppitagol hoore maa no toppiroowo](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) ena jogii ballal goɗɗo.
