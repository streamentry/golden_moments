Thigũkũ no icookie maririkano mega, na no irehe inegene, andũ aingĩ na magarũrũko ma mũthenya wa o mũthenya. Kũrĩ mũndũ ũtũũraga na ũrimũ wa kũriganĩrwo, gĩathĩ kĩega nĩ kĩrĩa kĩnini, gĩtarĩ ihenya, na kĩakĩtwo ũndũ-inĩ wa maũndũ marĩa no akene nayo o na rĩu. Mawoni maya nĩmagĩrĩire mũthenya wa Thanksgiving, Christmas, Hanukkah, Mwaka Mwerũ na mũthenya wa gũciarwo wa mũciĩ.

## Nĩ atĩa ingĩhaarĩria thigũkũ iria ihooreire?

Ambĩrĩria na mũtugo wa mũndũ ũcio wa o mũthenya na ũhaarĩrie mũthenya kũringana naguo. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/6-steps-to-make-thanksgiving-dementia-friendly/) ĩtaaraga atĩ gĩathĩ gĩkorwo tene mũthenya, tondũ kũrigiicĩrwo na kũaga ũhurũko nĩkuongererekaga hwaĩ-inĩ na ũtukũ (ihinda rĩmwe rĩĩtagwo sundowning). Irio cia thigũkũ cia mĩaraho kana cia rũciinĩ no ikorwo irĩ mũhũthũ gũkĩra irio cia hwaĩ-inĩ.

- **Iga ũigana wa andũ ũtuĩke wa kwagĩrĩra.** Gĩathĩ kĩnini, kana ageni magooka o anini ihinda rĩmwe, nĩ mũhũthũ gũkĩra metha ĩiyũire andũ aingĩ.
- **Gayanai wĩra.** Ũria andũ a mũciĩ marehe irio, magũre indo kana maikare na mũndũ ũcio rĩrĩa ũkũruga. Maaria wega ũrĩa ũngĩmateithia.
- **Iga macanjani matuĩke mũhũthũ.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) yugaga atĩ matawa marĩa makũhenũka no mamũrigiicĩrie, na atĩ icamũndi kana matunda ma gũcanja no maonekwo ta irio.
- **Tua ũrĩa mũgũtiga.** Angĩkorwo mũtugo ũmwe nĩũtuĩkĩte wa thĩĩna kana wa ũgwati, nĩ wega kũũtiga kana kũgeria ũrĩa ũhooreire.

## Nĩ ciĩko irĩkũ cia thigũkũ tũngĩruta hamwe?

Thuura wĩra na maũndũ marĩa marangaine na ũrĩa mũndũ ũcio angĩhota ũmũthĩ. Gũkorwo mũthenya-inĩ ũcio nĩ kwa bata gũkĩra ũrĩa wĩra ũcio ũgaakinyĩra.

1. **Tandikai metha hamwe.** Ambĩrĩriai na thaani, mũcooke matauro ma metha, mũcoke ikombe. Gũkunya matauro ma metha nĩ wĩra mwega wa gwĩkwo mũikarĩte thĩ.
2. **Thambiai na mũgayanie mboga.** Gũthambia warũ kana gũtinia mboco metha-inĩ ya rũgĩo nĩ ũndũ mũgitĩre na wa kũmenyerana kũrĩ andũ aingĩ arĩa maarugaga tene.
3. **Korogai, tukaniai kana igai irio thaani-inĩ.** Gũkoroga mũthũri wa cranberry, gũtukania mũtu wa keke mũhũthũ kana kũiga tũbiscuit thaani-inĩ nĩkũhotithagia moko gũkorwo na wĩra matarĩ hakuhĩ na riiko kana tũhiũ.
4. **Oha kĩheo.** Tinia karatathi na tepu mbere, ũreke mũndũ ũcio akunye na anorere. Gũoha wega mũno ti kĩo kĩene.
5. **Nyiitĩrĩra macanjani manini.** Thuura macanjani matarĩ kwenjeka na handũ hanini, ta mũcanjani wa mũrango kana mũtĩ mũnini.
6. **Thondekai kĩndũ kĩororo gĩa moko.** Mĩnyororo ya karatathi, mbica ya mathangũ kana karatathi ka thigũkũ na tũtembe nĩ njega. Kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [wĩra wa moko na maũndũ ma gũthondeka](/activities/arts-and-crafts/) kĩrĩ na mawoni mangĩ.
7. **Inai na mũthikĩrĩrie.** Nyĩmbo cia thigũkũ cia kũmenyerana nĩ mũhũthũ kũingĩra, o na kũrĩ mũndũ ũrĩa ũkwaria o hanini rĩu. Rora kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [nyĩmbo](/activities/music/) nĩguo wone ũrĩa ũngĩthondeka mũtaratara wa nyĩmbo.
8. **Rorai mbica cia tene cia thigũkũ.** Ruta mabuku ma mbica ma ikeno cia tene na ũmoringĩrĩrie kwaria ng’ano („Ta njĩra ũhoro wa metha ĩno“) handũ ha kũmooria nũũ ũrĩ mbica-inĩ. Kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [kũririkana maũndũ ma tene](/activities/reminiscence/) nĩgĩtaarĩirie ũrĩa ũngĩĩka ũguo.
9. **Thakai mũthako mũhooreri wa gĩkundi.** [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) ĩhuthagwo na karatathi mĩthaa mĩnini na ĩringaine na andũ a mĩthemba yothe, nacio [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) iheanaga andũ othe ũndũ wa kwaria.
10. **Hakai rangi hamwe.** [Mabuku ma gũhaka rangi](/games/coloring/) mũhũthũ no mahuthwo na karatathi nĩguo ciana na aciari akũrũ mahake hamwe metha-inĩ.
11. **Rorai filimi ya thigũkũ ĩrĩa mwendete.** Filimi ya kũmenyerana nĩ ya kũhuurũkia, na nĩ wega kũmĩrora o gĩcunjĩ.
12. **Thiĩi na ngaari handũ hanini kuona matawa.** Gũthiĩ kahora na ngaari gũkĩhĩtũka nyũmba iria icanjĩtio no gũkorwo kũrĩ mũhũthũ gũkĩra gĩathĩ kĩrĩ na andũ aingĩ.

Angĩkorwo kũruga kwarĩ kũndũ kũnene mũtũũrĩre-inĩ wa mũndũ ũcio, kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [wĩra wa mũciĩ ta ciĩko](/activities/household-tasks/) kĩrĩ na njĩra ingĩ cia gũtũma wĩra wa rũgĩo ũtuĩke mũgitĩre na wa gũkenia.

## Nĩ atĩa ingĩhaarĩria ageni?

Ĩra ageni maũndũ marĩa mangĩona mbere ya moke, makĩria andũ a mũciĩ arĩa matanooka gwa ihinda iraaya. Alzheimer's Association ĩtaaraga atĩ mũmeere atĩ magarũrũko ma kũririkana na mũtugo moimaga kũrĩ ũrimũ ũcio, ti kũrĩ mũndũ ũcio.

- **Gayanai mataaro manini mũhũthũ:** kwaria mũndũ ũmwe ihinda rĩmwe, kũheana ihinda rĩa kũrĩkia rĩciiria, gũtikaamũrũngie kana kũmũũria ciũria ta cia kĩgerio, na kũrũmbũiya mũtugo wa mũndũ ũcio ciugo ciarĩa hinya gũtaũkĩrwo.
- **Uga ũrĩa ũteithagia na ũrĩa ũthĩĩnagia.** Angĩkorwo mĩgambo mĩnene, kũhiũhio kana ciugo imwe nĩirehaga thĩĩna, uga ũguo.
- **Menyithania ageni mbere ya ihinda.** Onia mũndũ ũcio mbica cia arĩa marooka na ũmũhe ng’ano imwe kana igĩrĩ, kana mũhĩtũkie kũhoya gĩcunjĩ kĩnini na thimũ mbere ya mũthenya ũcio.

Kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [mataaro ma kwaria](/caregiving/communication-tips/) nĩkĩrĩ na ũhoro makĩria.

## Atĩa angĩkorwo nĩkũritũhĩtie mũno?

Haarĩria nyũmba nyinyi ya kũhuurũkĩra mbere ya ageni gũkinya, haraihu na inegene, ĩrĩ na indo cia kũhooreria iria amenyereire ta burangeti ya kwendwo, nguo njega kana kĩndũ kĩororo gĩa gwĩka. Mũhe ũhoro ũcio tene na kaingĩ: „Tũgũthiĩ gũikara handũ hatheru kahora hanini?“

Kuuma metha-inĩ, gũthiĩ toro tene kana gũtiga gĩcunjĩ kĩa mũthenya nĩ mũthia mwega, ti kwaga kũhota. Mũndũ ũcio angĩkorwo nĩathĩĩnĩkĩte, mũgambo mũhooreri, rwĩmbo rwa kũmenyerana kana gũthiĩ nyũmba ĩngĩ no imũteithie gũkĩra kũgeria kũmũtaarĩria. Kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [kũaga ũhurũko hwaĩ-inĩ](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) kĩrĩ na mawoni mangĩ ma hwaĩ-inĩ.

## Andũ a mũciĩ arĩa marĩ kũraya mangĩingĩra atĩa?

Kũhoya na thimũ ya mbica nĩkũhotithagia andũ a mũciĩ kũnyitanĩra hatarĩ kuongerera kĩrĩndĩ. Alzheimer's Association ĩtaaraga atĩ mũige mũtaratara wa mahoya macio, kwa ngerekano kũina nyĩmbo cia thigũkũ, kũgayanĩra mbica cia ikeno cia tene, kũruga irio o ro rĩmwe hamwe kana kũrora filimi ya thigũkũ ihinda o rĩmwe. Iga mahoya macio matuĩke makuhĩ, na ũũrie andũ maarie mũndũ ũmwe ihinda rĩmwe, tondũ kwaria andũ aingĩ o hamwe nĩ hinya gũtaũkĩrwo. Kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [kũhoya na thimũ kana mbica](/activities/phone-and-video-calls/) kĩrĩ na mawoni ma gĩcunjĩ kĩmwe kĩmwe.

Mũndũ ũcio angĩkorwo atũũraga handũ ha kũmenyagĩrĩrwo andũ marĩ na ũrimũ wa kũriganĩrwo, ũria mĩtaratara ya gũceera na ũhooe aruti a wĩra makũteithie kũhaarĩria kũhoya. Kĩonereria giitų kĩa [gũceera mũndũ ũrĩ handũ ha kũmenyagĩrĩrwo](/activities/visiting/) nĩkĩarĩrĩirie ũrĩa ũngĩthiĩ nakio na ũrĩa ũngĩtigana wega.

## Nĩ iheo irĩkũ iringaine na mũndũ ũrĩ na ũrimũ wa kũriganĩrwo?

Iheo irĩ mũhũthũ na igitĩre iria angĩkenagĩra rĩu nĩcio njega: kĩndũ kĩega gĩa kũrĩa, burangeti njoroku kana nguo ya guoko, nyĩmbo cia kũmenyerana, karenda ya ndemwa nene, kana ibuku rĩa mbica cia mũciĩ. Alzheimer's Association ĩtaaraga atĩ mũtigetie indo cia wĩra irĩ ũgwati, indo cia thĩinwa irĩ hinya, na methako ya mbaara mĩritũ.

Kũheana no gũkorwo kũrĩ kwa bata o ta kwamũkĩra. Kũiga tũbiscuit tũrĩa mũndũ ũcio aateithirie kũruga, kana kũmũreka oohe kĩheo kĩrĩa wagũrire, nĩkũmũhotithagia gũkorwo thigũkũ-inĩ. Mũthako wa mbica cia mũciĩ nĩ woni ũngĩ: [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ĩgarũraga mbica nini iria ithuurĩtwo ĩgatuĩka mũthako mũhooreri wa kũringania mbica thĩinĩ wa mũcari.

## Wĩmenyagĩrĩre wee mwene o nakuo

Thigũkũ nĩciongereraga wĩra wa kũmenyagĩrĩra mũndũ. Reke mũndũ ũngĩ anyite andũ ageni kana aruge nyama ya thigũkũ, ũkaane maũndũ marĩa makoragwo marĩ maritũ, na ũhuurũke rĩrĩa andũ angĩ mekũra na mũndũ ũcio. Wee kana mũndũ ũrĩa ũmenyagĩrĩra mũngĩkorwo mũtiagĩrĩire, kũoya guoko kuuma mũkĩra-inĩ wa njĩra kana kũhoya na thimũ nĩ njĩra njega cia gũceerana. Kĩonereria giitũ kĩa [kwĩmenyagĩrĩra kwa mũmenyagĩrĩri](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) kĩrĩ na ũteithio ũngĩ.
