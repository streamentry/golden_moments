Dɔgɔ ni hakili ka ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn fɛ kosɛbɛ, nka u ɲɔgɔn fɛ cogo min na, o bɛ kɛ fɛn min ka ɲɛnɛ bɛ ka dɔn sisan. Sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔw b'a fɔ ko dɔgɔ gɛlɛn b'a dɛmɛ ka dementia ka sɔrɔ ka kan. Dementia kɔrɔ, a b'a dɔgɔya ka kan kosɛbɛ. Nin kuma b'a jira sɛgɛsɛgɛ min b'a fɔ ani min tɛ a fɔ, b'a kɔnɔ dɔgɔ kɛcogo ɲumanw, ani dɔgɔ kɔlɔsili ɲumanw min kɛ dementia kɔnɔ kosɛbɛ. A tɛ dawa ka kuma dɔgɔ dɔgɔmabɔ kan; o kuma ka kɛ dɔkitɔrɔ ye.

## Dɔgɔ gɛlɛn b'a kɛ dementia ye wa?

Sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔw b'a fɔ ko dɔgɔ gɛlɛn waati jan kɔnɔ b'a dɛmɛ ka dementia ka sɔrɔ ka kan, nka sɛgɛsɛgɛ ma sɔrɔ ka ɲɛnɛ sisan.

- **Sɛgɛsɛgɛ min ye san 25 ye UK.** Mɛɛnw ye mɔgɔ ~8,000 ɲɛfɛ [Whitehall II sɛgɛsɛgɛ](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22354-2) kɔnɔ. Mɔgɔ min ye dɔgɔ ka se 6 kɔnɔ san 50 walima 60 kɔnɔ, o ye dementia ka sɔrɔ ka kan ka bon mɔgɔ min ye dɔgɔ ka se 7 kɔnɔ. Mɔgɔ min ye dɔgɔ ka se 6 kɔnɔ san 50, 60 ni 70 kɔnɔ, o ye dementia ka sɔrɔ ka kan ka bon mɔgɔ min ye dɔgɔ ka se 7 kɔnɔ tile bɛɛ, a ka kɛ 30% ka bon. O ye sɛgɛsɛgɛ ye min b'a jira ko fɛn ɲɔgɔn b'a fɛ, nka a ma jira ko o ye sababu ye.
- **Lancet Commission ka hakilina.** [2024 Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0) ma dɔgɔ sɔrɔ a ka fɛn 14 min b'a fɛ ka kɛcogo kɛ, ani o ma ɲɔgɔn fɛ. A y'a fɔ ko sɛgɛsɛgɛ ma sɔrɔ ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ sisan. Sababu dɔ ye "reverse causation" ye: dementia ka hakili yɛrɛw b'a daminɛ san caman sani dɔkitɔrɔ ka o dɔn, ani o b'a dɔgɔya ka kan. Sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔ kɔnɔ, dɔgɔ jan ni dementia ɲɔgɔn fɛ, o tɛna sɔrɔ tuma min na, a ye san fɔlɔ 5 bɛɛ bɔ sɛgɛsɛgɛ kɔnɔ.

Dɔgɔ gɛlɛn dɔ tile kelen-fɛɛ, o tɛ hakɛ ye. Dɔgɔ ɲɛnɛ, ka ɲɛfɛ tile bɛɛ, o ka ɲɛ kosɛbɛ, bɔnɛ ni ka ɲɛfɛ ka bɔ ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ bi ni sini, o ka ɲɛ.

## "Hakili ka sɔsɔli" dɔgɔ sɛgɛsɛgɛ ye mun ye?

Aw b'a fɛ ka kan ko dɔgɔ b'a "sɔsɔ" hakili. O ko kɔrɔ ka bɔ sɛgɛsɛgɛ ɲɛfɛ, nka o ka fɔlɔ ye.

- **Jiri (mice) kɔnɔ.** Sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔ san 2013 [NIH ka fɔ](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/brain-may-flush-out-toxins-during-sleep) ye ko hakili ka sɛnɛ kɔnɔ cɛ ka kɛ ~60% ka bon, waati min na jiri dɔgɔ. Ji b'a taa hakili kɔnɔ ka ɲɛ, ni sɛgɛsɛgɛkɛlaw b'a wele glymphatic system. Beta-amyloid, protein min bɛ Alzheimer ni ka ɲɔgɔn fɛ, o bɔra jiri dɔgɔlenw hakili kɔnɔ ka tɛmɛ.
- **Mɔgɔw kɔnɔ.** Sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔgɔman [2018](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1721694115) ye mɔgɔ 20 ye min ka se 22 ani 72 ye, mɔgɔ fɛɛrɛ tɛ dɔgɔ ka kɛ. Kɛ min ye dɔgɔ ka kɛ, o kɔ, a ye hakili ka scan kɛ. Dɔgɔ tɛ kɛ kɔ, beta-amyloid signal b'a ka bon ~5% hakili ka sɛnɛ dɔw kɔnɔ, o kɔnɔ hippocampus, min ye hakili ka sɛnɛ ye min bɛ hakili ka kɔnɔ. Sɛgɛsɛgɛkɛlaw y'o fɔ ka ɲɛnɛ fɔlɔ ye. Olu ma dɔn ko o fɛn tɛna bɔ ni sɛgɛ, walima ko o ye amyloid ka dɔgɔya ye walima ka caman dɔgɔ.
- **A b'a bɛ cogo fila la.** Lancet Commission b'a fɔ ko amyloid ka caman b'a kɛ dɔgɔ dɔgɔmabɔ ye mɔgɔ kɔrɔw ye.

Sɛgɛsɛgɛ nin ɲɛfɛ b'a dɛmɛ ka dɔn mun kosɔn sɛgɛsɛgɛkɛlaw b'a fɛ dɔgɔ kan. Olu tɛ jira ko dɔgɔ ɲuman b'a dɛmɛ ka Alzheimer ɲɛ.

## Dɔgɔ kɛcogo jumanw b'a dɛmɛ wa?

Mɔgɔ kɔrɔw b'a fɛ dɔgɔ ɲɛfɛ 7 ka se 9 tile kelen-kelen, i n'a fɔ mɔgɔ wɛrɛw, National Institute on Aging (NIA) [ka fɔ](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/sleep/sleep-and-older-adults) la. A ka fɛn min b'a fɛ, o ye nin ye:

- **Sɔrɔ waati kɔnɔ.** Sɔgɔsɔgɔ kɔnɔ ka kɛ ka sɔrɔ ka wuli waati kelen kɔnɔ tile bɛɛ, ko don siyɛn kɔnɔ fana.
- **Ka waati ɲɛnɛ dɔgɔ ka kɛ ka ɲɛ.** Ka kalan, ka mɛlɛ fɛn ɲuman, walima ka dibi ni ji ɲuman ka kɔ dɔgɔ kɔ.
- **Ka kɔnɔ kɛ ɲɛfɛ**, ka dɛ ni ka ɲɛ, ka kɛ ka dɔgɔ ni ka fuu.
- **Ka caman dɔgɔ kɛ tile kɔnɔ.** Kafe, ji ni kolanut, soda, ni cocolat b'a kɛ ka hakili ka sɔsɔ.
- **Ka kɔlɔsi dɔgɔ**, ko tɔ ni ka caman ka kɔ, NIA b'a fɔ ko a ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ.
- **Ka caman lafiɲɛ waati kɔnɔ dɔgɔ ka kɛ dɔgɔ kɔ sani 2 walima 3.** Ka ji caman min ka kɔ tile kɔnɔ, o fana ka kɛ dɔgɔ kɔ.
- **Ka caman kɔrɔya ka kɛ, nka ka kɛ sani waati kɔnɔ dɔgɔ kɔ 3 kɔnɔ.** Aw ka kɛ [kɔrɔya gɛlɛn](/brain-health/exercise/) ka kɛ.
- **Ka ekrɛn bɔ dɔgɔ kɔnɔ.** NIA b'a fɔ ko aw kana TV ye, kɔmpiyuutɛri, telefɔn walima tablet kɔnɔ dɔgɔ kɔnɔ.
- **Ka kɔlɔsi bɔnɛ fɔ sani o bɛ kɛ ka bɔ, walima ka sɔrɔ bɔnɛ kɔfɛ, ni aw b'a sɛbɛn.**

## Dɔgɔ gɛlɛya dementia kɔnɔ

Dɔgɔ kɔnɔ fɛɛrɛ b'a yɛrɛ kɛ dementia kɔnɔ kosɛbɛ. [NIA ka sɛbɛnnin](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/sleep/managing-sleep-problems-alzheimers-disease) b'a jira dɔgɔ caman walima dɔgɔ ntɛmɛ, ni ka wuli caman dɔgɔ kɔnɔ, ani ka dɔgɔ caman tile kɔnɔ. A b'a jira fana "sundowning": jiginni, sɔsɔli walima hakili ka ɲɛnɛ min bɛ daminɛ walima ka caman kɛ ni sɛgɛ ka dɔgɔya.

NIA ka kɛlɛnnikɛlaw ka ɲɛnɛ dɔw nin ye:

1. **Ka sɛgɛ ni ka ɲɛkɛ ka daminɛ.** Tile kɔnɔ, ka bɔ walima ka sɔrɔ fɛɛrɛ kɔnɔ. Ka mɔgɔ ka kɛ dɔgɔ ka kɛ ka kɛ tile bɛɛ. Sɛgɛ ka ɲɛ b'a dɛmɛ ka sɛgɛ wagati ɲɛnɛ.
2. **Ka baara fɛɛrɛ ɲuman waati sɔgɔsɔgɔ kɔnɔ.** Ka ɲɔgɔn sɔrɔ sɔgɔsɔgɔ kɔnɔ walima ka dumuni caman ka kɛ tile kɛ kɔnɔ.
3. **Ka dɔgɔ dɔgɔmabɔ kɛ dɔgɔ ni kɔnɔ.** Ka dɔgɔ dɔgɔmabɔ tile kɔfɛ kɔnɔ.
4. **Ka sɔgɔsɔgɔ kɛ dɔgɔ.** Ka fitinɛ dɔgɔya, ka kɔnɔ ka dɔgɔ kɔnɔ, ani ka dɔgɔ kɛ ka sɔrɔ ka dɔgɔ ni ka fuu, ni mɔgɔ b'a fɛ. [Late-day restlessness](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) kuma b'a dɛmɛ ka hakilina wɛrɛ dɔn.
5. **Ka dɔgɔ kɔrɔya sɔrɔ ka kɛ ka sɛgɛ.** Dɔgɔ ka kɛ ka kalan kɛ tile kɔnɔ tile bɛɛ waati kelen kɔnɔ, o ye NIA ka misali ye. [Tile bɛɛ baara](/activities/daily-routine/) fana b'a dɛmɛ.
6. **Ka kɔlɔsi dɔgɔ ni ka dɔgɔ caman kɔ.**
7. **Ka fitila dɔgɔ dɔgɔmabɔ kɛ** dɔgɔ kɔnɔ, kɔnɔ ni kɔnɔ kɔrɔ ni ji kɔnɔ.

Tile kɔnɔ ka wuli, NIA ka [ka so kɔnɔ ɲɛmajɔ ɲɛnɛ](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/alzheimers-caregiving-home-safety-tips) b'a fɔ ko fitila walima sensor, ji kɔnɔ kɔnɔ tɛɲɛ, kɔnɔ kɔrɔ sukuru ka ɲɛ kɔnɔ, ani dawa ka kɛ dɔgɔ bɛ kɔnɔ kɔnɔ min bɛ kɔlɔsi.

Dɔgɔ kɔnɔ kɛlɛn ni kɛlɛ kɔnɔ b'a dɛmɛ ka ɲɔgɔn ɲɛnɛ. Ka [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ka ɲɛ kɛ, walima ka [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ɲɛnɛ kɛ, o b'a dɛmɛ. Ka fɛn min ka sɔsɔ dɔgɔ kɔrɔ ka kɛ, ani ka bɔ ni mɔgɔ ye ko a ka ɲɛfɛ.

Dɔgɔ gɛlɛn b'a kɛ kɛlɛnnikɛla ka kɔ kɛ fana. [Caregiver self-care guide](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) b'a jira kɛcogo min na ka dɔgɔ ni dɛmɛ sɔrɔ.

## Sleep apnea kɔ cogo di?

Sleep apnea b'a kɛ ka ɲɛfɛ dɔgɔ kɔnɔ ka sɛgɛ fɛ ka ɲɛnɛ, caman tile kɔnɔ. NIA b'a fɔ ko a tɛ ɲɛfɛ, o b'a kɛ ka tɔ dan (high blood pressure), kɛnɛ ka dɛ walima hakili ka kɛ kɔnɔ. Dɔgɔ kɔnɔ kɛwale: ka caman sɔnɔ ni ka sɛgɛ tile kɔnɔ kɛ kɔnɔ. Mɔgɔ caman tɛ dɔn ko o bɛ kɛ ka. Lancet Commission b'a jira sɛgɛsɛgɛ 11 kɔnɔ ko mɔgɔ min bɛ sleep apnea la, o ka dementia ka sɔrɔ ka kan ka bon ~43%. Nka sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔw tɛ ka kilo ɲɛfɛ, o kosɔn ɲɔgɔn ma jiginni.

Sleep apnea bɛ dɔn ka ɲɛnɛ ka dɔgɔ ka kɛ. NIA b'a fɔ ko CPAP masin b'a dɛmɛ kosɛbɛ. Ni aw walima mɔgɔ min bɛ aw ka dɛsɛ ka caman sɔnɔ, ka dɔgɔ kɔnɔ ka sɛgɛ, walima ka tile kɔnɔ ka caman dɔgɔ, ka o fɔ dɔkitɔrɔ ye.

## Dɔgɔ dawa kɔnɔ kuma

An tɛ dɔgɔ dawa walima dɔgɔ nɛɛnɛ kɔnɔ ɲɛnɛ di. NIA b'a fɔ ko dɔgɔ dawa b'a dɛmɛ waati dogoman kɔnɔ, nka o tɛ insomnia kɛ. A b'a fɔ fana ko dawa dɔw min ye mɔgɔ min bɛ Alzheimer la dɔgɔ kɛ, o b'a kɛ dɔgɔ dɔgɔmabɔ, jɛ, ani hakili ka kɛ ɲɛ. Aw kana dɔgɔ dawa kɛ, bɔ, walima kɛ, dawa tɛ dɔkitɔrɔ walima farmasi kɔ, o bɛɛ ye dɔgɔ dawa ye, o bɛɛ ye over-the-counter dɔgɔ dawa ye.

## Dɔkitɔrɔ ni kuma

Dɔkitɔrɔ ye ko aw walima mɔgɔ min bɛ aw ka dɛsɛ, ko a bɛ ka dɔgɔ bɛɛ, walima a bɛ dɔgɔ caman kɔ kɔnɔ, walima a bɛ sɔnɔ ka kɛ ka dɔgɔ kɔnɔ, o bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛnɛ dɔkitɔrɔ kɔ. Ni dɔgɔ gɛlɛya dementia kɔnɔ tɛmɛ, [NIA ka ɲɛnɛ](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/tips-coping-sundowning) b'a fɔ ko aw ka dɔkitɔrɔ ka ɲɛnɛ ɲini. Dɔkitɔrɔ b'a ɲɛ ka sababu dɔw dɔn, i n'a fɔ kɔrɔya, dɔgɔ gɛlɛya wɛrɛ, bana, walima dawa ka kɛwale. Hakili ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ sɔrɔ, walima dɔgɔ ka kɛ ka hakili ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ, o ka kɛ ka sɛgɛ fɛ ka ɲɛnɛ. [When to see a doctor](/brain-health/warning-signs/) ka sɛbɛn ka ɲɛnɛ.

Nin sɛbɛn ye kuma gɛlɛn ye, tɛ dɔkitɔrɔ ka ɲɛnɛ ye. A bɛ kɛ [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/) ka ɲɛ.
