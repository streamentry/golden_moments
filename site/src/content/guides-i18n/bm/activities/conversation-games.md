Mɔgɔ caman bɛ o ko ka kɛlɛ dɔ ɲini min bɛ kɛ kuma ye, dɔɔnin tɛ. Kumakan 20 ni kɛlɛ wɛrɛw bɛ se ka kɛ ɲɛ, bawo kɛlɛ ka sariyaw ka fitinin, ani kuma bɛ se ka taa fɛ o fɛ. Dɔ ye ko i ka kɛlɛ ka kɔrɔ ka ɲɛ: mɔgɔ bɛ jaabi dɔ hakilina, ani i bɛ kumakan ɲini. Nin tɛmɛsiran bɛ ɲini cogo min na ka kɛlɛ nin ye siranin ni hɛrɛ kɛ, ani kuma kɛlɛ minnu bɛ ɲɛ dementia bɛ mɔgɔw fɛ.

## Mun na ka kɛlɛ ka sariyaw kɔrɔ?

Kɛlɛ bɛɛ kɔnɔ, mɔgɔ dɔ bɛ ɲini, ani mɔgɔ wɛrɛ bɛ dɔn ka jaabi. O bɛ kɛ dementia bɛ mɔgɔ ye ka bɔ ɲɛfɛ, ani o bɛ se ka kɛ dusu ɲɛsin. Ni mɔgɔ ye kɛlɛ ɲini ye, ani i ye kumakan ɲini, o bɛ kɛ mɔgɔ ye min ka kɔnɔ. A bɛ hakilina tanga, a bɛ ɲini ka jaabi ‘ɔn’ walima ‘ayi’, ani a bɛ ɲɛ i ka ɲini ye.

[Amerika Jamana Sɛbɛnnin Jɛkulu ka Ɲɛmɔgɔya Jɛkulu](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/communicating-someone-who-has-alzheimers) bɛ kɛlɛ ka ɲɛnamaya bɛ mɔgɔ ta ka fɛ ka kuma gɛlɛya, ka kuma kɛlɛ ɲɛnama ni kuma kelen, ani ka kuma hɛrɛ. O ko bɛ ɲɛ kumakan kɛlɛw ye.

## Kumakan 20 ka kɛcogo, siranin ni hɛrɛ

1. **Kɛlɛ ta sugandi** mɔgɔ ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ, i ko dugu, sɛgɛ, bagan walima kɔgɔ kɔnɔ.
2. **Mɔgɔ ka hakilina ka dɔ ɲini**, ani a ka fɔ dusu, walima a ka kaadi dɔ jɛ, i kana kuma o kuma ɲɛnama.
3. **Kumakan dɔ kelen ‘ɔn’ walima ‘ayi’ ye.** “O ye dɔ ye min bɛ dumuni ye wa?” “O bɛ bɔ ka bon ka tɛmɛ sapatu ma wa?”
4. **Jaabi bɛɛ ɲɛ.** Ɲɛ kɔrɔ, ka kɛ ka ɲɛ, walima ka sɛgɛ, o bɛɛ bɛ kɛ jaabi ye.
5. **Ka jɛnsɛnw ɲini kuma kɔrɔ** ani ka dusu ka. Ni i ye jɛnsɛn ɲɛ, ka nɔgɔn ka bon.
6. **Ka dɔgɔ dɔ di** ni mɔgɔ bɛ tuma minnu na ka fɛ: “O ye dugu kɔnɔ dɔ ye.”
7. **Kumakan hakilina kana ka** ani kana ka hakilina kɛ.

Ni mɔgɔ tɛ se ka kɛlɛ ka sariyaw kɔnɔ ka taa, i ka kɛlɛ dɔgɔ kɛ: i bɛ tɔw ɲini ka mɔgɔ ye ka ɲɛfɛ, ani a bɛ dɔ ɲɛfɛ min tɛ.

## Mun ye ɲɛ sugandi

- Ɲɛnɔgɔn walima ɲɛ nɔnɔ, o ye kɛlɛ ye
- Dɔnnin sɛgɛ, sɔrɔ walima sɔrɔ ka ɲɛfɛ
- Fini dɔ, i ko kulu, kongɔ walima sɛtɛ kulu
- Gɛrɛ walima dugu fɛ min bɛ dɔn ka ɲɛ
- Sɛgɛ fɛ min mɔgɔ bɛ se ka sɛgɛ
- Ɲɛ min bɛ fini dɔ ye

Mɔgɔ tɔw tɔgɔ, dusu ka ɲɛ, ani dɔ min bɛ se ka ɲɛ walima ka yɛrɛ jɔ, o ka kɛ ɲɛ.

## Kuma kɛlɛ wɛrɛw

- **Nin walima o.** I ka sugandi fila fɛ, ka ɲini mun ye dɔ ye ka fɛ: sɛn walima dɛgɛ, kulu walima ko, nɔnɔ ni fini. Jaabi ɲɛ tɛ.
- **Ni i bɛ se ka sugandi.** Kuma ɲɛnama gɛlɛya, i ko: “I bɛ se ka sugandi ka tɛmɛ kumasi ma wa?”
- **Kuma ka sɛgɛ ka kɛlɛ.** Kuma dɔ bɛ kɛ kuma tɔgɔ ye, i bɛ ɲɛ ka kɛ kuma ka sɛgɛ ye, ani i bɛ kuma ka keleya ɲini. Kɛlɛ ɲɛ ka mɔgɔ ka kɛlɛ kɔnɔ [ɲɛnabɔ kɛlɛw](/activities/finish-the-saying/) la.
- **Tɔgɔ ci sɛgɛ.** I ka mɔgɔ ka ɲɛfɛ, ka sɛgɛ, ka sɛgɛ ɲɛnama, ani i ka a ka ɲini ka ɲɛfɛ.
- **Kuma kaadiw.** Golden Moments ka [kuma kaadiw](/games/conversation-cards/) bɛ kuma ɲini kɛlɛ kɔrɔ, minnu bɛ se ka bɔ tabali kɔnɔ.

Hakilina ka kɛlɛ caman ɲini ka kɛ ɲɛnamaya kɔrɔ ni ɲɛnamaya ka tɛmɛsiran ye, [hakilina ɲini kumakanw](/activities/reminiscence-questions/) la. Kɛlɛ kɔrɔ, ni i bɛ kɛ ɲɛ dɔ ye ka kɛ ka ɲɛ dusu ka, [dementia bɛ mɔgɔw fɛ kuma ni kɛlɛ](/activities/trivia/) bɛ kɛ, o bɛ sugandi ni kɛ, ani dɔ tɛ jaabi ka ɲɛ.

## Ni mɔgɔ bɛ dusu ka ɲɛ, mun na?

Kɛlɛ bɔ, i ka ɲɛ, ka fɔ ko, “An ka kɛlɛ ɲɛ wɛrɛ.” Dusu ka ɲɛ bɛ ko ka kɛlɛ yɔrɔ bɔ, ka kɛlɛ ka kuma bɔ, a tɛ ko ka tɛmɛ. Ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ, [mɔgɔ dementia ka fɛ ka kɛlɛ kɛ cogo min na](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) bɛ kuma sariyaw ka kɔrɔ ka ɲɛ.

## Kɛlɛ bɛ mun kɛ, ani bɛ mun tɛ kɛ

Kuma kɛlɛw bɛ ɲɛ ani bɛ mɔgɔ ɲɛnabɔ. Olu bɛ se ka mɔgɔw ka ɲɛnamaya ka kɛ ɲɛ, ani ka kuma. Olu tɛ dementia ka ɲɛ ni a tɛ kɛ dɔgɔ. Ka kɛlɛ ka kɔrɔ, i ka hakilina nɔfɛ: siranin ni hɛrɛ kɛ ɲɛnamaya kɔrɔ.
