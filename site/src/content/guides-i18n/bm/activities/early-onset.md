Dementia kuma caman bɛ dɔ kɛ ko mɔgɔ min bɛ dementia la, a ye mɔgɔ ye min bɛ kɔrɔ ni mɔgɔ 70 ni 80 ye. Mɔgɔ min bɛ dementia la a ka 40 ni 50 ka kɔnɔ, a ka ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ ye fɛn wɛrɛ ye. A bɛ se ka kɛ kɛlɛbolo, a bɛ se ka kɛ sɛnɛ ni a bɛ se ka kɛ denw ka fɛ fana. A ka kɛlɛw ka kɛ ka ɲɛ ni mɔgɔ fɔlɔ ko ye. Nin sɛbɛn bɛ fɔ ko jumɛn bɛ kɛ, ani dɛmɛ min bɛ sɔrɔ sigiɲɔgɔnw ni denw ye.

## Dementia min bɛ fɔlɔ ni 65 ka tɛ, o ye jɔn ye?

Dementia min bɛ fɔlɔ ni 65 ka tɛ, o ye dementia ye min bɛ fɔlɔ ni mɔgɔ ye 65 ka tɛ, o bɛ wele ko "younger-onset". [Alzheimer's Association ka blog](https://www.alzheimersblog.org/2023/11/17/younger-onset-dementia-caregivers-share-the-importance-of-support-groups/) b'a fɔ ko Alzheimer's bɛ kɛ younger-onset ni a bɛ mɔgɔ kɛ 65 ka tɛ, ani a b'a fɔ ko mɔgɔ min bɛ dɛmɛ kɛ o cogo la, o bɛ "ko caman ye min bɛ kɛ gɛlɛya". Kɔnɔfɛn minnu bɛ kɛ, olu bɛ kɛ i n'a fɔ ko mɔgɔ fɔlɔ kɔnɔ fana, nka mɔgɔ ka miiri, a ka kɛlɛw ani a ka sigida ma kɛ i n'a fɔ ko kɔfɛ.

A bɛ kɛ ko mɔgɔ dɔw bɛ tɛmɛ ka tɛmɛ ka nɔ ​​bɔ ka dementia ka fɔlɔ dɔn, ani ɲɛmɔgɔw bɛ hakili to ko u tɛ mɔgɔ si ye min bɛ se ka kuma ni a ye. Ko mɔgɔ dɔw bɛ dɔn ko mɔgɔ wɛrɛw fana bɛ nin kɛlɛ kɛ, o bɛ dɛmɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ.

## Mɔgɔ ka kɛlɛ ka kɛ ka ɲɛ

Mɔgɔ minnu bɛ dementia la a ka 40 ni 50 ka kɔnɔ, u tɛ fɛ ka kɛ denw ye. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) b'a fɔ ko kɛlɛ ka sugandi ka kɛ a ka sɛgɛsɛgɛli ni a bɛ se ka kɛ ani a bɛ fɛ. Mɔgɔ min bɛ 50 kɔnɔ, o bɛ se ka kɛ nin kɛlɛw ye:

- **Kɛ a ka kɛlɛ fɛnw ye min bɛ a fɛ,** i n'a fɔ dɔgɔkɛ, sɔrɔ, karita, kitabu tɛmɛsira, walima dumuni, ni dɛmɛ dɔ bɛ se ka kɛ walima waati dɔgɔ dɔ.
- **Taa ni sigiɲɔgɔn dɔ ye, walima dɔgɔ ni wulu,** sira min a dɔnna kosɛbɛ.
- **Kɛ dumuni ka ɲɛmɔgɔya ye,** mɔgɔ bɛ kɛ min bɛ se ka kɛ.
- **Dɛmɛ ka kɛ walima kɛ kɛlɛ min bɛ a fɛ,** i n'a fɔ ka sɔrɔ ni ka ɲɛkɛlɛ kɛ kɔnɔ tɛmɛnen.
- **Taa yɔrɔ min bɛ a ka kɔrɔ,** ni sigiɲɔgɔn ni waati dɔgɔ dɔ.
- **Tɛmɛ ni a ka denmisɛnw, a ka balikukalanw walima a ka denmisɛnw, o bɛ kɛ ka o ni o ka kɛlɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ.**

Mɔgɔ ka sugandi ka kɛ. "I bɛ fɛ ka kɛ jɛɲɔgɔn ni wulu ye wa?" o bɛ ka ɲɛ ni "I ka kɛlɛ sugandi kɛ" ye.

## Musiki ni tɛmɛsiranw min bɛ a ka fɔlɔ kɔnɔ

Musiki ni tɛmɛsiranw min bɛ mɔgɔ ka 20, 30 ni 40 kɔnɔ, olu bɛ hakilina ɲɛnɛ ka kɛ ani kuma ka fɛ. [Musiki kɛlɛw](/activities/music/) bɛ a fɔ ka kɛlɛ ka kɛ playlist kɛlɛ ye, ani [tɛmɛsira kɛlɛw](/activities/reminiscence/) bɛ a fɔ ka tɛmɛsiranw ni kumaw ye ni ko a tɛ nin ye. Ni aw bɛ fɛ ka kɛlɛ dɔ kɛ min bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ mɔgɔ kɔrɔ ye, [sigida kɛlɛw](/games/conversation-cards/) ye, minnu bɛ ka kuma kɛ kɛlɛ, taamasiɲɔgɔn, sigiɲɔgɔn ni ɲɛmɔgɔw ko.

Sɛbɛnnin ni tɛmɛsiranw min bɛ sɔrɔ ni ɲɛmɔgɔw ni denw ka tɛmɛsiranw ye, olu fana bɛ kɛ ɲɛ. Ka tɛmɛsiranw ka kɛ fɛnw minnu bɛ mɔgɔ ni yɔrɔw ko min bɛ a ka kɔrɔ, o ye ɲɛ ye. [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) bɛ tɛmɛsiranw ka maran browser kɔnɔ ni o dɔ la.

## Kɛlɛ, tilɛ ni ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ kɛlɛw

Mɔgɔ caman min bɛ dementia la fɔlɔ ni 65 ka tɛ, u bɛ kɛlɛ kɛ, tilɛ kɛ, walima wari makɔnɔ. Nin tɛ kɛlɛ ye i n'a fɔ kɛlɛ ka kɔnɔ, nka u bɛ mɔgɔ ka se ka fɛn minnu kɛ, o bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ. Aw ka kuma ni a ka dokotoro ka tilɛ ni kɛlɛ kɔnɔ ka ɲɛnamaya, ani aw bɛ se ka miiri ko a bɛ fɛ ka dɛmɛ sɔrɔ wari walima sigiɲɔgɔnw ka kɛlɛ la. Sɛgɛsɛgɛli ye ka mɔgɔ ka dɔnkili maran, ka a to a ka kɛlɛ kɔnɔ a bɛ fɛ ka kɛ.

Ka dɔn ka tɛmɛ ni ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ ni ka ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ ka sigida ka kɛ, aw bɛ se ka taa [ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ ka sigida dɔgɔ](/activities/daily-routine/) ni [so kɔnɔ kɛlɛw kɛlɛ ye](/activities/household-tasks/).

## Ɲɛmɔgɔw ni denw ka dɛmɛ

Ɲɛmɔgɔw ni denw bɛ hakili to ko u bɛ kɛ dɔgɔ. Dementia la kɛlɛ bɛ kɛ kɛlɛ ye ka tɛmɛ kɛlɛ, ka ɲɛmɔgɔ ni sigida dɔ bɛ kɛ kɛlɛ kɔnɔ. Alzheimer's Association ka blog min b'a fɔ ko younger-onset sigiɲɔgɔnw bɛ dɛmɛ ka kɔrɔ, o bɛ dɛmɛ kɛ sɔrɔ ni kɛlɛ dɔw ye, i n'a fɔ sariya sigida, ani ka faamuya ko mɔgɔ dɔ bɛ u ka kuma faamu. A b'a fɔ fana ko Association bɛ sigiɲɔgɔn dɛmɛ ye tuma caman, ani sigiɲɔgɔn minnu bɛ dementia min bɛ fɔlɔ ni 65 ka tɛ, olu fana bɛ kɛ, ani sigiɲɔgɔn 3 bɛ kɛ online Tɛnɛnɛ ni Tarata.

Ni aw bɛ fɛ ka sigiɲɔgɔn ɲɛ, telefɔni ka na Association ka 24/7 Helpline na 800.272.3900. [Sigiɲɔgɔn ka kɛlɛ yɛrɛ ka kɛlɛ](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) bɛ kuma ka sɔrɔ, ka dɔgɔya, ani ka dɔn ni ka sɔnna ka ɲɔgɔn ye.

## Stage bɛ se ka kɛ ka ɲɛ

Mɔgɔ ka dementia stage bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ ka dɛmɛ min bɛ kɛ. A fɔlɔ kɔnɔ, mɔgɔ caman bɛ se ka a ka kɛlɛ kɛ ni dɔ dɔ ye, ani [fɔlɔ stage kɛlɛw](/activities/early-stage/) bɛ fɛn caman fɔ. A kɔfɛ, kɛlɛ bɛ kɛ dɔgɔ ani ka sɛgɛsɛgɛ dɔgɔ, i n'a fɔ [kɔfɛ stage kɛlɛw](/activities/late-stage/) kɔnɔ. Mɔgɔ ka kɛ ka yɛrɛ ka waati ka kɛlɛ ka kɛ, ani ka a ka kuma ni a bɛ se ka kɛ, ka a to a ka sugandi la.
