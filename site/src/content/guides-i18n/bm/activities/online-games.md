Mɔgɔ caman bɛ dɔnkili Internet-kan dementia la mɔgɔw ye ka sɛbɛ, bawo u bɛ fɛ ka dɔ ka kɛ ni u ye, min tɛ store, login walima credit card ka wajibi. Dɔnkili ɲɛfɔ min ka ca ka bon, olu ye ɲɛfɔ ni ɲɛfɛ ye, ani olu bɛ se ka tiɲɛ ka sɔrɔ. Olu ka kɛ jɔ dɔ ye min bɛ kɛ mɔgɔ ye ni i ye, ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ ye. Nin sɛbɛ bɛ ɲɛfɔ ko min bɛ kɛ ka lakika, dɔnkili min bɛ se ka kɛ ni min tɛ se ka kɛ.

## Dɔnkili jumɛn bɛ kɛ Internet-kan dementia la mɔgɔw ye?

Dɔnkili bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ ni mɔgɔ dementia la ye ni a bɛ ɲɛfɔ dɔ ka ɲɛ ka sɔrɔ, o ye ka kiri ni ka sɛbɛ tɛ. Ɲɛfɔ ni ɲɛfɔ min ye kelen ye, ɲɛfɔ min bɛ ɲɛfɛ ni ɲɛfɔ ye, walima sɔrɔ basket kɔnɔ, olu bɛ kɛ ni ɲɛ min bɛ ɲɛ bi. Dɔnkili min bɛ kiri, sɛbɛ, nɔmɛ walima tɛmɛn ye, olu bɛ kɛ ka kɔrɔ.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) bɛ ɲɛfɔ min bɛ mɔgɔ ka dɔnkili ye, min mɔgɔ bɛ kɛ ka se ka kɛ ka ɲɛ. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) bɛ ko ka dɔnkili sɔrɔ, ni mɔgɔ ka kɛcogo yɛrɛ bɛ yɛrɛmɔ, o ka tɛ ka bɔ ka dɔnkili bɔ.

## Golden Moments dɔnkili min bɛ wulia browser kɔnɔ

Dɔnkili o kelen o kelen bɛ wulia website kɔnɔ download tɛ ka wajibi. Olu caman bɛ kɛ tablet walima computer kan; dɔw bɛ se ka kɛ ka bɔ ka bɛn ka kɛ ka sɛbɛ.

- **[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/).** Ɲɛfɔ ɲɛfɔ caman ni ɲɛfɔ bɛ ɲɛ; timer ni score tɛ. A ka ɲɛfɛ ɲɛfɔ.
- **[Find the same](/games/find-the-same/).** Ɲɛfɔ kelen kan kɔnɔ, ani ɲɛfɔ min ye kelen ye, 2 ka se 4 ka sugandili kɔnɔ.
- **[Shadow match](/games/shadow-match/).** Nkalo nɔgɔn bɛ kan kɔnɔ; ɲɛfɔ min bɛ nkalo kɛ, o ye ɲɛfɔ ye.
- **[Sort together](/games/sort-together/).** Ɲɛfɔ dɔ, ka basket min bɛ a kɔnɔ, i ko kɔnɔ walima sɔrɔ.
- **[Picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Ɲɛfɔ dɔ bɛ kɛ ka kɛlɛ, ani ɲɛfɔ bɛ ɲɛ ka kɛ ka ɲɛnɛ.
- **[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Kuma dɔnkili min tɛ ka laban; sugandi dɔ walima laban bɛ ɲɛ.
- **[Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/).** Ɲɛfɔ karta ni kuma min bɛ kɛ ka kuma, ka nɔgɔ ka nɔgɔn ɲɛfɛ. A bɛ se ka bɔ ka kɛ.
- **[Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/).** Photo i ka sugandi ni photo ɲɛfɔ. Photo bɛ maana browser kɔnɔ o device kɔnɔ.
- **[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/).** Ɲɛnɛ kɛnɛ ka kɛ ni ɲɛfɔ; aw bɛ daminɛ, aw bɛ ɲɛnɛ.

Word search, coloring ni picture bingo bɛ kɛ ka bɔ ka sɛbɛ. [free printable dementia activities](/activities/printable/) bɛ ɲɛfɔ caman.

## Dɔnkili sɛbɛ cogo

1. **Waati ɲɛ ka kɛ** mɔgɔ ka bɛn. Tile ni ɲɛ, mɔgɔ dɔw bɛ ɲɛ fanga; aw ka ɲɛfɛ yɛrɛ dɔn daminɛ.
2. **Ekran ka kɛ ka ca.** Tablet stand kan, walima computer min browser ka zoom ka ca, o bɛ ɲɛ ni ɲɛ.
3. **Tab wɛrɛw ni notification kɛ ka tila.** Pop-up ka kɔnɔ bɛ mɔgɔ kɔnɔ ka kɔrɔ, o bɛ dɔnkili ka tiɲɛ.
4. **Mɔgɔ ka kɛlɛ sigi ka kɛ.** Ɲɛfɔ ka ɲɛ, ka tɛ ka kɛ ka kɛlɛ ni a ye. Ni aw sigilen, o bɛ kɛ ka kɛ kɛlɛ ɲɛfɛ.
5. **Dɔnkili kelen ka daminɛ 5 ka se 10 ka miniti.** Mɔgɔ ka se ka kɛ ekran kan walima ka ɲɛfɛ, aw ka kɛ kɛlɛ.
6. **Ka kɛ ni ɲɛ ye.** Dɔnkili kɛ ka ban, ka ɲɛfɔ dɔ kuma, walima ka ko ka tɛ ɲɛ.

## Privacy ni tɔgɔ device kɔnɔ

- **Photo bɛ device kɔnɔ.** Photo pairs bɛ photo min aw ye sugandi, o bɛ maana browser kɔnɔ o device kɔnɔ. Sani ko mun bɛ computer walima tablet ɲɛfɛ.
- **Link ni ads ka tila.** Dɔnkili yɛrɛ ka site kɔnɔ kɛ, ani banner walima prompt min bɛ mɔgɔ tɔgɔ ka kɛ, u kan tɛ ka kɛ.
- **Payment ni account ka ɲɛfɛ.** Mɔgɔ tɛ ka sigi kelen ekran kan min bɛ password walima card number ka wajibi.
- **Mɔgɔ min bɛ taa ka ɲɔ.** Tablet ka bɔ mɔgɔ fɛ ni mɔgɔ bɛ taa ka so ni ɲɛ.

Ladilikan caman ye, [how to choose dementia apps and games](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) ni [setting up a tablet or phone](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/) ɲɛfɛ.

## Dɔnkili nin bɛ min kɛ, ni bɛ min tɛ kɛ

Dɔnkili Internet-kan bɛ dementia la mɔgɔw di ka ɲɛnamaya ɲɛfɔ ɲɛnɔgɔn, ka ɲɛfɔ ɲɛ ni ɲɛ, ka kɛ dɔnkili ye ni ɲɛ tɛ. Olu tɛ dementia kɛnɛ, olu tɛ a ka ɲɛ, olu tɛ a tɛmɛ. [Federal Trade Commission ka 2016 ka baara Lumosity kan](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program), o ye misali ye, u ka hakili dɔnkili ka ko min tɛ se ka dɔn. Ka ɲɛ game, app walima site min bɛ ko ka hakili ka ca walima ka ɲɛ tɛmɛ, o ka ɲɛ.

Dɔnkili nin kɛ aw ka ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ ɲɛfɛ, ni dɔ bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛfɔ dɔ ka sɛbɛ, ka taama, ka kuma ni ka sɔrɔ. Ka dɔnkili ka kɛ ka ɲɛ, [how to play games with someone who has dementia](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ni [memory games that do not test](/activities/memory-games/) ɲɛfɛ.

## Waati min bɛ ka kɔrɔ

Mɔgɔ ka ɲɛ ka kɛ ka dɔ, ka kɛ ka kɔrɔ, ka kɛ ka kɔrɔ walima ka kɛ ka sɛbɛ, o waati ka kɔrɔ. Mɔgɔ ka sɛbɛ ka bɔ, o waati ka kɔrɔ. Ka kɔrɔ daminɛ, o ye ɲɛnamaya ɲɛfɔ ye. Aw bɛ se ka segin ka kɛ tile wɛrɛ, walima ka kuma, walima ka taama.
