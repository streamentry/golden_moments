Mɔgɔ caman bɛ internet la dementia wasa tɔw ta, katuguni u bɛ fɛ ka wasa dɔ kɛ ni mɔgɔ dɔ ye, min tɛ ko dɔ ka labɛn, ka kɛlɛ bɛɛ tɛ ko dɔ ka sɔrɔ. Wasa ɲuman ye wasa min bɛ foto la, ka sɔrɔ ka gɛlɛ tɛ, ani min bɛ se ka to tuma o tuma. Olu ka kɛ ka kɛ ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn ye, ka kɛ ɲɛfɛ ye, o tɛ kɛ ka kɛ ɲɛnamaya dɔ ye. Nin lakɔlidɛn bɛ fɔ ko o ye fɛn jumɛn ye ka ɲɛ, ka wasa dɔ kɛ cogo jumɛn na, ani o wasa ka fɛn jumɛn bɛ se ka kɛ ani fɛn jumɛn tɛ se ka kɛ.

## Internet wasa jumɛn bɛ kɛ dementia mɔgɔ ye?

Wasa bɛ kɛ dementia mɔgɔ caman ye tuma min o bɛ ɲɛfɛ dɔn ka ɲɛ, ka kuma dɔ dɔn ka kɛ. Foto ɲɔgɔnjɛ, foto dɔ sugandi ka dɔn ni foto dɔ ye min bɛ ɲɛ ka bɔ ka kɛ, walima fɛn dɔ ka ɲɛ ka sɔrɔ ka dɔn ni yɔrɔ min na, olu bɛɛ bɛ kɛ ni ɲɛ min bɛ ye bi. Wasa minnu bɛ kɛ ni kuma dɔ, kɛlɛ dɔ, nɔmɛrɛ dɔ walima ko min kɛra kɔfɛ, olu bɛ ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn kɛ.

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) bɛ fɔ ko wasa ka kan ka kɛ fɛn min bɛ mɔgɔ ka dusukun ta ani min bɛ se ka kɛ, ka mɔgɔ ka ɲɛ ta tɛmɛ dɛsɛ tɔw ma. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) bɛ fɔ ko tuma min mɔgɔ ka fɛnw bɛ dɔgɔya, o tuma ka wasa dɔ dɔgɔya ye fɛn ye, a tɛ ka bɔ.

## Golden Moments wasa minnu bɛ labɛn browser kɔnɔ

Wasa o wasa bɛɛ bɛ labɛn yɔrɔ min na website la, a tɛ ko dɔ ka labɛn. Caman bɛ kɛ tablet walima ordinatɛri la; dɔw bɛ se ka sɛbɛn fana.

- **[Foto ɲɔgɔnjɛ](/games/picture-pairs/).** Foto ɲɔgɔnjɛ bɛ ɲɛ ka bon, foto bɛ ɲɛ, tuma walima ɲɛfɛ tɛ yen. A bɛ ɲɛ ka bon daminɛ wasa ye.
- **[Foto ɲɛfɛ](/games/find-the-same/).** Foto kelen bɛ kan kɔrɔ, ani o kelen bɛ dɔ ni foto nani ɲɛ ka bɔ kɔrɔ.
- **[Sɛgɛn ɲɛfɛ](/games/shadow-match/).** Sɛgɛn dɔ bɛ kan kɔrɔ; aw ka foto min bɛ o sɛgɛn kɛ, o ɲɛ.
- **[Ɲɔgɔn sɔgɔsɔgɔ](/games/sort-together/).** Foto dɔ ɲɛ, ka o kɔfɛ ntɔ min na, i n'a fɔ kɔnɔ walima sɛnɛ.
- **[Foto ɲɛfɛ ni ɲɛsin](/games/picture-puzzle/).** Foto dɔ bɛ kɛ ɲɛsin ye, ani foto bɛɛ bɛ to ɲɛ kɔrɔ kɛ ladilikan ye.
- **[Kuma ɲɛfɛ](/games/finish-the-saying/).** Kuma ɲuman min laban tɛ; ɲɛ dɔ walima laban ɲɛ ka bɔ.
- **[Kuma karta](/games/conversation-cards/).** Foto karta ni ko dɔ ka kuma kɛ, o tɛ ko dɔ ka ɲɛ. A bɛ se ka sɛbɛn fana.
- **[Foto ɲɔgɔnjɛ](/games/photo-pairs/).** Foto ɲɔgɔnjɛ min aw ka foto min bɛ aw ka ordinatɛri kɔnɔ, olu ye. Foto bɛ to browser kɔnɔ o ordinatɛri kɔnɔ.
- **[Hakɛ ka ɲɛnamaya](/games/calm-breathing/).** Tuma ɲɔgɔn min bɛ ka ɲɛ ka ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn ye, ka ɲɛ. Aw bɛ a daminɛ, aw bɛ a to.

Kuma ɲɛfɛ, kɛnɛ foto ani ɲɔgɔnjɛ sɛbɛn fana bɛ sɔrɔ wasa tɔw ye min bɛ sɛbɛn. [Dementia wasa tɔw min bɛ sɛbɛn](/activities/printable/) ka ɲɛfɛ dɔ.

## Wasa sigiyɔrɔ labɛnni cogo

1. **Tuma ɲuman sugandi** tuma min mɔgɔ bɛ ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn ye. Tile kɔfɛ bɛ gɛlɛya mɔgɔ dɔw ye, katuguni aw ka ɲɛfɛn ɲɛ daminɛ.
2. **Ekran ka bon ka kɛ.** Tablet min bɛ sigi ni ordinatɛri min browser bɛ bon, o bɛ ɲɛ ani o bɛ se ka kɛ ni bolo.
3. **Tabi wɛrɛw ka tɔ, notifikasiyɔn ka tɔ.** Pop-up bɛ mɔgɔ siran, o bɛ wasa to.
4. **Sigi mɔgɔ yɔrɔ, a ɲɛ kɔrɔ tɛ.** Ɲɛfɛ ɲɔgɔn bɛ kɛ ni ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn ye, a tɛ kɛ ka ɲɛ ta.
5. **Wasa kelen daminɛ miniti 5 ka se 10 ma.** Mɔgɔ bɛ ekran ta walima a bɛ ɲɛ, aw ye kɛ tuma ɲɔgɔn ye.
6. **Wasa ɲuman kɛ kɔfɛ.** Ɲɛfɛ dɔ kɔfɛ, foto kelen ko, walima a ye sɛnɛ min ta ka ɲɛ.

## Hakɛ ani ɲɛnamaya shared ordinatɛri kɔnɔ

- **Foto bɛ to ordinatɛri kɔnɔ.** Foto ɲɔgɔnjɛ bɛ foto min aw ye sugandi, olu bɛ to browser kɔnɔ o ordinatɛri kɔnɔ. Ɲɛ ka ban: mɔgɔ wɛrɛ bɛ se ka ordinatɛri walima tablet ta wa?
- **Sira wɛrɛw ni ad ban.** Wasa bɛ se ka kɛ ni site kɔnɔ tuma, aw kana sira dɔ ta min bɛ dɔnniya ci.
- **Sɛnɛ ni dɔnniya tɛ mɔgɔ kelen ma.** Aw kana mɔgɔ to ekran kɔnɔ min bɛ password walima karta nɔmɛrɛ ɲini.
- **Ka ɲɛ ta ban bɔ.** Ni mɔgɔ bɛ taa ni tablet, aw ka a to yɔrɔ min tɛ se ka a bɛn.

Ni aw bɛ hakilina ɲɔgɔn ye, ka a sɛbɛn, [dementia apps ni wasa tɔw sugandi cogo](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) ani [tablet walima telefɔn daminɛni](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/).

## Nin wasa tɔw bɛ fɛn jumɛn kɛ ani fɛn jumɛn tɛ se ka kɛ

Internet wasa tɔw bɛ dementia mɔgɔ ka dɛmɛ ka ɲɛ, foto ɲɔgɔn ye, ka kuma ɲɔgɔn kɛ, ani ka kɛ fɛn ɲɔgɔn ye tuma min tɛ kɛ ni gɛlɛya ye. O tɛ dementia kɛ mɛn, a tɛ a ka ɲɛ, walima a ka ɲɛ tɛ. Misali min bɛ fɔ ka caya ni [Federal Trade Commission ka ɲɛfɛ 2016 la Lumosity kama](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program), olu ye hakili wasa ka kuma ye min tɛ se ka dɛsɛ. Aw ka hakilina, ni wasa, app walima site bɛ ɲɔgɔn kɛ, a bɛ fɔ ko hakili ka ɲɛ, a bɛ aw hakili ɲɛfɛ walima a bɛ fɔ ko a bɛ se ka dɔgɔya ka bɔ.

Aw ka wasa tɔw kɛ fɛn kelen ye tile kɔnɔ min bɛ se ka kɛ ni dɔnkili, sigiyɔrɔ, kuma ni ɲɛnamaya ɲɔgɔn ye. Ni aw bɛ kalan ka sɔrɔ wasa tɔw kɛ ka hakilina ye, ka a sɛbɛn [dementia ni wasa kɛ ni mɔgɔ ka ɲɛ ta cogo](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) ani [wasa min tɛ mɔgɔ gɛlɛya](/activities/memory-games/).

## Yɔrɔ min na aw bɛ ka ban

Ni mɔgɔ bɛ dimi, ka hɛrɛ bɔ, ka siran walima ka tɛmɛ ni o bɛ dimi, aw ka ban. Ni a ka hakɛ bɔ, aw ka ban. Wasa ka ban daminɛ, o ye ɲɛfɛn ɲuman ye. Aw bɛ se ka segin tuma wɛrɛ la, walima aw bɛ kuma, taa ni wulu wala min ka ɲɛ ye.
