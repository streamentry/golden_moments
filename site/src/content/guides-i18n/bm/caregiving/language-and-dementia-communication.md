Mɔgɔ minnu bɛ kaan caman fɔ, olu ka kumaɲɔgɔnya bɛ se ka yɛlɛma ni hakilijiginya bana bɛ daɲɛ sɔrɔli walima faamuyali gɛlɛya. Mɔgɔ dɔw bɛ daminɛ ka kaan dɔ fɔ min u ye a kalan kabini tuma jan; dɔw tɛ o kɛ. Fɛɛrɛ fɔlɔ min ka ɲi kosɛbɛ, o ye ka a ɲininka kaan jumɛn ka fisa a ma sisan, o kɔ ka tugu a ka fɛta ye.

[Alzheimer's Society b’a fɔ ko kaan sugandili bɛ se ka yɛlɛma ani a b’a laadi ka baara kɛ ni kaan ye min mɔgɔ in bɛ a fɔ tuma caman](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association fana bɛ segin ka taa mɔgɔ ka bange kaan na sɛbɛn i n’a fɔ kumaɲɔgɔnya yɛlɛmani min bɛ se ka kɛ](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Nin yɛlɛmaniw tɛ kɛ mɔgɔ bɛɛ ye, wa u tɛ mɔgɔ ka bana cogoya jira.

## Kaan jumɛn ka kan ka baara kɛ ni a ye?

Mɔgɔ in ɲininka a b’a fɛ ka kaan jumɛn fɔ nin baro in na. A ka sugandili bɛ se ka yɛlɛma ka kɛɲɛ ni barokun ye, yɔrɔ ye walima don waati ye. Ni a ye kaan yɛlɛma, kana o jate i n’a fɔ kɔrɔbɔli walima kana a wajibiya ka to kaan min na i bɛ se k’a faamu.

Ni i bɛ kaan min fɔ a b’a fɛ, baara kɛ ni daɲɛ dɔnnenw ye ani waati di a ma ka jaabi di. Ni i bɛ daɲɛ damadɔw dɔrɔn de dɔn, i ka tiɲɛ fɔ ani baara kɛ ni taamasiyɛnw ye, fɛnw ye walima jaw ye fana. [Alzheimer's Society ka kaan ladilikan](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) b’a jira ko kaan seko bɛ se ka yɛlɛma don o don, wa sɛgɛn, dimi walima bana bɛ se k’a nɔ bila a la.

## Mun bɛ se ka dɛmɛ don don o don baro la?

To kumaɲɔgɔnya ladilikɛnw la, ani dɛmɛ fara u kan min bɛ bɛn mɔgɔ in ka kaan ma:

- **Baara kɛ ni daɲɛ ni tɔgɔ dɔnnenw ye.** Mɔgɔ in walima mɔgɔ min na a bɛ da, o ɲininka daɲɛ jumɛnw bɛ fɔ don o don fɛnw, somɔgɔw ani deliko dɔnnenw ma.
- **Fɛn jira ani k’a fɔ fana.** Bolo digi minan dɔ kan, sugandili fila jira, walima baara kɛ ni foto walima fɛn dɔnnen dɔ ye. Taamasiyɛn bɛ se ka dɛmɛ don ni daɲɛ ɲuman sɔrɔli ka gɛlɛn.
- **A makɔnɔ.** Waati di mɔgɔ in ma ka lamɛn ani ka jaabi di. Kana a ka kumasen bɛɛ dafa walima kana ɲininkali kelen segin kaan caman na siɲɛ kelen.
- **Hakilina kelen kɛ siɲɛ kelen.** Ɲɛfɔli jan bɛ se ka gɛlɛya kaan o kaan na. Kumasen surun kɛ, i jɔ dɔɔnin, o kɔ k’a lajɛ ni a b’a fɛ ka kunnafoni wɛrɛ sɔrɔ.
- **Tulo ni ɲɛ lajɛ.** Nunɛti, tulomafɛn, yeelen ɲuman ani yɔrɔ min mankan ka dɔgɔ, olu bɛ se ka kaan faamuyali nɔgɔya.

Ni mɔgɔ in b’a fɛ ka kalan kɛ, a ɲininka yala kunnafoni nafama sɛbɛnnenw bɛ sɔrɔ a ka kaan na. Kana i jigi da bamanankan baarakɛminɛn otomatiki dɔ kan kɛnɛya latigɛ kama, sɔnni kama walima furakɛli ladilikan kama; kunnafoni nafamaw sinsin ni ladonnibaga ye.

## Ni n tɛ kaan min fɔ mɔgɔ in b’a fɛ, n bɛna mun kɛ?

A ɲininka yala a b’a fɛ a teri walima a somɔgɔ dɔ ka fara don o don baro dɔ kan min bɛ kaan fila fɔ ani min na a bɛ da. Kana miiri ko mɔgɔ in b’a fɛ mɔgɔ wɛrɛ ka kanbayanan kɛ a ye; a bɛ se ka taamasiyɛnw, jaw walima kaan wɛrɛ fɛ min aw fila bɛ bɛn a kan.

Kɛnɛya lajɛ kama walima baro wɛrɛ kama min bɛ kunnafoni kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen de wajibiya, kuma dɔgɔtɔrɔso walima ladonni baarakɛyɔrɔ fɛ ka kɔn. A fɔ u ye mɔgɔ in b’a fɛ ka kaan jumɛn fɔ, ani a ɲininka ni kanbayanan dɛmɛ bɛ sɔrɔ. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services b’a ɲɛfɔ](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html) ko somɔgɔw ni teriw bɛ se ka kɛlɛ ka kɛnɛya kunnafoni nafamaw bayɛlɛma ka ɲɛ; baarakɛyɔrɔ bɛ se k’a ka sugandiliw ni sigida sariyaw ɲɛfɔ. Mɔgɔ in bɛ se k’a latigɛ ni dɛmɛbaga dɔ bɛ taa ni a ye, k’a sɔrɔ baarakɛyɔrɔ bɛ kaan dɛmɛ min ka kan ka kɛ, o labɛn.

Lajɛ senfɛ, kuma mɔgɔ in fɛ tilennen, ani waati di kanbayananbaga ma ka kuma. A lajɛ ni mɔgɔ in ye ɲininkali faamu ani ni a ye se sɔrɔ ka jaabi di n’a yɛrɛ ka daɲɛw ye. Ni i bɛ a dɛmɛ, kunnafoni wɛrɛw fara a kan dɔrɔn ni a b’a fɛ i k’o kɛ.

## Tuma jumɛn na n ka kan ka kɛnɛya laadilikan ɲini?

Kaan gɛlɛyaw bɛ se ka yɛlɛma waati kɔnɔ, nka yɛlɛmani min bɛ kɛ yɔrɔ kelen, o tɛ kelen ye. [NHS b’a laadi ka kɛnɛya dɛmɛ ɲini joona ni mɔgɔ dɔ bɛ ɲagami yɔrɔ kelen](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Kana miiri ko kumaɲɔgɔnya bɔnɛ yɔrɔ kelen ye hakilijiginya bana ta dɔrɔn ye. Ni mɔgɔ in bɛ ɲagami yɔrɔ kelen, ni a man kɛnɛ kosɛbɛ walima ni a kununni ka gɛlɛn, kuma i sigida kɛnɛya dɛmɛ joona baarakɛyɔrɔw fɛ.

Yɛlɛmani minnu bɛ kɛ dɔɔnin dɔɔnin, olu lajɛ ani mɔgɔ in ka dɔgɔtɔrɔ ɲininka laadilikan kan. A sɛbɛn kaan jumɛn na a tun bɛ kuma, mun de ye a dɛmɛ ka kumaɲɔgɔnya kɛ, ani yala a tun sɛgɛnnen don walima a tun man kɛnɛ. O ye ɲɛfɔli ye baro kama, a tɛ bana dɔnni ye. An ka [dɔgɔtɔrɔso lajɛ gafe](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) bɛ se k’i dɛmɛ ka ɲininkaliw labɛn.

## Mun na n bɛ se ka fɔ dɛmɛbaga tɔw ye?

Ni mɔgɔ in sɔnna a ma, sɛbɛn surun dɔ mara mɔgɔ minnu a b’a fɛ u ka dɛmɛ don. A bɛ se ka kaan walima kan suguya jira min a b’a fɛ ka fɔ ani ka kalan, daɲɛ walima tɔgɔ dɔnnenw, ani fɛnw minnu b’a dɛmɛ ka baro faamu. A ɲininka yala a b’a fɛ mɔgɔ kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen dɔw ka fara lajɛw walima ladonni kunnafoniw kan. Kana kunnafoni gundo sɛbɛn minnu tɛ kan ka kɛ dɛmɛ kama, ani a fɔ mɔgɔ in ye jɔn bɛ se ka sɛbɛn in ye. Kaan sɛbɛn bɛ mɔgɔw dɛmɛ ka baro daminɛ ni dususuma ye; a tɛ kanbayananbaga nɔ bila kɛnɛya baro gɛlɛn dɔ la.

## N bɛ se ka mɔgɔ in ka sugandiliw dɛmɛ cogo di?

Kaan ye mɔgɔ ka jɛɲɔgɔnya fɛɛrɛ dɔ ye, nka a tɛ mɔgɔ in bɛɛ ye. A ɲininka a ka delikow, a diyanyekow, a ka kalan fɛɛrɛw ani mɔgɔ minnu a b’a fɛ u ka fara a kan. Ni mɔgɔ in sɔnna a ma, daɲɛw walima kumasenw sɛbɛn dɛmɛbaga tɔw ye ani a ɲɛfɔ u ye u ka baara kɛ ni u ye cogo min na. Sɛbɛnw mara gundo la ani kunnafoni minnu mɔgɔ in sɔnna ka tila, olu dɔrɔn de tila.

Kana mɔgɔ in wajibiya ka degeli kɛ, ka kanbayanan kɛ mɔgɔ wɛrɛw ye walima k’a jira ko a bɛ faamuya. Baro dɔw bɛ se ka nɔgɔya kaan dɔ la, dɔ wɛrɛw kaan wɛrɛ la. Mɔgɔ in ka dususuma, a ka gundo ani a ka sugandili nafa ka bon ka tɛmɛ kaan kelen dɔrɔn kan.

An ka [kumaɲɔgɔnya ladilikɛnw](/caregiving/communication-tips/) bɛ kuma nɔgɔman fɔli, lamɛnni ani jaw baara kan. Ni i b’a fɛ ka labɛn kɛ bana dɔnni kɔfɛ, i bɛ se ka [fɛɛrɛ fɔlɔw hakilijiginya bana dɔnni kɔfɛ](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) lajɛ.
