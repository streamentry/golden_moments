Esseroori korooromiyya dacrisiyyah burtitte kee kulli ayro gabat agattat maggo mari essera korooroma kinni, kaadu maggo garooqela maaqi gabat teetit korooromta. Duma aban gurral, lakqo luk inki numuk gacsa esserak aban waqdi, kas bayte num gariir kee mokkorul gaba kallaah yabbiddeemih maxco takke. Xabbowtih aban waqdi, xaagoora yaabbiinimih meqe margaqat yakke. Gubat 50 xabbi esserooray doorit leeh kaat walalan caagid lih tani, sarrah gaba-kalto mannal aban warraytam taturte.

## Kas bayte numuh esseroora mannal meqennal abaanam duudan?

Warrayte uddur ixxiga kassiisaanam kee aaxaguk meqem abina, kulli gacsa oggolaanamih caagid aba.

1. **Warrayte xaleela essera.** [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/what-is-alzheimers) iyyeemih gurral, Alzheimer dambisiyyi qimbisa asti qusbuk bariten ixxiga kassiitam gibiddiitaam kinni. Tabnaan sanootak duma suge gaaliita, maxcooca kee kulli ayro mano xabbowta baras kinni.
2. **Kullumay doorit kaah acuy.** Nammay hinnay sidiica dooritik nummaantol tani dooraanam, umaanem maleek gactamuk maggo xabbowta.
3. **Esseroora kaxxa xawuk aqriyaay nabam yaybula.** Alluwwaay tablet yaybulen waqdi yab aaxaguk gibiddiita marat gaba-kala.
4. **Gacsa sorkocobbaxuk acuy.** Numtin mayaxaginna tekkek, naba qimbisal ixxicaay warraya. Makina: "Atu aah taaxigee, kassiit."
5. **Lakqo makabinna.** Numtin marak makabinna, gabbotik makabinna. Maro lakqo faxak tenyek, kulli maro inki lakqol koboxa.
6. **Inki numuk mayessirina.** Gacsa kulli numuk maacisiyya. Nammayih taniinimik itta lih gaca: "Anu piyano kinni axceh. Atu maana tecte?"
7. **Walalah yan caagid abbaasita.** Gacsa qimbisa kinni, katuk hinna. Xaagoora kinni nummaanti fayla.
8. **Qawwalaylaha aba.** Tabna esserooray fantaaxawul xaagoora leeh gaba-kalan meqem kinni.

[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) qimbisa madqa xabbowtih xayyowsa: rooxi maleenik waasitaay isi numuk yeyyeceh axcuk maacisa. [Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) esseroori korooromiyya qimbisa caradit meqe taamah addat kassiisa. Fantaaxawu caradit, nammay doorituk mayisinaay walal caagidal gaca. Sarrah, gubat yan taah-hinnay-too-tiya esserooral gaca.

## Xayi maxcooca

Maxcok abuuruk gactam duma baritten afik yamaateeh xabbowtih tewqe. Ni [Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) korooroma maggo maggom leeh, inki taabisal gaba-kaltam duddan.

| Maxco | Doorit | Kaat walala |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kullayro inki tufax abak tenyek... | **Doktoruk wattowta**, robuk wattowta, muuk wattowta | Ah kok iyyak suge num iyyee? |
| Saaku ugutta caxaytu geytam... | Baas, **Burtuuka (Ciloo)**, Baabur | Atu saaku marih numtii? |
| Bakaarit manam xisbik mayisinaay sarrah... | **Dhaltamuk duma**, diicak duma, gaalak duma | Garo bakle dacrisak sugtee? |
| Gaba-kalten bartaaray kassiisan... | **Geyte bartaara**, bayte bartaara, xayyowte bartaara | Ku garol lakqo dacrise num iyyee? |
| Qari iyyeenim mannol... | Xalot tan arca, **Sorkocobbax yan arca**, Furnay yan arca | Inki carcat qarih axceh yoo abtem maana? |
| Gaba-kaban aysuk meqeek... | Dudda, **Mawqa maleek**, gariiruk | Kulli uddur waaqida mari iyyee? |
| Kulli qilsi qiblit leeh... | **Qado leemih noole**, kowmaata, bartaara | Ku manot meqe fantaaxaw kassiissa? |
| Taama yabaak... | **Xawuk kaxxa**, muusiqak kaxxa, xanxanak kaxxa | Ah yexce num kassiisa? |

## Taah hinnay too tiya esserooray dambiyyi gacsa sinni

Ta esseroora kulli caradah meqem kinni, sarra carad koboxak, nummaantoy abaanam faxxinta kaltaah. Esseraay, ankacisaay, isi gacsa kaadu ittal acuy.

- Kafeey makko shayi?
- Baxariro makko caxale qale?
- Kuta makko muuwa?
- Karra makko gilala?
- Paay makko keeki?
- Saawaraanam makko wagitil sugaanam?
- Ayroh mawqa makko ayroh qarba?
- Qarih saal makko qarih duma?
- Raadiyow makko rekordi?
- Fancak makko kuka?
- Rooda makko deezi ful?
- Baabur safar makko makinah safar?

Suwwoora lih walal faxxeenik, waraaqatal tewqe ni [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) korooromaantey wagit.

## Dacrisiyyah arci hinnay buttat esseroori korooromiyya mannal gaba-kalan?

1. **Yablon kee yaabben gurral mara dafisa.** Ayti-makina hayyaanam, kullaata hayyaanam, TV kalwaanam.
2. **Walalah kinni axcuk qimbisa.** "Warrayte ayrootak esseroora luk anioh, kaadu yook aysuk taaxigeenim asmata."
3. **Kulli essero namma adda aqriyaay doorit nabam yaybula.**
4. **Maroorik kulli arcak temqe gacsa oggola.** Meqe gacsa kulli num yoobbeh axcuk qimbisa.
5. **Kulli marah gacsa ixxica,** sarrah walalah yan caagid mara essera.
6. **Xayi margaqat kataya.** Inki essero tabna daqiiqah xaagool gactek, kabina.
7. **Gaaliit hinnay taah-hinnay-too-tiya korooromal gaba-kala,** kulli num xabbi caagidal gaba-kalon keh.

Aka buttaatih gurriitah, picture bingo kee gaaliit kobxisiyya lih, [group activities for care homes](/activities/care-home-groups/) wagita.

## Qokol mannal gariiroonah

Numtin rooxi luk yembulleh, cakkum yeeceh hinnay "Anu maaxiga" namma addak maggom yexcek, xabbowsa: nammay dooritul ooba, itta lih gacsa, hinnay taah-hinnay-too-tiya esserooral gaca. Butta inki esserol kulli ayró yabtam faxxek, toh nummaanti meqe gaba-kalto kinni. Qasbi kullumay nummaanti gacsal hinna suge. Meqe marah fantaaxawul marot sugen uddura suge.
