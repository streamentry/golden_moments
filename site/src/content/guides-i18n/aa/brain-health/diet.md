\"Sammi qafiyat le\" maaqo Alzheimer waassam xiqta axcuk yaabbenik tanoonuh
xeqta. Kinnih immay addat yan numma daguk tewqe. Marat gexsitah tan ceefto
gari maaqoh daddos kaskassoh xalu daggoysuh garaytam tascasse, kinnih immay
kah tayse qoolat baxsale fayda mabaahinno. Ta qolfa namma caalat kassiisa,
tohiik lakat kaskasso luk yaanih yan mara tayseeniih cattaanamal qaxaabta:
kullimik duma dooqaysak takmeeniih taaqubeeniimik, kaadu tohih hattooftaanam.

Tahay ummatta kas-leedda, dawoytah warrayti hinna. Ku maaqo hinnay gersi numih
maaqo tunkutteek duma dawoyto hinnay maaqo taaxigo luk yaaba. Ni
[medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/) waga.

## Mediteraane kee MIND maaqo warraytiimi?

Kaxxa Marih Caagidih Institit (NIA) ah akkanuk tascasse
([NIA on diet and Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/what-do-we-know-about-diet-and-prevention-alzheimers-disease)):

- **Mediteraane maaqo:** maggo caxacaxa, xaxxaqo, baaxo-midir, adasa, kuluc
  kee bad-maaqo, zaytun zayti; daguk caxxi cado, hanbaxa kee macaane.
- **MIND maaqo** (Mediterranean–DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay):
  Mediteraane maaqo kee dhiig-korik DASH maaqo kobox. Qaxatto xaxxaqoo kee beri
  midir kassiisa, baaxo-midir, adasa, subuq, kuluc aydah addat aynah takkeem,
  kee zaytun zayti. Caxxi cado, macaane, daadum, subac hinnay marzariin, kee
  salfa takke maaqo daggoysa.

## Ta maaqo kaskassoh xalu daggoysaa?

Sumaq koboxxa le. NIA axcuk: kullim hinna kinnih immay marih ceefto
Mediteraane kee MIND maaqo caxxi cado, subac leey kee sukkar mangoh yan
Masaaqit-maaqok kaskassoh xalu daggoysaah tabsituh garaytam tascasse.

2024 sanatih Lancet Commission ceefto deetiyit wagitte
([Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)):

- Cohort ceeftoh wagitto maaqoh qafiyat meqem 20% kaskassoh xalu daggoysuh
  garaytam tescesseq.
- Sidirrit cohort kobox MIND maaqo katayam kaskasso daggoysaah tabsittam
  tascasse.
- Kinnih immay Sweden sanatal 28,025 mara 20 sanatuk gexsitah yamballin ceefto
  ta maaqo katayeenik kaskassoh xalu madaggoysinna.

Marat gexsitah tan ceefto sabaab kee gaba-kallim tascassem ma-xiqta. Ta innal
yakme mari gersi caalatat baxsa leena, tamah galto, baritto, fantaaxaw kee
qafiyatah gari yableeninna. Commission gacisseemik: meqe maaqo kaskasso sinam
waassak tan axcuh dooqayte sumaq matan, kinnih immay mangom xaxxaqo kee
caxacaxa takmeeniih masnaca maaqo habaanaam meqe, sukkar kee dhiig-korik tan
xaluwitte daggoysuh catta. Ta Commission maaqo [14 risk factors](/brain-health/risk-factors/)
le ayro-wagitto addat ma-ooddinna.

## 2023 MIND maaqoh qoolat maay baahe?

Kulli ceeftok qandhi leeh tan abto 2023 sanatal New England Journal of Medicine
addat yumbullem
([MIND diet trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10513737/)):

- **Iyya:** 604 mara 65 sanat kee tohik amô mari, Chicago kee Boston
  magaalol yan mara, kas-dhibe maleh, kaskasso le ramid le mara, dooqayte
  handol mangom leeh maaqo kassiisi gari luk sugen mara.
- **Maa:** sidirrit sanatik, namma-exxak tinafa MIND maaqo takme, gersi namma-exxa
  sinni maaqo takme. Nammuy exxa ayrok 250 kalori daggoysaanam kaadu maaqoh
  taaxigoh katayo geyaanam faxximak suge.
- **Gaba:** kas cubbi **nammuy** addal daguk tayse. Namma exxa fanat yan
  baxsa xiiniseeh **sumaq male** yewqe. Sammi suwar koboxnam kibaluk baxsa
  male yallin.
- **Hando:** nammuy gari 5 kilo (11 paawundik gaba) hando daggoysen.

### Qoolat baxsa maleh kah yallin sabbat maay tekke?

Ceefto abbaasite mari mangom sabaabiyitte kassiise. Kontorol gari sinni maaqo
meqoysen, hando daggoysenik gexxik. Ceefto koboxnam xiqta axcuk kas cubbi
ceefto mara bica abam xiqta. Sidirrit sanat daggoowam xiqta. Mangom gari
baritto leeh Awroopa marak yemeeten, tohik gexxik kulli marah ma-gaca.

Nummat ceeftoh gaba tewqem: MIND maaqo sidiica sanatik daggoowu aben maaqok
taysem ma-tewqinno. Toh meqe maaqo fayda maleh tina axcuk hinna. Toh baxsale
maaqo kaskasso waassak tan axcuk kibal matanam kassiisa.

## Baxsale maaqo kee suplemen caagid maay iyyee?

NIA axcuk: baxsale maaqo takmeenimik hinnay kaltaanamik Alzheimer hinnay kas
daggoysa waassuh sumaq mayan. Beri midir, xaxxaqoo kee xurmud waginnaah sumaq
mabaahinno. NIA kaadu fitamiin hinnay suplemen Alzheimer waassak tublem mantu,
kaadu tohih ma-kassiisa axce. NIA kassiisuh: Alzheimer waassah tan axcuk
yantukkuqe yaaboti fayda maleh, lakqo bayissam xiqta, hinnay dawoyto luk
dhibe abam xiqta, tohik gexxik dawoytot gaca
([NIA on preventing Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

## Maaqo qafiyat kee hatto luk takke inna mannal abnaa?

Kaxxa marah, fantaaxaw tan maaqo tanoo, taaxigeeniih kassiisan maaqo, gidba
le siraat hinna:

- **Kicnoon maaqo maaqoh daddosat bicisa** Mediteraane daddosik: xaxxaqo,
  caxacaxa, adasa, baaxo-midir, kuluc kee zaytun zayti.
- **Kicnoon maaqo xisa.** Hatto kee maaqoh qawto faxximta, baxaabaxsaluk kaxxa
  marat.
- **Gersi mara luk akma** xiqtaanik. Marat takmiinim hatto le kobox.
- **Kobollat bicisa.** Xaxxaqo caxceenim hinnay disti koboxxa gaba-qokol;
  waga [household tasks](/activities/household-tasks/). Kicnoon kobox
  maaqo kaadu [reminiscence](/activities/reminiscence/) kassise koboxxi
  baaha.

Kaxxa mari kalori daggoysaanam hinnay hando habaanaam dawoyto luk yaaboonuk
maleh mabiyaan, soom kaadu baxsah gibda le. Waga ni
[fasting guide](/brain-health/fasting/).

## Kaskasso le num maaqo meqem yakmuh mannal cattam xiqtaa?

Kaskasso luk yaanih yan marah, dooqaysak takmeeniih taaqubeenim **faxximtam**
kulli wakti faxximta. Alzheimer's Association axcuk: mari maaqo takmuh
hawweenam xiqta, akmek tanoonuh yaadan, hinnay makkol mangomuk xallina,
maaqo wayti kaskasso le marat dhibe kassiisaah hando habissa
([Alzheimer's Association on food and eating](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/food-eating)).
Ta yaab Alzheimer's Association, NIA kee Alzheimer's Society baahen:

1. **Gaba luk takme maaqo ogga.** Gaba luk solisiyyah takme maaqo, sanbuusa
   xaloota, bakkoor cado, kuluc xaloota, lomiina gersi xaloota hinnay laqo
   luk bisle barokli, xanxafto maleh takmuh sinam cata.
2. **Xanxafto kee sahan baxsa celtaamal bicisa.** Intih wagittaano maaqo kee
   sahantok baxsa abuh gibdaak xiqta. Qadho sahan baxsiyye le gaali amol hee
   marah catta. Bica le suwar kee gofaali haba.
3. **Inki hinnay namma maaqo inki wakti ogga.** Mangom buuloysaanam num
   dhibuh gaca.
4. **Maaqoh wakti kassot heya.** TV alifa, tarabeesa qafuura abina, wakti
   ogga. NIA axcuk: sinam mayafxina kaadu gidba mabiina.
5. **Daggo maaqo kee makkoota daawa luk ogga.** NIA axcuk: daggo maaqo
   kee kalori le caxat takmeem ogga num meqem mayakmik
   ([NIA tips for eating well](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/tips-caregivers-helping-people-alzheimers-disease-eat-well)).
6. **Miyo yaaqubeenim catta.** Dagar miya hinnay gersi miya caxat ogga ayrok
   addat, tohiik amol lee mangom luk tan maaqo: caxacaxa, maraq, caano kee
   qasira. Num lee mayafxik, NIA axcuk: qasira, lee le maraq, caano, hinnay
   kafeh fantaaxaw bica.
7. **Laqo waga.** Num maaqo hinnay miya mangom laqot tanam ma-kassisaak
   xiqa.
8. **Kuubbayiita kee xanxafto meqoysa.** Sahan baxaabaxsale sahana, kaxxa gaba
   le kankaana, alifti le kuubbayiita takmuh tayse. Bicaay-leelit maxallina.
9. **Takmeem karru kee liciimuh gibid maleh bica.** Maaqo daguk qilsisa
   hinnay laale maaqo ogga. Num qunxuyta hinnay googo leeh gabaatek,
   dawoytot gaca.
10. **Gidbah akmiyam haba.** NIA kassiisuh: Alzheimer's dambirta marat
    maaqoh fantaaxaw daahirak daggoowa.

### Dawoytot gacnoonuh yan aalaamoota

Hando habaanaam, maaqo gibdaanaam, daalif hinnay gibdaano maaqo dagowtam
tascasse. Alzheimer's Society axcuk: ikok hinnay ilkoh dhibe, sammi cabux,
alila kee dawoytah baxsa maaqoh fantaaxaw daggoysam xiqta
([Alzheimer's Society on appetite](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/poor-appetite-dementia)).
Lee waytih aalaamoota NIA kassiisseemik: af qoxle, intih gexsit, wadhayih
dhaqba mangom kee kas bayle
([NIA on common medical problems](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/common-medical-problems-alzheimers-disease-information-caregivers)).

## Dawoyto luk yaaba

Ku maaqo hinnay gersi numih maaqo kaxxa baxsa tunkutteek duma, dawoyto hinnay
maaqoh taaxigo luk yaaba, baxaabaxsaluk sukkar, kullitti dhibe, liciimuh
gibdaano hinnay duma hando habaanam tekkeek. Kaskasso le numuh, kulli ayro
kah yakmeeniih yaaqubeenim akkanuk essera, kaadu suplemen cattaamal gaca.

Googo tekkek, hinnay kas-bayle daahirak yemeetek, intih gexsit mangom hinnay
lafa-luk gacanik dawoytok askariina dooqaysak geya.
