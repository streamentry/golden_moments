Ku kassiisi koros hinnay atu kaxxa kicni numuk koros tableem meysi le caagida. Ah ku cattiyaah doktortoh xagana abtam kee mannal massoowatam kassisiyyah tan waray. Num kas-baysit luk yanim kee wayam ku maawarta. **Doktorto dibuk kas-baysit xagana yaaxigem duuda**, kas-bayiyya bahta mangi caagid kas-baysiyya hinna. Intarnet gaba-xinto hinnay isih aban xaganat ma-kibalina.

## Kas-baysit naharsi astooti macay?

[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/10_signs) 10 naharsi asta warrayte. Kullim idaltut nummah tan koros lih qimbissaah baxsa warissa. Ni yabti inoh:

| Dooqaysiyyi faxximta asta | Idaltut mango koros |
| --- | --- |
| Kulli ayró maaqatta kalissa kas-bayiyya, qilsi caagida bayissiyya hinnay inki caagid takraarisseh essaaranta | Wacyikalah migaq hinnay xagana bayisseh sarrak kassitaanam |
| Meytatto hinnay caagid qokol gibda, tamqem tamo hinnay billo dacrisiyya | Buxo billol dagó hoxxat geytimiyya |
| Buxol, taamal hinnay dariifal taaxige taamoomi gaba kaqussiyyi gibda | Wacyikalah mikroweyv hinnay TV fooca dacrisiyyah cato faxa |
| Ayró, soso hinnay yan aracaak kas bayiyya | Ayróh ayro bayisseh sarrak kassitaanam |
| Fantaaxaw meklit, akriyo hinnay kuluul baxsaamak cusub gibda | Intih biyaakik tamaate inti-koros |
| Qangarat cusub gibda, yabal fanal solu hinnay aala hoxxah migaq yeceen | Wacyikalah meqe qangara geyiyyat gibda |
| Aala baxsa luk tan aracat xisaanaah elle sugte arac kas-kalah geyaanam | Aala wacyikalah bayisseh elle sugeenim kas-luk geyaanam |
| Meklit dambiyya, lakqo hinnay qaró dacrisiyyat | Wacyikalah dambiyyi meklit abaanam |
| Kaxxa koboxit, taama hinnay agattah marak gaba kalanam | Wacyikalah buxoh kobox hinnay agattah aracal gexaamih fayxi wayti |
| Qasbi kee mariinot koros, kas-bayiyya, shakkisso, meysit kee cato geyaanam | Inki inoh taama abbaah, taama korortek kaxxa naaqu geyaanam |

Inki asta dibuk kas-baysit maaqatta hinna. Kaxxa caagid **too numuk nummah sugtemak koros**, haysit kulli ayró maaqatta gibdah abbaaha.

## Nummah tan idaltu kee kas-baysit fan baxsa macay?

[National Institute on Aging (NIA)](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/memory-loss-and-forgetfulness/memory-problems-forgetfulness-and-aging) warissaah mangi idalti caagid sassah kassitaanam, aala wacyikalah bayissiyya hinnay billa bayissiyya yubleenim. Tah dagó kas-bayiyya kinnim takke. **Kas-baysiyya idaltut nummah tanim hinna.** Kaxxa kas-bayiyya kulli ayró maaqatta gibdah abta, makaado dirkisiyya, silki abiyya hinnay buxo geyiyyat.

NIA doktortot yabaanam meqeet yacee, num:

- Inki esseroora takraarisseh esserik
- Taaxige sugen aracooral bayik
- Tamo hinnay fan-warrayti dacrisiyyat gibda luk
- Wakdi, mara kee aracooral kas-bayiyya mangak
- Is dacrisiyya cabik, xalay-kalah maaqo kommoosiyya, amo-calso wayti hinnay salaamatal gibda luk

Gari maray **dagó kas kee meklit fantaaxaw (MCI)** luk yani yane. Toh kay idaltih marak gaba luk kas kee meklit gibda luk yan, maaqatta kulli ayróh dacrisak. NIA MCI Alzheimer's naharsi asta takkem duuddaah, MCI luk yan mari inkih toh ma-gexa iyye.

## Kas-bayiyya aka macat baaha duuda?

Kas-baysit kalah mangi caagid kas baxsa duuda, gari dacrisiyyah gaba leeh. NIA warraytam:

- **Gidih buurti, kule hinnay afkaxxa gibda**
- **Xalaftoh biyaakita**
- **Qaxu-biyaak kee cato**
- **B12 fitamiin dambiyya** hinnay meqe maaqo kommoose wayti
- **Xinto-dambi**
- **Alkoqol hinnay xalafitah qansar**
- **Biyaaka, dhiig-xisaab hinnay buxxi** samiiqil
- **Amo biyaakita**, concussion innaanom
- **Kaxxa qaxu hinnay kaxxa xinto**, taama cabiyya hinnay baqalti rabi innaanom

Ayti kee inti gibda kas-bayiyyaay axceenim duudda; ni [hearing guide](/brain-health/hearing/) wagita. Toh sabbatah doktortot dooqaysiyyi kaxxa maqaane leeh. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor) warissaah innah, doktorti dhiig xagana abbaah gidih buurti, fitamiin dambiyya kee biyaaka dooqaysaanam. Meqe [sleep](/brain-health/sleep/) kee qaxu-biyaak dacrisiyyi meqe sabbaba.

## Qilsih tamaate kas-bayiyya kaxxa caagidda

**Kas-bayiyya saaqoot kee ayrooral qilsih temeetek, safar-doolat askamem faxxinta.** Delirium kinnim takke. [VA describes delirium](https://www.va.gov/geriatrics/pages/delirium_when_should_i_worry_about_delirium.asp) kaxxa qilsi biyaak kinnim yexceeh, numuk qafiyyat doktorto seeca hinnay 15 seeca iyye. Astooti:

- Kassit qokol gaba-kalah korora
- Kassis cato wayti
- Kas fantaaxaw bayiyya
- Xinto wayti mangiyya hinnay qilsih cato geyiyya

Tah inkih numul tamaateem duudda, kas-baysit luk yan mara garih abbaaha. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) warissaah innah, kaxxa sabab biyaaka (asqasit biyaaka idaltit kee kas-baysit mara luk mango), dhiig fantaaxaw, sukkari biyaak dambiyya, amo biyaakita kee gari xalafta. Cato qambaltan wakdi, too num lih sugaah, qilsi qangarat sabri kaah acaya. Mangi esseroora me-esserina, yaceen xalafita waraagsita.

## Doktortoh xaganat mannal massowtaa?

Meqe xagana meqe waraagak qimbisa. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/visiting-your-doctor) aala baahaanam yexce:

1. **Koros warrayti** kas, qasbi kee mariinot koros, baxxa luk tan warrayti kee elle qimbiste wakdi
2. **Sugte kee tan qafiyyat caagida**, buxoh kas-bayiyya warrayti luk
3. **Kulli xalafta kee dukkaani xalafta**
4. **Fitamiina kee qokol maaqo**
5. **Ku esseroora**, makko xagana abaanam, manna wakdi tekkem kee awli wakdi natiija tammoowaam

Tonnaah too num taaxige buxoh num hinnay katays baahaanam cato le. [NIA explains](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-symptoms-and-diagnosis/how-alzheimers-disease-diagnosed) doktorti too num kee kay buxoh num esserak qafiyyat, xalafita, kulli ayró maaqatta kee mariinot koros dooqaysiyyi takke. Doktorti tonnaah:

- Kas, caagid meklit, kassis, xisaab kee af xagana yacee
- Dhiig, asqasa kee aka xagana abak aka sabab wagita
- Qaxu-biyaak kee aka kas qafiyyat wagita
- Samiiqih rasmi CT hinnay MRI abbaaha
- Kaxxa doktortol fanta, geriatrician, neurologist hinnay neuropsychologist innaanam

NIA Medicare Annual Wellness Visit kas xagana kaxxa caagid kinnim yexce, tamaat caagid ugsaanam meqe wakdi kinni. Doktorti qilsih abbaaha xagana kas-baysit xaganaay gaba mayantah. Usuk naharsi taamaay kaxxa xaganah fan gexa, xagana inki ayrók mangom abtaah duudda. NIA kas gibda le mara 6 ilaa 12 alsi koboxit doktortol gexaanam meqeet yacee.

## Caagid xannal ugsaanam

Aka numuk meysit luk tektek, salaam le araca kee dibuk sugan wakdi doora. Tuble caagid warrayta, usun qafiyyat cato kinnim warisak, koros waytam hinna. Umman qafiyyat wagiyya fayxi wayteem meysi kalta. Too num meklit elle culaah, kaxxa numu inoh kaal yabá. Ni [communication tips](/caregiving/communication-tips/) ku cattam duudda.

## Doktortol yabá

Kas, meklit, qasbi hinnay kulli ayró taamoomit cusub koros tublek, doktortol xagana aba. Qilsih kas-bayiyya tektek, 15 seeca hinnay numuk doktorto seeca. Gidih xagana abaanam dacrisiyyah gaba le caagida bahta, kas-baysiyya tektek, meqe wakdi geytimaah meytatto kee cato geyaanam duudda. Isi qafiyyat dacrisa; ni [caregiver self-care guide](/caregiving/caregiver-self-care/) wagita.

Ta fooca umman waray kinni, doktortoh xagana hinna. Ni [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/) wagita.
