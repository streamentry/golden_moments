Kas deebit kulli kaxxa baaxol yimixxigeeniih, mangom ku kas qusbaay idaltu kasik kalnam deqsita.
Atu kas-kassow le num dacrisaah tekkek, hinnay isi kas fakkon meqeemiik gactek, usun
qokol yallu duudaanam esseritinam celta caagida. Cilmi nummaak yubleem taamal edde
tani, fayxi xayyowtam kee maqaane aalle waytam inkih edde tani.

Ta yaabi umman yaaba, xakkiimih yab hinna. Nanu le [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/) wagit.

## Kas deebit kas-kassow waasisaa?

Kas-kassow waasisa deebi yanimih sumaq num magenninna. National Institute on
Aging (NIA) kompiyiter kee internet deebit kas yayseenaah iyyiinam gabbataanaah,
suuqut yandoore kas baritto applikeshinit qilsis qande yubleeni innaah tan maqaane
leemih diggoowe sumaq aalle waytoom baxxaqqissa ([NIA on preventing Alzheimer's](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-and-dementia/preventing-alzheimers-disease-what-do-we-know)).

Woo yab deebit tuuma hinna iyyaanam ma-kicna. Ken qiimati kaxxam fayxi,
garay kee kulli ayróh caadaay edde yan mara kas-kassowuk dacrisaanam hinna.

## "Xayi gaba-kaltu" iyyaanam macay, macah kaxxa caagid yakke?

Taama barittak, woo taamal kaxxam tayse. Cilmi abbaasite mari taamat barittoh
maqaane axcuk seecan. Kaxxa esserooy gaba-kaltu **fersam** yaabaanaami: celta
taamoomil (xayi gaba-kaltu) hinnay kulli ayróh manol, garay kasit kalu hinnay lakqo dacrisuu
innaah tanem (xer gaba-kaltu).

Sanat 2016-l kaxxa qilsis baritto abte kumpanitte meqe sumaq axcuk xayyowte qilsis
wagitte. Mari barite taamal yayseemih mangi sumaq edde yubleeni, xayi taamal yayseeniih
dagoo sumaq yubleeni, kulli ayróh manol yayseemih kaxxa dagoo sumaq suge. Mangi
qilsi gersi qilsis caagid kee carba sugeemih sabbatah diggoowe meqe fakkon yaceenim ma-duddaana
([Simons and colleagues, Psychological Science in the Public Interest](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100616661983)).

## Kompiyiter kas barittoh qilsis macal yabta?

Cochrane qilsis, meqe qilsis taturte maroor ittal culusak, 12 aydo kee wadir
suge kompiyiter barittol yabte:

- **65 sanat kee wadir yan fayya le idaltu:** 1,183 num le bicaal 8 qilsi aben. Sumaq
  dagoo hinnay kaxxam dagoo quwatah suge. Barittok sarrah dago maqaane tanem duuda,
  wadir edde raqtamih diggoowe sumaq mayan
  ([Cochrane review, healthy older adults](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32104914/)).
- **Kas fakkon dagoomih kalte mara:** 660 num le bicaal 8 qilsi aben. Num kas-kassow
  culem mayublinna, sumaq kaxxam quwata aalle wayteemih fakkon culum ma-duddaana
  ([Cochrane review, mild cognitive impairment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30864747/)).

## ACTIVE qilsi macal gace?

ACTIVE kaxxa US qilsi 1998-l qimbise, 65 sanat kee wadir yan 2,802 idaltu isi
manol sugeeniih kas fakkon kaxxa dambiyya aalle wayte mara inkih qimbise. Mara
sidiica buttal baxsiyyeenih (sissik baritto, kas baritto, hinnay fakkon baritto) hinnay
tu-aalle wayte buttal culusen. Baritto tabna maroor 5 elle caba 6 aydoh addat suge.
Mangi maroor duude marih garul **tanoomisiyye maroor** 1 kee 3 sanatih wadir xayyowen.

Sissik baritto kaxxam sissik waktil ubleeniim kasit kalu kee fakkon culum, mari yaysem
wagittaamal kaxxam daabisiyya abak sugeenim kinni.

Sanat 2026-l cilmi abbaasite mari 2,021 mara Medicare reekordil 20 sanatah xayyoyse ([ACTIVE 20-year analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12884427/)).
Yubleeniim baxxaqqa luk taamal edde tani:

- **Sissik baritto kee tiik yayse tanoomisiyye maro:** tu-aalle wayte buttak
  kas-kassow dagoomih edde yubleeni (hazard ratio 0.75, kaxxam 25% dagoo yakke).
- **Tanoomisiyye aalle wayte sissik baritto:** dagoo ma-succa.
- **Kas hinnay fakkon baritto:** kas-kassowul kaxxa baxxaqqa le maqaane mayublinna.
- **Kulli baritto butta tu-aalle wayte buttâ luk taturteek:** dagoh gactam celtak,
  statistiks baxxaqqa ma-xayyowte.

### Ta sumaqih xaquut

- Usuk **xakkiimi dacrisul sissik baritto kee tanoomisiyye maro** callal cula,
  umman kas deebit inkih hinna.
- Baxxaqqa le fakkon yemeetem **dagoo butta** (qimbóh maroor duudeeniih tanoomisiyye
  geye mara), inkih sissik butta hinna.
- Kas-kassow **Medicare reekordih kuud** luk qimmisen, numuk num wagitaanam hinna.
  Qimbóh marak 25% taturte mara Medicare Advantage leemih sabbatah reekord inkih aalle
  wayteenih caben.
- Qimbisen waqdi marin kas dambiyya ma-aallinna. Ta fakkon **kas-kassow cule marak**
  deebit wagittaamal tu ma-yabta.

Ta kaxxam meqe sumaq cilmi bicisa caagida. Tu applikeshin kas-kassow waasisa iyya
sumaq ma-yallu duuda.

## FTC Lumosity wagittaamal macal iyye?

Sanat 2016-l Lumos Labs, Lumosity ginnaani, Federal Trade Commission (FTC)
deedal xaagiyyah qidbaane abteemil 2 miliyon doolar yaxceenih yengeddeleni. FTC
iyyaanam kumpani saynis sumaq aalle kal isi deebit taamal kee barittol yayseeniih,
idaltu kas-kassow, dago kas-bada, kee Alzheimer's waasisaanaah iyyeenim edde yubleeni.
Amri kumpani meqe saynis sumaq aalle kal ta yab wadir ma-abnamal yengeddeleni ([FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program)).

Buxah maraay baritaanam: kas dacrisaah hinnay kas-kassow waasisa iyyak yabta deebi,
applikeshin hinnay barittol kaxxam kas-leela yakkoonay garci.

## Kas-kassow cule numuh deebit qokol yallu duudaa?

Meqe ayróh manoh edde amxagoonaah duuda, toom kaxxam kaxxa caagida. Kaxxam meqeh
cilmi aben caagid **kas walalalisu**: buttat aban fayxi le deebit, walal, cusiyya,
qangari deebit, puzzles kee muusika, kaxxa waktil buttal num dacrisak aban taama.

Sanat 2023 Cochrane qilsis 37 qilsi kee 2,766 dago kas-kassow le maral abteemi yuble
([Cochrane review of cognitive stimulation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39804128/)):

- kas fakkon qilsil **dagoo, xayi waktih maqaane** (quwatah tan sumaq);
- **marih yab kee garayul kaxxa baxxaqqa le yayseene**
  (fayya le sumaq, dagoo qilsis addat);
- manoh maqaane, kas kee caadat **dagoo maqaane**.

Maqaane tabna aydoh barittok sarrah qimmisen, xer waktil edde raqtam dagoo qilsi wagitte.
Kas walalalisu buttat aban caada kinni, toh sabbatah tabletil callih deebi gaca
numuh inkih ma-cula.

Sanat 2024 Lancet Commission kas-kassow le marah taamoomi kaxxam kaxxa caagidaay,
fayxi kee maqaane dacrisaanam edde yaaba ([Lancet Commission](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(24)01296-0)).

## Deebit nummaak macah meqe?

Meqeh dooran waqdi, deebit xayyowtam duuda:

- **Fayxi.** Nummantin rubuq saaqat fayxil gacaanam kaxxa meqe caagida.
- **Garay.** Garbul gacaanam walal dambiyya yan waqdi ittal gacaanamih fantaaxa celta.
  Wagit [how to play together](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).
- **Caada.** Kulli ayró inki waktil aban caada meqe salaama yacee.
- **Kaskaso.** Num abuu duuda taamah deebi dooran waqdi, usuk isi gaba yubleenaah,
  qilsi fakkon hinna.
- **Walal.** Suura hinnay kas-le qangara cusiyya fakkal gaca. Wagit
  [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) hinnay
  [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).

Wacaysi: waktih saaqatta, iskóor, kee kas carba celta hebeltoomik yaaxeenim waasi.
Num garci culek, caba. Sissikuk cawlaanam hinnay itta garbul dafan, kaxxa meqe gaba-kaltu.
Nanu le qokol [choosing apps and games](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) celta daabisa luk yan.

## Golden Moments elle gaca fantaaxa

Golden Moments deebit, celtaanam [picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/),
[picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) kee
[large-print word search](/games/word-search/), kaxxam fayxi kee ittal gacan waktih
abina. Saaqat aalle kal kee iskóor mali. Usun daawo **hinna**, kas-kassow
**ma-waasisaana** eleele kisaah ma-duddaana. Hebelto deebi xayyowtu waytam baxxaqqa
luk koh naaguraanam nanu kicna.

## Xakkiimik waraaris

Ku kas hinnay atu dacrisaa numih kasik meqe kaltoomik garci leek, applikeshin
kicnaanamak xakkiimik garay. Xakkiimi daawo geya caagid wagitak, meqe saynis
sumaq luk yan caagidil yaabam duuda, dhiig-fayya kee ayti wagitaanam innaah tanem.
Wagit [Can dementia be prevented?](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/) gersi yabih.
Sissik kas-kassow hinnay sarrih kaltoomik, sissik xakkiimi qokol gey.
