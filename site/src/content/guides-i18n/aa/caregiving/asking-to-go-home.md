"Arik gaco faxa." Maggo buxaaxih ta yaaba aabbiyya kaxxa gibdi yab kinnuk geytima, xannal ku dacarsituh tan num isi dabqih baroocal daaciseh yan waqdi. Ta haala kaxxa mayso le, dementia-h kulli gaddal takkem duuda, kaadu ta yabát sabab-le, meqe gital gacsa heela tan. Ta gilqo ta yaabak macah kinnim, iyyan qokol, tohik ugutak meqe taamoomi, kaadu nagaynan heela diggoysiyya qaddoysa.

## Macah dementia-luk yan num arik gaco korisaa?

Maggo waqdi, "ari" iyyaanam dabqak sa nagaynan kee wadirik tan aagaru. Alzheimer's Society qaddoossah tan ginah, toh dumi manô dabqak nagaynan hinnay meqem faxiyya kinni. Dementia dumi taaxige dabqoobik taamoomi hebeltoh ginah caraysisuh takke, kaadu num isi baxaabu kassiiseh yan waqdi hinnay baxiitik dumi aracal yaninkah kassiisam duuda.

Ta yab kaxxa cusub sababik ugteemik xawissiyya takkem duuda: qulul, xabco, suwwayti faxiyya, biyaaka, dacaytu hinnay yabxi, carooriyya, xiinaano, hinnay gibdi culumluk sugaanam. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-agitation-aggression-and-sundowning) biyaaka, culum, inkiinot sugaanam, maanoona kee daawayti giddik ugutta heela agitaasiyoh sabab kee caalat kinnim qaddoosa.

## Arik gaco faxa yiyan waqdi maacah iyyuu?

Yaabak sa aabbinat yab gacsa. [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) num bayuk yanim qaddoosiyyak xayyow iyya: ken aagaru numma, tohik ugutak dumaak meqe ruffo kee nagaynan kaah cula, tohik lakal xannal kallaaysa.

Meqeh qokola qangaraara:

- **Aabbinah migaq ece.** "Arik tan mano koo xagteh tanik mabla le."
- **Nagaynan edde yaaba.** "Tah nagay nan araca, anu koo lih suga." [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/anxiety-agitation) kaxxa salfan yaaba qaddoosa "Koo arike nagaynan leet tan."
- **Kassit kallaaysa.** "Ku arik yoh maqaissaay. Ku kusina mannak sugtee?" Dumi manok esseraanam duma aysuk geytimta toh kaah yoo esseriyyak sa.
- **Inkih hebelto taama abina.** "Dumaak shaahi qunxu kubbaaya dafeynaay," hinnay "Ta foocâ carqoobik yoo qokoltaa?"

Meqe af kee gaba gabaat haytam, usun gaba xagut faxeenik, yaabak kaxxa meqe caalat tacee.

## Macak waytaamuu?

- **Madaadaru hinnay qaddoosiyya.** "Akkuk arik tan" hinnay "Tah 40 sanatak ku dabqa kinnim" dementia qambalissam gibdinaano tacee, kaadu bayuk yanimih qambalisaamat gacta.
- **Esseró gibdi haytam.** "Makassiitannaa?" hinnay "Adreesi aydol tan?" gibdinaano korsa.
- **Kurrut afaafa alifiyya.** Usun yewqeenik, guranayto koboxsaay ken lih gexa. Inkit qunxu giclo gexaanam kaxxa faxo karsa.
- **Sissikaytu way.** Arik yaabaanankah gaba kaah cula, tohik lakal yaaba korsa.

## Ta waqdi qokola taamoomi

Yab kaak oggolteek lakal, sabab-le taama aagaru baysiyya kalik aysuk gacsa.

1. **Arik kee buxah boola wagita.** Xafot alifiyyat maqaise ("Woo buxah daqaara wagita") esseró kalik. Taleefoonit hinnay tablet-il, [Photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ku buxah boola qunxu giddi-masso taamâ fanah korsa, kaadu boola ku aalaatit raqta.
2. **Arik yaaba.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) dabqa, kusina, quliiyi kee ayroorat esseró tacee, hebeltoh bayisso le xawishshok kalah.
3. **Kaxxa kobox le muuziqa kallaaysa.** National Institute on Aging muuziqa agitaasiyo karsam qaddoosa. Wagita [music activities](/activities/music/).
4. **Qunxu maxco hinnay qeebi ece.** Qulul kee xabco giddih takkem duuda. Shaahi qunxu kubbaaya hinnay kobox le maxco dabqah mano kassisa.
5. **Giclo gexa hinnay derak dafe.** Gurusiyya kee ayrok diifu qokola, aysaak carra carrak duma.
6. **Taaxigen salfan taama ece.** Saaqo bicisiyya, carqoobi massoysiyya hinnay qasbi lee kariyya arik taama kassisa.
7. **Kullim sissikaytuk karsa.** TV alifa, culum kee diifuk xayyow, kaadu inkih [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) abina.

Inki heela kaltek, wadirlik gaca. Inkit salaamat dafeynaan meqe baxsalem kinni.

## Sabab biyaakittam wagita

| Wagita | Abaanam |
| --- | --- |
| Qulul hinnay xabco | Qeebi kee qunxu maxco kaah ece |
| Suwwayti faxiyya | Suwwayti gita kallaaysa, kaxxa culum maleh |
| Biyaaka hinnay gibdinaano | Raba, gaba hebelto aracal hayta hinnay qoxxaani wagita, xakimik garca |
| Carooriyya hinnay yabxi | Carqoobi massoysa hinnay daqayti diifu korsa |
| Culum hinnay marô kobox | Culum maleh yan dabqâ fanah gexa, TV alifa |
| Xiinaano hinnay daqarsiyya | Rammata, diifu xayyowsa, qunxu muuziqa faka |
| Carra carrah takkem | Sundowning gilqo guba wagita |

Alzheimer's Society kassaara tacee ta essero ayroorat aydoole takkem wagitinaankah, maaqok duma, daahoorik lakal, hinnay carra carra. Heela aagaru meqem bicisa.

## Xannal numma kaltaam maqaanee?

Tah kaxxa yab kinnuk geytimta, kaadu meqe buxaaxi baxsa le gital gacta. Alzheimer's Society inki gital maqaaneh tan iyyaanam mayan iyya: numma hinna yab marat kaxxa culum cula, tohik taagah kay maqaane fanah wagitaay konnow le gital suga. Penn Memory Center xakimtî mabla numma duma kinnim, kaadu xannal meqe yab numma qambalisa gaba kalik callat akkuk geytimtam iyya, kaadu ken manaqqoofina.

Maggo dacaris mari baris gita geyte: nummâ ruffo kee kallaaysot qimbisa ("Bar tahal sugna, kaadu anu koo lih an"), arik daqarah madaadaru kalah.

## Carra carrah kullim takkek macah abuu?

Carra carrah duma ugtam hinnay kaxxa gibdinaano gactam sundowning iyyaanamak taaxige. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-agitation-aggression-and-sundowning) aysuk meqe saaqaat heela, ayroh diifu kee taama, carra xiinaano xayyowsiya, kaadu kafeeyin kee alkoohil carrak wayya qaddoosa. Carrak duma diifu fakiyya qokola. Nanu bicisne [calm late-afternoon activities](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/) meqe qokol le.

## Awqu faxeenik nagaynan diggoysiyya

Arik gaco faxa iyyaanam gicloh awqu karsam duuda. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering) dementia lih yan marak taban numuk leceeyi num inki waqdi bayam duudan iyya.

**Dumaak bica:**

- ID hinnay xakimtô gaba-damiit migaq kee taleefoon numbre le, kaadu carqoot migaq hayya. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/coping-alzheimers-behaviors-wandering-and) MedicAlert kee Alzheimer's Association Safe and Found heela qaddoosa, toh lacag faxam duuda.
- Afaafih bilbila hinnay kaxxa alifiyya qusba hinnay guba hayya. Inki num inkiinot ma'alifina.
- Fooca dumi boola, kaadu gexaanam duudan aracoocik tiya bicisa, dumi dabqa hinnay taamâ dabqa innaah tanim.
- Meqe quliiyik garca, kaadu taleefoon numbre kenah ece.

**Bayeenik:** amol qimbisa gurrusiyya; Alzheimer's Association maggo mari 1.5 mayil giddol geytiman iyya. 15 daqiiqat ken geytam kalteek, 112 seeca kaadu num dementia lih yanim qaddoysa. Num qibnayto fanah radak tekkay tektek duddah 112 fanah seeca.

## Aydoolat xakimtô fanah seecuu?

Essero hinnay bartaabaro gabbi-gabbiik ugtek, hinnay saaqaat kee ayroorat kaxxa baytat gactek xakimtô fanah seece. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-changes-behavior-and-communication/alzheimers-caregiving-managing-personality-and) gabbi-gabbiik korisa haala, kaxxa biyaaka hinnay daawayti korsat lakal, duddah wagitaanam faxximtam iyya. Gabbi-gabbiik ugutta bartaabaro delirium takkem duuda, toh [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) kaxxa sissik cato faxxam iyya; suwwayti biyaaka idaltot kaxxa sababa kinnuk geytima.

Kaadu xakimtô duma geytimaanamal meqe kassiisa hayyaanam qokola, biyaaka, qansaru, xiinah gibdinaano hinnay daawayti heela kullim edde geytimtamih taagah.
