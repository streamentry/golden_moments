Dacayro qari cubbusiyya raaca, ruffa, amixxiga akiiy mango yakke sissikuk waytam dudda. Dooriyya numuh faxxiimiyya kee faxa luk xayyowtam cato geytuh kinni. Buxa mari kinnim maca kaxxamih moomini akiiy kulli num inki waqdi tature gufto kinni.

[Alzheimer's Association guide to long-term care](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-term-care), isi [residential-care checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) kee [Dementia Australia's residential-care guidance](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/care-options/residential-care) numuh faxxiimiyya, dacayse mari, taama, dariifa kee mekla uble waytam yascasseenih. A xuko inki qari baxsale doorissa akiiy arooca le mihso kee maali fayu arcisiyya badalta.

## Dacayro qari cubbusam faxximtaah ma waqdi yaaxigeenii?

Awayih num faxa maca cato yaaxigeenim kee usuk buxa addal akah xayyowtam dudda cubbus. Numuh nagaya, ayro dacayro faxxiimiyya, ayyunti manó kee faxa cubbus. Dacayro cule num akiiy isi cata hayse marih qaafiyat akah xayyowtam dudda cubbus.

Num ma waqdi guftam faxximtamih inki waqdi akiiy rorta mali. Num ayyunti dariifa dooris dudda, akiiy ossotina cato luk buxa addal geyiyya faxa dudda. Num luk yaaba, faxximta waqdi, isi dayla, ayyunti-dacayro akiiy dacayro massoosa luk yaaba. Guftoh gideennal, maca cato faxximtam cubbus.

Num faxe waqdi edde anuk dooritiyya geya. Isi maca faxximta essera: arac, maaqo, afa, caada, diini taama, yaqbude taama akiiy buxa mari kee kataysa boobiyya. Isi faxa, inkinnah dacayro faxxiimiyya milaagimak sugta waqdi, rorta kabxissa cato hayis dudda.

## Qariyya gexennal naharat maca yaaxigeenii?

Naharat, num faxa cato kabu rorta qimbis. Tah ayro taama cato, qaafiyat dacayro, ayyunti angaarawa, yaqbude afa akiiy barra addal waqdi aba gadda tekkem dudda. Aaxigem waytek, qaafiyat akiiy ayyunti-dacayro mihso essera.

Gexennal naharat qariyya seec. Numuh faxxiimiyya xayyosoonuh duddaah, qari geytimtaah, ciggilta taama macaay, kee isinnek maca xaagu faxximtaah essera. Tajaajila kee meklah kutbe le xaagu essera. Gacsa, maali cato kee idini caalat arac arac milaagima, tohih arooca xaagu elle xiqsissam faxximta.

Dacayse mari dementia dacayro baritto leeh, dacayro ikraaro ma waqdi bicisaah, kee buxa mari kusaaqitte ma waqdi osisoonuh duddaah essera. Ayro caalat ma waqdi taama takkay, maca taama xayyowtaah, kee mari ma waqdi dooritiyya akah geytamaanam essera. Kasiyya dacayro qari inki dooro kinni, laakin kulli num faxissa; kulli arac xayyosiyya cato faxximta.

## Boobiyya waqdi maca uble waytaa?

Takkay akah dudda waqdi, mango qariyya gex. Naharat waqdiik qellisa, tohuk lakal, baxsa le waqdi akiiy maaqo waqdi gexam dudda essera. Nammahayto boobiyya ayro caalat mango yab kee baxsa le dacayse mari geytuh cato hayis dudda.

Dacayse mari mari luk ma waqdi yaabanam ubul. Massakarih mari seecaah, gacsa akah fanah waqdi haysaah, kee ayro dooritiyya edde geyaah? Mari raaca geytimaah, isi fayxi kee dudda luk xayyowtam taama leeh? Taama rorta, maaqo araca, barra araca kee cakkih araca ublam dudda essera.

A numuh faxximtam detalitte ubul: cediyya le astooti, daaco fanah caddo gita, yaqbude uwwaytitte, qayni araca, afa kee caada cato, kee kacan mari ublamih akke. Meqe qimbo arac bicissam bicissa ayro dacayro ma waqdi taama kinnim yable mara.

Essereyyo kee gabatitte cassaam kah cata hayisak, aamine le kataysi akiiy buxa num gey. [Alzheimer's Association checklist](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/2f238875-3f5d-447e-adcc-dd9fc07f828b/alzheimers-dementia-choosing-residential-care-ts.pdf) tuubleemih cato hayis dudda. Ubulte xaagu yaabih cato kinnimih, laakin qari ku doorissa qamma kinniimik hinna cubbusin.

## Dacayro faxximeenimih maca essereyyo cata hayisa?

Baxsa le waqdi dacayse maqaane ma waqdi taama takkay, dacayse mari numuh ayro caalat ma waqdi baritaanam, kee isi faxxiimiyya milaagimem waqdi maca akah tekkem essera. Iyyi dacayro ikraaro cusaa, kee buxah maca caddok takkem ma waqdi essera. Num maaqo, numuh dacayro akiiy yaabiyyi cato faxak, a cato ma waqdi taama akah tekkem essera.

Buxa mari boobiyya, barra gexiyya, qaafiyat boobiyya, taama kee num yaqbude uwwaytitte geyiyya dudda essera. Qari dementia baxsale taama le waqdi, usuk maca baritto, taama kee ayro dacayro xayyosam essera. Taamah migaq bicissam bicissa, mari ma waqdi dooritiyya edde aniyya missoolitte essera.

Meklah detal le xaagu, maca edde aniyya, kee mekla ma waqdi milaagimtam essera. Aagagiitte nagga luk kawwis, kee maali akiiy madqa caalat caddok waytek, arooca le mihso essera. Baxsa le arac akiiy baaxo xaaguk geyteemik, tajaajila edde aniyya akiiy geytimta hinnan yaamine wayta.

Numuh kinnimiyya, angaarawa akiiy cakkih geyiyya ossotina cato faxak, ni [LGBTQ+ num dementia luk suge catoh xaagu](/caregiving/supporting-lgbtq-person-with-dementia/) ubul. Faxa migaq, boobise mari, numuh dacayro kee mihso caalatih essereyyo edde an.

## Guftoh ma waqdi ma cato hayis akkeeh?

Num faxe waqdi edde anuk dooritiyya geya. Qari gexiyya, wadir maaqo akiiy dago waqdiik raaca geyiyya, geytimta kee numuh meqe waqdi, dariifa yaaxigeenih cato hayis dudda. Num qarih uwwaytu, saro akiiy ayro caalat dooris dudda.

Numuh idini luk, faxximtam xaagu dacayse mari luk barsa. Tah isi faxa migaq, yaabiyya maqaane, ayro caalat, faxa maaqo, faxa taama kee isi raaca akah geyah cato haysa xaagu tekkem dudda. Xaagu dagoom kee dacayroh faxximtam akah tan. Num isi xaaguh yaabiyya addat geyam faxximta.

Qari idini haysek, yaqbude uwwaytitte gey. Dacayse mari numuh qari ma waqdi yaqbude takkay, gexiyya sahhaanak akah geyah tan essera. Taswiir, faxa baco akiiy yaqbude saaqata, kulli buxa missilsiyya bicissak mango faxximtam dudda.

## Guftok lakal ma waqdi edde aniyya ma waqdi xiqsissam duddaa?

Dacayse mari luk taama inxic, kusaaqitte ma waqdi osisaanam kee iyya seecam faxximtam barit. Num boobiyya kee taama ma waqdi yaceenim essera. Inkinnah mari mango dago boobiyya faxa; gersi mango waqdiik raaca geyiyya faxa. Isih meqe tekkem ubul kee inkih milaagim.

Dacayro faxxiimiyya kee faxa milaagimtam dudda. Num kee dacayse mari luk dacayro ikraaro cusso, caddok waytek essera, kee cata haysa qusba xaagu osis. Num usuk axciik amixxige akiiy kaxxam luk biyaakite waqdi, dacayro koysa usuk cab; [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) akah yable kalah, usuk amixxigiyya usuk qaafiyat cato faxximta.

Qari dooriyya kaxxa dooro kinni, kee qimbo dooriyya umman waqdi meqe akah waanam dudda. Xaagu urru, num edde an, ayro dacayro essera, kee guftok lakal yaabiyya foyya xiqsis.
