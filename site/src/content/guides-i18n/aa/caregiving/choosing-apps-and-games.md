App store-itte naba mara, kassi hiyyaffo kas waytiyoh kutaabita kuukittek kibul le. Qarre meqeh bicisiyyeenih yanin. Qarre qafiyatih maxcoosa, kuukit mokoroh korisa saaqat, hiyyaffo faylaata kee xulfo gaaboysa aalle way. Ta gooba hebelto appi isih farratta inna koh xayyossa. Nanu gersi taamoomi ma-darajisna hiyyaffo ma-korisna.

## Kas wayti appi doorak duma macal farratu faxxiima?

Appi macal koh abam faxxaam dumaak madaqa. "Inah kuuka" iyyaanam baxsa le caagid akke dultah tan, inki meqentah meqe doora gersi meqentah uma doora takkem duuda.

| Meqenta | Manna inna lita | Macal gubuh gurraana |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xabbicowti kas-koris | Sitta cato, baxsimsi, salfa le xiinisso, qangara kuukite | Bicsita caddo, tambullek raagta masoosite, saaqat gibdaali waytam |
| Kassiisi | Widir masoosite, eglisiyye kassi, barteyni wakhtih koora | Ku nafih masoosite, fakki le yab qokola, mokor esseroori mali |
| Deqsiyyi | Qusba uybula, deqsiyyi sonko, rooxi, qusba caxaa | Qimbis kee cabba salfah tan, xululumma waytam, amqeeli waytam |
| Sittallih aniyyi | Sitta luk kuuko hiyyaffo yab, sittallih hiyyaffo fiidiyoh | Namma marah bicissen, yabih fakki le xayyow |

Kas wayti le marah bicisen "kas-koris" wagitta xiina, barte mara geytimak kobor addat aban taamoomil geytimta, yab, qangara kuukite, xiinisso, koora kee meqe taamoomi lih. [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005562_can-cognitive-stimulation-benefit-people-dementia) cindha meqe baxsalu geyak yuble, xiinoolah caddol baxsimak. Woo mara-luk, baxsa le porogiram inki appi luk inki caagid hinna, tohul hebelto appi mango eglah taamoomik inki gaba kaxxaay hayyaassa.

## Appi atu qokolta numih duddal maqaaneh gelaam mannah aaxiga?

Mannaah gaba kalan kibaala, caddoh migaq hiyyaffo xannal gexaamih aysuk. "Inki caddol" yan namma num inti uybula, aabbi, gabah dacris, af kee kooftoh dacrisi caddol gibdi baxsa leenim duudan. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) widir doora kee ta saaku duddal kibaalaay, maysoh caddol hinna wacyi caddol egegan meqem tescese.

Meqe doora takkemih astooti:

- gibdi seksec maleh qimbisan hiyyaffo gubuh cuggaaqan;
- kiban, yab xayyossan hiyyaffo widir yab yaqben;
- mako ken ma-gibdissu wayta;
- saaku geytimak cabbaanaah wacyi leen.

Uma doora takkemih astooti:

- "Aduude wayyo" hiyyaffo "Ah kasaara kinni" iyyaanam aggiliyyaanam;
- macal tuquban egega waanam;
- daabro, aymeqiyya hiyyaffo koofto derre cabbaanam;
- atu inkih abtaak sa gaba kale waam.

Daabro asta kinni, derre kobbiseenim hinna. [what to do when they say no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) wagit. Appittek dereh caddoh malaatoh, [early-stage](/activities/early-stage/) kee [late-stage](/activities/late-stage/) taamoomi wagit.

## Bicisiyyah meqe caagidik macal gura hiyyaffo kela?

Deq le, salfa le, naba marah bicisen suurat guraay, gibdaali xayyossa hebeltoomik kalita.

| Macal guran | Kak madaqatan |
| --- | --- |
| Naba masoosite kee kutaabat, baxxaqqa le uybula | Cindha calaamat, qubuh tan koofto |
| Kooftoh caddol inki baxxaqqa le abto | Mango qafiyyitte, liistiyye hiyyaffo mango-caddoh ayfaaf |
| Saaqat waytam, hiyyaffo alsa duddan saaqat | Xaloot lakqo, sissik mayso, "ku saaqat duma" |
| Lakqo waytam, hiyyaffo xabbicowti qokol | Poyiintitte, mayso, tabsi, caddo, "kassih caddol" |
| Makot deqsiyyal gaba kalaanam | Bukkutu, caxxa-le X calaamat, "Mako" maxcoosa |
| Naba mara, baxxaqqa le addunyah masoosite | Cindha xayli suurat hiyyaffo kaskaso |
| Internet maleh taamita, akaawunti maleh qimbisa | Dumaak gulgulusso, culaanih hiyyaffo oobisaanih faxximta |
| Dacris kee cabba salfah tan | Kuuka gexa hiyyaffo appi qagita iyya uybula |
| Faylaata mali, inki wakhti hiyyaffo alsih gaddi baxxaqqa le | Faylaata, saanduq doora, sissik xayyow |
| Nammah hayto num cuggaaquh salfa le | Inki numuukiyya, mokor bicisiyye |

Faylaata kee xayyow mako luk tuqubaanih salfah tan.

## Appih sirri mannah kibaala?

Appi macal gaaboysa, iyyi lih eglisa, kee tet baysa duddan kibaala. Kooftoh caddol haytaak duma:

1. **Store-ih sirri xaloot kutaaba.** Google Play-ih [Data safety section](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) kee App Store-ih [privacy information section](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102399) appi gaaboyse kee eglise duuda xulfo kutaaban. Nammay kumpani bicise maraa a xulfo ken nafi yaceem tescese, tohul qimbis gaba kinni, audit hinna.
2. **Akaawunti faxxam essere.** Appi masoosite hiyyaffo salfa le kuukitte qellisa kaxxaay, ku migaq, email hiyyaffo baaxo geyte saaku yaaxigeenih sabab mayana.
3. **Idnitte kibaala.** Sitta cato kuuka dacayri, silki kutaaba, mikrofon hiyyaffo inkih tan masoosite faxxaa? Faxxiime waytam inkih alsa.
4. **Faylaata kee lafat kibaala.** Faylaata kee lafatooti appi mannah taamitak yan xulfo gersi kumpanittul rube duudan.
5. **Masoosite mannaah gexxaam egega.** Buxah maroh masoosite haytek, kooftoh addal raagtaa hiyyaffo server-il gexxaa? Ken baysa duddanaa?
6. **Bayisso kibaala.** Sirri qadaada hiyyaffo xulfo koborul mannah xulfo kee akaawunti bayisanam kibaala. Xulfo bayise waytam iyya appi kaxxa cakkisi faxa, masoosite hiyyaffo qafiyat caagid liti kaxxaay.
7. **Sirri qadaada kutaaba** macal gaaboystan, macah, anni wakhti, kee iyyi lih eglisan baxxaqqa le kutaabat geytuh.

### HIPAA kas wayti appitteh xulfo keltaa?

Titta addat magelta, mango maray tasgaddibem. U.S. Department of Health and Human Services [explains](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) HIPAA qafiyat sartan, qafiyat qokol eedeeneenit, kee ken taamah eglah tan maral taamitak yanim baxxaqqaysa. Mango addat ku nafi taamah culussa xulfo makelta, a eedeeneenit hiyyaffo ken egla a appi yaceenim akke waytek. App store-ik oobta "kas wayti appi" HIPAA-k derreh tantam duudda, qafiyat yabak tekkek kaadu.

### HIPAA galte wayta appitteh qafiyat xulfo macal kelta?

Federal Trade Commission-ih Health Breach Notification Rule mangom gelta. 2024 sanatal FTC [finalized changes](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule) a qadaada qafiyat appitte kee HIPAA galte wayta tekinolojiyil taamitak yanim baxxaqqeese. "Breach" iyyaanam idni maleh eglisiyaanam gelta, hacking caddol hinna, kumpanitte tunkuttoowe mara kee FTC aaxagissuh faxximta. Toh umaami tekkeh lakat qokol doora kinni, appi faxximtam duma gaaboysah qahdi hinna.

## Xiina kee sumaq maxcoosa mannah kutaaba?

Sumaq maxcoosa xabbicowlih kutaabaay, naba qahdiitel kaxxa cakkisi aala. Esseroori essertan:

- **Macal baxxaqqalsuk xiiniseen?** Inki tabsi porogiramih xiina appi addih inkih tan kuuka inki innal taamitak yanim mayassakaxxa.
- **Iyyi xiiniseen?** Qafiyat le naba maral geytimte meqenta kas wayti le numul taamitak raagtelem duudda.
- **Macal qagiiten?** Kuuka addat aysow geyaanam saaku mano, yab, wacyi hiyyaffo isih aniyyah baxsa luk tan.
- **Xiina mannak gibda le?** Randomized controlled trial (RCT) appi taamisa kobo kee taamise wayta kobo sittal qagita. Meqe bicisiyye kinni, kinnih immay inki cindha RCT meqe sumaq hinna. Eglite wayta xiinisen marak mango xiinoolah gur.
- **Biyaakuta korisaanih maxco litaa?** Mokoril cindha aysow yuble xiina appi kas wayti assakata, kalta hiyyaffo urrisaah sumaq hinna.

National Institute on Aging-ih gooba [cognitive health and older adults](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/brain-health/cognitive-health-and-older-adults) NIH-ih gaddi luk xiinisen naba kas-tabsi caddo baxxaqqaysa, tohuk lakat kompiyuter kee online kuukite kassi kee kas aysisaah tan iyya maxcoosak kutaaba mara kelta. Gaddih tan kas-tabsi appitte woo xiinal aben tabsi innaah tan cindha luk taniyoomuh meqe sumaq mayana iyya. Nanuk [brain games and the evidence](/brain-health/brain-games/) kee [what research says about preventing dementia](/brain-health/can-dementia-be-prevented/) kutaabak aaxaguk duddan.

### Mako luk tan maxcoh gadda caddol yan ceelallo macal?

January 2016 sanatal, FTC Lumosity iyya "kas-tabsi" porogiram bicise mara luk [announced a settlement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/01/lumosity-pay-2-million-settle-ftc-deceptive-advertising-charges-its-brain-training-program) aben. FTC a kumpani meqe sumaq maleh, ken kuuka idaltol kas kalalu assakataah mild cognitive impairment, dementia kee Alzheimer's biyaakutak kelta iyyan maxco luwon iyye. Kumpani $2 million acayuh sitta geete, sarrimih amaxcoh meqe xiina sayinsih sumaq liyom faxximta. Ah gaddih yab sumaq akke waytam kassisa meqe ceelallo kinni.

### Caxxa calaamat le qangaraara macal?

Appi hiyyaffo faylaata ah iyya wakhti cakkisi aala:

- dementia hiyyaffo Alzheimer's "kalta," "wadir gacta," "assakata" hiyyaffo "urrisa";
- xiina, cula mara kee qagiten caagid migaq maleh claims "clinically proven" iyya;
- "kas caddol" hiyyaffo kassi lakqo yaceek xannal gexam;
- kas "abura," "fayya haya" hiyyaffo "tabsa";
- maroh maxco sumaqah taamisa.

Maxcok agartu waytek, atu qokolta numih daktar essere.

## Golden Moments anni gaba caddol gelta?

Golden Moments salfa le eglah taamoomi xayyossa, kassi mokor, dawo hiyyaffo daktarih malaato hinna. Ni kuukitte, [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/), [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/), [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) kee [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) innah yanin, wacyi kee yabih bicisen. Usun kas wayti ma-gactan, ma-dadaalisan, ma-kaltan hiyyaffo ma-qagitan, nanu kas hiyyaffo samaduk hebelto maxco malla.

Nanu kaadu gaddi maleh yan appi biqisne, [Golden Moments for iPhone, iPad and Android](/app/). Naba taabilal xabbicowti masoosite sitta cato, atu doorta buxah masoosite lih kuukite kee internet maleh taamita masoosite kobo luk, akaawunti, faylaata, saaqat hiyyaffo lakqo maleh. A qokol gexso tet wagitta: tet App Store-ih sirri calaamat "Data Not Collected" iyya, kee [app privacy policy](/app-privacy/) masoosih kobil mannal dacrisaanam baxxaqqaysa.

Nee nummaysaanam aysuk nee farrattaanih nagay. Ta goobal tan qokol gexso taamisa, ni [privacy page](/privacy/) kee [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer/) kutaaba, atu qokolta numuh maqaaneh gele wayta hebeltoomik cabba, neetiyom tekkek kaadu.

## Qusba appi geeraran meqe inna macal?

Namma safta addat sittal geeraay macal yekkem kibaala.

1. **Dumaak bicisa** ni [tablet and phone setup guide](/caregiving/tablets-and-screens/) taamisak, salfa le caddol qimbisa.
2. **Wakhti cindha luk haya,** kibanto, yab kee deqsiyyi kibaala, meqe wakhtil cabsisa.
3. **Daabro bahsek cabba,** manga caddol korissiyye luk tekkek kaadu.
