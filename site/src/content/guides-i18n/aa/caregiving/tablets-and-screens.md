Tablet suwuroor, muuzika kee xabbicooba koboxissooti elle garab abaanah meceem takkem duuda. Kas bayisiyyam takkem duuda: gaba garcit aka appi faka, wacyi yab yayyaaqe, kudoobi dago takke, hinnay agale koboxissoh fanal ditak gexa. Atu abta cato massosiyya tamahak maggoom meceesa.

Tahammil iPhone, iPad kee Android addal yan xisne massosiyyah taamitan. Menu migaaqa aala gexsiisiyyah addat kee aala maaqabaabay baxsa luk sugtaamih sabbatah, baxiisa tekkek, ku aalah yan Apple hinnay Google garayto katay. Massosiyya isi gabanut aba, koboxissoh gaba kal qimbisaanuk duma.

## Kas-malkit le num tablet hinnay silki aagure lem xiqaa?

Numuk numul heele wayta. Maggo mari isi garih num luk yanim tablet aaguraanam kacan, usun qaxmeqeh isi gabanut aagure waytoonuuy. Luk koboxissaanamal qimbisa, tii appi fakkuk, dago daqiiqah. Kacanu kee yab bahteek, suga. Kas-bayti hinnay qaxu bahteek, suwur album, kitabsite xawofi hinnay muuzika keenih maysisiyyam tayse. Ni [printable activities](/activities/printable/) tii gaba celta.

Tablet aala silkiyk xabbacooba: screen kee suwuroor kaxxa, kaadu toh numih sissik qokol telefun hino.

## Tablet hinnay silki tii appil mannal dacrisiyyo?

iPhone kee iPad addal Guided Access haysita, hinnay Android addal app pinning haysita. Lammaanum koodo oobissaana fanah screen tii appil dacrisan, toh gaba garcit hinnay xabik aka num fakkewamih gidah.

### iPad kee iPhone: Guided Access

[Apple's Guided Access instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111795) wadirik:

1. **Settings** faka, **Accessibility** tibbixa, tohuk lakal **Guided Access** tibbixa kaadu faka.
2. **Passcode Settings** addal, Guided Access fakiyyah koodo haysita. Face ID hinnay Touch ID idnissom duudda.
3. Faxximtam: **Display Auto-Lock** addal, screen manna sissikuk alifam door koboxissoh wagtit, agale fanal dita akke waytuh.
4. Fattaah tan appi faka.
5. Garih xaloota sidiica adda tibbixa (iPhone), iroba xaloota (iPad), hinnay Home xaloota warrayte aalol.
6. Faxximtam: screen fanti gaba xagtaamih cato dacrisiyyah maro aba. **Options** (hinnay **Session Settings**) tibbixa xabaazah xaloofi alifah hinnay screen korsa waytuh.
7. **Start** tibbixa.

Koboxisso kabxissuh, woo xaloota sidiica adda tibbixa, fakiyyah koodo oobisa, kaadu **End** tibbixa. Apple warrayte gexsiisiyyah addat lamma adda tibbixaanam warse.

**Kaxxa meceela:** Apple Crash Detection kee Emergency Services (sissik meceela) Guided Access gexsaamal sugeewaytam warse. Num sissik qokol telefunuh faxe waam silkil Guided Access gexsak maraagina.

### Android: app pinning

[Google's Android help on pinning screens](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9455138?hl=en) wadirik:

1. **Settings** appi faka.
2. **Security** (hinnay **Security & location**) tibbixa, tohuk lakal **Advanced**, tohuk lakal **App pinning**. Geewaytek, ku aalah abnih garayto wagita.
3. **App pinning** faka. Ku PIN, sumbulo hinnay password fakiyyah faxximta.
4. Fattaah tan appi faka. Screen fanta fanah iro xaba kaadu tibbixa Overview fakkuh (hinnay 3-button navigation luk Overview tibbixa).
5. Appih mablo irobay, appih aala tibbixa, tohuk lakal **Pin** tibbixa.

Sumbuloh gexsiisiyyah kalqisiyyah, iro xaba kaadu tibbixa. 3-button navigation luk, Back kee Overview xaga kaadu tibbixa. 2-button navigation luk, Back kee Home xaga kaadu tibbixa. Tohuk lakal ku PIN, sumbulo hinnay password oobisa.

## Kulli iPhone hinnay iPad-ih xabbicooba heele yanii?

Yeey. Apple-ih Assistive Access warrayte garayto xabbicooba tiyal korisa. [Apple describes it](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-iphone/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ios) elle teeti warissam kas-malkit le maray kasik gaba cabti leeh iPhone isi gabanut aaguran xabbacowisuh massoowteem, kaxxa meceeloolay screen-il tani kee fanti koboxisso luk. Apple [the same feature on iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/assistive-access-ipad/set-up-assistive-access-devcd5016d31/ipados) xayyowta.

- **Iyya massossaam.** Apple yaaxige num, buxah num hinnay koboxisse num num taaxigem, appitte kee meceelo doortuh qoklam tayseeh warse.
- **Elle qimbisaanom.** **Settings > Accessibility > Assistive Access** fan gexa, **Set Up Assistive Access** tibbixa, tohuk lakal **Continue**.
- **Appitte.** Yabooriyya le appitte doorta. Garih Apple appitte xabbicooba meceelo le, kaadu maggo aka appitte isi gideh koboxowtam duudda.
- **Garayto.** **Rows** doora (garci yaafiqeena) hinnay **Grid** doora (suwuroor kee aala xayyowta kaxxa meceela).
- **Fakiyyah koodo.** Baxsale Assistive Access koodo haysitan fakkuh, awquh hinnay massosiyya korsaah. Apple cusub Apple Account haysitaanam kas-tacem koodo baytek aala massosiyya cusbisu waamih gidah.

Assistive Access kulli ayro isi aala aagura numuh meceeta. Guided Access hinnay app pinning koboxissoh xayi wagtiy xabbacowtam takke.

## Kudoobi kee suwuroor mannal kaxxowisso?

Kudoobi kee garaytoh gide kaxxowisa, tohuk lakal xaloofi screen addal raaqam wagita.

**iPhone kee iPad.** [Apple's font size instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102453) elle baxsaleennal, **Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size** fan gexa kaadu xabbata xaba. Kaxxa kudoobih, **Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text** fan gexa. Apple kaxxa kudoobi garih xaloofi xagiyya kas biyakiseem warse, tobbaaxek massosa.

**Android.** [Google's text and display settings help](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/11183305?hl=en) elle baxsaleennal, **Settings** faka kaadu **Font size** gulgula, tohuk lakal xabbata xaba. Screen kullim kaxxowisuh **Display size** gulgula. Kaadu **Bold text** fattisom duudda **Accessibility > Display size and text** addal. Garih appitte tamah makatayan.

Koboxissaay [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) kee [Find the Same](/games/find-the-same/) celtal, dago suwuroor hinnay doora haysitom duudda, toh screen-il tu xabbacowissa.

## Wacyi yab kee fakkintaane mannal dacrisiyyo?

Faxewaan appitteh wacyi yab alifa, kaadu koboxisso wagtit Do Not Disturb haysita.

**iPhone kee iPad.** **Settings > Notifications** fan gexa, **Notification Style** guba tan appi tibbixa, kaadu **Allow Notifications** alifa ([Apple's notification settings guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-notification-settings-iph7c3d96bab/ios)). Telefun, wacyi yab kee digir dago wakti alifah, Control Center faka kaadu **Focus** xalootak **Do Not Disturb** faka ([Apple's Do Not Disturb help](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105112)). **Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb > Set a Schedule** addal massosom duudda.

**Android.** Screen irobak guba fanah xaba kaadu **Do Not Disturb** tibbixa, hinnay **Settings > Modes > Do Not Disturb** fan gexa ([Google's Do Not Disturb help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9069335?hl=en)). **Notification filters** addal iyya mari kee appitte taturam duuddaam doortam duudda. Massosiyya aala maaqabaabayal baxsa le.

## Xabbicooba garayto mannal heelo?

Dago taaxigeen appitte tii garaytol haysita kaadu garih tanim abara. Tii massosiyyah tanim hinna; giddiyya xabiyya kinni.

- **Sidiicak laca appi doora.** Suwuroor, muuzika, video telefun appi kee tii koboxisso celtaam.
- **Qimbisaan screen-il haysita,** kulli wagti elle sugte aalal, kaadu aka tanim nammay hayto screen hinnay folder fan haysita.
- **Xabbicoy, fanti calaamat le wallpaper haysita,** tii kulla hinnay taaxigeen suwur celtaam, aala yubleemih gidah.
- **Faxewaytan appitte baysisa.** Dago appitte kas bayti dagossa kaadu data kobox dagossa.
- **Assistive Access** Apple aalol haysitaanam wagita aala koo cato abuh tekkek.

## Xabaaza kee aawa manna takke?

Aala acuwak duma meceeta xabaaza haysita. Guided Access luk, xabaazah xaloofi alifam duudda koboxissoh fanal korsita waytuh. Qukoh cato aala edde taniim kee taamitam wagita.

## Tablet elle sugtu waytam aala elle?

Cato kutukutu kee baditeewayta bilqah aala kaah haya.

- **Cato kutukutu hinnay case** teblet taraabazal solisuh tani num tu tabbaaxem mafaxxa, kaadu xagiyya xabbacowta.
- **Tii bilqa xayi aala** baditeewaytam, kulli ayro elle gexsan caxaara table-il. Tablet kulli wagti bilqam le kaadu tii aalal geytima.
- **Ifi kaxxa dagosa.** Screen ifi le daaqotik baxsale aalal haya.

## Screen caxale wagti manna takkem meceeta?

Tee xali doodo mayana. Tumaare xayi garboobay gexso, muuzika, buxah taama, yab kee baroora gexsaamal screen koboxisso koboxowtam meceeta. Barhinih sissik kas biyak bahtaamih sabbatah, screen koboxisso ayroh fanta fanah haysitaanam meceeta. Wagita [calm late-afternoon activities](/caregiving/late-day-restlessness/).

Tablet num luk koboxissaanamal haysitaanam, kalqisiyyah hinna, garih koboxisso dacrisa. Ni koboxissoh tatur [playing games together](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) mannal abaanam fasirta.

## Aalol ken sorkocobaxih dacarsiyya mannal dacrisiyyo?

Appitte koboxissam dagosa, kaadu qafiyatah seera dacrisaam ma-haysitina. U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) [explains](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) HIPAA seera fanti telefun kee tablet addal tan qafiyatah data dacrise waytam warse. Maggo aalal, HIPAA fanti appitteh addal oobisan data madacrisa appi doolat fanti doolin xagik ambaate waytek.

HHS elle kas-taceennal:

- faxewaytan hinnay num maaxigo appitte xaba, kaxxa meceelah "bilaash" tanim;
- aala gexsiisiyyah idni acuwe waayti appi celtaamal, kartool celtaam akke waytek.

Kaadu tamah wagita:

- **Fakiyyah koodo** aalal haysita, kaadu safe aalal kutaaba haya.
- **Idni gulgula** suwuroor, camera, microphone, telefun maqroor kee aala gexsiisiyyah, kaadu appi faxewaytam alifa.
- **Appi koboxissam wagita** bilqisak duma. Ni tatur [choosing dementia apps and games](/caregiving/choosing-apps-and-games/) sorkocobaxih dacarsiyyah doodo luk yani.
- **Buxah suwuroorih cusubow.** Num kacan suwuroor duma garab aba, kaadu appi elle dacrisam yaaxiga. [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) addal, doortan suwuroor isi browser addal raaqan.
- **App addih meqenta alifa** hinnay gati aala xaba num aagura aalal.
