Nammay kee mango afa yaaxige mara, dimentiya qangooru geytima innaa cedda korsimte waqdi, yaabak kok korsoonuh duddah. Mara inkinnah naharal bartaan afa yabbireenim qemboonaah, gersi mara maysabbaana. Qimboh gabbat taama faxa afa awayih elle meqe taama faxaah esser, tohuk lakal ta num elle yabbireem katatta.

[Alzheimer's Society afa dooriti num akke leemiik kok korsoonuh duddaah, ta num mango waqdi tascasse afa tascasseenim xayyoysaanam warisa](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/how-to-communicate-dementia). [Alzheimer's Association naharal barte afah udduurim yaabak takke korsitteh inkih taniimih warisa](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications). Ta korsitte kulli numut maadda hinna num caddoh taniim tamixxige weemih maabinno.

## Anu maca afa tascassa waanaay?

Ta num ta walal taama fan yabbire faxa afa esser. Kay dooriti caagidil, araca innaa ayro waqdi kok korsoonuh duddah. Usuk afitte korsimek, woh ekraaro innaa atu cedda afa fan yabbiruh kaa dirqissiy wayta.

Isin usuk doorita afat yabbireenik, yaaxigeenim qangooru tascasseenim kee gacsa akah tacen waqdi xayyoseenim. Inkih qangooru dagoom yaaxigeenik, woh waris, asta, tu ayti innaa taswiirite tascasse. [Alzheimer's Society afa caagidi miraaci](https://dementiaconnect.alzheimers.org.uk/about-dementia/stages-and-symptoms/dementia-symptoms/dementia-and-language) afa duuda ayro ayro akke leemiik kok korsoonuh duddaah, taagah daggoysi, lakka innaa biyakiyyaamal geytimtam warisa.

## Kulli ayro walal taama maca kaalih catta?

Mango waqdi yaaba miraacitte xiqsita, ta num afa akak gabbata qokolu osis:

- **Yaaxigeenim qangooru kee migaaqitte tascasse.** Ta num innaa usuk elle amantem num kulli ayro aytiitte, buxá maraa kee qagaaqagititte maca qangooru tascasseenim esseren.
- **Yabbiraah, ubul kee.** Kubbaay asta xayyos, nammay doorititte uquuk, innaa yaaxigeenim taswiir innaa tu ayti tascasse. Meqe qangara geytima gibdah tekke waqdi, asta kaalih catta.
- **Qambaala.** Ta num ankacisuh kee gacsa tacuh waqdi xayyos. Kulli maxco gabakalissooway innaa inkih essero mango afat kaadu esse wayta.
- **Inki waqdi inkih caagidi.** Xer maxco kulli afat gibda tekke. Qunxa maxco yab, qambaala, tohuk lakal ossotinah faxaa innaa mayfaxxa cubbus.
- **Ankacisiyya kee able wayta cubbus.** Mabla meeko, ankacsa caagidiitte, meqe ifu kee sahha arac yaaba ceddam sahhali abaanah.

Ta num kawsa doora faxek, faxxinta yuktubeenim caagidi kay afat tan innaa maytan esseren. Qellisa faxiimi, idini innaa dawa miraaci fan, otomaatik maqnisso elle amanta wayta; faxxinta caagidi qellisa farmoytit diggos.

## Ta num doorita afat anu mayabbire waytek maca abam faxximta?

Kulli ayro walal taama fan, nammay afa yaaxige num inkih yamaate kaa faxaa innaa mayfaxxa esseren. Usuk gersi num elle maqnisak faxaanam ma tekkelle; asta, taswiirite innaa ken nammay sinni afa doora duddah.

Qellisa aydaadah innaa faxxinta caagidi faxa gersi walal taakuh, aydaaduuk naharat qellisa buxa innaa qellisa farmoyta seec. Ta num doorita afa waris kee afa catta doorititte taniimih esseren. [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services buxá maraa kee kataysitte faxxinta qellisa caagidi diggo le maqnisso ma duddaanah warisa](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/faqs/may-an-lep-person-use-a-family-member-as-an-interpreter/index.html); farmoyti kay doorititte kee fanat faxximta caagiiditte qaddossa. Ta num inkih yamaate num kaa faxaa innaa mayfaxxa doori, qellisa farmoyti faxximta afa catta bicisa.

Aydaada waqdi, ta numul toobokoh yab kee maqnisá num yabbire waqdi xayyos. Ta num essero ceddam kee isih kay qangoorut gacsa akah tacenik cubbus. Atu kaalih cattu waytek, usuk faxek dubuk ossotinah caagidi osis.

## Ma waqdi qellisa caagidi essera waanaay?

Afa gibdi waqdi gexah kok korsoonuh duddah, laakin sissikuk korsam baxsa le. [NHS num sissikuk mameesite waytek faxxinta qellisa caagidi geytuh xayyoysa](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/). Sissikuk yaaba waamo dubuk dimentiya caddo kinnih tekkek ma tekkelle. Ta num sissikuk mameesite, kaxxam biyaakite innaa ugut gibdah tekke waytek, fanat sinni qellisa caagidi farmoytit seeco.

Qunxaanah takke korsitte fan, atu able caagiditte uktubah ta num qellisa farmoytah esseren. Maca afa tascasse, maca kaalih kaa yaabak cateh kee daggoyse innaa biyaakite innaa hinnaah uktub. Tah walal taama fan qaddoysiyya, aybalaala maaha. Ni [qellisa aydaada miraaci](/caregiving/doctor-visit-with-dementia/) essero bicisa kaalih kaa catta.

## Gersi kaalih cattu mara maca warisa waanaay?

Ta num idini xayyek, usuk faxa mara fan qunxa warqa bicisa. Elle yabbireh kee kawisuh doorita afa innaa luqha, yaaxigeenim qangooru kee migaaqitte, kee yaabak ceddam kaalih kaa cata caagiiditte elle tan duddah. Qellisa aydaada innaa dacarsitto osiyya fan, inkih faxa num yamaatuh faxaa innaa mayfaxxa esseren. Qokolu fan faxximta sinni caagiiditte dubuk xiqsita wayta; ta warqa ceela mayan num akah ableem kaa waris. Afa warqa walal taama sahhalit qembo weemih kaalih cata, laakin gibda qellisa walal taama fan maqnisá num ma badqisso.

## Ta num doorititte akku qokola waanaay?

Afa num isinni caagidi tekke gide, laakin inkih num maaha. Kay qagaaqagitte, elle faxximta caagiiditte, kawsa doorititte kee inkih faxa mara kaa esseren. Ta num idini xayyek, gersi kaalih cattu mara fan gabbat qangooru innaa maxco uktubah, woh akku tascasseenim qaddos. Yuktubeenim caagiiditte xay, usuk elle idini yexe caagiiditte dubuk kusaqis.

Ta num digiruh, gersi mara maqnisuh innaa ceddam uybuluuh ma dirqissiyya. Mara walalitte inkih afat sahhala, gersi walalitte gersi afat sahhala. Inkih afak raagtaamal, ta num meqe taama, cayla kee dooriti kaxxam faxximta. 

Ni [yaaba miraacitte](/caregiving/communication-tips/) sahhalit yabbiriyya, ankacisiyya kee taswiirite tascassiyya fan kaalih catta. Aybalaalak lakal bicisaanama faxek, [dimentiya aybalaalak lakal qimbo taama](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) ubul.
