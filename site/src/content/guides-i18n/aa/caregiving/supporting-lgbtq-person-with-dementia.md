LGBTQ+ numih migaq usuk inkih nummay yeyyeemik inkih exxa. Usuk akah faxximtam essera, usuk doorite qangoor luk yoh yaab, kee inkih ayim numih caalata yaxxeeni dudda dooris. Numih migaq, kaxxa, buxah qokol akké sirri faxxa weelo, dumaah raaqitte akké gufne marih tasmitewaytam.

[Alzheimer's Society's guide to supporting an LGBTQ+ person with dementia](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/help-dementia-care/supporting-lgbtq-dementia) numih faxxa esseriyya kee angaarawitteh mabloota tasmitewaytaanama takkuh gacsiyya hayte. [Alzheimer's Association's LGBTQ+ dementia resources](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) kay addal, xukkok meqe sinni daryata geytuh meqeh takku ceelooxa kee tasmite waytam. Mari baxsa le; tama ascabitte num maqnisuuh walalol cuggeyna kinnim, laakin numidda geytuh xayyos waytam.

## Migaq, pronounitte kee sirri maca esseram duddah?

Kacan kee qalal sinni wakti dooris. Atu, «Anu kok doorne migaq kee pronounitte maay kinnon?» akké «Ku migaq akké ku angaarawittel, koh qoklaanam faxximta caagidi tam?», akkalah esseram duddah. Usuk exxa akke yaabem, mali yaabem akké wadir radeem duddah. Usuk daryatal faxximta sinni numih caalata essera ma.

Inkih ayim yaxxeeni dudda essera. Inki num isi kaxxa taama abeynit yaaxigeenim faxxa hayta, laakin isi numih meqe akké isi jinsih migaq isi buxah marih inkih yoh warissanam faxxa hayewayta. Nummay baxsa araca baxxaqisiyya qangoor dooriyya duddah. Atu aaxiget oday dubuk, ramad marih, gufne marih akké taama abeynitit isi migaq caagidi yabbixxen wayta.

Usuk doori raaqimteemih faxxek, maca yunkuttubem faxxam kee inkih geyaanam faxxam essera. Xayyoysiyyah, baxsa raaqitte luk baxsale migaq tekkek, taama abeynit isi doorite migaaqal usuk akah seecem duddanam akkalah fokkaqa. Numih sirri uwway; inkih qokolah taama abeynik inkih caagidi faxa ma.

Dooritte korsam duddah. Usuk isi doori cabem duddek, labsis kee qusba daryatah bicissa haytuh wakti elle kaadu essera. Ayim yabbixxem faxxam qaddoosen tekkek, mabloota tasmitewaytam. Usuk naharat caboh qaddose doori katay, kee daryatah mudiyya akké oday yabbixxen caadooxah esserol mihratle aroocah qokoluuh essera.

## Daryatah walaloolut inkih yengeleenam faxximtaa?

Usuk inkih aamineh kee inkih edde anuk faxam essera. Usuk qokolah buxâ marih, kaxxa, kataysa, ramad akké «doorite buxah mari» yengeleen duddah; tama ramad akke sinni num kinni immay, buxah innah qoklaanam. Inki mari ramadak geytimaanam, gersi mari akah maabin. Usuk isi angaarawitte isi qangooral cabis.

Tama elle faxximta essero essera: Inkih gufne duddah? Bicissa korsimek, taama abeynit inkih seecaanam faxximta? Inkih oday geyaanam faxximta? Xayyoysiyyah oday yabbixxenak naharat idini akké waraq faxek, ayim faxxam esseraay usuk gexsa. Kaxxa akké doorite qokolu kinnim duumuk caddoh madqah mudiyya maytakke; caadooxa aracal baxxaqissa.

Usuk doorite angaaraw migaq xoqoysima. Taama abeyni kaxxah «kataysa» akké «ramad» akah seecak, usuk kaxxah axcuk yaaxigeenim faxxek, usuk ruffa duddah. Qanxaffe kee idini luk sugte mablo, taama abeynit mari akkalah culusaanam kee ayti caagiditte yabbixxem duddaanam, keenit qaddosiyya duddah.

## Daryata xayyoysiyyah maca esseram duddah?

Daryatah qari, buxal daryata akké ayro daryatah taama dooriteh, usuk ayti essero esserm faxxam kee atu akah elle anuk faxxam essera. [SAGE guide to affirming services](https://www.sageusa.org/resource/10-tips-for-finding-lgbt-affirming-services/) xayyoysiyyah numih caagidi yabbixxen qilsiyya tekkem takkuh yabem qaddoose. Xayyoysiyyaay isi waktiyanah abtoota qaddo qangooral koh cabsa essera.

- Atu doorite migaq kee pronounitte akkalah raaqissaanam? Taa oday inkih taama abeyni geytam dudda?
- Nummay inkih gufne duddah kee kaxxa akké gersi qokolu luk kacan wakti geytam dudda?
- Qarih, saro kee numih daryatal numih dooritte atu akkalah baritta kee massissa?
- Taama abeyni LGBTQ+ magaalitte kee dementia luk sugte marih ayti barittó geyta?
- Qari num akké qokolu sirri akké massak sinni abtootah ceelo cabuh dudda? Toh wadir maca tekkem?

[SAGE and Human Rights Campaign Foundation guide to inclusive long-term care](https://www.sageusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/lei-consumer-guide-finding-lgbtq-inclusive-ltcc.pdf) gufna, qari kee taama abeynit abtootah yunkuttube siyaasat fokkaqiyya takkuh gacsiyya hayte. Tama 2021 sanat yunkuttubem kinni; essera esseroorah xoqoysima, laakin waktiyanah aroocah caado caddo kinni akah mabloota tasmitewaytam. Siyaasat kee taama aracal baxxaqissa.

Astaara, kutbe waraye akké inki meqe gacsa ayro-ayro daryata manol yakkeenim inkinnah xiqsissoowa ma. Usuk gufne faxxek, taama abeynit akah seecaanaam kee usuk kaxxa akké doorite qokolu marih akkalah gacsaanaam uwway. Usuk salaamat yakkem faxxek, taama abeyni luk kacan sittalluk walala essera.

## Inkih numih mano qokolu maca duddah?

Numih ayro-ayroh ascabitte, caadooxa, afa, qaada, diini, kataysi kee ayyunti essera. Usuk migaq walaloolu faxximta takkem duddah, akké usuk mano gersi exxah wakti aybulluh dooriyya duddah. Inkih gufnal migaq caagidih walala abewaytam, akké inkih LGBTQ+ num inki elle tekkem abte tasmitewaytam.

Inki mari naharat cinwa akké baxsale abto geyteeh, taama ceeloora cule duddah. Gersi mari tama ceelo mali akah yaaba. Usuk umaanek yoh cabsaanam akké yoh xiqsissaanam faxennah mabloota yabbix, usuk sinni caalat elle abbix. Aydaadih, taswiiri akké turaabek suge caagidi ruffa haytek, usuk solisak faxxam akké caagid korsim faxxam essera.

Atu kaxxa, kataysa akké buxah num tekkek, atu qokol isih faxximtam. Usuk atu akkalah edde anam faxxam esseraay, gacsiyya faxxek, aroocah dementia akké LGBTQ+ magaalitteh egla taama gey. [Alzheimer's Association resource page](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/how-alzheimers-impacts-different-groups/lgbtq-dementia-resources) ayyunti kee qokolah catoh gexsit osissa; ayim geytimtam atu tan aracal caglisa.
